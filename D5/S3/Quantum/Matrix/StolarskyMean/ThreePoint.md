# Three point Stolarsky inertia

For each parameter `γ` with `0 ≤ γ < 2`, the exponential triple
`(exp (-2z), 1, exp (2z))` has a choice of `z > 0` for which the Stolarsky
matrix has at least two positive eigenvalues. A weighted Toeplitz positive
plane proves the inertia bound after the homogeneous diagonal congruence.
