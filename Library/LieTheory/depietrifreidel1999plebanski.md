# The four nondegenerate Plebański sectors

De Pietri and Freidel give the four sectors of the Euclidean Plebański action in *Classical and Quantum Gravity* 16 (1999), pp. 2187–2196. The four branches of the full bivector field are represented by the coframe bivectors and their internal Hodge duals, each with an overall sign.

For a simple chiral triple the two signs instead record the sign of its cubic Urbantke density relative to the chosen metric and its chirality. The vanishing wedge Gram examples concern the degenerate locus of the chiral constraint. The topological sectors in the article are branches of the full nondegenerate bivector theory.
