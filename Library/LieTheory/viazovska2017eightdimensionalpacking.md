# The E8 lattice and the exceptional branching

In the integral part of the lattice, square norm two requires exactly two coordinates of magnitude one. In the half-integral part it requires eight coordinates of magnitude one half. The even-sum condition in that part is equivalent to an even number of minus signs. The Lean construction doubles the coordinates and checks the subsystem, simple-root and weight-fiber identities in those coordinates.
