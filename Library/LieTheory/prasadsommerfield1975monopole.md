# Prasad–Sommerfield radial profiles

## Verified locator

Prasad and Sommerfield, *Physical Review Letters* 35, 760–762.
[Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.35.760)

## Formal correspondence

For positive dimensionless radius, the functions \(K(u)=u/\sinh u\) and
\(H(u)=u\cosh u/\sinh u-1\) satisfy the radial Bogomolny equations. The
normalized energy includes both the magnetic and Higgs-gradient
contributions. On these profiles it is the derivative of
\(H(u)(1-K(u)^2)/u\). This primitive tends to zero at the origin and one
at infinity, giving the mass \(4\pi v/g\) for positive \(v\) and \(g\).

The electric and magnetic charge model uses the antisymmetric pairing and
the upper-half-plane action on the complex coupling. Its mass invariance
uses the specified classical charge and coupling normalization.
