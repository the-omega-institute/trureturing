# Reflection positivity of Boltzmann functionals

Theorem IV.10 and Remark IV.11 give reflection positivity for nonnegative inverse temperature when the signed interaction across the reflection plane belongs to the reflection-positive cone. Section IX.1 and Corollary IX.2 treat the Wilson action by splitting bonds at their midpoints. The introduction to Section IX contrasts that construction with the crossed-bond gauge fixing of Osterwalder and Seiler. For finite groups the Haar law on each independent bond is uniform counting measure.
