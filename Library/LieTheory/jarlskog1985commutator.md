# The quark mass-matrix commutator

The determinant expresses CP violation through the commutator of the two Hermitian mass matrices. In three dimensions it factors into the imaginary mixing quartet and the products of eigenvalue differences. The sign of the formula depends on the order of the eigenvalue differences, the quartet orientation, and the choice of scalar multiplying the commutator.
