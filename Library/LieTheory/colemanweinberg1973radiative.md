# Radiative symmetry breaking in scalar electrodynamics

## Verified locator

Equation (3.7), p. 1893, sets the fourth derivative at the renormalization
field equal to the quartic coupling. Equation (4.5), p. 1894, displays the
finite subtraction of twenty-five sixths for massless scalar electrodynamics.
On p. 1895, equation (4.8) fixes the quartic at the minimum, equations
(4.10) and (4.11) give the individual squared masses, and equation (4.12)
gives their ratio. [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.7.1888)

## Formal correspondence

The formal potential retains the leading gauge-loop coefficient in the
power counting where the quartic is of order the fourth power of the
electric coupling. Its fourth
derivative singles out the finite subtraction when that coefficient is
nonzero. Setting the renormalization scale equal to the minimum fixes the
quartic coupling to eighty-eight times the logarithmic coefficient. Holding
that coefficient fixed, the quartic running preserves both the vacuum scale
and the full potential. For the separate gauge–Yukawa coefficient, the
stability criterion concerns the exact function obtained by retaining those
quartic and logarithmic terms.
