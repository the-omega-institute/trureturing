# Zero moment and closed affine orbit

The primary version is [arXiv:math/0605756v1](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0605756v1),
the moment-map formula (1), Theorem 4 and its adjacent proof.
For a complex reductive algebraic group acting on a complex vector
space, choose a compact form and an invariant Hermitian product.
Theorem 4 says that an orbit is closed exactly when it meets the
zero set of the associated moment map. The criterion is attributed
to Kempf and Ness; this note does not attribute its origin to
the repository or to this application.

The exact consumer is [VII, Theorem 104.2](../../docs/develop/theory/QUANTUM_ORIGIN_BILATERAL_POSITIVE_RESET_RIGIDITY_AND_SCALE_OBSTRUCTION_VII.md).
There the reductive group is the actual complex algebraic image
of the connected compact pure-transitive group. A real invariant
product on the whole original representation extends to the required
Hermitian product. Real skew-adjoint infinitesimal generators have
zero pairing with the real pure vector, so its moment map vanishes.
The theorem is applied to that vector and that affine orbit.

The conclusion is closedness of the orbit, not equality with an
invariant quotient fiber, smoothness of its projective closure,
or a classification of its convex hull. The cited version's
primary HTML supplies the stated formula and criterion; no distinct
edition or inaccessible article is used as its substitute.
This is a citation-only reference, without reproduction of the
primary text or any claim of physical realization.
