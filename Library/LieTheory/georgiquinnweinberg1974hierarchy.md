# One-loop gauge unification

## Verified locator

Georgi, Quinn, and Weinberg develop the unified boundary condition and
renormalization of the weak mixing angle in *Physical Review Letters* 33,
pp. 451–454.
[Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.33.451)

Martin's *A Supersymmetry Primer*, version 7, equations (6.4.7)–(6.4.8),
lists the Standard Model and MSSM coefficients and uses
$g_1=sqrt{5/3},g'$ and $g_2=g$. The formalization takes minus Martin's
coefficients so a positive coefficient is the sign of growth of
$alpha_a^{-1}$ in the logarithm of the scale.
[Version 7](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9709356v7)

## Formal correspondence

The formal meeting condition compares ratios of intercept differences and
slope differences. The mixing-angle calculation uses a common reference
inverse coupling and the same scale for both electroweak flows.
It yields $3/8$ at equality of the normalized couplings and an offset
proportional to $alpha_{mathrm{em}}(b_2-b_1)log(mu/mu_{mathrm{GUT}})$.
