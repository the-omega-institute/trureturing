## Formal correspondence

Each of the five Cartan coordinates assigns $1/2$ to occupation and $-1/2$ to vacancy.
The even half-spin weights have zero, two, or four occupied coordinates.
Under traceless special unitary Cartan action, those sets give a neutral singlet, the exterior square, and the dual fundamental.
The four-coordinate weights equal the negative weight of the missing coordinate because the trace is zero.

The charge $X$ is five minus twice the occupation count.
The combination $(X+4Y)/5$ equals minus two thirds of the sum of the three color Cartan coordinates.
The sixteen rows of the weight table give this charge, both weak weights, hypercharge, and electric charge.
The cubic trace vanishes for every real linear combination of the five Cartan generators.

The five-dimensional special unitary group acts on the ten real coordinates of its defining space by real and imaginary blocks.
This action is faithful, orthogonal, and has determinant one.
The half-spin weights describe the action of the spin double cover at the Lie algebra level.
