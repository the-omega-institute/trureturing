## Statement

Standard attribution, not checked against the article: the rotations of
four-dimensional Euclidean space, identified with the quaternions, are the maps
$x \mapsto p\,x\,q$ with unit quaternions $p$ and $q$, and the pairs
$(p, q)$ and $(-p, -q)$ give the same rotation.

## Encoding and boundary

The repository theorem concerns the unit quaternions only. It states that for
unit quaternions $p, q, p', q'$ the maps $x \mapsto p\,x\,q$ and
$x \mapsto p'\,x\,q'$ agree on all unit quaternions exactly when
$(p', q') = (p, q)$ or $(p', q') = (-p, -q)$. It does not state that every
rotation of four-dimensional space has this form, and it does not use the
cited article.
