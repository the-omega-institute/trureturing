# Urbantke reconstruction and Plebański simplicity

H. Urbantke's article appeared in *Journal of Mathematical Physics* 25 (1984), pp. 2321–2324. The cubic conformal metric is the construction to which the article is cited. In the oriented basis $(01,02,03,23,31,12)$, the Lean formulation fixes the density coefficient to $1/12$ and derives the determinant and exterior-square identities over characteristic-zero fields, including singular triples.

Jerzy F. Plebański's *On the separation of Einsteinian substructures*, *Journal of Mathematical Physics* 18 (1977), pp. 2511–2520, supplies the chiral two-form context. In the formalization, complex simplicity requires the wedge Gram matrix to be a nonzero scalar multiple of the identity. Lorentzian conformal reality follows from wedge orthogonality to the conjugate triple. The scalar graph with spatial coefficient $1+i$ satisfies simplicity and has diagonal cubic entries $1,2i,2i,2i$, which have no common real conformal representative.
