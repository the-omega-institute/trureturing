# Type D Weyl double copy

Section 3 relates the symmetric four-index Weyl spinor to the symmetric product of two Maxwell spinors with a scalar normalization. Squaring the corresponding binary quadratic doubles the two principal-root multiplicities. The algebraic quartic square fixes a normalization at a point in a chosen spin frame.
