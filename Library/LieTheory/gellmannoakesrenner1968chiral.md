# Gell-Mann–Oakes–Renner pion mass relation

## Verified locator

Gell-Mann, Oakes, and Renner, *Physical Review* 175(5), 2195–2199.
[Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.175.2195)

## Formal correspondence

In the leading two-flavor chiral potential the quark mass matrix is diagonal.
At the identity the directional Hessian is \(B_0(m_u+m_d)\) times the squared
Euclidean length of the pion direction. With the positive per-flavor condensate
magnitude \(\Sigma=B_0f_\pi^2\), the common pion squared mass satisfies
\(m_\pi^2 f_\pi^2=(m_u+m_d)\Sigma\). The equality is exact in this tree-level
potential. The physical relation allows higher-order chiral corrections.
