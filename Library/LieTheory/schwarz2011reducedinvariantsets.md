# Compact real actions and complexification

The specific primary version is [arXiv:1109.3646v1, §5, The real
case](https://arxiv.org/html/1109.3646v1). The setting is a compact
Lie group acting linearly on a finite-dimensional real module.
The section identifies the compact group and action as real
algebraic and its complexification as reductive, and discusses
complexification of invariant real algebraic sets and compact orbits.

The exact consumer is [VII, Theorem 104.2](../../docs/develop/theory/QUANTUM_ORIGIN_BILATERAL_POSITIVE_RESET_RIGIDITY_AND_SCALE_OBSTRUCTION_VII.md).
It uses the actual complex algebraic image of the connected compact
group on the same original carrier. The compact real form is
Zariski dense in that image. The consumer separately verifies
zero moment and applies the Kempf–Ness criterion, attributed in
the [Losev note](losev2006kempfness.md), to establish closedness of
the particular affine orbit.

Proposition 5.1 and Corollary 5.2 concern reducedness and saturation
with additional principal or almost-principal orbit conditions.
They do not license replacing the orbit closure by an entire
invariant-quotient fiber. Those conditions are not assumed in the
consumer, and no saturation conclusion is used.
