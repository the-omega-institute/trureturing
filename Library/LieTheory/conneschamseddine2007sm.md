# Finite real geometry of the Standard Model

Section 3 constructs the KO-dimension-six real structure. Section 4 gives the finite algebra obtained from the order-one restriction and relates its unitary action and inner fluctuations to the Standard Model. Theorem 4.3 and Remark 4.4 discuss unimodularity and the relation between scalar and color determinants.
