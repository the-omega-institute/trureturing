- Physics Letters B 59 (1975), pp. 85-87.

## Use and boundary

The Lean module specifies the BPST curvature profile directly in oriented electric
and magnetic coordinates. With unit color coordinates the squared two-form
length has coefficient 96 in the radial density. The trace pairing of weight
one half gives coefficient 48, so the action is eight pi squared and the
normalized charge is one at every strictly positive scale. The radial integral
and the stress cancellation are proved in the repository from these definitions.
