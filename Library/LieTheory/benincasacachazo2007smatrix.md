# Four-particle consistency and the classical double copy

Benincasa and Cachazo give the four-particle test in Eq. (13), the two shifts and their channel weights in Eqs. (32)–(34), the spin-one and spin-two species constraints in Eqs. (35)–(36), and the mixed graviton–matter amplitudes in Eq. (41). Their argument assumes constructibility under the shifts and uses the specified mixed-helicity vertices. The physical graviton–matter discussion considers matter spin greater than one.

Monteiro, O’Connell and White, *Black holes and the double copy*, JHEP 12 (2014) 056, give the Kerr–Schild metric and the gauge and scalar copies. The pointwise Weyl calculation uses the orientation, curvature sign and projective chart of the Weyl screen equivalence. The spinor calculation also uses the four-point form of the Kawai–Lewellen–Tye relation. [Kawai, Lewellen and Tye](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(86)90362-7)
