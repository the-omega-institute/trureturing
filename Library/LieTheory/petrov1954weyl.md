# Petrov's algebraic curvature classification

The English republication appears in *General Relativity and Gravitation* 32(8), pp. 1665–1685. It provides the classical context for the principal null directions of four-dimensional Weyl curvature. A modern account gives the principal-null-direction quartic in §III.2. [Petrov classification of analogue spacetimes](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.12771v3#S3.SS2)

The Lean module derives the quartic from a specified complex screen frame and the oriented bivector basis. Its finite sampling result uses interpolation of those screen contractions, including the leading coefficient for the ray at infinity.
