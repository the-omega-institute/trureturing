## Formal correspondence

The real two-state mass matrix has signed eigenvalues $-m_L,m_H$ with
$m_Lm_H=m_D^2$ and $m_H-m_L=M$. For positive $M$ the correction to
$m_D^2/M$ is exactly $m_L^2/M$. For square Dirac and symmetric invertible
Majorana blocks the shear gives the exact Schur block
$-M_D M_R^{-1}M_D^{\mathsf T}$ by congruence.

The ordered transition $\alpha\to\beta$ has propagation phase
$e^{-im_k^2 x}$. The CP difference is the neutrino probability minus
the probability computed with the conjugated mixing matrix. With
$J=\operatorname{Im}(U_{\alpha0}U_{\beta1}
\overline{U_{\alpha1}}\overline{U_{\beta0}})$, the difference is
$16J\sin a\sin b\sin(a+b)$, where
$a=(m_1^2-m_0^2)x/2$ and $b=(m_2^2-m_1^2)x/2$.
