# Local anomaly equations and local tomography

B. C. Allanach, B. Gripaios, and J. Tooby-Smith study additional abelian charges and Standard Model singlet fermions in *Physical Review D* **101** (2020), 075015. The one-charged-family case supplies the context for the labeled five-species and six-species cancellation equations. Their companion article, *Geometric General Solution to the U(1) Anomaly Equations*, explains the geometry of simultaneous linear and cubic charge constraints (arXiv:1912.04804).

Lucien Hardy's *Quantum Theory From Five Reasonable Axioms* uses multiplicativity of the number of state parameters for composite systems as part of its simplicity axiom (arXiv:quant-ph/0101012). This is background for the dimension comparison and the completeness of products of complex Hermitian observables.
