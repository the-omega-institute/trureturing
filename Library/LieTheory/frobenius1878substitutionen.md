## Statement

The classification standardly attributed to this article states: a
finite-dimensional associative algebra over the real numbers in which every
nonzero element is invertible is isomorphic to the real numbers, the complex
numbers or the quaternions. A later article whose title names the same
classification is R. S. Palais, The classification of real division algebras,
American Mathematical Monthly 75 (1968), no. 4,
https://doi.org/10.2307/2313414; its DOI registration lists this title, the
author R. S. Palais, volume 75, issue 4, April 1968, and only the first page
366.

## Encoding and boundary

The repository theorem does not invoke this classification. It assumes a set
of linear isometries acting irreducibly on a real inner product space of
dimension at most four and a set of linear isometries containing two
noncommuting elements and commuting elementwise with the first set. It
constructs two anticommuting skew operators with square minus one directly
from the commutant and derives that the space has dimension four and carries
quaternionic coordinates, for any prescribed unit vector, in which the first
set acts by right multiplication and the second by left multiplication by unit
quaternions. The dimension bound, the choice of the unit vector and the
coordinate form of both actions are part of the repository statement and are
not taken from the cited article.

## What this note does and does not attest

Attested here: the bibliographic records and DOIs of the 1878 article, its
reprint, and the 1968 article by Palais.

it was not checked against the article itself.
