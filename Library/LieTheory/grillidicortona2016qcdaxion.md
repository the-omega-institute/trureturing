# The two-flavor chiral axion potential

Section 2, equations (6) and (8) through (12), supplies the leading-order chiral mass term, its minimized square-root potential, the axion mass and the fourth derivative. The Lean normalization uses the pion mass squared equal to the chiral constant times the sum of the quark masses. The angular argument includes a constant offset and the derivative is evaluated where that offset is canceled.

The paper compares the separate dilute-gas cosine with the chiral potential in Figure 1 and discusses the semiclassical approximation in Section 3. Equations (25) and (26) give the scope of the subsequent chiral corrections. The finite vacuum count concerns a field with a specified integer winding over its PQ circle.
