# One-loop asymptotic freedom

## Verified locator

Gross and Wilczek, *Physical Review Letters* 30, pp. 1343–1346,
and Politzer, pp. 1346–1349, establish the one-loop ultraviolet behavior
of nonabelian gauge theories with fermions. The latter paper is *Reliable
Perturbative Results for Strong Interactions?*
[Gross and Wilczek](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.30.1343)
[Politzer](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.30.1346)

## Formal correspondence

The formal model supplies the coefficient
$11N/3-2n_f/3-n_s/6$ for fundamental Dirac fermions and complex
fundamental scalars, using positive sign for growth of the inverse
coupling. It proves the numerical flavor bound and logarithmic
consequences of the supplied coefficient. The positive zero of the
one-loop inverse coupling is a scale of the perturbative formula.
