## Encoding and boundary

The repository theorem `exists_basis_bracket_eq_smul_cross` concerns a real Lie
algebra of dimension three with an invariant bilinear form that is positive on
nonzero vectors and need not be symmetric; it states that a non-abelian such
algebra has a basis, orthonormal for the form, in whose coordinates the bracket
is a positive multiple of the cross product. The statement and its proof are
repository derivations. This note acknowledges the classical setting of
