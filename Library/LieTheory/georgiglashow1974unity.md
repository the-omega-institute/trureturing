# Unified gauge generators and fermion multiplets

## Verified locator

Georgi and Glashow, *Physical Review Letters* 32, 438–441.
[Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.32.438)

## Formal correspondence

The first three fundamental coordinates carry color, and the last two carry weak isospin.
The hypercharge generator has diagonal entries $-1/3,-1/3,-1/3,1/2,1/2$.
Its squared trace is $5/6$, and the third weak generator has squared trace $1/2$.
Their ratio is $5/3$.
The electric-charge generator has squared trace $4/3$, so the ratio of the weak to electric squared traces is $3/8$.

On matrix units the adjoint hypercharge is the difference of the two coordinate weights.
On the dual it is the negative individual weight, and on a wedge pair it is the sum of the two weights.
These give the off-diagonal gauge charges and the full weak and hypercharge table of the fifteen Weyl states.
For a traceless diagonal generator, the two fermion representations contribute opposite cubic traces.
