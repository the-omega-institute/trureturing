# Majorana neutrinos and electric charge quantization

The abstract includes the Standard Model with a right-handed neutrino among the examples. It relates electric charge quantization to anomaly cancellation and nonvanishing fermion masses, with a Majorana neutrino eliminating the charge freedom. The arbitrary-field parameterization uses the corresponding local anomaly and Yukawa equations as polynomial constraints.
