# Electroweak symmetry breaking by a scalar doublet

## Verified locator

Weinberg, *Physical Review Letters* 19, 1264–1266.
[Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.19.1264)

## Formal correspondence

The article introduces the scalar doublet,
its neutral vacuum, the massless photon and the massive weak bosons. The
formalization uses the Hermitian generators $T_a=\sigma_a/2$ and $Y=1/2$
on the doublet. The neutral component has $T_3=-1/2$, so its electric
charge $Q=T_3+Y$ vanishes. The vacuum is normalized to $v/\sqrt{2}$. The
gauge mass matrix comes from twice the squared norm of the covariant
gauge term on that vacuum.
