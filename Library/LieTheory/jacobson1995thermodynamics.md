# Local horizon thermodynamics and null optics

Ted Jacobson's article appeared in *Physical Review Letters* 75 (1995), pp. 1260–1263. The local equilibrium assumption sets the initial null expansion and shear to zero. The Raychaudhuri equation then identifies the leading area variation with a Ricci contraction. The weighted flux integral identifies the leading heat variation with the corresponding stress-energy contraction. With entropy density $\eta_S$ and Unruh temperature $\hbar\kappa/(2\pi)$, the Clausius relation fixes the null contractions of $R_{ab}-2\pi T_{ab}/(\hbar\eta_S)$. Metric compatibility, contracted Bianchi and stress-energy conservation determine the cosmological term on a connected chart.

R. Sachs, "Gravitational waves in general relativity. VI. The outgoing radiation condition", *Proceedings of the Royal Society A* 264 (1961), pp. 309–338, supplies the null optical equations. In the two-dimensional transverse screen, the trace term is the full contraction $R_{kk}$ and the complex shear is normalized so that its squared norm is the trace of the square of the trace-free screen deformation.
