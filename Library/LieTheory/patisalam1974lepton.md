# Lepton number as the fourth color

## Verified locator

Pati and Salam, *Physical Review D* 10, 275–289.
[Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.10.275)

## Formal correspondence

The color generator has diagonal entries one third on the three quark
colors and minus one on the lepton. Its commutator splits the complexified
adjoint into the neutral octet and singlet and the two charged
off-diagonal blocks.

One generation is written entirely in left-handed convention. It contains
a color fundamental with left weak weight and a color antifundamental
with right weak weight. The hypercharge generator adds the right weak
weight to half the color generator. The weight bijection uses the even
half-spin convention in the ten-dimensional spin representation.

The orthogonal vector action is the direct sum of the real exterior-square
action and the left and right quaternion actions. The four-element central
kernel identifies the quotient that embeds in the ten-dimensional special
orthogonal group.

Kramer and Stroppel treat the compact exterior-square cover in Example 5.9
of *Hodge Operators and Exceptional Isomorphisms between Unitary Groups*.
Section 1.8 explains why the special unitary action preserves the real
form, and Lemma 8.2 gives generation of the target by half-turns.
