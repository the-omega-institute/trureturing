# Conformal transformations and four-point cross-ratios

## Verified locator

Di Francesco, Mathieu, and Sénéchal, *Conformal Field Theory*, chapter 4.
[Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-2256-9)

## Formal correspondence

The four-point ratios divide the products of squared separations for the
pairings \((12,34)\) and \((14,23)\) by the product for \((13,24)\).
An inversion or special conformal map supplies one factor per endpoint in
each squared separation. The factors cancel because each product uses
all four endpoints once. In Lorentzian signature the denominator may
vanish for distinct points, so both denominator separations are required
to be non-null.

The real six-dimensional generator model uses signature \((2,4)\). Its
twistor representation uses the chiral Dirac matrices and the Hermitian
form represented by the time-like Dirac matrix.
