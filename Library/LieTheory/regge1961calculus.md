# Simplicial curvature and the Schläfli differential equality

T. Regge, *General Relativity without Coordinates*, Nuovo Cimento **19** (1961), 558–571, formulates gravity using flat simplices and curvature concentrated on codimension-two hinges. The Euclidean Schläfli equality makes the sum of the area-weighted angle variations within each four-simplex zero. The first variation of the finite action therefore pairs each area derivative with its deficit angle.

J. Milnor, *The Schläfli Differential Equality*, in *Collected Papers*, vol. 1, Publish or Perish, 1994, treats the classical differential equality. Equation (1) and the bibliography entry [Mil94] in Rivin and Schlenker give the curvature-dependent identity and the Milnor locator.
