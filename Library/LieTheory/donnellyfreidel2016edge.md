# Gauge and gravitational surface symmetry

William Donnelly and Laurent Freidel, *Local subsystems in gauge theory and gravity*, JHEP 09 (2016) 102.

Section 2 constructs the Yang–Mills boundary phase space and its electric-flux generators. Section 4 identifies gravitational surface symmetries with surface diffeomorphisms acting on local normal frame transformations. The unified corner algebra uses the shared scalar coefficient algebra, the direct product of the normal and internal Lie algebras, and tangential derivations acting on the coefficients. The normal quadratic invariant is obtained from the trace pairing on the trace-zero two-by-two matrices.
