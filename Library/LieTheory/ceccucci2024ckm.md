# Quark mixing and the Jarlskog invariant

## Verified locator

Chapter 12, Section 12.1, gives the three ordered rotation factors in
equation (12.3). The same section gives the signed quartet in terms
of antisymmetric symbols and identifies the area of each of the six
unitarity triangles with half the magnitude of the invariant.
[Particle Data Group review](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/reviews/rpp2024-rev-ckm-matrix.pdf)

## Formal correspondence

The formalization uses zero-based row and column indices. Its reference
quartet uses rows zero and one and columns zero and one in positive cyclic
order. For positively ordered side vectors $z_0,z_1$, the oriented area
$\operatorname{Im}(\overline{z_0}z_1)/2$ takes the negative of the
corresponding ordered quartet divided by two.

The original mixing and invariant references are
[Cabibbo](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.10.531),
[Kobayashi and Maskawa](https://doi.org/10.1143/PTP.49.652), and
[Jarlskog](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.55.1039).
