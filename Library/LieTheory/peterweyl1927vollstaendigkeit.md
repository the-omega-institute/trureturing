## Statement

Verified from the bibliographic record only: the title of the article names
the completeness of the primitive representations of a closed continuous
group. The theorem standardly attributed to this article, in its separation
form, states that for a compact Hausdorff group every element other than the identity
is moved by some continuous finite-dimensional unitary representation. The
interior of the article was not read, so no theorem, section or page of it is

## Encoding and boundary

The repository theorem `exists_unitaryRep_ker_subset` assumes a compact
topological group with no separation axiom and a neighbourhood of the
identity, and gives a natural number n and a continuous homomorphism into the
group of unitary n x n complex matrices whose kernel lies in the
neighbourhood. Its proof is a repository derivation through a compact integral
operator with a continuous kernel, the spectral theorem for compact
self-adjoint operators and a compactness argument; it does not use the cited
article. The neighbourhood form, the absence of a separation axiom and the
proof route belong to the repository statement; whether the article states
them is not attested here.
