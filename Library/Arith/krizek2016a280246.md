# OEIS A280246

The NAME line defines the sequence by

> a(n) = Product_{d|n} psi(d), where psi(m) is the sum of totatives of m (A023896).

The COMMENT line states

> Conjecture: a(n) is odd iff the sum of totatives of n (A023896) is odd.

The entry attributes the sequence to Jaroslav Krizek on December 30, 2016.
The formal statement uses every natural `n >= 1`, defines `psi` by its literal
finite coprime sum, and interprets the product over the positive divisors of
`n`. The cases at zero are outside the source statement.
