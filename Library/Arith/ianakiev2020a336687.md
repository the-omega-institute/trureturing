# OEIS A336687

The entry itself, created by Bernard Schott on 2020-07-31, concerns numbers m for
which tau(sigma(m)) and sigma(tau(m)) both divide m. The equality conjecture is a
separate comment by Ivan N. Ianakiev dated 2020-08-06. His given name is Ivan,
not Kival. The internal entry was read directly on 2026-09-09 (Singapore time),
at revision `#24 Aug 06 2020 05:05:55`.

The comment states:

> Conjecture: The only m such that m = tau(sigma(m))*sigma(tau(m)) are in
> {1,468,3240}. Verified for m up to 1*10^9. - Ivan N. Ianakiev, Aug 06 2020

The repository proves the two uniform estimates tau(n)^4 <= 9^4*n and
sigma(n)^4 <= 3*n^5, and derives m < 3^13 for every positive equality solution.
These bounds are repository-derived from the preregistered prime-power argument;
they are not attributed to Ianakiev or Schott. The finite classification is an
explicit unproved hypothesis in the companion equivalence theorem.

This implementation does not prove the A336687 conjecture. The OEIS page still
labels the equality statement a conjecture; no proof was found in the inspected
entry or the identifier-based GitHub Lean search. That limited search does not
establish that no proof exists elsewhere.

## Scope of the formal work

The two uniform estimates and the bound m < 3^13 are proved without hypotheses.
The three-solution equivalence carries the finite classification over the window
below 3^13 as an explicit hypothesis, and that hypothesis is not discharged here.
