# OEIS A146557

Max Alekseyev's entry, created October 31, 2008, counts collinear triples of
distinct points in `Z_n x Z_n` with no two on the same horizontal or vertical
line. Peter Bala added the square-divisibility conjecture on July 24, 2025.
The Lean sequence is defined by Alekseyev's formula, with integer subtraction.
Its identification with the geometric count is the OEIS author's formula and
is not re-proved here; the ordered matrix count differs by a factor of six.
The module proves the conjectured divisibility using cyclic composition
symmetry and coprimality, without a totient decomposition.
