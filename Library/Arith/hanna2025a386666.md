# OEIS A386666

Paul D. Hanna's entry specifies the generating function with constant
coefficient zero and coefficient of x equal to one. Its conjecture relates
three consecutive coefficient residues to A039966. A partition into distinct
powers of three is uniquely determined by ternary digits restricted to zero
and one; the empty partition gives A039966(0) = 1.

The formalization constructs an integer series by compositional inversion,
proves the functional equation and uniqueness, and identifies its reduction
modulo three with the signed Catalan series. The digit-support generating
function yields the three residue formulas.
