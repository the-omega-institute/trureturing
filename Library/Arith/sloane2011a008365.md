# OEIS A008365

The NAME of A008365 states:

> 13-rough numbers: positive integers that have no prime factors less than 13.

Gary Detlefs's COMMENT of December 30, 2011 states:

> Conjecture: Numbers n such that n^24 is congruent to {1,421,631,841} mod 2310. - _Gary Detlefs_, Dec 30 2011

The AUTHOR line names:

> _N. J. A. Sloane_

The AUTHOR line is undated; 2011 is the datable year of Detlefs's conjecture.

The four-residue characterization is refuted by the prime 17, since 17^24 is congruent to 1681 modulo 2310. The corrected five-residue set {1,421,631,841,1681} is disclosed, not claimed.
