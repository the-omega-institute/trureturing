# OEIS A384222

Omar E. Pol's entry, dated June 3, 2025, defines the row by splitting the
increasing divisor list into maximal consecutive sublists whose adjacent
ratios are at most two, then taking their lengths. Conjecture 1 states that
these lengths form a palindromic composition of the divisor count A000005(n).
The Lean module proves the sum and palindrome assertions for every positive n.
