# Generalized Newman Phenomena and Digit Conjectures on Primes

This note supplies the literature provenance for the Shevelev problem.
The all-prefix conjecture and the repository's proved dyadic slice are
different claims: the Lean theorem establishes strict odious majority only
at cutoffs 2^(6k) for k >= 1. It leaves arbitrary prefixes open. The transfer
construction, exact annihilator, and sign induction are repository proofs.
