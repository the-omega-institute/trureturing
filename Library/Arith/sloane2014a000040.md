# OEIS A000040

The NAME of A000040 states:

> The prime numbers.

Gary Detlefs's FORMULA of May 25, 2014 states:

> Conjecture: Sequence = {5 and n <> 5| ( Fibonacci(n) mod n = 1 or Fibonacci(n) mod n = n - 1) and 2^(n-1) mod n = 1}. - _Gary Detlefs_, May 25 2014

His FORMULA of May 28, 2014 states:

> Conjecture: Sequence = {5 and n <> 5| ( Fibonacci(n) mod n = 1 or Fibonacci(n) mod n = n - 1) and 2^(3*n) mod 3*n = 8}. - _Gary Detlefs_, May 28 2014

The AUTHOR line names:

> _N. J. A. Sloane_

The AUTHOR line is undated; 2014 is the datable year of Detlefs's conjectures.

Only the May 25 line is refuted: the composite 219781 = 271*811 passes both stated tests, while the literal statement also excludes the prime 2 at the degenerate boundary; the May 28 line is disclosed but not claimed. The Fibonacci-type pseudoprime status of 219781 is prior art recorded by OEIS A094401, A093372, and A212424.
