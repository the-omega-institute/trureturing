# Kernel-checked finite lcm exclusion

The primary source is [arXiv:2607.25628](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25628).
It states that any finite covering of the integers by distinct odd moduli
greater than one has

    lcm(moduli) > 10000.

The formalization is in Lean 4 and is transported to the StrictCoveringSystem
formulation in google-deepmind/formal-conjectures.
The reported proof uses a periodicity bridge, the density implication

    2 N ≤ σ₁(N)

for a cover by distinct nonunit divisors of its lcm, an odd abundancy floor,
and explicit Chinese-remainder capacity certificates for the 23 odd
abundant candidates below 10000. The paper reports no sorry, native_decide,
or solver axioms in its 63 published theorems; its stated axiom closure is
propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound.

This is a finite exclusion and does not prove the unrestricted conjecture:
it leaves lcm values above 10000 untouched. The result is directly reusable
for any bounded-lcm branch and should be cited instead of rebuilding those
capacity certificates. It gives no exact-two-owner, reciprocal-private-hull,
or whole-cover descent statement.

No Lean source is vendored here; this is a citation record of the external
formalization.
