# OEIS A373308

Paul D. Hanna's entry of June 20, 2024 defines the sequence by the expansion
of `Product_{n>=0} (1 - x^(2^n))^3` and states the quoted divisibility conjecture.
The infinite product is represented by its exact defining functional equation
`A(x) = (1 - x)^3 A(x^2)` with constant coefficient one: removing the first
factor and shifting the remaining factors gives this equation.

The Lean module constructs the integer coefficients recursively, proves this
functional equation and uniqueness, and proves the conjecture for every natural
index. It uses this characterization rather than a formal infinite-product
operator. Reduction modulo three gives `(1 - X)^3 = 1 - X^3`; strong induction
then shows that the reduced series is supported on multiples of three.
The other conjectures in the entry are not asserted here.
