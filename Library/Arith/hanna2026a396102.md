# OEIS A396102

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated June 6, 2026, defines the generating series
`A(x) = x + ...` by `A(A(A(x))) = (1+x) * A(A(x))` and records the
conjecture that every positive-index coefficient is congruent to one modulo
three. The Lean module constructs the integer series, proves its defining
equation and uniqueness with constant coefficient zero and linear coefficient
one, and proves the conjecture for every positive index.
