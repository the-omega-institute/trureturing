# OEIS A374570

Paul D. Hanna's entry of July 11, 2024 gives the generating equation and
the parity conjecture quoted above. The normalization is A(0) = 0 and
a(1) = 1. The shifted Catalan series C has constant coefficient zero.
The Lean module constructs the normalized integer solution, proves its
uniqueness, and proves the parity characterization for every n > 1.
