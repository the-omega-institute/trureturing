# OEIS A001222

Schulte's conjecture identifies the binomial power of the total prime-factor
count with a divisor convolution involving the total and distinct counts.
The claim is the quoted Conjecture sentence for all complex x, y and
positive natural n. Natural exponentiation gives `0^0 = 1`, including at
`n = 1`; no nonzero-base hypothesis is imposed. The difference Ω − ω is
natural subtraction, equal to the ordinary difference because ω ≤ Ω.
Other OEIS assertions are outside the claim.
