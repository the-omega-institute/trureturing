# OEIS A389476

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated October 5, 2025, specifies the generating
equation and conjectures the parity classification quoted above.

The Lean module constructs the unique integer power series with constant
coefficient zero satisfying the equation, and proves its linear coefficient
is one. Its inner series is
`U = X - (A * invOfUnit (1 - A) 1)^2`. Since `1 - A` has constant
coefficient one, this is exactly the unit-inverse interpretation of the
OEIS quotient. The generating theorem also proves the denominator-cleared
identity `(1 - A)^2 * (X - U) = A^2` and `A.subst U = X`.

Over `ZMod 2`, the series whose coefficient at `n` is one exactly when
`n + 2 = 3 * 2^m` or `n + 2 = 4 * 2^m` satisfies
`P = X + X^2 + X^2 * P^2`. Clearing the unit denominator and quadratic
uniqueness prove that this series satisfies the reduced reversion equation.
Uniqueness of reversion identifies it with the reduction of the integer
series, proving Hanna's conjecture for every positive index.
The shifted equalities are equivalent to the OEIS natural-subtraction
formulas: the two products are at least three and four, respectively.
