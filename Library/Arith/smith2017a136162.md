# OEIS A136162

The NAME of A136162 states:

> List of prime quadruplets {p, p+2, p+6, p+8}.

The Krizek COMMENT contains two statements:

> {11, 13, 17, 19} is the only prime quadruplet {p, p+2, p+6, p+8} of the form {Q-4, Q-2, Q+2, Q+4} where Q is a product of a pair of twin primes {q, q+2} (for prime q = 3) because numbers Q-2 and Q+4 are for q>3 composites of the form 3*(12*k^2-1) and 3*(12*k^2+1) respectively (k is an integer). Conjecture: {11, 13, 17, 19} is the only prime quadruplet {p, p+2, p+6, p+8} of the form {q*(nextprime(q))-4, q*( nextprime(q))-2, q*( nextprime(q))+2, q*( nextprime(q))+4} where q is a prime (for prime q = 3). - _Jaroslav Krizek_, Jul 07 2017

The AUTHOR line is:

> _Harry J. Smith_, Dec 17 2007

Harry J. Smith is the sequence author, while Jaroslav Krizek authored the
2017 conjecture. The year records the conjecture date rather than the 2007
sequence-author date.

The formal result attached to this note proves only the second,
nextprime-product sentence. The twin-prime-product sentence is not claimed
here and is proved in the OEIS entry itself by the displayed factors
`3*(12*k^2-1)` and `3*(12*k^2+1)`.
