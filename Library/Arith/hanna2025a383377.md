# OEIS A383377

Paul D. Hanna's entry of May 15, 2025 defines the generating series by the
coefficientwise absolute reciprocal sum and conjectures evenness above index one.
The Lean module constructs the unique integer series with constant coefficient
one satisfying this equation, then proves the conjecture.

The equation is expressed coefficientwise: at degree N, only summands indexed
from zero through N contribute, since every later summand is divisible by a
higher power of X. Thus the finite coefficient sum represents the stated formal
generating-function equation exactly. Reducing modulo two removes coefficientwise
absolute values. Finite geometric sums and the unit inverse identity then show
that the reduced generating series is 1 + X.
