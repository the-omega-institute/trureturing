# OEIS A228558 and A231329

Robert Price authored both entries, and neither target COMMENT has a separate
signature. The two conjectures are therefore attributed to the entry author.

One general prime-quotient exponent lemma settles both comments. The classical
odd-power factorization behind the lemma is cited as background, not claimed as
a new result.
