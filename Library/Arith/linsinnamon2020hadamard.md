# Revisiting a sharpened version of Hadamard's determinant inequality

Linear Algebra and its Applications 606 (2020), 192-200.

The introduction, equation (1) and its following sentence, states that the
determinant of a Hermitian positive definite matrix is at most the product
of its diagonal entries, with equality if and only if the matrix is diagonal.
This is the classical inequality, recalled by the authors before their
sharpening. The integer determinant loss of at least one is an arithmetic
corollary of strict inequality, rather than a separate claim of that paper.
