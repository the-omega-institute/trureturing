# Perfect 1-factorisations of K_{11,11}

arxiv: 2506.02455

This note cites the paper by Jack Allsop and Ian M. Wanless, arXiv:2506.02455
(2025). Section 5, "The classical families of perfect 1-factorisations",
identifies the classical family GK_{p+1} as due to Kotzig. The original
construction is attributed to Kotzig (1964), not to the authors of this
later attestation. The paper's bibliography cites A. Kotzig, "Hamilton
graphs and Hamilton circuits", in Theory of Graphs and its Applications,
Publ. House Czechoslovak Acad. Sci., Prague, 1964, pp. 63-82.

The repository formalizes the odd-prime family on Option (ZMod p): the
factor indexed by a pairs infinity with a and every other finite vertex x
with 2a-x. Its theorem proves perfect matching, unique factor ownership of
each edge, and Hamiltonicity of each union of two distinct factors. The
attestation is for the established family; it does not claim a solution of
Kotzig's conjecture for arbitrary even orders or formalize the separate
GA_{2p} family.
