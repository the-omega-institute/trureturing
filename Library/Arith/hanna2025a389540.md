# OEIS A389540

Paul D. Hanna's entry defines the generating function by
`A(x)^2 = A(2*x - 2*A(x))/2` and states the conjecture that its odd
coefficients occur exactly at powers of two. The Lean module constructs
the normalized integer series, proves its defining equation and uniqueness,
and proves the parity assertion for every positive index.
