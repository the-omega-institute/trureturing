# Second order Recurrences, quadratic number fields and cyclic codes

Shi, Wang, Zakariae, Kim, and Sole relate second order recurrences to quadratic
number fields and cyclic codes. The paper restates Wall's question — whether
there is a prime `p` with `pi(p) = pi(p^2)` for the Pisano period `pi` — records
that no such prime is known below `10^14`, and cites heuristics conjecturing
infinitely many. For its generalized recurrences it makes the connection precise:
equality of the periods modulo `p` and `p^2` corresponds, under stated
hypotheses, to failure of `p`-rationality of the associated real quadratic
field.
