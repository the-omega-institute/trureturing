# OEIS A396798: the fourth and eighth compositional iterates

Paul D. Hanna's June 16, 2026 entry defines the ordinary integer series
`A(X)=Sum_{n>=1} a(n)X^n` by `A=X+I4(A)*I5(A)`, where Ik is the k-fold
compositional iterate and the product is ordinary multiplication. There is
no factorial scaling. Revision 12, dated June 17, states these comments:

Fourth:

> Conjecture: [x^n] A^4(x) == 0 (mod 8) for n > 2.

Eighth:

> Conjecture: [x^n] A^8(x) == 0 (mod 8) for n > 1.

`IterateProductFourFiveEighthModEight.generatingSeries` is the common
zero-constant integer source, constructed by coefficient stabilization.
`IterateProductFourFiveEighthModEight.result` proves the eighth statement.
`IterateProductFourFiveFourthModEight.result` uses that same definition
and proves the fourth statement. In both arguments, the source equation
and uniqueness identify its reduction modulo four with X/(1-X).

For the fourth iterate, the second iterate reduces to X+2X^2 modulo four.
An exact integer quotient then writes its reduction modulo eight as
G=X+2X^2+4B. Substitution annihilation and ring identities give
G composed with G=X+4X^2, without specifying the remaining coefficients
of B. The eighth-iterate argument instead writes the fourth iterate as
X+4B and applies square-zero iteration modulo eight.

The fourth and eighth statements were preregistered in #7950 and #7943,
respectively. The original first comment's n>=1 period conflicts with its
own a(3)=9; that previously recorded non-kernel observation is not a new
formal refutation. Comments 2,3,5,6,7 and a proposed correction of the first
comment retain their separate statements and unresolved obligations.
The bounded source, cross-reference and exact-identifier searches found
no earlier settlement of these two clauses in their stated scope. They do
not establish global priority or exclude differently named general results.
