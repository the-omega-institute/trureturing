# OEIS A384270

Paul D. Hanna's entry of July 10, 2025 defines the generating function by
the displayed quotient and conjectures that its odd coefficients occur
exactly at powers of two. The normalization is A(0)=0 and a(1)=1.

The denominator has zero constant coefficient, so division by it is not
an operation in the integer power-series ring. The Lean module uses the
cross-multiplied identity, constructs its normalized integer solution, and
proves uniqueness and the conjectured parity at every positive index.
Writing A=X*B with B(0)=1 permits cancellation of X^4 and a contracting
update involving only integer addition, subtraction, multiplication, and
substitution. Thus the construction requires no divisibility assumption.
