# OEIS A374571

Paul D. Hanna's entry of July 11, 2024 defines the sequence by the displayed
functional equation and states the Fibbinary parity conjecture. The normalization
is A(0)=1. The equation alone leaves the constant coefficient free: its
degree-zero identity is A(0)=A(0).

The Lean module constructs the integer coefficients, proves the generating
equation and uniqueness with constant coefficient one, and proves the conjecture
for every positive index. Its predicate `Fibbinary n` is
`n &&& (n >>> 1) = 0`. At bit position i, this intersection is the conjunction
of bit i and bit i+1 of n, so its vanishing means no adjacent one bits.
The parity argument also covers index zero internally.
