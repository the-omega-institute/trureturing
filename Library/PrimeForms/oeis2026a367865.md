# OEIS A367865

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A367865

Velin Yanev stated the conjecture in the OEIS entry on 14 September 2025. [Immutable text](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oeis/oeisdata/e76c5af5f1b7e20756262f806f4b5d75766ff647/seq/A367/A367865.seq)

The source formula is

$$
\sum_{d\mid n} d\,\varphi(d)\,\mu(d)^2
= \frac{\operatorname{rad}(n)}{n}
  \sum_{k=1}^{n}
  \frac{\operatorname{rad}(nk)}{\operatorname{rad}(k)},
\qquad n\ge 1.
$$

The formalization uses the positive finite domains `Nat.divisors` and `Finset.Icc 1 n`, and interprets every quotient in `ℚ`.
