# Homologie nicht-additiver Funktoren. Anwendungen

Albrecht Dold and Dieter Puppe, *Homologie nicht-additiver Funktoren.
Anwendungen*, Annales de l'Institut Fourier **11** (1961), 201–312.
Theorem 2.9 is on printed page 213; Theorem 2.15 is on printed page 217.
The first supplies the additive total/diagonal homotopy equivalence, and
the second identifies the shuffle and Alexander–Whitney maps.

For the formal source described in `li2026bisimplicialeilenbergzilber`,
`C : Type u` has `Category.{v} C` and `Abelian C`, with independent
object and morphism universes. For every bisimplicial object `X`, the
natural-number total of its double alternating-face chain complex is
homotopy equivalent to the alternating-face chain complex of its diagonal.
The total differential on bidegree `(p,q)` is `d₁ + (-1)^p d₂`.
The software retains its abelian hypothesis; the additive formulation
of the reference does not enlarge the software's declared scope.
