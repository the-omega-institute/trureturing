# Universal Varieties for Signatures of Membranes

Conjecture 6.10, p. 32, states the following.

> For every sequence $s\in\bigoplus_{\ell=0}^{k}\bigoplus_{\nu\in\Sigma_{\ell}}(\mathbb{R}^{d})^{\otimes\ell}$ with entries satisfying the two-parameter shuffle identity (Theorem 2.10) there is a piecewise bilinear membrane such that its $k$-truncated signature coincides with $s$.

Definition 1.1, p. 2, uses the native derivatives
$\partial_{12}X_{i_j}(s_j,t_{\tau(j)})$ on the two weakly ordered unit
simplices and sets $\sigma^{(\emptyset)}(X)=1$. Section 4, p. 20, specifies
biaffinity on every closed rectangle
$[(i-1)/m,i/m]\times[(j-1)/n,j/n]$ for some grid sizes $m,n$.

Equation (4) acts on words by inverse row reindexing. Definition 2.6, p. 5,
sums over independently chosen block-increasing row and column permutations.
For nonempty inputs it prints the rank
$\lambda\circ(\tau_1\triangleright\tau_2)^{-1}\circ\pi^{-1}$.
The empty basis pair is the unit. Theorem 2.10, p. 6, equates the product
of two signature coordinates with evaluation on this shuffle product.
In Conjecture 6.10 the truncated equations are the ones with total input
degree at most $k$.

The degree-$(1,3)$ insertion identity for the integrals of Definition 1.1
uses the forward block permutation. With $d=2$ and $k=4$ the formal
counterexample obeys the printed equations and the empty-coordinate
normalization, and violates that necessary integral identity.
Realizability from the genuine shuffle equations remains open.
