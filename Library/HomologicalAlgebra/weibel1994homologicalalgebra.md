# An Introduction to Homological Algebra

Charles A. Weibel, *An Introduction to Homological Algebra*, Cambridge
University Press, 1994. Chapter 1 provides the standard finite-complex
homology and Euler-characteristic context. The repository theorem specializes
rank-nullity to an explicit three-term finite-dimensional chain and records the
kernel and quotient spaces as Lean types.
