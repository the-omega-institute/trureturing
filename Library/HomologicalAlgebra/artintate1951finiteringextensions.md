# The Artin–Tate lemma

E. Artin and J. T. Tate, "A Note on Finite Ring Extensions", Journal of the Mathematical Society of Japan 3(1) (1951), 74–77. The lemma is restated as Lemma 10.51.7 (tag 00IS) of the Stacks Project, Chapter 10 (Commutative Algebra), Section 10.51:

> Let $R$ be a Noetherian ring. Let $S$ be a finitely generated $R$-algebra. If $T \subset S$ is an $R$-subalgebra such that $S$ is finitely generated as a $T$-module, then $T$ is of finite type over $R$.

Pinned Mathlib states it as `Algebra.fg_of_fg_of_fg` (`Mathlib/RingTheory/Adjoin/Tower.lean`), with the references Atiyah–Macdonald Proposition 7.8, Altman–Kleiman 16.17 and Stacks tag 00IS.

## The consequence used

Let $k$ be a field and $T$ a $k$-subalgebra of $k[X]$. If $T$ consists of constants, it is generated by the empty set. Otherwise $T$ contains a nonconstant $f$ with leading coefficient $c\ne0$, and $X$ is a root of the monic polynomial $c^{-1}(f(Y)-f)$ in $Y$ with coefficients in $k[f]\subseteq T$. So $k[X]$ is generated by $X$ as an algebra and integral over $T$, hence a finitely generated $T$-module. The lemma with $R=k$ and $S=k[X]$ shows that $T$ is a finitely generated $k$-algebra. This derivation is written out here; it is not a claim of verbatim appearance in the cited sources.
