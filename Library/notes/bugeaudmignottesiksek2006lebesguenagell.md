# The Lebesgue-Nagell equation at D=3

Yann Bugeaud, Maurice Mignotte and Samir Siksek, *Classical and modular
approaches to exponential Diophantine equations II. The Lebesgue-Nagell
equation*, Compositio Mathematica 142(1) (2006), 31-62,
DOI 10.1112/S0010437X05001739. The primary author preprint is
arXiv:math/0405220.

## Claim and scope

Theorem 1 and the D=3 row in Section 16's solution table give no positive
integer solution to `x^2+3=y^n` for `n>=3`. Signs of `x` do not change the
equation. A negative `y` is impossible for odd `n`; for even `n`, replacing
`y` by its absolute value reduces to the positive case. Zero is impossible.
DCE.1 separately excludes the square case for `x>=2` by
`(y-x)(y+x)=3`. The classification is an external premise, not a Lean
certification and not a statement that `x^2+3` is never powerful.
