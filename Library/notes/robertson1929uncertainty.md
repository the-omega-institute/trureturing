# The Uncertainty Principle

H. P. Robertson gives the uncertainty relation for general pairs of quantum
observables. It anchors the uncertainty setting for the repository's narrower
finite witness: the standard Pauli X and Z matrices have no nonzero common
eigenvector.

The Lean theorem does not formalize Robertson's variance inequality, error and
disturbance, or an arbitrary pair of self-adjoint operators. It also does not
derive the Pauli observables from an observer window or classical ontology.
