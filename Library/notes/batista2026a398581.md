# First and maximum third denominators

The entry was read on 2026-09-10. Its conjecture is one-way. It explicitly
rejects the converse and records the example k=11 with first solution (3,9,99)
and a maximum-z solution (4,5,220). Its residue-four comment gives an exclusion
for k=5q+4, q>=1; that comment does not prove the other non-one residue classes.

The repository proof supplies the general integer comparison for all four
excluded residues. It uses positive residual gaps, a decreasing bound before
5x=2k, the strict denominator order after that point, and the impossibility of
a square congruent to three modulo four in the residual-eight branch.
