# OEIS A018800

The NAME of A018800 states:

> Smallest prime that begins with n.

The COMMENTS conjecture is printed as:

> Conjecture: If a(n) = (n concatenated with k) then k < n. - _Amarnath Murthy_, May 01 2002

The AUTHOR line is:

> _David W. Wilson_

The conjecture fails at its first index: `a(1) = 11` is 1 concatenated
with 1, and `1 < 1` is false; Sloane's 2014 existence proof is out of scope.
