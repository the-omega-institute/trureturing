# Multitwist homology and graph lattices

## Verified locator

Daniel Corey and Wanlin Li, *Forum of Mathematics, Sigma* 12 (2024), e54.
DOI: 10.1017/fms.2024.36.
https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2024.36

## Source scope

Section 3.1, Propositions 3.1 and 3.2, and Section 3.3, equation (3.4), give
the multitwist sum formula, square-zero property and cycle-pairing block in
an adapted symplectic basis. These are classical homological monodromy inputs.
Graph flow and cut lattices are also treated by Bacher, de la Harpe and
Nagnibeda, *Bulletin de la Societe Mathematique de France* 125 (1997), 167-198,
DOI 10.24033/bsmf.2303, https://doi.org/10.24033/bsmf.2303.

## Correspondence to the theory

Sections 40-45 of SURFACE_CONGRUENCE_OBSERVERS use actual cover homology and
the coset dual graph. A positive exponent-one multitwist has a block Q=Z^t Z;
its return cokernel has graph-critical-group torsion. Smith invariants give
finite-field ranks and composite-modulus periods. For the mod-p homology
cover the graph is K_(a,b), with a=p^(2h) and b=p^(2(g-h)).

Sections 52-58 study fixed probes of A_Q=I+U_Q in one symplectic marking.
Passive power traces and characteristic polynomials do not distinguish Q.
One-probe traces give -v^t Q v, hence only the diagonal in characteristic two.
For z=b_i and w=b_j+a_i the two-probe difference is
D_ij=-Q_ii-Q_jj+Q_ij, while D_i=-Q_ii. Consequently
Q_ii=-D_i and Q_ij=D_ij-D_i-D_j over every commutative ring.
This probe calculation is the volume's derivation, not a theorem attributed
to Corey and Li. Over a characteristic-two field with block rank at least two,
maximal probe depth two is necessary and sufficient for this recovery task.

## Boundaries

The probes share a marking and reset to the same operator. Simple-curve
operations on a covering surface need not descend to the base. Signed twist
recovery is relative to that oriented marking, whereas the unmarked,
unoriented mapping-torus classification retains the absolute twist exponent.

Recovering a homology block does not prove principal outer-kernel containment
for a prescribed simple-curve orbit. Deterministic probes of a fixed quotient
action still factor through that action and preserve its kernel obstruction.
No claim about all periodic-point counts or integral cokernels follows from
the passive spectral equality.
