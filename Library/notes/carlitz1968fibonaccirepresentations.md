# Fibonacci representations

## Verified locator

Leonard Carlitz, "Fibonacci Representations", *The Fibonacci Quarterly* 6(4)
(1968), 193-220. DOI: 10.1080/00150517.1968.12431213.

Theorem 2 gives `R(F_m) = floor(m/2)` and lists the representations of
`F_m` as sums of distinct Fibonacci numbers. This classification is the
literature anchor used for the A339621 even-index corollary.
