## Source scope

Theorems 1.5, 1.6 and 5.4 give the stated JSJ- and fibration-sensitive
profinite results; Section 1 specifies edge-gluing conventions and Question
5.1 retains its hypotheses. General unmarked graph manifolds need not all
be profinitely rigid. An unmarked profinite isomorphism does not automatically
recover a selected fibration or time section.

Related JSJ classification appears in Wilkes, *Profinite rigidity of graph
manifolds and JSJ decompositions of 3-manifolds*,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.08244, Theorem 10.9.

## Correspondence to the theory

Sections 46-51 of SURFACE_CONGRUENCE_OBSERVERS consider the mapping torus of
T_alpha^k, for an essential separating alpha, g>=2 and k!=0. Pure time-cyclic
covers all have H1=Z^(2g+1). A specified mod-p surface-homology cover, with a
fixed-point time section, has degree D=p^(2g). Put
G=1+D(g-1), a=p^(2 min(h,g-h)), b=p^(2 max(h,g-h)),
beta=(a-1)(b-1), and q=|k|. Its covered mapping-torus homology is

Z^(2G+1-beta) + (Z/q)^((a-2)(b-2)) + (Z/qa)^(b-2)
  + (Z/qb)^(a-2) + Z/(qab).

The torsion exponent is qD. The free rank determines beta, hence
a+b=D+1-beta and the unordered separating genera. These determine the
unoriented homeomorphism type within the specified one-twist family.
The bounded-exponent result uses one finite-coefficient observation.
The cover-lattice input is developed in Sections 40-45; the explicit homology
decoder is the volume's derivation, not a quoted theorem of Wilkes.

## Boundaries

The cover and deck projection are marked data; the cover is not asserted
characteristic in the whole mapping-torus group. This calculation does not
recognize arbitrary unmarked three-manifolds or establish a surface principal
outer-kernel inclusion. It gives no optimal or polynomial-time size bound.

Sections 52-58 recover signed k relative to an oriented symplectic marking
and fixed covering-surface probes. This differs from the unoriented
classification, which records |k|. Such probes need not descend to the base.
The manifolds have an epimorphism of their fundamental group onto Z and an
incompressible gluing torus; no simply connected sphere classification or
Ricci-flow estimate is deduced from this family.
