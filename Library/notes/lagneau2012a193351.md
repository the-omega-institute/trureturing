# OEIS A193351

The entry selects the natural numbers whose A071324 value, the alternating sum
of their divisors in nonincreasing order, is prime.

The entry records Lagneau's conjecture that every selected number greater than
three is a square or twice a square.

## Verified locator

- URL: https://oeis.org/A193351
- Locator: COMMENTS, "For k > 3, if the alternating sum of all divisors of k (divisors nonincreasing, starting with k) is a prime number, then k is either a square or twice a square."

The mathematical core is the classical parity fact that sigma(n) is odd if and
only if n is a square or twice a square; no priority claim is made.
