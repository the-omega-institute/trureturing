# OEIS A254748

The entry defines numbers without superdivisors by the three divisibility tests
in A247477. It states that averages of twin prime pairs are zerosuperdivisor
numbers.

The proof of the twin-prime mean claim is an elementary multiplicative-order
argument; no priority claim is made.
