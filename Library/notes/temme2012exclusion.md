# Symmetric exclusion as a specified quantum dynamics

## Primary source and locator

New Journal of Physics 14 (2012), 075004. The arXiv record identifies v2 as
revised on 1 April 2010; the preprint revision date differs from the journal
year. The primary v2 PDF text, sections II and IV and equation (16), supplies
the jump-operator setting and symmetric stochastic density current. PDF
screenshot calls failed in this session; the source was read as parsed text,
without a successful visual page inspection.

## Use and scope

The arithmetic RT construction uses symmetric raising/lowering jumps on
adjacent logical edge bits. The known exclusion mechanism is explicitly
credited here. Its arbitrary finite-graph first-moment identity is proved
directly, and then transported through a specified non-stabilizer central
network and its sharing-tree encodings. The resulting capacity diffusion is
not claimed as a new exclusion process, a derivation of gravitational dynamics,
or a proof that the entire quantum state is determined by its mean occupations.

The paper studies one-dimensional models and steady-state transport, including
coherent Hamiltonian additions. The RT statement does not import its full
steady-state theory or assume arbitrary coherent additions preserve the
population-only equation. Only diagonal additional Hamiltonians are allowed
in the stated exact closure result. No numbered theorem or Lean coverage is
attributed to this article.
