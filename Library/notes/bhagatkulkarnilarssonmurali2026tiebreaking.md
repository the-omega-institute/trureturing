# Tie-breaking in self interest cumulative subtraction games

Conjecture 4 (printed page 5) states:

> Consider any subtraction set S, and suppose both players act friendly in case of indifference. Then each player's PSPE utility is never worse than if both players have antagonistic tie-breaking rules.

The paper defines order on pairs coordinatewise. Definition 2 gives the terminal
zero outcome and, at every nonterminal heap, maximization of the mover's total
with friendly or antagonistic tie-breaking on the opponent's total. The dual
convention exchanges player roles after a move. Theorem 3 proves the prediction
when the subtraction set has two elements; the paragraph after it says no
method is known to pass directly from FvF to AvA for arbitrary sets. Section 6
reports no positive FvF-to-AvA discrepancy in random sets with maximum step
at most 25. This note cites the conjecture, not its truth.
