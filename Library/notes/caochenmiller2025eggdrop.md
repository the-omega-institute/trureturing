# Egg Drop Problems: They Are All They Are Cracked Up To Be!

Cao, Chen, and Miller study multidimensional egg-drop strategies. Their
Conjecture 1 states that, for positive side lengths and at least as many eggs as
dimensions, the worst-case number of drops for the paper's strategy is bounded
by the natural ceiling of a real-power expression. The coordinate model uses a
single broken-or-intact answer for each drop and asks for exact recovery of the
hidden critical point.

The statement used here is the text of Conjecture 1 on printed page 10 of arXiv
version 2, submitted on 2025-12-01. The repository formalization asks whether
any deterministic adaptive strategy can meet that budget. This is a weaker
existence assertion than success of the paper's named strategy, so its
refutation implies that the printed conjecture is false.
