# OEIS A397592

The NAME reads:

> G.f. A(x) such that the sum of the first n coefficients in A(x/n)^n equals 2^(n-1) for n >= 1.

The FORMULA specifies A(x)=Sum_{n>=0} a(n)*x^n and both equations:

> (1) 2^(n-1) = Sum_{k=0..n-1} [x^k] A(x/n)^n for n >= 1.

> (2) (2*n)^(n-1) = [x^(n-1)] A(x)^n / (1 - n*x) for n >= 1.

The original offset is 0 and a(0)=1. For an integer formal series A,
A_Q means A.map (Int.castRingHom Rat), the coefficientwise map to Q[[X]].
The literal source sums coeff j((rescale(1/m,A_Q))^m) over j=0,...,m-1
for every natural m>0 and sets this rational sum equal to 2^(m-1).
Powers are ordinary series multiplication. The row formula is source-attested;
using it in a proof requires its equivalence to this rescaling equation.

The sole conjecture is the quoted parity claim. Its author field is
Paul D. Hanna, Jul 10 2026. The entry's b-file link covers n=0,...,400;
the asymptotic formula is not a parity proof.

A397590 states a(n)=Sum_{k=0..n} 2^k T(n,k) for this y=2 series, and
A(X,0)=1-F(X), where F is A397591's series. F has zero constant term,
offset 1, and the different equation
[X^(m-1)](1-F)^m/(1-mX)=0 for m>1. The existing A397591 endpoint is
not an identification of the A397592 source. The source relations alone
do not prove integer coefficients for the entire parameter triangle.
