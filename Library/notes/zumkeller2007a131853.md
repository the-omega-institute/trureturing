# OEIS A131853

The entry lists the arguments whose binary digit polynomial vanishes at the
Gaussian imaginary unit and records Zumkeller's divisibility conjecture.

## Verified locator

- URL: https://oeis.org/A131853
- Locator: COMMENTS, "conjecture: a(n) mod 5 = 0."
- Reduction: Modulo 5 the proof reduces i to 2, since 2² = 4 ≡ −1, and obtains m ≡ Re z(m) + 2·Im z(m) (mod 5); this reduction is elementary and carries no priority claim.
