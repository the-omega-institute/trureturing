# Zur Quantenmechanik der Stoßvorgänge

Max Born's 1926 paper supplies the historical probability interpretation of
quantum transition amplitudes. It is recorded as context for the projection
weight formalized in `D5/S3/Quantum/FiniteDimensional`, not as a direct source
for the modern density-matrix trace notation.

The repository theorem does not derive the rank-one pure-state
modulus-square formula, the Born rule from an observer ledger, or any numerical
spectral-pruning or quartic-pricing claim. Those parts of the source residual
remain unresolved.
