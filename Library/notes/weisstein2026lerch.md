# The source definition of the Lerch kernel

Equation (1) specializes at z=1/2, a=1, and a natural exponent s to
the real series with j-th term (1/2)^j/(j+1)^s. Its summation starts
at zero. Equation (6) gives Phi(1/2,s,1)=2*Li_s(1/2). Both equations
are used only to identify the source's meaning.

The article distinguishes the classical Phi from its modified form
Phi-star in equation (2), and states that they agree when the real
part of a is positive. The source specialization a=1 meets that
condition. The definition used here is explicitly equation (1).
