# Fibonacci divisibility and gcd laws

## Verified locator

Thomas Jeffery and Rajesh Pereira, *Divisibility Properties of the
Fibonacci, Lucas, and Related Sequences*, ISRN Algebra 2014, Article 750325,
pages 1--5, DOI `10.1155/2014/750325`.

## Claim and scope

Proposition 6 states `F_a | F_am` for positive integers `a` and `m`.
Theorem 7 states `gcd(F_a,F_b)=F_gcd(a,b)`. Proposition 6 alone implies
that, for each positive `a` and integer divisor `d`, divisibility of every
`F_am` by `d` is equivalent to divisibility of `F_a` by `d`: one direction
uses `m=1`, and the other uses
transitivity.
