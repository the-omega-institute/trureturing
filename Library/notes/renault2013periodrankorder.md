# Period, rank, and order of Fibonacci recurrences

Marc Renault, *The Period, Rank, and Order of the (a,b)-Fibonacci Sequence
Mod m*, Mathematics Magazine 86(5) (2013), 372-380,
DOI 10.4169/math.mag.86.5.372.

## Claim and scope

Theorems 1-2 treat the modular period, zero rank, and order of an
`(a,b)`-Fibonacci recurrence. The classical Fibonacci sequence is the
`(0,1)` instance. The prime-power lifting and Chinese-remainder period
mechanisms cited in GPC.1 belong to this classical modular-period setting.
The exact specialization to the blocks `B_j=L_(3^j)^2+3`, their original
prime depths `h_p`, and the interlevel factorization in GPC.2 are derived
in the problem dossier; the article is not cited for any all-WSS conclusion
or for existence of a Wall-Sun-Sun prime.
