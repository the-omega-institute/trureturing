# OEIS A339669

The NAME of A339669 states:

> Number of Fibonacci divisors of Lucas(n)^2 + 1.

The COMMENTS observation is printed as:

> Particular attention must be paid to the regularity properties of the number of divisors of Lucas(n)^2 + 1 observed for n < 156, when a(n) = 1 or 2. From this observation, we propose two conjectures verified for n < 156.

The two numbered conjectures are printed as:

> Conjecture 1: a(6*n+3) = 1.

> Conjecture 2: a(6*n+1) = a(6*n+5) = 2.

The AUTHOR line is:

> _Michel Lagneau_, Dec 12 2020

The EXAMPLE line fixes the counting convention: it lists five Fibonacci divisors of
`Lucas(8)^2 + 1 = 2210`, namely 1, 2, 5, 13 and 34. The value 1 is counted once even though
both `F_1` and `F_2` equal 1, so the sequence counts distinct divisor values. The same line
shows that the two conjectures are restricted to odd indices for a reason: at the even index 8
the count is 5.
