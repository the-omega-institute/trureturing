# The Problem of Hidden Variables in Quantum Mechanics

Simon Kochen and Ernst P. Specker establish the contextual hidden-variable
obstruction now bearing their names. This note is retained only to delimit the
nearby literature: the repository's qubit theorem concerns a unital global
complex-algebra homomorphism on all of `M_2(C)`, which is not a
Kochen-Specker valuation on projections.

In particular, this source is not used as direct attestation that qubit
projection valuations are impossible. The selected residual's
Kochen-Specker, CHSH, hidden-address, and probability-interpretation claims all
remain unresolved.
