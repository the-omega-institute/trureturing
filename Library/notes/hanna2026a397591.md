# OEIS A397591

The NAME reads: "G.f. A(x) satisfies [x^(n-1)] (1 - A(x))^n / (1 - n*x) = 0 for n > 1."
The FORMULA specifies A(x) = Sum_{n>=1} a(n)*x^n; the offset is 1.
This fixes the zero constant term and the coefficient indexing. The formal
result is over integer power series, and also proves existence and uniqueness.

The quoted comment is explicitly labeled Conjecture in the retrieved entry.
Its author field credits Paul D. Hanna, Jul 09 2026. The only link is the
400-term b-file. The asymptotic formula does not prove parity. The b-file was
not opened (ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED); the first 21 DATA terms were compared with
an exact-integer recurrence probe, which is not a proof of the conjecture.

A397590 is a triangle, A397902 uses squared exponents, and A320415 is linked
by a reciprocal generating function. No theorem is transferred from those
entries. The parity proof here is a local derivation from normalized rows,
coefficientwise stabilization and the Catalan series modulo two. The OEIS
entry is the source of the problem, not a source of that proof.
