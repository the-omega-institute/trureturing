# The distinct-cycle-size conjecture

OEIS A398726 was contributed by Alois P. Heinz on August 8, 2026.
Its FORMULA section attributes the following conjecture to Vaclav Kotesovec
on August 14, 2026:

> Conjecture: a(n) ~ n * n!.

The NAME is “Sum of the distinct cycle sizes over all permutations of [n].”
For each permutation, repeated lengths contribute once, including length one
when a fixed point exists. Summing those weights over every permutation gives
a(n); the empty permutation contributes zero. The example a(3)=16 explicitly
includes the identity's contribution of one.

The formal source uses the parts of the permutation's partition, which include
fixed points, and converts that multiset to a finite set. It then sums the
lengths over that set and over every permutation of Fin n. The conjecture's
meaning is the real ratio to n*n! tending to one as n tends to infinity.
The positive-index denominator is nonzero; the value at n=0 is irrelevant to
the limit.

The known entry identity a(n)=sum_{k=1}^n k*A293211(n,k) expresses the same
statistic through presence of a k-cycle. A293211 and A122974 credit Dennis P.
Walsh for finite cycle-avoidance/presence formulas. Those identities are
background, not assumptions in the proof and not claimed new. A132961 instead
counts the number of distinct lengths, without weighting by length.
