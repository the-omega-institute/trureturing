# Quantum generalizations of Bell's inequality

B. S. Cirel'son proves quantum bounds for Bell-type correlations. The source
atom invokes the associated `2*sqrt(2)` CHSH maximum; this note records the
real literature anchor while keeping that clause outside the current formal
coverage.

The Lean theorem proves only that a Bell coefficient matrix is not a simple
tensor. It does not define the CHSH operator, prove the quantum upper bound,
or establish that the rounded decimal `2.8284` certifies an expectation value.
