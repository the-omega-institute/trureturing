# OEIS A100952

The NAME of A100952 defines the exceptional-number sequence:

> Numbers that cannot be written as p*q+r with three distinct primes p, q and r.

The COMMENT line records a weaker logarithmically bounded conjecture. The
repository result refutes only that sub-conjecture: every integer congruent to
5 modulo 6 fails, so no threshold works. The completeness conjecture for the
sequence is untouched.

- COMMENT (verbatim): A weaker conjecture: every integer greater than 60 (or some larger value based on further search) may be partitioned into a prime p and a semiprime qr, where the prime p is bounded by log(min(q,r)). Chen (1978) showed that all sufficiently large even numbers are the sum of a prime and the product of at most two primes. Zumkeller's conjecture effectively extends this from "even" to both even and odd integers. - _Jonathan Vos Post_, Nov 25 2004
