# A necessary geometric entropy inequality

## Primary source and locator

Physical Review D 87 (2013), 046003. The primary arXiv record identifies v2 as revised 2 February 2013; the initial preprint is from 2011. The title, authors, DOI and static RT monogamy statement were checked.

## Use and boundary

This is the existing theory reference [HHM11]. The central-network examples compute positive tripartite information from logical correlations, even though the fixed area contribution has zero tripartite information. Consequently their full entropy cannot have the stated exact pure-area interpretation. This does not contradict the area-plus-algebra entropy identity and is not a counterexample to RT for a state with an established gravitational dual.

No unrestricted quantum-corrected monogamy claim, new proof of the published inequality, or kernel-verified theorem is asserted.
