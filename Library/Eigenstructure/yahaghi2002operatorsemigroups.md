# Reducibility Results on Operator Semigroups

Bamdad Reza Yahaghi, PhD thesis, Dalhousie University, August 2002.

## Source scope

Chapter 4 fixes finite-dimensional **left** vector spaces over division rings
on printed page 105 (PDF page 117). Corollary 4.2.3, identified as Levitzki's
Theorem, states triangularizability of every semigroup of nilpotent
transformations on printed page 108 (PDF page 120); its proof is on printed
page 109 (PDF page 121). The result concerns a multiplicatively closed family,
with no finite-generation, identity, commutativity or common-exponent premise.

The common nonzero annihilated vector in positive dimension and the vanishing
of every ordered word of length at least the dimension are mathematical
consequences of this classical triangularization theorem. In a simultaneous
triangularizing flag, nilpotence makes every one-dimensional quotient action
zero, so each factor lowers the flag by one step. These two consequences are
not presented here as verbatim thesis statements.

`D5/S1/Eigenstructure/NilSemigroup` supplies independent minimum-rank and
quotient-induction proofs of these consequences. Its arbitrary-set formulation
also permits an empty family; on a nonzero space every nonzero vector then
qualifies. Products use native endomorphism composition, so in `[A, B, C]`,
`C` acts first. In dimension zero the identity is the zero endomorphism, covering
the empty product. These boundary conventions describe the repository
statements and do not assert identical wording in the thesis.
