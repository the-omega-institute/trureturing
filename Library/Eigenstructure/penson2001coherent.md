# Coherent states from combinatorial sequences

Equation (6) (physical page 2) specifies the positive moment problem.
Example 9 (physical page 3) supplies its weight for
\(c(n)=(3n)!/(n!)^3\), with endpoint R=27. The paragraph following
Example 10 states positivity of the displayed weights.
For any nonnegative shift s and real vector v, its moment representation
gives \(\sum_{i,j}v_i v_j c(i+j+s)=\int_0^{27}t^s(\sum_i v_i t^i)^2d\mu(t)\ge0\).
Thus every finite shifted Hankel matrix is positive semidefinite.
This is the positive-moment ingredient attested by the source; the formal
beta-integral product construction is an implementation of that ingredient.
Neither the source nor this note is credited with the full Sun assertion.
