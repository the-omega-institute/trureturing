# Monterde, Pal and Kirkland, Laplacian quantum walks on blow-up graphs

H. Monterde, H. Pal and S. Kirkland, arXiv:2504.11585v1 [math.CO]; Linear Algebra and its Applications (2025). Quotations are from the v1 TeX source, with the source's macros for the blow-up and the integers expanded.

The $n$-fold blow-up $\uparrow^nG$ has vertices $(j,v)$, $j\in\mathbb Z_n$; the set $T_v=\{(j,v)\mid j\in\mathbb Z_n\}$ consists of pairwise false twins. The theorem labelled `pt1` (Section 10, Edge perturbation):

> Let $n\equiv 0$ (mod 4) and $v$ be a vertex in $G$ with $\sigma_v(G)\subset\mathbb Z.$ Then the addition of a matching in $T_v$ results in LPST in $\uparrow^nG$ at $\frac{\pi}{2}$ between the end vertices of every edge inserted. Moreover, the resulting graph is periodic at all vertices in $T_v$ that are not incident to the newly added edges with period $\frac{\pi}{2}.$

The last question of Section 11 (Future work):

> Lastly, we ask: does Theorem \ref{pt1} hold for the adjacency case? That is, if $n\equiv 0$ (mod 4), will the addition of an appropriate matching in $\uparrow^nG$ induce adjacency LPST?

The same question is Problem 10 of the first author's thesis, *Quantum pure state transfer* (University of Manitoba, 2025), printed page 163.
