# Absolute-cosine Hadamard powers

## Locator

DOI: 10.1016/j.laa.2016.06.030

Article: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2016.06.030

Tanvi Jain, *Hadamard powers of some positive matrices*, Linear Algebra
and its Applications 528 (2017), 147–158. The conjecture occurs in the
last unnumbered paragraph of section 3, immediately after the proof of
Theorem 3.3. Zbl 1398.15038; MR 3652842.

Related author manuscript: https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.06803.
The first arXiv version omits the conjecture sentence; the journal
version is the source of the quotation below.

## Source statement

> We conjecture that the multiplicity of every nonzero eigenvalue of |C|∘r∘ (except the top eigenvalue) equals two.

Here the order is odd, n=2m+1 at least three, r is real and nonnegative,
and C has entries cos((a-b)pi/n). The notation denotes entrywise
absolute value followed by the entrywise real power. Proposition 3.2
and Theorem 3.3 provide Fourier and inertia information; equal signs
of distinct Fourier representatives do not imply their equality or
separation.
