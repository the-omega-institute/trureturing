# Andrade and Severini, Unitary equivalence between the Green's function and Schrödinger approaches for quantum graphs

F. M. Andrade and S. Severini, Phys. Rev. A 98, 062107 (2018); arXiv:1808.01154v2. Quotations are from the v2 TeX source.

An edge of length $\ell_{ij}$ contributes $z_{ij}=e^{ik\ell_{ij}}$. For the star graph $S_n$ the Schrödinger approach gives the quantum evolution map $U^S_{S_n}(k)=S_{S_n}(k)D_{S_n}(k)$, with $D_{S_n}(k)=\operatorname{diag}(z_{12},z_{12},\dots,z_{1n},z_{1n})$ and $S_{S_n}(k)$ built from the vertex scattering amplitudes $r_i$, $r_1$, $t_1$; the Green's function approach gives $U^G_{S_n}(k)=D_{S_n}(k)S_{S_n}(k)$. The paper proves that the two maps have the same eigenvalues with multiplicity, and states:

> The quantum evolution maps $U^{S}_{S_n}(k)$ and $U^{G}_{S_n}(k)$ are unitarily similar.

> Although a proof for a general $n$ is not known, we checked this for $n=2,3,4,5$ by using Specht's theorem.
