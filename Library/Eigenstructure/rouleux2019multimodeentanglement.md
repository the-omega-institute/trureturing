# Rouleux, Multimode entanglement for fermions

M. Rouleux, arXiv:1910.04790v1 [quant-ph] (10 October 2019), contribution to the conference ISQS26 (Prague, July 2019); HAL hal-02323515. Quotations are from the v1 TeX source, with the source's macros `\vec` and `{\bf C}` kept as written.

Section 3 treats antisymmetric tensors $x_1\wedge\cdots\wedge x_p$ in $\Lambda^p\mathcal H\subset\bigotimes^p\mathcal H$ for $\mathcal H=\mathbb C^d$, "degree being understood as degree of homogeneity", and antisymmetric forms that are not multilinear, such as the 3-fermion $a\wedge b+b\wedge c+c\wedge a$. Definition 3:

> The affine determinant of $x_0,x_1,\cdots,x_d$ in ${\bf C}^d$ is the antisymmetric 2-form in variables $x_0,x_1\cdots,x_d$ $$\widetilde\omega(x_0,x_1,\cdots,x_d)=\det(\vec{x_0x_1},\vec{x_0x_2},\cdots,\vec{x_0x_d})$$

Such forms arise by antisymmetrizing monomial generators, and $\omega$ is called non-degenerate when its vanishing for every $x_0$ forces $x_1,\dots,x_d$ to lie in an affine subspace of dimension $d-2$. The section states:

> We conjecture that: (1) the affine determinant is the only non degenerate antisymmetric tensor $\omega$ on ${\bf C}^d$ of degree $d$, depending on $d+1$ variables, and: (2) there is no non trivial antisymmetric tensor on ${\bf C}^d$ of degree $d$, depending on more than $d+1$ variables.
