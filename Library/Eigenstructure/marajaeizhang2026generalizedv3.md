# Generalized Fidelity, the Data Processing Inequality, and Convexity

This note cites arXiv:2609.09753v3 (20 October 2026), by Haobo Ma,
Reza Rajaei and Wenlin Zhang. It does not replace the repository's
historical version-1 note or change the attribution of frozen suppliers.

The introduction, equations (1)-(3), fixes the ordered generalized fidelity
and the generalized squared Bures quantity. Equation (3) is the weighted-root
Hilbert-Schmidt norm formula and permits positive semidefinite input states.
The reference is positive definite. The Qubit Case, Lemma 1, Lemmas 2-5
and Corollary 4 supply the two-dimensional root, Pauli/Bloch and fidelity
quotient identities used by the current formalization. These inherited
identities are credited to this source rather than claimed as new results.

Theorem 3 gives a qubit DPI violation. Thus the unresolved unrestricted
qubit question in version 1 is historical, not the status in version 3.
Corollary 7 gives scalar balance as a sufficient condition for every actual
qubit channel with positive definite transported reference. The contribution
history attributes the qubit formula and first two criteria to Rajaei,
and the qubit counterexamples and stronger Corollary 7 to Ma and Zhang.

The current CommutingMidpoint candidate proves a positive family with
commuting qubit density inputs, scalar input reference I/2, arbitrary
complex qubit outputs, and positive definite transported reference. Its
faithful and singular outside-balance triples satisfy Good over every
admitted canonical channel. This is a negative statement about scalar-balance
necessity, not a full replacement classification, a fixed-pair convexity
result, or a worldwide priority claim.

Afham and Ferrie, arXiv:2410.04937v2, define the generalized-fidelity,
squared-Bures and transported-reference conventions. Vuong,
arXiv:2605.28885v1, recomputes pair-dependent polar references; those output
references need not be the transported midpoint Phi(I/2). Neither source
is used as an assumed transported-reference channel-DPI bridge.
