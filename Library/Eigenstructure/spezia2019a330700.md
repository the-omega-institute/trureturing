## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A330700

OEIS A330700, COMMENTS, revision 23, second line of the Conjectures block
(unpaginated):

> For k > 2, the trace of the k-th exterior power of M(n) is equal to zero.

The matrix is specified in the preceding line of the same block and in
[OEIS A330287](https://oeis.org/A330287), COMMENTS, revision 31 (unpaginated):

> The matrix M(n) is defined as M[i,j,n] = i*j if i < 3 or j < 3 and M[i,j,n] = 2*(i + j) - 4 otherwise.

The row and column indices start at one, as in the displayed A330287 example
M(3) with rows (1,2,3), (2,4,6), (3,6,8). This is the principal perimeter
matrix of [OEIS A319840](https://oeis.org/A319840). In Lean, indices are
`Fin n`, source indices are `i.val + 1` and `j.val + 1`, and the integer
entries are interpreted in ℂ. The claim ranges over every positive natural
matrix order and every natural exterior order greater than two. The existing
`compoundMatrix` represents the exterior map in its ordered-subset basis;
`Matrix.trace` is its trace, including the empty-basis case k > n.

This source is an OEIS entry and has no identified arXiv version or DOI.
The scalar-sequence assertion about the absolute second exterior trace is a
separate conjecture. OEIS material is subject to the
