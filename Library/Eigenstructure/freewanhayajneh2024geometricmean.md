# On a conjecture related to the geometric mean and norm inequalities

## Verified locator

https://files.ele-math.com/articles/mia-27-15.pdf

DOI: 10.7153/mia-2024-27-15

S. Freewan and M. Hayajneh, *Mathematical Inequalities & Applications* 27 (2024), 193–200.
Conjecture 2 and the positive case following it occur on printed p. 194. The authors attribute
the first proposal to their LAA 670 (2023), 104–120 paper, DOI 10.1016/j.laa.2023.04.012;
the 2024 paper restates it.

## Source statement and definitions

> CONJECTURE 2. If A_i, B_i ∈ P_n for all i = 1, …, m, and t ∈ [0,1], then
> |||∑_{i=1}^m (A_i² #_t B_i²)^r||| ⩽
> |||((∑A_i)^{(1−t)pr}(∑B_i)^{2tpr}(∑A_i)^{(1−t)pr})^{1/p}||| for all 2r ⩾ 1
> and for all p > 0 and for all unitarily invariant norms.

Here $P_n$ denotes the positive definite complex matrices and

$$
A\mathbin{\#_t}B=A^{1/2}(A^{-1/2}BA^{-1/2})^tA^{1/2}.
$$

All real powers are spectral powers.

## Scope and literature boundary

Conjecture 2 is the $s=2$ specialization of Conjecture 3 in the same paper; the reported
OpenProblemsInNLA MI-21 refutation uses $s=1/2$ and does not settle that specialization.
