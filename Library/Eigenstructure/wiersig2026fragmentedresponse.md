# Passive response at a fragmented exceptional point

Page 6, Section VII of arXiv:2610.31268v1 asks whether a sharp bound of the
kind obtained for a single Jordan chain survives fragmentation, and whether
rank-one loss remains optimal when several chains are present. Equations
(6), (10), and (14)–(16) fix the resolvent, operator norm, decay operator, and
linewidth conventions. Equations (49) and (53) give the single-chain bound
and its equality condition.

The preregistered scope keeps the whole-space collision of the paper. There
is one spectral value on the whole matrix, its linewidth is strictly
positive, and the positive Jordan block sizes can be prescribed. The
spectral response uses the norm induced by Euclidean vector length.
