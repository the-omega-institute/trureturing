## Source statement

OEIS A400645, revision 21, NAME field (unpaginated):

> a(n+1) = round(sqrt(a(n)^2 + a(n-1)^2 + a(n-2)^2 - a(n-3)^2)).

COMMENTS field, complete paragraph:

> Numerical evidence suggests that the ratio of consecutive terms (a(n+1)/a(n)) tends to 1.3122819079... (sqrt(A289917)) which is the largest real root of the asymptotic equation of this sequence: x^8-x^6-x^4-x^2+1. This is related to A116732 where the asymptotic equation is y^4-y^3-y^2-y+1 via y=x^2.

The Mathematica field initializes `a[1] == a[2] == a[3] == 0, a[4] == 1`.
The OFFSET is `1,7`. Index zero is unused. Signed subtraction is taken in
integers, and an integer radicand has no half-integer square root.

The cross-reference OEIS A289917, revision 48, FORMULA field states:

> Equals (1 + sqrt(13) + sqrt(2*sqrt(13) - 2))/4. - _Vaclav Kotesovec_, Aug 27 2021

> Largest real root of x^4 - x^3 - x^2 - x + 1. - _Linas Vepstas_, Feb 06 2024
