# Approximate uniformity of independent finite convolution

Nilava Metya and Satyaki Mukherjee, *Approximate Uniformity in Finite
Convolution Models*, arXiv:2609.38243v1, §1 and §4.3, define ordinary
convolution of two independent probability vectors on a finite interval.
Both factors range over the closed real simplex.

[Equation (21)](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38243v1#S4.E21) minimizes the full
L1 distance from uniform on the ordinary output support. Conjecture 4.7
states its value is 1/(2n-1) for n≥3.
[Equation (22)](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.38243v1#S4.E22) gives an attaining
family. The source leaves the matching lower bound conjectural.

The source declares [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The source descriptions in this note are adapted and attributed under that
license. The squared-L2, KL and Wasserstein results in the source concern
different objectives and are not substituted for Conjecture 4.7.

The exact source-level resolution target and bounded literature scope are
in [preregistration #11735](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/11735)
and Problems/metya-mukherjee-independent-convolution-l1.md.
