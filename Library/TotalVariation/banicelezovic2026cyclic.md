# Cyclic dispersion under the multinomial law

## Verified locator

Nikola Banić and Neven Elezović, *Discrete total variation under the
multinomial, Part 5: cyclic total variation*, arXiv:2611.14735v1 (2026).
[Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14735v1) ·
[DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2611.14735](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.14735)

Equation (2.2) gives the count-vector mass for total sample size \(N\).
For uniform bin probabilities, the common factor in the factorial weights
is \(n^{-N}\). Equation (2.17) defines cyclic total variation \(d^c\), including
the edge from the last bin to the first. The normalization in Corollary 3.5
is \(\sigma_c^2=\operatorname{Var}(d^c)/N\).
[Part 5 v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14735v1)

Remark 3.12 asks about both global monotonicities of the exact dispersion.
Remark 3.13 separately asks about the absence of an interior mode for every
fixed sample size. The adjacent second-order constant appears in Part 5
v1 Theorem 3.10. [Remarks 3.12–3.13](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14735v1#page=15) ·
[Theorem 3.10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14735v1#page=14)
