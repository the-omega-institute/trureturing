# Cyclic dispersion under the multinomial law

Equation (2.2) gives the multinomial mass on count vectors with total sample
size \(N\). At the uniform probabilities, each factorial weight has the common
factor \(n^{-N}\). Equation (2.17) defines the cyclic total variation \(d^c\) by
including the edge from the last bin to the first. Corollary 3.5 and Theorem
3.10 use the normalization \(\sigma_c^2=\operatorname{Var}(d^c)/N\).

[Remark 3.12 on page 15](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14735v1#page=15) asks about
both global monotonicities of the exact dispersion. Remark 3.13 also asks about
the absence of a fixed-sample interior mode. A global interior maximum requires
a comparison with every other bin count in the fixed-sample domain.
