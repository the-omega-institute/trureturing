In arXiv:2401.10095v1, §2.1.2 Eq. (6) expresses the doubled-system
construction in terms of conjugated single-site SWAPs. Section 5.2.1
Remark 5 Eq. (109) gives the identity for the unitary and its adjoint on
two copies. Section 5.2.2 Lemma 9 Eq. (114) bounds the sewed-channel error
for an arbitrary qubit unitary using Pauli readout errors and half the
diamond norm. The formal estimate uses unhalved diamond distances
between full-input single-output channels on arbitrary finite local
dimensions. It applies trace duality to each local SWAP directly.
