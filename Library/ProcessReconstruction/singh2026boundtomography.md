# Ancilla Assisted Quantum Process Tomography using Bound entangled states

The least realignment singular value measures the smallest output-state
Hilbert–Schmidt separation per unit Hilbert–Schmidt separation of the
unnormalized channel Choi matrices. Actual CPTP pairs can attain this
direction through a trace-annihilating rank-one perturbation of the
strictly positive depolarizing Choi matrix. This uses a channel difference
realization as in Caiaffa–Piani and Lie–Jeong.

For Pauli-diagonal PPT probes an anticommuting Pauli pair and its product
give three Bell correlations whose absolute values sum to at most one.
Positivity before and after partial transpose supplies the eight joint
sign inequalities. This yields an upper bound of 1/(3d) on the least
singular value. The published rank-six Bell-lattice probe attains 1/12
at d=4. It also attains the same-class realignment trace norm upper
bound 3/2, obtained by summing the anticommuting-triple inequalities.
The probe is locally unitarily equivalent to the earlier state of
Benatti, Floreanini and Piani. Singh's v3 gives the probe's least
singular value, the separable upper bound and their benchmark comparison.
The broader PPT optimization in Lukács et al. Table 2 is numerical.
The analytic upper bound recorded here covers the Pauli-diagonal
class.
