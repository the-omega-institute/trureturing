# GNS detailed balance and modular covariance

Carlen and Maas develop the finite-dimensional detailed-balance
framework. At s = 1 the pairing in Eq. (2.3) is Tr(σ A†B).
Definition 2.10 applies self-adjointness in this pairing to the
Heisenberg semigroup. Theorem 2.9(1) and Remark 2.11 state its
covariance under the modular group, with attribution to Alicki.
For a Gibbs state σ proportional to exp(−βH) at β > 0, the
modular generator −log σ equals βH up to an additive scalar.

The explicit Schur and reset semigroups verify symmetry and covariance
directly. The general result supplies the terminology and precedent for
those properties. The proper-output equality and exact diamond distance
come from the selected rank-two channel pair.
