# GHZ coherence and proper output observations

Walck and Lyons exhibit a family with varying relative phase and identical
((n-1))-qubit reductions. In the first direction of the proof of their
main result, the two GHZ signs give different states with the same reductions.
The shared mechanism is that tracing out any single qubit annihilates the
two off-diagonal matrix units between the extreme words.

The process construction uses this coherence in a difference of channels
on arbitrary full inputs. The time-dependent factor and common Gibbs reset
are features of the constructed semigroups.
