# Uniqueness from a homomorphic channel marginal

The source considers completely positive maps from a tensor-product
observable algebra to a common target algebra. In the present Heisenberg
picture, that common target is the algebra of the entire input register.
The restriction of a square unitary conjugation to each output factor is
a star representation. Applying the product formula one factor at a time
gives the uniqueness mechanism for rank-one channels. The rank-two
construction uses a block pinching and confines the distinguishability
to the coherence that every proper partial trace removes.
