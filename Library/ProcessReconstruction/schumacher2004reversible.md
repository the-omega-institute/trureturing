Section I of quant-ph/0405174v1 explains that products of single-cell
observables linearly span the local observable algebra. Lemma 2(1) in
§II states that the global homomorphism is uniquely determined by
the local transition rule. In the finite tensor-product setting,
equality of the full-input single-output channels gives equality of
the adjoint homomorphisms on each single-site algebra and hence
determines the whole conjugation channel.
