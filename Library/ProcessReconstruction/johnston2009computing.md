In arXiv:0711.3636v1, Theorem 3 identifies diamond and completely
bounded norms of adjoint maps. Section 4.2 Theorem 26 gives the
stabilized norm of the difference between unitary conjugation and
the identity as the diameter of the smallest disk containing the
unitary spectrum. The formal proof uses a spectral half-plane and
pure-output overlaps to derive a bound from the largest spectral
chord, then applies the local SWAP estimate.
