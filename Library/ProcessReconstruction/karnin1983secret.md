Section I, p. 35, right column, gives the additive sharing display and
its binary explanation for q = 2. The uniform distributions on the
two parity classes coincide on every proper set of coordinates. The
formal obstruction uses this mechanism in an explicit unital
channel pair. A computational basis input gives orthogonal
preparations and unhalved diamond distance two.
