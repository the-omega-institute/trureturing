## Last-site question

The same arXiv v1 source asks in §2, printed pp. 9–10, around Eq. (19):

> It is also of interest to determine whether the position of the last spin tends to infinity as N → ∞, since according to our interpretation of the chain’s geometry the number 2ξN/π is the radius of the circle on which the spins lie.

The source motivates divergence by the inverse-error-function approximation
in Eq. (19), then cautions that Eq. (18) is accurate only up to terms of
order 1/N. The question concerns the cyclic site equations (6) in the
strictly increasing chamber. The source’s site ξN is the zero-based
coordinate N − 1 in `NearestNeighborLastSiteDivergence`.
