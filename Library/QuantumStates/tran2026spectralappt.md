## Remark following Theorem A

Page 4, verbatim source statement:

> Determining the exact maximum APPT purity remains open.

The same remark states, verbatim:

> Hence the inequality in Theorem A is strict for every $3 \otimes n$ system with $n \geq 3$, and the Dũng–Khôi conjecture is false in those dimensions.

The strict outer bound and the falsity of the Dũng–Khôi candidate are source results. They are distinct from the inscribed-polytope equality in Ahiable–Kothakonda–Winter Conjecture 6.7.
