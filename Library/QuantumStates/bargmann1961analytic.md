# Analytic realization of Fock space

V. Bargmann, Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics 14,
187–214 (1961), DOI 10.1002/cpa.3160140303.

The analytic realization sends occupation coefficients to the entire
power series with factorial normalization. Orthogonality to every
exponential vector annihilates that power series; coefficient uniqueness
then annihilates every occupation coefficient. Applying this argument
in each parameter gives totalness of the two-mode exponential family.
The formal constructions use these arguments as local steps on the
completed countable Hilbert sums, applying the pinned analytic-series
uniqueness API.
