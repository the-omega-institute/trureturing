## Statement and scope

Equations (6)–(8), pp. 2–3. With binary entropy in bits $h(x)=-x\log_2x-(1-x)\log_2(1-x)$, the scalar function is $f(c)=h((1-\sqrt{1-c^2})/2)$ on $0\le c\le1$. It is increasing and convex. A Euclidean unit two-qubit vector with amplitude matrix $X$ has marginal entropy $f(2|\det X|)$.
