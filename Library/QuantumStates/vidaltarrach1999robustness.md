## Verified locator

DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevA.59.141 (Phys. Rev. A 59, 141–155). Text read from
https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9806094, printed p. 15, Eq. (41):

> its random robustness is (Appendix B) R(Ψ|| 1/(n₁n₂) I) = n₁n₂a₁a₂,

for a pure state Ψ with Schmidt coefficients {a_i}, i = 1, …, min(n₁, n₂), where a₁ and a₂
are the two largest. Since 2a₁a₂ ≤ a₁² + a₂² ≤ 1, the random robustness is at most n₁n₂/2,
so |Ψ⟩⟨Ψ| + ½ I is separable and so is I + 2|Ψ⟩⟨Ψ|. The paper's construction uses the
Schmidt form.
