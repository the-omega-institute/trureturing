# Homogeneity and normalized pure transitivity

The primary source is [arXiv:2306.00362v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.00362v2),
Definitions 15 and 19 and Theorem 25. The cited version is v2; the bibkey
and year identify the first arXiv publication.

Definition 15 uses the full order-automorphism group of the cone,
transitive on its interior. These automorphisms need not preserve the
normalizing functional. Definition 19 instead requires normalized
order automorphisms to act transitively on all pure points of the chosen
base. Theorem 25 combines these two hypotheses to obtain self-duality
and Euclidean Jordan algebra structure. Its proof uses Vinberg's
homogeneous-cone theory. Neither hypothesis by itself is replaced by
transitivity on an arbitrary smaller orbit or by local face symmetries.

The exact consumers are [the L3 continuation, Theorems 93.2 and 93.3](../../docs/develop/theory/QUANTUM_ORIGIN_BILATERAL_POSITIVE_RESET_RIGIDITY_AND_SCALE_OBSTRUCTION_VI.md).
Their full-cone spanning certificate supplies homogeneity; the given
compact group supplies normalized pure transitivity. Whole minimal
two-pure faces select the complex type only after Theorem 25 has supplied
Jordan structure. The standard Jordan type and face geometry are cited
separately to [Barnum–Hilgert](barnumhilgert2019spectral.md), rather than
attributing them or a new classification to this application.

An original strictly positive normalization is transported by a positive
square-root congruence after the complex cone has been selected. The
source theorem does not identify an original read-return program, provide
all-cone extensions of local boosts, or turn mathematical homogeneity
into an available physical control family. No composite-system or
dynamical correspondence theorem of the paper is used by these consumers.
