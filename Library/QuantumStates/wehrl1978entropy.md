# General properties of entropy

A. Wehrl, Rev. Mod. Phys. 50, 221–260 (1978).

For a positive trace-one operator $\rho$ on a separable Hilbert space,
its von Neumann entropy is the extended nonnegative spectral sum
$\sum_j-\lambda_j\log\lambda_j$, with $0\log0=0$. For every complete
orthonormal basis $b$, its diagonal probabilities have the form
$q_i=\sum_j|\langle b_i,e_j\rangle|^2\lambda_j$ in an eigenbasis $e$.
Concavity and nonnegative summation give
$\sum_i-q_i\log q_i\geq\sum_j-\lambda_j\log\lambda_j$.
An eigenbasis, completed by a basis of the kernel, attains equality.
This also applies when the entropy is infinite. The characterization
therefore identifies spectral entropy with the infimum of diagonal
Shannon entropies over complete countable bases.

The formal attenuator model adopts the latter definition. Its finite
diagonal spectral formula is kernel-proved using concavity, the
subprobability row bound and Parseval column sums. Its arbitrary-unitary
invariance is checked by transporting complete Hilbert bases. The general
spectral characterization for arbitrary density operators is cited,
not kernel-proved. Bibliographic metadata and the standard eigenbasis
argument support this reference; the full text of Wehrl's review has
not been inspected.
