## Source and scope

Publisher: Physical Review A 114, 022454; publication date 24 August 2026. [Publisher abstract](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/l1t8-84dk). The arXiv reference above pins the accessible first version, submitted 21 January 2026. The publisher abstract and this version provide the source attribution here; no exhaustive version or priority audit is asserted.

The state space is Inv(j_1,j_2,j_3,j_4), the SU(2)-invariant subspace of the four face-spin tensor product. It has the established quantum-tetrahedron interpretation with fixed face areas. The paper studies multipartite entanglement using entropic fill, numerically including equal spins from 1/2 through 11, and compares invariant, generic and coherent tensors. The publisher describes a nontrivial dependence on coherent-state geometric labels.

## Correspondence to the HS4 continuation

The spin-1/2 case supplies the same two-dimensional invariant carrier used in [HS4 spatial completion](../../docs/develop/theory/HIGUCHI_SUDBERY_AURIC_NETWORK_CLOSURE.md), Sections 9-13. Its principal entropic-fill objective is different from the HS4 average two-versus-two von Neumann entropy. A coherent-state tetrahedron label, an entropic tetrahedron and a quantum volume operator must retain their separate definitions.

The HS4 continuation derives its signed triple-product spectrum, constant unsigned-volume magnitude on this carrier, boundary-data disk and entropy-deficit formula from explicit Pauli and swap operators. It does not claim those formulas were proved by this paper. In particular the spin-1/2 unsigned volume operator is scalar; a claim that one state has greater volume magnitude than every other state in that same singlet carrier would fail.

The paper supplies context for the entanglement/geometry investigation. It is not cited as a proof of the unrestricted HS4 conjecture, the logical-qubit complete-positivity tradeoff, a Fibonacci-to-intertwiner preparation map, or emergence of a smooth spacetime.
