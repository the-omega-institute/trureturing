## Question 6.2

*Counting Boolean antichains*, arXiv:2608.27126v2 (2026), §6, Question 6.2,
p. 22. [Versioned question](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27126v2#S6.Thmtheorem2).

> Is the order on Boolean antichains of maximal size in the Tamari lattice
> equivalent to the Tamari order, the order on non-crossing partitions, or
> the order on Dyck paths?

The ambient Tamari lattice at rank \(m\) consists of the full binary
parenthesizations of \(m+1\) ordered letters, with the order generated by
contextual right rotations. Equation (2.a) tests the join of the meets of
each two subsets of an antichain against the meet of their intersection.
The antichain excludes top, and the empty meet is top. Lemma 5.4.3 identifies
the laminar interval labels, and Theorem 5.4.4 counts the largest Boolean
antichains. The first sentence of Question 6.2 asks for the induced order
rather than the cardinality. [Source enumeration](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27126v2#S5.Thmtheorem37).

## Related results

Ceballos, Padrol and Sarmiento use bracket vectors for rotation order and
credit the classical Huang–Tamari model. Li's Theorem 3.1 gives Boolean
recognition for general lattices. The literature screen attached to issue
#33263 found neither a proof of the induced order on the maximum families
nor these two comparison boundaries in the inspected sources. The citation
queries and MathDB access limits are recorded in the issue.
