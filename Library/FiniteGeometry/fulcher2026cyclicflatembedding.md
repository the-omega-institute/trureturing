# Fulcher on transversal q-matroid direct sums

[Conjecture 4.8](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13550v1#S4.Thmtheorem8)
asks for the direct sum of two transversal q-matroids to have the
presentation formed by lifting the slots of each factor to the full
space of the other factor and concatenating the two lists.
Theorem 4.7 establishes this assertion for coordinate presentations.

[Definition 2.12](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13550v1#S2.Thmtheorem12)
uses the every-basis avoidance condition of Saaltink. Definition 2.13
takes the resulting partial q-transversals as the independent subspaces.

[Definition 2.7](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13550v1#S2.Thmtheorem7)
uses the minimum of the two factor ranks of the projections of a common
subspace, plus its codimension within the rank argument. The cited
construction is Michela Ceria and Relinde Jurrius, *The direct sum of
q-matroids*, Definition 38 and Theorem 45.
