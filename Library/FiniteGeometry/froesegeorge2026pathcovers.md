## Question 1

Section 4, p. 23 asks:

> Is the sequence $(r_k)_k$ where $r_k$ is the number of elements of rank $k$ in the lattice of path covers always log-concave or unimodal?

Definition 2 assigns a partition to the saturated paths from the least element to each endpoint, including the length-zero path at the least element. Extension by a Hasse edge takes each block into a single block at the upper endpoint. Theorem 2 orders these families with the coarser family below the finer one. Corollary 1 gives the rank as the sum of the numbers of blocks minus one at each endpoint. The height is the rank of the family of singleton partitions.

The printed counting identity in Definition 1 allows a singleton to map to the top of a two-element chain. Requiring the least element to map to the least element gives the cover maps associated to Definition 2. A base Hasse edge then lifts uniquely from each point over its lower endpoint. The lift of a rooted path determines its partition block by its endpoint. Conversely, the fibres of the cover are the endpoint-tagged blocks, and the order is generated by the block-extension edges. Comparing covers over the base gives the refinement order in Theorem 2. The Lean refutation uses the partition presentation directly.
