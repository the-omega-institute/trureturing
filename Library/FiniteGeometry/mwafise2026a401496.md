# Labeled Riordan complement enumeration

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A401496

The target is revision 9, FORMULA conjectures 1–5, unpaginated. The definition is
A401493 revision 10, COMMENTS, unpaginated.

> Number of labeled Riordan graphs of order n whose complement, with the same vertex labeling, is also a labeled Riordan graph.

> Conjecture: a(n) = 8*a(n-2) - 4 for n >= 3.

> Conjecture: a(n+1) - a(n) = 2^floor(3*(n-1)/2), so a(n) = 1 + Sum_{k=1..n-1} 2^floor(3*(k-1)/2).

> Conjecture: a(n) = A113836(n-1) + 1.

> Conjecture: a(2*k) = (108^(k-1) + 4)/7 and a(2*k+1) = (38^k + 4)/7.

> Conjecture: G.f.: x*(1 - 2*x)*(1 + 3*x)/((1 - x)*(1 - 8*x^2)).

> A Riordan graph of order n is a simple graph on the vertices 1..n whose adjacency matrix A satisfies A(i,j) = [t^(i-2)] g(t)*f(t)^(j-1) mod 2 for i > j, for some power series g, f over GF(2) with f(0) = 0. Equivalently, the strictly lower triangular part of A is the binary Riordan matrix (g,f) of order n-1 (Cheon, Jung, Kitaev and Mojallal, 2019).

The concatenations `108` and `38` in the fourth line are interpreted as the
products `10 * 8` and `3 * 8`. The literal integer-base interpretation disagrees
with the displayed sequence. Author confirmation of the intended typography
is unverified. A113836 is used for n at least two. Its zero-based list of positive
nonmultiples of three is i + Nat.div i 2 + 1, and each exponent is one less than
the corresponding list entry. Its empty finite sum extends to index zero.

Graphs on Fin n represent the fixed labels 1..n by i.val + 1. The counted objects
are graphs, rather than power-series presentations or isomorphism classes.
The first power series need not be a unit. The second has constant coefficient
zero. Formal generating functions have constant coefficient zero and require
no analytic convergence assumption.

## Context and scope

Cheon, Jung, Kitaev and Mojallal, *Riordan graphs I: Structural properties*,
arXiv:1710.04604v2, https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.04604v2, Section 5, Problem 3:

> Characterize Riordan graphs whose complements are Riordan in labelled and unlabelled cases. See Section~\ref{complement-sec} for relevant observations.

The contextual journal locator is DOI 10.1016/j.laa.2019.05.033. The journal's
exact wording was not checked. The enumeration here concerns fixed labels;
the unlabeled classification and count remain open in this delivery.

The finite quotient criterion is r * delta^2 = 0. When r first differs at degree
k, the attainable second parameters are exactly the coefficient tail beginning
at k + ceil((N + 1 - k)/2), for truncation length N + 1. Distinct finite normal
forms count graphs without counting multiple presentations.
