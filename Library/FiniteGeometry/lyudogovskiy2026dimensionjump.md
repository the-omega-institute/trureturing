# Local simplex dimension jump in the partition graph

Fedor B. Lyudogovskiy submitted arXiv:2603.29988v1 on 31 Mar 2026. Problem 8.4
in §8.3 on printed page 21 asks:

> How much can the local simplex dimension change along a single edge of
> $G_n$? More precisely, what bounds can one place on
> $|\mathrm{dim}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\lambda)-\mathrm{dim}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mu)|$
> $(\lambda\sim\mu)$?

The vertices and unit-transfer adjacency come from §2.1. Section 2.2 defines
local simplex dimension as the maximum clique size through the vertex minus
one. Section 2.3 describes star and top capacities, and §2.4, Theorem 2.1,
imports the local-dimension formula from Proposition 5.4 of *Local Morphology
of the Partition Graph*. [Local morphology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18696v1)

Problem 6.4(iii) of *Simplex Stratification and Phase Boundaries in the
Partition Graph* asks the uniform-boundedness formulation. Proposition 3.1 of
arXiv:2605.28981v1 gives a support-size jump bound, and its Problem 7.4 also
asks about the location of jumps. [Earlier question](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23228v1) · [Support jumps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28981v1)
