# Ganguly on graphical symplectic matroids

> Over fields of characteristic 2 with at least 4 elements, is representability of 𝒮(G) preserved under 1-sums and under joining two graphs by a bridge?

## Definitions and related results

Definition 2.9 specifies isotropic full-row-rank representations and
the determinant test on admissible bases. Section 2.4 allows loops and
multiple edges. Definition 2.10 tests spanning components of a signed
selection: each is a tree or has a unique cycle with an odd number of
starred edges. A loop counts as a cycle of length one and a parallel pair
as a cycle of length two.

Lemma 3.1 gives the equivalent test on all admissible sets. Lemmas 3.3
and 3.4 treat contraction of representations and insertion of unstarred
forest edges. Corollary 3.5 extends the graphical construction to loops.
Example 6.8 gives particular joins that remain representable and
1-sums that fail over the two-element field.
