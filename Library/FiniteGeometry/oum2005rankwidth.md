# Rank-width and vertex-minors

Sang-il Oum, Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B 95(1), 79–100 (2005).
In Section 3, Proposition 3.1 uses a systematic binary chart with basis
coordinates on one part of a bipartite fundamental graph. Its definition of
the matroid connectivity function is restriction rank on a set plus
restriction rank on its complement minus total rank plus one. With this
convention the cut rank is the connectivity function minus one. The proof
separates the adjacency cut into the two crossed blocks.

The graph-state application uses Oum's binary rank identity. Complement
cancellation in the physical Walsh sum, the parallel-copy cancellation,
and the compact projective obstruction are separate derived steps, not claims
in Oum's paper.
