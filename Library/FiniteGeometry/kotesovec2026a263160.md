# The historical A263160 target and current source assertion

A263160 is the diagonal of A263159. Coordinate labels and step order are
retained. Each step decrements a nonempty subset by one and the path stops
as soon as any coordinate is zero. The empty path is the sole path when
height or dimension is zero.

## Scope

The all-index diagonal limit is proved by
`D5.S1.Words.LatticePaths.KotesovecA263160.diagonal_limit` for the literal
finite first-zero count. Its independent review, audit completion, freeze and
delivery remain distinct obligations; the source-bound audit is unfinished
under [issue #29924](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/29924).
The stronger current assertion would cover the historical target if proved,
but its public proof coverage, prior-art completeness and global priority
remain ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED. No first-proof or numerical-fit claim is made.

The endpoint binomial-moment formula is prior Dima/A126086, Eger
[arXiv:1511.00622v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.00622v2), section 5.5,
equation (3), page 10, and Bala/A262809. It allows arbitrary finite positive
dimension and prescribed endpoints; the box-summed representation is not
claimed as a new external solution. The read GHOW1990 author PDF, pages
133–146, DOI 10.1007/BF01787724, gives a fixed-`k` endpoint asymptotic with
`O_k` error. The read Caughman2011 author text, DOI 10.1016/j.disc.2011.04.024,
gives exact endpoint formulas in arbitrary finite dimension. Neither reading
located the required uniform first-zero diagonal theorem.

added in public history #12 on March 23, 2016; #13 records approval. Targeted

proof of `log I(n,n-1) - log b(n) = o(n^2)`, with `b(n)=A262810(n)`, in its
searched scope. This is not a fixed-dimension exclusion or a reason to
reprove the existing full Lean argument. No resolved-problem KPI is claimed
