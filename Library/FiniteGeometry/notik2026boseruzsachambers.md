# The Bose and Ruzsa chamber questions

## Verified locator

Esther Notik, [arXiv:2611.22656v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22656v1),
DOI [10.48550/arXiv.2611.22656](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.22656).
[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22656v1#page=8)

The last bullet of §10, page 8, asks

> Prove the conjecture of Section 7 for all prime powers, for instance by structured dual
> certificates as for the Conway–Guy and Bohman sets in [12]; and ask the same for the rulers
> of Bose [4] and Ruzsa [15].

Section 7 conjectures that every Singer ruler is the only shortest point of its
chamber, with Singer rulers defined using cuts at largest cyclic gaps. Section 2 uses
gaps as coordinates and defines a chamber by the order and unit separation of
labelled interval sums.

Notik cites R. C. Bose, “An affine analogue of Singer's theorem”, J. Indian Math.
Soc. (N.S.) 6 (1942), 1–15, and I. Z. Ruzsa, “Solving a linear equation in a set of
integers I”, Acta Arith. 65 (1993), 259–282. [Ruzsa, Theorem 4.4](https://doi.org/10.4064/aa-65-3-259-282)
