# Veblen points and central elements

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.1002/jcd.21964

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12796

Journal of Combinatorial Designs 33(3), pp. 94–108. Definition 2.5 on
preprint p. 6 states the Pasch completion condition. Given triples
\(\{q,a,c\}\), \(\{q,b,e\}\), and \(\{v,a,b\}\), a Veblen point \(q\)
requires \(\{v,c,e\}\) to be a triple. Theorem 2.7 and Corollary 2.8 on
preprint p. 7 identify those points with the nonidentity central elements
of the Steiner loop.

After deleting \(q\), set \(c=m(a)\) and \(e=m(b)\), where \(m\) pairs the
points in each original triple through \(q\). The Veblen completion holds
exactly when the block through \(v,m(a)\) has third point \(m(b)\).
Failure is the condition for the switch at \(v,a\) to give a maximal
retained packing.
