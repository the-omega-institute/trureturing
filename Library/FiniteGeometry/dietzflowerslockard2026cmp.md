The same question is labelled `V.20` in the HTML version arXiv:2609.08799v1.

The source defines m-ary partitions and conjugate m-ary partitions in Definition 1.1 (printed page 386; PDF page 2 of 23) and asks on printed page 404 (PDF page 20 of 23), Question 5.14: “When $m$ is odd, are there any self-conjugate CMPs of odd powers of $m$?” The delivered theorem gives a negative answer for every odd natural base at least two and every odd exponent.
