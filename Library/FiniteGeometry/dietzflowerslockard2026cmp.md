# Dietz–Flowers–Lockard Question 5.14

## Verified locator

DOI: https://doi.org/10.13069/jacodesmath.v13i3.393

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08799v1

The source defines m-ary partitions and conjugate m-ary partitions in Definition 1.1 and asks on printed page 404, Question 5.14: “When $m$ is odd, are there any self-conjugate CMPs of odd powers of $m$?” The delivered theorem gives a negative answer for every odd natural base at least two and every odd exponent.
