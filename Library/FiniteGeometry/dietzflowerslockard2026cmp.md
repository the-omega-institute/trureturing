# Dietz–Flowers–Lockard Questions 5.12 and 5.14

## Verified locator

DOI: https://doi.org/10.13069/jacodesmath.v13i3.393

Question 5.12 is labelled `V.18` and Question 5.14 is labelled `V.20` in the HTML version arXiv:2609.08799v1.

The source defines m-ary partitions and conjugate m-ary partitions in Definition 1.1 (printed page 386; PDF page 2 of 23) and asks on printed page 404 (PDF page 20 of 23), Question 5.14: “When $m$ is odd, are there any self-conjugate CMPs of odd powers of $m$?” The delivered theorem gives a negative answer for every odd natural base at least two and every odd exponent.

On the same page, Question 5.12 asks “When $m$ is even, is $m^{m+1}$ the smallest odd power of $m$ that posseses a self-conjugate CMP?” [Question 5.12 in arXiv:2609.08799v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.08799v1#S5.Thmtheorem18)

Discussion 5.11(1) gives the attaining construction $(MR)^m$. Flowers and Lockard prove its existence and weight for all $m\ge2$ in `Conjugate m-ary partitions`, Australasian Journal of Combinatorics 81(3), 458–473 (2021), Lemma 4.2, printed page 467 (PDF page 10). The descending parts $m^m,m^{m-1},\ldots,1$ have multiplicities $1,m-1,m^2-m,\ldots,m^m-m^{m-1}$. The resulting diagram is the union of rectangles of heights $m^j$ and widths $m^{m-j}$ for $0\le j\le m$.
