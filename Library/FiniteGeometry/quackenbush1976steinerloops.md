# Steiner loop translations

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.4153/CJM-1976-118-1

URL: https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/S0008414X00020873

Canadian Journal of Mathematics 28(6), pp. 1187–1198. Section 1, p. 1187,
credits R. H. Bruck for the construction. It adjoins an identity \(1\),
sets \(1x=x1=x\) and \(x^2=1\), and defines \(xy\) for distinct original
points as the third point in their unique block. The identities in the
same paragraph include \(x(xy)=y\), which makes translation by \(x\)
involutive. This translation pairs \(1\) with \(x\) and exchanges the
other two points in every block through \(x\).
