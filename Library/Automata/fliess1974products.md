# Sur divers produits de séries formelles

Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de France 102, 181–191.
Section 2 assumes a finite nonempty alphabet and a commutative unital
coefficient ring. Section 3, Proposition 1, printed page 183, states in
particular that the Hadamard product of two rational series is rational;
the author attributes this closure result to Schützenberger. The remark
on printed page 187 explicitly gives the tensor-product representation
for the Hadamard product.

For real linear word statistics filtered by a deterministic finite
automaton, the coefficients are multiplied by the accepted-language
indicator. This is the classical Hadamard construction. The block lift,
chronological effect-word convention and finite-horizon parameter
correspondence are explained in the existing FiniteWordReachability
Blueprint narrative. No novelty or native permission realization follows
from the cited closure theorem.
