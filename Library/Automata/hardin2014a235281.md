## Mathematical use

The entry counts literal indexed square fillings. Set $a(m)=T(m,m)$,
where $T$ is the rectangular count of A235289. Its diagonal agrees with
the square count because the dimensions, alphabet and contiguous block
condition agree. Applying the source formula at index $m+7$ with
$m\ge1$ uses only indices in the published offset and gives the first
identity at index eight. The signed identity is over the integers.

The proof uses the rectangular profile sum and the transposition symmetry
of the rectangular count. Comparing with widths two and three determines
the dependence on width needed on the diagonal. The resulting diagonal
sequence is annihilated by $(E-1)(E^2-2)(E^2-3)(E^2-4)$ for the forward
shift $E$. The proof is repo-derived. Its conclusion is the displayed
seven-lag identity for the square sequence.
