## Source statement

> Number of n X 6 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally, diagonally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

## Mathematical scope

The count is the cardinality of the filtered finite set of functions
`Fin n × Fin 6 → Fin 6`. Horizontal bonds and both diagonals exclude equal
colours and a natural-number sum of five. There is no vertical or wraparound
bond. The top-left colour is zero. In strict row-major order, every 2, 3 or 4
has an earlier 1, and every 3 has an earlier 2. Colours may be absent.
The first-cell existential sets the height-zero count to zero.

The recurrence starts at height eleven. The proof uses exact scanner fibres,
an eleven-class transfer, and the integer relation between the first eleven
row distributions. Pairing that relation with all continuation vectors proves
the recurrence at unbounded height. The ten complete-sector classes are
proper six-cell pair-label rows modulo label permutation and reversal;
the remaining class has not yet seen colour 2. Initial values are 136 and
92672. No claim of global priority or physical realization is made.
