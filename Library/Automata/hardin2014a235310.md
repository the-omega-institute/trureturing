## Mathematical use

The COMMENT compares the NAME count with three literal counts on positive-indexed rectangles. The pairs of alphabet maximum and stress are $(5,6)$, $(6,8)$ and $(7,10)$. The bounded preregistration literature screen records the scope of the related window-sum and reconstruction papers. [Preregistration #34296](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/34296)
