## Source statement

The NAME is:

> Number of n X 2 0..7 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value
> 7-x(i,j) horizontally, antidiagonally or vertically, top left element zero,
> and 1 appearing before 2 3 4 5 and 6, 2 appearing before 3 4 and 5, and 3
> appearing before 4 in row major order (unlabeled 8-colorings with no clashing
> color pairs).

The FORMULA block labels the following as a conjecture from Colin Barker:

> G.f.: x*(1 - 17*x + 40*x^2) / ((1 - 4*x)*(1 - 16*x)).

The OFFSET is 1,2. The series adds coefficient zero at index zero. The
positive coefficients begin 1,3,36,528,8256,131328. A bond forbids equality
and complementary colours summing to seven. Main-diagonal neighbours are
unconstrained.

## Mathematical scope

The complementary-pair labels have fibres {0,7}, {1,6}, {2,5}, {3,4}.
The row-major scanner records whether colours one, two and three have
occurred. The first row is (0,1). The remaining rows split into three
classes according to whether only one, two, or all three flags are set.
Their transition counts are the rows (0,2,1), (0,4,6), (0,0,16). The
zero eigenvalue is transient; the later recurrence has characteristic
polynomial (z-4)(z-16). These are finite combinatorial counts and an
identity of formal power series over the integers.

## Literature boundary

related values "appear to reproduce OEIS A233196" after an index shift.
That passage does not prove the literal array-count identity. No claim
