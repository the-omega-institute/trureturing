## Mathematical scope

At positive height, count individual arrays with six columns and entries from 0 through 7.
The top-left entry is zero. The endpoints of each contained horizontal, vertical and antidiagonal
bond differ and have sum different from seven. For every occurrence of 2 through 6 there is a
strictly earlier 1 in row-major order. Every occurrence of 3 through 5 also requires a strictly
earlier 2, and every 4 requires a strictly earlier 3. Colours may be unused.

The proof checks the transfer entries and continuation vectors of the exact weighted 58-state
quotient. Its degree-22 relation starts with the vector for one additional row. Propagation by
matrix powers and application of the anchored initial vector give the recurrence in integers.
The first applicable height is 24, where the last lag uses the height-two count.
