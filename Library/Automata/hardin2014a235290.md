## Mathematical use

The indexed square count is the equal-side specialization of the
literal rectangular count. The residual of the signed diagonal shift
obeys the axial order-ten recurrence separately in both side
parameters. A finite initial window of zero residuals determines
the residual at all positive side parameters.
