## Mathematical use

The index is one less than the number of rows. The grid has two columns,
and each choice of entries at the indexed cells gives one array. The
condition tests the absolute difference between the two diagonal sums
at each consecutive pair of rows. With the published offset one, both
lags in the formula have positive indices precisely when the left
index is at least five. The identity uses integer subtraction.

The proof groups rows by their signed difference and weights each group
by its number of fillings. The two-step multipliers six and eight give
the two quadratic factors of the recurrence.
