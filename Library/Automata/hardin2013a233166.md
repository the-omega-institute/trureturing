## Source sentences

> Number of n X 6 0..7 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 7-x(i,j) horizontally, diagonally, antidiagonally or vertically, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 4 5 and 6, 2 appearing before 3 4 and 5, and 3 appearing before 4 in row major order (unlabelled 8-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

## Literal interpretation

The cell domain is `Fin n × Fin 6`, and all functions into `Fin 8` count.
Distinct neighbouring cells have absolute coordinate differences at most
one. Equivalently, each coordinate of either cell is at most the other
coordinate plus one. There are no boundary identifications. Earlier means
strictly smaller rank `6*row+column`. Absent colours are allowed; 7 has no
extra precedence clause. No quotient or surjectivity restriction is added.
The empty-height coefficient is zero solely to define the formal series.
The denominator in the conjecture has constant coefficient one.

## Structural correspondence

David Eppstein, *Coloring kinggraphs*,
<https://11011110.github.io/blog/2019/04/11/coloring-kinggraphs.html>,
describes differently coloured crossing zones and the labelled square-board
count. The immutable post is
<https://github.com/11011110/blog/blob/70cf5b26fec1e19e1d2e0b8521430ba91ea21408/_posts/2019-04-11-coloring-kinggraphs.md>.
The zone idea is an existing published construction. For a rectangular
four-colour king strip, every square contains all four colours; encoding
colours as two bits makes opposite edge differences equal. This yields a
first-row/first-column decomposition. The signed first-occurrence fibres,
their cardinality and the exceptional one-row count are separate obligations;
the rectangle identity alone does not imply the A233166 formulas.
