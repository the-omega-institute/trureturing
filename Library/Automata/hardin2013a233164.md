## Literal interpretation

The domain is every function from `Fin n × Fin 4` to `Fin 8`.
The first cell is zero. Horizontal, vertical and both diagonal neighbours
exclude equal values and sums of seven, without wraparound. Every 2, 3,
4, 5 or 6 has an earlier 1; every 3, 4 or 5 has an earlier 2; every 4
has an earlier 3. Earlier means strictly smaller row-major rank.
Colours may be absent. Seven has no additional precedence requirement.
No orbit divisor or surjectivity requirement is imposed.
The source's parenthetical description does not introduce another quotient.
Clarification from the original enumerator is unavailable.
