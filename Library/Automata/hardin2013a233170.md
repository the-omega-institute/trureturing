## Source statement

> Number of n X 4 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally, diagonally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabeled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

The FORMULA block contains:

> Empirical: a(n) = 128*a(n-1) - 2816*a(n-2) + 16384*a(n-3).

> Empirical g.f.: 2*x*(5 - 240*x + 2048*x^2) / ((1 - 16*x)*(1 - 112*x + 1024*x^2)).

The OFFSET is 1,1. The positive-height coefficients begin 10,800,78336.
The formal power series uses constant coefficient zero.

## Mathematical scope

Arrays are literal functions on `Fin n × Fin 4` with values in `Fin 6`.
Horizontal bonds and both diagonals between consecutive rows exclude equal
colours and pairs summing to five. Vertical bonds and wraparound are
unconstrained. The first cell has colour zero. For each occurrence of 2,
3 or 4 there must be an earlier 1, and for each 3 an earlier 2, using
strict rank `4*row+column`. Colours may be absent. There is no orbit divisor.
The count is defined independently of the rational series.

The proof reuses the width-independent literal counting bridge in
`HardinA233251TriangleStrip`, the scanner in `HardinA233175PairStrip`, and
the complementary-pair map `BooleanRankThreeK4.beta`. A kernel-checked
finite continuation certificate and induction over every continuation
length give the recurrence and the initial counts. Coefficient comparison
and formal inversion then give the full identity in rational power series.
Finite agreement and an eventual recurrence alone are insufficient.
No claim of global priority is made.
