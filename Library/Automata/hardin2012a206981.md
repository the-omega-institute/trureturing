Revision 17 of the OEIS entry has no page numbers. Its NAME is:

> Number of n X 2 0..1 arrays avoiding the patterns 0 1 0 or 1 0 1 in any row, column, diagonal or antidiagonal.

Its first FORMULA entry is:

> Empirical: a(n) = 2*a(n-1) + 2*a(n-2) - a(n-3). G.f.: -4*x*(-1-2*x+x^2) / ( (1+x)*(x^2-3*x+1) ).

## Mathematical use

The arrays are ordinary rectangles with contiguous triples and no wraparound.
Rows have indices in `Fin n`, columns in `Fin 2`, and entries in `Bool`, with
`false` representing zero. The array count is the `Nat.card` of the subtype
satisfying the four-direction predicate, independently of any recurrence.
The integer formal generating series has coefficient zero in degree zero
and the actual array count in each positive degree. The recurrence starts at
`n ≥ 4`. Its count subtraction is in integers; the guarded lagged indices
are natural subtractions. The generating-function assertion is expressed
by clearing its denominator, whose constant coefficient is one.

Width two leaves only vertical triples. Each legal positive-length column
corresponds to its first bit and an admissible word of adjacent XOR switches.
The existing admissible-word count and Mathlib's partial-sum reconstruction
laws give the Fibonacci-square count used to establish the first FORMULA.
The second empirical formula and wider arrays are outside this result.
