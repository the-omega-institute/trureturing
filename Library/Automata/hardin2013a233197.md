## Mathematical scope

Complementary-pair labels have fibres {0,7}, {1,6}, {2,5}, {3,4}. The
existing eight-colour scanner retains whether 1,2,3 have occurred. Legal
boundary rows have distinct adjacent pair labels. Five classes distinguish
whether 2 and 3 have occurred and whether the two outer labels agree.
The initial vector is (2,0,1,0,0). The class transfer rows are
(0,0,0,2,2), (0,0,0,8,8), (0,0,8,4,16), (0,0,0,16,16), (0,0,0,8,40).
The fully seen block has characteristic polynomial t²−56t+512; the
remaining sector supplies eigenvalue 8 and transient zero eigenvalues.
These counts connect the literal array filter to an integer power series.
