# Fixed-board Divisor Nim periodicity

## Verified locator

DOI and publisher URL: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00182-026-00991-5

## Scope

Morisawa supplies the broad eventual-periodicity background. This citation does
not assert that the paper states the comparison spacing built from the minimum
fixed heap and the least periods of every direct fixed successor, or the
gcd-two multiplier identity. The native formalization uses the literal finite
multiset game and its full Sprague–Grundy sequence.

The finite-memory argument uses bounded SG blocks sampled at a common forcing
phase. Equality of two such output states propagates along the exact recurrence.

The two-pile predecessor, *Odd case of 2-pile divisor Nim*, DOI
10.1007/s00182-025-00948-0, has a narrower domain. Nohagi's *Sprague-Grundy Value
for Common Divisor Nim*, arXiv:2312.05466, concerns a different move rule.
Neither is used as a proof of the arbitrary-multiset comparison-spacing result.
