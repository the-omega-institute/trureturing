NAME:

> Number of 4Xn 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value
> 5-x(i,j) horizontally, diagonally or antidiagonally, top left element zero,
> and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order
> (unlabeled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

> Empirical: a(n) = 64*a(n-1) +3328*a(n-2) -77824*a(n-3)

## Mathematical scope

The arrays are functions `Fin 4 × Fin n → Fin 6`. Bonds connect consecutive
columns at row distance at most one; vertical neighbours are unconstrained.
The pair-label map is `D5.S3.Observer.Separation.BooleanRankThreeK4.beta`,
with fibres `{0,5}`, `{1,4}`, `{2,3}`. The published pointwise bond equivalence
is `HardinA233251TriangleStrip.colour_bond_iff`.

Row-major normalization orders the first occurrences of the used pair labels
and fixes the first colour in each pair to its smaller member. For widths
at least two, `HardinA233177Ladder.counting_bridge` proves
`48*a(n) = 16^n*(L(n)+6)`, where `L` counts proper three-label patterns.
The fourteen-class column transfer proves the recurrence for arbitrary width,
and `HardinA233177Ladder.result` states the integer recurrence at index `n+9`.
