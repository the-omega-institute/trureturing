## Mathematical use

The NAME specifies the literal square count indexed by the side length
minus one. The COMMENT identifies it with the diagonal of A235280. With
the FORMULA's left-hand index written as $n+11$, the first full window
uses indices 1 through 12. The cardinalities are cast to integers for
the signed recurrence.

The repository proof counts pairs of complementary row fibers with
prescribed ranges of admissible first-cell shifts. Mixed second
differences recover the exact frequencies of their sizes. On the square
diagonal each summand obeys a first- or two-step shift law, yielding the
annihilating polynomial $(X-1)(X^2-2)(X^2-3)(X^2-4)(X^2-6)(X^2-8)$.
