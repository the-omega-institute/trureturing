## Source statement

> Number of n X 2 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to value 5-x(i,j) horizontally, antidiagonally or vertically, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order.

> Empirical g.f.: x*(2 - 34*x + 93*x^2 - 22*x^3 + x^4) / ((1 - x)*(1 - 18*x + x^2)*(1 - 6*x + x^2)). - _Colin Barker_, Oct 08 2018

The OFFSET is 1,1. Equal colours are permitted on all constrained bonds.
The row-major rank is 2i+j, and every occurrence of 2, 3 or 4 must
have a strictly earlier 1. Every occurrence of 3 must also have a
strictly earlier 2. Colours need not all occur. An empty array has no
upper-left cell, so the constant coefficient is zero.

## Mathematical scope

The width-two transfer uses the two shared first-use flags and the two
previous row colours, giving 144 states. The literal count is the
first-row aggregation of the continuation vector. Direct finite
certification establishes six continuation steps and a degree-five
relation on the full state space after one step. Propagating the
relation gives the recurrence for every index at least seven. The
initial coefficients also establish the recurrence at index six.
Coefficient comparison and the invertible denominator in
`PowerSeries ℚ` give the complete rational series.
