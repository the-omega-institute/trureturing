## Source statement

> Number of n X 3 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

The FORMULA block attributes the conjectures to Colin Barker and contains:

> G.f.: x*(3 - 64*x) / ((1 - 8*x)*(1 - 48*x)).

The OFFSET is 1,1. The power-series convention adds coefficient zero at
index zero. Only horizontal and antidiagonal bonds are constrained. A
bond forbids equality and complementary colours with sum five.

## Mathematical scope

The pair labels are the fibres `{0,5}`, `{1,4}`, `{2,3}` of the existing
map `BooleanRankThreeK4.beta`. After the first row, colour one has been
seen. Continuations split into two classes according to whether colour
two has been seen. A row has 48 continuations from the latter class.
From the former class, eight continuations remain there and twenty enter
the latter class. The first row contributes two arrays to the former
class and one to the latter. This gives the integer recurrence with
characteristic polynomial `(z-8)(z-48)` and the stated generating function.
The assertion concerns finite combinatorial counts and formal power
series; no physical realization or priority claim is asserted.
