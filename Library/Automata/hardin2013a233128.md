## Mathematical scope

The complementary-pair map is the published `BooleanRankThreeK4.beta`.
The row-major scanner and its exact fibre multiplicities are reused directly.
The 192 proper label rows have 20 orbits under relabelling and reversal.
Before colour 2 occurs there is one additional sector whose self-transition is 128.
The first-row distribution, in the order used by the formal class map, is

```text

```

produces vectors D(r). The coefficient identity D(14) = 7168 D(13) - ...
- 40564819207303340847894502572032 D(0) proves the scalar recurrence
when paired with the continuation vector at any height.
