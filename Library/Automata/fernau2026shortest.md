# Shortest resets of product automata

## Verified locator

https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.09136

https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09136v1

Section 2, p. 3, defines deterministic finite automata with total transitions and the action of words on states and subsets. Lemmas 5 and 6 and Proposition 7 in §3, pp. 5–6, identify product resets with common factor resets and bound the product's shortest reset length between the maximum and the sum of the two factor thresholds. A reset of the first factor followed by a reset of the second always gives a product reset. [PDF, §3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09136v1#page=5)

The fourth bullet of §7, p. 19, asks whether one can first synchronize the first automaton optimally, choosing among its shortest reset words, and then solve subset synchronization for the second automaton. The optimal-length reading asks whether this strategy can always attain the unrestricted product threshold. [PDF, §7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09136v1#page=19)
