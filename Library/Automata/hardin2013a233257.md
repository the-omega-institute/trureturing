NAME counts functions on a two-row strip with colours zero through five,
excluding equal or complementary colours on horizontal and antidiagonal
bonds, with top-left colour zero and the stated first-occurrence order.
Hardin's FORMULA is labelled empirical: `a(n)=16*a(n-1)-48*a(n-2)` for `n>3`.
Barker's conjectures give `a(n)=2^(2*n-3)*(4*3^n+9)/9` for `n>1` and
`x*(3-38*x+88*x^2)/((1-4*x)*(1-12*x))` as generating function.
OFFSET is `1,1`; the first four terms are `3,10,104,1184`.
The entry supplies no proof. Global openness is ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED.

## Mathematical scope

The Lean count filters all functions `Fin 2 × Fin n → Fin 6` by the literal
bond and row-major normalization predicates. The first-cell existential
sets the coefficient at width zero to zero. The complementary-pair map
has fibres `{0,5}`, `{1,4}`, `{2,3}`. For width `n+2`, the proper pair-label
patterns number `24*3^n`; exactly six use two labels. The canonical bridge
of `HardinA233177Canonical` weights each representative by one independent
side choice per cell after the first occurrence of each label. It yields
`a(n+2)=2^(2*n+1)*(4*3^n+1)` and all three stated formulas.
