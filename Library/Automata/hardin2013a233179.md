## Mathematical scope

Arrays are literal functions `Fin 6 × Fin n → Fin 6`. Bonds connect consecutive
columns at row distance at most one. Vertical neighbours are unconstrained.
The map `D5.S3.Observer.Separation.BooleanRankThreeK4.beta` identifies the
complementary pairs `{0,5}`, `{1,4}` and `{2,3}`. Its colour-side decomposition
leaves six Boolean choices per column, subject to fixed first occurrences.
At width at least two the counting bridge is
`48*a(n) = 64^n*(L(n)+6)`, where `L` counts proper three-label patterns.
The column transfer has 122 relabelling orbits, with 50 live orbits.
Its order-24 scalar relation and the width-26 finite instance give the
integer recurrence at index `n+26`.
