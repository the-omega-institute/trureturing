NAME:

> Number of nX4 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j)
> horizontally or vertically, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4,
> and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabeled 6-colorings with no clashing
> color pairs).

FORMULA:

> Empirical: G.f.: -2*x*(-5+352*x-7168*x^2+40960*x^3) /
> ( (16*x-1)*(24576*x^3-3840*x^2+144*x-1) ). - R. J. Mathar, Oct 31 2015

## Mathematical scope

The colour-pair labels are `{0,5}`, `{1,4}`, `{2,3}`. Before colour 2 is seen, the
proper boundary alternates between the first two labels. Afterwards there are three
classes under label permutation and reversal: alternating, exactly one repeated
nonadjacent label with unequal endpoints, and equal endpoints.

With the unseen sector first, the transition matrix and first-row distribution are

```text
16 16 32  8
 0 48 64 16
 0 32 64 16
 0 16 32 32

4 0 4 2
```

The first sector has characteristic factor `1-16X`; the other three have factor
`1-144X+3840X²-24576X³`. The literal counts start
`10, 896, 96256, 10674176, 1189609472, 132682612736`.
