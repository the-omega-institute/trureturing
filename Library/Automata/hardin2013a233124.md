# OEIS A233124

## Verified locator

Source: <https://oeis.org/A233124>, current revision 7, sequence export
<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oeis/oeisdata/main/seq/A233/A233124.seq>.
The entry is unpaginated. DOI: null. The locators are NAME, the Barker
G.f. FORMULA line, and OFFSET.

NAME:

> Number of n X 3 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j)
> horizontally or vertically, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4,
> and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing
> color pairs).

The FORMULA block attributes the conjectures to Colin Barker and contains:

> G.f.: x*(3 - 64*x + 192*x^2) / ((1 - 8*x)*(1 - 40*x + 128*x^2)).

The OFFSET is 1,1. Extending the finite count to the empty array gives
coefficient zero at index zero, since the distinguished first cell does not exist.

## Mathematical scope

Neighbours lie in different fibres of `BooleanRankThreeK4.beta`, namely
`{0,5}`, `{1,4}`, `{2,3}`. Diagonals are unconstrained. The normalization
is the literal earlier-occurrence condition in row-major order.

After the first row, colour one has occurred. Before colour two occurs,
the boundary labels alternate between zero and one. Once colour two has
occurred, the two endpoint labels are either equal or different. In that
class order the continuation matrix is `[[8,8,8],[0,24,16],[0,16,16]]`,
and the first-row distribution is `[2,0,1]`. Its characteristic polynomial
is `(z-8)(z^2-40z+128)`. This gives the formal generating-function identity
over the integers. The assertion is combinatorial and makes no claim
about physical realization or priority beyond the cited attribution.
