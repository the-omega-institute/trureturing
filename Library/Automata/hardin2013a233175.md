## Source statement

> Number of 2 X n 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally, diagonally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

> Conjectures from Colin Barker, Oct 10 2018: G.f.: x*(3 - 28*x - 16*x^2 + 224*x^3) / ((1 - 4*x)*(1 - 8*x - 32*x^2)).

The offset is `1,1`. The initial positive-width values are
`3,8,80,800,8576,92672`. The sequence and empirical recurrence are due to
Hardin; the generating function is attributed to Barker.

## Mathematical interpretation

There are four bonds between consecutive columns and no bond within a column.
The three complementary pairs are `{0,5}`, `{1,4}` and `{2,3}`. A bond is
legal exactly when its endpoints have different pair labels. The two Boolean
flags recording earlier occurrences of colours one and two express the
row-major convention. The final top-row flags are the initial bottom-row
flags. These records, together with the preceding pair labels, give a finite
column transfer. The series uses zero for its width-zero coefficient.

The generating-function identity is the mathematical claim registered at
<https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/27656>.
The radical expression, other strip widths and physical interpretations
are outside its scope. The source's conjecture label and a bounded literature
screen do not establish worldwide priority or exhaustive prior-art exclusion.
