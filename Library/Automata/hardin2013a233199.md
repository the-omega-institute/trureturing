# OEIS A233199

## Verified locator

https://oeis.org/A233199

The fixed source is record A233199 revision 6 at oeisdata commit
`d4ade8b4ca7c6ff0ac8931532918fd4f6176805e`. The relevant fields are
`%N` for the definition, `%F` for the empirical recurrence, and `%O`
for the offset `1,1`.
<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oeis/oeisdata/d4ade8b4ca7c6ff0ac8931532918fd4f6176805e/seq/A233/A233199.seq>

The counted arrays are functions from `Fin n × Fin 5` to `Fin 8` at
positive height, with top-left value zero. Each contained horizontal,
vertical, and antidiagonal edge has unequal endpoint colours whose
natural labels sum to a value different from seven. Every occurrence
of a colour in {2, 3, 4, 5, 6} requires an earlier 1, every occurrence
in {3, 4, 5} requires an earlier 2, and every 4 requires an earlier 3.
Here “earlier” means strictly smaller row-major rank `5i+j`.
Colours may be unused.

The formula is over the integers and applies at every height `n ≥ 12`.
The signed coefficients, from lag one to lag ten, are
`736, -203776, 33062912, -3665821696, 270851375104, -10685878632448,
-116548232544256, 35184372088832000, -1423137482249076736,
18446744073709551616`.

The ordinary proof of this exact assertion uses an anchored four-pair transfer
on fourteen row patterns and derives the signed fibre multiplicities.
<https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/28456#issuecomment-6753096553>
