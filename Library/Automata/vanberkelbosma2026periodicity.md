# Periodicity conjectures for all 2-sumfree sequences

## Locator

DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2609.18522

Versioned source https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18522v1

The arXiv:2609.18522v1 text is dated 16 September 2026. The Introduction on printed
pages 1–2 defines the two-seed strict greedy sequence and the minimal
preperiod of its first differences. Definition 8(e) and (k1) are on printed
pages 7–8. Conjecture 9 and Example 10 appear on printed page 10.

At seeds 5 and 21, the seed difference is 16, with representative 6 modulo
10 and quotient 1. Clause (e) gives 30 and (k1) subtracts 21, giving 9.
Example 10 lists 15 at the same coordinate. The distinction identifies the
exact displayed formula asserted by Conjecture 9.

The source reading used the supplied v1 PDF text. The issue records the
counterexample and an independent mathematical certificate. [#34312](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/34312)
