NAME:

> Number of 3 X n 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or
> value 5-x(i,j) horizontally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and
> 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order
> (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

FORMULA:

> Empirical: a(n) = 48*a(n-1) - 448*a(n-2) + 1024*a(n-3) for n>5.

> Empirical g.f.: x*(11 - 392*x + 3072*x^2 - 8192*x^3 + 16384*x^4) /
> ((1 - 8*x)*(1 - 40*x + 128*x^2)). - Colin Barker, Oct 11 2018

## Mathematical scope

The literal count filters all functions `Fin 3 × Fin n → Fin 6` by the
published bond and row-major predecessor conditions. Its value at zero is
zero. The pair map `BooleanRankThreeK4.beta` has fibres `{0,5}`, `{1,4}`
and `{2,3}`; `HardinA233251TriangleStrip.colour_bond_iff` identifies a legal
bond with distinct pair labels. The finite-cell canonical-form theorems of
`HardinA233177Canonical` supply the normalization and orbit counts.

The column representatives `000,001,010,011,012` give five relabelling
orbits. With pair-pattern count `L`, the canonical bridge is
`48*a(n) = 8^n*(L(n)+6)` for `n ≥ 2`. The cubic polynomial for `L+6` is
`t^3-6*t^2+7*t-2`; scaling by eight gives
`t^3-48*t^2+448*t-1024`. The integer power-series identity multiplies the
series by the denominator of Barker's expression, whose constant term is one.
