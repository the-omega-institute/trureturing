# Prefix corrections in the V-toothpick sequence

The [pinned preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.23172v1) defines first-touch
growth on the honeycomb lattice in §2.1 and Definition 3. Lemma 7 gives the
earliest-birth parity criterion. Theorem 12 supplies dyadic copy identities.
Equation (17) defines the kernel by removing the first nonzero delay from the
difference of the two adjacent-cut birth polynomials. Lemma 17 proves
independence of the enclosing dyadic height. Lemma 36 bounds the signed support
of the cut discrepancy. Equation (43) defines the period readout and prefix
correction.

[Section 9](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.23172v1#S9.p3) poses the all-prefix
question. Theorem 39 is stated for the eventual range and Appendix B,
Proposition 43 reports finite checks through degree 63.
