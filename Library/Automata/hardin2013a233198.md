# OEIS A233198

## Verified locator

https://oeis.org/A233198

The fixed source is oeisdata commit
`571197984aeda7b8b680fe3a1f6a43f9898cd441`, record A233198 revision 7,
2018-10-10. Its sequence export is
<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oeis/oeisdata/571197984aeda7b8b680fe3a1f6a43f9898cd441/seq/A233/A233198.seq>.
The relevant locators are NAME, Barker's generating-function FORMULA, and
OFFSET. The record credits the definition to R. H. Hardin and the empirical
generating function to Colin Barker. The record's data license is
CC BY-SA 4.0; this note cites the record and does not import its data table.

The objects are functions from `Fin n × Fin 4` to `Fin 8`, for positive
height `n`. The top-left colour is zero. Contained horizontal, vertical,
and antidiagonal bonds exclude both equal colours and natural sums equal
to seven. The main diagonal and wraparound bonds have no condition.
In strict row-major order, every occurrence of any of 2, 3, 4, 5, 6
requires an earlier 1; every occurrence of 3, 4, 5 requires an earlier 2;
every occurrence of 4 requires an earlier 3. Individual colours and
complementary pairs may be absent.

The offset is `1,1`. With zero constant coefficient, the empirical
generating function is

\[
\frac{X(11-1760X+99328X^2-3080192X^3+55050240X^4-369098752X^5)}
 {(1-16X)(1-192X+10240X^2-278528X^3+4194304X^4)}.
\]

The record is a specification and attribution for this expression. Its
label “Empirical g.f.” supplies no proof of the equality. The ordered
pair-fibre construction is a separate combinatorial deduction: pair
`r` is `{r,7-r}`, its first representative is forced, and subsequent
representatives carry independent binary choices.
