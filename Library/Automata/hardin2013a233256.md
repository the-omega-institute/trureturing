# OEIS A233256

## Verified locator

Source: <https://oeis.org/A233256>, revision 6; sequence export:
<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oeis/oeisdata/main/seq/A233/A233256.seq>.
Locators: NAME, the FORMULA lines for columns 1 and 2 and row 1, and OFFSET.
The b-file <https://oeis.org/A233256/b233256.txt> has indices 1 through 240.
DOI: null.

NAME defines `T(n,k)` as the number of `n × k` arrays over `0..5` with
horizontal and antidiagonal bonds excluding equal colours and colours
summing to five. The first cell is zero. In row-major order every 2, 3
or 4 has an earlier 1, and every 3 an earlier 2.

FORMULA labels these recurrences empirical:

- Column 1: `a(n) = 12*a(n-1) - 44*a(n-2) + 48*a(n-3)`.
- Column 2: `a(n) = 20*a(n-1) - 64*a(n-2)`.
- Row 1: `a(n) = 6*a(n-1) - 8*a(n-2)` for `n > 3`.

OFFSET is `1,3`. Antidiagonal enumeration puts `T(n,k)` at index
`(n+k-2)*(n+k-1)/2+n`. The three slices start respectively
`1,3,11,48,236`, `1,10,136,2080` and `1,1,3,10,36,136`.
The row-one recurrence fails at width three: its right side is `-2`.

## Mathematical scope

The literal counts filter all functions on the stated finite cell set.
The one-column graph has no bonds. Both a one-row graph and a two-column
graph are paths in row-major order. The public scanner and row-transfer
owners express the predecessor conditions and the exact filtered count.
The continuation identities are propagated by linearity, with valid
boundary states preserved at each step. These arguments prove the three
displayed recurrences at indices at least 4, 3 and 4, respectively.
