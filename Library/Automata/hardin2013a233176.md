## Mathematical scope

The pair labels are the fibres `{0,5}`, `{1,4}`, `{2,3}` of
`D5.S3.Observer.Separation.BooleanRankThreeK4.beta`. Two colours can lie on a
constrained bond exactly when their pair labels differ. A column has three
horizontal and four diagonal bonds to the next column. Vertical neighbours
are unconstrained.

The pointwise equivalence is
`HardinA233251TriangleStrip.colour_bond_iff`; its `Row` type represents a
three-cell column. The ladder's literal `Legal` differs from `widthLegal`
specialized with the triangle strip's `rowLegal`: the all-zero width-one
ladder is legal, whereas `rowLegal` rejects its column. The ladder predicate
also includes width zero, where the first-cell existential is false.

The scanner has the three reachable records `(false,false)`, `(true,false)`
and `(true,true)`, ordered by which of colours one and two has appeared.
Let `t,b,c` be the current records of the three rows, and `q,r` the final
records of the first two rows. A compatible continuation has
`t ≤ q ≤ b ≤ r ≤ c`. Its preceding column labels are bounded respectively
by `t,b,c`. These conditions give 168 states in 21 record classes, with
159 different nonzero transitions. The scalar count uses row-major scanning
along entire rows, although the finite transfer advances whole columns.
The claim concerns finite counts and formal integer power series.
