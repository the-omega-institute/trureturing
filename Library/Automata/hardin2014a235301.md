## Mathematical use

The count uses the entire indexed rectangle. The signed recurrence
holds for any positive fixed side and any varying index at least eleven.
The proof uses the congruence of both possible signed block differences
modulo six, an exact boundary-word representation and a weighted
transfer on parity, last letter and compatible column-edge masks.
Transposition gives the other axis.
