## Source statement

> Number of n X 5 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally, diagonally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabeled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

## Mathematical scope

The count is the cardinality of the filtered finite set of functions
`Fin n × Fin 5 → Fin 6`. There is no orbit divisor. The horizontal bonds
and both diagonals between consecutive rows exclude equality and a
natural-number sum of five. No vertical or wraparound bond is imposed.
The upper-left colour is zero. Every occurrence of 2, 3 or 4 has an earlier
1, and every occurrence of 3 has an earlier 2, in strict row-major order.
Colours may be absent. The first-cell existential makes the height-zero
count zero.

The formal recurrence holds at all heights at least eight. The proof uses
the width-independent literal counting bridge, exact scanner fibres, and
the existing seven boundary classes and first-row distribution. A finite
single-row transfer certificate feeds induction at every continuation
length. The eighth distribution row is the stated combination of the
preceding seven rows. This vector identity proves the recurrence for
unbounded height; matching the b-file is only a finite numerical check.
No claim of global priority is made.
