## Verified locator

Section 3.1 of the v2 manuscript gives the adjacency block display. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2407.05653) · [Version 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05653v2)

The copy partition in equation (2) groups vertices by their label in the attached graph. The displayed adjacency matrix has copy block \(A_H\otimes I_V\). Swapping each copy coordinate \((w,v)\) to \((v,w)\) in both rows and columns gives the copy block \(I_V\otimes A_H\) and the repeated columns of \(I_V+A_G\) in the upper cross block. The base coordinates are fixed. [Copy partition](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.05653v2#S3.E2) · [Adjacency block display](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.05653v2#S3.Ex4)
