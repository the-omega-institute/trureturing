# Adjacency-matrix factorization of windmills and complete graphs

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2512.24864

[Section 5, item 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24864v1#S5.I1.i1) defines the windmill by identifying one vertex from each of $d\ge1$ copies of $K_t$, with $t\ge3$. It asks to determine all pairs $(d,t)$ for which the resulting graph admits a factorization.

[Section 5, item 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24864v1#S5.I1.i2) asks for two non-transitive graphs $H$ and $K$ with adjacency matrices $B$ and $C$ such that $BC=J-I$, the adjacency matrix of a complete graph.

[Section 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24864v1#S1) defines factorization by taking ordinary products of the two graph adjacency matrices. Each matrix uses the same labelled vertex set.

## Complete-graph prerequisite

Farzad Maghsoudi, Babak Miraftab and Sho Suda, *On Matrix Product Factorization of Graphs* (2023), give the complete-graph criterion used for a single blade. The Windmill proof reconstructs the criterion on its labelled carrier. [Theorem 1.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.08615v1#S1.Thmthm2)
