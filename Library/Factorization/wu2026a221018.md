## Mathematical scope

The proof uses Fibonacci index divisibility at indices 3, 4 and 5. Divisibility
by k and k+1 forces an even Fibonacci value; divisibility feedback then forces
12 to divide k. Divisibility by the neighbours excludes the remainders 1 and 4
modulo 5. The three stated decimal remainders follow.

Infinitude, density and infinitely many occurrences of each allowed final
digit remain open. No downstream result relying on this conjecture was
identified in the sources screened in issue #21590.
