# Browne's Chebyshev numerator and floor product

## Verified locator

https://oeis.org/search?q=id:A164660&fmt=text

OEIS A164660, revision 19, supplies these verbatim FORMULA lines:

> a(n) = numerator(Sum_{m=1..n+1} IT(n,m)), n>=0, with IT(n,m):= A164658(n,m)/A164659(n,m) (coefficient triangle from the indefinite integral Integral_{x} T(n,x), n>=0, in lowest terms).

> a(n+1) = Product_{k=1..n} ( 1-2*(floor(k^n/n)-floor((k^n -1)/n)) ) = (-1)^(A003557(n)) for n>0 (conjecture). - _Anthony Browne_, May 29 2016

The record has no page numbers. Wolfdieter Lang introduced the sequence;
Anthony Browne authored the final conjectural FORMULA.

The coefficient definition is given in A164658, revision 21:

> a(n,m) = numerator(b(n,m)), with int(T(n,x))= sum(b(n,m)*x^m,m=1..n+1), n>=0, where T(n,x) are Chebyshevs polynomials of the first kind.

A164659, revision 10, gives the denominator of the same coefficients.
The primitive has constant coefficient zero. Thus its column j is the
coefficient of degree j-1 in T(m,x), divided by j, for 1 <= j <= m+1.
The row sum is rational, and its numerator is the reduced signed numerator
with positive denominator. The floor product uses rational subtraction and
division before taking integer floors. Its index set includes both endpoints.

A003557, revision 172, identifies its term as follows:

> n divided by largest squarefree divisor of n; if n = Product p(k)^e(k) then a(n) = Product p(k)^(e(k)-1), with a(1) = 1.

> a(n) = n/rad(n) = n/A007947(n) = sqrt(J_2(n)/J_2(rad(n))), where J_2(n) is A007434. - _Enrique Pérez Herrero_, Aug 31 2010

The Lean exponent is natural division by the existing natural radical,
including radical(1)=1. The theorem asserts both source equalities for
every positive natural index, including one. It derives the numerator
through a polynomial primitive and the product through counting multiples
of the radical; divisibility by four identifies their common sign.
