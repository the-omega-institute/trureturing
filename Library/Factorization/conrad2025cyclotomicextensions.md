# Cyclotomic extensions

The [author text](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/galoistheory/cyclotomic.pdf),
§1, printed p.1, identifies the complex $m$th roots of unity as
$e^{2\pi i j/m}$ for $0\le j<m$ and describes their equally spaced
circle positions. Section 5 defines cyclotomic polynomials and gives
$\Phi_p(T)=1+T+\cdots+T^{p-1}$ for prime $p$.

The associated elementary finite sum identity is
$(1-z)(1+z+\cdots+z^{m-1})=1-z^m$.
Thus any nontrivial $m$th root gives a zero sum, including $m=2,3,5$.
The identity is polynomial and needs no division until it is evaluated
at $z\ne1$.

This note supplies the classical circular readout, not an identification
between a FIB mode set and a polygon rotation action. A different grading
of the same configurations has a different generating polynomial.
