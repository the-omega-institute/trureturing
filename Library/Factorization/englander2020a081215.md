# Englander's self-power quotient conjectures

The definition on physical page 1 is
$a(n)=(n^{n+1}+(-1)^n)/(n+1)^2$. Physical page 63 states Conjecture XIV
for every integer $n>2$. Its six polynomials, indexed by $n$ modulo six,
are retained in `SelfPowerCubicRemainders.twiceR`; that definition records
twice each prescribed remainder using integer arithmetic. The equivalent
six integral polynomials at $n=6h+c$ are used in the proof.

Physical page 66 states Conjecture XVII for every positive integer $k$
and every integer $n>k$. One fixed polynomial of degree $k$ is assigned
to each of the $2k$ residue classes. The full existence assertion includes
$k=1,n=2$. Complex coefficients are allowed by the formal claim, hence
real and rational coefficients are included. The three allowed even inputs
$2,4,6$ have exact quotient values $1,41,5713$ and remainders $0,2,3$
modulo $n-1$. They cannot be values of an affine polynomial. This refutes
the full existence assertion independently of the additional printed
zero-class formula. No revised eventual or $k\ge2$ assertion is settled.
