## Source statements

OEIS A369673, NAME and COMMENTS, verbatim (unpaginated):

```text
a(n) = Product_{k=0..n} (2^k + 2^(n-k)).
Conjectures:
(C.1) a(n) is a square iff n is not divisible by 4.
(C.2) a(2*n+1) is not divisible by 5 for n >= 0.
(C.3) exponent of highest power of 5 dividing a(4*n) = 2*A127428(n).
(C.4) exponent of highest power of 5 dividing a(4*n+2) = 2*A127428(n+1).
```

OEIS A127428, NAME, verbatim (unpaginated):

```text
v_5( (10n)! ) - v_5( (5n)! ), where v_p(N) is the p-adic valuation defined in A112765.
```

OEIS A112765, NAME, verbatim (unpaginated):

```text
Exponent of highest power of 5 dividing n. Or, 5-adic valuation of n.
```

## Mathematical scope

Pairing the product factors leaves, at even indices, the factorization
`a(2m) = 2^(m²+1) Q(m)²`, where `Q(m) = Product_{j=1..m}(1+4^j)` is odd.
The parity of `m²+1` determines the square classification. At five, the existing
lifting-the-exponent theorem evaluates the odd-exponent factors, and factorial
valuation identities identify their sum with A127428. The Lean result proves all
four universal assertions, including zero. No general-base classification,
other-prime formula or asymptotic assertion is included.
