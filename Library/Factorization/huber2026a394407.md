# Huber's prime conjecture for A394407

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A394407

## Source statements

The unpaginated Name line reads:

> Positive integers k such that there is no m < k with phi(m)/tau(m) = phi(k)/tau(k).

The unpaginated Comment line states:

> Conjecture: All primes p != 3 are in the sequence; equivalently, for every prime p != 3 there is no m < p with phi(m)/tau(m) = phi(p)/tau(p) = (p - 1)/2; equivalently, the sequence is a supersequence of A045344.

Here phi is Euler's totient and tau counts positive divisors. The variables compared
by the sequence are positive integers, and the quotient is rational. The entry's
examples give phi(14)/tau(14) = 6/4 = 3/2 and the collision
phi(1)/tau(1) = 1/1 = 2/2 = phi(3)/tau(3), which excludes 3.
