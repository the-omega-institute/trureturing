# Reciprocal supernorms on the size ensemble

## Verified locator

https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.15303v1

DOI: 10.1007/s11139-024-00893-8

*The Ramanujan Journal* 65 (2024), 487–520.

## Source statement

Conjecture 3.5 in arXiv v1, page 10, equation (3.16), states:

> There holds Ŵ_size(n) ∼ e^γ/n, as n → ∞.

Equation (3.1) sums reciprocal supernorms over partitions of size n.
The supernorm multiplies p_k for every occurrence of a part k, with p_1=2;
the empty product is one. The journal version's conjecture numbering is unverified.
