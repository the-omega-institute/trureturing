# OEIS A145110

Granvik's entry counts matrix positions occurring in at least one nonzero
permutation term for the Möbius variant of the Redheffer matrix. Its example
at `n=8` displays 13 contributing positions and identifies the rows associated
with divisors of 8. The displayed array uses the transpose of the convention
`B(i,j)=1` when `j=1` or `i` divides `j`, with `B(n,n)=0`.

The cross-reference A143104 specifies the modification at `(n,n)` and its
Möbius determinant for `n>1`. A008683 reproduces the attributed determinant
argument. These determinant statements concern signed sums of permutation
terms; A145110 counts the union of their positions.

The data section lists the first ten terms. Revision 7 retains the twenty
conjectured values in the comment quoted in the frontmatter. Its extension
note attributes their relocation from the data section to N. J. A. Sloane.
The `n=1` datum is outside the range of the conjectured list: the literal
modified-matrix convention gives no contributing position there, whereas
the entry lists 1.
