# Kline's A025527 totient quotient conjecture

OEIS A025527 independently defines a(m) as m!/lcm(1,2,...,m). Its formula section states:

> Conjecture: a(A006093(n)) = phi(A000142(A006093(n))) / phi(A003418(A006093(n))), where phi is the Euler totient function. - _Fred Daniel Kline_, Jun 03 2017

A006093 enumerates the primes minus one, beginning with 1. A000142 is the factorial and A003418 is the prefix least common multiple. Both ratios are interpreted as exact rational values; phi(1)=1 includes the first prime.
