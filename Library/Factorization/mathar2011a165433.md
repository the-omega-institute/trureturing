# OEIS A165433

Source: https://oeis.org/A165433, FORMULA field, revision 17.
The conjecture is attributed to R. J. Mathar, Nov 14 2011; the sequence is attributed to Paul Barry.

> a(n) = Sum_{k=0..floor(n/2)} C(n-k,k)*(2k)!/(k!*2^k).

> Conjecture: 2*a(n) -3*a(n-1) +(3-2*n)*a(n-2) +(2*n-3)*a(n-3)=0. - _R. J. Mathar_, Nov 14 2011
