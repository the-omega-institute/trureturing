## Source statement

> Following convolution formulas have been conjectured for this sequence by Sequence Machine, with both computing the first 2^20 terms correctly:
>
> a(n) = Sum_{d|n} A007875(d)*A219009(n/d).
>
> a(n) = Sum_{d|n} A140773(d)*A355837(n/d).

The offset is one. The entry gives the parity sequence by
`a(n) = A000035(A140773(n)).` The source definitions are as follows.

- A038548 NAME. “Number of divisors of n that are at most sqrt(n).”
- A140773 FORMULA. “a(n) = Sum_{m|n} A038548(m) = Sum_{m|n} ceiling(d(m)/2), where d(m) = number of divisors of m (A000005). - _Manfred Boergens_, Feb 25 2021”
- A007875 NAME. “Number of ways of writing n as p*q, with p <= q, gcd(p, q) = 1.”
- A219009 program line. “a(n)=sumdiv(n,d,if(issquare(d),issquare(sqrtint(d)),0)*moebius(n/d))”
- A355837 FORMULA. “Multiplicative with a(p^e) = 2 * (e mod 2) * (-1)^((e+1)/2) for prime p and e>0.”

The A219009 sum is indexed by the positive fourth roots of the fourth-power
divisors. A355837 uses the empty product at one. The parity value is cast
to the integers in both equalities.
