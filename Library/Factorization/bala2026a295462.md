## Source statements

OEIS A295462, NAME (unpaginated):

> a(n) = (15*n)!*(2*n)!/((10*n)!*(6*n)!*n!).

OEIS A295462, REFERENCES (%D, unpaginated):

> For n >= 0, both a(n)/(10*n+1) and a(n)/(6*n+1) are integers.

> Conjecture: a(n) is odd iff the 1 bits in the binary expansion of n are separated by at least three 0's, that is, iff n belongs to A048718.

OEIS A048718, COMMENTS (unpaginated):

> Max. 1 one-bit occur in each range of four bits.

The rational quotient uses exact division. Binary position i has weight 2^i;
all occupied positions i < j satisfy i+4 ≤ j precisely when three or more
zero bits separate successive one bits. Zero has empty support.
The natural integer realizing the quotient is required explicitly before
testing its oddness.

The linked note proves the two divisibilities independently and contains no
parity characterization. The parity proof uses nonnegative floor summands
of the binary prime valuation. Separated support makes each summand zero;
the first gap of length one, two or three makes one summand equal to one.
