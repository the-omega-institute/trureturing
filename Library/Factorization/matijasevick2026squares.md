# Cubes beyond the reciprocal-sum square families

The cited version's Section 7, second closing question, reads:

> For the recursions of this paper the exponent m=2 is forced. Is there a natural family of rational recursions for which c a_n+b_n is always a cube, other than those conjugate to z -> z^3?

Problem 1.1 uses coprime positive numerator and denominator, positive integer
initial values, a positive rational constant c, and indices n at least 2.
The question does not define “natural.” The construction documented in
`Problems/arxiv-2610-00765-cube-recursion-family.md` uses the explicit minimal
interpretation of a uniform autonomous rational family over Q with a
nonnegative integer parameter, fixed degree, one formula, and one constant
independent of parameter, initial value and index. An unstated stricter
interpretation remains unverified.

The source's square results concern its reciprocal-sum class and do not assert
an impossibility for arbitrary rational maps. The family F_t in the repository
is a repository-derived answer under the stated interpretation, not a theorem
attributed to this paper. The supplied ordinary comparison inspected the
paper's source and compared the relevant conjugacy definitions in
Jones--Manes, *Galois theory of quadratic rational functions*,
arXiv:1101.4339v3, and the scope of Cahn--Jones--Spear, *Powers in orbits of
rational functions: cases of an arithmetic dynamical Mordell-Lang conjecture*,
arXiv:1512.03085v2. Quadratic-map Galois questions and rational values that are
powers do not supply the whole reduced-coordinate cube assertion.
