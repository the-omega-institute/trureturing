## Source statements

NAME: "Smallest prime factor of 2^(2^n+2) + 3."

The five preceding domains, with every parameter natural including zero, are:

- C1(k): there is m with k = 10m+2.
- C2(k): there is m with k = 36m+16 and m mod 5 is not 1.
- C3(k): there is m with k = 84m+22 and m mod 5 is not 0.
- C4(k): there is m with k = 58m+26, and none of C1(k), C2(k), C3(k).
- C5(k): there is m with k = 138m+6 and m mod 5 is not 2.

"Conjecture 6: a(44n+10) = 2383 for n >= 0 and when it is not covered by
Conjectures 1-5."

Thus the quantified assertion concerns every natural n outside all five
domains at k = 44n+10, with a(k) = Nat.minFac(2^(2^k+2)+3).

## Divisibility supplier

Matthew House's MathOverflow answer https://mathoverflow.net/a/485834
answers the separate question about repeated least factor 255127.
Its linked table https://pastebin.com/NGBCq27c states
`a(274+292j) == 0 (mod 1759)` and `a(10+44j) == 0 (mod 2383)`;
the table's a denotes the integer being divided, rather than its least factor.
Credit for this divisibility ingredient belongs to House and the prior table.
The answer explains eventual periodicity by reducing the inner exponent.
The native source fetch returned HTTP 200 and both lines were read directly.
Issue #31483's advisory had encountered HTTP 403; its table reading was
therefore a reported supplier rather than a native fetch at preregistration.

## Mathematical scope

The admissible intersection is n = 79 and
k = 3486 = 44*79+10 = 274+292*11. The residues of k modulo
(10,36,84,58,138) are (6,30,42,6,36). Each differs from the corresponding
progression offset (2,16,22,26,6); hence no prior domain applies, even before
the modulo-five and recursive restrictions are imposed.

Exact arithmetic gives 2^292 = 1 and 2^274 = 718 modulo 879, so
2^3486 = 718 modulo 879. Also 2^879 = 1 and 2^720 = 1756 modulo 1759.
Writing 2^3486+2 = 720 + 879*(2^3486 div 879), the power laws give
2^(2^3486+2)+3 = 0 modulo 1759. The standard Mathlib supplier
Nat.minFac_le_of_dvd yields a(3486) <= 1759 < 2383.
The delivered conclusion is the literal negation of the full source claim.
It does not determine a(3486) exactly or require primality of 1759.

## Prior-art scope and limits

The preregistration is https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/31483.
Its bounded searches include the repository, public catalogue, GitHub,
witness-value OEIS search, MathDB, and the Tsoukalas and Adamczewski leads.
The source's settlement line explicitly names only Conjectures 1 and 4.
The two linked AlphaProof Nexus files prove those cases; the current
formal-conjectures A248802 file likewise presents those two solved results.
Fresh repository and GitHub issue/PR searches found only the preregistration
for this exact target. A fresh public GitHub code search and current OEIS
reading supplied no authenticated Conjecture 6 resolution; OEIS search for
3486 and 1759 returned null. These are bounded observations, not a global
novelty or priority claim. The exact admissible overlap and source negation
are distinguished from the already published divisibility ingredient.

The failure mechanism is an admissible overlap with a smaller divisor.
Excluding the five listed domains does not exclude that overlap. Whether a
revised domain obtained by removing further progressions has constant least
factor 2383 is unresolved here. The separate C1/C4 results are unaffected.
