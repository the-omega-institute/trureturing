# Binary factorial plateaus

Definitions 3.3–3.4, Lemmas 3.6 and 3.11, and Example 3.7 on printed page 7
of arXiv:1505.02318v2 describe the factorial-divisibility inverse and its
constant plateaus. The positive plateau lengths are the ruler sequence.
The source's S2(0)=0 convention differs from the least-positive-index
convention F(0)=1 in A007843. The latter has an initial singleton, followed
by the positive plateaus of lengths 1+v2(r), r>=1.

Theorem 3.16 on printed page 8 is a separate Conolly relation. It does not
state Ianakiev's later two-clause shortest-palindrome conjecture. The
factorial plateau identification is classical support for that question,
rather than a new conjecture settlement.
