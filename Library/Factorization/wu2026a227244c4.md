## Mathematical scope

For every natural n outside residue 26 modulo 55, 59 is prime, divides the
signed integer G(168*n+43), and is no greater than every natural prime divisor
of that same integer. Here G(m)=((2:Z)^(2^m)+1)*2^(2^(2^m))-1, with natural
exponents and integer multiplication and subtraction. There is no upper bound
on n, no assertion at residue 26, and no necessity assertion for the exclusion.
