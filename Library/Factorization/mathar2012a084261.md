# OEIS A084261

The official FORMULA field defines

> a(n) = Sum_{k=0..floor(n/2)} C(n, 2k)*k!.

It states

> Conjecture: 2*a(n) -4*a(n-1) +(-n+2)*a(n-2) +(n-2)*a(n-3)=0. - _R. J. Mathar_, Nov 30 2012

The source specifies no onset. The formal statement uses every natural n >= 3,
with natural predecessor indices and integer arithmetic after casting values.
Both endpoints of the literal finite sum are included. Generating functions,
asymptotics and negative-index extensions are outside this assertion.

The bounded source qualification in issue #27208 reports no exact recurrence
settlement in its arXiv/Crossref identifier searches, the inspected A084261
passage of Fang–Hamaker–Troyka (arXiv:2009.00079v3), or the pinned jOEIS
finite-sum implementation. These readings do not establish worldwide absence
or priority. The recurrence proof is a repository derivation using pinned
Mathlib identities and normalization, classified bind-only with intended
open-problem-resolution admission; this note asserts no publication or freeze.
