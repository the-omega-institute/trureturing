# Factorial powers and the distinct-prime Euler product

## Source statement

Page 13, Conjecture 27:

> For any α > 1, and C > 0, for sufficiently large n one has
> a_α(n+1) > a_α(n) + C.

The function a_α(n) is the least positive integer k such that the product
of p_r/(p_r−1), over n≤r≤n+k−1, exceeds α. The prime indexing starts at
p_1=2. Both α and C are fixed before the eventual starting index is chosen.

Page 17, Conjecture 36:

> We have lim_{n→∞} H((n!)^n + 1) = 1.

Page 3 defines the function:

> We will write H(n) = ∏_{i=1}^{k} p_i/(p_i − 1).

Here the primes in the product are all the distinct prime factors of n.
