# OEIS A097967

The NAME is `a(n) = Sum_{k=1..n} (P(n,k) + C(n,k))`. The FORMULA field gives
`a(n) = Sum_{k=1..n} n!(k!+1)/(k!(n-k)!)` and records:

> Conjecture: a(n) +(-n-6)*a(n-1) +(6*n+7)*a(n-2) +(-13*n+14)*a(n-3) +4*(3*n-8)*a(n-4) +4*(-n+4)*a(n-5)=0.

The attribution is R. J. Mathar, May 29 2013. The archived history's latest
revision is 8, July 26 2015. The source specifies no onset; the registered
nonnegative-index interpretation is every natural n>=5, without an upper
bound. The sum is empty at zero, factorials and subtraction use natural
numbers, and factorial values are cast to the rationals before division.
All signed coefficients and recurrence arithmetic are rational.

The source also prints `A007526(n)-A000225(n)-1`. This subtractive identity
is inconsistent with the literal definition: at n=1 the literal value is 2,
whereas the printed right side is 1-1-1=-1. The formal proof uses the literal
sum and its directly proved decomposition b(n)+2^n-1. A007526's factorial
sum and first-order recurrence are known ingredients, not a novelty claim.
The A007526 bibliography, including Cabrera's 2025 article, has not been
fully checked for semantic consequences for this target.
