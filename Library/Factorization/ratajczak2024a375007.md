# A375007

## Verified locator

Canonical entry URL: https://oeis.org/A375007

The source is revision 9 (2024-08-18). OEIS entries have no DOI;
the canonical URL identifies this entry.

The sequence consists of positive integers t for which
t mod k = floor((t-k)/k) mod k, with 1<=k<=t, holds only at k=1 and k=t.
Revision 9 (2024-08-18) conjectures that
a(n)+1 is prime for n>6 and reports verification for terms below 10^8.
The first six terms are 1,2,3,4,8,24. No proof of this conjecture appears
in the complete entry. Its separate Fortunate-type conjecture is not addressed.

## Proof scope

Mathlib's Nat.minFac_prime, Nat.minFac_dvd and Nat.minFac_le_div supply the
standard ordered factorization of a composite number. The Lean proof uses
two explicit factor constructions and is a repository derivation, not a proof
attributed to this OEIS entry. No publication priority or exhaustive literature
coverage is claimed.
