## Source statement

The OEIS NAME is, verbatim:

> Permanent of the 2n X 2n matrix with the (i,j)-entry i-j (i,j=1..2n).

Its complete Conjecture 1 comment is, verbatim:

> Conjecture 1: (-1)^n*a(n) > 0 for all n > 0. Also, a(n) == 0 (mod 2n+1) if 2n+1 is composite.

Only the second sentence is asserted here. The offset is `1,2`, so `n` starts
at 1. The sign sentence and Conjecture 2 are outside the assertion.

## Mathematical interpretation

The integer `a n` is `Matrix.permanent` of the matrix on `Fin (2*n)` whose
entry is `((i.val : ℤ) + 1) - ((j.val : ℤ) + 1)`. Thus the represented source
indices are exactly `1,...,2n`. Mathlib sums products of `M (σ i) i`; the
source sums products of `M i (σ i)`. Inversion of permutations identifies the
two finite sums, as in `Matrix.permanent_transpose`.

For `n ≥ 1`, the natural modulus `2*n+1` is greater than 1. Consequently,
nonprimality expresses exactly the composite condition, and its cast to the
integers divides `a n` exactly when the source congruence is zero.

The argument expands the permanent of an additive integer matrix and groups
permutations by subset images. The multiplicities are `r!*(q-1-r)!` at order
`q-1`. For odd composite `q`, these coefficients are divisible by `q`.
The central splits for `q=9` give the products `720,576,720`.
