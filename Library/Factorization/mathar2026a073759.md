# Mathar's A073759 conjecture

## Verified locator

in the unpaginated FORMULA fields of https://oeis.org/A046666 and

## Source statements

A073759, NAME: "Largest number < n that is neither a divisor of nor relatively prime to n, or 0 if no such number exists." Offset 1.

A073759, FORMULA: "Conjecture: a(n) = 2*A046667(n) if n>=5. - _R. J. Mathar_, May 15 2026".

A046666, FORMULA: "a(n) = n - (smallest prime dividing n)." Offset 2.

A046667, FORMULA: "a(n) = A046666(n)/2." Offset 2.

The candidates are positive integers below n. The Lean definition uses the
supremum of their filtered finite interval, with zero for the empty set.
`Nat.minFac` gives the least prime factor for n at least two, and `Nat.div`
is division in the natural numbers. The theorem proves the conjecture for
every n at least five. Its proof identifies the maximum as n minus its least
prime factor and then uses evenness of this difference.
