## Source statements and interpretation

NAME: "Smallest prime factor of (2^(2^n) + 1)*2^(2^(2^n)) - 1."

"Conjecture 3: a(72*n+32) = 149 for n >= 0 and a(72*n+32) is not covered by Conjectures 1 and 2."

The formal result uses the disclosed progression-index CONDITIONAL interpretation:
two separately quantified natural noncoverage hypotheses imply the least-prime
conclusion. This is not an author-written quantified formula or an established
claim about author intent. C1 uses 20*k+16; C2 independently uses 220*k+8.
Both k binders and n include zero and have no upper bound.
