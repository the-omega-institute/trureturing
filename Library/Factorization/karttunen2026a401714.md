# Karttunen's radical-quotient divisor-sum conjecture

The official OEIS records attribute both entries to Antti Karttunen,
November 6, 2026. The pinned A401714 record states:

> %N A401714 a(n) = 1 if A003557(n) < A053669(n), otherwise 0, where A003557(n) is n divided by its squarefree kernel, and A053669(n) is the smallest prime not dividing n.

> %F A401714 a(n) <= A401715(n), conjectured.

The corresponding A401715 record states:

> %N A401715 a(n) = 1 if {sum of unitary divisors of n} plus {sum of squarefree divisors of n} >= 2*n, otherwise 0.

> %F A401715 a(n) >= A401714(n), conjectured.

Both entries have offset one. These reciprocal formula lines express one
assertion, rather than two distinct conjectures. The A401714 PARI definition
computes A003557 by dividing by the product of distinct prime factors.
The A401715 PARI definition uses `sumdiv` with the predicates
`issquarefree(d)` and `1==gcd(d,n/d)`; each sum includes the divisor one.
The formula lines involving A325973 use a different bound variable, but
the NAME and PARI definitions above specify the same input integer.

For every positive integer N, put rad(N)=product of its distinct prime
divisors and let q(N) be its least nondividing prime. The exact assertion
is N/rad(N)<q(N) implies 2N<=U(N)+S(N), where U and S are the two actual
positive-divisor sums. Neither a size cutoff nor a parity or support-size
restriction occurs. The source also records that the converse first fails
at N=240; that converse is not the conjecture.
