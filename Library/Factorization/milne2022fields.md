# Classical multiquadratic square classes

## Locator

Theorem 5.30, Example 5.31(b), and Remark 5.32, printed pages 75–76,
describe the Kummer correspondence, its degree and pairing, and rational
square classes represented by signed products of distinct primes. The
specialization used here is exponent two over the rationals, which contain
the primitive second root of unity minus one.

The formal module uses positive rational primes as radicands and the actual
intermediate field of the complex numbers generated by their positive real
square roots. Its scalar is an arbitrary rational number, including zero
and negative values. It proves the rational square kernel and exact degree
by simultaneous induction and an explicit quadratic-model range equivalence.
It does not claim the full Kummer correspondence or a novelty result.
