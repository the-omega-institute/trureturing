# OEIS A152020

The entry names the sequence as the positive reduced denominator of
`8/(9*n^2)`, divided by 9, with offset 1. Andrew Howroyd's formula of July 25,
2018 identifies the terms as `n^2/gcd(n^2, 8)`.

Andres Cicuttin's formula of September 19, 2017 identifies the same terms with
the positive reduced denominator of `(n-2)^3/n^2`. The formal definition uses
`Rat.den` and natural-number division by 9. The theorem proves that this
division is exact and gives Cicuttin's denominator formula for every positive
index.
