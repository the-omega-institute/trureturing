# The UNTILDE trace functional

Equation (2.3) defines the original spectral geometric mean using
L=A^(-1) #_(1/2) B and L^alpha A L^alpha. Its powered quasi-extension
uses A^p and B^p followed by the outer spectral 1/p power. Thus its
trace is S(alpha,p,A,B)=Re Tr[(L^alpha A^p L^alpha)^(1/p)], with
L=A^(-p) #_(1/2) B^p. All matrix powers are spectral powers.
Equation (2.4) is this original family's powered quasi-extension.
Equations (2.5)--(2.6) define the separate weighted spectral family,
denoted with a tilde in the source. The two definitions are not interchanged.

Proposition 5.19(1) gives necessary restrictions for concavity.
Problem 5.20 reports that sufficient conditions are unknown apart
from the midpoint alpha=1/2. These are source statements, rather
than sources for the off-midpoint proof.

The repository's off-midpoint obstruction uses real symmetric
positive definite density matrices A_t=diag(1-t,t) and
B_s=(1/2)[[1,1-2s],[1-2s,1]]. Its mathematical content is the
unequal-scale endpoint t=s^2, the positive-power interpolation
limit, and an affine Jensen violation for every p>0 and every
0<alpha<1 other than 1/2. The derivation is repository content;
it is not attributed as a theorem of Hiai's paper.

Vuong, Shudo and Osaka, arXiv:2609.00034v1, Theorem 4.1 covers
the p=1 slice through F_f(A,B)=Tr[A f(A^(-1)#B)^2]. Its inspected
statement has neither powered inputs nor the outer 1/p exponent.
The all-p obstruction requires its own endpoint proof. This
bounded source comparison does not certify global priority.
No positive midpoint classification is asserted here.
