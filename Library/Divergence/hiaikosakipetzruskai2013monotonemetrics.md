# BKM inversion and the metric transpose

Equations (1.1)–(1.2) on p. 3 identify the inverse pair on the full
complex matrix space at positive definite $D$. The resolvent map
integrates $(D+tI)^{-1}X(D+tI)^{-1}$ for $t\geq 0$. The inverse
integrates $D^{t}XD^{1-t}$ over $[0,1]$. Appendix C.2, p. 48, proves
the inversion using functional calculus for left and right
multiplication.

Appendix B, p. 47, uses the metric transpose
$(\Omega_{\rho}^k)^{-1}\circ\widehat{\Phi}\circ\Omega_{\Phi(\rho)}^k$,
where $\widehat{\Phi}$ is the Hilbert–Schmidt adjoint of the channel and
$\Omega^k$ denotes the metric operator. This gives the order of
composition used for the inclusion and trace expectation.
