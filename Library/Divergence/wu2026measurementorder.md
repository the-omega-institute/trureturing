# Wu, measurement-order dependence

Shengjun Wu, *Measurement-order dependence bounds uncertainty, contextuality,
steering, and Bell nonlocality*, arXiv:2611.24604v1 (2026).

## Verified locators

- Section II.A, printed p. 3, defines the two measurement-labelled joint
  records with the same input state. Lüders effects are
  $\sqrt{E_a}F_b\sqrt{E_a}$ and $\sqrt{F_b}E_a\sqrt{F_b}$. For projections
  these reduce to $P_aQ_bP_a$ and $Q_bP_aQ_b$.
  [Section II.A](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24604v1#S2.SS1)
- Definition 1, printed p. 3, defines $J(A,B\mid\rho)$ as the
  Jensen–Shannon divergence of those records using Shannon entropy in bits.
  [Definition 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24604v1#Thmdefinition1)
- Proposition 1(ii), printed p. 4, states common-unitary and outcome-relabeling
  invariance, and pure-state attainment.
  [Proposition 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24604v1#Thmproposition1)
- Section II.C, printed p. 4, defines $\Psi(t)$ by maximizing the score for one
  sharp qubit pair with $t=\sin(\theta)/2$. The paragraph after Theorem 1
  reports strict increase on a 250-point grid and asks for a proof.
  [Section II.C](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24604v1#S2.SS3.p3.1)
- Appendix A gives the orthogonal-pair value $h_2(3/4)-1/2$ in bits.
  Section VIII lists monotonicity among the remaining open problems.
  [Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24604v1#A1.p2.1)
  [Section VIII](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24604v1#S8.p2)
