# Fidelity for unassisted single-shot compression

## Verified locator

Section IV, Theorem 5, Eq. (30), evaluates the local-operation root fidelity
with a retained purification. It gives 2/3 for the maximally mixed qutrit
marginal compressed through a qubit, with Bell overlap 4/9. The trace-distance
comparison in the qutrit increment concerns the joint reference-output
state itself. [Primary preprint, §IV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14785v1)
