## Scope

The matrix logarithm's integral representation and differential belong
to classical matrix analysis. The precise resolvent differential and
BKM formula used here are checked in the full Petz source recorded in
[the BKM note](petz2001bkm.md). This book reference supplies background,
not a Lean theorem or a separately verified interior formula. The
uniform neighborhood control and the connection to the actual CFC.log
and fderiv expressions are proved in the repository.
