# Two imaginarity monotones induced by unified (α,β)-relative entropy

C. Wu and Z. Wu, arXiv:2506.09799v1 [quant-ph]; Commun. Theor. Phys. 77 (2025). Quotations are from the v1 TeX source.

Real states:

> $ \rho $ is a free state or real state, if $\rho=\sum_{jk} \rho_{jk} \ |j \rangle \langle k|$, where ${\rho_{jk}} \in \mathbb{R} $ for arbitrary $j$,$k \in \{ 0,1,\dots,d-1\}$. We denote the set of all real states by $ \mathcal F $.

The unified relative entropy (Eq. (7)):

> $H_{\alpha}^{\beta}(\rho||\sigma)=\frac{1}{(\alpha-1)\beta}[( \mathrm{tr}( \rho^{\alpha} \sigma^{1-\alpha}))^{\beta}-1]$,

The two quantifiers (Eqs. (13) and (20)):

> $M_{\alpha,\beta}^{H}(\rho)=\frac{1}{(\alpha-1)\beta}[( \mathrm{tr} (\rho^{\alpha} (\rho^*)^{1-\alpha}))^{\beta}-1]$, where $\alpha \in (0,1),\beta \in (0,1].$

> $M_{\alpha,\beta}^{E}(\rho)=\min_{\sigma \in \mathcal F} \frac{1}{(\alpha-1)\beta}[(\mathrm{tr}(\rho^{\alpha} \sigma^{1-\alpha}))^{\beta}-1]$, where $\alpha \in (0,1)$ and $\beta \in(0,1]$.

The conjecture (Example 1):

> We have not found a proof of $M_{\alpha,\beta}^H(\rho)\geq M_{\alpha,\beta}^E(\rho)$ for any $\alpha\in (0,1)$, $\beta\in (0,1]$ and any qubit state $\rho$, however, numerical experiments shows that it might be true, and it seems difficult to find a counterexample. It is also conjectured that $M_{\alpha,\beta}^H(\rho)\geq M_{\alpha,\beta}^E(\rho)$ for any $\alpha\in (0,1)$, $\beta\in (0,1]$ and any quantum state $\rho$.
