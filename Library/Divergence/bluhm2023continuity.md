# Continuity of Quantum Entropic Quantities via Almost Convexity

## Verified locator

Proposition 6.7, §6.2.1, pp. 27–28 of arXiv v3, equations (30)–(32).
[arXiv:2208.00922v3, p. 27](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.00922v3#page=27) ·
[DOI 10.1109/TIT.2023.3277892](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2023.3277892)

The example uses equally weighted classical flags with coalescing rank-one
signals and obtains a $\log 2$ jump in the Belavkin–Staszewski conditional
entropy. Section 6.2.2, p. 30, observes the corresponding mutual information
discontinuity. The acknowledgments on p. 48 credit Peter Brown for the example.
