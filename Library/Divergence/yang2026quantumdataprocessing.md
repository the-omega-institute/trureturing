# Quantum data processing equality

Lemma IV.1 and Appendix B prove the sharp scalar endpoint bound by evaluating an inverse exponential Gram matrix. Lemma IV.2, equation (IV.2), gives the two-sided scalar upper Poisson-weight bound. In Theorem IV.5, equation (IV.20), Yang applies that estimate within orthogonal spectral blocks of physical Kraus amplitudes. Theorem II.1 identifies the strip integrals with the relative-entropy loss for the paper's setting.

Lemma III.1, equation (III.2), gives the lower and upper Poisson weights and the boundary derivative formula for bounded harmonic functions with quadratic vanishing at the lower boundary point of the strip of width one half. The repository derives the integrability and identity for finite exponential overlaps by evaluating the modes, using reflection at the lower edge and a direct integrable bound at the upper edge. The compensated cosine transform uses the hyperbolic cotangent expansion in [DLMF 4.36.3](https://dlmf.nist.gov/4.36.E3).
