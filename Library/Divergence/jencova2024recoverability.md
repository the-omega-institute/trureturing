# Recoverability of quantum channels via hypothesis testing

## Verified locator

[Journal record](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11005-024-01775-2) · [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11707) · [Corollary 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.11707)

## Formalization scope

Corollary 1 gives the normalized forward-profile finite-interval formula under two-sided scalar Loewner bounds.

The scalar bounds determine the endpoints where the hockey-stick profile is affine or zero. The fixed-shift inertia calculation supplies an earlier step in the matrix integral route to this profile.

For two pairs with the same respective trace masses and common Loewner bounds, the affine terms cancel. The resulting signed difference integrates over the same bounded interval even when the pairs act on different finite-dimensional spaces.
