# Mean-trace concavity

Lemma 3.3 assumes strictly positive linear maps, an operator mean, exponents
in [0,1] with at least one nonzero, and an operator-monotone scalar function.
At equal exponents p>0, the identity scalar function and the same invertible
congruence map X -> Z adjoint X Z, it gives joint concavity of
Re Tr((Z adjoint (B^p #theta A^p) Z)^(1/p)).
The genuine mean has its base in the first argument:
X #theta Y = X^(1/2)(X^(-1/2) Y X^(-1/2))^theta X^(1/2).
The local operatorPowerMean has its base in its second argument instead.

The connected Hilbert-Schmidt derivation uses the classical arbitrary-vector
Lieb lift, genuine-mean joint concavity and both-input order monotonicity,
the common-right-factor identity, and forward attained trace Young.
The triple trace pairing has arbitrary Z. Its strictly positive optimizing
matrix and the final trace-power result require invertible Z. Noncommuting
inputs are retained: only left/right actions or powers of one input commute.

The source result is classical; it is not the open Problem 5.18 in
arXiv:2510.04691v3. That original problem also requires inverse-geometric
identification, the original parameter assembly, and a common-epsilon-I
positive semidefinite limit preserving the support constraint s(A)>=s(B).
The paper is cited; no paper proof text is transcribed.
