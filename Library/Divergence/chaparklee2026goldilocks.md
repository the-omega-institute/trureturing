# Single-shot blind compression for state ensembles

## Verified locator

Appendix D, Proposition D.2, gives a Kraus trace bound and a divisible
construction for Haar-average fidelity. Proposition 3.7 and Appendix F give
a nonisometric-decoder advantage for phase-ensemble average fidelity.
Appendix L gives bounds on average trace distance over ensemble members.
The qutrit increment compares joint states with a retained Bell reference.
[Primary preprint, Appendices D, F and L](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01258v2)
