# Diameter, focal radius and the actual immersion

The primary source is [arXiv:2504.13270v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.13270v1),
Theorem A and §§2.2–2.3. Its manifold is smooth, connected, compact and
without boundary; the ambient space is a complete simply connected space
form of curvature zero, one or minus one.

The focal radius is the supremum of the radii on which the normal
exponential has nonsingular differential at every shorter normal vector.
It does not require global injectivity of that map or a unique nearest
point. In Euclidean space §2.2 identifies it with the reciprocal of
the maximum normal curvature, namely
$\max_{p,\,|v|=1}|\mathrm{II}_p(v,v)|$.

Theorem A gives diameter at least twice focal radius. Equality is a
statement about the immersion: a totally umbilical embedded round sphere;
a real, complex, quaternionic or octonionic projective space with a
Veronese embedding; or a round sphere covering a real projective space
followed by a Veronese embedding. The last immersion is not injective.
Section 2.3 defines the projective embeddings by rank-one Hermitian
idempotents, with octonionic projective dimension one or two, and allows
scaling and totally umbilical inclusions into the ambient space.
The source invokes Sakamoto's planar-geodesic classification; this note
does not reproduce that classification or its proof.

The exact consumer is [the L3 continuation, Theorem 91.4](../../docs/develop/theory/QUANTUM_ORIGIN_BILATERAL_POSITIVE_RESET_RIGIDITY_AND_SCALE_OBSTRUCTION_VI.md).
There the manifold is the entire actual embedded pure orbit in one
invariant Euclidean metric. The additional all-tangent upper bound forces
equality in Theorem A. Convex-hull transport and the separately stipulated
whole-pair-face condition then select the complex positive cone.
The source itself does not derive that condition, select an invariant
metric, supply native operations, or establish a physical reconstruction.
