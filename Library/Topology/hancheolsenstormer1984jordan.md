# The associative JB complexification

Section 3.2.2, printed pages 78–79, PDF pages 88–89, uses the norm
N(r+is)=sqrt(norm(r²+s²)) on the complexification. The two-square
identity and the JB square laws give the norm estimates. Equivalence
with the coordinate product norm gives completeness; conjugation and
the square norm law give the C* identity. The original real algebra
embeds isometrically as the selfadjoint part.

Gelfand representation, transferring real spectral information, and
proving positivity of arbitrary quadratic representations require
