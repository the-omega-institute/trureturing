# The associative JB complexification

Section 3.2.2, printed pages 78–79, PDF pages 88–89, uses the norm
N(r+is)=sqrt(norm(r²+s²)) on the complexification. The two-square
identity and the JB square laws give the norm estimates. Equivalence
with the coordinate product norm gives completeness; conjugation and
the square norm law give the C* identity. The original real algebra
embeds isometrically as the selfadjoint part.

The citation paraphrases the construction and copies no source text.
The formal construction also permits the zero algebra. Sections
3.2.8-3.2.11, printed pages 80-81, establish the relation between
the real spectrum, inverses in the generated algebra and invertibility
of the ambient quadratic representation. Proposition 3.3.6, printed
page 83, proves that every quadratic representation preserves the
square cone, using invertible paths, closedness and positive
approximation. These statements concern arbitrary JB algebras,
without a special or finite-dimensional representation assumption.
