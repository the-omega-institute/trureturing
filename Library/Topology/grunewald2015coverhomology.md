# Cover homology, relative Frattini kernels and a fixed simple target

## Primary sources actually inspected

Grunewald, Larsen, Lubotzky and Malestein, *Arithmetic quotients of the mapping
class group*, arXiv:1307.2593v2, revised April 9, 2015.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.2593v2

The arXiv abstract and metadata were read. It associates arithmetic groups to
rational irreducible representations of finite groups. If the finite group
can be generated by fewer than g elements, the associated arithmetic group
is a virtual quotient of Mod(Sigma_g): a finite-index subgroup on each side
is involved. The full 46-page proof was not inspected in this increment.
No numbered theorem or unqualified global Mod(Sigma_g) quotient is attributed
here. A large virtual arithmetic image does not identify the kernel of a
separately specified finite quantum representation.

I. B. S. Passi, Mahender Singh and Manoj K. Yadav, *Automorphisms of abelian
group extensions*, arXiv:0911.4250v2; Journal of Algebra 324 (2010), 820-830,
DOI 10.1016/j.jalgebra.2010.03.029.
https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.4250
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2010.03.029

Abstract and bibliographic metadata were inspected, not the complete proof.
This is background for the classical extension-automorphism/cohomology
mechanism. The present theory supplies its particular cocycle computation
explicitly and makes no first-discovery claim for that mechanism.

Adam Klukowski, arXiv:2411.06867v2.
https://arxiv.org/html/2411.06867v2

Definition 3, Theorem 4, Corollary 11, Conjecture 13 and Lemma 14 were read
in the primary HTML. Theorem 4 already gives an abelianization reduction for
CSP of finitely generated nilpotent groups. Our relative finite-cover
factorization statements have different hypotheses and conclusions; neither
statement is a resolution of Conjecture 13. Corollary 11 controls intersections
with large multicurve stabilizers, not the entire principal-kernel inclusion
needed by Conjecture 13.

## Precise relation to the appended ordinary proofs

Sections 27-32 of the existing SURFACE_CONGRUENCE_OBSERVERS theory treat a
fixed characteristic finite-index subgroup N of the actual surface group.
For a centerless base S=pi/N with trivial induced outer action, they construct
a corrected homology representation Psi on H1(N,F_l), by removing the unique
inner base action. If C is the principal outer kernel for pi/[N,N]N^l and
K=ker(Psi), the proof gives K/C embedded in H1(S,H1(N,F_l)). Equality C=K
is proved for primes not dividing |S|; it is not asserted at all primes.
A fixed nonabelian finite simple target nevertheless factors through the
outer action exactly when it factors through Psi, at every prime.

The relative Frattini argument is written out: for finite Q and a normal
l-group P, the kernel among P-preserving outer automorphisms of the map to
Out(Q/Phi(P)) is an l-group. The proof uses the free action on lifts of a
Frattini basis and a central cocycle description of the restriction kernel.
No unproved Burnside theorem is introduced as a Lean axiom. The pinned
Mathlib GroupTheory/Frattini.lean was inspected; its definitions,
characteristicity and nongenerating result do not themselves constitute a
kernel-checked formalization of the full relative statement.

Consequently all finite l-group refinements over the same N have the same
factorization ability toward a specified nonabelian finite simple quotient
as the first mod-l homology layer. Under the centerless, outer-trivial base
hypotheses, a finite joint family at several primes succeeds only if one
single first homology layer already succeeds. This is about factorization
of the same source homomorphism, not equality of all outer kernels and not
an assertion that arbitrary finite quotients can be so refined.

The proposed finite test is the actual joint subgroup generated by paired
images of the same mapping-class generators in the fixed target and in Psi.
No successful cover/prime or paired quantum generator packet was constructed.
The remaining existence obligation is stated explicitly in the theory.
