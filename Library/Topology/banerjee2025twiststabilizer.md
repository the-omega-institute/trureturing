## Exact source scope

Definition 1 lets G be an arbitrary group and phi a surjective homomorphism
from a closed surface group. Mapping classes act by precomposition on
conjugacy classes of epimorphisms, with conjugation by the target group.
Proposition 3 asserts that a nonseparating Dehn twist preserves the conjugacy
class of phi if and only if its curve lies in ker(phi). It does not impose
abelianness, a centralizer condition, or a geometric realization of G.

Use a standard genus-two geometric basis a,b,c,d with [a,b][c,d]=1. A twist
about the nonseparating a-curve has a based representative fixing a,c,d and
sending b to ba, or the inverse convention. The presented-group action
preserves the relator since [a,ba]=[a,b]. Both sign choices give the same
counterexample. Replacing the lift by an inner automorphism of the source
does not change the target-conjugacy-class stabilizer condition.

The Lean claim specializes the universal statement to this surface, this
curve and this invertible presented-group map, retaining the universal target
and surjectivity condition. It includes one common conjugator for every
source element. Lean does not construct a topological surface, embedded curve
or Dehn-Nielsen-Baer correspondence; that source-to-presentation matching is
ordinary mathematics documented here and in the paper's geometric-basis proof.

## Exact counterexample and repair

Let D8 have generators r,s with r^4=s^2=1 and srs=r^-1. Define the actual
homomorphism by (a,b,c,d) -> (r^2,s,r,1). The surface relator maps to one;
r,s are both in the image, so the map is onto. Precomposition with b->ba
is exactly conjugation by r on the full representation, while phi(a)=r^2!=1.
Thus the reverse implication in Proposition 3 fails for its stated arbitrary
G scope. The forward implication remains true.

The converse proof goes from invariance up to target conjugation to the
pointwise equality phi(Tb)=phi(b), without excluding a nontrivial compensating
conjugation. The preprint makes a related claim about lifts that also needs
care: arbitrary point-pushing modifications cannot all be treated as the
same geometric based Dehn twist.

The corrected criterion is an explicit centralizer condition. With A=phi(a),
B=phi(b), and S generated by the images of all generators fixed by T,
phi composed with T^n is conjugate to phi exactly when some z in C_G(S)
satisfies zBz^-1=BA^n. Exact marked invariance is instead A^n=1.
If C_G(S) is contained in the center of G, the original equivalence is restored.

A torsion-free analogue uses the integral Heisenberg group, with [X,Y]=Z,
and images (Z,Y,X,1); precomposition is conjugation by X and Z!=1.
These target groups and epimorphisms are not asserted to arise from the
geometric inclusion of an ample curve into a smooth projective surface.

## Limits on the conclusion

This is a counterexample to one published group-theoretic proposition, not a
solution of an external open conjecture or a refutation of the article's
main geometric monodromy theorems. Proposition 4 invokes Proposition 3, so
that displayed proof step needs an independent justification. Vanishing-cycle
geometry may supply such a justification; no failure of those geometric
conclusions is inferred here. No message has been sent to the author or editor.

A single representation stabilized up to conjugacy must also be distinguished
from a principal congruence kernel acting on all representations in a finite
characteristic quotient. The D8 witness does not hide T from the latter:
the representation (r,1,s,1) detects it. The exact all-representation outer
period is four. The existing theory now proves this general exponent criterion.
