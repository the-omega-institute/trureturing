# Continuous reconstruction and smooth cohomological stability

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/src/2607.11171v1 .

Definition 2.1 uses real additive cocycles and continuous coboundaries for a
group action. In the smooth-category section, Conjecture `conj:SDC` asks
whether a Z^k action generated by commuting smooth orientation-preserving
circle diffeomorphisms, with at least one irrational rotation number, is
cohomologically C-infinity stable exactly when its rotation vector rho is
simultaneously Diophantine. This arithmetic condition means that some
gamma,tau > 0 satisfy

    max_i dist(q rho_i, Z) >= gamma / |q|^tau

for every nonzero integer q. Smooth cohomological stability means closedness
of smooth coboundaries within smooth cocycles in the Frechet topology.

The source proves the sufficient direction using simultaneous smooth
linearization and its linear-model proposition. Its status paragraph
identifies the remaining necessary direction for non-SDC rotation vectors
with non-linearizable actions, including singular simultaneous conjugacies.
The single-map Avila-Kocsard result and the source's continuous finite-image
classification do not settle that higher-rank smooth case. This is the
boundary stated in this source version; it is not an exhaustive later
literature or priority certification.

The bounded-real-cocycle theorem supplies a continuous reconstruction
criterion for an actual compatible cocycle over a minimal action. To use it
for a particular smooth action still requires its one-orbit all-elements
bound and minimality. It establishes neither smooth regularity of the
continuous transfer nor closedness of the smooth coboundary space. Those
bridges and the conjectured necessity remain unresolved here.
