## Correspondence

The forest classification is classical. The Lean construction uses nonempty
independent faces and their actual barycentric-coordinate realizations. Empty
vertex types form a separate case; an empty residual in leaf reduction gives
the two-point sphere through the augmented simplicial join. For a nonempty
residual, the ordinary join transports the contractible or spherical outcome.
The statement includes disconnected forests and the zero-sphere. It does not
specify a modulo-three formula for the dimension of a path.
