## Source statements

In the arXiv v2 text, eqs. (15)–(16) on pp. 10–11 give the Type I central ray and its row and column permutation representatives. The Type I− ray has negative parameter. Section IV, pp. 19–20, excludes unistochastic matrices along that ray using the phase equations treated in Appendix C, pp. 26–28.

Section IV, p. 21, asks whether the entire Type I− hyperoctants are free of unistochastic matrices. The repository proof uses the nine coordinates in the basis of outer products of the three nonconstant normalized Hadamard characters. It excludes a simultaneous strict negative sign in all nine coordinates. The star-shapedness of the full order-four unistochastic set is a separate question in the same paragraph.

## The Type I ray with three repeated columns

Communications in Mathematical Physics 259, 307–324 (2005).

Section IV, Eqs. (35)–(40), studies the type-I central ray using three
columns of equal squared moduli. After the column permutation in
Eq. (35), the first three columns have squared moduli
p(t) = (1−3t, 1+t, 1+t, 1+t)/4, and row normalization fixes the last
squared-modulus column to 1−3p(t). Thus this is the ray of
Korzekwa et al., arXiv:1812.09083v3, Eqs. (C36)–(C37). The type-I
ray direction is given in Eq. (16).

Appendix C, Eqs. (56)–(59) and the following lemma, gives no solution
when L = (1−3t)/(1+t) > 1 and solutions for 0 < L < 1 and the
endpoints L = 0, 1. Consequently the negative-parameter part
−1/9 ≤ t < 0 has no unitary lift. The nonnegative side 0 ≤ t ≤ 1/3
has a lift, including the flat matrix and the endpoint with one zero
weight. The ray parameter here labels the weight law and is independent
of the root variable in the general guarded cubic.
