# Monoid structure of bracelet matrices in the Birkhoff polytope

Conjecture 9 in Section III.A concerns the full set $L_d$ of real $d\times d$
bracelet matrices. Definition 7 requires bistochasticity and both the row and
column bracelet inequalities. The conjecture asks for identity membership and
closure under ordinary matrix multiplication for every dimension. The journal
citation and the questions about circulant matrices are in the [paper's reading
note](../QuantumBounds/rajchelkorzekwapuchalazyczkowski2021birkhoff.md).
