# Bozóki, Csató and Temesi, complete ranking profiles

Section 5, printed p. 6, Problem 5 asks for a realization criterion on the entire five-dimensional probability simplex. Its Equation (7) fixes the coordinate order $XYZ,XZY,YXZ,YZX,ZXY,ZYX$, read in strictly decreasing order. Section 7 raises the total support question for general realizable profiles. [arXiv:2611.23308v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23308v1#page=6)

The paper's authors supply the problem, its probability convention and the coordinate order. The fifteen-label criterion and proof are the Codex CLI contribution recorded in the repository's problem issue, including the convex compression and measure-theoretic arguments. The same issue records the independent mathematical reviews and the separate fourteen-label strengthening. [Issue #34280](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/34280)
