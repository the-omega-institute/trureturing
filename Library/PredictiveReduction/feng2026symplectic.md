# Symplectic embedding approximation and reduction

## Checked locator

arXiv:2606.04623, submitted 3 June 2026. The title retains the wording of the source. Parsed text was inspected at Theorem 2.2, Theorem 3.1 and Sections 3.1–3.2. The stated approximation uses a canonical linear embedding composed with symplectic shears, with neural parametrizations of scalar potentials. The displayed approximation guarantee is in the C0 norm on the specified domain.

## Relationship to the unified theory

This is current prior art for constructing symplectic reduced representations. Preservation of a geometric embedding does not, without additional assumptions, assert exact descent of a specified full vector field or losslessness of a specified statistical experiment. Section 13 separately defines those requirements.
