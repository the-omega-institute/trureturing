# Arithmetic placement of graph sensors

The journal bibliographic record and the accessible 26-page arXiv PDF were checked. The parsed text of Theorem 4.10, Corollaries 4.11 and 4.12, and the cycle-spectrum appendix was read. The corollary page screenshot failed, so no unseen diagram is used as evidence. The source concerns Laplacian consensus networks and their controllability/observability, rather than a Hamiltonian spring system.

The arithmetic-clock addition to the unified predictive theory reuses this established result and gives an independent modal calculation for an odd spring or LC ring after removing the free common mode. The two-site restriction on each sine/cosine eigenspace is full rank exactly when the site separation is coprime to the cycle length. Prime length makes every pair admissible; adjacent sites also work for composite lengths.

The gcd criterion is prior art. The derivation of a five-cycle frequency ratio equal to the golden ratio is elementary cycle spectral algebra, not a newly solved conjecture. Exact rank must be separated from conditioning: the worst normalized spatial mode gain still decays with cycle size. The note does not claim a complete noisy-time-window bound, a new graph theorem, or that the cited Lean sources already formalize this second-order application.
