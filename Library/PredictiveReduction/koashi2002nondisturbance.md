# State-family quantum sufficiency

## Source identity

Physical Review A 66, 022318 (2002). The corresponding preprint, quant-ph/0101144v2, has the different title *What is Possible Without Disturbing Partially Known Quantum States?* The parsed preprint states the partially known-state problem and develops the preserving-operation decomposition in Section V.

## Scope used here

The relevant object is a specified state family, not all density matrices on an arbitrary larger Hilbert space. Fixed redundant factors can be removed and restored through channels without disturbing that family. Dynamics must additionally preserve the encoded family or its logical observable algebra.

The unified theory's Section 13 gives a directly calculable two-qubit family with one fixed maximally mixed logical factor. Its CNOT encoding, recovery, and Hamiltonian intertwining are verified independently as a finite illustration of this known mechanism. No general quantum-compression theorem is claimed as new.
