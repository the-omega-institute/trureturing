# Completely positive versus positive sufficiency

## Checked locator

arXiv:2604.08380v1, submitted 9 April 2026. The full HTML was inspected at Sections 1.1–1.2. Equations (1.4)–(1.7) state the sufficient-operator-system setting, distinguish completely positive from merely positive recovery, and identify the corresponding associative or Jordan structures. The CPTP minimal sufficient algebra is related there to the Koashi–Imoto decomposition.

## Use in the unified theory

Section 13 restricts its physical encoding and decoding to completely positive trace-preserving maps. It uses an explicit family with a fixed redundant factor. No conclusion about arbitrary unknown states, general Jordan compression, or finite-temperature dynamical sufficiency is inferred from the example.

This source is a 2026 preprint and its entire proof has not been independently adjudicated. Its role is both an up-to-date sufficiency reference and a restriction against treating positive maps as physically valid channels.
