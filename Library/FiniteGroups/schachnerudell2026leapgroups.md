- Page 2 gives the hop definition with Question 1.1 and the generated subgroup in Question 1.3.
- Page 9 identifies P₁ as the path of length one. Page 21 poses Question 5.4 and the broader product question, Question 5.5.

Question 5.4 asks for the leap groups of the prism graphs $P_1\square C_n$ and the hypercube graphs $P_1^{\square n}$. The answer uses the original vertex permutation action and the closure of all adjacent permutations. The odd prisms have the full symmetric group. The even prisms and positive-dimensional cubes have the setwise stabilizer of their unordered pair of parity classes. The paper supplies the question and definitions. The subgroup equalities are derived in the repository source.
