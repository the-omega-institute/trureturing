# Transversal-free Latin arrays of orders three and four

A Latin array has no repeated symbol in any row or column. A transversal selects one cell from each row and each column with all selected symbols distinct. Section 4 and Table 1 of arXiv:1612.09443 give zero transversal-free classes at order three and two at order four. The order-four representatives use four and five symbols. Trisotopy allows permuting rows, columns and symbols, and transposing.

The Lean order-three proof checks the six column permutations. The order-four proof checks a tree of forced cell equalities. Its disjunction of a transversal, equal column symbol sets, or a specified motif is the form needed by the product-array argument.
