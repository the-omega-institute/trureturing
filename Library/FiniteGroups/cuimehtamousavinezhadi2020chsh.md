## Question and presentation

Section 1.4 (Further work), item 2, printed p. 7:

> In Section 5, we give efficient explicit presentations for $G_n$ and its multiplication table. Can we go further and characterize these groups in terms of direct and semidirect products of small well-known groups? The first few cases are as follows

The displayed cases are $G_3\cong\mathbb Z_3\times A_4$, $G_4\cong(\mathbb Z_2^3\rtimes\mathbb Z_4)\rtimes\mathbb Z_4$, $G_5\cong(\mathbb Z_2^4\rtimes\mathbb Z_5)\times\mathbb Z_5$, and $G_6\cong\mathbb Z_3\times(((\mathbb Z_4\times\mathbb Z_2^3)\rtimes\mathbb Z_2)\rtimes\mathbb Z_2)\rtimes\mathbb Z_3$.

Section 5, printed p. 24, defines

> $G_n=\langle P_0,P_1,J\mid P_0^n,P_1^n,J^n,[J,P_0],[J,P_1],J^i(P_0^iP_1^{-i})^2\text{ for }i=1,2,\ldots,\lfloor n/2\rfloor\rangle$.

Here the listed words are relators equal to the identity. Lemma 5.2, printed p. 25, states:

> For all $i,j$, the elements $P_0^iP_1^{-i}$ and $P_0^jP_1^{-j}$ commute.

Theorem 5.7, printed p. 26, states:

> $H_n\cong G_n$ for all $n\in\mathbb N$.

The group $H_n$ is generated by Alice's observables $A_0=X$ and $A_1=z_n^2D_0X$. The theorem identifies this matrix group with the presentation; it does not give the uniform product decomposition. The repository's decomposition concerns every $n\ge2$, with cyclic factors represented by `Multiplicative (ZMod m)` and the elementary abelian factor by functions on `Fin (n-2)`.
