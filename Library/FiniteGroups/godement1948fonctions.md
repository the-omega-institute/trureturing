## Encoding and boundary

The repository theorem
`D5.S3.FiniteGroups.GroupAlgebra.PositiveDefiniteClassFunction.exists_sum_character_of_finiteDimensional`
states, for an arbitrary group with no topology, that a class function whose
kernel `(x, y) ↦ φ (x * y⁻¹)` is positive semidefinite and whose sections span
a finite-dimensional space is a positive combination of the characters of
finitely many pairwise inequivalent irreducible representations by isometries,
with the dimension of the span equal to the sum of the squared degrees. That
theorem is a repository derivation. Functions of positive type on groups are
the classical background of the statement; this note acknowledges that
background and does not assert that the paper states the repository theorem.
