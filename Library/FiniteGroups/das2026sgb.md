Problem 4.9 asks: “Is there any finite non-abelian group G such that B(G) does not hold
the conclusions of Remark 4.1?” The graph has all ordered pairs of group elements and
all subgroups as disjoint vertex parts. A pair is adjacent precisely to the subgroup
it generates. The energy definitions use the whole graph average degree.

definition. The formal statement preserves $Q=D+A$ and makes no author-correction claim.

Problems dossier and prospective issue #28317.

proves the actual matrix transport, all fiber counts and the nonabelian estimate needed
for the complete globally centered inequality. The citing arXiv:2602.07587v1 treats
cyclic families; its order-one boundary is a different question.
