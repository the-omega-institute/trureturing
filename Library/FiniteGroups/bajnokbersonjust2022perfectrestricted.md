# On Perfect Bases in Finite Abelian Groups

B. Bajnok, C. Berson and H. A. Just, “On Perfect Bases in Finite Abelian Groups,”
*Involve* **15** (2022), 525–536. DOI: [10.2140/involve.2022.15.525](https://doi.org/10.2140/involve.2022.15.525).
The arXiv version is [arXiv:2211.13675v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.13675v1),
24 November 2022.

The paper defines: “A is a perfect restricted s-basis of G if each element of G can be written uniquely as the sum of at most s distinct elements of A.”
Question 6 asks: “Are there any noncyclic groups that have a perfect restricted s-basis of size s + 1?”
The formal settlement proves the answer is no for finite abelian groups. Its decisive mechanism is the fixed-point-free toggle involution on each coset of the subgroup generated by a basis element, together with the odd order forced by the proper-subset count.
