## Encoding and boundary

The repository module states, for an arbitrary monoid with no topology, that a
representation by isometries of a finite-dimensional real or complex inner
product space is semisimple, and, for an arbitrary group and a finite family
of representations by isometries, that a weighted sum of characters is a
positive semidefinite kernel for nonnegative weights and, for pairwise
inequivalent irreducible representations, only for nonnegative weights. These
statements are repository derivations. Complete reducibility of unitary
representations and positivity of their characters belong to the classical
background of harmonic analysis on groups; this note acknowledges that
background and does not assert that the book states the repository theorems.
