# On some problems regarding a group-theoretical generalization of the Gauss formula

Marius Tărnăuceanu, arXiv:2611.34371v1, 25 November 2026.
Section 1, p. 1, defines the invariant by summing the full automorphism-group
orders over all actual subgroups and dividing by the group order. Equation
(1) splits this into 1 plus the normalized noncyclic-subgroup contribution.

Theorem 3.1 gives the minimum `5/2` on the noncyclic finite p-groups and
its equality case. Theorem 3.3 proves the global gap `(2,5/2)`. Its proof
gives `1 + (p−1)/q` for a minimal noncyclic split extension of the stated
kind. The specialization `p=5`, `q=2`, with exponent 1, gives `3` on the
dihedral group of order 10. Table 1, p. 11, gives the first two cyclic-product
values `5/2` and `11/4` and reports value `3` on the dihedral group of order 12.

Remark 3.4, p. 10, ends with the following question.

> It remains an open problem to determine the accumulation points of Im(f) (or to decide whether Im(f) is closed and discrete in [1, ∞)).
