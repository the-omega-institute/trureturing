# On the sum of inverses of subgroup orders in finite groups

M. Tărnăuceanu, “On the sum of inverses of subgroup orders in finite groups,”
*Scientific Studies and Research, Series Mathematics and Informatics* **34** (2024), no. 1, 115–120.

The paper defines `f(G)` as the sum of `1/|H|` over the subgroup lattice and states
Conjecture 3.1: “There is no constant c > 1 such that f(G) > c, for all finite
non-cyclic groups G.” The paper obtains the limiting construction under the
assumption that infinitely many Sophie Germain primes exist. The formal settlement
uses the same split cyclic family with a growing exponent and removes that
assumption by choosing a prime congruent to one modulo the acting prime.
