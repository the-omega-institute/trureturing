# The auxiliary sequence A238474

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A238474

The [internal entry](https://oeis.org/A238474/internal), revision 15,
gives the FORMULA line:

> a(n) = (-1)^n*(n+3)!/(2*(n+1)) for n >= 0

The offset is `0,1`; the author line is Wolfdieter Lang, 1 March 2014.
The [history](https://oeis.org/history?seq=A238474) and formula are the
source for the total rational function `AdjacentDifferenceRefutation.b`.
Its denominator is nonzero for every natural input. In particular,
$b(0)=3$ and $b(1)=-6$.

The literal A378495 question substitutes $m=n-4$ for every $n\ge4$.
At $n=5$ the required absolute value is therefore 6. This source identity
does not itself say anything about derangement triangle cardinalities;
their comparison is a separate question in
[Kagey's note](kagey2024a378495.md).

The bounded source qualification recorded in
[#23900](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/23900)
read revision 15, its formula, offset and history. The rational formula
is used directly, with no integer-division or integrality premise and
no shift correction. No global literature-completeness claim is made.
