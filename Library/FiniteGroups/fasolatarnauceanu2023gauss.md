# On a group-theoretical generalization of the Gauss formula

Georgiana Fasolă and Marius Tărnăuceanu, *Czechoslovak Mathematical Journal*
73 (2023), 311–317. Theorem 1.1 counts the cyclic contribution by grouping
elements according to the subgroup they generate. Theorem 1.6 gives the
general finite abelian p-group automorphism formula. It specializes to
6 for the Klein four group and to `2^(k+1)` for `C_(2^k) × C₂` with `k ≥ 2`.
Proposition 1.3 and its proof give the value on the applicable minimal
noncyclic semidirect products. The discussion after Problem 3 explicitly
gives `f(D_(2n)) = (n+1)/2` for odd `n ≥ 3`, hence value 3 at `n=5`.
