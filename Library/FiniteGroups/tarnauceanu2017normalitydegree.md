# Normality degrees of finite groups

Marius Tărnăuceanu, “Normality degrees of finite groups,”
*Carpathian Journal of Mathematics* **33** (2017), no. 1, 115–126.

Section 1 defines the normality degree as $|N(G)|/|L(G)|$, where $L(G)$
is the entire subgroup lattice and $N(G)$ consists of its normal subgroups.
Both contain the trivial subgroup and the whole group, counted once when
they coincide. Section 4 states Conjecture 4.1 on printed p. 125.

> For every x ∈ (0, 1] ∩ ℚ there exists a finite group G such that ndeg(G) = x.

The preprint, arXiv:1312.1427v1, states the same conjecture as Conjecture 4.4
on p. 16. The conjecture asks for exact attainment and includes $x=1$.

The published ZM formula in §3.1, equation (3.13), specializes to the normal
and total subgroup counts for a prime cyclic kernel and a cyclic quotient
of order $2^B$ acting with image of order $2^t$. The corresponding ZM
normal-subgroup formula is also given by Marius Tărnăuceanu,
arXiv:1502.04776v1, Theorem 1 and Corollary 3.
