# The derangement triangle A378495

## Source and exact questions

The [internal entry](https://oeis.org/A378495/internal), revision 70,
defines the triangle read by rows: $T(n,k)$ is the number of derangements
in $S_n$ with no $k$-cycles, $1\le k\le n$. Its offset is `1,5`.
The author line attributes the entry to Peter Kagey, 29 November 2024.
The FORMULA field states:

> Conjecture: T(n,n-2) - T(n,n)   = (n-3)!*(n-4)*(n-1)/2 for n >= 5.

> Conjecture: T(n,n-1) - T(n,n-2) = abs(A238474(n-4)) for n >= 4.

The [history](https://oeis.org/history?seq=A378495) records the adjacent
formula in revision 59 and the terminal formula in revision 60.
Revision 70 retains both as conjectures. The second question uses
the zero-offset sequence in [Lang's note](lang2014a238474.md).

## Mathematical correspondence

`TerminalDifference.D` is the finite set of permutations of `Fin n`
moving every label. Relabelling `Fin n` by $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ gives the
source's symmetric group without changing cycle lengths or cardinalities.
`T` filters this actual set by absence of a cycle factor of support size
$k$; `noCycle_iff` identifies that predicate with absence of $k$ from the
multiset of nontrivial cycle lengths. Fixed points are already excluded,
so the source convention at $k=1$ is preserved.

The two source differences use rational subtraction after casting the
natural cardinalities. Factorials and index subtractions are natural
operations; division by two and the formula for A238474 use the rationals.
No conjectured cardinality formula is assumed in defining $T$.

## Result and limits

The terminal formula is proved for every $n\ge5$. The literal adjacent
formula is refuted by $n=5$, where the two sides are 20 and 6. These are
separate questions. The result does not refute Kallat's proved congruences,
establish a corrected shift, or supply a global novelty or priority claim.
The detailed statements, admission boundaries and unfinished audit are
in the two Problems dossiers. Literature completeness remains
