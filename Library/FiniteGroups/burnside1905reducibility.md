## Encoding and boundary

The result usually called Burnside's theorem states that the linear span of
an irreducible group of linear transformations of a finite-dimensional vector
space over an algebraically closed field is the whole endomorphism algebra.
The repository theorem
`D5.S3.FiniteGroups.GroupAlgebra.CharacterKernelRank.finrank_span_sum_character_kernel`
extends this to a finite family of pairwise inequivalent irreducible
representations of an arbitrary group and reads it through the weighted trace
pairing, giving the rank of a character kernel with nonzero weights. That theorem is a
repository derivation; this note acknowledges the classical background and
does not assert that a cited paper states it.
