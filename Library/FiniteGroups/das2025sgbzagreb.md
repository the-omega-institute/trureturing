The fixed source is arXiv:2501.06124v1. Problem 3.10 is on printed page 13:
“Is there any finite group G such that B(G) does not satisfy Hansen-Vukičević
conjecture?” Its native HTML anchor is `S3.Thmtheorem10`.

The graph has disjoint vertex parts $G\times G$ and $L(G)$, the set of all
subgroups. An ordered pair, with repeated entries allowed, is adjacent exactly
to the subgroup it generates. Subgroups with no generating pair remain as
isolated vertices. The first Zagreb index sums squared degrees over all
vertices; the second sums endpoint-degree products over unordered edges once.

Theorem 3.1 (`S3.Thmtheorem1`) gives
$M_1=|G|^2+|G|^4\sum_H\Pr_H(G)^2$ and $M_2=M_1-|G|^2$.
Here $\Pr_H(G)=r(H)/|G|^2$, where $r(H)$ counts all ordered pairs generating H.
Theorem 3.2 (`S3.Thmtheorem2`) gives both bipartite degree sums and the edge
count as $|G|^2$. Theorem 3.3 (`S3.Thmtheorem3`) makes the Zagreb inequality
equivalent to $|L(G)|\sum_H\Pr_H(G)^2\ge1$.
These unrestricted source identities are attributed to the paper. The candidate
formal proof combines their literal-graph counterparts with the standard
square-sum inequality; no originality or worldwide priority claim is made.
