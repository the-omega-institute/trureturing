Section 9, Problem 9.4, p. 14:

> Is there a finite semigroup for which ∼o is a congruence and S/∼o is not cancelative?

The two witnesses belong to S, without adjoining an identity. Congruence means
multiplication compatibility of this equivalence relation; cancellation of the
quotient is expressed by left and right cancellation on its representatives.
The source's Section 6 constructs an infinite noncancellative quotient.
The finite negative answer is derived in the associated Lean module.
