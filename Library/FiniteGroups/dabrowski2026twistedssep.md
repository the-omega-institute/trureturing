# Twisted symmetric exclusion processes and set-theoretical R-matrices

## Verified locator

Mathieu Dabrowski, Loïc Poulain d’Andecy and Eric Ragoucy,
*Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and Theoretical* **59**, 245203,
DOI [10.1088/1751-8121/ae751a](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8121/ae751a).
The source is [arXiv:2602.18204v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18204v2),
DOI [10.48550/arXiv.2602.18204](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.18204).

Section 3.1, equations (3.1)–(3.3), defines configurations, adjacent
exchanges and the twisted boundary exchange. Section 4 defines sectors and
their uniform stationary states. Section 5.1, Definition 5.1 and Proposition
5.2, gives the equilibrated branching probability as the intersection
cardinality divided by the initial sector cardinality. Section 7 asks:

> On the mathematical side, given two twisted SSEP, what are the conditions
> on the permutations defining the twists in order to have a non-zero
> probability of occupying any configuration after a certain number of switch
> between the twists of both models?

The formal application concerns this equilibrated-switch support question.
For at least three sites the criterion uses transitivity of the derived
subgroup of the group generated by the two twists. For two sites it uses the
orbit–stabilizer count for the subgroup generated by the paired twists.
These classifications are derived in the accompanying Lean source, rather
than attributed to the paper. The finite-group bucket records the group
criterion; the particle-process model remains in statistical mechanics.
