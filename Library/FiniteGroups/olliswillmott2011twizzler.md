# On twizzler, zigzag and graceful terraces

M. A. Ollis and D. T. Willmott, *Australasian Journal of Combinatorics*
**51** (2011), 243–257, Section 2 and Table 1.

The Walecki and twizzler constructions are published methods, not new results
of this repository. The five-entry tail is a shifted reversal of the Table 1
path (1,4,0,2,3). The column supplier verifies the exact formulas and their
inventories internally, including the directed middle and closing edges and
the locations of the additive triples. An undirected rotational terrace alone
does not supply those directed clauses.

The primary article is cited without redistributing its PDF.
