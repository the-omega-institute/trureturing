# On some batch code properties of the simplex code

## Verified locator

Henk D. L. Hollmann, Karan Khathuria, Ago-Erik Riet and Vitaly Skachek,
[arXiv:2110.07421v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.07421v5), version of
22 January 2023. The author TeX contains the complete proofs of Theorem 4.2
and Theorem 4.4, corresponding to pages 6–7 of this version's PDF.

Definition 4.1 requires distinct inputs and distinct outputs separately:
the outputs are the inputs plus their prescribed labels. It imposes no
distinctness on labels and no disjointness between the two sides.
Theorem 4.2 extends any service of length at most two less than the group
order by an arbitrary next label. Its two unused outputs support a finite
exchange chain. Theorem 4.4 builds a service of length one less than the
order and completes it using the missing input and output. Equality of
the two complete group sums makes the final difference the final label.

Theorem 4.4 credits Marshall Hall's original prescribed-difference result,
DOI [10.1090/S0002-9939-1952-0050579-7](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1952-0050579-7).
The original Hall article was not read. The proof source used here is the
complete servicing proof in the cited arXiv version. This is a formalization
of a known theorem and makes no claim of mathematical novelty. The source
is cited, not redistributed.
