## Mathematical interpretation

Rows and columns are relabeled by independently chosen permutations. Each
class contributes one if at least one representative is symmetric. Both
margins are prescribed for every index, and degree zero is included.
The rational function is expanded at zero in the formal power series ring
over the rationals. Its denominator has constant term one.

Ming Yean Lim, *The number of irreducibles in the plethysm s_lambda[s_m]*,
[arXiv:2503.21108v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.21108v1), Definition 1.1,
Theorem 3.5 and Remark 3.6, gives the semimagic-matrix setting and the twisted
orbit-counting route. The theorem counts transpose-fixed orbits. The remark
makes the separate size-four symmetric-representative criterion material.
