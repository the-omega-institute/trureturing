# The classical coordinates behind the relative tensor problem

## Verified locator

Jonathan Elmer and Kazal Kadr, *Some formulae relating modular
representations of elementary abelian p-groups*, arXiv:2510.07939v1,
https://arxiv.org/html/2510.07939v1: the introduction gives the faithful
two-dimensional action; Section 2.2 gives the relative category; and
the opening of Section 3, PDF page 6, gives formulas (11)--(12) for the
symmetric powers and their ordinary duals. The published identifier is
https://doi.org/10.1007/s13366-026-00855-9.

## Scope

The faithful two-dimensional action has a common fixed basis vector and
an injective additive off-diagonal parameter. Symmetric powers are modeled
by bounded polynomials under translation, and ordinary contragredients
use the inverse group element. These are classical ingredients, rather
than new classification results.

The formal normal-form construction also permits infinite exponent-p
abelian groups and arbitrary coefficient fields of characteristic p.
The genuine Mathlib quotient is identified with bounded polynomials in
every degree, without division by a factorial. The present Fin-index API
forces the coefficient field into universe zero; it does not require a
finite field or a degree below the characteristic.

Conjecture 3.7(a) is the external motivation, not a theorem attributed to
this source. The earlier repository counterexample refutes its
unconditional form. The corrected binomial criterion and its transport
to the actual original modules are separately derived repository results.
No priority claim or completion of Conjecture 3.7(b) follows from this note.
