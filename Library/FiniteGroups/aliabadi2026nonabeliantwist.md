# A nonabelian twist on differences of bijections

## Verified locator

Mohsen Aliabadi, [arXiv:2605.16478v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.16478v2); journal DOI
[10.61091/um128-18](https://doi.org/10.61091/um128-18).
The [official journal article](https://combinatorialpress.com/um-articles/vol-128/a-nonabelian-twist-on-differences-of-bijections/)
and the arXiv v2 version are the sources for Definition 2.1, Proposition 2.3,
and the complete Problem 7.6 with its following paragraph.

Problem 7.6 reads: “Does every finite nonabelian group admit a multiset A
of cardinality |G| satisfying the abelianization obstruction but not
quotient-realizable?” Mathematical typography is transcribed here.
The immediately following paragraph reads: “A positive answer would
characterize finite abelian groups as precisely those finite groups for
which the abelianization obstruction is the only obstruction.”

Quotient realizability means two bijections b,c from an index set of size
|G| to G whose multiset of right quotients b(i)c(i)^(-1) equals A.
Proposition 2.3 gives the necessary obstruction: the product, with
multiplicities, after the canonical projection to G/[G,G] is one.
Repeated identities are permitted. The question does
not require a product-one ordering in G or an identity-free multiset.

The associated formal result uses one nonidentity element of [G,G] and
|G|-1 identity entries. Its proof applies the canonical abelianization kernel
and the impossibility that a permutation moves exactly one point. This is
an application of those general results to the stated question, without a
claim to a new general theorem or priority. The source is cited, not redistributed.
