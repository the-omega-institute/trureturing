## Statement

> In a quandle product, is the intersection between two arbitrary parabolic subgroups again a parabolic subgroup?

Every subset of the index set generates a standard parabolic by taking the subgroup
generated by its factor images. Its conjugates in the quandle product are parabolic.
Both input subgroups in Question 9.4 are arbitrary such conjugates.

## Source results

Definition 2.4 presents the quandle product by imposing orthogonal commutations
and the twists \(ab=b(b\star a)\) on the free product. Example 2.6 specializes it
to a two-element chain with empty orthogonality and the twist \(b^{-1}ab=b\star a\).
Corollary 3.7 gives the intersection formula for standard parabolics generated
by stable subsets. Corollary 3.6, cited in the paragraph introducing Question 9.4,
concerns the word problem.
