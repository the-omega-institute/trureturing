# Cayley concentration through displacement geometry

The fixed source is arXiv:2508.18254v1, Section 4, especially Lemma 4.6,
Claim 4.7, Theorem 1.5 and Corollary 4.1. The displacement of a unit vector
under a finite isometric group action is subadditive and invariant under
inversion. A second-moment bound puts most original labels in a small
displacement ball. Separated translates force a plateau among successive
balls, and the plateau's left stabilizer is proper when the orbit sums to zero.

The formal localization uses the actual regular action and retains the
original labels. The shell count is the natural number
`(floor(4/tau)+2)/3`; the sufficient plateau bound is `k <= 4/tau`.
The threshold argument divides by the positive squared radius, including
zero correlation defect without dividing by that defect.

For nonsymmetric Cayley operators in nonabelian groups, an upper bound on
eigenvalue real parts does not imply an upper bound on every real quadratic
form. The numerical quadratic-form condition is the appropriate stopping
condition for a subsequent concentration descent and directed cut argument.
The general core permits identity loops and nonnormal subgroups.

This source supplies the structural cut input to later rainbow-path arguments.
It does not certify their matching, coupling, connecting or absorption proofs.
In particular directed proper colouring alone does not justify a two-edge
rainbow connector. Path consumers require original nonidentity labels.
The arbitrary-abelian proper-interval ordering problem and unrestricted AM
remain open here.
