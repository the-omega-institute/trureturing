# Multi-virtual twin representations

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2605.13090

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13090v1

Question 4.3 (page 17): “A natural question arises regarding the faithfulness of ζ₈: for a ≠ 0, ±1, is the representation ζ₈ faithful?”

Section 3 defines the group by generators sᵢ and ρᵢᵅ and relations (11)–(19).
Theorem 4.1(8) prescribes adjacent two-by-two blocks
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-a&-(a^2-1)/b\\b&a\end{smallmatrix}\right)$
and $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1/y_\alpha\\y_\alpha&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$,
with identity entries outside the block. It allows k ≥ 1, n ≥ 3, complex a,
and nonzero complex b and yᵅ, without a distinctness or genericity restriction.
Theorem 4.2 gives nonfaithfulness for families 1–7 and for family 8 at a ∈ {−1,0,1}.

The repository refutation uses k = n = 3 and the commutator
$[\rho_1^0\rho_1^1,\rho_1^0\rho_1^2]$.
A four-point permutation action detects its nonidentity; its matrix image is
identity because the two color-pair products are diagonal. The negative
answer concerns the universal question. Generic k = 1 and k = 2 and the
complete parameter classification remain outside this result.
