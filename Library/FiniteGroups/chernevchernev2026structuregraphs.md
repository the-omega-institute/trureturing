# Chernev and Chernev on structure graphs

nonexclusive distribution license. [Versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07521v1)

## Statement

Definition 3.1 gives the ordered-pair successor ((a,b) \mapsto (a+b,ab)) in an associative ring.
Hypothesis 6.1 states:

> The structure graph of a Galois field of extension degree > 1 different from GF (4) contains a cycle of length > 1.

Here the extension degree is over the prime field. A cycle consists of distinct ordered pairs with an
edge from each pair to the next, including the edge back to the initial pair.

## Source results

Lemma 5.1 gives the two-step path starting at ((-1,x)) in its discussion of odd characteristic.
Statement 6.2 derives an extension-field result in characteristics other than 2, 3 and 7 under
Hypothesis 5.5. Statement 6.3 proves the result in characteristics 3 and 7, using the complement
of the prime field in odd extension degree. Section 6 reports characteristic-two computations
through degree thirteen. The paper leaves the universal conclusion as Hypothesis 6.1.
