# OEIS A318245

A318245 (Bradley Klee, Aug 22 2018, offset 0, data `1, 12, 180, 2928, 49860, …`)
is

> Scaled g.f. T(v) = Sum_{n>=0} a(n)\*(3\*v/64)^n satisfies
> 9\*(5\*v-4)\*T + d/dv(16\*v\*(v-1)\*(3\*v-4)\*T') = 0, and a(0)=1; sequence
> gives a(n).

with formula fields

> 3\*n^2\*a(n) - 4\*(28\*n^2-28\*n+9)\*a(n-1) + 64\*(4\*n-5)\*(4\*n-3)\*a(n-2) = 0.
>
> For n > 0, a(n) mod 3 = 0 (conjecture, tested up to n=3\*10^6).

The comment field says that the period function T(v) "measures precession of
the J-vector along an algebraic sphere curve with local cyclic C_4 symmetry".
The entry cites W. G. Harter and C. W. Patterson, "Rotational energy surfaces
and high-J eigenvalue structure of polyatomic molecules", J. Chem. Phys. 80
(1984) 4252, doi:10.1063/1.447255, for the rotational energy surface of
octahedral molecules. N. Gegelia and D. van Straten, "The platonic elliptic
surfaces", arXiv:2608.25644 (2026), list the sequence as O(4) in Table 7.
