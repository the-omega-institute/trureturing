# Stationary energy eigenstates

## Verified locator

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stationary_state

The introduction gives H psi = E psi and the time evolution
psi(t) = exp(-i E t / hbar) psi(0). The section on stationary-state properties
explains that the overall complex phase cancels from observable probabilities.
For the rank-one density matrix the same cancellation reads
rho(t) = psi(t) psi(t)* = psi(0) psi(0)*.

The finite-dimensional formal statement uses hbar = 1, permits every real time,
and derives the vector evolution from the convergent matrix exponential series.
The zero-variance companion uses the density-state first and second moments.
