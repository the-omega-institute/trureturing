# Nash approximation on a compact parameter interval

The cited version is arXiv:2601.13164v1, posted 19 January 2026.
Theorem 1.1 assumes a locally compact semialgebraic source S, a Nash
manifold with corners Q, and an S^mu map from S to Q. It supplies Nash
maps into the interior of Q arbitrarily close in the S^mu topology.
The paper defines maps on a semialgebraic set through extensions to
open neighborhoods; section 2.2 describes the topology using positive
continuous semialgebraic tolerances for derivatives through order mu.

Quantum-volume section 218 uses only a closed compact interval,
Euclidean target and mu = 2. Its constructed section extends as a C4
semialgebraic map to a neighborhood of the interval, so these hypotheses
apply. The source supplies approximation, without asserting preservation
of the quantum moment constraint or the prescribed sharp two-jets.
Finite Hermite correction, normalization and a uniform scalar analytic
implicit correction establish those additional properties in section 218.
The paper does not establish the channel-specific five-dimensional
three-point attainment result or a global trivialization of its fibers.
