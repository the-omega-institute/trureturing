# Prescribed spectra of partial isometries

The cited version is arXiv:1703.02960v2, posted 15 March 2017; its manuscript
prints 16 March 2017. Lemma 2 constructs an upper triangular partial isometry
with diagonal zero followed by the prescribed open-unit-disk eigenvalues,
orthonormal nonzero columns and nonzero first-superdiagonal entries.
Theorem 1 characterizes similarity, not unitary equivalence: the spectrum lies
in the closed disk, unit-circle eigenvalues are semisimple, and the nullity at
zero bounds the geometric multiplicity of every open-disk eigenvalue.

These are classical matrix tools used within the shared-processor construction
in quantum-volume §213.4. They do not establish that the channel-specific sharp
three-dimensional models embed into one processor, nor that one preparation
curve attains sharp calibration at several prescribed points. The former is
proved there by the normalized eigenvector Gram identity; the latter retains
the distinct lower and upper bounds of §213.5. No general similarity theorem
is being promoted to unitary equivalence without that additional argument.
