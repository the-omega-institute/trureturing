# Parameter-dependent mixtures and SLD information

The cited preprint is arXiv:1403.8033v2, dated 13 November 2014; the
journal version is Physical Review A 91, 042104 (2015). Equation (4) is the
extended convexity inequality. Its classical term is essential when the
mixing probabilities depend on the parameter; ordinary convexity alone
would omit that preparation cost. The derivation around equation (5) uses
the block SLD obtained by adding the logarithmic probability derivative.

Quantum-volume §215.3 uses this established inequality as an intermediate
tool. A temporary orthogonal preparation label supplies the upper bound;
the actual mixture remains in the original shared program space. At zero
weights the manuscript checks the state and derivative directly: its cubic
amplitude zeros make both the zero block and its derivative vanish, and
also make the amplitude derivative zero. It does not import a continuous
Bures extension as the pointwise SLD value.

The source does not supply the channel-specific original-dimension spectral
necessity, the simultaneous sharp-calibration construction, its exact
minimal support dimension, or its capacity transition. Those conclusions
require the additional proofs in §§213–215.
