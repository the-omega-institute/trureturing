# Large-N AdS/CFT proposal

Maldacena's abstract describes a limit of particular conformal theories
containing a supergravity sector on an AdS background times a compact
manifold, and conjectures dualities with string/M theory. The proposal
concerns specific brane systems and large-N limits; it does not assign an
AdS bulk to every conformal or vertex operator algebra.

Interpreting the data of
[appendix 2143](../../docs/develop/theory/OBSERVER_ADELIC_COMPLETION_CONSTANT_THEORY.md)
as a holographic boundary theory requires an additional, concrete bulk and
boundary model, its limit, and its dictionary. Neither the theorem on
partial observation of group-indexed logarithmic traces nor the existence
of a VOA supplies that duality.
For the separate scope of relative entropy and modular flow in a chosen
holographic model, see [JLMS](jlms2016relativeentropy.md).
