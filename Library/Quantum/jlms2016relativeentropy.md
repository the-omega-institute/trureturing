# Holographic relative entropy

JLMS study the gravitational dual of a boundary subregion modular
Hamiltonian. Their abstract argues for equality of relative entropy of
nearby states with bulk relative entropy to leading order in the bulk
gravitational coupling, and relates boundary modular flow to bulk modular
flow in the entanglement wedge. The claim presupposes a chosen holographic
dual and does not follow from a VOA or a formal Mobius inversion alone.

The holographic interpretation of
[appendix 2143](../../docs/develop/theory/OBSERVER_ADELIC_COMPLETION_CONSTANT_THEORY.md)
therefore concerns additional data: a concrete dual model and dictionary,
a chosen boundary region and its bulk entanglement wedge, and compatible
nearby semiclassical states, region algebras, and modular flows. A
semiclassical code subspace is additional encoding data, not a consequence
of that appendix. The abstract's leading-order equality is not an exact
relative-entropy identity for arbitrary states, nor a universal AdS dual
for arbitrary VOAs. The broader duality proposal has its own scope in the
[Maldacena note](maldacena1998ads.md).
