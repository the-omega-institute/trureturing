# Positive maps and extendibility hierarchies from copositive matrices

The versioned source is [arXiv:2509.15201v4, Question 8.20](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.15201v4#S8.Thmtheorem20).
Its literal question reads:

> For mixtures of Dicke states, are the following two sets equivalent?

$$
\mathsf{PPTBExt}_{n}^{(\mathcal K_r)}
=
\mathsf{PPTBExt}_{n}^{(r-1)},\qquad r\ge 2.
$$

The operator coordinates are the nonnegative coefficients of the orthonormal
two-particle Dicke vectors. The star uses one root and r-1 interchangeable
leaves; the complete graph uses all r registers symmetrically. Marginals are
unnormalized partial traces and PPT means positivity under every register cut.
The formal statement retains the whole nonnegative cone, including zero.
