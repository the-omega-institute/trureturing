# OEIS A392387

The NAME of A392387 is

> Nullspace dimension of the periodic XX Heisenberg chain with n > 1 sites.

The COMMENTS state

> a(n) with n>1 is the dimension of the zero-energy subspace (nullspace) of
> the periodic spin-1/2 XX Heisenberg chain on n sites … By the
> Jordan-Wigner transformation, the system maps to free fermions … If the
> number of fermions |K| is odd, the momenta are quantized as k = 2*j*Pi/n …
> If the number of fermions |K| is even, the momenta are quantized as
> k = (2*j + 1)*Pi/n … The sequence counts the number of subsets M of these
> momenta such that the total energy E = Sum_{m in M} cos(m) is 0.

and

> Also, the number of subsets K of {1,...,n} such that the sum of cosines of
> the angles in {(2j + (1 + (-1)^|K|)/2 )*Pi/n | j in K} is zero.

The FORMULA section records

> Conjecture: a(2*p) = 2*(6^((p-1)/2)+1) for odd prime p.

The entry cites Y. Hu, F. Gerken and T. Posske, *Hidden Twisted Sectors and
Exponential Degeneracy in Root-of-Unity XXZ Heisenberg Chains*,
arXiv:2602.15098 (2026), whose appendix on the XX model (`Δ = 0`) rephrases the
nullspace degeneracy as the number of combinations of distinct angles whose
cosines sum to zero and calls this, equivalently the counting of certain purely
imaginary sums of distinct `N`-th and `2N`-th roots of unity, "an unsolved
mathematical problem".
