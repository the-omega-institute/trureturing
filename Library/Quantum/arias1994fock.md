# Factorization and Reflexivity on Fock spaces

The preliminary Fock-space discussion defines creation by left tensoring with a basis vector and gives word reversal as a unitary involution. Right append is the conjugate convention. The word-length operator is defined on finite-support vectors; no bounded extension is asserted.
