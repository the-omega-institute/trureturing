# Ky Fan sums under partial trace

## Verified source

Alexey E. Rastegin, Journal of Statistical Physics 148 (2012), 1040-1053;
arXiv:1202.3853v3. Proposition 1 proves the partial-trace inequality for a
family of unitarily invariant norms. Its p=1 specialization is equation (3.8):
the Ky Fan k-sum of the partial trace is at most the Ky Fan kn-sum of the
original operator when the traced factor has dimension n. The author notes
that this specialization had an earlier proof using Ky Fan's maximum principle.

## Use and boundary

In RT Section 36.1, projection onto a flat target factor gives a normalized
diagonal-block sum. Embedding its rectangular coefficient operator in a square
operator and applying the p=1 partial-trace bound yields the Ky Fan step of
equation (36.5). Compression and the d-fold repeated source Schmidt spectrum
are separate steps. The paper does not prove the sector-overlap estimate, the
reference-labelled channel test, or the full product-channel optimum.
