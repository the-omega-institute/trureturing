# Classical field conventions in the lecture notes

These are author-hosted lecture notes for the 2006 and 2007 Michaelmas
courses, used as secondary exposition, not as original primary papers.
[Chapter 1](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/qft/one.pdf),
Section 1.3.1, equations (1.29)–(1.33), states on-shell current conservation
and the boundary-flux requirement for integrated charges.
Section 1.3.2, equations (1.42)–(1.46), gives the scalar stress tensor and
energy; equations (1.60)–(1.63) give the complex scalar Lagrangian,
field equation, phase transformation and current.
[Chapter 6](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/qft/six.pdf),
equations (6.1)–(6.3), gives the Maxwell action and equations of motion;
equations (6.16)–(6.18) give the constrained Hamiltonian with
$(|E|^2+|B|^2)/2$ and the Gauss constraint.

The consumer is [Static §23, Assumption 23.6](../../docs/develop/theory/AURIC_FIB_ATOM_STATIC_SEAMS_TRANSITION_CIRCULATION_AND_FIBONACCI_TOGGLE_CYCLES.md).
Its lightcone factors are obtained by the displayed coordinate transform.
Its gauge-field stress uses its own explicitly positive internal form and
normalization; no Yang–Mills or Wilson original equation is quoted.
These formulas are intermediate comparisons inside a finite probability
application and do not identify its occupation with field strength,
Noether charge or an Einstein source.
