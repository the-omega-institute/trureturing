# Quantum estimates for classical polynomial optimization

## Verified locator

The publication DOI is 10.1088/1751-8121/aebddd. The versioned preprint is https://arxiv.org/html/2607.25445v3. The numerical conjecture is Eq. (33) in §2.3, printed pp. 8–9, with its scope given by Eq. (30) and its numerical origin described in footnote 1. [Equation (33)](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.25445v3#S2.E33)

## Operator and weights

Section 2.1, Eqs. (19) and (22), specifies normal ordering and the normalized occupation basis. Section 2.3, Eqs. (30)–(32), gives the resonant interaction and its weights for real σ ≥ 1. Equation (33) gives the fitted top eigenvalue (N − 1)(N + 2σM)/2. Footnote 1 describes the numerical basis for the formula.

Appendix A groups the ordered terms into half ranges with multiplicity two for each distinct-mode pair. Appendix B, Eqs. (47)–(48), gives the Gamma ratio and Raney convolution used for the classical inequality (34). [Versioned preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.25445v3)
