# Theory of monomer-dimer systems

The Princeton publication record reproduces the abstract of Heilmann and Lieb,
Communications in Mathematical Physics 25 (1972), 190-232. Its stated main
result places the zeros of the monomer-activity polynomial on the imaginary
axis when the dimer activities are nonnegative. It explicitly includes zero
activities. No theorem number is asserted here.

The repository specializes matching configurations to a finite path. Its
generating variable counts dimers, so the corresponding zeros are negative
real numbers. The repository supplies the explicit odd/even square-root
bidiagonal Gram realization and the separate normalized configuration-space
quantum readout. This note attributes the matching-polynomial zero principle;
it does not attribute that full matrix-and-readout packaging to the paper.
