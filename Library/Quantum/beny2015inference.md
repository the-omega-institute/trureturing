# Statistical observation and scale changes

## Source scope

New Journal of Physics 17, 083005 (2015), author manuscript v4. The author
abstract describes operational equivalence classes induced by imperfect
measurements and the role of scale or precision in selecting effective models.
The v4 record explicitly corrects an error at the end of Section V: the adjoint
recovery map does not simply factor over modes. No such factorization is used
in the computational-behavior volume.

## Use and limits

Section 59 keeps two distinct quantities under successive coarse channels:
relative-entropy loss, and failure of the channel to intertwine specified
dynamics. Their exact telescoping identity and norm triangle bound are proved
directly. These elementary composition results do not construct a Wilsonian
flow, establish locality of an effective action, or determine a critical
exponent.

The quantum recovery results in Sections 56-58 fix the physical generator,
conditional thermal states, logical generator and one CPTP map. They test
joint feasibility of statistical calibration and dynamical prediction. The
source paper motivates the observation-based interpretation; it is not cited
as a proof of the new spectral optimization or finite-window tradeoff.
