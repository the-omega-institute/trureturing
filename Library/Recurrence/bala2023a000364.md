# OEIS A000364

Peter Bala's comment of May 8, 2023 states the pure periodicity conjecture
quoted above. The entry defines the Euler secant numbers, with offset 0, by
`a(0) = 1` and, for positive `n`,
`a(n) = Sum_{j=1..n} (-1)^(j+1) * C(2*n,2*j) * a(n-j)`.
This is the coefficient recurrence of `sec(x) * cos(x) = 1`.
The first published values are `1, 1, 5, 61, 1385, 50521, 2702765,
199360981, 19391512145, 2404879675441`.

The formal definition uses this recurrence, reindexed by replacing `j` with
`j+1`, and proves its uniqueness. Reduction modulo 27 commutes with the
recurrence. The residues at indices 1 and 19 are respectively 1 and 10.
Since `phi(27) = 18`, these two residues refute pure periodicity with any
positive period dividing `phi(27)`.

The neighboring comment cites Knuth and Buckholtz (1967, Theorem 2): the
sequence starting at index 1 is periodic modulo every odd prime, with minimal
period `(p-1)/2` for `p == 1 (mod 4)` and `p-1` for `p == 3 (mod 4)`.
The counterexample at the composite modulus 27 does not contradict that
statement. No assertion of eventual periodicity is proved here.
