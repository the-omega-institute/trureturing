# OEIS A376527

Paul D. Hanna's comment of October 11, 2024 records the parity conjecture
quoted above. The entry defines

`a(n) = Sum_{k=0..n*(n-1)/2} A227543(n,k)^2`,

where A227543 is the Carlitz-Riordan q-Catalan triangle. Its row polynomials
are determined by the generating-function equation

`A(x,q) = 1 + x*A(q*x,q)*A(x,q)`.

Writing `A(x,q) = Sum_n R_n(q)*x^n` and comparing the coefficient of
`x^(n+1)` gives

`R_0(q) = 1` and
`R_(n+1)(q) = Sum_{i+j=n} q^i*R_i(q)*R_j(q)`.

At `q=1`, the recurrence becomes the Catalan convolution. Consequently the
sum of a row is the nth Catalan number. In characteristic two each coefficient
satisfies `u^2=u`, so the row's square sum has the same parity as its row sum.
The shifted Catalan series therefore places the odd indices exactly one below
a power of two.

The directed formal dependency is
`D5/S1/Recurrence/Parity/QCatalanSquareSumParity` to
`D5/S1/Recurrence/Invariants/CatalanCompositionSquareParity`, through
`catalan_unique` for the row-sum identification and `binary_catalan` for the
binary support.
