# OEIS A380678

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated February 2025, specifies the generating equation
and parity conjecture quoted above. The series has zero constant coefficient;
the sequence starts at offset one and has `a(n) = [x^n]A(x)`. The denominator
has constant coefficient one, so division denotes its formal unit inverse.

The module constructs the integral solution by degree contraction and proves
the stated equation, linear coefficient one, and uniqueness. In characteristic
two, `1-A^2 = (1-A)^2`. This identifies the reduced equation with the reversion
identity of the lacunary series in
`D5/S1/Recurrence/Invariants/QuadraticReversionDyadicSupportParity`.
Uniqueness then gives its coefficient support, proving the conjecture.
The formulation `n+2 = 3*2^m` or `n+2 = 4*2^m` is equivalent to the stated
index families because both products are at least two.
