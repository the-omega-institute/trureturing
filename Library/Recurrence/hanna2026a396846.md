# OEIS A396846

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated June 24, 2026, specifies the logarithmic generating
function and the second conjecture quoted above. The sequence starts at index
one with 1, 15, 805, 75663, 10440441, 1954144335. The separate conjecture
modulo three is not asserted here.

The formal meaning of the logarithm is `B * H = X * derivative H`, where
`B = Sum_{n>=1} a(n) X^n`, `[X^0]H = [X^1]H = 1`, and
`[X^n]H = 4n a(n)/(4n^2-1)` for `n >= 2`. Thus the series with coefficients
`a(n)/n` and zero constant coefficient is the formal logarithm of `H`.

The module constructs integral normalized coefficients `c(n)` with
`a(n) = (4n^2-1)c(n)` for `n >= 2`. Their recurrence proves the displayed
formal equation and its rational coefficient shape. A coefficient induction
proves uniqueness even among rational solutions with the same initial terms.
Oddness reduces the normalized convolution modulo eight to four times the
sum of its indices. The latter is four-periodic modulo eight, giving residues
1, 7, 5, 7 for successive positive indices.
