# OEIS A396846

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated June 24, 2026, specifies the logarithmic generating
function and the second conjecture quoted above. The sequence starts at index
one with 1, 15, 805, 75663, 10440441, 1954144335. The separate conjecture
modulo three is not asserted here as a theorem, and it does not need to be: it
already follows from frozen content filed under a different entry. Modulo three
the factor `4n^2 - 1` vanishes whenever `3` does not divide `n`, so `theta F = X`
and `theta^2 F = X` for `F = Sum a(n) X^n`; differentiating `theta H = F H` twice
more gives `theta^3 H = (X + F^3) H`, and `theta^3 = theta` in characteristic
three, so cancelling the unit `H` leaves `F = X + F^3`. The frozen
`D5/S3/Arith/TernaryTraceSupport` carries a series `t` with `t^3 = t - X` and zero
constant coefficient, whose coefficients are one exactly at the powers of three.
For any solution `f` of `f = X + f^3`, put `q = 1 - (f^2 + f t + t^2)`; then
`q (f - t) = 0` and `q` has constant coefficient one, hence is a unit, so `f = t`.
No induction is required, and the residue is one rather than two at every
supported index.

Writing that chain out as a new Lean declaration would be a second source for a
truth the graph already carries, so it is recorded here instead.

The formal meaning of the logarithm is `B * H = X * derivative H`, where
`B = Sum_{n>=1} a(n) X^n`, `[X^0]H = [X^1]H = 1`, and
`[X^n]H = 4n a(n)/(4n^2-1)` for `n >= 2`. Thus the series with coefficients
`a(n)/n` and zero constant coefficient is the formal logarithm of `H`.

The module constructs integral normalized coefficients `c(n)` with
`a(n) = (4n^2-1)c(n)` for `n >= 2`. Their recurrence proves the displayed
formal equation and its rational coefficient shape. A coefficient induction
proves uniqueness even among rational solutions with the same initial terms.
Oddness reduces the normalized convolution modulo eight to four times the
sum of its indices. The latter is four-periodic modulo eight, giving residues
1, 7, 5, 7 for successive positive indices.
