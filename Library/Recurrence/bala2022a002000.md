# OEIS A002000

The associated theorem proves Bala's conjectured congruence for every
natural `n` and every natural `r <= n`. The sequence is integer-valued
and defined by the NAME recurrence with initial value 7. Congruence is
expressed as divisibility of the integer difference by `3^(n+r+2)`.
Only the Conjecture line is settled; the Lucas representation, the 3-adic
limit, and the product formula are not claimed.
