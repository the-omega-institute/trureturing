# OEIS A380558

Paul D. Hanna's February 2025 entry specifies the integral formal series
`A(x) = Sum_{n>=2} a(n)*x^n` by `A(x - A(x)) = x^2/(1 - x^2)`. Its formula
field also gives `A(x) = B(x)^2/(1 - B(x)^2)`, where `B(x - A(x)) = x`.
These source formulas are literature-attested; the entry's parity line is
explicitly marked conjectural in revision 12, still live on 2026-09-28.

The linked [A004760](https://oeis.org/A004760) has corrected offset one and
begins `0,1,3,6,7,...`. Its positive values after `1` are exactly the integers
in `[3*2^j, 4*2^j)` for some natural `j`. Thus `k>1` in A380558 retains
the exceptional value `1`, yielding `n=2`, and the other odd coefficients
occur at twice those interval values. The upper endpoint is excluded.
