# A081459 Newton--Pell numerators

## Claim and scope

OEIS A081459 was authored by Amarnath Murthy on 2003-03-22. Artur Jasinski
recorded the recurrence `a(n+1)=2*a(n)^2-1` on 2008-10-12, and Ehren
Metcalfe recorded `a(n)=Lucas(3*2^(n-1))/2` on 2017-10-05. Consequently
`A081459(k+2)` begins `9, 161, 51841, 5374978561, ...` and is the layer
sequence for the indices `6*2^k`, for every integer `k >= 0`.
