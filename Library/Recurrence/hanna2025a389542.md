# OEIS A389542

Paul D. Hanna's entry, authored November 1, 2025, defines the generating
series by `A(A(x)^2 - x^2) = 4*A(x)^3`, with zero constant coefficient
and linear coefficient one. It states the coefficient conjecture quoted
above. The Lean module constructs the integer series by compositional
reversion, proves its equation and uniqueness, and proves both directions
of the mod-four classification for every index greater than one.

The integral inverse has the form `R = X*(1 + 2*H)`, where the
zero-constant correction satisfies `H = -X - H^2 - 2*X*H(4*X^3)`.
Degree contraction constructs H. Modulo two, H is the Catalan series;
the binary support theorem from
`D5/S1/Recurrence/Invariants/CatalanCompositionSquareParity` supplies
the powers-of-two support. Compositional inversion transports the
doubled correction modulo four to A.
