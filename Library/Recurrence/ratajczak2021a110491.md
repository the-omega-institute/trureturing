# OEIS A110491 and its T-transform conjecture

Lechoslaw Ratajczak added the following COMMENT to A110491 on August 4,
2021:

> Conjecture: {a(n-1), n>=1} is the T-transform of A093178, where T maps a sequence {b(n), n>=1} to the sequence {c(n)} defined by c(n) = det(M_n), where M_n is the n X n matrix with elements M_n(i,j) = b(2*j) for i>j and M_n(i,j) = b(i+j-1) for i<=j.

The source uses one-based matrix indices and positive orders. A093178 is the
sequence `b(r)=1` for even `r` and `b(r)=r` for odd `r`. Consequently the
lower branch is printed as `b(2*j)` even though its values are one. The Lean
definition retains that literal branch. A110491 is characterized separately
by

`a(0)=1`, `a(1)=2`, and
`a(m+2)=2*a(m+1)+4*(m+1)*m*a(m)` for every nonnegative `m`.

Thus the formal result at index `m` identifies the determinant of the
source's order-`m+1` matrix with `a(m)`. Neither the recurrence nor the
matrix is defined in terms of the other.
