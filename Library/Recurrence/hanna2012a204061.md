# OEIS A204061

Paul D. Hanna's entry defines a Pell-square exponential generating series
and conjectures that its coefficients reduce modulo five to the indicator
of indices with no digit two in base five. The formal statement reads the
exponential in the 5-adic field and proves 5-adic integrality and this residue
law; integrality over the ordinary integers is outside its scope.
