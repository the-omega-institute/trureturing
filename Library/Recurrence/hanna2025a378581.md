# OEIS A378581

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated January 8, 2025, gives the generating equation
and coefficient conjecture quoted above. The series has constant coefficient
one, and `a(n)` denotes its coefficient at natural degree `n`.

The theta series has coefficient one at degree zero, two at every positive
square degree, and zero elsewhere. The module constructs the unique integral
solution of the stated self-composition equation by stabilized coefficient
iteration. Over the integers modulo four, multiplication by two annihilates
the change caused by substituting `X*(1+2*S)` for `X`. Thus the solution has
the same coefficients modulo four as the theta series, proving both parts
of the conjecture for every positive degree.
