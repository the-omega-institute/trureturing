# OEIS A363560

Paul D. Hanna's entry, authored August 12, 2023, defines the coefficients of
the generating function by the cubic equation above, with constant term one.
It conjectures divisibility by three at every positive index outside the
residue class one modulo seven. The Lean module constructs the integer
series, proves its defining equation and uniqueness, and proves this
divisibility assertion. No nonvanishing assertion is made for the remaining
residue class.
