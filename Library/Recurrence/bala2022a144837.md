# OEIS A144837

The associated theorem proves Peter Bala's conjectured congruence for every
natural `n` and every natural `r <= n`, using his integer recurrence.
The extension `a(0)=1=Lucas(5^0)=Lucas(1)` gives `a(1)=11=A144837(1)`
and agrees with the OEIS domain `n >= 1`. Congruence is expressed as
divisibility of the integer difference by `5^(n+r+1)`. Only the Conjecture
line is settled; the Lucas representation, the 5-adic limit (A269591),
and A268922 are not claimed. Artur Jasinski authored the entry; Bala
authored the conjecture in the Nov 14 2022 block.
