# OEIS A220181

The entry gives the exponential generating function and offset-zero finite
formula quoted above. Peter Bala's June 1, 2022 comment conjectures pure
periodicity modulo every prime at positive indices, with period p - 1.
The conjecture does not assert that this period is minimal.

The formal sequence uses the finite formula. For any integer-valued weight w,
the sum of w(k) k^n k! Stirling2(n,k) has period p - 1 modulo p for n >= 1.
The A220181 formula equals (-1)^n times that sum with w(k) = (-1)^k.
For odd primes the period is even; modulo 2 the sign is trivial. Thus the
general theorem proves the quoted A220181 conjecture. The exponential
generating function identity is not separately formalized.
