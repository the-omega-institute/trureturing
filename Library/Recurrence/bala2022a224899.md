# OEIS A224899

Paul D. Hanna authored the entry on April 20, 2013. Peter Bala's comment of
May 29, 2022 states the prime-period conjecture quoted above. The entry has
offset 0,3 and begins 1, 1, 8, 163, 6272, 389581, 35560448, 4479975823,
744707981312. The conjecture remains labelled as such in the supplied entry;
no proof was located, which does not establish exhaustive absence of a proof.

Expanding `sinh(k*x)^k = 2^(-k) (exp(k*x)-exp(-k*x))^k` gives the finite
coefficient expression
`H(n,k) = (Sum_{j=0..k} (-1)^(k-j) C(k,j) (k*(2*j-k))^n) / 2^k`.
The formal sequence is `a(n) = Sum_{k=0..n} H(n,k)`. The e.g.f. identity
itself is not formalized. The module proves exact integer division through
the Stirling bridge
`H(n,k) = k^n k! Sum_{r=0..n} C(n,r) (-k)^(n-r) 2^(r-k) S(r,k)`.
Exponent subtraction is natural subtraction; terms with `r < k` vanish.

The bridge proves factorial divisibility and vanishing for `k > n`, so only
`k < p` contribute modulo a prime. For odd primes, inverting `2^k` yields a
fixed sum of positive powers and proves the asserted period `p-1`, without
claiming minimality. At `p=2`, period one is false: `a(1)=1` and `a(2)=8`
have different residues. The named claim `balaConjectureTwo` is the exact
period-one assertion refuted by `bala_conjecture_two_false`.
