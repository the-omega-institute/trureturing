# OEIS A378575

Paul D. Hanna's entry, authored December 1, 2024, defines the generating
series by the five-fold compositional equation quoted above and conjectures
that every positive-index coefficient is one modulo four. Here A^5 denotes
composition, not multiplication. The Lean module constructs the integer
series, proves its defining equation and uniqueness, and proves the
conjecture as the r=5, m=4 instance of a general shifted-iterate congruence.
