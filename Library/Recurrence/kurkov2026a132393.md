# Kurkov's full nested Stirling identity

## Verified locator

- URL: https://oeis.org/A132393
- Internal source: https://oeis.org/A132393/internal
- The internal entry was read on September 22, 2026. Its NAME identifies the
  triangle as unsigned Stirling numbers of the first kind, with row index n
  and column index k from zero through n.
- The exact May 24, 2026 FORMULA line is:

> Conjecture: T(n+m+1,m+1) = (n+m)! * (Product_{i=1..m} (n+i)^i) * Sum_{j_1=0..n} Sum_{j_2=0..j_1} ... Sum_{j_m=0..j_{m-1}} (-1)^(n*m + j_1 + j_2 + ... + j_m) * (Product_{1 <= q < p <= m} (j_q - j_p + p - q)^2) * Product_{t=1..m} (n+t-1)_{j_t} / ((n-j_t+t)^(m+1) * (j_t+m-t)!) where (x)_{n} is the falling factorial defined as (x)_{n} = Product_{k=0..n-1} (x-k). - _Mikhail Kurkov_, May 24 2026

The all-parameter domain is n >= 0 and m >= 1, with integer indices.
These are the natural well-defined bounds of the displayed sums; the source
does not print an additional quantifier sentence. The falling factorial is
descending. All denominators are positive on the summation domain.
