# Periodic shifts and the large Schroder diagonal

Kurkov's original natural array has P(0,m)=1 for every m. For positive n,
P(n,m)=P(n-1,m) when m<=n; otherwise
P(n,m)=P(n,m-1)+P(n-1,m+f(m-n)). The zeroth-row rule takes priority.
The conjectured sequence is P(n+1,n+1), beginning at n=0, with both
f(d)=d mod 2 and f(d)=0 if 3 divides d, 1 otherwise.
