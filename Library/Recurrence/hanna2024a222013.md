The formal equation uses the finite window `0 <= r <= N` at coefficient
degree `N`. Every denominator factor has constant coefficient one, and
division denotes its formal unit inverse. The summand contains `X^r`, so
every omitted index has zero coefficient at degree `N`. This gives the exact

Degree contraction constructs the unique integer solution. Modulo two, all
summands with `r >= 2` vanish because `2` divides `r!`. The remaining terms
give `C = 1 + X*C^2`. Thus `X*C` equals the zero-constant Catalan series modulo
two, and binary Catalan support gives `Odd(a(n))` exactly when `n+1 = 2^k`.
Since powers of two are positive, this is equivalent to `n = 2^k - 1`.
