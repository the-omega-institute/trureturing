# A000071: odd-base strong divisibility

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A000071, COMMENTS, Peter Bala, Dec 05 2022.
The entry's NAME is `a(n) = Fibonacci(n) - 1.` Its offset field is `1,4`,
so the first sequence index is 1.
The conjecture is quoted verbatim in `claim` above. The author field
attributes the conjecture to Bala, not the entire sequence entry.

The OEIS JSON source check on 2026-09-21 (Asia/Singapore) confirmed NAME,
the complete conjecture comment, offset `1,4`, revision 493, and
modification time `2026-05-30T04:28:26-04:00`.

## Scope and attribution

The full formal target has natural k, n, m, Odd k, n>0 and m>0.
It includes k=1, all index parities, and the primes 2 and 5. Natural
subtraction is faithful because positive Fibonacci indices have value
at least one. No offset change or additional hypothesis is imposed.

The bounded source investigation recorded in
[issue #9152](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/9152)
on 2026-09-21 inspected cited passages in arXiv:1701.06722v2,
2402.19301v1, 2408.14632v2 and 2503.11055v1 without finding this exact
settlement. These are the investigation's recorded findings; the paper
readings were not repeated during report and documentation preparation.
The checked OEIS revision still labels the clause Conjecture. The search
is bounded to the inspected sources and does not establish worldwide priority.

The external Lean search recorded in the same investigation found a
sequence definition in sequencelib and a shifted golden-orbit result in
rwst/lean-code, neither supplying the exact target theorem.
The implementation directly uses the verified frozen Lucas recurrence,
companion-power shape and determinant suppliers and the pinned Mathlib
periodic-point and prime-power divisibility contracts. The resulting proof
is a repository derivation of Bala's conjecture; Bala is credited with the
statement, and the proof uses the suppliers detailed in the problem dossier.
