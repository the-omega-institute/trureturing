# OEIS A372018

Manyama submitted the sequence on April 15, 2024. Mathar's formula dated
April 22, 2024 explicitly labels the odd-index equality as a conjecture.
The user approved reading the missing argument as n, consistently with the
offset-zero definition of A371364 and the displayed sequence values.
The formal statement uses that approved correction.

Squaring the defining equation and multiplying by 1-xA gives
xA³-A²+3xA+1=0. The constant coefficient 1 fixes the square-root branch;
1-xA has constant coefficient 1, so this denominator is a unit.
The repository constructs the series independently from this cubic.
The entry attests the problem, not the proof supplied by the Lean module.
