# Hankel transforms of three consecutive Catalan numbers

Bojičić, Petković, and Barry study Hankel transforms of linear combinations of
three consecutive Catalan numbers. The paper appeared in volume 54 of the
Hacettepe Journal of Mathematics and Statistics, pages 1470-1478.

## Relevance boundary

The paper is relevant source-family prior art, not a proof of A331474. Applying
the parameter constraints in Theorems 3.1 and 5.1 to match the first two
literal A331473 moments makes the corresponding formulas predict a third
moment of 9 or 10. The literal A331473 formula gives 12. Hence neither theorem
instantiates to the source used by the repository proof.

The repository proof still uses classical ingredients represented in that
literature family, including Catalan generating functions, continuants,
orthogonality, Gram determinants, and Hankel transforms. Those ingredients
remain literature-attributed; only the source-specific synthesis and final
resolution are described as repo-derived.
