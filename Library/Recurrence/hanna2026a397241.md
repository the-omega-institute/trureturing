# OEIS A397241

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated June 19, 2026, specifies the generating equation
and conjectures that every term is odd. The normalization is a(0) = a(1) = 1;
the integer sequence begins 1, 1, 1, 11, 191, 5279. Here [x^n] denotes the
degree-n coefficient of a formal power series. Only the oddness conjecture
is addressed.

The module constructs the unique normalized integer solution by coefficient
stabilization. For two series with constant coefficient one that agree below
degree n, the difference of their degree-n equation residuals equals the
difference of their degree-n coefficients. Over ZMod 2, the all-ones series
satisfies the same equation: central binomial coefficients are even, and
Lucas reduction gives evenness of choose(4m+1, 2m) for m > 0. Uniqueness then
identifies the reduced series with the all-ones series, proving the conjecture.
