# OEIS A378578

Paul D. Hanna's entry of January 3, 2025 defines the generating series by
removing all factors of three from each coefficient of `1 + x*A(x)^3`.
The constant coefficient is one. For positive n, the coefficient a(n) is
the three-free part of the coefficient of degree n-1 in A(x)^3.
The entry conjectures that a(n) has the parity of the ternary Catalan
number `binomial(3*n,n)/(2*n+1)` for every natural n.

The Lean module constructs the integer series by stabilizing successive
approximations and proves its defining equation and uniqueness. Removing
factors of three preserves parity. Frobenius and Lucas's theorem identify
the reduced coefficients with `binomial(3*n,n)` modulo two. Exact natural
division by `2*n+1` preserves this parity. The subtraction formula for the
quotient is stated only for positive n; the quotient at n=0 is one.
