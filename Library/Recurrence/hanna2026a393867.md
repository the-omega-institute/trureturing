# OEIS A393867

Paul D. Hanna's entries A393866 and A393867, dated March 29, 2026,
specify the coefficient equations and the divisibility conjecture quoted above.
Here `prime(n)` is the nth prime, with `prime(1) = 2`. The series `F` has
constant coefficient one and integer coefficients. The logarithmic derivative
sequence is indexed by `a(n) = [x^(n-1)] F'(x)/F(x)` for `n >= 1`.

The target is A393867's divisibility comment. Formula (2) of A393866 instead
prints `[x^n] A'(x)/A(x) == 0 (mod prime(n)) for n > 1`; its coefficient
index differs from the divisibility target.

The formal module constructs the unique integer solution to the defining
equations and proves A393867's divisibility conjecture. Binomial divisibility
makes each recursive correction integral, and the formal derivative power rule
isolates the logarithmic-derivative coefficient modulo the nth prime.
The companion `printed_formula_false` refutes A393866's printed formula at
`n = 2`: the coefficient of degree two in `F'/F` is 25, whereas `prime(2) = 3`.

The companion module `PrimePowerShiftLogDerivativeOdd` addresses exactly the
second comment, "Conjecture: all terms are odd." It proves consecutive
even-odd coefficients of F agree modulo two, then deduces `(1+X)F'=F` and
`(1+X)(F'/F)=1` over `ZMod 2`. This is a repository proof, not an attributed
proof from OEIS. Direct OEIS JSON retrieval on 2026-09-10 verified both NAME
lines, both conjecture comments, and the DATA prefixes. The explicit search
scope and zero-result arXiv/GitHub queries are recorded in
`Problems/oeis-a393867-all-terms-odd.md`; no priority claim is made.
