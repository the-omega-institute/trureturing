# OEIS A338040

Vaclav Kotesovec's entry, dated October 8, 2020, defines the sequence by the
exponential generating function quoted above. Peter Bala's May 31, 2022
comment states the prime-period conjecture. The asserted period need not be
minimal.

The entry's stated finite formula is
`a(n) = Sum_{k=0..n} 4^k * k^n * k! * Stirling2(n,k)`.
The offset is zero, with initial values `1, 4, 132, 11140, 1763076`.
The Lean definition uses this finite formula with Mathlib's
`Nat.stirlingSecond`; it does not separately formalize the exponential
generating function identity.

The proof restricts the sum modulo p to k < p. Terms beyond n vanish by the
Stirling diagonal bound, and terms with k >= p vanish because p divides k!.
Factorial-weighted Stirling inclusion-exclusion then expresses the result
as a sum of powers (k*j)^n with weights independent of n, including 4^k.
Fermat's little theorem, together with vanishing of positive powers of zero,
gives the conjecture. Induction gives every nonnegative multiple of p - 1.
