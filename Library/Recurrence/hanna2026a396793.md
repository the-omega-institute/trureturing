# OEIS A396793

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated June 7, 2026, specifies the generating equation
and coefficient conjecture quoted above. The normalization is A(0) = 0 and
a(1) = 1, with a(n) the coefficient of x^n in A. The listed terms, starting
at index 1, are 1, 3, -12, 81, -645, 5367.

The formal module constructs the unique normalized integer power series
satisfying the equation. For an integer series F congruent to X modulo 3
and with zero constant coefficient, its product F(X) F(F(X)) is congruent
to X^2 modulo 9. This lifting identity makes each coefficient correction
an exact integer quotient by 3 and a multiple of 3. Comparing a solution
with its truncated prefix then proves the conjecture for every n > 1.
