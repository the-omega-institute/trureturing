# OEIS A371364

The entry was submitted on March 19, 2024. Writing the inverse series as tB(t)
gives B(1-4tB)²=1-3tB and B(0)=1. The denominator 1-3tB has constant
coefficient 1. The Lean module independently constructs B from this equation,
proves uniqueness, and then compares it with the odd part of A372018.
The published entry does not prove that comparison.
