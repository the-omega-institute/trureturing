# OEIS A222014

Paul D. Hanna's comment of December 6, 2024 records the parity conjecture
quoted above. The entry defines the ordinary generating series by

`A(x) = Sum_{n>=0} n! * x^n * A(x)^(n^2) / Product_{k=1..n} (1 + k*x*A(x)^n)`.

At coefficient degree `N`, the formal equation is represented by the exact
finite window `0 <= r <= N`. Every denominator factor has constant coefficient
one, so division is its formal unit inverse. The explicit factor `X^r` makes
all terms with `r > N` invisible to coefficient `N`.

The exponent-parametric contraction contains this square-exponent term and the
triangular-exponent A222013 term as exact specializations. After reduction
modulo two, every term with `r >= 2` vanishes because `2` divides `r!`; the two
remaining terms yield `C = 1 + X*C^2`. The A222013 parity theorem then identifies
the odd coefficients with the indices one below a power of two.

The directed formal dependency is
`D5/S1/Recurrence/Parity/FactorialSquareExponentSumParity` to
`D5/S1/Recurrence/Parity/FactorialProductSumCatalanParity`, specifically through
`mod_two_identity` and `hanna_conjecture`.
