# OEIS A379204

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated December 20, 2024, specifies the bilateral
generating equation and the two coefficient conjectures quoted above. The
normalization is `A(0)=0`, `a(1)=1`, and `a(n)=[x^n]A(x)`.

For index `n=-r`, with `r>=2`, the summand is read as
`A^((r-1)^2-1) * invOfUnit(1+4*A^r,1)^(r-1)`. The index `-1` is the Laurent
term `A^(-1)`. After multiplication by `X*A`, this term contributes the isolated
`X` in `A=X+X*A*sum(bilateralTerm(A,n))`, and `bilateralTerm(A,-1)` is zero.
The sum at degree `N` uses exactly the finite window `-N-2<=n<=N`.
The order bound proves that every omitted summand has zero coefficient at `N`;
thus no bilateral infinite-sum operation on formal power series is needed.

The module constructs the unique zero-constant solution by degree contraction.
Modulo four, multiplying each summand by `A` and pairing indices `m` and `-m-2`
gives two copies of `A^((m+1)^2)`. The resulting equation implies `A=X` modulo
two. Doubling lifts equality modulo two to equality modulo four, yielding
`A=X*thetaSeries` modulo four. Hence the remainder is two exactly at the
positive-square shifts, and this classification implies evenness for `n>1`.
The companion remainder-zero classification includes the condition `n>1`,
since `a(1)=1`.
