# OEIS A122399

Vladeta Jovovic introduced the entry on August 31, 2006. Peter Bala's comment
of May 31, 2022 states the conjecture quoted above. The conjecture is Tier 1
by its 2022 date, independently of the entry's 2006 creation date. The cited
entry presents it as a conjecture without a proof note.

The sequence has offset zero and is defined by the finite sum in the claim.
The formalization uses Mathlib's `Nat.stirlingSecond`, proves its
factorial-weighted inclusion-exclusion formula, and reduces the sum modulo
each prime to the fixed window `0 <= k < p`. The resulting double sum is a
linear combination of powers `(k*j)^n`; Fermat's little theorem makes each
term periodic for positive `n`, with zero bases handled separately.

The result asserts that `p - 1` is a period and that every nonnegative
multiple of it preserves every positive-index term. It does not assert
minimality of that period. The window identity also holds at index zero.
