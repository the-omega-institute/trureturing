# OEIS A221077

The entry specifies the exponential generating function and records Peter
Bala's June 1, 2022 conjecture quoted above. Its initial data are
1, 1, 8, 160, 5888, 345856, 29677568, 3502489600, 544181977088.
The quoted entry retains the conjecture without a proof note; no proof was
located in the accompanying literature search. This is not a claim of
exhaustive absence.

## Finite formula and scope

Write S(n,m) for the Stirling number of the second kind. The Lean definition
sets a(0)=1 and, for n>=1, takes the following derived finite formula as its
definition:

    a(n) = Sum_{m=1..n} 2^(n-m) m! (m-1)! S(n,m) S(n+1,m).       (9)

The e.g.f. identity linking this definition to the NAME is not formalized.
The derivation below is mathematical prose, not an additional Lean theorem.
The formal results prove the period for (9) at every odd prime and refute
its p=2 instance. In particular, a(1)=1 and a(2)=8 have different residues
modulo 2. The claimed period p-1 is not asserted to be minimal.

For the derivation at positive n, put y=exp(2*k*x)-1. For k>=1,

    tanh(k*x)^k = (y/(2+y))^k
      = Sum_{m>=k} (-1)^(m-k) choose(m-1,k-1) 2^(-m) y^m.

The standard Stirling expansion gives

    n! [x^n] (exp(2*k*x)-1)^m = (2*k)^n m! S(n,m).

The summands vanish for m>n; hence coefficient extraction uses finite sums.
Interchanging the k and m sums leaves

    a(n) = Sum_{m=1..n} 2^(n-m) m! S(n,m) D(m,n),
    D(m,n) = Sum_{k=1..m} (-1)^(m-k) choose(m-1,k-1) k^n.

Multiplication by m, the identity
m*choose(m-1,k-1)=k*choose(m,k), and Stirling inclusion-exclusion at
exponent n+1 give m*D(m,n)=m!*S(n+1,m). Since m>=1, cancellation yields
D(m,n)=(m-1)!*S(n+1,m), proving (9) in prose.

## Formal period argument

Terms with m>=p vanish modulo p through m!, while terms with m>n vanish
through S(n,m). This gives the fixed window 1<=m<p. For odd p, 2 is a
unit. Inside this window m is also a unit, and
(m-1)!=m^(-1)*m! in ZMod p. Two applications of the frozen Stirling
inclusion-exclusion theorem give the fixed triple sum

    a(n) = Sum_{1<=m<p} Sum_{0<=j<=m} Sum_{0<=k<=m}
      (2^(-m) m^(-1) (-1)^(m-j) choose(m,j)
        (-1)^(m-k) choose(m,k) k) (2*j*k)^n                 in ZMod p.

Here negative powers denote field inverses. The coefficients do not depend
on n. The frozen positive-power period theorem applies to every base 2*j*k,
including zero, and proves equality at n and n+(p-1) for every n>=1.

The named proposition bala_conjecture_two is the p=2 instance for (9).
Its closed negation bala_conjecture_two_false supplies the refutation
certificate; the finite calculation is used only to refute this named claim.
