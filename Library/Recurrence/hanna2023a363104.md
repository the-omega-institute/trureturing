# OEIS A363104

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated May 21, 2023, specifies the generating equation
and conjecture quoted above. The sequence has offset zero and constant term one.

The formal reading first cancels the Laurent monomials at indices zero and
minus one, leaving `4*A` at index zero and zero at index minus one. The remaining
terms are power series. Pair index `m+1` with `-m-2`, for natural `m`: their
terms independent of `A` cancel, and the pair is divisible by four over the
integers. At degree `N`, the exact window is `-N-2 <= n <= N`; every index
outside this window has zero coefficient. No bilateral infinite-sum operation
on formal series is asserted.

Dividing the paired remainder by four gives an integral degree-contracting
iteration. Its stabilized coefficients define the unique solution of the
finite-window equation. Modulo sixteen, the terms linear in `A` telescope,
giving `4 = 4*A*theta_3 + 16*Q` for an integer power series `Q`. Cancellation
over the integers gives `1 = A*theta_3 + 4*Q`. The reduction of `theta_3` is
self-inverse modulo four, proving the series congruence and both coefficient
biconditionals at every positive index.
