# OEIS A369083

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated January 12, 2024, gives the generating function
and coefficient conjecture quoted above, with offset zero. The formal reading
over the integers is the cleared-denominator equation
`4*(A-1) = X*(5*A^2-rescale(-1,A)^2)`, together with constant coefficient one.
After embedding into rational power series this is the displayed OEIS equation.

The module constructs an integer solution by degree contraction and proves its
uniqueness. At an odd degree the coefficient of an integer series squared is
even: reduction modulo two and the Frobenius square identity show that it has
only even-degree support. This makes the coefficient division by two exact.
Eliminating the reflected series gives a quartic equation. Modulo two, it says
`F*(1+X*F)^3=1`. Thus `H=1+X*F` satisfies `H^4-H^3=X`. Factoring the difference
of two such equations gives a factor with constant coefficient one, proving
uniqueness among normalized series. Comparison with A380709 and the identity
`choose(4*n+3,n+1)=3*choose(4*n+3,n)` prove the coefficient conjecture.

The formal proof uses the frozen prerequisite
`D5/S1/Recurrence/Parity/AbsoluteReciprocalCubeParity`, statement identifier
`sha256:a7484b050055466f1a3091c41ffc3856281a6de69fd76b14ba71577ef1c07c75`.
Its integer generating series G satisfies
`G=1+X*absSeries(invOfUnit(G,1))^3`; its reduction modulo two has coefficient
one at zero and `choose(4*n-1,n)` at every positive index.
