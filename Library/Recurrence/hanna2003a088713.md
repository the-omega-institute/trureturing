# Hanna's quotient-composition source

Paul D. Hanna's entry is dated October 12, 2003. Its NAME reads:

> G.f. A(x) satisfies A(x/A(x)) = 1/(1-x).

Its generating-function example reads:

> G.f.: A(x) = 1 + x + 2*x^2 + 6*x^3 + 24*x^4 + 118*x^5 + 674*x^6 +...

The offset is `0,3`, the first coefficient is one, and the keyword `nonn`
specifies nonnegative coefficients. The formal interpretation uses a
rational power series F with constant coefficient one. The quotient x/F(x)
is X times the formal multiplicative inverse of F, and has zero constant
coefficient, so the substitution is defined. The right-hand side is the
actual unit inverse of 1-X. The theorem constructs the normalized rational
solution from this equation independently of the array, proves uniqueness,
and then constructs its unique natural coefficient sequence.
The other functional equations, reversion formulas, and programs in the
entry are not used as premises.
