# OEIS A397241

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated June 19, 2026, specifies the generating equation
and the two coefficient conjectures quoted above. Here [x^n] extracts the
degree-n coefficient of a formal power series. The normalized integer sequence
has a(0) = a(1) = 1. Its construction, generating equation, uniqueness, and
oddness theorem belong to `DiagonalPowerRatioAllOdd`; the present module uses
that same coefficient function and generating series.

Write G = 1/(1-x) for the formal geometric series and B = (1-2x^3)G. The
module identifies the generating series with B modulo both three and four.
Modulo three, extraction by residue class gives a contracting recurrence for
the diagonal residual. Modulo four, the identity B(x)^2 = B(x^2)(1+E), with
E = 2(xG+x^6) and E^2 = 2E = 0, gives five polynomial numerator states closed
under even and odd coefficient extraction. Strong induction proves the
required residuals vanish for every n > 1. The coefficient multiplier in the
uniqueness comparison is exactly one, so both reduced equations determine
the reductions of the existing integer solution.

Above degree two, B has coefficient -1. Consequently a(n) mod 12 = 11 for
n > 2, and reduction gives the two conjectured remainders. The quotient in G
is a formal unit inverse, not division of natural numbers.
