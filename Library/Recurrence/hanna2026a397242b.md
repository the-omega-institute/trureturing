# OEIS A397242

## ExponentialSquareWeightCatalanParity: Parity of every coefficient

Source scope: "OEIS A397242, o.g.f. A(x) = exp(x + Sum (n^2-1) a(n) x^n / n^2), parity conjecture".

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated June 19, 2026, gives the generating equation and
the first parity conjecture quoted above. The sequence has offset zero and
begins 1, 1, 2, 15, 244, 6420. The two conjectures modulo three are separate
statements and are not asserted by `ExponentialSquareWeightCatalanParity`.

The formal reading of the exponential equation is A(0)=1 and A'=L'A, where
L has zero constant coefficient, coefficient one at degree one, and
coefficient (n^2-1)a(n)/n^2 at degrees n>=2. Multiplication by X gives
X A'=M A. The integral normalization a(n)=n d(n) for n>=1 makes
M(n)=(n^2-1)d(n) for n>=2, with M(0)=0 and M(1)=1.
The Lean module constructs this integer solution, proves the differential
identity and its exact rational weights, and proves uniqueness among rational
solutions with that coefficient shape. Its public equation uses this formal
differential reading, rather than a separate exponential-substitution identity.

Modulo two, d(2m)=d(2m-1) for m>=1. The odd-index coefficients
e(m)=d(2m+1) satisfy the Catalan recurrence over ZMod(2). Thus X times their
generating series equals the reduction of the imported binary Catalan series
from `D5/S1/Recurrence/Invariants/CatalanCompositionSquareParity`.
Its binary support proves that a(n) is odd exactly when n+1 is a power of two,
including a(0)=1.

## ExponentialSquareWeightTernarySupport: The two ternary conjectures

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated June 19, 2026, gives the generating equation
and the two conjectures quoted above. The sequence has offset zero. The
exponents in both conjectures are nonnegative integers; distinct powers are
written with exponents i<j.

The coefficient function a and its integral normalization d are those of
`D5/S1/Recurrence/Residue/ExponentialSquareWeightCatalanParity`. That module
proves a(n)=n d(n) for n>=1, the formal identity A(0)=1 and X A'=M A,
the rational weights M(n)=(n^2-1)a(n)/n for n>=2, and uniqueness among
rational solutions with these weights. This is the formal differential
reading of the quoted exponential equation, with M=X L' and A=exp(L).

Over ZMod(3), put U(x)=Sum d(3m+1)x^m and V(x)=Sum d(3m+2)x^m.
The imported recurrence gives U=1+xVU and V=U-xV^2, hence V=1+x^2V^3.
Consequently T=xV satisfies T=x+T^3 and has coefficient one exactly at
powers of three. The series F=xU=T+T^2 also satisfies F=x+x^2 A modulo
three. Ternary coefficient recursion classifies F: its coefficient is one
at a power of three or twice a power of three, two at a sum of two distinct
powers of three, and zero elsewhere. The two supports are disjoint.
Shifting the index by two proves both conjectures, in both directions.
