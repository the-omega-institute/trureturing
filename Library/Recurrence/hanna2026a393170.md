# OEIS A393170

Paul D. Hanna's entry fixes `a(0) = 1` and defines the remaining coefficients of
the integer formal power series by requiring the degree-`n` coefficient of
`1/(A(x)-n*x)` to vanish for every positive `n`. It states that the
positive-index coefficients are odd exactly at powers of two. The module's
`h0 : constantCoeff A = 1` records this stated normalization; it is not an
additional restriction on the sequence described by the entry.
