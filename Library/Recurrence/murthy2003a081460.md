# A081460 Newton--Pell denominators

## Claim and scope

OEIS A081460 was authored by Amarnath Murthy on 2003-03-22. Ehren Metcalfe
recorded the formula `a(n)=F(3*2^(n-1))/2` on 2017-10-07. Amiram Eldar
recorded on 2023-04-07 the product

$$a(n)=\prod_{r=1}^{n-1} Lucas(3\cdot2^{n-1-r}).$$

At `n=k+2`, these formulas give `A081460(k+2)=F(6*2^k)/2`; combined with
A081459's Lucas formula, the product gives
`F(6*2^k)=2^(k+3)*Product_{0<=j<k} A081459(j+2)` for every integer
`k >= 0`.
