# OEIS A384267

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated June 19, 2025, defines the integer generating
series by coefficientwise absolute value of `1 + x/A(x)^2`. Its constant
coefficient is one. Conjecture C.1 asserts the quoted congruence for positive
indices; the quotient is exact natural-number division.

The formal series uses the unit inverse of its square. Degree contraction
constructs the normalized solution and proves uniqueness. Modulo two, the
shift `U = A + 1` satisfies `U = X + U^3`. Comparison with the ternary Catalan
series, followed by exact binomial division and Lucas reduction, gives C.1.
