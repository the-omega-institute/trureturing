# Hanna's A361552 partition-parity conjecture

Paul D. Hanna's OEIS entry, authored March 19, 2023, has revision #14,
January 18, 2024. The literal selected comment is:

> Conjecture: a(n)/2 == A000041(n-1) (mod 2) for n >= 1; that is, a(n) is even, and a(n)/2 has the same parity as the number of partitions of n-1, for n >= 1.

Its original defining formula is:

> G.f. A(x) = Sum_{n>=0} a(n)*x^n satisfies the following. (1) 2*x = Sum_{n=-oo..+oo} x^(n*(3*n+1)/2) * (A(x)^(3*n) - 1/A(x)^(3*n+1)).

## Classical suppliers

Euler's pentagonal number theorem is used directly through
`PowerSeries.WithPiTopology.hasSum_pentagonalSeries` and
`PowerSeries.WithPiTopology.hasProd_one_sub_X_pow`. The integer exponent
correspondence is `two_mul_natCast_pentagonal_neg` and `natCast_pentagonal`.
These are the pinned Mathlib proofs in
`Mathlib.Combinatorics.Enumerative.Pentagonal.Basic` and `.PowerSeries`.

Ordinary partitions are `Nat.Partition n`, not restricted distinct or odd
partitions. The product supplier is
`Nat.Partition.hasProd_powerSeriesMk_card_restricted` at predicate `True`,
from `Mathlib.Combinatorics.Enumerative.Partition.Glaisher`. The predicate
specialization is exactly the full finite set of ordinary partitions.
The geometric-factor inverse is the direct application of
`PowerSeries.WithPiTopology.one_sub_mul_tsum_pow_of_constantCoeff_eq_zero`.
The unique empty partition is supplied by `Nat.Partition.UniquePartitionZero`.

Mathlib's cited supplier files are authored by Weiyi Wang and released under
Apache 2.0. This source note retains attribution and short OEIS quotations;
it does not redistribute third-party source code. The proof applies the
classical identities without a claim of a new Euler or partition method.
