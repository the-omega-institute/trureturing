# Kotesovec's squared-Catalan Hankel limits

## Verified locator

The supplied primary-source audit read the live OEIS entries at
https://oeis.org/A277829 and https://oeis.org/A278770 and their exact
definitions. It reports these two conjecture lines:

- A277829, Vaclav Kotesovec, November 29, 2016: "Conjecture: lim
  n->infinity log(a(n))/n^2 = 2*log(2)."
- A278770, Vaclav Kotesovec, November 28, 2016: "Conjecture: lim
  n->infinity log(a(n))/n^2 = 2*log(2)."

For `C_m=binomial(2m,m)/(m+1)`, the one-based program for A277829 uses the
matrix entry `C_(i+j-1)^2`, while A278770 uses `C_(i+j)^2`. Reindexing rows
and columns from zero identifies these with
`D_1(n)=det[(C_(i+j+1))^2]` and
`D_2(n)=det[(C_(i+j+2))^2]`, respectively, over `0<=i,j<n`. The formal
determinant convention gives `D_r(0)=1`.

The second primary locator is https://oeis.org/A278770. This note groups the
two entries because the formal endpoint proves their two literal limit
assertions as one conjunction; it does not identify the sequences with one
another.
