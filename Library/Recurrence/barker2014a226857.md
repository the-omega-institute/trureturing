# OEIS A226857

The entry defines the numbers that are simultaneously sums and products of
two Fibonacci numbers. Colin Barker's 2014 comment gives the recurrence
conjecture quoted in the claim.

The formal sequence uses Fibonacci indexing with F_0 = 0 and the OEIS offset
one. Equal summands and equal factors are allowed, as in the entry's `%t`
program. The recurrence is proved for every n > 12 by classifying the member
set as `{F_k, 2F_k, 3F_k}` and identifying its increasing enumeration. The
empirical generating-function line is not separately claimed.
