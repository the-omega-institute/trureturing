# OEIS A396795

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated June 6, 2026, specifies the generating equation
and coefficient conjecture quoted above. The superscripts on A denote
compositional iteration, not ordinary powers. The normalized formal series
has integer coefficients, constant coefficient zero, and coefficient one
at degree one; a(n) is its degree-n coefficient. The OEIS offset is 1,2.

The module proves square-zero lifting for a variable natural modulus m:
if f is congruent coefficientwise to X modulo m and m divides i+j, the
product of its i-th and j-th compositional iterates is congruent to X^2
modulo m^2. It applies this theorem with (m,i,j)=(5,2,3). Successive exact
coefficient corrections construct the normalized integer solution of the
displayed equation. A coefficient perturbation argument proves uniqueness
and divisibility of every coefficient above degree one by five.
