# OEIS A319197

The associated theorem proves only the first COMMENT sentence: for all
natural numbers `n` and `m`, if `3 <= n`, then
`2^n` divides `Nat.fib (2^(n-2)*3*m)`. Natural-number subtraction in `n-2`
agrees with ordinary subtraction under this hypothesis. Because `2^n` is
positive, this divisibility gives the stated nonnegative-integral quotient.

The NAME supplies context, not an additional proved claim. The product
`A(n) = Product_{j=3..n} a(j)`, the `I(n; m)` factorization conjecture, and the
specific factors from A049660 and A253368 are not claimed.

Lengyel (1995), "The order of the Fibonacci and Lucas numbers", Fibonacci
Quarterly 33, gives the general exact 2-adic valuation `v_2(F_k)` as prior
literature; this module proves the divisibility directly by induction and
does not claim the exact valuation or priority over that result.

## Separate sharpness clause and the corrected normal form

The FORMULA of the same entry additionally says that there is no further
uniform factor greater than one, citing I(n;1)=1. This is a different
claim from the first COMMENT sentence above. Its literal n=7 specialization
uses the supplied offset-three values 1,9,161,51841,6989569 and the index96.
For their denominator D7, exact arithmetic gives F96=769*D7. Fibonacci
divisibility makes769*D7 a divisor of every F(96m), including m=0.
Thus the separately stated sharpness clause has a counterexample;
this does not invalidate the previously proved dyadic divisibility clause.
The corrected statement and its exact finite-prefix scope are recorded in
`LangDyadicSharpness.result` and in the existing target dossier's LS section.

The independently corrected layer b0=9, b(j+1)=2*b(j)^2-1 is already
literature-attested as A081459(j+2). Primary source: Amarnath Murthy,
OEIS A081459, https://oeis.org/A081459 . Its recurrence was entered by
Artur Jasinski in2008, and its formula A081459(n)=L(3*2^(n-1))/2 by
Ehren Metcalfe in2017. The associated denominator sequence is A081460,
https://oeis.org/A081460 . The corrected recurrence, Lucas interpretation,
Newton iteration, and telescoping product are not claimed as discoveries.

The ordinary character/rank follow-up uses standard Fibonacci rank and
valuation inputs; it does not give a Wall-Sun-Sun example. The elementary
correction concerns the supplied A319197 data and its explicit best-possible
assertion. It does not define the unprovided infinite tail of that entry or
