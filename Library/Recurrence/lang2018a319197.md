# OEIS A319197

The associated theorem proves only the first COMMENT sentence: for all
natural numbers `n` and `m`, if `3 <= n`, then
`2^n` divides `Nat.fib (2^(n-2)*3*m)`. Natural-number subtraction in `n-2`
agrees with ordinary subtraction under this hypothesis. Because `2^n` is
positive, this divisibility gives the stated nonnegative-integral quotient.

The NAME supplies context, not an additional proved claim. The product
`A(n) = Product_{j=3..n} a(j)`, the `I(n; m)` factorization conjecture, and the
specific factors from A049660 and A253368 are not claimed.

Lengyel (1995), "The order of the Fibonacci and Lucas numbers", Fibonacci
Quarterly 33, gives the general exact 2-adic valuation `v_2(F_k)` as prior
literature; this module proves the divisibility directly by induction and
does not claim the exact valuation or priority over that result.
