# OEIS A319197

The associated theorem proves only the first COMMENT sentence: for all
natural numbers `n` and `m`, if `3 <= n`, then
`2^n` divides `Nat.fib (2^(n-2)*3*m)`. Natural-number subtraction in `n-2`
agrees with ordinary subtraction under this hypothesis. Because `2^n` is
positive, this divisibility gives the stated nonnegative-integral quotient.

The NAME supplies context, not an additional proved claim. The product
`A(n) = Product_{j=3..n} a(j)`, the `I(n; m)` factorization conjecture, and the
specific factors from A049660 and A253368 are not claimed.

Lengyel (1995), "The order of the Fibonacci and Lucas numbers", Fibonacci
Quarterly 33, gives the general exact 2-adic valuation `v_2(F_k)` as prior
literature; this module proves the divisibility directly by induction and
does not claim the exact valuation or priority over that result.

## Separate sharpness clause and the corrected normal form

The FORMULA field additionally says that there is no further uniform factor
greater than one, citing `I(n, 1) = 1`. This is a different claim from the
first COMMENT sentence above. At `n = 7`, its supplied values give

$$
D_7=2^7\cdot1\cdot9\cdot161\cdot51841\cdot6989569
   =67205083036226688,
$$

whereas exact arithmetic gives

$$
F_{96}=51680708854858323072=769D_7.
$$

Proposition 6 of Jeffery--Pereira gives `F_96 | F_(96m)` for every positive
integer `m`, and `F_0 = 0` covers `m = 0`. Consequently `769 D_7` divides
every `F_(96m)`. This refutes the separately stated sharpness clause at
`n = 7`; it does not invalidate the proved dyadic divisibility clause.

The corrected layers

$$\ell_0=9,\qquad \ell_{j+1}=2\ell_j^2-1$$

are already recorded as `A081459(j+2)`. Artur Jasinski entered the
recurrence in 2008, and Ehren Metcalfe entered
`A081459(n)=L(3*2^(n-1))/2` in 2017. In particular,

$$6989569\cdot769=5374978561=\ell_3.$$

OEIS A081460 records `A081460(k+2)=F_(6*2^k)/2` and the corresponding Lucas
product. Together with A081459, this gives

$$F_{6\cdot2^k}=2^{k+3}\prod_{0\le j<k}\ell_j.$$

The recurrence, Lucas interpretation, Newton iteration, and product formula
are cited results rather than new claims of this repository.

The ordinary character/rank follow-up uses standard Fibonacci rank and
valuation inputs; it does not give a Wall-Sun-Sun example. The elementary
correction concerns the supplied A319197 data and its explicit best-possible
assertion. It does not define the unprovided infinite tail of that entry or
refute integrality at every later level.
