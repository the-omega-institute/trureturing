# OEIS A003319 and Kurkov's two arrays

The A003319 conjecture line is, verbatim:

> Conjecture: a(n) = A370380(n-2, 0) = A370381(n-2, 0) for n > 1 with a(0) = a(1) = 1.

It is attributed there to Mikhail Kurkov on April 26, 2024. The sequence counts
permutations of `{1,...,n}` which preserve no proper nonempty initial interval.
The formal subtype uses `Fin n`, so its interval `{0,...,k-1}` is the same
condition after translating labels by one. The formal `c(n)` is the actual
finite cardinality of this subtype.

The two referenced entries define arrays with natural row and column indices:

```text
U(0,k) = 1
U(m+1,k) = (k+2) U(m,k+1) + Sum_{j=0..k} U(m,j)

V(0,k) = 1
V(m+1,k) = Sum_{j=0..k+1} binomial(k+2,j+1) V(m,j)
```

These are formalized directly as `U` and `V`; neither array is used to define
the permutation count.

## Scalar recurrence sources

The supplied source audit read the complete 19-page author manuscript
`https://plouffe.fr/OEIS/citations/indec.pdf`, SHA-256
`ff0934dc8f7a404cfa2b01b698fbc9d5ced75a9087e14edb64df4be8f92ffb25`.
Its equation (2) is the first-block recurrence and its Theorem 1 gives the
equivalent positive scalar recurrence for indecomposable permutations. The
inspected manuscript contains no identification with either Kurkov array.

The supplied audit also read printed pages 10--12 of
`https://arxiv.org/pdf/0912.0573`, SHA-256
`f8c17f417b1b5239d8bce4ee5faf3fb02ced6137dbda5746fdb31d995af38ee6`.
Hetyei attributes
`c(n)=Sum_{i=1..n-1} i*c(i)*(n-i-1)!` to King and reconstructs the same
permutation decomposition. Substitution `i=n-j-1` identifies the two scalar
forms. These scalar facts are known mathematics and are proved only locally as
the bridge from the actual cardinality to the array argument.

## Source status and scope

The supplied audit read all 27 recorded A370380 revisions, all 15 A370381
revisions, A003319 revisions 413--462, the Sondow proof in the entry, and Barry
arXiv:1804.06801v1. It found no proof of either array identity in those
materials. Current conjecture labels and bounded search misses do not establish
novelty or priority.

The complete final Elsevier body corresponding to King's manuscript was not
directly inspected, and no manuscript/final equivalence or worldwide-priority
claim is made. The author manuscript and Hetyei establish the scalar
first-block recurrence, not the two array-border identities settled by the
formal result.
