# OEIS A003558 and Marcus's tent-map comment

The associated theorem identifies the least positive signed modular return
of 2 with the least return time of numerator 2 under
`tent N k = if 2*k <= N then 2*k else 2*N - 2*k`, for `N=2n+1` and `n>0`.
For `N>0` and `k<=N`, division by `N` identifies these two branches with
`x -> 1 - abs(2*x-1)` at `x=k/N`; natural subtraction does not truncate
the second branch in this range. The even orbit from numerator 2 remains
in that range.

Only the quoted Marcus sentence is settled. The entry's comments about
Kaprekar cycles, iteration of `x^2-2`, and the Kappraff-Adamson base
conjecture are not claimed. The offset is `%O 0,3`, but `n=0` is excluded:
then `N=1` and the starting point `2/N` is outside `[0,1]`.

Periodic orbits of the tent map on rationals are classical
dynamical-systems material, while this cycle-length identity was not found
stated in the searched literature described in the associated problem dossier.
