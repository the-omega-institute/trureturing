# OEIS A380708

Paul D. Hanna's entry, dated February 8, 2025, specifies the generating equation
and parity conjecture quoted above. Absolute value is applied coefficientwise
to the reciprocal series before squaring. The constant coefficient is one.
The fibbinary numbers in A003714 are the nonnegative integers with no adjacent
ones in their binary expansion.

The module constructs the unique integer solution by degree contraction.
Modulo two, absolute value disappears. If F is the reduced series and B its
reciprocal, then F = 1 + x*B^2 and B = 1 + x*B^3. The latter equation identifies
B with the reduced ternary series from StripThreeTernaryCatalanParity. Its
degree-f coefficient is binomial(3*f,f) modulo two. Frobenius gives
F = 1 + x*B(x^2). Lucas descent and the binary-digit recursions show that
binomial(3*f,f) is odd exactly when f is fibbinary, proving the conjecture.
