# OEIS A064618

The A064618 comment is attributed to Peter Bala, January 15, 2018, and is the
conjecture quoted above; the sibling entry A004123 carries its own note,
bala2022a004123. Both finite formulas use the Stirling numbers of the second
kind `S(n,k)`:

- A064618, offset zero: `a(n) = Sum_{k=0}^n (k!)^2 * S(n,k)`.
- A004123, offset one: `a(n) = Sum_{k=0}^{n-1} 2^k * k! * S(n-1,k)` for `n >= 1`.

The module defines `T(w,n) = Sum_{k=0}^n w(k) * k! * S(n,k)` over the integers.
It proves `T(w,n+phi(m)) = T(w,n)` modulo every positive `m` when `n >= m`,
for every integer weight `w`. Terms with `k >= m` vanish modulo `m`;
inclusion-exclusion gives a fixed sum of powers. Euler's theorem for unit
bases and vanishing powers for non-unit bases give the period at each prime
power dividing `m`, and prime-power divisibility combines the congruences.

The weights `w(k)=k!` and `w(k)=2^k` give the two assertions. In the entries'
own indexing, the proved onsets are respectively `n >= m` and `n >= m+1`.
Neither onset nor period is asserted to be minimal. The broader A004123
conjecture about every e.g.f. `G(exp(x)-1)` is outside these two assertions.
