# OEIS A395842

Hanna's entry dated May 20, 2026 defines a(n) as the coefficient of x^n
in the n-fold compositional iterate of the generating series of A177775.
That series starts x+x² and its coefficients are determined by equality of
the degree-n coefficients in the n-th and (n−1)-st iterates, for n>2.
The entry presents eventual evenness as a conjecture, with no proof attached.
The formal theorem treats every n≥2, including a(2)=2.

## Proof scope

The proof in DiagonalIterateEven is derived in this repository. Over the
field with two elements, it uses the compositional inverse H of X+X².
Iterate doubling and degree induction establish H's diagonal constraints;
uniqueness then identifies the reduction of the integer series with H.
No proof is claimed for the separate odd-coefficient characterization of
A177775, and no publication priority is claimed.
