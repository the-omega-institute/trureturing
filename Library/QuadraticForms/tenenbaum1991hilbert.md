# Hilbert inequalities for divisors

G. Tenenbaum, “Une inégalité de Hilbert pour les diviseurs”,
*Indagationes Mathematicae* **2** (1991), 105–114.
This is related literature for the treatment of divisor difference sums.
The rational finite-tent expansion and the two-block kernel criterion in the
formalization are repository derivations; no unformalized theorem from this
paper appears as an axiom or a premise.

The available source attribution identifies Theorems 1 and 2 and §2, Lemma 1.
This attribution does not establish an equivalence between those results and
the exact conjecture.
In particular, a Hilbert inequality or an asymptotic bound must not be substituted
for the strict threshold `n > 5040` without its precise constants and hypotheses.
