## Mathematical scope

The first assertion concerns every positive finite dimension, a real symmetric positive semidefinite Euclidean contraction, and a nonnegative Euclidean unit vector whose squared coordinates dominate the diagonal. Its errors are minima over all real coefficient vectors with support cardinality at most d. The second concerns every real Hilbert space and probability measure on its unit ball. Its barycenter is the Bochner integral, and the approximation dictionary is the actual topological support. The coefficients are arbitrary real numbers and support tuples may repeat. Its sum is an ordinary infinite series.

The source proposes both bounds; it does not attest their refutation. The equicorrelated matrix and finite atomic construction are repository derivations. Exact support, integrability, attainment and an eventual zero error tail are required to connect that construction to the original definitions. The source has no DOI. No worldwide priority or exhaustive openness claim is made.
