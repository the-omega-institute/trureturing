# An evolution algebra square table

Example 3.7 has the natural-basis multiplication
$e_1^2=e_1$ and $e_{i+1}^2=e_i$ for every positive integer i, with
$e_i e_j=0$ for distinct indices. In particular $e_1^2=e_2^2$.
This is an algebraic table; a complete Hilbert realization and a dense-square
nonuniqueness assertion require their own analytic and basis arguments.
