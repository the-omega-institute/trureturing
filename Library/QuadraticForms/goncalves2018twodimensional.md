# The two-dimensional algebra and its older classification

The algebra $A_{8,1}$ has natural basis $u,v$, with $u^2=u$, $v^2=u$
and $uv=0$. Lemma 3.15 writes an arbitrary natural basis as
$x u+y v$, $x' u+y' v$, with nonzero determinant and $xx'+yy'=0$.
Section 5.1 begins from arbitrary invertible multiplication-preserving
matrices and gives the full group $\{\operatorname{diag}(1,1),
\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)\}$ in characteristic different from two.
These are published ingredients, not new algebra or classification claims.

The cited passages do not assert infinitely many natural-basis classes
modulo permutation and independent nonzero rescaling for this algebra.
The Q14 application constructs and separates such an unbounded family.
This distinction describes the bounded source reading, not a global
literature census or a priority claim.

Related credited ingredients are arXiv:2303.00862v1, Theorem 26 and Remark
29 (the same full-group calculation), and arXiv:2006.14460v1, Corollary 2.13
and Proposition 2.16 (natural-basis block and extension structure). The
infinite quotient in arXiv:2505.14584v1, Example 6.3, uses a degenerate
algebra with an infinite full automorphism group and does not supply the
Q14 conjunction.
