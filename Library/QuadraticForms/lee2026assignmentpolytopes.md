## Verified locator

DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2611.12780

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12780v1

The final paragraph of §6.3 on printed and PDF page 34 asks “whether the
relation ‘rank at least d’ is of Latin-square type for all such m we leave
open.” The thresholds are $d=(m+3)/2$ for $m\equiv3\pmod 4$ and $d=(m+2)/2$
for $m\equiv0\pmod 4$. Propositions 6.4 and 6.5 treat $m=3$ and $m=4$,
respectively. The question concerns the fused relation on all symmetric
matrices over the finite field. [Lee v1, §6.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12780v1#page=34)

Section 2.3 on printed and PDF page 5 requires $n^2$ vertices, regular degree
$t(n-1)$, and exactly the restricted eigenvalues $n-t$ and $-t$, for positive
integers $n,t$. The restricted eigenvalues refer to eigenvectors orthogonal
to the all-ones vector. [Lee v1, §2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12780v1#page=5)

Schmidt's symmetric-bilinear-form valencies determine the degree of the
fusion by summing its rank/type classes. The universal positive-type
question is Lee's named assertion. [Schmidt, Proposition 2.4](https://doi.org/10.5802/alco.88)
