# Laplacian eigenvectors of generically weighted graphs have pairwise distinct coordinates

[Definition 1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.21979v1#S1.Thmtheorem1) requires
simple spectrum, nonvanishing of every coordinate of every eigenvector,
and pairwise distinct coordinates for each eigenvector orthogonal to the
constant vector. [Theorem 1.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.21979v1#S1.Thmtheorem2)
gives a nonzero homogeneous integer polynomial in the edge weights of each
connected simple graph on at least two vertices whose nonvanishing on the
positive orthant is equivalent to separation. The theorem uses the
combinatorial Laplacian. Its degree formula is specific to that theorem.

[Question 7.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.21979v1#S7.Thmtheorem5) asks:

> Do the analogues of Theorem 1.2 hold for normalized Laplacians, for adjacency matrices with a fixed support, and for directed graphs with a spanning diverging tree, the setting in which simple spectrum is generic by [4, Theorem 6.6]? The Krylov certificate of Section 3 adapts to any family of symmetric matrices; the construction of a witness is the part that depends on the operator.

The directed convention uses the receiving degree and complex right
eigenvectors. The generic simple-spectrum result mentioned in the question
is by Poignard, Pereira and Pade. [Cited paper, Theorem 6.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.01677v1#page=13)

The literature screen in the preregistration reports prior path and
bipartite arguments and a check of the cited directed result. Literature
priority and completeness remain unverified. [Preregistration](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/33474)
