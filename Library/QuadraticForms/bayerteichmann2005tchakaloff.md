# Common nodes for all specified coordinates

The specific primary version is [arXiv:math/0502473v2](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0502473v2),
Corollary 2 and Remark 2. Corollary 2 concerns a positive measure
on a measurable space, concentrated on a measurable subset, and
a Borel measurable finite-dimensional real vector map with
integrable first moments. The common positive nodes lie in the
specified subset. Remark 2 adjoins a constant coordinate for a
finite measure, preserving its total mass with the corresponding
one-coordinate increase of the cardinality bound.

The exact consumer is [VII, Proposition 100.5](../../docs/develop/theory/QUANTUM_ORIGIN_BILATERAL_POSITIVE_RESET_RIGIDITY_AND_SCALE_OBSTRUCTION_VII.md).
Use normalized Haar probability on the actual compact group $H$,
concentrated on $H$ itself. The coordinate map records the operator
$h$ on the original $n$-space and conjugation by $h$ on its
$s=n(n+1)/2$ dimensional symmetric-operator space. Its
$n^2+s^2$ coordinates are continuous and bounded, hence integrable.
With the constant coordinate, one common choice of at most
$n^2+s^2+1$ nodes preserves mass one and both operator integrals.

The same operator identity consequently applies to every original
face moment. The citation does not select a basis from a previously
unknown infinite face family, certify that family from samples,
or provide native preparations or legal randomization. It supplies
existential finite cubature for specified mathematical coordinates.
