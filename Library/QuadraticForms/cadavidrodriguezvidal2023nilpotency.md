# Equal-square orthonormal basis changes

Examples 3.6-3.7 exhibit complete natural orthonormal basis changes by
rotating equal-square pairs and normalizing. Equality of two squares makes
the mixed product cancel under a real orthogonal block. This mechanism
applies over complex scalars as well.
The displayed examples themselves have all square vectors in a proper closed
coordinate hyperplane; their algebraic squares are not norm dense in the
whole Hilbert space. The rotation method is a supporting ingredient and is
distinct from a dense-square counterexample.
