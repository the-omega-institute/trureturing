# Extremal graphs for a conjecture on the square energy of graphs

For connected unsigned graphs, Theorem 1.1 states that $s^+(G)=n-1$ exactly for
trees, and $s^-(G)=n-1$ exactly for trees and complete graphs. Conservation
and reversal of all signs transfer those results to the uniformly signed graphs,
and switching gives their balanced and antibalanced specializations.
[Source v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.17329v1)

Proposition 4.3 gives the exact normalized equality decomposition into
$((|B|-1)/|B|)^2 1_B 1_B^{\mathsf T}$ over the clique blocks $B$.
Proposition 5.2 uses vanishing at nonedges and commutation with the adjacency
matrix to exclude a bridge meeting a nontrivial block in an unsigned graph. The proof of
`HuHanSquareEnergy.result` uses the normalized matrix decomposition, extends
zero-sum data across all blocks, excludes attachments to a large block for an
arbitrary signing, and then determines the switching signs on a complete graph.
[Proposition 4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.17329v1#S4.Thmtheorem3)
