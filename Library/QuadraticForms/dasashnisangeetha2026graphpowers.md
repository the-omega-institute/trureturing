The source defines the positive integer power by joining distinct vertices
at graph distance at most the exponent. In Question 5.1 the degree matrix
and adjacency matrix are both taken after forming the power. The matrix is
real symmetric. The value is its largest real eigenvalue. The question
asks for both extremizers and cites Chen–Li–Wang–Meng for the case of
squares. Theorem 1.1 of the paper covers ordinary adjacency powers,
corresponding to α = 0.

The preregistration identifies the mixed range beyond squares and records
the bounded literature search and its access limits. The public settlement
records the independently replayed exact theorem.
