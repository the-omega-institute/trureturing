# Quadrics through general pairs and the whole entry locus

The primary text is [arXiv:math/0701889v3](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0701889v3),
the preliminary entry-locus discussion in §1, Definition 1.1,
Proposition 1.3(iii), and Example 2.5. The cited text is v3;
the bibliography year identifies the initial arXiv publication.
The field is $\mathbb C$, and the varieties in these definitions
are irreducible nondegenerate projective varieties in their given
embeddings. The secant defect is
$\delta(X)=2\dim X+1-\dim SX$; the preliminary discussion gives
generic equidimensional entry loci of that dimension.

Definition 1.1 gives the equivalent LQEL criterion that two general
points lie on a quadric hypersurface of dimension $\delta(X)$
contained in $X$. QEL instead requires the whole general entry
locus to be a quadric. Smoothness of $X$ is additional for the
word manifold. Lemma 1.2 explicitly assumes an LQEL-manifold;
its uniqueness conclusion is not used for a possibly singular
projective closure. Likewise the smooth-component clause following
that lemma has a manifold hypothesis, which is not dropped here.

The exact consumers are [VII, §§105–107](../../docs/develop/theory/QUANTUM_ORIGIN_BILATERAL_POSITIVE_RESET_RIGIDITY_AND_SCALE_OBSTRUCTION_VII.md).
Section 105 proves the general-complex-pair condition by finite-type
incidence from all actual real face quadrics, and uses the
preliminary generic entry-dimension statement. Section 106 proves
its own every-component smooth-quadric assertion from that incidence,
polarity and dimension bounds; it does not borrow a manifold
classification or infer irreducibility from the visible real sphere.

For the separate shortcut control, Example 2.5 supplies the Plücker
$G(1,5)\subset\mathbb P^{14}$ of dimension eight and QEL type
four. Proposition 1.3(iii) applies to a smooth (L)QEL-manifold of
positive defect and reduces the defect by one under a general
hyperplane section. Two such sections, with Bertini smoothness
and irreducibility, give dimension six, ambient $\mathbb P^{12}$
and defect two. This controls only the reduced complex implication;
no real compact affine orbit or whole original L3 cone for that
section is asserted.

These versioned primary passages supply definitions, generic
dimension and the example/section facts. They do not supply
smoothness at nonreal infinity, a component-count bound for the
original carrier, an invariant equalizing metric or physical
operations. This citation-only note reproduces no article body.
