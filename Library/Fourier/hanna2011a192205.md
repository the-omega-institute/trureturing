# Hanna's absolute coefficient root limit

The primary source is [OEIS A192205](https://oeis.org/A192205/internal),
by Paul D. Hanna, author line Jun 25 2011. The inspected internal-page
snapshot identifies revision 16, Aug 01 2013 18:16:08. The
[history page](https://oeis.org/history?seq=A192205) supplies a version
locator; a separately rendered historical revision body was not inspected.
The snapshot contains the following statements, attributed to Hanna and
the OEIS contributors under CC-BY-SA-4.0:

> a(n) = sum of absolute values of coefficients in (1-x-x^2+x^3)^n.
>
> Conjecture: limit a(n)^(1/n) = 16*sqrt(3)/9 = 3.079201..., which is substantiated by the observation that the sums of the coefficients squared in (1-x-x^2+x^3)^n equals binomial(4n,n) (cf. A005810).

Only the root-limit assertion is resolved here. The binomial observation
is neither an assumption nor a second result. The formal polynomial is in
Z[X]; coefficient absolute values are summed at indices zero through 3n,
including both endpoints, before casting the sum to R. At n=0 the sum is
one. The limit uses real powers and all natural indices tending to infinity.

## Proof and prior theory

The repository proof factors p(z)=(1-z)^2(1+z). For |z|=1 and u=Re(z),
|p(z)|^2=8(1-u)^2(1+u), whose maximum is 256/27, attained at
z=-1/3+(2*sqrt(2)/3)i. Thus M=16*sqrt(3)/9 is the maximum modulus.
The original evaluation bound gives M^n <= a(n). Finite Fourier Parseval,
Cauchy-Schwarz and the degree bound give

$$
a(n)^2\le (3n+1)M^{2n},\qquad
M^n\le a(n)\le (3n+1)M^n.
$$

Taking real nth roots for n>=1 and using (3n+1)^(1/n) -> 1 proves
the full limit. The integer-to-complex coefficient correspondence is
proved in the Lean module, rather than assumed from the source observation.
The Parseval supplier is
[`Polynomial.sum_sq_norm_coeff_eq_circleAverage`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679/Mathlib/Analysis/Polynomial/Fourier.lean)
in pinned Mathlib. The evaluation supplier is the existing
CountableHankelNegativeWitness.eval_bound, exposed without changing its
statement or proof.
