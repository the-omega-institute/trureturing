# The Laplace Transform

The DOI resolves to the De Gruyter/Princeton record of the book; its registry metadata lists 1942.

## Source statement

For a nonnegative locally integrable function on a real half-line, a finite abscissa of convergence of its Laplace transform is a singularity of that transform.

## Repository use

`D5/S3/Analytic/Laplace/NonnegativeBoundary.nonnegative_exponential_boundary` gives the boundary singularity in exponential coordinates for a general positive measure supported on the nonnegative real axis. Replacing the exponential parameter by the negative Laplace parameter gives the corresponding Laplace-transform statement.
