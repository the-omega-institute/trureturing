# A minimum problem for the Epstein zeta-function

The article is R. A. Rankin, Proceedings of the Glasgow Mathematical
Association 1 (1953), 149–158, available through the
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S2040618500035668). Its publisher abstract
explains that the variance of lattice points in a random circle is a
constant multiple of the Epstein zeta function at a particular point, and
states that the paper proves that the minimum there is attained by the
hexagonal lattice and extends the result to other real $s$. Cassels (1959) and Diananda (1964)
record Rankin's range as $s\ge1.035$.
