## Source claim

> Is F₁₁ congruent to a quartic polynomial times S_Z modulo the ideal I_Θ generated by the three-term relations, and can this be shown without exhaustive computation?

The final sentences ask for a proof directly in the polynomial ring and ask whether doubling covariance accounts for the factorization. The algebraic existence clause has an affirmative answer with a homogeneous weight-nine cofactor whose class is nonzero. A structural derivation uses the joint kernel of a trace coefficient and a dual-number coefficient. The character constraint alone is insufficient for divisibility. [Question 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24516v1#S8.I1.i1)
