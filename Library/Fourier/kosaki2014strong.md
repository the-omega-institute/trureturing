# Hyperbolic sine ratios and strong monotonicity

## Locator

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2014.07.021

Publisher locator: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2014.07.021

H. Kosaki, *Strong monotonicity for various means*, Journal of Functional
Analysis 267 (2014), 1917–1958.

## Mathematical scope

The supplied reading of institutional preprint PS2013-1 identifies
Lemma 2(i) and Corollary 3 with logarithmic integral formulas for
normalized hyperbolic sine functions and their ratios. Remark 4(ii)
records infinite divisibility. Theorem 12 in section 6 treats ratios
of two-parameter Stolarsky means.

For p at least one, put E(a,t)=sinh(at)/(at), with value one at zero.
The supplied application of Theorem 12 gives infinite divisibility of
B(p,t)=E(p,t)^(1/p)/E(p+1,t)^(1/(p+1)). The identity

$$
\rho_p=B_p^{2p}
 E_{p+1}^{-(p-1)/(p(p+1))}E_1^{-1/p}
$$

then supplies the density-balanced factor used in the Stolarsky
curvature decomposition. This does not assert positive definiteness
of the whole curvature defect or settle the matrix conjecture by
itself. The local Lean proof instead applies the compiled logarithmic
integral formula and nonnegative-density criterion directly.
