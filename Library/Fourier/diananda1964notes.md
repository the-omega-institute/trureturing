# Notes on two lemmas concerning the Epstein zeta-function

The article is P. H. Diananda, Proceedings of the Glasgow Mathematical
Association 6 (1964), 202–204, available through the
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S2040618500035036). Its publisher abstract
states the theorem: for $s>0$, $Z_h(s)-Z_Q(s)\ge0$, with equality if and only
if $h$ is equivalent to the hexagonal form $Q$. It records that Rankin proved
this for $s\ge1.035$, asked whether it holds for all $s>1$, and that Cassels
answered affirmatively and proved it for all $s>0$. The note treats two
lemmas used in that proof, in Cassels's notation.
