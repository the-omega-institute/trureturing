# Zhi-Wei Sun, OEIS A228884

The source is OEIS A228884, revision 17. Its comment states: “(ii) For any composite number n, all prime divisors of a(n) are smaller than n.” Its NAME is “Determinant of the n X n matrix with (i,j)-entry equal to the greatest common divisor of i-j and n.” Its Mathematica program is “a[n_]:=Det[Table[GCD[i-j,n],{i,1,n},{j,1,n}]]”. Both indices range from 1 through n, and subtraction takes place in the integers.
