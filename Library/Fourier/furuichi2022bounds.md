# Logarithmic means and hyperbolic positive definiteness

## Locator

DOI: https://doi.org/10.7153/mia-2024-27-27

Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.01134

S. Furuichi and M. Eghbali Amlashi, *On bounds of logarithmic mean and
mean inequality chain*, Mathematical Inequalities & Applications 27(2)
(2024), 379–399. The supplied source identifies Remark 3.2(i) in arXiv
version 2 with Remark 2(i) in the journal article.

## Source statement

> Thus these numerical computations support the conjecture that the function ϕr (t) for r < 1, is not positive definite.

Here ϕr(t) = t (cosh t)^(1-r) / sinh t, continuously extended by ϕr(0)=1.
Positive definiteness means that every finite matrix with entries
ϕr(x_j-x_i) is positive semidefinite.

## Scope

The source reports positive definiteness for r ≥ 1 and supplies numerical
obstructions at selected parameters below 1. The imaginary-axis method
uses an analytic extension to the disk of radius π/2; its values approach
zero on the positive imaginary radius, whereas analytic real positive
definite functions have values at least their value at zero there.
The analytic obstruction is a separate general result, rather than a
claim attributed to this paper.
