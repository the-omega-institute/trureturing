# A lemma about the Epstein zeta-function

The article is V. Ennola, Proceedings of the Glasgow Mathematical
Association 6 (1964), 198–201, available through the
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S2040618500035024). Its publisher abstract puts
a determinant-one form $h(m,n)=\alpha m^2+2\delta mn+\beta n^2$ into reduced
coordinates with $y>0$, defines $Z_h(s)$ for $s>1$, and notes that it
continues to the whole plane with a single simple pole of residue $\pi$ at
$s=1$. Montgomery's 1988 abstract lists Ennola among the authors who between
them proved the hexagonal minimum for every real $s>0$.
