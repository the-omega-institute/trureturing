## Source statement

> This family does not yield readily to our analysis. We have some evidence for the following:

> Conjecture 1. Let λ1, λ2, . . . , λn be positive numbers. The matrix [Sγ(λi, λj)] is in the class A if γ ≥ 2 but this is not always so when 0 ≤ γ < 2.

The source defines class A by positive entries and exactly one positive
eigenvalue. The continuous Stolarsky extensions at equal inputs and at
parameters zero and one are part of the intended mean definition.
Theorems 2.10 and 2.11 supply the parameters three and four. The
arithmetic parameter two is already covered by the earlier analysis.
