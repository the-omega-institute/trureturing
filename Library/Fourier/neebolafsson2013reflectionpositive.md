# Cauchy-mixture dilation for reflection-positive semigroups

Neeb and Ólafsson, *Reflection positive one-parameter groups and dilations*,
arXiv:1312.6161v2, [primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/1312.6161v2),
Example 3.4 and Section 4, give the one-parameter reflection-positive
dilation in which a positive semigroup parameter $\lambda$ contributes the
Cauchy spectral density

$$
\frac{\lambda}{\pi(\lambda^2+\xi^2)}\,d\xi
$$

and hence the Fourier coefficient $e^{-\lambda|t|}$.  The $\lambda=0$
component is the point mass at zero.  This note records only that mature
dilation intermediate.  The source-specific identification of the
parameter with the biased Fibonacci boundary operator $H$, the common path
law, and the time-zero observables is derived in
[Continuation XI, §§273--274](../../docs/develop/theory/FIB_ATOM_RECURSIVE_HOLOGRAPHIC_BOUNDARY_GEOMETRY_CONTINUATION_XI.md).
No native quantum preparation, Born rule, or operation contract is supplied
by the cited dilation.

The Markov and reflection-positive realization used alongside this
intermediate is Jorgensen--Neeb--Ólafsson, *Reflection Positive Stochastic
Processes Indexed by Lie Groups*, arXiv:1510.07445v3, Lemma 3.16 and
Theorem 3.22, [primary text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.07445v3).
