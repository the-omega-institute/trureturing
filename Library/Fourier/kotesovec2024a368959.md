# Complete monomial exponential sums

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A368959

The NAME field states:

> Numbers m such that Sum_{k=0..m-1} exp(2*Pi*i*k^s/m) <> 0 for all s>=2.

The COMMENTS field states:

> Conjecture 1: This is a subset of A001694.

> Conjecture 2: This sequence contains all the terms from A338325, plus 243, 972, 1944, 2401, ...

The NAME field of https://oeis.org/A338325 states:

> Biquadratefree powerful numbers: numbers whose exponents in their prime factorization are either 2 or 3.

These are OEIS field locators; the source has no page numbers. A001694 denotes
powerful numbers. Membership is restricted to positive integers. The ellipsis
in Conjecture 2 supplies no further quantified list beyond the four named terms.

## Mathematical scope

A prime dividing the modulus exactly once forces vanishing at that prime
degree. For a prime power p^(a+1), rational cyclotomic divisibility forces equality
of the coefficients at r and r+p^a for r<p^a in the power-map count polynomial.
Unequal fiber counts give nonvanishing. Prime squares, prime cubes, 243 and
2401 satisfy the resulting criterion; coprime products supply the family
and the products 972 and 1944.
