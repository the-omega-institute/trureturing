# On a problem about the Epstein zeta-function

The article is V. Ennola, Proceedings of the Cambridge Philosophical Society
60 (1964), 855–875, available through the
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100038330). Its publisher abstract
defines the Epstein zeta function of a positive definite binary form of
determinant one together with the hexagonal special form, and notes that
$Z_h(s)$ continues to the whole plane, regular except for a simple pole of
residue $\pi$ at $s=1$. This article is
distinct from Ennola's Glasgow lemma of the same year.
