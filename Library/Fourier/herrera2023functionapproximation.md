# Herrera's function-approximation conjecture

The paper defines
\[
E_f(c)=\inf_{g'\in\mathcal M_+^\wedge}
\left(\sup_{x\ge 0}(f-g)(x)-\inf_{x\ge 0}(f-g)(x)+cg'(0)\right),
\]
where \(\mathcal M_+^\wedge\) is the space of functions that are Fourier transforms of finite positive Borel measures on \(\mathbb R\). Section 5 uses the equivalent formulation in which \(g\) is \(C^1(\mathbb R)\) and \(g'\) has that Fourier-transform form.

The source states: “Let \(f\) be a non-negative function that is operator monotone on \([0, \infty)\). Then \(E_{f}(c) \leq Cf(c)\) holds for all \(c > 0\) when \(C = 1\).” This is Conjecture 2, numbered 1.10 in print. The paper proves \(E_f(c)\leq C f(c)\) with \(C<1.01975\) for \(f(x)=x/(x+1)\) using Gaussian approximants.

The conjecture implies the paper's Conjectures 1 and 3, which are commutator inequalities. A refutation of Conjecture 2 therefore removes that implication; the two commutator conjectures are not settled by this result and remain open here.
