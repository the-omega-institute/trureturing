## Mathematical scope

For every positive odd length and every nonzero binary mask, the source defines
the cyclic exponents from the word being tested and takes the integer trace of
the ordered product of the resulting 2 by 2 sign matrices. The question asks
whether the affine hull over the binary field of the nonzero trace support has
dimension at least one less than the length. Coincident cyclic indices at length
one, every word, and the literal increasing-representative product order are
part of the source convention.

## Source boundary

The source reports a weaker unconditional bound, finite checks and a five-bit
example. Those observations are context, not the formal result. The repository
theorem supplies the complete universal lower bound and does not claim that all
supports are hyperplanes or that the source's surrounding inverse-chi
discussion is itself a proved literature theorem.
