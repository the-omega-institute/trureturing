# Historical origin of the Dyson terminology

Physical Review 75 (1949), 486-502.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.75.486

The time-ordered series is a classical construction. The finite-dimensional
convergence and ODE facts used in the repository's discussion are supported
by Baake and Schlaegel, The Peano-Baker series, Section 2 and Section 3,
https://arxiv.org/html/1011.1775v3. Their equation (4) and following discussion
explicitly connect the Peano-Baker and Dyson terminology.

This historical citation is not a claim that Dyson's paper proves the exact
finite-interval Lean telescope, the independent Bochner simplex identity,
the empty physical-index boundary, or the computed full matrix-unit transport
formula. No Lean proof dependency or mathematical priority is inferred from
this citation.
