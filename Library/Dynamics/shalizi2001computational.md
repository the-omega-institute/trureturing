# Computational Mechanics: Pattern and Prediction, Structure and Simplicity

## Verified locator

Journal of Statistical Physics 104(3–4), 817–879, August 2001. Theorems 2–3 on page 15 of the author preprint cond-mat/9907176v2 establish the stated minimality and uniqueness. The preprint first appeared in 1999 and version 2 in 2000; the year field refers to the journal publication. The domain is stationary discrete-time processes over a countable alphabet. This source does not by itself prove minimality for arbitrary intervention systems or equality on probability-zero histories.
