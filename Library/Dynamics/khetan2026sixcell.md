# Khetan's six-cell orbit-closure boundary

Abhishek Khetan, *A Counterexample to Nivat's Conjecture for a Non-Convex Window of Full Affine Span*, arXiv:2607.09830v2, especially §3.5.

The paper leaves open whether every exact tiling by a six-point full-affine-span set in `ℤ²` has a nonzero-periodic configuration in the shift-orbit closure of the prescribed tiling. The eight-point construction does not settle the six-point boundary. The repository theorem `D5.S1.Dynamics.Symbolic.KhetanSixCellResidue.three_residue_column_dichotomy` proves only the conditional residue-propagation branch; the one-dimensional tiling rigidity and orbit-closure extraction remain separate obligations.

The current follow-up targets the finite section-cancellation classification: independent character cancellations force a six-point `2 × 3` product structure, after which the residue theorem applies.
