# OEIS A331859

The COMMENTS of A331859 describe the system:

> Suppose there is a block A of mass n sliding left toward a stationary block B
> of mass 1, to the left of which is a wall. Assuming the sliding is
> frictionless and the collisions are elastic, a(n) is the number of
> collisions between A and B plus the number of collisions between B and the
> wall.

and continue

> Since arctan(sqrt(1/n)) is approximately sqrt(1/n) for large values of n,
> a(n) = A121854(n) for most values of n.
>
> Conjecture: The values of n for which a(n) != A121854(n) is a subset of
> A331903.

The FORMULA section gives

> a(n) = ceiling(Pi/arctan(sqrt(1/n))) - 1.

the closed form of the collision count; its identification with the
collision dynamics is attributed to G. Galperin (2003), whose paper was not read
for this note. A121854 is `a(n) = floor(Pi*(sqrt(n)))` and A331903 is *Positions of
records in A331859*. The entry links the 3Blue1Brown videos by Grant Sanderson
on colliding blocks and Quanta Magazine (2020).
