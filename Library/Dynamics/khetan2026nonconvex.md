# The six-cell given-orbit-closure question

## Verified locator

https://arxiv.org/html/2607.09830v2

The arXiv submission history at https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09830 identifies
v2, submitted 18 July 2026, as its latest version in the metadata read on
12 November 2026.
The statements below are bounded to the pinned v2 text.

## Statement and exact scope

Definition 1.4 says that a finite window has full affine span when the subgroup
of $\mathbb Z^2$ generated by its integer differences is all of
$\mathbb Z^2$. Real affine span alone does not meet this condition.
Definition 1.6 defines an exact tiling $F\oplus T=\mathbb Z^2$: each site
has a unique expression $f+t$, with $f\in F$ and $t\in T$.
Equivalently, $\sum_{f\in F}1_T(x-f)=1$ for every lattice site $x$.

Conjecture 2 asks, for every such given $T$ and every exact full-span cluster
$F$, for a configuration in the shift orbit closure of $1_T$ which is again
an exact $F$-tiling and has a nonzero period. Theorem 1.1 refutes the general
conjecture at cardinality eight. Section 3.5 explicitly leaves cardinality six
open: the minimum counterexample size in that text is either six or eight.
This is a statement about the pinned paper, not a guarantee of contemporary
priority or the absence of other solutions.
