# Decision Making in the Arrow of Time

Physical Review Letters 115, 250602 (2015), page 250602-2, equations (1)–(6).
The publisher's full text identifies the forward/reversed path likelihood ratio
with the entropy-change functional. Equation (4) gives a mean sequential decision
time for continuous log-likelihood paths, including the excess term defined in
equation (5). Page 250602-4 discusses discontinuous jumps in the illustrative
chemical model and the resulting limitations of the small-error bound.

This is `literature-attested` background for path likelihood and sequential
decision times. It is not used to infer a fixed-horizon Bayes error from a KL
rate. The parity-kernel results instead derive their current formula and
dimension-dependent Poisson error curve directly. This note records no priority
claim for those deductions.
