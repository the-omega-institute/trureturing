# Length of Time's Arrow

Physical Review Letters 101, 090602 (2008). Pages 090602-3–090602-4,
the discussion after equation (13), equations (14)–(16), and Figure 2.
The paper defines time asymmetry using trajectory Jensen-Shannon divergence
and gives a rare-event mixture with large average dissipation but small overall
time asymmetry. It explicitly notes that average dissipation is sensitive to
unusual events and need not reliably measure whole-system time asymmetry.

This is a direct `literature-attested` qualitative precursor, not merely a
generic entropy reference. The parity-kernel addition does not claim the
large-dissipation/small-distinguishability mechanism as new. Its deductions
concern the specified stationary kernel, constrained optimized asymptotic,
fixed-horizon Bayes error curve, and exact rare-edge waiting law.
