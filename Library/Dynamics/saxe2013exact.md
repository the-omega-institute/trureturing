# Exact solutions to the nonlinear dynamics of learning in deep linear neural networks

## Verified locator

arXiv:1312.6120v3, pages 3–6. The first submission is 20 December 2013; version 3 is 19 February 2014. The decoupled exact solutions impose whitened inputs, suitable orthogonal initial alignment, and balanced scalar modes. The contextual-spacetime ML volume cites this as the deep-linear framework and proves its own synchronous finite-step identities; those discrete identities are not attributed to this paper.
