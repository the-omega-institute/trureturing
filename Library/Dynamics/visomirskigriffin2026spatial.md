## Biased lattice and spatial equation

Section 2, page 4: “If we replace each instance of $+1$ with $1 + a$ in the
interaction matrix $\mathbf{A}$, we obtain a biased Volterra lattice defined
by the system of differential equations,” and “Again, index addition and
subtraction is considered modulo $n$. We assume $-1 < a < \infty$ to maintain
the cyclic relation given in the graph.”

$$
\dot{u}_i=u_i(u_{i-1}-u_{i+1})-a u_i[C_n(\mathbf{u})-u_{i-1}],
\qquad C_n(\mathbf{u})=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}u_i u_{i+1}.
$$

Section 3, page 10: “Let $\mathbf{A}$ be the biased-cycle interaction matrix
(for an arbitrary cycle) assuming that diffusion is constant over species.
In one-dimension, Vicker's equation becomes,”

$$
\frac{\partial u_i}{\partial t}
=u_i(\mathbf{e}_i^T\mathbf{A}\mathbf{u}
-\mathbf{u}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{u})
+D\frac{\partial^2 u_i}{\partial x^2}.
$$

## Source statement

“Consider the one-dimensional spatial replicator, i.e., Eq. (6) with reaction
term generated by a $2n$-cycle with $n\geq 2$. Assuming periodic boundary
conditions and initial conditions of the form,”

$$
u_i(x,0)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{1}{2n}(1+\sin(x)),&\text{if }i\text{ is odd},\\
\dfrac{1}{2n}(1-\sin(x)),&\text{if }i\text{ is even},
\end{cases}
$$

“the dynamics will evolve to a frozen wave stationary solution. That is,
ecological niches will be maintained.”

The formal encoding uses `Fin (2*n)`, with index zero representing species
one. Its limit predicate asks for continuous periodic profiles different
from the homogeneous equilibrium and convergence in squared $L^2$ distance.
It omits the stationary equation, so it is implied by the source's stronger
stationary-wave assertion. The refutation at $n=25$ applies to every
classical solution satisfying the prescribed equation and data; it supplies
no existence theorem. The six- and eight-cycle simulations and the source's
separate travelling-wave results fall outside that refutation.
