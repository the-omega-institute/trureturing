# The information bottleneck method

## Verified locator

arXiv:physics/0004057v1, Section 3.1, page 9, equation (15). The manuscript is dated 30 September 1999; the year field and bibkey refer to the verified arXiv submission on 24 April 2000. The finite-alphabet objective is I(X;Z) minus beta times I(Z;Y), with a stochastic encoder and a specified joint distribution. This optimization does not imply exact predictive sufficiency, uniform intervention guarantees, or a unique general definition of intelligence.
