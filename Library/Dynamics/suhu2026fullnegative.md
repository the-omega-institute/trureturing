# Full negative regression and negative association

## Verified locator

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23313v1.

[Version 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.23313v1) gives the definitions and
the finite upper-event criterion in Section 2. Table 4.1 lists integer
weights in lexicographic row and column order. Propositions 4.1 and 4.2
establish full negative regression and failure of negative association for
the law obtained by dividing the weights by 99,999.

Lemma 4.5 gives the binary-by-chain argument with strictly positive cell
weights. Remark 4.7 on page 12 distinguishes the arbitrary-coordinate
minimum of the rectangular size from the minimum number of positive joint
atoms in the presence of zero weights.
