## Statement

Verified from the bibliographic record: the title of the paper names the
ternary operation $(abc)=ab^{-1}c$ formed from a group. The interior of the
paper was not read, so no theorem, section or page of it is cited, and no
statement about the converse construction, base points or commutativity is
attributed to it here.

Verified from a secondary source: B. Rybołowicz, A note on a free group,
arXiv:2101.04752v2, Section 2, defines a heap by the identities
$[[h_1,h_2,h_3],h_4,h_5]=[h_1,h_2,[h_3,h_4,h_5]]$ and
$[h_1,h_2,h_2]=h_1=[h_2,h_2,h_1]$, attributes the heap notion and the group
associated with a heap to H. Prüfer, Theorie der Abelschen Gruppen. I.
Grundeigenschaften, Math. Z. 20 (1924), 165–187,
https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01188079 (abelian case), and R. Baer, Zur
Einführung des Scharbegriffs, J. Reine Angew. Math. 160 (1929), 199–207,
https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.1929.160.199 (general case), and states that
choosing an element $e$ and the operation $[-,e,-]$ gives a group. The
bibliographic records of the 1924 and 1929 papers were checked against their
DOI registrations; their interiors were not read.
