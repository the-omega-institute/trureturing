# Chaudhary–Khandelwal on the Apéry spectrum at modulus kb

## Verified locator

[arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33046v1) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.33046)

The versioned PDF has SHA-256
`f236e1266f45aeaa25c968379b916283a218032d352298d32739d6c6263767c4`.
The printed page numbers below agree with the PDF page indices.

- §2, p. 5 defines the Apéry representative as the least semigroup element
  in its residue. Page 6 defines the two semigroups generated by the set and
  its reflection about the maximum.
  [Version 1, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33046v1#page=5)
  [Version 1, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33046v1#page=6)
- §3.1, pp. 7–8 defines the modulus, the two thresholds with the left
  residue taken modulo the modulus after negation, and the hole sets
  \(B_i\) and \(R_j\) on the strictly positive residues. The count \(f\)
  includes every nonempty closed interval contained in a hole set.
  [Version 1, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33046v1#page=7)
  [Version 1, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33046v1#page=8)
- §3.2, p. 8 takes \(f(B_i)\) for \(i\ge1\) and \(f(R_j)\) for \(j\ge0\),
  retains repetitions, orders decreasingly, and pads by zero. Its
  first index is \(1\). [Version 1, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33046v1#page=8)
- §8(2), p. 21 names \(\{0,a,b,kb\}\) with \(0<a<b\), coprime \(a,b\),
  and \(k\ge3\), at \(d=kb\). Here \(d\) is in both semigroups.
  The least positive right generator is \(d-b\), so \(b<d-b\) explains
  the printed remark that \(b\) is outside the right semigroup.
  [Version 1, p. 21](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33046v1#page=21)

The rectangular formulas and their correctness proof concern the entire
named family in §8(2). The source supplies the problem and spectrum
definitions. The spectrum computation uses the arithmetic hypotheses above.
