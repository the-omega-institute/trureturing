# One-joined path graphs

Section 1 defines a relabelled path by applying an arbitrary permutation to
the vertex labels. It joins the maximum label of the accumulated left graph to
label 1 of the right operand. The right labels are translated by the order
of the left graph. [arXiv:2611.34794v1, §1, pp. 1–2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.34794v1#page=1)

Section 3 opens with the two-vertex minimum of the cited operation and then
discusses joining the degenerate singleton. The singleton's sole vertex
carries both labels in the registered extension. [§3, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.34794v1#page=5)

Section 6 asks “Which trees can be formed by one-joining paths? Can we classify
those trees?” The paper's determinant and monoid results precede the question
and the same section raises higher joins separately. [§6, pp. 8–9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.34794v1#page=9)
