# Matrix ranks and ordered annihilator-digraph arcs

## Verified locator

Rasie Mekera, Defne Somer and Didem Yeşil, *Annihilator Digraphs and Extended
Zero-Divisor Digraphs of Semigroups and Rings*, arXiv:2608.09243v2 (2026),
§7, p. 17, ask “how the arcs of
\(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AG}}(M_n(F))\) can be characterized in terms of
matrix ranks.” The question appears in the paragraph beginning
“Future studies can characterize the semigroups and rings.”
[Exact v2 paragraph](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09243v2#S7.p2.1)

Definition 4.1 tests a distinct ordered pair of vertices by asking for an
annihilator of their product that annihilates neither endpoint on the
same side. The reverse arc uses the opposite product.
