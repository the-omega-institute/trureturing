# Validity and maximum violation of the entanglement-assisted quantum Singleton bound for linear stabilizer codes

Jae-Hyun Baek, Byung-Sun Won and Jon-Lark Kim use the symplectic dual
$U=\mathcal C^{\perp_s}$ of the sender's stabilizer space $\mathcal C$ to
specify distance. Section 2 takes the minimum symplectic weight on
$U\setminus\mathrm{Hull}(\mathcal C)$, where the weight counts nonzero
coordinate pairs. Degenerate codes are included.

Section 5 states:

> The code $[\![8,2,5;2]\!]_{3}$ attains this bound, and binary attainment is open.

At $k=\sigma=2$, the distance is $d=n-3$ and equality in the bound gives
$c=n-6$. Section 2 then gives $\dim U=n+k-c=8$ and
$\dim\mathrm{Hull}(\mathcal C)=n-k-c=4$. Nondegeneracy of the ambient
symplectic form identifies the hull with $U\cap U^{\perp_s}$.
