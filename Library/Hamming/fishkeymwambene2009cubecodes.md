# Binary adjacency codes of hypercubes

W. Fish, J. D. Key, and E. Mwambene, *Graphs, designs and codes related to the n-cube*, Discrete Mathematics 309 (2009), 3255–3269. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2008.09.024)

URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2008.09.024

Section 4, Result 2, attributes the binary adjacency row code's self-duality and minimum weight in even dimension to Fish and Key–Seneviratne. Lemma 2(1) gives the square of the binary adjacency matrix in every positive dimension. For even dimension it is zero. Since the adjacency matrix is symmetric, the orthogonal complement of the row code is the kernel. Self-duality therefore gives equality of kernel and image. [Full text](https://hdl.handle.net/10566/8519)

In the Lean proof, commuting coordinate flips give the square identity, and the coordinate contraction supplies an explicit preimage for every kernel word.
