# Felzenbaum, Holzman and Kleitman, *Packing lines in a hypercube*

The authors characterize the direction counts of pairwise disjoint cube edges. At the full total, the disjoint edges partition the vertices. The Lean statement represents this partition by a bijection between the even and odd profiles and records the direction of every pair. The Lean proof uses Hall's prescribed differences on the group of binary tails.
