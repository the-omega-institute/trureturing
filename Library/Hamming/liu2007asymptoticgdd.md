# Asymptotic existence theorems for frames and group divisible designs

Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A 114 (2007), 410–420.
The uniform \(k\)-GDD theorem is quoted as Theorem 2.2 of
arXiv:2611.34386v1. For fixed \(k\ge2\) and \(g\ge1\), it gives
existence for sufficiently large \(u\) with \(k-1\mid g(u-1)\)
and \(k(k-1)\mid g^2u(u-1)\). For \(g=2\) and \(n=2u\),
these are \(k-1\mid n-2\) and \(k(k-1)\mid n(n-2)\).
This existence theorem is external to the finite formalization.
