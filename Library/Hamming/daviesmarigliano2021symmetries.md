## Source statements

Section 4, Conjecture 4.4, PDF page 11 (Conjecture 9 in the HTML):

> If $G$ is an uncoloured circulant graph, then the number of orbits of the action of $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ on the set $V*V$ of unordered pairs of vertices of $G$ coincides with the number of distinct eigenvalues of its adjacency matrix.

The preceding definition on page 11 reads:

> By definition, these are graphs that have a symmetry which is a cyclic permutation of its vertices.

Corollary 3.3, PDF page 7, specifies the action:

> In particular, let $V * V$ be the set of unordered pairs of vertices of $G$ and $s$ the number of orbits of the entrywise action of $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ on $V * V$.

Its proof specifies that the number of elements in $V * V$ is $\binom{n+1}{2}$; diagonal pairs are included. The automorphism group is the full group, rather than the subgroup generated by a cyclic symmetry. The real symmetric adjacency matrix has entries one at adjacent vertices and zero elsewhere; distinct scalar eigenvalues are counted without multiplicity.

## Counterexample and scope

The thirty-vertex graph whose residues modulo 2, 3 and 5 disagree in exactly one coordinate is circulant. The Lean result proves at least eight unordered-pair orbits under its full automorphism group and at most seven distinct real adjacency eigenvalues. These bounds refute the universal equality; exact counts and infinite-family extensions are not separate Lean assertions here.
