## Verified locator

and adjacency as disagreement at exactly one position. Section V uses alphabet
sizes at least two. The paragraph before Section V.1 defines genuine
multipartiteness by the occurrence of every coordinate direction.

hypercube with specified edges*, arXiv:2404.03950. The internal binary
construction does not carry a novelty claim. The new result addresses the
complete mixed-alphabet statement. These notes paraphrase the mathematical
