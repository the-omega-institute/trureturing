# Near-maximum weak metric dimension of Hamming graphs

The source is arXiv:2609.32161v1, 26 September 2026. The abstract and current
HTML were checked on 28 October 2026; the submission history lists v1.

A weak k-resolving set S satisfies
sum over w in S of |rho(x,w)-rho(y,w)| >= k for every distinct x,y.
Here rho is Hamming distance, not the number of distinguishing landmarks.
For q >= 3, put kappa = 2 q^(d-1) and U_q(t) = q floor(t/2) + (t mod 2).

Lemma 6.3 gives the adjacent-coordinate frequency restriction and the bound
|T| <= U_q(t) for omitted sets T. Theorem 6.4 proves eventual equality
wdim_(kappa-t)(K_q square d) = q^d - U_q(t), under kappa > t, d >= 2, and
2(q-2)q^(d-2) >= d U_q(t)-t. Its construction uses simultaneous alphabet
translation orbits and an additional vertex for odd t.

Section 7 says that determining a sharp threshold for the nonbinary formula
in Theorem 6.4 is another natural question. The retained trureturing target
only concerns q=3 and t in {4,5}; it does not settle that whole nonbinary
question. The adjacent bound and orbit idea are attributed prerequisites.
The proposed improvement is the all-pair sufficiency estimate and the exact
least threshold for those two deficits. No global priority claim is made.
