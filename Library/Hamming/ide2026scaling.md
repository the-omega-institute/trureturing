## Source question

> It is therefore an open problem to find a necessary and sufficient condition for the coincidence of the scaling limits.

Issue #34418 specifies the full family of symmetric kernels, both endpoint eigenvalues and convergence along the full dimension sequence. The accompanying formal derivation gives the criterion conditional on the existence of either limit and an existence-inclusive criterion using the CT collision coefficients.
