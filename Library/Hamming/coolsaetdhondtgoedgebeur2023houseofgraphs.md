# Triangle-free cubic graphs on eight vertices

The two graph6 records are `GKh]@c` and `GLQkQc`. Their adjacency
lists give the Wagner graph and the three-dimensional cube up to relabeling.
A triangle-free cubic component has at least six vertices, so an
eight-vertex triangle-free cubic graph is connected.

## Verified locator

The catalogue's order-eight, girth-at-least-four file contains the two
graph6 records. The paper describes the database that hosts the file.
