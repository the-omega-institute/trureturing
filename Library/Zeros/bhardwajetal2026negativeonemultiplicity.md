# Multiplicity of negative one of independence polynomials of graphs

The introduction on page 1 assumes finite simple graphs and defines the
independence polynomial by counting independent sets of each size, with
constant coefficient 1. Page 2 introduces the notation for multiplicity at −1.

Question 5.8 on page 16 reads:

> Given a connected graph G on n ≥ 2 vertices. Is it true that
> mult₋₁ P_G ≤ ⌈n/2⌉ − 1?

The question follows Theorem 5.7's construction of realizable pairs of
multiplicity and independence number. Lemma 4.7 concerns the one-leaf
whiskering of the base graph. The three-leaf clique corona used for
Question 5.8 has 12 vertices, with multiplicity 6 and proposed upper
bound 5. The formula for its polynomial is a specialization of Gutman's
corona identity, as stated in Levit and Mandrescu, Theorem 1.2.
