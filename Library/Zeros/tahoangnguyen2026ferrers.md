# Ferrers graph stability

Question 4.1 on printed page 11 asks for necessary and sufficient conditions
on a partition with $m=\lambda_1\ge\cdots\ge\lambda_n\ge1$ for the independence
polynomial of its Ferrers graph to be stable. Definition 1.1 requires a nonzero
polynomial and puts every zero in the closed left half-plane. Definition 1.2
uses disjoint row and column sets, with an edge between row $i$ and column $j$
exactly when $1\le j\le\lambda_i$. Lemma 3.2 gives the independence polynomial
by counting the column-only sets and the sets with least row $i$.
[Versioned abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26962v1)
· [Versioned question](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.26962v1#S4.Thmthm1)

The arithmetic answer uses the unshifted polynomial of Definition 1.1.
The paper's well-covered sufficient condition appears in Theorem 3.5.
The pair-sum resultant coefficient criterion is the answer formalized here.
