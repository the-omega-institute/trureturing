# OEIS A133901

The entry selects from A128921 the decimal palindromes whose squares are not
palindromes. A128921 requires the reversed decimal representation of the
square to have a perfect-square numeric value.

The formal module proves that these selected palindromes are unbounded, which
is equivalent to infinitude over the natural numbers. It makes no density or
enumeration claim.
