# OEIS A284005: binary-weight products

A000120 is the number of ones in the binary expansion. Starting with
`a(0)=1`, A284005 multiplies one plus this weight along the positive
binary shifts. The NAME recurrence is stated for `n>1`; the sequence
data supplies `a(1)=2`, agreeing with its extension to all positive
indices. The initial terms are
`1,2,4,6,8,12,18,24,16,24,36,48,54,72,96,120`.

The binary weight is the frozen
`D5/S1/Digit/DyadicRowPolynomialRecurrence.wt`, reused by import.

## Scope

Only the quoted April 24, 2023 binomial identity is asserted. The 2019
bit-flip recursion, the other 2023 conjecture about the mod-2 binomial
transform of A329369, and the representation `A000005(A283477(n))` are
not claimed. The latter is retained above only as part of the verbatim
NAME quotation. Historical openness outside the searched surfaces is
unverified.
