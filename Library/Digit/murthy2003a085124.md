# Joint divisibility of an integer and its decimal digit product

For each positive integer n, the sequence is the least positive N for which
n divides N and the usual decimal digit product P(N) is a positive multiple
of n, or zero if no such N exists. Positivity excludes zero digits; decimal
expansions have no leading zeros. The domain includes n=1 and has no upper
bound on n, N, digit length or prime exponents.

## Scope

The source classification is existence exactly when all prime divisors of n
are at most seven and ten does not divide n. Well-ordering connects existence
to positivity of the source's least-or-zero sequence. The opposite divisibility
P(N) dividing N, studied by He and Sanna in arXiv:2403.14812v2, does not imply
this joint-divisibility classification. A bounded literature and Lean-library
screen found no exact resolution; this is not an exhaustive novelty claim.
