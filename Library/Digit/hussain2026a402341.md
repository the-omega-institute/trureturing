# The even-index valuation conjecture in OEIS A402341

Revision 13 of A402341 gives the central coefficient of
`(1 + x + ... + x^15)^n`, starting at index zero. Its coefficient formula
uses degree `floor(15*n/2)`. In the comments it identifies the conjectured
valuation at every even natural index with A000120 evaluated at `15*n/2`.
A000120 is the sum of the binary digits.

The formula section gives a central-binomial expansion from a Laurent
change of basis. The same basis is used in the formal proof of the valuation.
