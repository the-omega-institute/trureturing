# Seven and five versus binary carryless multiplication

A115770, revision 22, NAME defines the numbers k with
`7*k = A048720(11,k)`. The COMMENTS conjecture reads:
“Conjecture: also numbers k such that 5*k = A048720(13,k).
This has been checked for all k in range 0 .. 2^25.”
It is attributed to Antti Karttunen, Dec 21 2025.
The quantifier includes zero and has no upper bound.

A048720, revision 62, is binary polynomial multiplication over GF(2).
The binary digit of degree t is the remainder modulo two of the sum of
products of input digits of degrees i and t-i, for 0 ≤ i ≤ t.
All other multiplications in the conjecture are ordinary natural-number
multiplications. The row-five/row-seven conjecture in A115872 is the same
question, not a separate assertion.

The original locators are https://oeis.org/A115770/internal and
https://oeis.org/A048720/internal. The source entry records a conjecture
and a finite check; the finite check is not a universal proof.
No worldwide priority claim is made.
