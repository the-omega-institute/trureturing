# Hanna's three residues modulo five

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A365771

A365771 revision 23 contains the following name and comments; an OEIS entry
has no page numbers. A033042 supplies the digit-set interpretation.

## Source statements

> a(n) = binomial(2*n+1, n)/(2*n+1) * binomial(3*n-1, n) for n >= 0.

> Conjectures: given A033042 is the sums of distinct powers of 5, then

> (1) a(5*A033042(n)) == 4 (mod 5) for n > 0,

> (2) a(5*A033042(n) + 1) == 2 (mod 5) for n > 0,

> (3) a(n) == 0 (mod 5) for n > 0 except when n or n-1 equals 5*A033042(k) for some k >= 0.

The name of https://oeis.org/A033042 is "Sums of distinct powers of 5."
Its offset is zero and its initial values are 0, 1, 5, 6, 25.
Membership is expressed by all digits in `Nat.digits 5 m` being at most one.
Natural division and subtraction are used in the literal sequence formula;
the quotient is exact, and natural subtraction gives a(0)=1.

## Mathematical scope

The repository proofs use Lucas decomposition of the binomial product
`Nat.choose (3*n) n * Nat.choose (2*n) n`. Its residue is the indicator
of the base-five digit set, by strong induction through division by five.
For positive n the exact identity `3*(n+1)*a(n)=2*M(n)` determines the
residue unless n has last digit four; in that case a separate Lucas factor
vanishes. This proves the three conjectures and the complete positive-index
classification modulo five. It asserts no result for higher prime powers.

The preregistration literature screen did not find a prior settlement;
its completeness is ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED.
