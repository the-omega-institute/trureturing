# Binary carryless multiplier conditions

The name of A115801 gives the condition `9*k = A048720(25,k)`.
Its conjectural second condition is `15*k = A048720(19,k)`.
The quantifier is over all nonnegative integers, including zero.
A048720 denotes the two-argument binary polynomial multiplication table,
with binary digits interpreted as coefficients over GF(2).
The other two multiplications are ordinary natural-number multiplication.

## Verified locator

- https://oeis.org/A115801 (https://oeis.org/A115801/internal): NAME gives the first multiplier condition; COMMENTS states the conjectured second condition and the finite check, revision 20.
- https://oeis.org/A048720/internal : NAME defines the two-argument binary polynomial multiplication table over GF(2); FORMULA gives its binary exclusive-OR recursion; COMMENTS, revision 62.

The reported finite check in A115801 is distinct from the universal assertion.
