# Murthy's decimal prefix and suffix conjecture

## Verified locator

URL: https://oeis.org/A077739

[OEIS A077739](https://oeis.org/A077739), revision 5, has NAME:

> Smallest multiple of n beginning and ending in n and having a digit sum of n, or 0 if no such number exists.

Its COMMENTS states:

> Conjecture: a(n) = 0 if and only if n is 11, 17 or 19.

The AUTHOR field attributes the entry to Amarnath Murthy, November 20,
2002. These fields are available in the entry's `/internal` view. The
sequence starts at index one. The ordinary decimal expansion of a positive
integer has no leading zeros; interior zeros and overlap of the prescribed
prefix and suffix are allowed. The assertion has no bound on the value or
length of a candidate multiple. The source's finite data does not prove
its universal conjecture.

The source supplies the sequence and conjecture. The repository supplies
the proof, through decimal-block digit sums, a finite certificate for the
remaining small indices, and a symbolic construction for every index at
least 1000. The corresponding problem is
[the full zero-index classification](../../Problems/oeis-a077739-murthy-zero-indices.md).
The proof is repository-derived with this source acknowledged; the source
is not credited with an already published proof of the conjecture.
