## Interpretation and scope

Occurrences overlap: `cn(7,1)=3`, `cn(7,2)=2`, and `cn(7,3)=1`, giving
`a(7)=5`. Only Kurkov's quoted `%F` identity is the conclusion at issue.
At zero both finite index ranges are empty and the identity is `0=0`.

For positive `m`, the supplied A035263 valuation characterization says
`A035263(m)=1` exactly when `v_2(m)` is even, or equivalently
`1-A035263(m)=[v_2(m) odd]`. Thus the A329320 sum becomes a cardinality
of suffixes with odd valuation. This interpretation is not a separate
formal proof from the morphism defining A035263. The supplied A329320
attribution is Mikhail Kurkov, Nov 10 2019, with `[verification needed]`.
Historical openness outside the checked surfaces is unverified; no
exhaustive literature search or mathematical priority is claimed.
