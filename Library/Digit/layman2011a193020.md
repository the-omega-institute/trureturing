# OEIS A193020

For positive n, the published sequence a(n) counts the distinct output words
obtained by order-preserving interleavings of the ordinary binary expansion of n
with itself; different interleaving schedules that give the same word are counted
once. The formal definitions are total on natural inputs: binaryWord uses the
empty list at n=0, and distinctSelfShuffleCount counts its distinct self-shuffles.
This zero-input convention extends beyond the source's positive input domain.

The separate source assertions used here are the eventual identities
`a(2^k+1)=k^2` and `a(2^k-4)=k*(k-1)/2`, each with the source's
sufficiently-large lower-bound caveat. Each remains a distinct assertion.
The quotient in the minus-four identity is natural-number division.
