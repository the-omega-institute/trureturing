# OEIS A193020

For positive n, the published sequence a(n) counts the distinct output words
obtained by order-preserving interleavings of the ordinary binary expansion of n
with itself; different interleaving schedules that give the same word are counted
once. The formal definitions are total on natural inputs: binaryWord uses the
empty list at n=0, and distinctSelfShuffleCount counts its distinct self-shuffles.
The OEIS entry itself includes a(0). Only the selected eventual events here
use positive inputs; the formal empty-list convention at zero is not needed
for either selected event and is not asserted to be the source's convention.

The separate source assertions used here are the eventual identities
`a(2^k+1)=k^2` and `a(2^k-4)=k*(k-1)/2`, each with the source's
sufficiently-large lower-bound caveat. Each remains a distinct assertion.
The quotient in the minus-four identity is natural-number division.

## Scope of the source and proof

The count definition and the two separately selected eventual assertions are
attributed to John W. Layman and the OEIS contributors. The support proofs
are repository results, with acknowledgement of that source, rather than
proofs supplied by the entry. The lower-bound caveat is retained; the
repository proofs supply K=1 for plus one and K=3 for minus four.

The bounded literature search identified no exact whole-answer hit in its
inspected scope. This is not a certificate of worldwide openness, absence
of prior proofs or priority. Restivo's full chapter, the Li-Qin and Guo-Xie
journal bodies, uninspected Datko-Pawlik tables and displayed lists, remaining
citation branches, and broader or unindexed work were not read. Weighted
shuffle coefficients count multiplicities and do not by themselves supply
the distinct-support argument used here. No other A193020 identity is
resolved by these two claims.
