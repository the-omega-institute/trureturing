# Dynamical directions in numeration

Barat, Berthé, Liardet, and Thuswaldner survey numeration systems and their
associated compactifications, odometers, and G-scales. The article appeared in
*Annales de l'Institut Fourier* **56**(7), 2006, pages 1987–2092. The orchestrator
verified these bibliographic details and the abstract's scope from the NUMDAM
record page.

A quality review seat reported that the article treats the same Zeckendorf
compactification and states that ordinary addition does not extend continuously
to it, using the same two alternating boundary points. This specific passage
remains seat-reported: the orchestrator could not reproduce it because the
publisher PDF at IRIF returns HTTP 403, and the NUMDAM PDF has no extractable
text layer for the tools on this host. Neither the passage nor the alternating
cluster-point argument is recorded here as independently verified.

The repository theorem rules out every separately continuous binary operation
agreeing with finite addition. No claim is made that this stronger statement
has been verified in the published article.
