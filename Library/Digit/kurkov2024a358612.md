# Binary row indices in OEIS A358612

Mikhail Kurkov's A358612 describes extended Stirling numbers whose row
recursion follows the binary expansion of the row index. The final FORMULA
line, attributed to Kurkov on June 21, 2024, states:

> Conjecture: T(n, k) = (k-1)^g(n)*T(h(n), k-1) + k^(g(n)+1)*T(h(n), k) for n > 0, k > 1 with T(n, 1) = T(0, 2) = 1 where g(n) = A007814(n) and where h(n) = A025480(n-1).

The positive-column initial values are T(n,1)=1 for all n>=0,
T(0,2)=1 and T(0,k)=0 for k>=3. The source's scalar PARI program
specifies the latter zero extension. Its odd recursion is
T(2m+1,k)=k*T(m,k)+T(m,k-1) for m>=0,k>=2. Its even recursion is
T(2m,k)=k*T(m,k)+T(m,k-1)-(T(2m,k-1)+T(m,k-1))/(k-1)
for m>=1,k>=2. Values, coefficients and division are interpreted in Q.

For n>0, g(n) is the exponent of two dividing n and
h(n)=(n/2^g(n)-1)/2 is the natural parent index. The characterization
A025480(j)=(A000265(j+1)-1)/2 identifies this parent with A025480(n-1).
The formal totalization sets g(0)=h(0)=0 and T(n,0)=0. The source
recurrences and block identity never use column zero.

## The row-edge question

The same immutable A358612 source states, inside its Conjectured formulas block:

> T(n, A000120(n) + 2) = A341392(n) for n >= 0.

Its author is Mikhail Kurkov. The assertion includes the initial row and all
binary patterns. The source scalar PARI definition, restricted to k>0,
is exactly the recursion above: its n=0 branch is one for k=1,2 and zero
for k>=3; its k=1 branch is one for positive n; otherwise it divides n
by two and applies the odd or even clause. The separate vector program's
truncation is not an assumption of the formal scalar definition.

[A341392, revision 56](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oeis/oeisdata/d8ad22d9b98d2c427be8c6cd9f2672c600b8e3ae/seq/A341/A341392.seq)
has NAME:

> a(n) = A284005(n) / (1 + A000120(n))!.

Here its a is the rational quotient; in the Lean edge theorem the name a
instead denotes the numerator A284005, at its existing source owner
`D5/S1/Digit/KurkovWeightProductBinomial`.
[A284005, revision 106](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oeis/oeisdata/d8ad22d9b98d2c427be8c6cd9f2672c600b8e3ae/seq/A284/A284005.seq),
authored by Antti Karttunen, gives a(0)=1, a(1)=2 in its data and
NAME recurrence a(n)=(1+A000120(n))*a(floor(n/2)) for n>1.
The formal successor recurrence also gives the data value at one.
Thus preregistration notation P is literally the existing Lean a, not a
new sequence or an independently assumed edge formula. All quotient
arithmetic in the edge theorem is in Q; factorial and binary weight
are natural-valued. Integrality is not asserted.

The row-edge proof uses the original block law, proves vanishing strictly
above the weight edge, and combines the existing odd-parent product law
with factorial recursion. Ehrenborg and Steingrimsson's published
excedance-set recurrences (DOI 10.1006/aama.1999.0671) and the Alekseyev
formulas quoted by OEIS A329369 are known prerequisites or related
results, not claimed as new mathematics here. They alone do not establish
an identification of arbitrary literal scalar T coefficients. The complete
A329369 transform conjecture is outside the row-edge statement. Bounded
literature coverage and unresolved access limits are stated in the
[problem dossier](../../Problems/oeis-a358612-row-edge.md); no worldwide
novelty or priority claim is made.
