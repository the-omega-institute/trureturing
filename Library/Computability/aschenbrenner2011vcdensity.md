# Presburger finite traces

A fixed Presburger formula has polynomially bounded finite traces. The paper's
quasi-o-minimality theorem and ordered-group corollary provide the broader
model-theoretic setting. VC density and VC dimension are distinct quantities;
a bound on the former does not assert the same numerical bound on the latter.

The separated-atom proof uses finite threshold nesting, fixed source residues,
and Boolean product upper bounds. Arbitrary source chart maps can pull back
traces without being definable or injective. The paper does not state the
native-tree descriptor obstruction or establish any native controller
undecidability claim.
