# Quantum nonlocality without entanglement and state discrimination measures

S. Murshid, T. Gupta, V. Russo and S. Bandyopadhyay, arXiv:2506.20560v3 [quant-ph]; Quantum 10, 2174 (2026). Quotations are from the v3 TeX source.

The global and local optima:

> Let $f_{_{G}}\left(\E_{\psi}\right)$ and $f_{_{L}}\left(\E_{\psi}\right)$ be the respective global and local optimum, defined as $f_{_{G}}\left(\E_{\psi}\right) =\sup_{\M}f\left(\E_{\psi};\M\right)$ and $f_{_{L}}\left(\E_{\psi}\right) =\sup_{\M\in\text{LOCC}}f\left(\E_{\psi};\M\right).$

The states and the ensemble:

> Consider a set of $N$ linearly independent states in $\mathbb{C}^{N}$ with equal and real pairwise overlaps, $\S_{N} =\left\{ |\psi_{i}\rangle\in\mathbb{C}^{N}:i=1,2,\dots,N;\langle\psi_{i}|\psi_{j}\rangle=s\in(0,1)\ \text{for }i\neq j\right\}$.

> $\mathcal{T}_{N,k} =\left\{ |\psi_{i_{1}}\rangle\otimes|\psi_{i_{2}}\rangle\otimes\cdots\otimes|\psi_{i_{k}}\rangle:i_{1},i_{2},\dots,i_{k}\in\{1,\dots,N\};i_{a}\neq i_{b}\ \forall a\neq b\right\}$

> $\mathcal{E}_{N,k} =\left\{ \left(\frac{1}{^{N}P_{k}},|\psi_{i_{1}}\rangle\otimes\cdots\otimes|\psi_{i_{k}}\rangle\right):|\psi_{i_{j}}\rangle\in \S_{N}\right\}$
