# Chiral Symmetries and Multiparticle Entanglement

S. Denker, S. Imai and O. Gühne, arXiv:2506.15609v1 (2025-06-18). Quotations are from the v1 TeX source.

The geometric measure:

> The quantity $G(\ket{\psi})$ is known as the \textit{geometric measure of entanglement} and can be directly extended to the multiparticle case

> particles defined as $G(\ket{\psi}) = 1 - \max_{\ket{abc}} |\braket{abc}{\psi}|^2$.

The flip-conjugate symmetric subspace:

> \ket{\phi_n} &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{d+1}}
> \left (z_d^*\sum_{i=0}^{d-1}\ket{ini}+z_d\sum_{i=0}^{d-1}\ket{iin} \right)

> with $z_d=\nicefrac{1}{2}(1+\mathbf{i}\sqrt{({d+1})/({d-1})})$. Analogously, the complex conjugate vectors span a flip-conjugate symmetric subspace as well (denoted $\mathcal{H}_{\bar{\mathcal{I}}}$).

The conjecture:

> \textbf{Conjecture:}\textit{
> The geometric measure of entanglement for any state in the flip-conjugate symmetric subspace $\mathcal{H_I}$ (and respectively $\mathcal{H}_{\bar{\mathcal{I}}}$) is given by
> G(\ket{\psi}) = 1-\frac{d^2}{(d^2-1)(d+1)},
> where $d$ denotes the local dimension, which coincides with the dimension $\dim(\mathcal{H}_\mathcal{I})$ of the subspace.
> }
