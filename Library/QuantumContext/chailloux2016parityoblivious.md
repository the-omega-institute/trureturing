# Prior upper obstruction

The sixteen half-sign preparations and the four coordinate questions form the
four-bit parity-oblivious random-access-code obstruction. Its bias is lambda
divided by two, and the noncontextual bias bound one over four gives lambda at
most one half. This known obstruction does not by itself construct a model for
all twenty-four preparations and all twelve sharp questions.
