# Network nonlocality breaking channels

K. Mukherjee and N. Ganguly, arXiv:2510.26417v1 (2025). Quotations are from the v1 TeX source.

The conditions on the parameters of the non-unital channel class, Eq. (nu3):

> $|\lambda_1|\leq 1$, $|\lambda_3|+|t|\leq 1$, $\lambda_1^2+t^2\leq (1-|\lambda_3|)^2$

Theorem 3 and its sufficient condition, Eq. (resnon1):

> If any non unital channel $\mathcal{N}_{NU}$ from the class specified by Eqs.(nu1, nu2) be such that channel parameters satisfy $(|t|+|\lambda_3|)^2+\lambda_1^2\leq 1$ and $2t^2\cdot \lambda_1^2+\lambda_1^4+(|t|+|\lambda_3|)^4\leq 1$ then $\mathcal{N}_{NU}$ is $k$-use non $n$-locality breaking channel up to $n$-local inequality Eq.(ineqb) in any linear $n$-local network.

The observation and Conjecture 1:

> Comparing Eq.(resnon1) with that of the conditions provided by Eq.(nu3) over the channel $\mathcal{N}_{NU},$ it is observed that there exist no region in parameter space for which Eq.(resnon1) is violated whereas Eq.(nu3) is satisfied. However, a formal proof of the same is beyond the scope of present work. We thus provide the following conjecture:

> Conjecture.1: Any non-unital channel from $\mathcal{N}_{NU}$ (Eqs.(nu1, nu2)) acts as a non $n$-locality breaking channel in $\mathbf{N}_{lin}$.
