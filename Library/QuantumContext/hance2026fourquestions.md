# The twenty-four-cell preparation threshold question

The source asks:

> The 24-cell subtheory is therefore preparation contextual at least for $\lambda>2/3$; whether its further equivalences lower the threshold, and whether it is measurement contextual, we leave open, and both are linear programs.

The preparations are all eight signed coordinate axes together with all sixteen
half-sign vectors in four real coordinates. The measurements are the twelve
unoriented frame questions with both outcomes. Depolarization multiplies each
preparation vector by lambda while leaving the questions sharp.
The first open clause concerns preparation noncontextuality for the full convex
scenario. The separate measurement-contextuality clause is not settled here.
