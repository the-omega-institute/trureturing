# General entropy-like uncertainty relations in finite dimensions

Steeve Zozor, Gustavo Martín Bosyk and Mariela Portesi, arXiv:1311.5602v3 (quant-ph), 2014-10-07; published in J. Phys. A 47 (2014) 495302. Quotations are from the v3 TeX source.

Tsallis entropies (Section 2):

> S_\lambda(p) = \frac{\displaystyle 1 -\sum_k p_k^{\,\lambda}}{\lambda-1}

The overlap of two nondegenerate observables (Section 3):

> T_{ij} = \langle b_j | a_i \rangle,

> c(T) = \max_{i,j} | \langle b_j | a_i \rangle |,

The c-optimal bound (Section 3):

> \widetilde{\B}_{(h_A,\phi_A),(h_B,\phi_B);N}(c) \:\: = \min_{T \in \U(N):\: c(T)
> = c} \overline{\B}_{(h_A,\phi_A),(h_B,\phi_B)}(T)

The bound in the nondegenerate case (Section 3):

> \B_{(h_A,\phi_A),(h_B,\phi_B)}(c) = \min_{\theta \in [0, \gamma]}
> \left( \D_{(h_A,\phi_A)}(\theta) + \D_{(h_B,\phi_B)}(\gamma - \theta) \right)

Corollary 2 and the open case (Section 3):

> When   $c    >   \frac{1}{\sqrt2}$    and   $N=2$   or    $N   \geq    4$,   the
> bound~\eref{Cabnondeg:eq} is $c$-optimal,

> We suspect that  this corollary is also  valid when $N=3$, but we  have not been
> able to prove it yet.
