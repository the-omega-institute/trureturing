# Noisy Werner–Holevo channel and its properties

S. Roofeh and V. Karimipour, arXiv:2310.15353v7 [quant-ph]; Phys. Rev. A 109, 052620 (2024). Quotations are from the v7 TeX source.

The channel:

> $\Lambda_x(\rho)=(1-x)\rho + x\Lambda_{_{\mathit{WH}}}(\rho)=(1-x)\rho -\frac{x}{2}\rho^T+\frac{x}{2}\tr(\rho)\mathbb{I} \h 0\leq x\leq 1,$

(the display is written inline here).

The spectrum and the question:

> Counting the degeneracies of the eigenvalues, this shows that the determinant of the channel is equal to $Det(\Lambda_x)=(1-\frac{x}{2})^3(1-\frac{3}{2}x)^5.$ This shows that the determinant of the channel is negative in a certain range, namely $Det(\Lambda_x)<0,\h \frac{2}{3}<x\leq 1.$ In this range, the channel is not infinitely divisible and does not represent a Markovian evolution \cite{wolf_dividing_2008}. The question remains whether, within the range of $0\leq x\leq \frac{2}{3}$, the channel can be described as the exponential of Lindbladian dynamics. We will explore this question in our future work.
