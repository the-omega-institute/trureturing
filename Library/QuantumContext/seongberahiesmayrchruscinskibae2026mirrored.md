# Mirrored entanglement witnesses for multipartite and high-dimensional quantum systems

J. Seong, A. Bera, B. C. Hiesmayr, D. Chruściński and J. Bae, arXiv:2510.06863v2 [quant-ph]; Quantum Sci. Technol. 11 (2026). Quotations are from the v2 TeX source.

Entanglement witnesses:

> a state $\rho$ is entangled if and only if there exists an observable $W=W^{\dagger}$ such that $\tr [ W \rho] < 0 ~ \mathrm{whereas} ~ \tr[W \sigma_{sep} ] \geq 0~\forall \sigma_{sep} \in S_{sep}$,

Optimality:

> Suppose that for some $\epsilon >0$ and $P\geq 0$, we have $W^{'} = W-\epsilon P$. Here $W^{'}$ is also an EW. Let $D_{W }$ denote the set of states detected by an EW $W$, $D_W = \{ \rho \in S (\H \otimes \H) ~:~ \tr[W \rho] <0~\}$. It holds that $D_W\subset D_{W^{'}}$. In this case, two EWs can be compared and we call $W^{'}$ is {\it finer} than $W$. EWs which cannot be improved anymore, that is, {\it finer} than any other, are called optimal EWs.

The spanning property:

> We write by product states $|\psi_k \otimes \phi_k\rangle$ that vanish on a witness $W$: $\langle \psi_k\otimes \phi_k| W |\psi_k \otimes \phi_k \rangle=0$. Then, we call a witness $W$ has the spanning property if $\mathrm{spanning~property:~}\mathrm{span}\{|\psi_k\otimes \phi_k\rangle \} = \H \otimes \H$, and $W$ is optimal.

Generalized mirroring (Section V, "Bipartite systems: generalization"):

> We here generalize mirrored EWs in Eq. (\ref{eq:mr}) such that they are connected by some other observable, not an identity, $W + M = \mathbbm{K}$, where it follows that $\mathbbm{K}$ block-positive, i.e., it is nonnegative for all separable states since $W$ and $M$ are EWs.

Open problem 2 (Section VI):

> Optimal EWs can be mirrored via another EW, as shown in Eq. (\ref{eq:gmeew_form}). Since optimal EWs are generally not extremal, yet, it remains unanswered whether a pair of optimal EWs can be mirrored through another optimal EWs.
