# The Diversity of Entanglement Structures with Self-Duality and Non-Orthogonal State Discrimination in General Probabilistic Theories

H. Arai, Ph.D. thesis, Nagoya University; arXiv:2302.12361v1 (2023-02-23). Quotations are from the v1 TeX source.

DOVMs:

> In this thesis, we call a measurement in $\cM_2(\mathrm{SEP}(A;B),I)$ a \textit{Dual-Operator-Valued Measure} (DOVM),

The AQ class:

> We say that a measurement $\cM=\{M_i\}_{i=1}^2\in\rm{DOVM}(A;B)$ is Advantage Quantum (AQ)
> if one of the effects $M_i$ satisfies the inequalities $\lambda_1(M_i)<0$ and $1+\lambda_1(M_i)<\lambda_d(M_i)<1$.

The domain:

> \cD(X):=\{\rho\in\cL_{\mathrm{H}}^+(\cH_A\otimes\cH_B)\mid\Tr \rho X\ge0\}.

Quantum $n$-simulability:

> We say that $\cM$ is quantum $n$-simulable
> if there exists a natural number $n$ and a POVM $\cN=\{N_i\}_{i\in I}$ on $(\cH_A\otimes\cH_B)^{\otimes n}$ such that
> \Tr \rho^{\otimes n} N_i=\Tr \rho M_i \quad ^\forall\rho\in\cD(\cM)

The open problem:

> Similarly,
> it is considered that AQ measurement might be non-simulable
> because AQ measurement also has an extraordinary performance.
> This is an open problem.
