## Source statements

Pages 4–5, the reduced function of the partial negativity:

> ĥ(ρ) = √(δ1 δ2), … and δ1, δ2 are the two largest eigenvalues of ρ.

Page 5, equation (24):

> We conjecture that ĥ is subadditive, i.e., ĥ(ρAB) ⩽ ĥ(ρA) + ĥ(ρB) (24) holds for any ρAB ∈ S^{AB}. In what follows, we always assume that hF′, hAF, and ĥ are subadditive, and that ĥ is concave.

Table I marks this subadditivity with “We conjecture that they are subadditive.”
The two eigenvalues are ordered decreasingly with multiplicity. The reduced
states are the partial traces of the same joint density matrix.

## Scope and literature boundary

The source defines the function and states its subadditivity as a conjecture.
The repository's counterexample is a mixture of two orthogonal maximally
entangled states on a 5 × 5 system. Its value is 1/2 and the marginal values
are each 1/5. This refutes the unrestricted conjecture; universal validity in
local dimensions at most four remains open here.
