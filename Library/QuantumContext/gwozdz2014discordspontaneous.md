# Spontaneous emission can locally create quantum discord out of classical correlations

Marek Gwóźdź and Lech Jakóbczyk, arXiv:1306.0130v2 (quant-ph), 2013-11-02; published in Quantum Inf. Process. 13 (2014) 171–183. Quotations are from the v2 TeX source.

The geometric discord (Section II):

> D_{G}^{A}(\ro)=\frac{d}{d-1}\;\min\limits_{\PP^{A}}\,||\ro-\PP^{A}(\ro)||_{2}^{2},

> ||m||_{2}=\sqrt{\tr (m\,m^{\dagger})}.

The maximal mutual correlation (Section II):

> C_{M}(\ro)=\sup\limits_{a,b}\,|\omega_{\ro}(ab)-\omega_{\ro}(a)\omega_{\ro}(b)|.\label{maxtot}

> In the formula (\ref{maxtot}), the supremum is taken over all
> elements $a\in\cA$ and $b\in\cB$ such that $||a||=||b||=1$.

The conjecture (Section II):

> C_{M}(\ro)\geq \sqrt{D_{G}^{A}(\ro)}\geq N(\ro).\label{nierow}

> The second inequality was shown in Ref. \cite{Adesso} whereas the
> first one can be proved analytically for Bell-diagonal states and
> numerical simulations suggest that it is valid for all mixed
> two-qubit states \cite{J}.
