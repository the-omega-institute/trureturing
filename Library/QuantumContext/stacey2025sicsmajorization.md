# SICs, the Stabilizer Polytope and Majorization

Blake C. Stacey, arXiv:2509.16819v1 (quant-ph), 2025-09-20. Quotations are from the v1 TeX source.

The matrix (Section III):

> let us define the matrix $A(\ket{\psi})$ by
> [A(\ket{\psi})]_{mk} = \sum_j p_{m,j} p_{m,j+k} \, ,
> where $p_{m,j}$ is the probability of obtaining outcome $j$ when measuring in the basis labeled by~$m$, given the pure state $\ket{\psi}$.

The invariance assertion and the proposal:

> Acting upon $\ket{\psi}$ with a Clifford unitary can at most permute the rows and columns of the matrix $A$. … Consequently, Clifford unitaries will leave invariant the Frobenius norm of~$A$. Indeed, let $||\cdot||$ be any unitarily invariant norm; because permutation matrices are a very special subset of the unitary matrices, left- and right-multiplying $A$ by any pair of permutation matrices will leave $||A||$ unchanged. It seems, then, that we have a large family of new candidate monotones that are all extremized by Weyl--Heisenberg SIC fiducials.
