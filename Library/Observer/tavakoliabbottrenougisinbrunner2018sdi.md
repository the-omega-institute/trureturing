# Tavakoli–Abbott–Renou–Gisin–Brunner, semi-device-independent characterization of multipartite entanglement

A. Tavakoli, A. A. Abbott, M.-O. Renou, N. Gisin and N. Brunner, Phys. Rev. A 98, 052333 (2018); arXiv:1805.00377v1 (arXiv title "Semi-device-independent characterisation of multipartite entangled states and measurements"). Quotations are from the v1 TeX source.

Parties $A_1,\dots,A_n$ apply transformations $\mathcal T^{(k)}_{x_ky_k}$ to a shared state $\rho$ of local dimension $d$, and $B$ measures $\{M_{\mathbf b}\}$; the figure of merit $\mathcal A_{n,d}$ averages the probability of the winning string $C(\mathbf x,\mathbf y)$ with $b_1=\sum_ix_i$ and $b_k=y_k-y_1$ modulo $d$. An operator is fully separable if it is a positive combination of positive product operators. Result 2 bounds $\mathcal A_{n,d}\le d^{-n}(d^n-k+k/d)$ for a measurement with at least $k$ fully separable operators. For $n=2$, Appendix C attains the bound with $k=md$ and states:

> We leave it as an open question whether a similar partial tightness result holds in the more general $n$ partite case.
