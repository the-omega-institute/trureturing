# Porto, Ruffolo, Rabelo, Terra Cunha and Kurzyński, Trade-off relations between Bell nonlocality and local Kochen–Specker contextuality in generalized Bell scenarios

L. E. A. Porto, G. Ruffolo, R. Rabelo, M. Terra Cunha and P. Kurzyński, New J. Phys. 26(8), 083028 (2024); arXiv:2405.02964v2. Quotations are from the v2 TeX source.

In a generalized Bell scenario Alice performs one of two dichotomic measurements $A_0,A_1$ and Bob one context $\{B_i,B_{i+1}\}$ (indices mod $n$) of an $n$-cycle of dichotomic measurements. Behaviors $p_{x,C_B}(a,\boldsymbol b)$ that are nonsignaling and nondisturbing form the $\mathcal{NSND}$ polytope; generalized-local behaviors are mixtures $\sum_\lambda q(\lambda)p^\lambda_x(a)p^\lambda_{C_B}(\boldsymbol b)$ with nondisturbing Bob responses. An inequality $S$ is normalized when $S\le0$ on local (respectively noncontextual) behaviors and its maximum over $\mathcal{NSND}$ is $1$. Result 1 proves $S_{GBell}+S_{NC}\le1$ on $\mathcal{NSND}$ for $n=3,4,5$, and the paper states:

> Consider a bipartite generalized Bell scenario where Bob has an $n$-cycle contextuality setup. For any normalized generalized-Bell inequality $S_{GBell}$ and any normalized noncontextuality inequality $S_{NC}$ of Bob's local experiment, every $\mathcal{NSND}$ behavior satisfies expression (12).
