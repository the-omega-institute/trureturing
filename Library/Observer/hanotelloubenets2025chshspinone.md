# Hanotel and Loubenets, Nonviolation of the CHSH inequality under local spin-1 measurements on two spin qutrits

L. Hanotel and E. R. Loubenets, Phys. Scr. 100(5), 055115 (2025); arXiv:2503.05172v2. Quotations are from the v2 TeX source. The spin observables and the CHSH setting are those of the companion paper E. R. Loubenets and L. Hanotel, "High-spin measurements in an arbitrary two-qudit state", J. Phys. A: Math. Theor. 58(26), 265304 (2025), DOI 10.1088/1751-8121/ade4e8, arXiv:2412.03470v2.

For a spin-$s$ qudit of dimension $d=2s+1$, Alice and Bob measure spin projections $S_r=r\cdot S$ with unit vectors $r\in\mathbb R^3$; the CHSH operator is $S_{a_1}\otimes S_{b_1}+S_{a_1}\otimes S_{b_2}+S_{a_2}\otimes S_{b_1}-S_{a_2}\otimes S_{b_2}$, and since spin-$s$ observables have eigenvalues in $[-s,s]$ the local-hidden-variable bound is $2s^2$. The paper states:

> However, up to the moment the problem whether or not the CHSH inequality is violated under Alice and Bob high spin $s\geq1$ measurements is still open -- though specifically this Bell inequality has been used for proving [1, Lou.Kuz.Han:24] nonlocality of every pure nonseparable two-qudit state.

It then treats spin 1 and conjectures that no two-qutrit state violates the CHSH inequality under local spin-1 measurements.
