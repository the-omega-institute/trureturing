# Haapasalo and Pellonpää, Effective methods for constructing extreme quantum observables

E. Haapasalo and J.-P. Pellonpää, J. Phys. A: Math. Theor. 53, 245301 (2020); arXiv:1809.09935v1. Quotations are from the v1 TeX source.

An $N$-outcome POVM is a tuple of positive operators summing to the identity; it is extreme if $\mathsf M_j=\tfrac12\mathsf A_j+\tfrac12\mathsf B_j$ for all $j$, with POVMs $\mathsf A,\mathsf B$, implies $\mathsf A=\mathsf B=\mathsf M$. Rank vectors list the ranks of the outcomes from highest to lowest, with rank-one outcomes omitted. Section 4 states:

> In dimensions 1-4, we are able to completely characterize the possible rank vectors of extreme POVMs but in dimensions 5-7 some open questions remain.

> The new problematic case now is $(3_2,2_6)_7$.
