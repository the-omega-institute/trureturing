# ADM variables and conformal ends

Sections 3 and 4 give the lapse and shift decomposition, the unit normal covector and the canonical momentum in terms of the second fundamental form. The manuscript uses a sign for the second fundamental form paired with a minus sign in the momentum definition. The Lean formulas absorb that sign into the second fundamental form. The quadratic kinetic term is independent of this sign convention. Section 5.1 gives the asymptotic surface expressions for energy and momentum in equations (5.1) and (5.2). Section 6 derives static isotropic solutions.

For multiple punctures, Brill and Lindquist analyze the separate asymptotic ends and their interaction energy. In the formulas here the asymptotic mass is computed along each unit ray in the outer end, and the Penrose equality is evaluated at the isotropic Schwarzschild throat.
