# Exact support in the heat moment problem

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2608.08041
Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08041v1

Version 1, Section 2, Theorem 2.2 gives the two half-plane Paley–Wiener
theorems. Proposition 2.1 characterizes the Fourier transforms of
square-integrable functions supported on [0,T] by the paired Hardy
conditions at the two endpoints. The lower-bound argument uses these
conditions for the continuum Dirichlet heat problem.

## Use and exact scope

The finite-window moment result starts with a function on the prescribed
interval and estimates its Laplace transform directly. It uses the
finite Blaschke product for arbitrary distinct nonnegative nodes. The
reflecting cell grid has its own discrete rates, mass normalization and
endpoint observation.
