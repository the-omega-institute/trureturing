# Locally nonrotating frames and equatorial Kerr orbits

[Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1086/151796)

## Formal correspondence

In Boyer–Lindquist coordinates, the angular velocity of the locally
nonrotating frame is minus the time–azimuth metric term divided by the
azimuthal metric term. The time–azimuth determinant gives the two null
angular velocities and the open timelike interval between them outside
the horizon. For positive spin, this entire interval is positive in the
ergoregion.

The equatorial orbit formulas use units in which the speed of light and
gravitational constant equal one. The photon polynomial gives radii
three masses at zero spin and one and four masses at extremal spin. The
marginal-stability equation gives six masses at zero spin and one and
nine masses for the respective extremal prograde and retrograde limits.
The binding efficiency equals one minus the specific orbital energy.
At the extremal prograde radius, the specific energy uses the exterior
limit of the circular-orbit expression.

The area-sign comparison uses the neutral specialization of the
[Christodoulou–Ruffini mass formula](christodoulouruffini1971chargedblackhole.md)
at positive surface gravity. The superradiance threshold concerns a
horizon flux with a positive proportionality coefficient.
