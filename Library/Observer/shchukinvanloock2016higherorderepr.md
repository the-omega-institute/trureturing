# Shchukin and van Loock, Higher-order EPR correlations and inseparability conditions for continuous variables

E. Shchukin and P. van Loock, Phys. Rev. A 93(3), 032114 (2016); arXiv:1511.08444v1. Quotations are from the v1 TeX source.

For two bosonic modes with annihilation operators $\hat a,\hat b$ the paper proves, for every positive integer $n$,
$$\langle(\hat a^{\dagger n}\pm\hat b^n)(\hat a^n\pm\hat b^{\dagger n})\rangle\ \ge\ \begin{cases}n!&\text{for separable states (nS:1)}\\0&\text{for all states (nS:2)},\end{cases}$$
proves for $n=2$ that (bS:2) "is always strict and tight, i.e., the left-hand side can be arbitrarily close to zero (but never equal to zero)", and for $n\ge3$ exhibits a family whose limiting value $N_n^2\,n/4$ violates (nS:1) but stays positive. It concludes:

> Our conjecture is that the inequality (nS:1) cannot be violated by Gaussian states (including all inseparable ones) and the inequality (nS:2) is tight. We verified this by numerical computations, but we could not find rigorous proofs.
