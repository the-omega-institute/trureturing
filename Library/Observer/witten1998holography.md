# Anti De Sitter Space And Holography

The massive scalar calculation in the half-space coordinates gives the height power solution, its image under inversion, and the dimension relation. In boundary dimension three the indicial discriminant vanishes at mass squared times radius squared equal to minus nine quarters. The endpoint inequalities for real roots and the scalar dimension bound are algebraic. The existence of two distinct admissible quantizations uses boundary conditions and an open mass interval.
