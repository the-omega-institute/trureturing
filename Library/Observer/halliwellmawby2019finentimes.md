# Halliwell and Mawby, Fine's theorem for Leggett-Garg tests with an arbitrary number of measurement times

J. J. Halliwell and C. Mawby, Phys. Rev. A 100, 042103 (2019); arXiv:1906.04865v2. Quotations are from the v2 TeX source.

A dichotomic variable $Q=\pm1$ is measured at times $t_1,\dots,t_n$, giving pairwise probabilities $p(s_i,s_j)$ that agree on shared times, with correlators $C_{ij}=\langle Q_iQ_j\rangle$. Section 4.A derives the $n$-gon inequalities $n+2\sum_{i<j}s_is_jC_{ij}\ge1$ for odd $n$ and $\ge0$ for even $n$. Section 4.B, "Sufficient Conditions: A Conjecture", assumes all two-time inequalities and states:

> A set of sufficient conditions for the existence of a joint probability matching all possible pairwise probabilities consists of the $n$-gon equalities Eq.(\ref{neweq}) together with all possible three-time LG inequalities of the form
> $$1 + s_i s_j C_{ij} + s_i s_k C_{ik} + s_j s_k C_{jk} \ge 0,$$
> where $i<j<k$.

The paper proves the conjecture for $n=4$ and $n=5$ in the symmetric case, where correlators of an odd number of variables vanish.
