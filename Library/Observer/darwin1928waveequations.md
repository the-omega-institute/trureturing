# The Wave Equations of the Electron

The hydrogen calculation solves coupled radial equations in a Coulomb potential. Its fine-structure eigenvalue depends on the radial index and the magnitude of the Dirac angular index. The circular branch has radial index zero. The signed angular index fixes the component convention in the radial equations, while the energy depends on its magnitude.
