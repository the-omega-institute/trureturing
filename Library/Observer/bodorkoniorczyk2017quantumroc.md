# Bodor and Koniorczyk, Receiver operation characteristics of quantum state discrimination

A. Bodor and M. Koniorczyk, J. Russ. Laser Res. 38(2), 150–163 (2017); arXiv:1603.06801v1. Quotations are from the v1 TeX source.

The paper analyses the discrimination of two states $\varrho_N,\varrho_P$ through receiver operation characteristics: a discriminator gives the point $(\mathrm{FP},\mathrm{TP})$; the feasible ROC domain is convex, and the line of the best possible discriminators is the "optimal ROC curve". A measurement whose outcomes are ordered by decreasing likelihood ratio gives cumulative points $(Q_k,P_k)$. In the proof that the quantum Bhattacharyya coefficient is at most the square-root fidelity, the paper uses the observable
$$M=\varrho_N^{-1/2}\sqrt{\varrho_N^{1/2}\varrho_P\varrho_N^{1/2}}\;\varrho_N^{-1/2}\qquad(44)$$
and states:

> This obviously defines $d+1$ points in the feasible ROC domain, where $d$ stands for the dimension of the joint support of the two density operators. (It is an interesting open question whether these points are actually on the optimal ROC curve.)
