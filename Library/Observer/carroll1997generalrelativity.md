# Schwarzschild orbits and homogeneous cosmology

## Verified locator

Carroll, Section 7, equations (7.47)–(7.49), gives the radial energy
equation, effective potential and circular-orbit condition. Equations
(7.58)–(7.61) give the proper-time and redshift relations for two static
observers. [Lecture notes](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9712019)

## Formal correspondence

In geometric units the timelike effective potential is Carroll's
equation (7.48) with the timelike normalization and the Schwarzschild
choice of metric. The null potential factors out the angular momentum
squared and the conventional factor of one half. The static clock ratio
compares the proper-time lapse at the smaller radius to the lapse at the
larger radius.

Carroll's Section 8 gives the density scalings for dust, radiation, and
vacuum energy in equations (8.24), (8.28), and (8.31). Equations (8.35) and
(8.36) give the acceleration and expansion equations. The flat matter and
radiation power laws follow from (8.42) and (8.53). In the formal statements
the vacuum contribution is written as the explicit cosmological constant
term, using the vacuum pressure relation. [Cosmology chapter](https://ned.ipac.caltech.edu/level5/March01/Carroll3/Carroll8.html)
