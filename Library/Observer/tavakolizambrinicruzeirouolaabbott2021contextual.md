# Tavakoli, Zambrini Cruzeiro, Uola and Abbott, Bounding and simulating contextual correlations in quantum theory

A. Tavakoli, E. Zambrini Cruzeiro, R. Uola and A. A. Abbott, PRX Quantum 2(2) (2021) 020334, DOI 10.1103/PRXQuantum.2.020334; arXiv:2010.04751v3. Quotations are from the arXiv v3 TeX source.

In the Hameedi-Tavakoli-Marques-Bourennane (HTMB) scenario Alice receives two trits $x=x_1x_2$ and prepares $\rho_x$, and Bob receives $y\in\{1,2\}$ and outputs a trit $b$. The operational equivalences say that Alice sends no information about $x_1+x_2$ and about $x_1+2x_2$ modulo $3$. The HTMB inequality is $\mathcal A_{\mathrm{HTMB}}=\frac1{18}\sum_{x,y}p(b=x_y\mid x,y)\le\frac23$. After bounding $\mathcal A_{\mathrm{HTMB}}$ over models with pure preparations by $\lesssim0.667$, the paper writes:

> This gives us strong evidence (i.e., up to numerical precision) that pure states cannot violate the HTMB inequality~\eqref{eq:HTMB}, and we conjecture this to indeed be the case exactly.
