# Experimental scope of a local clock comparison

The primary paper is *Nature* 602 (2022), 420–424. Its abstract and the
spatially resolved frequency analysis report a gravitational-redshift
measurement within one millimetre-scale ultracold strontium sample.
The abstract quotes fractional frequency measurement uncertainty
$7.6\times10^{-21}$. This is literature evidence for the stated laboratory
clock comparison.

The experiment does not test a near-horizon suspension, a computational
complexity bound, or a mapping from FIB probability mass to a spacetime
lapse. The static exterior formulas and the conditional numerical
resource application are separately described in
[Reall's note](reall2024blackholes.md).
