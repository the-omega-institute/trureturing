# Weyl superenergy and principal null directions

## Verified locator

Classical and Quantum Gravity 17 (2000), 2799–2841. Section 4 defines the dominant superenergy property in terms of independent future causal arguments. Section 6.2 gives the Weyl superenergy tensor and specializes its trace and symmetry identities to four dimensions. [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/17/14/313) · [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9906087)

## Use and boundary

The spinor proof uses a Hermitian matrix for each vector and reduces the future causal contraction to a sum of squared complex moduli. Its rest-frame energy uses the explicit normalization of the spinor and the electric and magnetic Weyl matrices.

The principal-null-direction labels follow the Petrov classification, with multiplicity counted on the projective line so that infinity has its own chart. [Petrov’s classification](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1001910908054)
