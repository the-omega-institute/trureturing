# Optical focusing and extremal potentials

Bousso's light-sheet condition is nonpositive null expansion. The positive optical radius obeys a concavity inequality under null convergence. For negative initial expansion its initial tangent line bounds the time for which the radius can stay positive. The corresponding cosmological estimate uses the initial tangent line of the FLRW scale factor at the observation time.

The extremal-potential calculation uses the Euclidean inverse-distance kernel on the complement of its centers. A finite sum of these kernels is harmonic there and converges to its constant term along every unit direction.
