# Rainich electromagnetic stress identity

## Verified locator

Rainich, *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society* 27(1), 106–136.
[Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1925-1501302-6)

The square condition is restated as equation (2) in the modern treatment.
[Bergqvist and Höglund, *Algebraic Rainich theory and antisymmetrization in higher dimensions*](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/19/12/316)

## Formal correspondence

For signature \((+,-,-,-)\), let \(I_1=|B|^2-|E|^2\) and \(I_2=E\cdot B\).
The mixed electromagnetic stress matrix squares to the identity times
\((I_1^2+4I_2^2)/4\). Its square vanishes exactly when the two invariants
vanish, including the zero field. The square formula gives the Lorentzian
norm of the energy flux for any observer vector. The Poynting bound fixes
its future direction for a future causal observer.
