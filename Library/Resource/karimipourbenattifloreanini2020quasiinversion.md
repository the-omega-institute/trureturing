# Unitary quasi-inversion of qubit channels

## Verified locator

Theorem 1 on preprint p. 2, with proof in Supplementary Material A, gives a unitary optimum for every qubit channel. Haar-average fidelity and entanglement fidelity at the maximally mixed input are related by a strictly increasing affine transformation. [arXiv v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.06118v5) · [Phys. Rev. A 101, 032109](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.101.032109)
