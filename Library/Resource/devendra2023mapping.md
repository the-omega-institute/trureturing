## Verified locator

Positivity **27**, article 5. DOI 10.1007/s11117-022-00956-4. Versioned author text
at https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14991v2.

Section 2, pp. 3–4, defines separability as a finite sum of Kronecker products
of positive semidefinite factors. It defines complete positivity by positivity
of every left amplification at a positive integer dimension. Complete
copositivity means complete positivity after composing with transpose on the
output. The Choi matrix uses the input indices for the outer blocks.

Definition 3.1, p. 6:

> Let \(k\in\mathbb N\). A linear map \(\Phi:\mathbb M_{d_1}\to\mathbb M_{d_2}\)
> is said to be \(k\)-entanglement breaking (\(k\)-EB) if \(\Phi\) is
> \(k\)-positive map and \((\operatorname{id}_k\otimes\Phi)(X)\) is separable
> for every \(X\in(\mathbb M_k\otimes\mathbb M_{d_1})^+\).

Section 3, p. 14:

> Recall that a positive map is said to be decomposable if it can be written as
> a sum of a CP-map and a co-CP-map.

Section 7, p. 26:

> Open Problem 1. Is every \(2\)-EB map \(\Phi:\mathbb M_{d_1}\to\mathbb M_{d_2}\)
> decomposable?
