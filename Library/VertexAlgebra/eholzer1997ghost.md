# Fermionic ghost modes and Fock states

Section 2, equations (2.7)-(2.10), defines mathematical modes by powers
z to the power minus n minus one, the untwisted anticommutation relation
with index sum minus one, and the cyclic vacuum with its finite-product
Fock basis. Equation (2.5) gives weights 1-lambda and lambda and central
charge -2(6lambda(lambda-1)+1). Lambda=-1 gives weights (2,-1) and charge -26.
Equation (2.15) gives the stress-tensor modes.

The concrete finite-subset construction uses shifted physical modes:
b_m=b_math_(m+1) and c_n=c_math_(n-2). Hence the vacuum boundaries become
m>=-1 and n>=2 and the anticommutator index sum becomes zero. Its
normal-ordering creator thresholds are b_m with m<=-2 and c_n with n<=1.
At lambda=-1 and zero twist, the two terms of (2.15) combine, using the
paper's anticommutation relations, into coefficient m-k multiplying
the normal-ordered b_(m+k)c_(-k). This is a convention comparison, not
a proved identification of the concrete operator with the paper's field.
The construction's
statewise normal-ordering support and creation-generation results hold
over arbitrary commutative rings. These results do not establish the
anticommutation or Virasoro relations, a BRST operator, or a physical
string theory.
