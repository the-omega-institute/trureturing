# Universal affine C2 context

Section 3.8, printed pages 23–24 of arXiv `1605.00138v2`, describes the
universal affine vertex algebra. Equation (3.19) on printed page 24
identifies its C2 Poisson algebra with the classical coordinate algebra
of the dual Lie algebra. This is classical abstract context; it does not
itself prove the specified concrete bridge from the actual polynomial
Fock state field $Y$ to $C_2(F)=\ker\pi$ and its nonpositive-mode formulas.

The first arXiv submission is from 2016; the pinned v2 is from 2017.
