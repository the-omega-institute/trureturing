# C2 filtration and Poisson quotient context

Printed page 15 of arXiv `math/0409140v1` (2004), Corollary 3.6,
gives $E_1(W)=C_2(W)$ for a vertex-algebra module $W$. Proposition 3.7
recalls Zhu's Poisson algebra $V/C_2(V)$; equation (3.15) gives its
multiplication by mode $-1$ and bracket by mode $0$. This is classical
abstract context; it does not itself prove the specified concrete bridge
from the actual polynomial Fock state field $Y$ to $C_2(F)=\ker\pi$
and its nonpositive-mode formulas.
