# Modularity of vertex tensor categories

Huang's arXiv:math/0502533v2 abstract states the same grading,
self-duality, semisimplicity, and C2-cofiniteness conditions as the Verlinde
paper, and concludes that the braided module category is rigid, balanced,
and nondegenerate. This supplies a modular tensor category only after those
hypotheses and the vertex tensor category construction apply.
