# The P48q lattice and its order-three matrix

Nebe’s paper appeared in *Discrete Mathematics* 331 (2014), pp. 133–136.
Its Tables 1 and 2 identify the lattice and give the order-three type
with fixed dimension sixteen. The matrix entries come from the
[P48q entry of the Nebe–Sloane Catalogue of Lattices](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/P48q.html).
The HTML has SHA-256
`c1f04856618da946626600a588ebc14ebdb2683fe86786ff3b4828ac1ed10616`.

Let G be the matrix in the catalogue’s GRAM section and let A be the first
48 × 48 matrix in GROUP_GENERATORS. The certificate uses M = A⁴ in that
basis. For row coordinates the action is x ↦ xM and the Gram preservation
equation is M G Mᵀ = G. On column coordinates it is x ↦ Mᵀx.
The fixed-rank proof uses the concrete matrix, its cube and the two traces
to obtain the dimension recorded in the paper.
