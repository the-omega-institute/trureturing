## Source statement

Conjecture 1, page 17, Section 4.1, of arXiv:2511.16959v3 states:

```tex
Let $f(n)$ be the number of pairs $(m,k)$ such that $2 \leqslant k < m < n$ and $\langle r_n, r_m, r_k \rangle = \mathrm{Sym}_n$.
    Then 
    $$
     f(n) = \begin{cases}
        \frac{1}{13}n^2 + O(n) & n = 0 \pmod{4}; \\[6pt]
        \frac{1}{9}n^2 + O(n) & n = 1 \pmod{4}; \\[6pt]
        \frac{1}{10}n^2 + O(n) & n = 2 \pmod{4}; \\[6pt]
        \frac{1}{7}n^2 + O(n) & n = 3 \pmod{4}. \\[6pt]
    \end{cases}
    $$
```

The source permutation reverses the first L positions of {1,...,n} and fixes
the others. Position j corresponds to i = j - 1 in Fin n. The existing
`PrefixReversalTripleOddNonGeneration.prefixReversal` and `tripleSubgroup`
provide this action and the subgroup generated by the three permutations.
The strict inequalities select each pair of shorter lengths once.

Each O(n) assertion means that a nonnegative real constant and a natural
threshold exist for that residue, with the error bound holding for every
subsequent natural degree in the residue.

## Mathematical boundary

The refutation uses the residue-zero clause. A simultaneous congruence sieve
and a lattice estimate give f(n) ≤ (1146617856/14933966047)n² + 58198140n
for n ≥ 19399380 divisible by four. The quadratic coefficient is strictly
below 1/13. The other three residue assertions, the exact limiting
coefficient, existence of a limit and a matching lower bound remain open.
The source's separate generation theorems are not negated by this result.
No sufficiency of the sieve conditions is asserted.
