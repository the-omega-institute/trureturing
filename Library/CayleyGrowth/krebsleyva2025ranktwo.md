# Chromatic numbers of rank-two Abelian Cayley graphs

The primary statement is Conjecture 1.2 in §1.1.3, printed page 3 of
arXiv:2511.03028v1. Section 1.2 on the same page defines the standardized
Abelian Cayley graph of an integer matrix using the quotient of the free
abelian group on the rows by the integer span of the columns. The
generators are the signed standard coordinate cosets. Section 1.1.2 gives
the convention that a loop makes the graph uncolorable, with chromatic
number infinity. Lemma 1.3 on printed page 4 gives the equivalent
column-span test for loops.
