## Conjugate-flag construction

In arXiv:1802.04975v1, Section III.C, PDF page 8, the second lemma
labelled III.1 uses the state
$\rho_{AB}\otimes|{+i}\rangle\langle{+i}| +
\rho_{AB}^{\Gamma}\otimes|{-i}\rangle\langle{-i}|$.
The version also has a different Lemma III.1 on PDF page 4; the
page-8 lemma is the conjugate-flag construction used here.

The two-dimensional projectors are
$P_+=(1,i)(1,i)^*/2$ and $P_-=(1,-i)(1,-i)^*/2$.
Their transpose exchanges them. The map lift adapts this state
construction, with normalization, a composition law and recovery of a
map's square as proved in `IptPptSquaredEquivalence`.
