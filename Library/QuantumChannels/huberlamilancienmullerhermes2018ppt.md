## Tensor-block precedent

Lemma III.1, arXiv v1, PDF p. 4, considers
Z = X ⊗ (1 − ω) + Y ⊗ ω, where ω is the maximally entangled
projector on the auxiliary bipartite factors. It characterizes positivity
by X, Y ≥ 0 and PPT by two inequalities involving X and Y after
partial transpose. This is a tensor-block precedent for using auxiliary
factors to control positivity and partial transpose.

The map lift uses the two-dimensional conjugate projectors
P₊ = (1,i)(1,i)*/2 and P₋ = (1,−i)(1,−i)*/2 instead.
Their transpose exchanges them. Its normalization, composition law and
recovery of a map's square are repository-derived; Lemma III.1 does not
state that map lift or the formula ρ ⊗ P₊ + ρ^Γ ⊗ P₋.
