## Scope

Section 4, equation (4.1), defines Wielength using the complex span of words of
exactly one length. For the completely positive map
$\phi_S(X)=\sum_i v_iXv_i^*$, the following paragraph recalls the known inequality
$\operatorname{Wie}\ell(S)\geq q(\phi_S)$, allowing strict inequality.
The formalization proves its pointwise sufficient condition at the same length,
without assuming trace preservation or a normalization of the Kraus family.

Conjecture A concerns a general quadratic bound on exact-word length. This
version explicitly cites Shitov's preprint, https://vixra.org/pdf/2308.0028v1.pdf,
as a proposed resolution. Neither the conjecture's global status nor a bound on
the gap between Wielength and the primitivity index is established here.
