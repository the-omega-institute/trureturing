## Reading used by the repository

A MEMS with respect to fixed marginals maximizes the negativity over the two-qutrit states with those marginals, as in the two-qubit case and the numerical comparison of Section IV. In the region of Eq. (27), the repository states the conjecture as: every two-qutrit density matrix with marginals (24) has negativity at most that of the state (27), and refutes it at λ = (47, 4)/100, μ = (5, 1)/100.
