## Source statements

Section 9.2, Conjecture 9.5, PDF p. 23:

> For every PPT map Φ : Mₙ(ℂ) → Mₙ(ℂ), Φ ◦ Φ is ℂ-entanglement breaking.

Section 9.2, Conjecture 9.7, PDF p. 24:

> For 𝕂 ∈ {ℝ, ℂ}, every IPT map Φ : Mₙ(𝕂) → Mₙ(𝕂) satisfies Φ ◦ Φ is 𝕂-entanglement breaking.

Section 9.2, after Proposition 9.9, PDF p. 25:

> We do not know if these conjectures are equivalent.

Section 9.1, PDF p. 21:

> A completely positive map Φ : Mₙ(𝕂) → Mₘ(𝕂) is called PPT (IPT) if its Choi matrix is PPT (IPT).

The preceding definitions say that a positive matrix P is PPT when
(τₙ ⊗ idₘ)(P) ≥ 0, and IPT when (τₙ ⊗ idₘ)(P) = P.
Section 8, PDF p. 18, defines 𝕂-entanglement p-breaking by
(Φ ⊗ idᵣ)(P) ∈ SEPₚ(𝕂ᵐ ⊗ 𝕂ʳ) for every positive P and r ≥ 1, and states:

> For p = 1, we just call the map Φ 𝕂-entanglement breaking.

The separable cone is generated by rank-one products xx* ⊗ yy*;
coefficients are absorbed into their vectors. Theorem 8.1 gives the
Choi and measure-and-prepare characterizations over both fields.
Proposition 9.2 identifies CSEP ∩ IPT with real separability.
Proposition 9.9 gives PPT²(ℂ) ⇒ IPT²(ℂ) ⇒ IPT²(ℝ).

The Lean encoding uses Fin n for each matrix index, RCLike scalars,
input-first Choi coordinates, and ptFirst for τₙ ⊗ idₘ. Universal
quantification includes n = 0 and r = 0; those cases are vacuous and do
not strengthen the positive-dimensional conjectures.

## Applicability

The equivalence question concerns all dimensions, without a trace-preserving
or unital hypothesis. It does not assert that any of the three conjectures is
true. Chen, Yang and Tang, arXiv:1807.03636v2, Theorem 3 (PDF p. 2),
prove the complex PPT-squared conjecture for n = 3. Their Section II also
recalls the n = 2 case from the Peres–Horodecki criterion. Proposition 9.9
of the primary source transfers the dimension-three conclusion to IPT²
over both fields. The equivalence proof preserves the original implications
in each fixed dimension and sends IPT² in dimension 2n to complex PPT²
in dimension n in the converse direction. The dimension-doubling converse
does not transfer a dimension-three IPT² result to dimension three PPT².
