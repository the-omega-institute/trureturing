# Projection factorization of correlation matrices

## Source and locator

Literature status: `literature-attested`.

The primary locator is [arXiv:1812.01449v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1812.01449v1), proof of Theorem 1, printed pages 3–4, equations (9)–(13). A generating vector independent of the others is projected onto their span and normalized. For nonzero projection, the original Gram is the Schur product of this rank-lowered Gram and a rank-two correlation matrix. The proof treats zero projection separately. Lemma 8, printed page 9, iterates the construction. The [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.01449) identifies the journal DOI above and the 2019 online publication; the theorem and equation numbers here refer to the arXiv version.

## RT use and boundary

In [RT IV §106](../../docs/develop/theory/ARITHMETIC_HOLOGRAPHIC_RT_IV.md#106-exact-product-preserving-rank-saturation-and-global-rank-descent), the singleton outside-coordinate endpoint is exactly this factorization. Preserving fibre zeros and absorbing the rank-two factor into the opposite Gram are immediate applications. A singleton step together with the principal-rank inequality also gives the bare global rank-drop consumer, so rank reduction itself is not a new mechanism.

The additional repository-derived assertions concern the simultaneous uniform path, exact opposite-rank saturation, near-zero full-domain local-minimum preservation, and equality of the complete canonical common-witness subsets. They supply the stated RT obstruction transfers and physical representative applications. These statements do not exclude either residual rank class or prove the unrestricted quarter optimum.
