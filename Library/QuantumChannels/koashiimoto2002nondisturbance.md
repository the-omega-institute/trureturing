# Operations that do not disturb partially known quantum states

A decomposition characterizes operations preserving a family of partially known states; it provides broader background for component nondisturbance rigidity.

The finite complex-matrix results use completely positive trace-preserving maps and all density inputs.

## Verified locator

https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0101144

[Primary text, Theorems 3–4 and equations (103)–(106)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0101144v2#page=15)

## Formalization scope

Theorem 3 describes the blockwise action of operations that leave a given family of states invariant. Theorem 4 describes the decomposition of the family formed by independent tensor-product choices.
These results supply structural context for the finite pure-environment label-record theorem, which directly proves the dimension criterion for basis labels and a memory POVM.
