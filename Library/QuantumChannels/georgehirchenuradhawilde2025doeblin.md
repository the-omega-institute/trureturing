# Quantum Doeblin coefficients

Definition 7, Eq. (3.25), maximizes the trace of a positive semidefinite
operator whose tensor product with the reference identity is bounded above
by the unnormalized Choi matrix. Proposition 31, Eq. (6.21), gives the
complete hockey-stick bound at parameters at least one.
[arXiv:2503.22823v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.22823v3)

`CompleteHockeyStickChoiSupport.coefficient_le_of_replacement` proves the
bound for an exhibited replacement weight (p) and a residual channel.
On equal-reference-marginal pairs the replacement term in the amplified
difference is negative semidefinite. Trace of the positive part is
monotone in the matrix order, giving a coefficient at most (1-p).
Section 9.2 Question 1 asks about the bound with the Hermitian Doeblin
coefficient.
