## RT use and boundary

[RT IV §108](../../docs/develop/theory/ARITHMETIC_HOLOGRAPHIC_RT_IV.md#108-original-channel-bounds-from-one-group-translation-invariant-gram) uses finite-group representation methods as intermediate tools. Its central-partner D8/Q8 argument explicitly proves the eight-coordinate regular decomposition, invariant tensor projection and product-state overlap needed for an original-channel bound. The source's irreducibly covariant channels act on the irreducible representation dimension; their channel formulas are not substituted for the original C8-to-C4 product channel or its fibre-block complete norm.

The RT theorem assumes that one correct-output Gram has the specified regular translation symmetry and that the original partner operation is the central involution. It permits arbitrary original leakage and proves a sharp half-error bound with physical attainers. The noncentral-reflection action, nonsymmetric residual classes and unrestricted quarter optimum are outside that conclusion. No general representation theorem or exhaustive literature-priority claim is attributed to the RT derivation.
