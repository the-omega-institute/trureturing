## Source statement

> No complete cycle $C_{n}$, except for $C_{3}, C_{4}$, has the instantaneous uniform mixing property under the continuous-time quantum walk model.

The C21 exclusion is partial progress on the Ahmadi–Belk–Tamon–Wendler conjecture. The conjecture for all cycles of length at least five remains outside this result.
