## Source statement

> Given that uniform mixing results have already been proven for even cycles and odd prime cycles, we identify $C_{21}$ as the next open case. Our techniques of constructing and utilizing Gröbner bases of cyclic $n$-roots prove to be too computationally heavy for these larger values of $n$. Consequently, we do not employ these techniques for the $C_{21}$ case and recognize that other methods are needed for the proof.

The C21 exclusion is partial progress on the Ahmadi–Belk–Tamon–Wendler conjecture. The conjecture for all cycles of length at least five remains outside this result.
