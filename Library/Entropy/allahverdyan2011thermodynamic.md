# Exact qubit cooling over bounded reservoir spectra

With a zero target gap the initial qubit is maximally mixed. The optimizer uses bath levels at the two energy endpoints with equal multiplicities. Inverting the population bound gives the binary bandwidth formula after reducing an independent pair of records to maximally mixed qubit preparation.
