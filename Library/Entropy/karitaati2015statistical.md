# Preinjectivity and boundary determination

## Locator

DOI: 10.1007/s10955-015-1281-2.
Source: https://arxiv.org/html/1311.2319v3, Theorem 22, proof immediately
before equation (34).

Theorem 22 concerns preservation of the entropy of shift-invariant
probability measures by a preinjective factor map from a shift of finite
type onto a shift. In its proof, finite-type gluing produces a finite
splice with the same complete image; preinjectivity makes the splice
equal to the original configuration. Thus boundary and image patterns
determine the interior pattern.

The repository uses this classical mechanism with two outer collars on
the integer full shift. Its finite-family encoding allows arbitrary
alphabets; its complete-output fiber finiteness and bound
card(A)^(2(k+1)) require a finite alphabet and follow by generic counting.
These exact statements are repository deductions, not statements
attributed to the cited passage. No sharpness, effective inversion or
literature novelty is asserted.

Corollary 21 assumes a strongly irreducible shift of finite type for its
maximum-entropy preimage conclusion. Its full-shift specialization states
that a cellular automaton is surjective if and only if it preserves the
uniform Bernoulli measure. Neither that additional hypothesis nor a
balance certificate is a premise of the repository's complete-fiber bound.
