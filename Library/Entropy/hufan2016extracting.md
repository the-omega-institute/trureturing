# Hu and Fan, Extracting quantum coherence via steering

## Verified locator

Equation (6), p. 2 of arXiv v2, defines B-side measurement-induced disturbance as the infimum of $D(\rho\|(\mathrm{id}_A\otimes\Lambda_B)(\rho))$ over rank-one projective measurements $\Lambda_B$ fixing $\rho_B$. This includes all basis choices within degenerate eigenspaces. With the paper's base-two quantum relative entropy, swapping $A$ and $B$ and multiplying by $\ln 2$ gives the repository's diagonal discord in nats by the entropy-gain identity for pinching. Theorem 1, Eq. (8), p. 4, bounds steering-induced coherence by B-side measurement-induced disturbance.
