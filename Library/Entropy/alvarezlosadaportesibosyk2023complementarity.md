# Alvarez, Losada, Portesi and Bosyk, Complementarity between quantum coherence and mixedness

Y. Alvarez, M. Losada, M. Portesi and G. M. Bosyk, Communications in Theoretical Physics 75(5), 055102 (2023). No arXiv version was found. The text below is read from the journal PDF; its text layer keeps the prose and garbles the mathematical symbols, so the formulas are transcribed from the prose and the equation layout.

Section 2.2 defines, for a pure state, the coherence vector $\mu(|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|)=(|\langle0|\psi\rangle|^2,\dots,|\langle d-1|\psi\rangle|^2)$ (Eq. (9)), the set of pure-state decompositions of $\rho$ (Eq. (10)), and
$$D^{\rm psd}(\rho)=\Big\{\sum_kq_k\,\mu^\downarrow(|\psi_k\rangle\langle\psi_k|):\ \rho=\sum_kq_k|\psi_k\rangle\langle\psi_k|\Big\}\qquad(11)$$
with $\mu^\downarrow$ the decreasingly ordered vector. Then:

> the coherence vector of the state ρ, denoted as ν(ρ), is defined as the supremum (with respect to the majorization relation) of the set

$D^{\rm psd}(\rho)$, that is $\nu(\rho)=\bigvee D^{\rm psd}(\rho)$ (Eq. (12)). Section 2.3 uses the decreasingly ordered spectrum $\lambda(\rho)$.

The conjecture, at the end of Section 3.4 ("Bounds for qutrit systems: numerical results"):

> Finally, we state a conjecture for the greatest component of ν(ρ) ⊗ λ(ρ) that allows us to obtain a nonoptimal but nontrivial bound for the tensor-product majorization relation, which depends only on the dimension of the Hilbert space.
> Conjecture 5. Let $\rho\in\mathcal D(\mathcal H_d)$. Then $\nu_1(\rho)\lambda_1(\rho)\ge\frac1d$. (20)
> […] For d = 2 the conjecture is clearly valid, whereas for d = 3 we have numerical evidence of its validity.
