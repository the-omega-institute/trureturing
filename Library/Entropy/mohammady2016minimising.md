# Correlated records and the rank criterion for erasure

Section 4.1 considers a possibly correlated object and auxiliary with an independent thermal reservoir. Its object–auxiliary erasure examples include nonorthogonal records. The retained-average-record condition in the packet leads to the support and rank argument for a faithful bath.
