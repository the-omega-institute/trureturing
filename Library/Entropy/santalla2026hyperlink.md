## Source statements

Eq. (5): "Let us define the entanglement hyperlinks (EHL) of a pure state in analogy to the inclusion-exclusion principle, J_I ≡ Σ_{A⊆I} (−1)^{|I|−|A|} S_A."

Appendix B: "Let us put forward our proposal for the reconstruction formula, S_A = Σ_{|I| even} Λ_{|I|,|I∩A|} J_I, (B2) i.e. we sum over all EHL, each of them multiplied by a leg-factor Λ_{2l,p}, which depends on the rank of the EHL, |I| = 2l and the number of legs that the EHL has in block A, p = |I∩A|. If p = 0 or p = 2l then Λ_{2l,p} = 0 … Of course, expressions (B2) and (B3) remain a conjecture at this stage. It is still to be proved that the leg-factors Λ_{2l,p} exist and do not depend on N. If they exist, then complementarity symmetry imposes that Λ_{2l,p} = Λ_{2l,2l−p}."

(B5): "Λ_{2,1} = −1/2, Λ_{4,1} = 1/4, Λ_{4,2} = 1/2, Λ_{6,1} = −1/2, Λ_{6,2} = −1, Λ_{6,3} = −5/4, Λ_{8,1} = 17/8, Λ_{8,2} = 17/4, Λ_{8,3} = 47/8, Λ_{8,4} = 13/2."

Appendix B: "We should stress that the validity of Eq. (B2), with leg-factors that do not depend on N, has only been checked up to N = 9, and remains conjectural for larger sizes." … "Since all three reconstruction operations are again linear, we may claim that Eq. (5) has several different inverses. This apparent contradiction is due to the fact that we are considering vectors S with a special symmetry, i.e.: those corresponding to pure states, in which S_A = S_Ā."
