## Binary case

The two-label specialization in bits is 2e ≤ H(J|Y) ≤ h₂(e). The upper bound is binary Fano and the lower bound is the chord of binary entropy. The formalization identifies the posterior expression with the repository finite conditional entropy and constructs the equality cases with a fair label and finite stochastic views. Erasure attains the lower entropy bound at fixed error and a symmetric flip attains the upper bound.
