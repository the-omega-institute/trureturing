# Ordinary domination growth

The journal source is Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.7.6.
Pages 1–2 define ordinary dominating subsets and their polynomial weights;
at z=1 every distinct subset contributes one. Equations (15)–(17) concern
joint m,n growth, and the conjecture immediately after (22) is on p. 19.
The arXiv v1 version, https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08053v1, puts the conjecture
on p. 20. These versions have passage-level agreement at the target but
are not byte-identical.

The repository proof uses direct finite boundary repair and block tiling.
It does not import an asserted grid limit or a fixed-width transfer count.
