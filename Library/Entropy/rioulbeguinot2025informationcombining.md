# Rioul–Béguinot on Arimoto information combining

O. Rioul and J. Béguinot, “The role of Mrs. Gerber's Lemma for evaluating
the information leakage of secret sharing schemes”, Barron Festschrift
(Springer), HAL hal-05074668v1 (2025).

## Locator

Source: https://cea.hal.science/hal-05074668v1, §4, p. 20,
Proposition 1 and Remark 6.

Proposition 1:

> Proposition 1 It is easily checked numerically (see Figure 3) that: … if α ∈ [−1, 1.5] then u_α is decreasing; …

Remark 6:

> Remark 6 This Proposition answers the open question left as a conjecture by Hirche [20, Conjecture IV.6.], where we established that α̂ = 3/2 in his conjecture.

Their support is a numerical sign plot produced with SageMath (Figure 3);
no proof is given there. The proposition and remark supply the prior
numerical statement of the threshold $\hat\alpha=3/2$, without proving
the sign throughout the real parameter and bias domains.
