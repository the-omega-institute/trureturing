# Two-qubit marginal spectra for a fixed joint spectrum

Quantum Information and Computation 4(1), 12–26.

Equation (2), with necessity in §3 and sufficiency in Appendix B, gives the compatibility inequalities for the minimum eigenvalues of the two one-qubit marginals and the full sorted two-qubit spectrum. For the repeated spectrum with entries (1+r)/4 and (1−r)/4 each appearing twice, the inequalities give the triangular region of marginal biases s,t ≥ 0 with s+t ≤ r. [Bravyi, equation (2)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0301014v2)

The Lean proof obtains the needed region directly from the trace pairing of a rank-two projection with the sum of two lifted rank-one projections. It builds the extremal projection and carries the marginal statement to a unitary erasure that retains the average record state. The two-qubit spectral bound and its application have the following related finite-reservoir sources. [Reeb–Wolf, §3.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.4352v3) · [Mohammady–Mohseni–Omar, §2.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.02062v2)
