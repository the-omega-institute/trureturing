# Energy range in repeated cooling

Theorem 1 gives a qubit bound and a higher-dimensional geometric majorization bound. Theorem 2 treats attainment through repeated cycles. The range budget in that protocol is per machine cycle. For fresh independent baths collected into one product bath, the ranges add.

Appendix Lemma 4 gives partial-trace majorization. Eq. (43) forms successive spectral blocks of size equal to the discarded environment dimension.
