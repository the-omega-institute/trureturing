# Tarabunga, Frau, Haug, Tirrito and Piroli, A nonstabilizerness monotone from stabilizerness asymmetry

P. S. Tarabunga, M. Frau, T. Haug, E. Tirrito and L. Piroli, Quantum Science and Technology 10(4) (2025) 045026, DOI 10.1088/2058-9565/adfd0d; arXiv:2411.05766v1. Quotations are from the arXiv v1 TeX source.

Section V.C writes an $N$-qubit stabilizer state in the standard form $2^{-k/2}\sum_x(-1)^{x^\top Qx}i^{c^\top x}|Rx+t\rangle$ with $Q=Q_{\rm d}+Q'$ upper triangular, $R$ of rank $k$ and $t\in\mathbb F_2^N/\operatorname{Im}R$. For fixed $Q',c,R$ the states indexed by $(Q_{\rm d},t)$ form an orthonormal basis, and $p_{Q',c,R}(Q_{\rm d},t)$ is the distribution of squared overlaps with a state $\psi$; $q_R$ is the same distribution with $Q'=0$, $c=0$ for the state $\psi'$ of absolute amplitudes. Eq. (69), proved for integer $\alpha>1$, states $\min_{Q',c}S_\alpha(p_{Q',c,R})\ge S_\alpha(q_R)$. The section continues:

> Note that, while we are unable to prove Eq. (69) for the case $\alpha=1$, we conjecture that the lower bound remains valid, as we have not found any counter-examples.
