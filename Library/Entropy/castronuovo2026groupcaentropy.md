# Topological entropy of higher-dimensional group cellular automata

Theorem 6 (p. 21) gives two alternatives on the full shift of a finite group in
dimension at least two. Equicontinuity comes with zero topological entropy, and
sensitivity to initial conditions comes with infinite topological entropy. The
second research direction in §9 (p. 32) asks:

> It would be interesting to understand whether the dichotomy extends to cellular automata over finite monoids, and more generally which algebraic constraints force it.

The finite-monoid extension of Theorem 6 is the first clause. Table 2 (p. 31)
also conjectures equicontinuity in its monoid row at zero entropy. The same
full-shift Boolean OR map has zero entropy and fails equicontinuity, giving a
counterexample to that implication as well. The broader algebraic classification
and the possible finite positive entropy values remain open here.

The bounded literature screen for the registered question found the source v1,
the authors' [strong-transitivity preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22365v1),
the [freezing CA framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.06751v4), the
[update-family setting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.01917v2), and a
[general cellular automata entropy result](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0703167).
The screen found no settlement of the finite-monoid question in those sources.
