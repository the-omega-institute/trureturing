# Arias, Di Giulio, Keski-Vakkuri and Tonni, majorization in charge sectors

R. Arias, G. Di Giulio, E. Keski-Vakkuri and E. Tonni, arXiv:2301.02117v1 [cond-mat.stat-mech] (5 January 2023); JHEP 03 (2023) 175. Quotations are from the v1 TeX source, with cross-references omitted.

Charge sectors (Section 6): for a system with a $U(1)$ charge $Q=Q_A\oplus Q_B$ in an eigenstate of $Q$, $[\rho_A,Q_A]=0$, and

> $\rho_A=\bigoplus_q p(q)\, \rho_A(q)\,,$

where each block $\rho_A(q)$ corresponds to an eigenvalue $q$ of $Q_A$ and $p(q)=\mathrm{Tr}\,\Pi_q\rho_A$ is the probability of finding $q$ in a measurement of $Q_A$.

The question (Section 7, "Symmetry-resolved related issues"):

> For instance, given two density matrices such that $\rho\succ \sigma$, we may ask whether this majorization order survives once we decompose $\rho$ and $\sigma$ according to (6.1). One can try to diagnose this aspect using $M_A^{(n)}(q)$, given that these quantities are entanglement monotones also in a fixed charge sector. We leave this analysis for future investigations.
