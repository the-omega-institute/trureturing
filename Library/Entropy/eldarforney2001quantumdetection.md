# Eldar and Forney on the square-root measurement

## Verified locator

IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 47(3), 858–872 (March 2001).
Theorem 4 of the arXiv revision gives the square-root measurement for
equiprobable pure states generated by a finite abelian unitary group.
It proves minimum detection error and uses the Fourier transform of the
generating group, with reciprocal singular values set to zero at zero
singular values. [Journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/18.915636)
[ArXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0005132)

## Application to binary pure records

For the canonical binary word ensemble, the group is the product of
two-element groups, and its Fourier transform is the Walsh transform.
The Lean development proves the finite optimum using a diagonal upper
bound and a normalized Walsh measurement. It proves the labelled
coinciding-state endpoint and transports the attainable success set to
the original product carrier. The product formula allows different pairs and carriers at each position. The separate finite-scale limit for a common overlap uses the
logarithmic slope of the resulting scalar success curve.
