# Entropy gain under block pinching

## Verified locator

https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0612146

Section II.A, equations (2)–(4), defines relative entropy of superposition as the minimum
relative entropy to the block-diagonal states. The difference of the
entropy after block pinching and the entropy before block pinching
is the resulting measure. Equation (5) and supplementary Proposition 3 of
[Resource theory of coherence based on positive-operator-valued measures](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.00018),
by Bischof, Kampermann and Bruß, give the block-projective entropy identity.
Their Lemma 1, equation (7), and Appendix C extend the construction to POVMs
via a projective embedding.
