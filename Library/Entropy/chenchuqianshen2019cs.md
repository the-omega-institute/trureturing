# Chen, Chu, Qian and Shen, Separability of completely symmetric states in a multipartite system

L. Chen, D. Chu, L. Qian and Y. Shen, Phys. Rev. A 99, 032312 (2019); arXiv:1812.04182v4. Quotations are from the v4 TeX source.

A state is separable if it is a convex combination of pure product states. Writing a state on $(\mathbb C^N)^{\otimes d}$ as $\rho=\sum_{I,J}\rho_{I,J}|I\rangle\langle J|$ with multi-indices $I,J$, the state is completely symmetric when $\rho_{\pi(I,J)}=\rho_{I,J}$ for every permutation $\pi$ of the $2d$ index positions; in the multipartite system, separable means fully separable. The Conclusion lists among the future problems:

> Are multi-qutrit CS states separable?
