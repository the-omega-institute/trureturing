# The all-law transport question

## Verified locator

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2611.13904v1#S8

Source: arXiv 2611.13904, version 1, section 8; source file
`rucha4-11-26.tex`, lines 1327–1330. The interpretation fixes the displayed
classical target and replaces entropy by unrestricted non-invariant entropy
and pressure by the all-law calligraphic pressure, extending the comparison
law to every Borel probability.

The full shift has alphabet 1 through d and coordinates 1,2,... . Its metric
is zero on the diagonal and 2 raised to the negative first differing positive
coordinate otherwise. Conditional entropy uses the entire sigma-algebra
pulled back from Borel sets by the shift, as in equations (1)/(18), with no
extra integrability hypothesis from equation (19). Pressure is the supremum
over all laws in equation (10), not either restricted pressure in (9)/(12).
The target is the unique invariant classical KS variational equilibrium of
the same real Holder potential, as in equation (2) and its uniqueness convention.
The finite real bound may depend on d and the potential, with no sign condition.

The canonical candidate uses d=2 and the constant potential minus log 2.
The comparison law has a fair first symbol and a deterministic entire future.
The classical target is the actual fair Bernoulli law, whose KS variational
existence and uniqueness are supplied by the formal measurable-generator
argument. These descriptions do not substitute a finite-tail model, a
Ruelle-label predicate or an arbitrary target for the classical equilibrium.
