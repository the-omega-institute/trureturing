## Conjecture 3

Printed p. 11, Section 3.1:

> For every $(C_u,C_v)\in[0,1]\times[0,1]$, there exists $\theta(C_u,C_v)$, such that for every $p>\theta(C_u,C_v)$ the achieving test-channels $P_{U|X}$ and $P_{V|Y}$ are BSC with parameters $\alpha=h_b^{-1}(1-C_u)$ and $\beta=h_b^{-1}(1-C_v)$ respectively.

The source uses bits and the binary entropy inverse on [0,1/2].
The crossover is restricted to [0,1/2], with a threshold strictly below 1/2;
optimality means attainment and does not mean uniqueness.
The quantification includes arbitrary finite output alphabets and all budgets in [0,1].
