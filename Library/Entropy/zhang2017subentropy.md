## Question

> A similarly question can be asked for the strong subadditivity of subentropy: Q(ρ ABC) + Q(ρ B) ⩽ Q(ρ AB) + Q(ρ BC).

The quantifiers range over finite-dimensional tripartite density operators,
with the three displayed reduced states obtained by partial trace. The
question concerns subentropy, not von Neumann entropy.
