Bowen's article appeared in Transactions of the American Mathematical Society,
volume 153 (1971), pp. 401–414. This reference acknowledges the classical
factor-entropy framework; no novelty is claimed for entropy preservation by
uniformly finite-to-one compact factors.

The repository proves its exact compact-uniform-space statement independently:
a continuous surjective semiconjugacy, continuous source dynamics, Hausdorff
target, and an explicit uniform natural-number bound on finite fibers imply
equality of covering entropy. Its block-cover construction bounds the lift
cost over r blocks by M^r and sends the entropy cost log(M)/n to zero.

The formal statement includes empty spaces, infinite entropy and a source
that need not be Hausdorff. These exact hypotheses and endpoint conventions
are supplied by the Lean proof, not asserted to be a literal quotation from
