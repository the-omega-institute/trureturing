# Derksen–Friedland–Lim–Wang, Theoretical and computational aspects of entanglement

H. Derksen, S. Friedland, L.-H. Lim and L. Wang, arXiv:1705.07160v1 (the only version). Quotations are from the v1 TeX source.

For a tensor $\mathcal T\in\mathbb F^{\mathbf n}$ the nuclear norm $\|\mathcal T\|_{1,\mathbb F}$ is the minimum of $\sum_i\prod_j\|\mathbf x_{i,j}\|$ over decompositions $\mathcal T=\sum_i\otimes_j\mathbf x_{i,j}$, and $\alpha(\mathbf n,\mathbb F)$ is its maximum over unit tensors. A density tensor is the $2d$-mode tensor of a positive semidefinite trace-one matrix on $\mathbb C^{\mathbf n}$. Lemma `maxinsep` bounds $\log_2\|\mathcal A\|_1\le2\log_2\alpha(\mathbf n,\mathbb C)$ for every density tensor, with equality for $\mathcal T\otimes\overline{\mathcal T}$ when $\mathcal T$ is maximally entangled, and the paper states:

> We conjecture that equality in (6.4) implies that $\mathcal A$ is a pure density state corresponding to maximum entangled state.
