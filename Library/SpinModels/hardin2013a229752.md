## Source statement

> Number of defective 4-colorings of an n X 5 0..3 array connected horizontally, vertically, diagonally and antidiagonally with exactly two mistakes, and colors introduced in row-major 0..3 order.
>
> Empirical: a(n) = 9*a(n-1) - 33*a(n-2) + 63*a(n-3) - 66*a(n-4) + 36*a(n-5) - 8*a(n-6) for n>11.
>
> Empirical g.f.: x*(12 + 1732*x - 9355*x^2 + 13758*x^3 + 3670*x^4 - 20791*x^5 + 10370*x^6 + 686*x^7 - 1124*x^8 - 440*x^9 + 208*x^10) / ((1 - x)^3*(1 - 2*x)^3). - _Colin Barker_, Sep 21 2018

## Mathematical scope

The reading index n counts rows at fixed width 5. The colours are introduced
in row-major order. Recurrence subtraction is interpreted in the integers.
