## Source statement

> Number of defective 3-colorings of a 3 X n 0..2 array connected horizontally and antidiagonally with exactly two mistakes, and colors introduced in row-major 0..2 order.

> Empirical: a(n) = 15*a(n-1) - 81*a(n-2) + 185*a(n-3) - 162*a(n-4) + 60*a(n-5) - 8*a(n-6) for n > 10.

## Mathematical scope

The array has 3 rows and n columns. A defect is a monochromatic horizontal
or antidiagonal bond with endpoints in the array. Canonical colour introduction
uses the literal row-major ordinal i*n+j. Transfer sweeps columns and counts
labelled arrays before division by the six global colour permutations.
The exact budget is two. There are 15 equality-and-budget states, with
10 inherited from the frozen one-defect sibling. The forward relation has
degree 6 and starts at power 4; the recurrence holds for every
n at least 11. This concerns finite combinatorial counts.
