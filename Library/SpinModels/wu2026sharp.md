## Relation to the formal statement

The proof of Corollary 3.11 establishes the norm estimate on positive inputs in the finite signed-spin models. At β = 0 the state is tracial. For p = 3 and r = 4, the critical time is ½ log(3/2), with one-generator damping √(2/3). On the signed two-mode slice this is the degree damping of the endpoint map. Raising the normalized tracial norm inequality to the twelfth power gives the moment inequality in `A1_quartic_contractivity`.
