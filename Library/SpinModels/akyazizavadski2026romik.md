# Boundary of the rational nested-arch cylinder probabilities

The paper studies the completely packed O(1) loop representation of critical bond percolation. In Theorem 1.2 and (1.1), for each fixed integer $m\geq1$, the probability of $m$ nested arches on the cylinder of circumference $2N$ equals a rational function $R_m(N)$ for every integer $N>m$.

Conjecture 1.3, (1.2), asks:

> For every $k\geq 2$, the rational function $R_k$ from Theorem 1.2 satisfies $R_k(k)=R_{k-1}(k)$.

Corollary 4.2, (4.4), gives the probability as the Pfaffian of the inverse Kasteleyn matrix restricted to the prescribed ordered endpoints. In (5.25) the authors explicitly use $n=N-1$. Lemma 4.3, (4.8)–(4.11), writes the matrix in white-black blocks with zero-based indices $r,s=0,\ldots,m-1$. Equation (4.12) expresses the signed $p$ factor as a constant times the product of $(N^2-a^2)/(4N^2-a^2)$ for $1\le a<c$. Proposition 5.1, (5.1)–(5.3), gives the full bracketed formula for $H_N(c_1,c_2)$, for $N\ge1$ and $1\le c_2<c_1\le2N$, including its outer paired product and the inner finite sum. The source observes after (5.25) that the boundary is regular because its denominator factors do not vanish.

At $N=k$ the final white summation indices lie in $[k+1,2k]$. The paired numerator at $a=k$ kills all corresponding $p$ and $H$ terms and the delta in (4.10) contributes the unit edge. The normalized Pfaffian reduces to the first $2(k-1)$ endpoints. On the infinite integer tail, the published probability-to-Pfaffian identity determines the rational function uniquely.

Equation (2.8) identifies the probabilities of $F_N$ and $F_{N-1}$ with $1/A_N$, where $A_N$ counts alternating-sign matrices. This supplies the common enumerative value by the published open-range identification at size $k-1$. The formal algebraic result assumes the open-range probability-to-Pfaffian coincidence and proves the common smaller Pfaffian, the two regularity assertions and the two function identifications. The enumerative identity is attributed to the paper.
