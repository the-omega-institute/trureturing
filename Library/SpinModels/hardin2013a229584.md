## Source statement

> Number of defective 3-colorings of an n X 6 0..2 array connected horizontally and antidiagonally with exactly one mistake, and colors introduced in row-major 0..2 order.

> Empirical: a(n) = 50*a(n-1) - 805*a(n-2) + 4662*a(n-3) - 12150*a(n-4) + 14580*a(n-5) - 6561*a(n-6).

The offset is 1. The literal counts at n=1 through seven are
40, 1620, 49848, 1375152, 35690460, 890824020, 21639043284.

## Mathematical scope

A defect is a monochromatic bond. The in-row bonds join adjacent columns;
the between-row bonds join the upper-right cell to the lower-left cell.
Canonical colours first occur in increasing order. Every nonempty one-defect
array of this width uses at least two colours; each colour orbit has six members.
The continuation classes retain the equality pattern on the outgoing row tail
and the residual defect budget. There are 32 such classes.
The finite certificate verifies transition counts on canonical boundary patterns.
Colour permutations act bijectively on the next rows and preserve defects and
boundary classes, extending these counts to all labelled boundaries.
Forward weights give the annihilating relation; the first registered scalar
indices are checked separately from vector propagation.
