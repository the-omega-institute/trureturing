## Source statement

> Number of defective 4-colorings of an n X n 0..3 array connected horizontally, vertically, diagonally and antidiagonally with exactly one mistake, and colors introduced in row-major 0..3 order.

> Empirical (for n>=9): a(n) = 9*a(n-1) - 33*a(n-2) + 63*a(n-3) - 66*a(n-4) + 36*a(n-5) - 8*a(n-6). - _Vaclav Kotesovec_, Feb 14 2014

## Mathematical scope

A mistake is a monochromatic bond. The four bond orientations are horizontal, vertical, diagonal and antidiagonal. Colour labels first appear in increasing order along the row-major cell order. These are canonical counts, with no identification of different cell positions.

The displayed recurrence statements are empirical assertions in the source. The corresponding integer identities are derived here from the literal finite-array count. This note makes no claim about the source's generating functions or about literature priority.

The source attributes the empirical recurrence to Vaclav Kotesovec.
