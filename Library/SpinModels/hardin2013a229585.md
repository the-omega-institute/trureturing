## Source statement

> Number of defective 3-colorings of an n X 7 0..2 array connected horizontally and antidiagonally with exactly one mistake, and colors introduced in row-major 0..2 order.

> Empirical: a(n) = 84*a(n-1) - 2466*a(n-2) + 31428*a(n-3) - 206469*a(n-4) + 750384*a(n-5) - 1513404*a(n-6) + 1574640*a(n-7) - 656100*a(n-8).

## Mathematical scope

The reading index in #30580 is n at least 9; this index is separate
from the verbatim source quotation, which states no starting index.
The offset is 1, n counts rows and the fixed width is seven columns.
The first nine canonical counts are 96, 5724, 264300, 11035044,
435326724, 16551428868, 613195191972, 22285439501940, 798023885879412.

The in-row bonds join adjacent columns. The between-row bonds join an
upper-right cell to the lower-left cell in the next row. A defect is one
monochromatic bond. There are no periodic boundary bonds.
The coordinate change x = i+j, y = i sends these bonds to the two unit
directions of a square lattice. With positive antiferromagnetic Potts
coupling, exactly one defect means exactly one unsatisfied bond.

Canonical colours first occur in increasing row-major order. A nonempty
one-defect width-seven array uses at least two colours; each colour orbit
has six labelled members. The outgoing boundary has six cells. Its equality
pattern and remaining budget give 72 classes: 16 with budget one, and 56
with budget zero. The latter include both proper boundaries and boundaries
with one adjacent equality.

The finite transition histograms count labelled next rows at an exact
remaining budget. Colour permutations reconstruct arbitrary labelled
boundaries from canonical boundary patterns. For row weights W(t) = v T^t,
the certificate uses W(10) = sum(c(j) W(2+j), j = 0..7), where c is the
FORMULA coefficient list in increasing shift order. The scalar relations
at row counts 9 and 10 are separate from this vector propagation.
The arbitrary-length counting bridge and forward-shift theorem are the
frozen declarations in `StripDefects/DefectTransfer`.

The OEIS source labels the formula empirical. Agreement with finitely many
sequence terms does not establish its universal statement. This source
note supplies no claim about recurrence minimality, all widths, infinite
strips or a thermodynamic limit.
