## Source statement

> Number of defective 3-colorings of a 4 X n 0..2 array connected horizontally and antidiagonally with exactly two mistakes, and colors introduced in row-major 0..2 order.

> Empirical: a(n) = 27*a(n-1) - 288*a(n-2) + 1557*a(n-3) - 4644*a(n-4) + 8073*a(n-5) - 8343*a(n-6) + 5022*a(n-7) - 1620*a(n-8) + 216*a(n-9) for n > 17.

## Mathematical scope

The array has 4 rows and n columns. A defect is a monochromatic horizontal
or antidiagonal bond with endpoints in the array. Canonical colour introduction
uses the literal row-major ordinal i*n+j. Transfer sweeps columns and counts
labelled arrays before division by the six global colour permutations.
The exact budget is two. There are 42 equality-and-budget states, with
28 inherited from the frozen one-defect sibling. The forward relation has
degree 9 and starts at power 8; the recurrence holds for every
n at least 18. This concerns finite combinatorial counts.
