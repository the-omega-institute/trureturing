## Source statement

> Number of defective 3-colorings of an nX3 0..2 array connected horizontally, diagonally and antidiagonally with exactly two mistakes, and colors introduced in row-major 0..2 order.

> Empirical: a(n) = 15*a(n-1) -81*a(n-2) +185*a(n-3) -162*a(n-4) +60*a(n-5) -8*a(n-6) for n>7.

## Mathematical scope

The reading index in #31121 is n at least 8, separately from the verbatim
source formula. The array has n rows and 3 columns and exactly 2
monochromatic horizontal, diagonal and antidiagonal bonds. Canonical colour
introduction follows literal row-major order. These are finite combinatorial counts.
