## Source statement

> Number of defective 3-colorings of an n X 7 0..2 array connected horizontally and antidiagonally with exactly two mistakes, and colors introduced in row-major 0..2 order.

> Empirical: a(n) = 126*a(n-1) - 6345*a(n-2) + 165456*a(n-3) - 2474469*a(n-4) + 22918302*a(n-5) - 138045627*a(n-6) + 554666940*a(n-7) - 1494608922*a(n-8) + 2659094568*a(n-9) - 2986698420*a(n-10) + 1913187600*a(n-11) - 531441000*a(n-12) for n > 14.

## Mathematical scope

The offset is 1 and n counts rows of a seven-column array. In-row bonds join
adjacent columns. Between-row bonds join a cell to the cell one column to
its left in the next row. The strip has open boundaries. Exactly two bonds
have equal colours at their endpoints.

The coordinate change x = i+j, y = i sends these bonds to the two unit
directions of a square lattice. For positive antiferromagnetic Potts
coupling the two monochromatic bonds are the two unsatisfied bonds.

The first occurrence of each colour follows that of every smaller colour
in row-major order. Any nonempty array with two defects uses at least two
colours and has six labelled arrays in its colour orbit.

The recurrence concerns the canonical counts from n = 15, with
subtraction in the integers. The source labels the formula empirical.
