## Source statement

> Number of defective 3-colorings of an n X 4 0..2 array connected horizontally, diagonally and antidiagonally with exactly two mistakes, and colors introduced in row-major 0..2 order.

> Empirical: a(n) = 21*a(n-1) - 159*a(n-2) + 511*a(n-3) - 636*a(n-4) + 336*a(n-5) - 64*a(n-6) for n>9.

## Mathematical scope

The reading index in #31121 is n at least 10, separately from the verbatim
source formula. The array has n rows and 4 columns and exactly 2
monochromatic horizontal, diagonal and antidiagonal bonds. Canonical colour
introduction follows literal row-major order. These are finite combinatorial counts.
