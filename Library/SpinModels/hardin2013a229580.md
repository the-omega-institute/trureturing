## Source statement

> Number of defective 3-colorings of an n X 2 0..2 array connected horizontally and
> antidiagonally with exactly one mistake, and colors introduced in row-major 0..2 order.

> Empirical: a(n) = 8*a(n-1) - 16*a(n-2) for n>3.

The offset is 1. The inequality n>3 means every integer n at least four.
The literal counts at n=1 through seven are 1, 6, 40, 224, 1152, 5632, 26624.
At n=3 the proposed right-hand side is 32 while the count is 40.

## Mathematical scope

A defect is a monochromatic bond. The in-row bonds join adjacent columns;
the between-row bonds join the upper-right cell to the lower-left cell.
Canonical colours first occur in increasing order. For at least two rows,
every one-defect array uses at least two colours and its colour orbit has six members.
The labelled continuation transfer on the residual-budget classes is
[[4,4],[0,4]], with initial weights [6,3] and terminal weights [0,1].
Its polynomial is (x-4)^2. The first canonical count requires a separate
one-colour orbit, which explains the recurrence's starting index.
