## Source statement

> Number of defective 3-colorings of a 4 X n 0..2 array connected horizontally and antidiagonally with exactly one mistake, and colors introduced in row-major 0..2 order.

> Empirical: a(n) = 18*a(n-1) - 111*a(n-2) + 282*a(n-3) - 333*a(n-4) + 180*a(n-5) - 36*a(n-6) for n > 12.

## Mathematical scope

from the verbatim source quotation. A monochromatic bond contributes one
mistake. The array has 4 rows and n columns; the canonical order is the
literal row-major order i*n+j. Transfer proceeds by columns, without
changing that order. There are 28 equality-and-budget classes.
The exact budget is 1. The certified forward relation starts at
power 6 and has degree 6. This is a finite combinatorial statement,
with no thermodynamic-limit assertion.
