# Božović, *On k-limited domination: complexity and Sierpiński graphs*

D. Božović's arXiv:2610.01584v1 studies limited domination in Sierpiński graphs. Section 6, Conjecture 12 (p. 9) states verbatim:

> Let n, m, and k be integers such that n ≥ 1 and 1 ≤ k ≤ m − 1. Then γ_k^L(S(n, m)) = (m − k)·m^(n−1).

The graph and parameter definitions used by the formal statement are given in Section 2. The exact formula is proved here by a recursive coloring constant on linking edges and a lower-bound charging argument over the base cliques. The source's Theorems 10 and 11 are the special cases k = 1 and m = 3.
