# Archer and Laudone, pattern avoidance and the fundamental bijection

The paper studies permutations avoiding a pattern of length three whose images under the fundamental
bijection, and under its iterates, also avoid that pattern, and permutations fixed by an iterate of the
fundamental bijection; it closes with several conjectured enumerations.

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2407.06338

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06338v1

- Locator: Section 2, the fundamental bijection θ writes a permutation in standard cycle form, each cycle
  beginning with its largest element and the cycles ordered by increasing largest element, and erases the
  parentheses.
- Locator: Section 4, Conjecture 4.5: for n ≥ 2, t_n^2(132) = k^3 + 3k^2 + 2k − 1 (n = 3k),
  k^3 + 4k^2 + 4k (n = 3k + 1), k^3 + 5k^2 + 7k + 2 (n = 3k + 2), with the values 3n − 4, 2n − 1, n + 2
  and 5 for k = 3, 4, 5 and k ≥ 6, where t_n^k(σ) counts the π with π, θ(π), …, θ^k(π) all avoiding σ.
- Locator: Section 5, Conjecture 5.6: for σ ∈ {231, 312}, F_σ^3(x) = 1/(1 − x − x^2 − 2x^3),
  F_σ^4(x) = 1/(1 − x − x^2 − 2x^4 − x^5 − x^6) and F_σ^5(x) = 1/(1 − x − x^2), where F_σ^k counts the
  σ-avoiders fixed by θ^k.

## Reading of the statement

For σ ∈ {231, 312} the numbers of σ-avoiders fixed by θ^3 are 1, 1, 2, 5, 9, 18, 37, 73, 146, 293, 585
for n = 0, …, 10. The numbers t_n^2(132) for n = 1, …, 10 are 1, 2, 5, 9, 15, 23, 32, 44, 59, 75.
