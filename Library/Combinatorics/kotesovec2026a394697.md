# Toroidal king peak entropy

OEIS A394697, revision 24, defines a(n) as the maximum coefficient of
the independence polynomial of the n by n toroidal king graph, with
offset 3. Its conjecture is the claim quoted above. OEIS A247413,
revision 41, identifies the constant as the limit of
A063443(m)^(1/m²). A063443, revision 104, counts tilings of an m by m
square by unit and two-by-two squares and records the equivalent count
of nonattacking king configurations on the free board of side m-1.

Free king entropy and partition bounds are prior work of Calkin, James
and Purvis, *Counting Kings*, pp. 6–9. Liang,
[arXiv:2505.12776v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12776v2), Proposition 3.1,
establishes the free-rectangle maximum-versus-total entropy mechanism.
Neither that mechanism nor the free-board entropy result is claimed as
new. The bounded qualification in
[issue 27011](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/27011)
also distinguishes cylindrical and periodic-limsup results from the
specified all-square toroidal event. That qualification does not
establish global novelty or priority.
