# Mühlherr and Poullot 2026, acyclic orientations modulo 4

L. Mühlherr and G. Poullot, "Hamiltonicity of graphs of acyclic orientations
and acyclic polynomials", arXiv:2609.02249 (v1 2026-09-02, v2 2026-09-17).

The paper studies the flip graph AO(G) on the acyclic orientations of a graph
and writes ψ(G) for the number of acyclic orientations. At the end of §3.1 it
states:

> In view of the previous example list, we conjecture the following.
> Conjecture 1. For the following graphs G, we have ψ(G) ≡ 2 mod 4, and
> consequently G is not AO-Hamiltonian since G has an even number of edges:
> (1) The grid graphs Gr n,m = P n × P m for m, n ≥ 3 both odd;
> (2) The triangular grid graphs Tr n,m for m, n ≥ 3 both odd;
> (3) The generalized Petersen graphs Pet(n,k) for n even and k odd;
> (4) The complete tripartite graphs K m,n,p for n, m, p not all odd.

and notes that ladder graphs and complete bipartite graphs "were already dealt
with by Savage, Squire and West [SSW93]". The number of acyclic orientations is
(−1)^{|V|} χ_G(−1) = T_G(2, 0), the Tutte polynomial at the q = −1 point of
the Potts model (Stanley 1973).
