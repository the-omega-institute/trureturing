# Recovery from the full subset-sum multiset

The introduction defines the full subset-sum multiset with all indexed subset
occurrences, and the equivalence relation generated by reversing a zero-sum
submultiset. The main theorem, labelled `thm:main` in the source, characterizes
the abelian groups for which these are precisely the fibers of the full-spectrum
map. In particular, the additive group of the reals has this property, since its
only finite-order element is zero, of order one, and one belongs to `O_FS`.

For nonnegative real coefficients the resulting recovery is exact: reversing a
zero-sum submultiset can affect only zero coefficients. This known consequence
is the supplier used in `NonnegativeRecovery`; no originality is claimed for it.
The formal proof uses the least nonempty subset sum and cancellation in the
natural-coefficient additive monoid algebra. Removing one numerical zero removes
the empty-subset occurrence while retaining all other zero-sum occurrences.

The odd-length even-spectrum classification additionally uses the first moment,
complement reflection, and a parity obstruction with zero-coordinate repair.
Those parity-restricted conclusions are separate from this full-spectrum source.

## Verified locator

The cited source is [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2301.04635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04635v2),
Theorem 1.1 (the source's `thm:main`). Its zero-sum sign equivalence scope is
the equivalence generated by reversing zero-sum submultisets; this note uses
that scope for the full-subset-sum recovery statement and its nonnegative-real
consequence.
