# Rowmotion and generalized toggle groups

Lemma 3.17, pp. 14–15 of arXiv:1601.03710v5, characterizes commutation
of interval-closed-set toggles at distinct elements of a finite partially
ordered set. They commute precisely when the elements are incomparable,
or form a covering pair whose lower element is minimal and whose upper
element is maximal.

The corresponding Lean result includes equal elements and extends the
criterion to arbitrary partial orders. Its proof transfers flip legality
at an extremal covering pair and uses an open lower interval for necessity;
neither argument requires a finite carrier or local finiteness.

The claim in the source's sufficiency proof that both extremal flips always
occur needs care. Consider four points with order generated by x < y and
x < z < t. The singleton {t} is order-convex, x is minimal, y is maximal,
and x is covered by y, but adjoining x to {t} is not order-convex because z
is absent. Guard invariance is the property used by the Lean proof; it
does not require unconditional flips.
