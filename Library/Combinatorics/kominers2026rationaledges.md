# Kominers, *Squared edge lengths of regular simplices with rational vertices*

Theorem 1, §§2–3.3, classifies regular simplices of dimension $d$ in $\mathbb Q^m$ by their squared edge length $2a$. Centering the vertices at their rational centroid places them on the unit sphere exactly when $a=(d+1)/d$. In codimension zero, the table then requires $d+1$ to be a square for even $d$. For odd $d$, the square class of $a$ must equal that of $d+1$, so $d$ must be a square. If $d=k^2$ is odd, it is $1$ modulo $4$ and $d+1=k^2+1^2$ is a sum of two squares, satisfying the remaining condition. The formal proof constructs the centered unit vertices by rational similarities and a reflection.
