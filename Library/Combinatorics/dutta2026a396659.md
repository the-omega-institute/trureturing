# Edge-collision walks on a complete directed graph

## Source and conventions

OEIS A396659, revision 16, defines T(n,k) using n labeled vertices and a fixed
starting vertex. Every ordered pair of vertices is an available directed edge,
including self-loops. A qualifying walk visits every vertex, uses pairwise
distinct first k-1 edges, and repeats one of those edges at its final step.
The walks are ordered sequences with a distinguished first position.

The ratio conjecture quantifies over every n at least two. The same entry
separately asserts a factorial formula for T(n,n^2+1). The ratio argument under
consideration does not assume that formula. A source label of conjecture and
the bounded source checks do not establish worldwide openness or priority.

## Mathematical boundary

configurations of an almost-complete trail, and the append/delete
correspondence for the denominator are specified in CollisionPrefix.

cardinality identities, constructive Euler nonemptiness, a positive source
