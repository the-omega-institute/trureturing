# Growing Trees and Amoebas’ Replications

Vladimir Gurvich, Matjaž Krnc and Mikhail Vyalyi, *Growing Trees and Amoebas’
Replications*, arXiv:2401.07484v1/v2 and *Results in Mathematics* (2025).
Section 2 defines ordinary amoebas, extensions, total-edge minimum growths and
total confinement. Conjecture 1 is the complete finite-tree assertion settled
by the corresponding D5 modules.

## Source statement

The source assertion is: for any finite simple tree H, multiplicity function m,
and extension length l, if the maximum degree of H exceeds the number of roots
k by more than two, then the amoeba is totally confined. The finite reading
retains every initial finite tree containing H, every multiplicity-preserving
copy, every simultaneous family of arms and every minimum growth counted by
all host edges. Zero lengths, zero multiplicities and k=0 remain included.
The published statement and its arXiv versions leave infinite union limits as
a separate question; this note records the finite source convention used by
the formalization.
