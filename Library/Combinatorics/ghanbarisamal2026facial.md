# Facial edge types and the twist operation

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.01410v1.
This is the EUROCOMB'25 extended abstract submitted to arXiv on 2 May 2026.

Section 1 defines the three edge types using facial walks and traversal
direction. Section 2, Proposition 2.1 describes merging the two facial walks
incident to a regular edge. Section 3, Lemma 3.1 describes twisting singular
links and their crossing links. Section 3.1, item 8 states that a twisted
regular link is good singular.

## Attribution and scope

The ribbon-band operation and its edge-type changes are established
mathematics. A general permutation-orbit formalization supplies reusable
formal infrastructure, not a claim of external novelty. Its orbit effect
must be derived from the actual changed matching, rather than assumed as
classifier equivariance.

Equal good and regular probabilities additionally require a sampling law
invariant under the single-edge twist. The source's Section 4 exact-thirds
conjecture leaves its probability law unspecified. Twist symmetry alone
does not imply that either expectation is one third of the edge count.
