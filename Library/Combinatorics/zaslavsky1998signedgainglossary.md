# Signed incidence, switching, and the grid transport interface

Primary locator: the author's second edition, 16 September 1998, revision a dated 22 September 1998. The HTML was read on 27 September 2026. Relevant entries are oriented incidence matrix, unoriented incidence matrix, incidence matrix of a signed graph, and walk/trail/path. No external PDF was needed for these definitions.

The unsigned matrix has two positive endpoint entries for a link. In the signed convention, a positive edge has opposite endpoint signs and a negative edge has equal endpoint signs.
