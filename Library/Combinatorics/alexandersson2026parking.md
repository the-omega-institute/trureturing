# Alexandersson, parking functions and Chow polynomials

Section 1 defines parking words by their sorted letters and counts strict
interior peaks and adjacent ties. Theorem 1.2 gives the recurrence and
bound-one initial condition in equations (2) and (3). Section 5 gives the
four gap series (10) and the bivariate insertion operator (11).
[Theorem 1.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05131v2#S1.Thmtheorem2)
· [Equation (10)](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05131v2#S5.E10)
· [Equation (11)](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05131v2#S5.E11)

Problem 10.3 asks for a descent refinement with finitely many local
states. The maximum-letter decomposition yields distinct left and right
boundary factors and one block for each adjacent ascent–descent pair
around an old strict peak. These blocks give a trivariate insertion
operator. At descent marker one, the two boundary factors coincide and
the internal factors recover (10) and (11).
[Problem 10.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05131v2#S10.Thmtheorem3)
