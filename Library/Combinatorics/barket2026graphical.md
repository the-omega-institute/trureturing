# Central-quotient eigengaps

## Verified locator

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.12026
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12026v1

The paper defines directed Cayley edges by right multiplication and then uses the
underlying undirected graph. Equation (4.9) defines the normalised Laplacian as
`D^(-1/2) L D^(-1/2)`. Consecutive gaps are taken in the ascending spectrum,
with the first-large-gap threshold fixed at one.

Conjecture 4.4 states that, whenever this first-large-gap index exists for a
finite nilpotent group, it is either the final spectral index or the cardinality
of a quotient by a term of the upper central series. The same conjecture is
restated in the closing discussion.

For the cyclic group of order five generated by one element, the undirected
Cayley graph is the 5-cycle. Its normalised-Laplacian spectrum is
`[0, (5-sqrt(5))/4, (5-sqrt(5))/4, (5+sqrt(5))/4, (5+sqrt(5))/4]`, so the first
gap above one has index three. The two values allowed by the conjecture are four
and one.
