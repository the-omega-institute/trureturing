# On the Roots of Connected Domination Polynomials

Section 1.1 specifies finite, undirected simple graphs. Section 5.2, Open Problem 2 (Closure of total domination roots), states:

> Dod [25] determined the total domination polynomial of the lexicographic product G[K_n], but the closure of the total domination roots is not clear. Jafari and Alikhani obtained structural results on the location of total domination roots [23, 27], but no density statement is known. Since the Brown–Tufts form fails for total domination (see the discussion in Section 2), the backward closure mechanism cannot be used; in particular, it is not known whether the total domination roots are dense in the whole complex plane.

The local theorem concerns total domination, with selected vertices requiring selected neighbours. Its independent fibres have no internal edges, so the substitution used locally is different from the clique product discussed in the source. The source poses the whole-plane question; it does not attest the local density proof.

The Brown–Tufts article (10.1007/s00373-013-1306-z) and Dod article (10.1016/j.dam.2015.10.008) are contextual references whose complete bodies remain unread in the bounded source search. Their exact strongest theorems and proofs are not transferred here. This citation makes no universal prior-art absence or priority claim.
