# Blasiak–Kleinberg–Lubetzky, characteristic-two projection cancellation

The fixed primary input is arXiv:1108.2489v1. Lemma 6.2 and Eq. (6.1) contain the characteristic-two projection cancellation in the source's direct-sum setting. Lemma 6.3 and Theorem 6.4, Eq. (6.8), turn the cancellation into a codimension estimate.

The repository theorem `CharacteristicTwoNonFano.finrank_opposite_sum_le_two_deficiency` states a separate unrestricted submodule result: all four subspaces may overlap, have unequal dimensions, or be zero. The source mechanism is attribution and motivation; it is not treated as a proof of the unrestricted theorem, the seven-subspace `Phi` inequality, or the physical stabilizer bridge.
