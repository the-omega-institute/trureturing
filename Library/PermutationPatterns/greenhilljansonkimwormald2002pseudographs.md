# Permutation multiplicity over undirected cycles

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.1017/S0963548301005065

*Combinatorics, Probability and Computing* 11(3), 273–298. Section 1, author
version p. 3, the paragraph introducing the weight of a permutation pseudograph.
[Author version](https://www2.math.uu.se/~svante/papers/sj140.pdf)

## Mathematical scope

The source gives multiplicity two for each cycle component of length at least
three, retaining one possible successor permutation for each loop or double-edge
component. In the connected simple case with at least three labels, a given
cycle graph admits two distinct permutations, related by reversal. The same page
identifies the model with parameter zero as uniform on Hamilton cycles.

The Lean law restricts to actual labelled completed simple cycle graphs
containing a fixed graph. For each of the two successor permutations, the
orientation of the prescribed edges is forced. Uniform mass on the actual
lifts therefore gives the same mass to each completed graph. This last
finite transport makes the specified-edge model explicit.
