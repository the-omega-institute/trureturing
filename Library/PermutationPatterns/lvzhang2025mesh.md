# Lv and Zhang, joint equidistributions of mesh patterns 123 and 321

The paper studies pairs of mesh patterns 123 and 321 carrying the same shading and proves joint
equidistributions for symmetric and minus-antipodal shadings, leaving two pairs as a conjecture.

Section 2.4, Conjecture 2, printed page 9, states for the pairs S21 and S22 that the mesh patterns
123 and 321 with the displayed shadings are jointly equidistributed. The shadings are given only as
diagrams. For S21 the rendered page shows the shaded cells R = {0,1,2}² ∪ {(3,3)}, where a cell (i, j)
has column i and row j counted from the lower left. The source proves only the Wilf-equivalence of the
four patterns (Theorem 3) and notes that S22 is obtained from S21 by reverse and complement.
