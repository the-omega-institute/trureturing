# Etienne's formula for OEIS A183409

The NAME counts n by 2 binary arrays whose every top-left i by j rectangle has
at most i*j/2 ones and whose rows and columns are nondecreasing. The second
FORMULA attributes the displayed conjecture to Luce Etienne (2014). The entry
has offset 1,1; revision 20 retains that conjectural label.

The EXAMPLE includes the row words 00,01,10,11 and 01,01,10,10. These determine
the lexicographic interpretation of the word orders, rather than componentwise
sorting. Repeated rows and equal columns are allowed. Rows and columns retain
their original labels; there is no quotient by a symmetry group.

The mathematical target is the full all-height literal cardinality formula.
The separately listed empirical recurrence and generating function do not
replace that target. This citation records attribution and scope, without a
claim of worldwide priority, external acceptance or exhaustive literature coverage.
