# Clifton, Harris and Khaqan, parking functions inspired by board games

In §1.2, the arriving car takes its preferred spot. If occupied, the
displaced incumbent parks in the first vacant spot from the start of the
street. The outcome records the car labels in spot order.
[Parking rule](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.29890v1#page=4)

Definition 3.14 defines Motzkin paths by steps H, U and D that stay at or
above height zero and end at height zero. Lemma 3.18 counts these paths by
interior touches to the horizontal axis. The proof of Theorem 1.8 identifies
that refinement with the longest distinct initial preference segment of
reverse-outcome words.
[Definition 3.14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.29890v1#page=15) ·
[Lemma 3.18](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.29890v1#page=16) ·
[Proof of Theorem 1.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.29890v1#page=18)

Question 5.2 uses an increasing prefix followed by the remaining labels in
decreasing order. Theorem 1.8 and Corollaries 1.9–1.10 give the Motzkin
formulas for prefix lengths zero, one and two, respectively. Theorem 1.6
gives Catalan counts for the increasing-prefix outcomes with suffix lengths
zero, one or two. The three-label suffix in Theorem 1.6 and Propositions 3.11
and 3.13 is `(n,n−2,n−1)`. Question 5.2 uses `(n,n−1,n−2)` for a
three-label suffix. For example, its fiber at n = 5 and k = 2 has count
37 by Corollary 1.10, whereas the Catalan number C₅ is 42.
