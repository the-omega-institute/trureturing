## Mathematical interpretation

Mathlib's `Nat.Partition m` carries positive multiset parts of sum m.
Sort those parts in decreasing order. The existing integer recurrence
`TrimmedAlternatingPartitions.trimmedSums` starts from zero and replaces z by
a-z for each part a; its last endpoint, defaulting to zero on the empty
list, is the source's reverse-alternating sum. `Nat.card` counts finite
subtypes of partitions. The comparison includes every natural n, including zero.

The repository proof increases the first part of an odd-length source and
appends 1 to an even-length source. A two-step endpoint bound forces the
first two parts of every nonempty even-length source to agree. The images
therefore have respectively unique and repeated largest parts; the empty
source maps to [1]. This gives an injection for every source weight m into
odd-length partitions of weight m+1.
