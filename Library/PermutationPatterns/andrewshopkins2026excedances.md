# Excedance parity of integer partitions

The source defines an excedance of a decreasing partition as an index i with its ith part strictly greater than i. Indices start at one. Section 2, immediately after Corollary 7, suggests strict positivity of the even-minus-odd difference for every n at least 12. Table 1 explicitly labels the even count, odd count and their difference. These definitions and table determine the assertion; the even/odd labels in the displayed generating functions of Corollary 7 are inconsistent with them.

The formal assertion uses the full Nat.Partition carrier, sorted decreasingly, and cardinalities of the actual even and odd subtypes. At n = 28 these cardinalities are 1850 and 1868, respectively, giving a counterexample to the universal assertion. No generating-function identity is assumed. This does not assert worldwide priority or the globally least counterexample.
