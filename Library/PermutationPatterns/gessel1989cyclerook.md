# Cycle rook polynomials and alternating full cycles

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.1007/978-1-4684-0637-5_13

Ira M. Gessel, *Generalized rook polynomials and orthogonal polynomials*,
in *q-Series and Partitions*, IMA Volumes in Mathematics and its Applications 18,
Springer, 1989, pp. 159–176. Section 8, pp. 11–12 of the [author version](https://people.brandeis.edu/~gessel/homepage/papers/rookp.pdf).

## Mathematical scope

Section 8, author-version p. 11, contracts the compatible prescribed-entry set
of a permutation. Its number of cycles is the number of cycles of the contracted
permutation plus the number of directed cycles among the prescribed entries.
An acyclic prescribed relation has zero cycle-count shift.

Section 8, author-version p. 12, counts by cycles the permutations of two disjoint
sets that send each element to the opposite set. When the parts have the same
size n, the cycle-weight enumerator is n! times the rising factorial of the
cycle weight with n factors. For n positive, the coefficient of degree one
is n! times (n−1)!. It counts the actual labelled permutations with one orbit.

The formal completion law applies this count to the original carrier and
uses the compatible component-cycle bijection on the prescribed-edge event.
The probability mass is the number of actual alternating extensions divided
by the original count. On a positive event, the uniform conditional law
pushes forward along the same bijection. These probability statements are
the finite normalization and transport of the counts.
