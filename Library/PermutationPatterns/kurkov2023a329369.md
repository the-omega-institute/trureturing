## Source statement

> Conjecture 4: a(2^m*(2k+1)) = Sum_{i=1..wt(k) + 2} i!*i^m*A358612(k, i)*(-1)^(wt(k) - i) for m >= 0, k >= 0 where wt(n) = A000120(n).

The NAME and COMMENTS at lines 5–7 define `a(n)` by counting permutations
of `{1,…,M}` with strict excedance set consisting exactly of positions `M−i`
for `0<i<M` whose bit `i−1` of `n` is one, with `n<2^(M−1)`.
The [A358612 note](../Digit/kurkov2024a358612.md) gives the literal rational
scalar recursion and the second printing, which reverses the equality and
adds two to the integer sign exponent.
