Copyright 2026 Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang. The supplied manuscript is licensed under

Chrono AI, Singapore. Wenlin Zhang is affiliated with the National University of
Singapore and Chrono AI, Singapore. Toufik Mansour proposed the questions in the
12 November 2026 correspondence. No new affiliation, coauthorship, submission or
global novelty is asserted by this formalization.

The complete revision-3 source is *Ten Wilf classes, two pair equivalences and a
Catalan bijection for ascent sequences*, dated 12 November 2026. Section 1 fixes
ordinary nonnegative sequences with strict adjacent ascents and the literal xy-z
convention. Section 8, Proposition `prop:catalan-tree` and Theorem
`thm:catalan-bijection`, give the half-open exclusions, rank succession, bijection
and Narayana refinement for the pair 10-1,20-1. The new implementation adapts the
rank construction through a proved equivalence to nondecreasing inversion
sequences; the ordinary domain and both inverse identities are proved, rather
than assumed from the existing nondecreasing inversion-sequence supplier.
