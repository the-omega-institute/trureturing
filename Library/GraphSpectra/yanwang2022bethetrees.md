# Main spectra of equitable quotients

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.1016/j.laa.2022.01.001

Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.01101v1#page=4

Lemma 3, p. 4, states that a graph and the divisor of an equitable
partition have the same main spectrum. Yan and Wang credit
the result to Y. Teranishi, *Main eigenvalues of a graph*, Linear and
Multilinear Algebra 49 (2001), 289–303, their reference [7].

## Specialization to a double cover

The cell characteristic matrix lifts a quotient eigenvector to one
constant on each cell. Its transpose sums the coordinates of a graph
eigenvector over each cell. In the bipartite double cover the cells are
pairs above each vertex, the quotient adjacency is exactly the base
adjacency, and the two operations duplicate and add the layers.
The coordinate sum doubles under lifting and is preserved under
projection. This specializes the equitable quotient argument to
main-spectrum invariance under canonical double covers.
