# The observer trace and entropy in de Sitter space

V. Chandrasekaran, R. Longo, G. Penington, and E. Witten, *An algebra of observables for de Sitter space*, JHEP 02 (2023) 082.

## Verified locator

url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.10780

doi: 10.1007/JHEP02(2023)082

Section 2.4, Eqs. (20)–(30), gives the crossed-product trace, the positive observer energy projection, and normalization of the corner trace. Section 3 relates trace entropy to generalized entropy for the states and semiclassical regime specified there.

## Use and boundary

A finite Gibbs matter algebra gives a concrete field of matrix algebras over total energy. The positive observer energy projection retains coordinate (i) at total energy (y) precisely when its physical clock energy (y-E_i) is nonnegative. The corner trace integrates ordinary matrix traces against a scalar exponential measure. Its normalization fixes the reference state and the entropy ceiling. The finite field algebra has a diffuse center. The continuum factor and the identification of a gravitational area term use the operator-algebraic and physical hypotheses of the cited work.
