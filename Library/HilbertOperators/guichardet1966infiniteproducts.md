# Infinite tensor products and product-state representations

## Verified locator

Alain Guichardet, *Produits tensoriels infinis et représentations des relations
d’anticommutation*, Annales scientifiques de l’École Normale Supérieure,
series 3, 83 (1966), no. 1, pp. 1–52. DOI 10.24033/asens.1146.
Source https://doi.org/10.24033/asens.1146.

Proposition 1.2 describes weak equivalence of unit reference families.
Propositions 2.7–2.8 relate weak equivalence to unitary equivalence of the
product representations and give the converse for irreducible local
representations. Proposition 2.9 identifies the cyclic representation of a
product state, and Corollary 2.2 characterizes its purity.

The qubit case uses the defining irreducible representation of the full
two-by-two matrix algebra at each site.
