# Bipartite entangled non-orthogonal states

Xiaoguang Wang, J. Phys. A: Math. Gen. 35, 165 (2002).
The arXiv v5 preprint uses the title “Bipartite maximally entangled
nonorthorgonal states.” Section II, Eqs. (5) and (7)–(13), writes the
coherent superposition of two normalized nonorthogonal product vectors in
two-dimensional orthonormal coordinates. Equation (11) gives the squared
Schmidt coefficients, and Eq. (13) gives the concurrence in terms of the
two local overlaps and the original complex superposition coefficients.

For cut sides U and its complement, the two grouped overlaps are
products of the sitewise overlaps. In the notation of the Lean supplier,
Wang's concurrence has square 4d(U), so Eq. (11) gives the same two
spectral values used in the physical moment identity. The supplier also
handles one-dimensional or rank-one reductions and derives a uniform
normalization bound and the real functional-calculus trace formula. Wang's
paper is credited for the spectral calculation, not for the subsystem
monotonicity or overlapping-set submodularity of generalized
concentratable entanglement proved by the downstream theorem.
