# Set imaginarity and ordered trace words

Section II.A–B works with quantum states in dimension at least two and sets of
at least three distinct states. A set is free if a single unitary makes all
the states real in the same basis. Equations (1) and (2) use the trace of an
ordered product and its invariance under simultaneous unitary conjugation.

Appendix C, p. 11, prints Conjecture 1 with “set imaginarity free if and only
if” and asks for a word with nonzero imaginary trace and length between
three and d(d+1)/2 inclusive. The displayed statement does not require
distinct word positions to select distinct states. The introduction treats a
nonreal trace as a witness of set imaginarity, and Appendix C discusses
detection by longer words. Their converse detection direction would say that
real trace words force a common real basis.

Li, Wagner and Zhang prove a nonreal-triplet detection criterion for qubit
states in Corollary III.7 and give a dimension-four example detected by a
fourth-order invariant. Wang and Chen study qubit families under vanishing
invariant hypotheses. Arai and Nakayama distinguish Hermitian operators fixed
by a quaternionic antiunitary from those fixed by a conjugation. Related
work by Miyazaki and Matsumoto discusses general antiunitary symmetries of
Hermitian families. The present formal results concern Conjecture 1 and the
inferred converse detection direction.
