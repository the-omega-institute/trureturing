# Kadison inequality and a single continuous generator

R. V. Kadison, *Annals of Mathematics*, second series, 56 (1952), 494–503.

The supplied formal proof establishes the self-adjoint inequality for positive
unital maps from complex continuous functions on the closed unit interval to
an arbitrary ordered complex C-star algebra. Finite positive variance and
Mathlib's Bernstein uniform approximation replace a dilation argument.

Equality at the square of the interval coordinate yields the Jordan identity,
all coordinate powers, and then a genuine star-algebra homomorphism on every
continuous complex function by polynomial density. This is classical
single-generator multiplicative-domain rigidity, with an explicit alternate
proof. No claim of new mathematical priority or a solved open conjecture is
made. Complete positivity is not assumed, and the inequality is applied only
to self-adjoint inputs.
