## Verified locator

Theorem 1.2 on page 2 and its proof in Section 3 of the Zenodo v1 preprint give a pair of zero-one matrices over any field. For size three the pair is \(A=E_{21}+E_{13}+E_{23}\), \(B=E_{32}\), with one-based indices. Its exact word span is full precisely at lengths at least seven. Remark 6.2 leaves the universal complex size-three upper bound open. [Versioned PDF](https://zenodo.org/records/24562446/files/wielandt_lower_bound.pdf) · [Zenodo v1 DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24562446)

## Formal correspondence

For the size-three pair, the functional \(X_{11}-X_{13}-X_{21}+X_{23}\) vanishes on all exact length-six words. Nine exact length-seven words form a basis. The formal proof checks these identities over the integers and transports them to the field of the matrix entries.
