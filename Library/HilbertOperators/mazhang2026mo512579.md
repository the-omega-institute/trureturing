# Gram-determinant Cauchy–Schwarz inequality

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23920583

Source: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23920583

The written proof is by **Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang**, *MathOverflow 512579, revision 1:
The elementary symmetric inequality for a Hermitian Jordan product*, version 1 (published
2026-11-03), exact-version DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23920583, Lemma 2 (label
`lem:gram` in `note.tex`, section "Proof by Gram determinants"). The question is
Malkoun's MathOverflow 512579; see Library note malkoun2026mo512579.

## Statement

> For $U,V\in\CC^{m\times k}$, $|\det(U^*V)|^2\leq\det(U^*U)\det(V^*V)$.

Here the star is the conjugate transpose and empty determinants are 1. The proof lists
the rows of each k-element subset T in increasing order, puts u_T = det U[T,:] and
v_T = det V[T,:], and uses Cauchy–Binet: det(U*V) is the sum of conj(u_T) v_T,
det(U*U) the sum of |u_T|^2 and det(V*V) the sum of |v_T|^2. The Cauchy–Schwarz
inequality for these finite sums gives the bound. The combination of Cauchy–Binet with
Cauchy–Schwarz is classical; the note records it as a lemma for its Jordan-product
argument.
