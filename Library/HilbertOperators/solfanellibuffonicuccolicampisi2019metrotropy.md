# Solfanelli, Buffoni, Cuccoli and Campisi, Maximal energy extraction via quantum measurement

A. Solfanelli, L. Buffoni, A. Cuccoli and M. Campisi, J. Stat. Mech. (2019) 094003; arXiv:1905.10262v2. Quotations are from the v2 TeX source, with the source's macro `\Tr` kept as written.

The ergotropy $\mathcal W$ is "the maximal amount of energy that can be extracted by means of unitary operations", the minimum of the final energy $E'=\sum_kE_k\langle k|U\rho U^\dagger|k\rangle$ over unitaries subtracted from $E=\operatorname{Tr}H\rho$. The metrotropy $\mathcal M$ is "the maximal amount of energy that can be extracted from a finite quantum system by means of projective measurements": a measurement maps

> $$\rho' = \sum_k \pi_k \rho \pi_k$$

with a complete set of orthogonal projectors, assumed of rank one, $\pi_k=|\psi_k\rangle\langle\psi_k|$, and $\mathcal M=E-\min E'$. For stationary states the paper proves $\mathcal M\le\mathcal W/2$, with equality when the ergotropy permutation is an involution, and for non-stationary states it shows $\mathcal M\le\mathcal W$. Section 3.1 ends:

> Whether the relation $\mathcal M \leq \mathcal W/2$ holds in the case $[H,\rho]\neq 0$ remains an open question.
