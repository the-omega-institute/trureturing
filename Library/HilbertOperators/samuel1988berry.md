# Geometric phase for sequences of projections

J. Samuel and R. Bhandari, Physical Review Letters 60(23), 2339–2342
(1988), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.60.2339.

The article is the standard citation for extending the geometric phase
beyond cyclic adiabatic evolution, in particular to evolutions
interrupted by projective measurements, and for relating it to the
Pancharatnam phase. The repository's return phase of three successive
normalized projections around a Bloch triangle is an instance of this
setting.
