# Modular covariance and the affine group

Theorem II.9 gives the modular covariance of a one-parameter unitary translation group with nonnegative generator. The modular automorphisms rescale the translation parameter by an exponential with exponent `−2πs`. The explicit real affine group and its action on square-integrable functions realize this covariance. The sign of the Lie bracket uses the conventions `D(s) = exp(−isK)` and `T(a) = exp(iaP)`.
