# Sign leakage in approximately preserving quantum operations

Chapter 4, Lemma 4.6 obtains both leakage trace terms in equations
(4.23)–(4.24) and then bounds the positive-to-complement direction in
equations (4.25)–(4.29). The matrix estimate here uses separate spectral
floors on the positive and negative subspaces to retain both directions.
The error has trace zero, so pairing with the positive spectral projection
costs one half of the full trace norm. For equal floors, orthogonality
identifies the sum of the two squared Frobenius blocks with the squared
commutator.
