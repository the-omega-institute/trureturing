# Spectra of principal diagonal blocks

## Verified locator

https://doi.org/10.1080/0308108031000069164

Theorem 2.2(a) characterizes feasibility for a fixed global Hermitian
spectrum and prescribed spectra on the principal diagonal blocks.
Theorem 3.3 reduces the requisite inequalities. For a projection,
the global spectrum consists only of zero and one. The equal-block
entropy problem also fixes the block trace and optimizes a concave
spectral sum. Its attained value uses the rank caps and the common
subspace of the three padded supports.
