# Wilson lattice gauge reflection positivity

K. Osterwalder and E. Seiler, *Gauge Field Theories on a Lattice*, Annals of Physics 110 (1978), 440–471.

A temporal link reflection interchanges the configurations on the two sides of the reflection plane and reverses the seam links. The real trace of a plaquette that crosses the seam is a Hilbert–Schmidt pairing between the two spatial link matrices after boundary gauge transport. Haar integration over the seam gives a positive quadratic form on integrable half-space observables.

Wilson's formulation supplies the plaquette action and the rectangular loop observable. The strong-coupling estimate uses the nontrivial scalar action of a central group element to cancel terms in the plaquette expansion.
