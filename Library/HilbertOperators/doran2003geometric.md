# Rotors in the geometric algebra of space

C. Doran and A. Lasenby, Geometric Algebra for Physicists, Cambridge
University Press (2003), DOI 10.1017/CBO9780511807497.

The book is a standard textbook reference for rotors in the geometric
algebra of three-dimensional space and for its Pauli-matrix
representation. The repository uses the half-angle rotor
`(1 + ba)/√(2(1 + a·b))` in that representation.
