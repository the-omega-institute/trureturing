# Projection localization and matrix matching

Sections II.2.3.1–5 supply continuous functional calculus and the initial and
final projections of a partial isometry. Definition II.3.3.4 gives equivalence
and subordination of projections; Proposition II.3.3.5 gives unitary conjugacy
when the distance between projections is less than one. Section II.8.2.2(iv)
describes the CAR algebra and UHF inductive limits of full matrix algebras with
unital embeddings, including varying multiplicities.

The intrinsic finite-stage localization and one-sided matching statements are
derived using these classical tools, density, finite spectral rounding, and
matrix units. They are not attributed to a separately numbered theorem in the
book. No ambient corner realization or classification of an unknown response
follows from this algebraic supplier alone.
