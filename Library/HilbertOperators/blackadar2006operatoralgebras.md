# Projection localization and matrix matching

Sections II.2.3.1–5 and II.3.3.4–5 supply the classical spectral and projection tools;
II.3.3.5 treats unitary conjugacy of close projections. Section II.8.2.2(iv)
describes the increasing unital full-matrix model. Finite-stage localization and
one-sided matching are used intrinsically in the completed algebra. No ambient
corner realization or classification of an unknown response follows from this
algebraic supplier alone.
