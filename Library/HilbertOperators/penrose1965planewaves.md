# Astigmatic focusing in gravitational plane waves

The paper exhibits line focusing of null rays in plane-wave geometry. The constant quadratic vacuum profile yields one circular and one hyperbolic transverse solution. The first zero of the circular factor collapses one transverse dimension while the hyperbolic factor stays positive.
