# Pancharatnam phase of a closed sequence of states

S. Pancharatnam, Proceedings of the Indian Academy of Sciences, Section A
44(5), 247–262 (1956), DOI 10.1007/BF03046050.

The article is the standard citation for the phase acquired by a state
carried around a closed sequence of states, each step choosing the
representative in phase with the previous one. For two-level states this
is the phase of the return of a spinor taken through normalized
projections around a geodesic triangle of Bloch directions; the
repository proves its explicit form `(S − iD)/√(S² + D²)` in terms of the
three Bloch vectors.
