# Magnetic vacuum birefringence

## Verified locator

Annals of Physics 67 (1971), 599–647. The publisher record and bibliographic metadata identify the work on photon dispersion in a magnetic field. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916(71)90154-0)

## Use and boundary

The polarization calculation uses the quartic weak-field effective Lagrangian and derives its two quadratic forms in the probe fields. The rational phase-speed formulas are exact for that Lagrangian. The ratio of seven to four is the ratio of the derivatives of the refractive indices with respect to the dimensionless squared field at zero.

The magnetic quartic coefficient and the electric vacuum-persistence series belong to the constant-field effective-action calculation. Heisenberg and Euler derive the constant-field Lagrangian, and Schwinger gives the proper-time treatment and the imaginary part for an electric field. The formal development evaluates the magnetic coefficient and proves bounds and right-hand flatness for twice that imaginary part.
