# Solid angle of a plane triangle

A. Van Oosterom and J. Strackee, IEEE Transactions on Biomedical
Engineering BME-30(2), 125–126 (1983), DOI 10.1109/TBME.1983.325207.

The article is the standard citation for the closed formula expressing
the half solid angle of the triangle spanned by three unit vectors
through the scalar `1 + a·b + b·c + c·a` and the oriented volume
`a·(b×c)`. In the repository the same scalar and oriented volume are the
real and imaginary parts of the return phase of the three shortest qubit
gates around the Bloch triangle; the identification of that phase with
the solid angle is not formalized.

The DOI, authors, title, journal, volume, issue, pages and year are
verified against Crossref. The full text has not been inspected; this
reference attributes the classical formula as standardly cited, not a
verbatim equation number.
