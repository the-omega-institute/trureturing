# Two-sided counts at a fixed exponent above six elevenths

The primary is *A Chebychev's Type of Prime Number Theorem in a
Short Interval-II*, *Hardy--Ramanujan Journal* **15** (1992), 1--33,
[DOI 10.46298/hrj.1992.124](https://doi.org/10.46298/hrj.1992.124).

Theorem 1, equation (1.14), printed p.3, gives, for a fixed small
positive $\varepsilon$, $s=6/11+\varepsilon$ and all sufficiently
large $x$,

$$
0.969\frac{x^s}{\log x}
<\pi(x)-\pi(x-x^s)
<1.031\frac{x^s}{\log x}.
$$

The project uses only fixed $s$ chosen sufficiently close to $6/11$
and below the original available lag exponent. Such a choice is
available when that exponent is strictly greater than $6/11$.
No value at $s=6/11$, moving-$\varepsilon$ theorem, interval
asymptotic, exceptional-centre choice or RH premise is used.
The theorem concerns every sufficiently large centre, rather than
almost all centres. The numerical constants in the published
statement are reused; the full analytic proof and its numerical
computations have not been independently audited or Lean-verified.
No numerical common start or best-known-constant claim is supplied.
