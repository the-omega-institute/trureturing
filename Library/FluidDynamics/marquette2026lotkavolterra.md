# Subalgebras of integrals, commutants, and superintegrable deformations of Lotka–Volterra systems

Ian Marquette, Peter H. van der Kamp, G. R. W. Quispel, arXiv:2602.17945v1 (nlin.SI), 2026-02-20. Quotations are from the v1 TeX source.

The conjecture (Section 2):

> In arbitrary dimensions, we have not been able to prove the birationality of the change of variables, but we conjecture this to be the case, and we provide explicit formulas in Appendix \ref{secA}.

The integrals (Section 3), for $n=2m-1$ and $n=2m$ respectively:

> F_k:= \sum_{\ell=1}^{2k-1} x_\ell \prod_{s=k}^{(n-1)/2} \frac{x_{2s+1}}{x_{2s}},\quad k=1,\ldots,m
> F_k:= \sum_{\ell=1}^{2k} x_\ell \prod_{s=k}^{n/2-1} \frac{x_{2s+2}}{x_{2s+1}},\quad k=1,\ldots,m
> This other set is given by $G_k:=\imath(F_k)$, where $k=1,\dots,m$. We have $F_m=G_m=H$, and, when $n$ is odd, $F_1=G_1=C$

The conjecture restated (Appendix A):

> We conjecture that the transformation from the $n$ variables $x_i$ to the $n-1$ integrals and distinguished variable $x_n$ is a birational map. We treat the odd and the even dimensional case separately.
