# Variational symmetry and on-shell conservation

The original article appeared in *Nachrichten von der Gesellschaft der
Wissenschaften zu Göttingen, Mathematisch-Physikalische Klasse*, 235–257.
The cited text is M. A. Tavel's English translation, *Invariant Variation
Problems*, originally published in *Transport Theory and Statistical
Physics* 1(3), 183–207 (1971), reproduced as arXiv:physics/0503066v1.
Section 1 states Theorems I–II and explicitly imposes the Lagrange
expressions $\psi=0$ to obtain conservation laws; Section 2 derives
the divergence identities. Smoothness and the specified symmetry belong
to the hypotheses.

The consumer is [Static §23, Assumption 23.6](../../docs/develop/theory/AURIC_FIB_ATOM_STATIC_SEAMS_TRANSITION_CIRCULATION_AND_FIBONACCI_TOGGLE_CYCLES.md).
Its global phase current uses a separately declared complex scalar
Lagrangian and generator sign. Conservation of an integrated charge
additionally requires integrability and zero boundary flux. This
variational result supplies neither a field interpretation of five-state
probabilities nor the required field equations for such an interpretation.
