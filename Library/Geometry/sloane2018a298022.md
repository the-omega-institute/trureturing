# Trivalent coordination in the elongated triangular dual

The sequence is attributed to N. J. A. Sloane and the generating-function
conjecture to Colin Barker. The cem-d net is the face adjacency graph of the
elongated triangular tiling. Its triangle nodes have degree three, while its
square nodes have degree four.

## Verified locator

The [OEIS entry](https://oeis.org/A298022), revision 48, has the NAME line:

> Coordination sequence for Dual(3^3.4^2) tiling with respect to a trivalent node.

The Barker FORMULA lines are:

> Conjectures from Colin Barker, Jan 22 2018: (Start)
> G.f.: (1 + 2*x + 4*x^2 + 4*x^3 + 3*x^4 + 2*x^5 - 2*x^8) / ((1 - x)^2*(1 + x + x^2)).

The immutable source is
[oeisdata 70903bf0eb428e82cd158b15edad381081d7c09f](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oeis/oeisdata/70903bf0eb428e82cd158b15edad381081d7c09f/seq/A298/A298022.seq).
The mathematical statement clears the denominator in integer formal power
series and counts genuine graph-distance shells from an upward triangle.
It makes no convergence or physical realization assertion.

The separate homogeneous recurrence printed in the entry has an exception
at index eight: 33 + 23 − 17 is 39, while the listed term is 37.
The numerator's coefficient −2 at degree eight accounts for this difference.
The generating-function identity implies the homogeneous recurrence after
degree eight.
