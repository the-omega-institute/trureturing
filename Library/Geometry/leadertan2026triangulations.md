# Ear compression and cross-intersection

## Verified locator

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39116v1

Full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.39116v1

The general case in the proof of Theorem 2 replaces a selected ear trace
by its shifted trace and a feasible extension. The proof of Lemma 8
applies the same compression to two cross-intersecting families and
inducts on the number of crossing pairs of observed ears; it explicitly
omits the repeated compression details.

The paper's triangulation families use a common completion weight.
The repository's abstract local theorem instead assumes separate
one-to-two equations for two arbitrary nonnegative weights. Its iteration
also requires exact projection images and both marginal equations on
every reduced support. These are additional explicitly stated data,
not conditional counting facts supplied by this citation. The actual
conditioned polygon realization and a same-original-family deficit do
not follow from this citation alone. No publication-priority claim is made.
