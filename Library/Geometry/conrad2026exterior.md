# Exterior carriers and products

The [author-hosted notes](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/linmultialg/extmod.pdf)
are undated in their text; the bibliographic year denotes the PDF version's
creation year in its metadata, not an asserted first-publication year.
Theorem 4.2, p. 11, gives the ordered wedge basis and binomial rank.
Theorem 10.2 and Example 10.3, p. 44, give the exterior algebra and the
three-generator ranks. Example 8.9 and Theorem 8.10, p. 34, concern
decomposability and its obstruction; the four-dimensional coordinate
factorization used by the consumer is an explicit calculation there,
not a quotation of Example 8.9.

These are classical intermediate inputs to
[the five-face projection](../../docs/develop/theory/AURIC_FIB_ATOM_STATIC_SEAMS_TRANSITION_CIRCULATION_AND_FIBONACCI_TOGGLE_CYCLES.md),
Definition 22.1, Theorem 22.2 and Assumption 22.4. The exterior product is
not Boolean pointwise multiplication. Equal vector-space dimensions do
not identify the products or provide a probability law or a physical field.
