# Separation, retractions and homotopy extension in semialgebraic spaces

## Verified locator

## Star characterization and scope

For the first barycentric subdivision of a simplex, the open star of an
original vertex consists of the points whose barycentric coordinate at that
vertex strictly exceeds every other parent coordinate. Its closure is
described by the corresponding weak inequalities. The open star includes
the relative interiors of all incident subdivision faces, including the
original singleton vertex. Removing zero coefficients retains the
positive-support face chain in the strict case.

For a finite simplex, the closure of this open star is the union of all
incident closed subdivision simplices: closure commutes with a finite
union, and each simplex is the closure of its relative interior. This is
the closed star used in the tetrahedral formalization. An affinely
independent tetrahedron in a real normed vector space lies in its
finite-dimensional affine span, so an infinite-dimensional ambient space
does not enlarge the simplex case of the source result.

This existing result supplies the star-coordinate characterization used
in CFMP geometric realization Lemma 70.3.
