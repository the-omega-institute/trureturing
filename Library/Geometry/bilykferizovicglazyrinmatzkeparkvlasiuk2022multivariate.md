# Potential theory with multivariate kernels

D. Bilyk, D. Ferizović, A. Glazyrin, R. Matzke, J. Park and O. Vlasiuk,
*Potential theory with multivariate kernels*, Math. Z. 301 (2022).

Definitions 2.1–2.2 specify conditional positive definiteness using finite signed
Borel measures and the corresponding energy minimization condition. Equation
(5.1) specifies rotational invariance. Section 5, paragraph before Theorem 5.3,
states:

> We suspect that the property that σ minimizes I_K is equivalent to the fact that U_K^{σ^{n−2}} is conditionally positive definite, i.e. the two-input energy I_{U_K^{σ^{n−2}}} is minimized by σ. This conjecture is supported by all the examples known to us.

The source establishes necessity and a local converse in Theorem 5.3. The global
converse is refuted by the centered matrix family in the cited modules: for
three inputs, dimension at least three, and 0 < β < (d−2)/d, the averaged kernel
is conditionally positive definite while a Dirac probability measure has
strictly smaller energy than normalized surface measure.
