# De Lazzari, Motwani and Seynnaeve, The Linear Span of Uniform Matrix Product States

C. De Lazzari, H. J. Motwani and T. Seynnaeve, SIGMA 18 (2022), 099; arXiv:2204.10363v3. Quotations are from the v3 TeX source.

The uniform matrix product state parametrization sends $(A_0,\dots,A_{n-1})\in(\mathbb C^{m\times m})^n$ to $\sum_{i_1,\dots,i_d}\operatorname{Tr}(A_{i_1}\cdots A_{i_d})\,e_{i_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes e_{i_d}$; $\mathrm{uMPS}(m,n,d)$ is the closure of its image, and the paper studies its linear span. For bond and physical dimension two it states:

> Conjecture 3.8 (Corollary of Conjecture 3.7). $\dim\langle\mathrm{uMPS}(2,2,d)\rangle=\tfrac1{192}(d^4-4d^2+192d+192)$ for $d$ even, and $\tfrac1{192}(d^4-10d^2+192d+201)$ for $d$ odd.
