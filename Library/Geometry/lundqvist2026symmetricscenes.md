# Symmetric polyhedral scenes and parallel redrawings

Section 6 of arXiv:2609.27605v1 states:

> Conjecture 6.1. The necessary conditions of Corollary 3.10 and Corollary 4.8 are sufficient for Γ-generic pictures and hyperplane arrangements.

The lifting clause uses the free action on both sides of the connected labelled
incidence geometry, an orthogonal representation on real (d−1)-space, and a
one-dimensional sign character. Each hyperplane is incident to at least d points.
A symmetric picture is generic when all coordinates of its point-orbit
representatives are algebraically independent over Q. Corollary 3.10 gives

`|I|/|Γ| = |P|/|Γ| + d |L|/|Γ| − dim H(Γχ)`

and, for every subset J of incidence orbits,

`|J| ≤ |P(J)| + d |L(J)| − Σ_(X in c(J)) dim H(<X>_(ψ,p))`.

The component gain subgroup is generated by gains of unbounded closed walks.
H is the actual invariant normalized-hyperplane parameter subspace. The geometry
is a labelled triple (P,L,I); neighbourhood injectivity and sharpness are not
standing source hypotheses. Parallel hyperplane labels are therefore preserved.

The source permits the trivial character. In the C2 half-turn slice, H has
dimension 3 for the trivial subgroup and dimension 1 for a nontrivial subgroup.
The four-incidence hyperplanes in the counterexample exceed the standing
degree threshold without any affine-spanning inference.
