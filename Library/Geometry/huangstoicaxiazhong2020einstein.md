# Huang, Stoica, Xia and Zhong, Bounds on the Ricci curvature and solutions to the Einstein equations for weighted graphs

A. Huang, B. Stoica, X. Xia and X. Zhong, arXiv:2006.06716v1 (2020); arXiv lists v1 only, with no journal reference. Quotations are from the arXiv v1 TeX source.

For a graph with positive edge lengths, $P_{ij}$ is the length of a shortest path from $i$ to $j$, $d_i=\sum_{i'\sim i}P_{ii'}^{-2}$ and $c_i=\sum_{i'\sim i}P_{ii'}^{-1}$. $T_q$ is the infinite tree in which every vertex has degree $q+1$. On $T_q$ the tree equation of motion on an edge $ij$ is
$$\frac1{P_{ij}}\Big(\frac{c_i^2}{d_i^2}+\frac{c_j^2}{d_j^2}\Big)-\frac{c_i}{d_i}-\frac{c_j}{d_j}=0,$$
and the setting with all edges equal is the constant solution. The paper closes with two conjectures, the second of which reads:

> There exists no non-constant solution to the tEoM for $T_2$ with all edge lengths positive.
