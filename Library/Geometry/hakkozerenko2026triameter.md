# Triameter equality at k = 1

ArXiv:2611.17259v1, §7, Question 1, printed p. 19.

> Question 1. Theorem 1.1 determines the distances between the vertices of a triametral triple in graphs attaining the equality in (1), but not the whole graph. Characterize the graphs attaining this equality, at least for k = 1, that is, the graphs G with tr(G) = 4α(G) − 1.

The source considers finite simple connected graphs of order at least three. Section 2 defines triameter as the maximum sum of the three pairwise graph distances and shows that allowing repeated vertices leaves this maximum unchanged. Section 5 identifies 1-packings with independent sets. The characterization concerns the equality case at k = 1. The classification at general positive k remains open here.
