# Elements, Book XIII, Proposition 8

The [primary proposition in David Joyce's edition](https://mathcs.clarku.edu/~djoyce/elements/bookXIII/propXIII8.html)
states and proves the extreme and mean division of crossing diagonals
in an equilateral and equiangular pentagon. Its greater segment is a
pentagon side. Consequently the diagonal-to-side ratio $r>0$ obeys
$r^2=r+1$, hence $r=(1+\sqrt5)/2$.

The proposition supplies the classical pentagon ratio. Connecting that
quadratic to the spectrum of the specific FIB substitution matrix is an
algebraic identification; it does not transport polygon symmetries to
the FIB syntax or define physical lengths.
