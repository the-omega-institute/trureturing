# Exterior derivative and Hodge conventions

The cited version is the author-hosted HTML edition generated in 2021.
[Chapter 2](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/gr/grhtml/S2.html),
Sections 2.4.1–2.4.3, supplies the exterior derivative, exact and closed
forms, and the electromagnetic two-form. Equation (2.87) uses
$F_{0i}=-E_i$ with $c=1$.
[Chapter 3](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/gr/grhtml/S3.html),
"The Hodge Dual," equations (3.97)–(3.98), supplies metric-dependent
duality and opposite Riemannian/Lorentzian star-square signs.

The consumer is
[the five-face and null-field comparison](../../docs/develop/theory/AURIC_FIB_ATOM_STATIC_SEAMS_TRANSITION_CIRCULATION_AND_FIBONACCI_TOGGLE_CYCLES.md),
Assumption 22.4 and Proposition 23.3. Its three-dimensional Hodge signs
are calculated from the declared wedge-inner-product convention.
The ranks of exterior-power bundles are pointwise ranks, not real
dimensions of their full smooth section spaces. A globally exact
scalar gradient has zero exterior curvature on every topology.
These inputs do not supply a physical realization of the five-state law.

## Accelerated frames and the Newtonian comparison

[Chapter 1, §1.2.4](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/gr/grhtml/S1.html),
equation (1.26) and the following metric, gives the accelerated
Kottler–Möller coordinates, closely related to Rindler coordinates, on part
of Minkowski space. A positive lapse requires the specified right wedge
and time orientation. Nonuniform stationary clock rates there do not
imply nonzero curvature.
[Chapter 5, §5.1.2](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/gr/grhtml/S5.html),
equation (5.229) and its surrounding equations, imposes a stationary
weak perturbation of Minkowski space, nonrelativistic matter and suitable
boundary conditions to obtain the Newtonian Poisson equation and both
temporal and spatial metric perturbations. These are author-hosted HTML
lecture notes, used as secondary classical exposition.

[Assumption 25.6 of the static-seam continuation](../../docs/develop/theory/AURIC_FIB_ATOM_STATIC_SEAMS_TRANSITION_CIRCULATION_AND_FIBONACCI_TOGGLE_CYCLES.md)
supplies its continuum, units, metric signature and domains independently.
It uses these comparisons to limit what a scalar energy contrast or clock
readout determines, without deriving a physical gravitational source
from an occupation label or a graph Laplacian.
