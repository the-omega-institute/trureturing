# Tetravalent kisrhombille coordination sequence

The sequence is attributed to N. J. A. Sloane; the generating-function conjecture
is attributed to Colin Barker. The root is the edge with triangular-lattice
vertices (0,0) and (1,0). Its four neighbors are the two endpoints and the two
containing triangular faces in the simplex-inclusion graph.
