# Join-meet straightening on finite distributive lattices

Hibi's paper supplies the classical straightening-law context for replacing
an incomparable pair by its meet and join. The present Lean module uses only
the finite Boolean meet and join instance, its explicit potential argument,
and the resulting word-fiber consumer; it does not claim the classical
qualitative connectivity or kernel theorem as new.
