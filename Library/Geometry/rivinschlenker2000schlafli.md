# On the Schläfli differential formula

For a polyhedron of fixed combinatorics in a space form of dimension \(m+1\) and curvature \(K\), equation (1) gives \(KmV'=\sum_i W_i\theta_i'\) in the angle convention of the introduction. Here \(W_i\) is the \((m-1)\)-dimensional volume of the codimension-two face and \(\theta_i\) is its dihedral angle. When \(K=0\), the weighted angle variation vanishes. Theorem 2 derives the polyhedral formula from a smooth variation formula using the exterior angle, which changes the sign. The spherical triangle instance uses \(m=1\), \(K=1\), interior angles, and unit weights at the vertices.
