# Colesanti's Rogers–Shephard locator

## Verified locator

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/math/0512098v1

Andrea Colesanti, *Functional inequalities related to the Rogers–Shephard
inequality*, arXiv:math/0512098v1 (2005), introduction, equation (5), records
the classical difference-body inequality
\[
\operatorname{Vol}(K-K)\le \binom{2n}{n}\operatorname{Vol}(K)
\]
for convex bodies in dimension \(n\ge 2\), with equality for simplices.

The Boolean rotation module does not invoke this inequality as an opaque step:
its lower bound uses the exact signed-body volume and finite subadditivity on a
product of additive circles. This note is a geometric precedent and does not
claim that the full finite-codebook statement appears in the cited source.
