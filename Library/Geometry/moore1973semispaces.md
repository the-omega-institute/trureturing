# Lexicographic separation in finite dimension

Moore presents the structure of semispaces in Theorems 2.1 and 2.3,
pages 660–661, and finite-dimensional lexicographic separation in
Section 3, page 663. The reference establishes the classical provenance
of finite lexicographic separation. The specialized realization uses
compactness of a unit sphere followed by recursion on the span of the
rows that vanish on the selected direction. It retains a common
orthonormal list within the row span and bounds its length by that span's
dimension, for arbitrary index sets.
