## Source claim

> **Problem 10.1 (Bare Drazin equalities).** Let \(R\) be a unital associative ring, and let \(a,c\in R\)
> be finite-Drazin invertible with \(a+c\in R^\times\). Put \(b=(a+c)^{-1}\) and \(t=a-aba\).
> Characterize the pairs for which \(t\) is finite-Drazin invertible and
> \(t^D=a^D+c^D\), without assuming coincidence of the input core idempotents
> \(aa^D\) and \(cc^D\).

## Prior results

Definition 2.1 and the paragraph following Lemma 2.2 specify the commuting
outer inverse, its remainder, and the nilpotence condition for a finite
Drazin inverse. They cite Drazin's Theorem 1 for uniqueness and
bicommutation. Lemma 2.4 gives the two ordered parallel products.
Theorem 5.2(c) gives the nilpotent-residue test under coincident cores.

## Scope

Crossref, and MathOverflow located no earlier settlement. Koliha and
Patrício treat generalized Drazin inverses and spectral idempotent
coincidence. Li, Chen, and Mosić treat reflexive-inverse Hua identities
under range and null-space hypotheses. Generalized Drazin data,
reflexive inverses, and coincident cores address different hypotheses
from the bare finite-Drazin equality in Problem 10.1. Worldwide
