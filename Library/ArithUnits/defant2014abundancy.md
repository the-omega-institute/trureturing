# Defant's abundancy index in quadratic integer rings

Colin Defant, *An Extension of the Abundancy Index to Certain Quadratic Rings*,
*International Journal of Mathematics and Computer Science* 9(2) (2014), 63–82.
The integral bases and the set of radicands are in Section 1. The half-open
argument sectors, the divisor sum and the normalized index are in Section 2,
including Definitions 2.1 and 2.2. Conjecture 4.1 is on PDF page 23 of arXiv v1
and page 81 in print. [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.05416v1#page=23) ·
[Journal PDF](https://future-in-tech.net/9.2/R-AbundancyIndexQuadraticRings-ColinDefan.pdf#page=19)

> Let $d\!\in\! K$. If $p$ is an integer prime and $k$ is a positive integer,
> then $p^k$ is $n$-powerfully solitary in $\mathcal O_{\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{d})}$
> for all positive integers $n$.

Section 1 stipulates positive integer primes. Section 2 calls two nonzero
elements $n$-powerfully friendly when their indices agree and their absolute
values differ. Solitude means absence of such a friend. The sum is over actual
ring divisors in the specified sector, which selects one representative per
associate class. Nonassociated conjugate divisors are counted separately.
