## Source statement

> Infinite lower triangular matrix, M, that satisfies [M^3](i,j) = M(i+1,j+1) for all i,j>=0 where [M^n](i,j) denotes the element at row i, column j, of the n-th power of matrix M, with M(0,k)=1 and M(k,k)=1 for all k>=0.

> M(n, k) = the coefficient of x^(3^n - 3^(n-k)) in the power series expansion of 1/Product_{j=0..n-k}(1-x^(3^j)) whenever 0<=k<n for all n>0 (conjecture).

The NAME's first-row condition conflicts with lower triangularity. Both programs set the first column to one (`j=0` or `j==0`), as does the displayed matrix. The mathematical statement uses M(n,0)=1 and M(n,n)=1, with zero entries above the diagonal. Products are ordinary finite sums on every finite principal block. The reciprocal is a formal power series over the rationals: each denominator factor has constant coefficient one.

## Mathematical scope

The coefficient formula includes existence of a natural-valued source matrix and applies to every matrix satisfying these conditions, every positive row n and every k<n. A scaled convolution identity yields a matrix semigroup; its cubic power shifts both indices. Strong induction on rows establishes uniqueness and hence the coefficient formula. This argument is the repository's proof of the published conjecture, rather than a proof attributed to the OEIS entry.
