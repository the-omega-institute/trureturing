The sole COMMENTS conjecture:

> Conjecture: a(n) is not a perfect square except for n = 1, 6 and 96.

The first FORMULA:

> a(n) = Sum_{k=1..n} (n + 1 - k)^2*k/gcd(n + 1 - k, k)^3.

OFFSET is “1,2”; the sequence domain begins at n = 1. In the formal
expression, n and k are natural numbers and the sum is over `Finset.Icc 1 n`.
The selected summands are exact natural quotients: if d divides u and v,
then d³ divides u²v. Natural subtraction agrees with ordinary subtraction
on this interval. The extension at n = 0 is an empty sum and is excluded
from the conjecture. Perfect squares are represented by m² with m natural.
