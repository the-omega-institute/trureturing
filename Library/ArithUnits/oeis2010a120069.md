# Catalan partial-sum denominators

A120069 has offset one and defines a(n) as the positive reduced denominator
of the rational sum of C(k)/4^(k-1) for k from one through n, where C is the
ordinary Catalan sequence A000108. The quoted conjecture is the original
Groux Roland assertion. The separate A283208 formula is outside this result.

The corresponding numerator entry A119951 attributes to Pieter Mostert
(2012) the finite identity r(n) = 4 - binomial(2n+2,n+1)/2^(2n-1).
The proof uses its equivalent Catalan form
r(n) = 4 - (2n+1) C(n)/4^(n-1). This supplier is established literature,
not a separate new mathematical result. A finite telescoping proof derives
the identity from the pinned Mathlib Catalan and central-binomial relations.

The new public assertion is the universal denominator equality with its
original positive-index range. The literature check is bounded; the formal
proof alone does not certify worldwide priority or exhaustive prior absence.
