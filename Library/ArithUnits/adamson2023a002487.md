# Stern's diatomic series and Adamson's Farey-tree inverse relation

The year 2023 records Gary W. Adamson's December 18, 2023 comment. The OEIS
AUTHOR line credits N. J. A. Sloane. The objects are the Stern sequence
A002487 and the numerator and denominator sequences A007305 and A007306.

The Farey-tree listing starts with 0/1 and 1/1 at indices 0 and 1. Each
subsequent level lists only the new mediants, in increasing order in [0,1].
Writing `r = 2^m + j` with `1 <= j <= 2^m`, entry `r` is position
`2(j-1)+1` of full mediant row `m+1`. Thus entry 13 is 4/7, while
`stern(12)=2`.

Only Adamson's modular-inverse sentence is settled for positive natural
indices. Yurramendi's frequency conjecture, Torres's conjectures and other
Farey-tree properties are not claimed.

## Prior literature

Bates, Bunder and Tognetti, "Linking the Calkin–Wilf and Stern–Brocot trees",
European Journal of Combinatorics 31 (2010),
doi:10.1016/j.ejc.2010.04.002, gives a transposition relation between the two
trees and does not state this modular-inverse claim.

Yurramendi's 2014 `%F` identity on A002487,
`a(k+1)*a(2^m+k) - a(k)*a(2^m+(k+1)) = 1`, is stated there without proof;
the module proves its additive natural-number form for `k < 2^m` as the
private theorem `stern_determinant`.
