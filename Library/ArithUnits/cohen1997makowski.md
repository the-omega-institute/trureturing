# Cohen's contribution to the Mąkowski–Schinzel literature

Graeme Cohen, *On a conjecture of Mąkowski and Schinzel*, *Colloquium
Mathematicum* **74** (1997), 1–8. The title and author are those of the
publisher's landing page for the DOI below; no theorem number is asserted.
This reference identifies related literature. No conclusion from this paper is
used as an unproved premise of the formal theorems.

The formal theorems concern the support condition `η(n)≤42` and the condition
that `q-1` is `97`-smooth for every prime divisor `q` of `n`; they do not claim
to settle the unrestricted conjecture or to reproduce every range treated by
this source.
