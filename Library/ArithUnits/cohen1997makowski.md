# Cohen's contribution to the Mąkowski–Schinzel literature

Graeme Cohen, *On a conjecture of Mąkowski and Schinzel*, *Colloquium
Mathematicum* **74** (1997), 1–8. The title and author are those of the
publisher's landing page for the DOI below. Theorem 3 and its corollary are
on pages 5–6 of the full text. No conclusion from this paper is used as an
unproved premise of the formal theorems.

The support and smooth-predecessor theorems concern `η(n)≤42` and the
condition that `q-1` is `97`-smooth for every prime divisor `q` of `n`.
The Euler-condition theorem concerns a separate class defined by common
predecessor divisors. These theorems do not settle the unrestricted
conjecture or reproduce every range treated by this source.

## Prime grouping and the odd predecessor part

For an odd integer with nonempty non-Fermat prime support Q,
choose an odd prime r(q) dividing q−1 for each q in Q. Write c(r) for the
size of the group assigned to r. Cohen's Theorem 3 states that the group
conditions

    geom(r,c(r)) * product_{r(q)=r} (q−1)/q >= 1

imply sigma(phi(m)) > m. Its corollary replaces each group condition by
the sufficient reciprocal mass bound

    sum_{r(q)=r} r/(q−1) <= 1.

Let U be the odd part of the product of all q−1. The product R of the
chosen r(q) divides U. Abundancy monotonicity gives

    psi(m) >= abundancy(R) * product_{q in Q} (q−1)/q.

The right side is the product of Cohen's group factors. Thus those
factors being at least one implies psi(m) >= 1. The unconditional
psi sigma-totient criterion then recovers Cohen's strict conclusion.

## Literature comparison for the Euler common-divisor condition

The explicit condition r times the finite Euler product through r being
at most the gcd of the predecessors in the greatest-prime group is
a repository-derived sufficient condition. The bounded literature comparison
for this criterion covers Cohen (1997), Pomerance (1989), Ford (2001/02),
Grytczuk–Luca–Wójtowicz (2000), Luca–Pomerance (2002), Pomerance (2026),
Sándor (2005), and Grytczuk (2013). It does not identify that explicit gcd
criterion in these sources. The full text of Grytczuk–Luca–Wójtowicz
(2001) is unavailable in this comparison. This boundary does not assert
an exhaustive classification of the literature or established originality.
