# Translation-invariant polynomial algebra

## Verified locator

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ffa.2017.08.004

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.07677v2

The verified mathematical scope is Section 5 of arXiv:1609.07677v2:
in characteristic p, translation-invariant polynomials have the form
f(X^p-X). This is the existing invariant-algebra result credited below,
not a prior settlement of the named sparse-power iff.

The source is https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.07677v2, Section 5, also published
in Finite Fields and Their Applications 48, 271–288,
DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ffa.2017.08.004.

Over a field of characteristic p, the polynomials fixed by translation
X maps to X+1 are exactly those of the form f(X^p-X). In characteristic
two, X^4+X^2 = (X^2+X)^2 is an instance. This invariant-algebra mechanism
is an existing result; the sparse-power refutation claims no new classifier
or algebraic mechanism. Its direct proof only requires Frobenius and the
coefficient obstruction for the family (X^4+X^2)^r.

This note cites the mathematical result and contains no transplanted source
or proof code from the article.
