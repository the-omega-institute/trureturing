# Bounded square-weight corrections

## Verified locator

https://oeis.org/A384819

The source is Paul D. Hanna's OEIS A384819, approved revision 14.
There is no source arXiv identifier or linked source paper.

The NAME states verbatim:

> Nonnegative numbers a(n) < n for n >= 1 such that exp( Sum_{n>=1} (n^2 - a(n))*x^n/n ) is a power series with integral coefficients.

The sole COMMENTS line states verbatim:

> Conjecture: a(p^n) = p - 1 when p is prime for n >= 1.

OEIS has no page number; these locators are the NAME and COMMENTS fields.

The exponent has zero constant coefficient. The unused value a(0) imposes
no constraint. The positive source exponent n is renamed r, and the prime
p ranges over every natural prime, including two.

The integer-coefficient condition uses the existing IntegralSeries predicate.

## Mathematical mechanism

The integral exponential has an integer Euler ledger. Its logarithmic derivative
has coefficients given by the integer divisor sum. At successive powers of a
prime, subtracting the two divisor sums leaves one ledger term divisible by the
larger prime power. The bound 0 ≤ a(m) < m selects the unique residue and forces
stabilization at p − 1. Neither positivity of exponential coefficients nor
nonnegative Euler exponents is needed. A384820 is the associated integral
series; the source states no additional theorem depending on the conjecture.
