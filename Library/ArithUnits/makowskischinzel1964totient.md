# The sigma-totient inequality with bounded non-Fermat support

A. Mąkowski and A. Schinzel, *Colloquium Mathematicum* **13** (1964), 95–99.
The title field is a bibliographic locator; no article title is asserted.

Write `φ` for Euler's totient and `σ` for the sum of positive divisors.
The inequalities under consideration are `n ≤ 2σ(φ(n))` for positive integers
and the stronger `n ≤ σ(φ(n))` for odd positive integers.
A prime `q` is counted in `η(n)` exactly when it divides `n`, differs from `2`,
and `q−1` is not a power of `2`. Each such prime is counted once,
independently of its multiplicity.

The support condition `η(n) ≤ 42` is sufficient for both inequalities.
No bound on the exponents of the prime factors is imposed. The extension to
`n=0` uses the natural-number convention in which both sides vanish.
This is a partial result toward the unrestricted Mąkowski–Schinzel conjecture;
it does not establish the inequalities for `η(n)>42`.

The argument passes to the radical and to odd integers. In a minimal odd
counterexample, removing the prime sources of a totient prime forces a strict
reciprocal-mass inequality. Finite exclusions for small support primes and a
geometric tail estimate contradict that inequality. The power-of-two totient
case uses a product bound valid for arbitrary finite sets of Fermat indices,
without assuming that the known Fermat primes exhaust all Fermat primes.
