# Bouras's denominator sequence

OEIS A372763, NAME (unpaginated), states:
"Denominator of the continued fraction 1/(2-3/(3-4/(4-5/(...(n-1)-n/(n+5)))))."

Its COMMENTS (unpaginated) state:
"Conjecture 1: The sequence contains only 1's and the primes."
"Conjecture 2: Except for 2 and 3, all primes appear in the sequence once."

The index range starts at three. Evaluate the inner continued fraction by the right fold
of k − (k+1)/t over k = 2, …, n−1, with terminal rational value n+5; take its reciprocal
and then the positive denominator in reduced form. The uniqueness assertion quantifies
over every natural prime other than 2 and 3 and asks for exactly one index n ≥ 3.

The published data include a(10) = 11 and a(21) = 11. The refutation uses these two
indices and makes no claim to discovering the numerical values. Both fractions have
nonzero tails. It does not prove a global multiplicity classification or Conjecture 1.

Bouras, "A New Primes-Generating Sequence", arXiv:2509.09745v1,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09745v1, Table 2 also displays the repeated value.

The numerical experiment is in the-omega-institute/trureturing-experiments,
docs/reports/oeis-bouras-continued-fraction-occurrence-counts/, commit
