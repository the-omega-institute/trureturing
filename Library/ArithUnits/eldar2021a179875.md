# Eldar's adjacency conjecture

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A179875

OEIS A179875, revision 13, NAME:

> Numbers h such that h and h+1 have same antiharmonic mean of the numbers k < h such that gcd(k, h) = 1.

COMMENT:

> Antiharmonic mean B(h) of numbers k such that gcd(k, h) = 1 for numbers h >= 1 and k < h = A053818(n) / A023896(n) = A175505(h) / A175506(h).

Eldar's COMMENT:

> Conjecture: also numbers k such that mu(k) = 1 and mu(k+1) = -1, where mu is the Möbius function (tested on the first 10^4 terms).

The web entry has no page numbers. The conjecture is attributed to Amiram
Eldar; the sequence is attributed to Jaroslav Krizek.

## Definitions and scope

OEIS A053818, NAME:

> a(n) = Sum_{k=1..n, gcd(n,k) = 1} k^2.

OEIS A023896, NAME:

> Sum of positive integers in smallest positive reduced residue system modulo n. a(1) = 1 by convention.

The linked A175505 and A175506 programs set the mean at one to one.
The positive inclusive totative sum therefore includes the single term one
at modulus one. For every larger modulus, coprimality excludes the endpoint.
The quotient is rational. The target is equality of adjacent means, with
both implications, for every positive integer. Equality of reduced
numerators alone or denominators alone and infinitude are separate claims.
