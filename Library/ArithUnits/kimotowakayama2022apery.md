# Kimoto and Wakayama, Apéry-like numbers for non-commutative harmonic oscillators and automorphic integrals

K. Kimoto and M. Wakayama, Ann. Inst. Henri Poincaré D 10 (2023), 205–275 (published online 2022), DOI 10.4171/aihpd/129; arXiv:1905.01775v2. Quotations are from the arXiv v2 TeX source.

The normalized Apéry-like numbers $\tilde J_k(n)$ are rational numbers satisfying, for $n\ge2$,
$$4n^2\tilde J_k(n)-(8n^2-8n+3)\tilde J_k(n-1)+4(n-1)^2\tilde J_k(n-2)=4\tilde J_{k-2}(n-1),$$
with $\tilde J_2(n)=\sum_{j=0}^n(-1)^j\binom{-1/2}{j}^2\binom nj$, $\tilde J_k(0)=1$ for $k=1,2$ and $0$ otherwise, and $\tilde J_k(1)$ equal to $\frac23,\frac34,1,1$ for $k=1,2,3,4$ and $0$ otherwise.

Conjecture 3.6 states, for positive integers $m,n,s$ such that $mp\ge s$ and $mp^{n-1}\ge s$, two congruences modulo $p^n$; the second reads

> $$\frac{p^{2sn}\tilde J_{2s+2}(mp^n)}{p^{2s}\tilde J_{2s+2}(mp)}\equiv\frac{p^{2s(n-1)}\tilde J_{2s+2}(mp^{n-1})}{p^{2s}\tilde J_{2s+2}(mp)}\ (\not\equiv0)\pmod{p^n}.$$
