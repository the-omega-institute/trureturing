# Endpoint coefficients of the A391810 Laurent rows

## Verified locator

The written proofs are by **Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang**, *OEIS A391810: formula (5),
COMMENTS (C.1)--(C.10), and prime-index vanishing*, public version 4, exact-version DOI
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23885969, Corollaries 3 and 6. Paul D. Hanna posed the entry
conjectures in OEIS A391810, revision 39.

## Statement

T(n,k) is the coefficient of x^(-k) in the formal Laurent expansion at zero of
sum over j in Z of x^j (1-x^j)^(n+j). For m >= 1, T(2m-1,m^2)=(-1)^(m-1) and
T(2m,m^2)=(-1)^(m-1) m. For m >= 2, T(2m-1,m^2-1)=2(-1)^m and T(2m,m^2-1)=2m(-1)^m.
The proof evaluates the divisor-sum formula for T(n,k): only divisors h of k with
h + k/h <= n+1 contribute, which forces h = m for k = m^2 and {h,k/h} = {m-1,m+1}
for k = m^2-1; in even rows the extremal divisor pairs cancel in sign-reversing pairs.

## Corollary 6

Source PDF https://zenodo.org/records/23885969/files/note-v4.pdf (text extracted locally,
about 21,800 characters):

> Corollary 6. Formula (6) of A391810 holds. Namely, for
> A_n = −∑_{k=0}^{B_n} (−1)^k k T(n, k) = P_n′(−1), n ≥ 1,
> the formal ordinary generating function is
> ∑_{n≥1} A_n z^n = z(1 − z + 8z²)/((1 + z)²(1 − 2z)²).

The source proof sums the per-summand derivatives h(2^r + r 2^(r−1)) (odd h) and −h, h
(even h, r = 0, 1) as formal series in z. The formalization instead proves
27 A_n = (15n − 11) 2^n + (11 − 30n)(−1)^n and checks the order-four recurrence.
