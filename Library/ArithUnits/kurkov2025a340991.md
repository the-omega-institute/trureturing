# OEIS A340991: Kurkov's convolution-row recursion

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A340991

Text: https://oeis.org/search?q=id:A340991&fmt=text

The entry is unpaginated. Revision 24 has source timestamp
`Apr 06 2025 14:53:28`. Its NAME is:

> Triangle T(n,k) whose k-th column is the k-fold self-convolution of the primes; triangle T(n,k), n>=0, 0<=k<=n, read by rows.

Its coefficient formula is:

> T(n,k) = [x^n] (Sum_{j>=1} prime(j)*x^j)^k.

Its conjecture is:

> Conjecture: row polynomials are x*R(n,1) for n > 0 where R(n,k) = R(n-1,k+1) + x*R(n-1,1)*R(1,k) for n > 1, k > 0 with R(1,k) = prime(k) for k > 0. The same recursion seems to work for self-convolution of any other sequence. - _Mikhail Kurkov_, Apr 05 2025

## Mathematical scope

The coefficient type is an arbitrary commutative semiring K. A sequence
`a : Nat -> K` supplies the positive coefficients of `sourceSeries a`;
its constant coefficient is zero, and `a 0` is unused. `T A n r` extracts
the coefficient of degree n from the r-th power of A. `rowPolynomial A n`
sums `C (T A n r) * X^r` for `r = 0,...,n`.

The polynomial array `kurkovR` has initial values `C (a k)` in row one
at positive columns. Subsequent positive columns follow the quoted
recursion; row zero and column zero are totalized by zero. Commutativity
identifies the source's product order with `X * C (a k) * kurkovR a n 1`.
The asserted identity concerns precisely positive row indices.

The repository proof identifies every coefficient of a positive array
column with a coefficient of a power of the source series times a shifted
tail. Splitting a tail into its constant term and X times its successor
tail gives the array's two recursive summands. The resulting identity
uses neither positivity nor division nor a characteristic assumption.
