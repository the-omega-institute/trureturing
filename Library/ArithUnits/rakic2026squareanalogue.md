## Source claim

For group-invertible matrices \(A,B\), the conjunction \(A\le^-B\) and
\((B-A)^\#=B^\#-A^\#\) is equivalent to \(A^3=ABA=AB^2=B^2A\) and
\((B-A)^3=B^3-A^3\). Section 4 asks which results remain valid in rings
and identifies the use of Cayley–Hamilton in the proof of Theorem 2.13.

## Prior results

Hartwig and Nambooripad supplied the minus order on regular elements.
Rakić's Theorem 1.1(v)–(vi) gives its inner-inverse formulation for matrices.
Drazin's inverse framework includes the group inverse. Sarajlija gives
the three defining equations over a unital ring and the description of a
group-invertible element as a unit in its support corner.

## Scope

The extension concerns the two-sided minus relation and the specified
group inverse of the difference in a unital associative ring. The search
record covers the article's citations and adjacent work on inverse
addition, the sharp and core orders, and ring analogues. Worldwide
priority remains unverified.
