# Numerical ranges for the Mąkowski–Schinzel conjecture

Carl Pomerance, preprint on the Mąkowski–Schinzel conjecture.
The title field describes the topic; no exact preprint title or theorem number
is asserted. The numerical ranges associated with this reference are
`n≤10^60` and `P(n)<10^6`, where `P(n)` denotes the largest prime factor.
These are literature ranges, separate from the condition `η(n)≤42` in the
formal theorem. No equivalence or implication between these conditions is
asserted, and these numerical claims are not Lean-verified results here.

The formal theorem permits unbounded `n` and unbounded prime factors, subject
to at most forty-two distinct non-Fermat odd prime divisors. It does not claim
verification of either numerical range in this preprint or settlement of the
unrestricted conjecture.
