> `a(n) = A323884(25*n). [Conjectured by Sequence Machine]`

A322026 gives the published rank formula
`a(n) = A071521(A065331(n))`. In the formal definition, `A065331(n)` is the
product `2^v_2(n) 3^v_3(n)` and `A071521` counts the nonnegative pairs `(i,j)`
with `2^i 3^j` at most that product. The positive-index Dirichlet inverse `b`
of `a` is the A323883 term, and A323884 is the pointwise sum `a+b`.

The immutable source snapshot is
`f151d82acb28766e6034a902ed9fd0632b16f986` in `oeis/oeisdata`; the relevant
revision is #41. The formal carrier extends the rank function and its inverse
by zero at index zero, while the theorem includes every natural index and hence
implies the published positive-index identity.

The Lean proof uses the factorization invariance `a(5n)=a(n)`. A general
Dirichlet-convolution argument shows that the inverse of any unit-headed
arithmetic function with this invariance vanishes at every `25n`: subtract the
prime shift, convolve with the inverse, and use strong induction on divisors.
Thus `a(25n)=a(n)` and the inverse correction is zero.
