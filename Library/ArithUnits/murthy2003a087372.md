# OEIS A087372

The sequence takes the least positive natural exponent k for which
3^k + 2n is prime, and takes zero when there is no such exponent.
The index offset is one. The positive-exponent convention is fixed by
the example a(23) = 3: the earlier candidates 49 and 55 are composite,
while 73 is prime. Exponent zero would instead give the prime 47.

The boundedness conjecture quantifies over every index n at least one,
with one natural bound M and the strict inequality a(n) < M.
The source contains no cutoff on either indices or exponents.
