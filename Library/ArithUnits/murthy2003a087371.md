# OEIS A087371

The sequence takes the least positive natural exponent k for which
10^k + 2n - 1 is prime and strictly greater than ten, and takes zero
when there is no such exponent. The index offset is one.
Exponent zero cannot qualify: for n at least one the candidate is 2n;
an even prime must be two, which fails the strict threshold.

The boundedness conjecture quantifies over every index n at least one,
with one natural bound M and the strict inequality a(n) < M.
The source contains no cutoff on either indices or exponents.
