## Mathematical interpretation and suppliers

For every integer $q>1$, the rational formal logarithm has constant
coefficient zero and positive coefficient
$(q-1)((q^n-1)/(q-1))^{n-1}/n$. Its ordinary formal exponential is in
$\mathbb Q[[x]]$. Integrality asserts an integer witness for every
coefficient, including the constant coefficient; neither convergence
nor nonnegativity is assumed. The natural-parameter proof covers all
allowed integer parameters through the exact equality
$(q.\mathrm{toNat}:\mathbb Z)=q$.

`DoldExponentialIntegrality.integral_exp_of_dold` supplies the general
integrality theorem. Its licensed AINTLIB provenance remains in
`Library/RecurrenceComposition/aintlib2026dieudonnedwork.md`; this work
copies no further integrality implementation. The original
`HannaDiagonalGhostPowerIntegrality.fermat_lift` supplies the uniform
prime-power Fermat congruence. Euler's theorem, geometric-sum identities,
and Frobenius in `ZMod p` are pinned Mathlib results. The specific remaining
bridge is the Dold congruence for $(q-1)(1+q+\cdots+q^{n-1})^{n-1}$.
