# Purely imaginary independence roots

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08881v2

Version: arXiv:2001.08881v2. Question 2, printed page 11, asks:

> Are there graphs with purely imaginary independence roots at bi where b is irrational?

The introduction, printed page 2, defines the independence polynomial by
counting all independent subsets of a finite simple graph according to
their cardinality. The empty subset contributes one. Coefficients above
the independence number are zero.

The existential question has no restriction on graph order, connectivity,
independence number, or root modulus. Question 1 concerns different
minimum-order questions. The paragraph after Question 2 excludes certain
square roots of rationals of modulus greater than one; it does not exclude
an imaginary part whose square is one half.

The journal locator is Discrete Mathematics 343(12), article 112113.
