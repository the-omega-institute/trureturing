## Counting convention

The parent entry A286847 counts minimal dominating sets in Cartesian products
of paths. Minimal means that no proper subset dominates; it does not mean
minimum cardinality. Domination uses closed neighbourhoods, so a selected vertex
can dominate itself. The vertices retain their row and column labels. Neither
symmetry classes nor choices of private-neighbour witnesses are counted.
The series starts at width $1$; setting its constant coefficient to zero does
not redefine the actual count on the empty graph.

2026-10-26 source observation. The complete first generating-function expression
