## Verified locator

DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2602.07587

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07587v1

The fixed source is arXiv:2602.07587v1, by Shrabani Das, Ahmad Erfanian and Rajat Kanti Nath.
Section 1 defines the subgroup-generating bipartite graph of a finite group. Its two vertex
parts are all ordered pairs of group elements and all subgroups, with a cross edge precisely
when the pair generates the subgroup. Section 2 explicitly uses disjoint union.

Section 5 defines adjacency, degree, Laplacian, signless Laplacian and common-neighbour
matrices, real spectra with multiplicities, and their absolute energies. Only Laplacian and
signless Laplacian energies subtract the average degree of the entire graph. Hyperenergeticity
compares each energy with that of the complete graph having the same number of vertices.

The final paragraph summarizes ten assertions for the known orders $p^n$, $pq$, $p^2q$ and
$p^2q^2$, with distinct primes $p,q$ and $n\geq1$: adjacency nonintegrality; L, Q and CN
integrality; strict hypoenergeticity; four non-hyperenergeticity assertions; and $E\leq LE$.
It then invites extension to any cyclic group. This is an unlabelled extension invitation,
not a numbered conjecture. The finite context includes positive order one; the prime-power
hypothesis $n\geq1$ does not impose a nontriviality convention on that invitation.
The known families belong to Das–Erfanian–Nath: Theorem 2.1 recalls their earlier work,
and Theorems 2.2–2.4 and 5.3–5.18 supply the families used in the summary.

The Lean result negates the entire finite-cyclic universal, using the actual graph of the
order-one group and its integral adjacency spectrum. It asserts nothing about private author
intent or an extension restricted to nontrivial groups.
