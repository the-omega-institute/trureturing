## Source

A. G. Luiz and F. A. S. Vieira, “Independent [k]-Roman Domination on Graphs”, *Discussiones Mathematicae Graph Theory* 45 (2025), 1249–1272. The arXiv version is 2406.11688v1.

The source defines [k]-Roman dominating functions and independent [k]-Roman dominating functions in Section 1, gives the generalized Blanuša snark construction in Section 4 and conjectures in Section 6 that γ[kR](G) = i[kR](G) for k ≥ 2 on all generalized Blanuša snarks.

The closing conjecture (Section 6 of arXiv:2406.11688v1; published Section 5, p. 1270) states: “another interesting line of research is finding other classes of graphs with ∆(G) ≤ 3 for which γ[kR](G) = i[kR](G) for k ≥ 2. In fact, we conjecture that this property holds for all generalized Blanuša snarks.”
