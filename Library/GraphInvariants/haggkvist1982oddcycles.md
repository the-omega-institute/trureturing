## Attested claim

Every finite triangle-free graph with minimum degree greater than three-eighths
of its order admits a homomorphism to the five-cycle. The strict inequality is
`3 * |V(G)| < 8 * δ(G)`. Bipartite graphs are included, since a two-coloring
maps them to an edge of the five-cycle.

The formal target is the five-cycle with vertices reindexed by multiplication
by three in `Fin 5`. This reindexing preserves the homomorphism conclusion.
