## Verified locator

DOI: 10.37236/7299

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.06954

Published in Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 25(1) (2018). Campbell cites
this paper as reference [2]. Section 1 defines the cycle polynomial; Section 3
relates it to orbital chromatic polynomials. Propositions 3.5 and 3.6 give the
product and wreath-product constructions cited in Campbell's irreducibility discussion.

## Mathematical scope

The cycle polynomial is F_G(x) = Σ_{g∈G} x^{c(g)}, where fixed points count as
cycles. The reciprocal-pair identity compares F_G(−x), multiplied by (−1)^n,
with the sum of the chromatic polynomials of the graphs obtained by contracting
permutation cycles. An edge within one contracted cycle produces a loop and
contributes zero proper colourings. This is background for the Campbell
refutation; the sign-coupled counterexample is a separate result.
