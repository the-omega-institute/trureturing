# Partition and bond lattice Möbius functions

Section 7, Example 1, pp. 359–360, gives the interval identification and
Proposition 3: for the lattice of partitions of an n-element set,

$$
\mu(\hat 0,\hat 1)=(-1)^{n-1}(n-1)!.
$$

For a partition P, its upper interval is the partition lattice on the actual
blocks of P. Consequently its coefficient to the top depends only on the
number k of blocks and equals $(-1)^{k-1}(k-1)!$. The source attributes earlier
determinations to Schützenberger and, independently, Frucht and Rota.
Section 9 relates these partition coefficients to bond lattices of graphs;
the complete graph gives the full partition lattice.

The formal statement transports the integer coefficient to an arbitrary
commutative ring. Its moment–cumulant application retains the assumption that
every moment product is the sum of cumulant products over refinements. It does
not identify these weights with coefficients of a particular logarithm or
exponential generating function.
