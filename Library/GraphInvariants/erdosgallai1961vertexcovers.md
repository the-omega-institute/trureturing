# On the minimal number of vertices representing the edges of a graph

Publ. Math. Inst. Hungar. Acad. Sci. 6 (1961), 181–203. In the finite
graph application, edge-criticality means that deletion of any edge
strictly reduces the vertex-cover number. Isolated vertices may be
discarded. The resulting support has at most twice that number of vertices.
The reduction from a lower bound on the vertex-cover number to a subgraph
of exact cover number is Lemma 3.1 of the CPECC paper.
