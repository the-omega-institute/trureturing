# Well-covered Cartesian products

## Verified locator

https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.08716v1

Lemma 3.5, printed page 7, proves that the Cartesian product of connected
graphs of order at least three and girth at least four is not well-covered.
The introduction also recalls the well-coveredness of products of two
complete graphs.

The Cayley square argument using a deleted-vertex coloring does not invoke
Lemma 3.5. Its nearly empty layer treats connected graphs without a girth
restriction. This note identifies the related published obstruction; it
does not claim a Lean formalization of the general triangle-free product
lemma.
