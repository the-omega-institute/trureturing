# Well-covered positive graph powers

## Verified locator

https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09300v1

The introduction and Definitions 2.2–2.3 define graph powers by paths of
length at most the positive exponent and the property that all such powers
are well-covered. Distinct components remain nonadjacent. Well-coveredness
can equivalently be expressed by equal cardinalities of inclusion-maximal
independent sets, using complementation between independent sets and vertex
covers.

This reference supplies the terminology and power convention. The abelian
Cayley square characterization and the deleted-vertex layer construction
are separate mathematical arguments; they are not attributed to this paper.
