## Statement

The entry is the coordination sequence of the `tsi` uniform tiling of 3-space: $a(n)$ counts the vertices at graph distance exactly $n$ from a fixed vertex of its edge graph, with terms $1,8,28,60,106,164,236,\dots$. Its formulas are labelled conjectural:

> Conjectures from _Colin Barker_, Feb 11 2018: (Start)
> G.f.: (1 + 6*x + 12*x^2 + 6*x^3 + x^4) / ((1 - x)^3*(1 + x)).
> a(n) = (13*n^2 + 4) / 2 for n>0 and even.
> a(n) = (13*n^2 + 3) / 2 for n odd.
> a(n) = 2*a(n-1) - 2*a(n-3) + a(n-4) for n>4. (End)

## Encoding and boundary

The repository presents the net on $\mathbb Z^3$: square grids in the layers, and between consecutive layers prism slabs whose axes alternate between the two grid directions (each vertex joined to two vertices of the next layer), realized by regular triangular prisms with unit edges. It proves the two closed forms for every $n\ge1$ from the vertex $(0,0,0)$, through an exact distance formula (layer distance plus the $L^1$ distance to a rectangle determined by the slab orientations). The generating function and the recurrence follow from the closed forms but are not stated separately; the companion partial sums and the other uniform tilings are outside this conclusion.
