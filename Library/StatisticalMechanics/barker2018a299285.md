# OEIS A299285

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A299285

The entry was read through the plain-text endpoint `https://oeis.org/search?q=id:A299285&fmt=text` (revision 25, June 12, 2024). It was authored by N. J. A. ## Statement

The entry is the coordination sequence of the `tea` uniform tiling of 3-space: $a(n)$ counts the vertices at graph distance exactly $n$ from a fixed vertex of its edge graph, with terms $1,10,33,73,128,199,285,388,506,640,789,955,\dots$. Its formulas are:

> From _Colin Barker_, Feb 11 2018: (Start)
> G.f.: (1 + 8*x + 14*x^2 + 17*x^3 + 14*x^4 + 8*x^5 + x^6) / ((1 - x)^3*(1 + x)*(1 + x^2)).
> a(n) = 2*a(n-1) - a(n-2) + a(n-4) - 2*a(n-5) + a(n-6) for n>6. (End)
> [I suspect Barker's formulas only conjectures. - _N. J. A. Sloane_, Jun 12 2024]
> If the above formulas are true, then a(n) = (31 - 3*(-1)^n + 126*n^2 + 4*A056594(n))/16 for n > 0. - _Stefano Spezia_, Jun 08 2024

Here A056594 is the period-four sequence $1,0,-1,0$.

## Encoding and boundary

The repository presents the net on $\mathbb Z^3$: triangular lattices in the layers; between consecutive layers either a slab of tetrahedra and octahedra, whose orientation alternates between layers $k\equiv0$ and $k\equiv2 \pmod 4$, or a slab of triangular prisms for odd $k$. It proves Spezia's closed form, $8a(n)=63n^2+16,\,63n^2+17,\,63n^2+12,\,63n^2+17$ for $n\equiv0,1,2,3\pmod4$ and every $n\ge1$, from the vertex $(0,0,0)$, through an exact distance formula (layer distance plus the distance to a hexagonal region determined by the slabs crossed). Barker's generating function and recurrence follow from the closed form with $a(0)=1$ but are not stated separately; the partial sums A299286 and the other uniform tilings are outside this conclusion.
