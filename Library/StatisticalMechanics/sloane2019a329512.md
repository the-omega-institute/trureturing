# Diagonal honeycomb cylinders

The graph has vertices A(i,j) and B(i,j), for integer i,j. The neighbours of A(i,j)
are B(i,j), B(i−1,j), B(i,j−1). The cylinder identifies a vertex with all of its
translates through (n,n), preserving its A or B label. The entry specifies that
identified vertices are separated by a path of length 4n. Shells use shortest-path
distance from A(0,0), including the radius-zero shell.

## Context and conventions

Chaim Goodman-Strauss and N. J. A. Sloane, *A Coloring Book Approach to Finding
Coordination Sequences*, Acta Crystallographica A75 (2019), 121–134,
[arXiv:1803.08530](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.08530), describes the trunks and
branches method cited by the entry. It supplies context for coordination sequences;
the source's conjectural status is taken from the pinned OEIS revision above.

Natural subtraction in an exponent is truncated. For n≥1, 2n−2 is nonnegative.
The coefficient n−2 is formed in the integers, so its value at n=1 is −1.
The formal series ring is over the integers. Multiplication by (1−X)² clears the
two formal denominators; 1−X has constant coefficient one and hence is a unit.
No analytic convergence or physical realization is asserted.
