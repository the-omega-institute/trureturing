# Hexavalent kisrhombille coordination sequence

The sequence is attributed to N. J. A. Sloane; the generating-function conjecture
is attributed to Colin Barker. The graph can be realized as the inclusion graph
of the nonempty simplices of the triangular lattice. Its face nodes have degree
six. The root is the upward face with lattice vertices (0,0), (1,0), (0,1).
