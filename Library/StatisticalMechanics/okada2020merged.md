# Okada, Merged-log-concavity of rational functions

S. Okada, arXiv:2003.02112v2 (v1 4 March 2020, v2 30 January 2024; primary math.CO, cross-listed cond-mat.stat-mech and hep-th). Quotations are from the v2 TeX source `phases.tex`.

The pre-parcel of width $\mu$ is $U_a^b(\mu,w,\rho,x)={b\brack a}^w_{x^\rho}B(\mu,w,b-a,\rho,x)$, with ${m\brack m'}^w_x=\prod_i{m_i\brack m'_i}_{x_i}^{w_i}$, and the pre-merged determinant is $d(U)_a^b(\mu,w,\rho,e,x)=U_a^b(\mu,w,\rho,x)-U_{a^\vee}^b(\mu,w,\rho,x)\prod x(1,\lambda)^e$, where $a^\vee$ is the flip of $a$. For $\rho=\iota^\mu(1)$ the base shift function is $1$. A tuple $(\mu,a,b)$ is almost strictly pre-fitting if $0\le a_1<\dots<a_\mu\le b_1<\dots<b_\mu$, and $f\in\mathbb Q[z]$ is almost palindromic if its nonzero coefficients $f_{\lambda_1},\dots,f_{\lambda_\kappa}$, listed by increasing degree, satisfy $f_{\lambda_i}=f_{\lambda_{\kappa-i+1}}$. The paper states:

> Let $\mu\in\Z_{\geq 3}$. Then, $\dt(U)_{a}^{b}(\mu,\iota^{\mu}(1),\iota^{\mu}(1), 0, \iota^{\mu}(q))$ is not an almost palindromic $q$-polynomial, provided $\tp{\mu,a,b}$ is almost strictly pre-fitting.

With these parameters the polynomial is $\prod_i{b_i\brack a_i}_q-\prod_i{b_i\brack a_{\mu+1-i}}_q$.
