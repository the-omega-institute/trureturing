# O'Connor and Ramgoolam, Gauged permutation invariant tensor quantum mechanics, least common multiples and the inclusion-exclusion principle

D. O'Connor and S. Ramgoolam, arXiv:2506.18813v2 (2025). Quotations are from the v2 TeX source.

The rank-$s$ partition function is a sum over the conjugacy classes $p=[a_1^{p_1},\dots,a_K^{p_K}]$ of $S_N$, with distinct parts $a_i$ and multiplicities $p_i$. The pole order of the class $p$ at $x\to1$ is

$$\mathrm{Degree}([p,N,s])=\sum_{i_1,\dots,i_s=1}^K\frac{a_{i_1}\cdots a_{i_s}\,p_{i_1}\cdots p_{i_s}}{L(a_{i_1},\dots,a_{i_s})},$$

where $L$ is the least common multiple. Section 7.2 states:

> Computations with Mathematica and the analysis of special families give strong evidence that the leading contributions in the high temperature limit $x\rightarrow1$ come from $p=[1^N]$ followed by $p=[2,1^{N-2}]$.

and Section 9 asks: "It would be very interesting to find the proof of the general $s$ conjecture for the leading two terms." The case $s=2$ is proved in the authors' earlier paper (arXiv:2405.13150, Appendix B).
