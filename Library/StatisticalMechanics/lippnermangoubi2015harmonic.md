# Harmonic squares and ordered mixed differences

Gabor Lippner and Dan Mangoubi, *Harmonic functions on the lattice:
Absolute monotonicity and propagation of smallness*, Duke Mathematical
Journal **164**(13), 2577–2595. The source version is arXiv:1312.4550v3;
the relevant locators are Theorem 2.1, Claim 2.2 and Remark 2.3.

For the nearest-neighbour mean on the integer lattice in positive dimension,
write $P=(2d)^{-1}\sum_{v=\pm e_i}T_v$, $L=P-I$, and
$\delta_v=T_v-I$. For every real harmonic $H$, the classical identity is

$$
L^k(H^2)(x)=(2d)^{-k}
\sum_{(v_1,\ldots,v_k)}
\bigl(\delta_{v_1}\cdots\delta_{v_k}H(x)\bigr)^2.
$$

The sum is over ordered words, including repeated directions. The empty word
has identity difference operator. No boundedness or growth assumption is part
of this identity. Its summands show that every displayed Laplacian iterate is
nonnegative. Positivity of the finite binomial coefficients then gives
$P^N(H^2)(x)\geq\binom N3L^3(H^2)(x)$.

This attribution concerns an established mathematical identity. The
constant-Poisson subtraction, the growth estimates and the passage from
vanishing third differences to a quadratic polynomial are separate arguments.
No article text or external proof code is reproduced here.
