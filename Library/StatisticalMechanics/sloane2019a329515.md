# Odd-width honeycomb cylinders

The graph has vertices A(i,j) and B(i,j), for integer i,j. The neighbours of A(i,j)
are B(i,j), B(i−1,j), B(i,j−1). The odd cylinder identifies a vertex with its translates
through (n+1,n), preserving its A or B label.

## Verified locator

OEIS: https://oeis.org/A329515. Revision 32. The entry's NAME is:

> Coordination sequences of honeycomb cylinders of odd width 2*n+1.

Its conjectural FORMULA lines are:

> The g.f. for row n is (1/(1-x))*(1 + 2*x - (n-2)*x^(2*n+1) - n*x^(2*n+2)) + 3*x^2/(1-x)^2 - 3*x^(2*n+1)/(1-x)^2.

The formalization uses the denominator-cleared identity over `PowerSeries ℤ`.
