# Rodrigues, Kevrekidis and Dobson, N-break states in a chain of nonlinear oscillators

A. S. Rodrigues, P. G. Kevrekidis and M. Dobson, Phys. Rev. E 99(2), 022201 (2019); arXiv:1802.03477v2. Quotations are from the v2 TeX source.

The model has $M$ free oscillators with displacements $u_0,\dots,u_{M+1}$, clamped by $u_0=u_{M+1}=0$ and pre-stretched to separation $d$, with potential energy $V(\mathbf u)=\sum_{n=1}^{M+1}[\phi(d+u_n-u_{n-1})-\phi(d)]$ and equations of motion $\ddot u_n=\phi'(d+u_{n+1}-u_n)-\phi'(d+u_n-u_{n-1})$ for $n=1,\dots,M$. The interaction is $\phi(r)=r^{-12}-2r^{-6}$, with inflection point $r_i=(13/7)^{1/6}$. Linearizing about a steady state gives $\lambda^2\boldsymbol\delta=J(\mathbf u_0)\boldsymbol\delta$ with $J$ the Jacobian of the force field; positive $\lambda^2$ is a dynamical instability. A bond of length $r<r_i$ is elastic and one of length $r>r_i$ is broken. The abstract states:

> Each of these higher ``excited state'' solutions possesses an additional unstable pair of eigenvalues. We thus conjecture that $k$ break solutions will possess at least $k-1$ (and at most $k$) pairs of unstable eigenvalues.
