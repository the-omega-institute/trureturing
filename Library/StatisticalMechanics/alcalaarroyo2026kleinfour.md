# Binary Klein-four generation and the two monoid counts

The public supplementary source is version v1.1.0, at commit
`fae47ad9dbbd4cbc1462a058dffe6ad4c2e416f1`. The structural proof and the two count proofs are
attributed to Edgar Alcalá-Arroyo, Alonso Castillo-Ramirez, Haobo Ma and
Wenlin Zhang. Its MIT copyright and permission notice are retained in the
adapted Lean modules; the full notice below also covers the count exposition.

The public supplement pins `leanprover/lean4:v4.33.0` and Mathlib commit
`db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d`. This repository currently pins
`leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0` and Mathlib
`56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679`. These differ, so the supplier
is not a compatible direct dependency at the current pins. Retirement of each transplanted supplier declaration
is tested against the dependencies actually resolved at the repository's
own pins: when an equivalent declaration is available there, verify its
elaborated statement and any required domain correspondence, migrate all
live consumers to direct references, and remove the replaced transplant
after the affected proofs and repository checks pass without expanding
the axiom or sorry closure. Preserve the actual-domain transport proofs
that those consumers still require. Upstream acceptance alone does not
satisfy this condition. No equivalent replacement or direct-reference
migration is established here at the current dependency pin; this note
does not assert that the retirement condition is already satisfied.

The orbit model has two constant configurations, three two-point orbits
and two free orbits. A lawful lazy map preserves the merger implication,
as do invertible maps and composition. Conversely, elementary collapses
factor every map that does not merge the free orbits; a two-cell lazy map
and two nonmerging factors realize each map satisfying the merger
implication. The configuration equivalence identifies these transformations
with all cellular automata in the existing local-rule vocabulary.

This entry cites the published structural formalization and, for the
existing Factorization Blueprint exposition, both exact counts. On
`H = Multiplicative (ZMod 2 × ZMod 2)` and `Q = H → Bool`, with actual
function equality and right-to-left composition, the source-backed
conclusions are `Nat.card (closure (U ∪ L_H)) = 59712` and
`Nat.card (closure L_H) = 4007`. Here `U` is the full invertible CA family
and `L_H` includes every finite identity-containing neighborhood and every
exceptional Boolean pattern. Both monoids include the empty-word identity;
the identity is not itself lazy.

The Blueprint gives the full 16-point representation, both table inverse
laws, the structural factorization, the disjoint count
`256 · 224 + 74 · 32 = 59712`, the complete certificate deduction for 4007,
and both conjugation transports to `Function.End Q`. The adopted native
owners contain the structural facts; this exposition adds no Lean count
declaration. In particular the supplier Parent/Edge certificate family
and its 63 block proofs are not adopted current-pin premises. Its archived
standard-axiom audit is not a new current-pin check. The source-backed
written proof and current native acceptance are separate claims.

No priority, shortest-word, relative-rank or maximal-submonoid claim is made.

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23280244 (Zenodo metadata identifies the MIT-licensed
v1.1.0 supplementary software).

Source: https://github.com/the-omega-institute/klein-four-lazy-ca-supplement/tree/fae47ad9dbbd4cbc1462a058dffe6ad4c2e416f1

The structural membership equivalence is `KleinFour.membership_iff` in
`KleinFour/Main.lean`. Its generator and composition invariant is proved
in `KleinFour/Closure.lean`; the nonmerging and merging factorizations are
in `KleinFour/Factorization.lean` (`nonmerge_mem`, `merge_factor` and
`criterion_implies_membership`). These locations refer to the pinned
public source for binary configurations over the Klein four-group.

The count and certificate locators at that same immutable revision are:

- `KleinFour/Tables.lean`: `ca_tableOf`, `tableOf_ca`, `tables` and the full
  table representation.
- `KleinFour/Count.lean`: `goodParts`, `membersToTables`,
  `good_table_card` and `KleinFour.membership_card` (59712).
- `KleinFour/LazyData.lean` and `LazyCheck.lean`: exact candidate/generator
  data, packing, unpacking and lookup; array defaults and index moduli
  alone imply no certificate fact.
- `KleinFour/LazyFamilies.lean`: lawfulness of all 54 generators, complete
  neighborhood/pattern coverage, table equality and the identity root.
- `KleinFour/LazyBoolean.lean` and `LazyBounded.lean`: actual table
  composition, Boolean equality reflection and `CheckBlock`.
- Every `KleinFour/LazyBlocks/Block00.lean` through `Block62.lean`, assembled
  by `LazyEdges.lean`: all parent/index facts and all 54 edges at every
  candidate index. The final block includes the full tail through 4006.
- `KleinFour/LazyReachable.lean`: `ParentCertificate`, `EdgeCertificate`,
  reachability, closure, index injectivity and `enumeration : Fin 4007 ≃ D`.
- `KleinFour/LazyFinal.lean`: `KleinFour.lazy_membership_card` (4007),
  applying the complete certificates without certificate hypotheses.

These locators extend the exposition's source scope to both counts and
the lazy-only certificate. They do not extend native adoption, register a
new theorem, or include shortest-word claims or later package versions.
