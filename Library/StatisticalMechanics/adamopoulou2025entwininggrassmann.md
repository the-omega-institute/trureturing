## Source statements

§3, Eq. (18): "map S_{a,b} is given by u₁ = y₁²(1 + χ₁ψ₂)/(b x₁) + y₁(y₂ − a − ψ₁ψ₂)/b, v₁ = x₁, u₂ = b/y₁, v₂ = a + x₁x₂ − y₁/x₁ + χ₁χ₂, ξ₁ = ψ₁ − (y₁/x₁)χ₁, η₁ = χ₁, ξ₂ = ψ₂, η₂ = χ₂ + (y₁/x₁)ψ₂", with even variables x₁, x₂, y₁, y₂ and odd variables χ₁, χ₂, ψ₁, ψ₂ in Γ(n).

§4: "we show that these maps are measure preserving. This means that for each of them there exists a function m of the dynamical variables such that the Jacobian determinant J of the map can be written as J = m(x₁, x₂, χ₁, χ₂, y₁, y₂, ψ₁, ψ₂)/m(u₁, u₂, ξ₁, ξ₂, v₁, v₂, η₁, η₂). It can be verified that maps R_{a,b}, S_{a,b}, T_{a,b} in (29)–(31) are measure preserving with m … equal to 1, 1/y₁ and 1/x₁, respectively."

§5: "each of the maps R_{a,b}, S_{a,b}, T_{a,b} in (43)–(45) admits an invariant measure m. These measures are m = 1 for R_{a,b}, m = 1/y₁₁² for S_{a,b}, and m = 1/x₁₁² for T_{a,b}. … We conjecture that in Γ(n) the map R_{a,b} is volume preserving for every n, while the maps S_{a,b} and T_{a,b} preserve measures of the form y₁₁^{−n} and x₁₁^{−n}, respectively, with x₁₁ and y₁₁ defined similar to the cases n = 1 and n = 2."

## Reading used by the repository

Γ(3) is the exterior algebra on three generators with its eight monomials. Expanding the eight variables of Eq. (18) in their monomial coefficients gives a rational map on ℝ³², and J is the determinant of its derivative. Quotients by even elements are products with the two-sided inverse, which exists when the scalar part is nonzero. y₁₁ is the scalar coefficient of y₁.
