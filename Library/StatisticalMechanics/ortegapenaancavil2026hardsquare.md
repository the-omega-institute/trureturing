[Equation (2.2)](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.06780v1#S2.E2) uses the three
weights in the order $W_{pq},W_{pr},W_{qr}$ with one nonzero scalar
multiplying the right sum. [Definition 2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.06780v1#S2.Thmtheorem2)
specifies the analytic family. [Section 4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.06780v1#S4.SS1)
gives the ordered horizontal seam, coordinatewise vertical exclusion, and
blocked occupation weight for two horizontal sites. [Question 5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.06780v1#S5.Thmtheorem2)
asks whether Corollary 4.2 extends to blocks of $k\ge 3$ sites and arbitrary shapes.
