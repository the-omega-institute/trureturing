# Flores and Peltola, Generators, projectors, and the Jones-Wenzl algebra

S. M. Flores and E. Peltola, arXiv:1811.12364v2 (11 December 2018), math-ph. Quotations are from the arXiv v2 TeX source `WJalgebra_gen-sec1-intro.tex`; macros are expanded.

The Temperley–Lieb algebra $\mathrm{TL}_n(\nu)$ is presented by generators $U_1,\dots,U_{n-1}$ with $U_iU_{i\pm1}U_i=U_i$, $U_i^2=\nu U_i$ and $U_iU_j=U_jU_i$ for $|i-j|>1$, with $\nu=-q-q^{-1}$. For $q\notin\{\pm1\}$, $\bar{\mathfrak p}(q)$ is $p$ when $q=e^{\pi ip'/p}$ with $p,p'$ coprime and $\infty$ when $q$ is not a root of unity; $\bar{\mathfrak p}(\pm1)=\infty$. The Jones–Wenzl projectors satisfy $P_1=\mathbf 1$ and $P_{s+1}=P_s+\frac{[s]}{[s+1]}P_sU_sP_s$. For a multiindex $\varsigma$ with $\max\varsigma<\bar{\mathfrak p}(q)$, $\mathrm{JW}_\varsigma(\nu)=P_\varsigma\mathrm{TL}_{n_\varsigma}(\nu)P_\varsigma$, where $P_\varsigma$ is the composite projector.

Theorem 1.1, item 1, assuming $n_\varsigma<\bar{\mathfrak p}(q)$:

> The unit $\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{JW}_\varsigma}=P_\varsigma$ together with the $\varsigma$-Jones-Wenzl link diagrams $U_i:=P_\varsigma U^{\mathrm{TL}}_{s_1+s_2+\dotsm+s_i}P_\varsigma$ with $i\in\{1,2,\ldots,d_\varsigma-1\}$, forms a minimal generating set for the Jones-Wenzl algebra $\mathrm{JW}_\varsigma(\nu)$.

> Conjecture 1.2. Item 1 in theorem 1.1 holds whenever $\max \varsigma < \bar{\mathfrak p}(q)$.
