# OEIS A026023

A026023 (Clark Kimberling, offset 0, data `1, 2, 4, 8, 15, 30, 56, 112, …`)
is

> a(n) = number of (s(0), s(1), ..., s(n)) such that s(i) is a nonnegative
> integer and |s(i) - s(i-1)| = 1 for i = 1,2,...,n and s(0) = 3.

A comment by Robert M. Ziff (2014) reads

> a(n)/2^n is the probability that a random walker starting at x=4 and
> jumping +-1 with equal probability at each time step is not adsorbed at the
> boundary x=0 at time n.

and the formula field contains `a(2n) = C(2n+2, n), a(2n+1) = 2*a(2n).` and

> Conjecture: (n+4)*(n-1)*a(n) +(n-1)*(n+1)*a(n-1)
> -2*(n+1)*(2*n+1)*a(n-2) -4*(n-1)*(n+1)*a(n-3)=0. - _R. J. Mathar_, Sep 29
> 2012
