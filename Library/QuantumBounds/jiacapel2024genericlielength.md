## Source statements

Definition 3 (arXiv v3, p. 17) states: “The Lie-length of a Lie-generating system $U$ of a finite-dimensional Lie-algebra $\mathcal A$ is the minimal length of the (right-nested) brackets such that the brackets span the whole Lie-algebra, namely $\mathtt{Lie}\ell(U)=\min\{\ell\mid\mathcal A=\mathrm{span}\{U_n,n\le\ell\}\}$, where $U_n$ is defined as: $U_1=\mathrm{span}\{U\};\ U_n=\mathrm{span}[U,U_{n-1}]$, $n\ge2$.”

Conjecture 6 (arXiv v3, p. 19) states: “Let $S$ be a random Lie-generating set of $\mathfrak{su}(n)$, then $\mathtt{Lie}\ell(S)=\Theta(\log n)$ almost surely.”
