## Source statements

§2: "The n-qubit Clifford group C_n is the group of n-qubit unitaries generated by the single-qubit gates H, S, and the CNOT two-qubit gate. … The pure n-qubit stabilizer states are the ones generated by the Clifford group acting on |0^n⟩ … The set of all (mixed) stabilizer states is formed by all convex combinations (convex hull) of pure stabilizer states, S̄_n := conv{|ψ⟩⟨ψ| : |ψ⟩ ∈ S_n}."

§2, Eq. (2): "D_{α,z}(ρ‖σ) := 1/(α−1) log Tr[(ρ^{α/2z} σ^{(1−α)/z} ρ^{α/2z})^z] if (α < 1 ∧ ρ ̸⊥ σ) ∨ ρ ≪ σ; +∞ else."

§2: the data-processing inequality holds for "1. 0 < α < 1 and z ≥ max{α, 1−α}, 2. 1 < α ≤ 2 and α/2 ≤ z ≤ α, and 3. 2 ≤ α < ∞ and α − 1 ≤ z ≤ α"; the line α = 1 is included in the region D. Eq. (10): "D_{α,z}(ρ) := inf_{σ∈S̄} D_{α,z}(ρ‖σ)".

§5: "It is still an open problem whether the α-z Rényi relative entropies of magic in the range |(1−α)/z| = 1 are additive for all two and three-qubit states. Numerical simulations suggest that additivity holds. We discuss in Section 10 a possible direction to prove this result."

Table 1 caption: "in Section 10 we conjecture that additivity holds for all states."
