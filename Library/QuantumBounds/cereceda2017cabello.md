# Cabello's nonlocality for generalized three-qubit GHZ states

José L. Cereceda, arXiv:1609.04763v2 [quant-ph] (2016); Quantum Studies:
Mathematics and Foundations 4(3) (2017) 205–215. Quotations are from the
arXiv source.

The measurements (Section 2):

> Each of the qubits then enters its own measuring station where, for each run of the experiment, one of two alternative measurements is performed: $U_1$ or $D_1$ for qubit 1, $U_2$ or $D_2$ for qubit 2, and $U_3$ or $D_3$ for qubit 3. Each measurement gives the possible outcomes $+1$ or $-1$. The observables $U_k$ and $D_k$ have associated operators $\hat{U}_k =|u^{+}_{k}\rangle\langle u^{+}_{k}| - |u^{-}_{k}\rangle\langle u^{-}_{k}|$ and $\hat{D}_k =|d^{+}_{k}\rangle\langle d^{+}_{k}| - |d^{-}_{k}\rangle\langle d^{-}_{k}|$

The conditions of Cabello's argument are $P(U_1,U_2,U_3|+++) = P$,
$P(D_1,U_2,U_3|+++) = P(U_1,D_2,U_3|+++) = P(U_1,U_2,D_3|+++) = 0$ and
$P(D_1,D_2,D_3|---) = Q$, where

> $P(U_1,U_2,U_3|+++)$ denotes the joint probability that a measurement of $U_1$, $U_2$, and $U_3$ on qubits 1, 2, and 3, respectively, gives the outcome $+1$ for each of them.

For the generalized GHZ states the paper finds the maximum $C = P - Q = 9/64$
at $t = 1$, $x = y = z = 1$, $\gamma = -\arccos(7/8)$, and then states:

> Motivated by this fact, we conjecture that, for the case in which $Q >0$, the maximum quantum violation of inequality (ineq2) (subject to the fulfillment of conditions (hc2)-(hc4)) is achieved, over all possible states and choice of observables, for the GHZ state $|\psi \rangle =\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|v_1, v_2,v_3 \rangle + |w_1, w_2, w_3 \rangle)$, with the maximum violation being equal to $C_{\text{max}} =\frac{9}{64}$.

Here inequality (ineq2) is $C = P - Q \le 0$. The conclusions restate it:

> We conjectured that the value $\frac{9}{64}$ is indeed optimal over all entangled states of three qubits subjected to arbitrary local projective measurements.
