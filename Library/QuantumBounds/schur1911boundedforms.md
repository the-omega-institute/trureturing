# Weighted quadratic-form Schur estimate

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.1515/crll.1911.140.1

Source: https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.1911.140.1

Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik 140, pages 1–28. Crossref identifies the original article by its title and DOI.

## Application

For a finite index set, a symmetric nonnegative real kernel K and strictly positive weights f, the weighted edge inequality gives

$$
\operatorname{Re}\sum_{i,j}K_{ij}\overline{z_i}z_j
\leq\sum_i |z_i|^2\frac{\sum_jK_{ij}f_j}{f_i}.
$$

This is the finite quadratic-form version of the classical test. The configuration-dependent fermionic comparison weights and the fourth-moment row estimate are separate results of the present settlement.
