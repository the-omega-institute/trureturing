# CGLMP tightness

The abstract, PDF p. 1, states:

> In this paper we prove that the inequality introduced by Collins, Gisin, Linden, Massar and Popescu [11] is tight, or in other words, it is a facet of the convex polytope generated by all local-realistic joint probabilities of d outcomes.

Section III proves the facet property for all numbers of outcomes. The
Lean theorem `CglmpFacetRigidity.bipartite_rigidity` is its dual-functional
formulation: every bipartite local function vanishing on the saturating
strategies is a scalar multiple of the CGLMP slack. With a=A_1, A=A_2,
b=B_1 and B=B_2, the bracket expression is

$$
J_2=[A-b]_K+[a+B]_K+[-a+b]_K+[-A-B-1]_K.
$$

The sign of B differs by an outcome relabelling from the usual CGLMP
convention. In the theorem K is nonzero; its facet interpretation uses
K ≥ 2. The conclusion is literature-attested. The proof here uses a
short-arc potential and a single wrap coefficient, rather than the
source's explicit linearly independent saturating vectors.
