## Source statements

"Definitions": "Given a density matrix ρ of a system S, its purification is generically written as |Ψ^V_ρ⟩_{SA} = (ρ^{1/2} ⊗ V_{R→A})|I⟩_{SR}, where V is an isometry from R to A, i.e. V†V = 1_R, and |I⟩_{SR} = Σ_{n=1}^{rank ρ} |n⟩_S ⊗ |n⟩_R is an unnormalized EPR state. For ρ evolving unitarily by U_t = e^{−iHt}, the purification evolves as |Ψ^V_ρ(t)⟩ = (U_t ρ^{1/2} ⊗ V(t))|I⟩ = (U_t ⊗ V(t))|Ψ_ρ⟩ … Choosing V(t) = U_t^*, we define the time-dependent purification after time t by |Ψ^{U*}_ρ(t)⟩ = (U_t ρ^{1/2} U_t† ⊗ I)|I⟩ = (U_t ⊗ U_t^*)|Ψ_ρ⟩. (2)" Before Eq. (6): "For the time-independent and time-dependent purification, the evolution is governed by H ⊗ I and H ⊗ I − I ⊗ H^*, respectively."

"Inequalities among complexities": "Based on the three types of purifications defined above, we conjecture inequalities for the complexities of mixed states and their purifications, with analytical and numerical evidence provided in the next section. 1. Krylov operator complexity of the mixed state is upper bounded by the operator complexity of the time-independent purification and is lower bounded by the state complexity of the time-dependent purification, C_S(|Ψ^{U*}_ρ(t)⟩) ≤ C_K(ρ(t)) ≤ C_K(|Ψ^I_ρ(t)⟩) (4) where the equality holds in the pure state limit."

"Examples": "A one-qubit mixed state under a generic unitary evolution is simple enough that the analytic expressions of its Krylov CoPs can be written down … the conjectures in the last section are analytically confirmed."

Supplemental Material, Eq. (S.4): "the spread complexity of the state |ψ(t)⟩ is defined as C_S(t) ≡ Σ_{n=0}^{D−1} n|ψ_n(t)|²", where ψ_n(t) are the amplitudes in the Lanczos basis |K_n⟩ of the Krylov sequence |ψ⟩, H|ψ⟩, H²|ψ⟩, …; Eqs. (S.5)–(S.7): the operator analogue, built from O, LO, L²O, … with the Liouvillian L = [H, ·] and the Hilbert–Schmidt inner product.

## Reading used by the repository

For full-rank ρ the source's |Ψ_ρ⟩ is the canonical purification Σ_{ij}(√ρ)_{ij}|i⟩|j⟩, and the time-dependent purification is generated by H ⊗ 1 − 1 ⊗ Hᵀ (Hᵀ = H^* for Hermitian H). Both complexities are the mean Lanczos index Σ_n n|⟨K_n, φ(t)⟩|² of the normalized initial vector evolved by the generator, with K_n the Gram–Schmidt orthonormalization of the Krylov sequence; the operator space carries the Hilbert–Schmidt inner product through row-major vectorization.
