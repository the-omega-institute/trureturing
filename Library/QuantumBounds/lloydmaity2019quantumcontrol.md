## Source statements

Conjecture III (arXiv v1, p. 9) states: “Suppose A and B are random traceless Hermitian matrices in H_d. Then the set of all linearly independent nested commutators in the free Lie algebra of A and B are linearly independent in su(d).” The present result concerns fixed generator count and does not settle that stronger condition.
