## Moment decimation

For the Riesz product with frequencies $4^k$, $k\ge1$, moments vanish outside
multiples of four and satisfy
$m_{4j}=m_j+(m_{j-1}+m_{j+1})/2$.
Thus the pullback $g(z)\mapsto g(z^4)$ pairs Laurent polynomials with the
weighted form of density $1+\operatorname{Re}w$. This moment identity is
separate from equality of spatial-window projection norms.
