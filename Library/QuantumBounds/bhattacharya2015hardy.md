# Witnessing Genuine Mutipartite Non-locality

Some Sankar Bhattacharya, Arup Roy, Amit Mukherjee, Ramij Rahaman,
arXiv:1507.07327v1 [quant-ph] (2015). The title is spelled as printed.
Quotations are from the arXiv source.

The outcomes (Section 3, "Modified Hardy type argument for arbitrary
dimension"):

> The possible outcomes $x_i$ of each such local (i-th party) measurement can be $1,\dots d_i$.

The relaxed Hardy conditions (eq. relH) are
$P(1\dots1|\hat{u}_1\dots \hat{u}_N) = q >0$,
$P(1\dots 1\neg d_r1\dots 1|\hat{u}_1\dots \hat{u}_{r-1}\hat{v}_r\hat{u}_{r+1}\dots \hat{u}_N) = 0$
for all $r$, and
$P(1\dots 1d_i1\dots 1d_j1\dots1|\hat{u}_1\dots\hat{v}_i\dots\hat{v}_j\dots\hat{u}_N)= 0$
for all $i\neq j$, where

> Here $\neg d_r$ denotes other than $d_r$ it can be any positive integer less or equal to $d_r-1$ and $j\in \{1,....,N\}$ is kept fixed.

The no-signalling condition requires that summing over the outcome of
party 1 gives a marginal independent of party 1's input, and

> This condition also holds for the cyclic permutation of the parties.

The observation and the conjecture (Section 3, "Multi-partite Hardy Paradox
in GNST"):

> The optimal value of the probability of success, $q$ in(\ref{relH}) is $\frac{1}{3}$ for $n=3,4$ and $d=2,3,4,5$ in GNST.

> The optimal value of the probability of success, $q$ in(\ref{relH}) is $\frac{1}{3}$ for any arbitrary dimension for any number of parties in GNST.
