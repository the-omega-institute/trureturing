# Prime-race excursions

Crossley measures the change in the split–inert prime-ideal score when the starting height doubles. The measure on starting heights is $dy/y$. The Robin theorem weights each sampled height by the full gap to the next one.

## Verified locator

The table in [the proof](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-excursion-proportions/blob/b02cd1d888ef799eee54d0a878a7aa0b0e5ec065/PROOF.md#L13-L22) gives a lower bound of 10% for each sign at the threshold $1.9735\log 2$. The [published version](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24412164) includes the finite moment bounds.
