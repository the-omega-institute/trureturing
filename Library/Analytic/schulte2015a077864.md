# OEIS A077864

The OEIS entry records the expansion
`(1-x)^(-1)/(1-x-2*x^2-x^3)` and the initial values `1, 2, 5, 11, ...`.
Philippe Deléham's formula gives the order-four recurrence with these initial
values. Werner Schulte's 2015 formula conjectures that the coefficient at `n`
is the odd diagonal sum of the trinomial triangle A027907.

A027907 defines `T(n,k)` by the coefficient of `x^k` in
`(1+x+x^2)^n`, read row by row. The Lean definitions use the same polynomial
coefficient and the same rational generating series.
