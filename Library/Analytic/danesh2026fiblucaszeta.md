# Cancellation profiles of Fibonacci--Lucas zeta sums

The source is Payam Danesh, [arXiv:2609.33564v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33564v1),
submitted 27 September 2026. It is a preprint; its proofs and numerical
constants were not independently audited here, and no Lean verification is
claimed.

The paper studies finite Fibonacci and Lucas weighted sums of zeta values at
negative integers. For nonnegative weights of at most exponential growth it
gives a finite exponential expansion for the normalized absolute-term sum,
with an explicit fixed-order remainder, and proves total-variation
convergence of the normalized term magnitudes to a discrete distribution
determined by the even exponential generating function. In the Fibonacci and
Lucas cases the limiting distribution has its unique maximum at index eight;
the same index is the largest finite-sum term for every even degree at least
26. The paper also records factorial growth of the summation condition number
and places the signed identities in the Bernoulli-polynomial reflection
framework.

These are cancellation statements for a finite special-value sum. The zeta
values occur at negative integers, and the source does not introduce the
Riemann zeta Euler product, von Mangoldt coefficients, ordinary Möbius sums,
or the point value

$$
\frac{\sigma(N)}N.
$$

Consequently the result cannot be substituted for a signed Robin tail or for
the missing map from a FIB address to the same actual Robin candidate. The
fixed cancellation profile also does not imply square-root cancellation for
the ordinary Möbius function. Its use in this project is a literature
boundary: it rules out treating Fibonacci--Lucas special-value cancellation
as an already available RH bridge.
