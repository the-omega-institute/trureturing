# Unweighted complete sums and the source boundary

Lemma 3.1 on pages 6–7 restates a theorem of McIntosh for unweighted
complete binomial-product sums. It supplies related classical asymptotics,
but its inspected statement does not supply the arbitrary a weighting
needed for the complete denominator in the generalized Glasby–Paseman
ratio. That weighted estimate is attributed to
D5/L/Analytic/abel2013binomial, Theorem 3.1.

The bounded audit supplied with #9357 read all 10 pages. The first page
was consulted again on 2026-09-21 UTC for bibliographic identity: Journal
of Combinatorics and Number Theory, volume 4, issue 3, 2012, pp. 1–10.
The original McIntosh body, DOI 10.1006/jnth.1996.0072, remains unread in
that audit. Attribution through this restatement does not establish the
scope of every result in the original body.
