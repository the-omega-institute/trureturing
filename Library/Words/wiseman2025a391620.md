# OEIS A391620

NAME "Number of integer partitions of n that are not the first sums of any composition with all parts > 1."

Gus Wiseman's entry of December 30, 2025 counts partitions of positive n
that are not first sums of a composition with every part greater than one.
The conjecture and definition above are quoted from the entry supplied by
the orchestrator on September 8, 2026. The ordered-part reading writes the
partition in weakly decreasing order and requires equality with the list of
adjacent sums, as in the entry's example (5,4) from (3,2,2).

The Lean module proves this reading by backward reconstruction, and proves
equality of the corresponding counts on Mathlib partitions. Its list theorem
also allows zero parts; its counting theorem includes the empty partition at
n = 0, where both counts vanish. The proof is derived in this repository.
