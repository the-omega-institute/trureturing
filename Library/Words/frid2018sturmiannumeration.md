# Sturmian numeration systems and decompositions to palindromes

## Verified locator

European Journal of Combinatorics 71 (2018), pages 202–212.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2018.04.003
Preprint version: https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.11553v2

## Cited scope

The numeration-based palindrome analysis supplies context for the Fibonacci
prefix family in Frid's Numeration 2018 Conjecture 2. The conjecture's exact
source sentence, printed page 12 of the meeting abstract, is:

> For every k ≥ 1, the prefix of the Fibonacci word of length (100)²ᵏ⁻¹101 cannot be decomposed as a concatenation of at most 2k palindromes.

The repository's `goldenWord` uses `true` for the Fibonacci letter `0` and
`false` for `1`; this letter coding preserves palindrome decompositions.
