## Background and scope

Robert Brignall, Shalosh B. Ekhad, Rebecca Smith and Vincent Vatter,
*Almost avoiding permutations*, Discrete Mathematics 309 (2009), 6626–6631.
DOI: 10.1016/j.disc.2009.06.027.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.15111v1.
The end of Section 2 gives the same generating function as an empirical
candidate for the counting series of `Av(321)^{+1}`. Mathar's recurrence
is an OEIS conjecture and does not occur in that paper.

The formal statement concerns the displayed algebraic series over the
rationals: a root `s` has constant coefficient one and square `1-4X`, and
the series `A` satisfies `D*A=P-B*s`. Existence is part of the statement.
The recurrence quantifies over all such `A` and every natural `n ≥ 4`.
Lagged indices use natural subtraction; the polynomial scalars are rational.
Identification with permutation counts and the paper's Section 4 injection
and all-t questions remain open in this treatment.
