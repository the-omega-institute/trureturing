# The fixed-bottom conjecture in A398542

## Verified locator

The original entry is Charles Cornell Norton's A398542, authored August 1,
2026. The inspected source is revision 18, August 30, 2026, 18:45:04, at
https://oeis.org/A398542/internal. Its revision identifier, defining comments,
and formula were retrieved directly on September 21, 2026. The selected
formula is exactly:

> Conjecture: for every m >= 1 and every b in S(m), d(b,k) = p(k)*binomial(2*k,k) + q(k)*4^k for polynomials p and q depending on b, with deg p <= m-1 and deg q <= m-2. Verified for m <= 6 and k <= 12.

The preceding formula defines S(m) as the 132-avoiding permutations of [m]
and d(b,k) as the number of ways to complete the lower-left cell b with k
points above it. The comments impose 1324 avoidance on the whole gridded
permutation and 213 avoidance on that upper cell. Fixing this value cut
means that d(b,k) counts actual permutations of 1,...,m+k whose lower-value
subsequence is exactly b. The upper subsequence is standardized by subtracting
m. The upper cell may be empty, so d(b,0)=1.

The formal conclusion chooses p_b,q_b in Q[X] before universally quantifying
k in N. Rational coefficients are a sufficient, explicit choice; the OEIS
sentence itself does not specify the coefficient field. It proves
deg p_b <= m-1 and deg q_b <= m-2 for m>=2, and q_b=0 for m=1.
Lean uses natDegree, including natDegree(0)=0, together with this explicit
zero condition. Nat.centralBinom k is Nat.choose (2*k) k.

The preregistration and complete ordinary proof are in
https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/9332.
The current allocation is the first-tier recent fixed-bottom conjecture,
supported by that full ordinary proof. The historical tier-3/note-only
assessment in `oeis2026triage0911` is preserved unchanged. This conclusion
does not enumerate unrestricted 1324 avoiders, determine the full L-gridding
generating function, settle A398446, or constitute a second problem.

## Actual objects and the proof

All finite permutations are bijections of Fin(n), translated to the source's
1,...,n by adding one. `Contains` is the existing generic increasing-position,
relative-value-order predicate. `Actual b k` retains the actual permutation,
literal lower list equality, whole 1324 avoidance, and standardized upper
213 avoidance. `count b k` is Nat.card of this subtype, not a recurrence.

An ordered shuffle is equivalent to a weakly increasing list of upper gaps
in 0,...,m; equal gaps are allowed. Insertion and extraction are mutually
inverse even when either alphabet is empty. Under the two cell avoidance
conditions, the only possible mixed 1324 occurrence is a lower ascent
interleaved with an upper ascent. The first later bottom endpoint above a
prefix minimum defines dead(g), with sentinel m+2. Such an upper ascent
from gap g to gap t is legal exactly when t<dead(g). In particular
dead(0)=m+2 and g<dead(g); monotonicity of dead is neither asserted nor used.

`actualEquiv` identifies actual permutations with these configurations.
Restricting every upper gap to [l,h] keeps the same entire bottom b.
Splitting a nonempty upper permutation at its minimum gives left size a,
right size c, root zero, left values shifted by c+1, and right values shifted
by one. Both children avoid 213, either can be empty, and their actual
configuration intervals are [l,g] and [g,min(h,dead(g)-1)]. The split is
unique. Its cardinality gives N(l,h,0)=1 and, for every k>=0,

$$N(l,h,k+1)=\sum_{g=l}^{h}\sum_{a=0}^{k}
N(l,g,a)N(g,\min(h,\operatorname{dead}(g)-1),k-a).$$

Here N is the cardinality of `IntervalActual`, and N(0,m,k)=d(b,k).
Thus the recurrence is proved about actual objects.

In Q[[X]], let F(l,h) have these actual cardinalities as coefficients, C be
the pinned Catalan series, s=1-2XC, and T=s^(-1). The inverse exists because
the constant coefficient of s is one; Q[[X]] is not treated as a field.
The recurrence gives the exact series equation and F(g,g)=C. For l<h<=m
with h<dead(l), `guarded_polynomial` constructs

$$F(l,h)=T A(T),\qquad A\in\mathbb Q[X],\quad
\operatorname{natDegree}(A)\le 2(h-l)-2.$$

Endpoint terms contribute C F(l,h) and F(l,h) C. Interior children either
satisfy the strict guard at smaller width or are singleton Catalan states.
The identities s^2=1-4X, 4XT^2=T^2-1 and 2XCT=T-1 close the induction.
The full interval is guarded because dead(0)=m+2.

The live coefficient proof applies the pinned Catalan, inverse-unit,
binomial-series, central-binomial and ascending-Pochhammer APIs. Odd powers
T^(2r+1) have coefficient binomial(2k,k) times a rational polynomial of
degree r; even powers T^(2r), r>=1, have coefficient 4^k times a rational
polynomial of degree r-1. The finite polynomial A gives witnesses depending
on b alone, the two sharp degree bounds, and an empty even sum when m=1.
No analytic convergence hypothesis, opaque computation, or unverified Coq
theorem is used. A tree datatype and correctness of the separate official
program are not required by, and are not conclusions of, this theorem.
