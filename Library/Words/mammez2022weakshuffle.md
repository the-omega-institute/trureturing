# Mammez's weak shuffle normalization conjecture

C. Mammez, *Weak stuffle algebras*, International Electronic Journal of Algebra 32 (2022), 1–45, DOI 10.24330/ieja.1060709, arXiv:2003.09142v3. Proposition 3.9 proves normalization for alphabets of cardinality two or three; Conjecture 3.10 asks whether the same normalization holds for every countable alphabet.

The formalization proves the normalization for every alphabet, including uncountable alphabets. The rescaling is diagonal on words: each exceptional ordered pair contributes the inverse coefficient to the exponent counting its admissible initial occurrences.
