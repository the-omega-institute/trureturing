# A110037 and the independent paperfold recurrence

## Verified locator

url: https://oeis.org/A110037

The official https://oeis.org/A110037/internal page was retrieved and read on
2026-09-10. Its formula field credits the displayed difference conjecture to
Alan Michael Gómez Calderón, August 19, 2025, with domain n>=2. Its comment
identifies the signed count as (-1)^floor(n/2) times A090678(n), the parity of
A088567(n). The stored response still labels the difference formula Conjecture.

The official https://oeis.org/A073089/internal page was retrieved and read in
the same batch. Its offset is one. Its recurrence field gives a(1)=0;
a(4n+2)=a(8n+7)=a(16n+13)=0;
a(4n)=a(8n+3)=a(16n+5)=1; and a(8n+1)=a(4n+1).
The positive-index convention restricts a(4n)=1 and the halving branch to
n>0. The Lean value at zero is a totalization and does not enter the theorem.

## Proof and search boundary

Sloane and Sellers' Theorem 2 and Corollary 4 supply the count recurrence and
parity facts; their separate note is sloane2003nonsquashing. The count is a
finite set of finite sets of positive natural parts, with each part at least
the sum of smaller parts. Equality is allowed. The paperfold sequence has the
independent branch recurrence above.

Attempt 2 searched D5, pinned mathlib v4.33.0, and GitHub Lean code for
non-squashing, nonsquashing, paperfold, A088567, A073089, and A110037, without
an importable count-parity theorem in those scopes. Attempt 3 reuses those
receipts and locally proves the maximum-part bijection, the recurrence, and
the parity inputs; it then reuses the previously checked strong-induction
bridge. The implementation record is docs/reports/a110037-attempt3/report.md.
