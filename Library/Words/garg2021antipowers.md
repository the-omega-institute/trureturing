# Fibonacci prefix antipowers

## Verified locator

DOI: https://doi.org/10.46298/dmtcs.7134

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.10816v4

DMTCS 23(3), article 14. Section 3 contains Fact 14, Lemma 15,
Propositions 16–17 and Conjecture 18. Conjecture 18 is on printed page 8.

## Source statement

Conjecture 18 states verbatim:

> Let $F_n$ be an even Fibonacci number. Then, there is an $(F_n-1)$-antipower with block length $\frac{F_n}{2}+F_{n-1}$ that is a prefix of $\mathbf f$.

The source's definition in §3 (printed page 5) states verbatim:

> We prove that the Fibonacci word $\mathbf f$, which is equal to $\varphi^{\omega}(0)$ for $\varphi(0)=01$, $\varphi(1)=0$ and thus pure morphic but not generated by a uniform morphism, also satisfies Conjecture 3.

The abstract (printed page 1) states verbatim:

> Fici, Restivo, Silva, and Zamboni define a $k$-antipower to be a word composed of $k$ pairwise distinct, concatenated words of equal length.

The Fibonacci word is the fixed point beginning with 0 of the morphism
$0\mapsto01$, $1\mapsto0$. The Fibonacci numbers satisfy $F_1=F_2=1$.
A $k$-antipower consists of $k$ consecutive pairwise distinct blocks of equal length.

The encoding uses letters in `Fin 2`, the recursive prefixes $S_0=[0]$,
$S_1=[0,1]$, $S_{k+2}=S_{k+1}S_k$, and their compatible infinite limit.
Boolean `true` in the golden-word model corresponds to letter 0.
`Nat.fib` has the source's indexing, and `Nat.div` by 2 is exact whenever
$F_n$ is even. Blocks begin at $jL$, with $0\le j<F_n-1$ and
$L=F_n/2+F_{n-1}$.
