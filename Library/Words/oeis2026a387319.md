The explicit brief and `oeis2026triage0909.md` (A387319 row and paragraph)
preregister the first-tier OEIS conjecture. The exact target is
`Coincides k ↔ 1 < k ∧ ¬Nat.Prime k ∧ k ∉ ({4,8,10,25} : Finset ℕ)`, where
`Coincides k := ∃ m : ℕ, 6 ≤ m ∧ m ≤ k ∧ 3 ∣ m ∧
3 * ((2*k) % m) = m * ((2*k) % 3)`.
There is no cutoff and no extra hypothesis. No atom was supplied; this brief
is the source obligation.

Before implementing new content, attempt closure from pinned Mathlib and
frozen declarations. If successful, report bind-only and create no module.
Otherwise the proposed escape witness is the divisor/residue criterion:
writing m=3t, the equality is equivalent to t dividing 2k and
`(2k/t) % 3 = (2k) % 3`, with 2≤t and 3t≤k. For 3 not dividing k,
the residue condition is equivalent to t%3=1 (under that divisibility).
This criterion is to be used in the live construction and prime exclusion.
The planned witnesses are t=2 for multiples of 3; t=4 for remaining even
composites; and t=p or 2p for odd composites, with p the least prime factor.
The bound in the last branch requires separating k=25 explicitly.
This is a proposed proof route, not an already verified theorem.
