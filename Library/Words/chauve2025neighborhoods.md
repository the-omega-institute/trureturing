# On the size of the neighborhoods of a word

Equations (2.1)--(2.3) on printed page 2 define the Levenshtein neighborhood,
the condensed neighborhood obtained by excluding words with a proper
neighborhood prefix, and the super condensed neighborhood obtained by excluding
words with a proper neighborhood subword.

The conclusion on printed page 7 asks whether unary words have the smallest
condensed or super condensed neighborhoods among words of the same length over
a fixed alphabet. Version 2, dated September 3, 2025, retains that question.

This note attests the definitions and the published closing question, not the
repository's refutations. Bounded arXiv and Semantic Scholar searches found no
later settlement; that scope is not an exhaustive claim about all literature.
