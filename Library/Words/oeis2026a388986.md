## Provenance and preregistration

Skill context: `lean4`. One Codex implementation worker in the caller's

`e7e63c615460da48effcb4a76f96a3a6730af75d`; branch
`lane/math/a388986-euler-form`.

The caller's target is the exact A228058 inclusion question. For natural
p,a,r with p prime, p % 4 = 1, r odd, r > 1 and gcd(p,r)=1, put
N=p^(4*a+1)*r^2. Define U(N) as the sum of divisors d of N satisfying
gcd(d,N/d)=1, and S(N) as the sum of squarefree divisors of N. Prove
U(N)+S(N)<2*N. All normalized calculations take place in the rationals;
N is positive under the hypotheses. There is no sequence-index shift.

Before any Lean implementation, the proposed escape witness is the triage
finite-product bound: for a finite set F of integers q >= b > 1,
product (1+1/q^2) <= b/(b-1). The live route splits the prime support of r
into: no 3; only 3; 3 and another prime. The source divisor sums must be
connected to their actual prime-power products. This is a first-tier small
OEIS question, conditional on the literature and bind-only checks below.

## Independent numerical evidence

`attempt-1/numeric.py` uses smallest-prime-factor factorization and an
independent divisor sieve: for each d dividing N it separately tests
`gcd(d,N/d)=1` and squarefreeness of d. All 1,000,000 positive N through
10^6 agree with both prime-product formulas (zero mismatches). Of these,
12,941 are Euler-form instances: no 3 in r: 5,905; only 3: 6,104; 3 and
another prime: 932. All satisfy both proposed class bounds and U+S<2N.
Maximum observed (U+S)/N is 28/15, attained at N=45.

Positive nonempty witness: p=5,a=0,r=3,N=45,U=60,S=24,2N=90.
Negative control: p=5,a=0,r=1,N=5,U=6,S=6,2N=10. Only r>1 fails, and the
conclusion fails. Three-class witnesses are respectively N=245 (300+48<490),
45 (60+24<90), and 2205 (3000+192<4410).
Browne's support interpretation first differs at 240: U=408,S=72,2N=480;
rad(240)=30 divides 210<240, whose support includes the additional prime 7.
The sibling interpretation is not the implementation target.

## Lean checkpoints

The finite reciprocal-square product bound compiled without diagnostics
(fragment-1b.log) and was committed as a21c1d41a4. The proof uses minimum
induction with the lower bound shifted to q+1; this is an equivalent finite
route to the preregistered telescoping estimate, with no infinite product.
The first attempt left a (q-1) denominator; explicitly using its positivity
before field_simp resolved that failure.

The second Lean fragment compiles without diagnostics (fragment-2d.log).
For a unitary divisor d, coprimality identifies each exponent in d with the
full exponent in N. The resulting map d -> primeFactors(d) is injective
into the powerset of primeFactors(N), giving U(N) <= product(q^e+1).
Only this upper bound is needed; no equality or surjectivity is claimed by
that lemma. S(N)=product(q+1) reuses Mathlib's squarefree powerset formula.
Initial elaboration repairs were root namespace, a left-only rewrite of d,
and the List-to-Multiset coercion; the final check has no diagnostics.

The third fragment compiles without diagnostics (fragment-3b.log): a
nonempty finite set of integers >=3 excluding 4 satisfies
product(1+1/q^2)+product((q+1)/q^2)<5/3. The proof explicitly handles
F={3}, F containing 3 and another member, and F excluding 3. It consumes
the reciprocal-square bound and a one-selected-factor bound for the second
product. This is the triage three-class route; no numeric enumeration is
used by the Lean proof.
