## Definitions and proof

A block with positive common sum D and positive value x dividing D
contains D/x copies of x. A multiset b of such values records every block,
including repeated identical blocks. Its total weight is D times the
cardinality of b. At weight zero there is one empty system.

The existence count uses Mathlib integer partitions and asks whether some
D and b flatten to that partition. The system count independently counts
pairs (D,b) of the required weight. Neither definition uses a divisor sum.
The Lean proof establishes the multiplicity criterion and the unique
reconstruction of b from the flattened partition and D.

Let L be the lcm of the support. Every admissible D is tL. Dividing all
multiplicities by t gives a capable partition with the same support,
canonical common sum L, and weight n/t. Conversely, multiplying a capable
partition's multiplicities by n/d gives exactly one system at weight n.
Equality of support lcms recovers the scaling factor and proves uniqueness.

## Proof scope

Mathlib supplies the lcm API and Multiset.exists_smul_of_dvd_count, which is
used directly. ConstantBlocksDistinctRunSums concerns distinct sums and does
not supply the equal-sum normalization.

The main entry's normal-system cross-reference A382203 has distinct sums;
A382204 is the corresponding equal-sum entry. This discrepancy does not
alter either target definition.
