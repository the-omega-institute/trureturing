# Guo and Han, a family of automatic apwenian sequences

An integer sequence a is apwenian if a(0) = 1 and a(n) ≡ a(2n+1) + a(2n+2) (mod 2) for every n; equivalently, all
its Hankel determinants are odd. The paper studies automatic apwenian sequences. Its Theorem 2 shows that a purely
automatic sequence over {0, 1, 2}, that is a fixed point of a uniform morphism, is apwenian exactly when its parity
sequence is the period-doubling sequence, and Conjecture 2 asserts the same for every finite alphabet whose only odd
letter is 1.

The module `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Apwenian/GuoHan` proves Conjecture 2.

## Conjecture 1: finite-alphabet level generation

Section 3, Conjecture 1, printed page 6 of the author manuscript states:

> Let {u(n)}ₙ≥₀ be a 2-automatic apwenian sequence over {0,1}. Then there exist a finite alphabet
> Σ ⊂ N and a sequence σ over A(Σ) such that {u(n)}ₙ≥₀ = ϕ(σ) (mod 2).

Here A(Σ) consists of free-monoid endomorphisms taking every alphabet letter to a two-letter word
over Σ whose letter sum has the original letter's parity. Starting from X₀ = [1], the levels satisfy
Xⱼ₊₁ = σⱼ(Xⱼ), and ϕ(σ) is the successive concatenation X₀X₁X₂⋯. This is a level construction;
neither prolongability nor a fixed-point equation is part of the assertion. Several odd letters are allowed.

The result `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Apwenian/GuoHanGeneration.result` gives a constant directive as a
stronger witness. Its source automaticity premise is a finite DFAO evaluated on canonical binary
expansions in most-significant-first order. Either the input [0] or the empty input may represent zero.
The sequence is natural-valued with every value less than two; unused machine outputs need not be binary.

The construction adjoins a fresh zero-loop state and uses the finite set of occurring adjacent state
pairs sₙ = (a(n), a(n+1)). The ordered children are
L(q,r) = (δ₁(q), δ₀(r)) and R(q,r) = (δ₀(r), δ₁(r)); they send sₙ to s₂ₙ₊₁ and s₂ₙ₊₂.
Enumerating this occurring set with its root at index zero gives injective labels
e(s) = 2i(s) + ε(s), where ε is the first state's output parity. The root label is one, and
τ(e(s)) = [e(L(s)), e(R(s))] is sum-equivalent by the apwenian recurrence on the occurring pairs.
The exact ordered j-th level is [e(s₂ʲ₋₁₊ₖ)] for 0 ≤ k < 2ʲ. Concatenating the first j levels
gives exactly the first 2ʲ − 1 values of v(n) = e(sₙ). These lengths are unbounded, so this defines
a unique generated sequence, and v(n) modulo two equals u(n) for every n.

The author and institutional manuscripts agree on the inspected definitions and conjecture. The
publisher version-of-record body remains unverified. The literature screen recorded in
[preregistration issue 13031](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/13031) found
no equivalent settlement in its bounded author bibliography, HAL metadata, Crossref, Semantic Scholar,
arXiv and repository scopes. This is a bounded finding and makes no worldwide-priority claim.
