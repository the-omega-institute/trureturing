## Statement and repository proof

For natural numbers k, r, m with k greater than eight, k not a power of
two, r less than k, m at most k, and k squared plus one equal to r squared
plus 2 to the m, the conjectured interval is
floor(log base 2 of k) + 3 ≤ m ≤ 2 floor(log base 2 of k) + 1.
The addition form avoids truncated natural subtraction.

The repository proof first rules out exponent zero. A gap k−r equal to
one forces k to be a power of two; a gap equal to two contradicts parity.
Thus r+3≤k, and comparison with (k−3) squared gives 6k≤2^m+8.
The two defining inequalities for the integer logarithm then give the
claimed interval. The argument also covers r=0 and does not need m≤k,
although that defining restriction is retained in the theorem statement.
The example (12,9,6) attains the lower bound; it is not separately frozen.
