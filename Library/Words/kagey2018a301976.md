# OEIS A301976

The entry counts spanning edge-subgraphs of the `3 X n` grid graph in which
every vertex has degree different from one; isolated vertices are allowed.
It gives the initial values `1, 5, 43, 463` and records Colin Barker's
generating function and order-four recurrence together with Peter Kagey's
modulo-ten conjecture.

The formal module represents each vertical or horizontal grid edge by one of
five labels in its left or lower column. It proves Barker's recurrence for
every `n > 4` and Kagey's congruence for every `n > 2`. The generating-function
line is not separately formalized.
