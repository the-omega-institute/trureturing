## Mathematical statement

For n at least 1, a path from the top left to the bottom right has n-1
east steps and n-1 south steps. The expected number of paths containing
only ones, when each binary matrix has equal weight, is
C(2n-2,n-1)/2^(2n-1). This is the ratio of the auxiliary numerator and
denominator sequences in the conjecture. The formal result proves this
binomial formula; it does not define or enumerate the OEIS triangle.

The repository proof encodes the east steps by a fixed-size subset of
the step positions. Prefix counts give the two coordinates. Their sum
is the time index, so all visited cells are different. Assignments off
those cells extend uniquely to matrices supporting the path. Summing
first over paths gives C(2n-2,n-1)*2^((n-1)^2) incidences.

## Proof scope

The Lean derivation uses a double counting argument and standard finite subset
and function cardinality lemmas. It is repository work, not a proof attributed
to the OEIS note. No publication priority or exhaustive literature coverage
is claimed.
