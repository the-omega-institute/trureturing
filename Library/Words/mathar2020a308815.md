## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A308815

The source has no page numbers. Its FORMULA section contains this sentence:

> Conjecture: D-finite with recurrence: n*(2*n+1)*a(n) -32*(9*n-4)*(n-1)*a(n-1) +1024*(3*n^2-23*n+29)*a(n-2) +65536*(n-2)*(2*n-5)*a(n-3)=0. - _R. J. Mathar_, Jan 27 2020

Its PARI program defines the sequence by:

> a(n) = 16^n*sum(i=0, n, (-2)^i*binomial(n, i)*C(2*n-i));

Here `C` is the Catalan sequence. The NAME line instead prints:

> a(n) = 16^n*Sum_{i=0..n} 2^i*binomial(n, i)*Catalan(2*n-i).

The minus sign in the PARI formula agrees with the data beginning
`1,0,512,32768,3014656`. The Lean sequence uses that formula with Mathlib's
`catalan`, integer values and natural indices. The recurrence starts at `n ≥ 3`;
its polynomial coefficients are integers and its lagged indices are natural
subtractions.

## Mathematical use

The coefficient of degree `2n` in `(1-2X)^n C(X)` is the unscaled binomial
sum. A formal-series differential certificate annihilates that coefficient
and yields the recurrence after multiplication by `16^n`.

The experiment entry is
[`docs/reports/oeis-a308815-dfinite-recurrence/check.py`](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-experiments/blob/606aeab68b46632c86a284c8793cc78c2171e440/docs/reports/oeis-a308815-dfinite-recurrence/check.py),
SHA256 `d40f575913a33fcb60a373cfb2fb8bb80028ea340c3d255f780a485d87e6477b`.
The reported command `python3 check.py` exits zero with `bad= 0`: the signed
formula matches all 17 displayed terms and the recurrence holds for
`3 ≤ n ≤ 300`. These finite readings are separate from the Lean proof for all
`n ≥ 3`.
