# OEIS A270236

R. J. Mathar's entry counts occurrences of labels across restricted growth
functions. The functions begin with label 1, and each later label is at most
one greater than the maximum earlier label. The two formulas below were
entered as conjectures on March 13, 2016. Alois P. Heinz corrected the left
index of the second formula on March 18, 2016.

The formal model stores each restricted growth function in reverse
chronological order. Its recursive extension rule is equivalent to the
displayed forward definition, and its statistic sums `List.count p` over all
words of the requested length. Both divisions in the closed forms are
natural-number quotients.

> Triangle T(n,p) read by rows: the number of occurrences of p in the restricted growth functions of length n.

> The RG functions used here are defined by f(1)=1, f(j) <= 1+max_{i<j} f(i).

> Conjecture: T(n,n-1) = 2+n*(n-1)/2 for n>1.

> Conjecture: T(n+1,n-1) = 2+n*(n+1)*(3*n^2-5*n+26)/24 for n>1.
