# Fixed-order agreement of inclusion probabilities

Section 1, p. 3, asks whether agreement of all inclusion probabilities through a
fixed integer order determines the limiting distribution. Chen conjectures that
it is insufficient for every fixed order and identifies order two as the scope
of the paper's result.

Section 6, p. 17, restates the conjecture for the limiting distribution of a
sampling estimator. The formal witness uses a simple random sample without replacement
and a weighted law on the same fixed-size subsets. Both laws use the same
centered and scaled sample mean.
