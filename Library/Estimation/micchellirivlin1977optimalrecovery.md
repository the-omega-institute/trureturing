# A Survey of Optimal Recovery

For a scalar target, the midpoint of the infimum and supremum of a nonempty bounded compatible set has error at most half its diameter. This is a classical optimal-recovery principle.

PointwiseMinimaxUpper applies that principle to finite atomic probability laws and arbitrary pointwise noisy Laplace observations. The estimator has no computational restriction. The analytic diameter bound comes from the separation-free endpoint interpolation certificates. The resulting constant 1024 Ehi/eta^2 and its joint dimension-precision rate are repository-derived; this reference is not claimed to prove those analytic bounds.
