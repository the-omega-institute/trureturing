# The square minor and its restricted algebraic use

The primary version is [arXiv:math/0602052v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0602052v1).
Definition 1.1, PDF p. 2, defines a toric fiber product as the kernel of
the specified multigraded map. Example 1.2 on the same page takes
$z_{jk}\mapsto x_jy_k$ and identifies the kernel as the ideal generated
by all two-by-two minors. For a two-by-two table its single generator is

$$
z_{00}z_{11}-z_{01}z_{10}.
$$

The [pyramid reference, §十二](../../docs/develop/theory/AURIC_FIB_ATOM_PYRAMID_FOUNDATIONAL_FORMULAS_AND_RELATIONS.md)
uses that square relation as a classical algebraic ingredient. It gives
its own explicit monomial map, including the apex variable, and proves
the kernel and the unique nonnegative normal form by the integer
occupancy fiber. The subtraction of weak-composition counts and the
resulting generating function are shown in that consumer; they are not
quoted as formulas from this paper.

The introduction and §2 require the indicated linearly independent
degree set for the general generating-set and Gröbner-basis results.
No unrestricted gluing theorem is imported into the five-mode system.
An integer circuit or an algebraic fiber product supplies no probability
independence, chronological Markov assumption, native instrument or
recovery of arbitrary source histories.
