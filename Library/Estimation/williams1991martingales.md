# Stopped likelihoods and martingale tools

Williams's textbook supplies the classical background on martingales,
optional stopping, maximal inequalities and probability densities. A private
independent seed can be included in the initial sigma algebra. A bounded
stopping rule need not be independent of the martingale it stops.

The stopped Bernoulli density identity is an application of change of measure
to the stopped sigma algebra. The local absorption estimate in
`StoppedLikelihoodAbsorption` retains the random moment of the stopping
time inside the maximal-moment bound. That estimate, the common endpoint
dyadic budget and the resulting conditional logarithmic charge are
repository derivations; their particular constants and joint hypotheses
are not attributed to this textbook. Neither optional stopping alone nor
a deterministic-horizon bound supplies that random-time estimate.
