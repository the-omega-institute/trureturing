# Pál, Tóth, Bene and Vértesi, Bound entangled singlet-like states for quantum metrology

K. F. Pál, G. Tóth, E. Bene and T. Vértesi, Phys. Rev. Research 3, 023101 (2021), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevResearch.3.023101; arXiv:2002.12409v4. Quotations are from the arXiv v4 TeX source `metroboundent53.tex`.

Definition 2 gives the second family on $\mathbb C^2_A\otimes\mathbb C^2_B\otimes\mathbb C^d_{A'}\otimes\mathbb C^d_{B'}$ as
$\varrho=\frac{p_1}{d^2}\sum_{i,j}|z_{ij}\rangle\langle z_{ij}|+\frac{p_2}{2d}\sum_i|s_i\rangle\langle s_i|+\frac{p_2}{2d}\sum_i|10ii\rangle\langle10ii|$ with $|z_{ij}\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt2}(|00ij\rangle+\sum_kQ^j_{ik}|11jk\rangle)$, $p_1=\sqrt d/(1+\sqrt d)$ and $p_2=1/(1+\sqrt d)$.

It requires:
- every $Q^j$ orthogonal;
- each $|q_{ij}\rangle=\sum_kQ^k_{ij}|10kk\rangle$ either zero or $\pm\sqrt{d/N_m}$ times one of $d$ orthonormal vectors $|\bar q_m\rangle$, where $N_m$ counts the pairs assigned to $m$;
- orthonormal $|s_i\rangle$ in the $|01\rangle_{AB}$ sector with $(\sum_i|s_i\rangle\langle s_i|)^\Gamma=\sum_m|t_m\rangle\langle t_m|$, where $|t_m\rangle=d^{-1/2}\sum_{(i,j)\in\Xi_m}S_{ij}|01ij\rangle$.

Under these requirements the paper shows that $\varrho^\Gamma\succeq0$ and that the quantum Fisher information is $16\sqrt d/(1+\sqrt d)$. The paper states:

> For this family of states we have constructed analytical examples only for $d=3$ and $2^n.$ However, we believe that such states exist for all $d$.
