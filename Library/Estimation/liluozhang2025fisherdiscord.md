# Fisher discord as a quantifier of quantum complexity

H. Li, S. Luo and Y. Zhang, arXiv:2510.15375v1. Published as
*Fisher discord as a quantifier of quantum metrological complexity*,
Phys. Rev. A 114, 032449 (2026), DOI 10.1103/6kv3-hr92.

Equations (6), (7) and (10) define
$I_F(\rho,H)=\tfrac14\operatorname{tr}(\rho L^2)$,
$i[\rho,H]=\tfrac12(L\rho+\rho L)$,
$I_W(\rho,H)=-\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}[\sqrt\rho,H]^2$,
and $C=I_F-I_W$. The Hermitian SLD may be nonunique on the kernel;
the information value is independent of that freedom.

Section VI (Summary), verbatim:

> Both the quantum Fisher information based on the symmetric logarithmic derivative and the skew information does not increase under the partial traces, that is, I_F(ρ₁₂, H ⊗ 1) ≥ I_F(ρ₁, H), I_W(ρ₁₂, H ⊗ 1) ≥ I_W(ρ₁, H) … It is natural to consider such an open question: Whether quantum Fisher discord C(ρ₁₂, H ⊗ 1) = I_F(ρ₁₂, H ⊗ 1) − I_W(ρ₁₂, H ⊗ 1) for bipartite states does not increase under the partial trace.

The source establishes separate monotonicity of the two information quantities,
positivity of their difference, pure-state vanishing and direct-sum additivity.
Its partial-trace question concerns monotonicity of the difference.
