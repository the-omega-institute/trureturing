# Bernoulli-factory background

Nacu and Peres give the classical Bernoulli-factory framework used as
background for the factory discussion. The repository's stopped likelihood,
native execution bookkeeping and logarithmic total-call lower bounds are
separate deductions and are not attributed to this paper.
