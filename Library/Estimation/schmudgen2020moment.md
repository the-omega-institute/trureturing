# Support certificates from truncated observations

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.12698v1. Lecture 3, Theorems 3.1
and 3.2, PDF p. 24, gives the even and odd truncated $[a,b]$ moment
criteria. Lecture 9, Proposition 9.2(i), (ii), (iv), (v), PDF p. 68,
identifies $L(fg)$ with the moment-matrix quadratic form and puts every
representing measure on the zero sets of its kernel polynomials.

[LocalClock, §十四, 定理27](../../docs/develop/theory/AURIC_FIB_ATOM_LOCAL_CLOCK_KERNEL_AND_OUTPUT_RESOLVED_SEAM_VISIBILITY.md)
consumes $L(P^2)=0\Rightarrow\operatorname{supp}\nu\subseteq\{P=0\}$
to constrain the actual five-mode source. The measure there is already
supplied, so no claim that arbitrary positive semidefinite truncated
Hankel data alone represent a measure on $[0,1]$ is needed. For
reconstruction from putative data, the interval-localizing conditions
in Theorems 3.1 and 3.2 must also be retained.

Nonzero $P$ is required to deduce finite support in one variable.
Approximate residuals give the consumer's stated concentration and
finite-time moment error, not exact support. No general flat-extension
or full Borel-flow rigidity result is attributed to this scalar
application.
