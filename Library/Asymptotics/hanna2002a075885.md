## Source statement

The FORMULA reads verbatim:

> a(n) = 1 + Sum_{m=1..n} Product_{k=1..m} floor(n/k).

The COMMENT reads verbatim:

> Conjecture: limit a(n)^(1/n) = L where L = 2.200161058099... is the geometric mean of Luroth expansions, where log(L) = Sum_{n>=1} log(n)/(n*(n+1)) = 0.7885305659115... (cf. A085361).

The NAME reads verbatim:

> a(n) = 1 + n + n*[n/2] + n*[n/2]*[n/3] + n*[n/2]*[n/3]*[n/4] +... where [x]=floor(x).

The entry's offset is zero and its example gives a(5)=46. The empty product
is one and the empty outer sum gives a(0)=1. In the Lean statement the
source's series index is j+1 and the root index is n+1, so both range over
exactly the positive integers. Natural division represents floor(n/k);
the root and logarithmic series use real division. The decimal strings and
the ergodic interpretation identify the constant; they are not additional
claims of the formal statement.

## Mathematical scope

The sum lies between the full prefix product and (n+1) times that product.
Counting floor quotients turns its normalized logarithm into a dominated
series limit; finite summation by parts identifies the exact source series.
These ingredients use existing logarithm inequalities, quotient-ratio limits
and convergence theorems. No priority claim is made for their composition.

The stronger plateau estimate and the A010786 and A208060 asymptotics are
separate questions. The proof of the A075885 limit makes no assertion about
