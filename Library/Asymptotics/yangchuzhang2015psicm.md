# Yang–Chu–Zhang Conjecture 1(ii)

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.1186/s13660-015-0674-8

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.6411v1

The journal article is Journal of Inequalities and Applications 2015:157.
Its Conjecture 1 is in §4, p. 15. The corresponding preprint is Zhen-Hang
Yang, “Several completely monotone functions related to DeTemple’s sequence”,
arXiv:1409.6411v1.

## Source statement

Conjecture 1, p. 15:

> Let \(R(x)\) be defined on \((0,\infty)\) by (1.10). Then we conjecture that
>
> (ii) the function \(-G_{a}\) is completely monotone on \((0,\infty)\) if and only if \(a\leq155/294\).

Equation (1.2), p. 1, defines the psi function by
\(\psi(x)=\Gamma'(x)/\Gamma(x)\) for \(x>0\).
Equation (1.10) is
\(R(x)=\psi(x+1/2)-\ln x\).
Theorem 4, equation (3.3), defines
\[
G_a(x)=24\left(x^4+ax^2+\frac7{40}a-\frac{31}{336}\right)R(x)
-\left(x^2-\frac7{40}+a\right).
\]
Complete monotonicity includes derivatives of every order \(n=0,1,2,\ldots\),
with \((-1)^n f^{(n)}(x)\geq0\) at every positive real \(x\).

## Scope

The source sentence quantifies every real parameter through its asserted
if-and-only-if classification. The negative sign before \(G_a\) is essential.
The source's Theorem 4 instead concerns \(G_a\) with the opposite sign.
The clause (i) monotonicity assertion is a separate question.
