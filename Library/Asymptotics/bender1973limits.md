# Central and local limit theorems applied to asymptotic enumeration

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.1016/0097-3165(73)90038-1

Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/0097-3165(73)90038-1

## Cited result

Zhang and Zhao quote Theorem 2 as their Theorem 4.2, page 9: a real-rooted
polynomial sequence with nonnegative coefficients has both normal limit theorems
when its variance diverges. The Bernoulli-polynomial result proves the shifted
product form of this criterion through characteristic functions and Fourier inversion.
The original paper's full text is not used as an additional proof premise.
