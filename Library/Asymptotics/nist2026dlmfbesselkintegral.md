# Modified Bessel K as a hyperbolic integral

## Verified locator

https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.32.E9

DOI: 10.18434/T4J30S.

Equation 10.32.9:

> K_ν(z) = ∫_0^∞ exp(−z cosh t) cosh(νt) dt, |ph z| < ½π

For real $z>0$ and real $\nu$ the integral is real. In particular,
$K_0(z)=\int_0^\infty\exp(-z\cosh t)\,dt$.
