# The Exact Stabilization Time of Self-Stabilizing Coloring

Section 2 defines the random central daemon. Each step draws one enabled vertex uniformly and recolors it using a color absent from its neighborhood. Lemma 1 gives the permutation scheduler and its terminal history law. Proposition 5 relates peaks to rooted leaves. Theorem 10 gives the finite law for independently uniformly colored uniform rooted labeled trees and identifies independent conflicts on the tree edges.

Section 6 states “We leave open the central limit theorem for the 𝑘-colored tree, which the linear variance Eq. (18) and the 𝑛-th power in Eq. (19) suggest, and the regime 𝑝 → 0 of the bipartite model.” The fixed-color tree clause uses the recoloring count and its own standard deviation.
