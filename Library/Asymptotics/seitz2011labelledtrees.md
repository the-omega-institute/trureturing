# Peaks and leaves in labeled trees

Corollary 5.2.2 states the distributional identity for local minima and rooted leaves. In §5.3.1.2, Seitz reroots at the greatest label, recursively transforms the attached branches, and finally swaps the greatest label with the original root label. Reversing the label order gives the peak model. The pivot is then the least label, and the final swap exchanges it with the specified root.
