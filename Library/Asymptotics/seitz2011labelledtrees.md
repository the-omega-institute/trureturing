# Peaks and leaves in labeled trees

Corollary 5.2.2 states the distributional identity. Section 5.3.1.2 gives the bijective proof by rooting at the least label, splitting into attached branches and recursively replacing the branches. The fixed-root version includes a final relabeling of the pivot.
