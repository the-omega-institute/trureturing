# The diamond theta coefficients

## Locator

Revision 49 of [OEIS A005926](https://oeis.org/A005926/internal) attributes the following conjecture to Vaclav Kotesovec on November 26, 2026.

> Conjecture: Sum_{k=1..n} a(k) ~ Pi*n^(3/2)/48.

The generating function used in the conjecture is recorded in
[Andrew J. Morris's contribution](morris2026a005926.md).
The diamond theta formula and its finite-sum values appear in N. J. A.
Sloane, *Theta series and magic numbers for diamond and certain ionic
crystal structures*, J. Math. Phys. 28 (1987), 1653–1657, Table I(b).
[DOI 10.1063/1.527472](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.527472)

## Formal correspondence

The series is formed over the integers. Mathlib's formal power series
operations implement both substitutions and the Cauchy products. The
signed cube selects even total triangular degree with weight two, so
the nonzero degrees lie in the class 3 modulo 16. A005926 relates its
values at those degrees to successive differences of the cumulative
sequence [A217513](https://oeis.org/A217513).

The Lean limit uses the original coefficient indices from 1 through N
inclusive and real division by N to the real power 3/2. The shift by
three gives a zero coefficient at index zero. The limit uses all
natural cutoffs. The analytic input is Mathlib's
`tendsto_card_div_pow_atTop_volume'` applied to the centered unit ball,
with the fixed shifts handled by inclusion between centered balls.
