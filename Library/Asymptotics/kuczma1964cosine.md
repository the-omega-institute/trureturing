# On a characterization of the cosine

Kuczma, Annales Polonici Mathematici 16 (1964), pages 53–57, considers
the duplication equation `f(2x) = 2 f(x)^2 - 1` as equation (4), page 53.
Page 54 reports Forder's characterization of cosine among even solutions
that are twice differentiable at zero and satisfy `f(0)=1` and `f''(0)=-1`.
This is Kuczma's attribution to H. G. Forder, *Duplication formulae*,
Mathematical Gazette 41 (1957), pages 215–217, reference [3].

Kuczma's Theorem 1 uses a signed half-angle equation, continuity near zero
and period `2π`. Theorem 2 uses the duplication equation, continuity near
zero, a prescribed period and one value on a dense dyadic orbit. These
statements do not give the exact assumptions of the bounded quadratic-germ
theorem: a full-neighborhood remainder `o(t²)` and a uniform bound, without
separately assumed differentiability, evenness or periodicity. The Lean
proof compares two duplication solutions by a dyadic error estimate.
The citation attributes the classical functional equation and predecessor
characterizations; it makes no priority claim for the remainder formulation.
