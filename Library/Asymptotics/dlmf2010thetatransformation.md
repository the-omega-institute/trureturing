## Verified locator

Take $z=\theta/2$ and $\tau=i\alpha/\pi$ with $\alpha>0$ in the
Fourier series for $\theta_3$. Its integer series is
$\sum_j \exp(-\alpha j^2+ij\theta)$.
[DLMF 20.2.3](https://dlmf.nist.gov/20.2.E3)

In the modular transformation, the new parameter is $-1/\tau$.
Completing the square on the transformed side gives
$\sqrt{\pi/\alpha}\sum_j\exp(-\pi^2(j-\theta/(2\pi))^2/\alpha)$.
[DLMF 20.7.32](https://dlmf.nist.gov/20.7.E32)
