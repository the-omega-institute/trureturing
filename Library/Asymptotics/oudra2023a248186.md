## Source statement

> Least k such that 1/18 - Sum_{h=1..k} 1/(h*(h+1)*(h+2)*(h+3)) < 1/n^3.

> Conjecture: a(n) = floor(r*n + r^2/n - 1), where r = 3^(-1/3), for n > 2. - _Ridouane Oudra_, Oct 06 2023

The offset is `1,5`. The source program selects from Mathematica's `Range[z]`,
so the least index is positive. Its data start with `a(1)=a(2)=1`.
The residual is a real finite sum; integer floor is taken after real subtraction.
The assertion includes existence of the sequence at every positive index.

## Mathematical mechanism

Finite telescoping gives the residual `1/(3*(k+1)*(k+2)*(k+3))`.
With `r=3^(-1/3)` and `b=r*n+r^2/n`, expansion gives
`3*(b^3-b)=n^3+1/(3*n^3)`. For `n>2` the excess is strictly between zero
and one. Monotonicity of `3*(x^3-x)` for `x>=1`, together with integrality
at integer arguments, brackets the threshold by the two consecutive integers
`floor(b)` and `floor(b)+1`. The least admissible index is `floor(b)-1`.
