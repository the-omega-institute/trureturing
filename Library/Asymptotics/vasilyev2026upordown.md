## Source statement

> Does every force-divergent set, that is, every $A$ such that $T_S$ is unbounded for all $S \supseteq A$, contain a tail of a chain?

The universe is the positive integers. A chain tail from $x$ consists of
$x,U(x),U^2(x),\ldots$. The map $T_S$ is unbounded when some positive starting
point has an orbit that never enters a periodic cycle.
