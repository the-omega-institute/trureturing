# The A400585 summatory conjecture

## Verified locator

https://oeis.org/A400585

The internal entry is https://oeis.org/A400585/internal. The machine-readable
entry is https://oeis.org/search?q=id:A400585&fmt=json, revision 22.

## Statement

The NAME reads:

> a(n) = Sum_{d|n, n/d odd} binomial(d, 2).

The FORMULA conjecture reads:

> Conjecture: Sum_{k=1..n} a(k) ~ 7*zeta(3)*n^3/48. - _Vaclav Kotesovec_, Oct 01 2026

The offset is one. Divisors are positive and the cofactor quotient is exact.
Here zeta(3) means the convergent real series over positive integers of their
reciprocal cubes; the defining zeta series is also given by
https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.2.E1. The source provides the conjecture, not a proof.

## Scope

The Lean source defines the natural divisor sum a, the real positive-prefix
sum S, and the real cubic reciprocal series Z3. Its sole theorem gives the
ratio limit along all natural indices, with coefficient exactly 7*Z3/48 and
limit one. It introduces no parity, primality or finite-range restriction.
The proof uses divisor/cofactor reindexing, the finite binomial sum, the
floor-ratio limit and dominated-series convergence. The derived result does
not assert a literature priority claim. The generating-function formula and
the separate sigma-function formula in the entry are outside this theorem.
