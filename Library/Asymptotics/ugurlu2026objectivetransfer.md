# Objective transfer in single-node network criticality

## Verified locator

[Pinned arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34394v1) · [DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2609.34394](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.34394)

Section 2 counts attacked vertices in the damage and uses the best target
score among tied source optimizers. For original order $n$ and induced
survivor component sizes $c_i$, the three scores used here are $NC=m-1$,
$PC=\binom n2-\sum_i\binom{c_i}{2}$, and $LCC=n-\max_i c_i$. The fixed-budget
question applies these conventions to deletions of exactly $b$ vertices, with
$n>b$. Each transfer uses a positive optimum for its selected target.

Section 9 asks “whether the constants improve on trees or on bounded-degree
graphs, or whether the NC directions remain vanishing there.” The fixed-budget
NC clause is registered separately from the question about optimal PC–LCC
constants and the proportional-budget question.
