## Cited connection

Zhang and Zhao cite Harper alongside Bender immediately before Theorem 4.2,
page 9. The formal criterion uses the independent Bernoulli product represented
by a polynomial with nonpositive real roots. No additional assertion about
Harper's original theorem is required by the Lean proof.
