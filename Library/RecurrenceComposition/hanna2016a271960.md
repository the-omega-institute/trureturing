# OEIS A271960

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A271960

The entry is unpaginated. Its NAME is:

> G.f. A(x) satisfies: A(x)^2 = A( (x + 2*A(x)^2)^2 ).

Its OFFSET is 1. Its EXAMPLE begins
A(x) = x + 2*x^2 + 9*x^3 + 50*x^4 + 312*x^5 + ….
Its COMMENTS state:

> Conjecture: [x^(2^n-2^k)] (x/A(x))^(2^n) = 0 for k = 0..n-2 and n >= 2. - _Paul D. Hanna_, Jan 16 2026

The formal statement uses rational power series with zero constant coefficient
and linear coefficient one. The quotient x/A(x) is the multiplicative inverse
of the series whose nth coefficient is the (n+1)st coefficient of A.
It asserts existence and the coefficient identity for every normalized solution.

The proof uses the compositional inverse B and the odd series H=B+2X² with
H²=B(X²). Coefficient decimation gives the support restriction, and Lagrange
inversion transfers it to the conjectured coefficients.

found no earlier settlement of this support conjecture.
