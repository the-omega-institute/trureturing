# A124056 positive-integer occurrence

Quet's sequence has offset one and starts with one. Its next value counts indices
in the entire existing prefix whose values divide the current value, with
multiplicity and the current index included.

Della Corte's MathOverflow question 376929 asks about occurrence for this exact
recurrence. Sommacal's question 515669 explicitly asks whether every positive
integer occurs. The latter's revision one has identifier
`594621DD-640C-44F9-8BF7-1E725DADE64D`; the authenticated OEIS entry is revision 24.
These locators identify the problem and do not supply a completed Lean proof.

The local formalization establishes boundary properties, the fixed-LCM
consequence of finite occurrence of three, the conditional full-occurrence
implication from infinite occurrence of three, and the incompatibility of a
global sublinear multiplicity bound with unboundedness. It also proves the
finite-prefix contributor-rank inequality, ordered-prime-word split convolution,
its weight majorant for actual divisor counts, and the distinct-predecessor
level-set bound for multiplicities. The fixed fractional Dirichlet moment and
the uniform sublinear estimate remain unencoded. The unconditional
positive-integer occurrence theorem remains open in Lean.
