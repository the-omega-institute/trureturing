> More generally, if exp( Sum_{n>=1} C(n) * x^n/n ) equals a power series in x with integer coefficients, then exp( Sum_{n>=1} C(n)^n * x^n/n ) also equals a power series in x with integer coefficients (conjecture).

The expression is an ordinary formal power series. Its constant coefficient
is one. The sequence takes values in an arbitrary characteristic-zero field;
“integer coefficients” means the canonical image of the integers. Index zero
is unused. There is no positivity or analytic convergence condition.
The Lucas-number example in the entry does not restrict the universal comment.

The proof extracts the integer Euler ledger of the input, obtains its divisor
ghosts, preserves their local Gauss congruences under diagonal self-powers,
and reconstructs the output by integer Moebius inversion and formal
differential uniqueness. The classical Dold/Euler bridge is discussed in
Byszewski, Graff and Ward, *Dold sequences, periodic points, and dynamics*,
arXiv:2007.04031v4, DOI 10.1112/blms.12531. This supporting paper is not the
source of Hanna's conjecture.
