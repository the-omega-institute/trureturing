# Hanna's two binomial-square series

A246056, Name:

> G.f.: Sum_{n>=0} x^n / (1-2*x)^(2*n+1) * [Sum_{k=0..n} C(n,k)^2 * 2^k * x^k] * [Sum_{k=0..n} C(n,k)^2 * 3^k * x^k].

A246056, Comments:

> Conjecture: a(n) == 1 (mod 3) when n = 2*A005836(k) for k >= 0, and a(n) == 0 (mod 3) otherwise, where A005836 gives numbers whose base-3 representation contains no 2.

A246423, Name:

> G.f.: Sum_{n>=0} x^n / (1-3*x)^(2*n+1) * [Sum_{k=0..n} C(n,k)^2 * 2^k * x^k] * [Sum_{k=0..n} C(n,k)^2 * 3^k * x^k].

A246423, Comments:

> a(n) == 2 (mod 4) iff n = 2^k for k>=2, and a(n) == 0 (mod 4) elsewhere except at a(0)=1 (conjecture).

Both sequences have offset zero. The rational formal power series are defined
coefficientwise: the summand indexed by n has order at least n, so the coefficient
at m uses only indices n ≤ m. The integer lift proves integrality. The empty
base-three digit list of zero contains no two and includes the constant term in
the first conjecture. The second conjecture treats its constant term separately.

The exact integer coefficient transform is a binomial-square telescoping identity.
It gives zero at odd degrees and signed central binomial coefficients at even
degrees. The ternary Lucas recurrence gives the first residue rule; the binary
Lucas recurrence and a first-moment correction give the second. These proofs do
not assert the full Kummer valuation formula or a general Legendre-series API.
