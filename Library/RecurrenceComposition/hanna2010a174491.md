# Hanna's quadratic-exponent denominator conjecture

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A174491

Paired entry: https://oeis.org/A174490

## Statement

OEIS A174491 revision 7, unpaginated, %N:

> Denominator in the coefficient of x^n in exp( Sum_{m>=1} x^m/(m*2^(m^2)) ).

Its %F states:

> a(n) = 2^(n^2)*A006519(n) where A006519(n) = highest power of 2 dividing n [conjecture].

OEIS A174490 revision 7, unpaginated, %F:

> Denominators are A174491(n) = 2^(n^2)*A006519(n) where A006519(n) = highest power of 2 dividing n [conjecture].

The formal series is over the rationals. Its logarithmic input has coefficient zero at index zero and coefficient `1 / ((m : ℚ) * (2 : ℚ) ^ (m ^ 2))` at positive index m. Substitution into `PowerSeries.exp ℚ` expresses the source exponential. The positive reduced rational denominator is `Rat.den`. Since A006519 starts at one, the formula is restricted to positive n and reads `2 ^ (n ^ 2 + padicValNat 2 n)`; the constant coefficient and its denominator are separately one. Existence and every rational formal series satisfying that equation are included.
