## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A381737/internal?rev=28, revision 28, Name and
Formula fields, with offset 1,1. The name is “Orders k of Hermite polynomials
whose maximal coefficient in absolute value appears twice.” Conjecture 1 is
“a(n) = 2*n*(n + 2) + (n + 1)*(-1)^(n+1).”

The example uses the physicists polynomial H_8 and compares every coefficient
in absolute value. The assertion concerns exactly two distinct maximizing
exponents, and the formula enumerates all qualifying orders with n at least
one. The original coefficient formula is DLMF 18.5.13, already represented by
HermiteSignedMode.physicistsHermite and its polynomial_coeff theorem.

Conjecture 2 in the same Formula field is “a(n) = a(n-1) + 2*a(n-2) - 2*a(n-3) - a(n-4) + a(n-5).” The one-based offset makes its full well-formed range n at least six. The enumeration is the same complete increasing sequence of orders used in Conjecture 1.
