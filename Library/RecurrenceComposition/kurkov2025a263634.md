## Source statement

> Irregular triangle read by rows: row n gives coefficients of n-th logarithmic polynomial L_n(x_1, x_2, ...) with monomials sorted into standard order.

> G.f.: Log(1 + Sum_{i >= 1} x_i*t^i/i!) =  Sum_{n >= 1} L_n(x_1, x_2, ...)*t^n/n!. [Comtet, p. 140, Eq. [5a]. - corrected by _Tom Copeland_, Sep 08 2016]

> Conjecture: row polynomials are R(n,1) for n > 0 where R(n,k) = R(n-1,k+1) - Sum_{j=1..n-1} binomial(n-2,j-1)*R(j,k)*R(n-j,1) for n > 1, k > 0 with R(1,k) = x_k for k > 0. - _Mikhail Kurkov_, Mar 30 2025

## Polynomial interpretation

The coefficients lie in `MvPolynomial ℕ ℚ`. Variable zero is unused on the
positive-column recursion. The formal series has constant coefficient one
and coefficient `C ((i.factorial : ℚ)⁻¹) * X i` at every positive index.
`PowerSeries.logOf` expresses the formal logarithm. A source family has
value zero at index zero and the stated exponential generating series.
Existence is part of the proved assertion, followed by identification of
every such family with the first column at every positive row.

For the recurrence branch, `n = m + 2` and `j = r.val + 1`, with
`r : Fin (m + 1)`. Thus the weight is `m.choose r.val` and the second row
index is `m - r.val + 1`. Natural subtraction never truncates on this domain.
The proof uses formal differentiation and inversion of a series with constant
coefficient one, without analytic convergence hypotheses.
