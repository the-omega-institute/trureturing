## Arithmetic mechanism

For `c(n)=b(n)·3^n`, the prime-three Dold congruence follows from
`padicValNat 3 m + 1 ≤ m` when `m ≠ 0`. At every other prime, multiplication
by that prime preserves the three-adic valuation, and Euler's prime-power
congruence supplies divisibility of the power difference. The existing Dold
exponential-integrality theorem then gives every integer coefficient.
