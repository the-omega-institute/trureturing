# OEIS A393173

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A393173

The unpaginated entry's Name reads:

> G.f. A(x) satisfies [x^n] 1/(A(x) - n^2*x) = 0 for n >= 1.

Its Comments state:

> Conjecture: for n > 0, a(n) is odd iff n is a power of 2.

The offset-zero data fix a(0)=1. The formal statement quantifies over all
normalized rational power series satisfying this equation and asserts
existence, integrality of every positive-degree coefficient, and the quoted
parity equivalence. The separate modulo-four conjecture is outside that
statement.

The coefficient recursion is a strict contraction on normalized series.
Reduction modulo two identifies its fixed point with one plus the
power-of-two series. The characteristic-two identity S+S²=X makes the even
and odd inverse-diagonal conditions vanish separately.
