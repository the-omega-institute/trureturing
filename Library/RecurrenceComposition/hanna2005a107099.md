# OEIS A107099

The unpaginated Name states: "G.f. satisfies A(A(x)) = x + 4*x^3, where A(x) = Sum_{n>=0} a(n)*x^(2*n+1)."

The Comments state: "Coefficients [x^n] A(x) = 0 (mod 3) except at n = 3^k (conjecture)."

The Example states: "A(x) = 1*x + 2*x^3 - 6*x^5 + 36*x^7 - 266*x^9 + 2028*x^11 - 13596*x^13 +-..."

The series has rational coefficients, zero even coefficients and linear coefficient one.
The coefficient assertion ranges over every natural exponent and refers to the integer
coefficient equal to that rational coefficient. Existence and integrality are included.
The statement imposes no nonzero residue at powers of three. The stronger binomial
residue formula and coefficient growth are outside the formalized statement.
