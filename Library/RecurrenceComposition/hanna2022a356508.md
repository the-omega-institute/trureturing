# OEIS A356508 product-partition congruence

P. D. Hanna's OEIS A356508 entry was created 11 August 2022. Its Name field
states verbatim:

> G.f. A(x) satisfies: 2 = Product_{n>=1} (1 + x^n*A(x)) * (1 + x^(n-1)/A(x)).

Its Comments field states verbatim:

> Conjecture: a(n) == 2 (mod 4) at n = 2*k for all k > 0 that have an odd number of partitions (A052002), otherwise a(n) == 0 (mod 4) when n > 0.

The entry has offset zero and begins with
`1, 4, 14, 84, 444, 2928, 18214, 125428, 844534, 5989816, …`.
The formalization interprets the product coefficientwise: at degree m, factors
with n at least m+2 are one modulo X^(m+1), and `A⁻¹` is the formal inverse
of the rational series A.

## Settlement scope

The proof constructs the unique rational solution by coefficient contraction and
shows its coefficients are integers. After mapping to ZMod 4, the candidate
`2 * partitionSeries(X^2) - 1` squares to one. Pairing the two factors reduces
the product to the reciprocal partition identity, giving coefficient residue 2
times the ordinary partition number at n/2 for even n and zero for odd n.

Residues modulo 8, replacement of the constant 2 by a general scalar, and
Jacobi triple-product or asymptotic questions are outside this settlement.
