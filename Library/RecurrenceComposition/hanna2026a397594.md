## Source statement

The NAME field reads:

> G.f. A(x) such that the sum of the first n coefficients in A(x/n)^n equals 4^(n-1) for n >= 1.

The FORMULA field reads:

> G.f. A(x) = Sum_{n>=0} a(n)*x^n has the following properties.
> (1) 4^(n-1) = Sum_{k=0..n-1} [x^k] A(x/n)^n for n >= 1.
> (2) (4*n)^(n-1) = [x^(n-1)] A(x)^n / (1 - n*x) for n >= 1.

The COMMENTS field reads:

> Conjecture: for n > 3, a(n) is odd iff n is of the form 2^k-1 or 2^k+1 (k > 1).

The offset is zero and a(0)=1. The formal series has integer coefficients;
its rational rescaling is `PowerSeries.rescale ((m : ℚ)⁻¹)
(A.map (Int.castRingHom ℚ))`. The first m coefficients are those at
indices 0 through m−1. Powers are ordinary formal-series multiplication.
The quantified claim includes existence of an integer source series and
the parity equivalence for every integer series satisfying (1).

## Scope

Denominator clearing connects (1) and (2). Integer triangular normalization
gives forcing 4^n(n+1)^(n−1) in positive degree n. Coefficientwise iteration
constructs an integer source, and reduction modulo two identifies its
coefficients with the homogeneous dyadic-support solution. The statements
for the related reversion series B, arbitrary even parameters, full triangle
integrality, positivity and asymptotics are outside this result.
