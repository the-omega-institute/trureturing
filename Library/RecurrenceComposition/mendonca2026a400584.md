## Source statements

NAME:

> Irregular triangle read by rows: T(n,k) is the number of labelings of the internal vertices of the complete plane binary tree of depth n by + or min, with every leaf equal to 1, that evaluate to k at the root (n >= 1, 1 <= k <= 2^n).

First COMMENT:

> The tree has 2^n leaves and 2^n - 1 internal vertices. Each internal vertex applies its operator to the values of its two children. Left and right positions are distinguished; labelings related by exchanges of children are counted separately.

Fourth COMMENT:

> Auffinger and Cable report unpublished work of Albert Chen proving that the root-value probabilities decrease with k, independently discovered by Tam Cheetham-West. Here monotonicity must be understood in the weak sense, since the first two rows contain ties. Conjecture: T(n,k) > T(n,k+1) for n >= 3 and 1 <= k < 2^n. This strict-monotonicity conjecture has been verified by exact computation for 3 <= n <= 12, but is not proved in general.

First FORMULA:

> T(0,1) = 1 and T(n,k) = 0 for k < 1 or k > 2^n. For n >= 0 and 1 <= k <= 2^(n+1), T(n+1,k) = T(n,k)^2 + 2*T(n,k)*Sum_{u=k+1..2^n} T(n,u) + Sum_{i=1..k-1} T(n,i)*T(n,k-i), with empty sums equal to 0.

## Mathematical correspondence

`Op` has addition and minimum as its two labels. `LabeledTree n` retains the
ordered left and right subtrees and has fixed depth `n`. `value` evaluates
each leaf as one. `T n k` is `Nat.card` of the subtype with root value `k`.
The depth-zero tree is the single leaf. The conjecture retains exactly the
natural indices `3 ≤ n` and `1 ≤ k < 2^n`.
