# OEIS A378576

Paul D. Hanna's entry, authored December 1, 2024, defines the generating
series by the six-fold compositional equation quoted above and conjectures
that every positive-index coefficient is one modulo five. Here A^6 denotes
composition, not multiplication. The Lean module constructs the integer
series, proves its defining equation and uniqueness, and proves the
conjecture as the r=6, m=5 instance of a general shifted-iterate congruence.
