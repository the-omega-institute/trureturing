# OEIS A365574

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A365574

The entry is unpaginated. Its NAME states:

> Expansion of g.f. A(x) satisfying [x^(n-1)] (1 + (n+1)*x*A(x))^n / A(x)^n = n*(n+2)^(n-2) for n > 1.

Its COMMENTS state:

> Conjecture 1: a(k) is odd iff k = 2^n - 2 for n >= 1.

The rational generating function has constant coefficient one. The proposition asserts existence and that every normalized solution has integral coefficients with the stated parity, including k = 0. The proof constructs an integral compositional inverse of a rational function and determines all inverse coefficients by induction. Conjecture 2 modulo 16 remains open here.
