# OEIS A379202

## Verified locator

DOI: null

Source: https://oeis.org/A379202

The Name section states: "G.f. A(x) satisfies 1/x = Sum_{n=-oo..+oo} A(x)^n * (A(x)^n + 2)^(n+1)."

The Comments section states: "Conjecture: a(n) is even for n > 1."
It also states: "It appears that a(n) == 2 (mod 4) at n = A028309(k) for k >= 4."
OEIS entries have named sections rather than page numbers. The offset is 1,
and the initial coefficients are 1, 4, 20, 122, 850, 6432, 51324, 424694.

The formalization uses rational Laurent series for the integer powers and
inverse. For a power series A with constant coefficient zero and linear
coefficient one, the coefficient of degree j-1 in the bilateral sum is the
sum over integer indices from -j-2 through j+2. The omitted summands have
larger order. Pairing k with -k-2 proves cancellation modulo two after
isolating the index -1. Integral compositional reversion then proves the
quoted parity conjecture. The separate mod-four observation remains open
in this development.
