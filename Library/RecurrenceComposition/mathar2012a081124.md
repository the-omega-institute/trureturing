# OEIS A081124

The official FORMULA field defines the literal binomial transform by

> a(n) = Sum_{k=0..n} C(n, k)*floor(k/2)!.

It states

> Conjecture: 2*a(n) -4*a(n-1) +(-n+2)*a(n-2) +(n-1)*a(n-3)=0. - _R. J. Mathar_, Nov 24 2012

Revision 20 of the official text export has SHA-256
`b9d775567e0d024ecd630d22b2ebd352e3a4a61262672b01b6307cd66ea3b7bd`.
The source does not specify a recurrence onset; every natural `n>=3` is the
disclosed interpretation with all four indices nonnegative. Signed arithmetic
is interpreted in the integers after casting the natural sequence values.

The finite qualification in issues #27161 and #27174 includes
Goyt–Pudwell, *Avoiding colored partitions of two elements in the pattern sense*,
JIS 15 (2012), Article 12.6.2, Theorem 14 and Table 2 class 5,
https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL15/Goyt/goyt4.pdf and arXiv:1203.3786 v1/v3.
Those passages give the same-sum counting interpretation, without an exact
four-term recurrence settlement found in the recorded readings.
Mathar arXiv:2109.02112v1 is general generating-function background; no
application to this literal target is assumed.
The current jOEIS A081124.java implements the literal finite sum and supplies
no proof of the conjectured recurrence. Bounded searches found no exact
published settlement; worldwide absence and priority remain unverified.
The source's exponential generating function and asymptotic formulas are
outside the formalized recurrence assertion.
