## Formal interpretation and reuse

The logarithm is a formal rational power series. Its degree m coefficient is
the finite sum over positive n and 0 <= k <= n with n+k=m. The exponent of
the ordinary binomial coefficient is n+k. The exponential has constant term
one. Integrality means an integer image in every ordinary coefficient,
including degree zero, without factorial scaling or any positivity assertion.

The proof applies the frozen HannaA181070Integrality generic criterion,
its binomial divisibility and prime-scaling congruence, and its finite-diagonal
identification. Euler's theorem in the pinned Mathlib handles the additional
exponent step. The existing DoldExponentialIntegrality port suffices; no new
AINTLIB source is copied. Its licensed provenance is retained in
Library/RecurrenceComposition/aintlib2026dieudonnedwork.md.
