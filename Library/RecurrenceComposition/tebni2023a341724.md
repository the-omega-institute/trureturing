# OEIS A341724

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A341724

The entry is unpaginated. Revision 26 attributes both conjectures and the
column EGF to Mélika Tebni in its 2023 Comments and Formula blocks:

> For 0 < k < p and p prime, T(p,k) == 0 (mod p).
>
> For 0 <= k < n and n = 2^m (m natural number), T(n,k) == 0 (mod n).
>
> E.g.f. of column k: x^k / ((1-2*sinh(-x))*k!).

The array appears in Table 3 of Shannon and Ollerton's 2021 paper,
“A note on Ledin’s summation problem.” That paper supplies the array's
context; the conjectures quoted above are Tebni's 2023 OEIS contributions.
The factorial-normalized coefficient is zero for n<k and one for n=k.
The denominator is 1+2 sinh(x), so the zero column begins 1, -2, 8, -50.

The source definitions are literature-attested. The uniform proof of both
conjectures is repo-derived: multiply a column by its denominator, use the
binomial product formula, prove integrality by induction, and apply prime
interior-binomial divisibility or odd-index dyadic-binomial divisibility.
The proof includes the prime two, exponent zero and the entire dyadic zero
column. It does not assert a valuation formula or a congruence for every
composite row.
