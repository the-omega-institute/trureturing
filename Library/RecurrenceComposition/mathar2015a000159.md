## Source statements

The A000159 NAME field states:

> a(n) = A058087(n,n-3).

Its first FORMULA field, attributed to R. J. Mathar, Nov 02 2015, states:

> Conjecture: 2*(-252307*n + 1041077)*a(n) + (504614*n^2 - 3362985*n + 5118150)*a(n-1) + (1280831*n^2 - 7397886*n + 6461565)*a(n-2) + (746598*n^2 - 2913543*n - 1336090)*a(n-3) + (-405481*n^2 + 6175011*n - 15469320)*a(n-4) + (-375862*n^2 + 4098537*n - 8846430)*a(n-5) + 2*(-187931*n + 560630)*a(n-6) = 0.

The A058087 NAME field states:

> Triangle read by rows, giving coefficients of the ménage hit polynomials ordered by descending powers. T(n, k) for 0 <= k <= n.

Its FORMULA field, attributed to William P. Orrick, states:

> Row n of the triangle lists the coefficients of the polynomial U_n(t) = Sum_{k=0..n} (2*n/(2*n-k))*binomial(2*n-k,k)*(n-k)!*(t-1)^k, with higher order terms first (Kaplansky and Riordan).

The displayed Maple definition begins:

> U := proc(n) if n = 0 then return 1 fi;

The classical polynomial occurs in I. Kaplansky and J. Riordan,
*The problème des ménages*, Scripta Mathematica 12 (1946), 113–124,
equation (25), p. 121; the notation there is G rather than U.

## Mathematical scope

The sequence has offset 3,1. Descending row order makes a(n) the coefficient
of degree three in U(n). The recurrence ranges over every n at least nine,
so its six backward shifts remain in the source domain. The defining
polynomial uses rational division and the explicit branch U(0)=1.

The proof converts the coefficient series into a rational affine pullback
of the factorial series and extracts the recurrence from a second-order
formal differential identity. This does not prove the separate rounded
Bessel expression or the prime-shift divisibility conjecture.
