## Source statements

The unpaginated OEIS A375449 Name field states:

> Expansion of g.f. A(x) satisfying [x^n] x*A'(x) / (1 - n*A(x)) = n^(n+1) for n >= 1.

The unpaginated OEIS A375452 Name field states:

> Expansion of g.f. A(x) satisfying [x^n] x*A'(x) / (1 - n*A(x)) = n^n for n >= 1.

Each entry's Comments field states:

> Conjecture: for n > 1, a(n) is odd iff n is of the form 2^k-2 or 2^k-1 for some k > 1.

Both entries have offset one. The rational generating series has zero constant
coefficient. Its formal derivative and unit-denominator inverse give the literal
coefficient equation. The coefficient is identified with an integer before
asserting oddness; indices and support exponents are natural numbers.

## Mathematical scope

The diagonal recursion has an integral solution for every integer forcing
sequence. When the forcing value at each positive index is congruent to that
index modulo two, the reduced solution is T+T², where T has support at the
positive Mersenne indices. This gives the odd and even support families in the
quoted conjectures. The two forcing sequences are n^n and n^(n-1).
