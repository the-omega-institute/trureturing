# OEIS A352700

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A352700 (revision `#7`, March 31, 2022; authored by Paul D. Hanna, March 29, 2022).

The entry is unpaginated. Its `OFFSET` is `0,3`, and its `NAME` defines the generating function:

> G.f.: Sum_{n>=0} binomial((n+1)*(2*n+1),n)/(2*n+1) * x^n / C(x)^(n*(2*n+1)+1), where C(x) = 1 + x*C(x)^2 is the Catalan function (A000108).

Its `COMMENTS` state the six numbered conjectures:

> Conjectures: given g.f. A(x), let C(x) = (1 - sqrt(1-4*x))/(2*x) be the Catalan power series (A000108), then
> (1) A(x)^3 = A(x^3) (mod 3),
> (2) A(x) = C(x) + x^2*C(x)^3 (mod 3) = (2 - x)*C(x) - 1 (mod 3),
> (3) A(x) = C(x) (mod 2),
> (4) a(n) = binomial(2*n+1,n)/(2*n+1) + 3*binomial(2*n-1,n-2)/(2*n-1) (mod 3) for n >= 0,
> (5) a(n) = 2*A000108(n) - A000108(n-1)  (mod 3) for n >= 1,
> (6) a(n) = A000108(n) (mod 2) for n >= 0.

Its `FORMULA` field records the formal substitutions:

> (1) A(x-x^2) = Sum_{n>=0} binomial((n+1)*(2*n+1),n)/(2*n+1) * (x/(1-x))^n * (1-x)^((n+1)*(2*n+1)).
> (2) A(x/(1+x)^2) = Sum_{n>=0} binomial((n+1)*(2*n+1),n)/(2*n+1) * x^n / (1+x)^((n+1)*(2*n+1)).

Its `EXAMPLE` field gives:

> C(x) + x^2*C(x)^3 = 1 + x + 3*x^2 + 8*x^3 + 23*x^4 + 70*x^5 + 222*x^6 + 726*x^7 + 2431*x^8 + 8294*x^9 + ... + (C(2*n,n)/(n+1) + C(2*n-1,n-2)*3/(2*n-1))*x^n + ...

Its `PROG` (`PARI`) field computes `a(n)` by the finite degree-`n` truncation `sum(m=0, n, binomial((m+1)*(2*m+1), m)/(2*m+1) * x^m / C^(m*(2*m+1)+1))`.

For every natural `m`, `2*m + 1` divides `binomial((m+1)*(2*m+1), m)` in `N`, so each weight is an exact integer agreeing with rational division. Likewise `2*n + 1` divides `binomial(2*n + 1, n)` for every `n >= 0`, and `2*n - 1` divides `3 * binomial(2*n - 1, n - 2)` for every `n >= 2`. In conjecture `(4)` and the `EXAMPLE` expansion of `C(x) + x^2 * C(x)^3`, the standard negative-lower-index binomial convention `binomial(2*n - 1, n - 2) = 0` for `n < 2` makes the second summand vanish at `n = 0` and `n = 1`.

The companion comment `"Odd terms seem to occur only at positions 2^n-1 for n >= 0"` follows as a corollary of `(6)` together with the standard dyadic parity characterization of Catalan numbers, and is outside the six numbered conjectures `(1)-(6)` formalized as the primary target. Global literature completeness and continued external open status are `ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED`; no prior proof of `(1)-(6)` was found in the bounded OEIS and literature screen.
