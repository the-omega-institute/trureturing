## Source statement

The revision #14 NAME field reads verbatim:

> E.g.f.: exp( Sum_{n>=1} x^(n^2) / n^n ).

The FORMULA conjecture reads verbatim:

> a(n) == 1 (mod 6) (conjecture - valid up to n=1024; if true for n>=0, why?).

Over rational formal power series, let
`H(X)=sum_{m>=1} X^(m^2)/m^m` and `A(X)=exp(H(X))`.
The sequence term is `a(n)=n!*[X^n]A(X)`. The square exponent and power
denominator are literal source data. The all-index conclusion includes zero
and is expressed by an integer witness `k` with `a(n)=1+6*k` in the rationals;
integrality is part of the conclusion rather than a premise.
The source's reported check through 1024 is finite evidence, not an all-index proof.

## Mathematical scope

The repository-derived proof identifies the factorial-normalized inner
coefficients with natural factorial quotients. For every `m>=2`,
`6*m^m` divides `(m^2)!`, so this integral inner sequence reduces modulo six
to the identity sequence. The existing integral EGF composition law transfers
that reduction to the outer exponential. The resulting integer divisibility
is cast back into the exact rational equality. This proof is not attributed
to the source entry. The argument is unbounded and symbolic.
