## Source statement

OEIS A007820, revision 89, NAME and the unnumbered COMMENTS entry:

```text
%N Stirling numbers of second kind S(2n,n).
%C Conjecture: a(2*n+1) is divisible by (2*n + 1)^2. - _Peter Bala_, Mar 30 2025
```

The entry has offset zero. Its Stirling numbers count partitions of a labelled
set into nonempty, unlabelled blocks. Thus the assertion says that for every
natural number n, including zero, the square of 2*n+1 divides
S(2*(2*n+1),2*n+1). Mathlib's `Nat.stirlingSecond` is this same notion.

The entry also attributes a finite reflection formula to Vladimir Kruchinin.
That identity is prior literature. The deduction of odd-column square
divisibility uses its zero-order sign, the extra factor in the first-order
binomial coefficient, and cancellation of the coprime factor two.

The conjecture has no identified arXiv source or DOI. Completeness of the
literature screen in issue #22943 is ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED; no claim of a first
published proof follows from that screen.
