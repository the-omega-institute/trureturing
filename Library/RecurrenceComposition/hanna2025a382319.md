## Source statement

P. D. Hanna, OEIS A382319, Name (unpaginated):

> G.f. A(x) satisfies A(x) = x/(1-x) + Sum_{n>=1} A(x^n)^3.

Comments (unpaginated):

> Conjecture: a(n) == A382318(3*n) (mod 3) for n >= 0.

Both entries have offset one. The rational power series have zero constant
coefficient. The infinite sum is interpreted coefficientwise: at degree m,
sum coeff(m/n,F) over the positive divisors n of m; at m=0 the finite sum
is empty. In A382319 the cube is taken before Lambert summation.

## Mathematical relation

The two equations imply B = Sum_{n>=1} A(x^n) and A = x+B^3. The
integral fixed point is constructed by coefficient stabilization. Cubic
Frobenius modulo three identifies B(x)^3 with B(x^3), which gives the
coefficient congruences. The rational series and integer residues are the
objects stated by the two formal propositions.
