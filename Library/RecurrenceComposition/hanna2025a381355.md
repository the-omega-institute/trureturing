A381355 NAME:

> G.f. A(x) = x*F'(x)/F(x) where F(x) is the g.f. of A381353 that satisfies [x^n] F(x)^prime(n) = 0 for n > 1.

A381355 COMMENTS:

> Conjecture: a(n) is divisible by prime(n) for n > 1.

A381353 NAME:

> G.f. A(x) satisfies [x^n] A(x)^prime(n) = 0 for n > 1.

A381353 COMMENTS:

> It appears that [x^(n-1)] A'(x)/A(x) is divisible by prime(n) for n > 1 (see example section).

The PROGRAM initializes `my(A=[1,1])`; the first two coefficients are one.
A381353's offset is `0,4`, and A381355's is `1,2`. The prime schedule is
one-based, with `prime(1)=2` and `prime(2)=3`. The target coefficient is
`[x^(n-1)] F'(x)/F(x)`, equivalently `[x^n] (x F'(x)/F(x))`.

## Mathematical scope

The formal statement asserts existence of a rational source series and,
for every rational series with these initial coefficients and zero-diagonal
equations, an integer witness for each prime multiple at every `n>1`.
Integral coefficient corrections construct the source, and coefficientwise
uniqueness identifies every rational source with it. Formal differentiation
and reduction modulo the indexed prime then prove divisibility.
No assertion is made at index zero or one, or about the distinct
A393866/A393867 source equation.
