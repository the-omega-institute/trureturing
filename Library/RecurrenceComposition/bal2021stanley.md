# Stanley–Elder–Fine theorems for colored partitions

Section 6 treats overpartitions as an ordinary-partition component together with a distinct-part component. Its nonoverlined frequency decomposes by removing positive numbers of unmarked occurrences. Equation (6.2) identifies overpartitions containing a specified nonoverlined size with overpartitions of the reduced integer. Theorem 6.2 compares the threshold-one and threshold-three statistics.

These are prior ingredients of the finite counting argument; they are not attributed as new results. No claim of exhaustive literature absence follows from these sources.
