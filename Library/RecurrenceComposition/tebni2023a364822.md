The index starts at zero, with coefficients 1, 2, 9, 56, 465, 4832,
60249, 876416. The rational formal series for cosh and sinh have coefficients
1/n! at even and odd indices respectively, and zero otherwise. Multiplying
the nth ordinary coefficient of their quotient by n! gives a(n).

The two Lean results prove integrality and the stated congruences for every
odd prime and every natural exponent respectively. The proof uses the
binomial recurrence obtained by multiplying by 1 - 2 sinh(x). Substitution
of 2 sinh into the geometric series preserves integral factorial-scaled
coefficients; the binomial product formula gives integrality after
multiplication by cosh. Interior binomial coefficients vanish at a
prime index, and odd-index binomial coefficients vanish modulo a power
of two. The case m = 0 has modulus one. No uniform exact valuation theorem
for all even indices is asserted.
