# Callan's two A002067 conjectures

## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A002067

The source is the two consecutive unnumbered Callan COMMENTS headed “Conjectures” in OEIS A002067, revision 116. The entry has no page numbers and no conjecture arXiv identifier. Its NAME is:

> a(n) = Sum_{k=0..n-1} binomial(2*n,2*k)*a(k)*a(n-k-1).

The terms, offset 0,3 and Maple program set a(0)=1. The two source assertions read:

> a(n) is the Hafnian of the triangular array (u(i,j))_{1 <= i < j <= 2n} with u(i,j)=i. The Hafnian is the same as the Pfaffian except without the alternating signs just as the permanent of a matrix is the determinant without the signs.

> a(n) is the total weight of Dyck n-paths with weight defined as follows. Given a Dyck path, for each upstep, record its position in the path and the height of its upper endpoint; then multiply together all of these positions and heights. For example, the Dyck 4-path P = UUDUUDDD has upsteps in positions 1,2,4,5 ending at heights 1,2,2,3 respectively, and hence weight(P) = 480. (In fact the positions determine the heights because, for the k-th upstep, position + height = 2k.)

Positions and vertex labels are one-based. For zero semilength there is one empty path and one empty perfect matching; empty products equal one. All perfect matchings, including crossing ones, enter the hafnian.

The constant-row hafnian/path correspondence is prior art: David Callan, *A combinatorial survey of identities for the double factorial*, https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.1317v1, Section 3, Proposition 1. Dominici, *Asymptotic analysis of the derivatives of the inverse error function*, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0607230v2, Section 3, supplies the derivative-polynomial recurrence. These supporting papers are not the conjecture's source. The Lean proof uses a derivative convolution and Pascal's identity, without formalizing an analytic inverse-error function or a formal ODE.
