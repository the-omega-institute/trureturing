# The Granvik Mertens array

Mats Granvik's OEIS A192763 COMMENTS field states:

> The main diagonal is the Mobius function times the natural numbers A055615 (conjecture). For k>1 the first row is the Mertens function + 2 = A002321 + 2 (conjecture).

The entry's first Mathematica program, attributed to Granvik and Gérard,
sets the corner to one and the next two border cells to two. Michael Somos's
PARI and Haskell programs give the positive Euclidean reduction
$T(n,k)=T(k,((n-1)\bmod k)+1)$ for $n>k>1$, the zero row-sum
diagonal, symmetry, and the border update used in the formal definition.
All division is interpreted in $\mathbb Q$.

## Settlement scope

`GranvikMertensArray.result` states the diagonal and first-row identities
for all positive diagonal indices and all first-row indices at least two.
Its proof includes equality with the literal first Mathematica recurrence,
by well-founded uniqueness. The extension at a zero index is zero and is
outside the published positive-index array.

For coprime interior indices, signed Bézout descent identifies the terminal
Euclidean quotient with the quotient determined by the smaller inverse
residue. Inversion permutes reduced residues. Möbius convolution then
evaluates their border sum, and grouping the other cells by their gcd gives
the full row sum. This arithmetic identity forces the border update.
