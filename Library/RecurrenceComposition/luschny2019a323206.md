## Source statements

NAME: “A(n, k) = hypergeometric([-k, k+1], [-k-1], n), square array read
by ascending antidiagonals for n,k >= 0.”

COMMENTS: “Conjecture: A(n, k) is odd if and only if n is even or (n is odd
and k + 2 = 2^j for some j > 0).”

The hypergeometric notation means the finite sum from degree zero through
degree k of the coefficients `(-k)_j (k+1)_j / ((-k-1)_j j!)` times `n^j`.
The rising factorial of order zero and the power `0^0` equal one. Every
lower-parameter factor in this range is nonzero. The formal proposition
includes an integer whose rational image equals this finite sum; its exponent
is named e rather than j.

## Mathematical scope

The coefficient bridge gives integer ballot coefficients. Their row sum is
the ordinary Catalan number of index k+1. Evaluation modulo two therefore
reduces to the constant coefficient at even n and the Catalan row sum at odd
n. The binary Catalan support is reused from
`D5.S1.Recurrence.Invariants.CatalanCompositionSquareParity.binary_catalan`.
