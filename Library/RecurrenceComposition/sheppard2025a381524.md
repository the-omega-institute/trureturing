## Verified locator

Source: https://oeis.org/A381524/internal?rev=18, revision 18, Definition and
Comments, with offset zero. The definition is “Smallest exponent of x of maximal
coefficient (ignoring signs) in Hermite polynomial of order n.” The independent
conjecture is “Differences are either +1 or -1.”

The source's HermiteH convention and its examples fix physicists normalization.
Its order-five example chooses exponent three. At order eight the absolute
coefficients at exponents two and four tie, and the source chooses two. The
formal target keeps this smallest-index tie convention over all coefficients
from zero through n, including zero coefficients, for every natural n.
