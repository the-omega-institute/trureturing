# Kurkov's modified Entringer divisibility conjecture

OEIS A386381, revision 27, offset 0,4, FORMULA (unpaginated), states:

> Conjecture 1: if d is a divisor of a(k), then d is also a divisor of a(m*d + k) where m is any natural number. In particular, a(k) is a divisor of a(m*a(k) + k) for any k >= 0 where m is any natural number.

The sequence is the main diagonal of OEIS A386363, revision 24,
https://oeis.org/A386363/internal, whose NAME gives
`T(n,k)=T(n,k-1)+(n-2)*T(n-1,n-k)` for `1<k<=n`,
`T(n,1)=T(n-1,n-1)` for positive n, and `T(n,0)=0^n`.
Thus `T(0,0)=1`, the other left boundary values are zero, and
`a(0)=a(1)=a(2)=1`. Their revision identifiers are
`#27 Feb 27 2026 08:38:26` and `#24 Feb 27 2026 14:48:35`.

The Lean modulus is a positive integer. Only its occurrence in the sequence
index uses `Int.toNat`; positivity proves that this conversion is exact.
Sequence values and indices are natural numbers. Integer divisibility in
the universal statement and natural divisibility in the source's particular
statement have the same meaning under the standard embedding.

The proof uses the repository's earlier frozen
`IntegerNewton.int_newton_of_fwdDiff_iter_eq_zero`, together with Mathlib's
finite differences, binomial coefficients, Pochhammer polynomials, factorial
divisibility, polynomial evaluation and `ZMod`. The finite Newton expansion
is classical. No external Lean package or transplanted code is included.
LeanPool's `PolynomialIntegerValued.lean` at
`afb2ba0cbb6aaa7ba3e9d5332068efe09d0d0367` supplies a related exact Newton
ingredient, but is not an admitted dependency and is not used here.

The mathematical deduction from reflected row polynomials to arbitrary
divisor propagation is repository derived. The bounded literature and owner
screen in preregistration
[#31218](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/31218)
found no complete supplier for this assertion. Exact identifiers and
Entringer/boustrophedon structure were searched in repository content and
the pinned Mathlib; no full-target declaration was found. These searches
include private repository declarations. Global novelty, priority,

Ramassamy's *Modular Periodicity of the Euler Numbers and a Sequence by
Arnold*, arXiv:1712.08666v1, concerns ordinary Euler up/down numbers,
which differ from this sequence already at index 4. It is not a supplier
for the present theorem. The independently inspected ProveIt modified
Entringer asymptotics report excludes these divisibility conjectures from
its claims and is not correctness evidence for this proof. Conjecture 2
and the A060818 assertion in the same OEIS entry are separate questions.
