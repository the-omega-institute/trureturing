# OEIS A378258

The unpaginated Name states: "G.f. satisfies A(x) = B(x) + 5*A(x)^2 where B(A(x) - A(x)^2) = x."

The Comments state: "Conjecture: a(n) is odd iff n is a power of 2."

The offset is one. The normalized rational series has constant coefficient zero
and linear coefficient one. The formal proposition includes existence and
integrality and tests every natural exponent at least one.

The parity assertion is distinct from the entry's conjecture about residues
modulo six and its assertion about the first negative term.

The separate Comments conjecture states: "Conjecture: a(n) is not congruent to 1 nor 5 (mod 6) for n > 1."
Its exact target and bounded literature screen are preregistered in
[#25601](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/25601).
The corresponding modulo-six source unit reuses the same rational `IsGF`
predicate, quantifies every normalized solution and every natural degree greater
than one, and includes existence and an integer coefficient representative.
Its reduction modulo three derives reflection symmetry from the equations;
symmetry is not an added source assumption. Literature completeness remains
`ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED`; no global priority is asserted.
