## Source statements

> Conjectures: For all p prime, (a(p) + a(p+1) - 2) == 0 (mod p),
> a(p+1) == 1 (mod ((p+1)*p)).

> E.g.f.: exp(-1 + (1 + x)*(1 - log(1 + x))).

The second COMMENTS line inherits the prime quantifier of the first line.
Indices are natural numbers beginning at zero, and the coefficients are integers.
The formal statement includes existence of an integer sequence with this exact
exponential generating function and quantifies over every such sequence.
Mathlib's `PowerSeries.log ℚ` denotes the formal series for `log(1+X)`;
`PowerSeries.subst inner outer` substitutes into the outer series.

## Mathematical scope

The coefficient argument separates the term of expansion index zero from the
remaining terms. Factorial scaling makes the former equal to one and the latter
integer multiples of the coefficient index. The differential equation
`F' = -log(1+X) * F`, prime binomial divisibility and Wilson's theorem give the
prime-successor congruence. Coprimality of consecutive integers gives the product
modulus in the second assertion.

Paul D. Hanna's FORMULA in [OEIS A176118](https://oeis.org/A176118) already
states the rising-factorial expansion used in this argument:

> E.g.f.: Sum_{n>=0} (-1)^n * x^n/n! * Product_{k=1..n} (k + x).

The expansion is credited prior art. The scope here is the two A350759 COMMENTS
assertions; composite-index characterizations and higher prime-power refinements
remain separate questions. Completeness of the literature screen is
