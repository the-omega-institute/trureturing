# Hanna's conjecture for OEIS A219431

## Verified locator

## Source statements

OEIS A219431, unpaginated Name line:
"Logarithmic derivative of the g.f. of the overpartitions of n^2 (A219430)."

OEIS A219431, unpaginated Comment line:
"Conjecture: a(2*n+1) == 2 (mod 4), a(2*n) == 0 (mod 4)."

OEIS A219430, unpaginated Name line:
"Number of overpartitions of n^2; a(n) = A015128(n^2)."

The offset of A219431 is one. Its logarithmic derivative is the series
`x G'(x) / G(x)`, where `G(x) = sum_n pbar(n^2) x^n`. The overpartition
coefficient `pbar(m)` is defined by the product of `(1+x^k)/(1-x^k)`;
only factors with `1 <= k <= m` contribute to its coefficient at degree `m`.
The formal statement includes integrality of every positive-degree coefficient.

## Scope

The modular proof first reduces the overpartition coefficient to twice the
number of divisors modulo four. Positive squares have an odd divisor count.
The resulting square-sampled series is `1 + 2 sum_{n>=1} x^n` modulo four;
its logarithmic derivative has coefficient `2n` modulo four. The integer
unit inverse supplies integrality before reduction.

The completeness of the literature screen and the bibliographic attribution of
the standard overpartition congruence remain ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED. Congruences
modulo eight and other square-valued sampling sequences are separate questions.
