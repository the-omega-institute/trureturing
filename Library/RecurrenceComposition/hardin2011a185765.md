# OEIS A185765

The locators are the NAME and sole FORMULA fields in revision 9.

## Source statement

> Number of (n+1)X6 binary arrays with no 2X2 subblock trace equal to any horizontal or vertical neighbor 2X2 subblock trace.

> Empirical: a(n)=a(n-1)+30*a(n-2)+24*a(n-3)-95*a(n-4)-15*a(n-5)+64*a(n-6)-116*a(n-7)+16*a(n-8)+16*a(n-9) for n>12

The offset is `1,1`. Arrays have labeled rows and columns, binary entries,
open rectangular boundaries, and no symmetry quotient. A block trace is the
ordinary sum of its upper-left and lower-right entries in the natural numbers.
Horizontal and vertical neighbors have block origins one unit apart.
The recurrence embeds cardinalities in the integers before signed arithmetic.

## Mathematical mechanism

Each successive pair of rows is a state. Horizontal comparisons restrict states;
vertical comparisons restrict transitions between overlapping states. Summing
continuation counts over all initial row pairs counts the literal arrays.
For the polynomial

`Q(X)=X^9-X^8-30X^7-24X^6+95X^5+15X^4-64X^3+116X^2-16X-16`,

the continuation residual vanishes after five transitions. Its total also
vanishes at exponents three and four. Linearity propagates the vector identity
and gives the printed recurrence at every index at least thirteen.
