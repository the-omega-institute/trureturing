# OEIS A386830

The Online Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences, entry A386830, revision 10,
Aug 19 2025 03:35:05, defines Seiichi Manyama's sequence by

> a(n) = Sum_{k=0..n} 3^k * 2^(n-k) * binomial(3*n+1,k) * binomial(3*n-k,n-k).

Manyama's sequence attribution is Aug 05 2025. R. J. Mathar's conjecture
attribution is Aug 19 2025. The quoted claim includes both the recurrence
and D-finiteness. Editorial acceptance does not establish either assertion.

The entry has OFFSET 0,2 and no explicit recurrence onset. The interpretation
at every natural n >= 2 is the nonnegative-backshift interpretation adopted
in the problem dossier. It is not an author-stated onset, and the second
OFFSET component is not used as a recurrence bound.

The ordinary rational formal series must use the literal sum at every
natural index, including zero. The other FORMULA expressions in the entry
are useful proof candidates, not assumed equivalences. Related entries
A386829, A386831 and A386764 concern different sequences.
