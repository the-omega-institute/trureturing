# Graphs With the Same Edge Count in Each Neighborhood

## Verified locator

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2507.14473
- Edition: arXiv:2507.14473v1. The introduction defines triangle regularity, §1.1 states Conjecture 1, and §4 repeats it and considers finite abelian groups. https://arxiv.org/html/2507.14473v1#Thmconj1
- The arXiv record links the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## Attribution and scope

Sheffield and Xi ask whether every pair of local counts realized by a graph also has an abelian Cayley realization. Their constructions, nonexistence intervals and spectral arguments have their own hypotheses in §§3–4. The local counts are degree and the number of unordered edges induced by the open neighborhood.

Yair Caro and Xandru Mifsud give a twelve-vertex planar `(5,5)`-realization in Figure 5 and Tables 1–2 of *On `(r,c)`-Constant, Planar and Circulant Graphs*. Theorem 6 of the journal edition gives a congruence for cyclic constructions. https://doi.org/10.7151/dmgt.2551

Hak, Kozerenko, Lohvynov and Yarosh, *Regular K3-regular graphs*, identify the icosahedron as graph G1 in Appendix A. Section 4 repeats the Sheffield–Xi conjecture and gives compatible Turán-based families in the stated ranges. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23517v2

The abelian obstruction accompanying the icosahedral witness is the conclusion of the repository refutation. The bounded literature assessment supporting the choice of this target is recorded in issue #17356. https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/17356
