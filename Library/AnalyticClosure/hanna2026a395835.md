## Source statements

A395835 NAME, verbatim:

> Define F(n,x) as the power series in x satisfying F(n,x) = 1 + x*F(n,x)^2 - x^n*F(n,x)^(2*n), then for n >= 2, a(n) = M where x^M is the first power of x with a negative coefficient in F(n,x). Should no such M exist for some n, then there are no further terms and this sequence would be finite.

Each selected COMMENTS assertion reads:

> Conjecture: this sequence is infinite.

The other NAME sentences are quoted below with their respective entries.

A395836 NAME, verbatim:

> Define F(n,x) as the power series in x satisfying F(n,x) = 1 + x*F(n,x)^2 - x^n*F(n,x)^(2*n), then for n >= 2, a(n) = abs( [x^M] F(n,x) ) where x^M is the first power of x with a negative coefficient in F(n,x). Should no such M exist for some n, then there are no further terms and this sequence would be finite.

A395837 NAME, verbatim:

> Define F(n,x) as the power series in x satisfying F(n,x) = 1 + x*F(n,x)^2 - x^n*F(n,x)^(2*n), then for n >= 2, a(n) = [x^(M-1)] F(n,x) where x^M is the first power of x with a negative coefficient in F(n,x). Should no such M exist for some n, then there are no further terms and this sequence would be finite.

A395838 NAME, verbatim:

> First differences of A395835.

## Mathematical correspondence

All coefficients are integral. A first negative coefficient is selected with
Mathlib's IsLeast. The stopping rule requires every preceding parameter to be
defined. A395838 uses the forward differences M(n+1)-M(n): the table prepends
M(2) and shifts this tail by one index. This finite prefix and shift do not
change finite versus infinite length. No pointwise agreement at the table's
initial index is asserted.

The four infinitude assertions are equivalent to existence of a negative
coefficient for every parameter n at least two, and have one shared proof.
Their preregistrations are #21728, #21729, #21730 and #21731.
Infinite length imposes no distinctness condition on the values.
