# Contractive mixed-norm inclusions

## Locator

Author text: https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.05542

A. Llinares and D. Vukotić, *Contractive inequalities for mixed norm spaces
and the Beta function*, Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications
509 (2022), 125938; arXiv:2107.05542.

## Source statement

The paper characterizes contractive inclusions
$H(p,q,a)\subset H(u,v,b)$ under the hypothesis $p\ge u$.
It describes the complementary range $p<u$ as very difficult.

The constant-one point-evaluation conjecture corresponds to the
contractive inclusion
$H(p,q,\alpha)\subset H(\infty,\infty,\alpha+1/p)$.
For finite p this has $p<u=\infty$, outside the characterized range.
