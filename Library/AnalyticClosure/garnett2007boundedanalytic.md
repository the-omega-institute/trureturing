# Blaschke products of prescribed zeros

## Verified locator

DOI: 10.1007/0-387-49763-3
Source URL: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/0-387-49763-3

Chapter II, *Hp Spaces*, 48–97, DOI 10.1007/0-387-49763-3_2,
treats Blaschke products and factorization in the disk.

## Use and exact scope

The finite bound uses the factors of the specified distinct zeros only. Division removes
each such zero holomorphically, and the unit boundary modulus of that
factor preserves the supremum bound on each smaller disk. The limiting
estimate gives the corresponding product of pseudohyperbolic distances.
