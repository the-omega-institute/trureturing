# Effective linear independence for zeta ordinates

Conjecture 1.1 states that for each real \(d>0\) there is a positive constant \(C_d\), possibly ineffective, such that for every real \(X\ge 2\),
$$
\left|\sum_{0<\gamma\le X} k_\gamma\gamma\right|\ge C_d e^{-X^{1+d}},
$$
where the integers \(k_\gamma\) are not all zero and each satisfies \(|k_\gamma|\le N(X)\). The ordinates form a set, while \(N(X)\) is the zero count through \(X\). Under RH the imaginary part identifies each distinct positive zero, and the canonical count in \((0,X]\) uses analytic multiplicity. There are no real nontrivial zeros under RH.

Theorem 1.5 uses ELI and Assumptions 1–3 for a sequence of complex coefficients to bound the maximum and minimum of \(\Phi_{X^2}\) over \([2,X]\) on the scale \((\log\log\log X)^A\) for large \(X\). The actual harmonic-weighted sine cutoff result uses ELI as a premise and derives its translated-window bound from a finite recurrence estimate.
