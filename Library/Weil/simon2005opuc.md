## Finite orthogonal polynomial identities

Szego's monic recurrence reads
$\Phi_{n+1}=z\Phi_n-\overline{\alpha_n}\Phi_n^*$.
Geronimus identifies the coefficients with the Schur parameters of the
Caratheodory function. Cantero, Moral and Velazquez introduced the corresponding
five-diagonal matrix. For a real symmetric moment sequence these constructions
use real coefficients; conjugation of Laurent monomials is exponent reversal.
The Laurent order is $1,z,z^{-1},z^2,z^{-2},\ldots$.

The real Riesz specialization uses finite coefficient sums. Its Gram matrices
are positive definite, so every polynomial and normalization is defined.
The representation identifies one time step with multiplication by $z^{-1}$;
finite propagation then gives the amplitude identity and the cumulative
projection formula without an infinite-dimensional spectral theorem.
