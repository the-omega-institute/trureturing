# Montgomery pair correlation on the unit band

Theorem 1 and equations (1.1)–(1.4) give the uniform formula
$F_Y(\alpha)=Y^{-2\alpha}(\log Y+O(1))+\alpha+O((\log Y)^{-1/2})$
for $0\le\alpha\le1$. The ordinate cutoff is inclusive, and both entries of each ordered pair are counted with analytic multiplicity. Under RH the factor $w$ in the sum is $4/(4+(\gamma-\gamma')^2)$ and the normalization is $2\pi/(Y\log Y)$.

The average with weight $2(1-\alpha)$ is bounded uniformly for large $Y$. Under RH its individual pair contributions are nonnegative because the cosine transform of the triangular weight is $1$ at zero and $2(1-\cos z)/z^2$ elsewhere. The equal-ordinate terms contribute $2\pi\sum_{0<\gamma\le Y}m_\gamma^2/(Y\log Y)$ to the normalized average, with the sum over distinct ordinates. Section 7 uses the same diagonal comparison with a different test kernel to study simple zeros. [Theorem 1 and §7](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.04799)
