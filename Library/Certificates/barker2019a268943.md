## Counting argument

Prepending a letter partitions words into three states: no equality event;
a first event that is the cyclic successor of the first letter; and the remaining
words with an event. Their counts U, A, B start at (8,0,0) and transform as
(U,A,B) ↦ (7U,B,U+8A+6B). Their total counts satisfy
c(n+4)+34c(n+2)+56c(n+1)=13c(n+3), with c(1)=8, c(2)=64, c(3)=504.
Coefficientwise power-series identities give the rational formula.
Recurrence uniqueness and the roots 7, 3−√17, 3+√17 give the radical formula.
