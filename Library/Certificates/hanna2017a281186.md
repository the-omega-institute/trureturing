## Source statement

> Conjecture: if G(x) = exp( 1/G(x) * Integral G(x)^t dx ) for some fixed real t, then G(x) will consist entirely of nonnegative coefficients iff t >= 8/3.

The NAME, FORMULA and EGF normalization read:

> E.g.f. satisfies: A(x) = exp( 1/A(x) * Integral A(x)^(8/3) dx ).

> E.g.f. A(x) satisfies: A(x)^(8/3) = A'(x) * (1 + log(A(x))).

> E.g.f.: A(x) = Sum_{n>=0} a(n) * x^n / (n! * 3^n).

The real formal-series interpretation has constant coefficient one,
zero integration constant, and real power exp(t log G). The source's
PARI intformal program selects the zero integration constant. Coefficient
indices include zero. Multiplication by j! · 3^j preserves coefficient signs.
The quantified assertion includes existence for every real parameter.

## Refutation and limits

The repository derives from the source equation an integer-polynomial
certificate forcing the degree-720 coefficient to be negative at t=8/3.
The claim's existence and nonnegativity clauses therefore contradict each
other at that parameter. Independent existence and uniqueness, minimality
of the negative degree, interval negativity and corrected thresholds are
not separately asserted by the settling theorem.
