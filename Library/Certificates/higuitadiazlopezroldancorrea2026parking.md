# Stochastic dominance in discrete parking

## Verified locator

- Version 2 of the preprint is indexed at https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14718v2.
- Section 2.1 defines the static and sequential laws. Equation (1) gives the uniform static mass. Equation (2) sums the products of reciprocal available-set sizes over all orders of a jammed set. Equations (3) and (4) give the static and sequential CDFs.
