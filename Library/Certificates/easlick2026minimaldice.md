# Easlick, minimal-advantage dice cycles

Definitions 1 and 2 use fair independent rolls of nondecreasing n-tuples of labels in [n],
allow repeated labels, and identify a die by its sorted tuple. The standard tuple is included
in Example 1. Strict wins are unconditional: ties contribute zero and are not removed from
the sample space. Cycles have distinct vertices and include their closing edge.

Conjecture 1 is the conjunction S(6) and, for all integers n at least seven, not S(n),
where S(n) says the global maximum simple-cycle length is exactly n.

The source cycle data in Appendices G, H, I and J are by Terry Easlick,
[arXiv:2610.40321v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.40321v1), licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The eleven-face and uniformly padded cycles are derived constructions.
