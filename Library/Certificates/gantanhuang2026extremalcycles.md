Conjecture 1.7 is stated for positive integers $k\le d<n$ and a connected
$n$-vertex graph. It asserts that a path on $k+1$ vertices exists if the
number of vertices of degree at least $d$ is at least
$q(n-1)/d+(1-q/d)(n-1)q/(q+d(d-1))+2$, where
$q=\lfloor(k-3)/4\rfloor$. Section 2 uses finite simple undirected graphs.

The paper supplies the conjecture and its definitions. The repository proof
uses six clique vertices with three private leaves each at the parameters
preregistered for the finite test. The dossier records the exact threshold
and the relation to the earlier path question.
