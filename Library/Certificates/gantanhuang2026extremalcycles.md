# An extremal question of Erdős et al. on cycles

## Verified locator

- DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2611.22824
- Version 1 at https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22824v1
- [Conjecture 1.7](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.22824v1#S1.Thmlemma2026ErdosQuestion7) and [Definitions 2.1 and 2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.22824v1#S2)

Conjecture 1.7 is stated for positive integers $kle d<n$ and a connected
$n$-vertex graph. It asserts that a path on $k+1$ vertices exists if the
number of vertices of degree at least $d$ is at least
$q(n-1)/d+(1-q/d)(n-1)q/(q+d(d-1))+2$, where
$q=\lfloor(k-3)/4\rfloor$. Section 2 uses finite simple undirected graphs.

The paper supplies the conjecture and its definitions. The counterexample
uses the finite graph registered in [issue #31996](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/31996).
