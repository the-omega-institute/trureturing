# Convex subhyperlattices under the Birkhoff order

Definitions 1.1–1.3 and 1.10 specify the hyperoperation, the strong join hyperlattice axioms,
the induced order, and convexity. Proposition 3.1 specifies both clauses of the Birkhoff
order and proves that it is a partial order. Corollary 3.10 proves a lattice result for
P-hyperlattices. Example 3.11 has a strongness failure, so it does not settle the conjecture
for strong join hyperlattices. [Versioned text](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.10526v1#S3)

N Aishwarya Nayak, Bhargava K, Pallavi Panjarike, Kuncham Syam Prasad, Tapatee Sahoo,
and Harikrishnan Panackal state the conjecture in §5, printed p. 20.

> Based on our observations, we have conjectured that (CS(𝔏), ≤_b) is a lattice for every strong hyperlattice.
