> Number of (n+1)X5 0..1 arrays with the determinants of 2X2 subblocks nondecreasing rightwards and downwards.

> Empirical: a(n) = 18*a(n-1) -73*a(n-2) -256*a(n-3) +2060*a(n-4) -1216*a(n-5) -12612*a(n-6) +18224*a(n-7) +20992*a(n-8) -40064*a(n-9) -9984*a(n-10) +24576*a(n-11) for n>15

The source offset is 1,1. Subblocks use adjacent rows and columns, the
binary entries are ordinary integers, determinants are over the integers,
and boundaries do not wrap. Repeated rows and equal determinants are
allowed. In Lean the carrier is `Matrix (Fin (n + 1)) (Fin 5) (Fin 2)`;
`minor` applies `Matrix.det` to the literal adjacent block, `OrderedMinors`
requires `Monotone` in each coordinate, and `a` casts the `Nat.card` of
that subtype to the integers. The recurrence uses natural subtraction
in its arguments and integer arithmetic on its values.

The proof identifies the count with a finite-state transfer expression.
Its polynomial residual lies in an invariant span of paired boundary-bit
differences with zero total, which yields the scalar recurrence at all
source indices greater than 15. The source formula is attributed to
Hardin; this derivation is repository-derived.
