## Source statement

> In particular, is $\Psi_t(X_{H_h}(X;q))$ real-rooted for every such $h$ and $q$?

Here $h\colon[n]\to[n]$ is weakly increasing with $i\le h(i)$, and
$q$ is a nonnegative real weight. The question concerns the linear map
$\Psi_t$ on fundamental coordinates.

## Scope

The four-vertex path with $h=(2,3,4,4)$ at $q=16$ has image
$t^2(272+1904t+4913t^2)$. The residual quadratic has discriminant
$-1720128$. The refutation settles the indicated yes/no clause. The
classification of normalized maps in Question 6.7 is a separate question.
