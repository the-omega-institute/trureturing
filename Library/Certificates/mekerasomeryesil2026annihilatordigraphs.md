# Nilpotent adjacency in the annihilator digraph

## Verified locator

Rasie Mekera, Defne Somer and Didem Yeşil, *Annihilator Digraphs and Extended
Zero-Divisor Digraphs of Semigroups and Rings*, arXiv:2608.09243v2 (2026),
§7, p. 17. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.09243) ·
[Versioned question](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09243v2#S7.p2.1)

The vertex set is the set of nonzero left or right zero divisors. For
distinct vertices $x,y$, Definition 4.1 asks for either a left annihilator
of $xy$ lying outside both left endpoint annihilators, or a right annihilator
of $xy$ lying outside both right endpoint annihilators. The multiplication
order is part of the arc relation. Section 2 uses positive powers to define
nilpotence. [Definition 4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09243v2#S4.Thmtheorem1) ·
[Section 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09243v2#S2.p2.1)

A 2021 paper about noncommutative semirings uses a different graph rule.
Its abstract uses annihilator-set comparisons in both product orders.
The left annihilator of $E_{21}$ contains $E_{11}$, whereas the left
annihilator of $E_{12}E_{21}$ excludes it. This distinction matters for the
opposite matrix units. The bounded literature screen located no earlier
settlement under Definition 4.1. Worldwide priority and complete index
coverage remain unverified.
[2021 abstract](https://api.openalex.org/works/https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0042278) ·
[Search scope and limitations](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/35242)

## Matrix-rank question

In §7, p. 17, Mekera, Somer and Yeşil also ask “how the arcs of
\(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AG}}(M_n(F))\) can be characterized in terms of
matrix ranks.” This is the rank question in the paragraph beginning
“Future studies can characterize the semigroups and rings.” It uses
the ordered arcs of Definition 4.1.
