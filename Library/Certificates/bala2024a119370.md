# Bala's A119370 exponential identity

## Verified locator

The canonical OEIS entry is <https://oeis.org/A119370>.

The official OEIS data file, revision 37, is available at the immutable URL
<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oeis/oeisdata/65556c7cb2030c806e532786d4e1cb4a0fd57164/seq/A119/A119370.seq>.
Its 3,454 bytes have SHA-256 `a5c02107de6da03eef7f135e016434579b566e0686a2b8a51bdb35788c444c69`.

NAME states:

> G.f. satisfies A(x) = 1 + x*A(x)^2 + x^2*(A(x)^2 - A(x)).

FORMULA states:

> Conjecture: A(x) = 1 + x*exp(Sum_{n >= 1} g(n, x)*x^n/n), where g(n, x) = Sum_{k = 0..n} binomial(n, k)^2*(1 + x)^k. Cf. A105633 and A167638. - Peter Bala, Sep 10 2024

Another FORMULA gives the branch:

> A(x) = ((1+x^2) - sqrt( (1+x^2)^2 - 4*x*(1+x) ))/(2*x*(1+x)).

The first OFFSET component is zero. The second component is display metadata.
The independently specified source has constant coefficient one. All occurrences of x
in the exponential identity denote the same formal variable. The infinite sum starts
at n=1; its finite binomial row includes k=0 and k=n.

## Scope and prior suppliers

The mathematical setting is the ordinary formal power-series ring over the rationals.
The square root has constant coefficient one. Division by x means coefficient shifting
after proving divisibility; the remaining denominator 2(1+x) is a unit.
No analytic convergence or analytic branch selection is used.

Gessel's *Lagrange Inversion*, arXiv:1609.05988v1, equation (2.2.9), supplies the
classical logarithmic Lagrange coefficient formula. Section 3.4 supplies the Narayana
quadratic family. These are prior general methods; they are not evidence that the exact
A119370 source transformation was already published. Related Hanna formulas in A105633
and A216604 are prior assertions in different source families.

The qualified source and literature scope is stated in
<https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/30685>.
No authenticated exact whole-question prior proof was identified in that scope.
Worldwide priority and exhaustive literature coverage remain unverified.
