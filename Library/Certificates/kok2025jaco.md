# Integer sequences and linear Jaco graph parameters

Version 1 of the preprint was submitted on 22 July 2025. Section 2.3,
Conjecture 2.12, on printed page 9 proposes A000149 as the vertex-index
sequence of an exponential dominating set of the infinite linear Jaco graph.
OEIS A000149 is the sequence of natural floors of powers of e.

The arXiv source and version history and the current OEIS A000149 entry were
checked on 2026-09-12. Exact searches for the arXiv identifier, the conjecture
number with Jaco, a counterexample, and 2026 Jaco graph domination did not find
a proof or refutation. The paper's conclusion still leaves its conjectures for
future work. Kok's earlier *Research note: Domination of exact deg-centric Jaco
graphs* gives background constructions but does not settle this assertion.
Google Scholar and MathSciNet were not checked. These are bounded findings, not
an exhaustive literature claim, and no priority for the counterexample is
asserted.

This note attests the printed conjecture and the sequence identification, not
their truth. The formal result concerns Conjecture 2.12 only.
