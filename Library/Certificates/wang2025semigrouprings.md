## Source statement

Conjecture 5.3, TeX line 842, HTML anchor
[S5.Thmtheorem3](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.18724v1#S5.Thmtheorem3), states:

> Let S be a finite commutative semigroup, and let K be a field.
> Suppose that either (i) K is a splitting field of S, or (ii) Char(K)=p
> and exp(S) is a p-power. Then
> d(S,K)=max over a in S of {epsilon_a D_bar(a)(S/H)+d(Gamma(H_a),K)},
> where epsilon_a is 0 if the positive cyclic semigroup generated by a is
> a group and 1 otherwise.

Section 2, TeX lines 203–213, uses the ordinary semigroup algebra: all
finitely supported coefficient functions on S with convolution. An absorbing
semigroup element remains an independent basis vector. Definition 2.1
requires the product of `X^s_i-a_i X^e(s_i)` to be nonzero for **every** tuple
of individually nonzero scalars. The constant is a supremum of lengths.
The formal model takes the supremum of finite lengths in ENat; infinity
means unbounded finite lengths, with no infinite-product interpretation.

The positive cyclic semigroup contains positive multiples only. Its unique
idempotent is e(a); its period is the least positive eventual shift of the
positive multiples. The exponent is the LCM of periods, not of indices or
cyclic-semigroup sizes. Lines 245–246 define splitting by exactly exp(S)
solutions of `zeta^exp(S)=1` in K. Branch (i) imposes no algebraic-closure or
characteristic-zero condition.

Green H means equality of the principal ideals `{a} union {a+c:c in S}`.
The quotient is the congruence quotient with its induced operation.
Definition D, line 239, defines the relative Davenport constant at a by
irreducible sequences summing to a, with the empty proper subsequence
included and D_identity=0. It is not the global reducibility threshold.
The Schutzenberger carrier consists of actual translation maps on H_a
realized by stabilizing elements of S, identifying equal maps. No identity
is adjoined. The paper assigns d(empty,K)=0.

## Six-label mechanism

Take S={0,1} x {0,1,2}, with

$$(b,n)+(c,m)=(\max(b,c),\min(2,n+m)),$$

and K=Q. This is a finite commutative monoid, an admissible instance of the
source's semigroup quantifier. Every cyclic period is one and Q splits
exponent one. The identity is (0,0). The cyclic idempotent of (b,0) is
(b,0); that of (b,1) or (b,2) is (b,2).

For t=(0,1), the coefficient of X^t in `X^t-a X^(0,2)` is one for every
rational a. Conversely, choosing every coefficient to be one kills every
sequence of length at least two: idempotent labels give zero factors, and
for any two transient labels x,y the four products x+y, x+e(y), e(x)+y,
e(x)+e(y) coincide. Thus d(S,Q)=1, uniformly over all finite lengths.

Principal ideals distinguish all six elements, so the actual H quotient
retains their distinctions. At a=(1,1), the quotient sequence with terms
(0,1) and (1,0) sums to a, while its proper subsequences sum to (0,0),
(0,1), and (1,0). It is irreducible and its relative Davenport constant is
at least two. The positive cyclic semigroup generated by a is not a group,
so epsilon_a=1. Since the remaining summand is nonnegative, the conjectured
right side is at least two. Its exact maximum is unnecessary.

## Scope and provenance

The D5 claim quantifies over finite commutative semigroups, including those
without an identity, and over all fields satisfying either source branch.
Its counterexample is an ordinary rational semigroup algebra; no contracted
algebra or replacement for the relative constant is involved.
Positive products avoid a nonunital empty-product assumption. Adjoining
length zero to a supremum of natural lengths does not affect its value.
Empty sequence sums refer only to an identity already present in the
semigroup. The rational witness avoids zero-ring conventions.

The source and bounded later-literature screen supplied with issue
[23871](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/23871)
found no exact settlement in the inspected scope. This is not an exhaustive
absence or priority claim. The construction's novelty is not established.
Conjecture 3.26 is a separate question and is not settled here.
