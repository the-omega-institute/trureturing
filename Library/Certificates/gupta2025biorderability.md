## Formal source map and conventions

`PresentedQuandle` is the quotient of unrestricted finite expressions in eight
generators, action and inverse action by exactly equivalence, congruence, the
quandle axioms and all eight equations. Its Quandle instance and universal
evaluation/uniqueness proofs implement an algebraic presentation. The quotient
includes inverse-action terms; no positive-word shelf, finite quotient or full
conjugation group replaces the carrier. No separation or coloring map is an

## Mathematical mechanism and limits

In an arbitrary linearly ordered idempotent magma strictly increasing in each
variable, relations 3--8 force d=g. For d<g, monotonicity and its reflection give
`d<e<g`, `c<d`, `a<d`, `a<c`, `h<c`, and `e<h`, a strict cycle. The opposite
branch follows in the dual order. The exact finite coloring satisfies ALL eight
relations and extends to the whole carrier by the presentation universal property.
It separates d (color 0) and g (color 1). It is used only for separation, with no
injectivity or order on the target assumed. The closed contradiction concerns
all elements of the presented quandle, without a finiteness assumption.
