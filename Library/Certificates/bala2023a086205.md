# A086205 complex-shift determinant

The entry defines a(n) as the determinant of the n by n matrix with entries
binomial(i^2,j). Peter Bala's formula block dated February 19, 2023 asserts
that adding an arbitrary complex z to i^2 leaves this determinant unchanged.
Revision 35 retains this conjecture. The PARI definition uses matrix(n,n,i,j,...),
whose row and column indices are 1 through n. The sequence offset includes n=0.

For complex w and natural k, binomial(w,k) is the falling factorial divided
by k!, as in Mathlib's Ring.choose_eq_smul. At n=1,z=1 the shifted determinant
is 2 and the unshifted determinant is 1. The formal result concerns exactly
this printed universal assertion with one-based indices. It asserts no
corrected formula or global literature novelty.
