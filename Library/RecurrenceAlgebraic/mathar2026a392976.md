# A392976: inverse quintic and exact differential recurrence

## Published statement

The source offset is `0,3`. Zero is the sequence offset; three is not a
recurrence threshold. The source gives no explicit onset. The complete
valid-lag interpretation is every integer n at least five, including five.
All six signed terms and their lags zero through five are retained. There
is no negative-index extension or deleted exceptional index.

The coefficient carrier is the rational formal power series ring. The
zero-constant compositional inverse U is unique, both compositions equal X,
and X times A equals U; a(n) is the coefficient of degree n+1 in U.
D-finiteness means a nonzero homogeneous linear differential operator with
polynomial coefficients over Q annihilates A. No analytic branch, convergence
hypothesis or minimal-order assertion belongs to this interpretation.
