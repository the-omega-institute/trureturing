## Formal interpretation

The coefficient ring is the rationals. The positive ordinary series is
extended by the normalization $a_0=0$. For every $n\ge1$ the equation is
$[x^n]\exp(n^2 A)=n^2[x^{n-1}]\exp(n^2 A)$.
No coefficient integrality is a premise of rational existence or uniqueness.

For $B=\exp(A)$, the normalization is $B(0)=1$, and the companion equation is
$[x^n]B^{n^2}=n^2[x^{n-1}]B^{n^2}$ for every $n\ge1$.
Its exponential-generating-function entries are the rational numbers
$b_r=r![x^r]B$, for $r\ge0$; this is a change of coefficient convention,
not an assertion that the ordinary coefficients of either series are integers.
The quotient identification is $q_n=a_n/n$ for $n\ge1$ only.

## Formal boundary

The rational-model module proves unbounded normalized existence and uniqueness,
the exponential power identity, the logarithmic inverse, and uniqueness of the
normalized companion. At each prime dividing a positive index, it also proves
local integrality of the coefficient divided by its index from the actual
strict local prefix. The proof uses the finite exponential coefficient formula,
the bound $v_p(j!)<j$, and the source equation, with zero coefficients handled
separately.

If a first noninteger coefficient occurs at index $n$, the module proves
$n\ge2$ and that its reduced denominator has a prime divisor $p<n$ with
$p\nmid n$. It also proves that ordinary integrality of all coefficients implies
integer $n$-divisibility at every $n\ge1$. This implication has an explicit
integrality hypothesis and does not settle either conjecture unconditionally.

The source induction uses local integrality at primes $p<n$ coprime to $n$
for the actual integral strict prefix, supplied by the signed assembly argument. The fixed-assembly module
proves the exact signed-weight factorization into fixed and moved blocks for
arbitrary finite vertex and color types and every permutation. Its vertex
support and ambient marker set are independent, and only the fixed blocks
restrict their markers to fixed vertices. It also proves the exact signed
recurrence obtained by removing the unique block at an arbitrary chosen
vertex. The remaining support loses that block and retains the original
marker set. Both results allow arbitrary integer color weights and impose
no positivity assumption. The assembly-coefficient module proves the exact
identity that the signed assembly sum is $|S|!$ times the ordinary coefficient
of degree $|S|$ in $\exp(|T|^2 P)$. Here the positive coefficients of $P$ are
the signed color sums and its constant coefficient is zero. It also constructs
an actual finite palette with signs $+1$ and $-1$ for every arbitrary integer
strict prefix, and proves the same coefficient identity for that palette in
all degrees. Both marker choices still range over the original ambient set.
The coefficient module defines the actual permutation actions on full and
pointed assemblies, proves their weight invariance and exact fixedBy
correspondences, and proves signed Burnside by invariant positive and negative
weight fibers. It proves the fixed-point subtraction identity,
the Sylow divisor, complete factorial cancellation, and coprime-prime residual
local integrality for every integer strict prefix satisfying its lower equations.
The rational-model theorem `source_integral_and_divisible` transfers those lower
equations from the actual source and completes strong induction: every ordinary
coefficient is integral, and every positive coefficient is its index times an integer.
Rational existence, uniqueness and the normalized companion correspondence remain
the preceding independent source bridges. Escape-audit source reconstruction and
independent form/admission review are separate from these kernel proof terms.
No signed-counting assertion is used as an axiom or assumed premise.
No literature priority or exhaustive search claim is made.
