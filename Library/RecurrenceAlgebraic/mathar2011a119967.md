## Mathematical scope

The source offset is `0,3`; it does not specify a recurrence onset. The full
valid-lag reading uses every natural n≥4. Allowing any later onset means
there exists N≥4 such that the same expression vanishes for every n≥N.
The formal result excludes both readings by proving a positive residual
for every n≥4. Coefficients and residuals are integers, while sequence
indices and the finite sum are natural numbers.

The finite sum uses C(n,2k). The related A113409 formula uses C(n-k,k),
so its recurrence does not supply this result. Ordinary monotonicity of
binomial coefficients is provided by pinned Mathlib. No new binomial
identity or recurrence-generation method is asserted.
