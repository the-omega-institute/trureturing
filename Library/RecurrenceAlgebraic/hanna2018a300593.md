## Source statements

```text
%N A300593 O.g.f. A(x) satisfies [x^n] exp( n^2 * A(x) ) = n^3 * [x^(n-1)] exp( n^2 * A(x) ) for n >= 1.
%C A300593 It is conjectured that this sequence consists entirely of integers.
%F A300593 O.g.f. equals the logarithm of the e.g.f. of A300592.
%N A300592 E.g.f. A(x) satisfies: [x^n] A(x)^(n^2) = n^3 * [x^(n-1)] A(x)^(n^2) for n>=1.
%F A300592 E.g.f. A(x) satisfies: log(A(x)) = Sum_{n>=1} A300593(n)*x^n, a power series in x with integer coefficients.
```

Attribution to the OEIS Foundation and Paul D. Hanna. [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

The displayed series and offsets specify ordinary degree one as the first
entry of A300593 and EGF degree zero as the first entry of A300592. The
normalizations are $F(0)=0$ and $B(0)=1$. The EGF entries are
$\beta_r=r![x^r]B$ for every $r\ge0$. The logarithm has ordinary coefficients
$[x^m]F$ at every degree. The two coefficient equations are interpreted in
$\mathbb Q[[x]]$.

The bounded literature qualification reported no exact prior result in
the inspected repository and paper versions. [Preregistration #32030](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/32030)
