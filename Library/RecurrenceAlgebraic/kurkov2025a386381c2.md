# Ordinary Euler divisor propagation

A386381 revision 27 states Conjecture 2 for ordinary E=A000111, rather
than the modified Entringer diagonal used in Conjecture 1:

> if d > 2 is a divisor of E(k), then d is also a divisor of E(m*phi(d)+k) where m is any natural number. In particular, E(k) is a divisor of E(m*phi(E(k))+k) for any k >= 0 (with single exception at k = 3) where m is any natural number.

All parameters are natural numbers starting at zero. The two orders of
addition in the displayed indices agree. The restriction d>2 belongs to
the first assertion; only the particular assertion excludes k=3.

A000111 revision 803 identifies the exponential generating function as
sec(x)+tan(x). Its recurrence A''=A*A', with E(0)=E(1)=1, gives

E(n+2) = sum over i=0,...,n of binomial(n,i) E(i) E(n+1-i).

This positive integer recurrence is the Lean carrier's definition.
The differential equation and the integral signed-branch identities are
proved, rather than assumed as congruence premises. The even subsequence
is identified with the existing A000364 secant carrier by its existing
recurrence uniqueness theorem. The recurrence, signed correspondence and
secant identification are in `D5/S1/Recurrence/Periodic/EulerTwoPrimary.lean`
and its owning Blueprint, alongside the tangent valuation and secant parity
inductions. Riccati uniqueness directly uses the frozen normalized contraction
and power-agreement results with an arbitrary common constant coefficient. No weighted, signed-only or eventually
periodic sequence is substituted for E.

Paul Thomas Young, *Kummer congruences for values of Bernoulli and Euler
polynomials*, Acta Arithmetica 99 (2001), 277–288,
DOI 10.4064/aa99-3-5, Theorem 3.3 and equations (3.9)–(3.13), is a prior
ordinary supplier of the corrected odd-prime-power congruence. With
u=-1 and x=b/2, b=0 or 1, its correction is 1-epsilon*p^j.
The implementation reconstructs this component by a finite geometric
identity with integral derivatives; it does not import an unverified
p-adic theorem or claim a new Kummer congruence.

For odd Q=p^r, the finite identity for
F_b(t)=2 exp(bt)/(1+exp(2t)) deletes exactly the frequencies divisible by p.
Their removal contributes epsilon*p^j*A_b(j), where
epsilon=(-1)^(b*(p-1)/2). The remaining frequencies are units modulo Q.
The factor 1-epsilon*p^j is a unit for every j>0, including j<r.
Euler's theorem therefore transfers zero divisibility without a
starting-index threshold. This is a zero implication, not an assertion
that all uncorrected residues have period phi(Q).

The two-primary argument uses the elementary tangent bound
2^s|E(2s+1), even-index secant oddness and phi(2^a)|phi(d).
Prime-power assembly retains all composite moduli. The values
E(0),...,E(4)=1,1,1,2,5 handle the source boundaries; positivity and
monotonicity give E(k)>2 for k>=4.
