## Source statement

> G.f.: exp( Sum_{n>=1} [ Sum_{k>=0} C(n+k-1,k)^n *x^k ] *x^n/n ).
>
> Conjecture: this sequence consists entirely of integers.

## Formal interpretation

All series are ordinary formal power series over the rationals. The logarithm
has coefficient

\[
[x^m]L=\sum_{\substack{1\le n\le m\\0\le k\le m\\n+k=m}}
\frac{\binom{n+k-1}{k}^{n}}{n}.
\]

The inner bound depends only on the coefficient being taken; it places no
global restriction on the source index k. In particular it does not impose
k<=n. The formal exponential is PowerSeries.subst L (PowerSeries.exp Q),
has constant coefficient one, and the assertion is
forall m:N, exists z:Z, coeff m A=(z:Q). No factorial multiplies the coefficient.

## Mathematical correspondence

Set F(0,k)=0 and F(n,k)=binom(n+k-1,k)^n for positive n. The generic Dold
exponential criterion is applied with this array. The original owners supply
negbinomDiv, negbinomCong and power_lift. The exponent difference is controlled
by applying power_lift to U=b^p and V=b, using the all-residues Fermat identity
in ZMod p. Thus the dilated exponent is p*n. The coefficient bridge uses
literalUnbounded, including degree zero.
