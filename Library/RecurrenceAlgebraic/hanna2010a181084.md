## Formal interpretation

Over $\mathbb Q[[x]]$, put
$H(x)=\sum_{n\ge1}\sum_{k=0}^n\binom nk^{n+k+1}x^{n+k}/n$
and $A(x)=\exp(H(x))$. The assertion is
$\forall m\in\mathbb N,\ \exists z\in\mathbb Z,\ [x^m]A(x)=z$.
Both endpoints of the inner sum and degree zero are included. The coefficients
are ordinary coefficients; no factorial scaling or analytic convergence is
assumed. The second offset component does not restrict the degree quantifier.
