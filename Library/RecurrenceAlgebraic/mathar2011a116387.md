Its mathematical statement is:

> Expansion of 1/(sqrt(1-2*x-3*x^2)*(2-M(x))), where M(x) is the g.f. of the Motzkin numbers A001006.
>
> Conjecture: n*(17*n-142)*a(n) + (17*n^2 + 95*n + 138)*a(n-1) + (-391*n^2 + 2488*n - 2908)*a(n-2) + (-17*n^2 - 603*n + 1892)*a(n-3) + 2*(697*n-2021)*(n-4)*a(n-4) + 60*(17*n-47)*(n-4)*a(n-5) = 0. - _R. J. Mathar_, Nov 15 2011

OEIS A001006, revision 1176, supplies both the native quadratic
$M=1+xM+x^2M^2$ and the formula $M=c(x^2/(1-x)^2)/(1-x)$, with $c$
the A000108 Catalan generating function. These identify the constructed
series without assuming a recurrence for A116387. The square-root branch
has constant coefficient one. OFFSET `0,2` indexes the first coefficient
by zero; the source prints no recurrence onset. Every $n\ge5$ is included.

## Prior scope

The joeis implementation at `38f426e5a405344bbff33cd3b5d480ea41269b1b`
computes the binomial transform of A116383. That computation is prior
work, distinct from the exact six-term certificate. Ekhad, Yang and
Zeilberger's 2017 Mathar recurrence paper and companion output, and
Mathar's *P-finite Recurrences From Generating Functions with Roots of
Polynomials* (arXiv:2109.02112v1, Section 4), provide classical
algebraic-to-differential methodology. No novelty is claimed for that
methodology. The bounded comparison in issue #30173 located no complete
answer to this exact event; it does not establish global priority or
universal absence of earlier proofs.
