## Verified locator

[OEIS A186335](https://oeis.org/A186335), NAME “A transform of the central
binomial coefficients”, FORMULA, revision 11, gives these two lines:

```text
a(n)=sum{k=0..n, sum{j=0..n, binomial(k-j,n-k-j)*binomial(k,j)*A000984(n-k-j)}}.
Conjecture: n*a(n) +(-2*n+1)*a(n-1) +5*(-n+1)*a(n-2) +3*(2*n-3)*a(n-3) +5*(n-2)*a(n-4)=0. - _R. J. Mathar_, Feb 13 2015
```

[Source qualification #30472](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/30472)
attests the verbatim current FORMULA and NAME against OEIS JSON and the internal
entry. The offset is 0,3. The source omits the onset and negative-index convention.
The formal interpretation sums over natural pairs j ≤ k, k+j ≤ n, and
m=n-k-j ≤ k-j. Thus m ≥ 0, k ≤ n, and each binomial has ordinary nonnegative
arguments. Excluded terms are zero and A000984 is not evaluated at a negative
index. The recurrence holds in the integers for every natural n ≥ 4, equivalently
every integer n ≥ 4 with the nonnegative indices identified. This disclosed
support/onset interpretation is part of the assertion, not extra quoted source text.
The sequence is not defined by its recurrence.

## Formal bridge

The retained proof reindexes legal pairs by m=n-k-j and j, with inverse
k=n-m-j, retaining precisely 2m+2j ≤ n. Binomial multiplication identifies
the weights with the coefficients of B^(m+1), where B=(1-X-X²)^(-1).
For the central-binomial series C, the independently defined source series is
A=B C(X² B). Formal substitution has zero constant coefficient. The central
identity (1-4X)C'=2C, the inverse derivative and the chain rule give
P A'+Q A=0, with
P=1-2X-5X²+6X³+5X⁴ and Q=-1-5X+9X²+10X³.
Coefficient extraction using the frozen supplier
`D5/S1/Recurrence/A308815DFiniteRecurrence.euler_coeff` gives all five terms.
There is no assumed generating-function identity, finite-prefix surrogate,
recurrence-defined replacement or additional hypothesis.
