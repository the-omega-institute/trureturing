# Kurkov's A396981 positive-column sum

The source is the FORMULA line of A396981, Conjecture 1, after
“we have”. The prefatory Stirling identity and Conjectures 2–4 are outside
this assertion. Mikhail Kurkov authored the entry and conjecture.
Quoted OEIS material is attributed to Mikhail Kurkov and the OEIS Foundation
under CC-BY-SA 4.0.

> A(n, k+f(k)+1) = Sum_{j=0..n} A(j, k) * (n + f(k))! / (f(k) + j)! where f(n) = A002024(n).

The NAME supplies the independent defining equations:

> Array read by ascending antidiagonals: A(n, k) = A(n, k-A002024(k)) + (n+A002024(k)-1) * A(n-1, k) with A(n, 0) = 0^n, A(0, k) = 1.

The positive-index sequence A002024 repeats each positive integer s exactly
s times. Thus f(q) is the unique positive s satisfying
s(s−1)/2 < q ≤ s(s+1)/2. Its source has onset one.

The formal scope includes existence of a natural-valued array and the sum
for every lawful array, every n ≥ 0 and every k ≥ 1. The sum includes both
endpoints. Factorials are cast from natural numbers to the rationals before
division, and their denominators are nonzero. No assertion at k=0 or source
convention for f(0) is required.
