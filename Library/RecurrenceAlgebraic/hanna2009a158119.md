A155585's original OGF is
`F(X)=sum_(n>=0) n! X^n product_(k=1..n) (1+2kX)^(-1)`.
Each inverse is a formal unit inverse over the integers. Its separately
labelled EGF `exp(X)/cosh(X)` is not interchangeable with this OGF.
A157308 specifies `X/serreverse(X F(X))`. Write the reversion as `Q`, and
the normalized quotient as `B`, with `B*Q=X` and constant coefficient one.
A158119 explicitly takes the unsigned even bisection:
`a(m)=abs([X^(2m)] B)`. Hanna's unrestricted iff is the quoted claim above.
A157310 supplies an alias, not a distinct open problem.
