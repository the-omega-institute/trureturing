## Verified locator

Equation (1.1) on page 1 gives the denominator of the $[k/m]$ approximant
as the polynomial with coefficient $(-1)^j\binom mj(k+m-j)!/(k+m)!$
for $0\le j\le m$. The diagonal specialization takes $k=m$.
[Scardino, arXiv v1, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39051v1#page=1)

Equation (1.3) on page 2 defines $c_n(k,m)$ by Taylor expansion of the
reciprocal about zero, with $q_{k,m}(0)=1$.
[Scardino, arXiv v1, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39051v1#page=2)

Section 10 states that the diagonal case remains open. Its quantifiers
cover every odd positive $m$ and every nonnegative $n$. The quadratic
window theorem concerns sufficiently large odd $m$ and
$1/4\le n/(m+1/2)^2\le 1/2$.
[Scardino, arXiv v1, §10, p. 18](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39051v1#page=18)
