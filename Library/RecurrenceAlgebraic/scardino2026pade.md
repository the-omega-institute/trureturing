## Verified locator

Equation (1.1) on page 1 gives the denominator of the $[k/m]$ approximant
as the polynomial with coefficient $(-1)^j\binom mj(k+m-j)!/(k+m)!$
for $0\le j\le m$. The diagonal specialization takes $k=m$. Equation (1.3)
on page 2 defines $c_n(k,m)$ by Taylor expansion of the reciprocal about zero.
