## Verified source locator

[OEIS A176605](https://oeis.org/A176605), [internal entry](https://oeis.org/A176605/internal),
revision 19, last edited 2025-11-09, has NAME:

> Sequence defined by the recurrence formula a(n+1)=sum(a(p)*a(n-p)+k,p=0..n)+l for n>=1, with here a(0)=1, a(1)=1, k=0 and l=1.

FORMULA contains:

> Conjecture: (n+1)*a(n) +2*(-3*n+1)*a(n-1) +(9*n-13)*a(n-2) +8*(-n+3)*a(n-3) +4*(n-4)*a(n-4)=0. - R. J. Mathar, Feb 29 2016

The source question and full quantifiers are preregistered in
[issue #30568](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/30568#issuecomment-6884652919).
The source omits the linear recurrence onset. The formal interpretation is
all natural n ≥ 4, so every backshift is nonnegative. This is independent
of OFFSET 0,3. The nonlinear equation applies only for m ≥ 1: imposing it
at m=0 would contradict a(1)=1. The sequence values are natural; the
five-term relation casts the index and all sequence values into the integers
before signed arithmetic. Both convolution endpoints are included and +1
is outside the sum.

## Source-to-proof correspondence

For A=Σ a(n)X^n in Q[[X]], the original initial values and convolution give

$$
F(A)=X(1-X)A^2-(1-X)A+1-X+X^2=0.
$$

This is proved coefficient by coefficient, including the exceptional first
step. Differentiation and polynomial elimination give

$$
P(XA')+QA=R,
$$

where

$$
P=1-6X+9X^2-8X^3+4X^4,\quad
Q=1-4X+5X^2,\quad R=1-2X+4X^2-2X^3.
$$

With D=XF(A)' and K=1-4X²+8X³, L=1-5X+4X²-4X³,
the certificate is

$$
(2XA-1)\bigl(P(XA')+QA-R\bigr)=K F(A)+L D.
$$

The factor 2XA-1 has constant coefficient -1 and is nonzero in the
power-series domain. The frozen coefficient identity
`D5/S1/Recurrence/A308815DFiniteRecurrence.euler_coeff` and four shifts
produce exactly Mathar's coefficients. R has degree at most three, so
all n ≥ 4 are covered. No convergence assumption is used. The entry's
square-root generating functions and Kruchinin finite-sum formula are
source assertions, not premises of this proof.

The actual natural-valued source is constructed by well-founded recursion.
Its complete source conditions are checked in the Reg mirror and used
in the full-family variation proof. This also establishes that the
universal source domain is nonempty.
