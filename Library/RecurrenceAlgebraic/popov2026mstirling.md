# Popov's urn model and shift identity

## Verified locator

- Versioned abstract and DOI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12011v1 · https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.12011
- Theorem 5.1 specifies chronological placements, the two urn rules and indistinguishable urns and slots. [PDF p. 13](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12011v1#page=13)
- Definition 7.1 and recurrence (7.1) give the initial row, the negative-column convention and the recurrence. [PDF p. 21](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12011v1#page=21)
- Theorem 7.3 gives (7.2) by induction and Pascal's identity. [PDF p. 22](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12011v1#page=22)
- Theorem 7.13 generalizes Theorem 5.1 to capacity $m$. [PDF p. 26](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12011v1#page=26)
- Problem 9.1 asks for the direct bijection. [PDF p. 29](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12011v1#page=29)

## Mathematical attribution and license

Popov supplies the model, the recurrence, and the algebraic proof of the shift. The first-urn bijection uses the forced opening prefix to identify the distinguished urn in every orbit, leaving the selected tail positions and the original first-urn colors. The paper also gives the classical capacity-one identity and its block interpretation. [PDF pp. 3 and 29](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12011v1)
