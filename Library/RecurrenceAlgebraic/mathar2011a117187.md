# A117187 source definition and recurrence

Paul Barry's sequence has offset zero and generating function

`F(x)=(1+x)C(x²)/((1-x²C(x²))*sqrt(1-4x²))`,

where C is the standard Catalan generating function and the square root has
constant coefficient 1. The formal-series denominator has constant coefficient
1 and is a unit. R. J. Mathar prints the conjectural recurrence

`(n+4)(31n-12)a(n)+(37n²+24n-156)a(n-1)-4(31n²+81n+24)a(n-2)-4(37n+24)(n-1)a(n-3)=0`.

The source does not specify an onset. Every n≥3 is the complete domain on
which all three lags are nonnegative. The coefficients a(0),a(1),a(2),a(3)
are 1,1,4,4, but finite-prefix agreement does not establish the recurrence.

[A001791](https://oeis.org/A001791/internal), revision 320, September 9, 2026,

function and adjacent recurrence. Its index n=m+2 gives
`b(m)=A001791(m+1)=binomial(2m+2,m)`. The related
[A117184 triangle](https://oeis.org/A117184/internal), revision 5, is also
known. The adjacent identity used in this proof is not a new result.

Ekhad, Yang and Zeilberger,
[arXiv:1707.04654v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.04654v2), provides known
algebraic-to-recurrence methods. The bounded inspection covered its actual

not a new formula, adjacent identity or general method. Preregistration:
