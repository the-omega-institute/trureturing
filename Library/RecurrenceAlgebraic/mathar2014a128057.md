## Verified locator

Primary source: https://oeis.org/A128057/internal

OEIS A128057, revision 11, unnumbered NAME and FORMULA sections:

```text
Expansion of (1+x)/sqrt(1+4x^2).
Conjecture: n*(5*n-9)*a(n) +4*a(n-1) +4*(5*n-4)*(n-2)*a(n-2)=0. - _R. J. Mathar_, Dec 02 2014
```

The entry credits Paul Barry for the sequence and R. J. Mathar for the
conjecture. Its offset is `0,3`; the coefficient sequence begins
`1,1,-2,-2,6,6,-20,-20,70,70`. The square root has constant coefficient
one, and the quotient uses its unit inverse over the rational formal
power series. The conjecture line omits the onset. The interpretation
uses every natural `n ≥ 2`, on which all printed lags are nonnegative;
this is an interpretation, not a quoted author hypothesis. Polynomial
scalars use rational signed subtraction, and coefficient indices use
natural subtraction.

R. J. Mathar, *P-finite Recurrences From Generating Functions with Roots
of Polynomials*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.02112, Example 4, includes
A128057 and its generating function. The paper supplies a general
recurrence method. No explicit exact shorter-recurrence settlement was
identified in the bounded passage inspection. Global and implicit prior
settlement remain unmeasured; no global novelty or priority is claimed.
The distinct four-term recurrence in the OEIS entry is not the assertion
quoted here.
