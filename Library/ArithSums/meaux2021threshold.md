# Méaux 2021: fast algebraic immunity of threshold functions

Méaux's 2021 single-author paper is *On the Fast Algebraic Immunity of
Threshold Functions*. Its Lemma 2 title explicitly marks `([Méa19], Theorem
1)`, identifying the 2019 result as its source. The 2021 paper is distinct
from the Carlet--Méaux direct-sums paper.

**ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED:** the complete body of Lemma 2 and the exact coefficient
formula were not independently verified from the full 2021 article in this
run; only the bibliographic identity and the title-level attribution are used
as the provenance locator.

The local Lean theorem is a repository implementation of the cited reduction;
the subsequent adjacent-row identity and parity classification are repository
derivations.
