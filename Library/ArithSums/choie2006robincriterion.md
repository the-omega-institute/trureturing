# On Robin's criterion for the Riemann Hypothesis

The [author version v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0604314v2), abstract
and §1, printed p.1, states Robin's criterion as

$$
\mathrm{RH}\iff
\forall n\in\mathbb N,\quad n\ge5041\Longrightarrow
\sigma(n)<e^\gamma n\log\log n.
$$

Here $\gamma$ is the Euler–Mascheroni constant. The introduction also
writes the left side after division by $n$ as $\sum_{d\mid n}1/d$.
The authors attribute the equivalence to Robin, whose original paper is
their reference [9]. This note consumes the criterion as recalled in v2,
not a new proof of the criterion or of RH.

The finite divisor geometry of 5040 and any fixed prime support do not
discharge the quantifier over all larger integers. No numerical Robin
threshold, fifth-power reduction or global analytic error estimate is
consumed by this note.
