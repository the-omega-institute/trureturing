# OEIS A160627 and A160628

OEIS A160627 records the NAME (`%N`, verbatim):

> Numerator of Laguerre(n, 4).

Its COMMENT conjecture (`%C`, verbatim) is:

> Conjecture: all terms are odd. - _Robert Israel_, Mar 29 2018

OEIS A160628 records the NAME (`%N`, verbatim):

> Denominator of Laguerre(n, 4).

Its COMMENT conjecture (`%C`, verbatim) is:

> Conjecture: all terms are odd. - _Robert Israel_, Mar 29 2018

The sequence AUTHOR line is `_N. J. A. Sloane_, Nov 14 2009`; the year 2018
records the date of Robert Israel's two conjecture comments. Both conjectures
are proved by the one theorem `result`, whose two conjuncts establish numerator
and denominator parity. The definition `L` is the explicit binomial-sum
definition of the classical Laguerre polynomial at `x = 4`, rather than an
application of a Mathlib Laguerre polynomial API.

The first seven reduced numerator-denominator pairs are `(1,1)`, `(-3,1)`,
`(1,1)`, `(7,3)`, `(1,1)`, `(-13,15)`, and `(-83,45)`, agreeing with the two
OEIS sequences.
