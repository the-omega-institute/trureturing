# OEIS A208342

The entry defines a jointly generated polynomial pair and records the
coefficient triangle of the first polynomial. Its NAME and defining FORMULA
lines are reproduced in the claim above. The coefficient convention is
`T(n,k) = [x^(k-1)] u(n,x)`.

The final quoted FORMULA line attributes to Werner Schulte the conjectured
binomial-sum expression for every positive `k,n` with `k<=n`. The formal
module uses the coefficient recursion induced by the displayed polynomial
recursion and proves that expression for the stated range.
