# Ferreira's odd-power residue sum

The OEIS A399232 NAME is:

> a(n) = Sum_{k=1..4*n+2} (k^(4*n+1) mod (2*n+1)).

The first COMMENT is:

> a(n) appears to satisfy rad(2*n+1) = (2*n+1)^2 / ((2*n+1)^2 - a(n)).

The OFFSET is `1,1`. Thus the indices are all natural numbers `n >= 1`.
The remainder is the least nonnegative remainder, and `rad(m)` is the product
of the distinct prime divisors of `m`. The proposed quotient is rational;
its signed denominator must be strictly positive. The neighboring COMMENT
about numbers of the form `x*(x-1)` is outside this assertion.
