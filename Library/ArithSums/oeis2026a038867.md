# OEIS A038867

The sequence lists the difference between two cubes whose bases are five
apart. Klaus Purath added a comment on May 15, 2026 conjecturing that no term
is a perfect square, noting verification through one hundred thousand terms.
The caller found the statement still labelled Conjecture on September 9, 2026.

## The polynomial form already on the page

A formula added by Oliveira on September 12, 2025 expands the difference as
five times a quadratic. That expansion is the whole of what the page supplies;
it names the terms but says nothing about squares. What the conjecture needs
beyond it is one further step, and that step is short: the expansion is two
modulo three for every index, whereas a square is zero or one modulo three.
The two residue sets do not meet.

This is a different situation from an entry whose printed description already
determines the conjectured property. Here the printed formula is a polynomial
identity and the residue exclusion is not stated anywhere on the page.

## What the Lean module proves

`D5/S3/Factorization/CubeGapFiveNoSquare` states the conjecture for every
index and every candidate square. It also records the polynomial identity
separately, because subtraction of naturals truncates and the identity is what
shows the subtraction here does not.

All four public results depend only on propositional extensionality and
quotient soundness; no choice principle enters.

**ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED:** the identification of the printed sequence with the
module's definition is a human reading of the entry, not a machine proof that
the two denote the same terms.
