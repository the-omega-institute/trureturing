# OEIS A036236

The entry is the least inverse of A015910, the sequence `2^n mod n`:
it asks for the least positive index producing each residue, with zero
indicating that no such index exists. Max Alekseyev's comment proves the exclusion
of residue one using the least prime divisor and multiplicative order.
A formula credited to Farideh Firoozbakht gives the residue at `n = 3^k`
as `3^k - 1`. These are known ingredients of the A374911 proof, not results
whose originality is claimed by this implementation.
