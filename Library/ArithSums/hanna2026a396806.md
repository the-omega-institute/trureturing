# A396806: iterated-exponential parity

## Source and scope

Paul D. Hanna's entry A396806 (June 9, 2026) specifies the exponential generating
series satisfying `A(x) = x * exp(A^{[6]}(x))`, where `A^{[6]}` denotes the
6-fold compositional iterate of `A`, not the 6-th power. The offset is 1 and
the first terms are 1, 2, 39, 1804, 139625, 15563526, 2301954109.

The conjecture proved in this repository says that `a(n)` is odd exactly when
`n` is odd, for every `n >= 1`. It is obtained as the instance at iterate count
6 of a statement proved uniformly in that count, alongside the defining series
equation and uniqueness among rational formal series with zero constant
coefficient.

This entry carries further residue conjectures, on moduli 3 and 6, that this
repository does not prove.
