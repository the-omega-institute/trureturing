# Direct sums of monomials and threshold functions

Carlet and Meaux state the algebraic normal form of threshold functions as
Lemma 2 and attribute it to Meaux's 2019 Theorem 1. The formula reduces the
coefficient of each positive-degree squarefree monomial to the parity of one
binomial coefficient.

The source record uses the 2021 citation year. Crossref identifies the work by
DOI `10.1109/TIT.2021.3139804`; its metadata records DOI creation in 2021 and
journal publication in volume 68, issue 5, in 2022.

The local Lean theorem is an implementation of this literature result. The
adjacent support-count classification uses it as background and derives its
own row-difference and parity statements.
