# A391810

The entry defines an irregular triangle by the coefficients of nonpositive
powers of x in the formal expansion at zero of
`Sum_{k=-oo..oo} x^k * (1-x^k)^(n+k)`, for every positive integer n.
Formula (2) asks whether each full row sums to zero.
The row includes the constant coefficient and ends at `floor((n+1)/2)^2`.
Negative integer powers mean Laurent-field inverses; substituting x=1 into
the infinite expression is not part of this definition.

The official internal and JSON records identify revision 39, with the formula
attributed to Paul D. Hanna on December 21, 2025. The record was created on
December 28, 2025; its current revision is dated February 3, 2026.

## Verified locator

Formula (5) retains signed coefficients, includes k=0, and uses the same
inclusive upper bound `floor((n+1)/2)^2`. Its exact rational input to round
is `2^n/3`; nearest-integer ties go toward positive infinity. A001045 uses
its zero offset. The revision 39 official export used for this formula has
