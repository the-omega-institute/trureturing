# Werner's coefficient conjecture

OEIS A208509 revision 28 records the conjecture attributed to Knud Werner
in the FORMULA section. Clark Kimberling authored the sequence entry;
this note attributes the conjecture, rather than the entry, to Werner.
The jointly defined first family is A208508 revision 10.

The source laws are u(1,X)=v(1,X)=1,
u(n,X)=u(n-1,X)+X*v(n-1,X), and
v(n,X)=u(n-1,X)+v(n-1,X)+1 for n>=2.
The formal statement uses polynomials over the integers, every n>=1,
and every degree k>=0, extending rows by zero beyond their degree.
