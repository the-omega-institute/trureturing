# Gaiser, restricted generalized Schur numbers

For k ≥ 2 the paper studies S_r(k; l), the least n such that every r-colouring of {1, …, n} has a monochromatic
solution of x_1 + ⋯ + x_k = x_{k+1} with exactly l + 1 distinct integers. Theorem 1.1 determines S_2(k; l) for fixed
l ≥ 2 and large k, in particular S_2(k; 2) = k^2 + 2k for k ≥ 3. Proposition 6.1 gives a block 3-colouring showing
S_3(k; 2) ≥ k^3 + 3k^2 + k − 1, and Open Question 6.2 asks whether S_3(k; 2) = k^3 + 3k^2 + k − 1 for all large
enough k.

The module `D5/S3/Combinatorics/RestrictedSchur/RestrictedSchur` answers Open Question 6.2 negatively.
