# OEIS A370380

The entry defines the array by `A(0,k)=1` and
`A(n,k)=(k+2)A(n-1,k+1)+Sum_{j=0..k}A(n-1,j)`. Kurkov's comment of
October 27, 2024 conjectures that its first column, shifted by two indices,
is A003319, the connected-permutation sequence.

The formal theorem treats the connected-permutation sequence abstractly. It
assumes its initial value and Bowen factorial convolution; it does not develop
the permutation interpretation. The entry fetched on September 12, 2026 still
labels the first-column identity as a conjecture. Searches by sequence number
found no Lean formalization or arXiv result.
