# Problem H-655, The Fibonacci Quarterly 45(2)

Slavko Simic's Problem H-655 asks:

> Let {c_i}_{i=1}^n be a finite sequence of distinct positive integers and q > 1 be a natural number. Prove that ⌊Σ c_i q^{c_i} / Σ q^{c_i}⌋ = c, where c = max{c_i : i = 1, …, n}. Is it true that ⌊(q−1) Σ c_i q^{c_i} / Σ q^{c_i}⌋ = c(q − 1) − 1?

The 2009 follow-up adds:

> No solution was received for the inequality proposed in part 2. The proposer claims that it follows in an analogous way as the proof of part 1 but the argument needs a closer examination.

## Verified locator

- URL: https://www.fq.math.ca/Problems/Aug2009advanced.pdf (2009 follow-up, text layer).
- Original locator: https://www.fq.math.ca/Problems/advanced45-2.pdf, p. 187 (the original PDF has no text layer).
- Locator: the H-655 problem statement and editorial note in the August 2009 advanced problems follow-up.

Part 2 is proved here for n ≥ 2. The printed statement omits n > 1 and fails at n = 1 exactly as the proposer noted for part 1. Part 1 is not formalized; no priority claim is made.
