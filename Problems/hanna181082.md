# Hanna's OEIS A181082 integrality conjecture

## Problem

Paul D. Hanna, [OEIS A181082](https://oeis.org/A181082), NAME and first COMMENTS line; [preregistration #30033](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/30033).

> Expansion of g.f.: exp( Sum_{n>=1} [Sum_{k=0..n} C(n,k)^(n+k) * x^k] * x^n/n ).
>
> Conjecture: this sequence consists entirely of integers.

Over $\mathbb Q[[x]]$, the source logarithm is
\[
L(x)=\sum_{n\ge1}\sum_{0\le k\le n}\binom nk^{n+k}\frac{x^{n+k}}n,
\qquad A(x)=\exp(L(x)).
\]
The assertion is that every ordinary coefficient of $A$ is an integer image in $\mathbb Q$, including degree zero. The exponent is $n+k$, the coefficient is not factorial-scaled, and no analytic convergence or positivity premise is added.

## Route

Set $F(0,k)=0$ and $F(n,k)=\binom nk^{n+k}$ for $n>0$. The frozen generic Dold-exponential criterion for a natural array applies once
\[
n\mid kF(n,k)
\quad\text{and}\quad
F(pn,pk)\equiv F(n,k)\pmod {p^{v_p(n)+1}}
\]
hold for $n>0$ and every prime $p$. The first divisibility follows from the frozen binomial divisibility result. For the second, binomial prime scaling reduces the claim to a power congruence. If $p\mid\binom nk$, both powers vanish modulo the required prime power; otherwise coprime cancellation gives $p^{v_p(n)}\mid k$, and Euler's theorem supplies the extra exponent. The finite diagonal identity identifies the source logarithm with the generic criterion's logarithm, so the frozen integrality theorem yields the ordinary coefficient statement.

## Falsifier
