# Hardin A229589

## Source

[OEIS A229589](https://oeis.org/A229589), NAME and first FORMULA line;
registered in [#30838](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/30838).

> Number of defective 3-colorings of a 4 X n 0..2 array connected horizontally and antidiagonally with exactly one mistake, and colors introduced in row-major 0..2 order.

> Empirical: a(n) = 18*a(n-1) - 111*a(n-2) + 282*a(n-3) - 333*a(n-4) + 180*a(n-5) - 36*a(n-6) for n > 12.

## Interpretation

Let a(n) filter all arrays Fin 4 × Fin n → Fin 3 by exactly 1
monochromatic registered bonds and the original row-major canonical
condition. Counts are cast to integers for the recurrence.

## Gap

The source labels its recurrence empirical. The target quantifies over
every n at least 13. Finite agreement with a b-file is insufficient.
The registration's literature search found no proof in the OEIS links or
the cited Potts-strip literature; global completeness remains unverified.

## Route

The transfer retains a column and the residual budget. Global colour
permutations reduce these states to 28 classes. A finite kernel
certificate checks every transition, the terminal and initial weights,
and the forward-vector relation of degree 6 after shift 6.
`DefectTransfer.lumped_count` identifies the labelled count with the
transfer. Coordinate exchange preserves the set of labelled arrays;
`canonical_pattern_card` gives six labelled arrays per canonical array
once at least two colours occur. `DefectTransfer.forward_shift` propagates
the relation to arbitrary length and cancellation of six gives the result.
