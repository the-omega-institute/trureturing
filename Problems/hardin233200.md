# Hardin's signed recurrence for eight-colour width-six arrays

## Problem

Let $a(n)$ count literal $n\times 6$ arrays of colours $0,\ldots,7$ at
positive height $n$. The upper-left entry is zero. Each horizontal,
vertical or antidiagonal bond contained in the array has distinct
endpoints whose sum is different from seven. In row-major order, each
occurrence of $2,3,4,5,6$ requires a strictly earlier $1$, each occurrence
of $3,4,5$ requires a strictly earlier $2$, and each occurrence of $4$
requires a strictly earlier $3$. Colours may be unused. [Pinned source and licence](../Library/Automata/hardin2013a233200.md)

The OEIS entry has offset `1,1` and gives the signed recurrence

$$
a(n)=\sum_{j=1}^{22}c_j\,a(n-j)\qquad(n\ge 24).
$$

## Motivation

The recurrence computes the width-six counts from earlier values. The
same sequence is column six of the rectangular table A233202. [OEIS A233200](https://oeis.org/A233200)

## Gap

The OEIS formula is labelled empirical. The scoped source search found
the general proper-colouring transfer method. The counting bridge here
must also account for the zero anchor and the multiplicities created by
the strict first-use constraints. [Research source screen](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/34596)

## Route

Group the colours into the complementary pairs $\{0,7\}$, $\{1,6\}$,
$\{2,5\}$ and $\{3,4\}$. A legal bond joins different pairs. Scanning a
row tracks the three prior-colour flags and weights every pair pattern by
the exact number of admissible colour choices. The first cell contributes
one choice because its colour is fixed. The anchored distribution $u$
and the 58-state weighted transfer $T$ satisfy
$a(n)=u\cdot T^{n-1}\mathbf 1$ for every positive $n$.

The finite certificate verifies all transition rows and the continuation
vectors through $T^{23}\mathbf 1$. It proves
$T^{23}\mathbf 1=\sum_{j=1}^{22}c_jT^{23-j}\mathbf 1$.
Multiplication by $T^{n-24}$ and application of $u$ give the stated
identity. At height 24 the lagged counts run from height 23 down to 2.

## Falsifier

A literal legal-array count that violates the displayed signed identity
at any height $n\ge24$ would refute the recurrence.

## Evidence

`HardinA233200Recurrence.result` proves the exact signed identity using the
literal count and the checked transfer certificate. The scanner and
transfer bridges supply the counting interpretation at every positive
height. [Recurrence blueprint](../Blueprint/D5/S3/Combinatorics/TraceAvoidingArrays/ComplementaryPairs/HardinA233200Recurrence.md)
