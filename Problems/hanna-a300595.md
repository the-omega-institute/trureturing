# Hanna's OEIS A300595 ordinary integrality conjecture

## Problem

The conjecture appears in the NAME and COMMENTS fields of Paul D. Hanna’s
entry. [OEIS A300595](https://oeis.org/A300595)
[Preregistration #30292](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/30292)

The [source note](../Library/RecurrenceAlgebraic/hanna2018a300595.md) identifies
the immutable primary and companion entries and their licenses.

> It is conjectured that this sequence consists entirely of integers.

There exists exactly one $F\in\mathbb Q[[x]]$ with $[x^0]F=0$ such that
$[x^n]\exp(n^3F)=n^3[x^{n-1}]\exp(n^3F)$ for every integer $n\ge1$.
Every ordinary coefficient $[x^m]F$, for every integer $m\ge1$, is an integer.
Existence and uniqueness are over the rational series ring and precede
integrality. The constant coefficient is zero separately.

## Motivation

The question separates rational characterization from integer ordinary
coefficients of a logarithm. Integer coefficients of an exponential, or
integer EGF entries, would not suffice. The source names A300594 as its
companion; no additional theorem conditional on the conjecture is asserted.
