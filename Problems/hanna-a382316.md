## Route

Write $A=xF$, where $F(0)=1$. Clearing the Laurent denominator and cancelling $x^7$ gives

$$F(x)F(x^6)=xF(x^2)F(x^6)+F(x^2)^2F(x^3).$$

Put $G(x)=F(x)/F(x^2)$, using the unit with constant coefficient one. Unit cancellation and substitution composition give

$$F(x)=F(x^2)G(x),\qquad G(x)=x+F(x^2)G(x^3),\qquad F(0)=G(0)=1.$$

The private helper `coupled_of_normalized` proves this construction. The private helper `coupled_nonneg` proves simultaneous nonnegativity by strong induction, proving the coefficient of $G$ at each positive degree before the coefficient of $F$. The private helper `source_exists` applies the imported `PowerSeriesNormalizedContraction.exists_unique_fixed` to the normalized coefficient contraction and transports its fixed point through the algebraic equivalences. The proof also directly consumes coefficient-agreement operations from `UnitReversionSquareParity` and `AbsoluteReciprocalGeometricParity`.
