# An infinite twin-free theta family separating the Laplacian equalities

## Problem

Alfuraidan, Khan and Sauras-Altuzarra, *Metric dimension and maximum eigenvalue
multiplicity of graph Laplacians*, arXiv:2610.26875v1, Problem 30, p. 27, ask:

> Do there exist infinite families of connected twin-free graphs satisfying exactly one of the two equalities mm_L(G)=dim(G), mm_Q(G)=dim(G)? The computational census shows that finite twin-free examples occur in both directions.

The result answers this existence question affirmatively. Here $\dim(G)$ is
the minimum size of a resolving set for actual shortest-path distances,
$\operatorname{mm}_L$ and $\operatorname{mm}_Q$ maximize real eigenspace
dimension over every real eigenvalue, and twin-freeness excludes both true
and false twins. The signless Laplacian is $D(G)+A(G)$.

The concrete witness has $G_i=\Theta_{3,3,6(i+1)+4}$ and order
$6(i+1)+9$. The source permits either equality direction; the construction
uniformly supplies the Laplacian direction. No source clause is dropped.
The fixed finite carrier only labels the graph; pairwise nonisomorphism
expresses infinitude independently of labels.

## Route

The graph consists of three internally disjoint paths sharing their two
terminals, of lengths $3,3,r$. Exact distance formulas give a two-landmark
resolving set, and a lower-bound argument excludes a single landmark.
The neighborhood identities give the actual Laplacian and signless vertex
equations. Their solutions map injectively into the coordinate model.

Away from $t=1,-1$, the continuant collision exclusion bounds the coordinate
dimension by one. The singular terminal systems bound the Laplacian case
by two and the signless case by one. For $r=6k+4$, an injective linear map
from $\mathbb R^2$ supplies Laplacian eigenvectors at eigenvalue $1$.
Together these give $\operatorname{mm}_L=2$ and
$\operatorname{mm}_Q\le1$. Distinct orders exclude graph isomorphisms.
These are statements about actual eigenspaces, without a numerical
spectral surrogate.

## Falsifier

A failure of connectivity, twin exclusion, metric dimension two, the
Laplacian multiplicity equality, the signless upper bound, or pairwise
nonisomorphism in the specified all-natural family would refute the
formal construction. A finite census alone cannot establish its universal
quantifier.

### What the settlement shows

The decisive mechanism is that exchanging whole paths permits a
Laplacian eigenspace of dimension two without creating vertex twins,
while the signless terminal systems and nonsingular obstruction bound
every signless eigenspace dimension by one. The metric and twin statements
extend to every $r\ge10$; the spectral separation is proved for
$r=6k+4$, $k\ge1$. The argument settles the source's existence question
in the Laplacian equality direction. It supplies no reverse-direction
family, no complete theta classification, and no change to a separately
stated source theorem is claimed.
