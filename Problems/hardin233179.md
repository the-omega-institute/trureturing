# Hardin's recurrence for the six-row six-colour pair-exclusion ladder

## Problem

[OEIS A233179](https://oeis.org/A233179), by R. H. Hardin, has NAME

> Number of 6Xn 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally, diagonally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

and an empirical linear recurrence of order $24$ with integer coefficients
(the `%F` line, verbatim in
[issue #28655](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/28655)),
stated for $n>25$.

Each column has six cells, and consecutive columns are joined by the bonds
$(r,j)$–$(s,j+1)$ with $|r-s|\le1$: a six-row ladder with horizontal,
diagonal and antidiagonal bonds, and no bonds inside a column. A bond is
legal when its endpoints differ and do not sum to $5$.

## Route

1. **Canonical form.** The general first-occurrence canonical-form theorems
   of `HardinA233177Canonical` apply unchanged to six-row arrays: a legal
   colouring is a proper pair pattern with free side bits, and the
   row-major normalization selects the canonical relabelling of the pattern
   and fixes the side bit at each pair's first occurrence.
2. **Counting bridge.** For $n\ge2$ a proper six-row pattern uses two or
   three labels, and the two-label ones are classified by applying the
   frozen four-row classification
   `HardinA233177Ladder.two_label_classification` to rows $0$–$3$ and
   $2$–$5$. Hence
   $$48\,a(n)=64^n\bigl(L(n)+6\bigr)\qquad(n\ge2),$$
   with $L(n)$ the number of proper three-label pair patterns
   (`HardinA233179Ladder.counting_bridge`).
3. **Pattern transfer.** The $729$ column patterns fall into $122$
   relabelling orbits; the transfer restricted to the $51$ classes that
   carry the counts, with a finite vector certificate of order $24$ through
   `TransferMatrix.scalarRelationShift`, gives the order-$24$ relation for
   $L+6$ (`HardinA233179PairTransfer.recurrence`).
4. **Scaling.** Multiplying by $64^n$ multiplies the coefficient at lag $j$
   by $64^j$; this turns the relation for $L+6$ into exactly Hardin's
   coefficients. The base instance covers index $26$.

### What the settlement shows

Hardin's coefficient at lag $j$ is exactly $64^j$ times the coefficient of
the relation for $L+6$: the whole order-$24$ recurrence is a property of the
uncoloured pair-pattern transfer, rescaled by the $2^6$ free side bits per
column. The row-major normalization contributes only the factor $1/48$ and
the constant $6$ from the two-label patterns, exactly as for four rows. The
two-label classification at six rows needs no new argument: it is the
four-row classification on two overlapping windows of rows. The recurrence
starts only at index $26$ because the counting identity fails at width one.
