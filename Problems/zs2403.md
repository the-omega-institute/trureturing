# Zimmermann--Stoye Conjecture 1: codimension-one Euclidean Stiefel curvature

## Problem

Zimmermann and Stoye state in Conjecture 1 of arXiv:2403.01879v3 that for
`n ≥ 4` and `p = n − 1`, the Euclidean sectional curvature of every
orthonormal tangent 2-plane on the Stiefel manifold satisfies
`−1/2 ≤ K_e^St(X,Y) ≤ 1/2`, and that both bounds are sharp. Issue [#22071](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/22071)
preregistered this open problem, the block-coordinate conventions, and the
settlement target.

## Motivation

The frozen declaration
`D5/S3/Geometry/Riemannian/ZimmermannStoyeCodimensionOneCurvature.result`
proves the conjecture for every target dimension in the stated range. The

## Gap

The source gives the codimension-one curvature formula and conjectures the
sharp interval; it does not supply a proof for all `n ≥ 4`. The literature
screen recorded on issue #22071 does not establish an exhaustive search;
literature completeness is `ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED`.

## Route

The proof first diagonalizes the two-row Gram matrix by an orthogonal change of
tangent-plane basis and then separates the rows by a second orthogonal change.
The curvature expansion retains the coupled off-diagonal cross term. A real
skew commutator estimate, row-coupling estimate, and scalar two-square deficit
then give the lower and upper bounds. Explicit coordinate pairs attain `−1/2`
and `1/2` in every dimension `n ≥ 4`, so the bounds are sharp.

## Falsifier

A counterexample would be an `n ≥ 4` and an orthonormal tangent pair violating
one of the two inequalities. Failure of either endpoint assertion would be a
dimension in which no admissible pair attains the corresponding value. The
kernel-checked result excludes both kinds of counterexample within the formal
statement.
