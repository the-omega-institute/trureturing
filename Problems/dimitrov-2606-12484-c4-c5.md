# Dimitrov arXiv:2606.12484v1 Conjectures 4 and 5

## Problem

S. I. Dimitrov's arXiv:2606.12484v1, §2 (PDF p.4), asks whether the reciprocal-product inequalities called Conjectures 4 and 5 hold for every integer $n\ge2$ and $k\ge1$. The source definitions are Euler's totient $\varphi$, Dedekind's $\psi(n)=\prod_{p^e\parallel n}p^{e-1}(p+1)$, and the positive-divisor sum $\sigma$; all displayed powers are pointwise powers of function values. The exact formulas are recorded in `Library/ArithUnits/dimitrov2026c4c5.md` and the two preregistration issues #30196 and #30197.

## Motivation

The two conjectures compare the same three native multiplicative arithmetic functions through reciprocal products. They are full universal statements over the source's unrestricted integer and exponent range.

## Gap

The source leaves both displayed lower bounds as conjectures. The bounded prior screen found no exact C4 or C5 settlement in the repository or in the fixed prior Dimitrov formalization repository.

## Route

The proof orders the native values as $0<\varphi(n)\le n-1<n+1\le\psi(n)\le\sigma(n)$. An ordered ratio lemma bounds $(X+Y+Z)(X^{-1}+Y^{-1}+Z^{-1})$ once $Y/X$ is bounded below. A mixed-power lemma orders the three C4 terms and supplies the exact ratio lower bound. C5 applies the same lemma to the pointwise powers and performs the source's exact rational normalization.

## Falsifier

A source-scope counterexample would require an exact integer pair $n\ge2$, $k\ge1$ that violates the corresponding displayed inequality. No such counterexample is used by the settlement.

## Evidence

The complete claims and proofs are kernel checked in `D5/S3/Arith/Divisors/DimitrovLowerBoundsC4C5`. The module imports the existing native `Nat.totient`, `ArithmeticFunction.sigma 1`, and the local Dedekind `psi`; its private helpers are consumed on the live paths to `result4` and `result5`.

## Triage

### What the settlement shows

**Proved in this module:** Dimitrov Conjecture 4 holds for every integer $n\ge2$ and $k\ge1$, with every source term and denominator preserved. Dimitrov Conjecture 5 holds over the same complete range, including all pointwise powers and the factor $(n^2-1)^k$.

**Proved mechanism:** the native arithmetic ordering reduces both events to the ordered reciprocal-product bound; the C4 ratio is $n(n+1)^{k-1}/(n-1)^k$, and C5 reduces to the rational expression $(q^2+7q+10)/(q+1)$ with $q=((n+1)/(n-1))^k$.

**Boundary:** the result addresses the two exact conjectures in arXiv:2606.12484v1. It does not assert a result for other conjectures in Part III or worldwide literature priority.
