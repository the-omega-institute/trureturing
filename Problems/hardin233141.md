# Barker's generating function for OEIS A233141

## Problem

R. H. Hardin's OEIS A233141, revision 8, gives the array count as follows. [Pinned OEIS record](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oeis/oeisdata/976b0c73e00453b1c1e6f16d83108cb9bb744c3b/seq/A233/A233141.seq)

> Number of n X 2 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to value 5-x(i,j) horizontally, antidiagonally or vertically, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order.

Colin Barker's formula asks whether the following identity holds for the entire counting series.

> Empirical g.f.: `x*(2 - 34*x + 93*x^2 - 22*x^3 + x^4) / ((1 - x)*(1 - 18*x + x^2)*(1 - 6*x + x^2))`.

For each positive $n$, the coefficient of $X^n$ counts the functions from
$\operatorname{Fin}(n)\times\operatorname{Fin}(2)$ to $\operatorname{Fin}(6)$
satisfying the published conditions. The horizontal bonds join the two cells
in each row. Consecutive rows have two vertical bonds and the antidiagonal
bond from $(i,1)$ to $(i+1,0)$. A bond forbids endpoint labels summing to five
and permits equal labels. The cell $(0,0)$ has label zero. Each occurrence
of $2$, $3$ or $4$ requires a strictly earlier $1$, and each $3$ also
requires a strictly earlier $2$, using the row-major rank $2i+j$.
Unused colours are allowed. The series over $\mathbb{Q}$ has constant
coefficient zero, and division is formal inversion.

## Motivation

The width-two instance of `HardinA233251TriangleStrip.width_counting_bridge`
connects the literal count to the continuation transfer for the published
bond and first-use rules.

## Gap

The source labels Barker's formula empirical. The cited literature screen
found related proofs for different bonds or colour rules and found no proof
of this exact formula. [Issue #34553](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/34553)

## Route

Two first-use flags and the previous row give $2^2\cdot6^2=144$ states.
The exact continuation vectors satisfy a degree-five relation after one
transfer step. The first six positive counts supply the scalar recurrence
at degree six, and the shifted vector relation gives it at every higher
degree. Coefficient comparison then gives the numerator in every degree
after multiplication by the denominator. The denominator has constant
coefficient one, so it is invertible over $\mathbb{Q}[[X]]$.

## Falsifier

A positive height with a literal array count different from the corresponding
coefficient of Barker's series would falsify the identity.

## Evidence

The Lean theorem `HardinA233141.result` equates the series of literal
filtered counts with Barker's expression in `PowerSeries ℚ`. The source
offset is `1,1`. The anchor condition requires a first cell, yielding the
zero constant coefficient. [Issue #34553](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/34553)
