# A233178: the literal five-row recurrence

## Problem

The exact source count is of functions on `Fin 5 × Fin n` into `Fin 6`, with
entry `(0,0)` zero. Across consecutive columns, entries at the same row or
at adjacent rows must be unequal and have sum different from five. There are
no vertical or wraparound conditions. In increasing rank `i*n+j`, every
2, 3 or 4 has an earlier 1, and every 3 has an earlier 2. Colours may be absent.
All recurrence arithmetic is in the integers.

## Route

The proof connects the literal count to the following transfer recurrence.

Let G(n) have the literal vertices `(i,j)` and the edges just specified, and
let P(n) count all proper three-label colourings of G(n), with labels 0,1,2.
For n at least two, G(n) is connected and bipartite. Column parity is a
bipartition. Every row is connected by its horizontal edges; a diagonal edge
between columns zero and one connects each pair of consecutive rows.
Consequently G(n) has exactly two proper colourings with any prescribed pair
of distinct labels, and no proper colouring using a single label.

Map the six colours to the complementary pairs `{0,5}`, `{1,4}`, `{2,3}`.
The two source restrictions on an edge hold if and only if its pair labels
are different. Each proper pair-label colouring has independent choices of
one of the two colours in its pair at every vertex, subject only to the
first-occurrence conditions and the pinned first cell.

The action of the symmetric group on the three pair labels is free on these
proper colourings: at least two labels occur, and a permutation fixing those
two labels is the identity. Each orbit therefore has six members. There is
exactly one orbit using two labels, since each of the three pairs of labels
has exactly two proper colourings. The other `P(n)/6 - 1` orbits use all
three labels.

Each orbit has a unique representative whose labels occur for the first
time in the order 0,1,2 in the literal row-major traversal. In a normalized
six-colour lift, the first occurrence of pair zero is the initial cell and
must be colour zero. The first occurrence of the second pair must be colour
one: colour four requires an earlier one, and colours two and three also
require an earlier one. The first occurrence of the third pair must be colour
two, since colour three requires an earlier two. Conversely, these choices
at first occurrences, followed by arbitrary choices at every later cell,
satisfy all the literal first-occurrence rules. No surjectivity is used.

Thus an orbit using k pair labels contributes exactly `2^(5*n-k)` normalized
six-colour arrays. It follows, for every n at least two, that

```
a(n) = 2^(5*n-2) + (P(n)/6 - 1)*2^(5*n-3)
     = 2^(5*n-4)*(P(n)/3 + 2).
```

The divisions here are exact rational/integer identities justified by the
free action; they are not truncated natural subtraction or an orbit divisor
imposed on the source definition. The argument constructs and counts exactly
the source representatives.

Encode each three-label column by its five base-three digits, with row r in
digit r. This is a bijection with the integers from zero to 242. Define the
243-by-243 integer matrix T by `T(p,q)=1` precisely when every digit of p
differs from the digits of q at distance at most one in the row index.
Appending a column is exactly a transition of T, so

```
v(k) = T^k * 1,
P(n) = sum_p v(n-1)(p)       (n >= 1).
```

The finite data in `HardinA233178Chromatic.lean` specify the complete legal
successor lists and the vectors v(0) through v(11). The certificate is the
componentwise identity

```
v(11) = 6*v(10) + 31*v(9) - 97*v(8) - 163*v(7)
      + 417*v(6) + 93*v(5) - 514*v(4) + 192*v(3)
      + 72*v(2) - 36*v(1).
```

Multiplication by any further power of T proves this identity at every
subsequent shift. Summing components yields the same recurrence for P(n)
for every n greater than eleven. The ten coefficients sum to one, so adding
the constant six to P(n) preserves that recurrence. Substituting the exact
formula for a(n), each lag i acquires the factor `32^i`. These give, in lag
order,

```
192, 31744, -3178496, -170917888, 13992198144,
99857989632, -17660905521152, 211106232532992,
2533274790395904, -40532396646334464.
```

Every lag argument in the claimed recurrence is at least two, so the
normalization identity applies. This proves the literal E on paper. The Lean
source-count bridge uses the division-free identity
`48*a(n) = 32^n*(P(n)+6)` for every n at least two. It derives the anchored
scanner sum by an exact row-major function bijection, then sums the pointwise
symmetrization. Each missing-label fibre is the unique alternating colouring,
and the double-missing fibre is empty. Relabelling the initial pair gives the
factor three between anchored and unrestricted counts. The base-three column
equivalence and the original generic continuation theorem identify P(n) with
the actual transfer readout at every positive width.

## Falsifier

A natural n greater than eleven at which the literal integer recurrence
fails refutes E. A failed compile, unavailable resource, finite agreement or
method failure supplies neither a proof nor a refutation.

## Evidence

The pointwise scanner symmetrization is independently useful source content.
Its result holds at every word length k: four times the sum of the scanner
weights of q and of q with labels one and two exchanged equals
`2^k * (1 + absent(q,1) + absent(q,2) + 5*absent(q,1)*absent(q,2))`.
The correction retains the empty and all-zero words. The one-seen-label
identity is proved by induction, using the existing `full_row` theorem for
the completed scanner; a second induction proves the symmetrization.
The three-label certificate is a separate mathematical auxiliary.
