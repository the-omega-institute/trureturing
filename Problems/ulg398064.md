# Ulgenes's truncated-binomial Hadamard limit

## Problem

OEIS A398064, revision 39, FORMULA:

> Conjecture: H(x+1) = lim_{n->oo} n^{x+1} * Sum_{k=0..n} (-1)^k/(n+k) * binomial(x, k) such that H(1/4) = 0.79564036... . Changing the upper bound of the sum to oo yields Euler's product representation of the Gamma function.

The binding interpretation in [#22706](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/22706) is over every $x\in\mathbb C$. Put

$$S_n(x)=\exp((x+1)\log n)\sum_{k=0}^n\frac{(-1)^k}{n+k}\binom{x}{k}.$$

The logarithm is real for positive $n$. The entire source function satisfies $H(m+1)=m!$ and, away from positive integers,

$$H(y)=\frac{\psi(1-y/2)-\psi((1-y)/2)}{2\Gamma(1-y)}.$$

The assertion includes existence of such an entire function and convergence $S_n(x)\to H(x+1)$ for every such function and every complex parameter. No existence assumption is substituted for this conclusion.

## Route

Euler's Gamma-sequence limit gives convergence of the scaled generalized-binomial coefficient. A weighted continuous Riemann sum identifies the truncated sum on $\Re x<-1$. The paired digamma series identifies the Nielsen-beta integral. A reciprocal-Gamma derivative construction supplies an entire interpolation, and an exact finite-sum shift propagates the left-half-plane limit to all complex parameters. The proof uses no omitted infinite-tail theorem.
