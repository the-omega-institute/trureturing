# Zaman’s four-lag recurrence for spiral queens

## Problem

OEIS A395348 asks whether $a(n+4)=a(n)+8n+34$ for every natural $n\ge 2$.
Its zero-based $a(n)$ is Black's $(n+1)$st label in alternating play. The
origin is label zero. Both colors require an empty cell, and each color
avoids the rows, columns and diagonals of all previous opposing queens.
Every move uses the least legal label. [OEIS A395348](https://oeis.org/A395348)

## Motivation

Determine the recurrence for the complete two-color process, including
existence and uniqueness of infinite least-label play.
[Preregistration](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/34447)

## Gap

The OEIS entry records a conjecture, program and finite values. The bounded
search in the preregistration found no proof of the exact statement.

## Route

The reflected Irwin walk supplies the source cells. The inverse shell rank
supplies the label order. The opening covers turns zero through thirteen.
Uniform diagonal coverage and the available rows, columns and occupied
cells exclude earlier labels on the four subsequent shell turns. On shell
$r$, with $e=r\bmod 2$, the chronological placements are
$(r,e)$, $(3e-1,r)$, $(-r,e)$ and $(3e-1,-r)$. The prefix argument gives
uniqueness. Comparing shells $r$ and $r+2$ gives the recurrence, including
the separately checked starting case $n=2$.

## Falsifier

One natural $n\ge2$ with a different increment in the literal least-label
process would refute the conjecture.

## Evidence

The complete result is
`D5/S3/Combinatorics/Polyomino/OeisA395348FourLag.a395348_four_lag`.
The predicate `SpiralLegal` gives occupancy and the opposite-color line
tests. The predicate `SpiralGreedy` gives least-label selection at every
turn. The two conjuncts of the theorem give unique infinite play and the
identity for every such play, with Black at turn $2n$.
