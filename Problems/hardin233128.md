# Hardin's recurrence for the seven-column six-colour pair-exclusion square strip

## Problem

[OEIS A233128](https://oeis.org/A233128), by R. H. Hardin, has NAME

> Number of nX7 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally or vertically, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

and an empirical linear recurrence of order $14$ with integer coefficients
(the `%F` line, verbatim in
[issue #28672](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/28672)).

Each row is a path of seven cells, and consecutive rows are joined by the
seven vertical bonds $(i,j)$–$(i+1,j)$: a seven-leg strip of the square
lattice. A bond is legal when its endpoints differ and do not sum to $5$.

## Route

1. **Bonds and normalization.** The complementary-pair labels are the fibres
   $\{0,5\},\{1,4\},\{2,3\}$ of `BooleanRankThreeK4.beta`; the row-major
   normalization is the reused scanner of `HardinA233175PairStrip`, with the
   frozen `HardinA233255LiteralBridge.scanner_fibres` and the width-seven
   first-row fibres `HardinA233255PairTransfer.first_fibres`. The width-seven
   row type, `rowLegal` and its decidability are the frozen owners of
   `HardinA233255PairTransfer`.
2. **Classes.** Before colour $2$ occurs there is one boundary class. After
   it, the $192$ proper label rows of width seven fall into twenty classes
   under relabelling of the three pairs together with reversal of the row
   (the five bits $p(j+2)\ne p(j)$ modulo reversal: $32$ words, $8$
   palindromes, $20$ reversal orbits).
3. **Transfer.** Kernel decisions over single label rows, split across seventeen
   certificate modules by boundary label, give the
   twenty-one-by-twenty-one class transfer and the first-row distribution
   (total $528$). Induction on the height identifies the boundary-state
   counts with the class vector, and a finite relation of order fourteen
   gives the recurrence.

## Falsifier

### What the settlement shows

Each statement below carries its status. "Computed" means exact integer
arithmetic in `hardin-a233128/enumerate.py` and
`hardin-a233128/charpoly.py` of the-omega-institute/trureturing-experiments
(run `python3 hardin-a233128/enumerate.py && python3
hardin-a233128/charpoly.py`); these are not kernel-checked.

1. **Computed:** the class transfer has $21$ states, and its characteristic
   polynomial equals Hardin's polynomial of degree fourteen times
   $x^7+640x^6-147456x^5-33554432x^4+2415919104x^3+377957122048x^2-8796093022208x-1125899906842624$.
2. **Computed:** that degree-seven factor has no integer root (its constant
   term is $-2^{50}$, and no $\pm2^k$ with $k\le59$ is a root).
3. **Computed:** the first-row distribution generates a fourteen-dimensional
   cyclic subspace of the transfer, on which Hardin's polynomial vanishes;
   the seven modes of the degree-seven factor are therefore not excited.
4. **Computed:** the sector before colour $2$ is one boundary class with
   $2^7=128$ continuations per row, and $128$ is a root of Hardin's
   polynomial.
5. **Computed:** at width $W$ there are $3\cdot2^{W-1}$ proper label rows,
   and they form $1,2,3,6,10,20$ relabelling-and-reversal classes for
   $W=2,\dots,7$.
6. **Open:** a formula for the class count or the recurrence order at every
   width; the order is set by the modes the first row excites, which
   statement 3 determines only at width seven.
