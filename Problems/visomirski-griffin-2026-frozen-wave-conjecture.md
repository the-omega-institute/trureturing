# Visomirski–Griffin frozen-wave conjecture

## Problem

Matthew Visomirski and Christopher Griffin, *Coherent Structures and Travelling
Waves in Spatial Replicators from a Biased Volterra Lattice*, arXiv:2601.06314v1,
Section 4.2, Conjecture 2 (page 19), asserts:

> Consider the one-dimensional spatial replicator, i.e., Eq. (6) with reaction
> term generated by a $2n$-cycle with $n\geq2$. Assuming periodic boundary
> conditions and initial conditions of the form,

$$
u_i(x,0)=\begin{cases}(1+\sin x)/(2n),&i\text{ odd},\\
(1-\sin x)/(2n),&i\text{ even},\end{cases}
$$

> the dynamics will evolve to a frozen wave stationary solution. That is,
> ecological niches will be maintained.

The simulated parameters are $a=-1/2$, $D=1/100$, on $[-\pi,\pi]$ with periodic
boundary conditions. Preregistration: [#14205](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/14205).

## Gap

The source reports frozen-wave simulations for small even cycles. Those
simulations do not establish the universal size quantifier. The implemented
`claim` asks that, for every $n\geq2$, there exist a classical solution with the
stated data and a continuous, $2\pi$-periodic, nonhomogeneous limit approached in
squared $L^2$ distance. The stationary PDE is omitted from the limit predicate;
the source's stationary-wave assertion implies this weaker claim. Refuting it
therefore excludes the source's frozen-wave reading at the simulated parameters.

The classical-solution record retains continuity at time zero, spatial
periodicity, the time and first and second space derivatives and their joint
continuity at positive times, the literal spatial equation, and the initial
data. It supplies no solution-existence theorem or positivity assumption.

## Route

Uniqueness follows from finite-strip boundedness, local Lipschitz continuity of
the finite-dimensional reaction, and a vector energy estimate. The shift by two
species and the reflection combined with a shift by one preserve the equation
and data, so uniqueness enforces two parity profiles $p,q$ and
$p(t,-x)=q(t,x)$. A zero-data linear energy argument gives $p+q=1/n$.

Consequently $w=n(p-q)$ is odd and solves

$$w_t=Dw_{xx}+\mu(w-w^3),\qquad \mu=-a/(2n).$$

For $Y(t)=\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}w(t,x)^2\,dx$, periodic integration by parts and the
mean-zero Wirtinger inequality yield

$$\tfrac12Y'=-D\int w_x^2+\mu Y-\mu\int w^4
\leq-(D-\mu)Y-\mu\int w^4.$$

When $D>\mu\geq0$, the exponential comparison gives $Y\to0$. When
$D=\mu>0$, the bound $Y^2\leq2\pi\int w^4$ gives the Riccati estimate
$Y(t)\leq Y(0)/(1+(\mu/\pi)Y(0)t)$, again tending to zero. The species deviations
are $\pm w/(2n)$, so every squared distance to $1/(2n)$ tends to zero.

## Falsifier

At $n=25$, $a=-1/2$, $D=1/100$, one has $\mu=D$. Every classical solution with
the specified data homogenizes. A continuous periodic profile differing from
the homogeneous equilibrium has strictly positive squared distance from it;
the squared-distance comparison excludes convergence to such a profile. This
contradicts `claim` without assuming that a classical solution exists.

## Triage

### What the settlement shows

- **Proved — mechanism; kernel-checked declarations.** Private `solution_pair`
  forces the two parity profiles, $p+q=1/n$ and their reflection relation.
  Private `vg_family` derives the odd Allen–Cahn equation with $\mu=-a/(2n)$
  and applies `odd_allen_cahn_decay`. For $-1<a\leq0$, $D>0$, $n\geq2$ and
  $2nD+a\geq0$, every classical solution with the source data homogenizes.
  The critical equality is included; loss of niches is not restricted to a
  strictly diffusive inequality.
- **Proved — authors' threshold; kernel-checked specialization and paper
  argument.** At the simulated parameters $\mu=1/(4n)$. For $n\geq2$, multiplying
  $1/(4n)\leq1/100$ by the positive number $400n$ gives $100\leq4n$, equivalently
  $n\geq25$. Thus every cycle with at least 50 species homogenizes by VG-R.
  The boundary $n=25$ is the numerical specialization used in `result`.
- **Proved — nonzero stationary profiles when $D<\mu$; literature reading
  and paper argument.** With $D>0$, rescale time by $D$ and
  put $\lambda=\mu/D>1$. The Chafee–Infante Dirichlet problem on $(0,\pi)$ is
  $w_t=w_{xx}+\lambda(w-w^3)$. Moreira–Valero, arXiv:2107.04334v1, Theorem 2.1
  (page 4), applied to constant diffusion one, gives a nonzero pair after the
  first bifurcation at $\lambda=1$; equations (4)–(5), pages 2–3, state the
  classical problem and its stationary equation. Odd reflection across the
  zero endpoints produces odd $2\pi$-periodic stationary profiles: the ODE
  gives $w_{xx}=0$ at an endpoint, so value, first derivative and second
  derivative match in the extension. This correspondence is a paper argument,
  not a Lean existence theorem. At the authors' parameters this covers
  $2\leq n\leq24$, including six- and eight-cycles ($n=3,4$).
- **Open — convergence of the conjecture's data when $D<\mu$; literature
  boundary.** Whether the alternating sine data converge to one of these
  nonzero profiles is not settled in this delivery. Stationary existence alone
  does not prove that convergence.
- **Open — positive bias $a>0$; energy-sign boundary.** Then $\mu<0$ and the
  cubic energy contribution $-\mu\int w^4$ is nonnegative. The delivered theorem
  assumes $\mu\geq0$ and does not settle this regime. A maximum-principle bound
  such as $|w|\leq1$ is a separate obligation.
- **Open — per-size parameter choice; quantifier boundary.** The reading
  “for each $n$ there are $a,D$” is not refuted by a fixed-parameter universal
  claim's failure. The delivered `result` does not settle that reading.
- **Open — other source results; scope boundary.** The refutation does not
  invalidate the source's four-cycle analysis, the reported six- and
  eight-cycle simulations, or its separate travelling-wave results. A theorem
  or interpretation requiring the conjecture uniformly in cycle size must
  retain the diffusion threshold or replace that premise. No dependency
  analysis of the paper's other results is proved here.
- **Open — other domain lengths.** Rescaling suggests a threshold involving
  the first Laplacian eigenvalue; the formal delivery fixes period $2\pi$.
  No general-period theorem is supplied.

There are no computed Triage claims or numerical simulation scripts in this
dossier. The threshold equivalence is the exact paper argument above, with the
boundary specialization checked in the Lean refutation.

## ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED

The source and literature checks establishing that the named conjecture was
unsettled are the preregistration's recorded literature evidence. They are not
Lean statements. Existence of a classical replicator solution, convergence in
the complementary diffusion regime, positive bias, and the per-size parameter
reading remain outside the formal result. The stationary-profile citation and
