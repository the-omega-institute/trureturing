# Hanna's derivative-substitution congruences for A232686

## Problem

Paul D. Hanna's OEIS A232686 COMMENTS conjectures concern integer exponential coefficients $a_n=n![x^n]A$ of rational formal power series with $A(0)=1$. The source equation and its nominated congruences are:

> %N A232686 E.g.f. A(x) satisfies: A'(x) = A(x*A'(x))^2.
> %C A232686 CONJECTURES.
> %C A232686 a(n) == 0 (mod 2) for n>=2.
> %C A232686 a(n) == 0 (mod 2^2) for n>=4.

| Record | Formal equation | Nominated congruences | Settling declaration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A232686 | $A'(x)=A(xA'(x))^2$ | $2\mid a_n$ for $n\ge2$; $4\mid a_n$ for $n\ge4$ | `HannaA232686Congruences.result` |

The settling declaration belongs to `D5.S1.Recurrence.Residue`. It universally quantifies over rational formal solutions and concludes all-index integrality as well as the two displayed congruences. The source note records the OEIS record and its literal source text.

## Falsifier

A failure of the integral recurrence, residual divisibility or unit-cancellation hypotheses would also block the proof. Passing finite coefficient checks does not establish the assertions at every index.
