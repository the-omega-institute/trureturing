# Barker's generating function for the three-column six-colour pair-exclusion strip

## Problem

[OEIS A233251](https://oeis.org/A233251), revision 9, by R. H. Hardin, has NAME

> Number of n X 3 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

and FORMULA

> Conjectures from Colin Barker, Oct 10 2018: (Start)
> G.f.: x*(3 - 64*x) / ((1 - 8*x)*(1 - 48*x)).
> a(n) = 8^(n-1) * (6^n+3) / 3.

Each row is a path of three cells, and consecutive rows are joined by the two
antidiagonal bonds $(i,1)$–$(i+1,0)$ and $(i,2)$–$(i+1,1)$: a strip of the
triangular lattice with its vertical and main-diagonal bonds removed. A bond
is legal when its endpoints differ and do not sum to $5$.

## Route

1. **Reuse.** The row-major normalization (`Seen`, `allowed`, `advance`,
   `good`, `finish`) and the complementary-pair reduction of the bond rule are
   the public declarations of `HardinA233175PairStrip`.
2. **Two classes.** After any row, a continuation depends only on whether
   colour $2$ has been seen. A row continuing a "seen" state has $48$ legal
   extensions; a row continuing an "unseen" state has $8$ extensions that stay
   unseen and $20$ that see colour $2$. The first row gives $2$ unseen and $1$
   seen state. Only one-row facts ($6^3$ colourings and the Boolean flags) are
   decided by the kernel.
3. **Series.** Writing $U_n,S_n$ for the class totals,
   $U_{n+1}=8U_n$ and $S_{n+1}=48S_n+20U_n$, so
   $a(n+3)=56a(n+2)-384a(n+1)$, and comparing coefficients gives the identity.

## Triage

1. **Proved (kernel-checked):** Barker's generating function for every height,
   by `HardinA233251TriangleStrip.result`.
2. **Computed from item 1 (no separate Lean statement):** Barker's closed form
   $a(n)=8^{n-1}(6^n+3)/3$ for $n\ge1$. From the class recursion,
   $U_n=2\cdot8^{n-1}$ and $S_n=2\cdot48^{n-1}-8^{n-1}$, so
   $a(n)=8^{n-1}+2\cdot48^{n-1}=8^{n-1}(6^n+3)/3$; the orchestrator checked
   this against the recursion for $n=1,\ldots,29$.
3. **Open:** a common structural proof of A233175 and A233251 by
   complementary-pair normalization over a general connected constraint graph.

### What the settlement shows

The triangular strip decouples into two independent geometric growth rates:
$8$ while colour $2$ is still unused and $48$ once it is used, with $20$
transitions from the first regime to the second per row. The factor $(1-8x)$ is
the "two pairs only" sector and $(1-48x)$ the full three-pair sector, the same
split that the A233175 dossier records as an open structural explanation of
its denominator.
