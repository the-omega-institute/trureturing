# A connected K₄-free graph with nonreal clique roots

## Problem

Hossein Teimoori Faal, *Sums of Polynomials and Clique Roots*,
arXiv:2112.09744v1, Conjecture 4.1, page 5:

> Let G be a connected K₄-free graphs, then G has only clique roots.

Section 2, page 3, defines a clique root as a real root of the clique
polynomial. Thus the conjecture asserts that every complex zero is real.
The [source note](../Library/Zeros/teimoorifaal2021sums.md) gives the
locators; [#22515](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/22515)
contains the preregistered literal statement. The conjecture is **Refuted**.

## Motivation

Connectedness and exclusion of four-vertex cliques do not force the cubic
clique polynomial to split over the reals. A seven-vertex graph already
exhibits the obstruction.

## Route

The private witness `F7` on `Fin 7` has edges
$03,04,05,06,13,14,15,16,23,25,34,35,46,56$.
The spanning path is $[0,3,1,4,6,5,2]$. Finite kernel decisions establish
K₄-freeness and clique counts $[1,7,14,9,0,0,0,0]$. The existing
`TeimooriFaalFlatCliqueRoots.cliquePoly` counts these cliques, giving
$p(X)=1+7X+14X^2+9X^3$. The module uses
`Cubic.discr_eq_prod_three_roots` to show that a real splitting would make
the discriminant nonnegative; its actual value is $-31$. The module then uses
`Polynomial.Splits.of_splits_map` and `IsAlgClosed.splits` to contradict the
assertion that every complex zero is real.

## Falsifier

A formal refutation must exhibit a finite connected K₄-free graph and a
complex zero of its integer clique polynomial with nonzero imaginary part.
The theorem `result` proves the negation of the full universal claim. It
imposes no additional hypotheses on that claim.

### What the settlement shows

**Proved in this module:** the connected K₄-free hypotheses permit the
seven-vertex witness and a cubic clique polynomial with a nonreal complex
zero. The obstruction is negative discriminant, rather than the absence of a
real zero. The polynomial identity and nonsplitting statements are the
private declarations named above; no root approximation is required.

**Computed:** the proposed graphs $G_m$ have clique coefficients
$[1,6m+1,12m^2+m,8m^3]$ for $m=2,\ldots,8$, are K₄-free, have one real root
and no repeated root, and possess the displayed cut of size $m$. Connectivity
under every deletion of fewer than $m$ vertices is checked only for
$m=2,3,4,5$, over $14,191,2626,36457$ deletion sets respectively.

**Open in Lean:** a uniform $G_m$ construction and its exact connectivity,
clique counts and root conclusions for all $m\ge2$. The single F7 witness
establishes no unbounded-connectivity theorem.

**Literature boundary:** the source's Proposition 3.3 states a positive
triangle-free result; its hypotheses exclude the triangles in F7. The
chordal questions impose additional structure absent from the settled
claim. They are not proved or refuted here. Conjecture 4.1 occurs in the
source's final open-questions section; no earlier proved result in that
source depends on its truth. Its refutation supplies no conclusion about
the other conjectures or chordal questions.
