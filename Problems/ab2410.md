# Acosta Babb's weighted Bessel-kernel positivity conjecture

## Problem

Ryan L. Acosta Babb, *A partial converse to the Riemann–Lebesgue lemma for Bessel–Fourier series of order zero*, arXiv:2410.17681v1, Section 4, Conjecture 1, states:

> For all, $0\leq\alpha\leq\frac12$, $M\in\mathbb N$ and $x\in[0,1]$, $K_M^\alpha(x)\geq0$.

The selected definition is the source's explicit weighted display

$$
K_M^\alpha(x)=\sum_{m=1}^M\left(1-\frac{m}{M+1}\right)j_m^\alpha J_0(j_mx)\quad(M>0),
\qquad K_0^\alpha(x)=1.
$$

Here $j_m$ is the $m$th positive zero of $J_0$. The printed partial sum is $S_m^\alpha(x)=\sum_{k=1}^m j_m^\alpha J_0(j_kx)$. Its factor has index $m$, so the printed Cesàro equality does not follow. The explicit weighted display, rather than that equality, is the target fixed by [#22175](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/22175).

### What the settlement shows

**Proved in the module:** at the single point the larger negative contribution of the second mode defeats the conjectured nonnegativity. This is the mechanism established by `point_negative`; a uniform endpoint-flux argument is not part of that theorem.

**Explanation by rational arithmetic:** the mode estimates in `point_negative` give the sufficient upper comparison

$$
\frac{2(1551/1000)(1/800)-(2349/1000)(187/100000)}{3}
=-\frac{17171}{100000000}<0.
$$

**Proved in prose from the definition:** the exceptional zero-mode kernel remains $K_0^\alpha(x)=1$, since this is the zero branch of `K`. This surviving statement requires no assertion about other mode counts or exponents. **Literature-attested:** the journal's weaker uniform lower bound in Lemma 3.3 is compatible with a negative interval and is not refuted by the point witness. It is not on this module's dependency path.

**Open:** the sharp transition, the proposed uniform rectangle, and other positivity ranges require further analytic work. Theorem 5.1, Corollary 5.2 and the conditional Proposition 5.3 discussion lose the proposed positivity-based route; their existence conclusions and Conjecture 2 remain undecided here.
