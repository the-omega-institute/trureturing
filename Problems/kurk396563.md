# Kurkov's three-parameter Stirling-minor Vandermonde sum

## Problem

Mikhail Kurkov's sole FORMULA conjecture in OEIS A396563, approved revision 15,
asks whether the unsigned first-kind Stirling determinant
$B(n,k,m)=\det[c(n+m+i,n+j)]_{1\le i,j\le k}$ equals

$$
\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{n\ge j_1\ge\cdots\ge j_m\ge0}
(-1)^{j_1+\cdots+j_m}
\prod_{1\le q<p\le m}(j_q-j_p+p-q)^2
\prod_{t=1}^m\frac{(n+t-1)_{j_t}(n-j_t+t)^{n+k}}{(j_t+m-t)!}}
{\displaystyle\prod_{i=0}^{m-1}(n+i)!}.
$$

Here $n,k\ge0$, $m\ge1$, $c$ denotes unsigned first-kind Stirling numbers,
and $(x)_r$ is the falling factorial. The empty determinant is one.
The primary source is <https://oeis.org/A396563/internal>;
the preregistration is <https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/22587>.

## Route

Write $N=n+m$ and $D_N(X)=\prod_{r=1}^N(1-rX)$.
The first-kind coefficient calculation and triangular monic basis change
identify the source minor with a corner of the Toeplitz matrix of $D_N$,
with sign $(-1)^{km}$. The inverse series has coefficient
$[X^r]D_N^{-1}=S(N+r,N)$, where $S$ denotes second-kind Stirling numbers.

The private `inverse_corner_minor` compares complementary corners of arbitrary
rational matrices $A,H$ of size $k+m$ under $AH=I$ and $\det A=1$:

$$
\det[A_{m+i,j}]_{0\le i,j<k}
=(-1)^{km}\det[H_{k+i,j}]_{0\le i,j<m}.
$$

For the Toeplitz matrices of $D_N$ and $D_N^{-1}$, the hypotheses follow from
series multiplication and the unit diagonal. The map $l_t=n-j_t+t$
identifies nested tuples with increasing $m$-subsets of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$.
Factorial cancellation and Cauchy–Binet turn the literal sum into a signed
moment determinant. Stirling inclusion–exclusion evaluates those moments;
column reversal identifies it with the inverse Toeplitz corner.
The two corner signs cancel, giving `result`.

## Falsifier

A rational inequality between the two literal expressions at any
$n,k\ge0$, $m\ge1$ would contradict `result` in the stated formal system.
The symbolic proof includes both zero-boundary parameters and uses no finite
checking assumption.

### What the settlement shows

Proved: the decisive mechanism is complementary-corner duality for determinant-one
inverse Toeplitz matrices, together with an exact conversion of the literal
chain sum to the inverse corner. The generic rational corner lemma extends
beyond the Stirling matrices. The public settlement covers the literal
conjecture's entire natural-parameter range.

The source array is the case $m=2$, so the conjectural sum now gives a proved
expression for that array. The source programs define the determinant directly;
the settlement does not change their definitions. No further source result
was identified as conditional on this conjecture. The separate positivity,
integrality, $m=0$ and Schur-function questions remain open here.
