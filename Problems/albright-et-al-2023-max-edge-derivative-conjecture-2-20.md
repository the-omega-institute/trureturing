# Albright et al., Conjecture 2.20

## Problem

Albright, Hadaway, Pomerance, Jeffries, Lorenzen and Nix state in
*On the Edge Derivative of the Normalized Laplacian with Applications to
Kemeny's Constant*, arXiv:2211.01495v2, §2.4, p. 14:

> Over all graphs, the maximum edge derivative for any pair of vertices
> x, y is 1

The source domain is finite connected simple unweighted graphs of order at
least two. The pair consists of distinct vertices and includes both adjacency
cases. The value for an eigenvalue of positive multiplicity is the mean in
Lemma 2.3, p. 4. The same maximum appears as Conjecture 2.24 in arXiv v1.
[Conjecture 2.20](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.01495v2#S2.Thmthm20) ·
[Lemma 2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.01495v2#S2.Thmthm3)

### Statement correspondence

| Source component | Lean expression |
| --- | --- |
| Arbitrary order at least two | `n : ℕ`, `2 ≤ n` and vertex type `Fin n` |
| Connected simple unweighted graph | `G : SimpleGraph (Fin n)` and `G.Connected` |
| Any distinct pair | `x y : Fin n` and `x ≠ y` |
| Degree-changing perturbation | Frozen `paramAdj G x y t` and `normLap` with the weighted row sums |
| Eigenvalue and entire basis | `eigenspace G x y lam` of the unperturbed matrix acting on Euclidean space, and `OrthonormalBasis (Fin k) ℝ` of that subspace with `0 < k` |
| Lemma 2.3 mean | `edgeDerivative G x y lam b` with the two endpoint coordinates divided by the square roots of their degrees |
| Native derivative and basis independence | `HasDerivAt` of `rayleighAverage` and equality of the values in any two full bases |
| Maximum one | Universal upper bound and equality at `pathGraph 3`, pair `{0, 2}`, eigenvalue `1` of multiplicity one |

The proof uses positive degrees for the scalar estimate and differentiation.
Connectedness and order at least two supply positive degrees in the source
domain. The full eigenspace is finite-dimensional and admits an orthonormal
basis indexed by its dimension, so every eigenvalue occurs in the universal
clause.

## Motivation

The normalized Laplacian measures spectral response while the perturbation
changes both one symmetric pair of adjacency entries and the endpoint degrees.
An order-independent ceiling controls the first-order increase for every
eigenspace, including when the perturbation inserts a missing edge.

## Gap

The paper gives a degree-one twin attaining case and a broader
eigenvalue-dependent upper bound in Theorem 2.19. The uniform bound needs
control of the simultaneous mass on the two endpoints, especially for a nonedge
with positive normalized adjacency eigenvalue. The scoped literature screen
in [#28967](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/28967)
distinguishes this derivative from degree-preserving perturbations and
derivatives of Kemeny's constant.

## Route

The proof differentiates the degree and adjacency factors entrywise and
compresses on the fixed eigenspace. The bound uses the quadratic forms
of the identity plus and minus normalized adjacency. For a nonedge, the
coordinate restriction to its two endpoints has zero adjacency quadratic
form. Positivity on this restriction minus its projection onto the unit
eigenvector bounds the combined mass on the two endpoints. The adjacent cases
use the two integer degree bounds and the eigenvector equation at a leaf.
For attainment, the path's `1`-eigenspace is the line generated by
`(1,0,−1)/√2`. The same vector has perturbed eigenvalue `1 + t/(1+t)` near zero.

## Falsifier

A derivative above one for an admissible graph, pair and positive-multiplicity
eigenvalue would falsify the upper bound. The derivative identification and
basis-independence clauses would fail if the compressed trace had a different
derivative or if two full orthonormal bases gave different means. The attainment
certificate requires the stated pair to be a distinct nonedge of the connected
three-vertex path, and the eigenvalue-one space to have dimension one with
derivative exactly one.

## Evidence

`D5.S3.Combinatorics.SpectralGraph.AlbrightMaxEdgeDerivative.result`
proves the upper bound, derivative identity and basis independence in
`claim`, together with the attaining instance. The derivative differentiates
the weighted row sums on a two-sided neighborhood of zero. The eigenspace
compression uses the fixed unperturbed space, as required for the
positive-multiplicity average in Lemma 2.3. Admission is as the named external
conjecture preregistered in
[#28967](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/28967).

## Triage

Theorem. The upper bound holds at every order in the source domain and is
attained globally by the three-vertex path. At order two the sole connected
graph is \(K_2\), whose normalized Laplacian is constant near zero under the
pair perturbation.

## ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED

Literature completeness, priority and the absence of an unlocated external
settlement remain unverified. The formal result gives global attainment and
does not classify all attaining triples or the maximum at each fixed order.
