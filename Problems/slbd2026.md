# A faithful mixed-state counterexample to KD-real SLD exclusion

## Problem

Matéo Spriet, Christopher Langrenez, Raymond Brummelhuis and Stephan De Bièvre, *What Is Special About the Kirkwood–Dirac Distributions? Only They Produce Natural Conditional Expectations*, arXiv:2511.01996v2, §6.3, immediately after (6.23), state:

> In view of what precedes, it is tempting to conjecture that, if $\hat \rho$ and $\hat X$ are both KD real, the optimal observable $\hat L$ must not be KD-real itself, provided $\rm I_{QF}$ does not vanish.

The conjecture is unnumbered; Proposition 6.5 is the published pure-state theorem. The journal article, DOI 10.3390/sym18061008, retains the conjecture after (168), with the pure-state result numbered Proposition 7. The claim is bound to arXiv v2. Source and literature evidence are in [#22488](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/22488); the source note is [sprietlangrenez2026kdreal](../Library/HilbertOperators/sprietlangrenez2026kdreal.md).

## Motivation

The frozen settling declaration is `D5/S3/Quantum/Information/KirkwoodDiracSLDRefutation.result`. It refutes the universal mixed-state extension using a faithful nine-dimensional state, a noncommuting KD-real generator, and a KD-real SLD with source QFI $1/6$.

## Gap

The pure-state exclusion does not imply mixed-state exclusion. A KD-real Hermitian sector can contain noncommuting matrices; Hermitian does not mean entrywise real in either original basis. The explicit counterexample supplies the missing mixed-state test.

## Route

The module verifies literal rational and $\sqrt 3$ matrix entries. Its private Fourier matrix supplies a unitary second basis with no zero overlaps. A projection certificate proves positivity of $\rho=(I+E)/12$, and the trace is one. Entrywise coefficient calculations establish KD reality of $\rho$, $X$ and $L$. The SLD equation and $\operatorname{Tr}(\rho L^2)=1/6$ contradict the universal conclusion.

The public result is the designated refutation result (`basis=refutes`) and is exempt from four-slot escape registration (CLAUDE.md §3.9).

## Falsifier

A source restriction excluding faithful dimension-nine inputs, a change in the KD-symbol convention, or a failure of the literal SLD/QFI identities would invalidate this translation. The source formulation and the unchanged public definitions fix those conventions. The conjecture's negation does not claim failure of the published pure-state theorem or the restricted mixed-state examples.

## Triage

### What the settlement shows

1. **Proved, kernel-checked:** the literal universal mixed-state claim is false (`SprietKirkwoodDiracSLD.result`). The faithful $d=9$ witness and its QFI $1/6$ are checked by the consumed `Probe.rho_positive` and `Probe.qfi_value` calculations, together with the KD-reality and SLD helpers listed in Evidence.

2. **Computed:** for $p=3$ and every real $0<t<1$, the experiment's family is faithful and KD-positive. With its rank-three projection $E$ and Hermitian generator $X$, it has $L_t=-6it[X,E]/(4+t)$, a KD-real SLD, and QFI $3t^2/(4+t)$. Evidence: the exact experiment above; this uniform family is not a Lean theorem here.

3. **Computed identities, with range justified by algebra:** the same rational matrix identities are checked with $t$ real. They apply on $-1\le t\le2$, with nonzero QFI when $t\ne0$; the states are faithful on $-1<t<2$. For $t=3u-1$, substitution in the checked scalar expressions gives eigenvalues $(1+t)/9=u/3$ and $(1-t/2)/9=(1-u)/6$, KD entries $(1+t)/81=u/27$ and $(2-t)/162=(1-u)/54$, and QFI $3t^2/(4+t)=(3u-1)^2/(u+1)$. On $0\le u\le1$ all eigenvalues and KD entries are nonnegative, and on the interior they are positive; $4+t=3(u+1)>0$. This substitution and sign argument are proved in prose from the experiment's identities; no additional computational range or Lean family theorem is claimed.

4. **Computed:** at $t=1/2$, the density eigenvalues are $1/6$ with multiplicity three and $1/12$ with multiplicity six, and KD entries are $1/54$ and $1/108$. Evidence: the exact experiment's projection decomposition and member certificate.

5. **Computed:** the $p=3$ family has a nonzero commutator while its Jordan equation has the KD-real solution above. Evidence: the exact symbolic matrix checker. The concrete Lean realization verifies the single instance; abstract closure of a KD-real star-algebra under Jordan inversion is not formally proved here.

6. **Computed:** both original-basis marginals are $1/9$ and both tangent diagonals vanish in the $p=3$ family. Hence their classical Fisher information is zero. Evidence: the exact experiment's marginal and derivative identities; the final consequence follows because every summand is a zero squared derivative divided by $1/9$. This agrees with the source Proposition 6.3.

7. **Proved in prose by hypothesis separation:** the published pure-state Proposition 6.5 and the two restricted mixed-state examples are not negated. This witness is faithful and has two distinct positive eigenvalues, so it is not pure. Both mixed-state examples first require the $A$ basis to diagonalize $\rho$. The first additionally assumes real transition coefficients; the second assumes $X=f(A)+\Pi^B_{b_0}$. In this counterexample $A$ is the computational basis and $\rho_{0,3}=1/36$, as follows by substitution in the displayed witness matrix, so its state is not diagonal in that basis. Its Fourier transition matrix also has non-real entries. Thus neither source example asserts the unrestricted conclusion refuted here. No Lean proof of these neighbouring theorems is claimed. The exact experiment additionally checks one pure-qubit fidelity instance, without proving the general source theorem. The source's established results keep their stated hypotheses; no proved result is inferred false merely because this conjectured extension fails.

8. **Open:** the arbitrary odd-prime family and the general KD-real star-algebra/Jordan-map mechanism as Lean theorems. The preregistration supplies an ordinary proof outline, while the experiment checks the finite graph laws only for $p=3,5,7$.

9. **Open:** minimal counterexample dimension and classification of bases or extra hypotheses for which mixed-state exclusion survives. A witness in dimension nine proves an upper bound on the least possible counterexample dimension, without a minimality claim.
