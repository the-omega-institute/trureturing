# Olairez Question 6.2 on the dihedral permutohedron

## Problem

Colin Olairez, *Triangulation, Volume, and Symmetries of a Dihedral Group
Analogue of the Permutohedron*, arXiv:2610.28672v2, §6, p. 21, asks:

> What is the full group of affine symmetries for the dihedral permutohedron?

For every natural n ≥ 3, take Qₙ to be the real convex hull of the
orbit of (1,…,n) under the permutation matrices of Eq. (2). The group
consists of all real affine bijections of the intrinsic affine span of
Qₙ that preserve Qₙ. Its multiplication is composition.

## Motivation

The source exhibits an additional affine symmetry for odd n in §5.
The full group question asks which affine maps preserve the standard
orbit hull, for both odd and even n. The lattice-preserving subgroup
and the arbitrary-basepoint orbit have their own conditions.

## Gap

Voskresenskii and Klyachko identify the normalized del Pezzo model

4.3(v). In a consistently oriented cycle, the relation that the

## Route

The original action uses the inverse permutation index. Its orbit is
the union of the paper rotations rⱼ and their antipodes fⱼ. Write
c = ((n + 1)/2)·1. The forward cyclic difference is a real linear
equivalence on the sum-zero direction. With zero residue representing
paper label n, it sends rⱼ − c to 1 − n e₍₁₋ⱼ₎ and fⱼ − c to its
negative. The two centered vertex families map onto the signed wrap vertices.
The centered convex hull maps onto their convex hull.

The wrap vectors span the sum-zero space, and every linear dependence
has constant coefficients. Their signed hull has exactly the signed
wrap vectors as extreme points and has centroid zero. An affine
preserver fixes the centroid, permutes antipodal pairs, and preserves
the dependence among the positive wraps. The dependence forces the
signs to agree. Transport through the difference equivalence recovers
the action on one-based labels.

## Falsifier

A discrepancy with the two source matrices, a point in either orbit
description missing from the other, or a wrap index inconsistent with
the original label would invalidate the transfer. An affine preserver
giving unequal signs on two transported pairs would contradict the
constant-coefficient dependence. The parameter range includes n = 3
and both parities.

### What the settlement shows

**Proved.** The affine span has equation Σᵢ xᵢ = n(n + 1)/2. For each
permutation σ of the one-based labels and sign ε, the unique preserver
acts by rⱼ ↦ c + ε(r_{σ(j)} − c) and fⱼ ↦ c + ε(f_{σ(j)} − c). Every
preserver arises uniquely this way. The product law gives Sₙ × C₂
of order 2n!, with the sign factor generated by x ↦ 2c − x.

**Proved.** The same wrap formula and common-sign argument apply to
odd and even n. The independent volume and triangulation statements
require no conclusion of Question 6.2.

action of Lₙ on rotations as j ↦ 2j − 1 modulo n, using the row pattern
of Pₙ in Lemma 5.3. The classification therefore identifies the pair
for that map as this permutation and positive sign. Corollary 5.5 uses
the paper's additional integral property to conclude unimodular equivalence.
