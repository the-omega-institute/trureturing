# Hanna's ternary matrix coefficient formula

## Problem

Paul D. Hanna's OEIS A078122, revision 10, second FORMULA, states:

> M(n, k) = the coefficient of x^(3^n - 3^(n-k)) in the power series expansion of 1/Product_{j=0..n-k}(1-x^(3^j)) whenever 0<=k<n for all n>0 (conjecture).

The source matrix is natural-valued and lower triangular, has first column and diagonal equal to one, and satisfies the cubic-shift equation on every finite principal block. The NAME prints $M(0,k)=1$; both source programs and the displayed matrix instead use $M(k,0)=1$. The first-column interpretation is the explicit convention of [#23570](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/23570).

## Gap

## Route

For base three, private `F` expresses the coefficients and private `G` is the corresponding natural-valued finite-difference recursion. Double induction proves scaled convolution. The private matrix family `EN` has finite principal blocks satisfying an additive semigroup law. At scale one its cube shifts both indices; the boundary values give a source matrix. Strong induction on rows identifies every source matrix with this one, and the arithmetic exponent identity gives the published coefficient formula.
