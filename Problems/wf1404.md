# Wallman–Flammia unrestricted two-contractivity

## Problem

Joel J. Wallman and Steven T. Flammia, *Randomized Benchmarking with
Confidence*, arXiv:1404.6025v4, Section VII, Definition 15 (PDF page 23)
and the unnumbered conjecture immediately before Proposition 16 (PDF
page 24), state:

> A channel $\Lambda:\mathcal D_d\to\mathcal D_d$ is $n$-contractive
> with respect to a group $\mathcal G\subseteq\mathcal U(d)$ if
> $(\Lambda^{\otimes n})^{\mathcal G}$ has at most one eigenvalue of modulus 1.

> We conjecture that *all* nonunitary channels are in fact $2$-contractive
> with respect to any unitary 2-design.

The conjecture is proved for the finite exact-design scope fixed in
[preregistration #22351](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/22351).
The source is [the versioned source note](../Library/Eigenstructure/wallmanflammia2014confidence.md).
The module has generality `I`, utility `none`, and admission basis
`open-problem-resolution (#22351; Proved)`.

## Motivation

Proposition 16 treats nonunitary unital channels. The remaining case is
nonunital noise in arbitrary dimension. Its trace-preserving matrix has
lower blocks that prevent replacing the channel by its unital part
without a positivity argument. The proof instead controls the complete
traceless tensor square and keeps those lower blocks.

## Route

The proof identifies the balanced averaged Gram matrices with scalar
multiples of the padded traceless projection. A rectangular factorization
bounds the norm of the full complex tensor square. The coefficient is
the channel's unitarity and is strictly below one for a nonunitary CPTP
channel, by the Choi-purity argument. A trace-adapted basis gives four
lower-triangular tensor sectors: a scalar identity, two one-copy sectors
and the traceless tensor-square sector. Rectangular compression transfers
the spectral bounds without imposing an orthonormal basis on the trace
kernel. The characteristic-polynomial factorization isolates the scalar
root while every other sector is strictly inside the unit circle.

The public settling theorem and the private consumed helpers are
`bind-only` compositions of existing frozen and pinned Mathlib owners.
No escape witness is claimed. The external named settlement supplies
the module's admission basis independently of that proof-shape label.

## Falsifier

A counterexample to the formal statement would require a dimension
$d\ge2$, a nonunitary CPTP channel, and a finite exact unitary 2-design
subgroup whose specified two-copy twirl has at least two unit-circle
roots counted with algebraic multiplicity. Unitary channels do not meet
the nonunitarity premise. An approximate design, gate-dependent channel
family or compact infinite average does not meet the statement's scope.

### What the settlement shows

The decisive estimate controls non-Hermitian as well as Hermitian
directions, and rectangular compression permits arbitrary coordinates
on the trace kernel. Nonunital terms remain in lower blocks and do not
change the characteristic-polynomial factorization. These mechanisms are
the kernel-checked claims named in the first three Triage rows; they do
not assert an extension to approximate designs or higher tensor powers.

As a mathematical consequence of the source's argument, its Theorem 17
asymptotic-variance conclusion applies whenever its remaining assumptions
hold and the 2-contractivity premise is supplied by this settlement.
This is an application of the paper's argument, not a formalization of
Theorem 17 in this module. No channel-independent positive spectral gap
is inferred from channel-dependent strict inequalities.
