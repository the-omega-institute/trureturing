# Hardin's recurrence for the $4\times n$ two-defect three-colour strip

## Problem

[OEIS A229697](https://oeis.org/A229697), by R. H. Hardin, has NAME

> Number of defective 3-colorings of a 4 X n 0..2 array connected horizontally and antidiagonally with exactly two mistakes, and colors introduced in row-major 0..2 order.

and the empirical recurrence

> Empirical: a(n) = 27*a(n-1) - 288*a(n-2) + 1557*a(n-3) - 4644*a(n-4) + 8073*a(n-5) - 8343*a(n-6) + 5022*a(n-7) - 1620*a(n-8) + 216*a(n-9) for n > 17.

Both quotations are from the [OEIS text export](https://oeis.org/search?q=id:A229697&fmt=text).
The assertion registered in [issue #31299](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/31299) is this recurrence for every $n\ge 18$, with four rows, two unsatisfied bonds, and $n$ columns. The sequence offset is one.

For an array $f:\operatorname{Fin}4\times\operatorname{Fin}n\to\operatorname{Fin}3$, a defect is a bond whose endpoint colours agree. The HA bonds are $(i,j)$--$(i,j+1)$ for $j+1<n$, and $(i,j+1)$--$(i+1,j)$ for $i+1<4$ and $j+1<n$; vertical bonds are absent. These horizontal and antidiagonal bonds make the $4$-row arrays sheared square-lattice strips with staircase ends, and the source sweeps columns. They are the two-defect counterpart of the settled A229589 strip in the A229588/A229589 pair registered by #30838. There are no periodic boundary bonds.

## Motivation

The settling declaration is `D5/S3/StatisticalMechanics/Potts/StripDefectsTwo/HardinA229697Strip.result`. The external assertion is preregistered in [issue #31299](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/31299); the source note has bibkey `hardin2013a229697`.

The coordinate change $x=i+j$, $y=i$ sends horizontal and antidiagonal bonds to the two unit directions of the square lattice. The finite $4\times n$ graph has staircase ends, and the source sweeps its columns without changing the row-major normalization order. With antiferromagnetic Potts energy
$E(f)=J\sum_{\{u,v\}\in B}\mathbf{1}_{f(u)=f(v)}$ and $J>0$, these are states of the three-state Potts antiferromagnet with exactly $D=2$ unsatisfied bonds on the sheared square strip (HA). Row-major normalization counts one representative from each global colour relabelling orbit in the nonconstant range used by the proof. The theorem is a finite combinatorial statement under this bond and energy convention.

## Gap

The OEIS formula is labelled empirical. The registered target is its validity for every $n\ge 18$ from the literal array definition; agreement with a finite list of sequence terms does not supply that statement.

The literature check (the OEIS entry and its links; arXiv searches for Potts strips with unsatisfied bonds and for defective colourings; Chang–Salas–Shrock, J. Stat. Phys. 107 (2002) 1207–1253, which treats rectangular square-lattice strips) located no proof of this recurrence.
The search scope is the one recorded in #31299; worldwide priority and literature completeness remain unverified.

## Route

1. **Literal count and normalization.** `a` filters every original `Fin 4 × Fin n → Fin 3` array by the exact HA defect count and the row-major canonical condition. `DefectTransfer.relabel_defects` gives colour-permutation invariance. The source uses coordinate exchange only on labelled arrays, while `HardinA233177Canonical.canonical_pattern_card` supplies the six-to-one orbit bridge back to the original row-major arrays. A constant $4\times(m+1)$ array has $7m$ HA bonds, so it cannot lie in the two-defect sector; the colour action is therefore free on the accepted arrays.
2. **Exact-budget transfer.** A swept column is `Row = Fin 4 → Fin 3`; the within-column `horizontal` set is empty and `between` contains the horizontal and antidiagonal cross-column bonds. The residual budget ranges over $0,1,2$. The 42-state transfer extends the settled one-defect A229589 transfer: its leading 28 states carry the inherited residual-budget sectors and 14 tail states are the new two-defect sector. The finite certificate checks terminal acceptance, budget preservation, all transition sums, initial weights, and the forward table for this block extension.
3. **Propagation.** `DefectTransfer.lumped_count` identifies the labelled exact-budget count with the matrix-power scalar product. The certified forward relation has degree nine and starts at power eight; `DefectTransfer.forward_shift` propagates it to every later power. The six-element colour-orbit bridge then gives the displayed canonical recurrence at every index at least $18$.
