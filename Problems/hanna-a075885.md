# Hanna's A075885 root limit

## Problem

Paul D. Hanna's OEIS A075885, revision 28, gives the formula:

> a(n) = 1 + Sum_{m=1..n} Product_{k=1..m} floor(n/k).

Its conjecture reads:

> Conjecture: limit a(n)^(1/n) = L where L = 2.200161058099... is the geometric mean of Luroth expansions, where log(L) = Sum_{n>=1} log(n)/(n*(n+1)) = 0.7885305659115... (cf. A085361).

Define $p(n,m)=\prod_{k=1}^{m}\lfloor n/k\rfloor$ for $0\le m\le n$,
with empty product $1$, and $P(n)=p(n,n)$. Then
$a(n)=1+\sum_{m=1}^{n}p(n,m)$, including $a(0)=1$.
The proved assertion is the existence of $L>0$ such that the real series
$\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\log(j)/(j(j+1))$ converges to $\log L$ and
$a(n)^{1/n}\to L$ over positive integers. The decimals and the description
by Lüroth expansions identify the source constant; no numerical accuracy or
ergodic theorem is included in this assertion. Preregistration: #23630.

## Route

All factors in a prefix are positive integers and at least one, so
$P(n)\le a(n)\le(n+1)P(n)$ for $n\ge1$. For
$d_j=\log(j+2)-\log(j+1)$, finite rearrangement gives
$\log P(n)=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\lfloor n/(j+2)\rfloor d_j$.
The bounds $0\le d_j\le1/(j+1)$ provide the summable majorant
$1/((j+1)(j+2))$. The imported
`D5.S3.ArithSums.A400361PillaiMean.quotient_ratio_limit` supplies the
pointwise normalized-quotient limit, and its `hasSum_kernel_one` supplies
the majorant's summability. Dominated convergence gives
$\log P(n)/n\to C=\sum_{j\ge0}d_j/(j+2)$.

Finite summation by parts identifies this sum with the source series:
$\sum_{j<N}d_j/(j+2)=\sum_{j<N}\mathrm{logSummand}(j)+\log(N+1)/(N+1)$.
The final term tends to zero. The sum/product sandwich and
$\log(n+1)/n\to0$ give $\log a(n)/n\to C$.
Continuity of the exponential yields the required $L=\exp C>0$.
