# Three two-strand boxes refute Jones–Wenzl generation at a root of unity

## Problem

S. M. Flores and E. Peltola, *Generators, projectors, and the Jones-Wenzl algebra* (2018), arXiv:1811.12364v2, Section 1, Conjecture 1.2, TeX source `WJalgebra_gen-sec1-intro.tex`, lines 544–546, with macros expanded and references resolved:

> Item 1 in theorem 1.1 holds whenever $\max \varsigma < \bar{\mathfrak p}(q)$.

Here $q\in\mathbb C^\times$, $\nu=-q-q^{-1}$ and $\varsigma=(s_1,\ldots,s_d)$ has positive entries, with total size $n_\varsigma=\sum_i s_i$. The source also permits the exceptional multiindex $(0)$. The bound $\bar{\mathfrak p}(q)$ is infinite at $q=\pm1$ and otherwise is the order of $q^2$, interpreted as infinite when that order is infinite. The composite Jones–Wenzl projector $P_\varsigma$ places projector boxes of sizes $s_i$ side by side, and
$\mathrm{JW}_\varsigma(\nu)=P_\varsigma\mathrm{TL}_{n_\varsigma}(\nu)P_\varsigma$.

Item 1 of Theorem 1.1, at lines 502–516, states that, assuming $n_\varsigma<\bar{\mathfrak p}(q)$, the unit $P_\varsigma$ and the projected boundary generators
$U_i^{\mathrm{JW}}=P_\varsigma U_{s_1+\cdots+s_i}^{\mathrm{TL}}P_\varsigma$, $1\le i<d$, form a minimal generating set. The conjecture replaces the total-size hypothesis by the individual-box hypothesis.

Issue [#25910](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/25910) fixes the generation assertion `claim`: for every nonzero complex $q$, every list $\varsigma$ of positive sizes individually below $\bar{\mathfrak p}(q)$, and every $T\in\mathrm{TL}_{n_\varsigma}(-q-q^{-1})$, the sandwich $P_\varsigma TP_\varsigma$ belongs to the ambient unital complex subalgebra generated by $P_\varsigma$ and the $U_i^{\mathrm{JW}}$. This assertion is refuted, which suffices to refute the conjecture independently of minimality. The definitions and source locator are in [the literature note](../Library/StatisticalMechanics/florespeltola2018generators.md).

## Motivation

The distinction between existence of the individual projector boxes and generation of their whole corner determines whether the source's generator description extends to arbitrary admissible root-of-unity parameters. The source connects its generating sets to generators-and-relations presentations and to applications in quantum Schur–Weyl duality and conformal field theory (introduction, lines 475–478 and 560–567).

## Route

All the representation certificates below concern the fixed parameter and matrices defined in this module.

1. **Admissible parameter.** Put $r=\sqrt3$ and $q_0=\exp(\pi i/6)$. The private lemma `parameter` establishes $q_0\ne0$, $\bar{\mathfrak p}(q_0)=6$ and $-q_0-q_0^{-1}=-r$. Thus the positive list $\varsigma=(2,2,2)$ satisfies the individual-size restriction $2<6$.
2. **Projector and witness.** The private lemmas `projector` and `generators`, for exactly $q_0$ and this list, identify the composite projector and its generating set as
   $P=(1+r^{-1}U_1)(1+r^{-1}U_3)(1+r^{-1}U_5)$ and $\{P,PU_2P,PU_4P\}$. The private definition `middle` is $W=U_2U_1U_4U_3U_2U_5U_4$; set $Z=PWP$.
3. **Representation.** In the quadratic rational algebra with $\omega^2=3$, `exactRelations` verifies the five fixed matrices $A_i$, indexed by $i\in\mathrm{Fin}\,5$: $A_i^2=-\omega A_i$ for every $i$; $A_iA_jA_i=A_i$ when $i+1=j$ or $j+1=i$; and $A_iA_j=A_jA_i$ when $i+1<j$ or $j+1<i$, using the natural values of these indices. These are precisely the three relation families needed to define the complex algebra homomorphism `rho` after `scalarEmbedding` sends $\omega$ to $r$. The private lemma `rho_gen` identifies $\rho(U_{i+1})$ for every $i\in\mathrm{Fin}\,5$, and `rho_box` identifies $\rho(P)$ with the embedded fixed matrix `exactP`.
4. **Separating row.** For the fixed row $\ell$ defined by `row`, the private lemmas `row_projector` and `row_generators` give $\ell\rho(P)=\ell$ and $\ell\rho(PU_2P)=\ell\rho(PU_4P)=-(r/3)\ell$. Its coordinate numbered $6$ from zero is zero. The private lemma `exactWitness` gives the corresponding witness coordinate $-2\omega/3$ before scalar embedding, and `row_witness` establishes $(\ell\rho(Z))_6\ne0$.
5. **Subalgebra obstruction.** The private definition `rowStabilizer` is the unital complex subalgebra of matrices $M$ satisfying $\ell M=c\ell$ for some $c\in\mathbb C$. The proof of `separation` puts all three generator images in this subalgebra; every matrix in it has $(\ell M)_6=0$, whereas the witness does not. Consequently `separation` establishes $Z\notin\mathbb C[P,PU_2P,PU_4P]$ in the ambient presented algebra. Applying `claim` at the nonzero parameter $q_0$, the positive admissible list $(2,2,2)$ and $T=W$ contradicts this lemma, giving `result : ¬ claim`.

### What the settlement shows

**Proved by `result`:** it is false that, for every nonzero complex $q$, every list $\varsigma$ of positive natural sizes with each $s\in\varsigma$ satisfying $s<\bar{\mathfrak p}(q)$, and every $T\in\mathrm{TL}_{n_\varsigma}(-q-q^{-1})$, the sandwich $P_\varsigma TP_\varsigma$ belongs to the ambient unital complex subalgebra generated by $P_\varsigma$ and all $P_\varsigma U_{s_1+\cdots+s_i}P_\varsigma$ with $1\le i<d$.

**Computed, not formalized:** None.

**Argued, not formalized.**

- *Mechanism and missing condition.* Existence of each box does not ensure that its projected boundary operators generate all sandwiches. For the fixed counterexample, `row_projector` and `row_generators` preserve the line spanned by the fixed row $\ell$, while `row_witness` shows that the sandwich $Z$ leaves it: a multiple of $\ell$ has coordinate $6$ zero. Sums, products and scalar multiples of line-preserving operators still preserve that line. Thus the obstruction concerns generation itself, before any question of a minimal generating set.
- *Nearby statements.* Restoring $n_\varsigma<\bar{\mathfrak p}(q)$ gives precisely the source's Theorem 1.1, whose hypothesis excludes this counterexample. The source's Corollary 3.6 states generation for two positive boxes under $\max(s_1,s_2)<\bar{\mathfrak p}(q)$ (`WJalgebra_gen-appD-genlem-subsec2.tex`, lines 95–102); the present counterexample requires three boxes. The module supplies no characterization for other lists or root orders, and does not settle generation when $n_\varsigma>\bar{\mathfrak p}(q)$ or the extension of Theorem 1.1, item 2, beyond its stated range; these questions remain open here.
- *Effect on the paper.* The source's active references to Conjecture 1.2 discuss the proposed extension and its special cases (introduction, lines 539–556); no other named theorem is identified there as conditional on the conjecture. Theorem 1.1 retains its strict total-size hypothesis. A presentation of the full counterexample corner cannot use only $P,PU_2P,PU_4P$: adding relations to those generators only identifies more expressions and cannot supply the missing sandwich. Thus the proposed general extension of that generator description is unavailable. This conclusion does not assess the later quantum Schur–Weyl or conformal-field-theory applications mentioned in the introduction.
