# Bala's A193237 congruences modulo five and seven

## Problem

Peter Bala's FORMULA line in OEIS A193237 states: “Conjectures: a(5*n+4) == 0 (mod 5) and a(7*n+5) == 0 (mod 7) (both checked up to n = 200).” The source is preregistered in [#22112](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/22112). The companion claim is [#22113](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/22113).

Write $T_j=j(j+1)/2$ and $F(x)=\sum_{j\ge0}(2j+1)3^j(-1)^{T_j}x^{T_j}$. The question concerns the constant-one reciprocal cube root $A(x)=F(x)^{-1/3}$ and every natural index, without a finite cutoff.

2. **Proved, kernel-checked private helper:** for any integer weights $w$ and integral power series $A$, the assumption $A^3\operatorname{triangular}(w)=1$ implies both congruences. The private `triangular_inverse_congruences` in `BalaA193237ModFiveSeven` is used by `resolve`; it assumes neither desired congruence and does not need the divisibility-by-nine hypothesis.
3. **Proved, kernel-checked private helper:** for every integer power series $F$ with constant term one and positive-degree coefficients divisible by nine, an integral normalized reciprocal cube root exists. The private `integral_inverse_of_nine`, consumed by `resolve`, proves this general statement. The construction writes $F=1+9H$, obtains $U=H-3U^2-3U^3$ by coefficient contraction, forms $B=1+3U$ with $B^3=F$, and inverts $B$.

These are `triangular_support_five`, `cube_support_five`, `triangular_support_seven` and `square_support_seven` in the shared module. `prime_power_support`, `mul_prime_power_vanish`, `transfer_five` and `transfer_seven` give the Frobenius transfer.
