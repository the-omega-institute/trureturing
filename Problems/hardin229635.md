# Hardin's recurrence for the width-six strip with two defects

## Problem

The NAME and first FORMULA of OEIS A229635 state the following count and recurrence.

> Number of defective 3-colorings of an n X 6 0..2 array connected horizontally, diagonally and antidiagonally with exactly two mistakes, and colors introduced in row-major 0..2 order.

> Empirical: a(n) = 54*a(n-1) - 1140*a(n-2) + 11637*a(n-3) - 54252*a(n-4) + 33192*a(n-5) + 658756*a(n-6) - 1921212*a(n-7) - 1566516*a(n-8) + 12850374*a(n-9) - 7108644*a(n-10) - 35018232*a(n-11) + 43506046*a(n-12) + 38500440*a(n-13) - 85687812*a(n-14) - 200796*a(n-15) + 75241308*a(n-16) - 31685592*a(n-17) - 25769200*a(n-18) + 20878068*a(n-19) + 213348*a(n-20) - 3655770*a(n-21) + 608916*a(n-22) + 230904*a(n-23) - 61761*a(n-24) - 2790*a(n-25) + 1800*a(n-26) - 125*a(n-27) for n > 30.

The offset is one. The registered claim includes all 27 signed lags over the integers at every natural index $n\ge31$. It counts actual arrays $f : \operatorname{Fin} n\times\operatorname{Fin}6\to\operatorname{Fin}3$ up to the first-occurrence rule. ## Motivation

With a positive coupling constant, the three-state Potts antiferromagnet on this strip pays one unit of energy per monochromatic bond. The recurrence gives an exact computation at arbitrary strip length from earlier literal counts. The width-six one-defect count A229532 and width-five two-defect count A229634 use neighbouring transfer problems.
