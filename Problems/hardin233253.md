# Barker's generating function for the five-column six-colour pair-exclusion strip

## Problem

[OEIS A233253](https://oeis.org/A233253), revision 7, by R. H. Hardin, has NAME

> Number of n X 5 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

and FORMULA

> Empirical g.f.: 4*x*(9 - 1120*x + 30720*x^2) / ((1 - 32*x)*(1 - 480*x + 18432*x^2)). - _Colin Barker_, Oct 11 2018

Each row is a path of five cells, and consecutive rows are joined by the four
antidiagonal bonds $(i,j)$–$(i+1,j-1)$, $1\le j\le4$: a five-leg strip of the
triangular lattice without vertical or main-diagonal bonds. A bond is legal
when its endpoints differ and do not sum to $5$.

### What the settlement shows

The two factors of the denominator are the two sectors of the row-major
normalization: $1-32x$ counts arrays that never use colour $2$ (hence only
the pairs $\{0,5\}$ and $\{1,4\}$, with $2^5$ continuations per row), and
$1-480x+18432x^2$ is the nonzero part of the four-class transfer once all
three pairs are available. Width $3$ has a one-dimensional second sector
($1-48x$); at width $5$ the boundary shapes no longer collapse to one class,
which is why the second factor becomes quadratic.
