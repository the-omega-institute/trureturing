# Literal six-column king strips

## Problem

OEIS A233166, revision 8, defines:

> Number of n X 6 0..7 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 7-x(i,j) horizontally, diagonally, antidiagonally or vertically, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 4 5 and 6, 2 appearing before 3 4 and 5, and 3 appearing before 4 in row major order (unlabelled 8-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

Its formula block states:

> Empirical: a(n) = 192*a(n-1) - 8192*a(n-2) for n>3.
>
> Conjectures from _Colin Barker_, Oct 09 2018: (Start)
> G.f.: 4*x*(59 - 10304*x + 356352*x^2) / ((1 - 64*x)*(1 - 128*x)).
> a(n) = 64^(n-1) * (2^n+60) for n>1.
> (End)

For each natural height $n$, let $a(n)$ count all functions
$f:\operatorname{Fin}(n)\times\operatorname{Fin}(6)\to\operatorname{Fin}(8)$
satisfying the literal constraints. Boundaries are free. Earlier means
strictly smaller row-major rank $6i+j$. Absent colours are allowed;
colour 7 has no extra precedence condition. Functions themselves count,
without a quotient or surjectivity requirement. Put $a(0)=0$ only for the
formal series $F(X)=\sum_{n\geq0}(a(n):\mathbb Q)X^n$.
The full assertion is

$$
a(1)=236,\qquad
F(X)=\frac{4X(59-10304X+356352X^2)}{(1-64X)(1-128X)},
$$
$$
\forall n\in\mathbb N,\quad n\geq2\Longrightarrow
a(n)=64^{n-1}(2^n+60),
$$
$$
\forall n\in\mathbb N,\quad n\geq4\Longrightarrow
(a(n):\mathbb Z)=192(a(n-1):\mathbb Z)-8192(a(n-2):\mathbb Z).
$$

Division in $\mathbb Q[[X]]$ denotes the unique formal inverse of the
denominator, whose constant coefficient is one.

## Route

differences on opposite edges propagate the first-row and first-column
decomposition. For each pair colouring, first occurrences have their lower
literal representative and every other cell has an independent Boolean
sign. At height at least two all four pair labels occur. The exact sign
fibre and the bijection between canonical labelings and the twenty-four
label permutations give the literal-to-labelled counting relation.

The zone bijection reduces the labelled count to vertical walks for each
first-row offset word. A kernel-checked finite classification of those

and one hundred fifty with none. The walk length remains arbitrary. The
resulting literal formula yields the integer recurrence. The exceptional
one-row weighted count and coefficient comparison give the whole formal
series identity.
