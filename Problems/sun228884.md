# Composite prime divisors of the gcd determinant

## Problem

Zhi-Wei Sun's OEIS A228884, COMMENTS clause (ii), states: “For any composite
number $n$, all prime divisors of $a(n)$ are smaller than $n$.” The source
defines $a(n)$ as the determinant of the $n\times n$ matrix whose $(i,j)$
entry is $gcd(i-j,n)$ for $1\leq i,j\leq n$.

The formal claim uses the literal integer-entry matrix `C`, its determinant
`D`, the hypotheses $4\leq n$ and $\neg\operatorname{Nat.Prime}(n)$, and
integer divisibility by a natural prime $q$.

## Motivation

The result settles the composite-index prime-support assertion for the source's
gcd determinant while retaining the source's separate positivity and
divisibility assertions as distinct questions.

## Gap

The source entry contains no proof of clause (ii). The settlement supplies the
unbounded proof for every composite index represented by the formal claim.
Priority and completeness beyond the cited OEIS source remain
`ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED`.

## Route

CRT reindexing gives $D(uv)=D(u)^vD(v)^u$ for coprime positive $u,v$. The gcd
Fourier transform gives an integer eigenvalue product on each prime-power
component. For exponent at least two, every prime divisor of a local factor is
below the prime power; exponent-one factors are bounded by $2p-1$, and the
remaining composite cofactor gives $2p\leq n$.

## Falsifier

An index $n\geq4$ with $\neg\operatorname{Nat.Prime}(n)$ and a natural prime
$q\mid D(n)$ with $q\geq n$ would refute the settled claim. The finite checks
below are evidence for bounded ranges only and do not replace the kernel proof.
