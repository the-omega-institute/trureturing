# Barker's generating function for the kisrhombille coordination sequence at a tetravalent node

## Problem

[OEIS A298040](https://oeis.org/A298040), revision 36, by N. J. A. Sloane, has NAME

> Coordination sequence of Dual(4.6.12) tiling with respect to a tetravalent node.

and FORMULA

> Conjectures from _Colin Barker_, Apr 01 2020: (Start)
>
> G.f.: (1 + 4*x + 18*x^2 + 16*x^3 + x^4 - 4*x^5) / ((1 - x)^2*(1 + x)^2).

The tiling is the barycentric subdivision of the triangular lattice. Its
vertices are the vertices, edges and faces of the triangular lattice, and two
of them are joined when one contains the other. The tetravalent nodes are the
edge midpoints. The claim is the full generating function of the graph
distance shells about such a node, including the $n=0$ term.

## Gap

preregistration's bounded screen found no proof for this root. A later reading

also found none.

## Route

1. **Distance formula.** An explicit edge-rooted potential vanishes only at the
   root and changes by at most one along each adjacency. The general bound
   `VertexPositionRatioRefutation.potential_dist` gives distance $\ge$
   potential. Every simplex of positive potential has an adjacent simplex of
   potential one less, so strong induction on the potential constructs a walk
   attaining it. Hence the distance equals the potential.
2. **Balls.** Each of the six coordinate slices of a ball is one of the
   following:
   - a polygon shared with the face-rooted sequence;
   - the Tea hexagon `TeaCoordinationSequence.hexagon_card`;
   - a polygon with two disjoint rows.

   Explicit affine bijections identify the two face counts with each other and
   the two non-horizontal edge counts with each other. This gives
   $|B_{2k+1}|=18k^2+26k+5$ and $|B_{2k+2}|=18k^2+46k+25$.
3. **Shells and series.** Taking differences gives $a(0)=1$, $a(1)=4$,
   $a(2k+2)=20(k+1)$ and $a(2k+3)=16k+24$. Multiplying the series by
   $(1-X^2)^2$ leaves exactly the numerator.

## Falsifier

A shell around the edge root whose size differs from the corresponding
coefficient would contradict `result`.

## Triage

1. **Proved (kernel-checked):** Barker's generating function for the
   coordination sequence at a tetravalent node, by
   `KisrhombilleEdgeCoordination.result`.
2. **Proved (kernel-checked), internal:** $a(n)=(9+(-1)^n)\,n$ for every
   $n\ge2$. This is Barker's closed form "for n>1". These are private steps of
   `result`.
3. **Proved (kernel-checked), internal:** $a(1)=4$. Consequently the entry's
   other line, "For n>0, a(n)=10n if n even, otherwise 8n", is false at $n=1$,
   where it gives $8$. It holds for all $n\ge2$. That line disagrees with the
   entry's own data and was not registered as a target.
4. **Open:** the kisrhombille coordination sequence at a vertex node is the
   12-valent root A298036, whose OEIS entry records a proof note by Charlie Neder (2019). The other
   Laves tilings are handled separately; the trivalent cem-d node A298022 is
   preregistered as #27565.

### What the settlement shows

Moving the root from a face to an edge keeps the same polygonal ball structure
as the face-rooted case. Only the potential offsets change, and the
period-two linear shells $10n$ and $8n$ appear from radius two onward. The
single exceptional term $a(1)=4$ is the degree of the edge node. It is the only
place where the closed form starts late.
