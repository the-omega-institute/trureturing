# Bilgici Question 1, algebraic existence clause

## Problem

> Is F₁₁ congruent to a quartic polynomial times S_Z modulo the ideal I_Θ generated by the three-term relations, and can this be shown without exhaustive computation?

The final sentences ask for a direct proof in the polynomial ring and ask whether doubling covariance accounts for the factorization. [Section 8, Question 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24516v1#S8.I1.i1)

## Motivation

A factorization in the theta quotient supplies the quartic sought in Question 1. The paper's evaluation map kills every theta generator, so applying it to the factorization also gives an explicit cofactor for the evaluated component. [Sections 7–8](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24516v1#S7.SS1)

## Route

The orbit sum A(p) sums 5ʲTʲ(p) for j from 0 through 8, where T(Zₓ) = (−1)ˣ⁺¹Z₂ₓ. The quartic is A(6Z₁Z₂Z₃Z₉ + 14Z₁²Z₅Z₇). The complete coefficient identity expresses [BC³]₁₁ − S_ZQ as thirteen scalar multiples of terms A(MΘ(a,b,c,d)) with M cubic. Doubling transports each integer theta generator to the generator at the doubled tuple, so every term lies in the ideal.

For quotient nonvanishing, substitute Zⱼ ↦ X raised to the nonnegative integer tⱼ and reduce modulo X¹⁰. Signed-index rules make each product Z at u+v times Z at u−v invariant under reducing either argument modulo 19 and changing its sign. The tuples in {0,…,9}⁴ therefore suffice to check that every theta generator maps to zero. The image of Q has coefficient 1 at X⁹, so Q survives in the quotient.

The structural proof works in the same global ideal. Under the univariate trace, the two leading terms of a strictly ordered theta generator cancel. Its remaining order is at least 55, and the tuple (9, 5, 4, 0) attains 55. Alternation and the signed-index rules give the exact image ideal (X⁵⁵) under the untruncated polynomial-valued trace for all integer tuples. Reducing the trace modulo X⁵⁵ gives a map from the theta quotient to F₁₉[X]/(X⁵⁵), so its coefficients in degrees strictly below 55 are well-defined on quotient classes. Over the dual numbers, the signed index r evaluates to r + εr⁵. On paired indices this evaluation is a two-by-two determinant in the two squared labels, so the Plücker relation annihilates theta. Taking the trace coefficient in degree 11 and the ε coefficient gives two linear tests on the quotient.

Straightening uses the multiplicities of a centered degree-seven word and a secondary rank on normalized residues. Re-pairing preserves the centered condition and lowers the lexicographic rank in the induction. The resulting four-word span bounds the dimension of the character-four, degree-seven, weight-eleven component. Two projected words give independent detector columns, and trace coefficients in degrees 30 and 49 distinguish two independent cubic multiples. These four classes form a basis. The kernel of the joint test is exactly the image of multiplication by S_Z from degree four, weight nine, character four. The class of the word 5899999 after character projection has nonzero first detector, so covariance and the two grades alone cannot imply divisibility.

A signed full-field expression for the literal component proves its character and identifies its ε test with a five-variable moment of total degree at most 54. The moment vanishes because every monomial has an exponent below 18 in at least one coordinate. The trace has order 30 and leading coefficient 1, which both kills the degree-11 test and certifies that the class survives. The joint kernel gives divisibility, and the trace coordinates give a homogeneous quartic representative with nonzero quotient class.

The local finite checks cover the 15 multiplicity profiles of seven, with up to four free labels after normalization in the direct cases. Two complement checks handle the squarefree and single-double cases. The proof checks 15 specialized re-pairing relations, six signed scaling specializations, and the scalar change of generators. It also checks the three terms of S_Z, four nine-step projected words, the signed field and positive-pair index changes, and the lowest trace terms from the 36 pairs in B and the nine indices in C. The proof uses universal theta identities, well-founded induction on arbitrary centered words, and moment vanishing on the full field. It avoids enumerating the theta generators, the degree-seven word orbits and their support tables, and the coefficients of F₁₁. The paper gives no cutoff for what counts as exhaustive computation. ## Falsifier

A coefficient discrepancy disproves the polynomial certificate. Changing the first theta row coefficient from 3 to 4 changes the certificate-side coefficient of Z₁²Z₂Z₃²Z₄Z₆ from 15 to 13. Any generator with a nonzero trace coefficient below X⁵⁵ would invalidate the structural trace bound. The ordered tuple (9, 5, 4, 0) checks its sharpness.

### What the settlement shows

- **Proved.** The specified homogeneous quartic gives the factorization and survives in the theta quotient. Both conclusions are in `D5/S3/Arith/Partitions/BilgiciThetaQuartic.result`. The decisive coefficient identity gives explicit polynomial multiples of theta generators.
