# Hardin's recurrence for width-five eight-colour arrays

## Problem

For each positive height $n$, let $a(n)$ count the arrays
$\operatorname{Fin}(n)\times\operatorname{Fin}(5)\to\operatorname{Fin}(8)$
with colour $0$ at $(0,0)$. Each contained horizontal bond
$(i,j)$–$(i,j+1)$, vertical bond $(i,j)$–$(i+1,j)$ and antidiagonal bond
$(i,j+1)$–$(i+1,j)$ must join unequal colours whose natural labels do not
sum to $7$. In the strict row-major order of $5i+j$, each occurrence of
$2,3,4,5,6$ requires an earlier $1$, each occurrence of $3,4,5$ requires
an earlier $2$, and each occurrence of $4$ requires an earlier $3$.
Colours may be absent.

With OEIS offset `1,1`, the empirical assertion is that for every
natural $n\ge 12$, in integer arithmetic,

$$
\begin{aligned}
a(n)={}&736a(n-1)-203776a(n-2)+33062912a(n-3)-3665821696a(n-4)\\
       &+270851375104a(n-5)-10685878632448a(n-6)-116548232544256a(n-7)\\
       &+35184372088832000a(n-8)-1423137482249076736a(n-9)
       +18446744073709551616a(n-10).
\end{aligned}
$$

## Motivation

The frozen width-counting bridge for the eight-colour strips expresses
the literal array cardinality as a sum over anchored first rows and
their continuations. The width-five problem uses that bridge with the
same bond rule and strict first-use prerequisites. [Issue #34599](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/34599)

## Gap

The pinned OEIS entry labels the recurrence empirical. The ordinary
free-strip chromatic polynomial counts proper colourings. Recovering
the literal array count requires the choices of representatives within
each complementary pair and the global first-use restrictions.
[Ordinary strip transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0204587v3)

## Route

For a colour $c$, use the pair label $\min(c,7-c)$. The continuation
depends on the equality pattern of the boundary row's pair labels and
the lower representatives $1,2,3$ seen in the prefix. Relabelling
eligible pairs preserves their orientation and gives $1$, $8$, and
$14$ boundary classes at the three post-row stages.

The transition matrix $T$ counts admissible rows between classes, and
the vector $u$ counts admissible anchored first rows by class. The
kernel-checked counts yield $a(n)=u\cdot T^{n-1}\mathbf{1}$ for every
$n\ge 1$. The relation at $T^{11}\mathbf{1}$ uses powers ten through
one with the displayed coefficients. Multiplication by further powers
of $T$ propagates it to every claimed height.

## Falsifier

A height $n\ge 12$ at which the literal cardinality differs from the
displayed integer sum would refute the assertion.
