# Hardin's recurrence for the width-5 two-defect king strip

## Problem

[OEIS A229752](https://oeis.org/A229752) has the following verbatim NAME:

> Number of defective 4-colorings of an n X 5 0..3 array connected horizontally, vertically, diagonally and antidiagonally with exactly two mistakes, and colors introduced in row-major 0..3 order.

Its FORMULA lines, verbatim from the
[text export](https://oeis.org/search?q=id:A229752&fmt=text), are:

```text
Empirical: a(n) = 9*a(n-1) - 33*a(n-2) + 63*a(n-3) - 66*a(n-4) + 36*a(n-5) - 8*a(n-6) for n>11.
Empirical g.f.: x*(12 + 1732*x - 9355*x^2 + 13758*x^3 + 3670*x^4 - 20791*x^5 + 10370*x^6 + 686*x^7 - 1124*x^8 - 440*x^9 + 208*x^10) / ((1 - x)^3*(1 - 2*x)^3). - _Colin Barker_, Sep 21 2018
```

The assertions are registered in
[issue #30655](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/30655).

Let $T_2(n,k)$ denote `KingTwoGrid.T n k`, the literal count of functions
$\operatorname{Fin}(n)\times\operatorname{Fin}(k)\to\operatorname{Fin}(4)$
with exactly two monochromatic bonds and canonical first appearances of
colours in row-major order. Horizontal, vertical, diagonal and antidiagonal
bonds are each counted once when both endpoints lie in the rectangle.
The boundaries are free. Canonical first appearances identify colour
relabellings while keeping cell positions distinct.

Here $a(n)=T_2(n,5)$, column $5$ of A229755. Claim C5 is the
quoted recurrence for every natural $n>11$, equivalently $n\ge12$,
with counts cast to $\mathbb Z$.
The quoted empirical generating function is not a separate registered
claim and is not asserted by this result.

## Motivation

For the physics reading, these are states of the four-state Potts
antiferromagnet on king-lattice strips: the square lattice with both
diagonals in each elementary square. With equal positive penalties for
monochromatic bonds, the counted states have exactly two unsatisfied
bonds. The count is the degeneracy at that fixed bond count up to
relabelling of the four states. The statements concern finite canonical
configuration counts.

The settling declaration is `D5/S3/StatisticalMechanics/Potts/KingDefects/HardinA229752.result`.
The source bibkey is `hardin2013a229752`.

## Route

1. **Exact defect budget.** Use `DefectTransfer` with $q=4$ and
   total budget $D=2$. The boundary is a row $p$ and a remaining budget
   $d\in\{0,1,2\}$. The in-row set $H$ consists of horizontal bonds;
   the between-row set $V$ consists of vertical, diagonal and antidiagonal
   bonds. Appending $r$ costs
   $\operatorname{bondDefects}(V,p,r)+\operatorname{bondDefects}(H,r,r)$.
   A transition is allowed only when that cost is at most $d$, and it
   subtracts the cost from $d$. The initial weights charge the first row's
   horizontal defects; the terminal indicator accepts exactly $d=0$.
   `Width5.Certificate.transition_counts` proves the successor multiplicities
   for all valid boundaries, including their colour relabellings. Together
   with `Width5.Forward.structural_certificate`, these discharge
   `DefectTransfer.lumped_count` for the 43-class integer transfer.

2. **Reuse of zero-temperature structure.** `Width5.ZeroTemperature.raw_iff_owner` transports the proper
   zero-budget block to the frozen width-six trace-avoiding king owner
   `HardinA233166KingStrip` through `Width6.ZeroTemperature.raw_iff_shift`.
   It extends a width-five row $p$ to
   $(p_0,p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4,p_3)$ and proves equivalence of the zero-cost
   relations before restricting successors back to width five.
   `Width5.ZeroTemperature.transition_counts` then counts the exact allowed
   shifts by their transfer classes.

3. **Literal grid and canonical colours.** `GridBridge.defects_rows`
   splits the row-list cost into the four literal king-bond sums, and
   `GridBridge.literal_bridge` identifies the labelled transfer count with
   `KingTwoGrid.L`. `KingTwoGrid.labelled_eq_twentyFour` gives
   $L(n,5)=24T_2(n,5)$ for $n\ge2$. Its three-colour condition uses the
   two-colour $2\times3$ obstruction and an injective grid embedding;
   `KingGrid.canonical_count_three` supplies the canonical normalization.
   In general this normalization excludes the single $2\times2$ rectangle;
   that exception does not occur at width 5.

4. **Forward certificate and propagation.** Write $M$ for `Data.T` and
   $W_i=\operatorname{forward}(M,\mathrm{initial},i)$.
   `Width5.Forward.structural_certificate` pins the initial vector,
   successive row-vector multiplications, and

   $$
   W_{11}=-8W_{5}+36W_{6}-66W_{7}
       +63W_{8}-33W_{9}+9W_{10}.
   $$

   `DefectTransfer.forward_shift` propagates this relation after every
   further transfer multiplication. Pairing with the terminal vector,
   using the literal bridge and cancelling $24$ gives the stated recurrence
   for every $n\ge12$.

## Falsifier

A natural height $n>11$ for which the literal $T_2(n,5)$ violates
the quoted recurrence would contradict `HardinA229752.result`.

## Evidence

The formal evidence for C5 is `D5/S3/StatisticalMechanics/Potts/KingDefects/HardinA229752.result`, stated over the literal
