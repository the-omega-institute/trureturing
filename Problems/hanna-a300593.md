# Hanna’s OEIS A300593 ordinary integrality conjecture

## Problem

Paul D. Hanna asks whether every coefficient of the normalized ordinary
series satisfying
$[x^n]\exp(n^2F)=n^3[x^{n-1}]\exp(n^2F)$ for $n\ge1$ is an integer.
[OEIS A300593](https://oeis.org/A300593/internal) · [Source note](../Library/RecurrenceAlgebraic/hanna2018a300593.md) · [Preregistration #32030](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/32030)

| Formal power law | `expSeries ((n : ℚ)^2) F = (expSeries 1 F)^(n^2)` for every natural `n` |
| A300592 EGF reconstruction | `eCoeff B r / r!` reconstructs `B` in every ordinary degree `r ≥ 0` |

## Motivation

The conjecture asks for all-degree integrality of a rational series defined
by a coefficient recurrence. Its companion formulation identifies the same
ordinary series as the logarithm of the normalized A300592 EGF.
[OEIS A300592](https://oeis.org/A300592/internal)

## Gap

Triangularity gives a unique rational solution. Integrality also requires
ruling out every prime divisor of each coefficient's reduced denominator
despite the division by $n^2$ in the triangular residual.

## Route

Triangular existence and uniqueness use the rational model with separate
scalar functions $n^2$ and $n^3$. In the integrality induction, the strict
prefix $P$ uses the earlier integer coefficients and has zero coefficient
in degree $n$. Coefficient agreement transfers each positive lower equation
to $P$. For a fixed $n$, write
$A=[x^{n-1}]\exp(n^2P)$ and $B_n=[x^n]\exp(n^2P)$.
The triangular residual gives $[x^n]F=nA-B_n/n^2$.

For $p\nmid n$, the actual pointed permutation action gains one independent
ambient vertex. Its fixed-point sum at a permutation with $f$ fixed vertices
gains a factor $f$. Combining this with the full and ordinary pointed sums
identifies the enlarged subtraction as
$Mf!([x^f]\exp(f^2P)-f^3[x^{f-1}]\exp(f^2P))$, where $M$ is the moved-block
weight sum. Nonidentity Sylow elements have $1\le f<n$ and contribute zero
by the lower equations. The identity has $f=n$ and $M=1$. The signed Burnside
divisor is $p^{v_p(n!)}$, so cancellation gives nonnegative valuation of
$B_n-n^3A$. The scalar $n^2$ is a unit at this prime.

For $p\mid n$, let $s=v_p(n)\ge1$. The constant exponential summand
contributes zero to both positive degrees. The linear summand also
contributes zero to degree $n$ because $[x^n]P=0$. The bounds in
`HannaA300591RationalModel.exp_prefix_bound` give $v_p(nA)\ge3s$ and
$v_p(B_n/n^2)\ge2s-1$ for the respective nonzero terms. The resulting
nonnegative valuation rules out a prime in the reduced denominator in this
case as well.

The rational model's formal exponential power law and exp/log inverse
identities transfer the two equations in both directions. The factorial
EGF convention reconstructs the normalized companion series degree by degree.

## Falsifier

A rational series satisfying `IsSource` with a coefficient in any natural
degree whose reduced denominator exceeds one would refute the conjecture.
