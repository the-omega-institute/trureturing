# Barker's generating function for the trivalent cem-d coordination sequence

## Problem

[OEIS A298022](https://oeis.org/A298022), revision 48, by N. J. A. Sloane, has NAME

> Coordination sequence for Dual(3^3.4^2) tiling with respect to a trivalent node.

and FORMULA

> Conjectures from Colin Barker, Jan 22 2018: (Start)
> G.f.: (1 + 2*x + 4*x^2 + 4*x^3 + 3*x^4 + 2*x^5 - 2*x^8) / ((1 - x)^2*(1 + x + x^2)).
> a(n) = a(n-1) + a(n-3) - a(n-4) for n>5. (End)

The dual of the elongated triangular tiling $3^3.4^2$ is the prismatic
pentagonal tiling, the cem-d net, one of the eleven Laves tilings. Its
trivalent nodes are the triangles of $3^3.4^2$.

## Motivation

The settling declaration is
`D5.S3.StatisticalMechanics.ElongatedTriangularDualCoordination.result`. The
external assertion and its conventions are preregistered in
[issue #27565](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/27565).
The source note is `Library/Geometry/sloane2018a298022.md`. Coordination
sequences of periodic nets are crystallographic shell counts. The companion
tetravalent sequence A298024 is confirmed in the literature, but the
trivalent sequence was not.

## Gap

The entry labels the generating function a conjecture. The preregistration's
bounded screen found that Shutov–Maleev (2020) cover the 2-uniform graphs but
contain no cem-d section, and that their A298024 confirmation does not
transfer to the trivalent node.

## Route

1. **Metric.** An explicit nonnegative potential on the three tile types
   changes by at most one along every edge. The general bound
   `VertexPositionRatioRefutation.potential_dist` gives the lower bound.
   Every non-root vertex has a neighbour of potential one less, so a geodesic
   attains the potential. The graph distance therefore equals the potential.
2. **Shells.** A coordinate equivalence identifies each distance shell with a
   finite union of six strip families and two central rows. This proves that
   every shell is finite and gives its size.
3. **Series.** For $n\ge5$,
   $a(n)=14\lfloor n/3\rfloor+(0,5,9)$ according to $n\bmod 3$. Indices
   $0,\ldots,8$ are normalized individually, and coefficient extensionality
   gives the power-series identity.

## Falsifier

A radius $n$ whose shell size differs from the coefficient of $x^n$ in
Barker's generating function would contradict `result`.

## Evidence

The kernel declaration is
`D5.S3.StatisticalMechanics.ElongatedTriangularDualCoordination.result`. Its

## Triage

1. **Proved (kernel-checked):** Barker's generating function for every
   coefficient, by `ElongatedTriangularDualCoordination.result`.
2. **Computed from item 1 (no separate Lean statement):** the printed recurrence
   $a(n)=a(n-1)+a(n-3)-a(n-4)$ fails at $n=8$, where it gives
   $33+23-17=39$ while $a(8)=37$. It holds for every $n\ge9$, because the
   numerator has degree $8$ and the denominator
   $(1-x)^2(1+x+x^2)=1-x-x^3+x^4$ has degree $4$. The entry's range "$n>5$"
   should read "$n>8$".
3. **Proved (kernel-checked), internal:** the closed form
   $a(n)=14\lfloor n/3\rfloor+(0,5,9)_{n\bmod3}$ for $n\ge5$.
4. **Open:** the coordination sequences of the remaining Laves duals whose
   trivalent or higher nodes still carry only conjectural generating
   functions.

### What the settlement shows

The cem-d net has growth period three, the length of its strip pattern, and
gains $14$ vertices per period. The single correction term $-2x^8$ in the
numerator records where the boundary of the ball first meets the square
strips on both sides of the root. That interaction is also why the
homogeneous recurrence starts three steps later than printed. The
potential-plus-geodesic method that settles the kisrhombille and honeycomb
cases settles this Laves dual unchanged.
