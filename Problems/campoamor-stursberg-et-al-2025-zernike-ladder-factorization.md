# Factorized ladders for generalized quantum Zernike Hamiltonians

## Problem

Campoamor-Stursberg, Herranz, Latini, Marquette and Blasco, Nonlinearity 39, 055008, arXiv:2502.02491v2, Conjecture 1(iii), asks for number and ladder operators constructed from the quantum symmetries (2.8), satisfying (2.9)–(2.10) with the factorization (5.1) for every $N\geq1$. Preregistration: [#19957](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/19957).

The binding polynomial formulation is proved by `D5.S3.Quantum.CampoamorStursbergZernikeLadder.result`. Operators are complex linear endomorphisms of $\mathbb C[q_1,q_2]$. For every $N\geq1$ and every complex coefficient sequence there are two symmetries with the kinetic terms and correction span of (2.6), and number and ladder operators in the unital algebra generated by $K_1,K_2,K_3$, satisfying all four identities of the claim.

## Route

Write $D=q\cdot p$, $C=q_1p_2-q_2p_1$, $x=p_1+ip_2$, $y=p_1-ip_2$, $q_+=q_1+iq_2$, $q_-=q_1-iq_2$, and $F(t)=\sum_{k=1}^N\gamma_k t^k$. The polynomial divided difference is

$$Q_F(s,t)=\sum_{k=1}^N\gamma_k\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}s^{k-1-j}t^j.$$

For commuting $s,t$, telescoping the geometric sum gives $F(s)-F(t)=(s-t)Q_F(s,t)$. This identity is an explanation of the mechanism, not a separately exported theorem. Core `ProductIdentity.factorization` supplies the abstract ordered-product identities; `HamiltonianIdentity.symmetry` supplies the corresponding commutation identity.

The explicit ladders are

$$K_+=\tfrac12\bigl(x^2+xq_+Q_F(D,i(C+2))\bigr),\qquad K_-=\tfrac12\bigl(y^2+yq_-Q_F(D,i(2-C))\bigr),\qquad K=C/2.$$

Weyl shift relations establish both ordered products directly in the endomorphism ring. Set $I=H/2-(K_++K_-)/2$ and $I'=H/2+(K_++K_-)/2$. Normal ordering puts their corrections in the defining span. The identities $K_2=(K_++K_-)/2$ and $K_3=K_+-K_-$ reconstruct the ladders in the required generated algebra. These statements describe the construction used by `result`; no localization or faithful-localization bridge is used.
