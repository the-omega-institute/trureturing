# Ordinary generation in Conjecture 4.2

## Problem

Klanderman, Lewis, Monson, Shibata and Van Niel, *Characterizing Transfer Systems
for Non-Abelian Groups*, arXiv:2511.13439v2, Conjecture 4.2, printed page 15:

> Let T be a G-transfer system with two connected components, one containing e
> and one containing G. If the connected component containing G only has the
> edge G → G, then T is not LSP and is compatible with the transfer system
> generated by adding the edge e → G to T.

The paper assumes finite G throughout. The quantified assertion is the whole
conjunction over all finite groups and all actual-subgroup transfer systems,
with source-ordered compatibility and ordinary least transfer generation.
Definitions, source hashes, license and bounded literature status are in the
[owning source note](../Library/FiniteGroups/klanderman2025transfersystems.md).
The unchanged full event is preregistered in
[issue 26660](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/26660#issuecomment-6642151632).

## Motivation

The source decorates transfer-system lattices by saturation, cosaturation and
lesser simple pairing. Conjecture 4.2 proposes a component criterion for LSP
behavior and an explicit compatible extension. The distinction between an
ordinary transfer closure and a compatibility closure is decisive here.

## Gap

The bounded source, library and later-paper checks in the source note found no
explicit resolution of the exact assertion. Rubin supplies ordinary least
generation; existing compatible-pair literature supplies a separate additional
closure. Neither is silently identified with the other. These are bounded
findings, not a certificate of worldwide novelty.

## Route

Use Mathlib's actual Q8 = QuaternionGroup 2 and subgroups
Z = zpowers(a 2), A = zpowers(a 1), with e < Z < A < Q8.
Let T(K,H) mean K = H or (K = e and H is proper). It satisfies all five
transfer axioms. All proper subgroups lie in e's component and the whole group
is isolated, so there are exactly the two required actual graph components.

Its ordinary generated extension U has precisely K = H or K = e: the displayed
relation is a transfer system containing the generators, and restriction of
e → Q8 gives all e → H. Source compatibility at B = e, C = Z and ambient A
would require Z → A, which is absent because e < Z < A.

## Falsifier

The formal refutation would miss the target if it omitted non-LSP from the
claim, reversed the ordered pair, used proxy components or selected subgroups,
weakened the transfer axioms, or replaced ordinary generation by saturation or
compatibility closure. The closed claim retains both conjuncts; only the second
is used in its negation. All relations range over Subgroup G and graph
components are Mathlib SimpleGraph.ConnectedComponent values. The generated
system is defined by ordinary leastness and identified on every subgroup pair.

### What the settlement shows

**Proved by `result`:** the full universal conjunction is false. In the actual
Q8 witness, ordinary closure supplies all bottom-source arrows and identities
but omits Z → A, which compatibility demands. The strict chain
e < Z < A < G exposes that difference. The conjunction is refuted through its
compatibility clause; this makes no separate claim that non-LSP is false.

Open: whether the non-LSP assertion holds under the stated component
hypotheses, and which additional hypothesis repairs ordinary-generation
compatibility. Compatibility closure is a different operation, so substituting
it is a new assertion requiring its own source and proof. The primary's
Conjecture 4.1 and group-width theorems are separate; no change to those
statements is established here.
