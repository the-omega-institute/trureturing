# Integer existence and dyadic parity for OEIS A397594

## Problem

Paul D. Hanna's OEIS A397594, revision 15, states:

> G.f. A(x) such that the sum of the first n coefficients in A(x/n)^n equals 4^(n-1) for n >= 1.

Its FORMULA gives:

> (1) 4^(n-1) = Sum_{k=0..n-1} [x^k] A(x/n)^n for n >= 1.
> (2) (4*n)^(n-1) = [x^(n-1)] A(x)^n / (1 - n*x) for n >= 1.

The conjecture is:

> Conjecture: for n > 3, a(n) is odd iff n is of the form 2^k-1 or 2^k+1 (k > 1).

The series is $A(X)=\sum_{n\geq0}a(n)X^n\in\mathbb Z[[X]]$, with
$a(0)=1$. Rational rescaling means coefficientwise mapping to
$\mathbb Q[[X]]$ followed by $X\mapsto X/m$. Powers are ordinary formal
products. There is no analytic convergence assumption.

## Route

Write $R_n(A)=[X^n]A^{n+1}/(1-(n+1)X)$ and let $N_n(A)$ be the
existing normalized row operator. Clearing formula (1)'s denominators gives
$R_n(A)=(4(n+1))^n$. The imported `exact_normalization` gives
$R_n(A)=(n+1)N_n(A)$ for $n>0$. Cancellation in $\mathbb Z$ therefore
gives $N_n(A)=t_n=4^n(n+1)^{n-1}$.

Add the series with zero constant term and positive coefficients $t_n$ to
the imported correction map `advance`. The resulting map preserves constant
coefficient one and improves coefficient agreement by one degree, using
`advance_zero` and `advance_agree`. The imported
`PowerSeriesNormalizedContraction.exists_unique_fixed` supplies a fixed
integer series. Its fixed-point equation gives $N_n(A)=t_n$; the source
equivalence then gives formula (1).

For $n>0$, $t_n$ maps to zero in $\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z$. The imported
`normalized_map` and `normalized_unique` identify the source modulo two
with the imported homogeneous `solution`. The imported `hanna_conjecture`,
applied to `generatingSeries` using `generating_equation`, gives the stated
dyadic-neighbor parity criterion above degree three.

## Triage

1. **Proved in prose:** denominator clearing and triangular normalization
   give $t_n=4^n(n+1)^{n-1}$ for $n>0$. Indeed, the coefficient-rescaling
   formula turns the rational sum multiplied by $(n+1)^n$ into
   $\sum_{j=0}^n[X^j]A^{n+1}(n+1)^{n-j}=R_n(A)$.
   Formula (1) makes this $(n+1)^n4^n$. Dividing the integer identity
   $R_n(A)=(n+1)N_n(A)$ by the nonzero integer $n+1$ gives the asserted
   forcing. Reversing these equalities gives formula (1); the geometric
   convolution is formula (2). The row $m=1$ is exactly $a(0)=1$.
2. **Kernel-checked existence:** `result`'s first conjunct constructs an
   integer source using `exists_unique_fixed` on the forced correction map.
   **Proved in prose source equivalence:** the reversible equalities in item
   1 show that the normalized forcing and the two source equations coincide.
   This equivalence is also used locally as `hnormalized` in `result`.
3. **Kernel-checked parity:** `result`'s second conjunct gives exactly
   $n=2^k\pm1$ with $k>1$ for every $n>3$ and every integer source.
   **Proved in prose mechanism:** $4^n(n+1)^{n-1}$ is even for every
   positive $n$, so normalization commutes with reduction and its forcing
   vanishes. Normalized uniqueness supplies the homogeneous comparison
   described in Route, before the parity endpoint is applied.
4. **Computed:** the pinned experiment in Evidence reproduces degrees
   0–24, checks the two source identities through 39/100 respectively, and
   checks the odd indices through 100. The command exits 0 with the script
   SHA-256 stated there; no broader experimental range is asserted.
5. **Open:** arbitrary even parameters, full triangle integrality,
   positivity and asymptotics are outside `claim` and are not settled here.

### What the settlement shows

**Kernel-checked:** an integer source exists and every integer source has
the conjectured all-index parity support, by `result`.
**Proved in prose:** the decisive mechanism is integral normalization
before reduction modulo two; reducing the original equations before
cancelling $n+1$ would discard information in even rows.
The normalized uniqueness argument also shows uniqueness among integer
sources: two such sources have constant coefficient one and identical
normalized positive coefficients, so `normalized_unique` equates them.
This is a prose consequence, not an additional public Lean theorem.

**Open:** extension to arbitrary even parameters and a complete integral
parameter triangle requires further statements. The source's reversion
formulas (3)–(4), positivity and asymptotics receive no settlement from
this claim. The parity conjecture for parameter four is discharged; no
unproved dependency of another source formula is assumed resolved.
