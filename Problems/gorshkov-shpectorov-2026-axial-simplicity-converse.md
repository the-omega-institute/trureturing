# Gorshkov–Shpectorov Problem 3.20, converse implication

## Problem

> Is it true that the simplicity of an axial algebra $A$ implies the simplicity of the inner Lie algebra $DA_X$ for any generating set of axes $X$? Vice versa, does simplicity of $DA_X$ imply simplicity of $A$?

The second sentence is the converse in Problem 3.20, §3.3, p. 13 of arXiv:2606.30048v1, and Problem 2.20, §2.3, p. 17 of the journal edition. The Lie algebra $DA_X$ is the Lie subalgebra of the linear endomorphisms generated by $L_aL_b-L_bL_a$ for $a,b\in X$. The published question imposes no connectedness or indecomposability condition on the axial algebra. [Source and definition locators](../Library/LieTheory/gorshkovshpectorov2026axialquestions.md)

## Motivation

A multiplication ideal may be invisible to commutators of left multiplications. The converse asks whether Lie simplicity nevertheless guarantees simplicity of the axial algebra itself.

## Route

Adjoin a scalar summand $\mathbb Qz$ to the rational spin factor $J_3=\mathbb Q\oplus\mathbb Q^3$ with componentwise multiplication, where $z^2=z$. Choose the six signed spin axes $(1/2,\pm e_i/2)$ in the first summand and $z$. These seven axes span the five-dimensional carrier. Spin axes gain $\mathbb Qz$ in their zero eigenspaces. For $z$, the eigenspaces at $1,0,1/2$ are $\mathbb Qz,J_3,0$. The products respect the Jordan-half fusion law and the axes are primitive.

Every selected commutator acts by a skew block on the three vector coordinates and by zero on both scalar coordinates. The positive-coordinate axis commutators generate all elementary skew blocks after scaling by four, so the selected span is precisely the image of $\mathfrak{so}_3(\mathbb Q)$. The frozen `OrthogonalLieStructure.isSimple_of_card_ne_four` at `Fin 3` supplies its Lie simplicity and `LieRadicalTransfer.isSimple_of_equiv` transports the ideal dichotomy and nonabelianness through the actual selected-span equivalence. ## Falsifier

The scalar line $\mathbb Qz$ is a nonzero proper multiplication ideal. The inner Lie algebra of the specified seven axes is simple, while the axial algebra has that proper ideal.

`theorem`. The rational case supplies a counterexample to the second universal implication as printed.

### What the settlement shows

- **Refuted in Lean.** A finite-dimensional rational primitive Jordan-half axial algebra has a simple selected-axis inner Lie algebra and a nonzero proper algebra ideal. The witness is $J_3\oplus\mathbb Qz$ and the settling declaration is `AxialInnerLieConverseRefutation.result`.
- **Mechanism.** Adjoining the scalar summand leaves the selected commutator algebra acting trivially on the additional coordinate. The question with a connectedness or indecomposability restriction remains open here.
- The first sentence of the question has its own counterexample and separate refutation. [Forward dossier](gorshkov-shpectorov-2026-axial-simplicity-forward.md)
