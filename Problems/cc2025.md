# Costa–Catarino bidimensional Fibonacci matrices

## Problem

Costa and Catarino, *On Matrices with Bidimensional Fibonacci Numbers*, Annales Mathematicae Silesianae 40(1), 59–76 (2026), DOI 10.2478/amsil-2025-0014, state an unnumbered conjecture at the end of §4 (p. 73): every order-two bidimensional Fibonacci matrix has nonzero determinant for all nonnegative indices. The source definitions are Definition 2.1 (p. 63), Lemma 2.5 (p. 64), and Definition 3.1 (p. 67). Preregistration and the verbatim statement are in issue [#22448](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/22448).

## Route

The closed form `sourceClosedForm` satisfies both guarded recurrences and the four corner conditions. A two-stage order-two recurrence uniqueness argument identifies every source sequence with it. Expanding the determinant and taking its imaginary coordinate reduces the problem to a signed sum of consecutive Fibonacci products. The frozen Cassini/Vajda identity gives the sign relation, while positivity and strict monotonicity of the products rule out cancellation. The resulting theorem is `D5.S1.Recurrence.CostaCatarinoBidimensionalFibonacci.result`.

## Falsifier

A counterexample would be a natural pair `(m,n)` and a source sequence satisfying the four corners and both recurrences whose matrix determinant is zero. The delivered theorem rules out every such pair in the stated source scope. Generalized recurrence parameters, other corner data, and negative-index extensions are outside this claim.
