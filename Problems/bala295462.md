# Bala's A295462 binary-spacing parity conjecture

## Problem

Peter Bala's [OEIS A295462](https://oeis.org/A295462/internal), native record #19,
defines $a(n)=(15n)!(2n)!/((10n)!(6n)!n!)$ and states in its REFERENCES (%D) field:

> Conjecture: a(n) is odd iff the 1 bits in the binary expansion of n are separated by at least three 0's, that is, iff n belongs to A048718.

The offset includes $n=0$. The rational quotient is interpreted by exact division;
its natural realization is explicit before oddness is tested.
[Preregistration #22428](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/22428)
fixes the statement and conventions.

lowest bad pair: spacing annihilates every nonnegative valuation summand, whereas
the first gap below four forces one summand to be positive. The finite-support
