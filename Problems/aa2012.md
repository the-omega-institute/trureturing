# The Shapley allocation of a graph energy game can leave its core

## Problem

Gerardo Arizmendi and Octavio Arizmendi, *The graph energy game*,
arXiv:2012.14114v2, Conjecture 3.10, page 8:

> For the energy game, the Shapley value is in the core.

The source defines the energy of a finite simple undirected graph as the
sum of absolute adjacency eigenvalues, with multiplicity. Its game has
$w_G(S)=\mathcal E(G[S])$. For each vertex $i$ its allocation is

$$
\phi_i(w_G)=\sum_{S\subseteq V\setminus\{i\}}
\frac{|S|!(|V|-|S|-1)!}{|V|!}
\bigl(w_G(S\cup\{i\})-w_G(S)\bigr).
$$

The core consists of all $x$ satisfying
$\sum_{i\in S}x_i\ge w_G(S)$ for every coalition $S$ and
$\sum_{i\in V}x_i=w_G(V)$. The conjecture quantifies over every finite
simple graph, without connectedness, degree or bipartiteness assumptions.
[Preregistration #21951](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/21951)
binds this universal proposition and its expected refutation.
The [Library note](../Library/GraphInvariants/arizmendi2020energygame.md)
records the versioned source and the literal defining formulas.

## Motivation

A scarce-side vertex of $K_{2,m}$ has a large average marginal
contribution over predecessor coalitions. For $m\ge50$ this average exceeds
its marginal contribution to the grand coalition. Efficiency then conflicts
with the lower payoff bound of the coalition excluding that vertex.

## Route

For a coalition with $r$ vertices on the two-vertex side and $k$ vertices
on the other side, its energy is $2\sqrt{rk}$. The proof obtains this from
the adjacency matrix identities $A^3=rkA$ and $\operatorname{tr}(A^2)=2rk$.
The existing frozen declaration
`D5.S3.Arith.Lattices.Klartag.trace_mul_self_eq_sum_sq` supplies the
Hermitian trace-square identity used by `energy_of_cubic`.

Writing $q=\sqrt2$, $H=(m+1)(m+2)$,
$S_1=\sum_{k=0}^m\sqrt k$ and $S_3=\sum_{k=0}^m k\sqrt k$,
`scarce_payoff` gives

$$
\phi_a=\frac{2((m+q)S_1+(q-2)S_3)}{H}.
$$

The private sum bounds give
$S_1\ge\frac23m\sqrt m$ and
$S_3\le\frac25m^2\sqrt m+m\sqrt m$.
The sign $q-2<0$ requires the upper bound on $S_3$.
`scarce_formula_gap` establishes
$\phi_a>2(\sqrt2-1)\sqrt m$ for every $m\ge50$.
`family_not_core` combines this inequality with the efficiency conjunct
and the coalition inequality at $V\setminus\{a\}$ to derive a contradiction.
`result` specializes to $m=50$.

## Falsifier

The source claim would require both efficiency and every coalition
inequality on $K_{2,50}$. The contradiction in `family_not_core` rules out
that conjunction. No separately formalized universal efficiency theorem
is asserted. An exact prior literature settlement would remove the
novelty basis; the literature screen below is explicitly incomplete.

### What the settlement shows

The proved mechanism is an imbalance between a scarce-side vertex's
average marginal contribution and its grand-coalition marginal
contribution. `scarce_formula_gap` and `family_not_core` show that
connectedness and minimum degree 2 do not rescue the universal conjecture;
the two graph properties of the concrete witness are computed as item 3.
The sufficient bound $m\ge50$ is proved, without an optimality claim.

The nearest verified surviving example here is $P_3$, computed in item 4.
The source's vertex-energy allocation theorem concerns a different
allocation and survives this refutation. Proposition 3.11's marginal
lower bound and Proposition 4.13's assertion that the 2-energy Shapley
value equals degree are separate published statements; neither uses
Conjecture 3.10 in its proof. They are literature readings, not new Lean
results. No additional source conclusion is invalidated by this delivery.
