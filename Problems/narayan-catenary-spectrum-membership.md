# Narayan's catenary spectrum membership conjecture

## Problem

Ashwin Narayan, *The Delta Set of a Numerical Monoid Generated by a Generalized Arithmetic Sequence*, Conjecture 18.2 on p. 22, states

> $\left\lceil\frac{a-k}{k-1}\right\rceil+h+d\in C(S)$.

Section 3 on p. 2 takes positive natural numbers $a,h,d,k$ with $\gcd(a,d)=1$, $1<k<a$, and $h>1$. The numerical monoid $S$ has atoms $a,ah+d,\ldots,ah+kd$. This tuple supplies the coordinates of the full fiber $Z(n)$. For two factorizations, subtract their pointwise minimum and take the larger residual length. The catenary degree of $n\in S$ is the least threshold for which finite chains of edges at or below that distance join all pairs in $Z(n)$. The spectrum $C(S)$ consists of those elementwise degrees. [Pinned source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02495v1#page=22) · [Section 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02495v1#page=2) · [Distance and chains, Omidali §2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.1646v2) · [Library note](../Library/Factorization/narayan2026deltaset.md)

## Motivation

The conjecture asks for a specific elementwise catenary degree. Omidali's Theorem 3.8 computes the monoid-wide maximum $\lceil a/k\rceil h+d$. At $(a,h,d,k)=(5,2,1,2)$, the value in Conjecture 18.2 is six and the monoid-wide maximum is seven. [Tier 1 preregistration #33794](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/33794) · [Omidali, Theorem 3.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.1646v2)

## Gap

An element of the spectrum requires an actual represented value with a bounded chain connecting every pair and a strict obstruction at every smaller threshold. A pair at the conjectured distance alone does not establish that obstruction.

## Route

Let $t=\lceil(a-k)/(k-1)\rceil$, $N=t+h+d$, and $n=ah(t+1)+d(a+t)$. Write $Y=\sum_{i=1}^kz_i$ and $W=\sum_{i=1}^k iz_i$. Coprimality gives $W\equiv t\pmod a$. The value equation and the generator bounds restrict the fiber to two classes. The low class has $W=t$ and $z_0=h(t+1-Y)+d$. The high class has $W=a+t$, $Y=t+1$, and $z_0=0$. The low vector is $(h+d)e_0+te_1$. The high vector is $te_k+e_{2+\rho}$ for $\rho=(a-2)\bmod(k-1)$. The two terms add when the high indices coincide.

High vectors have zero copies of the first positive-index generator. Shared copies with a low vector have total at most $t-Y$. The low length is $N+(h-1)(t-Y)$, so the larger residual length of each cross pair is at least $N$. The exchange $ha+g_i=g_1+g_{i-1}$ for $i\ge 2$ preserves value and increases $Y$ by one. It connects the low class by edges at most $h+1$. Every high pair has distance at most $t+1$. The low vector lies at distance exactly $N$ from each high vector.

## Falsifier

A parameter tuple in the stated domain with no represented element of degree $N$ would refute the conjecture. A cross-class chain at threshold $N-1$ or failure of full connectivity at threshold $N$ would invalidate the proposed degree of the exhibited element.

## Evidence

The theorem `D5/S3/Arith/Additive/NarayanCatenarySpectrumMembership.result` identifies the injective atom tuple of the cofinite generated monoid, proves that $n$ is represented, and checks that its fiber is exhausted by the two disjoint inhabited classes. It proves cross-class separation at every threshold below $N$, characterizes reachability at $N-1$, and proves that $N$ is the least connecting threshold and belongs to the spectrum. It uses the frozen generic coordinate definition `D5/S3/Arith/Additive/NarayanExpandedGeneratorDeltaRefutation.Z` on the original tuple. ## Triage

### What the settlement shows

- **Proved** by `NarayanCatenarySpectrumMembership.result`. The lower bound from the common part and the exchange in the low class give the sharp threshold on the exhibited fiber. The proof includes $k=2$, $k=a-1$, $a=3$, and $h=2$, with multiplicity when the two exchanged indices coincide.
- **Outside this result.** Conjecture 18.1 concerns the low-degree spectrum on a narrower domain. Conjecture 18.3 and the paper's delta-set theorem concern different length invariants.
