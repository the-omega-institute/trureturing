# Barker's identities for the four-column complementary-pair exclusion strip

## Problem

[OEIS A233252](https://oeis.org/A233252), revision 8, defines:

> Number of n X 4 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

Barker's generating function and closed form are:

> G.f.: 2*x*(5 - 208*x) / ((1 - 16*x)*(1 - 144*x)).

> a(n) = 2^(4*n-3) * (4*9^n+9) / 9.

The recurrence is `a(n) = 160*a(n-1) - 2304*a(n-2)` for `n ≥ 3`.
The source has offset `1,1`; the empty-height count is extended by `a(0)=0`.

## Statement echo

`HardinA233252TriangleStrip.a n` counts functions `Fin n × Fin 4 → Fin 6`
satisfying the first-cell condition, horizontal exclusions, antidiagonal
exclusions, and the row-major scanner. The antidiagonal joins `(i,j+1)` to
`(i+1,j)`. The scanner requires an earlier 1 for each occurrence of 2, 3,
or 4, and an earlier 2 for each occurrence of 3; no colour is required to occur.

The theorem `HardinA233252TriangleStrip.result` gives all three identities:
the recurrence for every `n ≥ 1` in the shifted form at `n+2`, the integer
formal power-series identity, and the denominator-cleared closed form
`9*a(n+1) = 2^(4*n+1)*(4*9^(n+1)+9)` for every natural `n`.
