# Sun Conjecture 4.2(i) absolute-difference permanent congruences

## Problem

Zhi-Wei Sun, *Arithmetic properties of some permanents*, arXiv:2108.07723v7,
Conjecture 4.2(i), equations (4.3) and (4.4), asks whether for every odd prime
$p$ the permanents of the matrices with entries $|j-k|$ and $|j-k+1|$ are
congruent to $-1/2$ and $1/2$ modulo $p$, respectively. The integer encoding is
$A_s(j,k)=|((j.val:\mathbb Z)+1)-((k.val:\mathbb Z)+1)+s|$ for
$j,k : \operatorname{Fin} p$ and $s=0,1$.

### Statement echo

The source permanent is the finite sum over permutations of products of
row-indexed entries. The Lean claim clears the invertible denominator 2 and
states both divisibilities for the same universally quantified odd prime.

## Motivation

The result settles the two explicitly numbered congruences in the source's
open-conjecture section and gives a uniform prime-order mechanism for both
absolute-difference shifts.

## Gap

The source left both congruences conjectural. The bounded literature screen in
issue #22003 found the Brualdi--Newman prime circulant congruence as the classical
ingredient, but no prior proof of this absolute-difference pair. Literature
completeness and worldwide priority remain `ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED`.

## Route

For odd prime $p$, alternating row and column signs reduce each absolute-
difference matrix modulo $p$ to a circulant. Prime-order conjugation of
permutation weights cancels nonfixed orbits in characteristic $p$; the fixed
permutations are translations. Fermat's identity evaluates their weights, and
the alternating sum over $0,\ldots,2m$ with $p=2m+1$ is $m+s$. Clearing the
factor 2 gives the two integer divisibilities.

## Falsifier

The settled claim is
\[
\forall p\in\mathbb N,\;p\text{ prime}\to p\text{ odd}\to
((p:\mathbb Z)\mid 2\operatorname{per}(A_0)+1)\land
((p:\mathbb Z)\mid 2\operatorname{per}(A_1)-1).
\]
Its falsifier would be an odd prime and one failed divisibility.

### What the settlement shows

**Proved by `result`:** both absolute-difference permanent congruences hold for
every odd prime. The decisive mechanism is the alternating-sign reduction to a
prime circulant, cancellation of nonfixed permutation-weight orbits, and the
fixed-translation evaluation.

**Scope:** the entry reduction is proved for odd natural orders and shifts
$s=0,1$; the counting step requires primality. The argument does not settle
other shifts, composite moduli or prime-power strengthening.

**Effect on the source:** Conjecture 4.2(i), including (4.3) and (4.4), is
settled positively. Part (ii) remains a separate question; the screened
Jiang--Sun result concerns that reciprocal-sum formula rather than this pair.
