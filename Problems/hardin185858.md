# Hardin–Barker A185858: the generating function and closed form

## Problem

[OEIS A185858](https://oeis.org/A185858), revision 9, NAME and FORMULA,
contains the following source statements, without page numbering:

> 1/128 the number of (n+2) X 3 binary arrays with no 3 X 3 subblock trace equal to any horizontal or vertical neighbor 3 X 3 subblock trace.
>
> Conjectures from _Colin Barker_, Apr 17 2018: (Start)
>
> G.f.: 2*x*(2 + 3*x) / (1 - 4*x - 9*x^2).
>
> a(n) = ((2 - sqrt(13))^n*(-4+sqrt(13)) + (2+sqrt(13))^n*(4+sqrt(13))) / (3*sqrt(13)).
>
> (End)

The offset is 1. For $n\ge1$, the arrays have $n+2$ rows and three
columns, entries in $\{0,1\}$, ordinary contiguous-block traces, and no
periodic boundary or symmetry quotient. Write $C_n$ for the literal count
and $a_n=C_n/128$ in $\mathbb Q$. With $A(x)=\sum_{n\ge1}a_nx^n$, the
assertion is

$$A(x)=\frac{2x(2+3x)}{1-4x-9x^2},\qquad
 a_n=\frac{(2-\sqrt{13})^n(-4+\sqrt{13})+(2+\sqrt{13})^n(4+\sqrt{13})}
 {3\sqrt{13}}\quad(n\ge1).$$

## Route

For each column $c$, a cyclic row shift exposes the cells $(i+c,c)$ for
$0\le i<n$. These form independent triples and leave six free binary cells.
A prescribed trace word $s$ therefore has
$64\prod_i\binom3{s_i}$ arrays. The weights for traces $0,1,2,3$ are
$1,3,3,1$. Splitting off the first triple gives four extension equations.
Their weighted total satisfies the recurrence with coefficients 4 and 9;
symmetry gives integral normalization and the first two normalized values
4 and 22. Coefficient comparison proves the formal-series identity.
`LinearRecurrence.geom_sol_iff_root_charPoly` supplies the geometric
solutions used in `radical_recurrence`, and
`LinearRecurrence.eq_iff_eqOn_range_order` supplies the uniqueness step in
`closed_formula`.

## Falsifier

An array-count coefficient at any positive index violating either source
formula would contradict `result`. The literal definition includes all
adjacent block origins and excludes periodic wraparound and quotient counts.

### What the settlement shows

The diagonal supports are disjoint despite the overlap of the contiguous
blocks. Their six-cell complement explains the factor 64; pairing equal
trace weights gives the remaining factor two in `count_normalized`.
The two recurrence roots are $2\pm\sqrt{13}$, and matching the first two
coefficients identifies the source's radical expression. Both source
formulas hold for every positive index, rather than only for observed
terms. The prescribed-trace fiber statement also covers zero length.
No inspected downstream source theorem relies on the conjectures;
neighboring entries require their own array correspondences.
