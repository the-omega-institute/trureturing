# Gibonacci power-window gcd normalization

## Problem

Section 6.1, Question 6.2, PDF p. 14 of arXiv:2204.12626v2 asks:

> Fix $G_0, G_1 \in \mathbb{Z}$ and set $d := \gcd(G_0,G_1)$. Consider the two Gibonacci sequences $(G_n)_{n\geq 0}$ and $(G_n^\prime)_{n\geq 0}$, where $(G_n^\prime)_{n=0}^\infty$ is generated by the relatively prime initial conditions $G_0^\prime = \frac{G_0}{d}$ and $G_1^\prime = \frac{G_1}{d}$. Then the following identities holds:
>
> $\mathcal G_{G_0,G_1}(k)=d\cdot\mathcal G_{G'_0,G'_1}(k)$; $\mathcal G^2_{G_0,G_1}(k)=d^2\cdot\mathcal G^2_{G'_0,G'_1}(k)$.
>
> The first formula holds from Guyer-Mbirika [4, Theorem 19], while the second formula holds from Theorem 3.6 in this current paper. Will it be the case that $\mathcal{G}_{G_0, G_1}^n\!(k) = d^n \cdot \mathcal{G}_{G_0^\prime, G_1^\prime}^n\!(k)$ for all $n \geq 3$?

For every signed integer seed pair $(a,b)≠(0,0)$, every natural
$k\geq1$ and every natural $e\geq3$, let $G(0)=a$, $G(1)=b$ and
$G(n+2)=G(n+1)+G(n)$. Define the nonnegative gcd of the entire shift family by

$$C_e(a,b;k)=\gcd_{t\in\mathbb N}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k}G(t+i)^e\right).$$

With $d=\gcd(a,b)>0$, the target is

$$C_e(a,b;k)=d^e C_e(a/d,b/d;k).$$

The quotients are exact integers. Both gcds satisfy their divisibility iff
for every integer divisor, including zero and negative divisors. Individual
zero seeds, zero windows and odd exponents remain in scope.

## Motivation

The paper gives the corresponding seed-content scaling for first and
second powers and asks whether it persists for every higher power.

## Gap

The printed question concerns the entire infinite shift family. A finite
prefix, an exponent-three instance or a conditional gcd-existence premise
would not discharge it.

## Route

Linearity of Mathlib's seeded recurrence gives termwise scaling by $d$.
Distributing powers and the finite window sum gives scaling by $d^e$ for
every shift. The set-gcd universal property, cancellation by a positive
scalar and nonnegative divisibility antisymmetry give scaling of the
whole-family gcd. Exact integer quotients and normalized-seed gcd one are
proved from the seed gcd.

## Falsifier

A nonzero signed integer seed pair, a positive window length and a natural
exponent at least three for which the two fully specified nonnegative
infinite-family gcds violate the equality.

## Evidence

The result declaration is
`D5/S1/Recurrence/GibonacciPowerGcdNormalization.result`.
Its conclusion includes $d>0$, both exact seed reconstructions,
coprime normalized seeds, both recurrences, both gcd nonnegativity
assertions, both universal integer-divisor iff specifications and the
whole scaling equality. The definitions use Mathlib's `Nat.setGcd` on the
full range of natural absolute window values. The axiom closure of every public declaration is contained in
{`Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, `propext`}.

## Triage

Proved: the whole assertion holds in the stated nonzero-pair domain. The mechanism is homogeneous
scaling of the recurrence and the gcd universal property, with no parity
restriction on the exponent or positivity restriction on individual terms.
Proved: the private window-scaling identity also permits any natural exponent and
length; the public result retains exactly the source exponent and length
hypotheses. Proved: the generic gcd step applies to any integer family and any
positive scalar. No new public generalization is asserted. This result
supplies the normalization requested in Question 6.2; it does not supply a
closed formula for the coprime-seed gcd or settle the separate Question 6.1.
Open: further closed forms for the coprime-seed gcd and the separate Question 6.1 remain unresolved here.
