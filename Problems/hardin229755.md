# Hardin's width-one line of the two-defect king table

## Problem

[OEIS A229755](https://oeis.org/A229755) has the following verbatim NAME:

> T(n,k) = number of defective 4-colorings of an n X k 0..3 array connected horizontally, vertically, diagonally and antidiagonally with exactly two mistakes, and colors introduced in row-major 0..3 order.

Its FORMULA lines, verbatim from the
[text export](https://oeis.org/search?q=id:A229755&fmt=text), are:

```text

k=2: a(n) = 6*a(n-1) -12*a(n-2) +8*a(n-3) for n>5
```

The assertions are registered in
[issue #30655](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/30655).

Let $T_2(n,k)$ denote `KingTwoGrid.T n k`, the literal count of functions
$\operatorname{Fin}(n)\times\operatorname{Fin}(k)\to\operatorname{Fin}(4)$
with exactly two monochromatic bonds and canonical first appearances of
colours in row-major order. Horizontal, vertical, diagonal and antidiagonal
bonds are each counted once when both endpoints lie in the rectangle.
The boundaries are free. Canonical first appearances identify colour
relabellings while keeping cell positions distinct.

Here $a(n)=T_2(n,k)$ for a fixed column $k$. This dossier settles
**the $k=1$ FORMULA line only**, claim C1: the six-term recurrence for every
natural $n>9$, equivalently $n\ge10$, with counts cast to $\mathbb Z$.
The $k=3,4$ lines are settled through A229750 and A229751; the $k=5,6$
lines (A229752, A229753) are registered on #30655 and settled separately. The $k=2$ line follows from Chang–Shrock 2001 as
recorded on #30655 and is not claimed here. The $k=7$ line and the
registered two-variable closed form G2 stay open.

## Motivation

For the physics reading, these are states of the four-state Potts
antiferromagnet on king-lattice strips: the square lattice with both
diagonals in each elementary square. With equal positive penalties for
monochromatic bonds, the counted states have exactly two unsatisfied
bonds. The count is the degeneracy at that fixed bond count up to
relabelling of the four states. The statements concern finite canonical
configuration counts.

The settling declaration is `D5/S3/StatisticalMechanics/Potts/KingDefects/KingTwoColumnOne.result`.
The source bibkey is `hardin2013a229755`.

## Route

1. **Canonical one-column words.** A one-column king array has only
   adjacent vertical bonds. `KingPath.normal_path` identifies its row-major
   colour convention with `HardinA233177Canonical.Canonical`.
   The prefix argument reuses the introduction rules of the frozen
   trace-avoiding owner `HardinA233196Strip.normal_cons`.
   `KingTwoColumnOne.accepted_iff` proves that acceptance is exactly the
   prefix condition together with an adjacent-equality cost of two.
   The grid-edge sum and the canonical first colour zero give
   `KingTwoColumnOne.literal_bridge`:
   $T_2(m+1,1)=\operatorname{count}(m,1,0,2)$.

2. **Exact-budget path transfer.** On the four-colour alphabet, after the
   first cell a state records the number $a\in\{1,2,3,4\}$ of introduced
   colours and the remaining budget $d\in\{0,1,2\}$. There are $a-1$
   existing colours different from the preceding one, one equal colour
   when $d>0$, and one new colour when $a<4$. The twelve states have index
   $3(a-1)+d$; the terminal vector indicates $d=0$.
   `KingTwoColumnOne.count_step`, `transfer_counts` and `count_matrix`
   identify the accepted-word count with the matrix count. This canonical
   transfer includes two-colour words directly; a uniform divisor of $24$
   is not used for the path.

3. **Backward certificate and propagation.** If $M$ is the path transfer
   and $e$ its terminal vector, the ten-row `backwardTable` pins
   $B_i=M^ie$ for $0\le i\le9$. The certificate gives

   $$
   B_9=-27B_3+108B_4-171B_5+136B_6-57B_7+12B_8.
   $$

   `DefectTransfer.forward_shift`, applied to $M^{\mathsf T}$, propagates
   this relation through every further height. The literal path bridge
   converts it to C1 for all $n\ge10$.

4. **Relation to the other column proofs.** The width-three through
   width-six results use the same frozen `DefectTransfer` owner with
   $q=4$, horizontal in-row bonds, vertical and both diagonal between-row
   bonds, and exact total budget two. Their zero-temperature blocks reuse
   the frozen trace-avoiding king owners, and their forward/Krylov
   certificates propagate the order-six relation. Those column claims
   have their own dossiers; they are not additional claims of this one.

## Falsifier

the quoted $k=1$ recurrence would contradict `KingTwoColumnOne.result`.
