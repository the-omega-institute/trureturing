# Fillman–Lim Question 2: gap-richness and three set operations

## Problem

Jake Fillman and Yi-Sheng Lim, *On the identification of certain classes of gap-rich sets*, arXiv:2609.21029v1, Section 6, Question 2, p.20:

“If X₁, X₂ ⊆ ℝᵏ and X₃ ⊆ ℝˡ are gap-rich, are any of the following sets X₁ ∪ X₂, X₁ + X₂, and X₁ × X₃ gap-rich?”

Preregistration: [#29166](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/29166). The question assumes arbitrary gap-rich subsets in positive dimensions. It imposes no compactness or perfectness restriction. The closed proposition `FillmanLimGapRichClosures.claim` is the disjunction of the three separately universally quantified closure assertions. Its negation refutes all three.

## Gap

Separate gap-richness of two alphabets supplies no joint compatibility at resonance energies. The construction gives each input empty exceptional set while making every proposed output exceptional set contain a nondiscrete subset. A finite test of word lengths would not establish either input gap-richness or the output obstruction for every perturbing length.
