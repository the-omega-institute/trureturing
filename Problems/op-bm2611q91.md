# Flag completion need not preserve normality

## Problem

Bruno Benedetti and Giuliamaria Menara, *Diameter bounds for complexes that are close to flag or close to normal*, arXiv:2611.08071v1, page 21, Question 9.1:

> In Main Theorem A with k ≥ 2, does normality of Δ̂ follow from normality of Δ together with (Gₖ) and purity of Δ̂? (For k ≤ 1 it does, by Proposition 3.5.)

The affirmative assertion quantifies over finite complexes on all $n$ vertices and natural $d,k$ with $2\le k$ and $k+2\le d$. A pure complex of rank $d$ has a facet and every facet has $d$ vertices. Normality requires the dual graph induced by the facets containing each face, including the empty face, to be nonempty and connected. Distinct facets are adjacent when their intersection has $d-1$ vertices. The flag completion is the clique complex of the one-skeleton. The condition $G_k$ prohibits minimal nonfaces $M$ with $3\le |M|$ and $|M|+k\le d$.

## Route

Use ten vertices $0,1,2,3,4,5,a,z,w,b$ with $a=6,z=7,w=8,b=9$. The original fifteen tetrahedra are

$$zw01,zw12,zw23,zw34,zw45,zw50,az01,az12,az23,az34,wb01,wb50,wb45,wb34,ab01.$$

All vertices occur and every facet has four vertices. For each earlier/later pair, a ridge of the later facet in an earlier facet contains their intersection. The private `shelling_normal` constructs paths by strong induction on the sum of the two facet positions; `delta_normal` applies it. The completion consists of the same tetrahedra and $ab34$ and remains pure of rank four. Its $ab$-star contains the distinct facets $ab01$ and $ab34$, whose intersection has only two vertices. The private `completion_not_normal` reduces the induced graph to the edgeless graph and contradicts the distinctness of these two vertices. The numerical bounds make $G_2$ vacuous.

## Triage

### What the settlement shows

1. **Proved, kernel-checked ten-vertex instance.** Normality and unchanged pure rank do not force normality of the flag completion, even with every vertex present and two distinct facets in the obstructing star. Evidence: `result`, using the private `delta_pure`, `delta_normal`, `gap_delta`, `completion_pure` and `completion_not_normal`; these witness properties are used inside its proof, not exported as separate public theorems.
2. **Proved, kernel-checked construction with a prose interpretation of its obstruction.** The private `delta_normal` uses ridge-attachment path induction; `completion_eq` and `clique_classification` identify the completion generated by the fifteen original tetrahedra and $ab34$. In the proof of `completion_not_normal`, the induced $ab$-star graph equals the edgeless graph and has two distinct vertices. Its facets are exactly $ab01$ and $ab34$: inspecting the sixteen displayed facet sets leaves only those two containing both $a$ and $b$, and they intersect in $ab$, so neither pair is adjacent. The exact-two interpretation is this explicit finite-set argument, not an additional exported Lean theorem.
3. **Computed, pinned exact checker, $6\le N\le24$.** Both purities, original normality, $G_2$, disconnected $ab$-star and connected full completion dual graph hold in the tested range. At $N=6$, the face vectors are $(10,32,38,15)$ and $(10,32,40,16)$. Evidence: the experiment entry above, including command, exit code and script SHA-256.
4. **Open, uniform Lean family theorem.** The arbitrary-$N$ outline in #29020 is not a uniform formal theorem in this module. Only the $N=6$ member is kernel-checked; the finite checker does not prove the assertion for every $N\ge6$.
5. **Proved in the published source, not newly formalized here.** The $k\le1$ preservation range remains Proposition 3.5 of the source. The instance has $d=4,k=2$; $3\le |M|$ and $|M|+2\le4$ are incompatible, explaining its vacuous $G_2$. Evidence kinds: source Proposition 3.5 for preservation; the kernel-checked private `gap_delta` for this instance.
6. **Proved in prose by checking the source hypotheses.** Main Theorem A explicitly assumes that the completion is normal. This instance has a completion which is not normal, so it fails that hypothesis and does not refute Main Theorem A or its diameter bound. Evidence: the source's Main Theorem A and `result`'s private obstruction.
7. **Open, surviving neighbour.** Preservation under nonvacuous $G_k$ with $2\le k<d-2$, and a classification of additional sufficient conditions, are not settled by this witness. No uniform assertion in that range is claimed.

The failure mechanism is the disconnected overlap of two connected neighbourhood paths: completion exposes that overlap as a genuine facet-star. It does not rely on a dimension change, ghost vertices or an empty star. The full completion dual graph being connected is a computed observation in the finite family; it is not the definition of normality.
