# Spezia's Pell circulant permanent-to-determinant ratio

## Problem

Stefano Spezia's OEIS A392131, revision 16, FORMULA states:

> Conjecture: lim_{n->oo} A392132(n)/a(n) = 1.

OEIS A392132, revision 10, FORMULA states the same conjecture as:

> Conjecture: lim_{n->oo} a(n)/A392131(n) = 1.

In A392131, $a(n)$ is the signed determinant; in A392132, $a(n)$ is the permanent. These are two records of one assertion. The source is OEIS-only, submitted in 2025 and published in 2026, with no source DOI or arXiv identifier. Issue [#21886](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/21886) fixes the conventions and the settlement criterion. The source quotations and locators are in [the Library note](../Library/Asymptotics/spezia2026pellcirculant.md).

### Statement echo

Let $P_0=0$, $P_1=1$ and $P_{k+2}=2P_{k+1}+P_k$. With indices in $\operatorname{Fin}(n)$, the matrix is $C_n(i,j)=P_{n-1-\operatorname{val}(i-j)}$, with natural subtraction truncated at zero and modular subtraction in $i-j$. Its first row is $(P_{n-1},P_0,\ldots,P_{n-2})$, the initial right rotation specified by the source COMMENT and four-by-four EXAMPLE. The formalization reuses the existing Pell sequence `D5.S1.Recurrence.PellCompanionGcd.P` and Mathlib's `Matrix.circulant`, `Matrix.det` and `Matrix.permanent`.

| Source clause | Formal counterpart |
| --- | --- |
| A392131 COMMENT: first column reversed Pell vector, first row initially rotated right | `C`, with the literal binding vector and Mathlib's modular circulant indexing |
| A392131 determinant, including the source empty value | `A392131 n = Matrix.det (C n)`; the determinant retains its sign |
| A392132 permanent of the same matrix | `A392132 n = Matrix.permanent (C n)` |
| The ratio tends to one | `claim`: every real $\varepsilon>0$ admits $N\ge2$ such that every $n\ge N$ has $|A392132(n)/A392131(n)-1|<\varepsilon$ |
| No additional matrix-family hypothesis | `result : claim` has no hypotheses |

The threshold condition $N\ge2$ specifies an equivalent tail of the limit; it imposes no extra condition on the sequence. Both empty values are one. The zero determinant at $n=1$ does not affect a tail assertion.

## Motivation

The conjecture compares the unsigned sum over permutation products with its signed counterpart for a dense, sequence-defined matrix. Normalization reveals a unit diagonal and geometrically decreasing off-diagonal weights. A uniform estimate on all nontrivial cycles explains why both permutation sums approach the identity contribution.

## Falsifier

A different initial rotation or an absolute determinant in the denominator would describe a different claim. `C` uses the source's initial rotation, and `A392131` uses the signed determinant. The four-by-four matrix has first row $(5,0,1,2)$, determinant $640$ and permanent $772$.

Finite agreement does not prove convergence. The all-index conclusion follows from the compiled cycle-activity estimate and the normalized limits, without assuming positivity of every finite determinant. The limit implies eventual nonvanishing; positivity for every $n\ge2$ is a separate question.
