# The Poncini–Rasmussen central factorial transfer expansion

## Problem

Poncini and Rasmussen, *Integrability of planar-algebraic models*, arXiv:2206.14462v2, Appendix C, page 47, state Conjecture C.12:

> For every n ∈ ℕ and all u ∈ F, we have Ť_n(u) = 2 Σ_{k=0}^{n−1} (1 + u²)^{n−k} (2u)^k Σ_{j=0}^{⌊k/2⌋} [n − k − 2 + 2j over 2j]!! (−1)^j m_{k−2j}(h_n)/(k − 2j)!

The domain is $n\ge1$ and an algebraically closed field $F$ of characteristic zero. The source's coefficients and ordered operators are (C.9)–(C.15), the Hamiltonian is (C.40), the central factorial polynomials are (C.44), and the double factorial quotient is (C.65). The source note is `D5/L/StatisticalMechanics/poncini2023planar`. Preregistration: [#19637](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/19637).

## Motivation

The source establishes membership in the algebra generated by the Hamiltonian and an inverse-Vandermonde interpolation formula. This result supplies the explicit central factorial coefficient formula conjectured in (C.66). It is a settlement of that named assertion; prior-art completeness is not established.

## Gap

The central step is the scalar coefficient identity for every $m,l,k\in\mathbb N$ with $l,k\le m$, evaluated at the Hamiltonian eigenvalue $m-2l$. The identity must be combined with the transfer matrix built from the literal ordered double row and auxiliary trace, rather than an assumed product formula.

## Route

### What the settlement shows

**Proved in Lean:** `central_spectral_coeff` identifies the coefficient of $t^k$ in $(1+t)^{m-l}(1-t)^l$ with the double factorial sum at $x=m-2l$. `scalar_settlement` gives the scalar eigenvalue identity at every allowed index and spectral parameter. `result` lifts this to the literal transfer matrix for every length in the claim.

**Explanation:** the central difference recurrence and formal binomial substitution produce the double factorial quotient. Multiplying the finite coefficient identity by the homogeneous weights produces a polynomial identity, so the proof imposes no condition $1+u^2\ne0$. The Walsh square identity allows matrix cancellation in characteristic zero. These identities describe the decisive mechanism; they are not numerical extrapolations.

**Computed:** the finite experiment checks exactly the scalar and matrix ranges stated in Evidence. **Open:** extensions to positive characteristic, other boundary coefficients or different vertex weights, and any claim of prior-art completeness. No sharp quantitative estimate is asserted by this equality.

**Proved in prose:** the explicit equality supplies the polynomial-algebra membership (C.45) directly. It gives explicit coefficients for the same transfer matrix addressed by the source's interpolation formula. No additional dependent source result is claimed formally here.
