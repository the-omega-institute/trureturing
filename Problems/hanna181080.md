# Hanna's OEIS A181080 integrality conjecture

## Problem

Paul D. Hanna, [OEIS A181080](https://oeis.org/A181080), NAME and first COMMENTS line; [preregistration #22222](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/22222).

> Conjecture: this sequence consists entirely of integers.

The source logarithm is $L(x)=\sum_{n\ge1}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom nk^{n-k+1}\frac{x^{n+k}}n$ and the source generating function is $A(x)=\exp(L(x))$ over $\mathbb Q[[x]]$. Its coefficients are integers in every degree, including zero. The binomial expression is the ordinary binomial coefficient with exponent $n-k+1$. This is a formal-series assertion with no analytic convergence assumption.

## Route

For a natural-valued array $F$, assume $n\mid kF(n,k)$ whenever $n\ne0$, and for each prime $p$ assume
$F(pn,pk)\equiv F(n,k)\pmod{p^{v_p(n)+1}}$ for $n\ne0$.
The rational diagonal series has coefficient $\sum_{n=1}^m F(n,m-n)/n$.
The integer logarithmic coefficient is the sum of $F(n,k)+kF(n,k)/n$ along $n+k=m$. Exact divisibility makes this integer and multiplication by $n$ gives $(n+k)F(n,k)$. Pairing indices divisible by $p$ with their unscaled indices and cancelling prime units proves the Dold congruence. `HannaA181070Integrality.integralGeneric` applies the existing `DoldExponentialIntegrality.integral_exp_of_dold` to this integer sequence.

`HannaA181070Integrality.coeffLift` provides the binomial prime-scaling congruence by applying power-series Frobenius and prime-power lifting. `coefficientDivisibility` supplies $n\mid k[x^k]U^n$ from the derivative of a power. The publicized existing owner `HannaLegendreCentralBinomialTransform.coeff_one_add_pow` supplies the coefficients of $(1+X)^n$ and is used by `binomDiv` and `binomCong`. Negative binomial coefficients come from Mathlib's power of the constant-one coefficient series.

3. **Proved in prose:** the exponent argument separates units and nonunits and explains the $+1$ margin. For $k,b>0$ and $p\mid b$, $v_p(n)\le v_p(k)+v_p(b)$ follows from $n\mid kb$. Since $v_p(k)<k$ and $v_p(b)\ge1$, $(k+1)v_p(b)\ge v_p(n)+1$, so both powers vanish at the required modulus. For $p\nmid b$, $p^{v_p(n)}\mid k$ and $(p-1)k$ is a multiple of $\varphi(p^{v_p(n)+1})$; Euler's congruence identifies the powers. The zero cases are immediate. The resulting congruence itself is kernel-checked by `HannaA181070Integrality.variableExponent`.
4. **Proved in prose:** A181080 uses $n-k$ for its congruence proof, while the source logarithm keeps exponent $n-k+1$ and diagonal weight $n+k$. Indeed $n\mid k\binom nk$ and $n\mid n\binom nk$ imply $n\mid(n-k)\binom nk$ by subtraction when $k\le n$; coefficients vanish for $k>n$. The private `HannaA181080Integrality.cong80` applies that argument on the settling result's proof path. The literal logarithm definition retains the stated exponent and weight.
