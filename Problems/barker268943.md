# Barker's generating function and radical formula for OEIS A268943

## Problem

R. H. Hardin's [OEIS A268943](https://oeis.org/A268943), revision 8, has
Colin Barker's two FORMULA conjectures. Its NAME states:

> Number of length-n 0..7 arrays with no repeated value unequal to the previous repeated value plus one mod 7+1.

An event is an adjacent equal pair, including overlapping pairs. List the event
labels in position order. Consecutive labels advance by one modulo eight.
Empty and singleton event lists are allowed. For $n\ge1$, let $c(n)$ count
these literal words, with no identification under rotation or relabeling.
Barker's conjectures are

$$
F(X)=\frac{8X(1-5X-7X^2)}{(1-7X)(1-6X-8X^2)}
$$

for the series with constant coefficient zero and positive coefficients $c(n)$,
and

$$
c(n)=\frac{-272\,7^n+(153-37\sqrt{17})(3-\sqrt{17})^n
 +(3+\sqrt{17})^n(153+37\sqrt{17})}{34}\quad(n\ge1).
$$

## Route

The private state counts classify a word by whether it has an event and,
when it does, whether the first event is the successor of its first letter.
Write these three counts as $U,A,B$. Prepending a letter gives
$(U,A,B)\mapsto(7U,B,U+8A+6B)$, initially $(8,0,0)$.
`count_partition`, `phaseCount_cons` and `phaseCount_step` establish the literal
counting bridge. `stateSeries_equations` gives the coefficientwise equations;
`sourceSeries_formula` derives the rational expression. `count_recurrence`
and `closed_recurrence` give the same order-three recurrence for the literal
count and the radical expression. `radical_count` uses the imported
`LinearRecurrence.eq_iff_eqOn_range_order` and their initial agreement.
The public `result` combines the series existence, uniqueness and formula with
the radical identity. These private names belong to the settling module.

## Falsifier

contradict `result`. Altering the event relation to unequal successive labels
changes the problem and does not falsify this statement.

### What the settlement shows

The decisive mechanism is the three-state refinement: event existence alone
does not determine whether duplication is legal. Retaining the endpoint's
relation to the next permitted event closes the transition system. Its total
has the proved recurrence, and recurrence uniqueness connects it to the three
exponential modes. The source's empirical recurrence is thereby proved for
indices $n\ge4$. No source theorem is stated to depend on the conjectures.
The finite general-alphabet evidence remains computed; its uniform extension
and neighboring columns remain open here.
