# Hardin's recurrence for the four-row antidiagonal pair-exclusion strip

## Problem

[OEIS A233259](https://oeis.org/A233259), by R. H. Hardin, has NAME

> Number of 4 X n 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

with the empirical recurrence
$a(n)=160a(n-1)-6144a(n-2)+86016a(n-3)-393216a(n-4)$ for $n>8$ (verbatim in
[issue #28807](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/28807)).

A bond joins horizontally adjacent cells $(r,j),(r,j+1)$ and antidiagonal
cells $(r,j+1),(r+1,j)$; cells of one column are not bonded. A bond is legal
when its endpoints differ and do not sum to $5$.

### What the settlement shows

"Computed" means exact arithmetic in `hardin-a233259/transfer.py` of
the-omega-institute/trureturing-experiments at commit `c1b7da09` (run
`python3 hardin-a233259/transfer.py`); it is not kernel-checked.

1. **Proved:** dividing the roots of Hardin's polynomial by the column
   factor $16=2^4$ gives $y^4-10y^3+24y^2-21y+6=(y-1)(y^3-9y^2+15y-6)$, the
   relation of $L(n)+6$; the root $1$ carries the constant $6$ of the
   two-label patterns.
2. **Computed:** the $81$-state transfer on label columns has
   characteristic polynomial
   $\lambda^{65}(\lambda-2)(\lambda+1)^2(\lambda^2-6)^2(\lambda^2-5\lambda+5)(\lambda^2+7\lambda+8)^2(\lambda^3-9\lambda^2+15\lambda-6)$;
   only the cubic factor survives in $L(n)$.
3. **Computed:** $L(n)+6$ satisfies the order-four relation exactly for
   $n\ge9$ and fails at $n=5,\dots,8$, matching Hardin's starting index.
4. **Computed:** across rows two to four of A233256 the surviving factor of
   $L(n)$ is $y-3$, $y^2-5y+2$ and $y^3-9y^2+15y-6$ (row two from the
   A233257 closed form, row three from `Problems/hardin233258.md`).
