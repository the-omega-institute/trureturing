# Mathar's eight-term recurrence for OEIS A379464

## Problem

[OEIS A379464, revision 20](https://oeis.org/A379464/internal) defines $a_n$ as the number of length-$n$ paths from $(0,0)$ to $(n,0)$ with steps $(1,1),(1,0),(1,-3)$, staying on or above $y=-2$. Its unnumbered FORMULA conjecture is reproduced verbatim in [the source note](../Library/FiniteGeometry/mathar2025a379464.md) and preregistered in [#22234](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/22234).

For all natural $n\ge7$, the eight polynomial multiples of $a_n,a_{n-1},\ldots,a_{n-7}$ in that FORMULA sum to zero. Polynomial arithmetic is in $\mathbb Z$; indices use natural subtraction. The empty path is included. The asserted Lean proposition is the exact recurrence, with no separate formal D-finiteness predicate.

## Route

The total-three cycle lemma counts marked rotations with strictly positive prefix sums for every integer word whose increments are at most one. Marked-rotation counting and a fixed-cardinality subset enumeration give the zero-free path count. Removing three initial up-steps relates positive words to the boundary-$-2$ paths. Generic optional-letter insertion supplies the horizontal steps.
