# Barker's generating function for the kisrhombille coordination sequence at a hexavalent node

## Problem

[OEIS A298038](https://oeis.org/A298038), revision 45, by N. J. A. Sloane, has NAME

> Coordination sequence of Dual(4.6.12) tiling with respect to a hexavalent node.

and FORMULA

> Conjectures from _Colin Barker_, Apr 03 2020: (Start)
>
> G.f.: (1 + 6*x + 22*x^2 + 6*x^3 + x^4) / ((1 - x)^2*(1 + x)^2).

The tiling is the barycentric subdivision of the triangular lattice. Its
vertices are the vertices, edges and faces of the triangular lattice, and two
of them are joined when one contains the other. The hexavalent nodes are the
face barycentres. The claim is the full generating function of the graph
distance shells about such a node, including the $n=0$ term.

## Motivation

coordination sequence counts the sites in each shell around a site.

## Gap

The entry still records Barker's formulas as conjectures. The preregistration's
bounded screen found no proof for this root. Goodman-Strauss–Sloane (2019)
treats other nets. The 12-valent root A298036 has a separate proof, and later
kisrhombille distance papers study tile adjacency, which is a different graph.

## Route

1. **Distance formula.** An explicit score is attached to each of the six
   simplex types. On vertices and faces it is an instance of the existing
   lattice potential `TeaCoordinationSequence.residual`. Each edge changes the
   score by at most one, so the general potential bound
   `VertexPositionRatioRefutation.potential_dist` gives distance $\ge$ score.
   Every simplex of positive score has a neighbour whose score is one lower.
   Strong induction on the score then constructs a walk of exactly that
   length. Hence the distance equals the score.
2. **Balls.** For each type, the simplices of score at most $n$ form a lattice
   polygon. Their cardinalities are computed from rectangles minus corner
   triangles, together with the existing hexagon count
   `TeaCoordinationSequence.hexagon_card`. The results are
   $|B_{2k}|=18k^2+12k+1$ and $|B_{2k+1}|=18k^2+24k+7$.
3. **Shells and series.** Differences of consecutive balls give $a(0)=1$,
   $a(2k+1)=12k+6$ and $a(2k+2)=24(k+1)$. Multiplying the series by $(1-X^2)^2$
   leaves exactly the numerator.

## Falsifier

A shell around the face root whose size differs from the corresponding
coefficient would contradict `result`.

3. **Open:** the tetravalent-node sequence A298040 (registered separately as
   #27078), which concerns the same graph with an edge root.

### What the settlement shows

The graph distance on the subdivision is controlled by one piecewise-linear
potential per simplex type. For vertices and faces, this potential is the same
hexagonal potential that governs the tea coordination sequence. Balls are
therefore unions of lattice polygons. That makes the shells eventually linear
with period two: $24(k+1)$ on even radii and $12k+6$ on odd radii. The other
roots of the same graph (item 3) are amenable to the same potential once the
root is moved to an edge.
