# Hardin's recurrence and Barker's generating function for the three-row antidiagonal pair-exclusion strip

## Problem

[OEIS A233258](https://oeis.org/A233258), by R. H. Hardin, has NAME

> Number of 3 X n 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

with Hardin's empirical recurrence $a(n)=48a(n-1)-448a(n-2)+1024a(n-3)$ for
$n>5$ and Colin Barker's empirical generating function
$x(11-392x+3072x^2-8192x^3+16384x^4)/((1-8x)(1-40x+128x^2))$ (verbatim in
[issue #28806](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/28806)).

A bond joins horizontally adjacent cells $(r,j),(r,j+1)$ and antidiagonal
cells $(r,j+1),(r+1,j)$; cells of one column are not bonded. A bond is legal
when its endpoints differ and do not sum to $5$.

## Route

1. **Labels.** The complementary-pair labels are the fibres
   $\{0,5\},\{1,4\},\{2,3\}$ of `BooleanRankThreeK4.beta`; a bond is legal
   exactly when its endpoints carry different labels.
2. **Canonical form.** The frozen `HardinA233177Canonical.normalized_card`
   and `canonical_pattern_card`, with the frozen
   `HardinA233177Ladder.two_label_classification` for the two-label
   patterns, give the counting bridge $48\,a(n)=8^{n}(L(n)+6)$ for
   $n\ge2$, where $L(n)$ counts the proper three-label patterns.
3. **Transfer.** `HardinA233258PairTransfer` counts $L(n)$ by a column
   transfer on relabelling classes of label columns and proves the
   order-three relation of $L(n)+6$; scaling by $8^n$ gives Hardin's
   coefficients and the generating function.

## Falsifier

### What the settlement shows

"Computed" means exact arithmetic in `hardin-a233258/transfer.py` of
the-omega-institute/trureturing-experiments (run
`python3 hardin-a233258/transfer.py`); it is not kernel-checked.

1. **Proved:** Hardin's polynomial factors as
   $(x-8)(x^2-40x+128)$; dividing the roots by the column factor $8=2^3$
   gives $y^3-6y^2+7y-2=(y-1)(y^2-5y+2)$, the relation of $L(n)+6$. The
   root $1$ carries the constant $6$ of the two-label patterns.
2. **Computed:** the $27$-state column transfer on label columns has
   characteristic polynomial
   $\lambda^{15}(\lambda-2)(\lambda-1)^3(\lambda+1)^2(\lambda^2-5\lambda+2)(\lambda^2+4\lambda+2)^2$;
   only the factor $\lambda^2-5\lambda+2$ survives in $L(n)$, together with
   the root $1$ of the constant.
3. **Computed:** $L(n)+6$ satisfies the order-three relation exactly for
   $n\ge6$ and fails at $n=4,5$, matching Hardin's starting index $n>5$; the
   nilpotent part $\lambda^{15}$ of the transfer accounts for the transient
   (not verified beyond this reading).
