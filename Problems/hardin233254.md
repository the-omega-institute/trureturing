# Barker's generating function for the six-column six-colour pair-exclusion strip

## Problem

[OEIS A233254](https://oeis.org/A233254), by R. H. Hardin, has NAME

> Number of n X 6 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

and FORMULA

> Empirical g.f.: 8*x*(17 - 7488*x + 974848*x^2 - 40108032*x^3) / ((1 - 64*x)*(1 - 1600*x + 368640*x^2 - 21233664*x^3)). - _Colin Barker_, Oct 11 2018

Each row is a path of six cells, and consecutive rows are joined by the five
antidiagonal bonds $(i,j)$–$(i+1,j-1)$, $1\le j\le5$: a six-leg strip of the
triangular lattice without vertical or main-diagonal bonds. A bond is legal
when its endpoints differ and do not sum to $5$.

### What the settlement shows

The factor $1-64x$ is the sector before colour $2$: each row continues in
$64$ ways there ($F'=64F+\dots$). The cubic factor
$z^3-1600z^2+368640z-21233664$ is the characteristic polynomial of the
fully seen $(U,Z,W)$ recursion, so the nonzero transfer of the fully seen
sector is three-dimensional at width $6$, against one dimension at width $3$
and two at width $5$. The label moments obey the order-three relation
$u\mapsto 81r-90t+25u$, which is where the third dimension comes from.
