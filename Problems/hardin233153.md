# Hardin's recurrence for the width-6 three-state sum-exclusion strip

## Problem

[OEIS A233153](https://oeis.org/A233153), by R. H. Hardin, has NAME

> Number of n X 6 0..2 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to value 2-x(i,j) horizontally or antidiagonally.

with an empirical recurrence of order 3 (verbatim in
[issue #29204](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/29204)).
It is column 6 of the table A233155.

A bond joins horizontal neighbours $(i,j),(i,j+1)$ and antidiagonal
neighbours $(i,j+1),(i+1,j)$; a bond is legal when its two values do not sum
to $2$. There is no normalization and no condition on the values themselves.

## Route

1. **Row transfer.** `HardinA233155RowTransfer` gives the width-generic
   literal count as a sum over sequences of horizontally legal rows joined by
   antidiagonal compatibility; at width 6 there are $3\cdot2^{5}=96$
   legal rows.
2. **Lumped transfer.** A finite certificate gives the exact class successor
   counts and initial class multiplicities of a quotient of the legal rows;
   tabulated powers certify the vector relation at offset one, and the owner
   `TransferMatrix.scalarRelationShift` extends it through every further
   power, which gives the order-three relation from the source's starting index.

## Falsifier

recurrence would contradict `result`.
