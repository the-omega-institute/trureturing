# Hardin's recurrence for the six-column six-colour pair-exclusion square strip

## Problem

[OEIS A233127](https://oeis.org/A233127), by R. H. Hardin, has NAME

> Number of nX6 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally or vertically, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

and FORMULA

> Empirical: a(n) = 1984*a(n-1) -1314816*a(n-2) +411303936*a(n-3) -70111985664*a(n-4) +6893422510080*a(n-5) -389708152569856*a(n-6) +11733988091625472*a(n-7) -145241087982698496*a(n-8)

Each row is a path of six cells, and consecutive rows are joined by the six
vertical bonds $(i,j)$–$(i+1,j)$: a six-leg strip of the square lattice. A
bond is legal when its endpoints differ and do not sum to $5$.

### What the settlement shows

The class transfer is block triangular. The sector before colour $2$ is
one-dimensional with eigenvalue $64$: only the pairs $\{0,5\}$ and
$\{1,4\}$ are available, so each row has $2^6$ continuations, giving the
factor $x-64$ of Hardin's polynomial. The ten classes after colour $2$ have
characteristic polynomial $x^3\,C(x)$ with
$C(x)=x^7-1920x^6+1191936x^5-335020032x^4+48670703616x^3-3778497478656x^2+147884313935872x-2269391999729664$;
the three zero modes are transient, and $(x-64)\,C(x)$ is exactly Hardin's
polynomial. Across the square strips of widths two to six the sector after
colour $2$ has $1,2,3,6,10$ classes, the number of relabelling-and-reversal
orbits of proper label rows; zero modes and modes not excited by the first
row (the mode $-32$ at width five) do not enter the recurrence.
