# Hanna's A381355 prime-index divisibility

## Problem

Paul D. Hanna's [OEIS A381355](https://oeis.org/A381355), revision 13,
COMMENTS (unpaginated), states:

> Conjecture: a(n) is divisible by prime(n) for n > 1.

Its NAME defines $a_n=[x^{n-1}]F'(x)/F(x)$, where the source
[OEIS A381353](https://oeis.org/A381353), revision 19, satisfies
$[x^n]F(x)^{p_n}=0$ for every $n>1$. Its PROGRAM initializes
`my(A=[1,1])`, fixing $[x^0]F=[x^1]F=1$. Here $p_n$ is the one-based
prime schedule, with $p_1=2$. Source and literature evidence is recorded in
[preregistration #23982](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/23982)
and the [Library note](../Library/RecurrenceComposition/hanna2025a381355.md).

## Motivation

The conjecture relates an implicit source to the logarithmic derivative
of that same source. Integral construction and rational uniqueness identify
this common realization; independent finite coefficient lists do not do so.

## Falsifier

A rational source satisfying the stated initial and diagonal equations,
and an index $n>1$ whose logarithmic-derivative coefficient is outside
$p_n\mathbb Z$, would contradict `result`. No extra existence or
integrality hypothesis is assumed by that theorem. The experiment's
$n=1$ value is $a_1=1$, which is not divisible by $p_1=2$; this boundary
is outside the source assertion.

### What the settlement shows

The decisive mechanism is an integral coefficient correction followed by
cancellation before reduction modulo a prime. Frobenius makes the required
correction integral because $0<n<p_n$. Stabilization gives a genuine infinite
series, and rational coefficient induction gives uniqueness. The coefficient
of degree $n-1$ in the derivative identity vanishes over the integers; after
cancelling the nonzero prime, all lower positive coefficients in the prime
power vanish modulo that prime, leaving the required logarithmic-derivative
coefficient. These claims are kernel-checked by the private declarations
named in Route and the public `result`.

The resulting conclusion settles the A381355 COMMENTS conjecture and the
matching A381353 COMMENTS assertion under the source initialization.
The wider schedule/slope family is open as a formal target. There is no
asymptotic or sign-pattern conclusion, and no consequence for the distinct
A393866/A393867 source equation. No inspected source identifies an additional
result that depends on the conjecture.
