# The exact two-contrast Young envelope

## Problem

Abed, Nikolaeva and Novikov, *How characterisations of traces and central elements fail*, arXiv:2610.34388v1, Conjecture 11.1, final clause, page 57:

> and the lower bound of Proposition 5.22 is the two-contrast envelope of item 8 for even n.

For $n=2r$, $r\ge1$, $0\le\delta<1$ and $0\le\eta\le1$, the envelope is

$$V_{2r}(\delta,\eta)=\frac{4\delta\eta}{1+\eta^2-2\delta\eta}.$$

## Motivation

The two contrast parameters constrain the density and both actual input moduli simultaneously. An estimate for only one parameter or for independently optimized spectral data does not establish this envelope.

## Gap

## Route

For $m,M,u,v\ge0$, $mI\le R\le MI$ and $uI\le H,K\le vI$ with $\operatorname{Tr}H=\operatorname{Tr}K$, the private theorem `trace_interval_comparison` proves

$$(Mu+mv)\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}(RK)\le(Mv+mu)\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}(RH).$$

It applies `RHLinalg.trace_mul_nonneg_of_posSemidef` to the four endpoint-gap products and combines the resulting scalar inequalities. Unitary Gram orbits have the required common trace. A unitary polar factor is obtained through `D5.S3.Quantum.Algebra.GramUnitaryExtension.exists_unitary_mul_eq_of_conjTranspose_mul_eq`. Spectral bounds and the weighted Young estimate give the upper bound without an evenness hypothesis.

For attainment, put $k=(1+\delta)/(1-\delta)$ and $\ell=(1-\eta)/(1+\eta)$. Repeating the density block $\operatorname{diag}(k,1)$ and the input block with off-diagonal entries $1,\ell$, with $A=B$, attains the displayed quotient. The private diagonal-spectrum owner in `AlternativeMeanLinearization` computes the contrast. The denominator equals $(1-\eta)^2+2\eta(1-\delta)>0$ on the stated domain.
