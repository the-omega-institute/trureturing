# One generating measurement does not make pseudo-broadcasting equivalent to non-contextuality

## Problem

P. Jokinen, M. Weilenmann, M. Plávala, J.-P. Pellonpää, J. Kiukas and R. Uola, *No-Broadcasting Characterizes Operational Contextuality*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 133, 240201 (2024), arXiv:2406.07305v1, Appendix D, state:

> One can also see that $\mathcal S(\mathcal A)$ is a Cartesian product of $k$ simplexes if $\mathcal A$ is generated by $k$ independent measurements. This leads to a conjecture that if $\mathcal A$ is generated by $k$ measurements, then $1\mapsto k$-pseudobroadcasting is equivalent to non-contextuality. This is only a conjecture since even if $\mathcal A$ is generated by $k$ measurements, $\mathcal S(\mathcal A)$ in general is only a subset of a Cartesian product of $k$ simplexes. We leave resolving this issue and proving or disproving the conjecture for future work.

This quotation is from `Arxiv.tex`, line 482, in the arXiv v1 source archive (534 lines; md5 `cc2fa845c0b0f4155ed18dcb76e4c92e`). The source macros `\hS`, `\hA` and `\btn{k}` are expanded to $\mathcal S$, $\mathcal A$ and $1\mapsto k$; the wording is unchanged. The main-text conjecture is on line 348. The definitions and further quotations are in [the literature note](../Library/QuantumContext/jokinenetal2024nobroadcasting.md).

Issue [#21862](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/21862) fixes the reading of generation: $\mathcal A$ is the real convex hull of the binary classical post-processings $\sum_o c_oM_j(o)$, with $0\le c_o\le1$, of $k$ finite POVMs. A subtheory has a convex set $\mathcal T$ of density matrices and a convex set $\mathcal A$ of effects, contains $I$ among its effects, and is closed under $E\mapsto I-E$. The conjectured equivalence is refuted by a qubit subtheory generated by one four-outcome POVM.

## Motivation

The source's Theorem 5 (`Arxiv.tex`, lines 336–338) identifies measurement and preparation non-contextuality with $1\mapsto n$ pseudo-broadcastability for every $n\in\mathbb N$. The conjecture would replace this entire hierarchy by its $k$-copy condition whenever $k$ measurements generate the effects. It would make feasibility at that single level a certificate of non-contextuality. The source's finite preparation and measurement tests impose linear conditions on the candidate pseudo-broadcasting map.

## Falsifier

The refutation depends on matching the source's subtheory definitions and the registered meaning of generation:

- `GeneratedBy` uses arbitrary finite POVMs and all their binary classical post-processings, followed by real convex hull. It imposes no independence, distinctness or norm-one condition on the generating measurements.
- `PseudoBroadcastable` requires a complex linear map that preserves the trace of every input matrix, reproduces every allowed one-copy marginal probability, and gives nonnegative expectations for every product of allowed effects on every allowed preparation. It requires no complete positivity or positive semidefinite output matrix.
- `Noncontextual` allows any label type and summable families. The preparation weights are nonnegative and normalized on $\mathcal T$; each response matrix has trace one and nonnegative evaluations on $\mathcal A$; their weighted responses reproduce all allowed Born probabilities. Global positivity of $g_\lambda$ or $s_\lambda$ is not assumed. Excluding only finite-label models or only positive semidefinite response matrices would not suffice.
- `claim` quantifies over all natural $d,k$, including zero, but its proof is contradicted at $d=2,k=1$. Neither a zero-dimensional system nor the $k=0$ case supplies the counterexample.

Adding independence of the generators would change the conjecture being settled. The one-POVM witness has an operational relation among its outcomes, and that relation must also be respected by the response functionals.

### What the settlement shows

**Proved by `result`:** it is false that for every natural dimension $d$, natural $k$, and sets $\mathcal T,\mathcal A$ of complex $d\times d$ matrices, `Subtheory` and `GeneratedBy` with $k$ imply the equivalence between $k$-copy `PseudoBroadcastable` and `Noncontextual`.

**Computed, not formalized** (exact rational arithmetic):

- The Born probability table, in the state and outcome orders used above, has rows $(2/5,1/10,9/20,1/20)$, $(2/5,1/10,1/20,9/20)$, $(1/10,2/5,9/20,1/20)$ and $(1/10,2/5,1/20,9/20)$. Each selected score is $17/20$, exceeding $3/4$ by $1/10$.
- The four outcome matrices have real linear rank three. Their relation $M_0+M_1-M_2-M_3=0$ is verified exactly.
- The 16 corner evaluations with $u,v\in\{0,1\}$, $z=1-u$, $w=1-v$ and $x,y\in\{0,1/2\}$ have largest separator contribution $3/8$.
- For $a,b\in\{\pm1\}$, the four trace-one matrices $\sigma_{ab}=(I+aX+bZ)/2$ have determinant $-1/4$ and response tuples $((1+a)/4,(1-a)/4,(1+b)/4,(1-b)/4)$. All entries of these four tuples are nonnegative.

**Argued, not formalized.**

- *Why the measurement count misses the factors.* Here $X=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ and $Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$. The real span of the effects is $\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{I,X,Z\}$, since $I=\sum_oM_o$, $X=2(M_0-M_1)$ and $Z=2(M_2-M_3)$. These three matrices are linearly independent, so the displayed relation among the four $M_o$ is the only one. A normalized functional $\varphi$ positive on $\mathcal A$ therefore corresponds exactly to four numbers $q_o=\varphi(M_o)\ge0$ with $q_0+q_1=q_2+q_3=1/2$: these conditions respect the sole relation, give $\varphi(I)=1$, and ensure positivity on every nonnegative post-processing. Consequently $\mathcal S(\mathcal A)\simeq[0,1]^2$ under $(q_o)\mapsto(2q_0,2q_2)$. It is a square, the product of two segments, rather than a simplex. One four-outcome generator gives a constrained slice of its outcome simplex; it does not give one simplex factor. The relevant state space consists of all allowed positive functionals, not only quantum density matrices.
- *The counterexample is not two-copy pseudo-broadcastable.* Suppose a map $\Xi$ satisfied the module's two-copy conditions for these fixed $\mathcal T,\mathcal A$. For $a,b\in\{\pm1\}$ put $w_{ab}(\rho)=4\operatorname{tr}[\Xi(\rho)(M_{xa}\otimes M_{zb})]$. These are nonnegative, linear in $\rho$, and sum to one on $\mathcal T$, because each outcome pair sums to $I/2$ and $\Xi$ preserves trace. Use the trace-one response matrices $\sigma_{ab}=(I+aX+bZ)/2$. Their evaluations on $M_{xc}$ and $M_{zd}$ are respectively $\delta_{ac}/2$ and $\delta_{bd}/2$, hence are nonnegative on all of $\mathcal A$. Summing $w_{ab}\delta_{ac}/2$ over $a,b$ gives $\operatorname{tr}[\Xi(\rho)(M_{xc}\otimes I)]=\operatorname{tr}(\rho M_{xc})$; the other marginal similarly reproduces $M_{zd}$. Linearity extends reproduction to every allowed effect. Nondegeneracy of the finite-dimensional trace pairing represents each linear weight $w_{ab}$ as $\operatorname{tr}(\rho G_{ab})$. This is a four-label `Noncontextual` model for the fixed witness, contrary to `witness_contextual`. Thus the example separates one copy from two copies; it does not supply a contextual two-copy pseudo-broadcastable subtheory.
- *A corrected statement using simplex factors.* Let $r\ge1$, let $\mathcal T$ be a nonempty compact convex set of density matrices in finite dimension, and let $\mathcal A$ be the convex hull of finitely many effects, contain $I$, and be closed under complements. If $\mathcal S(\mathcal A)$ is affinely isomorphic to a Cartesian product of at most $r$ finite simplexes, the surviving criterion is equivalence of $1\mapsto r$ pseudo-broadcastability and non-contextuality. This uses the number of factors of the actual dual state space. G. Aubrun, A. Müller-Hermes and M. Plávala, *Monogamy of entanglement between cones* (2022 preprint, arXiv:2206.11805v2, DOI 10.1007/s00208-024-02935-4), Theorem 2 (`main.tex`, lines 259–266), gives the corresponding finite-level criterion for finite-dimensional closed proper cones, with the normalization functional in the interior of the dual cone. Its product-of-simplexes implication is established on lines 572–605. Taking the preparation and effect cones in their real linear spans supplies these hypotheses here: compact normalized preparations give a closed proper preparation cone, while finite effect generation and complement closure give a closed effect cone with order unit $I$.

  More explicitly, write the product as $\prod_{j=1}^q\Delta_j$ with $q\le r$. Its barycentric coordinate effects $h_{j,o}$ are positive on $\mathcal S(\mathcal A)$, so finite-dimensional cone duality places them in the closed cone generated by $\mathcal A$; for each $j$, $\sum_oh_{j,o}=I$. Apply a putative $r$-copy map to one such coordinate effect per factor, with $I$ in unused copies. The resulting joint weights are nonnegative by conic linearity and sum to one. Their $j$th marginal reproduces every coordinate of factor $j$, by the map's marginal identities. The mixture of the product vertices with these weights therefore reproduces the entire input functional. The weights are linear preparation functionals and each product vertex is a normalized positive response functional, giving a finite non-contextual model. The reverse implication is the source's Theorem 5, since non-contextuality supplies pseudo-broadcasting for every $n$, in particular $n=r$. For the square $q=2$, so two copies suffice for this implication; their existence is excluded by the preceding argument.
- *Effect on the source.* The all-$n$ equivalence in Jokinen et al.'s Theorem 5 does not depend on the conjecture. Its implication from non-contextuality to each finite level survives, so infeasibility at any finite level still certifies contextuality. What the counterexample excludes is inferring non-contextuality merely from feasibility at the number of generating measurements. The reported numerical agreement at three copies for the particular Mazurek scenario (`Arxiv.tex`, lines 348–350) is not contradicted by this different one-generator example; it does not establish the universal conjecture. No verdict for every unrestricted $k\ge2$ is supplied by this settlement.
