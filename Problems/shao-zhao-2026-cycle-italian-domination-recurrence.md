# Shao–Zhao cycle Italian-domination recurrence

## Problem

P. Shao and C. Zhao, *Counting Weight-k Italian Dominating Sets on Trees and Cycles*, arXiv:2610.00108v1, §9 (verbatim):

> Several directions remain open for future work: … • Deriving a complete linear recurrence for the total count Σ_k d_I(C_n, k) using the transfer matrix formulation (the observed limiting ratio ≈ 2.7843 is the dominant eigenvalue of the corresponding transfer matrix).

The preregistered issue is [#12435](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/12435). The settled statement is that, for every natural `n ≥ 3`, the total count `a(n)` satisfies
`a(n+5) = 2a(n+4) + 2a(n+3) + a(n+2) − a(n+1) − a(n)`, with
`a(3), …, a(7) = 23, 60, 167, 467, 1297`.

## Motivation

The source leaves the transfer-matrix recurrence open. The frozen declaration
`D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/CycleItalianDominationRecurrence.result` resolves that named question for labelled cycles and supplies the initial values needed to determine every later count.

## Gap

Before this module, the source's complete constant-coefficient recurrence and its determining initial values were absent from the repository. The source's other §9 directions, including unimodality and closed forms for `d_I(C_n, 2n−j)`, remain outside this settlement.

## Route

Represent a function on `Fin n` by its consecutive value pairs in `Fin 3 × Fin 3`. The Italian condition is exactly the admissibility condition on each transition of the nine-state transfer matrix `T`. The closed-walk equivalence gives `a(n) = trace(T^n)`. Grouping rows into the five classes `{(0,0)}`, `{(1,0)}`, `{(2,0)}`, `{(*,1)}`, and `{(*,2)}` gives `T = R S` and `S R = Q`. The exact identity
`Q^5 − 2Q^4 − 2Q^3 − Q^2 + Q + I = 0`
and trace cyclicity yield the displayed recurrence; direct traces give the five initial values.

## Falsifier

A counterexample would be a natural `n ≥ 3` for which the integer recurrence fails, one of the five initial values differs, or the closed-walk correspondence fails for an Italian function or a closed transfer-matrix walk. The Lean theorem `result` checks the universal statement and the initial values in the kernel.

## Evidence

- **Proved:** `result` in `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/CycleItalianDominationRecurrence.lean` proves the recurrence and all five initial values with the standard three axioms.
- **Computed:** exhaustive enumeration for `n = 3..14` gives `23, 60, 167, 467, 1297, 3612, 10058, 28003, 77970, 217095`, agreeing with the recurrence.
- **Computed:** Hankel ranks of the enumerated sequence give rank five and exclude orders at most four; the characteristic polynomial is irreducible over `ℚ` and its dominant root is `2.7843303…`, matching the source's observed ratio.
- **Computed:** the weighted transfer matrix `Q(w) = diag(1,1,1,w,w²) Q` yields the weighted recurrence recorded in the implementation probe.
