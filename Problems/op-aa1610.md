# The hidden-matching cloning bound

## Problem

Equation (21) conjectures that the uniform mean of the two marginal fidelities is at most $1/2+1/n$ for every even dimension $n\ge2$ and every completely positive trace-preserving map from one $n$-dimensional system to two. The settlement is **Proved**. [Amiri and Arrazola, arXiv:1610.06345v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.06345v1) · [Library note](../Library/HilbertOperators/amiriarrazola2016hiddenmatching.md) · [Preregistration #24701](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/24701)

## Motivation

The paper uses the mean single-copy marginal fidelity to bound the sum of the two verifiers’ mean errors for a maximal disjoint set of matchings. The dimension-uniform estimate removes the finite numerical cutoff for this step.

## Route

Let $L=2n^2Q$ be the rescaled performance matrix on input–output triples. An odd coordinate multiplicity admits a bit flip that changes the sign of the product in the uniform average. The remaining fourth moments are given by the three pairings, with the all-equal case counted once. The resulting $L$ is real, symmetric and entrywise nonnegative.

For a triple $(a,b,c)$, the weight is $2(n-1)$ if all entries agree, $2$ if only $b=c$, and $n$ otherwise. The weighted row sum is bounded above by $(n+2)$ times the row weight. The scaled complex quadratic row estimate then bounds the form of $L$ on any tensor vector. Finite Kraus representation expresses the Choi matrix as a sum of outer products whose squared norms sum to its trace $n$. The tensor trace pairing identifies the objective with the Choi trace against $Q$. Summing the quadratic bound yields $(n+2)n/(2n^2)=1/2+1/n$.

## Falsifier

A counterexample would give an even $n\ge2$ and a CPTP map with mean fidelity strictly greater than $1/2+1/n$ under equation (4)'s input ensemble and equation (11)'s two marginal expectations. The endpoint $n=2$ is included.
