# The Jeudy–Rouleux global-permutation Lie-algebra expectation

## Problem

C. Jeudy and M. Rouleux, *Generalized exchange operators for a system of spin-1 particles*, arXiv:2411.03952v1, Section 3.1, pp. 6–7; DOI 10.1088/1742-6596/2912/1/012043. The source is recorded in [jeudyrouleux2024exchange](../Library/LieTheory/jeudyrouleux2024exchange.md). The named expectation is preregistered in [#19130](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/19130).

> Moreover since that the CSCO of the P- and Q-reps are the same in this case (see [Jin], p.70), we expect that Schrödinger Hamiltonian P_S, as well as other Hamiltonians, belongs to L.

The settlement refutes the membership assertion for the Schrödinger Hamiltonian at $(S,N)=(1,3)$. It uses all six global permutations of the three spin labels, hence also refutes membership in the smaller Lie algebra generated by the transpositions alone. It asserts nothing about other Hamiltonians.

## Motivation

The expectation would connect particle exchange Hamiltonians with the Lie algebra generated by global spin-label permutations. These operations act on different aspects of a configuration: particle exchanges permute positions, whereas the global action permutes labels uniformly.

## Gap

Equality of complete sets of commuting observables does not establish this Lie-algebra membership. A global label permutation preserves equality between any fixed pair of particle labels; particle exchanges can move that equality to another pair.

## Route

Let $O$ be the diagonal projector onto configurations satisfying $x_0=x_1$. Every global permutation preserves this relation, so it commutes with $O$. The centralizer of $O$ is a Lie subalgebra under the matrix commutator; therefore it contains $L$.

For $y=(0,0,1)$, the Hamiltonian gives

$$
Hy=(0,0,1)+(1,0,0)+(0,1,0),
$$

where each configuration denotes its basis vector. Only the first summand satisfies equality of the first two labels. Consequently $HO-OH$ has entry $1$ at row $(1,0,0)$ and column $(0,0,1)$.

## Falsifier

Membership $H\in L$ would force $HO-OH=0$. Its displayed entry equals $1$, contradicting that equality. The kernel-checked public conclusion is `D5.S3.Quantum.Algebra.JeudyRouleuxGlobalPermutationLie.result`.

## Triage

### What the settlement shows

- **Kernel-checked mechanism, $(S,N)=(1,3)$.** The three private theorems `q_commutes`, `lie_span_le_commutant`, and `commutator_entry` establish that $L$ commutes with $O$ while $H$ does not; the public `result` establishes $H\notin L$. Equality of the first two particle labels is the separating invariant.
- **Computed only, five cases.** The experiment entry records $H\notin L$ and $P_{23}\notin L$ for exactly $(d,N)\in\{(3,3),(2,3),(2,4),(3,4),(4,3)\}$. The listed dimensions and commutator maxima are numerical readings. A universal theorem for $d\ge2,N\ge3$, and exact single-exchange exclusions, remain **open in this delivery**; neither is a theorem of this module.
- **Open, enlarged space.** Section 6's $\widehat L=L_1+L_1^*$ is outside `claim` and `result`. The source describes the dual family only modulo a two-dimensional subspace. This settlement supplies no membership or exclusion theorem for that enlarged space. Matching CSCO data alone supplies no Lie-membership implication.

The nearest statement retained by the proof is the exact inclusion of $L$ in the centralizer of $O$. The source's other spectral and representation results are outside the refuted assertion. Any use of the Section 3.1 membership expectation as a premise for this Hamiltonian is invalid; membership in the enlarged Section 6 space requires a separate argument.
