# Hardin's recurrence for the six-column diagonal pair-exclusion strip

## Problem

[OEIS A233172](https://oeis.org/A233172), by R. H. Hardin, has NAME

> Number of n X 6 0..5 arrays with no element x(i,j) adjacent to itself or value 5-x(i,j) horizontally, diagonally or antidiagonally, top left element zero, and 1 appearing before 2 3 and 4, and 2 appearing before 3 in row major order (unlabelled 6-colorings with no clashing color pairs).

with an empirical recurrence of order ten (verbatim in
[issue #29162](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/29162)).

A bond joins horizontal neighbours $(i,j),(i,j+1)$ and diagonal neighbours
$(i,j),(i+1,j\pm1)$; vertical neighbours are not bonded. A bond is legal when
its endpoints differ and do not sum to $5$.
