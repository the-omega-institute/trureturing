# Hardin A229691

## Source

[OEIS A229691](https://oeis.org/A229691), NAME and first FORMULA line;
registered in [#30838](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/30838).

> Number of defective 3-colorings of an nX5 0..2 array connected horizontally and antidiagonally with exactly two mistakes, and colors introduced in row-major 0..2 order.

> Empirical: a(n) = 45*a(n-1) -729*a(n-2) +4995*a(n-3) -13122*a(n-4) +14580*a(n-5) -5832*a(n-6) for n>7.

## Interpretation

Let a(n) filter all arrays Fin n × Fin 5 → Fin 3 by exactly 2
monochromatic registered bonds and the original row-major canonical
condition. Counts are cast to integers for the recurrence.

## Gap

The source labels its recurrence empirical. The target quantifies over
every n at least 8. Finite agreement with a b-file is insufficient.
The registration's literature search found no proof in the OEIS links or
the cited Potts-strip literature; global completeness remains unverified.

## Route

The transfer retains the outgoing row tail and the residual budget. Global colour
permutations reduce these states to 27 classes. A finite kernel
certificate checks every transition, the terminal and initial weights,
and the forward-vector relation of degree 6 after shift 1.
`DefectTransfer.lumped_count` identifies the labelled count with the
transfer. `canonical_pattern_card` gives six labelled arrays per canonical array
once at least two colours occur. `DefectTransfer.forward_shift` propagates
the relation to arbitrary length and cancellation of six gives the result.

## Falsifier

A literal canonical count violating the recurrence at an index at least
8, or a mismatch in any transition sum, falsifies the proposed claim.
