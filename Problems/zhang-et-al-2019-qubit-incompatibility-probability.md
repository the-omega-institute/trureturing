# The incompatibility probability of random qubit measurements

## Problem

L. Zhang, H. Xiang, X. Li-Jost and S.-M. Fei, *Incompatibility probability of random quantum measurements*, Phys. Rev. E 100, 062139, arXiv:1912.12321, Section III.C, PDF p. 5, states:

> We *conjecture* that the incompatibility probability of a pair of random qubit measurements $\mathbf A$ and $\mathbf B$, generated by $(a_0,\boldsymbol a)$ and $(b_0,\boldsymbol b)$ in Eq. (4), is given by $\mathbf{Pr}[\Theta^{\mathrm{NJM}}_{2,2}]=\frac14.$
