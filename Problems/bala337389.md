# Bala's all-column diagonal formula for Manyama's array

## Problem

OEIS A337389, revision #40, NAME and final FORMULA, defines Seiichi Manyama's array and records Peter Bala's conjecture:

> Square array T(n,k), n >= 0, k >= 0, read by antidiagonals, where column k is the expansion of sqrt((1+(k-4)*x+sqrt(1-2*(k+4)*x+((k-4)*x)^2)) / (2 * (1-2*(k+4)*x+((k-4)*x)^2))).

> Conjecture: the k-th column entries, k >= 0, are given by [x^n] ( (1 + (k-2)*x + x^2)*(1 + x)^2/(1 - x)^2 )^n. This is true for k = 0 and k = 4. - _Peter Bala_, May 03 2022

The source is OEIS-only; no arXiv identifier or numbered paper conjecture applies. Its normalized Taylor branches have constant coefficient one.

## Motivation

The conjecture identifies a nested-radical column with a diagonal of powers of one rational series. The natural-column identity simultaneously includes the special columns two and three.

## Gap

The radical definition and diagonal expression are independent source formulas. The theorem establishes their equality in every degree, with normalized-pair existence and universal quantification over all such pairs. Finite coefficient comparisons do not establish this assertion.

The cancelled-factor normalization follows directly: $[t^0](Y±2t)=[t^0]Y±2[t^0]t=1±2·0=1$. This is a prose derivation from the displayed hypotheses.
