# Jenzer's permuted rotor-loop formula without the star condition

## Problem

Nik Jenzer, *Star-Ordered Rotor Loops in Clifford Algebras*, arXiv:2610.21593v1,
Theorem 5.5 and the following scope remark, TeX Remark 5.7, rendered Remark 5.5,
page 19, asks whether the permuted loop formula remains true without the star condition:

> Nevertheless, numerical evidence suggests that the formula also holds without the star condition in that case: tested on the triangle family from Remark 4.4, which violates the star condition, under all six permutations of {1, 2, 3} with random parameter sets; the largest deviation encountered was in the range of double machine precision. A general proof would have to capture the cancellation of the odd off-diagonal terms in coarser groups than pairs; we have not determined such a grouping.

For an anticommuting unit bivector family $B_1,\ldots,B_k$ in a finite-dimensional
nondegenerate real Clifford algebra, $k\geq1$, put
$c_i=\cos(\theta_i/2)$, $s_i=\sin(\theta_i/2)$, $w_i=s_i^2$,
$U_i=c_i+s_iB_i$, and
$\Theta_k(\tau)=(U_1\cdots U_k)(\widetilde U_{\tau(1)}\cdots\widetilde U_{\tau(k)})$.
Then the proved statement is

$$
\langle\Theta_k(\tau)\rangle_0
=\sum_{S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k\}}
\eta(|S|)(-1)^{\iota(\tau,S)}
\prod_{i\in S}w_i\prod_{i\notin S}(1-w_i),
\qquad
\eta(m)=(-1)^m(-1)^{m(m+1)/2}.
$$

Here $\iota(\tau,S)$ counts pairs $i<j$ in $S$ with
$\tau^{-1}(i)>\tau^{-1}(j)$. No star condition is assumed.

## Gap

The source's termwise pairing for the identity permutation does not give the
arbitrary-permutation formula. A family may have a nonzero odd full-support
scalar coefficient, so discarding every nonempty subset coefficient would
assume the star condition that the question removes.

## Route

`JenzerPermutedRotorLoopAlgebra.NativeBridge.scalar_cyclic` proves cyclicity
of the native scalar coefficient. Its tracial support lemmas eliminate
all unequal subset pairs except complementary odd full support.
`JenzerPermutedRotorLoopWords.ExteriorSum.signed_top_sum_zero` proves that
the balanced exterior word has zero odd top coefficient. The private
`JenzerPermutedRotorLoop.scalar_pair_split` expresses the exceptional terms
with that common coefficient. Their sum is zero; the diagonal subset-square
and squared trigonometric-weight formulas supply exactly the source sum.
These names are relative to `D5.S3.QuadraticForms`.

## Falsifier

A source-family instance, real angle vector and permutation violating the
displayed identity would refute the claim. A construction that changes the
native scalar projection, replaces bivectors by arbitrary even elements,
or assumes vanishing full support does not address this claim.
Finite experiments alone do not settle the universal statement.

### What the settlement shows

The decisive mechanism is aggregate cancellation: cyclicity removes the
proper off-diagonal supports, and the odd full-support obstruction is the
odd top coefficient of a balanced exterior word. The named theorems in the
first row prove these parts and their use in the settling identity.
The source claim retains its full finite-dimensional nondegenerate scope,
while native scalar cyclicity is proved for every real quadratic form on a
real module, including degenerate forms and infinite-dimensional modules.
No nonvanishing trigonometric coefficient is required.

The result removes the star hypothesis from source Theorem 5.5, resolving
its following scope remark. It makes no star-family classification, no
higher-grade claim and no separately packaged general tracial rotor theorem.
The source's reverse-order corollary remains its inverse-factor cancellation
statement; it is not an additional resolution claim here.
