# Gy's coefficient log-concavity question

## Problem

Gy asks whether strong coefficientwise $q$-log-concavity of a sequence
in $\mathbb R[q]$ implies coefficient log-concavity of both symmetric
triple-product differences at every $1\le j\le p\le n$.
[Published question](https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/aa108/aa108.pdf#page=16) · [Registration and producer credits](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/31896)

| Source clause | Lean declaration | Correspondence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $f\le_q g$ | qLe | Every coefficient of $g-f$ is nonnegative. |
| $x_i x_r\ge_q x_{i-1}x_{r+1}$ for $r\ge i-1\ge0$ | StronglyQLogConcave | The Lean left index is the source left index minus one. The equality case is included. |
| $e_d(X_m),h_d(X_m)$ on $X_m=(x_1,\dots,x_m)$ | inputPrefix, e, h | Evaluation of Mathlib's subset and multiset symmetric polynomials on that prefix. |
| Two triple-product differences on printed p. 15 | E, H | All six factors of each difference keep their source degree, prefix and sign. |
| Adjacent log-concavity of the coefficients | CoeffLogConcave | The determinant is required at every $1\le k<\operatorname{natDegree}(f)$. |
| Every sequence and every $1\le j\le p\le n$ | claim | The strong premise implies the conjunction of the two output conditions. |
| Negative answer | result | The theorem negates claim. |

## Motivation

A common polynomial factor persists through the products and can control the
coefficient determinant of the resulting difference.

## Gap

The input condition controls coefficient signs of pairwise product
differences. The output question asks for inequalities between neighboring
coefficients of each triple-product difference.

## Route

Put $g_t=1+q+tq^2$ and $x_k=(k+a)g_t$. The input products in each
required comparison differ by $(r-i)g_t^2$ in the shifted indexing, so every
required coefficient is nonnegative when $t\ge0$. At $n=j+2$ and
$p=j+1$, both output differences equal $C g_t^4$, where
$$
C=\frac{j(j+1)^2(j+2)(2a+j+2)(4a+j+3)}{48}.
$$
The first three coefficients are $C,4C,(6+4t)C$. For $j\ge1$, $a>0$
and $t>5/2$, the third coefficient is positive and the determinant at
index one is $(10-4t)C^2<0$.

## Falsifier

The common witness $a=1,t=3,j=1$ gives $C=10$ and coefficients
$10,40,180$ for both outputs. The determinant is $-200$ in each
case.

## Evidence

In the Lean proof, the elementary degree-two identity follows from
Newton's identity. The homogeneous degree-two sum is counted by ordered
pairs with diagonal terms counted once. The positive coefficient at
degree two gives the interior guard at degree one.

## Triage

The categorical answer to the coefficient log-concavity question is a
refutation of each output assertion. Conjecture 5.1 concerns coefficient
nonnegativity of $E$ and $H$ and lies outside this result.
