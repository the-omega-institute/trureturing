# Tebni's OEIS A350759 prime congruences

## Problem

Mélika Tebni's OEIS A350759 revision 17 lists these COMMENTS conjectures:

> For all p prime, (a(p) + a(p+1) - 2) == 0 (mod p),  
> a(p+1) == 1 (mod ((p+1)*p)).

Its FORMULA line is the exponential generating function
`exp(-1 + (1 + x)*(1 - log(1 + x)))`. The resolved Lean statement is
`D5/S3/Arith/Congruence/TebniA350759PrimeCongruences.claim`: an integer sequence with that exact EGF exists, and every such sequence satisfies both congruences. The settling declaration is
`D5/S3/Arith/Congruence/TebniA350759PrimeCongruences.result`, which proves `claim`.

### Statement echo

The Lean `sourceSeries` is the published formal EGF, with `PowerSeries.log ℚ` interpreted as the formal `log(1+X)` and `PowerSeries.subst` taking the inner series first. `IsSourceSequence a` states that the EGF of the integer sequence `a : ℕ → ℤ`, with coefficients cast to `ℚ`, equals `sourceSeries`. The first conjunct of `claim` is existence of such an `a`; its second conjunct quantifies every such `a`, every natural `p`, and `Nat.Prime p`, then asserts
`Int.ModEq (p : ℤ) (a p + a (p + 1) - 2) 0`
and
`Int.ModEq (((p + 1) * p : ℕ) : ℤ) (a (p + 1)) 1`.
The natural product is formed before its cast to `ℤ`, and the prime p=2 is included.

## Route

The proof first obtains the exact rising-factorial expansion of the source EGF. After factorial scaling, the coefficient at every index n is `1+n*z` for an integer z: the index-zero term is 1 and every other term is divisible by n. The exact differential equation for the source EGF gives an exponential generating-function convolution. For a prime p, interior binomial coefficients vanish modulo p; the endpoint is handled by Wilson's theorem, with p=2 handled in the same formal development. The all-index residues at p and p+1, together with coprimality of consecutive integers, yield the product modulus and the first congruence.
