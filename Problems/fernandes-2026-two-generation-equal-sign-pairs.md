# Two-generation of the equal-sign subgroup of a permutation product

## Problem

For integers `m, n ≥ 2` let `S_m × S_n` act on the disjoint union of `[m]` and `[n]`, and let

    Γ(m,n) = { (σ₁, σ₂) ∈ S_m × S_n : sgn σ₁ = sgn σ₂ }

be the index-two subgroup of pairs whose product action is even. Vítor H. Fernandes states
the following in "Groups of permutations that are even on maximal proper subsets, and related
monoids", arXiv:2605.12342, May 2026, quoted from the paper's Conjecture 1:

```text
For all integers m ≥ n ≥ 2 such that (m,n) ∉ {(2,2), (3,3), (4,3), (4,4)},
the group Γ_{m⊕n} has rank 2.
```

The four exceptions are the known small ranks: `rank Γ(2,2) = 1` and
`rank Γ(3,3) = rank Γ(4,3) = rank Γ(4,4) = 3`.

## Motivation

Γ(m,n) is the kernel of the sign-difference homomorphism into `ℤˣ`, so it contains
`A_m × A_n` with index two. Two-generation of such an extension is not implied by the
two-generation of its factors, and the conjecture asserts that two generators suffice
uniformly outside a finite exceptional set.

## Gap

The repository had no formalisation of Γ(m,n) nor of any two-generation statement for it.
The conjecture is recorded as open in the Formal Conjectures corpus
(`FormalConjectures/Arxiv/2605.12342/Conjecture1.lean`, `@[category research open]`).

## Route

`Γ m n` is defined as the kernel of `(sign ∘ fst) * (sign ∘ snd)⁻¹`. Because `sign` is
injective on `Perm (Fin 2)`, the projection `Γ(m,2) → S_m` onto the first factor is injective.
The two generators are the lifts of `finRotate m` and of the transposition `(0, finRotate m 0)`:
the full cycle is paired with whichever element of `S_2` matches its sign — the identity when
`m` is odd and the transposition when `m` is even — and the transposition is paired with the
transposition. Their projections are a cycle and an adjacent transposition, which generate
`S_m`; injectivity of the projection then lifts that to `Γ(m,2) = ⟨g₁, g₂⟩`.

## Falsifier

A pair `(m, 2)` with `m ≥ 2` for which no two elements of Γ(m,2) generate it; equivalently,
a failure of the injectivity of the first projection or of the generation of `S_m` by a full
cycle together with an adjacent transposition.

## Evidence

The theorem is proved without `sorry`, `native_decide` or added axioms; its axiom closure
is exactly `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.

## Triage

`theorem`. This settles the conjecture for the whole family with second degree `n = 2` and
`m ≥ 2`. The statement proved here is slightly stronger than the conjecture restricted to that
family, since it includes the excluded pair `(2, 2)`: `Γ(2,2)` has order two and is therefore
generated by two elements, the paper listing it as exceptional because its rank is one rather
than two. The conjecture as a whole, for general `m ≥ n ≥ 3`, remains open and is not claimed
here. Concrete degrees with `n ≥ 3` were reached by kernel-level exhaustion over the second
factor, but a single positive finite instance is not an admissible contribution under the
utility rule for computational content, so no such instance is delivered.

## ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED

The paper text is taken from the Formal Conjectures transcription of Conjecture 1 rather than
from the arXiv source; the implementation seat had no network. The stated ranks of the four
exceptional groups are quoted, not reproved here.
