# Nilforoushan-Parvaresh Conjecture 8.1

## Problem

Conjecture 8.1 of Arman Nilforoushan and Farzad Parvaresh, *Improved upper
bound on the number of distinct k-decks for any k and alphabet size by counting
the independent parameters*, arXiv:2609.23106v1, states:

> D_{q,k}(n) = Theta(n^{E_q(k)}) for every fixed q >= 2, k >= 1.

The paper defines `D_{q,k}(n)` as the number of distinct exact `k`-decks of
length-`n` words over a `q`-letter alphabet and defines
`E_q(k) = sum_{j=1}^k j L_q(j) - 1`, where `L_q(j)` is the number of actual
length-`j` Lyndon words over that alphabet. Preregistration issue #9512 records
this complete target, not one of the cases already proved in the source.

The formal target quantifies over an arbitrary finite linearly ordered type
`A`, assumes exactly `2 <= Fintype.card A`, takes every natural `k` with
`1 <= k`, and proves `Real.IsTheta` at `Filter.atTop` for the real-valued
cardinality of `exactKDeckImage A k n`. Its exponent is
`weightedLyndonExponent A k - 1`, with
`weightedLyndonExponent A k = sum_{i=0}^k i * actualLyndonCount A i` and
`actualLyndonCount A i` the cardinality of the subtype of actual Lyndon words
of length `i`. The zero term vanishes, so this is the source exponent. There is
no free proxy parameter and no extra hypothesis on `n` or on `k`.

## Motivation

The source proves the general upper bound but leaves the matching lower bound
open outside `k=2` and the binary `k=3` case. A complete resolution must
therefore build many decks realized by actual positive words, preserve exact
source length, and prove that the actual Lyndon coordinates determine the
complete deck. Dimension counting or a free family of abstract parameters is
not sufficient.

## Gap

The paper's affine-dimension calculation concerns relations generated by the
subsequence-count product rule and explicitly distinguishes that calculation
from realizability by actual decks. The formal gap is the general lower bound
with the source exponent, together with an unconditional passage from Lyndon
coordinates to all scattered counts. The existing frozen Vivion theorem is a
different binomial-complexity refutation and supplies only reusable definitions
and basic count identities.

## Route

The route has ten connected owners. First, recursive standard bracketing gives
each actual Lyndon word an integral homogeneous polynomial with that word as
leading monomial. Binary and indexed fixed-source schedule theorems retain
occurrence identity, including duplicate factor occurrences, and show that an
interleaving of nonincreasing Lyndon factors cannot exceed their concatenation.

Second, the actual Magnus polynomial has scattered-count coefficients.
Recursively generated positive pairs agree below their level, and their first
possible difference is the corresponding standard-bracket direction. The
full indexed family spans every standard bracket. Literal power substitution
then scales the first unequal degree while preserving all lower degrees.

Third, one selects as many independent actual positive-pair directions as
there are actual Lyndon words. Direction-major, scale-minor digit blocks give
equal-length positive words whose lower coefficients are fixed and whose
degree-`r` coefficients encode a complete base-`2^r` digit array injectively.
Combining these blocks over all degrees yields the weighted-Lyndon lower growth
of the actual positive-word cutoff-Magnus ball.

Fourth, padding a ball representative to a fixed length loses only the one
source-length degree of freedom and injects the construction into actual exact
`k`-decks. Conversely, the overlap-infiltration identity and the indexed
shuffle leading order prove unconditionally that agreement on actual Lyndon
coordinates through `k` implies agreement on every scattered count through
`k`. For a fixed deck length, erase one singleton Lyndon coordinate and recover
it from total length; the remaining coordinates inject exact decks into the
expected box. The lower and upper bounds are then the two directions of
`Real.IsTheta` at `atTop`.

## Falsifier

The resolution would fail if any formal object counted proxy directions rather
than the actual Lyndon-word subtype, if duplicate indexed shuffles were
identified and changed product multiplicities, if the positive-pair words did
not share the asserted lengths and lower coefficients, if the multi-scale map
were not injective, if padding changed the cutoff data, if exact-deck equality
did not imply all shorter counts at a common source length, if Lyndon recovery
required the conclusion as a guard, or if the erased singleton coordinate
could not be recovered from the common total length. A proof only for a fixed
alphabet, fixed `k`, sufficiently large `k`, or an abstract free parameter
would not settle the registered statement.
