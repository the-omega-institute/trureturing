# Sample-covariance logarithmic energy need not decrease

## Problem

Chafaï, Dadoun and Youssef ask in Question 1.3 (arXiv:2212.06090v2,
PDF page 4) whether the penalized logarithmic energy of the mean empirical
spectral measure of $X_nX_n^*/n$ decreases to the square Marchenko–Pastur
value for every centered complex entry law of unit second moment.
The source and binding conventions are specified by
[#27294](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/27294).

### Statement echo

| Source clause | Lean counterpart |
| --- | --- |
| One fixed centered complex law of unit variance | `SampleCovarianceSpectralAndSmooth.EntryLaw`; the same measure is used for every dimension |
| Independent identically distributed entries | `matrixLaw`, the finite product law transported to matrices |
| Eigenvalues with algebraic multiplicity of the normalized Gram matrix | `gramEigenvalue` and `empiricalSpectralMeasure`; each eigenvalue is divided by the positive dimension |
| Expectation before energy | `meanSpectralMeasure`, followed by `penalizedEnergy` |
| Logarithmic singularity and extended real energy | `logPositiveKernel`, `logNegativeKernel`, `logarithmicEnergy`; the diagonal positive part is infinite |
| Linear moment penalty | `firstMoment` and `penalizedEnergy` |
| Consecutive nonincrease in every positive dimension | First conjunct of `SampleCovarianceLogEnergyRefutation.claim`, with dimensions $n+1,n+2$ |
| Convergence to the square Marchenko–Pastur energy | Second conjunct of `claim`; `marchenkoPastur` uses the source density |

The public definition `claim` preserves the preregistered proposition.
Integrability and probability normalization in `EntryLaw` express the
source's expectation and variance assumptions, rather than extra regularity.

## Gap

The source proves Gaussian/Laguerre formulas and asks the universal
sample-covariance question. This refutation supplies a fixed smooth entry
law for which the penalized energies increase from dimension two to three.
It does not settle convergence alone for arbitrary entry laws.

## Route

The Bernoulli variable has values $-1/\sqrt{255}$ and $\sqrt{255}$,
with probabilities $255/256$ and $1/256$. Independent circular Gaussian
noise of amplitude $\varepsilon$ is added and the result is divided by
$\sqrt{1+\varepsilon^2}$. `Smooth.smoothLaw_EntryLaw` proves the required
center and second moment for this one law.

The two-copy Gram determinant multiplies all eigenvalue differences.
Its logarithm therefore gives the mean-measure logarithmic energy.
For the common constant rank-one configuration in dimension $n=s+1$,
the first nonzero perturbation order is $2s^2+1$, hence three at $n=2$
and nine at $n=3$. Gaussian-polynomial logarithm bounds justify the
small-noise limit. The weighted order slopes satisfy $L_2<L_3$;
`Energy.exists_fixed_epsilon` chooses one positive noise amplitude and
`result` contradicts the consecutive nonincrease clause at $n=1$.
All qualified names in this paragraph are relative to the delivered
covariance modules, as detailed below.

## Falsifier

A failure of the refutation would require the displayed admissible smooth
law, energy identity, asymptotic comparison or fixed-amplitude extraction
to fail in the stated formal system. `result` proves the negation of the
closed source claim with no hypothesis asserting a counterexample.
It does not prove that every entry law violates monotonicity.

### What the settlement shows

1. **Proved (kernel-checked): failure mechanism.**
   `SampleCovarianceBernoulliOrders.CommonOrder.poly_common_order` proves
   order $2s^2+1$ for every nonzero real constant rank-one base in dimension
   $s+1$. `Events.k_common` specializes it to the Bernoulli common event;
   `Events.L_gap` proves $L_2<L_3$.
   `SampleCovarianceLogEnergyRefutation.Energy.E_limit` transports the slope
   to penalized energy and `Energy.exists_fixed_epsilon` selects a fixed
   positive amplitude. `result` refutes the universal question.
2. **Proved (kernel-checked): general determinant facts.**
   `GaussianPolynomialLog.Covariance.pairDet_degree_le` bounds the degree by
   $2n^2$ for arbitrary base and noise matrices over its declared ring.
   `Covariance.pairDet_identity_noise_ne_zero` supplies a nonzero identity-noise
   specialization for arbitrary base pairs.
   `SampleCovarianceBernoulliOrders.Noise.poly_ne_zero` proves the universal
   complex Gaussian-coordinate polynomial is nonzero.
   The constant rank-one order assertion above holds for every real $c\ne0$;
   no claim for arbitrary complex $c$ beyond the declared statements is made.
3. **Computed: checker result in the tested scope.**
   The experiment entry gives the immutable source, command, exit 0,
   SHA-256 and `ALL_CHECKS_OK` reading. Its exact-order hints have the stated
   degree omission. The kernel-checked order argument supplies the settlement;
   the corrected enumeration is not claimed as a new independent computation.
4. **Open: neighbouring questions.**
   Convergence alone for arbitrary `EntryLaw` is unsettled here.
   The source's Gaussian/Laguerre conclusions are unaffected: the witness is
   a non-Gaussian mixture and does not contradict those hypotheses.
   No monotonicity assertion for other entry laws, mixture probabilities,
