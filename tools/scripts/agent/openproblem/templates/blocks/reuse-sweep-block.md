
## Reuse sweep before the door (binding; CLAUDE.md §§3.1, 4.3)
A new definition equal to a D5 owner or a pinned-Mathlib definition violates §4.3. Before the first door:
0″. **Claims stay literal; proofs use owners.** When a binding convention that appears in `claim` has a body equal to an existing owner, the reviews require two things at once: the claim must state the preregistered convention, and no duplicate declaration may exist. Satisfy both this way.
   - State `claim` with the convention's preregistered body **inlined literally**, and add no fresh `def` for it.
   - Use the owner inside the proofs, through a compiled correspondence (a `private`, consumed helper).
   - Say both in one sentence of the module comment.
   - Never state the public claim through a substituted owner. Never keep a fresh `def` equal to an owner.
0′. **Closed owners are reusable whether or not they are frozen.** Closed imports may be unregistered; state membership is not an import prerequisite (`skills/codex-formalize/SKILL.md`). Reuse every applicable closed D5 owner on `origin/dev`, frozen or not.
   - Check the owner's state file and distinguish closed source reuse from frozen membership. An absent state file does not prevent a closed import.
0. **Binding conventions do not license duplicates.** A preregistration's Lean conventions fix the mathematical content of each definition, not a duplicate declaration. Before you write any convention as a new `def`, search for an equivalent owner:
   - frozen D5 owners, including private ones, by the concept and its defining condition (for example `IsTree`, `vecMulVec`, `insertionSort`, `Equiv.swap`, `NamedSpanningTree`);
   - pinned Mathlib, likewise.

   If an owner is equivalent (possibly after specializing a parameter), preserve any literal preregistered claim as in item 0″; otherwise state the claim with the owner. Record the correspondence in the module comment and dossier statement echo. Prove the correspondence inside the consuming proof if needed. Do not add a public identity theorem for it.
1. For EVERY `def`/`structure`/`abbrev`/`instance` the delivery adds, public or private, search origin/dev `D5` by its **defining expression**, not its name, and check each plausible candidate with a scratch `example : @mine = @candidate := rfl` (after the needed cast or `funext`). Report one table row per declaration (name, short defining expression, candidates tried, rfl-check exit code, verdict `unique` or `duplicate-deleted`); a sweep limited to the names a reviewer pointed at is not a sweep. Use `git grep -n -P` on the key shape (for example `normSq .* = 1`, `\.1 \+ 1 = .*\.1`, `Orthonormal`, `blochMatrix`, `cfc`) and on the concept vocabulary. Search pinned Mathlib with `rg` likewise.
1′. **The duplicate criterion.** A definition is a duplicate when (a) its body re-derives by hand a construction an existing declaration or a composition of existing declarations provides (a hand-written linear equivalence, tensor product, gate product, rank-one projector, coordinate map, …) — rebuild the body from those declarations, keeping the name if the source names the object or several consumers share it — or (b) it is a pure forwarding name (its body is an existing declaration, or an entry or row of an existing construction, with no new construction) — delete it and use the existing expression. Compare each body also against compositions of the obvious upstream constructions, not only against single declarations. (c) It states the same defining condition as an existing declaration over a different carrier reached by an injective cast or map (`SignType` → `ℤ`, `ℕ` → `ℤ`, `Fin n` → `ℕ`, a subtype → its ambient type, …) — delete it, apply the owner to the cast (`Owner (fun i => (a i : ℤ))`), and prove the cast correspondence inside the consuming proofs. Search the owner by its quantified condition with the carrier abstracted, not by its type signature.
(d) It is an instance of a **generic** owner stated over a typeclass-ordered or typeclass-structured carrier (`[LE R]`, `[Zero R]`, `[One R]`, `[Mul R]`, …) once the pinned instance for your carrier is unfolded. Example: `IsEffect (E : R) := 0 ≤ E ∧ E ≤ 1` under `MatrixOrder` is exactly `E.PosSemidef ∧ (1 - E).PosSemidef`, by `Matrix.nonneg_iff_posSemidef` and `Matrix.le_iff`. An abstract carrier does not distinguish a generic owner from its specialization. Instantiate it with your carrier and its pinned instance, try the correspondence proof, and reuse it as in (a)–(c) or item 0″.
1″. **Theorem-level reuse.** For EVERY theorem/lemma the delivery adds, public or private, check whether a frozen D5 owner or a pinned-Mathlib theorem already gives its conclusion (after specialization, a cast, or a `.re`/`.1`/`.mp` projection): `git grep -n -P` on the conclusion's shape across origin/dev `D5` (for example `trace_mul_nonneg`, `PosSemidef.*trace`, `eigenvalues.*le`) and `rg` in pinned Mathlib, then a scratch `example : <statement> := by exact?` with the candidate owners' modules imported. Such a helper is not reproved: import the owner and apply it, inline or in a consumed adapter whose proof is the owner's application. Respect SL-010: a module tagged `generality: G` may import only `G` modules, so pick a `G` owner (or Mathlib) when the lighter owner is `I`. When the only owner is a `private` theorem in an `I` module and the consumer is `G`, extract it, unchanged, into a new shared `G` module that both import (CLAUDE.md §4.3): the old owner imports it and is re-pinned through the canonical writer, and the extracted theorem becomes a public audit target. Search a stronger owner by its general shape too (for example an analytic-function null-set theorem dominates a polynomial one). In the Stage A envelope, report one row for **every** theorem and lemma the delivery adds, whether or not it has a hit: its name, the `exact?` outcome on its exact statement (the term it found, or `none`), the conclusion-shape search hits, and the verdict (`reused`, `not-applicable` with the reason, or `no-owner`). A theorem without a row means the sweep was not run, and the pre-freeze mirror rejects the delivery. The sweep is complete only when every declaration has a row.
1‴. **Within the delivery and against the current dev.** (a) Check every helper against every other declaration of this delivery: a helper whose conclusion is a specialization of a delivered declaration must apply it (make the general one public if needed), not re-prove it. (b) Repeat items 1′ and 1″ against `origin/dev` after the door's merge, before the builder commit, covering every module frozen on dev since the lane began (`git log --diff-filter=A --name-only <lane base>..origin/dev -- 'Golden/Frozen/state/D5/**'` lists them); a private frozen owner that gives the conclusion is made public at its source and applied. (c) For finite Fourier and character sums, roots of unity and geometric series, check `AddChar.sum_mulShift`, `ZMod.stdAddChar`, `Finset.geom_sum_eq` and the repository's frozen Fourier modules before writing a proof. Report each check with its command and exit code.
2. Read every **SL-028** observation from the applicable scoped checks or required CI; follow the CI-gate block for gate scope. An observation that a new declaration is "definitionally identical" to an existing one must be resolved by reuse: delete the new definition, import the owner, and use it directly. Normalization goes in a local `have`; there is no alias and no identity theorem. The only alternative is to state in the PR body why the reuse is impossible.
3. Report the search conclusion in the PR body, not the commands: the scope searched (names, defining expressions, conclusion shapes, owners), each decisive hit with its reuse decision, and the SL-028 resolution. A per-command or per-query table is a command log (CLAUDE.md §2.10, §3.1) and review seats reject it.

**Dependency identities in the module comment.** Where the second comment block names a frozen dependency with an identity, use the declaration's `statement_id` from the Lean report (generated by script from `.lake/build/stratalint/raw-lean-report.json` at the merged dev base), never the module pin in `Golden/Frozen/state/<module>.lean.json`. If a module pin is given at all, label it "module statement_id".

**Namespaces follow files.** Every module declares its declarations under the namespace of its own path and file name (`D5.<path segments>.<FileName>`, with sub-namespaces under it if wanted). Never open another module's namespace, a shared workflow namespace, or a scratch namespace in a delivered file; when a declaration moves between files, its namespace moves with it, and every reference, Scribe Lean name and comment-block label follows. Check with `rg -n '^namespace' <the lane's directories>` before handing over.

**One-line facts are not exempt.** The sweep also covers helpers proved by a single `decide`, `simp` or `omega`, such as `∀ a : ZMod 2, a = 0 ∨ a = 1`. Search `D5` for the same statement shape, including private declarations (`git grep -n -P 'ZMod 2\)\s*:\s*\w+ = 0 ∨'`). Make an exact hit public at its owner and apply it, rather than re-proving it. Review rounds reject a re-proved one-liner exactly as they reject a re-proved theorem.

**Imports named by anchors stay.** The import-deletion check never removes an import that the module header names in `anchors:` (`mathlib/module/<M>` requires `import <M>` in that module; SL-017 blocks an anchor its import closure does not reach). Keep such an import even when the build passes without it, or drop the anchor only if the source being ported had none.

**Imports by constant ownership, not by deletion.** A `D5.*` import is kept only if BOTH hold: (i) the module uses at least one constant defined in that imported module itself, and (ii) deleting the import breaks compilation (if it does not, another kept import already brings the module in and the direct line is redundant; delete it). Judge (i) by constant ownership (collect each declaration's used constants with `Lean.Expr.getUsedConstants` and map each to its defining module). An import that only relays other modules is a false prerequisite in the Freeze event, even when deleting it breaks compilation: replace it by direct imports of the modules that define the used constants. Report one row per import: module, import, a used constant defined there (or `none`), kept or replaced.

**Definition and instance sweep by body (binding).** For EVERY new `def`, `abbrev`, `structure` field and `instance`, public or private, search `origin/dev` `D5` by the defining expression, not only by the name. Use its distinctive head symbols, for example:
- `git grep -n -P 'Complex\.exp \(.*Real\.pi' D5`;
- `git grep -n 'Finset.univ.filter (fun' D5`;
- `git grep -n 'diagonalAlgHom\|→⋆ₐ' D5`;
- for an instance, `git grep -n 'DecidableRel (SimpleGraph.pathGraph'` and the instance class.

Include `private` owner declarations. A frozen owner with the same body up to casts or normalization is reused:
- import it when SL-010 allows (a `G` module imports only `G`), make it public at its source if private, and normalize inside consumers;
- when SL-010 forbids the import, delete the new definition and write the owner's expression inline (a forwarding name is a duplicate).
- **A publication is a mirror change.** Each owner declaration whose `private` you remove gets a `Describe` node in that owner's Scribe: its exact `DeclarationHandle` and its complete statement or defining expression, with every binder and hypothesis and real Lean names. Then run `make emit` so the owner's `.md` lists it. Review seats reject a public declaration that has no node in its owner's Scribe.

**Equality, not definitional equality, decides.** A candidate owner counts as the same object when a short proof gives `∀ x, new x = owner (adapted parameters) x`, after reparametrizing, reordering arguments, coercing, or composing with an affine map of the argument. A failed `rfl`, a different telescope or a different argument order does not clear it. Try the proof (`funext` plus `simp`/`ring`/`norm_num`, cases on order) for each candidate before deciding, and record the result. Search Mathlib too: for example clamps (`Set.projIcc`, `max 0 (min 1 t)`), indicators, `Real.toNNReal`, `Nat.dist`, `Finset.sum_range` forms. Where an owner exists, use it with the adaptation inside consumers, and add no new definition.

The CI `current` job reports remaining definitional duplicates as SL-028; treat each as a reuse finding before review. Report one row per new definition: name, search pattern, hit or none, decision.

**A publicized owner is an escape-audit target (CLAUDE.md §3.9; binding).** When the sweep makes a frozen private owner public at its source, that declaration becomes a new public theorem of a selected D5 module, and the escape audit covers it like any other.
- Attempt its four-slot registration in the owner's mirror `Reg/D5/<owner path>.lean`.
- If the attempt does not reach `DTR-Declared`, ship no Reg file. Instead, name the declaration in the lane's unfinished-audit issue, with its GID, its declaration `statement_id` and the gap reading.
- The PR body's escape-audit paragraph names it with its status.
- A publicized owner **definition** must also be mirrored in its owner's Scribe with its formula: a `Definition(…)` node that renders the definition body symbol by symbol, never `StatementSource.WithoutFormula()`. Re-emit the owner's `.md` in the same delivery.
- **Report capacity.** Before publishing an owner, count its transitive D5 importers. With more than 20, search for an equal public owner by conclusion shape. If none exists, report `open` with the importer count and measured build constraint before editing that owner.

- **Touched owners carry no diagnostics.** Any frozen owner that the delivery edits (publication, move or re-pin) must leave the delivery with no `#print`, `#check` or `#eval` line, including lines that predate the edit. Take such readings in scratch files.

**Closed `have` steps are helpers too.** A `have` inside a proof whose statement mentions no local hypothesis or bound variable is a closed fact. Examples are `(Real.sqrt 2 / 2) * (Real.sqrt 2 / 2) = 1 / 2`, an identity between fixed matrices, or positivity of a fixed matrix. Sweep it exactly like a lemma: search by statement shape in `D5` (private declarations included) and in pinned Mathlib, then try `exact?`. If an exact frozen hit exists, make it public at its owner and apply it, rather than re-proving it inline. Report the number of closed `have` steps checked and each hit; a mirror that finds an unreported closed duplicate rejects the delivery.

- **Concrete data is not a duplicate.** A definition whose body is concrete data (a matrix, vector, polynomial or number given by explicit literal entries, such as a counterexample's witness state) does not duplicate a generic owner solely on the grounds that an owner applied to chosen constants reproduces it. Only a body that rebuilds a *construction* (an operation on its arguments, such as a product, a sum over an index, a reindexing or a sandwich `P * U * P`) can duplicate an owner. Do not report, and do not apply, an "owner" rewrite of literal witness data.

- **Pre-existing owner code is out of scope.** The sweep covers the declarations, imports and local facts that the delivery adds or changes. Edits to a frozen owner are limited to the required publication, move or re-pin and removal of diagnostic commands. Do not report, and do not apply, a reuse, import or style change to the owner's pre-existing code. Imports named by the owner's `anchors:` header stay in any case (SL-017).
