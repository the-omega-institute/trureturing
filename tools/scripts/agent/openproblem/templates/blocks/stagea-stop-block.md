
## Stopping rule (binding)
**You may return only when one of these holds:**
- **(a)** `result : claim` compiles with no hypotheses and with the standard three axioms only, and the acceptance items above are met;
- **(b)** a mathematical obstruction, stated with exact evidence: a counterexample, a false step, or a definition that cannot be made literal;
- **(c)** you have worked at least four hours.

On (c), return with:
- every proved lemma compiled into the modules;
- the remaining obligations stated as exact Lean statements.

Returning early with only definitions or a plan is not accepted.

**Return as soon as the work is settled.** When every item is settled, return at once under (a) or (b), whatever the elapsed time: each item is proved, or shown false with a compiled counter-evidence and a source-faithful repair. Idling, sleeping or "holding" until a time limit is reached is forbidden; the time limit in (c) is a floor for giving up, not a target.
