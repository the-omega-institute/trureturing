# Autonomous Research Round

## Scope and authority

This file packages a frontier generation, filtering and continuation workflow; it has no authority of its own. `docs/develop/spec/golden-ledger-repo-spec.md` is the sole normative specification; `CLAUDE.md` is the invariant frame governing how work is done; and `agents/CONTEXT.md` is the finite-context map and routing aid. Live harness output is the decisive judge of fact about the current tree. If this file disagrees with any of them, they win and this file is the bug.

The invoked AI session is the orchestrator. Complete one bounded round yourself: reconcile existing work, dispatch generation and isolated prediction seats, preregister, dispatch implementation and independent evaluation, consume machine evidence, settle P/I and mathematics separately, deliver eligible content and leave an exact successor. Proceed between stages without caller instructions. Use the existing codex/sshx runners, `python3 tools/scripts/agent/nyx/nyx.py ask <brief-file> <output-file>`, `gh` and make doors; no additional round executable is required. The host supplies repeated invocation through `/goal` or scheduled tasks; this skill supplies no persistent runtime. Its research-hit policy adds neither an admission state nor a human-review gate and does not change `CLAUDE.md` §§3.2–3.3. A valuable application or surprising answer does not authorize an otherwise inadmissible new declaration.

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `agents/CONTEXT.md` and the applicable owner below before execution. Use the current goal and actual harness capabilities, rather than inheriting a template's goal or claiming a tool result from its documentation.

## 1. Observe and bound the round

### Bounds and ownership

Fix a stable goal scope and host invocation identity, the round's wall-clock/call budget, absolute UTC deadline and stopping conditions before generation. Keep the goal key unchanged across revisions and host wakes. Reentry retains the invocation's limits and completion disposition; an already ended invocation performs only missing reporting/delivery, never starts another unit. Each invocation starts at most one new unit. For a continuing research line, bound this round's contribution without replacing the line's preregistered success and stopping criteria with an hourly deadline. Fix unit limits before its first model call and carry them into the preregistration prose outside the strict block. A later invocation may finish reporting or delivery but cannot extend that unit's mathematical deadline, refund calls or restart its execution clock. Reserve reporting time before dispatch; required checks retain their own registered budgets and are never shortened to fit a research window.

Use at most one active research unit, at most three simultaneous child seats and at most one oracle seat. Before **each** model invocation, including retries, capability probes and runner fallbacks, durably precharge one call and its time allocation against the round, unit and any enclosing pilot budget. Unknown/failed calls remain spent; a runner must not retry or spawn uncharged descendants. Do not dispatch if an allocation would exceed a limit. Each child needs a host/runner-enforced timeout no later than its reserved deadline, with a job handle that can be joined or cancelled; a prompt deadline alone is insufficient. If the host cannot enforce or recover that bound, record the capability gap instead of launching an unbounded child. The pilot, when in scope, has six host invocation windows in 48 hours, at most one new unit per window, at most 60 model calls total, at most 3600 seconds of mathematical work per unit and at most 1200 seconds per call; declare the actual tighter round allocations before use. Keep process and scientific pilot outcomes separate; no extra windows or calls to obtain a hit. These are bounded unit/pilot settlements, not a cap on the broader authorized goal's iterations.

Serialize dispatch for the **goal**, not the whole worktree. Use an existing host atomic exclusion primitive or a native Git compare-and-swap claim shared by all linked worktrees: store a unique orchestrator-attempt owner/session identity with `git hash-object -w --stdin`, then claim a goal-keyed ref outside branch/tag namespaces with `git update-ref <claim-ref> <owner-oid> <expected-old-oid>` (zero old OID for an absent ref). Only successful acquisition permits generation, dispatch or writes; a losing entrant reports the active owner and returns a recoverable handoff. Recheck ownership before each dispatch/mutation. Keep job handles and the minimal checkpoint in untracked host storage associated with the absolute `git rev-parse --path-format=absolute --git-common-dir`, surviving claim release. Never steal on elapsed time alone: transfer by compare-and-swap from the observed old owner only after confirming that its orchestrator and every child exited or were cancelled/joined. Unknown liveness/ownership is a named gap. Release with `git update-ref -d <claim-ref> <owner-oid>` only after checkpointing and joining writers, never an unconditional delete. This coordinates research dispatch, not unrelated edits or Git index operations (§6.1); do not publish the local claim ref. Separate clones/hosts require an actually shared host exclusion primitive. A GitHub read followed by a comment is not atomic exclusion; without shared exclusion, do not race another owner.

### Read carriers and recover

Observe the current branch, immutable base, working changes, in-flight PRs and CI, open preregistration issues, `Problems/` dossiers, `Library/`, existing `D5/` results and relevant research-line obstacles for recovery, reuse and duplicate checks. These observations are context for generation, not a queue from which to select topics. Use `git status --short`, `git rev-parse HEAD`, `gh pr list --state open`, `gh issue list --state open --search 'Preregister in:title'`, and the selected PR's `gh pr checks <number>` as applicable. Check query limits/pagination before claiming an exhaustive screen. Unavailable queries are named missing readings, not empty work queues. Respect other worktrees and live workers under `CLAUDE.md` §§5.11, 6.1.

On entry, including in a new session, inspect the existing preregistration issues and research-line records within the current goal and authorization: recover unsettled units and unfinished deliveries first, then reconcile registered successors before fresh generation. Include closed issues when discovering successors: use `gh issue list --state all --search 'Research unit in:title'` and `gh issue list --state all --search 'Preregister in:title'` as applicable, and follow their result and research-line links. `gh issue view <number> --comments` is a reading aid; acquire authoritative issue metadata and **all** comments with `gh api repos/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/<number>` and `gh api --paginate --slurp 'repos/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/<number>/comments?per_page=100'`. Validate page/record shape, identity, bodies and timestamps before using the flattened comments. Search truncation, partial pagination, malformed output and timeout are missing readings, never an empty queue or proof that a write did not occur. For a pending issue-creation action, inspect paginated REST issues in its fixed scope/time interval, including closed issues; search-index absence is insufficient. A successor supplies prior results and an exact unresolved boundary to §2's generator; it is not an accepted assignment or a topic-selection stage. Record each missing reading or capability and its dependent work under §5.9 in the existing carrier, or the round report when the carrier cannot safely be written. Block only dependent work and continue independent preparation or verification for this unit within its original limits; do not open a second unit while ownership or an external write is unresolved. Report incomplete work separately from completed idle filtering.

Before resuming, derive the first incomplete stage from the carrier's candidate, immutable preregistration, prediction evidence, execution start, solver/check results, independent evaluation, P/I conclusion, linked content PR and its exact-head required/MERGED evidence, terminal and successor. Reconcile these with the actual branch/base, working changes, live jobs, limits and authorization. Maintain only a minimal untracked host checkpoint: goal/invocation/unit identities, fixed limits and consumed allocations, current action and job handles, carrier URLs/digests, pinned branch/head/base and next unfinished obligation. GitHub and current machine evidence determine scientific/delivery state; the checkpoint supplies recovery intent and spent budget, never mathematical truth. Write it atomically before dispatch or mutation. If it is missing or corrupt, reconstruct only from attributable evidence; if spent budget or an attempted action cannot be bounded, do not assume zero or repeat it. Do not commit this checkpoint, prompts, streams or snapshots, or create a candidate database/global latest pointer.

Before every external mutation, including issue/comment creation, push, PR creation, auto-merge/merge or closure, reread its full carrier/remote state and reconcile a stable action identity. Put a marker such as `<!-- research-action:<goal>/<unit-or-generation-attempt>/<purpose>/<revision> -->` outside strict blocks in issue/PR payloads. Retain the exact payload digest and intent **before** sending; retries reuse the identity and payload. For push use branch plus exact commit; for merge use PR plus exact head/base. One exact, unchanged match means reuse the existing URL/result. Duplicate markers, differing payloads or edited commitments are conflicts, not permission to choose a favorable copy. An unknown response is an attempted write: reread and adopt one exact match; if none, multiple or incomplete matches leave the outcome uncertain, suspend that mutation without resending or inventing a new identity. Only evidence that no request was sent permits a later first send. Persist the pending obligation for the next invocation; record a carrier gap in the round report if that carrier is unsafe to write. Never edit/delete old comments or strict blocks. These are session rules, not a server-side exactly-once guarantee.

Resume only the remaining work under the original criteria; reuse completed work whose evidence and inputs remain current, and do not reset predictions, execution start or deadline. Lost worker output is not permission to repeat a possibly completed call: recover its existing job/result, otherwise record the unknown result and spent allocation. Malformed/timeout generation, solver or evaluator output cannot establish a result; lost predictions stay missing. A genuinely new attempt needs an explicit remaining allocation and must not duplicate a still-live or unknown attempt. If a deadline or stopping trigger has been reached, settle from existing evidence under §5 rather than starting again. Pin the implementation head, immutable comparison base and delivery target separately. A moving target branch alone does not invalidate an unchanged proof; a changed candidate/dependency/implementation head invalidates affected checks and evaluation until rerun for that revision within the original limits. Never silently rebind old evidence or alter the registered event to accommodate a new head.

Determine whether a unit has ended from its terminal or owning route's settlement evidence, not merely the issue's open/closed flag. An ended unit, including an invalid or unresolved terminal, is not solved again and receives no second terminal or altered prediction. Finish only missing reporting or delivery obligations without duplicating a terminal. Further mathematical work on an ended question requires a fresh preregistration citing the old result and preserving its conclusion and boundaries.

Reuse prior results first. Search repository declarations, including private ones, then pinned Mathlib, then admissible third-party Lean libraries in the order of §3.1; check literature and in-flight work for duplicates and stronger results. Compare statements, assumptions, quantifiers and actual consumers, not just names or import counts. Do not assign implementation for an already solved target or create a wrapper to count it as new work.

## 2. Generate results against two independent evidence columns

Dispatch GPT Pro or another available AI to reason deeply at the frontier and directly propose mathematical results, rather than search for topics to assign. Use `python3 tools/scripts/agent/nyx/nyx.py ask <brief-file> <output-file>` or the installed sshx oracle broker runner under `CLAUDE.md` §5.11; discover its actual installed contract rather than inventing a repository runner path. Do not draw research topics from atoms, `search-atoms`, digestion queues or the digestion ledger of existing theory volumes. Atom lookup belongs only to downstream formalization/coverage after a result has been generated. Supply existing results, literature and unresolved boundaries as context for reuse and reasoning, not as a replacement topic queue. Inline the checked relevant frozen statements and assumptions, both escape criteria defined below, and every required output from the next paragraph; a link alone is insufficient context. Every GPT Pro brief follows `CLAUDE.md` §5.11, including the repository URL and the exact instruction 「深入思考与推理, 如数学推理按照正式定义定理证明推理」.

Require candidate definitions, a complete quantified theorem or construction with parameters and assumptions, a proof/derivation and explicit remaining gaps, the proposed non-binding fact and its live role, direct known dependencies (GID and `statement_id` for frozen prerequisites) and affected statements/consumers. Require drafts for both ordinary escape registration and the AURIC `Application`: actual source, original theorem occurrence, parameter/state/output families, readouts, task, layers, source/specialization/reconstruction bridges and the evidence obligations for requested readings. The generator remains responsible for the full mathematical proposal even when it cannot execute Lean. Its proof and contract drafts are proposals, not verification. For physical claims, distinguish model consequences from empirical truth and identify proved, assumed and external premises.

Define a research hit by two separately evidenced columns on the **same new result**, following `CLAUDE.md` §§3.2–3.3, 3.9 and `docs/develop/spec/auric_fib_atom_escape_audit_spec.md` §§1, 4, 5, 9:

| Column | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **P — proof content** | An independent evaluator establishes `proof_shape: content` and a §3.2 escape witness on the reduced live proof path, with normal admission and §3.3 use obligations satisfied. Bind-only helpers need real consumers under the existing exception and supply no escape content. Ordinary registration and its actual verdict remain governed by §3.9, including disclosure of any unfinished audit under its linked-issue exception. |
| **I — task information** | A source-faithful, compiled `Application` analysis contract and a preregistered nontrivial information objective for its fixed actual task, established by one of the two routes below. The source bridges and task relevance must be proved; a positive count or a decodable contract alone does not establish I. |

For the generation brief, inline §3.2's actual test: expand local aliases/new helpers, tactics and definitional equalities up to existing frozen/pinned upstream premises. Instantiation, premise substitution, logical projection/recombination and normalization alone are bind-only. An intermediate escape witness must occur in the elaborated transitive dependency closure, escape those operations, differ from the conclusion by more than definition/alias/restatement, and survive reduction/dead-term deletion on its live derivation path; a dispensable dependency is insufficient. Alternatively the conclusion itself may be produced directly by a non-binding computation/construction/estimate, without inventing an intermediate. Inline the information test too: the joint kernel identifies lawful states when all fixed readouts agree; adding a readout must remove an actual collision relevant to the fixed task, or prove the task boundary below. These operational summaries remain subordinate to the cited definitions, and neither tactic names nor raw dependency counts classify content.

- **Capture route:** prove genuine unique capture by an added readout, or strict refinement of the old joint kernel, and prove how the captured distinction affects the registered task. Witness states belong to the same lawful source/parameter fiber; an extra coordinate that separates states without affecting that task is insufficient.
- **Boundary route:** prove a nontrivial limitation or sufficiency result for the registered task: lawful realizations with identical old readouts and different task answers, a task's constancy or nonconstancy on observation fibers, or a proved association/continuation residual bound. Preserve the stated domain, quantifiers and task correspondence. This route can establish I without positive unique capture; a restatement of definitions is insufficient for P.

Bind the `Application` to the original compiled occurrence and complete dependent telescope, not a convenient true substitute. All source, readout, task and layer fields must refer to the same actual implementation; state explicit specialization and reconstruction maps. Verified correspondence requires this client's Lean proof terms, accepted axiom closure and current compiler/source binding evidence; compiling an interface alone is insufficient. Keep states/histories, source laws and readout catalogs distinct. Request finite counts/rates or FIB coordinates only with their additional source, finite-presentation, reflection and law evidence under the AURIC specification; qualitative kernels and source contracts do not require those optional acquisitions. Ordinary proof registration and information analysis are independent: neither validates the other or changes its verdict.

Before directed proof, fix the domain, old/added readouts, task, layer order, information objective and chosen route. Do not change them after observing outcomes to claim a hit; a changed target or witness needs a fresh preregistration preserving the old settlement. Report P and I separately, with evidence or named gaps. These are research evidence columns, not new repository admission states. Do not impose `δ=0` or either `ε` endpoint as a rejection gate: zero unique capture does not imply absent proof content, and exact finite `ε=0` describes injectivity, not novelty. Do not sum proof escape, information gain, surprise, cost or research value into a total, ranking proxy or new value function.

Check three controls on the same lawful source and task: **identity readout** can remove all collisions by returning the state but that tautology is not new content; **duplicate readout** preserves the joint kernel and has no unique capture; **unrelated coordinate** may refine a kernel while leaving the task unaffected. Report their actual readings and scope, including zero or unavailable readings, and do not settle any of these effects as a new research result. Do not enlarge the source or change the task to manufacture a favorable control. A control that cannot be lawfully instantiated has that specific gap, not an invented value.

For claims about declaration-level consumers, use `make -s -C tools compiled-dependencies` with the fixed revision and explicit finite module domain in `tools/scripts/agent/research_readouts/README.md`. Preserve acquisition/completeness and the distinction between type/constructor, raw value and module-import edges. Missing data is unmeasured, not zero; private intermediates carry paths without adding public users. Raw value occurrence is not §3.2's reduced live-path test, indispensability, an escape witness or research value; a finite query establishes no whole-D5 ordering.

## 3. Preregister before directed proof; isolate predictions

After exploratory generation and duplicate/initial literature screening, and before any directed mathematical probe, solver attempt, computation or Lean implementation, create or reuse the unit's GitHub issue. Retain its complete candidate and definitions, quantified binary event, assumptions, proposed non-binding fact and intended live path, P/I obligations and fixed information objective from §2. Fix success, refutation and stopping criteria, exact round/unit deadline and call/time limits, affected statements, intended proof shape and admission/use basis, scoped literature findings and seat ownership in immutable comments outside the strict block. Give the carrier §5.2's actual skill/producer/family, mixing/verification scope and session recovery identity. Generated proof drafts do not count as independent settlement; an already settled result is reused, not repackaged as a new prediction experiment. A probe that might settle the event also requires this prior registration. No post-hoc witness or prediction is accepted.

Give prediction seats **only the event and its assumptions/quantifiers**, with no generated proof, proposed witness, contract solution, probe result or solver output. Use fresh isolated sessions with no inherited conversation, solution-bearing workspace or retrieval/tool access to those materials. Obtain a codex prediction and attempt at least one attributable non-GPT-family prediction, such as Claude through the host's available interface; inspect actual carrier/model identity, not a seat's self-label. Check the installed interface and capability before claiming it works. If the other family is unavailable, record the attempted capability and failure, freeze only genuine predictions and continue the unit; do not invent a probability, rename GPT as another family or claim cross-family coverage. Obtain and freeze their probabilities before directed execution; prediction seats do not solve or validate the unit. Keep generator, predictor, solver and independent evaluator roles separated; no seat evaluates its own proposal or implementation. Oracle seats may generate, reason or predict but never run verification, judge proof shape/fidelity or supply validation results. Codex executes downstream Lean/kernel, axiom-closure, registration, Application and required-check verification.

For machine-recomputable predictions, append exactly one unchanged `research-prereg-v1` comment using the schema in `tools/scripts/agent/research_readouts/README.md`: event, deadline, binary Brier rule/version, surprise threshold and actual seat/model-family probabilities (or the explicit empty array). The registration stamp comes from REST `created_at`; require `updated_at == created_at`. Read back the complete carrier and match the exact event, candidate/contract digests and frozen probabilities before recording execution start and dispatching a solver. Keep P/I objectives, source/readout/task/layer contracts, controls, budgets, action markers, continuity links and successor text outside the strict `research-prereg-v1` and `research-terminal-v1` JSON blocks; do not add schema fields or edit prior blocks. Changed events or witnesses require a fresh unit/issue and preregistration before further directed work, preserving the old settlement; never append a second preregistration or terminal block to the old issue.

Then run budgeted reuse/binding/normalization probes through §4. A failed cheap probe is only a bounded observation, not proof of difficulty or content. Route known or wholly bind-only internal results to reuse without filler Lean or a harder variant manufactured to pass a screen. High predictor confidence does not determine proof shape or reject content. After two consecutive bind-only targets outside the external named-problem exception, change representation under §3.2 before new generation. An external named problem still needs §3.6's verbatim source, full statement, tier and scoped literature readings before any probe; internal descendants cannot inherit `open-problem-resolution` from their parent. That existing exception can permit delivery with bind-only shape, but does not establish P or a two-column research hit.

Before formal novelty language or delivery, perform §3.7 literature due diligence using codex network search, Claude Code `WebSearch`/`WebFetch` or GPT Pro search for legally readable original sources. Follow §5.11's alternate reading routes; Firecrawl is optional. An executable carrier or orchestrator must obtain and inspect the original text, version and theorem conditions; an oracle summary is a search lead, not verification. Keep the minimal scoped findings and source evidence in their existing owner. Use `literature-attested` for known results and `repo-derived` for repository derivations; use `suspected-novel` only after serious scoped search found no prior result and source checks were completed. An unread source remains a specific unverified gap; no global originality claim follows from a search miss.

Advance at most one active unit per round. A registered successor cites its source unit/result and exact unresolved statement, and must satisfy current reuse, preregistration, P/I, admission and use rules; its predecessor's judgment does not transfer. If completed generation/filtering yields no eligible new result, report idle with the bounded scope and dispositions; do not create filler Lean or an idle-state file. Incomplete reads/capabilities follow §1's dependent-work rule and are not a completed empty screen.

## 5. Settle and reason forward

At the deadline, call-budget exhaustion or a registered stopping trigger, stop further mathematical dispatch, cancel/join remaining mathematical workers and settle from the evidence obtained under the original limits using §2.7's local-settlement vocabulary. Separate proved/refuted claims, invalid attempts and unresolved obstacles; an incomplete run does not prove either answer. Report P and I independently for the same result; neither an admitted result with missing I nor information gain without P is a two-column research hit. An unresolved long-line substep keeps its line's broader criteria. Record actual checks and their scope; local success is not remote required-CI success, and publication is not merged delivery. Perform settlement/reporting with the reserved allocation; if interrupted, leave the minimal untracked checkpoint and carrier obligation for §1 reconciliation, never a reset attempt or a claim that settlement happened. Pending content delivery may remain resumable after mathematical settlement; it keeps priority over a fresh unit.

Then perform §3.6's post-settlement reasoning in an existing external problem dossier's `## Triage`, marking each statement proved, computed or unresolved. `Problems/` accepts only questions explicitly posed by external literature authors, with a resolvable bibkey and matching DOI/URL under spec §11.20.3. Keep internal candidates' results and boundaries in their existing research-line or formal-result owner; do not create an internal `Problems/` dossier or a second store. A refutation should explain the failed mechanism, missing condition, surviving weaker forms and effects on dependent claims. A proof should identify its mechanism or invariant, potential generality, tightness and neighboring applications, and the effects on dependent claims. Keep this as mathematical results and boundaries, not a session narrative.

For every non-idle round, retain the necessary mathematical conclusions, P/I verdicts with separate evidence or named gaps, literature/control findings, unresolved boundaries and an exact successor candidate in the unit's original preregistration issue or existing research-line record. Include links to the source result/evidence, the fully quantified successor statement and assumptions, the checks still needed and any PR delivery obligation; if no successor was identified, state why, including missing readings. Mark whether each successor is merely proposed or already consumed by a linked fresh unit, so another wake cannot start it twice.
