# Record symmetry no-go — preregistration

Target: formalize the obstruction that an equivariant self-adjoint idempotent is scalar under
an irreducible representation, while idempotence forces the scalar to be zero or one.

The proposed admission basis is `escape-witness`. The witness is the binary scalar equation
`P = r • 1` together with `P² = P`, yielding `r ∈ {0,1}`. The reverse implication and the
two-record consequence are planned as direct uses of this obstruction. This is independent
content about idempotence and does not restate or conjoin the frozen entropy or consensus
theorems.
