# Notik's Golomb ruler certificate archive

The lower-bound tree literals and row data in `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Golomb/NotikSmallerBounds.lean` and `NotikData{8,9,10}.lean` in the same directory are adapted from Esther Notik's v3 proof archive and are available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The adaptation translates the JSONL records into Lean constructors with integer coefficient and weight literals and groups the tree subexpressions into declarations. The ordered equality tables in `NotikData8.lean` and `NotikLeaves{9,10}{A,B}.lean` are new certificates keyed to leaves of the adapted trees. [Source DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24309145](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24309145) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
