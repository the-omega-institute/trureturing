The original author, copyright, and full Apache-2.0 license are retained. The complete immutable upstream tree contains no NOTICE-named file. This is a known source transplant, not a new mathematical theorem or an independent proof.

The retained declarations are `ProbabilisticTheory.IsTraceClass`, `hasSum_norm_sq_inner_basis`, `hasSum_norm_sq_apply_of_selfAdjoint`, `summable_inner_abs_of_hilbertBasis`, `trace`, `summable_inner_of_nonneg`, and `trace_eq_of_hilbertBasis_of_nonneg`. The two definitions, three internal summability/Parseval lemmas, and two positive-trace lemmas retain the upstream namespace and nine public Mathlib imports.
