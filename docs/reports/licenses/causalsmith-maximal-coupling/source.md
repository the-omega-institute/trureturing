# Individual-law overlap-coupling source

The private individual-law declarations in `D5/S3/Estimation/DataProcessing/MeasurableMaximalCoupling.lean` are selectively transplanted from Jiyuan Tan's Apache-2.0 Causalean source at commit `a7085d88d2e4b30003db7097ca374f4718da0f01`, repository https://github.com/Jiyuan-Tan/CausalSmith. The selected source files are `Causalean/Stat/Minimax/TotalVariation.lean`, `Causalean/Stat/Minimax/Scheffe.lean` and `Causalean/Stat/Minimax/OverlapCoupling.lean`. Copyright (c) 2026 Jiyuan Tan. All rights reserved. `LICENSE.txt` and `NOTICE.txt` reproduce the complete upstream license and notice.

The transplant retains the consumed event-TV, Scheffe, common-part, residual-mass and overlap-marginal proof bodies. Namespace qualification, private visibility and receiver imports are adapted. The conditional-distribution compression construction and its results are not included. The upstream pins Lean v4.33.0 and Mathlib `db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d`; the receiver pins Lean v4.35.0 and Mathlib `56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679`. Unequal pins exclude dependency-form reuse.

Retirement is checked against the receiver's adopted Mathlib revision.
