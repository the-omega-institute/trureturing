# Combinatorial rigidity source attribution

The Laman sparsity, safe reverse repair, Henneberg preservation, reconstruction, and real planar rigidity proofs are selected source ports from Bryan Gin-ge Chen, CombinatorialRigidity, commit `021c2284bc387efee3c3b20a18eb5f769638b578`.

Source: https://github.com/bryangingechen/CombinatorialRigidity/tree/021c2284bc387efee3c3b20a18eb5f769638b578

The original copyright and author notices are retained. The complete Apache 2.0 license is in `LICENSE.txt` (11357 bytes; SHA256 `b40930bbcf80744c86c46a12bc9da056641d722716c378f5659b9e555ef833e1`). The source tree contains no NOTICE file.

`SparseSets.lean` selects `EdgesIn.lean`, the cardinality support extensions, and the sparsity definitions, degree bounds, transport, and tight-set closure from `Sparsity.lean`. `SafeRepair.lean` selects the tight-blocker and nonedge-repair constructions from `Sparsity.lean`. `Moves.lean` selects `Laman.lean`, `Henneberg.lean`, and the reconstruction portion of `HennebergReverse.lean`, together with their consumed support extensions. `Framework.lean` selects the placement space, orientation-independent edge map, kernel monotonicity, isomorphism transport, openness, and injective two-vertex seed from `Framework.lean`. `HennebergRigidity.lean` selects the consumed Type I and Type II geometric proofs from `HennebergRigidity.lean` and their support from `Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/AffineSpace/AffineSubspace/Cover.lean` (`biUnion_ne_univ_of_top_notMem`, `affineSpan_ne_top_of_ncard_le_finrank`) and `Mathlib/Topology/Separation/Hausdorff.lean` (`Injective.eventually_of_continuousAt`, `Injective.eventually_update_of_continuousAt`). `TrivialMotions.lean` selects the consumed translation, skew-map and independent-family kernel lower-bound proofs from `TrivialMotions.lean`. `PlanarForward.lean` selects the strong cardinal induction and Laman-to-placement implication from `LamanTheorem.lean`. `PlanarLaman.lean` selects its same-vertex row-extraction converse and final equivalence. Its endpoint replaces the finite enumeration argument by the equivalent `Finite` instance and `Nat.card` bound.

`PlanarRows.lean` selects only the row-functional, dual-space factoring, row-basis extraction, moment-curve perturbation, and planar induced-sparsity declarations from `RigidityMatroid.lean`. Its consumed support identities are `LinearMap.range_dualMap_eq_span_image_dualBasis` from `Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Dual/Basis.lean`, `Polynomial.eval_det_X_add_C` from `Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Polynomial.lean`, and `Matrix.det_powerDifferences` from `Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Vandermonde.lean`. Existing receiving cardinality and finite-injection APIs replace the donor cardinality wrappers.

No external matroid structure, generic-rigidity matroid, Jacobs theorem, three-dimensional endpoint, unused row-lift API, or four-vertex example is included.

Modified files reorganize declarations, replace donor module packaging with receiving imports, remove unused declarations, and adapt cardinality and simplification APIs to the receiver. Evaluation lemmas absent from compiled proof dependencies are omitted, with their defining expressions unfolded at the same simplification sites. Exact host neighbor-set and incidence-set cardinality APIs replace donor aliases.

The source uses Lean 4.36.0-rc1 and mathlib `ba5499e069f24171b4be2229dea365c897205f5e`; this port targets Lean 4.35.0 and mathlib `56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679`. Retirement requires equivalent telescopes and graph semantics at the actual receiver pin, followed by compiling direct replacements after migration. Upstream acceptance alone does not establish this condition.
