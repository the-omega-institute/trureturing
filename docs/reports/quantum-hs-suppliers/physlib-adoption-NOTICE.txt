The new bridge's preregistered missing fact is the actual absolutely convergent
basis synthesis from square-summable prescribed weighted columns. It constructs
the bounded operator rather than extracting or assuming an extension. Its
operator bound and exact columns are consumed by the original-operator bridge;
the full actual inverse domain is retained. The private basis-extensionality
adapter is bind-only and consumed by that bridge. The supplied declarations are
attributed library reuse, not this delivery's new mathematics. No new standalone
HS Cauchy-Schwarz wrapper or finite matrix replacement is delivered.

The bridge is `proof_shape: content`, `admission_basis: escape-witness`.
The synthesis intermediate is non-definitional to the conclusion, unavailable
in the supplier stack, and lies on its live proof path. All bind-only adapters
have actual consumers. `utility: none`: the delivery is general operator
analysis, not certified-instance, finite enumeration, numerical reduction or
checker computation. Axioms, current compilation, scoped report and source
binding must be reported from actual artifacts; this provenance note alone
certifies none of those. No global literature novelty claim is made.

Each transplanted supplier is retired when an equivalent directly consumed
statement exists at the receiving repository's own pinned Mathlib revision;
that event is checked against that revision, not hypothetical upstream acceptance.
