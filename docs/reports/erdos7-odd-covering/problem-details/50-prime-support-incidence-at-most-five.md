# Prime-support incidence at most five is noncovering

Let a finite family have pairwise distinct odd numerical moduli greater than one. Group mixed originals by their exact distinct prime support, and suppose each prime belongs to at most five such supports. Then, using the standard conditional Shearer theorem, the genuine full survivors U satisfy

    rho(U) >= 721/57600,
    H(U) >= (721/57600) product_(p in P)(p−2)/(p−1).

Here H is full original CRT Haar, and rho is the product of the actual pure-prime-power survivor laws. Thus the uniform law on U has full Haar density at most

    (57600/721) product_(p in P)(p−1)/(p−2).

Original exponent heights, residues, the number of originals sharing a support, support rank and the number of primes are unrestricted. This strictly strengthens the support-incidence-at-most-four class. It does not settle unrestricted odd covering systems.

The conditional avoidance-ratio input is
[Scott–Sokal, Theorem4.1(a), equations(4.1)–(4.3)](../../../../Library/Arith/scottsokal2003repulsive.md),
[primary v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0309352v2).
Its P_Lambda/Q_Lambda monotonicity supplies the same-law conditional
interface used in Section4. These are ordinary mathematical deductions
and exact finite regressions; no new Lean verification is asserted.

## 1. Keep the genuine product source and original labels

For each original prime p, remove exactly its actual pure p-power classes from the full original-height coordinate. Their surviving Haar mass s_p obeys

    s_p >= 1−sum_(a>=1)p^(−a)=(p−2)/(p−1)>0,

since numerical distinctness permits at most one pure exclusion per exponent. Let nu_p be uniform on those actual survivors and rho=product nu_p.

For a distinct mixed support E, let A_E be the union of its actual original classes. Every original label of support E has probability at most product_(p in E)p^(−a_p)/s_p. Full exponent vectors are injective by numerical distinctness. Completing those nonnegative sums gives

    rho(A_E) <= product_(p in E) b_p =:w(E),
    b_p=1/(p−2).

The supports are not truncated, and no distinctness of projected numerical cofactors is presumed. Events with disjoint supports depend on disjoint whole prime coordinates. Thus A_E is independent of the sigma-algebra generated by every collection of support events disjoint from E. This is the required dependency graph relation; its event-vertex degree need not be five or bounded.

## 2. Signed matching polynomial and the exact certificate

For a finite simple family F of nonsingleton prime supports, define

    Z(F)=sum_(M subset F pairwise disjoint)(−1)^|M| product_(E in M)w(E).

It is the independent-set polynomial of the support-intersection dependency graph evaluated at negative event upper bounds. For a prime set S, F−S means delete every whole support meeting S. In particular, F−E never means trim each support by removing E.

For p>=5 set

    t_5=5/12, t_7=4/17, t_p=1/(p−3) for p>=11.

The five largest distinct nonempty monomials in these t values are

    t_5, t_7, t_11, t_13, t_5t_7,

with total39/40. This is an infinite statement with a finite ordering certificate: every omitted singleton is at most1/14, every other pair at most5/96, and every support of rank at least three has product at most the product of a pair other than {5,7}, because it contains such a pair and all factors are at most one. Both1/14 and5/96 are below5/51=t_5t_7.

Without5, the four largest monomials are t_7,t_11,t_13,t_17, summing2531/4760<3/5. The largest pair is t_7t_11< t_17. Without7, the four largest are t_5,t_11,t_13,t_17, summing599/840<3/4; again t_5t_11<t_17. Hence, for any p>=5 and at most four distinct nonempty co-supports T avoiding p,

    sum_T product_(q in T)t_q <= (p−2)−1/t_p.

For p=5,7 the right sides are3/5 and3/4. For p>=11 it is1, and the global five-term bound39/40 already suffices.

## 3. The degree-drop induction is well founded

On primes at least5, induct simultaneously on the number of prime vertices to prove

    Z(F)>0,
    if deg_F(p)<=4, then Z(F)/Z(F−{p}) >= b_p/t_p.

The graph hypothesis throughout is maximum prime incidence at most5. Empty families have polynomial1. Isolated vertices, if included in the chosen carrier, have ratio1>=b_p/t_p and cause no problem.

The exact whole-support deletion identity is

    Z(F)=Z(F−{p})−sum_(E in F, p in E)w(E) Z(F−E).

A matching either uses no support through p, or exactly one such support E; all its other supports must be disjoint from E. This establishes the identity without any assumption about separator independence.

For each selected E through p, every q in E\{p} has lost at least that very support E after deleting p. Its degree in F−{p} is therefore at most4. Deleting additional q coordinates removes more whole supports and never raises degree. Each successive polynomial is on fewer vertices than F, so the inductive degree-four ratio applies. Telescoping gives

    Z(F−E)/Z(F−{p}) <= product_(q in E\{p})t_q/b_q.

Every denominator is positive by the smaller-vertex positivity induction. Substitution gives

    Z(F)/Z(F−{p})
      >=1−b_p sum_(E in F,p in E) product_(q in E\{p})t_q.

The co-supports are distinct and nonempty because original supports are distinct and nonsingleton. At degree at most5 their sum is at most39/40, and b_p<=1/3. The ratio is therefore at least27/40>0, proving positivity. At degree at most4 the refined co-support bound gives the stronger ratio b_p/t_p, closing both inductive assertions.

This is not circular use of a degree-four theorem on a degree-five object: deletion of p removes E before any of its other coordinates is queried, and all queried objects have fewer vertices.

Restore3 only after proving the ternary-free induction. F−{3} has positive polynomial. For each support E containing3, the same removed-edge degree drop applies to every q in E\{3}. Since b_3=1 and at most five supports contain3,

    Z(F)/Z(F−{3}) >=1−39/40=1/40.

Every support subfamily obeys the same incidence and rank hypotheses, so every induced polynomial is positive, as required for Shearer's theorem. Positivity of the full polynomial alone would not have sufficed.

## 4. Conditional Shearer ratio and the full actual survivor mass

Let B be the actual event of avoiding all support events whose supports exclude3. Conditional Shearer gives

    rho(avoid all mixed originals | B)
      >=Z(F)/Z(F−{3})>=1/40.

This conditions the existing full product law. It does not select a new convenient separator marginal or replace the conditional law by a product.

For a nonsingleton support E entirely on primes at least5, put r=|E|. AM–GM gives

    product_(p in E)b_p
      <=[(1/r)sum_(p in E)b_p²]^(r/2)
      <=(1/r)sum_(p in E)b_p²
      <=(1/2)sum_(p in E)b_p².

The middle inequality uses r>=2 and an average at most1. Summing with incidence at most5 bounds the total ternary-free event mass by (5/2)sum_(p>=5)b_p².

For p>=5, p−2 is3 or5 modulo6. Retain the first term of each progression and use its convex centered-cell integral for the remainder:

    sum_(p>=5 prime)b_p²
      <=1/9+integral_(1/2)^infinity(6t+3)^−2dt
        +1/25+integral_(1/2)^infinity(6t+5)^−2dt
      =1/9+1/36+1/25+1/48
      =719/3600.

The ordinary union bound is now used only for B, giving rho(B)>=721/1440. Multiplying the genuine conditional lower bound yields rho(U)>=721/57600. Since U lies inside the product of actual pure survivors,

    H(U)=(product s_p)rho(U),

which proves the full Haar result and density bound. If3 is absent, the stronger direct reserve721/1440 applies.

## 6. Source and class boundaries

The old-source extension statement uses the actual conditional Haar law on all genuine old survivors. It is not valid for every measure supported on that set. The actual distinct classes

    1 mod5, 0 mod15, 25 mod75, 125 mod375

leave old point0 mod125 alive for the old-only constraint, while their three mixed originals cover all ternary choices0,125,250 mod375 over that point. A point mass there has no avoiding extension. The genuine conditional-Haar law still gives overall survivor probability269/300. This confirms the source qualification, rather than disproving the theorem.

The six rank-five complement supports have each prime incidence5 and incidence cycle rank19. At the largest prime, five distinct exact predecessor supports occur. They are consequently outside the four-predecessor-support numerical-order hypothesis, while satisfying this theorem. Unbounded-degree incidence forests and low-cycle-rank classes can fall outside degree5, so no false containment of all existing classes is asserted.

The arbitrary-head continuation after obtaining full density is inherited
from the existing [joint-density restart](48-incidence-forests-with-arbitrary-original-heights.md#6-a-computable-unrestricted-large-prime-tail)
and BBMST. It is not part of the new matching-polynomial certificate, and no particular small numerical cutoff is asserted. The needed extra condition is the existing sufficient gap/entrance criterion, not an assumption that every arbitrary covering family already has that gap.
