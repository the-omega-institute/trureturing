## One physical chain and complete assigned charges

All physical prime heights resolve the entire given family, including
earlier powers appearing in later-ending moduli. Put nu13 for the
supported13 probability. For a prime p>=17 let mu_<p be the normalized
FULL physical law obtained by applying every preceding prescribed
physical kernel to nu13. Let eta_<=p be the corresponding actual killed
chain and b_p the assigned bad charge under mu_<p.

Every physical kernel is normalized even on a fully forbidden fibre.
All earlier killed inputs are dominated by their physical inputs.
Induction on positive-kernel composition, or the union bound on the
single physical chain, therefore gives

    eta_<=q(1)>=1-sum_(17<=p<=q) b_p.             (F1)

This does not assume independence of the actual bad events. No killed
input is substituted for the physical input when estimating b_p.

The established pure-base T8 bounds are

    b17<=H13,8/8, b19<=H17,8/10.                 (F2)

For p>=23 include every actual p-ending class, including pure powers,
in its full-uniform forbidden union. Its actual fibre fraction obeys

    alpha_p<=sum_(e>=1)p^-e A_e,

where each A_e is a completion of that exponent's own partial old
cofactor load. Complete only absent old labels and retain every existing
residue; different exponents retain independent own tests.
Put t_p=(p-1)/2. Since t_p sum_(e>=1)p^-e=1/2, convexity gives

    (alpha_p-1/2)_+
      <=sum_(e>=1)p^-e(A_e-t_p)_+.

    b_p<=2/(p-1)*H_<p,t_p,                      (F3)
