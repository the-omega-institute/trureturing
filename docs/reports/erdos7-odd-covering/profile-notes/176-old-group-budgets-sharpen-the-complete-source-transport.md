The coefficient-change payment in170's general finite-LP
transport can retain every old group budget. Applying that

    sigma<=1/26, rho<=1/940
       ==> K<=509.0308869911512196...<509.289.     (BC1)

There is no independent r cutoff: the original actual inequality

This is an ordinary local-domain result with exact rational

## 1. Price coefficient changes on the old feasible set

Let disjoint groups G_g partition the coordinates. Write

    L(a,u,b)=max sum_i a_i x_i,
    0<=x_i<=u_i, sum_(i in G_g)x_i<=b_g.

All capacities, budgets and coefficients are nonnegative. For
old(a,u,b) and new(a',u',b'), take U_i>=a'_i and
D_i>=(a'_i-a_i)_+. Then the stronger general transport is

    L(a',u',b')<=L(a,u,b)
       +sum_i (u'_i-u_i)_+ U_i
       +sum_g (b'_g-b_g)_+ max_(i in G_g)U_i
       +L(D,u,b).                                (BC2)

Start with a new feasible vector x. Clip it to the old capacities,
then reduce each disjoint group's total to its old budget if
necessary. As in170, the resulting old feasible vector y pays
at most the first two increments for all mass removed. The
remaining objective satisfies

    sum_i a'_i y_i
       <=sum_i a_i y_i+sum_i D_i y_i
       <=L(a,u,b)+L(D,u,b).                       (BC3)

This proves(BC2) for every new feasible vector. The last term is
at most sum_i u_i D_i,170's previous payment, because y obeys
the old capacities as well as its old budgets. The two old LP
maxima need not occur at the same vector; taking them separately
raises the bound and is valid.

Since the groups are disjoint, L(D,u,b) is the sum of its group
capacity optima. For a dual price gamma_g>=0, each exact group
support has the form

    gamma_g*b_g
       +sum_(i in G_g)u_i*(D_i-gamma_g)_+.        (BC4)

The helper's capacity routine constructs a feasible sorted fill
and the matching dual, and verifies equality. These234 group
supports cover26 objectives times3 defect vertices times3 groups.
All78 coefficient payments strictly improve their cap-only bounds.
