# The assigned source losses share one complete J error budget

On both whole actual J neighborhoods of136,

    qJ>=1-delta, rho=S-S0>=0, delta+rho<=1/1000,

the complete independently labelled identity test has an old49
direction40 replacement reserve at least

    43720531/2373046875
      =0.01842379577942387...>9/500.               (JY1)

The bound retains one actual source, its actual carrier law, all six
capacity defects and every infinite exponent tail. The improvement
uses actual source-deletion sets to charge two Q losses and the late
B loss to one H deficit. The raw original source cylinders may
overlap; no mutual disjointness of those cylinders is assumed.

This is an ordinary continuous-domain proof with exact rational
checks. It replaces the original old49 direction40 margin once; a
complete global comparison is a separate consumer. No larger source
domain, Lean verification or unrestricted Erdos7 result is asserted.

## 1. One actual union controls the missing Q deletion

Use136's notation and distinguished carrier(root0,cell1). Write

    h0=eta0+eta1, h1=eta2+eta3+eta4, h=h0+h1,
    p=z-3/4, a=1/4-alpha1,
    b=1/4-(beta2+beta3+beta4), Delta=p+a+b,
    lambda=1/72-(late2+late3+late4).

The original concentration and complete capacity constraints imply

    p,a,b>=0, Delta<=3delta/4, 0<=lambda<=delta/72,
    1/2<=h<=1/2+delta/18<=5/9,
    1/6<=h0<=1/6+delta/18,
    (6-delta)/18<=h1<=1/3.                       (JY2)

The first labels5,15,45,25 are present; their first-five slots
P,A,B,Q are distinct by136. Let H be the fifth slot and set

    ell=h/5-Lambda(H), ell<=5E5.                  (JY3)

Here ell and the late-source quantities are full old-coordinate
masses. The sets and absolute values in the next display instead
refer only to the five coordinate with its normalized Haar width.
Let D5 be the complete pure5 deleted set, and let Dalpha be the
additional root1 alpha deletion after D5. Equivalently, Dalpha is
the raw alpha union with its already deleted D5 part removed. Put

    R5=D5 minus P, |R5|=1/20-p,
    U=D5 union Dalpha, |U|=1/2-p-a,
    R=U minus(P union A), |R|=1/10-p-a.           (JY4)

Both P and A are contained in U. These are actual set unions:
alpha1 records additional deletion, so Dalpha and D5 are disjoint
as assigned sets, regardless of overlap among the raw cylinders.

The first alpha label in A and its complete deeper tail of width
at most1/20 give

    alpha1<=1/5-|R5 intersect A|+1/20,
    |R5 intersect A|<=a.                        (JY5)

The first beta label lies in root1 cell L and slot B. Its additional
width is at most1/5-|U intersect B|; all deeper beta labels together
have raw width at most1/20. Thus85's root-wide packing inequality is

    sum_(l>=2) beta_l<=1/5-|U intersect B|+1/20,
    |U intersect B|<=b.                         (JY6)

It implies both |R5 intersect B|<=b and |R intersect B|<=b. This
does not identify beta projections in different ternary cells.
Partition R5 into its A,B,Q,H pieces, and R into B,Q,H. Exactly,

    1/20-|R5 intersect Q|
      =p+|R5 intersect A|+|R5 intersect B|+|R5 intersect H|
      <=Delta+|R5 intersect H|,

    1/10-|R intersect Q|
      =p+a+|R intersect B|+|R intersect H|
      <=Delta+|R intersect H|.                  (JY7)

Both right sides are nonnegative, so the positive parts of the
two missing-deletion quantities obey the same inequalities. No
negative loss is used to cancel another positive loss.

The first line is the Q deletion on root0; the second is the full
pure5-plus-alpha Q deletion on root1. Multiplying by h0,h1 gives
the total missing Q deletion at most

    h*Delta+HQ,
    HQ=h0*|R5 intersect H|+h1*|R intersect H|.    (JY8)

The first two actual H pieces are root0 times(R5 intersect H) and
root1 times(R intersect H). They are disjoint because their ternary
roots differ. This root separation is essential: the two five
sets R5 and R themselves need not be disjoint.

## 2. The late B loss uses the same H deficit

Let LB,LH be136's actual additional root1 late deletion at five
depth b=1, assigned respectively to slots B,H after the earlier
source stages. The full raw budget is1/90. The first exclusions
leave only B,H or Q as possibilities, and in Q the actual source25
deletes at least one fifth of each relevant rectangle. Charging the
unused capacity, absent labels and other-root assignments to the
same lambda gives136 JT8:

    LB+LH>=1/90-5lambda,
    (1/90-LB)_+<=5lambda+LH.                    (JY9)

The assigned late H piece is disjoint from both earlier H pieces
in(JY8). All three belong to the same actual source deletion in H.
Consequently their measures, with the original product widths,
satisfy

    HQ+LH<=h/5-Lambda(H)=ell.                   (JY10)

Combining(JY8)--(JY10) proves

    positive missing Q/B deletion
      <=h*Delta+ell+5lambda
      <=(5/9)*Delta+ell+5lambda.                (JY11)

This comparison applies to full columns and root-columns. It also
applies with136's multiplier

    w=1-[1_root0+1_cell1+1_H+1_(root1 times H)]/5,

which lies in[0,1] and equals1 on root1/B. The Q contribution is
bounded by its total positive loss. For B, a full column or a
root1-column reads only the total LB, independent of its division
between the two allowed cells; root0 has no compulsory late B
subtraction. No conclusion for arbitrary cellwise B weights follows
from this argument.

Compare at actual widths to130's normalized table, before its
compulsory1/90 B subtraction. Only eta0 can exceed its face width,
and eta0-eta0*<=delta/18. All table coefficients are in[0,1/5],
so the positive width error is at most delta/90. Thus the complete
table comparison becomes

    table_error<=delta/90+(5/9)*Delta+5lambda+ell
               <=179delta/360+ell.             (JY12)

The earlier inequalities(JY5)--(JY6) also explain why a pure5 tail
piece in A or B cannot be ignored: its lost Q deletion is paid by
the alpha or beta shortfall. Treating the raw source families as
pairwise disjoint would not justify(JY12).

## 3. Retain all six defects with their separate prices

Keep exactly136's common actual nonnegative defects,

    (E5,E15,E5d,E15d,E3,omega),
    E5+E15+E5d+E15d+E3+omega<=rho+7delta/36=:R0. (JY13)

Define

    chi=6/(3-2delta),
    P=E5+E5d+chi*(E15+E15d)+omega,
    Q=E3+omega,
    c5=5+5delta/9, c15=30/(6-delta),
    cpi=delta*(1/18+delta/90).                  (JY14)

The chi ratio follows from h1/(h1-h0)<=chi. Correct-root deep
3*5 labels cost their own capacity deficiency; wrong-root labels
lose at least(h1-h0)5^-b against a desired mass at most h1*5^-b.
The complete positive ternary-projection error is therefore at
most P, sharpening136's bound3R0. The five-projection error is Q.

For cofactor5, every wrong or missing first slot loses at least
1/50 against its cap. Its target H section has mass at most
h/5<=1/10+delta/90, which gives c5. For cofactor15 set

    h1min=(6-delta)/18, h0max=(3+delta)/18,
    etamin=(2-delta)/18, g15=h1min/25.

The P,A,B,Q and wrong-root losses are respectively at least

    h1min/5, h1min/5, etamin/5, h1min/25,
    (h1min-h0max)/5.                            (JY15)

The minimum is g15=(6-delta)/450. In particular,

    (h1min-h0max)/5-g15=(1-delta)/50>0,
    etamin/5-g15=2*(1-delta)/225>0.

Missing labels lose their complete cap. The target root1/H section
has mass at most1/15, so(1/15)/g15=c15. These estimates use only
the original136 domain; their rational formulas do not extend its
source theorem to a larger region.

The actual shallow-carrier error on any event is at most
kappa*(h0+eta1)/5<=cpi. For the pure5 test the additional comparison
of the actual subtraction -q/90 with its face value costs at most

    (delta/4+2ell)/90=delta/360+ell/45.

Together with(JY12), this is delta/2+(46/45)ell. Substitute
ell<=5E5, then add the cofactor replacements and the single
virtual-to-actual error. The four shallow excess bounds are

    u3=u9=2delta+P,
    u5=delta/2+cpi+((91+5delta)/9)*E5
                            +c15*E15+E3+omega,
    u15=179delta/360+cpi+(10+5delta/9)*E5
                            +c15*E15+omega.     (JY16)

Each original test label keeps its own independently selected

using the one budget(JY13); they are not separate residuals.

## 5. The old49 replacement and the whole triangle

    R3(delta,tau-delta)=Rmin+B1*delta+B2*delta^2,
    Rmin=43720531/2373046875,

in(JY23) makes its monotonicity a separate obligation. For R4,

    partial_rho R4=5-C(delta)<=5-244/9<0,

    partial_delta R4
      =-527/90-L+delta/180
         -(10/9)*(rho+7delta/36)-(7/36)*C(delta)
      <=-527/90-L+tau/180-(7/36)*(244/9)
      =-3737978417987/227812500000<0.            (JY25)
