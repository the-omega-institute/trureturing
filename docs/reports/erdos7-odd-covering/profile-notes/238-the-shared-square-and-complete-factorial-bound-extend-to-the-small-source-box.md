# The shared square and complete factorial bound extend to the small source box

On the complete source rectangle sigma<=d=10^-8, rho<=R=10^-11,
with common wrong-slot gap G=1/60, the ordinary199 square proof extends to

    integral A^2 dmu <=8201/1800+epsilonQ,
    epsilonQ=2.8511321532832083e-7,
    Q=4.556111396224327.

The existing138 UniformFactorialHead formula, evaluated with its
own (coarser, already proved) pre-cap parameters over all12500 original heads,
gives

    integral Phi5(A)dmu <=0.8597222442921766
                       =619/720+2.2069954375732947e-8.

These bounds keep each original test independent. They do not use the stronger
face-only174 value2303/2700. Neither requires a new207 quadratic-head scan.
The following derivation supplies a new uniform modulus for199, while138's
existing proof supplies the factorial result directly.

## 1. Uniform source tables and fixed raw LP

Use235/234's independently guarded25 raw capacities and three disjoint group
budgets. Their total increments are

    DeltaC=1.7611111236944446e-9,
    DeltaB=1.5277777916666667e-9,
    Delta=DeltaC+DeltaB.

For every nonnegative coefficient bounded by M, clipping a new raw vector
first at old node caps and then at old group budgets loses at most M*Delta.
The remaining vector is feasible for the exact face LP. This holds also after
multiplying the coefficient by any of199's six families of indicator masks.

For the actual retained density of116,

    w_actual<=w_face+dw, dw=d/5+R/G=2.6e-9.

The face cap sum is97/360 and0<=w_face<=1. For old-cylinder operators use the
larger138 pre-cap increments v0,v1; set v=max(v0,v1). The descendant-table
increment is at most d/18, supported in cell0. The original exact exclusions
stay zero. Define

    profile=6*v/18+d/20=3.0500000000833335e-9,
    tau=1/18+3/20+1/72.

For any bounded nonnegative table z<=6, the complete old-tail operator T of199
changes by at most profile when only pre/descendant tables change. Indeed C3
increases at most6v, and each of C5,C15,C45 at most6d/18; apply their complete

## 2. The head-square keeps its forced27 credit

    Xi27>=0, Xi27<=Phi27, Xi27(1)<=e27.

    e27<=d/360+Cbar*R,
    sum_s(q_s^face-q_s_actual)_+<=d/2+10R.

The second formula follows from its exact lower slots:
qA,qB>=1/5-d/4, qQ>=3/20, qH>=1/5-10R, qP=0.
Consequently a valid deliberately enlarged loss of the199 head credit is

    credit=36*((d/2+10R)/135+5*(d/360+Cbar*R)).

The factor5 bounds the sum of the five per-slot error caps; one may instead
use the smaller total-defect cap, but that improvement is not needed.

    H_new(ell)<=H_face(ell)+eH,
    eH=36*Delta+36*dw*(97/360)+credit+36R
      =1.570900011020625e-7.

This uses the same forced27 family once in the head-square. The ideal
reference may be signed; the argument only integrates the displayed valid

## 3. The two complete crosses

For BO, source128/138 supplies the positive excess

    nu(1)<=omega<=R, nu<=(1-w)Lambda<=Haar3 times Haar5.

    V=6*[sum_(a>=3)min(R,3^-a)
         +3sum_(b>=2)min(R,5^-b)
         +sum_(a>=3,b>=1)min(R,3^-a*5^-b)]

    2BO_new<=2T_face(w_face B)+eBO,
    eBO=2*(profile+6*dw*tau+V)=3.848903748880958e-8.

For BZ,199's actual pure7 domination and entire depth sum1/5 are unchanged.

## 4. Entire ordered tail square

Use the existing138 complete nonnegative distinct-tail-pair bound Ptail,
which is derived from128's disjoint cap series, not by subtracting unknown
moments. Let Odiag be the complete old omitted-label diagonal bound:

    Odiag=sum_l[c_l sum_(n>=s_l)p_l^-n
                +sum_(n>=s_l)min(eps_l,H_l*p_l^-n)]+1/72,
    eps=(kappa R,R+d/240,R,R),
    (p_l,s_l)=((3,3),(5,2),(5,2),(5,2)).

Let Zdiag=Rlinear/5, where Rlinear is128/138's complete raw old-label cap sum
s+N3+N9+D/18+(h+h1+em)/4+1/72, with the same conservative source envelopes.
Then the actual tail square has bound

    integral(O+Z)^2 <=2Ptail+Odiag+Zdiag.

This is the exact distinction between distinct unordered pairs and their
diagonal labels. At zero radius the result is2977/1800, exactly199's tail.

    eTail=4.0954176356215405e-8.

All positive-seven diagonal and self-pair factors are retained. In particular
the distinct-pair138 formula is not itself mistaken for an ordered square.
