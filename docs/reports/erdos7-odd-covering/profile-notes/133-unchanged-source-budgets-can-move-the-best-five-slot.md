# Unchanged source budgets can move the best five slot

The actual assigned source parameters do not determine the raw pure5
slot marginal. Moving one original pure5 congruence class from Q into
the best slot H preserves every assigned source parameter but changes
that marginal. Consequently the proposed estimates

    |q_s-q_s*|<=Delta,
    A*-A+X<=17*sigma/400                         (SM0)

are false on the actual concentrated source domain, even for finite
original congruence families. The missing quantity is the best-slot
loss already retained in116 and117.

A corrected signed slot identity gives

    max_s |q_s-q_s*|<=Delta+u,
    u=min(r/h,r1/h1,(r-r1)/h0),
    A*-A+X<=299*sigma/10800+4*u/135.             (SM1)

The extra term can be paid inside the existing shallow defect
coordinates, including their shifted versions. No second residual
budget or new wrong-slot shift payment is needed. These are ordinary
source and measure results with exact rational checks; they do not
validate other source-uniform estimates or establish a new global K
or Lean result.

## 1. One original modulus moves, and every source budget stays fixed

Use48's variant398 source, at finite height N or with its complete
infinite families. Fix k>=4, with N>=k in the finite case. The original
pure5 source label5^k has residue5^(k-1). Replace only that residue by4.
Thus the source deletion cylinder moves from

    D={y:y=5^(k-1) mod5^k} in Q
    to E={y:y=4 mod5^k} in H,
    |D|=|E|=epsilon=5^-k.                        (SM2)

Keep every other original source label, mixed-seven label and pure7
label with its original modulus and residue. In physical first-five
coordinates(P,A,B,Q,H)=(1,2,3,0,4).

Every other positive-five source cylinder has residue c*5^(b-1),
with c=1,2 or3; c=1 occurs only for the pure5 label at its own depth.
The5-adic first nonzero digit proves that D misses all these other
cylinders. E also misses all of them: its first digit is4, whereas
their first digits are0,1,2 or3. If eta denotes the actual pure3
survivor, the raw source change is therefore exactly

    Lambda_new-Lambda_old=eta tensor(1_D-1_E).   (SM3)

The original pure3 source is unchanged. The pure5 deletion has the
same total mass. Because D and E avoid every other positive-five
source label, the additional alpha, beta and late deletion budgets
are individually unchanged. Consequently deficit,alpha,beta,late,z,
d_c,eta_c,n_c and s all remain the same. The original shallow3/9
carriers and their normalized weights also remain unchanged, so qK
and its concentration deficit sigma remain unchanged.

The marginal and best-slot changes are

    qQ_new=qQ_old+epsilon, qH_new=qH_old-epsilon,
    r=h*epsilon, r1=h1*epsilon, u=epsilon.       (SM4)

H remains an actual best raw-source slot. To check this over every
finite height, put t=sum_(a=3..N)3^-a and q=sum_(b=1..N)5^-b.
Then h=5/9-t,h1=1/3. Its gaps over B,A,Q after the move are

    H-B=1/45-h*epsilon,
    H-A=(h1+t)/5-h*epsilon,
    H-Q=q-1/5-2*h*epsilon.

They are strictly positive for N>=k>=4. P is empty. Thus the
chosen H is not an arbitrary relabeling of a worse slot. For k=4,
r<1/520, as required by the enlarged packing domain.

## 2. Finite sources already refute both omitted-loss estimates

For48's finite height N source,126 gives

    p=a=b=5^-N/4, Delta=3*5^-N/4,
    sigma_N=1-(18*t)^2*(4*q)^4*(1-7^-N).          (SM5)

These quantities are unchanged by(SM2). At N=5,k=4,

    |qH-qH*|=1/625 > Delta=3/12500,
    sigma_5=0.07394380607948110...<2/27.

This is an actual finite source counterexample to the first inequality
in(SM0), with the same original moduli and all116 packing conditions.

For the second inequality take N=9,k=4 and the original head

    ell=(1,2,4,0,4,1,4).                         (SM6)

In124's notation I=c=0 and J=K=1. Its deep-five reference A5 uses
only root1 and cell2, whose masses are unchanged from the face even
at finite N. Also D=z, so X=0. Thus the lost source reference is
exactly

    A*-A+X=(2/90+1/135)*epsilon
           =4/84375=0.0000474074074... .          (SM7)

But sigma_9=0.0009163565506933027..., giving

    17*sigma_9/400=0.0000389451534...<4/84375.

The second proposed bound is therefore false for a finite original
family as well. No passage to an infinite source is needed for either
refutation. These refutations concern the two specified source
estimates; a larger consumer with other error payments must be audited
separately.

## 3. The complete original-label limit preserves the same obstruction

The residue disjointness proof in section1 holds at every depth and
for all complete families. The unchanged source parameters tend to
the saturated398 parameters, while the marginal displacement epsilon
persists. Thus the complete source has sigma=Delta=0 but u=epsilon>0.

This construction also has a valid actual residual. In48's complete
mixed-seven family only the original first-five mixed labels lose the
new H slice. Their old-coordinate ternary masses sum to

    h+h1+1/9+sum_(a>=3)3^-a=1.

The restored Q cylinder meets none of the mixed positive-five labels,
whose nonzero digit there is4. Pure3 mixed labels see the same total
five mass and hence no change. If H_N denotes126's original mixed
old-coordinate sum, its new value is H_N-epsilon at every finite N.
All original seven classes remain disjoint. With

    u_N=(1-7^-N)/5,
    kappa_N=(1-7^-N)/(5+7^-N),

the actual deleted mass and virtual mass are respectively
kappa_N*(H_N-epsilon) and u_N*(H_N-epsilon). The nominal capacity T
is unchanged. Therefore

    rho_new=rho_old+kappa_N*epsilon,
    E5_new=E5_old+u_N*h*epsilon,
    E15_new=E15_old+u_N*h1*epsilon.               (SM8)

The complete pure3, deep5 and deep15 defects stay unchanged; the
single union error becomes(u_N-kappa_N)*(H_N-epsilon). Every missing
old and seven depth remains in the full nominal capacities.

At the complete limit,

    r=epsilon/2, r1=epsilon/3,
    rho=epsilon/5, omega=0.                      (SM9)

Thus this is an actual source displacement with an allowed positive
residual, not a collection of independently chosen marginal parameters.
The limit formulas follow from disjoint cylinder measures and complete
geometric sums, rather than an extrapolation of finite tests.

## 4. The corrected five-slot modulus is an exact signed identity

Consider an arbitrary actual source in116's packing domain. The
original source5,15,45 labels define the distinct slots P,A,B, with
H the actual best slot and Q the remaining one. Put

    deltaA=1/5-qA, deltaB=1/5-qB, deltaH=1/5-qH.

The complete post-first-depth capacities of the alpha and beta
families are1/20.88's assigned-budget argument therefore gives

    0<=deltaA<=a, 0<=deltaB<=b.

The same pure5 deletion in H lies above both ternary roots, so117's
DP16 gives0<=deltaH<=u. P is completely pure5-deleted. Conservation
of the total raw pure5 mass z=3/4+p now gives the full identity

    q-q*=(0,-deltaA,-deltaB,
                    p+deltaA+deltaB+deltaH,-deltaH),
    q*=(0,1/5,1/5,3/20,1/5).                    (SM10)

In particular max_s|q_s-q_s*|<=Delta+u. Actual assigned budgets
control A and B, but they do not control H without its retained
loss. Equation(SM3) shows that this missing observation cannot be
recovered by any postprocessing of the unchanged parameter vector.

## 5. Preserve the same head in the corrected mean reference

For124's original head define the nonnegative slot weights

    v_j=[I_(j=s5)+J*I_(j=s15)]/90
                                  +K*I_(j=s45)/135,
    zeta=I/90+c/135.

Then A=zeta*z+sum_j v_j*q_j+A5, and(SM10) gives exactly

    A*-A+X=A5*-A5-p*(zeta+vQ)
          +deltaA*(vA-vQ)+deltaB*(vB-vQ)
          +deltaH*(vH-vQ)+X.                    (SM11)

Define dA=(vA-vQ)_+,dB=(vB-vQ)_+,dH=(vH-vQ)_+.
A useful same-head upper is

    A*-A+X<=A5*-A5+a*dA+b*dB+u*dH+X.            (SM12)

All signs and head labels remain those of the original test.
For a uniform concentrated-source bound, a,b<=sigma/4 and

    dA+dB<=sum_j v_j<=4/135.

Hence the combined A/B charge is at most sigma/135. This retains
the fact that one head supplies all slot weights.

The total nonselected ternary deficit is at most sigma/2. The root0
mass cannot fall below its face value, while the root1 and cell
terms in A5 have coefficients at most2 each. Its formula in124
therefore gives A5*-A5<=sigma/450. Also Mxi<=3,M<=5 and
z-D<=3*sigma/8 give X<=13*sigma/720. Adding these terms yields

    A*-A+X<=299*sigma/10800+u*dH
           <=299*sigma/10800+4*u/135.            (SM13)

The simpler replacement17*sigma/400+4*u/135 is also valid, if a
consumer retains its old sigma coefficient. Neither version is a
sigma-only estimate.

## 6. Pay the omitted observation inside the existing shifted budget

Let117's positive certified gaps be G5,G15, and choose a common
lower gap0<gbar<=min(G5,G15). Its labelwise inequalities are

    E5>=r/5+G5*q5, E15>=r1/5+G15*q15.

Consequently the same shifted coordinates already used in127 obey

    y1=E5-gbar*q5>=r/5,
    y2=E15-gbar*q15>=r1/5.                       (SM14)

There are two useful linear repairs for the missing term in(SM13):

    u*dH <= (5*dH/h)*y1,
    u*dH <= [5*dH/(h+h1)]*(y1+y2).              (SM15)

The second follows from r+r1>=u*(h+h1). For a fixed radius
sigma<=delta<=2/27, use h>=1/2 and
h+h1>=(15-delta)/18. The corresponding uniform head prices are

    single: 10*dH on y1, at most8/27;
    double: L_b=90*dH/(15-delta) on both y1,y2,
                                  at most8/(45-3*delta). (SM16)

At delta=2/27 the latter scalar price is72/403. In a cost with mean
coefficient a1, multiply these additions by a1. All other residual
coordinate prices remain unchanged. Neither repair adds a new
gbar*q5 or gbar*q15 payment: the retained losses r/5,r1/5 already
lie inside y1,y2 in(SM14).

The two price vectors need not dominate each other. The double
version reduces the first price but increases the second. A consumer
may form a complete valid bound with each fixed choice and compare
them after its complete maximization. Taking a pointwise minimum
inside a vertex-only optimization requires a separate argument.

Without the shifted polytope, a scalar version is

    4*u/135<=8*r/135,
    4*u/135<=8*rho/(45-3*sigma).                 (SM17)

Here rho is still the original common budget, not an additional
allowance. The shifted prices preserve more of its existing
allocation structure.
