# A common-mask cell capacity and its sharp hinge refinement

The actual23-survivor-weighted29 square in327 can be bounded cell by
cell without substituting a physical law for the killed law. Exact
cellwise load distributions give a sharper rearrangement envelope.
These are ordinary finite-measure inequalities and conditional input
interfaces, not a new positive rebate for every actual family, a new
Lean declaration, or unrestricted-prime continuation.

## Localize before discarding the actual23 mask

Use exactly327's finite original family, complete prime-power heights,
old19 survivor measure sigma, and normalized full-Haar clipped kernels:

    mu=sigma K23, xi=sigma K23^-, r=mu-xi=sigma D23,
    m=sigma(1), b23=r(1), f=alpha29^2,
    k29=1/[4 delta29(1-delta29)], 0<delta29<=1/2.

Here0<=xi<=mu, and327(TS3) gives

    s29>=m-b23-k29 xi(f).                              (MC1)

For a finite partition P of the SAME complete through23 space, retain
s_C=xi(C), a verified cap0<=f<=M_C on C, and an upper bound
U_C>=E_C:=mu(1_C f). Positivity and domination give, separately on C,

    xi(1_C f)<=E_C<=U_C, xi(1_C f)<=M_C s_C.

Consequently

    xi(f)<=B_P:=sum_C min{U_C,M_C s_C},                (MC2)
    s29>=m-b23-k29 B_P.

If E_C is known exactly, the same inequality gives

    r(f)>=sum_C(E_C-M_C s_C)_+.                       (MC3)

An upper U_C cannot replace E_C in(MC3). For example f=0, xi=0 and
U_C=1 obey the upper-bound assumptions, but removed energy is zero.
A separately certified lower bound on E_C may replace E_C there.
No optimizer or independently maximizing family is identified across
cells or stages.

Each actual29-ending label d*29^e retains its own old-cofactor indicator
I_(d,e) and its original residue. The full-uniform fibre union satisfies

    alpha29<=g29:=sum_(actual(d,e))29^-e I_(d,e).

A certified g29<=G_C on C therefore permits M_C=min(1,G_C)^2. Pure
powers have d=1 and remain included. A coarse partition does not truncate
any original height. If only depths through h are explicitly observed,
the omitted load is still sum_(e>h)29^-e A_e with its own exponent tests;
a common per-exponent bound A_e<=A_C, when justified, prices this by

## Additional cellwise hinges yield the sharp mass-constrained envelope

Suppose the physical distribution of f within each cell is available.
For0<=s<=mu(C), define

    H_C(s)=inf_(t>=0){t s+mu[1_C(f-t)_+]}.             (MC4)

The pointwise bound f<=t+(f-t)_+ and xi<=mu give
xi(1_C f)<=H_C(s_C). Thresholds0 and M_C recover

    H_C(s_C)<=min{E_C,M_C s_C}.                       (MC5)

Moreover H_C(s) is exactly the largest nu(f) over submeasures
0<=nu<=mu|C with nu(C)=s. Sort C's positive-mass atoms by decreasing f;
retain all mass above a threshold, no mass below it, and just the needed
fraction at equality. For this submeasure both inequalities used in
(MC4) are equalities. The boundary cases s=0 and s=mu(C) use thresholds
at least max f and zero respectively. This also proves attainment in
the finite setting.

This optimization describes a relaxation of the fixed actual mask;
it does not authorize moving survivors between original CRT points.
A fractional threshold level need not be realizable by a hard mask.
For the actual fixed xi it remains an upper bound, so

    xi(f)<=sum_C H_C(s_C),
    r(f)>=sum_C[E_C-H_C(s_C)].                        (MC6)

One need not know the complete distribution. At any finitely many
thresholds with certified V_C(t)>=mu[1_C(f-t)_+], use

    Hbar_C(s)=min_(t in T_C){t s+V_C(t)}.

For M_C>0, including0,M_C with V_C(0)=U_C,V_C(M_C)=0 preserves(MC2),
and an interior threshold can improve it. If M_C=0, then f=0 on C;
set Hbar_C=0 directly, including when the supplied U_C is positive. Every V_C(t) concerns the same actual cell and
physical law. Global source hinges do not supply these masked inputs
without a new transport argument.

The refinement can be strict. For four atoms of mass1/4, let
f=(1,1/4,1/4,0), M=1, s=1/2. Then E=3/8 and the elementary cap is3/8,
whereas t=1/4 gives H(s)=5/16. The hard mask retaining the first two
atoms attains5/16; the resulting removed-energy lower bound is1/16.
This is a finite-measure illustration, not an asserted original-modulus
family. By contrast E,M,mu(C),s alone admit the coarse extremum:
place f=M on mass E/M and f=0 on the rest, then retain the highest
values first. For M>0 and0<=E<=M mu(C), this realizes min(E,Ms).
Thus a uniform improvement requires genuinely additional information.

## What the proved Kakeya result supplies, and what the original labels lack

The [Wang--Zahl2025 primary theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17655)
establishes Hausdorff and Minkowski dimension3 for every Kakeya set in R3.
The [Guth--Wang--Zahl2026 streamlined proof](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.14411v1),
Theorem1.1 and Remark1.2, states the quantitative form: for every beta>0
there is eta>0 such that, at sufficiently small delta, a family of
delta-tubes with

    Delta_max(T)<=delta^-eta, lambda(T,Y)>=delta^eta

satisfies |U(T,Y)|>=delta^beta #T |T|. Here
Delta_max is the maximum over convex K of sum_(T contained in K)|T|/|K|,
and lambda is total shaded volume divided by total tube volume.
Direction-separated tubes satisfy the required anti-clustering; the
generalized theorem itself uses anti-clustering, not a mandatory
one-to-one direction label. This is the three-dimensional set theorem,
not a theorem for all Euclidean dimensions or a positive-volume claim.
The [author's research list](https://jzahl.github.io/research.html)
listed both principal proofs as submitted when checked on2026-09-19.

For an original modulus family, put Q=lcm(d_i), R=Z/QZ. Its classes are
C_i=a_i+d_i R. Since every d_i divides Q and is greater than one, d_i is
a nonunit in R. These proper ideal cosets are not primitive affine lines:
in dimension one, a unit direction generates all of R. Under CRT their
parameters are prime-power prefix depths, not separated directions.
An embedding into another ambient space would need a new proof that
its direction, measure and incidence hypotheses hold.

Even irredundancy does not force dispersion. For distinct odd primes
p_1,...,p_N greater than3 take the distinct original moduli d_i=3p_i
and residues a_i=0. Every C_i lies inside the same coarse cell C=[0]_3.
Each is nevertheless indispensable to this union: CRT gives a private
point with x=0 modulo3 and p_i, and x=1 modulo every other p_j.
For uniform measure u on Z/(3 product_i p_i)Z, independence of the prime
coordinates conditional on C gives exactly

    sum_i u(C_i)=(1/3)sum_i 1/p_i,
    u(union_i C_i)=(1/3)[1-product_i(1-1/p_i)].        (MC7)

The concentration sum_i u(C_i)/u(C)=sum_i1/p_i is unbounded as the
finite prime inventory grows, by the classical divergence of the prime
reciprocal series. In particular no positive absolute
constant c can make u(union_i C_i)>=c sum_i u(C_i) follow from
oddness, distinctness and irredundancy alone. This does not refute the
scale-dependent Kakeya estimate above: no compatible delta or tube
geometry has been established. These families leave every nonzero
mod3 class uncovered. A hypothesis that the family covers everything
could impose additional structure not implied by distinctness.

For any original family, with u uniform on Z/QZ, the exact arithmetic
incidence data are

    u(C_i intersect C_j)=1/lcm(d_i,d_j)
        if a_i=a_j modulo gcd(d_i,d_j), and0 otherwise.

Indeed the simultaneous congruences are compatible precisely under
that gcd condition and then form one class modulo the lcm. Expanding
the square of L=sum_i1_(C_i) gives

    u(L^2)=sum_i1/d_i
        +2 sum_(i<j, compatible)1/lcm(d_i,d_j).       (MC8)

This formula has no angle-separation factor. Also a Kakeya lower bound
on the volume of a union does not directly give the complement mass
needed for odd covering. Every finite modulus family is Q-periodic;
aperiodic tiling is not part of this statement.

The useful parallel is localization on one common shading before
estimating energy. Equations(MC2)--(MC6) are elementary finite-measure
and rearrangement arguments, not applications of the Kakeya theorem.

## The observation interface and the remaining obligation

RRO in [dev snapshot d614145](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/blob/d61414584006c6e504bbc8d31ca261f6a4f70196/docs/develop/theory/RECURSIVE_RELATIONAL_OBSERVATION.md)
provides a related distinction in a different model. Section109 constructs a
joint four-window statistic whose mean has the hidden direction sign,
and characterizes consistency by pairwise separation of ring emission
rows. Sections112--113 retain one
actual hidden/sensor process;113 additionally proves that deterministic
independent calibration budgets m_n/log(n+1) tending to infinity are
necessary and sufficient, over its stated model class, for a common
rule with fixed-model constant times1/(n+1) error. Those sensor, signal
and calibration hypotheses are not supplied by an odd modulus family;
the results are not imported as covering inequalities.

Here327 already identified xi(f) as the missing observation. Its two
actual families show that no function of the displayed separate-stage
readings can recover xi(f); adding common-mask cells changes the
available information. Merely computing those old readings more
accurately does not. The new interface specifies sufficient cellwise
data and their sharp dominated-measure relaxation; it does not certify
that actual effective9 or aligned-J sources satisfy better cell
constraints. The unresolved step is a theorem or certificate for those
actual common-mask inputs strong enough to make

    k29 sum_C Hbar_C(s_C)<m-b23,

followed by control of subsequent primes. The first discriminating
check for a proposed observation scheme is327's actual A/B pair: if
the retained data remain identical, that scheme has not recovered the
missing masked moment. No uniform positive rebate or unrestricted
continuation is claimed here.
