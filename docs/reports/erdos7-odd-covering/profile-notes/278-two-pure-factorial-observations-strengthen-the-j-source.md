## The complete source inequality

Let B=1+I3+I9+I5+I15+I45 be the original head and let A=B+O+Z,
where O consists of all omitted zero-seven labels and Z of all
positive-seven labels. For every integer B>=1 and O,Z>=0,

    Phi_k(B+O+Z)
      <=Phi_k(B)+(B-k+1)_+*(O+Z)+binom(O+Z,2).             (PF1)

For k=2 this is equality on the whole domain. For k=3 it is equality
when B>=2. When k=3 and B=1, the left side is
(T-2)_+*(T-1)_+/2 with T=O+Z and the right side is binom(T,2),
which dominates it for every nonnegative integer T. Thus no finite
tail cutoff is used.

The complete quadratic continuations are

    Phi3(n)=(n^2-5*n+6)/2 for n>=3.                       (PF2)
