## 2. Four complete deep families share the actual residual

Let Vdeep6 contain exactly the actual original labels3^j*7^e with
j=3,...,6 and all e>=1. Put dmax=max_l d_l and

    Edeep6=sigma6*dmax-Vdeep6(1)>=0.               (QV4)

The deep-cylinder argument of91(D2)--(D4) applies individually to each
of these four depths. To make its dependence on the depth explicit,
let C_j lie in cell l, let K_l be the shallow five-coordinate survivor
set, k_(l,J)=|K_l intersect F_J|, and x=Lambda(C_j)/3^-j. Then

    X_(l,J)<=eta_l*k_(l,J), 0<=x<=d_l,
    Lambda(C_j intersect F_J)/3^-j
                        >=(x-d_l+k_(l,J))_+.

For kappa>=vmax, adding kappa*(dmax-x) to v_l times the last positive
part gives a nonincreasing function of x. At x=d_l it equals
v_l*k_(l,J)+kappa*(dmax-d_l). Summing with the complete positive
weights3^-j*6/(5*7^e), including the defects of absent labels, proves

    Vdeep6(v*I_J)>=sigma6*min_l[
          v_l*X_(l,J)/eta_l+kappa*(dmax-d_l)]
                                            -kappa*Edeep6. (QV5)

Late source deletion is already included in the complement estimate;
it is not paid a second time. Original deep residues may vary with
both j and e. No common deep carrier is assumed.

The five, fifteen and selected deep label families are disjoint.
Consequently85/91's residual accounting becomes

    (E5-r/5)+(E15-r1/5)+Edeep6+omega
                         <=rho-(r+r1)/5=:rho'.     (QV6)

All summands are nonnegative. On91's domain

    Delta<=1/18, 0<=r<1/2500,

the same general5/15 packing gap G>=241/22500 applies. For one branch
take kappa=vmax and

    P_v=max(vmax,(sum_l eta_l*v_l)/(5*G)).          (QV7)

This simultaneously bounds the coefficients of Edeep6, omega, and the

## 3. The source-matrix dual retains every derivative coordinate
