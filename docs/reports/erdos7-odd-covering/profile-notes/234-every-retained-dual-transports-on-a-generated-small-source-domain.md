# Every retained dual transports on a generated small source domain

The original195 source construction gives exact rational parameters for every
rectangle0<=sigma<=d<=1/12 and0<=rho<=R<=1/3000 with R>0. Recomputing these
parameters transports all6292 heavy and2792 survival dual records of225/226
on the smaller rectangle

    d=1/100000000, R=1/100000000000, G=1/60.       (SB1)

For each of the four original hinge objectives, its complete infinite tail
uses the same seven-coordinate residual as every complete row record. All
eight exponent cuts are fixed at17. Each original dual is repriced once;
its three raw support vertices are shared by the four tail consumers.

The result is a uniform additive error for a correctly identified original
face candidate. It does not itself transport the old pruning or prefix
alternatives, or prove a complete head or52-cost comparison. In particular
no raw dual objective value is maximized across unrelated tests. These are
ordinary mathematical and exact rational results, with no Lean or unrestricted
Erdos7 claim.

## One all-bank error for each original test

Let B be the union of the two original retained-dual banks. For lambda in B,
230 returns its source term C_lambda, signed raw-polytope support J_lambda(q),
and seven residual prices p_lambda.231 compiles the chosen fixed cuts for the
actual objective f into tail intercept A_f, wrong-slot coefficients b_f and
seven prices t_f. Its original complete face tail is T_f.

For each fixed pair(lambda,f), define

    E(lambda,f)=max_(q in V) [C_lambda+A_f+J_lambda(q)
                      +G*b_f.q
                      +(R-G(q5+q15))*max_i(p_lambda_i+t_f_i)-T_f],
    V={(0,0),(R/G,0),(0,R/G)}.                    (SB2)

The current230 specialization has zero survivor-mass price in every original
record. Thus the original rho can be replaced by R; the remaining expression
is convex in q, so V controls the whole domain. The raw support may be signed.
All original inequality/equality row signs, capacities, budgets and residual
coordinates are retained. The source and the full geometric continuations
are each charged once.

For each actual test f the certificate computes

    E_f=max_(lambda in B) E(lambda,f),
    allowance_f=max(0,E_f).                      (SB3)

The original test's relevant duals form a subset of B. Hence(SB3) bounds their
transport error without recovering the individual key partition. Including
an unrelated test's prices can enlarge this residual bound; it does not make
that unrelated test's objective a candidate for f. Only the correctly
source-bound original face value may be increased by allowance_f.

This distinction matters because the source banks contain hash-keyed dual
vectors without a complete per-key objective label. The two heavy scans
report3134 and3158 newly used keys, totaling6292; the two survival scans
report2322 and470, totaling2792. Their individual maximizing keys are known,
but those counts and the branch-decision digests do not reconstruct the full
assignment. The all-bank residual supremum avoids inventing that assignment.

Both heavy scans also report seven prefix-available branches and zero
prefix-bounded early skips. The latter does not prove that a prefix could
never control the later minimum of candidates. Any complete head join must
retain all originally permitted alternatives, including such prefix uses,
or prove that another transported candidate controls them.

If the original accepted value on every branch is the minimum of candidates
F_i and is at most M_f, and each candidate has a uniform additive error e_i,
then

    min_i(F_i+e_i)<=min_i F_i+max_i e_i<=M_f+max_i e_i. (SB4)

Equation(SB4) explains the valid downstream interface. It does not require
the joint face dual alone to be<=M_f on every branch. It does require a proved
error for every candidate that may realize the original minimum.233's pruning
errors and a separate prefix error can therefore be combined with(SB3);
this note only supplies the complete retained-dual component.

## Reusable evaluator and exact accounting

`frontier/retained_small_domain_all_bank_transport.py` exports:

- `parameters(d,R,gap=None)`: pointwise source/packing/ratio guards and
  rebuilt25 raw caps and three group budgets. An optional gap is accepted
  only after comparison with all actual gap guards and the triangle condition.
- `tail_error_from_row(row,par,tail)`: equation(SB2) from a current230 row
  and one231 tail support. The three already-priced raw vertices are reused.
- `calculate(base,delta,rho,gap,cut)`: every original dual and all four
  independently source-bound objective coefficients, with the complete
  parameter proof and both geometric branch inventories checked.

The caller of `tail_error_from_row` must bind the row to the same parameters
and raw polytope. `calculate` establishes that obligation by directly
reconstructing each row from its original dual. It does not scale a stored
large-domain error. The whole7163-inequality/56-equality inventory is checked
for both nested/disjoint alternatives, and every original bank row must
satisfy230's zero old survivor-cap/zero survivor-mass/nonnegative raw-mass
specialization.

The certificate stores a lossless shared price table for each entire bank,
all source parameters and guards, one complete maximizing witness per test,
and every maximizing key. The common table can feed other original hinge
vectors without repeating row transportation. The direct complete-row
supports require27252 LP optimizations; the original inherited227/229
providers additionally evaluate their own intermediate supports. The four
new tail consumers perform only scalar rational operations on each row.
The four final maximizing cases are also replayed through231's public
`evaluate_row_support` API.

`certificates/source_norms/retained_small_domain_all_bank_transport.json`
is the standalone exact result. Source pins include the original225/226
certificates,195's parameter proof, and the current row/tail providers.
Original dual-column feasibility remains inherited from the pinned source
certificates; all their off-face constraint prices are recomputed here.

A fixed finite cut retains a nonzero geometric face intercept. Therefore the
cut17 result is a bound on(SB1), not a zero-intercept assertion at d=R=0.
Adaptive cuts growing as errors shrink can recover the face limit, as follows
from231's complete geometric support formula. Neither observation removes
the separate pruning, prefix, full52-cost and signed-denominator obligations.
