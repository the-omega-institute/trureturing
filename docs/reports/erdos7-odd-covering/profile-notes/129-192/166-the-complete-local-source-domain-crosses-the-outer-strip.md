For a finite capacity LP with fixed group budgets, let old caps
be u_i, cap increments du_i>=0, and old/new objective coefficients
be a_i,b_i. Assume uniform upper bounds b_i<=B_i and
(b_i-a_i)_+<=D_i. Then

    LP(b,u+du)<=LP(a,u)+sum_i du_i*B_i+sum_i u_i*D_i.           (EX6)

Indeed clip any new feasible vector coordinatewise to u. It
remains feasible for the old group budgets. The clipped-off part
costs at most sum du_i*B_i; changing the coefficients on the
remaining vector costs at most sum u_i*D_i. Take the supremum.
This proof applies to every original layout, not only maximizers.
