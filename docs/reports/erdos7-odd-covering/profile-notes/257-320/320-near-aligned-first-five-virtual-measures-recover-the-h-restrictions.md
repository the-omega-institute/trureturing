Work on319's actual finite aligned effective9 domain, with

For every e>=1 put u_e=6/(5*7^e). Let F_e and T_e times F'_e be

## A strict adjusted gap holds for every bad carrier

Put

    gamma_p=h/25-g,
    gamma_a=h1/25-g.

Then

    gamma_p>=17/1350>0,
    gamma_a>=4/675-delta/450>=7997/1350000>0.

    h1/5>=h/25, eta(L)/5>=h/25

on delta<=1/1000. Its raw mass therefore obeys

    u_e*m<=kappa*d.

therefore gives

    Vp(Gp^c)<=kappa_p Dp,

## The actual H source has little extra hole

Let ell=Mp-Lambda(H)>=0. Every good cofactor5 label contributes
u_e ell to Dp. Every bad present label contributes at least
u_e gamma_p, and an absent one contributes u_e Mp. Since
Mp>=ell and Mp>=gamma_p, summation over all depths gives

    Dp>=(1/5)min(ell,gamma_p).                       (FV4)

If epsilon_*<17/6750, then Dp<gamma_p/5, hence

    ell<=5Dp<=5epsilon_*.

The original135 assigned source mass implies eta(I)>=5g. Since
Lambda is dominated by eta tensor Haar5 and vanishes on I times H,
there is a nonnegative extra-hole measure

    R_H=(eta tensor Haar5)|H
           -(eta|I tensor Haar5)|H-Lambda|H.

Its total mass is

    R_H(1)=h/5-eta(I)/5-Lambda(H)
           =ell-[eta(I)/5-g]<=ell.

Thus on this numerical defect domain,

    ||Lambda|H-[(eta tensor Haar5)|H
                       -(eta|I tensor Haar5)|H]||var
       =R_H(1)<=5Dp<=5epsilon_*.                    (FV5)

The actual eta|I is retained in(FV5). There is also a paid version
using the full ambient135 rectangle zeta=Haar|(I times H). Put

    c=zeta(1)-eta(I)/5.

By eta(I)>=1/27-5lambda and eta<=Haar3, 0<=c<=lambda. The
nonnegative measure zeta-(eta|I tensor Haar5)|H has mass c and is

    ||Lambda|H-[(eta tensor Haar5)|H-zeta]||var

       <=ell+lambda<=5Dp+lambda.                    (FV6)

The bracketed reference in(FV6) may be a signed measure because eta
can have a pure3 hole inside I. It is not an actual endpoint source.
This is a bound for transporting bounded integrals, with the ambient
