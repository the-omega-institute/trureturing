## Global barrier increases preserve the complete raw-mass budget

For fixed independent layouts, write the signed margin for cost f as

    m_C(theta,b,c)=C*s-U(theta,b,c)-W(C-f(b),t)/5.

The selected complete source term U and event correction t do not depend
on C. In either the five- or six-cofactor formula for W, every coefficient
of a maximum or a ternary-root maximum is nonnegative. For delta>=0,

    max(a+delta*b)<=max(a)+delta*max(b)

and the same inequality for the two root sums give

    W(k+delta*1,t)<=W(k,t)+delta*cap0,
    cap0=W(1,0).
