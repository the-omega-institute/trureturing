# Correlated unit conditioning pays for the generated rectangle defects

Deleting all actual unit classes can retain a larger carrier than the rectangular subcarrier of [report522](522-fixed-product-subcarriers-absorb-shared-mixed-unit-projections.md). Its conditional law is generally correlated. The missing product identity can be replaced by an exact rectangle defect, paid in the SAME mixed account as the remaining actual mixed classes. This bridge preserves the complete all-unit-conditioned law rather than discarding extra projections.

Two finite controls distinguish three claims. A one-cell unit hole has a uniform defect small enough to admit an actual family whose521 raw ancestor budget fails. A two-label unit hole has a uniform worst-rectangle defect exceeding the entire unit-column capacity. Nevertheless, the pure unions generated by that same actual family's fixed phases have a much smaller defect, and their common joint account passes. Neither example is claimed to escape every522 product-subcarrier certificate.

The product/common-source setup is reused from463--465, the phase-response boundary from [report504](504-equal-axis-totals-have-different-mixed-continuations.md), the ancestor transport from [report520](520-actual-modulus-inventories-and-divisor-transport.md) and the source/row repair from518--522. The calculation below is ordinary mathematical synthesis and exact finite verification, not Lean certification, a new foundational wrapper or a claim of literature novelty. The source construction retains its attribution to Michael Schroeder's Nine Prime Divisors in Odd Distinct Covering Systems, edition1.0.1, through those reports; see the [library entry](../../../../../Library/Arith/schroeder2026nine.md).

## A fixed joint law and its actual accounts

Use the finite old templates of [report518](518-finite-prefix-templates-allow-arbitrary-old-residue-tails.md) and the actual numerical-label convention of520/522. The original family is finite, with pairwise distinct odd numerical moduli greater than1. The old primes are P={3,5,7,11,13,17,19}; R,S are finite disjoint outside-prime groups, disjoint from P. Every original later modulus has its unique factorization m=d*n_R*n_S. Its one fixed old selector, full original residue and numerical identity are retained.

On a finite outside period resolving every original class, define V to be exactly the complement of the union of ALL original d=1 cylinders, pure and mixed. Assume its actual product-Haar mass lambda=H_outside(V) is positive, and define the ONE law

    rho=H_outside(. intersect V)/lambda.

Let rho_R,rho_S be its marginals; no independence is asserted. Since unit old conditions are vacuous, old-prefix repair changes neither V, lambda nor rho.

For a comparison old point x, let A_x and B_x be the surviving R and S sets after all its active pure nonunit original classes. Write

    t_x=22(1-rho_R(A_x)), u_x=28(1-rho_S(B_x)),
    R_x=(22-t_x)(28-u_x),
    Delta_x=[rho_R(A_x)rho_S(B_x)-rho(A_x times B_x)]_+.

This Delta is a query on the fixed joint law, not a product of separately chosen favorable marginals. Choose a constant D>=0 that bounds Delta_x for EVERY old activation permitted by the full-common REPAIRED comparison family. A bound only for unrepaired activations does not discharge this premise. A global bound over every rectangle suffices, but can be strictly more expensive than the bound over these generated pure-union pairs.

For a finite divisor-closed old-label set E containing1 and all original old cofactors, define

    b_d^R=22 sum_(pure-R original m=d*n_R) rho_R(a_m mod n_R),
    b_d^S=28 sum_(pure-S original m=d*n_S) rho_S(a_m mod n_S),
    b_d^M=616 sum_(mixed original m=d*n_R*n_S)
                   rho((a_m mod n_R) times (a_m mod n_S)).

The mixed prices are actual JOINT probabilities, not products of marginal query prices. Every predeleted d=1 class has price zero. Add the defect once to the unit row of the mixed account:

    btilde_d^M=b_d^M+616D*1[d=1].                     (J1)

Supply three nonnegative divisor-supported transport matrices. Their row sums pay b^R,b^S,btilde^M; each ancestor column has total capacity at most1 in its own account. Require additionally

    sum_d T^R_(d,1)<=1-eta,  0<eta<=1.                (J2)

The defect and all mixed expenses share T^M, including its single unit column. It is invalid to pay them using separate copies of that capacity. No matrix existence follows merely from conditioning.

Then, for

    theta=min(1/3696,17eta/1232),

the original full survivor density satisfies

    H_full(original survivors)>lambda*theta/270000.   (J3)

## Lower-bound embedding into the retained row domain

At x, let L_x be the sum of actual rho-probabilities of its active mixed original cylinders. Their incremental union loss inside A_x times B_x is at most L_x. Thus the actual conditional survival s_x obeys

    616s_x>=R_x-616(L_x+D),  s_x>=0.                  (J4)

For finitely many comparison old points and nonnegative weights w, the same fixed selectors and h_d(w) from520 give

    w*t<=sum_d b_d^R h_d(w)<=N(w),
    w*u<=sum_d b_d^S h_d(w)<=N(w),
    w*c<=sum_d btilde_d^M h_d(w)<=N(w),
    c_x=616(L_x+D).

The constant D contributes exactly616D*sum_x w_x through h_1(w)=sum_x w_x. This is why a common constant may be paid once in the unit row without assigning a new source or budget to each tested point.

To supply the exact algebraic domain used by the retained rows, define

    y_x=min(R_x,c_x), sstar_x=(R_x-y_x)/616.

Then0<=sstar_x<=s_x, y_x>=0, w*y<=N(w), and

    (22-t_x)(28-u_x)-y_x=616sstar_x.                   (J5)

These are literally519 G4--G5. Ordinary pair481 uses the capped abstract mixed deletion and clipped lower minimum. The weighted triple estimates499 M3 and501 C2 already use a lower bound on survivor sums. Four-point505 uses only J3 there, namely w*R<=N(w)+sum(w)/6 for a tuple with all survivals at most1/3696. Equation(J5) supplies each of those inputs; actual strict badness implies sstar strict badness. No geometric row or optimizer must be recomputed.

The pure-R reserve gives t_x<=Q_x-eta because h_1=1 for a singleton. At the former qualitative(21,26,35) pair, the522 corner values136,28,17eta,84-7eta yield616(sstar_x+sstar_y)>=17eta. Original and comparison full fibres agree off that exceptional set at every outside point, so their conditional probabilities agree there under this same rho. The good old set has Haar mass>1/270000. ## Exact defect kernel for a finite unit-hole table

After pure-unit conditioning, let the two finite uniform group carriers have p and q points. Let H_ij in{0,1} mark the UNION of mixed-unit holes; overlapping original rectangles are not counted twice in H. Write

    h=sum_ij H_ij, r_i=sum_j H_ij, c_j=sum_i H_ij,
    Z=pq-h>0.

The all-unit-conditioned law has entries(1-H_ij)/Z. Its centred joint kernel is

    C_ij=[Z(1-H_ij)-(q-r_i)(p-c_j)]/Z^2.              (J6)

Consequently

    rho(A times B)-rho_R(A)rho_S(B)=sum_(i in A,j in B) C_ij.

Both row and column sums of C are zero. This retains the exact common phase response needed for a generated rectangle test; total surviving mass Z alone does not supply it. The uniform negative defect is max_(A,B)(-sum_(A times B) C)_+, whereas a generated-query defect maximizes only over the declared comparison pure-union pairs.

For one rectangular hole I times J of sizes r,s, (J6) factors as

    rho(A times B)-rho_R(A)rho_S(B)
       =-[(p|A intersect I|-r|A|)
          (q|B intersect J|-s|B|)]/(pq-rs)^2.          (J7)

Indeed, set a=|A|, b=|B|, u=|A intersect I| and v=|B intersect J|. The joint rectangle has ab-uv surviving cells, and its two marginal numerators are qa-su andpb-rv. Subtracting their normalized product gives numerator

    (pq-rs)(ab-uv)-(qa-su)(pb-rv)
       =-(pu-ra)(qv-sb),

which proves(J7) by direct expansion under the same normalizer.

Each bracket has symmetric extrema r(p-r) and s(q-s), attained by the hole sets and their complements. Thus the exact uniform negative defect is

    Dall=r(p-r)s(q-s)/(pq-rs)^2.                       (J8)

This is a signed covariance identity; it does not assert favorable association after conditioning. In particular a one-cell hole at p=22,q=28 has

    616Dall=616*21*27/615^2=38808/42025<1.

## Remaining boundary

The numerical controls establish the displayed actual query/account entries. Their source consequence uses the conditional ordinary bridge, the retained source result and the explicit full-comparison defect premise. No finite enumeration establishes an unrestricted all-family estimate.

A useful next input is a compact description of the pure-union pairs actually generated by a full-reference comparison. The exact kernel(J6) evaluates their missing joint relation. A uniform rectangle maximum can be too expensive; testing only the unrepaired or already observed pairs can be too weak. The required boundary sits between these choices: every allowed comparison activation, on one unchanged conditioned law, with defect and mixed deletions paid together.

Unrestricted Erdős#7 remains unresolved. The positive unit mass, all-comparison defect bound, shared transports and finite old templates are explicit premises of this result.
