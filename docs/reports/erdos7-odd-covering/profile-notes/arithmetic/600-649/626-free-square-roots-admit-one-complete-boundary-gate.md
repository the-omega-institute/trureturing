# Free square roots admit one complete boundary gate

Both square-to-linear first-root anchors can be removed from the matching
interface of [Report624](624-arbitrary-central-pure-phases-admit-two-complete-boundary-gates.md).
The seven star originals at each outside head prime can have independently
chosen outside first roots, with arbitrary collisions and higher lifts.
The complete same-source gate has the exact uniform lower bound

    gamma=190452499219361/189995609279692800 >1/1000.       (FS1)

The resulting ten-prime Haar survivor mass is at least

    (2673/110656)gamma
      =18854797422716739/778672375572358758400 >1/42000.   (FS2)

One may also add a network in which every outside owner is at least2^115
and has any fixed finite union of smaller head/owner parents. With only
the pure owner powers and assigned mixed owner inventories, the
extendible head mass is greater than1/44000. This branch includes no
additional small owners, private blocks or Type I blockers.

These are ordinary mathematical results and exact integer certificates,
not new Lean verification. Each edge's twelve retained labels must still
share one endpoint pair; the restricted mixed inventory and literal head
prime set also remain. Unrestricted Erdős #7 is not settled.

## 1. Exact scope and one actual source

Use the literal core P0={3,5,7,11,13,17,19}, Q={7,11,13,17,19}, and
continuation P1={23,29,31}. Originals are distinct numerical moduli and
each has one residue fixed globally. All pure originals have arbitrary
phases and arbitrary finite heights. Retain the seven stars at each q:

    3q,5q,15q,9q,25q,3q^2,5q^2.

Their central components are arbitrary. Their outside first roots are
now arbitrary independently, including collisions. After the pure
deletions and central15 mask, an already source-null original needs no
further avoidance. For each missing or source-null star slot, choose a
live auxiliary deletion: its central leaf is nonnull and its root point,
when applicable, is outside the15 mask. This choice may change the
auxiliary central role when the original role was itself source-null.
Every source-live original keeps its actual role. Fix all auxiliary
data once before constructing the outside survivor source; they are
additional deletions, not changes to original numerical labels or
phases and not new members of the original inventory. Both square-star
higher lifts are arbitrary.

At every edge{q,s}, the twelve retained numerical labels remain

    3^a5^b q^i s^j,
    (a,b) in{0,1}^2, (i,j) in{(1,1),(2,1),(1,2)}.

They share a first root at q and a first root at s, but endpoint pairs
may differ between edges. Their central phases and higher lifts are
arbitrary. The common first-root rectangle contains all twelve labels.
If this rectangle is already source-null, its originals are already
avoided and a live auxiliary rectangle may be imposed once instead.
Every other mixed P0-original must have maximum exponent at least3,
both central exponents at most1, or support cardinality at least5.
Originals touching23,29 or31 are unrestricted. The35 star and120 pair
labels are removed once from the remaining-original inventory.

Construct the actual central pure-source probabilities exactly as in
Report624: rho3<=2H3, rho5<=(4/3)H5, supported on the complements of all
actual pure originals. Normalize an actual or auxiliary central15
rectangle to(row0,column0), transporting the whole family and queries
together. At q use the actual root-balanced pure survivor rho_q with
first-root cap r_q=1/(q-1), square cap a_q=1/[q(q-2)], and its full-height
prefix caps. All these laws refer to the same actual family.

At a fixed central leaf the union bound now pays all seven star events:

    Z_real=1-r_q(R1+C1+P+L+M)-a_q(R2+C2).               (FS3)

Here R1,R2 are the central row indicators of3q,3q^2, C1,C2 the column
indicators of5q,5q^2, P the15q root-point indicator, and L,M the9q,25q
leaf indicators. In contrast to the anchored case, a square event is
paid even when the corresponding linear event is active. Thus no
outside-root inclusion is assumed. All possible collisions only improve
the actual survivor mass relative to this union bound.

If f_q is the actual star-survivor mass, uniformly thin that entire
survivor to mass Z_real. The resulting xi_q<=rho_q preserves every
actual cylinder cap. This does not require distinct roots or a rootwise
allocation. Strict positivity below makes the construction valid.

## 2. Whole-vector alignment and a strict matching region

For fixed other roles, align R1,R2 with weights r_q/(r_q+a_q) and
a_q/(r_q+a_q); independently do the same for C1,C2. This expresses the
whole central-leaf vector in FS3 as a convex combination of vectors

    Z_q(l,m)=1-(r_q+a_q)(1_(l//3=R)+1_(m//5=C))
                 -r_q(1_((l//3,m//5)=(I,J))+1_(l=L)+1_(m=M)). (FS4)

The weights apply to the whole vector, not separately to individual
queries. Every induced-support matching response is multiaffine in
these five outside blocks, and Report624's complete gate is concave in
each response array. The actual gate is at least the convex average
of the aligned gates, hence at least their minimum; an individual
aligned gate need not be smaller than the actual one.
The numerical central source-corner comparison is separately concave
as before. It does not replace the already constructed actual
higher-digit source by an unrealizable corner law.

For every actual and comparison vector,

    1-5r_q-2a_q <= Z_q <=1,
    min_q(1-5r_q-2a_q)=23/210.                          (FS5)

Normalize product xi_q/Z_q and impose the ten actual edge rectangles.
Their dependency graph is L(K5), with event caps

    u_qs=r_q*r_s/(Z_q Z_s).

At the worst endpoints of FS5 the signed avoidance polynomials for all
1024 edge subsets are strictly positive. Exact enumeration gives

    min_A S_A=690649152194/4928698829051,
    max_e sum_(f disjoint e)u_f=6088291/10673725<1.       (FS6)

The latter bounds each coordinate derivative by a negative number, so
all induced polynomials stay positive throughout the entire box. The
same strict conditional matching construction of Report623 supplies

all32 simultaneous response grids

Its mass is H_empty and its query bounds use H_T on this same law.
No query chooses a different conditional source. Source domination

## 3. Keep the complete gate and shared template information

Use the full512-entry L/W arrays from
[Report604](604-fixed-pair-activation-admits-ten-central-square-stars.md).
With c=1084133/201247200, g=1-c and c_j=g L_j+c W_j, the gate is

    G(H)=g sum_(l,m)w_l v_m H_empty(l,m)
          -sum_j c_j max_(s in menu_j)sum_(l,m)s_lm H_(T_j)(l,m). (FS7)

All central grids vanish on the masked15 rectangle. The full-height
selector menus remain exactly Report624's menus: the deep ternary
selector is delta_l and the deep quinary selector is(4/5)delta_m,
not those constants multiplied by the current leaf masses. Zero
selectors are retained, including for signed intermediate grids.

There are the same20 central source types, indexed by

    THREE=((0,1),(0,3),(3,4),(3,0)),
    FIVE=((0,1),(0,5),(5,6),(5,0),(5,10)),
    CASES=THREE x FIVE.

For each source there are5320 templates(R,C,I,J,L,M): two rows, four
columns, seven unmasked root points, five nonnull ternary leaves and

Expand the matching responses at the common reference vector

    mu_q=1-(3047/2660)r_q-3a_q/4.

This is a Taylor expansion point in the global box, not a newly assumed
probability source. For each of the ten pairs e={q,s}, allocate one tenth
of the constant term and one fourth of each incident linear term to e.

    J_(e,T)=2 integral_0^1(1-t)
               H_(T union e)(mu+t(Z-mu)) dt             (FS8)

when e is disjoint from T, and zero otherwise. All higher-order terms
are included by this integral; there is no truncated Taylor remainder.

Store a shared state(R,I,C,E), E=1_(J=C), for each outside block. Of32
binary/column combinations, two are impossible because(I,J) cannot be
the masked point(0,0). Thus there are30 shared states. Retaining this

For every edge and every ordered shared-state pair, minimize the signed
pair lower gate over the actual templates and their simultaneous source
symmetries. This gives ten30-by30 tables for each source. Summing their

## 4. Refine only the states below the desired margin

The coarse certificate alone does not prove FS1. Select every shared
state whose exact coarse lower value is at most1/1000. The full scan
selects114 states in case12 and10 in case14, and none elsewhere.

A shared state need not determine J: if E=0, all compatible J unequal
to C must be included. Expanding every compatible value, still excluding
(I,J)=(0,0), gives170 fixed RCIJ layouts in case12 and10 in case14.
The expansion is exhaustive. In particular state24=(R1,I1,C1,E0)
permits J=0,2,3; treating it as a fixed-J state would omit real branches.

For one such complete five-block layout and root cell d=(i,j), put

    B_q(d)=1-(r_q+a_q)(1_(i=R_q)+1_(j=C_q))
                    -r_q 1_((i,j)=(I_q,J_q)).

Every one of the still-free leaf choices satisfies the simultaneous box

    B_q(d)-2r_q <= Z_q(l,m) <= B_q(d).                  (FS9)

This box belongs to one fixed global layout and is inside FS5. The
polynomial in FS8 has at most three remaining vertices. Its coordinate
derivatives are matching polynomials on at most two vertices, positive
on the global box. Thus J_(e,T) is monotone in each remaining endpoint.
Substituting the lower and upper endpoints of FS9 gives rational bounds
J^- and J^+. The old expansion point mu need not belong to the narrower
box: the segments from mu to either endpoint remain in the global box.

For a lower mass response use J^- delta_q delta_s when the product is
nonnegative, and J^+ delta_q delta_s when it is negative. For an upper
query response reverse the choice. Apply the same signed rule to every
cell, support and pair. Because each coefficient c_j is nonnegative
and selector maxima are subadditive, the complete gate is superadditive
on these signed response arrays. The sum of the ten lower pair gates
therefore bounds FS7 from below.

At each fixed RCIJ layout there are95 admissible(L,M) choices per block.
Enumerate all95^2 choices per edge, ten edges per layout. Pair minima
again serve only as lower bounds; no independent pair choices are
spliced into an actual source.

## Reproduction and remaining frontier

The portable producer `unanchored_square_source_certificate.py` and its
two integer kernels live beside the certificate data under
`frontier/cover-geometry/`. They use the existing canonical source-corner,
template-orbit, matching-polynomial and complete coefficient data. One
run generates every coarse table, exhausts its shared states, expands
all selected layouts and executes the full conditional repair.

    python3 -I -S -B -O docs/reports/erdos7-odd-covering/frontier/cover-geometry/unanchored_square_source_certificate.py

The construction removes the two square anchors, retains arbitrary
central pure phases and allows collisions among all seven stars. It
does not yet remove within-edge endpoint sharing, the mixed-inventory
restriction, the literal head primes or restrictions on small owners
and ordinary private interfaces. The refined shared states are a
sufficient lower-bound computation for this task, not a proof of a
minimal boundary or of finite closure for arbitrary process geometries.
