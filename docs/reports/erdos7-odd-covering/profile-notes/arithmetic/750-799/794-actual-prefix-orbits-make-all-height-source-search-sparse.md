# Actual original-prefix orbits make an exact all-height source LP sparse

For an arbitrary finite actual family on a fixed set of primes, the complete
query-norm optimization can be performed on the joint orbits of its actual
original-prefix trees. Averaging on those orbits preserves the full original
survivor and any Haar density cap, while decreasing every fixed numerical
query maximum separately. No maximum is exchanged with an infinite sum.

A concrete128-original family of ternary depth64 has more than7*10^30 actual
survivor residues, but only252 surviving joint orbits. An exact rational law
with Haar density at most3 improves its complete eight-prime query budget
including the unit from4.3260597511574073... to4.1771555207201705.... This
example was already known noncovering. The result is an exact representation
and a usable common-source search, not a new uniform bound below28 or a
resolution of unrestricted Erdős #7. The proof and finite arithmetic are
ordinary mathematics; no Lean verification is claimed.

Its Gibbs law, [Report534](../500-549/534-one-entropy-budget-controls-every-pure-prime-chain.md)'s

Prefix-tree automorphisms preserving cylinder depths and numerical exponent

[Report689](../650-699/689-actual-pure-support-averaging-gives-a-finite-all-height-query-interface.md)
already averages specified kernels on fixed shallow conditioning atoms with
actual pure holes. Its objective is a grouped normalized all-height supremum,
and its support envelope must be measurable on those chosen atoms.

Here the partition is generated by EVERY local prefix of the complete actual
original family, including mixed originals and all their finite heights. The
preserved query comparison is for each numerical modulus separately. This
supplies a direct sparse realization of530's all-label LP. No new claim is
made for the general convex symmetrization principle. The support and

## 2. One averaging operator contracts every numerical query

Fix a finite prime set P, product p-adic Haar probability H, and a finite
actual family of distinct P-smooth numerical moduli d>1 with globally fixed
phases a_d. Let U be its complete survivor. Set

    E_p=max_d v_p(d), with E_p=0 when p is absent;
    L=product_p p^E_p;
    q_d(mu)=max_(a mod d) mu([a]_d),
    B_P(mu)=sum_(d P-smooth, d>=1)q_d(mu).

For probabilities q_1=1. The usual nonunit norm is R_P=B_P-1.

At p, form the finite p-ary trie containing the full paths to every actual
local original prefix a_d modulo p^v_p(d). A trie node includes its literal
prefix value. Let G_p be the automorphisms of the complete depth-E_p p-ary
tree which fix EACH named local original cylinder as a set. They may not
permute different named original cylinders. Let G=product_p G_p.

For every probability mu supported on U, there is one probability bar_mu on
the same U such that

    q_d(bar_mu)<=q_d(mu) for EVERY numerical P-smooth d;          (PO1)
    mu<=D H implies bar_mu<=D H.                                (PO2)

First replace mu inside each L-cell by uniform Haar tails, as in530 FT3.
This preserves every original. If g=gcd(d,L), a maximizing g-cell splits
into d/g descendants of modulus d. One old descendant has at least the
average mass, proving contraction for that particular numerical d.
This proves all such contractions for the single Haar-tail operator.

Next average the finite G action on the L-cell probability. Extend each
finite tree map by the identity on later digits. Every action preserves
Haar, sends a d-cylinder to another cylinder of the SAME modulus d, and
fixes every original rectangle individually. Thus it preserves U, the Haar
density cap, and q_d before averaging. Convexity of each maximum proves
PO1--PO2 after averaging. It is the same law for all labels and phases.

Relative entropy to H also does not increase under these Haar-preserving
averages. Consequently an input satisfying534's unused-query-plus-entropy
condition still satisfies it afterwards. This does not supply its missing
total query bound.

## 3. The joint orbit variables retain actual mixed support

At a trie node, a child lying on a path to a named original prefix is fixed
individually by G_p. All other children can be permuted and have unrestricted
subtrees; together they form one orbit bucket. At a TRIE LEAF, meaning a node
with no deeper trie child, the entire remaining cylinder forms one bucket.
A named node with deeper named descendants is not a trie leaf and must not
terminate the construction early.

On the finite resolving window Z/p^E_p, these are exactly the G_p-orbits.
Their Haar lifts partition the p-adic coordinate. The finite group is
transitive on the resolving residues in each bucket; it is not transitive
on all suffix values when extended by the identity. For a lifted bucket A
that is the union of k cylinders with prefixes R at depth t,

    H_p(A)=k/p^t.

Its probability of an e-cylinder with phase a, under normalized Haar on A,
is exactly

    c_A(a,e)=#{r in R:r=a mod p^e}/k,                 e<=t;
    c_A(a,e)=1_(a mod p^t in R)/(k p^(e-t)),           e>=t.       (PO3)

At e=t these expressions agree. A terminal single cylinder uses k=1.

The finite G-orbits are products of the local resolving-window orbits.
Every original indicator is constant on their Haar lifts because each
original local prefix was named. Thus U is a union of WHOLE lifted joint
orbits. Retain precisely those surviving orbits A and their product Haar
masses h_A. A probability which has already received BOTH the Haar-tail
average and the finite G-average is specified by

    v_A>=0, sum_A v_A=1, and optional v_A<=D h_A,                 (PO4)

and is normalized Haar inside each A. The joint weights v_A are arbitrary;
no independence between orbit coordinates is imposed. All mixed original
relations survive in the actual allowed joint-orbit set.

For g=product_p p^e_p dividing L, its exact maximum is

    q_g(v)=max_(a_p) sum_A v_A product_p c_(A_p)(a_p,e_p).        (PO5)

One representative of every distinct coefficient row suffices. The explicit
menu consists of every bucket prefix reduced modulo p^e, using a zero suffix
when its depth is below e, and then removes duplicate rows. If a query contains
bucket descendants, the corresponding prefixes supply a representative. If
it lies inside one free bucket, every descendant of that bucket at the same
depth has the same coefficient row. Hence every literal phase is represented.

For implementation, visit a node's free-child bucket first, then its occupied
children in increasing digit order. A trie leaf emits its entire cylinder.
Joint indices are lexicographic, omitting exactly the forbidden joint orbits.
The certificate records the actual bucket depths and prefix lists as well as
this convention, so its source vector does not require the producer to decode.

If N_p is the number of actual trie nodes, the number of local buckets is at
most N_p, and

    N_p<=1+sum_(original d) v_p(d).                               (PO6)

The source has at most product_p N_p joint variables, instead of L. For a
fixed number of primes this is polynomial in the explicit prefix-input size.
It can still be large when many primes or independent prefixes occur; no
uniform small-state conclusion is asserted.

## 4. Every higher numerical query remains in the exact objective

The averaged law has Haar suffixes above E_p. For arbitrary d put g=gcd(d,L).
The individual query identity is

    q_d=(g/d)q_g.                                                (PO7)

Grouping its nonnegative sum by g gives530's exact tail expression

    B_P(v)=sum_(g|L)w_g q_g(v),
    w_g=product_(p:v_p(g)=E_p) p/(p-1).                           (PO8)

The maximum in q_g was already taken on this ONE law before summation.
There is no sum-of-maxima/max-of-sum interchange. When E_p=0, the free prime
contributes p/(p-1) even at g=1. The numerical unit must remain present when
comparing B_P with the continuation threshold28.

Thus the infimum of B_P over all actual supported probabilities, with or
without the stated density cap, equals the minimum of the sparse rational LP

    minimize sum_(g|L)w_g t_g;
    v satisfies PO4;
    t_g>=sum_A v_A product_p c_(A_p)(a_p,e_p)
          for EVERY distinct global coefficient row;
    t_1=1.                                                       (PO9)

This minimum statement is conditional on feasibility. If U is empty, or the
chosen D cannot carry a probability on U, both the original problem and the
LP are infeasible. Otherwise compactness gives attainment. Averaging proves
one comparison; every orbit law being an allowed actual law proves the other.

## 5. A complete depth64 dictionary becomes an executable source search

Use the actual two-chain family from
[Report535](../500-549/535-mixed-chain-moments-retain-shared-prime-correlations.md)
MC5. For j=1,...,n include original C_j of modulus3^j and D_j of modulus5*3^j:

    C_1:1 mod3;      C_j:2+3^(j-1) mod3^j for j>=2;
    D_1:(2 mod3,0 mod5);
    D_j:(3^(j-1) mod3^j,0 mod5) for j>=2.

Each D_j means its unique globally fixed CRT phase. Take n=64 and include
all128 originals. Use the eight-prime query set3,5,7,11,13,17,19,23; the last
six coordinates are genuinely free and retain Haar. The full period and
survivor count are

    L=17168419101462562423289245446405,
    |U mod L|=7439648277300443716758673026778.

The3-trie has254 nodes and253 orbit buckets; the5-trie has2 of each. Exactly
252 joint orbits survive. The finite numerical query index has130 slots.
The full literal coefficient menus contain24765 row products; duplicate
restrictions on the survivor can be removed during optimization.

The exact full survivor-Haar law has

    R_P=16419404927218650568920939288048954793 /
        4936593493699264029142651004264595456
       =3.3260597511574073... .                                   (PO10)

An LP with Haar density cap3 supplies a candidate vector. Its floating
solution is only a proposal. The retained vector has integer numerators over
10^12; it is capped against every exact capacity3 h_A, normalized exactly,
and reevaluated against all phase rows with rational arithmetic. It has72
positive orbit masses and gives

    R_{3,5}=3125000000157/2000000000000;
    R_P=2635259875106138123/829440000000000000;
    B_P=3464699875106138123/829440000000000000
       =4.1771555207201705...<28.                                (PO11)

Its Haar density is at most3. All128 originals remain in the support test,
and every query height remains inPO8. The law need not assign positive mass
to every survivor orbit; the optimization allowed the entire actual survivor.
No numerical LP optimality is claimed. This improves the query norm on an
already known noncovering example; it introduces no new noncoverage class.

## 6. Verification and the remaining uniform obligation

The [certificate](../../../frontier/cover-geometry/actual-prefix-orbit-source/actual_prefix_orbit_query_certificate.json)
contains every literal numerical original and phase, local bucket descriptor,
joint orbit index and rational source mass. The standard-library
[consumer](../../../frontier/cover-geometry/actual-prefix-orbit-source/actual_prefix_orbit_query.py)
reconstructs the family, every actual support predicate, all orbit Haar
capacities, every finite numerical maximum and the complete Euler tails.
Its [result](../../../frontier/cover-geometry/actual-prefix-orbit-source/actual_prefix_orbit_query.json)
is compared with a complete fresh replay. No optimizer is used in verification.

For n=1,...,5 it independently enumerates literal integer survivors, starts
with deliberately nonuniform actual laws, and compares every literal phase
maximum before and after the orbit average. It also compares every averaged
literal maximum withPO5's menu. These finite controls validate implementation;
the common-operator and infinite-tail proofs above carry the general scope.

```sh
python3 -I -S -B -O docs/reports/erdos7-odd-covering/frontier/cover-geometry/actual-prefix-orbit-source/actual_prefix_orbit_query.py
```

The next unrestricted source question remains whether EVERY actual eight-prime
family admits a suitable full-survivor law with B_P<28 and the required common
moment control. PO9 provides an exact representation for a given family, not
a positive lower bound on all its feasible optima. Additional future original
cylinders need not be constant on the old buckets: when adding them, refine
the named-prefix trees and retain the new actual support relations. The
recorded bucket descriptors, uniform conditional laws and new cylinders
suffice to compute this refinement and deletion. The fixed old whole-bucket
representation is not closed under that operation without refining its
partition; no independently chosen source laws are combined.
