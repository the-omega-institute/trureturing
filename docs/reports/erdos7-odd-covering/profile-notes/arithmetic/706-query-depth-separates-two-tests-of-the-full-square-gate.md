# Query depth separates two tests of the full-square gate

Keep the actual109 source, its original phases and every original-loss fee
from [Report705](705-the-complete-square-also-excludes-the-retention-target.md).
The full query inventory can be charged by its genuine squared-load maximum
instead of the independent all-height query envelope. The resulting gate is
stronger, and 705 does not exclude it.

Two exact certificates below distinguish query depth from the information
retained by a source weight. At the minimum actual period, a central-only
field defeats every dual made from complete common-centre query layouts.
The finite calculation first uses the shallow-centre/Haar-tail family; the
exact reduction below then covers every centre modulo the full period. At infinite query height, a different dual excludes every
central-only field; an explicit truncation payment gives a finite-depth
version. Neither statement settles the full gate on arbitrary fields or
unrestricted Erdős #7. These are ordinary mathematical deductions and
integer certificates, not new Lean verification.

## Complete query replacement keeps the actual unit and all original loss

Write P=(3,5,7,11,13,17,19), c=1084133/201247200 and g=1-c. For the
complete 512 fee vector C and query envelope W from695/705, put

    A_j=C_j-cW_j>=0.

There are 499 positive original-loss coefficients and 13 zero coefficients.
The four fullmode8 original-loss additions remain in A. The zero groups are
0,1,2,4,8,16,32,64,96,128,160,256,384. The query unit is not part of W.

For Q=product_p p^E_p, let Gamma_Q(nu) maximize the complete divisor-load
square over ONE phase per numerical divisor. Different divisor phases may
be incompatible. Define

    K_Q(nu)=Gamma_Q(nu)-nu(1),
    G_Q(nu)=g nu(1)-sum_j A_j S_j(nu)-c K_Q(nu).       (FQ1)

Here S_j is the unchanged complete screen, with one fixed outside tuple
across its central sum. All quantities use the same source. To check the
consumer, let eta<=nu be the actual survivor after the remaining original
deletions. Every nonunit square factor is nonnegative, so

    Gamma_Q(eta)<=eta(1)+Gamma_Q(nu)-nu(1).

The existing original-loss estimate gives
g[nu(1)-eta(1)]<=sum_j A_j S_j(nu). Consequently

    eta(1)-c Gamma_Q(eta)>=G_Q(nu).                   (FQ2)

This is a legal strengthening of the old envelope gate. It neither erases
the original loss nor replaces the actual unit eta(1) by nu(1).

The minimum period resolving the 109 actual originals has exponent vector

    E0=(6,6,2,2,2,2,2),
    Q0=1190750449028765625.

This resolves the actual109 originals, not every possible later old
cofactor. Such later cofactors may require a larger Q. Any larger resolving
period on P has E>=E0. Extra query labels do not add
forbidden originals. They do increase Gamma, hence decrease G_Q. Excluding
a gate at a larger Q does not exclude the gate at Q0.

## One exact boundary for a declared family of full layouts

Fix h=(2,2,1,1,1,1,1). Choose one centre prefix a modulo product p^h_p,
and extend it by independent Haar centre digits on each prime axis. Use
a mod d for every divisor query label d. Every realization is one legal
complete layout; this family is only a subset of all layouts.

For source point x, if r=v_p(x-a)<h_p, set B_p,E(x,a)=(r+1)^2. If the
shallow prefixes match, set

    B_p,E(h)=(h+1)^2+sum_(j=1..E-h)(2h+2j+1)p^(-j),
    B_p,infty(h)=(h+1)^2+2(h+1)/(p-1)+(p+1)/(p-1)^2.

The full finite load is product_p(min(v_p(x-a),E_p)+1). Averaging its
square over the chosen centre tails therefore gives the exact kernel

    k_a,E(x)=product_p B_p,E(x,a)-1.                  (FQ3)

The tail probability of a further j-digit match is p^(-j); the increment
of the square is 2h+2j+1. This proves the formula. Tail independence concerns
the sampled centre, and imposes no product assumption on the source law.
The kernel is constant on joint shallow source atoms even for a singular
source. Define

    M_h,E(nu)=max_(all shallow a) integral k_a,E dnu,
    R_E(nu)=g nu(1)-sum_j A_j S_j(nu)-c M_h,E(nu).

Then M_h,E<=K_Q and G_Q<=R_E. The same definitions with infinite factors
give a separately declared all-height centre relaxation. On the same
compatible full-depth measure define

    K_infty(nu)=sup_(E cofinal) K_(product p^E_p)(nu),
    G_infty(nu)=g nu(1)-sum_j A_j S_j(nu)-c K_infty(nu).

Monotone convergence of the nonnegative centre kernels gives
M_h,infty<=K_infty. In particular G_infty<=R_infty. With the same full
query envelope, put G_env(nu)=g nu(1)-sum_j C_j S_j(nu). Then
G_env<=G_infty<=G_Q: the middle criterion is stronger than the independent
envelope gate but weaker than its finite-Q version. These definitions
do not identify the true Gamma maximum with a coherent-centre maximum.

For each screen choose a legal probability or subprobability row mixture;
also choose one such law on entire centre prefixes. Write qbar_j for the chosen screen row mixture. Their debit

    d_E=sum_j A_j qbar_j+c integral k_a,E d beta(a)

obeys

    R_E(f sigma)<=integral f(g-d_E) d sigma
                 <=integral[g-d_E]_+ d sigma          (FQ4)

for every 0<=f<=1. Subprobabilities are allowed because all omitted charges
are nonnegative. Centre probabilities are fixed across all source points,
all numerical query labels and all pair occurrences.

## At infinite height, central-only fields have a different obstruction

A separate rational dual supplies one admissible row law per positive
original-loss budget and one common centre law. For central cell t, put

    m_t=sigma(cell t),
    d_t=integral_(cell t) d_infty d sigma.

For every field f constant on these central cells,

    G_infty(f sigma)<=R_infty(f sigma)
      <=sum_t f_t(g m_t-d_t)
      <=sum_t[g m_t-d_t]_+
      =8464599701038081260373338639016103662163117/4622283206637991152868786176000000000000000000
      =0.0018312594279991752... <193/100000.             (FQ6)

Every coefficient is integrated against the same joint actual source.
The positive part is taken AFTER averaging inside the central cell; that
order is legitimate because f_t is constant there. It is not an upper for
fields allowed to depend on exterior coordinates. For such fields, taking
the positive part pointwise before integration can give a larger value.

The finite field FQ5 and infinite dual FQ6 have different height scopes.
Neither can replace the other. In particular an infinite-centre debit
cannot be charged to a finite Gamma without paying its truncation error.

## An explicit payment returns the infinite dual to finite queries

For E>=h the matching-factor error is exactly

    B_p,infty(h)-B_p,E(h)
      =p^(h-E)[(2E+1)/(p-1)+2p/(p-1)^2].

At an earlier mismatch the error is zero. Replacing a mismatch by a full
shallow match increases both the finite baseline and the nonnegative
error. Consequently, uniformly in x and a,

    0<=k_a,infty(x)-k_a,E(x)<=Delta_E,
    Delta_E=product_p B_p,infty(h_p)-product_p B_p,E_p(h_p).

The same inequality holds after any allowed centre mixture. With
centre-mixture mass beta(1)=124999999999/125000000000 and the unchanged
source mass sigma(1)=305684996597/646498195200, the finite
central-only box upper therefore satisfies

    U_E<=U_infty+c beta(1) Delta_E sigma(1).                  (FQ7)

The exact correction gives:

| Common exponent floor | Corrected central-only upper | Below 193/100000? |
|---|---:|---|
|16|0.0020202460491570793...|No|
|17|0.0018977424215866035...|Yes|

Since the truncation error decreases in every exponent, the second bound
holds for every vector with E_p>=17. The failure of this particular uniform
correction at floor 16 is not a proof that the actual criterion succeeds
there, nor a claim that 17 is the optimal depth. The exact rationals for
each tested floor are in the verifier result.

This excludes only the stated sufficiently deep finite-query criterion
for central-only retentions. Padding a shallow period and excluding the
padded gate cannot be used to exclude its stronger unpadded version.

bounds. No combination of these
diagnostics proves noncoverage for arbitrary original families.
