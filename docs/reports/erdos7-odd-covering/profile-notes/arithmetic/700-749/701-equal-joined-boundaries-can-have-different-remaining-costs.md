## Meaning for boundary refinement

The first example requires a third-order relation to recover S14. The second shows that even supplying the whole joint distribution of first roots does not recover the deeper screen S1. A sufficient boundary for the COMPLETE gate must also retain the relevant conditional depth responses or supply a valid bound on them inside each boundary fibre.

No unique-filling conclusion follows just from a pair of marginals, a shallow joint law or a selected-block separator. A proposed uniqueness route must first establish the additional support/saturation constraints and then prove injectivity on that actual feasible set. Conversely, optimization does not require reconstructing every original field: Report699's simultaneous pooling may improve costs while discarding distinctions. The present examples distinguish exact cost determination from that weaker, useful comparison.

These counterexamples neither exclude every field for G33 nor provide a paying field. Uniform control over arbitrary original phases, heights and prime supports remains a separate missing bridge for unrestricted Erdős#7.
