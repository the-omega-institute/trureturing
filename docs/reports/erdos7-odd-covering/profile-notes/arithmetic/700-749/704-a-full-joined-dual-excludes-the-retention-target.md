This is an all-retention upper bound for the stated criterion, including

## The complete objective and its two independent budgets

Use699's exact33-label ownership: the eight central labels
{3,5,9,15,25,45,75,225}, the five exterior primes, and twenty exterior-pair
composites. There are33 unary atoms of weight3 and88 pair atoms of weight2.
A complete layout a chooses one residue for each numerical label, shared by
every occurrence of that label. Its charge is L_a, and

    K33(nu)=max_a integral L_a dnu.

Keep all512 complete screens S_j, all original losses and the fullmode8
additions in C_j. With c=1084133/201247200, g=1-c, and699's selected
ownership coefficients v_j, put

    r_j=C_j-c v_j>=0,
    G33(nu)=g nu(1)-sum_j r_j S_j(nu)-c K33(nu).     (FJ2)

The certificate specifies one probability mixture of admissible query rows
within EACH positive r_j budget, and a separate probability mixture of
complete layouts within budget c. It contains216507 query rows across506
positive budgets and81 positive complete-layout probabilities. Every mixture
has integer numerators summing to the common denominator10^12. The six zero
budgets have no rows. Zero-probability layouts are omitted.

First apply the finite certificate to a category field obtained by the
689/690 averaging; the later reduction covers arbitrary original fields.
For q_jt the normalized row functions and alpha_jt,beta_a these probabilities,
define the one debit density

    d(x)=sum_j r_j sum_t alpha_jt q_jt(x)
         +c sum_a beta_a L_a(x).                   (FJ3)

Rows involving pooled free roots denote the precise group averages described
below. Each query average is bounded by its corresponding screen, and the
complete-layout average is bounded by K33. Consequently, on the same source,

    G33(f sigma)<=integral f(g-d) d sigma
                 <=integral [g-d]_+ d sigma=U.     (FJ4)

The first inequality pays every original loss and query remainder exactly
once. The second uses0<=f<=1. It requires neither stationarity nor a match
between the maximizing queries and a field attaining the positive residual.
In particular U is not asserted to be the true gate optimum.

## Complete layouts before symmetry averaging

The source is invariant under permutations of free roots9,...,q-1 for
q>=11. For one complete layout, apply the SAME permutation at q to every
incident label's q-component; CRT reconstructs all transported residues.
Average the entire charge over this group. Each transported object is still
one legal complete layout, so its average remains bounded by K33.

To compute that average, first intersect each pair atom by generalized CRT.
Incompatible phases give the zero event. Only after that decision may a
compatible free root be replaced by its pooled token. Linearity then permits
the sum of averaged atoms. Averaging the two incidence indicators separately
and multiplying them could create a nonzero intersection from incompatible
phases; this operation is not used.

The independent audit compared three compilers on262 complete layouts,
including32 arbitrary-phase controls and a conflict control. It also evaluated
all48 simultaneous free-root permutations in a small control on20 points.
The exact group averages agreed with the compiled pooled responses, while the
incompatible11/33 pair remained zero. These checks supplement the preceding
whole-layout proof; they do not infer arbitrary-law compatibility from pair
marginals.

For a final category z in root-major central cell(l,m), let m_z be its OLD

    R_(l,m)=(82/81 if l=4 else1)(1876/1875 if m=10 else1).

different cells, and separate central marginal caps do not replace the joint

ordinary redistribution argument supplies the source-class conclusion.

## Relation to the failed inherited-density transfer

[Report702](702-inherited-dual-density-has-no-common-joined-layout-law.md)
proved that no complete-layout mixture can dominate its specified inherited
R pointwise. That result remains true. The present certificate changes the
query-row probabilities as well as the complete-layout probabilities and
computes the resulting residual integral directly. It assumes no domination
of R and does not transfer698's bound by holding those rows fixed.

The distinction resolves the question left by the field evaluations in699
and700: their negative values alone did not rule out other retentions;
(FJ1)--(FJ4) now rule out the target for every retention on this source.
The bound is still positive, so it does not rule out smaller positive gates.

same other fees as Gnew(nu)=G33(nu)+c Delta(nu), reaching193/100000 requires

      =15433666172366172506991758993853073774886681
       /123465320552655673851495075840000000000000000
      =0.1250040586561633... .                    (FJ5)

This is a necessary bound on the credit at that same actual retained measure.
It cannot be supplied by an improvement attained on an unrelated field or
source. Changing the source or original-loss criterion needs a new comparison;
(FJ5) is not an invariant of every conceivable noncoverage method.
