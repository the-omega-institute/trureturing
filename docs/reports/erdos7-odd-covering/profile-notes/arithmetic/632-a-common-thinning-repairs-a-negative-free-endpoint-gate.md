## 6. What this closes and what remains

This fixed globally coherent central layout admits one complete free-endpoint source certificate, with arbitrary outside roots and lifts, arbitrary outside-core pure phases and heights, the declared remaining mixed inventory, and the full23/29/31 continuation. It supplies a concrete case where selecting a layout-dependent common thinning succeeds after simple strict-cell deletion leaves a negative full gate.

Reports608 and611 already explain why the thinning must serve every query and why its dependence on the actual layout matters. The new result does not establish positivity for every arbitrary central layout, does not justify switching thinnings inside a Jensen comparison, and does not realize arbitrary pure laws by comparison corners. The remaining main obligation is a uniform construction for the entire arbitrary-endpoint, arbitrary-central-phase class, while paying the complete inherited continuation costs.
