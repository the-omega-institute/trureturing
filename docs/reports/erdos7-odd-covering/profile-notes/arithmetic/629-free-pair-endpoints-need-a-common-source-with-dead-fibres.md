# Unshared within-edge endpoints: a common-source bridge and an actual local obstruction

This is ordinary mathematics and exact finite arithmetic, not Lean verification. The scope is the next restriction after [Report626](626-free-square-roots-admit-one-complete-boundary-gate.md): the twelve retained numerical labels on an outside edge no longer share their endpoint roots. Every numerical label still has one globally fixed phase. The results below do not settle unrestricted Erdős #7.

## 1. The union cap retains every label's global central role

Let Q={7,11,13,17,19}, r_q=1/(q−1), a_q=1/[q(q−2)]. Fix one actual family and its actual pure survivor laws. At central leaf c, remove the actual stars and thin each entire star survivor as in626, obtaining ξ_q(c)≤ρ_q with mass Z_q(c)>0. First-root cylinders have ξ_q mass≤r_q, and square-prefix cylinders have mass≤a_q, on these same laws.

For e={q,s}, its twelve numerical labels are 3^a5^b q^i s^j, with(a,b)∈{0,1}² and(i,j)∈{(1,1),(2,1),(1,2)}. Each active label defines one actual outside prefix rectangle. Let E_e(c) be their union. It remains an event depending only on coordinates q,s. Hence L(K5) remains a valid ordinary dependency graph under ν_c=product ξ_q/Z_q, despite loss of the rectangle shape of E_e itself.

For each outside exponent type t, use the globally fixed full-slot central activation upper field

B_t(c)=1+1_{row(c)=R_t}+1_{col(c)=C_t}+1_{(row(c),col(c))=(I_t,J_t)}.

Each of the last three roles corresponds to a different numerical label.
For a missing slot, choose its role once as an auxiliary cap; it is not a
new original. Rows or columns excluded by the central pure law and the
masked point(0,0) contribute zero on the central domain considered below.
Roles are fixed once for the full family, not selected again at another
cell. The displayed full-slot upper field retains the unconditional1 and
all three decorations, even if some actual labels are missing or
independently source-null. Dropping such terms could give a smaller valid
cap, but that smaller field is not identified with this displayed formula
or its section6 mean identity.

With κ_11=r_qr_s, κ_21=a_qr_s, κ_12=r_qa_s, the product submeasure satisfies

(ξ_q⊗ξ_s)(E_e(c)) ≤ β_e(c)
 =r_qr_s B_11(c)+a_qr_s B_21(c)+r_qa_s B_12(c).

Thus ν_c(E_e(c))≤u_e(c)=β_e(c)/(Z_q(c)Z_s(c)). This cap is valid for independently chosen first roots and lifts. It is a union upper bound, not an assertion that its summands are simultaneously disjoint. Clipping a cap at1 is permissible but supplies no strict one-event Shearer margin when the clipped value equals1.

## 2. One good cell still supports all32 queries on one law

For every edge subset A, let

The conditional avoidance argument from [Report623, section 4](623-a-common-matching-source-admits-arbitrary-edge-endpoints.md) needs only this dependency graph, not the fact that each old event was one rectangle. Let F_c avoid all ten actual union events. Then

ν_c(A_query|F_c) ≤ ν_c(A_query) S_{edges outside T}(u)/S_all(u)

for a cylinder query supported on T⊆Q. The same rare-independent-coin proof applies. Define the ONE submeasure

ζ_c=(product_q Z_q) S_all(u) ν_c(.|F_c).

It is dominated by product ξ_q, supported outside every retained original, and satisfies simultaneously

where H_T is the unnormalized matching polynomial on Q\T with the cell's actual fixed cap field β_e(c). Its coefficients are products of β_e(c), not the old constant r_qr_s. All32 support responses describe this one conditional source. The full-height prefix bounds and central selector menus are inherited unchanged.

## 6. The central cap field is a joint object

Write W_i for a central row mass, V_j for a column mass, M=W_0V_0 and μ=1−M. On unmasked cells the row, column and point masses are

A_i=W_i−1_{i=0}M,
C_j=V_j−1_{j=0}M,
D_ij=W_iV_j·1_{(i,j)≠(0,0)}.

A source-null central role has mass0. For each globally fixed full-slot activation upper field,

sum_c μ_c B_t(c)=μ+A_{R_t}+C_{C_t}+D_{I_t,J_t}.

Multiplying by κ_t and summing gives the corresponding exact mean of β_e. This does not permit independent maximization of β_e at every cell. Before masking, a2-by2 mixed row/column difference cancels the constant, row and column terms of B_t and leaves only that ONE group's point indicator difference; its magnitude is at most1. The corresponding weighted difference of β_e has magnitude at mostκ_11+κ_21+κ_12. Thus the cap vector is not an arbitrary collection of eight unrelated values.

These identities identify concrete joint data for a prospective boundary: each edge carries three globally fixed row/column/point role groups, in addition to the star-central data. Their covariance with the star losses is not determined by the individual means.

## 7. What remains to connect this bridge uniformly

An adaptively chosen good-cell mask can break the old separate-concavity argument. Already with one edge, fixed Z_s=1 and β=1/2, strict-good-cell truncation gives the mass(z−1/2)_+. At z=1/4 and3/4 it has values0 and1/4, but at their midpoint it is0<1/8. Thus masking does not generically preserve concavity. This mass-only counterexample does not assert a particular failure of the complete512-cost gate; it shows that its old concavity premise cannot be inherited for free.

A precise sufficient route is to partition the globally coherent central-role domain into classes, choose one FIXED mask Γ per class, and certify strict Shearer on Γ throughout that class's simultaneous Z and β bounds. With Γ and the full β field fixed, each H_T remains multiaffine in the Z blocks, and the complete gate is separately concave in each Z block. With the responses and Γ fixed, the inherited central-weight comparisons remain separately concave. These statements do not assert joint concavity in all fields and weights. Conditional cell boxes can supply the simultaneous bounds. If alignment or a source-corner transformation changes the mask, the necessary common-mask or comparison inequality is an additional proof obligation.

The unresolved main target is a uniform positive full gate for arbitrary global central roles and independently fixed endpoints of all twelve labels per edge. This work supplies a valid general-event common-source bridge, a genuine local obstruction to pointwise positivity, exact joint activation constraints, and positive restricted examples of the masking route. It does not yet close that uniform target.
