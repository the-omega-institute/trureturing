# Matching completion slots across fresh prefix depths

Keep the conditional actual-source branch of Report870: a globally
count-then-modulus-sum minimal odd distinct whole cover, ternary height
two, q=113 of height one, and common period 9qW with W coprime to 3q.
All q-bearing originals are deleted together. Their row-cofactor
inventories are the divisibility downsets P2 contained in P1 contained
in P0. The unchanged q-free originals leave exactly E0 on 9W.
Every one of the 113 literal q-colors covers this same E0.
These hypotheses are not asserted for every possible odd cover.

There is a sufficient integer completion certificate whose labels,
count and total modulus cost are all paid by these actual inventories.
The unresolved step is to force a color, or a jointly verified mixed
literal cover, to satisfy that certificate.

## A packet and its available divisor representatives

For one complete color v, create one packet (i,z) for each original i
of that color and each safe old word z modulo nine compatible with
its original ternary phase on which its literal cofactor class meets E0. Its anchor is m_i, and its cofactor
phase is the original residue projected modulo m_i. Its higher ternary
digits are unrestricted. These packets cover E0.

For a finite packet set A and a divisor bank B, put

$$
D_B(p)=\{d:d\mid m_p\}\cap B,\qquad
\nu_B(X)=\left|\bigcup_{p\in X}D_B(p)\right|.
$$

Repeated anchors are distinct packet indices. At one fixed fresh
height, distinct representatives from these sets supply distinct
numerical AP labels. Each AP keeps its own packet's literal cofactor
phase projected to the chosen divisor and its prescribed ternary
prefix. The CRT enclosure covers the whole corresponding child,
not only a point chosen from it.

## Reserve matching obligations, not particular divisors

Suppose a bank satisfies the baseline Hall inequalities

$$
\nu_B(X)\ge2|X|\qquad(X\subseteq A).
\tag{MC1}
$$

Make three clones of each packet, all with the same divisor
neighborhood. The transversal matroid of this family is the usual
system-of-distinct-representatives matroid. The first two clones of
every packet form an independent set by MC1. Contract this independent
set and restrict to the third clones. Identifying each third clone
with its packet gives a matroid M_B on A.

A subset F is independent in M_B precisely when

$$
2|X|+|F\cap X|\le\nu_B(X)\qquad(X\subseteq A).
\tag{MC2}
$$

Indeed these are Hall's inequalities for the two compulsory clones
of every packet together with a third clone for packets in F. For
an arbitrary subfamily of clones, project to its set X of owners;
its size is at most the left side of MC2, and its neighborhood is
that union of divisor sets. Conversely, taking all available clones
above X yields the displayed necessary inequality.

The rank formula is

$$
r_B(S)=\min_{X\subseteq A}
\bigl(\nu_B(X)-2|X|+|S\setminus X|\bigr).
\tag{MC3}
$$

This is the standard matching-deficiency formula for the compulsory
and selected third clones, followed by subtraction of the contracted
rank 2|A|. These are applications of Hall's theorem and transversal
matroid contraction, not new matroid constructions being asserted
without their independence hypotheses.

Fixing two particular divisors first does not implement this
contraction. For anchors 25 and 35, the legal reservations {1,25}
and {5,7} prevent completing the first packet from the one remaining
divisor 35. Reassigning to {1,5,25} for the first packet and {7,35}
for the second completes it. The contracted object preserves the
matching obligation while allowing this reassignment.

## Three nested banks yield a paid certificate

Let K_v(J) count packets of color v whose anchors belong to J:

$$
K_v(J)=\sum_{i\in C_v,\ m_i\in J}|R_i|.
$$

There is no factor three here: these are old-word packets, before
creating their children. Consider the three nested banks P0, P1, P2.
The following condition is sufficient:

$$
\boxed{
3K_v(J_1)+2K_v(J_2)+2K_v(J_3)
\le |J_1|+|J_2\cap P_1|+|J_3\cap P_2|
}
\tag{MC4}
$$

for every nested triple of divisor downsets generated by packet
anchors, J1 contained in J2 contained in J3, including the empty set.
It yields a cover of the whole E0 with labels in

$$
27P_0\ \cup\ 81P_1\ \cup\ 243P_2.
\tag{MC5}
$$

Here is the reduction to the published matroid partition theorem.
At level h put s_h(X)=nu_h(X)-2|X|. MC4 with J1=J2 empty implies
baseline MC1 for the smallest bank and hence for every larger bank.
Edmonds--Fulkerson matroid partition applied to the three completion
matroids asks for |S|<=sum_h r_h(S) for all S. Substitution of MC3
makes this equivalent to

$$
\left|\bigcap_hX_h\right|\le\sum_hs_h(X_h)
\qquad\text{for every tuple }(X_1,X_2,X_3).
\tag{MC6}
$$

The maximum over S of |S|-sum_h|S minus X_h| is the size of the
intersection, as is seen by considering membership of each packet.
For i<j, nesting of the banks gives

$$
s_i(X)+s_j(Y)\ge s_i(X\cap Y)+s_j(X\cup Y).
\tag{MC7}
$$

Count each divisor separately. Divisors in both banks satisfy the
usual coverage-submodularity inequality; those only in the larger
bank use monotonicity under intersection. The modular terms 2|X|
balance. Replacing X_i,X_j by their intersection and union preserves
the full intersection and does not increase the right side of MC6.
Among minimum-right-side tuples with that intersection, maximize
sum_h h|X_h|. An out-of-order pair would strictly increase this last
quantity, so a nested minimizing tuple exists.

Saturate each member by all packets whose anchors are in its generated
downset. Its divisor neighborhood stays the same and the demand only
increases. The downsets stay nested. For the resulting chain MC6 is
exactly MC4. Matroid partition therefore supplies disjoint completion
classes F1,F2,F3 with union A.

At every level, match two clones of every packet plus a third for
its completion class. A packet completed at level h uses children
0 and 1 at each earlier level, continuing through child 2, then all
three children at level h. Discard its unused slots after completion.
Every assigned AP contains the corresponding whole child. Each bank
is used injectively, and cofactors prime to three ensure that labels
from different depths cannot collide.

Write S_a=sum of m in P_a and N=sum_a |P_a|. The deleted sum and
maximum replacement sum are

$$
D_\Sigma=113S_0+339S_1+1017S_2,
$$

$$
27S_0+81S_1+243S_2=\frac{27}{113}D_\Sigma<D_\Sigma.
\tag{MC8}
$$

MC5 uses at most N outputs, all fresh against retained originals.
It covers the exact deleted residual, so global count-then-sum
minimality rules out MC4 for every actual complete color. Equivalently,
every such color must admit a generated chain violating MC4. This
bounds the length of a witness chain for one certificate, not the
number of obstructions across all colors.

After the first-stage sampler of Report870, the same argument uses
banks P1,P2 to repair active depth-three parents. The sufficient test
is 3K(J1)+2K(J2)<=|J1 intersect P1|+|J2 intersect P2|. Together with
at most n0 first-stage labels in 27P0, count and cost are again paid
by MC5--MC8. The remaining obligation is this actual active-packet
inequality; pointwise miss probabilities do not imply it.

## Arbitrary finite depth has a simpler Hall characterization

For this paragraph fix a common bank D(p)=Div(m_p), common packet
root depth, and require parent-attributed coverage: each output has
one owner, and every parent's full ternary tail is covered by its
own outputs. Every output has strictly positive relative depth;
none is placed at the original root. Do not credit an output to
several parents. A finite plan with distinct typed labels
(relative depth, divisor) exists
if and only if

$$
\boxed{\nu(X)>2|X|\quad\text{for every nonempty }X\subseteq A.}
\tag{MC9}
$$

For necessity, let L be the greatest relative depth in a finite plan.
A depth-h prefix covers 3^(L-h) of its parent's 3^L tails. At that
depth at most nu(X) labels are available to parents in X. Even
allowing overlaps in this upper bound, coverage requires

$$
|X|3^L\le\nu(X)\sum_{h=1}^{L}3^{L-h}
=\nu(X)\frac{3^L-1}{2},
$$

which implies MC9. This reasoning is specific to parent-attributed
coverage.

Sufficiency uses Hall directly, without matroid partition. While
K parents remain, choose any one of them for completion. Request
two representatives for each remaining parent and a third for the
chosen parent. A nonempty clone subfamily with owner set X has at
most 2|X|+1 members, which is at most nu(X) by MC9 and integrality.
Hall supplies the representatives. Cover two children of each parent
and all three of the chosen parent. Delete that completed parent
and repeat inside the remaining child of each unfinished parent.
MC9 is inherited by every remaining subset.

For an initial K packets this terminates within K levels and uses

$$
\sum_{j=1}^K(2j+1)=K^2+2K
\tag{MC10}
$$

outputs. Cofactors prime to three turn the typed labels into distinct
integer moduli. This establishes finite repairability in that
restricted interface; it does not pay the depth-dependent modulus
sum or force MC9 from the actual covering system.

A unit or prime singleton has at most two available divisors and
fails MC9. Thus arbitrary depth cannot fix every deficient packet
when each packet must pay for its own complete tail. Shared-output
coverage and different source partitions are outside this necessity
statement. The three-bank certificate is sufficient and is not a
normal form for all legal prefix forests.

## Shared outputs require one common divisor assignment

A numerical output may serve several packets. This gives a sufficient
certificate beyond parent-attributed completion. Choose actual packets
whose union contains E0. For every selected divisor d in P0 choose one
old word z_d modulo nine, one child c_d in {0,1,2}, and one phase beta_d
modulo d. Require, for every packet p and every child c, some selected d
satisfying

$$
d\mid m_p,\qquad \alpha_p\equiv\beta_d\pmod d,\qquad
z_d=z_p,\qquad c_d=c.
\tag{MC11}
$$

The output for d is the CRT class with ternary residue z_d+9c_d modulo
27 and cofactor residue beta_d modulo d. It contains that whole child
of every packet satisfying MC11. Hence these outputs cover E0. The
assignment is common to all packets: a numerical divisor cannot receive
separate phases or child choices in different colors.

There is at most one output 27d for each d in P0. Thus the count is at
most n0 and the modulus sum at most 27S0, paid by the same actual deleted
inventory. All labels are fresh. The prime fan is a special case of
this certificate. No actual-source theorem presently produces the
required global assignment.

The same construction applies after first-stage sampling. Use the
active depth-three parents and divisor bank P1. A shared-child
assignment yields repair labels in 81P1. Together with the first-stage
labels in 27P0, count is at most n0+n1 and cost at most 27S0+81S1,
within the paid palette. Simultaneous service of every active packet
remains an additional condition.

## A phase-sensitive necessary bound for shared whole-child repair

Fix a finite bank B, common root depth r, and outputs at strictly
positive relative depths. Credit an output to a packet only when its
divisor divides the packet anchor and its literal projected phase and
old word match, so it contains the whole corresponding child. Require
every packet's complete tail to be covered by its credited outputs.
For a packet subfamily X define

$$
C_X(d)=\max_{z,\beta}
\#\{p\in X:d\mid m_p,\ z_p=z,\ \alpha_p\equiv\beta\pmod d\}.
\tag{MC12}
$$

At any depth, the single numerical label for d can credit at most
C_X(d) parent tails. A finite plan of greatest relative depth L
therefore requires

$$
|X|3^L\le\left(\sum_{d\in B}C_X(d)\right)
\sum_{h=1}^{L}3^{L-h}.
$$

Consequently, for every nonempty X,

$$
\boxed{\sum_{d\in B}C_X(d)>2|X|.}
\tag{MC13}
$$

This is necessary, not a sufficient characterization. For singleton X
it recovers the divisor-stock obstruction. Shared coverage can remove
collective Hall deficits without removing each packet's own finite-tail
requirement. Requiring this service of whole packet tails is stronger
than covering E0 alone.

Two packets with the same old word, anchor 25, and respective literal
phases zero and one demonstrate a finite obstruction to free sharing.
In the bank Div(25)={1,5,25}, the maximal credits are 2,1,1. At every
depth the total credit is at most four, while

$$
4\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}3^j=2(3^L-1)<2\cdot3^L.
\tag{MC14}
$$

No finite plan in this bank and whole-child interface covers both
complete tails, even allowing outputs to credit both packets. This is
a local interface example, not a whole odd covering system. It does
not exclude repairs using new cofactors outside Div(25), partial
cofactor intersections, or a different target partition.

## Finite height normalization preserves actual arithmetic coverage

The canonical prefix-height normalization theorem has an exact CRT
application to arbitrary finite families of APs 3^h_i d_i. Assume
d_i divides W, 3 is coprime to W, every d_i is positive odd, h_i>r,
and the numerical labels are distinct. Let their union cover the
periodic target E(x modulo 3^r, x modulo W).

There is a subfamily J and new heights and residues with
r<h'_i<=r+|J| and h'_i<=h_i, still covering exactly the same specified
target in the sense of inclusion. Its side residues modulo d_i and
old residues modulo 3^r are unchanged, its numerical labels remain
distinct odd nonunits, and its total modulus sum does not increase.
The resulting union need not equal the old union or equal E.

The correspondence uses a common finite CRT period for any supplied
prefix length, with W held fixed. It does not alter the ternary
expansion of an integer while pretending its W-residue is preserved.
Mathlib's finite digit equivalence and CRT supply both transports;
the frozen normalization result acts between them. Applied to a
plan with at most N outputs and r=2, this gives maximum height N+2.
It does not create a plan when no plan was supplied.

## Evidence and unresolved source obligation

MC1--MC7 reuse finite Hall, transversal matroid contraction, matching
deficiency and matroid partition. The original source is Jack Edmonds
and D. R. Fulkerson, *Transversals and Matroid Partition*, Journal of
Research of the National Bureau of Standards 69B(3), 147--153 (1965),
[DOI 10.6028/jres.069b.016](https://doi.org/10.6028/jres.069b.016).
The [official paper](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/69B/jresv69Bn3p147_A1b.pdf)
was read directly: Section 1, the unnumbered Theorem, part (b), page 147, supplies the
transversal matroid for a finite indexed family, allowing repeated
sets; Section 4, Theorem 1c, page 150, supplies partition into
independent sets under the all-subsets rank inequality. Section 5,
page 152, defines contraction and its rank formula. In that paper's
notation M times E0 contracts the complementary ground set, so here
E0 consists of the third clones and the contracted complement is the
two compulsory clones. MC1 supplies their full rank 2|A|. The
argument and its conditions above are ordinary mathematics, not a
new Lean proof of matroid partition.

The direct Hall matching step of MC9, inheritance by pending subsets,
and MC10 have exact Lean applications with zero build exit and only
standard axioms. The fixed-reservation failure at anchors 25,35 also
has an exact finite check. The general parent-attributed finite-tree
necessity and construction are the ordinary arguments above, not a
single compiled forest theorem.

The shared-output CRT implication from one actual packet cover of E
to a cover of that same E has an exact Lean application. The divisor
credits for the two anchor-25 packets and the finite geometric bound
MC14 also compile exactly, with default budgets and standard axioms.
The general shared-forest necessity argument for MC13 is the ordinary
counting proof above, not a compiled whole-forest theorem.

The arithmetic height-normalization application is compiled from
actual AP coverage to actual AP coverage, including all residue,
label and cost conclusions. An inhabited application starts with
labels 27,135,189,3^10*11 and residues 0,90,126,0, covering x=0 modulo
both 9 and 385. It obtains a nonempty replacement with height at
most six. All checks use default budgets and standard axioms. These
are transient applications of existing results, not new binding
modules or additional canonical theorem publications.

No actual-source theorem currently forces MC4 for one complete color,
or its active-packet variant for a legal sample. Separate optimal
choices of color, matching or literal phase cannot be combined as
one plan without a common realization. The source-forcing and paid
whole-cover contradiction remain unresolved, as does unrestricted
Erdős #7.
