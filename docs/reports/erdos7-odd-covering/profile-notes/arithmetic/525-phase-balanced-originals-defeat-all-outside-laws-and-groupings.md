# Phase-balanced original labels defeat every outside-law raw transport

The positive-carrier and correlated-defect criteria in [reports521](521-pure-unit-conditioning-restores-a-uniform-fibre-threshold.md)--[524](524-actual-pure-class-pairs-pay-correlated-defects-through-endpoint-ancestors.md) retain a real premise: their original pure accounts must admit divisor-supported transports. Choosing a favourable outside probability law cannot make that premise automatic. An actual family with one fixed old reference has the same excessive total expense under EVERY outside law, while its true deletion can be confined to an arbitrarily small old cylinder.

The obstruction uses the raw original-label account of [report520](520-actual-modulus-inventories-and-divisor-transport.md) and its conditional-marginal versions. It does not refute a union-aware account, an old-activation-weighted estimate, or Erdős#7. Every constructed family has an explicit uncovered old cylinder. The phase-cycle mechanism develops the general obstruction supplied by an external research response; the smaller control and arbitrary-depth version below are checked directly. Standard CRT, probability additivity and the transport cut inequality are the proof ingredients. No claim of literature priority or Lean verification is made.

## One actual family at arbitrary old depth

Fix integers H>=0 and K>=1. Use the old prime3 and the outside group S={29,31}. For q in{29,31} and i in{1,...,Kq}, take the original numerical modulus

    m_(q,i)=3^(H+i) q.

Choose its unique residue a_(q,i) in[0,m_(q,i)) satisfying

    a_(q,i)=1 mod3^(H+i),
    a_(q,i)=i modq.                                     (B1)

These are60K pairwise distinct odd moduli greater than1. Within one q the exponent is distinct; across the two q values their prime factorizations differ. CRT supplies one fixed actual residue for every complete label. Every old selector is the same A=1; choosing B=2 is compatible with the finite-prefix hypotheses of [report518](518-finite-prefix-templates-allow-arbitrary-old-residue-tails.md), and at old primes absent from these cofactors the conditions are vacuous. No residue, law or selector is chosen separately at different tested old points.

Let rho be ANY probability law on an outside finite period resolving these cylinders, and let rho_29,rho_31 be its marginal laws modulo29 and31. The law need not be uniform, independent, or of full support. It may be any law produced by product restriction or by joint unit conditioning.

For each q, the indices1,...,Kq contain each residue moduloq exactly K times. Therefore

    sum_(i=1)^(Kq) rho_q(i modq)=K.                     (B2)

In the pure-S account, the original label(q,i) has expense28rho_q(i modq), placed at its actual old cofactor3^(H+i). Summing all rows gives

    sum_d b_d^S=56K                                    (B3)

for EVERY rho. This uses additivity of a single marginal law; correlation between its two coordinates cannot alter the sum. The equality also holds for probability laws supported on the complement of additional unit classes, because their marginal probabilities still sum to1.

## A shared ancestor cut rejects every transport

The union of divisors of all positive-expense source labels is contained in

    E_0={1,3,...,3^(H+31K)}, |E_0|=H+31K+1.            (B4)

Zero-expense rows do not enlarge this upper bound. A divisor-supported transport cannot use an ancestor outside E_0 to pay any of these actual source rows. Allowing a larger ambient E, or other original labels, does not create new destinations for these rows.

If each destination column has capacity at most1, summing their capacities and the required row payments forces

    56K<=H+31K+1.

Consequently

    25K>H+1                                           (B5)

rules out every pure-S transport of the stated form, simultaneously for all outside probability laws. In particular it rules out the full report520/521/522/523/524 certificate on this group assignment, before any mixed or pair expenses are considered. This is a fixed-group statement: it does not classify every possible repartition of the outside prime support or every alternative proof certificate.

For every desired old depth H there is a finite K satisfying(B5), for example K=floor((H+1)/25)+1. Thus the obstruction does not depend on a shallow old label or on a large conditional probability at a particular outside phase.

## Actual deletion remains small

Every class(B1) implies

    x=1 mod3^(H+1).

Hence their actual union lies inside one old cylinder, and under full Haar measure its mass is at most3^(-H-1). In particular the survivor mass of this constructed family is at least

    1-3^(-H-1).                                      (B6)

All points in the old root2mod3 survive, whatever their outside coordinates. More generally, on any product law nu tensor rho for which nu is an old probability law satisfying nu<=C H_old, the union mass is at most C3^(-H-1). This latter bound is conditional on the stated domination; it asserts no independent construction or survival property of nu.

Adding unit originals does not change(B3) for any resulting nonempty conditioned outside law. But(B6) concerns the original nonunit family only: added unit deletions must be accounted for separately. Under conditioning that has already avoided those units, the same old-cylinder bound controls the remaining nonunit loss.

Thus an arbitrarily small actual loss can coexist with failure of every raw ancestor transport. The account omits WHERE old activation occurs. Numerical distinctness prevents repeating a modulus, but it does not prevent many different numerical moduli from acting only inside the same tiny old cylinder.

## Exact finite controls

For H=0,K=1 there are60 original labels. Their total pure-S expense is56 for every law, while the reachable ancestor cut has32 columns: the deficit is24.

For H=100,K=5 there are300 original labels. Their total expense is280 for every law, while the reachable cut has256 columns: the deficit is again24. All actual deletion is contained in1mod3^101, despite this fixed positive capacity deficit. With the inherited domination constant27/2, its source-weighted loss is at most(27/2)3^(-101); that estimate uses only the domination inequality, not a replay of the source construction.

These finite controls require no enumeration of the old period. Exact integer CRT verifies the individual numerical labels; the counts of each residue in the two phase cycles establish(B2) and are a certificate for every law. Summing over one hand-picked probability vector would not establish that quantifier.

## An obstruction to every two-group assignment

The fixed-group restriction above can be removed by taking q in{23,29,31}, with the same indices i=1,...,Kq and the same actual CRT residues(B1). There are83K original labels. Their old cofactors still form one chain with maximum exponent H+31K. Each prime contributes exactly K units of unscaled expense under every outside law, regardless of joint correlations.

Divide these three outside primes into any two groups R,S, including an empty group. Some group G has at least two primes. Its scale c_G is22 or28. The original rows belonging to G have total expense

    c_G |G| K>=44K,

and can use only the H+31K+1 chain columns already described. Therefore

    13K>H+1                                          (B7)

rules out at least one pure account in EVERY grouping. A chosen joint law, product restriction, component law or subsequent unit conditioning cannot change the phase-cycle equality. This conclusion concerns certificates requiring the raw original pure accounts; it grants no right to retain or delete different original labels in different tested fibres.

For H=0,K=1, the83-label family fails all eight assignments, with the smallest obstructed-account deficit12. For H=100,K=8, the664-label family also fails every assignment; the smallest such deficit is3. In the latter case the group{29,31} at scale22 already requires352 units but has only349 reachable columns. The whole nonunit union nevertheless lies inside1mod3^101. More generally K=floor((H+1)/13)+1 provides an all-partition obstruction at every old depth.

Additional outside primes absent from these original labels cannot pay their expenses: an allowed destination must divide an actual old cofactor. Additional unit classes can alter the conditioned law, but every resulting probability law still satisfies the same marginal sums. Actual losses of those additional classes remain separate from(B6).

## Consequence for the remaining proof problem

Positive unit-avoiding carriers, retained joint phases and better defect routing address genuine losses of information in the boundary. They cannot make this specific raw transport premise universal. A next sufficient condition must use additional old-activation information, charge a controlled omitted subfamily against the same source, or supply a different residue-unrestricted continuation theorem.

The present families are deliberately noncovering and supply method counterexamples only. The three-prime family rules out automatic feasibility even after choosing the grouping and the outside law. It settles neither the feasibility of stronger accounts nor the unrestricted distinct-odd covering problem.

## A direct omitted-tail application beyond the literal two-root templates

This is an application of the existing original-cylinder deletion budget ([Chapter47, FA99](../../problem-details/47-finite-label-core-and-omitted-mass.md); [report457, FF4–FF5](457-finitely-many-early-phases-control-all-core-heights.md)), not a new general continuation theorem.

Keep the nine later originals of [report524](524-actual-pure-class-pairs-pay-correlated-defects-through-endpoint-ancestors.md):

    (m,a)=(23,0),(29,0),(31,0),(69,25),(87,31),(93,64),
          (667,2),(713,2),(6003,1).

Adjoin any finite family of pairwise distinct P-smooth old-only originals, with arbitrary residues and heights, where P={3,5,7,11,13,17,19}. The unchanged outside law and report524 pair certificate give the resulting core survivor U the uniform full-Haar bound

    H(U)>delta=89/99681120000.

The old-only family is allowed by the inherited source theorem. It introduces no later-account rows; a larger ancestor set only requires zero extension of the displayed certificate. One source is used within each given family, not one source chosen for all different old-only families.

For any finite additional original family T, disjoint in numerical moduli from this core, resolve both families on one full CRT carrier. Its actual cylinder C_m has Haar mass1/m, so

    H(U minus union_(m in T) C_m)
      >=H(U)-H(U intersect union_(m in T) C_m)
      >=H(U)-sum_(m in T)1/m
      >delta-sum_(m in T)1/m.                         (A1)

All original residues stay fixed. This final Haar deletion requires no template for T, no independence of its intersections, and no reconstruction of the old source.

For example set H=17, K=2, Q={23,29,31}. For each q in Q and1<=i<=Kq add

    m_(q,i)=3^(H+i)q,
    a_(q,i)=alpha_(q,i) mod3^(H+i),
    a_(q,i)=i modq,                                  (A2)

where every alpha_(q,i) is an arbitrary residue modulo3^(H+i). CRT gives one original residue per complete numerical label. These166 additional moduli are pairwise distinct, differ from the nine later core moduli because their ternary exponents exceed2, and differ from every old-only modulus because they contain q outside P.

The complete original-label deletion payment is independent of these old phases:

    L_(H,K)=3^(-H)/2 sum_(q in Q)(1-3^(-Kq))/q
           <2279/(41354*3^H).

At H=17,K=2,

    L_(17,2)=434742523691479775140827210162556
                /1018747721933476885468884179145795814831959
             =4.2674208180449603...*10^(-10),
    delta-2279/(41354*3^17)
       =15333572629/32897247772324320000

Thus all175 later originals, together with the arbitrary old-only family, leave full-Haar survivor mass greater than4*10^(-10), for EVERY choice of the old phases in(A2). H=16,K=2 has this particular exact payment above delta; it is only a failed sufficient bound, not a covering example or an optimal-depth claim.

To leave every literal two-root template of518–524, take the three old phases alpha_(23,1),alpha_(23,2),alpha_(23,3) in three distinct roots modulo3. No choice of the two template references, before or after inspecting the family, can match all three; increasing template depths cannot repair a first-root mismatch. This concerns the original full-family template premise. It does not rule out a separate proof that first removes redundant classes.

The raw transport obstruction also survives arbitrary alpha. Its proof uses the old numerical cofactors and the outside phase multiplicities, not the old residues. For every outside probability law, each q-cycle still has total marginal expense K before scaling. Under every two-group assignment some group has at least two primes, so its expense is at least44K=88. These source rows can use only the chain columns1,3,...,3^(H+31K), at mostH+31K+1=80 columns. Additional old-only labels or a larger ambient ancestor set do not supply destinations dividing these rows beyond that chain. This statement concerns the raw original-label accounts, including laws obtained by unit conditioning or product restriction; it does not refute union-aware or activation-weighted accounts.

    sum_(v3(m)>20)1/m
       <=Z_(P union Q)*3^(-21),
    Z_(P union Q)=86822723/26542080,
    delta-Z_(P union Q)*3^(-21)

This is a separate full-smooth-tail application of(A1), not an additional uncharged tail on top of(A2). The bound for v3(m)>19 exceeds delta. These cutoffs express only this sufficient reserve payment.

The remaining premise is not the availability of arbitrarily deep digits: those can already be paid. What is still missing is a certificate that retains enough mass for unrestricted shallow old-phase patterns, or selects a certifiable original core whose actual omitted union costs less than its SAME-source survival reserve. The current application supplies that split for the displayed family only. It does not make such a core or budget exist for every distinct odd family.
