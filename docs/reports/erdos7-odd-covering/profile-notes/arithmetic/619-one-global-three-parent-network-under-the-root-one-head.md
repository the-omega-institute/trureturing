phase class of [Report617](617-all-central-star-phases-admit-root-one-continuation.md). Every outside owner may now use any ONE fixed
tuple of at most three distinct smaller parents. All three may be head
primes, two may be heads and one outside, one may be a head and two
outside, or all may be outside. Arbitrary crossings and mergers between
these entries are allowed. The extendible head Haar mass is greater
than1/53000; the complete finite family's density is greater than
1/(53000 Q_off).

This removes every parent-type distinction from the outside network under
that head hypothesis. It retains one fixed tuple per owner and the ordinary
private-interface restrictions. It does not admit an unrestricted head,
several independent tuples at one owner, or arbitrary many parents.
The result is ordinary mathematics with exact rational certificates, not
new Lean verification or a resolution of unrestricted Erdős #7.
