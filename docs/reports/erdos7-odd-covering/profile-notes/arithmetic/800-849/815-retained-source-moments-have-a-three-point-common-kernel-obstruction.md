## 6. What is and is not excluded

The general retained-moment bridge is reusable. The negative dual has the narrower scope ofh16, these literal selected phases, these three points of the cap relaxation, one shared first-digit retained kernel, and the stated full hinge and tail comparisons.

The three cap points need not be realized exactly by finite pure inventories. They suffice to obstruct a certificate claiming uniform positivity on the enclosing cap domain. No impossibility for the smaller set of actually realized laws is asserted.

The previous all-threshold obstruction used a different normalization and a global source lower bound. It cannot be carried over without another argument. This report does not exclude a different threshold, a kernel chosen after learning the actual source law, finer source-domain restrictions, or a sharper conditional hinge. A nonpositive sufficient comparison is not an actual covering.
