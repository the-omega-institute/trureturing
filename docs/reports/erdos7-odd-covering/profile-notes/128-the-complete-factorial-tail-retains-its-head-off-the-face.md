# The complete factorial tail retains its head off the face

The complete second-factorial tail now has a computable off-face bound
on the same actual-source domain as116,121 and125. For every complete
original357 test A, with its own independently labelled six-head layout ell,

    T5(A)=integral Phi5(A)dmu <= J128(ell)+P128,
    Phi5(v)=(v-5)_+*(v-4)/2.                         (F128.1)

Both terms use one actual source and the same complete capacity and
union-error coordinates. J128 retains the head before maximization;
P128 includes every unordered pair of distinct omitted original labels.
On the entire actual saturated K faces these terms recover112 exactly:

    max_ell T5 upper=619/720.                        (F128.2)

## 3. The unbounded old cross term uses a positive excess

The bound mu<=w*Lambda+Omega and the raw bound mu<=Lambda imply

    nu=(mu-w*Lambda)_+,
    nu(1)<=omega, nu<=(1-w)*Lambda.                (F128.7)

Thus the error in the old cross term has both a mass bound and a
decaying cylinder bound. A flat omega payment for every omitted label
would have divergent total and is not used.

For a nonnegative25-cell function z define the five raw coefficients

    C3(z)=max_c sum_s p_(c,s)*z_(c,s),
    C5(z)=max_s sum_c d_(c,s)*z_(c,s),
    C15(z)=max_(r,s)sum_(ROOT(c)=r)d_(c,s)*z_(c,s),
    C45(z)=max_(c,s)d_(c,s)*z_(c,s),
    Cmix(z)=max_(c,s)z_(c,s).

These bound, respectively, each original pure3 label a>=3, pure5
label b>=2,3*5^b label,9*5^b label, and mixed label a>=3,b>=1,
after multiplication by its corresponding3^-a or5^-b or product.
They are116's actual raw source/descendant bounds, applied separately
to each original cylinder. The complete baseline is

    O(z)=C3(z)/18+[C5(z)+C15(z)+C45(z)]/20
                                      +Cmix(z)/72. (F128.8)

Set M=max h<=2 and e=M*omega. From(F128.7), the full excess payment is

    Eold(h)=sum_(a>=3)min(e,C3((1-w)h)*3^-a)
       +sum_(b>=2)sum_(i=5,15,45)min(e,Ci((1-w)h)*5^-b)
       +sum_(a>=3,b>=1)min(e,Cmix((1-w)h)*3^-a*5^-b).
                                                        (F128.9)

Consequently

    Cold(ell)<=O(w*h)+Eold(h).                    (F128.10)

Every sum in(F128.9) is explicitly computable. For e,c>0, if N is
the first n>=n0 with c*p^-n<=e, then

    sum_(n>=n0)min(e,c*p^-n)
       =(N-n0)*e+c*p^-N/(1-p^-1).                (F128.11)

For the double sum choose A>=3 first such that c*3^-A/5<=e.
Sum the finitely many rows3<=a<A by(F128.11), then add

    c*[sum_(a>=A)3^-a]*[sum_(b>=1)5^-b].

At e=0 or c=0 the sum is zero. This retains the entire complement
and makes Eold identically zero on the face. The baseline(F128.8)
then becomes112's old-tail operator exactly, head by head.

## 5. Every tail-pair cap is a weighted complete125 series

Write m(a,b)=(2a+1)(2b+1). Let C0(a,b) be125's surviving cylinder
cap outside the six-head rectangle. Its four pure families are
OT4--OT8; for a>=3,b>=1 use3^-a*5^-b. Let Cr(a,b) be the complete

    Cr(0,0)=s, Cr(1,0)=N3, Cr(2,0)=N9,
    Cr(a,0)=D*3^-a, a>=3,
    Cr(a,b)=(h,h1,max eta)_a*5^-b, a<=2,b>=1,
    Cr(a,b)=3^-a*5^-b, a>=3,b>=1,
    N3=max(n0+n1,n2+n3+n4), N9=max_c n_c.         (F128.14)

At an LCM exponent pair(a,b) outside the head, the ordered O-by-O
and O-by-all-old multiplicities are respectively:

| Region | kOO(a,b) | kOA(a,b) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a>=3,b<=1 | (2a-5)(2b+1) | (2a-2)(2b+1) |

| a>=3,b>=2 | m(a,b)-12 | m(a,b)-6 |

Both counts are zero inside the head. To derive them, count old-tail
labels in each exponent rectangle:

    N_O(a,b)=(a+1)(b+1)-(min(a,2)+1)*(min(b,1)+1).

Taking the two finite differences of N_O(a,b)^2 counts O-by-O
pairs with exactly this maximum. Applying the same differences to
N_O(a,b)*(a+1)*(b+1) counts O-by-all pairs. The diagonal contributes
one at every pair outside the head. Therefore the exact assigned-cap
partition is

    POO=(1/2)*sum_((a,b) in O)(kOO(a,b)-1)*C0(a,b),
    PO7=(1/5)*sum_((a,b) in O)kOA(a,b)*Cr(a,b),

    Lraw=sum_(a,b>=0)Cr(a,b),
    P77=(1/2)*[(4/15)*Qraw-Lraw/5],
    P128=POO+PO7+P77.                            (F128.15)

The factor4/15 is the complete ordered positive-seven pair factor

the indicated Lraw/5. These are identities between known nonnegative

No shallow surviving cylinder cap is required: the maximum of two
old-tail exponent pairs is still outside the head. All four pure

    sum_(n>=N)(an+b)*p^-n
       =p^-N*[(aN+b)/(1-p^-1)+a*p^-1/(1-p^-1)^2]. (F128.16)

The mixed and raw parts are products of these complete geometric
sums. Thus(F128.15) requires no finite depth approximation. On the face,

    POO=479/3600, PO7=121/720, P77=97/240,

    Qraw=173/48, Lraw=55/72.                     (F128.17)

## 6. The quadratic costs can share126's original head

For any exact positive all-load expansion

    f(v)=a0+sum_t a_t*(v-t)_++theta*Phi5(v),
    a0,a_t,theta>=0,

use126's finite branch C_b, additional mean credit c_b, complete
omitted tails R_k,Zplus, and bounded error price Ma. Then

    integral_mu f(A)
       <=a0*S+sum_t a_t*(R_(k_t)+Zplus)+Ma*omega+theta*P128
         +max_b[C_b-a1*c_b+theta*J128(ell_b)].    (F128.18)

This covers113's ten positive quadratic expansions with the same
original head and positive-seven projections before maximization.
The multiple appearances of omega bound different nonnegative
integrands against the same actual error; they are not independent
allocations of rho. The full nonlinear source/defect optimization
remains a separate obligation.

The remaining raw81 row f(v)=(v²-81)_+ has negative coefficients
in113's exact Phi5 expansion. A complete positive fallback is

    (v²-81)_+ <=(243/91)*Phi5(v), v>=1 integer.  (F128.19)

For v<=9 the left side is zero. For v>=10 the difference equals
(v-18)*(61v-1089)/182, nonnegative at every integer because
17<1089/61<18. Equality occurs at v=18, so243/91 is the least
possible multiplier of Phi5 alone. This supplies a finite complete
off-face bound for that row as well; it does not improve its existing
face bound, which must be retained when making a face comparison.
