# An actual overlap forces nonlinear complete-tail prices

The obstruction to a fixed linear error price is present in actual
original-label configurations. It is not confined to the class of
arbitrary Haar-dominated measures used in115. On the saturated398
source, change one original deep3-times7 residue. The resulting
positive surplus is entirely union overlap, but its complete pure3
test tail has an unbounded integral-to-mass ratio.

For every a>=3 and e>=1 there is an actual limiting configuration,
with the same saturated K source and shallow carrier law, such that

    rho=omega=9/(10*7^e*3^a),
    all five-family and pure3 capacity defects=0,
    q5=q15=0, sigma=0.                            (AO1)

For a specified independently labelled complete old pure3 test tail Z,

    integral_Omega Z =rho*(a-3/2),
    integral_Omega Z^2=rho*(a^2-3*a+3).           (AO2)

Consequently neither complete tail moment admits a fixed linear
price in rho, even on these actual all-omega perturbations. Actual
finite original-label witnesses are given below. These statements do
not assert attainment of129's full cost envelope, a new global K,
or resolution of unrestricted Erdős #7.

## 1. A single collision inside an existing original deletion

Use48's complete398 construction. Write

    C1=[3] mod9,
    T_a=[3+3^(a-1)] mod3^a, a>=3,
    G_(j,e)=[j*7^(e-1)] mod7^e.

All T_a are disjoint. All G_(j,e),1<=j<=6,e>=1, are disjoint.
The original forbidden9*7^e uses C1 times G_(1,e). The original
forbidden3^a*7^e uses T_a times G_(2,e). Their old-coordinate
carriers and seven-coordinate factors are independent CRT data.

Change only the latter original label's seven class from2 to1,
keeping its old residue T_a. Its new cylinder is contained in the
existing9*7^e forbidden cylinder, since T_a is a subset of C1.
Its former cylinder was disjoint from every other original mixed7
forbidden cylinder. Thus the union loses exactly the former cylinder
and gains nothing. All original moduli are retained, odd and distinct;
only one residue changes. In particular a>=3 prevents coincidence
with the original9*7^e modulus.

Let q be the pure5 survivor measure. Throughout C1, the398 source
has no ternary, alpha, beta or late deletion beyond pure5. Hence

    Lambda restricted to T_a
       =Haar3 restricted to T_a tensor q,
    q(1)=3/4.

The normalized pure7 survivor assigns G_(j,e) mass6/(5*7^e).
The survivor increment on the old coordinates is therefore exactly

    Delta_mu=[6/(5*7^e)]*(Lambda restricted to T_a),
    Delta_mu(1)=9/(10*7^e*3^a).                 (AO3)

The virtual measure V does not change: every old carrier and its
seven mass are unchanged. The baseline398 deletion has delta=V.
After the collision, delta decreases by Delta_mu, so

    Omega=V-delta=Delta_mu,
    S=53/360+Delta_mu(1).

Every original cofactor capacity remains saturated, and neither
shallow forbidden3/9 nor the5/15 carriers change. This proves(AO1).
The fact that the new cylinder is redundant does not make its old
cofactor cap deficient; union overlap and individual capacity loss
are different nonnegative terms in57's decomposition.

## 2. Exact complete test moments

Put t=3+3^(a-1). For each original pure3 test modulus3^k,k>=3,
choose the independent test residue t mod3^k, and define

    Z=sum_(k>=3)1_[t mod3^k].

These are choices for independent original test labels, not a
restriction requiring arbitrary tests to share residues. They form
a valid nested witness. On T_a every test at3<=k<=a equals1.
Under the normalized measure Omega/rho, ternary digits beyond a
are independent Haar digits. If L counts the additional matching
levels, then

    Pr(L>=j)=3^-j, j>=1,
    E[L]=1/2,
    E[L^2]=sum_(j>=1)(2*j-1)*3^-j=1.

The count is finite almost everywhere and Z=a-2+L. Expanding its
first two moments proves(AO2). This includes every exponent beyond a;
no tail is discarded.

More explicitly, with c_e=9/(10*7^e),

    integral_Omega 1_[t mod3^k]
       =min(rho,c_e*3^-k),
    integral_Omega Z
       =sum_(k>=3)min(rho,c_e*3^-k).             (AO4)

Thus one complete min-geometric cap series is attained exactly by
this actual defect. At fixed e, rho tends to0 as a tends to infinity,
and

    integral_Omega Z
       =(rho/log3)*log(1/rho)+O(rho),
    integral_Omega Z^2
       =(rho/(log3)^2)*log(1/rho)^2
          +O(rho*log(1/rho)).                   (AO5)

The logarithms are natural. The constants in the lower-order terms
may depend on the fixed seven depth e.

## 3. Finite original-label witnesses

Truncate the398 construction to exponents0<=a',b',e'<=N, omitting
only the unit. For N>=max(a,e), it contains exactly(N+1)^3-1
original odd moduli. Apply the same single-residue change. The old
and new seven cylinders still have the same normalized mass, and
the same containment and disjointness statements hold literally.

The source pure5 mass and pure7 normalization are

    z_N=3/4+1/(4*5^N),
    u7_N=(5+7^-N)/6.

The exact increase in survivor mass is

    Delta_N=(7^-e/u7_N)*z_N*3^-a.               (AO6)

Every source parameter and every individual capacity defect remains
unchanged by the move. The defects are not zero at finite N; missing
infinite tails remain present in their definitions.

For the finite original test tail

    Z_N=sum_(k=3..N)1_[t mod3^k],

write m=a-2 and h=N-a. The increments satisfy

    integral_Delta_mu_N Z_N
       =Delta_N*(m+sum_(j=1..h)3^-j),
    integral_Delta_mu_N Z_N^2
       =Delta_N*(m^2+2*m*sum_(j=1..h)3^-j
                      +sum_(j=1..h)(2*j-1)*3^-j).    (AO7)

In particular at N=a the ratios are a-2 and(a-2)^2. Therefore
arbitrarily large moment-to-increment ratios already occur for two
finite actual families and finite independent original tests. For
fixed a,e, taking N to infinity gives(AO1)--(AO2). All complete source
and forbidden tails have the geometric domination supplied in48.

## 4. Consequence and boundary

A proposed improvement of129 cannot replace the complete tail
modulus by L*rho solely on the grounds that the defect is an actual
union overlap or that every individual forbidden capacity is sharp.
Both properties hold here. Any valid improvement needs additional
information about which heads or costs can exploit the overlap.

This result concerns the specified old pure3 tail and its square.
It does not prove a matching increase for every curvature cost,
for a cost after mean subtraction, or for the joint original-head
maximizer in129. Those may retain compensating terms. The example
also does not assert that a general Haar-dominated defect is
realizable, or that the whole relaxed residual simplex is realizable.
