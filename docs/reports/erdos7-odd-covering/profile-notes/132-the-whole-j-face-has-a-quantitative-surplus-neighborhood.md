    E5+E15+E5d+E15d+E3+omega<=epsilon.            (JS3)

## 6. Positive union error is realized by original labels

The Omega direction is not merely an artifact of a relaxed simplex.
Start with50's complete off-diagonal J-saturating family. Its original
cofactor5 at a given seven depth e uses old carrier H and seven class3;
cofactor15 uses old carrier root1 times H and seven class1. The classes
are G_(j,e)=[j*7^(e-1)] modulo7^e, mutually disjoint across(j,e).

Change only the seven class of the original label15*7^e from1 to3.
Its new cylinder lies inside the existing5*7^e deletion. Its old
deletion was disjoint from all other original deletions. Every old
cofactor carrier and its capacity remain unchanged, but the deleted
union loses precisely

    u_e*Lambda(root1 times H)
       =[6/(5*7^e)]*(1/15)=2/(25*7^e).

Thus the complete limiting family has

    epsilon=omega=2/(25*7^e)>0,
    E5=E15=E5d=E15d=E3=0, kappa=0.

For e>=3 it lies in(JS2). All moduli remain original, odd and pairwise
distinct; changing a residue does not duplicate a modulus. At a finite
truncation N>=e the exact additional union loss is

    2/[5*(5+7^-N)*7^e].                         (JS16)

The preexisting absent-tail defects remain at finite N. Only the
complete limit has all those defects zero. Taking N to infinity
recovers the actual positive-surplus face configuration above.
