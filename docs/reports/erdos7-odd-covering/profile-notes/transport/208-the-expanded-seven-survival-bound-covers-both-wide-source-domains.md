## 3. Each selected inequality is fixed before maximizing the domain

Use201's head B, threshold prefixes k_t=min(t-1,4), and bridge

    f_t(v;c,s;q)=wbar(c,s;q)*(v-t)_++g_(t,m(c,s))(v),

The enlarged candidate has m<=4 and tail(WS4). For either candidate
and every fixed complete head/projection tuple, choose each selected

Hold that choice fixed throughout the entire source domain.

This order is needed because a minimum of convex functions need not
be convex. For the fixed choices actually used here:

* Each finite LP coefficient and each selected forward increment
  is affine in q=(q5,q15). The LP has fixed enlarged caps and budgets,
  so its optimum is a maximum of affine functions and is convex.
* Each raw cylinder operator is a maximum of affine functions of q.
  The mixed-intersection corrections are maxima of affine increment
  differences with positive fixed factors1/675 or1/2025. The fourth
  step uses the maximum of2*(mid-low) and(high-low), also affine
  before its maximum. Each fixed selected option is therefore convex.
* Nonnegative coefficients preserve these assertions. The bridge's
  load convexity holds because wbar>=wface>=2/5>6/35. Thus all
  selected differences and intersection payments have the required
  nonnegative signs throughout the domain.
