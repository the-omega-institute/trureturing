## Two exact discounted potentials cover every changing path

For one prime p, let n_j be the number of earlier labels assigned to cell
or slot j, and let the next label have index j. Its contribution to the
complete distinct-pair block BR+binom(R,2) is bounded by

    p^-depth*(A_j+c_j*n_j).                         (RP4)

For2BR+R^2, the diagonal and both orders of each pair instead give

    p^-depth*(2A_j+c_j+2c_j*n_j).                    (RP5)

Write either reward as a_j+d_j*n_j, put r=1/p and define

    V_j(n)=(a_j+d_j*n_j)/(1-r)+d_j*r/(1-r)^2,
    V(n)=max_j V_j(n).

For a chosen k, direct affine identities give

    a_k+d_k*n_k+r*V_k(n+e_k)=V_k(n),
    a_k+d_k*n_k<=(1-r)*V_k(n).

For j!=k the component V_j is unchanged by the count update. Thus the
second inequality bounds the instantaneous reward by(1-r)V(n), while
the first handles the changed component. Taking the maximum gives

    a_k+d_k*n_k+r*V(n+e_k)<=V(n).                   (RP6)

Iterating(RP6) bounds every sequence of independently changing cells or
slots. The remaining discounted potential is O(Nr^N), tending to zero.
Multiply by the initial depth weight3^-3 or5^-2 to obtain

    pair3<=max_c(A3_c/18+c3_c/36),
    pair5<=max_s(A5_s/20+c5_s/80),

    square3<=max_c(A3_c+c3_c)/9,
    square5<=max_s(A5_s/10+3c5_s/40).                (RP7)

The helper checks all constant and count coefficients in(RP6), including
the nonnegative unchanged-component slack, for both objectives and each
cell/slot. These are affine identities valid for every count; they are
not a finite-depth approximation to an arbitrary path.
