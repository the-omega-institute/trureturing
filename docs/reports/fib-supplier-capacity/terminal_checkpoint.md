# Fixed TM58 terminal behavior and checkpoint capacity

These mathematical experiment files support the ordinary all-cap proofs in
[TM61.3–7](../../develop/theory/RECURSIVE_RELATIONAL_OBSERVATION_TRANSPORT_MEMORY_COMPLETION.md#TM61-T3).
The source, authentic supplier formula, original literal target and consumer
are exactly TM58. The checkpoint follows its Size_H single-leaf refusal and
precedes target output. It is not a new source or action interface.

Run with Python 3.9 or newer, using only the standard library:

```sh
python3 -I -S -B /path/to/terminal_checkpoint.py
```

The default compares independently recomputed finite arithmetic with the
retained [terminal_checkpoint.json](terminal_checkpoint.json). `--write`
regenerates that data; `--certificate PATH` selects another result path.
The exact rational Clifford multiplication, leaf products and matrices are
imported from the existing [h15_joint_cover.py](h15_joint_cover.py), not copied
into another arithmetic implementation. No solver or network is needed.

The finite load domain is every h = 2 through 128, all its positive unit
composition parameters, and all four cap residues, hence H = 8 through 515.
Every hidden tag-0 composition is retained before distinct literal original
targets are counted. The data give each authentic supplier symbol's AA and
other target counts, total target counts, and all genuine prefix omissions.
Every target's rank is checked against the corresponding inverse dictionary.
These calculations check the paper predictions K0 = h + floor(h/2) - L - 1
and K1 = K0 at residue zero, otherwise h + floor(h/2) - 2L, with
L = ceil(h/4). The all-H theorem rests on TM61's counting and matching
lower/upper proofs, not this finite window.

The actual tree domain is every positive composition at h = 2 through 20,
all four residues, and two literal bracket choices for the Atomic360 word:
left associated and recursively balanced. There are 1,330 composition
parameters and 10,640 full tree executions. Each constructs the actual
ordered-tree candidates, preserves brackets under substitution, checks the
three initial unit products, performs the real whole-candidate guards and
Size_H filling, and checks the final read product using exact rational
matrices. It checks right and left positive refusals, rho refusal, the
original literal target decoder, acceptance equality, and the original
simultaneous call/depth bounds. It does not emit the target before the
mathematical checkpoint. All other words and brackets are covered by the
paper's Atomic360/TM30/TM47 lifting, rather than claimed finite enumeration.

Two explicit actual source pairs refute recovery of the original target
from terminal behavior and authentic i alone. At H = 61, (z,w) = (9,16)
and (10,16) both have i = 1 and terminal E = A but distinct original tag-0
targets. At H = 60, the actual AA point (5,6) and RR point (9,16) have i = 1
and terminal E = 1 but distinct original targets. At H = 21, i = 2 and
(z,w) = (2,3), the prefix is genuinely omitted; the actual OA terminal
product is A. Inserting the unperformed residue-one prefix would instead
give B. These are source-contract falsifiers, not failures of the original
TM58 decoder with its full acquired record.

At H = 60 through 63, the four symbol loads are 17, 12, 14, 13;
their (AA, other) counts are (3,14), (5,7), (7,7), (9,4). Thus the checkpoint
capacities are 17 without a new Read and 17,14,14,14 with one new Read.
The supplier alphabet value four is reused from TM60.5; this experiment
does not reprove its global lower bound.

The checkpoint capacity assumes TM61.2's complete accessible-summary
contract: public H and authentic i remain available, and every other
history-dependent accessible control field, output, advice, pointer and
archive content is counted jointly. There is no free old target emission.
Common decoder initialization and restricted access to old records are
explicit conditional interfaces. The experiment does not implement a
physical archive deletion, install that access restriction, or establish
the paid bridge to an already installed controller. One subsequent Read
is one actual source call; retained archives, source identity, permissions,
contexts, guards, encoding, installation, local computation and output
retain their costs. Alphabet savings are not full controller state,
peak paid memory, global supplier optimality or physical-device evidence.

The source-specific deductions are repo-derived. Generic fiber recovery,
finite ranking and state identification are reused, with their conditions,
from the sources cited in TM61. These finite files establish neither an
all-H enumeration nor Lean/kernel certification, literature priority or
physical realization. The experiment is project-authored mathematical
content under the repository's [license](../../../LICENSE).
