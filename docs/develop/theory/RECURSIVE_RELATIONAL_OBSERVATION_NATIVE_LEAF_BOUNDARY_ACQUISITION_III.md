Each executed call consumes its whole reply, End, local acknowledgement and Return; retained state is synchronized before the next settled cut.

## 76. The binary Read obstruction survives original adaptive growth

**Lemma 76.1 (grading through retained-cell inclusions).** For each current native material tree $s$, define the diagonal sign matrix

$$
D_s e_p=(-1)^{|p|}e_p,
\tag{76.1}
$$

where $|p|$ is its original-root material depth. Then $D_sA_s=-A_sD_s$. If $E$ is the fresh-zero inclusion for any original successful growth, then $D_{s'}E=ED_s$.

Freeze a finite acquisition path, including its actual addresses, Add operands and growth choices. Let $H$ be its homogeneous map from the entry field on $u$ to the present field on $s$, and let $T$ be its number of acquisition Ticks. Then

$$
D_s H=(-1)^T H D_u.
\tag{76.2}
$$

At a Read of a current terminal $f$, the homogeneous reply to an entry monomial $A_u^ke_e$ vanishes unless

$$
k\equiv |f|-|e|-T\pmod 2.
\tag{76.3}
$$

**Proof.** Every material edge changes depth by one, proving anticommutation entry by entry. Both growth templates preserve old depths and extend old values by zero, proving the inclusion identity. The homogeneous map initially is the identity. Add contributes only a fixed affine offset. Hold, Read and identity refusals preserve its homogeneous part. Growth composes it with $E$ and preserves (76.2). Tick composes it with the current adjacency and reverses its sign. Induction proves (76.2) along the whole frozen path, including its intermediate Read cuts.

Also $D_u A_u^k e_e=(-1)^{k+|e|}A_u^ke_e$. Applying (76.2), the vector $HA_u^ke_e$ has sign $(-1)^{T+k+|e|}$ under $D_s$. Its coordinate at $f$ is therefore zero unless that sign equals $(-1)^{|f|}$. This gives (76.3). All statements concern actual retained coordinates; no identification of the two different adjacency operators across growth is assumed. $\square$

**Theorem 76.2 (two effective parities on every length-two transcript).** Fix any earned branching $u\in\mathcal D$, its authentic complete map/access, and the physically established whole-zero origin followed by Add$(e,1)$; Hold at its first terminal $e$. $$

$$

On every terminating transcript for a word of length two, successful scalar Reads must include both values of the effective parity $|f|-|e|-T$ in (76.3). In particular the worst-case late Read requirement is at least two, even with adaptive original growth.

**Proof.** The actual encoded field is

$$
x_w=F_w(A_u)e_e,\qquad
F_w(z)=z^m+\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}d_i z^{m-1-i}.
\tag{76.4}
$$

This follows by induction from the actual Tick/Add successors. For length two it is $A_u^2e_e+d_0A_ue_e+d_1e_e$. Take one terminating path at such an entry. If it has no effective-even Read, replace $d_1$ by $1-d_1$. The entry difference is $\pm e_e$. If it has no effective-odd Read, instead replace $d_0$; the difference is $\pm A_ue_e$.

In either case freeze the original path. Lemma 76.1 makes the reply difference zero at every one of its Reads. Inductively the alternative entry actually follows this same path: before the first Read all nonnumerical replies and initial receiver data agree; at each Read its complete literal rational agrees; identical receiver state then selects the same next command, operand, address, growth, complete framing and stopping decision. Growth guards and bindings agree because the structural path agrees. Common Adds cancel from homogeneous differences. Thus the final reported word is identical on two different admitted binary words, contradicting exactness on both. Each missing parity gives this contradiction, proving the claim. The argument proves necessity of both parities, not sufficiency of arbitrary parity-diverse samples. $\square$

**Theorem 79.2 (complete two-omission defect).** For every tree of Definition 79.1,

\tag{79.4}

After extension to $\mathbb R$, the restriction of symmetric $A$ to this invariant subspace is symmetric, and therefore has an orthonormal eigenbasis. Root each component off the $a$-to-$b_*$ path toward its path attachment. Peel it from the farthest vertices inward. Vertices with no children in this rooting are original leaves, hence measured, and their values are zero. At such a zero leaf the eigen-equation forces its sole parent value to zero. Inductively, when all children of a component vertex are zero, that vertex is already zero if it has a child, because a processed child's equation forced it; if it has no child it was a measured terminal. Its own equation then forces its parent to zero. This proves all off-path coordinates are zero and finally forces the attached path coordinate $h_{v_j}$ to zero for each $j\in\mathcal J$.

The remaining equations are exactly the unit-adjacency eigen-equations on the $(d+1)$-vertex path, with zeros at those attachment positions. Conversely, any path eigenvector with these zeros extends by zero off the path to a full-tree eigenvector. At an off-path root its equation sees the zero attachment; other off-path equations are zero; the path equations are unchanged. This proves both directions of the reduction, including all attachment equations.

The path has $m_*-1$ vertices. Its eigenvectors and eigenvalues are

$$
\psi_h(v_j)=\sin\bigl((j+1)h\pi/m_*\bigr),\qquad
\lambda_h=2\cos(h\pi/m_*),\qquad 1\le h<m_*.
\tag{79.5}
$$

$$
m_*\mid (j+1)h\quad\text{for every }j\in\mathcal J.
\tag{79.6}
$$

The rational matrix (79.4) has the same rank over $\mathbb Q$ and $\mathbb R$: the existence of a nonzero minor determines rank over either field. Its rational nullity is thus also $g-1$.
