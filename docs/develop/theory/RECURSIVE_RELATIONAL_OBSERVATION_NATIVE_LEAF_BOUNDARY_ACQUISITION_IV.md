## 103. A persistent sector behind the designated fresh sensors

**Definition 103.1 (one source and the control task).** Retain the entire
installation of Definition 75.1, Convention 75.2 and Definition 87.1:
the actual rational field, unit symmetric adjacency, native persistent
material, authentic original root and orientation, exhaustive raw table,

finite backing. In particular Tick returns only ACK, Add and Read use
actual terminal bindings, and Hold prevents unrequested evolution.
Absent, branch and wrong-type identities, RangeRefusal, BadFrame
(including the Reset tag), proper incomplete pending frames and disabled
missing-right dispatch keep their distinct meanings. Every call consumes
its whole output, End, acknowledgement and Return. There is no Reset,
clone, interior port, competing writer, field-dependent timing, capacity,
allocation-name or provider-advice channel. Physical conformance, genuine
zero origin, exact arithmetic, rights and actual record isolation remain
**ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED** installation premises, rather than acquired facts
about hardware.

Fix an earned branching $u\in\mathcal D$ with $a$ alpha leaves, $c$ beta
leaves, $b=a+c\ge3$ and $N=2b+c-1$ material cells. Choose $k\ge1$
pairwise disjoint logical cherries whose children $p_i,q_i$ are both
beta, with $p_i$ the left child. Their addresses and every selector below
are computed from this earned tree and translated through the genuine
table; an address in a formula creates no new address-Read service.
For every $\theta\in\mathbb Q^k$ prepare, on this specimen, the old field

$$
x_\theta=\sum_{i=1}^k\theta_i(e_{p_i}-e_{q_i}).
\tag{103.1}
$$

Section 104 gives two separately paid preparations. Before attachment,
all accessible parameter-dependent producer inputs, operands, predictors,
unused packet suffixes, reply copies and advice are cleared or isolated;
their work and capacities remain in the account. The receiver has the
family promise and field-independent earned context, rather than $\theta$.

Perform exactly Definition 89.1's growth, in a fixed original-leaf order,
before any grown-graph Tick: $P_p;S_p$ at alpha and $S_p;S_{pL}$ at beta.
Write $E$ for old-cell inclusion, $B$ for the resulting adjacency and $C$
for restriction to the one designated sensor per original leaf. The
alpha sensor is $pR$; the beta sensor is $pLR$. Thus

$$
M=5a+7c-1,\qquad s=2a+3c,\qquad
O=\begin{bmatrix}C\\CB\\\vdots\\CB^{M-1}\end{bmatrix}.
\tag{103.2}
$$

Here $s$ counts all current terminals, whereas $C$ has $b$ rows. At a
beta the old inherited label really ends at $pLLL$, and $pR,pLR$ have
their distinct fresh labels. All future original rights survive. Only
the assay and feedback in §105 restrict their numerical calls to the
designated sensors. Full future service is still the entire original
menu, not that sensing policy. No revocation or changed future task is
hidden in the restriction.

**Lemma 103.2 (the local sector on the actual material).** In the grown
coordinates put

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_i&=e_{p_i}-e_{q_i},&b_i&=e_{p_iL}-e_{q_iL},\\
c_i&=e_{p_iLL}-e_{q_iLL},&d_i&=e_{p_iLLL}-e_{q_iLLL},\\
e_i&=e_{p_iR}-e_{q_iR},&f_i&=e_{p_iLR}-e_{q_iLR},\\
h_i&=(a_i-c_i)/2,&H\theta&=\sum_i\theta_i h_i.
\end{aligned}
\tag{103.3}
$$

These letters with subscripts denote vectors, not the leaf counts.
For every chosen cherry,

$$
\begin{gathered}
Ba_i=b_i+e_i,\quad Bb_i=a_i+c_i+f_i,\quad Bc_i=b_i+d_i,\\
Bd_i=c_i,\quad Be_i=a_i,\quad Bf_i=b_i,\\
Bh_i=(e_i-d_i)/2,\quad B^2h_i=h_i,\quad Ch_i=CBh_i=0,\\
B^2a_i=2a_i+c_i+f_i,\quad B^2f_i=a_i+c_i+f_i,\quad
B^4a_i=6a_i+5c_i+4f_i.
\end{gathered}
\tag{103.4}
$$

Consequently $H$ is injective, $B^2H=H$, and $CB^jH=0$ for every
integer $j\ge0$. Every current terminal coordinate of $H\theta$ is zero.

**Proof.** In either beta cone the edges, besides the old parent edge,
are $p-pL-pLL-pLLL$, $p-pR$ and $pL-pLR$. Contributions of $p_i,q_i$
to their common parent cancel. Applying the neighbor-sum update at
each of these six pairs gives the first six identities, with no exterior
contribution. Subtraction gives $Bh_i$ and one further update gives
$B^2h_i=h_i$. None of $a_i,c_i,e_i,d_i$ is supported at a designated
sensor; $a_i,c_i$ are supported only at current internal vertices.
This proves the observation and terminal assertions. Applying the same
six identities twice and four times gives the last line. Supports from
different cherries are disjoint, and $(H\theta)_{p_i}=\theta_i/2$.
Thus $H$ has rank $k$ and all the conclusions hold simultaneously for
independent rational parameters. $\square$

## 105. Acquired control reaches the invisible family

**Theorem 105.1 (performed acquisition and cancellation).** For every
source and every $\theta$ in Definition 103.1, the following actual
receiver schedule ends at $H\theta$:

1. After fresh-only growth, Hold; execute two Tick;Hold pairs. Read
   the $k$ genuine designated terminals $p_iLR$, in index order.
2. From those replies compute and actually Add $-11\theta_i/2$ at
   $p_iLR$ and $+11\theta_i/2$ at $q_iLR$, in that order for each $i$.
3. Execute two more Tick;Hold pairs. Actually Add $+3\theta_i/2$ at
   $p_iLR$ and $-3\theta_i/2$ at $q_iLR$, then Hold.

The scalar replies in step 1 are exactly $\theta_i$. The receiver
therefore earns every control operand from its own literal observations
and can install the full current predictor at epoch two and maintain it
through the remaining calls. Besides growth, the receiver vector is

$$
(\mathrm{Read},\mathrm{Add},\mathrm{Tick},\mathrm{Hold})
=(k,4k,4,6).
\tag{105.1}
$$

Zero parameters cause no skipped calls. The six Holds are the initial
Hold, four Tick-following Holds and the final Hold. Whole transactions
are completed between the displayed actions; no extra pre-Tick Hold
is inserted into this particular template.

The mixed-family alternative template is a distinct chronological
choice with the same bill and endpoint: after the epoch-two Reads and

Adds and Hold. Its cancellation segment has three Holds, as does step 3

More explicitly the displayed cancellation segment is
first Adds;Tick;Hold;Tick;Hold;final Adds;Hold, while the original one is
first Adds;Hold;Tick;Hold;Tick;final Adds;Hold. Hold acts identically on
the field, so (105.2) applies to either schedule. Their Time windows
retain their respective call positions; they are not identified as
the same literal history. Each full receiver schedule has six Holds.

**Proof.** The entrance after growth is $Ex_\theta=\sum_i\theta_i a_i$.
At epoch two, (103.4) gives the actual field
$\sum_i\theta_i(2a_i+c_i+f_i)$. Its $p_iLR$ value is $\theta_i$,
and all other sectors contribute zero there. This proves genuine
acquisition rather than an assumed response oracle. The first paired

Two real Ticks and the last paired Adds give

$$
\sum_i\theta_i\bigl[B^4a_i-(11/2)B^2f_i+(3/2)f_i\bigr]
=\sum_i\theta_i(a_i-c_i)/2=H\theta.
\tag{105.2}
$$

Every equality follows from the actual update and Lemma 103.2. The
epoch-two formula supplies the entire field from the acquired parameters.
Updating it under each actual Add, Tick and identity call initializes
and maintains Theorem 64.1's synchronized full-service invariant,
including the complete parser, guards and label lifecycle. $\square$

**Theorem 105.2 (persistent designated ambiguity and full-service

**Proof.** Lemma 103.2 proves dimension, invisibility and $B^2H=H$.

## 106. Exact late tasks, retained advice and adaptive growth

**Definition 106.1 (the complete late information contract).** Start a
late receiver at the settled $H\theta$ cut, with the same earned graph,
bindings, rights and known phase. Fix a known rational matrix
$R\in\mathbb Q^{r\times k}$ of rank $d$. The literal vector $s_0=R\theta$
is genuinely retained. The entire other accessible receiver state is
$\rho_\kappa(R\theta)$ for fixed field-independent context $\kappa$.
This includes code inputs, reference bytes, selectors, parser and
counters, prior controls, output/transport buffers, provider records,
advice and observable resource attributes. Because the literal vector
is included, equal $R\theta$ gives equal complete initial information.
The case $d=0$ is a genuinely cold entrance. It requires actual clearing
or isolation of the epoch-two samples, cancellation operands, full
predictor and every dependent copy. A receiver retaining any of them
does not meet that contract. Computation of $R\theta$, clearing work and
surviving or isolated capacities are charged.

An exact reporting receiver owes $\theta$, equivalently the initial
dark full-service class. An exact current-predictor receiver instead
owes a self-contained predictor at its own actual final graph and field,
correct for every common sufficiently backed finite original continuation.
It need not report $\theta$. It may not defer numerical acquisition until
a later query, consult another dependent archive, or obtain its prediction
by rereading the plant. Actual subsequent use of the retained prediction
and genuine source rights is a separate operation. These are precisely
the current-prediction meanings of Definition 61.1, restricted here to
the attained family. Require pointwise exact finite termination on every
$\theta\in\mathbb Q^k$. Only successful scalar Reads carry numerical
source information; all other complete outcomes have their original
field-independent content.

**Theorem 106.2 (reporting under arbitrary original growth).** Every
terminating path of an exact reporting receiver in Definition 106.1
uses at least $k-d$ new successful scalar Reads. It may adaptively use
the entire original menu, including native growth and arbitrary rational
Adds. The same bound holds when it also owes a current predictor.

**Proof.** Fix its realized finite path at $\theta$ and freeze the chosen
frames, operands, addresses, edits and stopping decisions. Every current
field is affine in the initial parameter: growth is a persistent
zero-inclusion, Tick is a known linear adjacency and frozen Adds are
common translations. If there are $m$ successful Reads, their replies
are $A_*\theta+c_*$ for an $m$-row rational matrix $A_*$. If $d+m<k$,
choose nonzero rational $\eta\in\ker R\cap\ker A_*$. The alternative
$\theta+\eta$ starts with identical complete receiver data. Induction
on complete calls gives the same path: each scalar agrees, all other
replies, raw moves, framing, rights and guard outcomes agree, and identical
local states choose identical next requests and stop. Pending finite bytes
and disabled calls do not add rows. Its owed initial class differs by
Theorem 105.2, so the common report cannot be exact on both inputs.
This is the affine transcript supplier of §§49,61 applied to this newly
attained image, rather than a dimension bound on arbitrary advice. $\square$

**Corollary 106.3 (the original fixed-graph current task).** If growth is
excluded during the late experiment, an exact current-predictor receiver
also needs $k-d$ successful scalar Reads on every path, without an
initial-parameter report. In particular the original cold fixed-graph
bound is $k$.

**Proof.** On the indistinguishable pair in the preceding proof, after
$t$ actual Ticks the final field difference is $B^tH\eta$; common Adds
cancel. This is nonzero because $B^2H=H$ and $H$ is injective. If $t$
is odd a $p_iR$ Read separates an unequal coordinate immediately; if
$t$ is even a further Tick;Hold and that Read separates it. Identical
self-contained predictors cannot predict both complete services. $\square$

**Theorem 106.4 (adaptive-growth no-report current task).** The same
$k-d$ pathwise bound holds for the exact current-predictor task even
when arbitrary adaptive original growth is permitted during acquisition.
No report of the entry parameter is required.

**Proof.** Use the same finite-path rows and $\eta$ as in Theorem 106.2.
It remains to show that the final difference survives the frozen path;
Theorem 106.2 alone does not establish this.

Initially $H\eta=B(BH\eta)$ lies in $\operatorname{im}B$ and is nonzero.
Group all actual growths before the next Tick into their composite
old-cell inclusion $E_1$, and write $B_1$ for the then-current symmetric
adjacency. Native persistence gives $E_1^{\mathsf T}B_1E_1=B$. Lemma
60.1 therefore makes $B_1E_1$ injective on $\operatorname{im}B$.
After this Tick the nonzero difference is in $\operatorname{im}B_1$.
Repeat between each successive pair of Ticks: if the preceding difference
is in the preceding adjacency image, compression and that same lemma
make the next growth-then-Tick map injective on it. Common Add operands
cancel in differences throughout; all other calls leave differences
unchanged. A final block of growth with no Tick is an injective inclusion.
If there is no Tick anywhere, the entire difference is just the composite
inclusion of $H\eta$, also nonzero.

Thus every frozen finite path is injective on $H\mathbb Q^k$. This is
the image-wise application of the existing growth-survival proof in
Theorem 60.2, stronger here than using only its ambient rank inequality.
Both indistinguishable executions end at the same final material tree
with a nonzero rational field difference. All-terminal forcing on that
actual final tree supplies a finite common terminal/Tick continuation
separating it, by Convention 75.2. Equal complete final receiver states
cannot be correct self-contained predictors for both. The contradiction
proves $m\ge k-d$, without a deferred learning step or an entry report.
Symmetry, old-old compression and the original no-Reset interface are
load-bearing; no such conclusion is asserted for a different erasing
growth or nonsymmetric update. $\square$

**Proposition 106.5 (the smaller designated-only task).** On the fixed
grown graph, restrict every Read and Add to the designated sensors,
with Tick, Hold and field-independent identity outcomes. For equal
retained advice $R\theta$, all parameters have identical entire
transcripts under every common adaptive experiment of this restricted
menu. If $d<k$, this menu cannot recover the missing parameters or

**Proof.** Differences remain $B^tH\eta$ for $\eta\in\ker R$; $C$ kills
them at every $t$. Equal replies inductively force equal operands and
requests. A nonzero $\eta$ exists when $d<k$, and Theorem 105.2 still
separates its full original service. Growth or nondesignated Reads
would change this restricted experiment; their impossibility is not
claimed. $\square$

All these rank statements concern arbitrary rational parameters and
the complete *linear* advice contract. They make no $k$-rational-slot
minimum for nonlinear archives. For an optional field-independent random
tape, the deterministic bounds apply to each tape correct and terminating
on every input. If exactness and termination are only almost sure for
each rational $\theta$, intersect those probability-one events over the
countable set $\mathbb Q^k$, as in Corollary 61.3; the bounds then hold
almost surely for every fixed input. No bounded-error claim follows.

executed word; having executed it does not imply that its values survived
in callable memory.

**Lemma 112.1 (equivalence on one compatible stopped branch).** Fix a
finite word $w$ and two rational parameters $\theta,\theta'$ satisfying
$R\theta=R\theta'$ and $A_w\theta=A_w\theta'$. The two subprobability
laws restricted to execution of $w$ and stopping there, represented by
the seed and its finitely many noisy replies, are mutually absolutely
continuous. If that branch has positive probability under one parameter,
it has positive probability under the other.

**Proof.** There are countably many finite words: all syntax, rational
operands, addresses and finite framing are countable. Real noisy outputs
are coordinates of a word's branch, not extra discrete letters. Suppose
$w$ contains $m$ successful noisy calls. On coordinates
$(\omega,y_1,\ldots,y_m)$, reconstruct successive histories by inserting
the frozen commands, their exact replies and their structural outputs.
Equality of the advice and of all the exact rows implies equality of these
inserted exact replies, including every prefix. The same policy therefore
defines the same measurable selection-and-stopping region $E_w$ for both
parameters. At a common history the scale is the same, although the
current terminal means can differ.

Relative to seed law $\lambda$ times Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb R^m$,
the restricted measure has density

$$
\mathbf1_{E_w}(\omega,y)
\prod_{j=1}^m
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\,\sigma(h_j)}
\exp\left(-\frac{(y_j-\mu_{\theta,j})^2}{2\sigma(h_j)^2}\right).
\tag{112.3}
$$

This follows by iterating the conditional observation kernels and the
causal selection indicators; Tonelli applies to their nonnegative
products. Every factor is finite and strictly positive on $E_w$ under
both parameters. The empty product is one when $m=0$. Thus both restricted
measures have exactly the same null sets, proving the assertion. Complete
exact replies, reconstruction of controller state and the final report are
measurable functions of these same coordinates. $\square$

The lemma asserts neither equivalence of the entire mixed stopped laws
nor equivalence of infinite paths. An exact read on another branch can
have unequal Dirac returns. Infinitely many positive-noise samples can
also separate means; for instance averages of independent
$\mathcal N(\theta,1)$ samples converge to $\theta$. Neither operation is
licensed by a finite restricted-branch equivalence.

**Theorem 112.2 (mixed exact/noisy rank necessity).** Under Definitions
111.1--111.3, any common policy satisfying either the exact entrance-report
task or the exact current-native-predictor task obeys, for every rational
$\theta$,

$$
\mathbb P_\theta\{\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}V_W=k\}=1,
\qquad
\mathbb P_\theta\{m_0\ge k-d\}=1,
\tag{112.4}
$$

where $W$ is its stopped finite word and $m_0$ counts new successful
$\mathrm{Read}_0$ calls. Arbitrary finite positive-noise sampling,
adaptive rational control and original growth do not reduce this exact
read coordinate. In particular $d<k$ excludes an all-noisy solution of
either task.

**Proof for the entrance report.** If the deficient-rank stopping event
has positive probability for some $\theta$, countability and almost-sure
finite stopping give one positive-probability finite word $w$ on it.
Choose nonzero rational
$\eta\in\ker R\cap\ker A_w$ and put $\theta'=\theta+\eta$.
Lemma 112.1 applies to this single branch. Correctness under $\theta$
means its report is $\theta$ except on a null subset of that branch.
Equivalence transports that null subset to the branch under $\theta'$.
Correctness under $\theta'$ likewise requires report $\theta'$ there
almost everywhere. The branch has positive measure, so both requirements
cannot hold. This proves full rank almost surely. The inequality follows
from $\operatorname{rank}V_w\le d+m_0$. $\square$

**Proof for current native service.** The same branch argument needs a
separate bridge: different entrance parameters must still give different
complete current services. This is exactly the persistence mechanism of
Theorem 106.4, reused here, with the following parameter correspondence.
Initially the difference $z=H\eta$ is nonzero and lies in
$\operatorname{im}B$ because $B^2H=H$. Group consecutive growths before
a Tick into an old-cell inclusion $E$, with old adjacency $A$ and new
symmetric adjacency $A'$. Native persistence gives
$E^{\mathsf T}A'E=A$. If $z=Av$ and $A'Ez=0$, compression implies
$Az=0$, and symmetry gives

$$
\|z\|^2=(Av)^{\mathsf T}z=v^{\mathsf T}Az=0.
\tag{112.5}
$$

Thus $A'E$ is injective on $\operatorname{im}A$. After each Tick the
surviving difference is again in the new adjacency image. Common Adds
cancel, both read types are nondestructive, and final growth without a
Tick is an injective inclusion. Induction proves $D_W\eta\ne0$ at the
same final tree on this branch, including words with no Tick.

For that actual final tree, the all-terminal forcing supplier in
Convention 75.2 has full controllability row rank. If $J$ includes its
terminal coordinates and its adjacency is $A$, then for some finite $I$

$$
K=[J,AJ,\ldots,A^I J],\qquad KQ=I_{\rm cells}.
\tag{112.6}
$$

Consequently $K^{\mathsf T}D_W\eta\ne0$. Symmetry identifies these
entries with terminal observations after finitely many Ticks. An actual
common finite Tick/Hold/$\mathrm{Read}_0$ continuation therefore
separates the two fields, with adequate backing and genuine grants as
required by the task. This is separation of complete original services,
not merely of a chosen estimand. Full-service correctness is a measurable
condition: finite rational query/frame words are countable, and equality
of their prescribed finite outputs and successors is a countable family
of measurable conditions on the predictor. On the common branch the same
retained predictor cannot be correct for both separated services.
Lemma 112.1 transports the two correctness null sets as before, giving
the contradiction and (112.4). $\square$

The bridge uses symmetric persistent growth, original terminal access and
