# Semantic aggregation of the actual flow: a uniform private-label bound for the common-model risk frontier

**Mathematical status.** This volume presents one ordinary mathematical construction and its proofs. It has no Lean kernel verification. The source and frontier comparisons identified as suppliers are used at their immutable versions; the new statements concern semantic approximation of stationary regular tables.

## 1. The unchanged source and the remaining state quantifier

**Scope 1.1.** The object of study is one finite observer on the original common-depth stopped source. The result below does not choose between exact common conf/conf attainment, unattained zero excess, and a positive unrestricted gap. It proves that the regular stationary risk frontier can be approximated using an explicit number of private returned labels depending only on the requested accuracy. The replacement preserves both acquired-flow equations and has exact individual same-update generation. This supplies an effective bounded-shape comparison that is absent from an acquisition-horizon bound alone.

**Definition 1.2 (source, operations and complete targets).** Fix $m=2,d=1,\ell=2,n=4$. Install a finite or countable probability prior $\mu$ on positive integers with $\mu(1),\mu(2)>0$. Before the first actual Read draw one K. Conditional on that same K=k, all paid seed and payload letters are independent, with α probability

$$
r_k=F_{k+1}/F_{k+3},\qquad r_1=1/3,\qquad r_2=2/5,\qquad r_k\in[3/8,5/13]\quad(k\ge3).
$$

Retain the complete original finite control $C_0$ of [PAID] (Definitions 1.1–1.3). Equal seed pairs are paid rejections; αβ and βα accept the two original seeds. At payload phase p, α completes marker 0 and β suspends. At suspension, α returns to p and β completes marker 1. The first three completions advance the original marker tree. The third record is fully written before it is latched. Fourth completion enables only its original matching Stop; delivery is unique, and pending and delivered cuts have no Read. Both seeds, every marker triple, all finite rejection words and all finite payload-return histories remain in the domain. Bare fields, selectors, records B,Q⁺,Z, parser, permissions, completion and delivery are not replaced by a phase name.

Write $H_p$ and $H_\beta$ for all positive finite fourth-segment histories in the respective phases. $H_3$ consists of third-latch histories before any fourth-segment Read and remains a proper designated subdomain, not a replacement for $H_p$. Put $a_r=r(1-r)$ and

$$
w_{j,0}=(\beta\alpha)^j\alpha,\qquad w_{j,1}=(\beta\alpha)^j\beta\beta\quad(j\ge0).
$$

The p carrier consists of these completed words and its infinite noncompletion word. The suspended carrier consists of β, the words $\alpha w_{j,b}$, and its infinite noncompletion word. The supplied fixed-parameter probabilities are

$$
P_{p,r}(w_{j,0})=r a_r^j,\qquad P_{p,r}(w_{j,1})=(1-r)^2a_r^j,
$$

$$
P_{\beta,r}(\beta)=1-r,\quad P_{\beta,r}(\alpha w_{j,0})=r^2a_r^j,\quad P_{\beta,r}(\alpha w_{j,1})=r(1-r)^2a_r^j.
$$

Infinite noncompletion has actual mass zero but remains in the carrier. The supplied measurable bijection $I_c$ retains every future letter and inserts its original control, record, permission, completion and Stop events on the current complete record fiber c. It preserves TV and commutes with conditioning and deleting one original operation block [FLOW] (Definition 2.4). At the same acquired history h the actual target is

$$
T_h^\mu=(I_{C_0(h)})_*\sum_k\nu_h(k)P_{s,r_k},\qquad
\nu_h(k)=\frac{\mu(k)r_k^{A(h)}(1-r_k)^{B(h)}}{\sum_i\mu(i)r_i^{A(h)}(1-r_i)^{B(h)}}.
$$

Both counts include every acquired Read, including rejected pairs and partial parses. These posterior rows are analysis quantities, not observer inputs. There is no source reset, fresh depth, future-event conditioning, extra observation, clock or post-Stop operation.

**Definition 1.3 (risks and defect).** An observer has a fixed finite COMPLETE carrier, source-independent initialization and fixed time-homogeneous source-independent acquired-letter updates. Its decoder reads one actual configuration. At an original returned operation cut, write $P_x(z,z')$ for the installed update on the acquired legal operation block $x$, and $q_z(x)$ for its synthetic probability. Every individual decoded full law obeys

$$
D_z([x]E)=q_z(x)\sum_{z'}P_x(z,z')D_{z'}(E)
$$

for every residual event $E$. The notation $[x]E$ includes the original derived control and record events. This identity is the exact per-configuration same-update generation obligation on the whole original domain. Its successive cylinder probabilities define the law of the finite installed generator; a marginalized identity alone would not imply it. Synthetic generation makes no actual source call. COMPLETE includes the original control and records, installed program and table representation, selectors, workspace, addresses, output control and all persistent randomness. Abstract real tables are finite mathematical stochastic rules, not effective exact-real samplers.

For the actual conditional configuration row $\rho_h$ and individual decoded laws $D_z$, let

$$
Q_h=\sum_z\rho_h(z)D_z,\qquad
e_{\rm law}(h)=TV(Q_h,T_h^\mu),\qquad
e_{\rm conf}(h)=\sum_z\rho_h(z)TV(D_z,T_h^\mu).
$$

TV is the supremum over events, hence half the ℓ¹ distance on the fourth-tail carrier. Write $R_{j,s}=\sup_{h\in H_s}e_j(h)$. Actual-history-average risk is a different quantity. The supplied separate minima are

$$
\rho_p=1116529/22781250,\qquad \rho_\beta=239/6750.
$$

For each legal next operation block $x$, put $q_h(x)=Q_h([x])$. When $q_h(x)>0$, condition on that block and delete it using the original residual map, and define

$$
\delta(h,x)=\operatorname{TV}(\operatorname{res}_xQ_h,Q_{hx}).
$$

When $q_h(x)=0$, set $\delta(h,x)=0$ without defining a conditional law. The actual successor and its risk remain in the domain. Let $\Delta_4$ be the supremum over all positive actual fourth-segment histories and their legal next Reads, and $\Delta_{\rm all}$ the supremum from before the first Read through all original returned Read/Stop cuts. Sampler-internal steps grant no new original observation or query cut. PendingStop has its deterministic zero-defect successor, and the delivered empty supremum is zero. Only this marginalized coherence is allowed to be approximate.

**Definition 1.4 (stationary regular table).** A table has finite nonempty p and suspended label sets X,Y, probability rows π,τ, row-stochastic kernels B:X→Y and A:Y→X, and emission vectors u,v satisfying

$$
\pi B=\tau,\qquad \tau A=\pi,\qquad 1/3\le u_x,v_y\le2/5.
$$

All entries of $\pi,\tau,B,A$ lie in $[0,1]$; the indicated row sums are one, and zeros and ones are allowed. The interval $[1/3,2/5]$ restricts only the emissions $u,v$. Before the third latch retain only the original control and synthesize fair letters. In the original update that completes the third record, write and latch that record before source-independently sampling an X label with row π. Use B after an acquired p-β and A after an acquired suspended-α. Completing letters clear the private label and execute the original completion and Stop rule. Every seed and record fiber uses the same private table while retaining its own complete records.

Let $Q_x,W_y$ be the raw complete laws generated by this table. If $\beta_*$ and $\alpha_*$ prefix the indicated letter to a continuation law, their defining equations are

$$
Q_x=u_x\delta_\alpha+(1-u_x)\beta_*\sum_yB_{xy}W_y,
$$

$$
W_y=(1-v_y)\delta_\beta+v_y\alpha_*\sum_xA_{yx}Q_x.
$$

These are equations for the installed generator, not a posterior filter. At every actual fourth-segment p history the label row is exactly π, and at every suspended history it is τ. Conditioning on the actual source word does not multiply these rows by synthetic letter probabilities.

A generated full return has probability at most

$$
\lambda=(2/3)(2/5)=4/15.
$$

Consequently every p law has residual mass at most $\lambda^L$ after L returns, and every suspended law has residual mass at most $(2/5)\lambda^L$ after its initial α and L returns. All these generated laws complete almost surely without conditioning on completion.

**Supplier 1.5 (what is reused).** Denote the class of Definition 1.4 by $S_\mu$ and the entire finite-observer class by $M_\mu$. PAID supplies the all-positive-history identity

$$
R_{{\rm conf},p}=\sup_{k:\mu(k)>0}\sum_x\pi_xTV(Q_x,P_{p,r_k}),
$$

with its suspended and law-order counterparts, for the same table, prior and full-history domains. For every observer class $\mathfrak A$ under consideration, use $J(\mathfrak A)=\inf_{M\in\mathfrak A}\Phi(M)$. Define

$$
\Phi(M)=\max\{R_{{\rm conf},p}(M)-\rho_p,\ R_{{\rm conf},\beta}(M)-\rho_\beta\},\qquad J_S=\inf_{M\in S_\mu}\Phi(M).
$$

The supplied regularization and risk-only stationary reduction imply

$$
J(M_\mu)\le J_S\le(61/11)J(M_\mu).
$$

They also equate finite common endpoint existence between these classes, without preserving a fixed resource budget. Stationary folding can change the defect and is not used as a hard-defect-preserving reduction. These are [PAID] (Corollary 2.3) and [CLIP] (Theorem 4.2), not new conclusions here.

## 2. Uniform approximation with one regenerated mechanism

**Theorem 2.1 (uniform private-label bound).** Fix $0<\eta\le1$ and define

$$
L=\min\{l\ge1:\lambda^l\le\eta/2\},\qquad
m=\left\lceil\frac{2(L+1)}\eta\right\rceil,\qquad
N(\eta)=(m+1)^{2L+1}.
$$

Here $m$ is the quantization-grid integer, distinct from the fixed source parameter $m=2$ in Definition 1.2. For every table M of Definition 1.4 there is one stationary regular table $\widehat M$ with at most $N(\eta)$ private returned labels in each phase. There are maps f,g from the positive stationary supports of its old p and suspended labels to the new labels such that

$$
TV(Q_x,\widehat Q_{f(x)})\le d_p:=50\eta/11,\qquad
TV(W_y,\widehat W_{g(y)})\le d_\beta:=42\eta/11.
$$

Here $\widehat Q,\widehat W$ are the laws actually generated by the new emissions and the new acquired-letter kernels. The new actual rows are exactly the pushforwards $f_*\pi$ and $g_*\tau$ at every corresponding original history. Therefore, on the same installed prior and every same actual history,

$$
|e_j(h;\widehat M)-e_j(h;M)|\le d_s\quad(j\in\{{\rm law},{\rm conf}\},\ h\in H_s).
$$

All four risk coordinates consequently satisfy the same bounds, and

$$
|\Delta_{\rm all}(\widehat M)-\Delta_{\rm all}(M)|\le302\eta/11.
$$

Both models retain exact individual same-update generation on the full original operation domain. No source operation or record is changed. The comparison does not assert that f,g intertwine the old label process pathwise, that the old table is strongly lumpable, or that a prescribed hard defect bound is retained.

Taking $\eta=11\varepsilon/302$, for rational $0<\varepsilon\le1$, gives a computable private-label bound approximating all five coordinates within ε, independent of the original number of labels, its smallest positive stationary mass, its kernel entries, the prior masses and the acquired-history length. This is a bound on returned labels; total COMPLETE representation and sampler costs are addressed in §10.

The following sections give the construction and proof.

## 3. A finite partition of complete generated laws

**Lemma 3.1 (bounded complete-law diameter).** The positive stationary p labels can be partitioned into at most $(m+1)^{2L}$ blocks and the suspended labels into at most $(m+1)^{2L+1}$ blocks, each of full-law TV diameter at most η.

Proof. Delete zero stationary labels first. If $\pi_x>0$ and $B_{xy}>0$ then $\tau_y>0$; similarly $\tau_y>0$ and $A_{yx}>0$ imply $\pi_x>0$. Thus the retained sets are closed under both acquired and synthetic noncompleting transitions. Deleted labels have zero conditional probability at every relevant actual history.

At p use the 2L coordinates $Q_x(w_{j,b})$, $0\le j<L$. Assign to x the integer vector obtained by applying $\lfloor mq\rfloor$ to these coordinates. Each integer belongs to $\{0,\ldots,m\}$. Labels in one block differ by at most 1/m in each distinguished coordinate. The total mass of each law outside those coordinates is at most $\lambda^L$. Including all remaining finite words and the infinite word, their full TV distance is at most

$$
L/m+\lambda^L\le\eta.
$$

At suspension include the single β coordinate and the 2L coordinates $W_y(\alpha w_{j,b})$. The same argument gives a bound

$$
(2L+1)/(2m)+(2/5)\lambda^L\le\eta.
$$

The claimed block counts follow. The partitions use finite word probabilities of the installed model, not source observations. For a rational input table these probabilities and the partition are computable by finite rational arithmetic. For opaque real parameters this is a mathematical partition, not a promised effective compiler. ∎

## 4. Aggregating both actual flows exactly

Let a range over nonempty p blocks and b over nonempty suspended blocks. Put

$$
\widehat\pi_a=\sum_{x\in a}\pi_x,\qquad
\widehat\tau_b=\sum_{y\in b}\tau_y,
$$

and define the conditional weights $\theta_x^a=\pi_x/\widehat\pi_a$ and $\varphi_y^b=\tau_y/\widehat\tau_b$ on their respective blocks. All denominators are positive. Install

$$
\widehat B_{ab}=\sum_{x\in a}\theta_x^a\sum_{y\in b}B_{xy},\qquad
\widehat A_{ba}=\sum_{y\in b}\varphi_y^b\sum_{x\in a}A_{yx},
$$

$$
\widehat u_a=\sum_{x\in a}\theta_x^a u_x,\qquad
\widehat v_b=\sum_{y\in b}\varphi_y^b v_y.
$$

The new kernels are row-stochastic and their emissions remain in [1/3,2/5]. Direct summation gives

$$
(\widehat\pi\widehat B)_b=\sum_{y\in b}(\pi B)_y=\widehat\tau_b,
\qquad \widehat\tau\widehat A=\widehat\pi.
$$

Equivalently, each new edge flow is the sum of the old flows across its pair of blocks. This stationary-weighted aggregation is a mature construction; [MS] (§3, discussion following Proposition 9) gives the corresponding one-chain identity. Here it is used on both alternating acquired flows.

Install π̂ at the original third latch, not at a new cut. Induction over every acquired noncompletion letter gives the new conditional rows π̂ and τ̂ at every fourth-segment history. These are exactly the block pushforwards of the old rows. This remains true after arbitrarily many actual returns, without an error accumulating with their number. No lower bound on individual positive block masses enters this identity.

For analysis define reference averaged laws

$$
\mathcal Q_a=\sum_{x\in a}\theta_x^aQ_x,\qquad
\mathcal W_b=\sum_{y\in b}\varphi_y^bW_y.
$$

Every member of a block is at TV distance at most η from its reference average. In particular $|u_x-\widehat u_a|\le\eta$ and $|v_y-\widehat v_b|\le\eta$, since u and 1−v are complete-word probabilities.

The decoder of the new machine is not assigned these averages. It is defined by the installed generator using û,v̂,B̂,Â. The difference between these two objects is essential.

## 5. The covariance defect and exact regeneration

For a family of suspended laws W′ define

$$
\mathcal T_p(W')_a=\widehat u_a\delta_\alpha+(1-\widehat u_a)\beta_*\sum_b\widehat B_{ab}W'_b.
$$

For a family of p laws Q′ define

$$
\mathcal T_\beta(Q')_b=(1-\widehat v_b)\delta_\beta+\widehat v_b\alpha_*\sum_a\widehat A_{ba}Q'_a.
$$

**Lemma 5.1 (reference residual and regenerated-law bound).** The reference averages satisfy

$$
TV(\mathcal Q_a,\mathcal T_p(\mathcal W)_a)\le5\eta/3,
\qquad
TV(\mathcal W_b,\mathcal T_\beta(\mathcal Q)_b)\le7\eta/5.
$$

The actual regenerated laws satisfy

$$
\max_aTV(\widehat Q_a,\mathcal Q_a)\le39\eta/11,
\qquad
\max_bTV(\widehat W_b,\mathcal W_b)\le31\eta/11.
$$

Proof. Average the old p generator equation within a block. Replace every successor $W_y$ by its block average $\mathcal W_{g(y)}$. The resulting TV error is at most

$$
\sum_{x\in a}\theta_x^a(1-u_x)\sum_yB_{xy}\eta\le2\eta/3.
$$

Write $S_x=\sum_y B_{xy}\mathcal W_{g(y)}$, a probability law on the suspended carrier. The remaining difference between the averaged noncompletion measure and the new factored emission/update measure is

$$
\sum_{x\in a}\theta_x^a(\widehat u_a-u_x)S_x.
$$

Its total mass is zero. For each event, the absolute value of this signed measure is at most $\sum_x\theta_x^a|\widehat u_a-u_x|\le\eta$. The completing α masses already agree. This proves the p residual bound.

At suspension, replacing $Q_x$ by $\mathcal Q_{f(x)}$ costs at most 2η/5. Factoring the averaged v_y from the acquired A row produces a signed covariance measure bounded on every event by $\sum_y\varphi_y^b|v_y-\widehat v_b|\le\eta$. The completing β masses agree. This proves the suspended residual bound.

Let $E_p$ and $E_\beta$ be the two maximum regenerated-to-reference distances in the statement. The new generated laws obey $\widehat Q=\mathcal T_p(\widehat W)$, $\widehat W=\mathcal T_\beta(\widehat Q)$. Prefixing preserves TV, and mixing contracts it. Hence

$$
E_p\le5\eta/3+(2/3)E_\beta,\qquad
E_\beta\le7\eta/5+(2/5)E_p.
$$

Substitution and $1-4/15=11/15$ give $E_p\le39\eta/11$ and then $E_\beta\le31\eta/11$. These compare full probability laws. Their existence and normalization follow from the finite regular generators and the uniform return-survival bound, so no infinite table or renormalization on completion has been introduced.

Finally, an old member is within η of its block reference. The triangle inequality gives 50η/11 and 42η/11 for its distance to the new generated law. ∎

The covariance term is the missing obligation in simply declaring an averaged complete law to be the decoder of an averaged acquired kernel. The proof does not assume that block-transition rows of two trace-close states are close.

## 6. Same-history risks, marginalized defect and original control

**Proof of Theorem 2.1.** Lemma 3.1 gives the label bound. Section 4 gives exact acquired-flow balance. Lemma 5.1 gives the per-configuration complete-law comparisons.

At one actual p history, compare the new and old forecasts by coupling an old label x with its block f(x). This is an analysis coupling with marginals π and π̂. Under the same current transcript map $I_c$, their complete laws are at TV distance at most $d_p$. For configuration risk, the reverse triangle inequality yields

$$
\left|\sum_x\pi_xTV(Q_x,T)-\sum_a\widehat\pi_aTV(\widehat Q_a,T)\right|\le d_p
$$

for every probability target T on that same raw carrier. For law risk, first mix the coupled laws and apply the reverse triangle inequality; the same bound follows. Use $\tau,g,d_\beta$ at suspension. Taking T to be the actual posterior mixture gives the claimed inequalities on every positive acquired history, including every prior in Definition 1.2. Taking suprema gives all four risk comparisons. Thus each of the four combinations of p-order and suspended-order is controlled by this same replacement, not by joining different phase constructions.

For the defect, recall the elementary conditioning estimate: if $\operatorname{TV}(P,Q)\le t$ and E has positive probability under both laws, with $Q(E)\ge q>0$, then

$$
TV(P(\cdot\mid E),Q(\cdot\mid E))\le2t/q.
$$

Indeed, for any event A, subtract the two normalized A∩E probabilities by first changing the numerator with denominator Q(E), then changing the denominator. Each term is bounded by t/Q(E). Deleting the operation block cannot increase TV.

The two p marginal forecasts differ by at most $d_p$ and their β probabilities are at least 3/5. Their two actual successor forecasts differ by at most $d_\beta$. Therefore the p-β defects differ by at most $10d_p/3+d_\beta$. At suspension the α probabilities are at least 1/3, giving $6d_\beta+d_p$. Consequently

$$
|\Delta_4(\widehat M)-\Delta_4(M)|\le
\max\{626\eta/33,302\eta/11\}=302\eta/11.
$$

All these conditioning events are positive. The declared zero-event convention remains the convention for the full class and is not used to remove an actual successor.

For the source/control bridge, execute the new private update as a product with the unchanged original update on $C_0$. In the third-completion update, finish the original record write and latch before sampling the private label. Every subsequent acquired letter produces exactly its original event block. The label never alters a menu, record, completion decision, matching Stop or delivery. Induction over original operations therefore preserves the whole original execution and its current record fiber on every same source history, including both seeds and every marker triple.

From any retained configuration define the full decoder to be the law of this installed generator. Its cylinder identity is precisely the synthetic letter probability times the same acquired-letter transition followed by the successor decoder. Before the latch, both models use fair synthetic letters and deterministic original control. The latch sampler is part of that same update in actual and synthetic execution. Thus their pre-latch marginalized defects are zero even though their pre-latch complete laws can differ. Completing fourth letters leave the deterministic original pendingStop residual; pending and delivered defects are zero. Hence $\Delta_{\rm all}=\Delta_4$ for both tables, proving the stated full-domain defect comparison.

The old and new generators both complete almost surely, but their legal infinite noncompletion outcomes remain in the full carriers with zero mass. Nothing conditions on a future completion. This completes the proof. ∎

**Corollary 6.1 (exact semantic blocks and zero-defect boundary).** Suppose finite blocks have full-law diameter zero instead of η. The construction in §4 then preserves every corresponding individual decoded law, all four same-history risks, and the marginalized defect exactly. It does not require equality of old transition rows into blocks.

Proof. Both covariance residuals and both successor-replacement errors in Lemma 5.1 vanish. Its two inequalities force $E_p=E_\beta=0$. The risk and defect proof then has $d_p=d_\beta=0$. ∎

For positive η, an old zero defect gives only a new defect at most 302η/11. Sending η to zero gives an approximation family, not exact zero defect at each member. No fixed hard defect budget follows from this limiting statement.

## 7. A bounded-shape certificate for the all-shape infimum

For a positive integer N, let $J_N$ be the minimum of Φ over stationary regular tables with N p labels and N suspended labels. Zero-weight labels are allowed. Every smaller table embeds in this shape: add zero-weight labels and send no positive-weight row to them. Complete each added zero-weight row by any stochastic row, with regular emissions. The balance identities are unaffected, since those rows carry zero stationary mass. These added labels do not change any relevant law or risk. Fixed-shape existence of the minimum is supplied by [CLIP] (Proposition 6.3).

For clarity, the level-$t$ partition at p distinguishes $w_{j,0},w_{j,1}$ for $0\le j<t$ and merges all other outcomes, including infinite noncompletion, into one residual cell. At suspension it additionally distinguishes $\beta$ and uses $\alpha w_{j,b}$. Write $z_x^{p,t}$ and $z_y^{\beta,t}$ for the generated probability vectors, and $p_{s,r}^{t}$ for the corresponding true fixed-parameter vectors. The true residual masses are $a_r^t$ and $r a_r^t$. Define

$$
F_{p,t}(M,r)=\frac12\sum_x\pi_x\|z_x^{p,t}-p_{p,r}^{t}\|_1,
\qquad
F_{\beta,t}(M,r)=\frac12\sum_y\tau_y\|z_y^{\beta,t}-p_{\beta,r}^{t}\|_1.
$$

For a finite parameter menu $S_t$, the finite objective and its minimum on the padded stationary shape are

$$
\phi_t(M)=\max\left\{
\max_{r\in S_t}F_{p,t}(M,r)-\rho_p,
\max_{r\in S_t}F_{\beta,t}(M,r)-\rho_\beta
\right\},\qquad
j_{N,t}=\min_{M\text{ on the }N\text{-by-}N\text{ shape}}\phi_t(M).
$$

For a finite exact supported depth menu $S$, use $S_t=\{r_k:k\in S\}$ at every level. For infinite support use the menu in Theorem 7.1. These finite partitions and optimization variables are analysis data, never runtime posterior or law vectors.

**Theorem 7.1 (uniform comparison of unbounded and bounded shapes).** With $N=N(\eta)$,

$$
0\le J_N-J_S\le5\eta.
$$

For a prior with finite exact supported depth menu S, let $j_{N,t}$ be the finite polynomial minimum obtained by comparing each supported target on the level-t complete-word partition of [CLIP] (Definition 5.1). Then

$$
j_{N,t}-5\eta\le J_S\le j_{N,t}+(6/25)^t.
$$

For infinite support, use

$$
S_t=\{r_k:k\le t,\ \mu(k)>0\}\cup\{r_*\},\qquad r_*=(3-\sqrt5)/2.
$$

The corresponding inequality is

$$
j_{N,t}-5\eta\le J_S\le j_{N,t}+E_t,
\qquad E_t=(6/25)^t+(25/171)(25/64)^t.
$$

The limit parameter is a target-closure point, not a new actual depth.

Proof. Class inclusion gives $J_S\le J_N$. Given any finite stationary table, Theorem 2.1 supplies a table in the padded N-by-N shape whose two configuration risks increase by at most $50\eta/11<5\eta$. Apply this to a sequence approaching $J_S$ and then take the bounded-shape minimum. This proves $J_N\le J_S+5\eta$ without assuming that $J_S$ is attained.

The supplied future-partition estimate [CLIP] (Theorem 5.2) gives the following shape-uniform bound. On a residual cell with predicted mass $a$ and target mass $c$, refinement can add at most $\min(a,c)$ to TV: its half-$\ell^1$ contribution is at most $[(a+c)-|a-c|]/2$. Thus full minus partition conf-risk is at most the true residual mass, after configuration averaging. Here $a_r\le6/25$, so the p error is at most $(6/25)^t$ and the suspended error at most $(2/5)(6/25)^t$. Taking the phase maximum and minimum yields

$$
0\le J_N-j_{N,t}\le(6/25)^t
$$

for the finite menu. For infinite support, [CLIP] (Proposition 5.3) adds at most $(25/171)(25/64)^t$, uniformly over all table parameters; this bounds the smaller suspended support error as well. Combining the two comparisons proves the stated intervals. ∎

The lower bound is genuinely over all finite regular shapes. Its proof is not an enumeration of the N-by-N shape promoted to a universal conclusion; Theorem 2.1 supplies the missing universal comparison and its explicit 5η correction.

**Corollary 7.2 (finite algebraic certificate for unrestricted positivity).** Under either support presentation above,

$$
J(M_\mu)>0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\eta\in\mathbb Q\cap(0,1],\ \exists t\ge2:
 j_{N(\eta),t}>5\eta.
$$

A certified rational lower bound $j_{N(\eta),t}$≥c>5η implies

$$
J(M_\mu)\ge(11/61)(c-5\eta)>0.
$$

Proof. The displayed certificate gives $J_S>0$ by Theorem 7.1, and the supplied comparison gives the quantitative full-class bound. Conversely, if $J(M_\mu)>0$ then $J_S>0$. Choose η with $5\eta<J_S/3$ and then t with $E_t<J_S/3$. Since $j_{N,t}\ge J_N-E_t\ge J_S-E_t$, the strict inequality follows. ∎

This corollary supplies a route to an arbitrary-finite-kernel obstruction. It does not provide a value c satisfying its hypothesis for the original frontier.

## 8. Effective all-shape risk values under explicit source data

**Definition 8.1 (effective support presentation).** The input either lists a finite exact support S containing 1 and 2, or declares that the support is infinite and provides a total membership procedure for positive integers. The declaration of infinitude is part of the input contract; no procedure for deciding finite versus infinite support is asserted. Positive masses themselves may be opaque real numbers. They need not be evaluated to compute $J_S$, because Supplier 1.5 expresses stationary regular full-history risks as suprema over supported parameters. This support contract is exact: an opaque prior without either presentation does not provide an algorithmic input. In the infinite case, an infinite subset of positive integers is unbounded. Therefore its supported $r_k$ approach $r_*$ along a subsequence, and the continuous target-risk functions include the limiting value in their supremum. This justifies adding $r_*$ to the finite menu without adding a new actual depth.

**Theorem 8.2 (computability of the regular all-shape infimum).** Given Definition 8.1 and rational $\varepsilon>0$, one can compute rational numbers $l\le J_S\le u$ with $u-l\le\varepsilon$. There is also a procedure that terminates with a positive rational lower bound for $J(M_\mu)$ exactly when $J(M_\mu)>0$. The theorem does not assert that $J(M_\mu)$ itself is computable from the multiplicative comparison.

Proof. It suffices to treat $\varepsilon\le1$. Choose positive rational η with $5\eta\le\varepsilon/3$, compute $N(\eta)$, and choose $t\ge2$ with $E_t\le\varepsilon/3$. The finite menu uses $E_t=(6/25)^t$. Each finite word probability in the padded shape is a polynomial in its emission and kernel entries. With $U=\operatorname{diag}(1-u)$, $V=\operatorname{diag}(v)$, $H=UBVA$, $f=u$ and $g=UB(1-v)$, the p coordinates are $H^jf,H^jg$ and $H^t\mathbf1$; the suspended coordinates are $1-v$, $VAH^jf$, $VAH^jg$ and $VAH^t\mathbf1$. Configuration risk is a finite weighted sum of absolute differences, and the objective is a finite maximum. Row normalization, nonnegativity, the two flow identities and the emission bounds are finite polynomial constraints.

For any rational threshold q, feasibility of a table with finite objective at most q is therefore a first-order formula over the reals with rational coefficients, after auxiliary variables encode absolute values. Keep the threshold as a free variable during elimination, then substitute its rational value. In the infinite-support presentation, the only additional parameter is $r_*$; it can instead be encoded by $z^2-3z+1=0$ and $1/3<z<2/5$. Quantifier elimination for real closed fields [QE] (Theorem 1) decides these finite formulas. No genericity, strictly positive kernel entries, or absence of degenerate rows is required.

The finite objective lies in [−1,1]. Rational bisection using these exact feasibility decisions computes $a\le j_{N,t}\le b$ with $b-a\le\varepsilon/3$. Theorem 7.1 yields

$$
\max\{0,a-5\eta\}\le J_S\le b+E_t.
$$

The interval width is at most ε. Every step terminates under the stated support presentation. The arithmetic and sign tests are external symbolic computation, not observer services.

Run this interval algorithm at successively decreasing positive rational accuracies. Stop when its lower endpoint is positive and output 11/61 times that endpoint. This is sound by Supplier 1.5. If $J(M_\mu)>0$, then $J_S>0$ and sufficiently narrow intervals have a positive lower endpoint, so the procedure terminates. If $J(M_\mu)=0$, then $J_S=0$ and no such output occurs. ∎

**Corollary 8.3 (computable PAID finite-query infima).** Fix a finite rational prior and finite nonempty families of actual positive p and suspended fourth-segment histories. At each history use a specified finite complete-word partition, distinguishing finitely many completed words and merging all other outcomes into a residual cell, as in the PAID witness families. Let K be the infimum over all finite stationary regular tables of the maximum of their designated p-conf and suspended-conf finite-history excesses. Then K is a computable real number. In particular each PAID $K_n$ is computable for this source presentation.

Proof. At each selected actual word, Bayes normalization gives a rational target vector. The new and old actual label rows are the block-pushforward rows, and Theorem 2.1 bounds their full-law configuration-risk difference by less than 5η. Pushing both laws to any selected finite partition cannot increase the per-configuration law distance used in that proof. Thus, if $k_N$ is the minimum of this finite-query objective on the padded $N(\eta)$-by-$N(\eta)$ shape,

$$
0\le k_N-K\le5\eta.
$$

Each distinguished completed-word probability is a finite sum of finite products of table entries, and the residual probability is one minus their sum. Thus the objective is a finite polynomial expression with absolute values and rational target coefficients. Quantifier elimination and rational bisection approximate $k_N$ to any requested accuracy. Combining that interval with the preceding inequality computes K. K can be negative; no nonnegative truncation is applied here. This statement supplies no computability claim for an arbitrary measurable finite partition lacking effective polynomial cell probabilities. ∎

Theorem 8.2 is not a horizon for arbitrary original transient reports. The observer has already been restricted to the fresh-latch stationary regular class, and the quantity being computed is an infimum over replacement tables. The whole-program acquisition obstructions discussed in [PAID] (§7) and [CF] (§13) have different original-report quantifiers.

**Corollary 8.4 (effective rational near-optimizers, not exact optimizers).** Under Definition 8.1, for every rational $\varepsilon>0$ a finite rational stationary regular table with $\Phi\le J_S+\varepsilon$ can be produced by a terminating mathematical algorithm. Its original operations and individual generation remain exact. No uniformly bounded description or sampler budget independent of ε is asserted.

Proof. Choose η with $5\eta\le\varepsilon/8$ and compute $N=N(\eta)$. Compute an upper bound U for $J_S$ with $U-J_S\le\varepsilon/8$. Choose t with $E_t\le\varepsilon/8$.

Rational stationary regular tables are dense for the risk coordinates within each fixed positive-support shape. Apply [FLOW] (§4) to the alternating edge flows $\pi_xB_{xy}$ and $\tau_yA_{yx}$. Conservation at each vertex and total mass one on each layer permit a finite directed-cycle decomposition. Approximate its positive coefficients rationally and divide by the common rational layer total. Vertex outflows and their normalized edge rows then converge to the original rows and kernels while preserving both balances exactly.
