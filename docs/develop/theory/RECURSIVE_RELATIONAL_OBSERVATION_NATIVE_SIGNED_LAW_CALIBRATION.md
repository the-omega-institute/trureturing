# Recursive Relational Observation: Native Signed Law Calibration

## 1. A paid known design with signed old links

This is a continuation of [Native Leaf Boundary Acquisition, §§3 and
8.3][NLBA] and [Native Leaf Boundary Acquisition II, §§17, 64–65 and
77–79][NLBAII]. It treats a commissioned signed law on a known original
design. Positive unknown-design acquisition, acquisition of the design
itself, and physical certification of the installation are different
tasks. The new conclusions concern the signs removed by real native
births, the signs surviving the entire native menu, and the inherited
splits indispensable for acquiring that menu without later learning.

**Definition 1.1 (original design and material).** A logical term is
$u::=\alpha\mid\beta\mid\langle u,u\rangle$, with ordered addresses in
$\{L,R\}^*$. Retain the original reachable domain $\mathcal D$: no
logical alpha address ends in $L$. The original operations are promotion
$P_p:\alpha\mapsto\beta$ and split
$S_p:\beta\mapsto\langle\beta,\alpha\rangle$ at a logical leaf $p$.
Their actual guards retain their original absent, branch and wrong-type
distinctions. A beta marker is a material vertex, never a logical target.

Materialize alpha at $p$ by the single vertex $p$, beta by vertices
$p,pL$ and edge $p-pL$, and a pair by its branch vertex $p$, its two
child material trees, and edges to their roots. Every material address
is persistent. Fix any $u\in\mathcal D$ with $b\ge3$ logical leaves,
of which $c$ are beta. Let $V$ be its material vertices, $K$ its logical
branch vertices, $I$ its beta owners, and $B$ its material terminals.
Then

$$
 |K|=b-1,\qquad |I|=c,\qquad |B|=b,\qquad
 N=|V|=2b+c-1.
\tag{SC.1}
$$

Here $B$ comprises each alpha itself and each beta's marker. These are
exactly the degree-one vertices; the root is a branch since $b\ge3$.
The leftmost logical leaf ends in $L$ and hence is beta, so $1\le c\le b$
in this domain. Empty selected subsets below are allowed; a purported
$c=0,b\ge3$ reachable instance has empty domain. The results do not
replace the domain by arbitrary rooted trees or the one-leaf cases.

**Definition 1.2 (SCWLAB installation).** For every design in Definition
1.1, every symmetric rational matrix $T$ with arbitrary rational diagonal
and

$$
 T_{vw}\ne0\ \Longleftrightarrow\ v\sim w\quad(v\ne w),
\tag{SC.2}
$$

and every $x\in\mathbb Q^V$, commission one actual source with this law
and field. Edge signs and magnitudes, diagonals and field are unknown to
the receiver. Authentic $u$, root/orientation, the complete initial raw
label/address bijection $\lambda:\{1,\ldots,b\}\to B$, codecs and
current/fresh rights are paid supplied design/context. Their values
contain no advice about $T$ or $x$. The provider's coefficient and field
commissioning, reception, clearing or isolation of dependent preparation
records, source/reference backing and design transmission belong to the
same run's initial bill $\mathcal I(u,T,x)$. A known design is an input,
not an achievement of the numerical inverse. The family includes zero
fields, singular and indefinite laws, repeated eigenvalues and arbitrary
nonzero signed rational links.

At every settled cut the field commands are

$$
 \mathrm{Tick}:z\mapsto A z,\qquad
 \mathrm{Add}(j,q):z\mapsto z+q e_{\lambda(j)},\qquad
 \mathrm{Read}(j):z\mapsto z_{\lambda(j)},\qquad
 \mathrm{Hold}:z\mapsto z,
\tag{SC.3}
$$

where $A$ is the current law. Tick, Add and Hold return fixed ACKs;
Read returns its whole literal rational. Only requested Ticks evolve
the field. Hold prevents unsolicited evolution during finite local work.
There is no Reset, clone, interior read/actuator, coefficient port,
eigenvalue port, supplied response table or field-dependent timing,
allocation, work or capacity channel.

**Definition 1.3 (actual positive births and complete interface).** A
successful $P_p$ retains old $p$, adds zero $pL$, installs edge $p-pL$
with coefficient $+1$ and fresh diagonal zero, and moves the acted raw
label to $pL$. A successful $S_p$ retains owner $p$ and marker $a=pL$,
makes $p$ a branch and $a$ the left beta owner, and adds zero vertices
$d=pLL$ and $r=pR$ with edges $a-d,p-r$, both physically $+1$, and
fresh diagonals zero. All old coefficients and fields persist. The acted
raw label moves to $d$, the next label is appended at $r$, and every
other binding persists. Current/fresh grants follow this installed
lifecycle. Growth has no attached Tick. No decoder convention creates
these positive edges or grants.

Use the entire serial interface of [NLBAII, §§17 and 64][NLBAII]. Growth
returns its original complete Growth/refusal frame; raw range errors,
BadFrame, including a complete former Reset tag, and proper incomplete
frames keep their original distinct behavior. A proper prefix retains
its parser/pending bytes and causes no completed edit or completed
reply. Missing rights disable a call rather than inventing a numerical
reply. Every full input, whole output, End, local acknowledgement and
Return is consumed, and the corresponding retained update finished,
before the next settled call. Exclusive serialization, retained actual
references, adequate finite material/work/communication backing, exact
arithmetic, no competing writer/revocation/crash, commissioning
conformance, genuine Hold and zero/unit births are supplied premises.
As hardware assertions they are **ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED**. None is inferred
from the numerical data. Every continuation discussed is finite and
sufficiently backed; no infinite execution with fixed finite backing is
postulated.

**Definition 1.4 (the acquired task and equivalence).** Fix this common
design, binding, authority, codec and parser context. Two current sources
are service equivalent if every common finite complete input word has
the same legal domain and whole visible outputs, including refusals and
pending behavior. Adaptive words use the common prior outputs and,
when present, a fixed common field-independent random tape. Full
successor transport will be proved, not assumed as part of this test.

A startup acquisition is one common causal procedure, with no
coefficient/field-dependent initial advice, terminating pointwise on
every admitted $T,x$. At its advertised cutoff it returns a
self-contained model evaluating every subsequent menu reply and model
successor without further numerical learning from the source. A Read's
predicted value is determined before receiving that Read's actual value.
Ordinary continued actual calls can verify the predictor, but their
unknown numerical replies cannot repair an incomplete startup model.
A handle that forwards later paid learning assays is a different task.
Exactness is required on every admitted path; an all-path-exact randomized
procedure is interpreted at each fixed common tape. No positive-error
or finite-precision converse is implicit.

## 2. The exact sign fiber of a partially split source

**Definition 2.1 (partial birth experiment).** Let $J\subseteq I$. In a
fixed design-derived order actually split each original owner $p\in J$
once, with no Tick or Add. These logical leaves are prefix incomparable,
so every such split is enabled and does not change another selected
leaf. Let $A_J$, $E_J:\mathbb Q^V\to\mathbb Q^{V_J}$ and $C_J$ be
the resulting law, literal old-cell inclusion and current terminal
projection in the actual raw order. Put $B_J=C_J^{\mathsf T}$,
$n_J=N+2|J|$ and $k_J=b+|J|$. The terminal experimental data are

$$
 \mathcal F_J(T,x)=
 \left((C_JA_J^rB_J)_{r\ge0},
       (C_JA_J^rE_Jx)_{r\ge0}\right).
\tag{SC.4}
$$

This definition describes terminal impulse/free responses; it supplies
no response oracle. Section 4 earns a finite encoding on one actual
source. For a subset $S\subseteq V$, write $G_S$ for the diagonal sign
matrices $D$ with $d_v=1$ off $S$; they act on old pairs by
$(T,x)\mapsto(DTD,Dx)$.

**Lemma 2.2 (signed infection, with its exact data requirements).** On
a known finite material tree with $n$ vertices and all $k$ terminals
observed, nonzero signed rational links and arbitrary diagonal,
terminal moments through $2n-1$ and free returns through $n-1$ determine
the law and field exactly up to terminal-fixing diagonal signs.
Equality of these finite data is equivalent to such a sign relation;
it also implies equality of all the infinite data. No nonzero-field,
invertibility or simple-spectrum assumption is needed.

Proof. Reuse the moment pairing and tree-forcing argument of [NLBA,
Lemma 3.2 and Theorem 3.3][NLBA], retaining the sign left undetermined
by normalization. For completeness its signed inferential step is as
follows. For vector polynomials $p=\sum z^r p_r$, put
$\mathcal E_A(p)=\sum A^r Bp_r$. Moments compute

$$
 [p,q]=\sum_{r,s}p_r^{\mathsf T} C A^{r+s}Bq_s
       =\mathcal E_A(p)^{\mathsf T}\mathcal E_A(q),
 \qquad [p,zq]=\mathcal E_A(p)^{\mathsf T}A\mathcal E_A(q).
\tag{SC.5}
$$

Initialize $q_\ell=e_\ell$ at every terminal by constant polynomials.
Suppose the already represented orthonormal vectors are
$q_v=d_v e_v$, $d_v\in\{1,-1\}$. Choose the first represented $v$
having exactly one unrepresented neighbor $w$ in the known tree.
Subtract from $Aq_v$ its projections on all represented vectors,
including its diagonal projection. The realized residual is exactly
$d_v A_{wv}e_w$. Its squared norm, computed by (SC.5), is
$A_{wv}^2>0$. Divide its polynomial by the positive rational square
root $|A_{wv}|$ and obtain a represented $q_w=d_w e_w$.
That root is effectively obtainable: the numerator and denominator
of the reduced positive square are integer squares, whose positive
integer roots can be found and checked. This operation does not
assert that the old link was positive.

All leaves force a finite tree: if any unmarked vertices remain but
no force is possible, keep them and their marked neighbors, retaining
exactly the edges incident to an unmarked vertex. Every unmarked
vertex has degree at least two, since no leaf is unmarked; each retained
marked neighbor has at least two unmarked neighbors by the assumed
failure to force. The resulting nonempty finite forest would have
minimum degree at least two, a contradiction. Thus $n-k$ steps
represent every coordinate. After $s$ steps degrees are at most $s$;
the residual norm needs moments at most $2s+2$, final matrix pairings
at most $2(n-k)+1\le2n-1$, and free pairings at most
$n-k\le n-1$.

The computed matrix entries $[p_v,zp_w]$ are $(DAD)_{vw}$ and the
free pairings are $(Dz)_v$. All selections and positive roots are
deterministic functions of the same finite data and known addresses.
Two realizations with equal data therefore give the same reconstructed
pair, and composing their diagonal signs gives a terminal-fixing sign
conjugacy. Conversely $DB=B$, $CD=C$ and $D^2=1$ show that this
conjugacy preserves every moment and free return. This proves both
inverse directions, including equality of the infinite sequences.
$\square$

This is the mature infection normalization, rather than a new general
inverse-tree theorem. The positive-sign version is already in NLBA;
[Burgarth–Maruyama, §II, Lemma and equations (1)–(3)][BM] use its
known-topology residual and prescribed coupling sign with different
Hamiltonian/spectral ports. Here moments implement the pairings and
the unprescribed sign is explicitly retained.

**Theorem 2.3 (exact owner/branch fiber).** For any two admitted old
pairs on the same design,

$$
 \mathcal F_J(T,x)=\mathcal F_J(T',x')
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists D\in G_{K\cup(I\setminus J)}:
       T'=DTD,\quad x'=Dx.
\tag{SC.6}
$$

The same iff holds for the finite ranges in Lemma 2.2 with
$n=n_J$. Every fiber contains exactly
$2^{b-1+c-|J|}$ distinct old laws and pairs, even for $x=0$.

Proof. Lemma 2.2 gives a diagonal grown sign matrix $\widetilde D$
fixing every current terminal and relating the grown pairs. All fresh
vertices are terminals, hence have sign $1$. For every split $p$, the
known edge $p-pR$ is $+1$ in both grown laws, so its conjugacy equation
forces $d_p=1$. Likewise the known $pL-pLL$ edge forces $d_{pL}=1$.
Every unsplit original beta marker and every original alpha is still
a terminal, so those signs are $1$ too. Thus all original terminals,
and exactly the selected beta owners in addition, are fixed; among
old vertices only $K\cup(I\setminus J)$ can vary. Restricting the
grown law and free-field equation to old cells gives (SC.6).

Conversely extend any displayed old $D$ by $1$ at the fresh vertices.
Both endpoints of every new edge have sign $1$, so the physical $+1$
birth constraints persist; the grown pairs are conjugate and all data
agree. There are no additional alternatives. For distinctness, if
$DTD=T$, every nonzero edge gives $d_vd_w=1$. Connectedness makes
all signs equal, and a fixed terminal makes them all $1$. The action
is therefore free on the law itself, regardless of field. Counting
its $b-1+c-|J|$ independent signs proves the cardinality. This includes
$J=\varnothing$ and $J=I$. $\square$

**Proposition 2.4 (an acquired signed local reference).** After the
actual split at $p$, with fresh terminals $r=pR,d=pLL$,

$$
 (A_J^3)_{d,r}=T_{pL,p}.
\tag{SC.7}
$$

Proof. Their tree distance is three and their unique length-three
walk is $r,p,pL,d$. A diagonal stay or backtrack cannot traverse that
distance in three steps. The walk product is
$1\cdot T_{p,pL}\cdot1$. Thus this particular old signed link becomes
a terminal cross response in the actual native units. The scan in
Section 4 acquires its column after subtracting only previously earned
free/forced contributions. A normalization in a decoder without these
real $+1$ edges would not establish (SC.7) as a calibrated reference.
$\square$

## 3. Full future service and the pathwise inherited split price

**Theorem 3.1 (exact full-native-menu equivalence).** On the entrance
class of Section 1,

$$
 (T,x)\sim_{\rm service}(T',x')
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists D\in G_K:\quad (T',x')=(DTD,Dx).
\tag{SC.8}
$$

For the forwardly constructed equivalence, every complete adaptive
continuation has matching whole outputs and a sign transport of its
full law/field successor at every cut.

Proof. An original branch stays a branch forever. Neither $P$ nor $S$
can target it. Extend $D\in G_K$ by $1$ at every future birth and use
the same design, bindings, rights, codec and parser on both sources.
All current terminals have sign $1$, so terminal Add and Read
intertwine. Tick intertwines since
$(DAD)(Dz)=DAz$. Hold is identity. A promotion attaches at a
sign-$1$ alpha. A split attaches at a sign-$1$ beta owner and its
sign-$1$ marker. All new coefficients are consequently still $+1$;
zero diagonal/field births and old coefficient/field retention agree.
The raw move/append rules and grants also agree. Guards depend on
the common logical code; range, malformed and disabled behavior on
the common context; proper prefixes on the same parser bytes. None
depends on a hidden coefficient sign. This checks every operation
and identity/refusal branch of the complete interface. Inducting on
received prefixes and completed calls proves the stated successor
invariant. An adaptive next input is identical because its complete
past visible record is identical.

For necessity, perform the particular common continuation that splits
all original beta owners and then the finite free/pulse experiment
of Section 4. Equality of all future service gives equality of this
actual experiment's complete returns. Its deterministic subtractions
give equal data (SC.4) for $J=I$. Theorem 2.3 leaves precisely $G_K$.
The proof of the scan uses only Lemma 2.2 and the existing rational
recurrence; it does not assume (SC.8). Thus the implication is not
circular. $\square$

**Lemma 3.2 (birth anchors persist).** After all original beta owners
have been split, and after any further finite native growth, equality
of current terminal moment/free data is exactly a sign conjugacy
supported on the original $K$.

Proof. Consider the forest consisting only of edges installed by
post-commissioning positive births, with singleton components included.
Immediately after the first all-owner splits, each old alpha is a
terminal singleton; each original beta owner is connected to its new
right terminal; each old beta marker to its new left marker terminal.
Only old $K$ vertices are unanchored singletons. Every other component
meets a current terminal. A later promotion extends a component at a
terminal and moves that terminal one edge outward. A split extends it
at the beta owner and marker, with new terminal descendants. Each
affected component still has a terminal and every new vertex belongs
to such a component. No new birth edge touches $K$.

Apply Lemma 2.2 on the current known design. A conjugacy between two
laws satisfying the same known $+1$ birth edges has equal endpoint
signs along each such edge. Terminal sign $1$ therefore propagates
through every component except the singleton vertices in $K$.
Conversely the extension of $G_K$ preserves all these data as in
Theorem 3.1. The claim includes arbitrary current rational fields,
not just the initial free or pulse trajectory. $\square$

**Theorem 3.3 (every-path inherited-owner necessity).** Every exact
startup acquisition in Definition 1.4 must successfully split each
original beta owner before stopping, on every terminating path.
Consequently at least $c$ successful splits, $2c$ fresh cells and $c$
appended labels are necessary on each such path. These are structural
coordinates of this no-later-learning task, not total-resource or
Read/work/bit minima.

Proof. Fix a terminating path leaving an original beta $p$ unsplit.
Let $D_p$ flip only its owner coordinate, and compare the actual
entrance with $(D_pTD_p,D_px)$. This is another admitted commissioned
signed source with exactly the same supplied design/context. Until
$S_p$, that beta remains a logical leaf. Its marker is not a logical
target, no enabled descendant edit is possible, and edits elsewhere
cannot attach a new edge to $p$. Extend $D_p$ by $1$ on all births
elsewhere. Terminal inputs/outputs, Tick, Hold, all those growth
successors, guards, refusals and frames satisfy the same conjugacy
induction as above. Equal preceding outputs give equal choices,
operands, local computation and stop. Thus the entire stopped
receiver state, including every accessible retained record, is
identical on the two runs.

At the cutoff the inherited $p$ is still unsplit and its signed old
edge $p-pL$ is nonzero with opposite values on the two sources. A
later lawful $S_p$ followed by the finite terminal free/pulse scan
distinguishes their services: equal complete returns would imply
equal earned length-three moment in (SC.7), a contradiction. This
is one legal sequential experiment, with its actual free/pulse
offsets, rather than an assumption of resetting either field. Hence
one stopped autonomous evaluator cannot be correct for both current
services. Uniform exactness contradicts the hypothesized path.
Repeat for each original $p\in I$. Every successful inherited split
really allocates two cells and appends one label, proving all three
coordinates. Fixing a common tape proves the all-path-exact randomized
version. No statistical-error lower bound is asserted. $\square$

For a fixed old topology, the larger terminal-fixing sign quotient
already suffices, as [NLBA, Proposition 8.3][NLBA] establishes. Allowing
future learning, supplying authentic sign advice, prescribing old
positive signs, or adding interior reference ports changes a premise
of Theorem 3.3. In particular selecting an all-positive representative
of an uncalibrated signed old tree can fit its old terminal data and
still predict the wrong (SC.7) after a real split. The distinction is
observable future service, not a preferred notation for hidden cells.

## 4. One attained paid scan at its actual advanced endpoint

**Definition 4.1 (the performed schedule).** Actually Hold once. Split
all $c$ original beta owners in the fixed design order, with no Tick

$$
 M=N+2c=2b+3c-1,\quad k=b+c,\quad
 A=A_I,\quad E=E_I,\quad C=C_I,\quad B=C^{\mathsf T},
 \quad H=2M-1,\quad \tau_j=jH,\quad L=(k+1)H.
\tag{SC.9}
$$

Scan all $k$ raw terminals, in increasing raw-label order, at epochs
$0,\ldots,H$ of the unmodified grown field $Ex$. At epoch $\tau_j$,
after the preceding block's final scan, actually Add$(j,1)$; then
scan at relative epochs $0,\ldots,H$ for that pulse block. A Hold
is issued at every sampled epoch. Consecutive block boundaries retain
both scans/Holds, the first before and the second after the pulse.
One Tick separates consecutive epochs within a block. Earlier pulses
are never removed. Each call is serially completed. There is no
preliminary old-law numerical scan.

**Theorem 4.2 (attainment, complete acquisition and actual endpoint).**

$$

 r_A=\sum_{j=1}^k A^{L-\tau_j}Be_j.
\tag{SC.10}
$$

$$
 (S,P,\mathrm{Read},\mathrm{Add},\mathrm{Tick},\mathrm{Hold})
 =\bigl(c,0,2Mk(k+1),k,(k+1)(2M-1),1+2M(k+1)\bigr),
\tag{SC.11}
$$

$2c$ fresh source cells and $c$ appended labels, in addition to its
own paid installation/preparation and local computation/storage/traffic.

Proof. Apply the rational sequence and persistent-pulse arguments
of [NLBAII, Lemmas 77.2–77.3][NLBAII] with order cap $R=M$, current
law $A$, initial vector $Ex$ and $k$ terminal channels. Their statements
concern a rational matrix and an earned cap, not positivity or
invertibility: a monic annihilator of degree $M$ exists by
Cayley–Hamilton. A fitted monic order-$M$ recurrence matching $2M$
vector returns continues the genuine sequence because the product of
the fitted and genuine annihilators annihilates their difference,
whose first $2M$ values vanish. Thus the reuse applies also to zero
returns, singular laws and repeated spectra.

Explicitly the free block acquires $f_h=CA^hEx$ for $0\le h\le H$.
The actual $j$th pulse block returns

$$
 y_{j,h}=f_{jH+h}
   +\sum_{i<j}h_i((j-i)H+h)+h_j(h),\qquad
 h_i(t)=CA^tBe_i.
\tag{SC.12}
$$

Continue only the already earned free sequence and earlier pulse
columns and subtract them in this identity. It earns the next
$h_j(0),\ldots,h_j(H)$ before continuing that column. Induction on
$j$ obtains every required moment and free value on this same run.
Computed continuations are not further measurements. The genuine
$3$-step moment (SC.7) is among these earned impulse entries.

Lemma 2.2 and Theorem 2.3 with $J=I$ now return the branch-sign
class. An effective representative respecting every known birth

Linearity on the one field therefore gives exactly (SC.10).
The receiver can compute its representative by replaying the known

**Theorem 5.2 (current orbit, exact inverse directions and task kernel).**

3. Time recovers the entrance orbit $(T,x)/G_K$ and the actual current
   orbit by (SC.10). Conversely that entrance orbit determines all

   direction manufactures provenance or an old preparation journal.

The current claims remain valid at each settled cut after any finite
native continuation, using the then-current $n,k$, moments through
$2n-1$ and free germ through $n-1$ and the maintained birth provenance.
The claim about Time retains the fixed startup-window domain.

Proof. Boundary is invariant under $G_K$. Lemma 2.2 reconstructs a
terminal-fixing signed representative from its finite data, and
Lemma 3.2 forces its remaining sign freedom to be precisely $G_K$.
Conversely evaluate powers of any representative to produce exactly
the same Boundary. Thus, denoting this evaluation by $\beta$ and

## 6. Exact history scope and a singular same-source counterpair

**Proposition 6.1 (the restricted entrance archive criterion).** Fix
one acquired law representative independently of the field, the fixed
schedule and its $E,L,r_A$. Let an explicitly computed retained linear
archive be $D_{\rm ar}x$ in that representative. On this fixed chart,
current field plus archive recovers the entire entrance field, and
hence the mathematical startup Time, if and only if

$$
 \ker(A^LE)\cap\ker D_{\rm ar}=\{0\}.
\tag{SC.15}
$$

Without an archive the iff is injectivity of $A^LE$, not a claim that
every admitted law is invertible or loses history. The minimum rank
of a sufficient linear archive on this chart is
$\dim\ker(A^LE)$, and since $A$ is symmetric and $L\ge1$,

$$
 \ker(A^LE)=\{v\in\mathbb Q^V:Ev\in\ker A\}.
\tag{SC.16}
$$

Proof. Subtract the known $r_A$ in (SC.10). The stacked rational map
$x\mapsto(A^LEx,D_{\rm ar}x)$ is injective exactly when (SC.15)
holds; rational elimination gives its inverse on its actual image.
A nonzero common-kernel difference gives two different entrances
with the same displayed data, proving necessity. By Lemma 2.2 their
initial free responses differ, so their fixed measured Time records
do differ. An archive must be injective on $\ker(A^LE)$, giving the
rank lower bound. Choose a rational basis of this kernel, extend it
to the old space and retain its dual kernel coordinates to attain
the bound. This is a paid computed archive of already acquired data,
not free advice or a new source measurement. Symmetric real spectral
decomposition gives $\ker A^L=\ker A$, and restriction along the
rational injection $E$ proves (SC.16). Nonzero-minor ranks agree over
$\mathbb Q$ and $\mathbb R$.

This is the existing linear archive argument of [NLBAII, Proposition
77.5][NLBAII] restricted to the actual old-cell inclusion. An archive
comparison is made in a fixed law chart; under a different sign chart
its coordinate map must be transported too. It removes field-history
loss, not the law gauge of Proposition 5.4. $\square$

**Example 6.2 (failure with actual positive births).** Take the
reachable $u=\langle\langle\beta,\alpha\rangle,\alpha\rangle$,
with old edges
$\varepsilon-L,\varepsilon-R,L-LL,L-LR,LL-LLL$ all of weight $+1$.

$$

 \qquad \eta_v=0\text{ otherwise}.

$$

cancels the contribution $1$ from $R$; at $L$ the contributions from

same actual positive-length pulse schedule from entrances $0$ and

and current predictor class. Its initial free scans differ at the
unchanged terminals $R,LR$. The two actual runs have different Time.
This concretely rules out a current-to-entrance inverse without
(SC.15); the costs of those two alternative preparations are not
pooled into one run. Conversely invertible $A$ makes $A^LE$ injective,
but that is only a sufficient subcase. On this fixed passive startup

singular Tick can erase historical distinctions while exact current
prediction remains valid.
