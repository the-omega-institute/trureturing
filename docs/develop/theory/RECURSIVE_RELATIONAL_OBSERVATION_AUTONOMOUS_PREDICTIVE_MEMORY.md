# Recursive relational observation: autonomous predictive memory

## 1. Source, mathematical status and the closed consumer

**约定 1.1（ordinary reference input）。** This volume is ordinary mathematical reference input to trureturing. Its proofs are not Lean-kernel verification. The mathematical source is [Parameterized cross-product recovery](RECURSIVE_RELATIONAL_OBSERVATION_PARAMETERIZED_CROSS_PRODUCT_RECOVERY.md), abbreviated PCR. References to its numbered results mean their ordinary statements and proofs, with their original hypotheses; they do not turn a prose result into a compiled application. The new operational result concerns a finite interpreter serving successive identical requests. It does not assert physical acquisition, recovery of arbitrary source syntax, or a universal reconstruction of time or spacetime. The volume follows the new-volume, numbered-claim convention for generic-v1; additions and corrections belong after its final append anchor.

**定义 1.2（one actual history）。** A source is a nonempty finite ordered tree generated by leaves $\alpha,\beta$ and ordered nodes $\langle u,v\rangle$. One preparation $p=(a,b)$ in oriented $\mathbb R^3$ is fixed for the whole execution:

$$
E_p(\alpha)=a,\qquad E_p(\beta)=b,\qquad
E_p(\langle u,v\rangle)=E_p(u)\times E_p(v).
$$

The substitution preserves ordered nodes and satisfies

$$
\rho(\alpha)=\beta,\qquad \rho(\beta)=\langle\beta,\alpha\rangle.
$$

Write $x_j=E_p(\rho^jt)$, $q_j=\|x_j\|_2$, and
$\Delta=\|a\|^2\|b\|^2-(a\cdot b)^2$. The standing parameter domain is

$$
L,H>0,\qquad 0<d_0<L^4,\qquad
\|a\|,\|b\|\le L,\qquad \Delta\ge d_0,\qquad q_0,q_1,q_2\le H.
$$

The source actions remain Read of $x_0$, one destructive $\rho$, Read of $x_1$, one destructive $\rho$, Read of $x_2$, and Stop. The target is the entire vector sequence $x_3,x_4,\ldots$ in that same acquired frame, with unweighted Euclidean absolute error at most $E$ at every output. A proof may use rotated preparations or tree counts to establish a bound; these are not observer actions or input ports.

**定义 1.3（autonomous finite interpreter）。** After installation and acquisition closure, a deterministic interpreter accepts only the identical finite request NEXT with its fixed end marker. Its first successful request emits a finite vector approximating $x_3$, its second approximates $x_4$, and so on. There is no supplied future index, source-size bound, phase, clock, fuel, source code, preparation, name, oracle, or source-dependent hidden interpreter. Internal position and phase, when used, are ordinary changing machine data. Emitted history is a write-only external stream: it cannot be read back. Each finite request terminates; infinitely many requests are not required to have finite total time or finite total output.

Fix a conventional binary interpreter with finitely many length-framed integer registers, finite control and bit arithmetic. The complete space charge includes its fixed finite description, installation input, retained word, every live numerical register and scratch field, register lengths, input and tape positions, head and address data, loop counters, changing control, and the complete current output buffer. A source-dependent program or table is part of the charged word. No unbounded address or clock is hidden in finite control. Under the complete binary configuration convention, fewer than $2^{B+1}$ different configurations have descriptions of length at most $B$. Constants for the fixed representation convention are absorbed in space bounds.

Let $M_{\rm num}(N,E)$ be the minimum worst-case peak of this complete numerical account on actual histories with at most $N$ initial leaves, allowing source-aware finite-word selection and one interpreter shared across all sources, $N$ and accuracies. Mandatory external evidence is excluded only from this numerical account and is charged separately in §8. The original source domain remains unbounded: $N$ is a proof stratum, not an installed input. Source-aware selection establishes existence until a lawful acquisition contract is supplied.

## 2. Actual-source relations used by the interpreter

**引理 2.1（transport, projection and the three directions）。** Use $F_0=0,F_1=1,F_{n+2}=F_{n+1}+F_n$ and $\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$. For a nonzero unit label with actual initial counts $(m,n)$, put $r=\|b\|$ and $s=\sqrt\Delta$. The ordinary source algebra of PCR §§1–5,16 gives

$$
q_1=r^m s^n,\qquad q_2=r^n s^{m+n},\qquad
q_{j+2}=q_jq_{j+1}\quad(j\ge1).
$$

The future directions have period three. With $w=x_2\times x_1$,

$$
|x_0\cdot w|\ge q_2^2,\qquad
q_2\le q_0q_1,\qquad
x_3=\operatorname{sgn}(x_0\cdot w)\,w,
$$

where the sign of zero is zero. A zero unit label has $x_j=0$ for $j\ge1$, even when $x_0\ne0$. If $\Delta=0$, the unconditional algebra gives $x_j=0$ for $j\ge2$.

**证明。** The interpretation transport
$E_p(\rho t)=E_{(b,b\times a)}(t)$ holds at the leaves and is preserved by every ordered node. The transported pair is orthogonal. On an orthogonal pair, homogeneity supplies the two displayed norm monomials; cyclic permutation of its three oriented unit axes is a proper rotation of order three and commutes with cross products. This proves the recurrence and direction cycle by node induction, including all label signs.

To specify the actual projection premise, set
$A=\|a\|^2,B=\|b\|^2,C=a\cdot b$, $K_0=b\times a$,
$U=K_0\times a=Ca-Ab$ and $V=K_0\times b=Ba-Cb$.
The upper triangular cross table for $a,b,K_0,U,V$ is

$$
\begin{aligned}
a\times b&=-K_0,&a\times K_0&=-U,&a\times U&=AK_0,&a\times V&=CK_0,\\
b\times K_0&=-V,&b\times U&=CK_0,&b\times V&=BK_0,\\
K_0\times U&=-\Delta a,&K_0\times V&=-\Delta b,&U\times V&=-\Delta K_0 .
\end{aligned}
$$

These identities follow from the vector triple-product identity, with diagonal zero and reversed entries negated. Structural induction yields the descriptors used in PCR §16. In a nonzero unit-label branch the $C$ factors disappear and one can use signed positive monomials in $A,B,\Delta$ times $a,b,K_0,-U,V$. Their positive projections onto the correctly signed direction of $w$ are respectively
$s/r,r,s,s^2/r,rs$. Give $A,B,\Delta$ degrees $(2,0),(0,2),(2,2)$ and those five vectors degrees $(1,0),(0,1),(1,1),(2,1),(1,2)$. Each table entry preserves degree. If the descriptor has $A$ exponent $i$, its signed projection is
$(AB/\Delta)^i q_2/q_1\ge q_2/q_1$, since $AB\ge\Delta$. Multiplication by $\|w\|=q_1q_2$ gives the first inequality, and comparison of a projection with $q_0$ gives the second. The same signed table fixes the sign of $x_3$. Zero and dependent branches follow without division. These are properties of jointly realized source histories, not assumptions about arbitrary triples.

**引理 2.2（positive deficit and the proof-only leaf bound）。** Suppose $q_0,q_1,q_2\le1$. Put

$$
\delta_j=1-q_j^2\quad(j\ge1),\qquad
D=\delta_1+\delta_2,\qquad g(x,y)=x+y-xy.
$$

Then $q_2\le q_1\le1$, the future norms do not increase, and

$$
\delta_{j+2}=g(\delta_j,\delta_{j+1}),\qquad
D/2\le\delta_3\le D,\qquad \delta_4\ge\delta_3 .
$$

If $d_0>1$ and the actual initial tree has at most $N$ leaves, then

$$
D\ge\frac{c_0}{N},\qquad c_0=1-\frac1{d_0}>0.
$$

Consequently $D>0$: a unit neutral history is absent in this domain. A bin
$\sigma=2^{-h}$ satisfying $\sigma\le D\le4\sigma$, $h\ge0$, obeys

$$
h\le \max\left(0,\left\lceil\log_2\frac{4N}{c_0}\right\rceil\right).
$$

**证明。** Projection gives $q_2\le q_0q_1\le q_1$. The multiplicative recurrence proves monotonicity from index one. The identity for $\delta$ is direct multiplication of $1-\delta_j$. On $[0,1]^2$, $g$ lies between the larger input and their sum, proving the remaining elementary comparisons.

For a nonzero label set $u=-\log q_1$ and $v=-\log q_2$, so $0\le u\le v$. Elimination from the two norm monomials gives the PCR §67 integer bridge

$$
Q\log s=nu-mv,\qquad Q=m^2+mn-n^2\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}.
$$

For $n>0$, $Q=0$ would give a rational root of $X^2+X-1$; for $n=0$, $Q=m^2>0$. Also
$|nu-mv|\le(m+n)v$. Thus
$v\ge\log\sqrt{d_0}/(m+n)$, a weaker consequence of the stronger estimate in PCR §67.1. Hence

$$
D\ge1-q_2^2\ge1-\exp\!\left(-\frac{\log d_0}{m+n}\right)
\ge\frac{1-1/d_0}{m+n}.
$$

The last step is the chord bound for the concave function
$1-\exp(-t\log d_0)$ on $0\le t\le1$. A zero label has $D=2$ and satisfies the same bound directly. Finally $\sigma\ge D/4\ge c_0/(4N)$ proves the bin bound. Neither counts nor $N$ need to be available to the interpreter.

## 3. A finite deficit word and its semantic contract

**定义 3.1（finite fields and outputs）。** For $n\ge0$, let gamma$(n)$ consist of $\operatorname{bitlength}(n+1)-1$ zeros followed by the canonical binary expansion of $n+1$. Its length is
$2\lfloor\log_2(n+1)\rfloor+1$. A signed integer $z$ is gamma-coded through
$\zeta(z)=2z$ for $z\ge0$ and $\zeta(z)=-2z-1$ otherwise. A decoder counts leading zeros, then reads the initial one and exactly the indicated number of further bits. It rejects truncation, nonbinary data and trailing fields.

A deficit word has the fixed eight-bit mode $10111001$, followed by gamma$(K)$, gamma$(h)$, two positive mantissa/signed-exponent pairs $(M_0,v_0),(M_1,v_1)$, and nine signed coordinate integers in phase order $0,1,2$ and coordinate order $1,2,3$, then end-of-word. These are fifteen integer fields. Put

$$
\begin{aligned}
\epsilon&=2^{-K},&R&=h+K+12,&T&=2R,&S&=K+12,\\
P&=2K+\left\lceil\log_2(T+2)\right\rceil+32,
&\alpha&=2^{1-P}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Require $K\ge1,h\ge0$, $1\le M_i<2^{P+2}$,
$-h-P-2\le v_i\le0$,
$2^{-h-2}\le M_i2^{v_i}\le1$, and the first value no greater than the second. Each coordinate integer has absolute value at most $2^S+1$. Comparisons with dyadic powers use integer lengths before shifting; no string of $h$ zeros is constructed for a range test.

The numerical semantics are stronger than these syntax tests:
$\sigma=2^{-h}\le D\le4\sigma$, and the represented positive seeds
$\widehat\delta_3,\widehat\delta_4$ satisfy

$$
(1-\alpha)\delta_i\le\widehat\delta_i\le(1+\alpha)\delta_i
\quad(i=3,4).
$$

The three represented $S$-grid vectors $\widehat\xi_r$ approximate, within $4\,2^{-S}$ in Euclidean norm, the true directions

$$
\xi_0=x_3/q_3,\qquad \xi_1=x_1/q_1,\qquad \xi_2=x_2/q_2.
$$

This branch requires $q_3>0$. Zero and tiny futures use the disjoint fixed mode $10110110$ with no numerical fields. Its semantic premise is $\sup_{j\ge3}q_j\le\epsilon/4$, or an exact zero-future promise; mere recognition of its mode does not establish that premise. A periodic mode, when justified by a neutral promise, retains three finite vectors and a counted residue; it is treated in §10.

Each numerical output is a leading V, gamma of a precision, three gamma-coded signed numerators representing that dyadic vector, and a terminator. The zero mode emits the precision-zero zero frame. There are no implicit coordinates or free precision fields. The current full frame is buffered and charged, emitted once, and can then be erased. Malformed finite words or request frames are consumed through their declared end and return the fixed finite error frame without advancing position. The bounds below concern semantically valid compact words; a materialized oversized or malformed word pays its entire actual input length.

**命题 3.2（source-aware existence and description length）。** For any contracting nonzero unit-read history and any $K\ge1$, a word satisfying Definition 3.1 exists. Its length is

$$
O\!\left(1+K+\log(h+K+2)\right).
$$

The same fixed interpreter and grammar work for all sources, without $N$ or parameters encoded as unrepresented real constants.

**证明。** Choose the largest dyadic $\sigma\le\min(D,1)$. This gives
$\sigma\le D\le2\sigma$ when $D\le1$, and $\sigma=1$ otherwise. For a positive $z\le1$, let $e=\lfloor\log_2 z\rfloor$ and retain
$\lfloor z2^{P-e}\rfloor\,2^{e-P}$. This notation defines a mathematical selection; it is not an oracle call by the closed interpreter. The downward relative loss is less than $2^{-P}<\alpha$. This rounding is monotone, including changes of leading bin. Since $\delta_3\ge\sigma/2$ and $\delta_4\ge\delta_3$, the two seeds satisfy the order and lower range tests. Round each true direction coordinate to its nearest $S$-grid point, with half ties toward positive infinity. Its vector error is less than $2^{-S}$, and its integer range is as required.

The mantissas need $P+O(1)$ bits; the signed exponents have magnitude $O(h+P+1)$ and take $O(\log(h+P+2))$ bits; the nine coordinates need $S+O(1)$ bits each. Gamma$(K)$ and gamma$(h)$ have logarithmic lengths. Since
$P=O(K+\log(h+K+2))$ and $S=O(K)$, the stated length follows. All scales and signs are finite recorded data. This is source-aware existence, not acquisition from the original finite reports.

## 4. Finite arithmetic without an expanded decay denominator

**引理 4.1（one rounded update）。** Suppose $0<x,y\le1$ are represented finite positive dyadics. Let $e$ be the leading binary exponent of $\max(x,y)$ and keep $d=2^{e-P}$ as a signed binary exponent. Define finite integers

$$
A=\lfloor x/d\rfloor,\qquad B=\lfloor y/d\rfloor,\qquad
C=A+B-\lfloor ABd\rfloor,
\qquad G_P(x,y)=\min(1,Cd).
$$

Then

$$
|G_P(x,y)-g(x,y)|\le2d\le\alpha g(x,y).
$$

The integers satisfy $A,B<2^{P+1}$, $AB<2^{2P+2}$,
$C<2^{P+2}$ and $C\ge\max(A,B)\ge2^P$. The returned value is stored exactly as $(C,e-P)$, or $(1,0)$ if clipped. There is no further unaccounted normalization rounding.

**证明。** Put $x'=Ad,y'=Bd$. Both lie in $[0,1]$ and differ from the inputs by less than $d$. Since both partial derivatives of $g$ lie in $[0,1]$,
$0\le g(x,y)-g(x',y')\le2d$. Also
$0\le Cd-g(x',y')<d$. Clipping at one cannot increase the distance to $g(x,y)\in[0,1]$, proving the absolute error bound. Since
$g(x,y)\ge\max(x,y)\ge2^e$, it implies the relative bound.

The leading bin gives $A,B<2^{P+1}$ and at least one is at least $2^P$. Further, $ABd\le\min(A,B)$ because $Ad,Bd\le1$; this proves $C\ge\max(A,B)$. Its upper bound follows from $C\le A+B$. All products have the displayed widths.

**引理 4.2（multiplicative error transport）。** For $a,b\in[0,1]$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
g(ca,cb)&\ge c\,g(a,b) &&(0\le c\le1),\\
g(\min(ca,1),\min(cb,1))&\le c\,g(a,b) &&(c\ge1).
\end{aligned}
$$

Starting from Definition 3.1's two seeds and applying the pair update
$(x,y)\mapsto(y,G_P(x,y))$, after $t$ updates each component is bracketed by its true counterpart times $(1-\alpha)^{t+1}$ and $(1+\alpha)^{t+1}$. For $0\le t\le T$, each absolute deficit error is at most

$$
2\alpha(T+2)\le\epsilon^2\,2^{-30}.
$$

**证明。** Expansion proves the first inequality. For the second, if neither input is clipped, $c^2ab\ge cab$ proves it. If one is clipped, the left side is one and the right side is at least $c\max(a,b)\ge1$. Monotonicity of $g$, Lemma 4.1 and these two inequalities extend each bracket by one factor at an update; the unchanged component satisfies the enlarged bracket too.

For $n\le T+2$, the definition of $P$ gives $\alpha n\le2^{-2K-31}$. Thus
$(1+\alpha)^n-1\le e^{\alpha n}-1\le2\alpha n$ and
$1-(1-\alpha)^n\le n\alpha$. True deficits are at most one. Finally
$2\alpha(T+2)=2^{2-P}(T+2)\le2^{-2K-30}$.

**命题 4.3（finite implementation of the update and output）。** The operations just defined admit finite integer executions in
$O(P+S+\log(h+K+2))$ simultaneous bits on the specified register interpreter. They do not construct a denominator of length $h$.

**证明。** The leading exponent of a represented positive dyadic $(M,v)$ is
$\operatorname{bitlength}(M)-1+v$. Dividing by $d$ is an integer shift: compare its signed shift count with the actual operand length first. A right shift at least that length gives zero. A left shift is performed only when the bound $A,B<2^{P+1}$ proves its bounded resulting width. The same rule applies to $\lfloor ABd\rfloor$, whose result is bounded by $\min(A,B)$. Products use schoolbook integer arithmetic and linear-width scratch. Thus a very large numerical exponent is handled by its binary description and a comparison, not a long zero-filled integer.

The returned mantissa has at most $P+2$ bits. Its leading scale does not fall below the largest input leading scale, by $C\ge\max(A,B)$, unless clipped to one, which is larger. The pair's minimum remains no smaller than the initial smaller seed's leading bin. Hence throughout the active execution all represented exponents have magnitude at most $h+P+O(1)$.

For a current represented deficit $\widehat\delta\in[0,1]$, form
$a_*= \lfloor2^{2S}\widehat\delta\rfloor$ with the same shift guards. It has $O(S)$ bits. Binary integer-square comparison on $[0,2^S]$ finds

$$
m_*=\left\lfloor\sqrt{2^{2S}-a_*}\right\rfloor.
$$

This is a finite interval search with $O(S)$ comparisons of $O(S)$-bit integers, including exact-square equality. Its dyadic amplitude $m_*2^{-S}$ differs from $\sqrt{1-\widehat\delta}$ by at most $2\,2^{-S}$: taking the floor in $a_*$ changes the square-root argument by less than $2^{-2S}$, and the final integer root contributes at most $2^{-S}$. Multiplying this amplitude by a stored direction and rounding the three coordinates back to the $S$ grid uses only $O(S)$-bit products and emits a full $O(S)$-bit vector frame. Register lengths, shift counts and search counters have their actual binary widths.

## 5. Installation, whole-future error and peak space

**定理 5.1（one closed autonomous realization）。** Install a semantically valid deficit word by parsing its complete finite frame, retaining its two seeds and directions, computing $P,S,T$ from $K,h$, and setting the internal update count $t=0$ and phase $r=0$. On each NEXT with $t<T$, output the finite amplitude construction in Proposition 4.3 from the first deficit and direction $\widehat\xi_r$, apply one pair update, increase $t$ by one and $r$ modulo three, and return to the ready state. Once $t=T$, enter the permanent zero mode. Every output approximates its entire-future target with error less than $\epsilon/16$ in the active part and less than $\epsilon/512$ in the permanent tail.

Let

$$
B_*=1+K+\left\lceil\log_2(h+K+2)\right\rceil.
$$

The complete numerical peak, including installation, all control, addresses, scratch and full current outputs, is $O(B_*)$. A conservative installation and active-request bit-time bound is $O(B_*^4)$; the total finite active part costs
$O((h+K+1)B_*^4)$ bit operations. Tail requests need constant numerical space and time if the other registers are erased. These are finite bounds, not claims of optimal running time.

**证明。** At active count $t$ the true first component is $\delta_{3+t}$ and the phase is the direction of $x_{3+t}$. Lemma 4.2 and
$|\sqrt a-\sqrt b|\le\sqrt{|a-b|}$ for $a,b\ge0$ give amplitude error from deficit propagation at most $\epsilon2^{-15}$. The integer-square output contributes at most $2\,2^{-S}$. The stored direction has norm at most $1+4\,2^{-S}<2$ and direction error at most $4\,2^{-S}$; the final coordinate rounding has vector error less than $2^{-S}$. Therefore active vector error is bounded by

$$
2\bigl(\epsilon2^{-15}+2\,2^{-S}\bigr)+5\,2^{-S}
<\epsilon/16.
$$

The stopping argument uses the true source, not a rounded threshold. Since future norms decrease and $q_{j+2}\le q_j^2$ for $j\ge1$,

$$
q_{3+2R}\le q_3^{2^R}
=(1-\delta_3)^{2^{R-1}}
\le\exp(-\delta_3\,2^{R-1})
\le\exp(-2^{K+10})<\epsilon/512 .
$$

Here $\delta_3\ge2^{-h-1}$ and $R=h+K+12$. Every later true norm is no larger, so zero outputs control the entire unbounded tail. The argument includes $\delta_3=1$, for which the norm is already zero. The separately selected tiny/zero mode has its own uniform bound and never divides by a norm.

The finite program consists of parsing, length comparison, bounded shifts, integer addition, multiplication and division, bounded interval searches, finite conditional branches and output framing. Its description is fixed and charged once. $T$ is computed by binary additions; only its $O(\log(h+K+2))$-bit value is stored. The active count is saturated at $T$, the phase has three values, and the pair updates need a fixed number of $O(P)$-bit registers. All signed exponents have $O(\log(h+P+2))$ bits by Proposition 4.3. Direction and output registers have $O(S)$ bits. Thus they coexist in $O(B_*)$ bits, with a constant number of length-framed registers and $O(\log(B_*+2))$-bit physical positions and addresses. Source-dependent memory allocation, lengths and the program counter are included.

Schoolbook products and divisions use linear scratch and quadratic bit time; the integer-square search uses $O(S)$ such comparisons. Parsing and binary length computations are finite scans of the actual compact word. These give the displayed conservative quartic bound without hidden real arithmetic, exponentiation or an installed table. The number of active requests is $T=O(h+K+1)$, which proves the total active-time bound. Each later NEXT still pays its actual fixed request and output transport; an infinite service has infinite total transmission.

## 6. Matching lower bounds from actual sources

**引理 6.1（a contracting prefix needs different ready configurations）。** Consider any deterministic closed generator whose NEXT calls terminate, whose complete live configurations use at most $B$ bits, and whose errors are at most $E$ on a true trajectory tending to zero. If its first $J$ targets have norms greater than $2E$, their $J$ ready configurations, immediately before the corresponding requests, are pairwise distinct. Hence $2^{B+1}>J$.

**证明。** Repetition of one ready configuration would repeat the finite subsequent computation and its output. The deterministic ready-state transition would then cycle forever under identical NEXT requests. An output at the repeated position has norm at least its target norm minus $E$, strictly greater than $E$. It recurs at arbitrarily late positions; the true targets tend to zero, so correctness would force that same output norm to be at most $E$ in the limit. This is a contradiction. Every changing state component and possible source-dependent program is in the counted configuration. Time, an unreadable emitted stream or an external call count cannot distinguish the repeated states.

**引理 6.2（legal long prefixes with no input size oracle）。** Fix
$L>1$ and $1<d_0<L^{2/\varphi^2}$. Choose a fixed $s$ with
$\sqrt{d_0}<s<L^{1/\varphi^2}$ and put $z=\log s>0$. For sufficiently large positive multiples $k$ of six, let

$$
m=F_k,\qquad n=F_{k+1},\qquad
t_k=\rho^{k+1}\alpha,\qquad
a=s^{1+n/m}e_1,\qquad b=s^{-n/m}e_2 .
$$

This is one actual tree and one common preparation, with $N_k=F_{k+2}$ leaves, $\Delta=s^2>d_0$, legal leaf bounds and

$$
q_0=q_2=e^{-z/F_k},\qquad q_1=1,\qquad
q_j=e^{-zF_{j-1}/F_k}\quad(j\ge1).
$$

For fixed $E_0<1/2$ there is a constant $c$ such that the targets
$3\le j\le k-c$ all exceed $2E_0$, whereas the whole future tends to zero.

**证明。** The leaf substitution matrix sends $(m,n)$ to $(n,m+n)$, so the counts of $\rho^{k+1}\alpha$ are $(F_k,F_{k+1})$ and their sum is $F_{k+2}$. Every substituted $\alpha$ has a nonzero oriented unit label. The preparation is orthogonal, its product of leaf norms is $s$, and
$n/m\to\varphi$ gives $\|a\|\to s^{1+\varphi}=s^{\varphi^2}<L$; $\|b\|<1<L$. Orthogonal homogeneity gives $q_1=1$. Cassini's identity, obtained by induction from the Fibonacci recurrence, gives
$m^2+mn-n^2=(-1)^{k+1}=-1$, and then $q_0=q_2=e^{-z/m}$. The norm recurrence proves the formula for every $j$.

For two-step Fibonacci growth, $F_{\ell+2}\ge2F_\ell$. Choose $c$ sufficiently large that
$z2^{-\lfloor(c+1)/2\rfloor}<-\log(2E_0)$, increasing it if necessary for the small initial indices. For $j\le k-c$,
$F_{j-1}/F_k\le2^{-\lfloor(c+1)/2\rfloor}$, proving the strict prefix bound. Unbounded Fibonacci growth proves decay to zero.

**定理 6.3（sharp numerical-core peak）。** Fix

$$
L>1,\qquad 1<d_0<L^{2/\varphi^2},\qquad
H=1,\qquad 0<E\le E_0<1/2.
$$

For all sufficiently large proof-only $N$, uniformly over that accuracy interval,

$$
M_{\rm num}(N,E)
=\Theta_{L,d_0,E_0}
\left(1+\log(1/E)+\log\log(N+2)\right).
$$

The upper uses a single fixed finite interpreter. The lower concerns actual joint sources and complete running configurations; it is not a lower inferred from acquisition precision or a count of real registers.

**证明。** Choose the least $K\ge1$ with $2^{-K}\le E$. It has
$K=O(1+\log(1/E))$. Proposition 3.2 and Theorem 5.1 give the source-aware upper; Lemma 2.2 bounds $h$ by $O_{d_0}(\log(N+2))$. Tiny or zero sources use the smaller branch.

For the time-scale lower, take the largest sufficiently large multiple of six with $F_{k+2}\le N$. The elementary estimates
$F_j\le2^j$ and $F_{j+2}\ge2F_j$, with fixed initial constants, imply $k=\Theta(\log(N+2))$. Lemmas 6.1–6.2 force
$B\ge\log_2(k-c-2)-1$, uniformly for $E\le E_0$. This is the needed $\Omega(\log\log(N+2))$ lower.

For precision, fix one sufficiently late legal member whose $q_3=r_*>2E_0$. Proper rotations of the entire preparation preserve its tree, determinant, leaf bounds and all three read bounds. Its first future vector ranges over the sphere of radius $r_*$. A maximal set with pairwise chordal distances greater than $2E$ has at least $r_*^2/E^2$ points: maximality covers the sphere by caps of chordal radius $2E$, each of area at most $4\pi E^2$, whereas the sphere has area $4\pi r_*^2$. The strict inequality $E\le E_0<r_*/2$ ensures this is a nontrivial packing. No two such sources can have the same installed ready configuration, because its first output cannot lie within $E$ of both first targets. The complete configuration count gives
$B\ge2\log_2(r_*/E)-1$, and hence
$\Omega_{E_0,r_*}(1+\log(1/E))$ including a positive baseline.

Increase the common $N$ threshold to include this fixed tree and the positive part of the prefix lower. The maximum of two nonnegative lower bounds is at least half their sum. ## 7. Lawful initialization from a paid same-history archive

**定义 7.1（preclosure archive, distinct from later access）。** The original three Reads may additionally capture a separately paid immutable archive of those same values in the same frame and historical versions. Before acquisition closure it accepts finite precision requests. At $b\ge1$, a canonical reply consists of mode $11001001$, gamma$(b)$ and exactly nine signed-numerator fields, then end-of-frame, with $|n_{ik}|\le2^{b+1}$. It represents $y_i=n_i2^{-b}$ and promises, simultaneously,

$$
\|y_i-x_i\|\le2^{-b},\qquad \|x_i\|\le1,\qquad i=0,1,2.
$$

The encoder's accuracy input is a finite gamma-coded $K\ge1$ with the promise $2^{-K}\le E$. Its actual input and storage are charged. Selection of the least such $K$ in the source-aware upper is mathematical existence; lawful initialization needs this finite accuracy input or a separately represented tolerance from which it can be computed. No comparison with an unrepresented real $E$ is an interpreter operation.

The same actual history is used at every precision; errors may be arbitrarily correlated. Replies may be nonorthogonal, off the source image, and have the wrong computed orientation. The parser rejects wrong precision, truncation and trailing data, but cannot verify the semantic promise.

The archive's discovery request is the fixed eight-bit token $01011010$. Its separately charged precision state begins at one and doubles after each such request. A final request is the fixed token $01011011$ followed by gamma$(b_f)$. Thus only the final arbitrary precision is explicitly transmitted. Each finite request has a finite response; supplier latency, storage, precision production, source/version binding and authenticity are paid and have no bound inferred from the number of returned bits. After final compression all name capabilities close. This contract supplies no new source Read, reset or re-preparation and is not implied by an older once-only receipt.

**引理 7.2（all-valid-name scale discovery）。** With $e=2^{-b}$ set

$$
l_i=[1-\|y_i\|^2]_0^1\quad(i=1,2),\qquad
\operatorname{lo}=\max(0,l_1+l_2-6e),\qquad
\operatorname{hi}=\min(2,l_1+l_2+6e),
$$

where $[u]_0^1=\min(1,\max(0,u))$. At discovery precisions $b=1,2,4,\ldots$, stop only if
$\operatorname{lo}>0$ and $\operatorname{hi}\le2\operatorname{lo}$. Take $\sigma=2^{-h}$ to be the largest dyadic not exceeding $\min(\operatorname{lo},1)$, equality included. On every actual $D>0$ and every valid name this procedure stops, and its first returned bin satisfies
$\sigma\le D\le4\sigma$. Its successful precision obeys

$$
b_c\le2\max\left(1,\left\lceil\log_2(48/D)\right\rceil\right).
$$

**证明。** $\|y_i\|\le1+e$ implies
$|\|y_i\|^2-q_i^2|\le(2+e)e\le3e$. Clipping is nonexpansive, hence
$|l_i-\delta_i|\le3e$ and $D\in[\operatorname{lo},\operatorname{hi}]$.
If $e\le D/48$, then
$\operatorname{lo}\ge D-12e\ge3D/4$ and
$\operatorname{hi}\le D+12e\le5D/4$, so the guard holds for all valid replies. Doubling gives the displayed first-success bound. When $\operatorname{lo}\le1$, largest-dyadic selection gives
$\sigma\le\operatorname{lo}<2\sigma$ and $D\le2\operatorname{lo}<4\sigma$. When $\operatorname{lo}>1$, $\sigma=1$ and $1<D\le2$. Selection is finite rational comparison by numerator and denominator lengths followed by exact adjustment; it uses neither a true logarithm nor a real equality oracle.

**引理 7.3（robust finite orientation）。** For simultaneous reports with $e\le1/2$, set

$$
Z=\operatorname{sgn}\bigl(y_0\cdot(y_2\times y_1)\bigr)(y_2\times y_1).
$$

Then $\|Z-x_3\|\le9\sqrt e$ for all actual unit-read histories. In particular wrong or zero computed signs, actual zero, tiny vectors and off-image reports are allowed.

**证明。** The cross-product difference is at most
$q_2 e+(q_1+e)e\le3e$. Expanding the triple-product difference gives at most
$e\|w\|+\|y_0\|3e\le7e$. If the computed sign is correct, the cross bound suffices. If it is wrong or zero, Lemma 2.1 gives
$q_2^2\le|x_0\cdot w|\le7e$. Since $\|w\|=q_1q_2\le q_2$,
$\|Z-x_3\|\le3e+2\sqrt{7e}\le9\sqrt e$.
Actual zero follows by the same bound without division. Independence or source-image membership was never used.

**定理 7.4（finite all-valid-name initialization）。** After Lemma 7.2 returns $h$, compute $P,S$ as in Definition 3.1 and request once

$$
b_f=\max(b_c,\ h+P+80,\ 4K+96).
$$

This yields, by finite rational and integer operations alone, either a valid tiny/zero mode or a valid deficit word. Closing the archive afterward gives Theorem 5.1's complete autonomous service, pointwise on every neutral-free plateau history.

**证明。** At final $e=2^{-b_f}$, form the two clipped losses $l_1,l_2$ and the seed candidates

$$
d_3'=g(l_1,l_2),\qquad d_4'=g(l_2,d_3').
$$

On the unit square the derivative bounds for $g$ give
$|d_3'-\delta_3|\le6e$ and $|d_4'-\delta_4|\le9e$.
Since both true seeds are at least $\sigma/2$, their relative error is at most $18e/\sigma$. The inequality
$e\le\sigma2^{-P-80}$ makes this much smaller than $2^{-P}$. Round the positive candidates downward with $P$ significant fractional bits as in Proposition 3.2. Combining their input and rounding errors gives relative error less than $\alpha=2^{1-P}$. They remain ordered and at least $2^{-h-2}$, and meet the finite mantissa/exponent range tests.

Before normalizing directions, form outward rational intervals

$$
I_i=\left[\max(0,\|y_i\|^2-3e),\ \min(1,\|y_i\|^2+3e)\right].
$$

Their product interval $I_1I_2=[a,b]$ contains $q_3^2$ and has width at most $12e$. Select zero if
$b\le(\epsilon/4)^2$; otherwise select the positive branch if
$a\ge(\epsilon/8)^2$. At the given final precision at least one guard holds: if both failed, the interval width would exceed
$3\epsilon^2/64$, contrary to $12e<3\epsilon^2/64$. Boundary equalities are accepted. A zero decision gives
$q_j\le q_3\le\epsilon/4$ for every $j\ge3$. A positive decision gives
$q_3\ge\epsilon/8$, and therefore $q_1,q_2\ge\epsilon/8$. None of these tests is a real zero or sign oracle.

Use the directions of $Z,y_1,y_2$ in the positive branch. They are nonzero, since their errors are much smaller than $\epsilon/8$. For nonzero $u$ and an approximation $v$,

$$
\left\|\frac v{\|v\|}-\frac u{\|u\|}\right\|
\le\frac{2\|v-u\|}{\|u\|}.
$$

Thus Lemma 7.3 bounds their true-direction errors by
$144\sqrt e/\epsilon$. To implement normalization finitely, write the relevant rational vector as a nonzero integer vector $n$ times one positive common factor, put $Q=\sum n_i^2$, and for each coordinate find the largest integer $z_i\in[0,2^{S+5}]$ with

$$
z_i^2Q\le n_i^2\,2^{2S+10}.
$$

Attach the sign of $n_i$ and round the represented coordinate
$z_i2^{-(S+5)}$ to the nearest $S$ grid. The initial coordinate error is less than $2^{-(S+5)}$ and this final rounding contributes at most $2^{-S-1}$. The total vector error from finite normalization is less than $2\,2^{-S}$. Since $b_f\ge4K+96$,
$144\sqrt e/\epsilon\le144\epsilon2^{-48}<2^{-S}$.
The combined direction error is therefore less than $4\,2^{-S}$, and the finite integer coordinate range holds.

Dot products, clips, cross products, seed products, signs of rational numerators, range tests and the displayed integer-square searches have finite executions. The source truth and archive promise are hypotheses; syntax and interval arithmetic alone do not certify them. The theorem is pointwise total because $D>0$ in the stated domain, not because a finite name prefix decides neutrality.

## 8. Distinct entry and evidence accounts

**定理 8.1（preclosure numerical cost and compact entry）。** For the canonical archive pipeline of §7, let $A_*=1+h+K$. After allowed preclosure compression and erasure, the closed numerical peak is $O(B_*)$, not $O(A_*)$.

**证明。** Lemma 7.2 gives $b_c=O(h+1)$ because $D\ge2^{-h}$. Further $P=O(K+\log(h+K+2))$, so $b_f=O(A_*)$. The discovery precisions sum to less than $2b_c$; adding the one final request preserves $O(A_*)$. A canonical reply has length at most
$18b+2\log_2(b+1)+80$, so total returned bits have the same bound. Discovery uses $O(\log(b_c+1))$ fixed tokens; the final gamma field for $b_f$ has $O(\log(A_*+1))$ bits. The archive's changing precision and all its work are paid separately.

A constant number of $O(A_*)$-bit integer/rational fields suffices for clips, outward intervals, seeds, common-denominator direction calculations, scale comparisons and output compression. This is an encoder cost for already supplied valid names, not a production bound on exact real measurements. The allowed compact entry at closure is exactly the small word and Theorem 5.1's counted machine state.

**命题 8.2（raw entry is a different task）。** If the closed interpreter is required to receive a fully materialized raw triple of actual length $R_{\rm raw}$ before compression, that input itself immediately costs $R_{\rm raw}$ bits. For a sufficient canonical final reply the encoder uses

$$
\Theta_{L,d_0,E_0}(1+K+\log(N+2))

for sufficiently large $N$, whereas the compact closed peak has the smaller order in Theorem 6.3.

**证明。** The upper follows from Theorem 8.1 and Lemma 2.2. In the actual family of Lemma 6.2,
$D=1-e^{-2z/F_k}=\Theta(1/F_k)$, so every valid returned bin has
$h=\log_2F_k+O(1)=\Theta(\log N_k)$. The pipeline requests at least $h+P+80$ and $4K+96$. In its exact unit-axis $x_1$, a coordinate numerator in any valid canonical reply at precision $b$ has magnitude at least $2^b-1$, and hence gamma length $\Theta(b+1)$. The final reply and the required received/working precision therefore cost

**定义 8.3（full evidence and supplier costs）。** If an interface mandates $M$ literal evidence bits, an evidence wrapper consists of mode $10110111$, gamma$(M)$, those $M$ bits and one core. Its exact extra word length is
$M+8+2\lfloor\log_2(M+1)\rfloor+1$. ## 11. Exactness, rational descriptions and unbounded trajectories

**定理 11.1（bounded autonomous space is stronger than finite description）。** A deterministic closed generator with bounded complete live space and terminating identical requests has an eventually periodic output stream drawn from a finite output set. Consequently:

1. No individual unbounded true trajectory can be tracked at any fixed finite $E$ in bounded autonomous space.
2. At $E=0$, a positive contracting trajectory tending to zero but never equal to zero cannot be produced in bounded autonomous space.
3. Exact-zero and finite-representable neutral periodic trajectories are exceptions, with all actual representation/output widths counted.

**证明。** There are finitely many ready configurations. Their deterministic transition under NEXT must eventually repeat, hence cycle. Termination gives one finite output per transition; the outputs in the finite prefix and cycle form a finite set and are bounded. This proves the first claim. A periodic nonzero exact output cannot tend to zero, while a zero cycle cannot match a trajectory that stays positive forever, proving the second. The exceptions use a constant zero configuration or a three-valued counted phase with stored exact finite vectors.
