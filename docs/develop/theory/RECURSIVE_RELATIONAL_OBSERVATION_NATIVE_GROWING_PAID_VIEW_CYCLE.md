**Definition 3.3 (S mask codec and parser).** Encode a finite prefix-closed
support by visiting its prefix trie in preorder and emitting two bits
`lr` at each vertex: whether left, then right, child exists. All masks
00, 01, 10, 11 are admitted. The S-body contains only these bits, with no
per-node length, source contents, seed, endpoint sidecar or replay count.

pending path, Take two bits, Insert its path in the node list, and push

accept only when stack and bit source exhaust together. Exact layout
validation is Definition 3.5. Empty input is invalid; a one-vertex tree is
00. Its exact body size is $2V$ bits for $V$ vertices.

**Definition 3.4 (B contour codec and parser).** For a nonempty finite
terminal antichain $B$, take its unique prefix trie. Encode its depth-first
left-before-right contour by two-bit tokens

$$
D_0=00,\quad D_1=01,\quad U=10,\quad E=11.
$$

Emit $D_b$ on descent to child $b$, $U$ on the matching return, and $E$
at a terminal. In particular a root-only antichain has body $E$. A unary
left tree has body $D_0EU$, and a unary right tree $D_1EU$.

The parser keeps a path and a stack of frames `[path,leftSeen,rightSeen,
leafClosed]`. At a $D_0$ require no previous child and no E; at a $D_1$
require no previous right child and no E; mark that child and push a fresh
frame at the extended path. At E require no child and no prior E; emit
this path and close the frame. At U require a nonroot frame which is either
closed by E or has at least one completely returned child; pop it. A frame
closed by E admits only U, or EOF when root. EOF requires exactly the root
frame and a valid completed root. D0 after D1, duplicate edges, underflow,
odd bit count, missing E and unreturned descent fail. Lexicographic
Insert of the emitted terminals fixes their canonical list order.

Thus the parsed object is the terminal set, rather than a purported old
permission or an endpoint description. A trie with $V$ vertices and $L$
terminals has exactly $V-1$ descents, $V-1$ returns and $L$ E tokens, hence
body length $4(V-1)+2L$.

Its finite encoder uses work jobs Visit(path) and EmitToken(token). On a
Visit with no child emit E. Otherwise form, in left/right order, the three
jobs EmitToken(Db), Visit(path-b), EmitToken(U) for each existing child.
Prepend that finite job list to the old work list by reverse-item traversal
and Push. Starting with Visit(root) gives exactly the stated contour.
Child membership is Find in the finite trie; no recursive native call or
unpriced contour constructor is used.

**Lemma 3.6 (codec domains and exact inverses).** The two parsers terminate

grammar; induction on closed frames gives both inverse laws.

## 4. Compact-body counts and the absolute-path alternative

**Proposition 4.1 (exact compact sizes).** At a target cut let $C$ count
all actually constructed tape cells, $c$ present finite locations and
$q=q_\circ$. The two actual geometric bodies have sizes

$$
\begin{aligned}
V&=147+2q+c+24C,& L&=74+q+8C,\\
p_S&=2V=294+4q+2c+48C,\\
p_B&=4(V-1)+2L=732+10q+4c+112C.
\end{aligned}
$$

For native ordinal $k$, $C=2+A(k)+k$, with $A(k)\le k$. For this one fixed
apparatus these body sizes are linear in $k$; wrappers, M/T bodies and the
native computation constructing or decoding them are additional.

Proof. UAS Theorem 4.2's recount uses the same 74 tape components and this
new finite $q$. Every tape cell adds 24 vertices/eight terminals; a present
finite bit adds one vertex and no terminal. Definitions 3.3–3.4 give the
two sizes. Initially there are two meter cells; each native row constructs
one receipt cell and at most one nonmeter cell. Hence the displayed $C$
identity and bound. No bound on retained runtime material follows. ∎

**Proposition 4.2 (which path-list prices fail to transfer).** If instead
one encodes each absolute path by the RCA list codec, let $D_L,D_I,D_1$
sum terminal depths, internal depths, and depths of one-child vertices.
Then

$$
\begin{aligned}
p_S^{\rm paths}&=1+2(V+D_L+D_I)=4D_L+2D_1+3,\\
p_B^{\rm paths}&=1+2(L+D_L),\\
p_S^{\rm paths}-p_B^{\rm paths}&=2(V-L+D_I).
\end{aligned}
$$

In particular PVC's full-binary simplification omits $2D_1$ on this unary
carrier. For the meter's $m=k+2$ cells under an outer socket at depth $a$,
absolute path lists alone require at least

$$
\begin{aligned}
p_S^{\rm paths}&\ge24m^2+204m+2a(24m+1)+3,\\
p_B^{\rm paths}&\ge8m^2+90m+2a(8m+1)+3.
\end{aligned}
$$

Proof. A path item of length $d$ costs $2d+2$ bits and the list has one
terminal bit. If $b,u$ count two-child/one-child vertices, $L=b+1$ and
$V=2b+u+1$. Summing child depths over edges gives
$D_L=D_{\rm binary}+2b+u$; internal depth sum is
$D_{\rm binary}+D_1$. Substitution gives the three identities. The unary
three-vertex chain already has S path-body size 13, whereas $4D_L+3=11$.

A cell component has 23 vertices, total relative depth 66 and eight
terminals at relative depth four. Its $m$-cell tape spine has $24m+1$
vertices, total depth $(24m+1)a+12m^2+78m$, and terminal depth sum
$(8m+1)a+4m^2+37m$. Substitution in the item counts gives the lower bounds.
They hold for this explicit absolute-path codec class. Prefix-sharing
mask/contour bodies avoid the premise. No total-price superiority, smallest
codec or time/energy conclusion is inferred. ∎
