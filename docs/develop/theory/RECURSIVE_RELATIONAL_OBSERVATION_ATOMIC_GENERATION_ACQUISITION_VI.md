## 38. A hopping-only word on the same unknown code

**Definition 38.1 (reference word on the acquired six links).** Products
below act rightmost first; a displayed chronological list acts from left

$$
J_{mz},\ J_{mu},\ J_{mw},\ J_{mz},\ J_{mu},\ J_{mw},\ J_{mu}.
\tag{VI.7}
$$

$$
T=J_{mo}J_{oy},\qquad A=B_{ud}(\pi/4),\qquad
C_\star=T^\dagger KTK,\qquad U_\star=A^\dagger C_\star A.
\tag{VI.8}
$$

Thus the chronological execution is $A$, the seven blocks of $K$,
$J_{oy},J_{mo}$, the same seven blocks of $K$,

**Proof.** The use of a branching junction and clean retained auxiliaries
is supplied by Brod–Childs [equations (4),(6) and §IV](https://arxiv.org/html/1308.1463v1)
and the [repository matchgate note](../../../Library/QuantumStates/brodchilds2014matchgates.md).

sign. That full $P/B$ word cannot be imported as a hopping-only word:
a single $J$ multiplies a single-occupation exchange by $i$, while it
fixes double occupation. The following calculation supplies exactly the
replacement needed on this actual junction.

For bits at $(m,u,z,w)$ initially $(x,b,0,0)$, each block in (VI.7)
exchanges its two endpoint bits. It contributes $i$ exactly when those
bits differ. Literal bit tracking gives the following complete clean

| Initial $(x,b)$ | Unequal-endpoint digits | Final $(m,u,z,w)$ | Total phase |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $(0,0)$ | $0000000$ | $(0,0,0,0)$ | $1$ |
| $(0,1)$ | $0110011$ | $(0,1,0,0)$ | $i^4=1$ |
| $(1,0)$ | $1001101$ | $(1,0,0,0)$ | $i^4=1$ |
| $(1,1)$ | $1111110$ | $(1,1,0,0)$ | $i^6=-1$ |

Consequently, on the whole clean-helper subspace, with arbitrary state

$$
K(\xi\otimes|00\rangle_{zw})
=((-1)^{n_mn_u}\xi)\otimes|00\rangle_{zw}.
\tag{VI.11}
$$

This is an isometry identity on the four dirty center/partner columns,

$$
T^\dagger n_m T=n_y
\tag{VI.12}
$$

on all eight columns. Conjugation cancels the routing phases exactly.

$$
T^\dagger KT=(-1)^{n_yn_u},\qquad
C_\star=(-1)^{n_u(n_m+n_y)}.

$$

These identities hold with arbitrary occupations and coherences on

Z_u=\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad

For $Q_0H$ one has $n_m+n_y=0$, and for $Q_1H$ one has

## 39. Effective finite angles and complete-output accuracy

**Definition 39.1 (the finite command interface).** A requested tolerance
is any $\varepsilon>0$ together with an effective supplied rational
$0<\varepsilon_-\le\varepsilon$. An arbitrary real presented without

$$
e_b=20\,2^{-b}\le\varepsilon_-.
\tag{VI.15}
$$

Machin expression $16\arctan(1/5)-4\arctan(1/239)$ with $N=b+3$

therefore give approximants to $\pi/2,\pi/4$ with error at most $2^{-b}$.
Negation supplies the required negative angles. Their magnitude is less
than two, so $J=b+1$ padded magnitude slots with exponents $-b,\ldots,0$
suffice. Each slot executes the supplied elementary signed rotation
with its actual classical enable digit, including disabled identity
slots. Catalogue acquisition, rational arithmetic, finite descriptions,

For each chosen $b$ its corresponding finite catalogue and calibration
are separately supplied. The quantifier over positive tolerances ranges
over these finitely supplied commissions; it does not assert that one

**Theorem 39.2 (uniform accuracy retaining leakage and command labels).**

 \Omega_b(\rho)-\widehat\Omega_{\star,b}(\rho)\right\|_1

\tag{VI.16}
$$
