# Fixed dyadic offsets: precision risk and received MSB payload

Fix a public positive rational unit and a nondegenerate nonnegative rational offset interval. One positive dyadic offset can be fixed in that interval before the receiver, the depth parameter and the random tape. An explicit linear-depth family of actual initialized handoffs then agrees at every low precision and at every Reference precision. For this family, uniform exact success above one half forces a high-precision Extent initiation with a corresponding probability. Under the whole-integer MSB protocol specified below, a separate stopping argument forces actual receipt of linearly many value bits, in both tail probability and expectation. Completed numerical information alone does not imply this payload bound: LSB-first and parity-preheader encodings give attained counterexamples. An expressly installed centered service has a constant-size value on the public two-source promise while retaining linear numeric precision and paid finite arithmetic.

These are conditional, unexecuted symbolic proofs for the stated digital source and interfaces. They supply neither a kernel certificate nor an empirical or physical realization. Initialized geometry and the actual merger are credited source results; the quantitative fixed-offset and encoding comparisons are the present synthesis.

## 1. Actual initialized source and original operations

All indices, depths and source times are nonnegative integers. Digits run from low to high. Set

$$
G_0=1,\qquad G_1=2,\qquad G_{j+2}=G_{j+1}+G_j,
\qquad \alpha=(\sqrt5-1)/2,\qquad \phi=\alpha^{-1},
$$

$$
K=\{x\in\{0,1\}^{\mathbb N}:x_jx_{j+1}=0\text{ for every }j\ge0\}.

The endpoint-inclusive arithmetic successor $T$ finds the first adjacent $00$, clears the positions below its first position, puts one there and retains the higher digits. If no such pair occurs, it returns $Z(0)$, including on either pure alternating word. It is not positional deletion. The supplied finite-core identity is

$$
T^hZ(a)=Z(a+h)\qquad(a,h\ge0).
\tag{1}
$$

Every word in $K$ uniquely parses into complete blocks $0$ and $10$. For a finite complete block word $p$ put

$$
\mathcal B=\{0,10\}^{<\omega},\quad C_p=\{pt:t\in K\},\quad
 d=|p|,\quad N_p=\sum_{j<d}G_jp_j,\quad k_p=G_{d+1}-N_p-2.
\tag{2}
$$

$$
\mathfrak b_p\le i\le k_p,\qquad
\mathfrak b_p+N_p=G_d-1,\qquad k_p+N_p=G_{d+1}-2,
$$

$$
\mathfrak b_{p0}=\mathfrak b_{p10}=k_p+1,\qquad

\tag{3}
$$

Every $x\in C_p$ actually attains every return in this interval with acquired word $p$; the empty prefix occurs only at return zero. These statements are the initialized execution and lifetime results of PAID Definitions 1.2, 2.2 and Theorems 3.1–3.2, 12.2–12.3.

\tag{4}

Advance increments time to $i+1$. Every Return retains the actual original prefix, absolute time, cursor, caches $N_p,G_d,G_{d+1},k_p$, endpoint and binding, with empty scratch and pending output. The whole current point is $T^ix$ and the exact unread endpoint is $\operatorname{tail}_{|p|}x$. Each finite continuation is productive and is the suffix of that initialized run, including for alternating inputs. No execution searches an infinite alternating source for a nonexistent $00$.

A driver makes a genuine finite initialized sequence of Next calls, hands over the installed persistent exclusive turn at an actual Return, and ceases concurrent activity. The receiver starts with source-independent local state, the fixed acknowledgement and local Step count zero. A proper handoff has $p\ne\varnothing$; a proper death has $i=k_p$. The family below uses only proper deaths.

## 3. One fixed dyadic offset and explicit linear depth

**Lemma 3.1 (A fixed offset and all coarse cells).** Before the receiver, $N$ and tape, choose ONE positive reduced dyadic

$$
\beta=A/2^m\in(u,v),\qquad A\ge1,\quad m\ge0,
\quad a=\operatorname{bitlen}(A),\quad\kappa=a-m.
\tag{9}
$$

If $m>0$, reduction makes $A$ odd. Write the fixed public unit in reduced form $q_0=Q/R$, $Q,R\ge1$. One explicit fixed choice is

$$
t=\operatorname{bitlen}(Q)+1,\qquad N_0=\max(1,m),
\qquad d_N=2(N+t)\quad(N\ge N_0).
\tag{10}
$$

With $d=d_N$ put $z_A=q_0\alpha^d$, $z_B=q_0\alpha^{d+1}$. Then

$$
0<z_B<z_A<2^{-N},\qquad
 E_\beta(d,n)=E_\beta(d+1,n)=R_\beta(n)
 \quad(1\le n\le N).
\tag{11}
$$

The two Reference replies agree at EVERY finite precision $n\ge1$. The offset's reduced height and encoding length are independent of $N$.

Proof. Choose a dyadic grid with spacing strictly smaller than $v-u$. The integer immediately above $2^m u$, divided by $2^m$, is strictly above $u$ and at most one grid spacing above it, hence below $v$; it is positive since $u\ge0$. Reduction preserves its value. This proves the existence in (9) without inspecting the receiver or tape.

The equation $\alpha^2+\alpha=1$ implies $\alpha>1/2$: otherwise its left side is at most $3/4$. Hence $\alpha^2=1-\alpha<1/2$. Since $q_0<2^{\operatorname{bitlen}(Q)}$, the fixed $t$ satisfies $q_0 2^{-t}<1$. Therefore

$$
z_A=q_0(\alpha^2)^{N+t}<q_0 2^{-N-t}<2^{-N}.
$$

Because $N\ge m$, $K_N=2^N\beta=A2^{N-m}$ is a positive integer. Both initial values belong to the SAME half-open precision-$N$ cell $[\beta,\beta+2^{-N})$. To verify every coarser precision explicitly, write

$$
K_N=q\,2^{N-n}+r,\qquad0\le r<2^{N-n}.
$$

For $0\le\eta<1$, $r+\eta<2^{N-n}$, so the floor of $(K_N+\eta)2^{n-N}$ is $q$, equal to $\lfloor2^n\beta\rfloor$. Apply this to $\eta=2^Nz_A$ or $2^Nz_B$. This includes $n<m$, coarse zero replies and arbitrary integer parts. The offset is exactly at the LOWER precision-$N$ wall; positivity stays in the cell and the strict upper inequality excludes carry. No open neighbourhood about a dyadic wall is assumed. Reference equality at every finite precision follows directly from its identical fixed parameter. $\square$

## 6. Stopping at high-query initiation

**Theorem 6.1 (Precision risk with unrestricted References).** Fix $N\ge N_0$ and its two actual worlds before revealing the tape. Let $\tau$ be first Step INITIATION or a prior terminal decision, whichever occurs first. A terminal decision is the decision to give the final task answer or stop; prior output fragments remain in the history. Let $H_N$ be initiation of an Extent query with numeric precision $n>N$ strictly before this cut, stopped at the first such initiation. Reference precision and count are unrestricted. Then $H_N^A=H_N^B$ as measurable tape events and

$$
s_A+s_B\le1+\mu(H_N),
\tag{22}
$$

where $s_A,s_B$ are exact original-target success probabilities. The same statement holds for the public A/B label task. In particular, if both successes are at least $p>1/2$, then in BOTH worlds

$$
\Pr(H_N)\ge2p-1.
\tag{23}
$$

Proof. On any fixed tape, compare full finite histories before a high initiation or first Step. All Extents so far have $n\le N$, so Lemma 3.1 gives equal WHOLE values and frames; every Reference is equal at every finite precision. Consequently every received prefix, completion, local computation/count, tape access/response, control and output fragment agrees by causal induction. A first high command is selected from this identical preceding state, before its possibly differing reply. It is initiated on exactly the same tapes. This proves equality of events, beyond equality of their probabilities. Measurability follows from the assumed measurable finite histories: a finite-index first-event condition is measurable and the event is their countable union.

On $H_N^c$, a stationary finite terminal answer is identical, including an answer during a low Extent or any Reference prefix. If a first Step is initiated instead, its intermediate prefixes coincide by Lemma 5.1 and Lemma 5.2 couples every later finite history at arbitrary precision, so any later finite answer is identical. Infinite stationary local work, unbounded References or a permanent receipt stall without a completed answer gives no success. No common full-measure set over arbitrary worlds is needed; nontermination is failure. If almost-sure totality is assumed, only the two fixed null failure sets need be removed.

The original targets in (15) differ. Thus, for their finite-correctness events $C_A,C_B$,

$$
\mathbf1_{C_A}+\mathbf1_{C_B}\le1+\mathbf1_{H_N}
$$

pointwise. Integrating on the ONE law gives (22), and (23) follows. The identical argument uses the two distinct correct A/B labels. Public disclosure of $\beta,N$ or the pair promise is common information and changes none of the equal-history steps. $\square$

This is a bound on INITIATION. A high query need not complete and its receiver may see only a header or prefix before answering. There is no completion or reception lower bound in (22) alone.

## 7. Whole-integer MSB replies and their common prefix

**Definition 7.1 (Exact online format).** For the payload theorem, each request has a source-independent service tag, complete finite $n\ge1$ and request ID. IDs are chosen by the receiver or one common source-independent rule. The reply, in order, echoes tag/ID/$n$, gives a fixed nonnegative-sign convention, a self-delimiting header for value length $L$, then exactly $L$ ordinary binary VALUE bits MOST-significant first, fixed delimiters and its distinct completion. Positive values have no leading zero; zero is the single value bit $0$, with $L=1$.

A length header can be $1^L0$ or a fixed self-delimiting binary code of $L$. It depends only on $L$ and common fields. No value parity, checksum, hash, private-state padding, work/timing field or other content-dependent side information precedes the value prefix. Receipt is sequential bit/event receipt. No random access to a suffix or hidden whole-word delivery occurs. Seriality forbids new queries or Step inside an unfinished frame. Early final decisions are permitted with their already received bits and remaining issued-service debt. Any source-independent equal framing additions are paid separately.

**Lemma 7.2 (Exact length and uniform leading bits for unbounded precision).** Put

$$
K_N=A2^{N-m}>0,\qquad k_N=\operatorname{bitlen}(K_N)=N+a-m=N+\kappa.
\tag{26}
$$

At EVERY requested $n>N$, the two initial whole floor integers have equal complete value length

$$
L_n=n+a-m>k_N,
\tag{27}
$$

and their first $k_N$ value bits are exactly $\operatorname{bin}(K_N)$. In particular at least $N-C_\beta$ leading bits agree, where $C_\beta=\max(0,m-a)$ is fixed.

Proof. Because $n>N\ge m$, the offset shift is an integer and

$$
E_\beta(d_i,n)=K_N2^{n-N}+v_i(n),\qquad
v_i(n)=\lfloor2^nz_i\rfloor,\qquad0\le v_i(n)<2^{n-N}.
\tag{28}
$$

Thus its binary word is $\operatorname{bin}(K_N)$ followed by EXACTLY $n-N$ residual bits, padded with high zeros within that residual block. No residual carry enters $K_N$.
