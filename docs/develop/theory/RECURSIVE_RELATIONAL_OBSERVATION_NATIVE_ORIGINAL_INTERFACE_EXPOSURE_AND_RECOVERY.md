$$
\mathrm{RD}(\text{length},5);\ \mathrm{IN}(6);\ \mathrm{EX}(70);
\ \mathrm{WR}(70,6);\ \mathrm{MR}(70);\ \mathrm{MR}(\text{length}).
$$

It tests the already supplied length, not the payload bit. No bank
assignment, payload import or payload-dependent branch occurs inside
this loop. Both lengths are three. The first two iterations therefore

returns to its common ready state after each bit, as in AIF Definition
2.2; its future choices are not an endpoint coordinate. The two histories
differ at their first payload IN and remain different histories. They

## 5. A sufficient recovery condition across different reached entries

**Definition 5.1 (original experiment certificate for a starting cut).**
Fix $h\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{gc}}$ and put $N=n(h)$. The following is a

for added cuts by the results above.

parameters and only permitted public branching, satisfying the following
requirements.

1. Its first node is an original primitive Supply or Advance, executed
   from $h$ as exactly one actual native row, with no preliminary driver
   or checking rows. Uniformly over $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{gc}}$, whenever
   that node has the same executed response kind, it is one such row and
   its complete original successor retains the actual meter. Thus its
   successor receipt count from a starting cut $z$ is $n(z)+1$.
2. From $h$, $E_N$'s actual continuation before outer00 obtains an authentic

   AcquisitionOffset$(N-a)$ and completes GENERAL's authenticated cycle,

   its real original admission, lawful input domain and response
   correspondence. Semantic nodes, if used, retain all the executed
   original-action responses required by Definition 1.3.
3. A fixed extraction from existing released finite outputs in the
   response identifies one canonical body of a fixed
   $V\in\{S,B,M_J,T\}$ and its actual GENERAL target binding. It adds no
   output to $Q$. From $h$ this is a body for target tally $N$.
   Its success meaning is uniform: for any $z\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{gc}}$
   where $E_N$ is defined and has that successful extracted response
   form, the extraction comes from the actual authenticated GENERAL
   publication/callback route on that continuation, with matching
   source/seed/code, an authentic completed predecessor in GENERAL
   Definition 1.4's domain, genuine completed cover, accepted target
   tally $N$ and checked body. Recognition of a printed numeral or a
   self-consistent body alone is insufficient.

Requirement3 is an occurrence and interface-authenticity condition.

experiment, but their supplied numerals are real finite input. The

**Theorem 5.2 (starting-tally and authenticated-target separation).**
If $h\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{gc}}$ has the original-interface certificate
of Definition 5.1, then

$$
\forall k\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{gc}},\qquad h\sim_Q k\ \Longrightarrow\ h=k.
$$

Consequently, on any $D\subseteq\mathcal C_{\mathrm{gc}}$ whose members
have these certificates, for each $V\in\{S,B,M_J,T\}$ separately,

$$
V(h)=V(k)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad h=k
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad h\sim_Q k,
\qquad h,k\in D.
$$

There is no common fixed Ready or source-entry parameter in this
conclusion. All original-interface requirements of Definition 5.1,
including its uniform authenticity clause, remain hypotheses.

Proof. Suppose $h\sim_Q k$. The one-node prefix of $E_N$ is defined
and executed from $h$, so partial response equality forces the same
executed kind from $k$. It cannot become undefined, unexecuted `none`
or an executed refusal of a different kind. Requirement1 and equality
of the complete original meter successors give

$$
N+1=n(k)+1,
$$

hence $n(k)=N$. This is a mathematical selection from a required response,
not a permitted private-state branch of the experiment.

Equality of the full partial maps also gives the identical defined
response to $E_N$. The fixed extraction in requirement3 therefore
returns identical bindings and bodies. Its uniform authenticity clause
identifies genuine GENERAL publications for target tally $N$ on both
actual continuations. Since each starting prefix already has length
$N$, its continuation's prefix of length $N$ is precisely that starting
prefix. The two authentic targets are thus $h$ and $k$, not a common
older acquisition cut. GENERAL Theorem 6.3 identifies the two bodies
with the same independently defined view of those respective targets.
Their equality, followed by GENERAL Corollary 4.4, gives $h=k$.

Identical histories of the fixed initialized apparatus have identical
original partial response maps. GENERAL Corollary 4.4 gives the two
directions involving each natural view. This proves the second
assertion. Only $h$ needed a certificate to separate it from all $k$;
no independent returnability assumption for $k$ was used. ∎

**Convention 5.3 (why an eventual old target is insufficient).** Retrieving
AcquisitionOffset0 from two continuations returns the same acquisition

5.2 must be the synchronized starting tally $N$. Likewise, equality of

Such an endpoint-only comparison is not the original $Q$: Definition

not a counterexample to $Q$ or a substitute experiment language.

The theorem proves equality kernels on its stated domain. It does not
prove lawful transition transport on a forward-closed domain, nor a
currently executable actor procedure for recovering every view.

## 10. Exact realization after forgetting annex contents

**Theorem 10.2 (no uniformly finite supplement).** For every authentic
