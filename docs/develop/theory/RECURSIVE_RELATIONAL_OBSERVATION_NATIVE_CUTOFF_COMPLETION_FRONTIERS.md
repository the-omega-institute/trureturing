## 1. Three completion tasks on one stationary source

**Convention 1.1 (scope and attribution).** This volume gives exact
conditional memory/query frontiers above two actually acquired boundaries.
The first boundary leaves height-one subtrees unresolved; the second leaves
only terminal leaf labels unresolved. A separate sweep interface permits
different actual acquisition orders and prices their recollection. These
are distinct tasks. The supplied canonical prediction correspondence,
paid boundary acquisition and generic decision-tree counting are credited
suppliers, not new four-view results.

One Codex CLI author with repository-prior exposure supplies the ordinary
proofs, using `formal-thinking-and-answer` and `theory-volume-template`
under a theory-only scope. The conclusions are `repo-derived`; literature
contracts are identified in §11. No inference below uses an uncommitted
peer source or an assumed independently realizable collection of fields.

**Definition 1.2 (source, literals and original prediction task).** Let

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal T&::=\alpha\mid\beta\mid\langle\mathcal T,\mathcal T\rangle,
&h(\alpha)&=h(\beta)=0,\\
h(\langle u,v\rangle)&=1+\max\{h(u),h(v)\},
&\rho(\alpha)&=\beta,\\
\rho(\beta)&=\langle\beta,\alpha\rangle,
&\rho(\langle u,v\rangle)&=\langle\rho(u),\rho(v)\rangle.
\end{aligned}
\tag{CCF.1}
$$

Pairing is free and ordered. Every finite word $w$ over $L,R$, including
the empty word, is a legal original-root Read. Its reply $r_t(w)$ is
alpha or beta at the corresponding leaf, branch at a pair, and absent
after the path passes a leaf. The four replies are
$\mathcal A=\{\alpha,\beta,\mathsf{branch},\mathsf{absent}\}$.

The ordered constructor, substitution rule and literal observation are
separately stipulated relations. The two leaf symbols alone supply none
of pairing, observation, execution, reference, permissions or records;
the latter services and their common realization are declared below.

For public $D\ge1$, fix the full original family and permitted targets

$$
\mathcal F_D=\{\langle\alpha,v\rangle:h(v)\le D\},\qquad
W_D=\{a:|a|\le D-1\},\qquad \mathcal P_D=\{Ra:a\in W_D\}.
\tag{CCF.2}
$$

The one actual $t\in\mathcal F_D$ stays stationary. The original task
exports before an arbitrary later $p\in\mathcal P_D$, permits at most
one fresh Read selected before its reply and every count, and then must
answer $r_{\rho^k(t)}(p)$ for every later authentic $k\ge0$ with no
further Read. Here $\rho^k(t)$ is a prediction target. No actual
substitution, graft, inverse, source copying, reset or physical clock is
installed by (CCF.1).

**Assumption 1.3 (paid acquisition and complete accessible state).** Every
preparation algorithm is deterministic, effective, source-blind initially,
and independent of the later target and counts. Common installed code
depends on public inputs only. The only source information is obtained
through charged original-root Reads. Every actual call counts, including
repeated, constant, absent and empty-word calls. The submitted-letter
charge is the sum of word lengths; peak length is their maximum, or zero
if there are no calls.

After each declared export, all surviving accessible source dependence is
in its complete finite name: selectors, control, operands, buffers,
lengths, allocation, counters, handles and accessible timing included.
Every other accessible field is a function of that name and public inputs.
Paid handoff, access isolation, lawful entry, same-source continuity and
authentication are explicit service premises. They remove access to old
transcripts and discarded work; no implementation or numeric service
tariff follows from a name count. The original register remains a hidden
environmental source, accessible only through the paid literal interface.

**Definition 1.4 (pooled completion and task equivalence).** A completion
protocol starts with a genuinely acquired boundary $e$ or $c$ specified
below and a further complete refinement $C(t)$, also obtained by paid
preparation before any later target or count. It receives no selected
target, makes at most $q$ fresh adaptive Reads at arbitrary finite words,
and exports a finite passive answer state. Its two possible tasks are:

1. Prediction completion: answer every $p\in\mathcal P_D$ at every
   subsequently supplied authentic $k\ge0$ without further Reads.
2. Literal completion: answer $r_t(w)$ for every finite stationary
   original-root word $w$ without further Reads.

Sources are equivalent for each task when all its required answers agree.
A finite tree or canonical boundary plus a public recursive evaluator is
a finite passive implementation; an infinite response table need not be
stored literally. These completion tasks neither reconstruct past events
nor change the original one-selected-target service. A lower bound for
completion is not a lower bound for its original $M_1(D)$ objective.

No product condition is imposed on $C$: it may correlate all source
coordinates. Query selection may use the entire complete name and every
fresh reply, including variable stopping. All lower bounds allow this.

**Convention 1.5 (actual runs and failures).** An executed Read at $w$
has label $(\mathsf{Read},w,\mathsf{ok},r_t(w))$, leaves $t$ unchanged,
and changes the actual control and any declared record according to that
same reply. Absent is a successful literal observation. Every finite
literal is legal; no executed-failure service is assumed. An unsupported
source update has no executed successor and creates no event. The results
cover these Read runs and, in §§7–8, the explicitly restricted sweep runs.
They do not assign four views to additional histories with executed
failures, source edits, arbitrary preparation transactions or physical
timestamps.

## 2. Credited canonical boundaries and their acquisition

**Convention 2.1 (canonical supplier).** Reuse [AAR, Theorems 12.3 and
16.3][AAR], [CBN, Theorem 2.2][CBN] and [PPF, §2][PPF]. Put

$$
C_0=\langle\alpha,\alpha\rangle,\qquad
S_1=\{\alpha,\beta,C_0\},\qquad
S_{j+1}=\{\alpha,\beta\}\sqcup
\{\langle x,y\rangle:x,y\in S_j\}.
\tag{CCF.3}
$$

For $j\ge1$, $\pi_j(v)$ preserves each reached constructor and labelled
leaf through relative depth $j-1$, replacing a branch at that cutoff by
$C_0$. Its attained image on $h(v)\le D$, $1\le j\le D$, is exactly
$S_j$. Write $N_j=|S_j|$, so $N_1=3$ and $N_{j+1}=2+N_j^2$.
The actual reply table on $W_j$ reconstructs $\pi_j(v)$, and

$$
r_{\rho^k\langle\alpha,v\rangle}(Ra)
=r_{\rho^k\langle\alpha,\pi_D(v)\rangle}(Ra)
\quad(a\in W_D,\ k\ge0).
\tag{CCF.4}
$$

The all-count trajectory factor and this canonical sufficiency are
credited. In particular (CCF.4) is not a new reconstruction of actual
execution time. A cutoff branch is recorded by its constructor; the alpha
children used in its canonical representative are placeholders, not
acquired original labels.

## 3. The actual six-state fiber above the shallow boundary

**Definition 3.1 (unresolved positions).** Fix $D\ge2$ and an attained
$e\in S_{D-1}$. In lexicographic order let

$$
A_e=\{a\in\{L,R\}^{D-1}:r_e(\operatorname{parent}(a))
=\mathsf{branch}\},\qquad f=|A_e|,
\tag{CCF.8}
$$

and put

$$
U=\{\alpha,\beta,
\langle\alpha,\alpha\rangle,\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle,
\langle\beta,\alpha\rangle,\langle\beta,\beta\rangle\}.
\tag{CCF.9}
$$

Every $Ra$, $a\in A_e$, is an actually present original-root address
of length $D$. For a source with boundary $e$, write $x_a=v|_a$.
These are mathematical coordinates of actual sources, not free acquired
inputs or instructions to graft an executing register.

**Theorem 3.2 (complete actual fiber and arbitrary-word tests).** For
every such $D,e$, the map

$$
\{\langle\alpha,v\rangle\in\mathcal F_D:
\pi_{D-1}(v)=e\}\longrightarrow U^{A_e},\qquad
\langle\alpha,v\rangle\longmapsto(x_a)_{a\in A_e}
\tag{CCF.10}
$$

is a bijection. Every finite original-root Read is either fixed by $e$,
or belongs to exactly one cone $Ra\{L,R\}^{<\omega}$ and has this
local form:

| Suffix after $Ra$ | Reply as a function of $x_a$ |
| --- | --- |
| Empty | alpha, beta or branch, according to the root of $x_a$ |
| $L$ or $R$ | absent when $x_a$ is a leaf; otherwise that child label |
| Length at least two | absent |

Thus each test has at most three attainable replies on the whole fiber.
On the subfamily in which every $x_a$ is a pair, every nonconstant test
has only two attainable replies. These statements concern arbitrary
words, without restricting adaptive competitors to a prefix menu.

Proof. The boundary fixes all reached constructors and labelled stopping
leaves through depth $D-2$. At each branch at depth $D-2$, both children
are present, and the height promise leaves each child subtree height at
most one. Its possibilities are exactly (CCF.9). Conversely, replace the
two artificial children of every such cutoff branch in $e$ by any chosen
members of $U$. No choice intersects another, every ancestor remains a
branch, and the resulting $v$ has height at most $D$ with the same
$\pi_{D-1}(v)$. All other stopping leaves are already fixed. This gives
both inverse maps in (CCF.10), including the singleton case $f=0$.

The addresses $Ra$ are prefix-free. A word entering one such cone has
exactly the local reply displayed, since $x_a$ has height at most one.
A word entering no cone either stays in the fixed skeleton, passes a
fixed earlier leaf, or lies in the fixed outer root/left cone. Its reply
is fixed. In the all-pair subfamily, root replies are constant branch,
child replies are binary labels, and longer replies are constant absent.
This proves the full-word classifications. $\square$

**Proposition 3.3 (the two exact task partitions).** On the fiber
(CCF.10), two sources have the same entire original prediction menu at
all counts if and only if their constructor vectors

$$
z=(z_a)_{a\in A_e},\qquad z_a=r_v(a)\in
\{\alpha,\beta,\mathsf{branch}\}
\tag{CCF.11}
$$

agree. They have the same stationary literal-response function if and
only if their entire $x$-vectors agree. There are exactly $3^f$ prediction
classes and $6^f$ literal classes.

Proof. The vector $z$ together with $e$ reconstructs $\pi_D(v)$:
retain a leaf constructor and replace a branch constructor at each $a$
by $C_0$. Equation (CCF.4) supplies every original target and count.
Conversely the original permitted targets include each $Ra$; at count
zero their replies recover $z$. Each trit vector is attained by choosing
$\alpha,\beta,C_0$ independently in (CCF.10), so the class count is
$3^f$. For literals, equality of $x$ gives the same actual source.
Distinct elements of $U$ differ at their root or a child, so one of
$Ra,RaL,RaR$ distinguishes any different coordinate. All $6^f$ vectors
are attained. $\square$

## 4. Sharp conditional refinement/query frontiers

**Theorem 4.1 (prediction completion).** Fix any $D\ge2$, attained
$e\in S_{D-1}$ and integer $q\ge0$. Under Assumption 1.3 and Definition
1.4, retaining $e$ as part of the complete name, the least number of
attained refinement values conditional on $e$ that permits prediction
completion in at most $q$ fresh Reads is

$$
P(f,q)=3^{\max\{f-q,0\}}.
\tag{CCF.12}
$$

This minimum ranges over arbitrary correlated refinements and adaptive
arbitrary-word completion protocols. No preparation resource cap is
imposed on acquisition of the refinement.

Proof. Restrict correctness to the actual $3^f$ sources with every
$x_a\in\{\alpha,\beta,C_0\}$. They have distinct prediction answers
by Proposition 3.3. For any fixed complete refinement value, every query
has at most three possible replies on this restricted family by Theorem
3.2. A deterministic decision tree of depth at most $q$ has at most
$3^q$ terminal transcripts, also when it stops early. This follows by
induction on its remaining depth: a terminal node counts one and an
internal node has at most three child subtrees. Queries, stopping and
local work are determined by the name and preceding replies, so they
cannot introduce additional branches. A correct terminal transcript
identifies at most one of these constructor vectors. Consequently $m$
attained names satisfy $m3^q\ge3^f$. Together with $m\ge1$ this gives
the lower bound (CCF.12), irrespective of correlations in a name fiber.

Let $s=\max\{f-q,0\}$. During additional preparation Read the first
$s$ addresses $Ra$, $a\in A_e$, and retain their root constructors.
Export exactly this tuple with $e$, isolating all other dependence. During
completion Read the other $f-s\le q$ roots, reconstruct $z$, and apply
Proposition 3.3. Every retained trit tuple is realized by the actual
fiber, so its attained alphabet is exactly $3^s$. All queries are actual
original-root words of length $D$, chosen from $e,D,q$. This is an
effective paid acquisition, rather than a supplied source-aware code.
$\square$

**Lemma 4.2 (a concrete local complement and its decoder).** Define

$$
b(\alpha)=\alpha,\qquad b(\beta)=\beta,\qquad
b(\langle u,v\rangle)=v\quad(u,v\in\{\alpha,\beta\}).
\tag{CCF.13}
$$

Given $b(x_a)$, one actual Read at $RaL$ recovers $x_a$ completely.
Without this bit, two actual Reads suffice.

Proof. If the first reply at $RaL$ is absent, $x_a$ is a leaf and
equals the supplied $b(x_a)$. Otherwise the reply is its left leaf
$u$, and $x_a=\langle u,b(x_a)\rangle$. When no bit is retained,
first Read $RaL$. On absent, Read $Ra$ to get the leaf label;
on a nonabsent reply, Read $RaR$ to get the right leaf. Each branch
uses exactly two calls and determines $x_a$. Presence of $Ra$ follows
from $e$, so an absent parent cannot occur. $\square$

**Theorem 4.3 (literal completion).** Under the same quantifiers and
complete-state convention as Theorem 4.1, the least conditional attained
refinement alphabet for stationary literal completion is

$$
L(f,q)=
\begin{cases}
2^q6^{f-q},&0\le q\le f,\\
2^{2f-q},&f\le q\le2f,\\
1,&q\ge2f.
\end{cases}
\tag{CCF.14}
$$

The formulas agree at their endpoints, including $f=0$.

Proof of necessity. Literal completion separates all $6^f$ actual sources
by Proposition 3.3. On this family every arbitrary word has at most three
replies, so the same terminal-tree argument gives

$$
m3^q\ge6^f.
\tag{CCF.15}
$$

Restrict the same protocol to the simultaneously realizable all-pair
subfamily. Its $4^f$ choices of left and right labels are distinct
literal tasks. On it every arbitrary word is constant or binary by
Theorem 3.2, even when the name correlates coordinates. Thus

$$
m2^q\ge4^f.
\tag{CCF.16}
$$

The maximum of $1,6^f/3^q,4^f/2^q$ is exactly (CCF.14): the ratio
of the last two nonconstant expressions is $(3/2)^{f-q}$, so they
cross at $q=f$, and $4^f/2^q$ reaches one at $q=2f$. Each displayed
dominant value is an integer. No independent-coordinate assumption on
the encoder or restriction on submitted word lengths was used.

Proof of attainment. For $0\le q\le f$, retain $b(x_a)$ at the
first $q$ positions and retain the complete six-valued $x_a$ at each
other position. Lemma 4.2 uses one fresh call for each of the first $q$
positions and none for the rest. The complete conditional alphabet has
exactly $2^q6^{f-q}$ names.

For $f\le q\le2f$, leave the first $q-f$ positions unencoded and
retain $b(x_a)$ on the other $2f-q$ positions. Use two fresh calls
on each unencoded position and one on each encoded position. The total
is $2(q-f)+(2f-q)=q$, and exactly $2^{2f-q}$ names are attained.
For $q\ge2f$, retain no refinement and use the two-call decoder at
every position. In all cases the reconstructed $x$-vector gives the
whole original source and hence every stationary literal.

These names can actually be acquired before the request: apply the
two-call routine on every position whose complete value or bit is to
survive, compute the declared fixed-format name, and isolate the rest.
All required words act on the same stationary original source. Theorem
3.2 realizes every possible retained tuple, so these are exact attained
alphabets, rather than counts of unused storage strings. The local
six-state routines and public frontier order require no optimal-strategy
oracle or source-specific installed program. $\square$

## 5. Complete alphabets and separate resource coordinates

**Corollary 5.1 (total retained alphabets when the boundary survives).**
For fixed $D\ge2$ and a uniform public budget $q$, if $e$ remains part
of the complete pre-completion name on every original source, the exact
total minimum alphabets are

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal P_D^{\rm keep}(q)&=\sum_{e\in S_{D-1}}P(|A_e|,q),\\
\mathcal L_D^{\rm keep}(q)&=\sum_{e\in S_{D-1}}L(|A_e|,q).
\end{aligned}
\tag{CCF.17}
$$

Conditional on a fixed $e$, the minimum final passive answer alphabets
are $3^f$ and $6^f$, respectively. In particular, literal completion
with $q=f$ needs exactly $2^f$ additional pre-completion names, or
$f$ fixed-width data bits conditional on $e$.

Proof. Different visible $e$ values have disjoint complete names. Apply
Theorems 4.1 and 4.3 on each attained fiber for the lower sums. One common
effective program reads the retained $e$, constructs its frontier, and
runs the corresponding attaining protocol, proving equality. Final
passive states with the same value must give the same entire task
answer, so Proposition 3.3 supplies the two final lower bounds. Storing
$z$ or $x$, with $e$, attains them. Substitution $q=f$ in (CCF.14)
gives the bit statement. $\square$

The sums require boundary preservation; encoders permitted to discard or
merge different $e$ values are outside this optimization. A conditional
$f$-bit supplement is not total observer memory. A single dictionary for
all complete names in (CCF.17) uses the ceiling of the logarithm of that
sum in fixed-width bits. Installed dictionary size, its generation and
unused bit strings are separate matters.

**Proposition 5.2 (attaining acquisition and completion charges).** After
the credited acquisition of $e$, the constructions above have these
sufficient additional charges, with $f=|A_e|$:

| Task and phase | Reads | Submitted letters | Peak length when calls occur |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Prediction refinement preparation | $\max\{f-q,0\}$ | $D\max\{f-q,0\}$ | $D$ |
| Prediction fresh completion | $\min\{f,q\}$ | $D\min\{f,q\}$ | $D$ |
| Literal refinement preparation | at most $2f$ | at most $2f(D+1)$ | at most $D+1$ |
| Literal fresh completion | $\min\{2f,q\}$ | at most $(D+1)\min\{2f,q\}$ | at most $D+1$ |

All charges are zero for a phase with no calls. Literal preparation can
skip every position with no surviving field; its displayed uniform bound
is sufficient, not an optimum. No submitted-letter optimum is asserted
for these literal refinement/completion constructions.

Proof. The prediction queries are the specified roots $Ra$, all of
length $D$. Lemma 4.2 uses only $RaL,RaR$ of length $D+1$ and,
on its leaf branch, $Ra$ of length $D$. Preparation uses at most two
calls per position with a surviving value or bit. The fresh call counts
were computed in the attaining proofs. Summing word lengths and taking
their maximum gives the stated bounds. $\square$

**Convention 5.3 (storage, code and actual source image).** The acquisition
and completion programs operate on tuples arising from one actual
$t$, its actual deterministic Read history and the associated computed
control. Their hidden environment field is the same $t$; their visible
working field is the acquired partial table, local operands and control;
their reference binds the phase to that source and epoch; their permission
is the declared literal menu. The port reference, epoch and type tags are
fixed across alternative source inputs; any additional accessible
source-dependent identity belongs in the complete name. Extending a run
by any selected literal appends its truthful successful event and
recomputes these fields from
that same run. This construction defines the joint image and its
successors, rather than independently assigning archive, reference or
permission values. Assumption 1.3 supplies actual lawful handoff and
continuity; syntax of a tuple does not establish a physical service.

Complete export retains only the stated name and its public functions.
Temporary raw observations, branch-dependent selectors and stopping
times must be isolated or included in that name. Lexicographic frontier
generation, the local six-entry tables and a direct address-list
implementation use at most $O(D2^D)$ bits of acquisition/completion
working storage, apart from public input representation and output. This
is a sufficient algorithmic bound, not a storage optimum or a bound on
later prediction evaluation. Installed public code, its construction,
local computation, output materialization,
source-bound access, isolation and count representation remain separate
resources. The original preparer's acquisition in (CCF.6), refinement
preparation, completion, final answer storage and services are distinct
charges, even when a single implementation performs them consecutively.

With only $e$, the unchanged individually selected original task still
needs at most one fresh Read, by PPF. With no refinement, target-free
prediction completion requires exactly $f$ fresh Reads, whereas literal
completion requires exactly $2f$, by (CCF.12) and (CCF.14). The extra
rightmost-label relation in (CCF.13) is exactly priced for the specified
literal completion interface at budget $f$; it is not necessary for the
original selected-target service or a unique syntactic form of memory.

## 6. Hidden original leaf labels after full prediction acquisition

**Definition 6.1 (terminal-label coordinates).** Fix $D\ge1$ and an
actually acquired $c=\pi_D(v)\in S_D$. Let

$$
\begin{aligned}
B_c&=\{a\in\{L,R\}^{D-1}:r_c(a)=\mathsf{branch}\},
& b_c&=|B_c|,\\
U_c&=\{RaL,RaR:a\in B_c\},&r&=|U_c|=2b_c.
\end{aligned}
\tag{CCF.18}
$$

Order $U_c$ lexicographically. For
$\theta\in\{\alpha,\beta\}^{U_c}$, let $t_\theta$ replace the
artificial two children of each cutoff branch in $c$ by their indicated
labels and retain the outer $\langle\alpha,\cdot\rangle$.

**Theorem 6.2 (exact original-source fiber and exact-address sensitivity).**
The map $\theta\mapsto t_\theta$ is a bijection onto

$$
\{\langle\alpha,v\rangle\in\mathcal F_D:\pi_D(v)=c\}.
\tag{CCF.19}
$$

This fiber has $2^r=4^{b_c}$ actual sources. Its entire original
prediction menu is constant for every $k\ge0$. For every finite word
$w$, the stationary reply is fixed throughout the fiber unless
$w\in U_c$; at $u\in U_c$ it is exactly $\theta(u)$.

Proof. The boundary fixes every reached constructor and labelled leaf
through relative depth $D-1$. At a branch at depth $D-1$, the height
bound forces its two original children at depth $D$ to be leaves, whose
labels are freely and jointly realizable. They are the only undetermined
source data. This gives the bijection and its cardinality. The prediction
statement is precisely the credited (CCF.4).

Changing one child label leaves every strict ancestor branch, every
proper extension of that leaf absent, and every other cone unchanged.
Changing any subset of these labels therefore affects exactly its
corresponding literal addresses and no other words. This covers empty,
outer-left, off-target and arbitrarily long words as well as words in the
right cone. $\square$

**Theorem 6.3 (label-complement frontier and no-supplement acquisition).**
For every fixed $D,c$ and integer $q\ge0$, the least conditional attained
refinement alphabet permitting full literal completion of (CCF.19) with
at most $q$ fresh adaptive arbitrary-word Reads is

$$
C(r,q)=2^{\max\{r-q,0\}}.
\tag{CCF.20}
$$

With no additional source information beyond acquired $c$, every correct
completion execution Reads every $u\in U_c$. The exact simultaneous
minimum resource vector is

$$
(\mathsf{Reads},\mathsf{letters},\mathsf{peak})
=\begin{cases}
(r,(D+1)r,D+1),&r>0,\\
(0,0,0),&r=0.
\end{cases}
\tag{CCF.21}
$$

Acquiring and retaining all hidden labels requires exactly $2^r=4^{b_c}$
supplementary names conditional on $c$ when no fresh Reads remain.

Proof. For any fixed refinement, Theorem 6.2 makes every fresh query
constant or binary. A depth-$q$ tree has at most $2^q$ terminal
transcripts. A correct literal completion separates all $2^r$ actual
sources, so $m2^q\ge2^r$, together with $m\ge1$. Retaining the
first $\max\{r-q,0\}$ labels in the public order and Reading the
rest attains (CCF.20), with actual paid preparation of every retained
label. Every binary tuple occurs, so all those names are attained.

For the pointwise no-supplement statement, fix any actual execution and
suppose it omits $u\in U_c$. Flip just $\theta(u)$. The acquired $c$
is unchanged and every other literal reply is unchanged. Deterministic
replay gives the same queries, control, termination and output on the
flipped source. The output cannot answer $u$ correctly on both, a
contradiction. Each required $u$ has length exactly $D+1$; a longer
word in its cone is absent and cannot replace that Read. Thus all three
lower charges in (CCF.21) hold on every execution when $r>0$.
Reading each $u$ once attains them. At $r=0$ the boundary already
specifies the full source, and no call is needed. Finally put $q=0$
in (CCF.20). $\square$

This pinpoints the original labels excluded by CBN's prediction
representation. It neither improves PPF's acquired prediction theorem
nor turns $r$ additional completion Reads into the original single late
Read. The refined memory is conditional on $c$; a total complete name
must also retain or otherwise account for its acquired boundary.

## 7. A separately declared actual acquisition sweep

**Definition 7.1 (source and schedule domain).** Fix $D,c$ as in §6,
and a public nonempty finite set $\Sigma$ of permutations of $U_c$.
Put $R=|\Sigma|$. The sweep interface permits an external selector to
choose the next address subject to its public prefix rule. Its actual
histories are exactly

$$
H_{c,\Sigma}^{\rm pref}=
\{(\theta,\tau):\theta\in\{\alpha,\beta\}^{U_c},\quad
\tau\text{ is a prefix of some }\sigma\in\Sigma\}.
\tag{CCF.22}
$$

Every pair in this set is permitted: a selector can supply the indicated
prefix on that same stationary $t_\theta$. This is a declared execution
service, not multiple possible runs of one fixed deterministic original
preparer. A fixed public sweep is the special case $R=1$. If an actual
interface restricts orders to a source-dependent proper subfamily, the
product domain (CCF.22) and the frontier below do not apply without a new
analysis.

For a prefix $\tau=(u_1,\ldots,u_j)$, define

$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{\theta,\tau}&=\{u_i\mapsto\theta(u_i):1\le i\le j\},\\
T_{\theta,\tau}&=
[(\mathsf{Read},u_i,\mathsf{ok},\theta(u_i))]_{i=1}^j,\\
\operatorname{Perm}_\Sigma(\tau)&=
\{u:\tau u\text{ is a prefix of some }\sigma\in\Sigma\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{CCF.23}
$$

A full joint state consists of the source/epoch/type binding, the same
hidden stationary $t_\theta$, its acquired $c$, the work table
$d_{\theta,\tau}$, the retained actual prefix/archive
$\tau,T_{\theta,\tau}$, and permission
$\operatorname{Perm}_\Sigma(\tau)$. The source binding authenticates
continuity; it is not an extra source-value identifier available for free
decoding. The joint state set is the image of (CCF.22) under this one map,
not a product of separately chosen field sets.

**Proposition 7.2 (lawful sweep transitions and joint closure).** At a
state represented by $(\theta,\tau)$, selecting
$u\in\operatorname{Perm}_\Sigma(\tau)$ performs exactly one actual
original-root Read. Its full output is
$(\mathsf{Read},u,\mathsf{ok},\theta(u))$; its successor is the joint
state represented by $(\theta,\tau u)$, with the new map entry,
appended archive and recomputed permission in (CCF.23). Every such
successor lies in the same joint image. An invalid selector has no
executed successor or record. At $j=r$ the sweep terminates. Each
terminal $(\theta,\sigma)$ pays exactly $r$ Reads and $(D+1)r$
letters, with peak $D+1$ if $r>0$ and zero otherwise.

Proof. Prefix legality in (CCF.22) is exactly the permission rule. All
$u\in U_c$ are actual leaves of $t_\theta$, so their paid replies are
the labels specified in Theorem 6.2. Appending the event yields precisely
(CCF.23) for the same $\theta$ and the extended prefix. That prefix is
again in (CCF.22), which proves joint successor closure including its
archive and permission fields. Each permutation contains every address
once, so its length and charges have the stated values. $\square$

This explicit closure concerns only the specified successful sweep
image. Preparation of $c$, local computation, repeated diagnostic Reads,
executed failures and other update menus are not extra histories silently
covered by it. All acquisition of $c$ and the binding/archive services
remain paid premises. No physical durations are recorded.

**Definition 7.3 (old-trace recollection after isolated export).** At the
end of the sweep export one complete finite value
$E(\theta,\sigma)$. It may correlate all labels and the actual order.
After paid isolation, a common deterministic recollector knows only
$E,c,D,\Sigma,q$, makes at most $q$ fresh adaptive original-root Reads,
and must return the exact old ordered event content $T_{\theta,\sigma}$.
The old prefix, selector script, permission state, schedule handles,
timing and label buffers are unavailable unless represented in $E$.
The source remains $t_\theta$ with the same authenticated binding.

The attainable input domain is
$H_{c,\Sigma}=\{\alpha,\beta\}^{U_c}\times\Sigma$.
The name count is over source-and-history inputs, not the original
source-only $M_1(D)$. Reconstructing the old event content does not
manufacture an authenticated receipt that those events occurred. If a
callable historical receipt is required, a service must bind the archive
or its code to committed events and authenticate that binding.

## 8. Exact joint price of discarded labels and sweep order

**Theorem 8.1 (sweep recollection frontier).** For every public
$D\ge1$, attained acquired $c\in S_D$, nonempty public
$\Sigma\subseteq\operatorname{Sym}(U_c)$, and integer $q\ge0$, under
Definitions 7.1–7.3 the least complete attained export alphabet conditional
on $c,\Sigma$ is

$$
M_{c,\Sigma}^{\rm recall}(q)
=R\,2^{\max\{r-q,0\}}.
\tag{CCF.24}
$$

In particular unrestricted sweep order has $R=r!$, zero-fresh-Read
recollection needs exactly $2^r r!$ names, and even unlimited fresh
source Reads require at least $r!$ retained history distinctions.

Proof of necessity. Fix $\theta$. Every subsequent source reply depends
on $\theta$ only, so two different $\sigma$ values sharing an export
would replay identically under any deterministic recollector. They have
different ordered traces because their distinct addresses occur in
different positions. Correctness therefore requires $R$ different names
on this one source: $m\ge R$.

For any fixed export, Theorem 6.2 gives at most two reply branches at
each fresh query node. A depth-$q$ tree has at most $2^q$ terminal
transcripts. A correct terminal transcript identifies at most one old
trace. The map $(\theta,\sigma)\mapsto T_{\theta,\sigma}$ is injective:
the trace lists every address once with its label and actual order.
Thus all $2^rR$ distinct traces require

$$
m2^q\ge2^rR.
\tag{CCF.25}
$$

Combining (CCF.25) with $m\ge R$ gives (CCF.24), including $q\ge r$.
The argument restricts no correlations of the export and permits every
arbitrary-word adaptive recollection protocol.

Proof of attainment. Let $a=\max\{r-q,0\}$. During the actual sweep
record its address order and compute its rank in a public enumeration
of $\Sigma$. Retain this rank together with the first $a$ labels in
the fixed order of $U_c$. Those labels were actually Read during the
sweep. Export exactly this pair and isolate every other dependence.
Every rank/label tuple occurs on (CCF.22), so exactly $R2^a$ names
are attained.

On recollection Read the other $r-a=\min\{r,q\}$ labels at their
original-root addresses. Combine them with the retained labels and list
the events in the retained permutation order. This yields exactly
$T_{\theta,\sigma}$. The fresh charges are
$r-a$ Reads, $(D+1)(r-a)$ letters and peak $D+1$ when nonzero.
For $r=0$, the permutation and label vector are both empty and the
single name needs no Reads. Ranking and decoding are effective finite
computation from public $\Sigma$, not an optimal-strategy oracle.
$\square$

**Corollary 8.2 (the chronological relation that source rereading misses).**
On the terminal sweep image, given the acquired full label vector, the
least alphabet needed to distinguish the actual orders is $R$. One
attaining relation is the first address together with the directed
$\mathsf{Next}$ edges between consecutive distinct addresses in the
actual sweep; the empty order has a separate public empty convention.
This relation and the label map recover the event trace, and the trace
recovers both. A direct online record has a sufficient
$O(r\log(r+1))$-bit workspace implementation; the minimum attained
order-code width is $\lceil\log_2R\rceil$ bits.

Proof. The fixed-source lower bound in Theorem 8.1 still applies when
all labels are supplied. Ranking attains it. Since a sweep is a
permutation, its first address and successor chain visit each address
exactly once and end after $r$ occurrences. Following the chain and
attaching each known label reconstructs the trace. Consecutive events
in a trace give exactly this chain. A list of $r$ indices in a public
$r$-element address list uses the stated workspace; finite ranking can
scan the installed enumeration with an index of at most
$\lceil\log_2(r!)\rceil$ bits. $\square$

The alphabet result does not minimize local computation, transient
storage, installed enumeration size, archive creation, authentication,
handoff or access costs. Retaining a larger record while computing its
rank is allowed before isolation and is charged as workspace. A surviving
schedule buffer or accessible time field must join the complete export.
When $R=1$, there is no conditional order supplement: the public sweep
can be replayed from its labels. The factorial term arises only from the
explicit choice interface, never from a claim that one deterministic
preparer has several executions on the same source.

## 9. Concrete view kernels and the remaining four-view bridge

**Proposition 9.1 (natural views on the same terminal sweep image).** On
$H_{c,\Sigma}$ define the following maps before assessing recovery:

| Expression | Concrete value | Equality kernel on $H_{c,\Sigma}$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bare spatial support | Actual node addresses and ordered parent/child incidence of $t_\theta$ | All pairs |
| Labelled spatial expression | That actual support with its original alpha/beta leaf labels | Equality of $\theta$ |
| Actual event time | The ordered executed Read trace $T_{\theta,\sigma}$ | Equality of $(\theta,\sigma)$ |
| Original predictive boundary | Every $r_{\rho^k(t_\theta)}(p)$, $p\in\mathcal P_D$, $k\ge0$ | All pairs |
| Stationary literal boundary | Every $r_{t_\theta}(w)$ for finite $w$ | Equality of $\theta$ |

Here the kernels are for the stated readout-content tasks. They do not
replace PG's full dynamic response contract, which also contains original
joint successors, archive/writer append and legality/failure fields.

Proof. The shape of $t_\theta$ is fixed by $c$, so bare support is
constant. Theorem 6.2 identifies all original labels with $\theta$,
and hence the labelled spatial expression is injective in $\theta$.
The trace is injective in the full pair as in Theorem 8.1. Equation
(CCF.4) makes the predictive boundary constant. The literal boundary
reads every $u\in U_c$, so it recovers $\theta$; conversely $\theta$
fixes the actual source and all its replies. These are direct native
kernel calculations on one actual image. $\square$

When $r>0$, the predictive boundary cannot recover original labels;
when $R>1$, even complete labelled spatial data cannot recover the actual
sweep order. A memory nonredundant for the original predictive task must
forget all these conditional distinctions. A memory sufficient for the
literal task must retain or acquire the labels but need not distinguish
the sweep orders. A memory for exact old-trace recall must distinguish
both. These conclusions use the task-sufficiency versus nonredundancy
direction of [PG, Proposition 43.1][PG]; no new generic quotient or
factorization theorem is asserted. [Atlas, Theorem 6.3][Atlas] requires
each view individually to have the task kernel, rather than only their
joint map.

**Example 9.2 (two independent decisive distinctions).** At $D=1$ take
$c=C_0$, so $U_c=\{RL,RR\}$. The four sources
$\langle\alpha,\langle u,v\rangle\rangle$ for independent
$u,v\in\{\alpha,\beta\}$ have identical original prediction
trajectories at the only original target $R$, but differ at the actual
literals $RL$ or $RR$. On the one source

$$
t=\langle\alpha,\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle\rangle,
\tag{CCF.26}
$$

the permitted unrestricted sweeps $RL;RR$ and $RR;RL$ have the same
source, full labelled support, acquired label map, call count two,
letter total four and future stationary literal responses. Their actual
ordered event traces differ. This is a same-source order witness, not
an unknown age, prediction count or endpoint-cost distinction.

**Open problem 9.3 (unchanged original interface).** The exact original
general $M_1(D)$, sharp $\lambda$ and effective all-size certificates
remain unresolved. The conditional minima above do not alter the class
of original arbitrary correlated encoders, variable shapes, all targets,
arbitrary late words or all authentic counts. They do not construct four
natural mutually recoverable minimal views for that original task.

A proposed native bridge now has a concrete design choice. For literal
completion it must acquire the six-state or terminal-label complement
at the stated budgets, or use a different declared acquisition interface.
For actual ordered events it must either restrict to a known schedule or
install and charge a chronological relation and callable authenticated
archive. Adding an archive without adding its task queries can violate
nonredundancy for original prediction. A source reference, complete tree
or authentic $k$ cannot substitute for the erased sweep order.

The remaining global bridge must exhibit four natural task expressions
with individual exact task kernels, one actually acquired joint history
image, every lawful selector input and whole successor, including any
executed failure, and PG's appropriate full/next contracts. Coherent
bonding across scales, actual coverage/ThreadComplete and whole-edge
legality are additional obligations. Fixed-depth source completion,
successful sweep closure and exact event-content recollection establish
none of those global conditions automatically.
