## 2. Finite words, placement and initial installation

**Definition 2.1 (word language and canonical framing).** Reuse RCA
Definitions 11.1 and 12.1. A register holds a finite binary word; instructions
are empty, copy, literal cons, tail, empty/first-bit test, sequence, finite
case and while. Internal naturals are $1^n$, wire naturals are $U(n)=1^n0$,
and

$$
F(w)=U(|w|)w,\qquad
\operatorname{List}([])=0,\qquad
\operatorname{List}(w::r)=1F(w)\operatorname{List}(r).
\tag{PVC.2}
$$

A tuple is an exactly specified arity list. Parsing exhausts its enclosing
field. Symbols and finite tags use their fixed bit order; naturals always
include their terminal zero on the wire. Reverse, Cat, Length, Equal, Take,
ReadUnary, ReadFrame, ReadList, Nth, Replace and AppendItem mean RCA's actual
acyclic expansions. A word argument is never a host address or native
state number selected by its value.

The following additional macros fix the word algorithms used below.
Prefix cancels copies of two words, accepting when the first ends without
mismatch. LexLess cancels their common prefix; a proper prefix precedes its
extension, and otherwise 0 precedes 1. Find scans a list with Equal and
retains an item and its constructed unary index. OrderedInsert traverses a
sorted list, emits the argument before the first greater item, and either
rejects equality or suppresses it, according to its fixed call-site mode.
Emit means Cat of literal 1 and Frame(item), followed eventually by the
terminal 0. Push prepends one such framed item; pop is ReadList. A tree
traversal stack is a word list, never a native return stack.

Fixed-record field projections are Nth with literal finite indices.
Dynamic indices are actual unary words. To enumerate prefixes of a path,
start with the empty word, emit it, then consume its bits one at a time,
appending the consumed bit and emitting the new prefix. Deduplication and
membership are repeated finite scans. No sorting or graph traversal is
atomic. These descriptions determine a finite acyclic list of helpers;
every nested loop consumes a copied finite cursor or an explicit countdown.

## 5. Finite algorithms for the directed cycle

**Definition 5.1 (raw spatial encode/decode).** For a raw finite record $Y$,

If the meter component's outer path has length $a_m$ and the target has
$k$ receipts, then in particular

$$
p_S\ge1+k(k+1)+2k(a_m+2).
\tag{PVC.15}
$$

Proof. Each path-list item has $1+(|p|+1)+|p|=2|p|+2$ bits and the
list has one final bit. Summing over nodes or terminals gives the first
expressions. The elementary full-binary-tree identity
$D_B=D_I+2I$ follows by summing child depths over all edges: total
nonroot depth is $2D_I+2I$, also $D_B+D_I$. Since $V=2I+1$,
substitution gives $p_S=4D_B+3$ and the difference.

The receipt numbered $j=1,\ldots,k$ is cell $j+1$ of the meter word,
whose first cell is L, and has cell-root path length $a_m+j+1$.
These distinct mandatory support
items alone contribute $\sum_j(2(a_m+j+1)+2)$, plus the list terminator,
which is PVC.15. Wrapper framing can only add bits; PVC.10 then applies
to the actual full result length. These bounds concern explicit path
lists, not every tree codec, succinct representation or native algorithm.
They imply no total fee dominance: validation, banks, installation and
retained later costs also change. ∎
