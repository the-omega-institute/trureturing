## 1. Scope, evidence and the acquisition question

This volume prices a missing acquired relation: ordered parent/child incidence
that numerical observations and their authentic archives do not disclose. It
uses the repository's actual native updates, actual full-source producer and
paid finite-capacity archive runtime as positive suppliers. Its results concern
finite source families and explicitly declared dynamic interfaces.

Two regimes are distinguished. Independent three-leaf bracket choices give
binary-informative probes, sharp label/probe tradeoffs and sharp submitted-address
work. Balanced leaf words give a different, exponentially large common numerical
boundary fiber after a real accepted update, and a four-valued literal-query
lower bound. The latter bound is not claimed sharp.

## 2. Actual sources, distinct ports and complete resource contracts

**Definition 2.1 (ordered source and literal reply).** Let $\mathcal T$ be the
free algebra of nonempty finite ordered full binary trees

$$
t::=\alpha\mid\beta\mid\langle t,t\rangle.
$$

The atoms are distinct. Ordered pairing and the following substitution are
additional declared structure:

$$
\rho\alpha=\beta,\qquad
\rho\beta=\langle\beta,\alpha\rangle,\qquad
\rho\langle t,u\rangle=\langle\rho t,\rho u\rangle.
\tag{DAF.1}
$$

Write $w(t)$ for the left-to-right leaf word, $c(t)=(a(t),b(t))$ for composition,
and $\lambda(t)=a(t)+b(t)$. A literal address is a word over $\{L,R\}$ starting
at the current register root. The total reply $\operatorname{Lit}(t,p)$ is
$\alpha$, $\beta$, `branch` or `absent`: the root reports its constructor;
descent through a branch follows its named child; descent through a leaf reports
`absent`. Every finite address, including the empty address, is a legal query.
This is exactly `ActualTreeReadoutAcquisition.Address`, `Reply` and `readout`,
with false denoting $L$, true denoting $R$, and true leaves denoting $\alpha$.

**Definition 2.2 (native and numerical menus).** The native menu of
`NativeSerialMachine.Op` has source-free local steps, actual literal Read,
unconditional actual `ApplyRho`, and Stop with the complete output. Its one
register, epoch and chronological action trace have the `nativeStep` semantics.
Its transition function receives control only. It has no graft operation.

The numerical menu is the distinct TM30/TM57 contract at public finite $H$:
$\mathcal T_H=\{t:1\le\lambda(t)\le H\}$. Numerical Read returns current
Clifford $E(t)$ and preserves the source. A requested $\rho$, `Left(v)` or
`Right(v)` forms the entire actual candidate $\rho t$, $\langle v,t\rangle$ or
$\langle t,v\rangle$, respectively, with a complete named actual nonempty
context $v$. It accepts and installs the candidate iff its leaf count is at
most $H$; otherwise it executes reject and retains the old source. Reject
reveals no candidate $E$. There is no reroot, reset, inverse, private-packet
read, source-duplication or source-size port.

TM57 additionally supplies actual paid installation, finite control and tapes,
material/code generation, archive access/append/freeze, owned complete copies,
record-only verification and consumer use. PR57.T3 makes every visible primitive
cut a prefix-causal function of public initialization and already committed
original responses. This includes instruction/address labels, ownership,
availability, references, ticks, fees and partial delivery. It is not a theorem
that numerical Read implements the native literal port.

**Definition 2.3 (comparison and hybrid interfaces).** For the bracket lower
bounds we allow a stronger comparison interface: one current source, native
literal Read, actual unconditional $\rho$, and insertion of the current source
exactly once into a known ordered one-hole context. We even grant the entire
current leaf word as a free semantic observation. Contexts are constructed from
public inputs and acquired data; they cannot contain additional unacquired
source advice. There is no operation that copies the unknown source occurrence.
The genuine native submodel omits grafts. Guarded numerical modifications are
included by making their complete word-count test and mutating only on accept.
This is a mathematical comparison interface, not a claimed supplier composition.

For the balanced lower bound we instead retain the exact fixed-$H$ numerical
menu and TM57 services, and explicitly add a nonmutating `LitRead(p)` on the
same actual current source. Its only new source-dependent observation is the
four-valued literal reply. Any visible preparation, execution, delivery and
recording schedule for this added port must be determined by public data and
the already acquired prefix, consuming the reply only at release. This is a
hypothesis of the hybrid model. The original PR57.T3 proves that property for
the original services, not automatically for a new literal instrument.

**Definition 2.4 (complete information and costs).** All policies are common,
deterministic and causal, and terminate sourcewise on the stated finite family.
Public prior, scaffold, program and initialization are fixed independently of
the hidden member. Supplementary source information, if allowed, is a complete
label $\ell(t)$ in an attained alphabet $L$. Every other accessible initial
source-dependent field must factor through that label: code selection, control,
buffers, retained archives, handles, names and timing included. A $b$-bit budget
means $|L|\le2^b$, not merely that one chosen payload has $b$ bits. Authenticity
and source binding of an allowed label are separate channel hypotheses.

Raw query count charges every literal invocation, including repetitions and
`absent`. Literal-address work is the sum of all submitted $L/R$ letters from
the current original register root, with no free focus navigation. These are
coordinates of a resource account, separate from source/material acquisition,
code installation, address generation/serialization/seeking, names and reference
widths, archive reads/append/seal/copy, decoder work, simultaneous storage,
workspace and runtime. Granting free computation or word observations in a
lower-bound comparison does not assert zero physical cost for those actions.

In TM57, $N=2H-1$ source token slots are allocated and zeroed, and private
installation supplies exactly $2N$ bit ingress occurrences with their actual
writes and movements; validation uses fixed public scans. An owned $k$-bit copy
has the PR57.D7 body cost $8k+4$ core occurrences, with allocation, positioning,
length/identity descriptors and serial output additional. Sender, mailbox and
destination coexist and their storage is counted. These concrete service costs
are retained when the information lower bound grants unlimited archive work;
an unlimited work allowance is not a zero-cost archive implementation.

**Definition 2.5 (two source targets).** The initial-tree target is exact
reconstruction of the installed $t$, equivalently its whole literal response
function. The local current-tree completion target is a terminating acquisition
whose local state permits a common decoder to answer every address of the actual
terminal current tree without further source access. A callable service retaining
a lawful live source port and paying for later queries is a different target.
Neither target is TM57's initial $q_H$ output or the stationary late-target task.

## 3. A bracket product with preserved dynamic locality

**Definition 3.1 (one bit convention).** Fix $m\ge1$ and a public ordered full
binary scaffold $S$ with exactly $m$ holes and no other leaves. Hole addresses
$p_1,\ldots,p_m$ are pairwise prefix-incomparable; put $d_i=|p_i|$. Set

$$
G_0=\langle\alpha,\langle\alpha,\alpha\rangle\rangle,
\qquad
G_1=\langle\langle\alpha,\alpha\rangle,\alpha\rangle,
\qquad
U_x=S[G_{x_1},\ldots,G_{x_m}],\quad x\in\{0,1\}^m.
\tag{DAF.2}
$$

Thus $\operatorname{Lit}(U_x,p_iL)$ is $\alpha$ for $x_i=0$ and `branch` for
$x_i=1$. Interchanging the two displayed gadgets replaces every bit by its
complement; it changes none of the bounds. Every $U_x$ has $3m$ alpha leaves,
word $\alpha^{3m}$ and distinct ordered syntax. At $H\ge3m$ they are actual
TM57 sources with a common initial $q_H$.

**Lemma 3.2 (single-occurrence dynamic invariant).** At any fixed complete
acquired prefix $h$ in Definition 2.3's bracket comparison interface, there are
a common known one-hole context $C_h$ and integer $e_h\ge0$ such that every
compatible source has current tree

$$
C_h\bigl[S[\rho^{e_h}G_{x_1},\ldots,\rho^{e_h}G_{x_m}]\bigr].
\tag{DAF.3}
$$

The context has exactly one occurrence of its hole. Its material and the exponent
are determined by $h$, not separately chosen for each compatible source.

Proof. Initially $C_h$ is the identity and $e_h=0$. Literal Reads, word
observations and local work preserve both. A graft composes its known one-hole
context with $C_h$, preserving one occurrence. Applying $\rho$ to a context
means substituting all its constant leaves and preserving its hole. Because
$\rho$ preserves each original branch constructor, an accepted substitution
replaces $C_h$ by that substituted context and increments $e_h$ in (DAF.3).
A rejected guarded request leaves the tree unchanged. At a fixed acquired prefix
the deterministic requests and supplied actual contexts agree. All current
words in (DAF.3) agree, so their complete candidate word counts and guard outcomes
also agree. Induction over these actual actions proves the formula.

Here $e_h$ counts successful substitutions of the retained original occurrence.
It is not the complete action-word length: Reads, local steps, grafts and
executed rejects remain distinct events. With grafts, (DAF.3) is not the global
certificate $r=\rho^e(U_x)$ used by `actual_register_execution`. Without grafts
and with native unconditional updates it is precisely that source equation.

**Theorem 3.3 (dynamic one-coordinate locality).** At a fixed compatible prefix
as in Lemma 3.2, any literal query depends on at most one bracket coordinate
$x_i$ and has at most two attainable replies. If it distinguishes the two values
of coordinate $i$, its submitted address length is at least $d_i+1$.

Proof. Let $c_h$ be the hole path in $C_h$. The variable gadget roots occur at
the common prefix-incomparable paths $c_hp_i$. An address stops in the context
or scaffold, enters a known constant, exits the tree, or enters exactly one such
gadget cone. In the first three cases its reply is independent of $x$; in the
last it is a function of only $\rho^{e_h}G_{x_i}$. A single address cannot enter
two cones. Hence the range has at most two elements, despite the four global
reply constructors. Both possible gadget roots are branches at every epoch,
so a distinguishing address must pass strictly below that root. Its length is
at least $|c_h|+d_i+1\ge d_i+1$. Substitution never contracts the scaffold;
outer grafts can only add this prefix. This is a claim about one common actual
family under the same acquired prefix, not independent marginal realizability.

**Lemma 3.4 (literal recovery at a known pure-update epoch).** Suppose the
current tree is exactly $\rho^eU_x$ for known $e\ge0$. Then

$$
\operatorname{Lit}(\rho^eU_x,p_iLR^{\lfloor e/2\rfloor})=
\begin{cases}
\alpha,&x_i=0,\ e\text{ even},\\
\beta,&x_i=0,\ e\text{ odd},\\
\mathsf{branch},&x_i=1.
\end{cases}
\tag{DAF.4}
$$

Proof. Put $T_j=\rho^j\alpha$. Then $T_0=\alpha$, $T_1=\beta$ and
$T_{j+2}=\langle T_{j+1},T_j\rangle$. The left subtrees of the two updated
gadgets are $T_e$ and $\langle T_e,T_e\rangle$. Following $\lfloor e/2\rfloor$
right edges in $T_e$ ends at $T_0$ or $T_1$. In the other left subtree, if
$e=0,1$ no right edge is taken and its root is a branch. For $e\ge2$ the first
right edge enters $T_e$, and the remaining edges end at $T_2$ or $T_3$, both
branches. The preserved scaffold supplies the prefix $p_iL$.

Under the more general invariant (DAF.3), prepend the known $c_h$ to the same
address. Its length is $|c_h|+d_i+1+\lfloor e_h/2\rfloor$. That is an explicit
possible address cost, not an optimal post-update length theorem. Native updates
are unconditional; guarded TM57 updates count toward $e_h$ only on accept.

## 4. Why complete paid numerical archives do not bypass acquisition

**Lemma 4.1 (word closure).** The leaf-word map obeys

$$
w(\rho t)=\sigma(w(t)),\quad
\sigma(\alpha)=\beta,\quad\sigma(\beta)=\beta\alpha,
\qquad
w(\langle t,u\rangle)=w(t)w(u).
\tag{DAF.5}
$$

Equal words therefore give equal $E$, leaf counts, complete candidate guard
results, and words after each common accepted action of Definition 2.2.

Proof. Structural induction gives the two identities. The Clifford leaf product
is associative, so $E$ is a function of the ordered word. Leaf count is word
length. Apply the identities to the entire same named candidate; acceptance
installs those equally worded candidates and rejection preserves the equally
worded old trees. No rejected candidate response is introduced.

**Theorem 4.2 (complete numerical archive non-bypass).** With common public
initialization and no supplementary structural label or literal port, every
finite complete visible TM57 prefix is identical on all the $U_x$, including
actual guarded updates, arbitrary named left/right grafts, executed rejects,
fees, identities, archive snapshots, verification and consumer results. More
generally, after fixing a supplementary label and an acquired literal-answer
prefix in the declared hybrid contract, all intervening original numerical and
paid archive segments agree on its compatible bracket sources.

Proof. Initially the words are all $\alpha^{3m}$. At a common acquired prefix
the policy chooses the same original request and complete actual context.
Lemma 4.1 gives the same Read or guard response and preserves word equality.
Induction gives the same original records, control choices and stopping decisions.
PR57.T3 now gives the same complete visible primitive trace from those public
inputs and original responses. Its simulator covers precommit and partial-copy
cuts, not only finished macro calls; addresses, ticks, fees, availability and
identity serialization therefore supply no omitted channel.

PR57.D7/L4 copies the actual appended rows and contexts bit for bit. Their
public fields, including original preparation occurrence, request ordinal,
generation fields, context identities, response and fence references, agree.
PR57.D8/T4 verification is computation on the same snapshots, public prior and
proposal. It has the same surviving source set, refusal or receipt and consumer
output. It does not select a compatible source as the actual source. Repeating
archive reads, enumeration or authentic copies acts on equal acquired data.

For the conditional version, hold the label and literal-answer prefix fixed.
Lemma 3.2 makes the intervening current words common, so the same induction
applies to each numerical segment. An added literal segment contributes only
its declared answer and observations determined by that prefix. Its source-blind
visibility is the explicit added-port hypothesis of Definition 2.3. This does
not assert that original PR57.T3 already consumes native literal transcripts.

**Corollary 4.3 (actual modifications are included).** At $H=3m$, every $U_x$
accepts the first guarded $\rho$ and rejects the immediately following one:
the candidate leaf counts are $3m$ and $6m$. At $H\ge3m+1$, a graft of one
known alpha leaf followed by the first substitution can both accept. Thus
Theorem 4.2 covers genuinely evolving sources, not only Stop or reject examples.

Proof. Initially all $3m$ leaves are alpha. The first substitution makes them
beta without changing their number; the second doubles their number. A preceding
alpha graft gives an all-alpha tree with $3m+1$ leaves and the same calculation.

## 5. Sharp label, handoff and literal-work frontiers

**Theorem 5.1 (sharp complete-label/probe frontier).** Let $M\ge1$ and
$q\ge0$ be integers. A receiver with an authentic complete initial label of at
most $M$ attained values, no other initial source-dependent access, and at most
$q$ literal probes can recover every initial $U_x$ only if

$$
2^m\le M2^q,
\qquad
q\ge\max\{0,m-\lfloor\log_2 M\rfloor\}.
\tag{DAF.6}
$$

The necessity holds despite all comparison operations and all numerical/archive
services above. Over choices of truthful labels it is sharp when acquisition
can begin on the unmodified source. For $M\le2^b$ it implies $b+q\ge m$.

Proof. Fix an attained label. Between literal replies all accessible data and
choices are determined by that label and the acquired prefix, by Theorem 4.2.
Theorem 3.3 gives at most two reply branches at the next literal node. Terminal
reply paths are prefix-free: if one stopped path were a proper prefix of another,
common deterministic control and the same intervening observations would force
the other execution to stop there too. A binary tree of maximum query depth $q$
has at most $2^q$ terminal leaves, by induction on $q$, including early stops.
One correct output leaf can serve at most one $x$. Summing over at most $M$
labels gives (DAF.6). Taking integer ceilings yields the displayed logarithmic
form; no assumption that $M$ is a power of two is made.

For sufficiency put $k=\max\{m-q,0\}$. The inequality gives $2^k\le M$.
The producer supplies the first $k$ actual bracket bits. Before any modification,
the receiver queries $p_iL$ for each remaining bit and reconstructs $U_x$ with
the public scaffold. These are $m-k\le q$ genuine literal calls. The complete
label has at most $2^k$ attained values. Label acquisition/export/source binding,
program installation and all storage/runtime remain separate services and costs.
The original TM57 observer has no private-source complement-export port; this
attainment is conditional on the stated label channel. At $b=0$, the $m$-probe
algorithm is a genuine native controller on this promised family.

**Corollary 5.2 (success count).** Under a deliberately chosen uniform
evaluation distribution on $\{0,1\}^m$, any such deterministic reconstruction
protocol is correct on at most $M2^q$ members and has success probability at most
$\min\{1,M2^{q-m}\}$.

Proof. The same leaf count applies without assuming uniform correctness. Each
leaf outputs one tree and can be correct for at most one member. Uniform mass
converts this count to the displayed fraction. This is not a probabilistic
assumption about the physical source or a randomized-policy theorem.

**Theorem 5.3 (sharp read-only preparation/code-only handoff).** Fix integers
$p,q\ge0$, $M\ge1$. A common source-blind read-only preparer uses at most $p$
literal calls, leaves $U_x$ unchanged and passes only a complete code of at most
$M$ names. The receiver has no preparation transcript or other source-dependent
channel and uses at most $q$ fresh literal calls. Such exact reconstruction is
possible, conditional on this actual isolated handoff, iff

$$
p+q\ge m,
\qquad M2^q\ge2^m.
\tag{DAF.7}
$$

Proof. Preparation has at most $2^p$ terminal acquired paths by Theorems 3.3
and 4.2. Since its code is computed from those paths and common public data,
the attained code image has size at most $\min\{M,2^p\}$. The receiver proof
of Theorem 5.1 gives

$$
2^m\le\min\{M,2^p\}\,2^q.
$$

This implies both inequalities in (DAF.7). Conversely let
$k=\max\{m-q,0\}$. Those inequalities give $k\le p$ and $2^k\le M$.
Preparation reads $p_iL$ for $k$ positions and passes exactly those bits.
After the isolated handoff the receiver reads the remaining $m-k\le q$
positions. The source is unchanged and every bit comes from a truthful call.

The isolation is substantive. If preparation may modify the source, a
reply-dependent graft or update count can store information in the physical
register outside the code. If earlier archives, source-dependent timings,
controller states or names remain accessible, they belong in the complete code
alphabet. Neither native effective history accessibility nor TM57's copy
authenticity is itself this code-only isolation service.

**Theorem 5.4 (sharp per-run raw calls and submitted letters).** With no
supplementary source-dependent initial label, every uniformly correct
initial-$U_x$ reconstruction protocol makes, on every input run, at least $m$
informative literal calls, and has total submitted-address work at least

$$
\sum_{i=1}^m(d_i+1).
\tag{DAF.8}
$$

Both bounds are simultaneously attained by the initial queries $p_iL$. For a
perfect scaffold with $m=2^h$, the optimum is $m$ calls and $m(h+1)$ letters.

Proof. Fix one actual successful run. If it has no query distinguishing bit
$i$, flip that bit and hold every other bit fixed. Induct over the run. Common
acquired data gives common control, context choices and guard decisions.
Numerical/archive observations agree by Theorem 4.2. Queries involving another
coordinate agree by locality. Queries involving $i$ agree by the assumed absence
of a distinguishing suffix on this run. Hence the flipped source has exactly
the same complete acquired history, termination and output, a contradiction.
Thus every bit must be informatively queried. One query concerns at most one
bit; choose one for each $i$ and apply Theorem 3.3's length bound. At epoch zero
$p_iL$ has exactly length $d_i+1$ and reveals that bit, proving simultaneous
attainment. For the perfect scaffold every $d_i=h$.

The per-run claim uses no extra label. An arbitrary bounded label can have very
unequal fibers, so the worst-budget counting theorem is not a per-run assertion
that every input needs $m-b$ calls. Address work in (DAF.8) is not peak storage,
total tape work, runtime or physical energy.

**Corollary 5.5 (finite-cap structural price).** For every $H\ge3$, choosing
$m=\lfloor H/3\rfloor$ gives $b+q\ge\lfloor H/3\rfloor$ for exact
structural recovery in the augmented label/literal contract, despite arbitrary
authentic numerical archive work. The unchanged numerical-only observer fails
already at $H=3$.

Proof. Use the actual family (DAF.2) with $3m\le H$ and Theorem 5.1. With
$b=q=0$ its two $m=1$ members have equal complete numerical observations and
different literal responses at $L$.

## 6. Balanced words: exact two-window image and concentrated fibers

This regime varies leaf words as well as brackets. It does not inherit the
one-coordinate binary-branch bound.

**Definition 6.1 (balanced finite source slice).** Fix $s\ge1$, put $n=4s$,
$H=8s$, and let $\mathcal B_s$ be every actual ordered tree of composition
$(2s,2s)$, with all leaf orders and all bracketings retained. Write
$C_r=\frac1{r+1}\binom{2r}{r}$ for the Catalan number. The credited actual
shape/label correspondence gives

$$
T_s:=|\mathcal B_s|=C_{4s-1}\binom{4s}{2s}.
\tag{DAF.9}
$$

The source's first three candidate sizes are $4s,6s,10s$, so its initial
numerical boundary is tag 1:

$$
q_H(t)=(1,(2s,2s),E(t),E(\rho t)).
\tag{DAF.10}
$$

Use the faithful normal coordinates from TM28:

$$
N(\epsilon,k,p)=(-1)^\epsilon S^kA^p,\qquad
(\epsilon,k,p)(f,\ell,q)
=(\epsilon+f+p\ell\bmod2,\ k+(-1)^p\ell,\ p+q\bmod2).
\tag{DAF.11}
$$

For a word in this slice number beta occurrences $j=0,\ldots,2s-1$, and let
$a_j$ be the number of alpha leaves preceding beta $j$. Put

$$
z_j=(-1)^{a_j},\qquad
P=\sum_{j\text{ even}}z_j,\qquad
Q=\sum_{j\text{ odd}}z_j,\qquad
I_s=\{-s,-s+2,\ldots,s\}.
\tag{DAF.12}
$$

**Theorem 6.2 (exact balanced two-window image).** The normal exponents of
$E(t)$ and $E(\rho t)$ are

$$
(k_0,k_1)=(P-Q,P+Q),\qquad (P,Q)\in I_s^2,
\tag{DAF.13}
$$

and every point of $I_s^2$ is realized by an actual balanced word. Consequently
$q_H(\mathcal B_s)$ has exactly $(s+1)^2$ values.

Proof. A beta at position $a_j+j$, counting positions from zero, contributes
$(-1)^{a_j+j}$ to $k_0$ in (DAF.11); an alpha contributes zero. Thus $k_0=P-Q$.
In the substituted word alpha contributes the block $B$ and beta contributes
$BA=S$. Every beta block is even and contributes $(-1)^{a_j}$ to the exponent.
The $2s$ alpha blocks contribute alternating $+1,-1$, because only alpha blocks
change the preceding parity. They cancel. Hence $k_1=P+Q$.

Each of $P,Q$ is a sum of $s$ signs and lies in $I_s$. To realize any prescribed
sign word $z_0,\ldots,z_{2s-1}$, set $a_0=0$ if $z_0=1$ and $a_0=1$ otherwise,
then increment $a_j$ by one precisely when the desired sign changes. This gives
a nondecreasing sequence with last term at most $1+(2s-1)=2s$. The actual word

$$
\alpha^{a_0}\beta\,
\alpha^{a_1-a_0}\beta\,\cdots\,
\alpha^{a_{2s-1}-a_{2s-2}}\beta\,
\alpha^{2s-a_{2s-1}}
\tag{DAF.14}
$$

has exactly $2s$ of each atom and the required signs. Independently choose the
$s$ even and $s$ odd signs to attain every $P,Q\in I_s$. The map in (DAF.13)
is injective, with inverse $P=(k_0+k_1)/2$, $Q=(k_1-k_0)/2$.

TM29.5's actual-image phase formula here gives

$$
p_0=p_1=0,\qquad
\epsilon_0=(2s-k_0)/2\bmod2,\qquad
\epsilon_1=(4s-k_1)/2\bmod2.
\tag{DAF.15}
$$

Thus the exponent pair determines both whole windows, and normal-form
uniqueness gives the converse. Composition is fixed. Brackets do not affect the
associative product; every realizing word has actual full-tree bracketings.
This proves the exact number of initial boundary values on the whole slice.

**Lemma 6.3 (exact second moments).** For a uniformly enumerated balanced word,

$$
\mathbb E k_0^2=\frac{4s^2}{4s-1},\qquad
\mathbb E k_1^2=\frac{8s^2}{2s+1},\qquad
\mathbb E(k_0^2+k_1^2)<5s.
\tag{DAF.16}
$$

Proof. Let $J_r$ indicate a beta at position $r\in\{0,\ldots,4s-1\}$.
Then $k_0=\sum_r(-1)^rJ_r$. Uniform balanced enumeration gives
$\mathbb E J_r=1/2$ and, for distinct $r,u$,
$\mathbb E J_rJ_u=2s(2s-1)/(4s(4s-1))$. Since
$\sum_r(-1)^r=0$, the off-diagonal sign sum is $-4s$. Expansion gives

$$
\mathbb E k_0^2
=2s-\frac{2s(2s-1)}{4s-1}
=\frac{4s^2}{4s-1}.
$$

For $k_1$, distribute the $N=2s$ betas into the $r=2s+1$ gaps before, between
and after the alpha leaves. Each balanced word corresponds to exactly one weak
composition $(b_0,\ldots,b_{r-1})$ of $N$, so these compositions are uniform,
and $k_1=\sum_i(-1)^i b_i$. There are
$W=\binom{N+r-1}{r-1}$ of them. Differentiating one or two factors in
$\prod_i(1-z_i)^{-1}$ and then setting every $z_i=z$ gives the coefficient
identities

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E b_i&=N/r,\\
\mathbb E[b_i(b_i-1)]&=2N(N-1)/(r(r+1)),\\
\mathbb E[b_i b_j]&=N(N-1)/(r(r+1))\quad(i\ne j).
\end{aligned}
\tag{DAF.17}
$$

For example the corresponding total factorial moment is
$2[z^{N-2}](1-z)^{-(r+2)}=2\binom{N+r-1}{r+1}$; divide by $W$ for the second
line. Removing the factor 2 gives the mixed moment; one derivative gives
$\binom{N+r-1}{r}/W=N/r$. The $r$ signs sum to 1, so their off-diagonal
product sum is $1-r$. Expansion now yields

$$
\mathbb E k_1^2
=N+\frac{N(N-1)}r
=\frac{8s^2}{2s+1}.
$$

Finally the sum is
$5s+s/(4s-1)-4s/(2s+1)<5s$ for every integer $s\ge1$.

$$
|\mathcal F_s|\ge K_s,
\qquad
K_s=\left\lceil
\frac{C_{4s-1}\binom{4s}{2s}}
 {\min\{(s+1)^2,22s\}}
\right\rceil.
\tag{DAF.18}
$$

Choose a largest such fiber using a fixed public ordering to break ties, before
choosing the hidden source. On every member, one actual guarded $\rho$ accepts,

$$

$$

since $2\sqrt{5s}\le5s$ and $1\le s$. Thus some boundary fiber contains

## 11. 原生有限树的 q 取得桥

本节把一个 paid initial $q$ Read 精确展开为原生字面取得。它只增加一个已有来源、菜单、目标和费用的对应，不改写前十节的比较接口，也不把原来的长期目标缩成这个有限结果。

### 11.1 合同与量词

**定义 11.1（原生 q 取得合同）。** 令 $\mathcal T$ 是有限非空有序二叉树

$$
t::=\alpha\mid\beta\mid\langle s,t\rangle,
\qquad q(\alpha)=a,\quad q(\beta)=b,\quad q(\langle s,t\rangle)=q(s)\times q(t),
$$

其中 $a=e_1,b=e_2,k=b\times a$，并令 $S=\{0,\pm a,\pm b,\pm k\}$。$L(t)$ 是叶数；若 $P(t)$ 是从根到叶的地址集合，令

$$
E(t)=\sum_{p\in P(t)}|p|.
$$

一个原生取得器是固定的确定程序 $\delta:C\to\operatorname{Op}(C,S)$ 和固定初态 $c_0$，从配置 $(c_0,0,t,[])$ 开始；这里 epoch 为 $0$，物理来源是唯一的 $t$，取得记录为空。$\operatorname{Op}$ 只有 `localStep`、对当前来源的 literal `read`、无条件 `applyRho` 和带完整输出的 `stop`。程序的 transition 只接收 control；不存在对 register、epoch、clock 或未记录 history 的隐藏读取；已取得的 replies 可以按声明方式存入 control，历史仍只按实际发生次序追加。它必须对每个有限 $t$ 有限停止并输出初始来源的 $q(t)$，包括 $q(t)=0$ 的来源。

这是 all-domain 正确性合同。没有来源特定的程序或初始化、大小承诺、graft、数值 $q$ 端口、reset、copy 或 timing channel；所有 `applyRho` 都是同一寄存器上的真实更新。输出初始 $q(t)$ 的要求允许程序在已知更新次数后作有限算术解码，但不供应一个数值读口。下述不等式只对实际输入满足 $q(t)\ne0$ 的运行陈述；零输入仍属于正确性域和竞争者域。

**约定 11.2（实际资源）。** 给一条有限运行 $\tau$，令

$$
R(\tau)=|\operatorname{reads}(\tau)|,\qquad
J(\tau)=\{p:\text{某个 }\operatorname{read}(e,p,y)\text{ 出现在 }\tau\},
$$

并令 $W(\tau)=\sum_{\operatorname{read}(e,p,y)\in\tau}|p|$，重复调用重复计费。$R$ 是实际 Read 次数，$|J|$ 是不同 literal 地址数，$W$ 是提交地址字母的 tariff。$W$ 只有在每次查询都传送完整地址词时才等价于传送字母数；它不是 CPU 时间、能量或内存量。

### 11.2 叶锥见证与动态下界

对叶地址 $p\in P(t)$，写 $t^{p}$ 为只把该叶的 $\alpha/\beta$ 标签翻转所得树。$\rho$ 保持每个原有 branch constructor，因此在 $\rho^e t$ 中，原地址 $p$ 下仍有同一原叶的后继锥；若地址 $u$ 不以 $p$ 为前缀，则

$$
\operatorname{readout}(u,\rho^e t)=\operatorname{readout}(u,\rho^e t^p).
$$

这也覆盖 $u$ 在 $p$ 之上停止、离开该锥或落在另一个原锥的情形。没有 graft 时不会产生把一个未知来源出现复制到别处的第二锥。

**引理 11.3（非零 q 的每叶敏感性）。** 若 $q(t)\ne0$，则对每个 $p\in P(t)$ 都有 $q(t^p)\ne q(t)$。

**证明。** 对叶直接由 $a\ne b$ 得到。设 $t=\langle s,u\rangle$。非零叉积迫使 $q(s),q(u)$ 是互相正交的非零轴；翻转位于 $s$ 时，归纳假设给出 $q(s^p)\ne q(s)$。在七值集合 $S$ 上固定正交轴 $q(u)$，映射 $z\mapsto z\times q(u)$ 在所有与 $q(s)\times q(u)$ 相等的候选中只能取 $z=q(s)$：其余 $0,\pm q(u)$ 和第三轴的差均不平行于 $q(u)$。故叉积改变；右子树同理。证毕。

**定理 11.4（自适应 rho 下的叶锥见证）。** 令 $\delta,c_0$ 满足定义 11.1 的 all-domain 正确性。对任意有限树 $t$、任意由控制器根据已经读到的回复自适应产生的 `localStep`/`applyRho` 调度，以及该调度的实际正确停止运行 $\tau$，若 $q(t)\ne0$，则每个 $p\in P(t)$ 都有事件

$$
\operatorname{read}(e,u,y)\in\tau,\qquad p\preceq u.
$$

**证明。** 假设某个 $p$ 没有这样的事件。沿 $\tau$ 逐事件重放来源 $t^p$：local step 和 applyRho 的控制转移不看来源；对每次 read，假设和叶锥事实给出相同回复，所以控制、epoch、所有更新、记录和 stop 均相同。两条运行因而输出相同值，但正确性要求它们分别输出 $q(t)$ 与 $q(t^p)$，这与引理 11.3 矛盾。这里 $t^p$ 可以是零 q 竞争者，故若只在 promised-nonzero 域声明正确性，重放论证并不成立。证毕。

**推论 11.5（同时的 sourcewise 资源下界）。** 在定理 11.4 的条件下，对每个 $q(t)\ne0$ 的实际运行，

$$
R(\tau)\ge |J(\tau)|\ge L(t),\qquad W(\tau)\ge E(t).
$$

**证明。** 从每个叶锥见证事件各选一次。叶地址两两 prefix-incomparable，所以所选事件的地址彼此不同，给出 $R\ge|J|\ge L$；每个所选地址长度至少为其叶深度，求和给出 $W\ge E$。额外重复读和更深地址只增加相应费用。证毕。

### 11.3 无 graft 的来源与 epoch 账

**引理 11.6（原始来源证书）。** 在任意无 graft 的前缀中，若已经成功执行了 $e$ 次 `applyRho`，当前寄存器严格为 $\rho^e(t)$；所有 local steps、Reads、replies 和 executed stop/reject 仍按顺序留在记录中。对两个共同获得的前缀，控制器选择的下一 primitive 只由共同控制和共同记录决定。因而 source-independent 的 `applyRho` 只能旋转当前 q，

$$
q(\rho^e t)=R^e q(t),\qquad R^3=I,
$$

不能制造第二个来源、擦掉原始 epoch 或把旧版本回复当作新版本缓存。该来源证书是 `NativeSerialDepth.transfer` 所用的同一 replay 条件；定理 11.4 的叶锥论证允许任意 $e$ 的自适应序列，因此不把固定 epoch 偷换成静态查询模型。

**边界 11.7（零 q 与资源坐标）。** 证明下界时 $q(t)\ne0$ 只用于保证每个叶都能被翻转见证；它不是取得器的输入承诺。零 q 树和翻转后落入零 q 的树仍必须被同一个 all-domain 程序正确处理。localStep 数、applyRho 次数、控制存储、输出计算、地址生成和物理寻址都不被 $R,|J|,W$ 偷换计入；本节只结算 literal Read 事件及其提交词长。

### 11.4 完整原生生产与 comb 量级

**定理 11.8（未改变来源的完整上构造）。** 复用 `NativeFullSourceProducer.sourceControl` 和 `initialControl`，并把已取得的完整树在 handoff 控制中作有限的 $q$ 计算；只把原 producer 的 terminal `stop` 替换为进入 handoff 的 source-independent `localStep`，此前每个 Read、回复和 localStep 保持不变。对任意有限 $t$，该共同程序从 $(c_0,0,t,[])$ 逐节点访问根和所有后继节点，保留全部 literal 回复、epoch、local steps 与停止前的顺序记录；它不执行 `applyRho`，物理来源始终为 $t$。若 $\tau_t$ 是这条实际记录，则

$$
R(\tau_t)=|J(\tau_t)|=2L(t)-1,
\qquad W(\tau_t)=2E(t)-2L(t)+2.
$$

**证明。** `nodes t` 恰含根及每个内部节点和叶，每个地址只访问一次；因此节点数为 $2L-1$，且这些地址就是 $J$。更具体地，叶时 trace 是根地址的一次带实际 `alpha/beta` 回复的 Read；分支时 trace 先记录根的 `branch` Read，再按左子树、一个 `localStep`、右子树、一个 `localStep` 的实际次序追加。于是 `reads` 是 `(0,address,reply)` 的完整列表，而不是只保留终态的摘要。设内部节点深度和为 $I(t)$。根分解给 $I(t)=E(t)-2L(t)+2$，故所有节点地址长度和为 $I+E=2E-2L+2$。生产程序的有限终止、epoch 不变和实际读记录由 `native_source_producer` 的同一逐树归纳提供；handoff 只在这条完整前缀之后增加 source-independent local steps。

令 $C_1=\beta$、$C_{m+1}=\langle\alpha,C_m\rangle$。这是叶字为 $\alpha^{m-1}\beta$ 的 alpha/beta comb，且 $q(C_m)$ 沿 $\pm b,\pm k$ 的有限循环，始终非零。它满足

$$
L(C_m)=m,\qquad E(C_m)=\frac{m^2+m-2}{2},\qquad
R(\tau_{C_m})=2m-1,\qquad W(\tau_{C_m})=m(m-1).
$$

所以非零 comb 给出线性实际 Read 数和二次地址长度；下界只保证相同的线性、二次量级，不主张这些常数或该构造在所有程序中的最优性。

### 11.5 Process Geometry 44.8 的消费者边界

**命题 11.9（stationary q handoff）。** Process Geometry 44.8 的 paid initial $q$ Read 可以由定理 11.8 的 literal 前缀实现：在最后一个实际节点 Read 后，以 source-independent `localStep` 进入 44.8 的反馈控制，交付解码出的 $q(t)$；handoff 发生在 absorbing `stop` 之前。此阶段保持原来源 $t$、epoch $0$ 和完整 chronological record，因而其真实 trace 与一个单独的 numeric-port event 不同。44.8 后续反馈若使用 graft 或其他额外权限，仍属于该消费者自己声明的菜单，不属于本原生取得合同。

三位只编码 $S$ 中取得的方向值（包括零的编码）；它们不编码完整 observer state、来源语法、地址档案、控制、epoch 或记录。因而这里没有总 observer-memory 上界。该桥只覆盖这个 paid initial $q$ 读；不能推出每一个 feedback function 都支付本节的 acquisition 下界，也不能说任意使用 $\rho$ 的取得器都保持原来源。

### 11.6 方法归属、未决边界与长目标

**记录解释 11.10。** 生产 trace 中的 `read(0,p,y)` 是一次实际根起始地址提交，`localStep` 是实际控制动作；没有把递归调用栈、解码表或 handoff 交付伪装成来源信息。重复地址若由另一阶段再次出现仍计入 $R$ 和 $W$，而 $J$ 只去重字面地址。这个区分正是动态 adaptive schedule 与静态 distinct-address 集合之间的接口。


对 zero competitor 的处理是本节 all-domain 条件的关键：即使某个 $t^p$ 恰好落在 $q=0$，它仍要有有限真实运行，且该运行的控制和记录不能被 promise 排除。相反，若把目标改成“只在 nonzero 输入上输出”，则下界至多是一个不同的 promised problem，不能回填这里的 replay contradiction。

本节还保留三种不同对象：初始来源 $t$、当前寄存器 $\rho^e(t)$、以及由 literal replies 解码出的有限语法记录。它们在无 graft 的 handoff 前可以互相按声明的映射关联，但不因此成为数值端口、完整 observer state 或物理取得。该区分也是后续任何 continuation correspondence 必须重新证明的接口。

敏感性、certificate 和确定性 decision-tree 的一般方法归于 Buhrman–de Wolf 的 2002 survey（Definition 2、Proposition 1，pp.8–9）；它们不是本仓库的新发明。仓内 supplier 是 `NativeSerialMachine` 的真实菜单与 `Run`/`transfer`、`NativeDepthPatterns` 的原地址局部性，以及 `NativeFullSourceProducer` 的固定完整生产。本文的 repo-derived 增量只是把这三类既有材料装配成精确的原来源、原目标、实际地址 tariff 和 Process Geometry 44.8 消费者对应；没有新增 Lean 验证或文献原创性声明。

本桥不关闭原任务的六项缺口： (i) complete finite B-program correspondence；(ii) actual acquired I/O and exact UJB；(iii) unchanged original-port continuation and records；(iv) coherent whole-resource backing；(v) spatial/boundary continuation correspondence；(vi) complete proof/check/reconciliation/acceptance。PR26130 与 experiment PR168 仍是分开的未完成草稿。C133 的 relation-to-N5 与 FIB full64 same-partition 义务也原样保留。更长的目标仍是寻找尽可能精简的关系结构，并在内部 lawful observers 下证明 space、time、boundary、memory 何时互相恢复；本节的有限 q 桥既不完成该目标，也不替它改写这些未决义务。

**接受判据与反例边界。** 该桥的必要条件是七值 q 表、同一来源的四动作菜单、全域正确性和真实 chronological record 同时成立。若发现一个非零 q 树及其单叶翻转具有相同 q，或发现不读该叶锥即可在零 q 竞争者上保持同一完整 trace 而输出仍正确，则定理 11.4 立即失效；若 handoff 以 numeric-port 事件替代 literal trace，或在 handoff 前发生 graft/reset/copy，则命题 11.9 也不再是本合同。现有材料没有给出这些反例，因而本节结论是该明确合同下的 propose/accept，而不是超出其量词的普遍获取定律。
