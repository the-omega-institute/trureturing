# Finite source frames and effective complete-field memory

> This reference volume uses `generic-v1`. Its statements and proofs are ordinary
> mathematics, not new Lean verification. Published content is immutable;
> corrections and extensions belong after the final append anchor. All repository
> mathematical suppliers are the versions at the immutable revision

This volume derives finite coordinate barriers from the complete acquired input,
without a supplied barrier list or contact certificate. A terminating admission
test recognizes a class on which joint-prefix persistence has an exhaustive
finite-exclusion or all-depth finite-source answer. On the entire finite target-row
image, it also computes the minimum number of extra complete field values needed
for a uniform finite-read service. Its finite parameters are fixed before the
all-depth quantifier. A signed six-hole example consumes both conclusions and has
no entire atomic-output anchor.

The admitted class is an increment in the unchanged programme of recovering
spatial, temporal, boundary and memory expressions from minimal relations. It
neither completes that programme nor decides its unrestricted joint-prefix
question. Source frames and certificates below are mathematical constructions;
only actual paid acquisition supplies runtime observations.

## 1. Native sources, complete input and authentic service

**Definition 1.1 (native trees and four-valued prefixes).** Let

$$
\mathcal T::=\alpha\mid\beta\mid\langle s,t\rangle,
\qquad
\rho\alpha=\beta,
\quad \rho\beta=\langle\beta,\alpha\rangle,
\quad \rho\langle s,t\rangle=\langle\rho s,\rho t\rangle.
\tag{FFM.1}
$$

Every source is a nonempty finite free ordered tree. Repeated occurrences are
retained; equal values at different occurrences do not identify their positions.
An address is a finite word over $\{L,R\}$, with root $\varepsilon$. The reply
$r_u(p)$ is one of $\alpha,\beta,\mathsf{branch},\mathsf{absent}$; strict
extensions of a leaf are absent. The entire subtree $u|_p$ is defined only when
$p$ is reached. The height $\operatorname{ht}(u)$ is its maximum leaf depth,
with root depth zero. Put

$$
p_h(u)=(r_u(p))_{|p|\le h},
\qquad u\equiv_h v\iff p_h(u)=p_h(v),
\qquad h\in\mathbb N.
\tag{FFM.2}
$$

Prefixes are inclusive and include absence. They are valid attained reply tables,
not arbitrary strings of four symbols. Powers of $\rho$ have fixed finite natural
exponents in the native constraint syntax. The operation is interpreted by
(FFM.1), not a free unary constructor. Its bare action is nonexpanding in the
prefix topology; no strict contraction is assumed without an explicit pair guard.

**Definition 1.2 (the entire acquired fiber).** Fix
$I=(P,R,H_0,H_{\rm rel},Q,N)$ as in [ARH, Definitions 1.1–1.2][ARH]. The cuts
$P,Q$ are finite prefix-free sets of original addresses. The complete row $R_A(u)$
at a cut $A$ has the ordered reached subset $A\cap\operatorname{Pos}(u)$, the
whole concrete numbered-hole exterior, and the complete equality partition of the
whole reached subtrees. Empty cuts and the root cut are allowed. The old row $R$
is attained. A literal $(a,p,c)$ requires $r_{\rho^a u}(p)=c$. A signed record
$e=(a,p,b,q,\bowtie)$ requires

$$
\operatorname{Rel}_e(u)\iff
p\in\operatorname{Pos}(\rho^a u)\ \land\ q\in\operatorname{Pos}(\rho^b u)
\ \land\ (\rho^a u)|_p\ \bowtie\ (\rho^b u)|_q,
\qquad \bowtie\in\{=,\ne\}.
\tag{FFM.3}
$$

Both endpoints must be reached for either sign. Lists retain repetitions. Every
record within one realization concerns that same original $u$ at its stated
actual epoch. Set

$$
D=\{u\in\mathcal T:R_P(u)=R,\ H_0(u),\ H_{\rm rel}(u)\},
\qquad f(u)=R_Q(u),\qquad D_r=\{u\in D:f(u)=r\}.
\tag{FFM.4}
$$

Every old equality, cross-class and off-task disequality, absent-address
constraint and concrete exterior remains imposed. The target is the full original
$Q$-row, including its entire exterior; it is not selected partition bits.

**Assumption 1.3 (paid complete handoff).** Service conclusions require exactly
[ARH, Assumption 1.3 and Proposition 2.1][ARH]. One common source-total paid
producer acquires the old row, literals and endpoint descriptions on one
original-root register, compares those paid finite descriptions, and reaches an
authentic certified $N$ at least every recorded epoch. A complete access cut
leaves an accessible state that recovers $I$ and factors through $I$ and common
source-independent parameters. Its entire original-source fiber is $D\ne\varnothing$.
Every accessible source-bearing cache, address, count, length, control position,
timing field, permission, reference, archive and handle is included. This is an
access premise, not a physical-erasure theorem.

A post-cut consumer starts at the actual $\rho^N u$. It is deterministic,
effective, correct and terminating on every $u\in D$. Its actual source actions
are paid four-valued $\mathsf{Read}(p)$ at any finite original-root physical word,
real forward same-register $\mathsf{ApplyRho}$, and absorbing $\mathsf{Halt}$.
There is no source-selector, rank, equality, reset, reroot, clone, clock,
old-version or limit-tree port. Computation and updates supply no unlisted
source-dependent information. Preparation, descriptions, comparison, complete
cuts, authentication, local work, literal word length, updates, retention, memory
and new reads have separate charges. Sourcewise finite acquisition does not imply
a uniform finite acquisition cost. The theorem does not certify an actual
physical handoff; that empirical premise is ASSUMED-UNVERIFIED unless separately
supplied.

**Definition 1.4 (selected joint persistence).** Supply a finite set $B$ of
distinct attained complete rows, $B\subseteq f(D)$ and $|B|\ge2$. Define

$$
J_B(h)\iff
\exists (u_r)_{r\in B}\in\prod_{r\in B}D_r\quad
\forall r,s\in B\quad u_r\equiv_h u_s.
\tag{FFM.5}
$$

There is one common original prefix and one legal finite source for each full
row. These sources are alternative originals of the common producer contract.
No single original carries incompatible labels, and unrelated pairwise witnesses
do not suffice. Every component separately satisfies the whole input (FFM.4).

**Assumption 1.5 (exact ordinary suppliers).** The following pinned contracts are
used with their original domains. [NSF, Lemma 5.1 and Theorem 5.2][NSF] compile
fixed native observations, signed guards and complete row tests exactly and decide
finite-source existential feasibility with original witnesses. [NPL, Theorem 4.1,
Corollary 4.2 and Proposition 4.3][NPL] decide the exact finite or infinite image
of a designated native expression, including fixed navigation and auxiliary
variables; finite images are completely enumerated with original witnesses.
[NPL, Theorem 5.3][NPL] decides and enumerates the entire finite complete-row
image. [NLC, Theorem 4.1 and Corollary 4.2][NLC] decide simultaneous minimum-leaf-depth
escape for a nonempty finite tuple of designated native outputs, retaining the
whole predicate. A DEEP answer constructs a whole finite assignment for every
requested depth; a BARRIER answer bounds the minimum of that tuple on every
original solution. These are ordinary proof suppliers, not evidence of a newly
compiled application.

## 2. Automatically generated exact source frames

**Definition 2.1 (a source frame).** A frame consists of a finite cell list,
ordinary constructor terms $W_\eta(v_1,\ldots,v_{k_\eta})$, complete predicates
$\Phi_\eta$, reached original representative addresses $p_{\eta,i}$, and a
computable inclusive cell-separation depth $d$. Every coordinate occurs at its
representative address, and repeated occurrences of its name use the same value.
The required maps are

$$
\begin{aligned}
V_\eta&=\{\mathbf v\in\mathcal T^{k_\eta}:\Phi_\eta(\mathbf v)\},
&D_\eta&=\{W_\eta(\mathbf v):\mathbf v\in V_\eta\},\\
W_\eta(\mathbf v)|_{p_{\eta,i}}&=v_i,
&D&=\coprod_\eta D_\eta.
\end{aligned}
\tag{FFM.6}
$$

Filling and taking these actual representative subtrees are inverse on a cell.
Two members of $D$ with equal $d$-prefixes lie in the same cell. Predicates admit
NSF's fixed-observation syntax and its exact compiler. In particular no condition
is replaced by its marginal projection. $W_\eta$ itself contains only finite
grounds, pairing and shared coordinate names, not a native transport decorator.
Zero-coordinate cells are allowed.

**Proposition 2.2 (two input-generated frames, with both source directions).**
The complete input effectively generates the following two frames without any
supplied representation certificate: the coarse old-row class frame, and the
finite original-prefix refinement of [ARH, Proposition 3.2][ARH].

Proof. For the coarse frame let $C$ be the old numbered-hole exterior and
$\pi$ its partition. Choose one reached original representative $p_b$ per old
class. Set $W(\mathbf v)=C[(v_{[j]})_j]$. Impose all pairwise inequalities
$v_b\ne v_c$ for distinct classes, all old absent-address conditions, and every
literal and signed predicate of (FFM.4) on this same filled tree. There is one
cell and $d=0$. Filling gives the stipulated concrete exterior, reached set and
partition and satisfies every retained record. Conversely any original $u\in D$
gives $v_b=u|_{p_b}$, equal at all occurrences of its class and different across
classes; filling reconstructs this same $u$. The recover/fill inverse is the
full correspondence of [NativeCutFibers, `rowFiberEquiv`][CUT], including
`legal_values_row` and the private `recover_fill` and `fill_injective` proofs.
It retains classes outside the target. Navigation into a coordinate is handled
by NSF's compiler, not by an observer reading that unknown coordinate for free.

For the refinement choose the finite natural number

$$
a=1+\max\bigl(\{0\}\cup\{|q|:q\in Q\}
 \cup\{|p|:(n,p,c)\in H_0\}
 \cup\{|p|,|q|:(n,p,m,q,\bowtie)\in H_{\rm rel}\}\bigr).
\tag{FFM.7}
$$

Replace each old class by each possible original depth-$a$ pattern: early leaves
are concrete and reached frontier positions have coordinate names. Reuse one
pattern and its coordinates at every occurrence of the class. Names are distinct
for different classes and frontier positions. These finitely many pattern choices
define the cells and their ordinary terms $W_\eta$. Choose one original occurrence
of each frontier name as its representative $p_{\eta,i}$.

Retain the whole old-class inequalities between their filled patterns. Every
fixed endpoint either reaches a displayed original subterm, whose epoch-$n$ value
is its interpreted $\rho^n$ image, or enters a displayed early leaf's finite
iterate, with its actual reach guard computed before either sign is applied.
This is ARH Proposition 3.2's endpoint analysis. It supplies exactly equivalent
literal and whole signed conditions; invalid endpoints reject a cell for either
sign. It also retains every uncut residual in the full target exterior. Filling
a satisfying valuation therefore gives an original in $D$. Conversely an
original in $D$ chooses its unique actual class patterns and actual frontier
subtrees, satisfies those same guards and signs, and is reconstructed as itself.
This proves both directions and disjointness, not just existence of replacement
witnesses. The depth-pattern inverse is [NativeDepthPatterns, `depthEquiv`][DEPTH],
with its private `fill_decompose` and `decompose_fill` proofs.

Let $d=\max_b(|p_b|+a)$, with empty maximum zero. An inclusive prefix through $d$
determines every chosen class pattern: at depth $a$ it already determines whether
the frontier is reached, and below it records every early leaf and absence.
Thus equal $d$-prefixes determine the same cell. Coordinates are actual reached
subtrees in either direction, and all their repeated uses remain shared. With
no old classes the exterior is already a concrete source; there is a single
zero-coordinate cell. All constructions are finite. $\square$

**Lemma 2.3 (finite trees are isolated by inclusive prefixes).** If a finite
tree $t$ and a finite or infinite valid native tree $s$ have the same replies
through $\operatorname{ht}(t)$, then $s=t$. If $t$ is the actual subtree of $u$
at a reached original address $p$, agreement of $u,v$ through
$|p|+\operatorname{ht}(t)$ forces $v|_p=t$.

Proof. At every leaf of $t$, the common reply specifies that same leaf label;
valid trees have no descendants there. At every branch it specifies both reached
children. Induction on $t$ determines the whole tree. Prefix agreement at $p$
first gives its reached ancestors and then this entire subtree table. $\square$

## 3. Synthesized finite barriers and decidable admission

**Definition 3.1 (exact finite coordinate projections).** On either generated
frame decide the whole projection

$$
Y_{\eta,i}=\{v_i:\Phi_\eta(\mathbf v)\},
\qquad
F_\eta=\{i:Y_{\eta,i}\text{ is finite}}.
\tag{FFM.8}
$$

For $i\in F_\eta$, enumerate the exact finite native set
$A_{\eta,i}=Y_{\eta,i}$. Discard empty cells by NSF first. The finite index list is

$$
\mathcal F_\eta=\prod_{i\in F_\eta}A_{\eta,i}.
\tag{FFM.9}
$$

The product over no indices has one empty tuple. For $\mathbf f\in\mathcal F_\eta$
and a full row $r$ put

$$
D_{\eta,\mathbf f,r}=
\{W_\eta(\mathbf v):\Phi_\eta(\mathbf v),\
 v_i=f_i\ (i\in F_\eta),\ R_Q(W_\eta(\mathbf v))=r\}.
\tag{FFM.10}
$$

These slices retain the entire joint predicate. A tuple of individually possible
finite coordinates can be jointly impossible; all its slices are then empty.
Such tuples are harmless indices, not invented realizations. The finite barrier
parameters $\eta,\mathbf f$ are chosen before any all-depth witness quantifier.

**Definition 3.2 (the canonical frame contact).** Let $\widehat{\mathcal T}$ be
the finite or infinite valid ordered native trees, with the same reply convention,
and let $\Omega$ have a branch at every address. Set

$$
K_{\eta,\mathbf f}=W_\eta(\mathbf f,\Omega\text{ on every coordinate outside }F_\eta).
\tag{FFM.11}
$$

For $X\subseteq\mathcal T$, membership $K\in\overline X$ means that every finite
inclusive prefix of $K$ is realized by some actual finite member of $X$. It
imposes no retained disequality or row partition on $K$. Every (FFM.11) is
rational, having finitely many distinct subtrees: a finite constructor term uses
finite grounds and $\Omega$. This is a mathematical limit description, not a
new runtime source.

**Proposition 3.3 (terminating projection, slice and contact computations).**
From the complete input and one generated frame, one can compute (FFM.8)–(FFM.10),
decide whether each selected slice is empty, finite nonempty or infinite, and
list every source of every finite slice. For an infinite slice one can decide
$K_{\eta,\mathbf f}\in\overline{D_{\eta,\mathbf f,r}}$, with finite whole-source
witnesses for every requested depth on a positive answer.

Proof. For each coordinate designate $v_i$ as NPL's output, with all other
coordinates and constraints still present. NPL Theorem 4.1 and Proposition 4.3
give its exact projection and finite list, including any hidden compiler
witnesses. This computes $F_\eta$ and the finite index list. In a slice designate
the entire $W_\eta(\mathbf v)$ as output and impose the complete row test by
NSF Lemma 5.1. NPL Corollary 4.2 enumerates its exact finite source image or
returns infinitude. Every output source satisfies all of (FFM.10). Conversely
any original source in that slice gives its actual representative coordinates
and is in that output image. No direction identifies a marker family with all
solutions.

Let $L_\eta=\{1,\ldots,k_\eta\}\setminus F_\eta$. If it is empty, all coordinates
are fixed, and the slice has at most one source; an infinite slice cannot occur.
Otherwise designate the nonempty tuple $(v_i)_{i\in L_\eta}$ under the whole slice
predicate for NLC Corollary 4.2. Write $\mu(t)$ for the minimum leaf depth of a
finite tree, so $\mu(t)>h$ is equivalent to its agreement with $\Omega$ through
$h$. The exact equivalence needed here is

$$
K_{\eta,\mathbf f}\in\overline{D_{\eta,\mathbf f,r}}
\iff
\forall h\ \exists\mathbf v\text{ satisfying (FFM.10)}\quad
\forall i\in L_\eta\quad\mu(v_i)>h.
\tag{FFM.12}
$$

For the right-to-left implication, fill these same coordinates at every repeated
occurrence. Agreement of each with $\Omega$ through $h$ implies agreement of the
ordinary whole term with (FFM.11) through $h$, by constructor induction; the fixed
coordinates remain exactly $\mathbf f$. For the reverse implication put
$b_\eta=\max_{i\in L_\eta}|p_{\eta,i}|$. A source in the slice agreeing with $K$
through $h+b_\eta$ has, at each representative address, only branch replies
through its relative depth $h$. Its actual coordinate therefore has $\mu(v_i)>h$.
These are the actual coordinates recovered from that original source. Both
implications preserve the same original and the entire slice.

NLC's EMPTY/DEEP/BARRIER trichotomy now decides (FFM.12). The slice is already
known infinite and hence nonempty; DEEP constructs all coordinates together,
including auxiliaries, and filling gives the required finite original. BARRIER
bounds their simultaneous minimum and refutes (FFM.12). Separate unboundedness
of individual coordinates is insufficient. Native normal ranks, scalar/block
incidence, positive feedback, normality and negative records remain inside the
supplier's full joint predicate until it constructs a whole assignment. They are
not removed by (FFM.8). $\square$

**Definition 3.4 (recognized frame class and OUTSIDE).** A generated frame is
admitted for $B$ if every slice (FFM.10), $r\in B$, is either empty, finite, or
infinite with a positive canonical contact test in Proposition 3.3. The
recognizer generates and tests the coarse frame first and the prefix refinement
second, stopping at an admitted frame. If both fail it returns OUTSIDE. Every
list and supplier call is finite and terminating. No unbounded enumeration of
certificates or contexts is performed. OUTSIDE leaves the unrestricted
$\forall h\ J_B(h)$ question undecided; a failed canonical test is not a proof of
finite exclusion.

## 4. Exhaustive joint persistence on an admitted frame

**Definition 4.1 (an effective exclusion depth).** For an admitted frame and
selected $B$, let $\mathcal L_{\eta,\mathbf f,r}$ be the complete source list of a
finite slice, including the empty list. Compute

$$
\begin{aligned}
H_B=\max\bigl(&\{d,0\}\ \cup\
 \{|p_{\eta,i}|+\operatorname{ht}(t)+1:
       \eta,\ i\in F_\eta,\ t\in A_{\eta,i}\}\\
 &\cup\{\operatorname{ht}(u)+1:
       \eta,\ \mathbf f\in\mathcal F_\eta,\ r\in B,
       \ D_{\eta,\mathbf f,r}\text{ finite},\
       u\in\mathcal L_{\eta,\mathbf f,r}\}\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\tag{FFM.13}
$$

Empty contributions add no values; all maxima and lists are finite. The extra
one makes the isolation bounds deliberately conservative. $H_B$ need not be the
least exclusion depth.

**Theorem 4.2 (automatic finite-barrier joint alternative).** For complete input
(FFM.4), distinct attained $B$ with $|B|\ge2$, and a frame admitted by Definition
3.4,

$$
\boxed{
\forall h\in\mathbb N\ J_B(h)
\iff
\exists\eta\ \exists\mathbf f\in\mathcal F_\eta\
 \forall r\in B\quad D_{\eta,\mathbf f,r}\text{ is infinite}.}
\tag{FFM.14}
$$

If the right side holds, a terminating construction for each requested $h$
returns one actual finite source in every entire $D_r$, all agreeing with the
same $K_{\eta,\mathbf f}$ through $h$. If it fails, $\neg J_B(H_B)$ with the
explicit computed (FFM.13). Thus admission yields a terminating exhaustive
decision, not merely successful recognition of a particular contact.

Proof. If the right side holds, choose that one finite parameter tuple
$\eta,\mathbf f$ once. Admission gives DEEP for each of its selected infinite
slices. For a requested $h$, Proposition 3.3 returns a whole finite source
$u_r(h)$ in each slice, agreeing with that same (FFM.11) through $h$. Every
constraint and every full finite row label is true on its own witness. Thus all
sources agree with one another, proving $J_B(h)$. This uses neither separate
pairwise contacts nor signs on a completion point.

For the converse assume that the right side fails, and suppose a tuple witnesses
$J_B(H_B)$. Cell separation first places all sources in one $D_\eta$. At each
finite coordinate choose its value $t$ from one tuple component. It belongs to the
exact $A_{\eta,i}$. By (FFM.13), agreement reaches its representative address and
beyond $\operatorname{ht}(t)$. Lemma 2.3 forces this same actual coordinate in
every component. Hence the entire tuple has one common $\mathbf f$, satisfying
the original joint constraints in every component.

There is at least one selected row $r$ whose slice at that $\eta,\mathbf f$ is
not infinite. An empty slice cannot supply its supposed witness. For a finite
slice the witness $u_r$ belongs to its exact list, so (FFM.13) and Lemma 2.3 isolate
that whole source. Every other component with the same $H_B$-prefix is this very
$u_r$. One original has a unique complete $Q$-row; it cannot lie in two distinct
$D_r$. Since $|B|\ge2$, this is a contradiction. Thus $\neg J_B(H_B)$, and also
$\neg(\forall h\ J_B(h))$.

This negative argument excludes every possible joint prefix, not only prefixes
near the contacts inspected by admission. The positive construction and this
exclusion are disjoint and exhaustive because the finite slice classification
is exact. Termination follows from Proposition 3.3 and finitely many index,
source-list and maximum computations. Positive witness calls occur only after
the finite decision; their source sizes can depend on $h$. $\square$

**Corollary 4.3 (degenerate domains).** Logical emptiness of $D$ is decidable by
NSF; it has no acquired-entry or field-minimum conclusion. Unattained labels or
non-row descriptions fail the input check. For a nonempty $D$, a singleton
attained $B$ has $J_B(h)$ for every $h$, using one finite source; an empty $B$ is
vacuous. These cases do not use the distinct-row exclusion argument. Duplicate
serialized labels must be identified as the same full row before applying the
set formulation. A frame with no coordinates, or a split with no live coordinates,
has only finite slices. A nonempty finite entire $D$ therefore has finite prefix
determination, independent of admission: take one more than the maximum height of
its sources. The root target cut has one constant row; an empty target cut returns
the entire source as its exterior, so an infinite source fiber then has infinite
row image.

Proof. Feasibility and row validity are NSF's complete-source queries. Empty
products and singletons have their usual unique-tuple meanings. Coordinate-free
filling yields a single ground tree. Lemma 2.3 isolates each source in a finite
source list. The statements about the two cuts follow directly from Definition
1.2. None supplies a physical source for an empty logical fiber. $\square$

## 5. The entire finite row image and the minimum complete field

**Definition 5.1 (whole-image admission and multiplicities).** Use NPL Theorem
5.3 on the complete input. If it returns the exact finite nonempty image
$A=f(D)$, run Definition 3.4 with all $r\in A$ and use one resulting admitted
frame throughout this section. Put

$$
t_{\eta,\mathbf f}
 =|\{r\in A:D_{\eta,\mathbf f,r}\text{ is infinite}\}|,
\qquad
M_* =\max\bigl(\{1\}\cup\{t_{\eta,\mathbf f}:\eta,\mathbf f\}\bigr).
\tag{FFM.15}
$$

Define $H_A$ by (FFM.13) with all rows in $A$. For an attained prefix $p$ let
$S_h(p)=\{r\in A:\exists u\in D_r, p_h(u)=p\}$ and
$m_h=\max_p|S_h(p)|$. These sets always test the entire original source.

**Theorem 5.2 (computed optimal depth multiplicity).** Under whole-image
admission,

$$
\boxed{m_{H_A}=\min_{h\ge0}m_h=M_*.}
\tag{FFM.16}
$$

All quantities on the right and the minimizing depth $H_A$ are effectively
computed from $I$, without a supplied contact or minimizing-depth certificate.

Proof. A common $H_A$-prefix determines one cell and all its finite coordinate
values, by the argument of Theorem 4.2. If it meets a finite row slice, a witness
from that slice is isolated as a whole finite source by (FFM.13); consequently
this prefix meets exactly one row. Otherwise every row meeting it has an infinite
slice at the same $\eta,\mathbf f$, so its multiplicity is at most
$t_{\eta,\mathbf f}$. Hence $m_{H_A}\le M_*$.

For every fixed $\eta,\mathbf f$ with $t_{\eta,\mathbf f}>0$, admission and
Proposition 3.3 give, at every depth, finite originals from all those infinite
row slices with one prefix of $K_{\eta,\mathbf f}$. Thus $m_h\ge t_{\eta,\mathbf f}$
for every $h$. Also $m_h\ge1$ because $D\ne\varnothing$. Therefore $m_h\ge M_*$
for every $h$, proving (FFM.16). The lower bound uses a finite joint tuple at each
depth, rather than pretending a limiting partition has all its finite labels.
For $t=0$ or $1$ it uses nonemptiness as appropriate. Every finite computation
uses the exact entire $A$, so an omitted off-task row cannot reduce the answer.
$\square$

**Definition 5.3 (paid extra complete field).** Use [SJM, Definition 2.1][SJM].
A field $E:\mathcal T\to C$ is source-total effective, with finite nonempty
conditional image $E(D)$. It is actually computed from the same acquired original
before the complete cut. Its program and installed data depend only on $I$ and
common source-independent parameters. The accessible state after that paid cut
recovers and factors through $(I,E(u))$. Let $M(D,Q)$ be the least $|E(D)|$ for
which a common legal consumer recovers the full $R_Q(u)$ with uniformly finitely
many post-entry reads. The field is an additional acquisition outcome; an offline
projection list is not already this field.

**Theorem 5.4 (whole-image memory and uniform service criterion).** Under
Assumption 1.3, finite nonempty $A$, whole-image admission, and Definition 5.3's
paid field premises,

$$
\boxed{M(D,Q)=M_*,}
\qquad
\boxed{\text{uniform finite-new-read unassisted service exists}
       \iff M_*=1.}
\tag{FFM.17}
$$

The same field minimum holds for all legal adaptive serial-forward services and
for static services at authentic $N$. A computable optimal encoder and static
consumer are obtained at $H_A$. Cardinality here is neither bits nor total
resource cost. No minimum raw-read, update or literal-length price is claimed.

Proof. The complete-field equality is the exact application of [SJM, Theorem
2.2][SJM], $M(D,Q)=\min_hm_h$, with $D,f,I$ exactly (FFM.4), all rows $A$ supplied
by NPL, and a computed minimizer from Theorem 5.2. Its lower bound covers arbitrary
effective fields, including fields not constant on a row: for each field value a
uniformly bounded four-reply serial tree has finitely many submitted addresses.
A common sufficiently deep original prefix makes two same-field runs identical,
by original-address locality at every declared epoch. Different full rows with
that prefix require different complete field values. It follows that every
admissible field has at least $m_h\ge M_*$ attained values.

For its constructive upper bound, order the exact list $A$ by serialization.
At supplied $H_A$ decide, for every finite four-valued table $p$ and $r\in A$,

$$
\exists u\in D_r\quad p_{H_A}(u)=p.
\tag{FFM.18}
$$

These are NSF's fixed-coordinate whole-source queries. Discard tables meeting
no row. The remaining lists are exactly $S_{H_A}(p)$. During paid original
acquisition compute $f(u)$ and $p_{H_A}(u)$ and retain the zero-based rank of the
actual row in its list. On concrete sources outside $D$ use default rank zero;
concrete membership is decidable, so this extends the encoder source-totally.
The cut must actually make every remaining source-bearing field factor through
$(I,E(u))$. A prefix realizing $m_{H_A}$ attains all its ranks, so there are
exactly $M_*$ conditional values. This is SJM's encoder, now with an effectively
synthesized minimizing depth, not a free source selector.

At the actual register $\rho^N u$, recover the original $H_A$-prefix by
[DelayedOriginalReadout, `original_decoder_correct` and
`original_decoder_service`][DELAY]. The paid current replies determine its list;
the retained rank determines the whole row. A static menu has upper bounds

$$
q\le2(2^{H_A+1}-1),\qquad a=0,\qquad
\max |p|\le H_A+\lceil N/2\rceil,
\quad
\ell\le2(2^{H_A+1}-1)(H_A+\lceil N/2\rceil).
\tag{FFM.19}
$$

Every actual read is charged, including absence and repetitions. These bounds
exclude preparation, local compilation and retention charges. They neither
identify the offline candidate with the actual original nor issue old-version
reads.

If $M_*=1$, (FFM.16) says every attained $H_A$-prefix has one full row, so the
same static decoder without an informative field recovers it. Conversely an

## 7. Limits of admission and the remaining programme

**Proposition 7.1 (canonical admission is incomplete).** Existing attained

Proof. SPC's exact attained family uses a proper finite one-hole context $C$,

$L_C=\langle K,\langle K,K\rangle\rangle$ with $K=C[\rho^a K]$.

the hole address, side trees are $\rho^{aj}g$ for a fixed nonempty finite ground

distinct finite subtrees. Bare $\rho$ is not used as a contraction; the context's
proper hole supplies the guard.

If either frame were admitted for that pair, Theorem 4.2 and its actual all-depth
witnesses would supply some $K_{\eta,\mathbf f}$ in both exact strata's closures.
But that point is rational by Definition 3.2 and the common point is uniquely
non-rational, a contradiction. Thus admission is not universal. This argument
uses the published exact supplier family, not a pending source or a limit
partition carrying finite signs. It refutes only universal admission by this
contact language. $\square$
