# Concurrent acquired clock bridges for complete native boundary records

## 1. Sources, acquisition and the exact retained interface

**Definition 1.1 (native history).** Let $\mathcal T$ be the finite nonempty
ordered full trees generated by $\alpha,\beta$ and pairing. The only source
change is

$$
\rho\alpha=\beta,\qquad \rho\beta=\langle\beta,\alpha\rangle,
\qquad \rho\langle x,y\rangle=\langle\rho x,\rho y\rangle.
\tag{CAB.1}
$$

An observer submits an entire materialized finite word $p\in\{L,R\}^*$
to atomic $\operatorname{Read}(p)$, receiving $\alpha,\beta,\mathsf b$
or $\bot$ according as its position is a leaf, a branch or absent. The
empty word is legal. A nonempty continuation beyond a leaf is absent.
An independent writer can apply any finite number of global updates
between any two observer events, including local steps and Return.
There is one authentic register and root throughout. There is no reset,
rerooting, other mutation, clock port, equality port, subtree handle,
snapshot, source size, installed source table or scheduler observation.

The observer is a fixed effective finite program with source-independent
initialization. Its public cut and expressly declared public parameters
are its inputs. It cannot update the source. Safety is required on every
legal returning history. Termination is required when the total number
of updates during the pending call is finite, but unknown, and each
pending observer operation eventually executes. No protected entry or
known interference count is assumed. A validation Read and the subsequent
Return are distinct events.

**Definition 1.2 (complete row).** For a finite prefix-free cut $P$, let
$O=P\cap\operatorname{Pos}(u)$. The exterior $C_P(u)$ replaces exactly
the reached subtrees by holes marked with their entire addresses and
retains every other constructor and concrete leaf. Define

$$
R_P(u)=(C_P(u),\pi_P(u)),\qquad
p\sim_{\pi_P(u)}q\ \Longleftrightarrow\ u|_p=u|_q\quad(p,q\in O).
\tag{CAB.2}
$$

Different classes assert disequality. Every blocked cut word has its
concrete blocking strict-prefix leaf in the exterior. This is the
`literalRecord` of [ACPD], including its faithful correspondence
`canonicalRow_record_iff`; it is not a vector of constructor replies.
Empty, root, deep, mixed and blocked cuts are allowed. An empty cut
retains the whole tree; a root cut has one constant hole and singleton
partition.

**Definition 1.3 (handoff and recovery).** For $m\ge1$, chronological successful captures
have designated validation events $v_0\le\cdots\le v_{m-1}$ on the same
authentic pure history. Put $t=U_{v_0}$ and write
$U_{v_i}=\rho^{n_i}t$, where $0=n_0\le\cdots\le n_{m-1}$ count genuine
updates between these events. The history promise supplies no $n_i$.
A later decoder receives exactly the ordered public cuts, complete rows,
and supplementary records specified by its theorem, together with this
promise. It has no new hidden-source access or historical archive.

All accessible source-dependent state at a handoff counts: guard and
operand words, replies, control, counters, attempts, transcripts, earlier
outputs, pointers, region identities, lengths and allocation distinctions.
Our positive constructions clear and retract nonoutput regions, reset
control, and use fixed output-region identities with extents determined
by canonical output bytes. Cleanup is finite paid computation. A host
retaining extra fields has a richer interface and must charge them.
The output is acquired data, not a freely replayable authenticity
certificate. The common-root promise is not certified by matching clocks.

**Definition 1.4 (separate costs).** Use the charged finite-sequence model
and row encoding of [CWS, Definitions 2.1–2.2][CWS]. Sixteen-symbol cells cost
four bits. An exterior has tokens for branches and concrete leaves;
a hole has `H`, its literal address and a delimiter. After the exterior
come its delimiter, every upper-triangular equality bit, and the row
terminator. With $c$ exterior cells and $r$ holes the row has
$s=c+\binom r2+2$ cells. A private row candidate becomes output by ownership
transfer, without a second payload copy.

For a call with invocation source $u$ and $b$ updates through Return, put

$$
V=\rho^b u,\quad N=|\operatorname{Pos}(V)|,\quad H=\operatorname{height}(V),
\quad M=|P|,\quad K_P=1+\sum_{p\in P}(|p|+1),
\quad F=N+K_P+\binom M2+2,
\quad m_* =\max(1,M-1).
\tag{CAB.3}
$$

These are analysis parameters, not observer advice. Count $T$ actual
Reads, $A=\sum|p|$ delivered address letters, and
$Q=\max(\{0\}\cup\{|p|:\operatorname{Read}(p)\})$ separately.
Also separate auxiliary writable paths/control, complete operand payload,
row and supplement candidates, actual final retained payload, immutable
program/public input, and local bit/cell work. A scalar register uses its
binary length. An immutable long literal still costs its installed bytes
and full delivery. Source storage, writer work and port-internal copies
are additional implementation costs, never assigned zero.

**Mathematical citation 1.5 (supplied mechanisms).** [CNA, Theorem 2.2][CNA]
supplies persistence of present positions and branches, and the certificate
that two equal leaf replies at one address enclose no update. [CWS,
Definition 2.4 and Theorems 3.3–4.4][CWS] supplies RA: descend left to a guard
leaf, construct the complete exterior by stackless preorder, compare
every pair of hidden subtrees by rereading their constructors in common
left-first preorder, then reread the guard. A mismatch ends a comparison;
equal branches descend and equal leaves move to the next pending right
sibling. It stores paths and the candidate row, not hidden-tree values.
Its every attempt is finite under finite interference, its failed
validation intervals are disjoint, and

$$
J\le b+1,\qquad T_j\le H+2+m_*N,\qquad A_j\le HT_j,
\qquad Q\le H,\qquad |B_j|\le F.
\tag{CAB.4}
$$

The monotone finite final tree bounds moving traversals, including failed
ones. RA's complete pair comparisons retain both equalities and
disequalities. All uses below retain this body, including its treatment
of blocking leaves. [DOR] supplies the right clock
$c(\alpha)=0,c(\beta)=1,c(\langle x,y\rangle)=2+c(y)$,
its exact advancement, and its right-ray reply intervals. [IDC, §6][IDC]
supplies exponential/binary acquisition on a stationary source. The
constructions below connect these suppliers to new retained consumers
and account for their moving attempts.

## 2. A phase-sensitive relation already acquired by the row guard

**Definition 2.1 (left guard stamp).** Set

$$
\ell(\alpha)=0,\qquad\ell(\beta)=1,\qquad
\ell(\langle x,y\rangle)=1+\ell(x).
\tag{CAB.5}
$$

For a leftmost leaf at depth $d$ with label bit
$e=\mathbf1_{\{\beta\}}$, this is $d+e$. Define RA$\ell$ to be RA
with one change at acceptance: before releasing its left-only guard
and leaf label, retain the canonical integer $k=d+e$ beside its full
row. Its validation event remains the final guard Read. Encoding and
cleanup follow that event and use no source operation.

**Theorem 2.2 (all-cut acquisition with no extra Reads).** For every source,
cut and legal returning history, RA$\ell$ returns exactly
$(R_P(U_v),\ell(U_v))$ at its designated validation event $v$. It adds
zero Reads and zero delivered address letters to the same RA run,
including failed attempts. Under unknown finite interference and observer
progress it terminates with at most $b$ failures and $b+1$ attempts.
For every chronological authentic family of these captures, the decoder

$$
\operatorname{Dec}\bigl((P_i,R_i,k_i)_i\bigr)_{ij}=k_j-k_i
=n_j-n_i
\tag{CAB.6}
$$

recovers all relative update counts from the declared retained inputs.

Proof. Structural induction gives $\ell(\rho u)=\ell(u)+1$: alpha
goes from zero to one, beta goes from one to
$1+\ell(\beta)=2$, and an original pair reduces to its left child's
identity. Iteration gives advancement by the exact update count. In
the notation of [NAR, Proposition 12.8][NAR], its canonical density
formula also gives $\varphi^{\ell(u)}=W(u)f_u(L^\infty)$: the terminal
word on that ray is $L^d$ and has weight $d$. This applies the same
boundary-density mechanism to the other extreme ray; clock arithmetic
itself is not a new acquisition result.

Every branch on the acquired guard path persists. When RA first observes
the leaf at $L^d$, it is therefore the current leftmost leaf. The equal
validation reply certifies no update from that observation through the
whole row computation and validation. The candidate is the complete row
of $U_v$ and $d+e=\ell(U_v)$. Saved finite data still encode that value
if updates occur during encoding, clearing or before Return. The Reads,
attempt boundaries and failure charges are those of RA. Its finite
attempt proof and finite added arithmetic give termination. Substitution
of $\ell(U_{v_i})=\ell(t)+n_i$ proves (CAB.6). $\square$

**Proposition 2.3 (literal and retained cost of the stamp).** RA$\ell$
has the bounds (CAB.4). Its auxiliary writable storage, excluding the
row and operand payloads, is
$O(H+1+\log(K_P+2))$ bits, including all heads and control. The dedicated
operand region uses at most $4(H+1)$ bits; the row candidate at most
$4F$ bits. Forming the stamp needs $O(\log(H+2))$ temporary bits.
For the retained natural $k$, Elias gamma coding of $k+1$ uses

$$
g(k)=2\lfloor\log_2(k+1)\rfloor+1
\tag{CAB.7}
$$

payload bits, with any container framing charged separately. Immutable
input and code cost $4K_P+I_{\rm code}+O(1)$ bits. On return only
the actual $4s$ row bits, the stamp and their framing remain.

Proof. The extra arithmetic uses the already stored guard length and
label, and $k\le H+1$. RA's resource proof applies to every failed
candidate and path. No stamp is appended for a failed validation.
Gamma coding and clearing use finite bit operations and do not alter
the query trace. A direct implementation adds
$O((1+\log(H+2))^2)$ local bit operations to the successful attempt,
in addition to RA's charged local work. Candidate and output ownership
are not simultaneous copies. $\square$

**Proposition 2.4 (row-determined omission).** If the retained exterior's
leftmost descent reaches a concrete leaf rather than a hole, the stamp
in Theorem 2.2 may be omitted and reconstructed from that row. The
presence rule depends only on the row. This includes every empty cut
and any blocked cut leaving that leaf exposed.

Proof. The literal path and label give exactly $d+e$. If the walk reaches
a hole this argument gives no hidden leaf information, and the specified
protocol retains the stamp. The decoder can make the same presence
decision from its actual row, without an extra mode channel. $\square$

## 3. A cheap disconnected family and a complete-state obstruction

**Definition 3.1 (asymmetric and equal-hole families).** Put
$T_n=\rho^n\alpha$. Let $t_h$ be the perfect height-$h$ binary tree
whose leaves are alpha, $h\ge1$, and put

$$
u_h=\langle\alpha,t_h\rangle,\qquad
\bar u_h=\langle t_h,\alpha\rangle,\qquad
U_a=\langle T_a,T_a\rangle.
\tag{CAB.8}
$$

Use the cut $P=\{L,R\}$. Both hidden holes are reached and address-marked.
The complete row of every iterate of $u_h$ has their disequality bit;
that of every $U_a$ has their equality bit. There is no literal hidden
subtree in either row. Each row has ten cells in Definition 1.4.

**Theorem 3.2 (same-program asymmetric acquisition bounds).** On a
quiescent capture of $\rho^n u_h$, the all-source program RA$\ell$ has,
with $D=\max(1,n)$,

$$
T=3D+3,\qquad A=\frac{3D^2+5D}{2},\qquad Q=D,
\qquad k=n+1.
\tag{CAB.9}
$$

Its row candidate has exactly ten cells and its auxiliary-plus-operand
storage is $O(D+1)$ bits, independent of $h$. In particular, at $n=0$
the actual costs are six Reads, four letters and peak length one,
although $|\operatorname{Pos}(u_h)|=2^{h+1}+1$.

For a call on this ray with $b$ updates and final exponent $n_f$, every
attempt, successful or failed, has the same upper bounds with
$D=\max(1,n_f)$, and the call has at most $b+1$ attempts. Every candidate
still has ten cells; no bound depending only on $h$ is substituted for
the actual temporal exponent.

Proof. The native recurrence is
$T_0=\alpha,T_1=\beta,T_{n+2}=\langle T_{n+1},T_n\rangle$.
Thus its leftmost leaf depth is $\max(n-1,0)$, and the global guard
depth of $\rho^n u_h$ is $D$. The exterior body reads only the root.
The comparison of the two holes starts at their roots and follows only
left suffixes. At each level before the left leaf, both replies are
branches; at that leaf the right reply is still branch, since its
leftmost depth is greater by $h$. This proves actual disequality and
ends the comparison before any sibling is visited. There are exactly
$D$ compared relative positions.

The guard search reads depths $0,\ldots,D$, validation reads depth $D$,
and the comparison reads two words at each depth $1,\ldots,D$.
Adding the root exterior Read gives (CAB.9), including the two replies
at the final mismatch. The stamp advances from $\ell(u_h)=1$.

Under interference a right comparison reply occurs no earlier than its
left partner. Its subtree has at least the partner's current left depth
plus $h$. A left branch is therefore followed by a right branch, and
a left leaf by a right branch. No moving comparison enters a sibling.
The guard and comparison depths are bounded by the final left depth,
so the same sums bound every attempt. The cut always hides the two
children, making the row size constant even on failed attempts.
All path lengths and indices are $O(D+1)$. $\square$

**Proposition 3.3 (an incomparable exact-clock competitor).** Any
deterministic source-blind all-source procedure acquiring the exact
right clock $c(u)$ on quiescent inputs must, on $u_h$, query
$R^{h+1}$. Consequently its peak operand and total letter traffic there
are at least $h+1$. In contrast, on $\bar u_h$, replacing RA's left
guard search by right descent gives a validated right stamp $c=2$ with
six Reads and four letters. Neither fixed extreme-ray choice dominates
both orientations.

Proof. Flip only the rightmost alpha leaf of $u_h$ to beta. The two
sources have identical native replies at every address other than
$R^{h+1}$; strict extensions are absent in both. Their complete
$\{L,R\}$ rows are the same disequality row, whereas their right
clocks differ by one. If the procedure omitted that address on $u_h$,
deterministic transcript induction would make it omit it on the flipped
source and return the same value, a contradiction. This compares the
exact $c$ target, not every sufficient temporal relation.

For $\bar u_h$ the right guard is alpha at depth one, the exterior reads
the root, and the pair comparison terminates at its first branch/alpha
mismatch. The guard search, comparison and validation give the stated
counts. Its left stamp instead equals $h+1$ and its left guard is deep.
Both protocols still work on all sources without family advice. $\square$

**Theorem 3.4 (unbounded complete inter-capture distinctions).** Consider
any deterministic initially source-blind effective protocol correct for
exact two-capture lag recovery on all native sources under Definition
1.1, with both cuts $\{L,R\}$. It may retain arbitrary data, use arbitrary
finite source-independent immutable code/literals, and make the second
producer depend causally on the first state. Let $S_a$ be its entire
accessible state after a quiescent first call on $U_a$, including all
earlier outputs. Then the $S_a$ are pairwise distinct. On $0\le a\le A_0$
there are at least $A_0+1$ retained values, requiring at least
$\lceil\log_2(A_0+1)\rceil$ bits of fixed-width capacity. RA$\ell$
attains exactly this alphabet by keeping the constant row and $k=a+1$.

Moreover the first call on each $U_a$ must submit some word of length
at least $1+\lfloor a/2\rfloor$. This bounds both its peak materialized
operand and its total delivered letters, but not its universal mutable
workspace.

Proof. If $S_a=S_{a'}$ for $a\ne a'$, choose $B=\max(a,a')$ and,
after the first Returns, apply $B-a$ and $B-a'$ genuine updates to the
respective same registers. Both second invocations now have actual source
$U_B$ and the same accessible state. With a paused writer their entire
second computation, Reads and outputs coincide, but the required lags
are different. Each alternative is a lawful pure history; no source is
reset. This contradiction includes any causally chosen later encoder.
The alphabet count follows, and (CAB.5) gives the attaining values.

The minimum leaf depth of $T_a$ is $\lfloor a/2\rfloor$, from its displayed
recurrence, as in [NLC, Lemma 3.1][NLC]. Thus every address shorter than
$1+\lfloor a/2\rfloor$ replies branch on both $U_a$ and $U_{a+1}$.
If all queries on the former were shorter, their deterministic first
computations would coincide through termination and give equal states,
contrary to the first part. A paid immutable word could supply the
necessary long operand with small mutable selection state; hence operand
length alone proves no universal mutable-space minimum. $\square$

**Proposition 3.5 (final compression does not remove intermediate cost).**
In the zero-lag histories on $U_a$, the two final rows and lag zero are
identical for all $a$, but every universal protocol of Theorem 3.4 still
needs its distinct $S_a$ at the intervening handoff.

Proof. The protocol must also tolerate the unequal lawful future bursts
used in that theorem. The future zero-lag restriction cannot be used
before it is known. A final relative output may discard its baseline;
this does not retroactively remove the acquired baseline state. $\square$

## 4. A fast acquired right guard and matching atomic-family traffic

**Definition 4.1 (total moving right search).** At a present base address
$a$, read $a$. A leaf finishes with its actual label and relative depth
zero. On branch, probe $aR^1,aR^2,aR^4,\ldots$. A leaf finishes with
its actual depth and label. On the first absent reply save its exponent
$r$ and the last branch exponent $l$, allowing $l=0$. While $r-l\ge2$,
read $aR^{\lfloor(l+r)/2\rfloor}$. A leaf finishes; branch replaces $l$;
absence replaces $r$. If the endpoints become adjacent without an
observed leaf, finish with INVALID. Never infer an unobserved leaf
label. All powers are fully materialized literal words.

This is [IDC, Definition 6.1][IDC]'s search with a finite failure exit for
mixed replies. A returned leaf is an authentic observation regardless
of earlier search motion. On a stationary source the strict branch/absent
bracket contains the rightmost leaf, so INVALID is impossible.

**Theorem 4.2 (right search as the row guard).** Replace RA's left search
by Definition 4.1 at the root. On INVALID clear and restart. On a leaf
at $R^d$ with bit $e$, save $d,e$, execute the unchanged complete-row
body, and validate by rereading $R^d$. Accept only the identical leaf,
and return the complete row and $z=2d+e$. This single source-blind
program returns exactly $(R_P(U_v),c(U_v))$ for every source, cut and
returning history. It terminates under unknown finite interference and
observer progress. The total number of failed searches and failed row
validations together is at most $b$.

With $h_R=\lfloor c(V)/2\rfloor$ and
$S_R=2\lceil\log_2(h_R+2)\rceil+4$, each round satisfies

$$
T_j\le S_R+1+m_*N,\qquad
Q\le\max(H,2h_R),\qquad A_j\le\max(H,2h_R)T_j.
\tag{CAB.10}
$$

There are at most $b+1$ rounds. For the root cut the body has no Reads,
so $T_j\le S_R+1$, $Q\le2h_R$ and $A_j\le2h_RT_j$.

Proof. For a rightmost leaf at depth $d$, [DOR] gives $c=2d+e$.
Matching this leaf at final validation makes the entire intervening
row body stationary by the native certificate. Earlier search Reads
need not belong to that interval. This proves joint correctness at
$v$ and, by the supplied advancement law, relative-lag recovery by
subtraction of the acquired $z$ values.

For termination, the finite final right depth bounds every exponential
branch reply. If it is positive, the first power of two at least that
depth is at most twice it and cannot reply branch. Bisection decreases
the positive numerical width strictly, even if the upper reply is stale.
There are at most $S_R$ search Reads, and no search word exceeds
$2h_R$; when $h_R=0$ the root Read is already a leaf. Every RA body
is finite by its final-tree bound. A search INVALID cannot occur with
no update during that search, and a failed validation cannot occur with
no update between its leaf and reread. The successive failure intervals
are disjoint, including across these two kinds of failure. Their
combined number is therefore at most $b$. They are not two independent
retry allowances. RA's body bound then gives (CAB.10). $\square$

**Proposition 4.3 (stationary search does not transfer without an exit).**
On $T_6$ the replies at $R^2$ and $R^4$ are branch and absent. Apply
two global updates, then pause the writer. The midpoint $R^3$ on $T_8$
replies branch and leaves the stored bracket $(3,4)$ adjacent. A
floor-midpoint loop without a width check repeats depth three forever.
Definition 4.1 instead ends this round with INVALID.

Proof. The right clocks are six and eight. The exact reply intervals
of [DOR] give all three replies. The repeated midpoint would remain
three after the writer has stopped. Thus finite interference alone
does not make that stationary-only loop total. $\square$

**Proposition 4.4 (right-guard storage and local work).** Theorem 4.2
uses at most $4F$ row-candidate bits, $O(\log(H+2))$ stamp-candidate
bits, $4(\max(H,2h_R)+1)$ operand bits, and
$O(H+1+\log(K_P+2))$ auxiliary bits. Its immutable installation is
$4K_P+I_{\rm code}+O(1)$. At a root cut the row has four cells and
auxiliary search control can be $O(\log(h_R+2))$ bits, but its mutable
flat operand still uses $O(h_R+1)$ bits. Retention consists only of
the actual complete row and a framed gamma code of $z+1$.

Proof. Generate each right power from a binary exponent, retaining only
the two endpoints, current midpoint, leaf exponent and label. Other
paths and row indices are RA's. Candidates are cleared before retry,
so failed capacities do not accumulate. The fixed number of integers
has logarithmic width. Add to [CWS, Proposition 4.5][CWS]'s row work at most
$O(S_R(H+1+L^2)+L^2)$ elementary bit/cell operations per round for
search, word generation, arithmetic and cleanup, where
$L=1+\lceil\log_2(F+K_P+H+2)\rceil$. This deliberately loose finite
bound charges literal construction and avoids a unit-cost integer.
All other accounts follow by ownership transfer and canonical cleanup.
$\square$

**Theorem 4.5 (all-program atomic-orbit lower bound and attained orders).**
Let an arbitrary deterministic source-blind protocol recover exact
two-capture lag on all native sources, with root cuts and the complete
state convention of Theorem 3.4. For an analysis integer $B\ge2$,
consider its quiescent first calls on $T_n$, $B\le n\le2B$.
Their complete first states must have at least $B+1$ distinct values.
Put $d_B=\lfloor B/2\rfloor$ and
$q_B=\lceil\log_4(B+1)\rceil$. On some one of these calls it submits
at least $q_B$ words of length at least $d_B$. Hence

$$
\max T\ge q_B,\qquad \max A\ge d_Bq_B,
\qquad \max Q\ge d_B.
\tag{CAB.11}
$$

Theorem 4.2 on these same inputs attains worst-case orders
$\Theta(\log(B+2))$ Reads, $\Theta(B\log(B+2))$ letters and
$\Theta(B)$ peak operand length, with $O(\log(B+2))$ retained stamp
bits and a constant complete row. These are source-family orders,
not universal mutable-space optimality or exact Read constants.

Proof. Continue each first call by $2B-n$ genuine updates and a
quiescent second call at the identical $T_{2B}$. Equal complete first
states would produce identical continuations with distinct required
lags. This proves the state separation even if later producers are
causal functions of the retained state.

The native saturation supplier `atomic_extremes` in [CLI] says
$w(p)+2\le n$ implies a branch reply, with
$w(p)=\#L(p)+2\#R(p)\le2|p|$. Every address shorter than $d_B$
therefore replies branch on this entire family. Contract all query
nodes with one surviving reply in the finite decision tree induced
by these terminating calls. It has at least $B+1$ distinct terminal
states and degree at most four. A tree all of whose paths have fewer
than $q_B$ branching nodes has fewer than $B+1$ leaves. Some run
therefore contains $q_B$ informative queries, all of length at least
$d_B$. The traffic product in (CAB.11) is on that same run.

For attainment, $c(T_n)=n$ and its rightmost depth is $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$.
(CAB.10) gives the upper orders, and the state and traffic lower bounds
give the matching orders. The source has $2F_{n+1}-1$ nodes, where
$F_0=0,F_1=1$, by the native recurrence. The protocol avoids acquiring
that exponentially large hidden literal tree. In comparison RA$\ell$
on these root cuts uses $n+1$ Reads for $n\ge2$. $\square$

## 5. One acquired invariant for complementary cuts and literal memory

**Definition 5.1 (exposed coordinates and calibration).** Reuse the
absence-safe coordinates of [NAR, Definition 11.2][NAR]. For $w$, let
$a(u,w)$ be its longest reached prefix, and put

$$
J_w(u)=w\!t(a(u,w))+c(u|_{a(u,w)}),\qquad
w\!t(a)=\#L(a)+2\#R(a).
\tag{CAB.12}
$$

They obey $J_w(\rho^n u)=J_w(u)+n$, including blocked words.
Write $j_L=J_L,j_R=J_R$ and $\delta=j_R-j_L$. Explicitly,

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
u&j_L(u)&j_R(u)&\delta(u)\\\hline
\alpha&0&0&0\\
\beta&1&1&0\\
\langle x,y\rangle&1+c(x)&2+c(y)&1+c(y)-c(x).
\end{array}
\tag{CAB.13}
$$

These $j_L$ coordinates are not the leftmost stamp $\ell$ of §2.
The row of $P=\{R\}$ determines $j_L$ from its entire concrete left
tree or its blocking root leaf. The row of $Q=\{L\}$ analogously
determines $j_R$. The integer $\delta$ is invariant under $\rho$.

**Definition 5.2 (acquired complementary calibration).** In an RA attempt
for $P=\{R\}$, construct the entire row candidate. If its root is a
concrete leaf, set the calibration candidate to zero. Otherwise its
left exterior is a complete finite tree $x$; compute $c(x)$ by a
finite scan of those paid bytes. The reached right hole has persistent
base $R$. Apply Definition 4.1 below it to acquire a provisional
$c(y)$. On INVALID reject the attempt. Otherwise set
$\delta'=1+c(y)-c(x)$ and perform RA's original final left-guard Read.
Accept the row and $\delta'$ only on its identical leaf reply.
Only the complete row and a canonical signed integer are exported.

**Theorem 5.3 (joint calibration acquisition and complementary recovery).**
Definition 5.2 is safe for all finite sources and terminates under
unknown finite total interference and observer progress, with at most
$b$ failed attempts. Suppose its first handoff is $(A,\delta)$ at $v_0$,
and a later ordinary RA call at cut $\{L\}$ returns only its complete
row $B$ at $v_1$ on the authentic common history. The decoder with
exactly $A,\delta,B$ and these public cuts computes

$$
n=j_R(B)-j_L(A)-\delta.
\tag{CAB.14}
$$

This is the genuine update count between $v_0$ and $v_1$. It also
effectively recovers the entire first captured source $U_{v_0}$ and
every finite intermediate source $\rho^iU_{v_0}$ for $0\le i\le n$.

Proof. Matching the saved left leaf brackets every row Read, comparison,
local scan and hidden-clock Read in one stationary interval. Hence
the accepted candidate is the exact complete row and (CAB.13)'s
calibration at $v_0$. A blocked root leaf needs no hidden query. For
a candidate pair the root was reached as branch, so its right base
persists even in a failed attempt. Definition 4.1 and RA are finite
under the final-source bound. INVALID or failed validation is impossible
without an update after the saved guard. Those intervals are disjoint,
so the same finite-interference argument gives at most $b$ failures.

The exposed coordinates both advance by one. Thus
$j_R(B)=j_R(U_{v_0})+n$ and (CAB.14) follows. In particular the
beta-to-pair birth satisfies $j_L=j_R=2$ after the update; no child
is falsely assumed present before its birth. The equation holds for
zero lag and negative calibration as well.

If $A$ is a blocking root leaf, it already gives the whole first
source. Otherwise write it as $\langle x,H_R\rangle$. Because an
original pair stays paired, $B=\langle H_L,\rho^n y\rangle$ for
$U_{v_0}=\langle x,y\rangle$. Apply [IDC, Reuse 2.2][IDC]'s effective
inverse-image parser $n$ times to the actual retained right tree.
The one-step image grammar is the disjoint union
$\beta$, $\langle\beta,\alpha\rangle$, and pairs of image trees;
their inverse values are respectively alpha, beta and pairs of
inverses. Disjointness makes the inverse unique. Authenticity assures
that these $n$ inverse operations succeed and recover $y$. Pair it
with the retained $x$ and iterate (CAB.1) locally for the requested
finite sequence. These are computations on retained descriptions,
not inverse actions or fresh queries on the source. $\square$

**Proposition 5.4 (all-attempt calibration costs).** Set
$S_H=2\lceil\log_2(H+2)\rceil+4$. Each attempt of Definition 5.2 obeys

$$
T_j\le H+2+N+S_H,\qquad
A_j\le H(H+2+N)+(2H+1)S_H,\qquad Q\le2H+1.
\tag{CAB.15}
$$

Multiply the per-attempt bounds by $b+1$ for a call. A row candidate
uses at most $4(N+5)$ bits, a calibration candidate
$O(\log(H+2))$ bits, auxiliary paths/control $O(H+1)$ bits, and a
complete operand region at most $4(2H+2)$ bits. The code and two
fixed cuts are constant source-independent installation. The retained
decoder payload is the two actual full rows plus the signed calibration.

Proof. There are no hidden-pair comparisons at a singleton cut.
The RA bound applies with $K_P=3$. A hidden search uses at most
$S_H$ Reads, with a one-letter base and at most twice its final
subtree right depth. This gives the loose safe bound $2H+1$, including
absent probes and failed attempts. Every row path is in $V$; the
syntactically complete candidate left tree has height at most $H-1$.
Its computed $c$ and a provisional acquired hidden $c$ are bounded by
$2H+1$, so their difference has logarithmic width even before rejection.
All failure allocations are cleared before the next attempt.

For a signed integer $z$, map it to the natural $2z$ when $z\ge0$,
and to $-2z-1$ when $z<0$, then gamma-code that natural plus one.
This costs $O(\log(|z|+2))$ bits with paid framing. On a successful
source $|\delta|\le2H+1$. The candidate scan and arithmetic can be
implemented in addition to CWS's local-work bound within
$O((F+(H+1)S_H)L^2)$ bit/cell operations, with
$L=1+\lceil\log_2(N+H+8)\rceil$. It accounts for materialization,
binary indices and cleanup without a stored hidden tree. $\square$

**Proposition 5.5 (reconstruction is a further paid consumer).** Theorem
5.3 does not make its later literal row or reconstructed source free.
For $X=U_{v_0}$ and $Y=\rho^nX$, writing all intermediate trees requires
their actual total literal size $\sum_{i=0}^n|\operatorname{Pos}(\rho^iX)|$
if all are retained. A streaming consumer may retain only the current
tree and its requested output, but pays for every emitted cell.

Proof. The rows are literal data by definition. The inverse parser and
forward substitution visit finite descriptions; simple sequence scans
and copying yield finite local work and storage bounded by a polynomial
in $n+1$, $|\operatorname{Pos}(Y)|$ and the input sizes. Retaining all
listed trees includes each cell of each description. No part of the
acquisition or scalar decoder establishes compression of these objects.
$\square$

## 6. Exact calibration alphabets and unrestricted-address acquisition

**Theorem 6.1 (exact finite skew alphabet).** In the explicitly public
variation $\delta(u)\in S$, where $S\subset\mathbb Z$ is nonempty and
finite, the minimum number of joint supplementary retained values for
the complementary-cut task is exactly $|S|$. The lower bound permits
supplements at either capture and causal dependence on earlier retained
data, counting every decoder-visible source-bearing field in their
joint value. Fixed-width supplementary payload therefore costs exactly
$\lceil\log_2|S|\rceil$ bits. The complete rows, framing, public encoding
of $S$ and acquisition state are additional. Without a finite skew
promise no uniformly finite supplemental alphabet suffices.

Proof. Acquire $\delta$ by Theorem 5.3 and retain its index in the
public set; the later row-only capture and (CAB.14) give attainment.
For necessity choose $a=\max(0,1-\min S)$ and put
$m_s=a+s-1\ge0$. First capture
$X_s=\langle T_a,T_{m_s}\rangle$ quiescently at $\{R\}$.
All complete first rows are $\langle T_a,H_R\rangle$, with singleton
partition, and $\delta(X_s)=s$. Choose $B>\max_s m_s$ and perform
$B-m_s$ genuine updates on each same root before its quiescent
$\{L\}$ capture. All second rows are $\langle H_L,T_B\rangle$,
again with singleton partition, yet the lags are distinct and positive.
No omitted guard or equality bit distinguishes the rows. Equal joint
supplement values would therefore force a wrong decoder output. Taking
finite sets of arbitrary size gives the unbounded conclusion. $\square$

**Theorem 6.2 (matching first-acquisition traffic on a hidden-age family).**
Let a deterministic source-blind first producer, correct on all sources,
retain a complete $\{R\}$ row and a supplement sufficient with a later
row-only $\{L\}$ handoff. It may query arbitrary addresses, recompute,
reread, and use any finite source-independent immutable installation.
For each analysis integer $h\ge1$, test its quiescent first calls on

$$
X_k=\langle\alpha,T_k\rangle,\qquad 2h\le k\le4h.
\tag{CAB.16}
$$

At least $2h+1$ first supplementary values are necessary. On some one
call it makes at least $q_h=\lceil\log_4(2h+1)\rceil$ Reads of length
at least $h+1$. Thus its worst-case $T,A,Q$ are at least
$q_h,(h+1)q_h,h+1$ respectively. The all-source procedure of §5
attains worst-case orders $\Theta(\log(h+2))$ Reads,
$\Theta(h\log(h+2))$ letters and $\Theta(h)$ operand peak, a seven-cell
first row and $O(\log(h+2))$ supplemental bits on this family.

Proof. The first rows are all $\langle\alpha,H_R\rangle$. After
$4h+1-k$ genuine updates the later rows are all
$\langle H_L,T_{4h+1}\rangle$. These are lawful strictly positive
lags on each original root. Since no second supplement is permitted,
the first supplements must distinguish every $k$.

Every whole-root word of length at most $h$ has the same reply across
the family. Outside the right cone this follows from the fixed root
and left leaf. Inside it the suffix has length at most $h-1$, and
native saturation gives branch since
$k-2(h-1)\ge2$. Contract constant-reply nodes in the induced decision
tree as in Theorem 4.5. Its $2h+1$ distinguishable leaves force a path
with $q_h$ informative Reads, each at length at least $h+1$. This
proves all three lower bounds on an allowed arbitrary-address program.

The first left guard is depth one, and the row body reads just the
root and its alpha child. The only unbounded search is the hidden
right-clock search, with $O(\log(h+2))$ queries of length $O(h)$.
The candidate uses one branch, one alpha, one three-cell hole and
two terminators: seven cells. The calibration $k+1$ has the stated
binary size. Its mutable operand implementation occupies $O(h)$
bits; the lower bound does not prohibit immutable operand competitors.
$\square$

**Proposition 6.3 (finite-interference boundary of the cheap first row).**
For invocation source $X_k$ in (CAB.16) and $b$ updates during that
call, the final exposed left subtree is $T_b$. The same §5 procedure
has at most

$$
(b+1)O\bigl(|\operatorname{Pos}(T_b)|+b+\log(k+b+2)\bigr)
\tag{CAB.17}
$$

Reads, operand peak $O(k+b+1)$ and row-candidate size
$O(|\operatorname{Pos}(T_b)|+1)$. Its calibration remains $k+1$.
The second complete row in the collision family contains $T_{4h+1}$
and has exponentially many literal cells in $h$.

Proof. The left guard length is at most $1+\max(b-1,0)$; the exterior
visits only the root and positions in the final left tree. The hidden
right clock is at most $k+b$, and there are no pair comparisons.
Apply the bounded search and failure count to these actual subtrees.
Invariance gives the unchanged calibration. The native size formula
$|\operatorname{Pos}(T_j)|=2F_{j+1}-1$ gives the last statement. Hence
the quiescent constant-row result is not an end-to-end logarithmic
storage claim under arbitrary interference. $\square$

## 7. An acquired interval with exact hidden query and operand prices

**Definition 7.1 (public skew window, unknown common age).** Fix public
integers $d\ge1$ and $q\ge1$. The source domain is

$$
X_{a,r}=\langle T_a,T_{a+d+r}\rangle,
\qquad a\ge0\text{ unknown},\qquad 0\le r<q.
\tag{CAB.18}
$$

The first cut is $\{R\}$; the second is $\{L\}$. All later lags are
allowed. Updates increase $a$ and preserve $r$. The first producer
must export the full row and a supplement; the second exports only
the full row. The public variation restricts sources, not programs:
competitors may read anywhere, retain arbitrary paid intermediate
state and use any finite source-independent installation. No interval
endpoint depending on $a$ is input advice. A hidden Read means any
actual first-call Read whose whole-root address begins with $R$.

**Theorem 7.2 (sharp acquired-window bridge).** On the domain (CAB.18):

1. The minimum attained supplementary alphabet is exactly $q$;
   fixed-width payload is $\lceil\log_2q\rceil$ bits.
2. For a fixed quiescent common age $a$, put $M=a+d$, $U=M+q-1$ and

$$
h_q=\min\{h\ge0:q\le3\cdot2^h-2\}.
\tag{CAB.19}
$$

   For $q\ge2$, the minimum worst-case hidden Read count over
   $0\le r<q$ is exactly $h_q$, and the minimum worst-case peak
   length of a hidden whole-root operand is exactly

$$
D_q=1+\left\lfloor\frac{M+q-2}{2}\right\rfloor.
\tag{CAB.20}
$$

   One uniform initially source-blind program attains both, using
   endpoints computed from its acquired first row. For $q=1$ it
   makes no hidden Reads and has no supplementary payload bit.
3. For $q\ge2$, with $D_0=1+\lfloor M/2\rfloor$, every such exact first producer
   has some quiescent run with at least $h_q$ informative hidden
   Reads, all of length at least $D_0$. Consequently its worst-case
   hidden letter traffic is at least $D_0h_q$. The attaining program
   uses at most $D_qh_q$ hidden letters on every quiescent run.

The exact interval formulas in parts 2–3 use [AOF, Theorems 8.3,
9.1–9.3][AOF]. The additional assertions here concern acquired endpoints,
whole-root arbitrary-address competitors, actual lag consumers and
concurrent completion, not a new interval-search formula.

Proof. At a first validation age $a_v$, retain
$r=c(T_{a_v+d+r})-c(T_{a_v})-d$. A later visible right tree has clock
$a_v+d+r+n$, while the first visible left tree has clock $a_v$;
their difference minus $d+r$ gives $n$. To force $q$ records, fix
$a$ and use the lawful positive lags $q-r$. All complete first rows
are $\langle T_a,H_R\rangle$ and all complete later rows are
$\langle H_L,T_{a+d+q}\rangle$. Their singleton partitions coincide.
The distinct lags force distinct first supplements. Retaining $r$
attains the alphabet. This is also Theorem 6.1 for
$S=\{1+d,\ldots,d+q\}$.

For the acquisition lower bound fix $a$. The root and every address
outside the $R$ cone have a reply independent of $r$. Contract such
queries and any other constant-reply nodes of the finite induced
decision tree. Distinct first records still identify every actual
hidden age in $[M,U]$. Each remaining query is $R p$ for an arbitrary
native suffix $p$. By [AOF, Definition 8.2][AOF] every suffix has one of
the actual $E,P_t,S_t$ response functions, rather than an arbitrary
partition of the interval. Its capacity after $h$ informative tests
is at most $3\cdot2^h-2$, since the nonsingleton cells are at most
two interval tails and the other two cells are singletons. Root and
$S_t$ queries have no larger capacity. Thus some path has at least
$h_q$ informative hidden queries. Giving a competitor $a$ for the
purpose of this lower-bound reduction can only help it; the original
program need not know $a$.

If every hidden suffix had length less than
$\lfloor(U-1)/2\rfloor$, the two ages $U-1,U$ would reply branch
identically at every such suffix. Root and left-side queries cannot
separate them either. Deterministic induction then contradicts the
required different first records. Adding the mandatory leading $R$
proves (CAB.20)'s lower bound. Furthermore every suffix shorter than
$\lfloor M/2\rfloor$ replies branch throughout the hidden interval.
Each informative hidden query costs at least $D_0$ letters. The
capacity argument applies to the tree of informative queries itself,
so $D_0h_q$ is a same-run bound, not a product of unrelated maxima.

For attainment, perform RA's left guard and complete first-row body.
The paid literal left candidate has a computable right clock $C$.
Form the provisional interval $[C+d,C+d+q-1]$ by local arithmetic.
Apply the native joint-optimal interval reader of [AOF, Theorem 9.2][AOF]
below the reached base $R$, then validate the original left guard.
The explicit finite control and treatment of mixed replies are given
in Definition 7.3. On a quiescent call $C=a$, so the interval is
correct, the hidden queries identify $a+d+r$ in at most $h_q$ calls,
and every suffix has length at most $\lfloor(U-1)/2\rfloor$.
Subtracting $C+d$ gives $r$. This attains both minima and the claimed
letter upper bound. Theorem 7.4 proves that this same program is
safe and terminates under the full concurrent model. $\square$

**Definition 7.3 (runtime interval control).** The interval reader does
not install an age-specific table or store an unbounded table-state
identifier at unit cost. Compute $h_q$ from the paid public integer
$q$. Maintain a surviving interval $[l,u]$ and remaining budget $h$,
initially $[C+d,C+d+q-1]$ and $h_q$. A singleton halts with its age.
An empty interval or a nonsingleton at budget zero marks INVALID.
Otherwise set $t=l+(3\cdot2^{h-1}-2)$ and use the actual $P_t$
query of [AOF, Definition 8.2][AOF], feeding its reply to its absent,
alpha, beta or branch cell and intersecting with $[l,u]$. Decrease
$h$ by one after every query.

To enforce the peak bound, put $U_0=C+d+q-1$ and
$m=\lfloor(U_0-1)/2\rfloor$. Replace a too-long shortest $P_t$
representative by the bounded-domain query and four-entry reply map
of [AOF, Theorem 9.1][AOF]. For $U_0\le2$ use the hidden root and its
finite age table. Otherwise use $R^m$ when replacement is needed.
On its absent cell the old test is absent; on its leaf cells its
corresponding ages are $2m,2m+1$; on its branch cell the only reachable
age up to $U_0$ is $2m+2$. Evaluate the old $P_t$ response at those
ages by binary arithmetic. Prepend the whole-root base $R$ to every
submitted word. A response with an empty surviving cell rejects the
attempt. Reaching adjacent or empty data never creates an unbounded
search. For $q=1$, no hidden query is needed and the supplementary
value is zero.

This is a finite arithmetic generator of a depth-$h_q$ tree, not a
stored $q$-leaf tree. A mapped reply is provisional local data, not an
actual observation at an unqueried word. Beyond the provisional bound
$U_0$ the map can be false; the enclosing guard, not that map, supplies
acceptance authority.

**Theorem 7.4 (every concurrent interval attempt is paid and finite).**
For a call of Definition 7.3 starting at $X_{a,r}$, let $b$ be its
updates through Return, $a_f=a+b$, $N_L=|\operatorname{Pos}(T_{a_f})|$
and $H_L=\operatorname{height}(T_{a_f})$. The program of Theorem 7.2
is safe at its final guard, terminates with at most $b$ failures and
$b+1$ attempts, and each attempt has at most

$$
N_L+H_L+4\text{ ordinary RA Reads},\qquad h_q\text{ hidden Reads}.
\tag{CAB.21}
$$

For $q\ge2$, put
$D_b=1+\lfloor(a_f+d+q-2)/2\rfloor$. Its hidden operands have length
at most $D_b$, so all attempts together have at most
$(b+1)h_q$ hidden Reads and $(b+1)D_bh_q$ hidden letters. Ordinary
RA letters are at most $(b+1)(H_L+1)(N_L+H_L+4)$.

The row candidate uses at most $4(N_L+6)$ bits. Supplement payload
uses $\lceil\log_2q\rceil$ bits. Auxiliary paths and all numerical
control use
$O(H_L+1+\log(a_f+d+q+2))$ bits. A separate complete operand region
uses $O(\max(H_L+1,D_b)+1)$ bits. Public $d,q$, framing and the fixed
code have their actual immutable binary costs. For $q=1$ remove the
hidden-query and $D_b$ terms. The final first handoff contains only
the actual full row and $r$; the second row has its own full RA bill.

Proof. The original root remains paired. Every reached left position
persists in $T_{a_f}$. Even a mixed candidate is a syntactically complete
tree, and its rightmost leaf was actually read at some relative right
depth $j$ with bit $e$. All its right ancestors had replied branch
and persist. Therefore its candidate clock $C=2j+e$ equals the left
subtree's actual clock at that leaf event. In particular
$a\le C\le a_f$, including failed attempts. This is an acquired
bound, not an assumed supplied interval. The final left depth bounds
the guard; the exterior visits at most the root and $N_L$ left nodes.
There are no pair comparisons. These facts give (CAB.21) and the
candidate bound.

Every iteration of the hidden reader reduces $h$, including a reply
inconsistent with any stationary candidate. Consequently it ends
within $h_q$ queries even when the source crosses its provisional
upper endpoint. Its generated operands obey the runtime bound
$1+\lfloor(C+d+q-2)/2\rfloor\le D_b$. Matching the final leaf
makes the entire row and hidden reader stationary. Then $C$ is the
true current common age and $r\in[0,q-1]$ puts the hidden source
inside that runtime interval. The supplied interval proof yields
the correct $r$. INVALID or a failed guard therefore requires an
update after the saved leaf; disjoint attempts charge distinct updates.
This proves safety, termination and the full failure account.

Only a constant number of binary integers and four reply-map entries
are maintained. The capacity numbers used before halting are $O(q)$;
thresholds and provisional ages have $O(\log(a_f+d+q+2))$ bits.
No interval enumeration or installed $a$-specific literal table is
needed. The row and paths have the stated left-tree bounds, while
full hidden operands are independently materialized and cleared.
Add to RA's local work a scan of the left candidate and, per hidden
query, $O(D_b+L_b^2)$ bit/cell operations, where
$L_b=1+\lceil\log_2(N_L+a_f+d+q+8)\rceil$; initialization and
cleanup cost at most $O((N_L+1)L_b^2+L_b^3)$ more per attempt.
These are loose finite work bounds, not optimal time claims. $\square$

**Proposition 7.5 (meaning of the exact prices).** Theorem 7.2's call
and peak minima concern hidden native queries on a fixed-age test
family, and its alphabet result permits unbounded unknown common age.
Neither minimizes the entire call's work or complete row storage.
For fixed $q\ge2$ and $a+d\to\infty$, hidden traffic is
$\Theta((a+d)h_q)$; the lower and upper bounds remain within a
constant factor if $q=O(a+d)$. In all cases the first row literally
contains $T_a$ and the later row literally contains $T_{a+d+r+n}$.

Proof. The two length factors are
$1+\lfloor M/2\rfloor$ and $1+\lfloor(M+q-2)/2\rfloor$.
Their comparison gives the traffic assertions. The row descriptions
follow from the actual cuts. Their literal sizes can be exponential
even when the retained supplementary alphabet has only $q$ values.
The hidden-query theorem cannot identify a small scalar-control width
with small total writable work, candidate storage or operand storage.
$\square$

## 8. Cut components, causal compression and the limits of invariance

**Definition 8.1 (a public cut menu).** For a finite public list of $m\ge1$
cuts, put $D=\max(\{0\}\cup\{|p|:p\in P_i\})$ and

$$
S_i=\{w\in\{L,R\}^D:\text{no }p\in P_i\text{ is a prefix of }w\}.
\tag{CAB.22}
$$

Join $i,j$ when $S_i\cap S_j\ne\varnothing$. Let $\mathcal C$ be the
components, $c_0$ their number, $C_*=C(0)$, and $r_C$ the least index
in component $C$. [NAR, Theorem 12.3][NAR] supplies row-only recovery of
$n_i-n_{r_{C(i)}}$ by exposed $J_w$ factors along a spanning forest.
Its whole-row collision theorem supplies a fiber of
$2^{c_0-1}$ different chronological epoch vectors. These results concern
complete rows with the actual history promise; neither assumed equality
links nor a supplied hidden boundary replaces that input.

**Corollary 8.2 (causal acquired bridges for every menu).** For any public
menu, including cuts with $S_i=\varnothing$, acquire $k_0=\ell(U_{v_0})$
and keep it as paid intermediate state. At each other component's
first capture acquire $k_{r_C}$ by Theorem 2.2 and emit
$\eta_C=k_{r_C}-k_0$. All captures retain their complete row. After
the last selected representative erase the baseline and local stamps.
The final decoder needs exactly the public menu, rows and
$(\eta_C)_{C\ne C_*}$, in public order, to recover every $n_i$.

Proof. Put $\eta_{C_*}=0$. By actual acquisition and common history,
$\eta_C=n_{r_C}$. Add the supplied within-component differences.
A representative is known before its call because the entire menu
is public. Each subtraction occurs only after both acquired values
exist; no future record is used earlier. The baseline occupies its
actual binary size until its last use, and accumulators and rows are
also retained and charged. This establishes an attained causal
interface, not a new graph-integration theorem. $\square$

**Theorem 8.3 (one-time acquired invariant menu calibration).** Suppose
instead that every $S_i$ is nonempty and the entire finite menu is
public before the first capture. Choose $w_C\in S_{r_C}$, including
$w_*\in S_0$. During the first row's guard interval acquire

$$
\kappa_C=J_{w_C}(U_{v_0})-J_{w_*}(U_{v_0})\qquad(C\ne C_*).
\tag{CAB.23}
$$

Only the row and these signed integers survive the first acquisition;
all later captures retain just their complete rows. This is possible
with native Reads under unknown finite interference. The final decoder
recovers every relative epoch, even across disconnected components.

Proof. The anchor $J_{w_*}$ factors through the first complete row.
For each other endpoint, follow its prefixes from the root until a
leaf or the endpoint is reached. A blocking leaf gives its weighted
prefix length plus label bit. At a reached endpoint acquire its right
clock by Definition 4.1 and add the prefix weight. Do this sequentially
inside the saved leaf's interval, aborting on INVALID, and then perform
the final guard Read. Persistence makes all reached bases lawful. On
acceptance the interval is stationary and gives exactly (CAB.12),
including initially absent endpoints. Each subroutine is finite under
the final height; a failed attempt requires an update in its guard
interval. The previous disjoint-interval argument proves termination.

Let $q_C$ be the exposed $J_{w_C}$ computed from representative row
$r_C$, and let $q_*=J_{w_*}(U_{v_0})$ be computed from row zero.
Advancement gives
$q_C-\kappa_C-q_*=n_{r_C}$. Combine these with Definition 8.1's
row-only offsets. The constants (CAB.23) are invariant along the
orbit, but their later use includes advancing coordinates exposed by
actual rows. No future source value is obtained by the initial
calibration alone. $\square$

**Proposition 8.4 (menu acquisition and retention bills).** Put
$e=c_0-1$. With first-call parameters (CAB.3), Theorem 8.3 adds at
most $e(\min(D,H)+1+S_H)$ Reads per attempt to RA and uses operands
of length at most $2H+1$. All attempts number at most $b+1$.
The supplement candidate costs $O(e\log(H+2))$ bits, separate from
the row candidate. Additional working registers need
$O(\log(e+D+H+2))$ bits beyond RA paths and a reused full operand.
Final payload is the actual complete rows and the signed calibrations;
later arithmetic and outputs are additional.

Proof. Prefix traversal reads at most $\min(D,H)+1$ positions; if
it reaches a branch endpoint the right search adds at most $S_H$.
Re-reading that endpoint is allowed and included in this loose sum.
Its base length plus twice its descendant right depth is at most
$2H$; the displayed $2H+1$ is a uniform safe bound. On a successful
source $0\le J_w\le2H+1$, since the prefix weight is at most twice
its length and the remaining clock is at most twice the remaining
height plus one. Failed provisional quantities have the same finite
height bounds, so logarithmic signed fields suffice. Endpoints are
processed sequentially, not by storing all hidden trees.

The public menu and selected endpoints are separately paid literal
input/control. For example, an elementary finite realization enumerates
all $2^D$ frontier words, stores their $m$ incidence bits and their
length-$D$ words, constructs the $m^2$ adjacency bits, and selects
representatives and a forest. This has a finite explicit public
workspace bound
$O(2^D(D+m)+m^2+mD+m\log(m+1))$ bits, besides the cut tapes.
One can discard this geometry after extracting the needed public plan,
or retain and charge it. No compact-menu optimization is assumed.
Local endpoint work includes full word construction and binary
arithmetic, bounded for example by
$O(e((D+1)+(H+1)S_H)L^2+eF L^2)$ per attempt with
$L=1+\lceil\log_2(F+K_P+D+e+H+2)\rceil$, added to RA's work.
Source-independent menu preprocessing and source-dependent candidates
are distinct accounts. $\square$

**Proposition 8.5 (invariant-only obstruction and field-count boundary).**
An initial record, even a full initial tree, cannot recover the lag
of a later root-cut row with no later advancing supplement. Likewise,
on the orbit of $\langle\alpha,\alpha\rangle$, complete
$\{L,R\}$ rows together with source functions invariant under $\rho$
at every capture cannot recover arbitrary lags. The $c_0-1$ integer
fields in Corollary 8.2 or Theorem 8.3 are sufficient organizations,
not representation-independent lower bounds on the number of fields.

Proof. Fix the same first capture and retained initial state. Perform
zero versus one update before a quiescent later root capture. Its
complete row is the same root hole with singleton partition. Both
decoder inputs coincide while the required lags differ. For the
two-hole orbit, both subtrees are always equal, so every complete
row is the same equality row and every invariant source function
is unchanged. The same zero/one comparison applies. Public index
labels or fixed coding of those invariant values cannot separate it.
This is why §2 retains phase-sensitive data for arbitrary cuts.

Integers can encode tuples, and some particular disconnected row
fibers determine more than the uniform overlap guarantee. Thus a
count of scalar fields is not an alphabet lower bound. The supplied
$2^{c_0-1}$ collision fiber gives a lower bound on joint attained
supplementary values, while §§3, 4, 6 and 7 prove their stated native
state or traffic bounds. None asserts a general scalar dimension
minimum. $\square$

**Proposition 8.6 (calibration is not a globally least partition).** For
paired first sources, retaining
$\nu(y)-\nu(x)$, where $\nu$ is the supplied intrinsic inverse depth,
also suffices with the complementary rows to recover the lag.
Consequently §5's right-clock calibration does not define a coarsest
necessary all-source retained partition.

Proof. [IDC, Reuse 2.2][IDC] gives
$\nu(\rho^n y)=\nu(y)+n$ and an effective computation of $\nu$
from a full literal tree. The first row exposes $x$ and the second
exposes $\rho^n y$, so their intrinsic depths minus the retained
difference recover $n$. For an initially atomic root, the row
already gives the whole initial tree and the §5 clock formula handles
its continuation, so this alternative extends to an all-source
scheme with a row-determined branch.

Take $x=\alpha$ and $y$ either alpha or
$\langle\alpha,\alpha\rangle$. Both hidden trees have intrinsic
depth zero by the image grammar. This alternative merges their
first supplementary values, while (CAB.13)'s calibrations are one
and three. Hence the latter distinction is not forced in every
sufficient representation. Computing the alternative from a hidden
tree has its own acquisition cost; no cost dominance follows.
$\square$

**Definition 9.3 (precise residual).** The general all-source Pareto optimum
over cut geometry, sufficient acquired relations, peak writable work,
immutable installation, total queries, literal traffic, candidate/output
storage and retained information remains open here. In particular no
optimum dependence on interference $b$, no optimum for an unknown
adaptive future menu, and no universal mutable-space minimum is proved.
Changing clock reference causally would require its own acquired
cross-reference data and a charge for the surviving reference state.
Finite skew windows and public menus are explicit variations, not
advice silently granted to the all-cut theorems.

The established mutual recovery has a specific domain: under authentic
complementary captures, the acquired calibration and complete literal
rows recover relative updates and the first captured source, hence
its finite intermediate memories. Under arbitrary cuts, acquired
phase-sensitive stamps recover relative updates; the rows and stamps
need not recover hidden trees. None identifies a designated origin
before the first validation, physical duration, or the placement of
unobserved events. A minimal relational structure simultaneously
recovering all spatial, temporal, boundary and memory descriptions
requires further conditions beyond these interfaces.
