Knowing the multiplicative unit does not suffice for finite exact recovery of an unknown initial paid frontier when a fixed rational additive offset remains hidden in a public interval of positive width. This holds for every supplied positive rational unit and every supplied rational interval of positive width, even when the handoff is promised to be a proper death return. Each finite nonempty prefix of the original unread raw tail has a separate obstruction. All finite query precisions remain available.

The results are ordinary conditional symbolic mathematics for the explicitly specified clock-driven producer and Step/Extent interface. Their proof uses genuine initialized histories, a legal interior reference, finite floor-cell margins and actual first-Step coalescence. It gives neither an executed mathematical verification nor new Lean/kernel certification. The mathematical suppliers are [PAID][PAID], [NORMAL][NORMAL], [TARGET][TARGET], [CAPPED][CAPPED] and [DRIFT][DRIFT]. In particular PAID Sections 27–30 already prove the weaker initial-boundary negative with both unit and offset hidden. The contribution here fixes the unit publicly in advance and restricts the sole hidden offset to each prescribed bounded interval.

## 5. A legal reference and finite interior margins

**Lemma 5.1 (Interior irrational reference under the same public data).** For each (8), there is an actual proper zero-source death handoff with a legal rational offset $b_{\rm ref}\in(u,v)$ whose stationary true value $z_{\rm ref}$ is irrational and belongs to $(u,v)$.

**Proof.** Take $b_{\rm ref}=(u+v)/2$. First $\alpha>1/2$, since otherwise $\alpha^2+\alpha\le3/4$, contradicting its defining equation. Consequently $\alpha^2=1-\alpha<1/2$, so $\alpha^{2t}<2^{-t}$ for every positive integer $t$. By the Archimedean property choose a finite positive $t$ with $2^t>q_0/(v-b_{\rm ref})$, and put $k=2t\ge1$. Then $q_0\alpha^k<v-b_{\rm ref}$. The actual zero-source history of Lemma 4.1 attains $p=0^k$, $r=G_{k+1}-2$ at death with this same public $P,q_0,u,v,B$ and private $b_{\rm ref}$. Its value

$$
z_{\rm ref}=b_{\rm ref}+q_0\alpha^k
\tag{23}
$$

is strictly between $u$ and $v$.

Conjugation of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$ sends $\alpha$ to $-\phi$. If $\alpha^k$ were rational, it would equal its conjugate $(-\phi)^k$, impossible because the first has absolute value less than one and the second greater than one. Multiplication by a nonzero rational and addition of a rational preserve irrationality. Thus $z_{\rm ref}$ is irrational and strictly inside every finite dyadic floor cell containing it. This is a legal positive-depth reference, not an unattainable limit at depth infinity or unit zero. Its depth is chosen to fit this same prescribed interval, rather than assuming one shallow reference fits all intervals. $\square$

**Lemma 5.2 (Finite first decision with two branches).** If a candidate receiver is finite on every legal proper death world for a chosen target, its interaction on Lemma 5.1 reaches after finitely many local actions either completed output without any Step or the issue of its first actual Step. Let $F$ be the finite set of all precisions requested before that decision; it may be empty. Preserve the entire ordered finite prefix, including repetitions, frames, local output prefixes and control actions.

**Proof.** If neither decision occurred after finite actions, the receiver could only continue stationary queries, pauses or local computation and could not finish on this legal reference. That violates its assumed finiteness. Before a Step the reference depth and value (23) are fixed, so each queried $n$ yields $a_n=\lfloor2^nz_{\rm ref}\rfloor$. Release supplies no invisible motion or informative reentry; any eventual no-Step answer remains in the first branch, while indefinite noncompletion fails finiteness. A boundary decoder is not forced to Step by comparing different tails at one fixed cut: those tails would have the same boundary. The proof therefore retains both branches. $\square$
