By RCA Definitions 11.1–11.2, the latest active version is the most recent
complete sealed bank on $p_h$. Tail reaches its actual R; the two MLs

Thus the first F encountered by that backward loop is precisely the
previous version's terminal F or the initial sentinel. The scan reads

select. No prefix of an older version is included. Its final MR reaches

and its physical append position can depend on $P$. EX70 changes neither
