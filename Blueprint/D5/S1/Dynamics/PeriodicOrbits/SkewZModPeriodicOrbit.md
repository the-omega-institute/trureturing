*Commentary.*

For two lifted points (x,a) and (x,b) over a periodic base point, the base coordinate forces every connecting iterate to be a multiple of the base minimal period.

Each such block adds the same Birkhoff holonomy h in the cyclic fiber. Consequently the two forward periodic orbits coincide exactly when the displacement b-a belongs to the additive subgroup generated by h.
