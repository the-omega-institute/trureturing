# Noncommutative Holonomy in Finite-Group Skew Products

## Abstract

Ordered noncommutative holonomy controls periods and orbit families of a finite-group skew product.

Let f be a self-map of X, let c assign a group element to each base state, and update the fibre by right multiplication. The ordered product of charges along the actual base orbit is the holonomy; its order matters when the fibre group is noncommutative.

A returning base cycle therefore acts on the fibre by one right translation. For a finite fibre group, the lifted period is the base period times the order of that translation. Two lifts lie on the same periodic orbit exactly when their displacement belongs to the subgroup generated by holonomy, and the resulting orbit-family cardinality is the corresponding subgroup index.
