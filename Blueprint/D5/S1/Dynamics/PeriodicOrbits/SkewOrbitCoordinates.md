# Coordinates on a lifted periodic orbit

## Abstract

A periodic skew orbit splits into base positions and a holonomy phase subgroup.

*Commentary.*

Let `x` be a periodic base point with minimal period `p`, and let `h` be the Birkhoff sum of the charge over one turn. If `h` has finite additive order, then the map

`(i,s) ↦ (f^i(x), a + s + BirkhoffSum(i,x))`

is a bijection from `Fin p` times the subgroup generated by `h` onto the lifted periodic orbit through `(x,a)`. The fiber group may be infinite.

Writing an orbit time as `n = i + p k` gives the remainder position `i` and a phase `k • h`. Finite holonomy order makes every integer multiple representable by a nonnegative multiple, while distinct remainders have distinct base points. Thus each base-position fiber is a translate of the same holonomy subgroup.
