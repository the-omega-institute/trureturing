# A common nonzero period for a given six-cell tiling and its orbit closure

## Abstract

Primitive normalization and the full six-cell consumer for the given tiling.

The lattice is ℤ×ℤ. The tile F is an arbitrary finite set with six elements; FullDifferenceSpan F means that the ℤ-submodule generated by all actual differences p−q with p,q∈F is the whole lattice. The predicate T is given. ExactCover F T is the equation Σ[f∈F]1_T(x−f)=1 at every lattice point x. The negative exact-cover mask and the positive-shift action are precisely the existing exactCoverMask and coefficientAct, with no replacement tiling or finite enumeration of possible tile shapes.

*Commentary.*

For an arbitrary complex lattice Laurent polynomial P, take a nonzero integer vector v and a nonzero nonunit complex one-variable Laurent polynomial A such that lineLaurent(v,A) divides P. The conclusion constructs a primitive integer vector u, a positive natural number g, and a nonzero nonunit Laurent polynomial B with v=g·u, lineLaurent(u,B)=lineLaurent(v,A), and lineLaurent(u,B) dividing P. Thus divisibility is literal in the original lattice ring; no coprimality, root or primitive line divisor is an additional premise.

Mathlib Int.exists_gcd_one supplies v=g·u with g the positive gcd of the two coordinates, including either zero coordinate and either sign of a coordinate. Define B by the exponent map n↦g n on ℤ. The AddMonoidAlgebra mapDomainRingHom is a ring homomorphism on finite Laurent support, so negative exponents are retained. Its injectivity for positive g follows from integer multiplication cancellation and mapDomain_injective. This proves B≠0. Induction on Laurent monomials proves the exact equality of the two line images.

To prove B nonunit, clear A to q.toLaurent=A·T^n and remove the full zero-root multiplicity of q. The remaining polynomial s has nonzero constant term and is nonconstant, since a constant s would make A a unit. The fundamental theorem of algebra gives a nonzero root α of A. Algebraic closure gives β with β^g=α; positivity of g and α≠0 imply β≠0. Evaluation on every integer monomial proves B(β)=A(β^g)=0. Evaluation sends units to units, while zero is not a unit of ℂ. This proves nonunitness without assuming any factor or cancellation equation. The polynomial clearing argument uses the same Mathlib suppliers as KhetanLineFactorCancellation.

*Commentary.*

For every F:Finset(ℤ×ℤ) and every given predicate T on ℤ×ℤ, assume F.card=6, FullDifferenceSpan F and ExactCover F T. The conclusion is an integer vector t≠0 with Period T t and, for every c in the closure of {x↦indicator T(x+s) | s∈ℤ×ℤ}, the equation c(x+t)=c(x) for every x. The closure is taken in the product topology on ℕ-valued configurations. It is the actual original shift orbit closure. A period of T itself, common to the entire closure, is stronger than existence of a periodic point in that closure.

The proof splits on whether there exists a primitive u satisfying OnlyLineDirection for the complex image of the actual tile mask. In that branch the same T supplies an integer indicator with range contained in {0,1}. ExactCover gives coefficientAct(mask,indicator)=1 with the negative-mask convention x−f. Nonemptiness follows from cardinality six. At any p∈F the coefficient of the mask at −p is exactly one, so the mask is nonzero. The integer supplier integer_one_direction_periodizer_periodic applies to this indicator. The equivalence between primitive coordinates and integer coprimality is Int.isCoprime_iff_gcd_eq_one. Injectivity of the natural-to-integer cast and the definition of indicator transfer its period back to T.

If no primitive u satisfies OnlyLineDirection, its failure at (1,0) produces an actual nonzero nonunit line divisor in a nonzero integer direction. Primitive normalization supplies u and a genuine factor in that direction. Failure of OnlyLineDirection at this u then produces a second actual divisor with nonzero determinant against u. Normalize that divisor as well. The determinant scales by its positive integer multiplier, so the two primitive directions remain nonparallel. These are the actual divisors passed to two_line_factors_orbit_closure_periodic, which derives fiber cancellation and uses the full-span six-cell geometry. No line-factor restriction remains in the final theorem telescope. If there are no genuine line divisors, (1,0) satisfies the first branch vacuously.

The closed fixed-point set of translation by t contains every shift of a t-periodic indicator, and therefore its closure. This proves the common-period conclusion in the one-direction branch. In the two-direction branch the existing consumer already supplies the common period; the original indicator belongs to the closure through the zero shift, so the same t is a period of the original T. Neither branch constructs an unrelated exact-cover complement.
