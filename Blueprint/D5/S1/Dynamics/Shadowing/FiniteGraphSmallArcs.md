*Commentary.*

A sufficiently small closed ball in the affine realization lies in one edge interval or a small star about a vertex. Any continuous parametrization of a closed interval into the ball that is locally injective in the interior is injective on the interval. Its image diameter is the distance between the two endpoint images.
