# The Exponential Differential Field

## Abstract

The normalized series -log(2-exp X) is transcendental over the rational function field generated by X and exp X.

Work inside the fraction field L of rational formal power series. The rational function field K=Q(t) embeds into L by t mapping to X. The exponential is transcendental over K, so H=K(u) embeds by u mapping to exp X. The derivation on K sends t to one, and that on H also sends u to u. These derivations agree with the formal power series derivative after embedding into L. Generality I: this module uses the fixed rational series. Transcendence and primitive identities are known differential algebra. The chosen Lean carriers and coordinate identifications are repository interfaces; the Ax theorem, Pila-Tsimerman exponential formulation and pinned Mathlib APIs were compared, and do not state these exact carrier identities.
