# The Lonely Runner value and one-deletion statement

The formal definition `OneDeletionDefs.lonelyValue` is the supremum over all real
times of the infimum of the nearest-integer distance among the retained speeds.

The fixed claim quantifies over `N ≥ 2` and `1 ≤ r ≤ N`; it requires the lower
bound `1/N` and characterizes equality by `r = N` or `N = 2`.
