# Golden Logarithmic Scale

`D5/S1/Scale/Log` assigns a scale only to nonzero golden integers. Its value is the floor of the base-golden-ratio logarithm of the absolute real embedding; zero is represented by `none`, not by a fabricated integer value.

Multiplication by the `n`-th integral power of the fundamental unit translates the scale by exactly `n`. The law holds for every integer exponent, including negative powers.
