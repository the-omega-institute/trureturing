# Golden Real Embedding

`D5/S1/Scale/Embedding` sends the golden integer `a + b*phi` to the real number `a + b*goldenRatio`. The defining identity `goldenRatio^2 = goldenRatio + 1` makes this map a ring homomorphism, while irrationality of the golden ratio makes it injective.

Multiplication by the embedded conjugate recovers the integer norm. Taking absolute values gives the corresponding multiplicative relation between the real embedding, its conjugate, and the norm.
