# Golden Power Logarithmic Scale

## Abstract

The logarithmic scale of every natural golden power is integral.

**Theorem 1.1 (Natural golden powers have integral logarithmic scale).**

$$\operatorname{logb}(\varphi,\varphi^{n}) = n$$
