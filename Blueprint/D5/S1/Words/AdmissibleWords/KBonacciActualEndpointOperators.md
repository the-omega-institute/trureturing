# Actual endpoint operators and complete windows

## Abstract

Classify actual Boolean-word endpoint transfers, complete-window products, and their faithful transfers with absorbing rejection over every field; apply them to the complete fixed paid-record classification over the reals.

Fix any field K and an integer k at least two. The live states are the integers from zero through k-1. A false bit resets a live state to zero. A true bit increments it when the result is below k and otherwise rejects. Rejection is absorbing. Words are finite Boolean functions read in increasing coordinate order. List.ofFn preserves their length and every coordinate; conversely, a list is exactly List.ofFn of its own coordinate function. In the display, arithmetic on Fin k states uses their natural values, and natural subtraction is truncated at zero. Finite families use image and card for image and cardinality. The get operation on an option is used only in branches where that option is present. The matrix unit denotes the identity on the indicated state space.

The run from live state s agrees with the original runAdmissible scanner with maximum fuel k-1 and initial fuel k-1-s. A word is DBonacciAdmissible exactly when its run from zero accepts. The live transfer L_w has column s equal to the basis vector at the actual terminal state, or the zero column on rejection. The totalized transfer retains one additional rejection basis state, represented by none, and sends every column to its actual terminal basis vector. These are column-input matrices.

*Commentary.*

For a word containing a false bit, let p and t be the lengths of its initial and final true runs. If its run from zero accepts, both endpoints are below k, their sum is strictly less than the complete word length, and the run from any state s ends at t exactly when s+p is below k. Thus L_w=E(p,t)=e_t r_p^T, where r_p(s) is one for s+p below k and zero otherwise. If a word rejects from zero, it rejects from every live input.

Reading u and then v gives L_(uv)=L_v L_u. For two arbitrary length, zero-containing words accepted from zero, the displayed endpoint product is their actual composition. The composed run from s accepts exactly when both t_1+p_2 and s+p_1 are below k, and then ends at t_2. When t_1+p_2 reaches k, every input rejects.

At width m=k+1, every accepted word contains a false bit. The possible endpoints are exactly p,t below k with p+t at most k. The actual word consisting of p true bits, m-p-t false bits, and t true bits realizes each pair. Its length is exactly m, with every false bit retained. The all-true width-m word rejects and induces the zero live matrix. Endpoint matrices are nonzero and distinct over every field. There are N_k nonzero one-window operators and N_k+1 one-window operators in total.

For every k at least three, the windows with endpoints (k-1,0) and (0,k-1) compose to (k-1,k-1), outside the one-window domain because its endpoint sum exceeds k. For k=3 these actual windows are 1100 and 0011. Consequently the one-window family is not closed under composition for any k at least three.

For every positive integer r, every actual word of length r*m induces either rejection or one of the k squared endpoint matrices. Conversely, any pair (p,t) is realized by the two full windows (p,0) followed by (0,t). The entire family generated by positive numbers of complete windows is therefore exactly all endpoint matrices plus zero, is closed under multiplication, and has k squared plus one elements. This includes k=2.

Allowing r=0 adds exactly the empty-word identity. Every nonempty complete-window operator has rank at most one, whereas the live identity has rank k and k is at least two. The resulting monoid is closed under multiplication and has k squared plus two elements.

The faithful lift Gamma sends A to the matrix whose live block is A, whose live rows in the rejection-input column are zero, whose rejection row in live column s is 1 minus the sum of A's column s, and whose rejection diagonal entry is one. Gamma is injective, preserves identity and multiplication, and the actual totalized word transfer is Gamma(L_w). In particular Gamma(0) is e_bottom times the all-one row and is nonzero. The rejection-input column always stays at rejection.

The totalized one-window, positive-window, and empty-inclusive families are exactly the respective Gamma images. They have the same three operator counts, the generated families are closed, and the same two actual windows witness one-window nonclosure when k is at least three. These counts concern operators, not historical states.

Adjoining a final false bit to any original admissible n-coordinate word preserves admissibility, gives exactly n+1 coordinates, and ends the actual run at zero. Zero resets the tail while remaining an actual position; endpoint parameters never remove internal positions.

## Application: complete fixed paid-record classification

This application combines actual_endpoint_operators with finite-dimensional linear algebra, finite-order rigidity and the arbitrary-factorization theorem. It gives source Theorem 12.2 of KBonacci endpoint tomography and rank-one relation boundaries as a consequence of those suppliers. The application uses the real field and every integer k at least three. A world consists of an unknown strictly positive object c and an unknown nonzero nonnegative reader l, both indexed by Fin k. Zero reader coordinates and arbitrary unknown nonempty support remain allowed. Every preparation supplies the same c and uses the same l. The target is the initial relation with entries c_i l_t, without selecting absolute scales for the factors.

$$
\begin{aligned}\operatorname{World}\left(k\right) = \{(c,l):(\operatorname{Fin}\left(k\right) \to \mathbb{R}) \times (\operatorname{Fin}\left(k\right) \to \mathbb{R}) \Vert ((\forall (i:\operatorname{Fin}\left(k\right)), (0 < c_{i})) \land (\forall (i:\operatorname{Fin}\left(k\right)), (0 \leq l_{i})) \land l \ne 0)\}\\C_{j} = \sum_{i:\operatorname{Fin}\left(k\right),i<j}c_{i}\\target\left(w, i, t\right) = c_{i} \cdot l_{t}\end{aligned}
$$

An arrangement A is any finite ordered list of finite window lists. Each inner list starts a fresh preparation and ends by abandoning that object. Empty lists, repeated preparations and repeated windows are retained. A window is a literal Boolean word of length k+1 whose run from tail zero accepts, equivalently one with no internal run of k ones. Legality does not require acceptance from the current tail: a legal window can kill an object at a seam. All lists and their stopping positions are fixed before any values are read. The preparation count P is A.length and is any finite nonnegative integer; the window count W is the sum of the inner lengths. There are no resets, copies, returns, extra observations or reads inside a window. The classification assumes exactly W=k(k-1).

The complete record contains A itself and, for every run in its original position, the compulsory initial value D=sum_i c_i l_i followed by the compulsory terminal value after each sequential matrix action. Thus it contains P+W paid values, with repeated values, empty preparations, the killing-window read and every subsequent zero all present. It assumes neither a decoder nor a supplied observation certificate.

For a run with windows u_1,...,u_n, the state at position h is the actual transfer of the cumulative word u_1...u_h applied to c. This follows by unfolding sequential evolution and using L_(uv)=L_v L_u and matrix-vector associativity. Every such word has length at least k+1. The endpoint theorem makes its transfer either zero or one unique nonzero E(p,t). In the second case direct matrix multiplication gives C_j e_t with j=k-p between one and k. Strict positivity of c makes C_j positive. Consequently zero transfer means a dead object, whereas a zero terminal reading C_j l_t can still come from a live object when l_t=0.

Extract each nonzero endpoint label (j,t) from these actual cumulative operators and let S(A) be their set, removing repetitions only for this set. The chronological record is never shortened. The labels depend only on A, independently of the world. Every terminal value is the corresponding C_j l_t or a constant zero. Since W is positive, A contains a run and an actually paid D. Equality of two complete records is therefore equivalent to equality of D and every selected C_j l_t: one direction projects their actual positions, and the other reconstructs every position, including repeated and zero entries. There is at most one label per window, so the sum of the column cardinalities is |S|<=W.

Use the source prefix convention j=1,...,k in the following classification. Equivalently, with the zero-based row r=j-1, the required pattern omits exactly r=t. The notation Record(A) denotes the complete record codomain just defined, and B_k denotes all arrangements satisfying the exact budget.

For necessity, fix a column t and restrict to the lawful reader e_t. If fewer than k-1 distinct prefixes occur there, the linear map with rows selecting coordinate t and the selected prefix sums has a nonzero kernel: an injective map from the k-dimensional object space into a space of dimension |J_t|+1 would contradict LinearMap.finrank_le_finrank_of_injective. For such a nonzero kernel vector h, the objects with coordinates abs(h_i)+1 and abs(h_i)+1+h_i are both strictly positive. With the same reader they have equal D and all selected products but different initial relations. The actual record correspondence makes this a full record collision. Therefore each column has at least k-1 labels, for any finite number of preparations. The budget forces equality in every column and exactly one omitted zero-based row m(t).

A direction h=e_r-e_(r+1) changes only prefix r+1. At r=k-1 take h=e_r instead. If row r is missing from column t and neither r=t nor r+1=t, the reader e_t annihilates h, so D and every selected product agree for the same two positive objects, contradicting identifiability. Thus m(t)=t or m(t)+1=t, with the first column and last-prefix boundary included. If t=r+1, a simultaneous omission of (r,r) and (r,t) is also impossible: choose reader e_r+e_t and direction e_r-e_t. Its initial dot product vanishes and its only altered prefix is omitted in both active columns. It again preserves the entire paid record while changing the target.

These two collision restrictions make m a strictly increasing self-map of Fin k. Indeed, for a<b, the alternatives m(a)=a or m(a)+1=a and m(b)=b or m(b)+1=b imply m(a)<m(b), except potentially when b=a+1 and m(a)=m(b)=a. That exception would omit both (a,a) and (a,b), which the adjacent-support collision excludes. Pinned mathlib's StrictMono.apply_eq applies to this finite linear order and gives m(t)=t at every t. Translating back to source prefix indices proves that S is exactly S0. The finite-order theorem supplies this rigidity; no separate induction principle or new rigidity theorem is needed.

For sufficiency, the actual positions of the S0 entries and one paid D are already known from A. Positive prefixes make a selected product zero exactly when its reader coordinate is zero, so equal data reveal the same support in both worlds. If that support is a singleton t, D supplies c_t l_t. Adding this increment to the observed preceding prefix recovers the omitted C_(t+1) l_t; at t=0 the preceding value is C_0=0, and at t=k-1 the observed C_(k-1) handles the final boundary. All prefix differences then give every entry of the sole nonzero column.

If the support contains distinct a and b, choose a row r different from both, which exists because k>=3. The two observed positive-prefix products at that row determine the relative reader scale. Column b then fills column a's omitted row, and column a supplies all its prefix products. Comparing any other column at a row where it is observed yields its reader scale relative to a, including scale zero. Subtracting consecutive prefix products gives every c_i l_t. These are scalar cancellations and finite-sum identities; no strictly positive-reader restriction is imposed and no new observation is taken.

Finally, apply `D5/S3/ConceptDynamics/Sufficiency/UniversalSufficiencyFactorization.universal_sufficiency_factorization` with X equal to this full world type, q_C equal to the complete record map and T equal to the initial relation. The all-one object and reader witness Nonempty X for every k in scope. Constancy on record fibers is exactly the just established identifiability. The theorem extends the target-image factor to unattained record values by choice; projecting its value gives an arbitrary total decoder. Conversely, any correct decoder forces target equality on equal-record pairs. No continuity, measurability or computability assumption is involved.

The result classifies all legal fixed arrangements at this exact window budget, not just an optimal construction, and puts no upper bound on their finite preparation counts. Its required ingredients from source 11.2 are the actual-record correspondence, per-column bound and full nonempty-support completion given here; the unrelated complete three-resource theorem is not a premise.
