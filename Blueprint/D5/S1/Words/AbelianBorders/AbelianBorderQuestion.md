*Commentary.*

The ternary word has a bounded decomposition into equal-frequency blocks and satisfies the cylinder and tangential-line conditions, yet it has infinitely many nonempty weakly abelian unbordered factors. This answers Question 2 negatively even when the suffix of a border may be the entire word.
