# Prefix Count and Factor Sets

## Abstract

Prefix growth on the subshift generated by a word equals the cardinality of its factor set.

**Theorem 1.1 (Word-subshift prefixes are exactly word factors).**

$$\forall x: \mathbb{N}^N, \forall n: \mathbb{N}, \operatorname{prefixCount}(\operatorname{wordSubshift}(x), n) = \operatorname{Finset.card}(\operatorname{wordFactorSet}(x, n))$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S1/Words/Complexity/PrefixCountFactorSet.prefixCount_wordSubshift_eq_factorSet_card` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Every prefix realized in the word subshift is a factor of the base word, and every base factor is realized by the corresponding shifted point. The equality therefore identifies symbolic prefix growth with the classical factor-complexity cardinality.
