# The OEIS A338632 Modulo-Four Congruence

## Abstract

Hanna's continued-fraction coefficients are two modulo four above degree one.

P. D. Hanna, OEIS A338632 revision 13 (no page), states verbatim: "G.f. A(x) satisfies: 1 = A(x) - x/(A(x) - 3*x/(A(x) - 5*x/(A(x) - 7*x/(A(x) - 9*x/(A(x) - ...))))), a continued fraction relation." Its formula line states verbatim: "a(n) = 2 (mod 4) for n > 1 (conjecture)." The formalization reads the relation through finite truncations, where tail(A,j,r) is a rational power series and coeff(n,A) is the degree-n coefficient. The operation smul is scalar multiplication, and val denotes the indicated rational cast.

**Definition 1.1 (Finite tails of the continued fraction).**

$$\forall A \in \operatorname{PowerSeries}\left(\mathbb{Q}\right),\; \forall j \in \mathbb{N},\; (\operatorname{tail}\left(A, j, 0\right) = A) \land (\forall r \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{tail}\left(A, j, r + 1\right) = A - \operatorname{smul}\left(2 \cdot (\operatorname{val}\left(j\right): \mathbb{Q}) + 1, X\right) \cdot \operatorname{inv}\left(\operatorname{tail}\left(A, j + 1, r\right)\right))$$

*Commentary.*

For rational PowerSeries A, tail(A,j,0)=A and the successor tail subtracts the odd numerator (2j+1) times X divided by the next tail. The inverse is the formal power-series inverse supplied by the coefficient field.

**Definition 1.2 (The coefficientwise continued-fraction relation).**

$$\forall A \in \operatorname{PowerSeries}\left(\mathbb{Q}\right),\; \operatorname{IsGF}\left(A\right) \Leftrightarrow (\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; \exists R \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall r \in \mathbb{N},\; (R \le r) \implies (\operatorname{coeff}\left(n, \operatorname{tail}\left(A, 0, r\right)\right) = \operatorname{coeff}\left(n, 1\right)))$$

*Commentary.*

IsGF(A) says that for every degree n, some truncation depth R makes the degree-n coefficient of every later tail(A,0,r) equal to the constant series one.

**Definition 1.3 (Hanna's modulo-four conjecture).**

$$(\exists A \in \operatorname{PowerSeries}\left(\mathbb{Q}\right),\; \operatorname{IsGF}\left(A\right)) \land (\forall A \in \operatorname{PowerSeries}\left(\mathbb{Q}\right),\; (\operatorname{IsGF}\left(A\right)) \implies (\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; (2 \le n) \implies (\exists m \in \mathbb{Z},\; (\operatorname{coeff}\left(n, A\right) = (\operatorname{val}\left(m\right): \mathbb{Q})) \land (m \bmod 4 = 2))))$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S1/Recurrence/Invariants/HannaA338632.claim` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Paul D. Hanna (2026). *OEIS A338632, continued-fraction generating function with odd numerators*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A338632>.

*Commentary.*

The claim asserts existence of a series satisfying IsGF and says that every such series has an integer coefficient congruent to two modulo four at each degree n at least two.

**Theorem 1.4 (The modulo-four conjecture is proved).**

$$(\exists A \in \operatorname{PowerSeries}\left(\mathbb{Q}\right),\; \operatorname{IsGF}\left(A\right)) \land (\forall A \in \operatorname{PowerSeries}\left(\mathbb{Q}\right),\; (\operatorname{IsGF}\left(A\right)) \implies (\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; (2 \le n) \implies (\exists m \in \mathbb{Z},\; (\operatorname{coeff}\left(n, A\right) = (\operatorname{val}\left(m\right): \mathbb{Q})) \land (m \bmod 4 = 2))))$$
