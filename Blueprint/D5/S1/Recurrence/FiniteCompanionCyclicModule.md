# First-vertex cyclic companion module

## Abstract

Forward companion time is cyclic at the first vertex over every commutative ring.

A is any commutative ring, u is any element, and q is positive. CommRingType includes the ring instance; Positive(q) is the premise 0<q. The zero ring and q=1 are included. No involution, unit, nontriviality, domain or field assumption occurs. Row(A,q) means Fin q -> A. Forward time is exactly (Tv)(0)=(sum over j in Fin q of v(j))*u and (Tv)(j+1)=v(j). The linear map extends FiniteCompanionSquareDefect.time with J(a)=a*u. The vector e0 has 1 at vertex zero and 0 elsewhere and serves as the cyclic generator.

Set H(u,j)=X^j-C(u)*sum over k<j of X^k, and P(u,q)=H(u,q). CyclicQ is the canonical ring quotient A[X]/(P), with its polynomial-module action. projection sends a polynomial to its class. coordinatePolynomial(u,q,v) is sum over j in Fin q of C(v(j))*H(u,j). Module.AEval' time is the row module where X acts by forward time; of is its underlying A-linear identification. evaluate(f)=f(T)e0 is polynomial-linear.

**Theorem 1.1 (Exact polynomial kernel).**

$$\forall A \in CommRingType, u \in A, q \in Nat, hq \in \operatorname{Positive}\left(q\right),\; \operatorname{ker}\left(\operatorname{evaluate}\left(u, q, hq\right)\right) = \operatorname{principalIdeal}\left(\operatorname{P}\left(u, q\right)\right)$$

*Commentary.*

The kernel is exactly the principal ideal generated by P. The quotient coordinate map sends f(T)e0 back to projection(f), so no additional polynomial relation is lost. Conversely P(T)e0=0 annihilates every multiple of P. This exact equality is used by the quotient equivalence constructor.
