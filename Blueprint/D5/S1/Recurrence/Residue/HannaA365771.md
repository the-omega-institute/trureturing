# Hanna's Catalan-binomial residues modulo five

## Abstract

Base-five digit descent proves Hanna's first residue and the shared classification.

The sequence is a product of a Catalan quotient and a binomial coefficient. All indices and arithmetic operations below are in Nat. Nat.div denotes natural integer division; subtraction is truncated at zero. The quotient in a is exact. The digit predicate includes zero. OEIS entries have no page numbers; quotations refer to A365771 revision 23, Name and Comments, and A033042, Name.

**Definition 1.1 (The sequence).**

$$\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{a}\left(n\right) = \operatorname{Nat.div}\left(\operatorname{Nat.choose}\left(2 \cdot n + 1, n\right), 2 \cdot n + 1\right) \cdot \operatorname{Nat.choose}\left(3 \cdot n - 1, n\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S1/Recurrence/Residue/HannaA365771.a` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Paul D. Hanna (2023). *OEIS A365771: Catalan-binomial residues modulo five*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A365771>.

*Commentary.*

a(n) = binomial(2*n+1, n)/(2*n+1) * binomial(3*n-1, n) for n >= 0. The index n is a natural number, including zero; Nat.choose denotes binomial.

**Definition 1.2 (Sums of distinct powers of five).**

$$\forall m \in \mathbb{N},\; (\operatorname{InA033042}\left(m\right)) \Leftrightarrow (\forall d \in \mathbb{N},\; (d \in \operatorname{Nat.digits}\left(5, m\right)) \Rightarrow (d \le 1))$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S1/Recurrence/Residue/HannaA365771.InA033042` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Paul D. Hanna (2023). *OEIS A365771: Catalan-binomial residues modulo five*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A365771>.

*Commentary.*

Sums of distinct powers of 5. The value m is in A033042 exactly when every digit in Nat.digits 5 m is zero or one. The offset is zero: 0, 1, 5, 6, 25.

**Definition 1.3 (Hanna's first conjecture).**

$$claim \Leftrightarrow (\forall m \in \mathbb{N},\; (0 < m) \Rightarrow ((\operatorname{InA033042}\left(m\right)) \Rightarrow ((\operatorname{a}\left(5 \cdot m\right)) \bmod 5 = 4)))$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S1/Recurrence/Residue/HannaA365771.claim` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Paul D. Hanna (2023). *OEIS A365771: Catalan-binomial residues modulo five*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A365771>.

*Commentary.*

Conjectures: given A033042 is the sums of distinct powers of 5, then (1) a(5*A033042(n)) == 4 (mod 5) for n > 0, Here m ranges over the positive values of A033042, rather than their enumeration indices.

**Theorem 1.4 (Removing the last base-five digit).**

$$\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; (0 < n) \Rightarrow ((\operatorname{InA033042}\left(n\right)) \Leftrightarrow (((n) \bmod 5 \le 1) \land (\operatorname{InA033042}\left(\operatorname{Nat.div}\left(n, 5\right)\right))))$$

For positive n the digit condition separates into the last digit being at most one and the same condition on Nat.div n 5.

**Theorem 1.5 (The complete positive-index classification).**

$$\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; (0 < n) \Rightarrow ((\operatorname{a}\left(n\right)) \bmod 5 = \operatorname{if} (\operatorname{InA033042}\left(n\right)) \operatorname{then} (\operatorname{if} ((n) \bmod 5 = 0) \operatorname{then} (4) \operatorname{else} (2)) \operatorname{else} (0))$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S1/Recurrence/Residue/HannaA365771.residue_classification` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Acknowledgement.* Paul D. Hanna (2023). *OEIS A365771: Catalan-binomial residues modulo five*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A365771>.

*Commentary.*

Lucas decomposition makes Nat.choose (3*n) n * Nat.choose (2*n) n congruent to the indicator of the digit predicate. Strong induction on n proves this through division by five. The exact identity 3*(n+1)*a(n)=2*M(n), where M is that binomial product, determines the residue at last digits zero through three. At last digit four, Nat.choose (3*n-1) n vanishes modulo five directly by Lucas. The resulting classification is used by all three congruences.

**Theorem 1.6 (Hanna's first congruence).**

$$\forall m \in \mathbb{N},\; (0 < m) \Rightarrow ((\operatorname{InA033042}\left(m\right)) \Rightarrow ((\operatorname{a}\left(5 \cdot m\right)) \bmod 5 = 4))$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S1/Recurrence/Residue/HannaA365771.result` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Acknowledgement.* Paul D. Hanna (2023). *OEIS A365771: Catalan-binomial residues modulo five*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A365771>.

*Commentary.*

Multiplication by five appends a zero base-five digit. The classification therefore gives residue four for every positive m with digits zero or one.
