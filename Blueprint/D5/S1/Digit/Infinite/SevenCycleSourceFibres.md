# Unique seven-cycle address fibres

## Abstract

Unique seven-cycle address fibres.

The two periodic sources have arbitrarily late pairs of zero bits. Every seam address is eventually alternating, so no shifted source is a seam address. The signed-series fibre classification therefore gives a unique actual address at each orbit scalar.
