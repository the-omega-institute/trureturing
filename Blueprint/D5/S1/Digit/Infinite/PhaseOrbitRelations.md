*Commentary.*

The rotation orbit relation on the circle and the relation of eventual merging at possibly different successor times are countable Borel equivalence relations. Two legal streams eventually merge at possibly different times exactly when their phases belong to the same rotation orbit; they merge at a common time exactly when their phases agree. For each positive integer j, all streams of phase minus j times the golden ratio reach the zero row after j steps. Two distinct streams in that fibre remain distinct at every earlier step.
