# Seven-phase source coordinates

## Abstract

Seven-phase source coordinates.

The observation radius is lambda minus g^7*(g-1/5)/(4*(1+g^7)). The two actual source streams repeat 3,3,5,5,3,2,2 and 0,3,5,5,3,2,2. Their guards are 0,0,0,1,1,0,0, and their common suffix uses the original branch maps.
