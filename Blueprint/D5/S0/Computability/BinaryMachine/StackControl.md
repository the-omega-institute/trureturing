# Decision continuations and exact word movement

## Abstract

Decision continuations and exact word movement.

Both Boolean halts of a test dispatch to their respective continuation, counting the terminal jump. Word movement clears its destination and performs two reversals, so it preserves bit order and empties scratch. Fixed fallback words are installed by literal one-bit pushes. Disjoint finite control labels support branches with unrelated programs.
