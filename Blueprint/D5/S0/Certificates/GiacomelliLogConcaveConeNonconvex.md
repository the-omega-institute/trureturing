# Giacomelli's cone of infinitely log-concave initial data

## Abstract

*Commentary.*

Take d = 3, p = (1, −3, 2), q = (3, −2, 0). Strong induction shows that U = (1, 1, 1) and V = (1, 2, 4) generate 1 and 2ⁿ. Induction on the iterate shows that each positive iterate is zero whenever 1 ≤ i ≤ n. Both sequences are nonnegative, so U and V belong to coneInf p q. Their midpoint is (1, 3/2, 5/2). At index 1 its first determinant is (3/2)² − (5/2)·1 = −1/4. This contradicts convexity. Characterization and simple connectedness are separate questions.
