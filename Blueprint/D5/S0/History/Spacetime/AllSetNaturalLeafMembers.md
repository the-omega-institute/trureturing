*Commentary.*

In any universe, the encoding of the finite von Neumann ordinal of a natural number is its natural leaf. Its semantic members are exactly the natural leaves of all strictly smaller natural numbers. The set of members is the range of the natural leaf map on those smaller indices, including the empty range at zero.
