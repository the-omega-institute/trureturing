# The Universal Cyclic Covariant Algebra

## Abstract

Continuous matrix fields represent every cyclic covariant pair in a unital complex C-star algebra.

Let M be a positive integer, I = Z/MZ, and A_0 the continuous complex functions on the finite discrete set I. Let B_M be all continuous I by I complex matrix fields on AddCircle 1, with the supremum of the L2 operator norm. The homeomorphism from AddCircle 1 to the unit circle sends t to the winding coordinate z(t). Define D(f) to be the constant diagonal field, and S_M to have entry one in row j+1, column j, with the wrap entry in row zero multiplied by z. For M = 1, S_1 = z.

The integer action is alpha_n(f)(i) = f(i-n). Throughout, B is any unital complex C-star algebra, pi is a unital complex star homomorphism from A_0 to B, and v is unitary with v pi(f) v* = pi(alpha_1(f)) for every f. The construction includes nonfaithful representations, arbitrary target dimension, and proper spectra of v to the M-th power.

**Definition 1.1 (A lift defined on every continuous matrix field).**

$$\theta\left(F\right) = \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in I} \operatorname{chi}_{w}\left(F_{ij}\right) E_{ij}$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/ContinuousObservables/CyclicCovariantUniversal.cyclic_covariant_lift` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Set w = v^M and p_i = pi(delta_i). Using the representatives from zero through M-1, set E_ij = v^i p_0 (v^j)*. Covariance gives orthogonal projection supports, hence E_ij E_kl = delta_jk E_il, E_ij* = E_ji, and the diagonal sum is one.

For a continuous scalar coefficient, chi_w first restricts along spectrum(w) into the circle and its inverse parameter homeomorphism, then applies continuous functional calculus. Its values commute with the E_ij because w and w* do. This proves multiplication and star preservation of the finite matrix assembly. Centrality in the whole target algebra and equality of spectrum(w) with the circle are unnecessary. Automatic contractivity applies to the resulting star homomorphism on the complete field algebra.

**Theorem 1.2 (Every readout is preserved).**

$$\forall f \in A_{0}, \theta\left(D\left(f\right)\right) = \pi\left(f\right)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/ContinuousObservables/CyclicCovariantUniversal.cyclic_covariant_lift_readout` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The diagonal coefficients are constant scalar functions. Functional calculus sends them to the scalar algebra map, while E_ii = p_i. The finite delta-function expansion therefore recovers pi(f).

**Theorem 1.3 (The winding update is preserved).**

$$\theta\left(S_{M}\right) = v$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/ContinuousObservables/CyclicCovariantUniversal.cyclic_covariant_lift_update` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The ordinary successor entries contribute E_(j+1,j). At the wrap, functional calculus sends z to v^M. Each contribution is v p_j, including the single wrap contribution when M = 1, so their sum is v.

**Theorem 1.4 (Existence and uniqueness on the full continuous carrier).**

$$\exists! L: B_{M} \to B, (\forall f, L\left(D\left(f\right)\right) = \pi\left(f\right)) \land L\left(S_{M}\right) = v$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/ContinuousObservables/CyclicCovariantUniversal.cyclic_covariant_universal_property` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The quantified L is a unital complex star homomorphism. The direct field lift supplies existence for every covariant pair above. There is no additional assumption that v^M is one.

For uniqueness, the weighted-shift power formula identifies the original S_M^i D(delta_0) (S_M^j)* with the constant standard matrix units. The cardinal power S_M^M is the scalar coordinate field. Stone-Weierstrass uniqueness, transported by the parameter homeomorphism, determines a scalar continuous-function homomorphism from that coordinate. Finite entry reconstruction then determines the homomorphism on every continuous matrix field.

**Theorem 1.5 (Faithful readouts and closed generation).**

$$\operatorname{Injective}\left(D\right) \land S_{M} \in U\left(B_{M}\right) \land \operatorname{Covariant}\left(D, S_{M}\right) \land \overline{\operatorname{StarAlg}\left(D, S_{M}\right)} = B_{M}$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/ContinuousObservables/CyclicCovariantUniversal.cyclic_covariant_model` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Diagonal evaluation proves D injective. The original winding shift is unitary and implements alpha_1 in the stated orientation. Apply the constructed universal property to the closed star subalgebra generated by these elements. Inclusion composed with the resulting lift is the identity by uniqueness, so the subalgebra is all of B_M.

**Theorem 1.6 (Comparison with any separately universal crossed product).**

$$\exists! \phi: A \sim B_{M}, \phi\circ\iota = D \land \phi\left(u\right) = S_{M} \land \operatorname{Isometry}\left(\phi\right)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/ContinuousObservables/CyclicCovariantUniversal.cyclic_covariant_unique_isomorphism` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The quantified Phi is a complex star-algebra equivalence. Suppose A has a covariant inclusion iota and unitary u and satisfies the full crossed-product universal property. The comparison needs only its universal contracts for targets A and B_M. The universal maps in both directions have identity composites by uniqueness. They give a complex star-algebra equivalence, which is automatically isometric. The equivalence is unique relative to the specified readout, cyclic origin, orientation, and wrap edge.
