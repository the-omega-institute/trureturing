# Trace-Rank Jordan Recovery

## Abstract

Complete transfer traces and ranks recover the periodic and zero-block profiles.

**Theorem 1.1 (Rank differences recover zero blocks).**

$$\forall Y \in Type, tau \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Y, Y\right), zeroBlocks \in \operatorname{BlockMultiset}\left(\right), k \in \operatorname{Nat}\left(\right),\; \left(\operatorname{Finite}\left(Y\right) \land \left(\left(\forall j \in \operatorname{Nat}\left(\right),\; \operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{finrank}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{range}\left(\operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(tau\right), j\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{card}\left(\operatorname{PeriodicCore}\left(tau\right)\right)\right) = \operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{blockProfileDimension}\left(zeroBlocks\right), \operatorname{blockKernelTower}\left(zeroBlocks, j\right)\right)\right) \land 0 < k\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{finrank}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{range}\left(\operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(tau\right), \operatorname{pred}\left(k\right)\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{card}\left(\operatorname{PeriodicCore}\left(tau\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{finrank}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{range}\left(\operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(tau\right), k\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{card}\left(\operatorname{PeriodicCore}\left(tau\right)\right)\right)\right) = \operatorname{blockCountAtLeast}\left(zeroBlocks, k\right) \land \operatorname{blockCountExactly}\left(zeroBlocks, k\right) = \operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{finrank}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{range}\left(\operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(tau\right), \operatorname{pred}\left(k\right)\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{card}\left(\operatorname{PeriodicCore}\left(tau\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{finrank}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{range}\left(\operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(tau\right), k\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{card}\left(\operatorname{PeriodicCore}\left(tau\right)\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{finrank}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{range}\left(\operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(tau\right), \operatorname{pred}\left(\operatorname{add}\left(k, 1\right)\right)\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{card}\left(\operatorname{PeriodicCore}\left(tau\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{finrank}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{range}\left(\operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(tau\right), \operatorname{add}\left(k, 1\right)\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{card}\left(\operatorname{PeriodicCore}\left(tau\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)$$

*Commentary.*

At every positive index, successive residual ranks count zero blocks of at least that size, and the next difference counts exact size.

**Theorem 1.2 (Trace and rank spectra determine the Jordan profile).**

$$\forall YLeft \in Type, YRight \in Type, tau \in \operatorname{Function}\left(YLeft, YLeft\right), sigma \in \operatorname{Function}\left(YRight, YRight\right), cycleCountsLeft \in \operatorname{Function}\left(\operatorname{Nat}\left(\right), \operatorname{Nat}\left(\right)\right), cycleCountsRight \in \operatorname{Function}\left(\operatorname{Nat}\left(\right), \operatorname{Nat}\left(\right)\right), zeroBlocksLeft \in \operatorname{BlockMultiset}\left(\right), zeroBlocksRight \in \operatorname{BlockMultiset}\left(\right),\; \left(\operatorname{Finite}\left(YLeft\right) \land \left(\operatorname{Finite}\left(YRight\right) \land \left(\left(\forall r \in \operatorname{Nat}\left(\right),\; 0 < r \Rightarrow \operatorname{trace}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{Finsupp}\left(YLeft, \operatorname{Complex}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(tau\right), r\right)\right) = \operatorname{trace}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{Finsupp}\left(YRight, \operatorname{Complex}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(sigma\right), r\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall k \in \operatorname{Nat}\left(\right),\; \operatorname{finrank}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{range}\left(\operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(tau\right), k\right)\right)\right) = \operatorname{finrank}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{range}\left(\operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(sigma\right), k\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{apply}\left(cycleCountsLeft, 0\right) = 0 \land \left(\operatorname{apply}\left(cycleCountsRight, 0\right) = 0 \land \left(\left(\forall r \in \operatorname{Nat}\left(\right),\; 0 < r \Rightarrow \operatorname{trace}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{Finsupp}\left(YLeft, \operatorname{Complex}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(tau\right), r\right)\right) = \operatorname{finsetSum}\left(\operatorname{divisors}\left(r\right), \operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(d, \operatorname{Nat}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{natCast}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(d, \operatorname{apply}\left(cycleCountsLeft, d\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall r \in \operatorname{Nat}\left(\right),\; 0 < r \Rightarrow \operatorname{trace}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{Finsupp}\left(YRight, \operatorname{Complex}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(sigma\right), r\right)\right) = \operatorname{finsetSum}\left(\operatorname{divisors}\left(r\right), \operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(d, \operatorname{Nat}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{natCast}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(d, \operatorname{apply}\left(cycleCountsRight, d\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall k \in \operatorname{Nat}\left(\right),\; \operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{finrank}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{range}\left(\operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(tau\right), k\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{card}\left(\operatorname{PeriodicCore}\left(tau\right)\right)\right) = \operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{blockProfileDimension}\left(zeroBlocksLeft\right), \operatorname{blockKernelTower}\left(zeroBlocksLeft, k\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall k \in \operatorname{Nat}\left(\right),\; \operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{finrank}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{range}\left(\operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{transferOperator}\left(sigma\right), k\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{card}\left(\operatorname{PeriodicCore}\left(sigma\right)\right)\right) = \operatorname{natSub}\left(\operatorname{blockProfileDimension}\left(zeroBlocksRight\right), \operatorname{blockKernelTower}\left(zeroBlocksRight, k\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(cycleCountsLeft = cycleCountsRight \land zeroBlocksLeft = zeroBlocksRight\right)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/ObserverMemory/FunctionalGraphs/TraceRankJordanRecovery.trace_rank_spectra_determine_jordan_profile` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Commentary.*

Möbius inversion uniquely recovers periodic cycle counts from traces. Stable-image ranks recover the periodic dimension, after which all residual ranks uniquely recover the nilpotent zero-block multiset.
