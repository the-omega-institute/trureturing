*Commentary.*

Fix m>=5, k=2m-2 and P=ZMod(2m-1). H(0),H(1) lie in B={1,...,m}; their ordered membership pair is ell. Its full image has three labels. Two-block original GLOBAL feasibility is equivalent to Pi3: the internal image on U={2,...,m-1} has at most two labels, and m is odd or one of the two boundaries is nonbase. For the base-internal clause, the internal image is {0,X}, with X nonzero. The third label Z must occur at one or both boundaries. Thus all six rows of table 29.19 apply, without an additional parity or boundary compatibility condition. The target has arbitrary labels A(v)!=B(v) separately, arbitrary cross-value coincidences, arbitrary Type universes and independently observed f(none). Every original value, phase, inherited tail and unknown legal or rejected history is retained.

Necessity directly uses the same-stream row theorem: any correct two-block competitor has even rows, its second row is zero on U, and its row pairs refine both original support coordinates. Hence r<=2. If both boundary labels are base, the full image is exactly {0} union the internal image. With n=3 and r<=2 this forces r=2 and excludes base internally. The already established nonbase-internal obstruction then forces odd m. This comparison retains rejection and early stopping; scheduled rows on stopped sources supply no acquired endpoint or fee.

For sufficiency, U is nonempty because it contains phase two. The bound r<=2 therefore gives r=1 or r=2. The homogeneous construction handles r=1.
