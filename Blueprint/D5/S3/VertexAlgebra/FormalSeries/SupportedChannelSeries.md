# Supported collision coefficients and reconstruction

## Abstract

SupportedChannelSeries

Classical binomial-series algebra is applied to lower-supported vector coefficients. Mathlib supplies binomialSeries_add and native MvPowerSeries substitution. The finite diagonal arguments establish both the signed inverse expansion of (x-y)^N and the positive-factor inverse expansion of (y+t)^A. Native substitution has zero constant arguments; its antidiagonal coefficient fiber is finite even when the numerator has infinite support. Coefficient readouts are complex-linear and need not preserve multiplication.
