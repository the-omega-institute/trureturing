# Elliptic Modular Transport

## Abstract

Integral changes of basis transport the original period lattice, its regularized Weierstrass function, and the normalized continued annular kernel under every integral determinant-one modular transformation.

*Commentary.*

Let gamma have entries a,b,c,d, with ad-bc=1, and put j=c tau+d. The coordinate map (m,n) maps to (am-bn,-cm+dn), with inverse (r,s) mapping to (dr+bs,cr+as). Composing these integral linear maps with the original period-pair lattice coordinates gives an actual integer-linear equivalence from the lattice generated by 1,tau to the lattice generated by 1,gamma tau. Its complex action is l mapping to l/j; its inverse is multiplication by j. Consequently w/j belongs to the new lattice if and only if w belongs to the old lattice.

*Commentary.*

For every gamma,tau,w satisfying both 0 < Im w < Im tau and 0 < Im(w/j) < Im(gamma tau), let q=exp(2 pi i tau), z=exp(2 pi i w), q'=exp(2 pi i gamma tau), and z'=exp(2 pi i w/j). Then the original annular series at q',z' equals j squared times the original series at q,z minus c j/(2 pi i). Apply the existing strict-strip Weierstrass-E2 identity at both points and the continued modular covariance. The first strip excludes lattice points because an integer strictly between zero and one cannot occur as the second lattice coordinate.

The two strict strips are independent convergence requirements. For tau=i, w=(1+i)/2 and gamma=S, the first strip holds while w/j=(1-i)/2 is outside the second. Only the continued identity applies there. The multiplicative substitution remains singular at z=0 and gives no analytic annular boundary value at that point. These classical geometric results concern scalar functions; they supply no original-field thermal trace, four-point convergence, nonunimodular sector closure, full VOA or CFT construction, anomaly or fusion theory, string theory, or AdS/CFT correspondence.
