Actual half currents and finite Sugawara normal sums

Let D have natural rank, an integral symmetric Gram matrix G and even diagonal. Rank zero is included. P is the actual complex MvPolynomial on Fin(rank(D)) times N. No positivity or unimodularity is assumed. Current Heisenberg and support results require no inverse matrix.

Write Gc=D.G.map(Int.cast). Sugawara commutator, central and spectrum laws assume exactly H*Gc=1 and Gc*H=1. Symmetry of H is derived from these two identities and symmetry of G; it is not an additional premise. No desired current or Virasoro law, grading or homogeneity is assumed.

For integer j the physical frequency is j+1/2 and the opposite parameter is -j-1; there is no zero-frequency mode. Negative j acts by multiplication by X(i,(-j-1).toNat). Nonnegative j acts by (j+1/2) times the actual Gram-weighted polynomial derivative.

R(p) is the supremum of k+1 over variables actually appearing in p. Normal pairs use the physical creation-left split j<0, including j=-1. Their finite support is established before forming the linear End operator normalSum. rawL_m=(1/2) sum_i,l H_i,l normalSum_i,l(m). L_m=rawL_m+delta_(m,0) rank/16 identity; the second host proves that shift is forced by the actual contraction.
