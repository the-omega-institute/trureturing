## Abstract

Original Binomial External Resolvent Contractions

Every fixed original input and output mode has a polynomial-geometric l1 majorant. The estimate follows from the generalized binomial recurrence, a natural binomial bound and geometric summability; l2 membership alone is not used to justify double sums.

The external odd contraction is the absolutely convergent double series in the explicit all-index odd kernel. The square contraction contains the direct identity pairing and an absolutely convergent triple series in the literal Cauchy kernel and explicit odd kernel. Basis expansions on the actual carrier prove that these are the bounded bilinear contractions.

The literal mixed corrected source row has exactly the stated binomial column and frequency-root factor. Uniform norm convergence of its finite cutoffs supplies the external-block limit. All pairings use ordinary complex bilinear transpose.
