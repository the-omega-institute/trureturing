*Commentary.*

The two rotations in distinct tensor slots have a common even displacement space. Their literal lift exponent has four independent blocks after a parity and spectator split. Each block pairs the sum of two coordinates with the sum of two coordinates on the other half, and its character sum is eight. The product is 4096. With the two individual ground traces normalized to 256, this gives the reflection-ground trace used in the weight-one argument for a suitable choice of discrete torsion.
