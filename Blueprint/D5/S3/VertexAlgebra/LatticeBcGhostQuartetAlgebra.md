D is the original LatticeGeneratingFieldLocality.LatticeData, with arbitrary natural rank, integral symmetric Gram matrix and even diagonal. Carrier D is the finite-support sum over every integral charge of the actual complex oscillator polynomial space. State D is Carrier D tensor Fock Complex. The existing Fock, b, c, M, G, Q and J are used without replacement. No positivity, positive-energy cutoff, restricted charge spectrum, surrogate differential or assumed locality, Jacobi, spectrum or nilpotence is added. Isotropic and negative-norm charge sectors are not removed.

This retention establishes quartet prerequisites and per-mode identities, not a total K contraction, the cohomology of the full original Q, a physical no-ghost theorem, a string construction or AdS/CFT. Full-Q nilpotence is a separately previously accepted result outside this scoped graph, not imported or certified here. Wherever that separate route is invoked, both Gram-inverse equations and the rank-26 condition remain required; these are not premises of statements that do not use that route. The larger VOA/CFT/string/AdS goal remains active and open.

Write <u,v>_G=sum_i,j u_i G_ij v_j. The directional matter current is a_u(m)=sum_i u_i neutralMode_i(m); O_u(m)=a_u(m) tensor id acts on the original State. Its commutator is the actual integer-mode Heisenberg law, and the original C and B supply the fermionic anticommutators and commute with O.

For m nonzero, d_m=C(-m) O_l(m), r_m=|m|^(-1) O_k(-m) B(m); both are zero at m=0. Statewise finite-support proofs on the actual tensor carrier justify dSum=sum^f_m d_m and rSum=sum^f_m r_m as linear maps. Delta=p*dSum and R=p^(-1)*rSum are defined on the same State, not on an assumed finite-dimensional sector.

K is the actual finite sum of rawKAtom, defined with <l,k>_G=1. For positive m its normal atom is |m|^(-1)(O_k(-m)O_l(m)+m C(-m)B(m)); for negative m the matter factors are reversed and the ghost term is -m B(m)C(-m), with the same |m|^(-1) factor. The zero atom vanishes. The raw-to-normal correction is proved using the actual Heisenberg and CAR relations.

The anticommutator Delta R+R Delta=K requires the dual pairing and p nonzero. The separate square-zero laws require <l,l>_G=0 and <k,k>_G=0 respectively. J grades Delta by +1, and therefore also grades the actual Q-Delta by +1. H is arbitrary in original_Q_quartet_algebra: this grading statement does not claim Q squared is zero.
