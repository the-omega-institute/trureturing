## Abstract

An actual original-Q complex on all finite superpositions in the exceptional sector.

**Theorem 1.1 (Native differential correspondence).**

$$\forall v \in \operatorname{sector}\left(D\right), \operatorname{inclusion}\left(\operatorname{differential}\left(v\right)\right) = \operatorname{Q}\left(D, H, \operatorname{inclusion}\left(v\right)\right)$$

*Commentary.*

Both actual inverse-Gram equations are assumed. Basis extensionality turns the native Q calculation into equality of linear maps, hence invariance of the entire sector. The differential on an arbitrary state is twice its c0 ground coefficient times the pure {1,5} ground tensor.

**Theorem 1.2 (Genuine original nilpotence on this sector).**

$$\forall v \in \operatorname{sector}\left(D\right), \operatorname{Q}\left(D, H, \operatorname{Q}\left(D, H, v\right)\right) = 0$$

*Commentary.*

The derived differential has square zero because its sole image vector is killed. The native correspondence proves the original Q square is zero on every vector of this complete sector, for every rank admitting both inverses, including rank 26. This does not assert Q squared is zero on the unrestricted full carrier.

*Commentary.*
