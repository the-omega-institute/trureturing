Actual arbitrary-ground tensor operators and supported half fields

Let D have natural rank, an integral symmetric Gram matrix G and even diagonal. Rank zero is included. P is the actual complex MvPolynomial on Fin(rank(D)) times N. No positivity or unimodularity is assumed. Current Heisenberg and support results require no inverse matrix.

Write Gc=D.G.map(Int.cast). Sugawara commutator, central and spectrum laws assume exactly H*Gc=1 and Gc*H=1. Symmetry of H is derived from these two identities and symmetry of G; it is not an additional premise. No desired current or Virasoro law, grading or homogeneity is assumed.

Let T be any Type with AddCommGroup T and Module Complex T, with no finite-dimensional, irreducible or Nontrivial premise. tensorEnd(A) is the actual TensorProduct.map A id. tensorCurrent, tensorRawL and tensorL are these actual operators. Tensor induction gives contextual current and stress bounds on every actual tensor vector, including arbitrary sums of pure tensors.

The tensor current and Virasoro laws follow by actual TensorProduct.map transport of the retained operator equalities. On P tensor T the current central factor is delta_(j+k+1,0)(j+1/2)G_i,l and the shifted Virasoro central factor is delta_(m+n,0)(rank/12)(m^3-m). There is no scalar-commutant assertion on P tensor T.

The same actual transport gives the monomial tensor energy, L_0(1 tensor t)=rank/16 (1 tensor t), all positive L_m(1 tensor t)=0, and all negative ground finite creation formulas from the first host. In particular L_-1(1 tensor t)=(1/2 sum_i,l H_i,l X(i,0)X(l,0)) tensor t. It generally is nonzero: rank-one G=2,H=1/2 gives (1/4)X(0,0)^2. This ground state differs from a VOA vacuum.

HalfField is HVertexOperator on integer exponents in t=sqrt(z). The supported actual currentField and tensorCurrentField have coefficient -2j-3 equal to the physical current with index j. The supported actual stressField and tensorStressField have coefficient -2m-4 equal to the shifted L_m. There is no Y_theta(omega) identity or assumed all-state twisted Y.

At rank zero, the current colour is empty and the modes, shift and stress fields vanish.
