# Finite-group orbit fixed lines and orbit count

## Abstract

Finite-group orbit spans have orbit-sum fixed lines, with rank equal to orbit count.

A finite group acting through linear equivalences on linearly independent indexed orbits has a fixed subspace generated by one orbit sum per orbit. This turns group action into an exact finite-dimensional count for charged lattice orbit sectors; it does not identify a whole C2 quotient.

*Commentary.*

The hypotheses give a finite group, a representation by invertible linear maps, permutation of the indexed orbit, and linear independence of the orbit vectors. The conclusion is an equality of submodules: the intersection of the orbit span with the representation fixed submodule equals the span of the finite orbit sum.

**Theorem 1.2 (The orbit sum is nonzero).**

*Commentary.*

**Theorem 1.3 (The fixed space is spanned by one sum per independent orbit).**

*Commentary.*

For a finite family of linearly independent G-orbits, the coefficient-shift argument forces each invariant coefficient function to be constant along its orbit. The fixed submodule is therefore exactly the span of the corresponding orbit sums.

**Theorem 1.4 (The fixed-space rank equals the orbit count).**

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean. ∎

*Commentary.*

The orbit sums remain linearly independent because the original orbit vectors are independent and have disjoint index supports. Consequently the invariant submodule has dimension exactly the cardinality of the orbit index type, including in characteristics dividing the group order.
