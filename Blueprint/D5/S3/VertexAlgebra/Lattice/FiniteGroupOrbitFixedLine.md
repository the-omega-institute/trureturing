# Finite-group orbit fixed line

## Abstract

Finite-group orbit spans have a single Reynolds fixed line.

A finite group acting through linear equivalences on a linearly independent orbit has a fixed subspace inside that orbit span generated by the orbit sum. This records the precise group-action statement used for charged lattice orbit sectors; it does not identify a whole C2 quotient.

**Theorem 1.1 (The fixed orbit submodule is the orbit-sum line).**

*Commentary.*

The hypotheses give a finite group, a representation by invertible linear maps, permutation of the indexed orbit, and linear independence of the orbit vectors. The conclusion is an equality of submodules: the intersection of the orbit span with the representation fixed submodule equals the span of the finite orbit sum.

**Theorem 1.2 (The orbit sum is nonzero).**

*Commentary.*

Linear independence of the orbit vectors makes the all-one finite linear combination nonzero. Thus the fixed submodule identified above is a genuine one-dimensional line.
