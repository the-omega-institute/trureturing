# Finite Dimension of the Fixed Neutral Image

## Abstract

Two reductions in word length give a finite spanning family for the fixed vacuum-charge image.

LatticeData D consists of a natural-number rank and a symmetric integral Gram matrix G with even diagonal. A word is a list of pairs (i,n), with i a basis index and n a zero-based natural oscillator index. Total index is the sum of the n entries. The cutoffs below use the actual rank-24 Golay-Leech lattice data.
