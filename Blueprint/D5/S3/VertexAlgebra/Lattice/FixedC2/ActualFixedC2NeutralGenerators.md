# Quadratic Generators of the Neutral Image

## Abstract

Low-index quadratics generate the neutral image as a complex algebra.

LatticeData D consists of a natural-number rank and a symmetric integral Gram matrix G with even diagonal. Charges are integer tuples indexed by the basis. The actual complex carrier consists of finitely supported charge families of oscillator polynomials, with oscillator indices starting at zero.

**Theorem 1.3 (The neutral image is the algebra generated by the low quadratics).**

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/VertexAlgebra/Lattice/FixedC2/ActualFixedC2NeutralGenerators.neutral_image_eq_low_adjoin` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Assume G(i,i) is nonzero for every basis index i. The neutral image equals the underlying complex submodule of the algebra generated by the classes of pairs ((i,0),(j,s)) for all basis indices i and j and 0 ≤ s ≤ 6. A quadratic class of total index at least seven vanishes. At smaller total index, translation normalizes it to a scalar multiple of the indicated pair. Induction on even word length uses the product of a pair with a shorter word and the shorter contraction corrections. The neutral span contains the vacuum class and is preserved by multiplication by each low quadratic, giving the reverse inclusion.
