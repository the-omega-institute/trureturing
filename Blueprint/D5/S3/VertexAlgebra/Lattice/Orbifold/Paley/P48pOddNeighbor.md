*Commentary.*

A rational catalogue row belongs to the odd neighbor exactly when its half-frame pairings are integral and their residue modulo three has zero frame syndrome. The neighbor is obtained by adjoining half of frame row zero to the integral rows pairing evenly with that frame vector.
