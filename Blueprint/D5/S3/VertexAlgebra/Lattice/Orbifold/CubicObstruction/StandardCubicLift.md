*Commentary.*

Let G act faithfully by complex linear equivalences U on the actual lattice carrier for D. Assume each U(t) preserves every integer vertex mode and sends the ground vector at charge a to the ground vector at charge(t)(a), scaled by a complex unit phase(t,a). Here charge is a group homomorphism to the additive automorphisms of the charge lattice under composition. Let x project to a bilinear isometry g of exact order three and let s project to negation.

For y=s x s^(-1) and z=(x y)^2, the theorem gives charge(z)=g, order(z)=3, and phase(z,a)=1 for every charge a fixed by g. Negation commutes with g, so y projects to g and z projects to g^4=g. The leading charged product makes the phase of x multiplicative on the fixed charges. In particular its phases at a and -a multiply to one. Evaluating y s=s x on the ground vector at -a identifies the phase of y at a with that of x at -a. Thus x y and z act as the identity on each fixed-charge ground vector.

Diagonal character conjugacy compares U(z) with the native standard lift using their agreement on all fixed-charge phases. The native cube is the identity on the whole carrier, so faithfulness gives z^3=1 in G. The nontrivial projection gives exact order three. A twisted conformal spectrum requires a spectral comparison for this cubic. An ambient associator obstruction also requires identifying the restriction to the subgroup generated by z with the cyclic class from the VOA.
