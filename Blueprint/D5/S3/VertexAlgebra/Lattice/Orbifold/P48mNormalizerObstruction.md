# The P48m Normalizer and Weight-Two Obstruction

## Abstract

The P48m rank and group-order premises force two independent fixed weight-two states under compatible represented actions.

*Commentary.*

Nebe’s arXiv:1312.3780v2, Theorem 3.2 identifies P48m as an extremal even unimodular lattice of rank 48 with an order-five isometry s of type 5-(8,16)-8. Section 2 defines the rational fixed dimension as the second entry of the pair, giving 16. Remark 3.3 gives order 1200 for the normalizer of the cyclic subgroup generated by s. The Nebe–Sloane Catalogue of Lattices gives the full automorphism group order 1200 in the P48m entry GROUP_ORDER (https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/P48m.html#GROUP_ORDER).

Let D have rank 48 and positive-definite Gram form, and let G act through a rational Gram-preserving representation ρ on its rational Cartan space. Let π be a group homomorphism from H to G and A a complex representation of H on the actual lattice carrier. Assume A transports each rational current J(x) to J(ρ(π(h))x) under h and preserves all integer vertex modes. The theorem assumes an element s of G with rational fixed rank 16 and both G and the normalizer of the cyclic subgroup N generated by s of order 1200.

The equal finite orders make the normalizer all of G, hence N is normal. Its rational fixed space W is G-invariant by conjugation and has dimension 16. Gram preservation and positive definiteness give an invariant orthogonal complement of dimension 32. For each block U in this decomposition, blockQuadratic is the actual half-normalized sum (1/2) Σ_i J(b_i)_{−1} J(b^i) for a rational basis and its Gram-dual basis. The block-quadratic theorem gives both states weight two and invariance under A. Their mode-one actions on rational currents are the respective block projections, proving complex linear independence since both blocks are nonzero.

*Commentary.*

Under the preceding hypotheses, suppose the A-invariant subspace of the lattice carrier has an injective complex-linear map into a complex vector space V with a linear operator L whose weight-two eigenspace is finite-dimensional. Assume the map carries every invariant state of lattice grade two into the eigenspace of L at eigenvalue 2, as for a compatible conformal inclusion. The two invariant quadratic states have linearly independent images there, so that eigenspace has complex dimension at least two.

For a central-charge-48 target satisfying these hypotheses this obstructs a one-dimensional weight-two space. The global existence question for an extremal vertex operator algebra of central charge 48 remains open.
