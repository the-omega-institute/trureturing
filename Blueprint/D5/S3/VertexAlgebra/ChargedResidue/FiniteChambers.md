*Commentary.*

Each contraction chamber has its own natural range. A supported sparse quadratic convolution identifies each ordered sum with its signed chamber coefficient. The signed difference is evaluated by the two-pole formula, keeping both negative-degree tests. The identity is used in the actual complete residue diagonal for arbitrary translated input monomials.
