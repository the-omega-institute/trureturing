# Finite simplicial cell induction

## Abstract

Finite simplicial sets admit induction by boundary attachments.

**Definition 1.1 (The costar of a nondegenerate simplex).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Geometry/FiniteSimplicialCellInduction.costar`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Geometry/FiniteSimplicialCellInduction.costar` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* The DifferentialGeometry contributors (2026). *Maximal simplicial cells and finite cell induction*. URL: <https://github.com/qinz1yang/differential-geometry/tree/afc0a69081be678c24cb55c5111236af329bfda1>.

*Commentary.*

For a simplicial set X in an arbitrary universe and a nondegenerate simplex s of X, the costar contains exactly those simplices x for which the subcomplex generated by s is not contained in the subcomplex generated by x. The functorial map condition makes this a subcomplex of X.

*Commentary.*

If a nondegenerate simplex s of X is maximal, the standard boundary inclusion and its attaching map into the costar, followed by the characteristic map and the costar inclusion into X, form a pushout square. The proof checks the square degree by degree using the boundary pullback, the covering equality, and injectivity off the boundary.

**Theorem 1.13 (Induction by finite simplex attachment).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Geometry/FiniteSimplicialCellInduction.finite_cell_induction`

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Geometry/FiniteSimplicialCellInduction.finite_cell_induction` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Citation.* The DifferentialGeometry contributors (2026). *Maximal simplicial cells and finite cell induction*. URL: <https://github.com/qinz1yang/differential-geometry/tree/afc0a69081be678c24cb55c5111236af329bfda1>.

*Commentary.*

Let P be a proposition on simplicial sets in any fixed universe. Assume P for every initial simplicial set. For every natural number n and simplicial sets X and Y, every attaching map g from the standard n-simplex boundary to X, characteristic map r from the standard n-simplex to Y, and map b from X to Y, assume P of Y whenever the square with the boundary inclusion, g, r and b is a pushout and both P of that boundary and P of X hold. Then P holds for every finite simplicial set. The proof first inducts on a dimension bound and then on the number of nondegenerate simplices, using a maximal cell and its costar. No regularity or injectivity of boundary attaching maps is assumed.
