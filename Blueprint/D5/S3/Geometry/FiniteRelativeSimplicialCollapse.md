# Finite collapse geometry

## Abstract

Finite labeled collapse sequences give relative deformations, link replay, and cone extension fixing the actual apex.

A Sequence is an ordered finite list of actual free pairs. The realization composes their coordinate retractions, records every intermediate frame including the terminal n+1 frame, and fixes the entire final realization. Link, deletion, lifted-pair, and cone-extension APIs preserve original labels and distinguish combinatorial empty faces from geometric points.
