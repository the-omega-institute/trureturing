# Noncommutative edge return orbits

## Abstract

For the original paired-edge relation, the six edge slots split into two families and every actual edge chain is exactly an orbit of the corresponding return word.

**Definition 1.6 (Return subgroup).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/ReturnCycleWords.returnGroup`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/ReturnCycleWords.returnGroup` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

returnGroup is the subgroup closure of the selected return word, so inverse and product powers remain inside the same actual family.

For the low return word ell = v inverse times u squared, centrality has a concrete consequence when u and v generate the entire group. Assume the subgroup closure of {u,v} equals the whole group and ell belongs to its center. Centrality gives Commute(u,ell), while the group identity v = u squared times ell inverse expresses v in terms of u and a central element.

The existing inverse, power and product rules for Commute therefore give Commute(u,v). Every pair in {u,v} commutes; Mathlib's Subgroup.isMulCommutative_closure then proves that their generated subgroup is commutative. The generation assumption identifies this subgroup with the whole group. Thus a group generated by these two elements is commutative whenever its low return word is central.

Consequently, in a noncommutative group generated by the marked pair u,v, the low return word cannot be central. The generation hypothesis concerns the same marked pair used in the edge relation. A concrete matrix group still requires identification of the marked matrices, proof of their generation, and the actual return orders before this application supplies information about its edge degrees.

**Theorem 1.7 (Actual edge equivalence is exactly a return orbit).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/ReturnCycleWords.actual_edge_orbit_iff`

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/ReturnCycleWords.actual_edge_orbit_iff` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For every group G, every type Omega with the opposite-group action, all u,v in G and occurrences a,b in Omega times Fin(6), the equivalence closure of the original edgeRel(pairing u v) relates a and b exactly when they have the same low/high family and some element of the corresponding returnGroup sends the normalized coordinate of a to that of b. The forward proof checks every actual face-slot generator and propagates the invariant through reflexive, symmetric and transitive chains. The reverse proof expands a subgroup word into actual three-step cycles and connects every occurrence to its normalized representative. No finiteness, faithfulness, transitivity or freeness assumption is used. This characterizes the edge relation only; it does not assert endpoint orientation, link topology, metric geometry, Euler characteristic or a polygon-manifold realization.
