# From local angle envelopes to all global curvature faces

## Abstract

The actual occurrence-counted curvature has a uniform inward margin on an explicit global four-cycle box.

T is an arbitrary finite type of tetrahedra. E is an arbitrary global-edge type with decidable equality. An incidence s has edge:T -> Fin(6) -> E and low:E -> Bool. In the local order (12,13,14,34,24,23), slots 0 and 3 are high and the other four are low. FourCycle(s) requires this equality at every local slot, degree eight for every low global label, and degree at least twelve for every high label. Degree is the actual cardinality of the fibre of edge, including repeated labels.

For each local target, frame is an explicit permutation induced by a tetrahedral vertex relabeling. Its zeroth coordinate is the target; for a low target its slots 0,1,3,4 are low. All frame coordinates read the SAME global vector x:E -> Real. C(s,x,o) is the previous exact six-variable cosine after this relabeling. The angle is arccos(C), and K(s,x,e) is 2pi minus the sum over all occurrences o with source e.

Set m=card(T x Fin(6)), t=pi/(m+2), c=cos(t), and delta(T)=min(1/8,(1-c)/(2+c)). The lower vector L(s) has value 5/4 at low labels and 1+delta at high labels. U(s) has value 2 at low labels and 8/5 at high labels. Box(s,x) means L(s)(e)<=x(e)<=U(s)(e) for all e. The size-independent eta is min(pi, min(2pi-8arccos(293/400), min(8arccos(1577/2236)-2pi,12arccos(37/43)-2pi))).

Application: the actual physical covolume energy for arbitrary finite incidence. Take arbitrary finite types T and E, decidable equality on E, and s:Incidence(T,E). For this application edge:T->Fin(6)->E is arbitrary; the field low is unused, and no FourCycle or manifold-gluing hypothesis is imposed. Keep all occurrences o=(t,q) in T times Fin(6), with source(o)=s.edge(t,q), star(e) the full source fibre and d_e=card(star(e)). Define the continuous linear read R_t(p)_q=p_(s.edge(t,q)) and the open global genuine domain D_s={p:E->Real | R_t(p) belongs to U for every t}. Use the same six original slots, the same source angles and the same realization-family contract as in the LengthGramBody physical-covolume application. Repeated global labels within a tetrahedron are allowed. The domain requires positive lengths only on labels that occur; an unused coordinate p_e is unrestricted.

For any such realization family G put E_G(p)=sum_t cov_G(R_t(p))-2*pi*sum_e p_e. Here cov_G(a)=2*(mu3(range(G(a)))).toReal+sum_q a_q*theta_q(a) is the actual physical covolume of the original measured cut body, with its native normalized Hausdorff mass and complete positive-sheet coordinate contract. Thus this global energy uses those local physical masses; no scalar volume or antiderivative is assigned independently. On D_s it is independent of the admissible family G, since every local covolume is choice independent. It is C-infinity there, as a finite sum of the smooth local covolumes composed with the actual linear reads, followed by subtraction of the displayed linear term.

Let K_e(p)=curvature(s,cosh(p),e)=2*pi-sum_(o in star(e)) theta_(o.2)(R_(o.1)(p)). This is exactly the original occurrence-counted curvature: its relabeled six-variable source cosine reads the same global cosh vector. The local physical differential gives D E_G(p)[v]=sum_t sum_q theta_q(R_t(p))*v_(s.edge(t,q))-2*pi*sum_e v_e. Regrouping the full occurrence set by its source label changes the first sum to sum_e(sum_(o in star(e)) theta_(o.2)(R_(o.1)(p)))*v_e. Therefore D E_G(p)[v]=-sum_e K_e(p)*v_e for every p in D_s and every global direction v. In particular the Euclidean coordinate gradient of this actual physical energy is -K(p); its stationary points are precisely the zero-curvature vectors in D_s.

Differentiate the same finite angle sums on D_s. For every v,w:E->Real the actual Hessian satisfies D^2 E_G(p)[v,w]=-sum_e v_e*(D K_p[w])_e=sum_t sum_i sum_j (R_t(v))_i*H_G(R_t(p))_{ij}*(R_t(w))_j. The first equality differentiates the negative-curvature gradient; the second applies the angle chain rule and regroups its occurrence fibres, using H_G=J from the local physical identification. Since each H_G is symmetric, so is the global Hessian. This is the pullback along the actual maps R_t, even when they repeat coordinates: if s.edge(t,i)=s.edge(t,j), both local slots and their H_ij cross terms remain in the formula.

Fix any compact C contained in U and choose the preceding local constants 0<mu<=M before T,E,s,G,p and v. Whenever every R_t(p) lies in C, substitute z=R_t(v) in the local quadratic bounds and sum over t. The full source fibres give the exact identity sum_t sum_q(R_t(v)_q)^2=sum_e d_e*v_e^2: each occurrence contributes once, including repeated labels. Consequently mu*sum_e d_e*v_e^2<=D^2 E_G(p)[v,v]=-sum_e v_e*(D K_p[v])_e<=M*sum_e d_e*v_e^2. These constants are uniform over all finite incidence data and admissible realization choices whose local reads lie in this one C. The local bounds are established by compactness and positivity as above; summing them introduces no additional spectral hypothesis. If C is empty and T is nonempty, the local-read condition has no solution; if T is empty, all degree-weighted terms and the curvature-derivative pairing vanish.

Pointwise positive definiteness of the local H_G makes the global Hessian positive semidefinite. Its quadratic form vanishes exactly when every R_t(v)=0, equivalently when v_e=0 at every label with d_e>0; the remaining directions are precisely those supported on unused labels. Thus every positive-degree coordinate is detected even when local labels repeat, while a strict positive global Hessian requires every label to occur. At d_e=0, star(e) is empty, K_e(p)=2*pi, E_G depends on p_e only through -2*pi*p_e, and its Hessian has zero row and column there. These are derivative statements on the supplied genuine domain, not a global coercivity or full-domain convexity assertion. If T is empty, D_s is all of E->Real and E_G=-2*pi*sum_e p_e. If E is empty, existence of s.edge forces T empty as well, and the energy and all displayed sums are zero.

The physical energy also gives a derivative identity along supplied trajectories, without adding an existence assumption to the preceding construction. If a differentiable path n has n(t) in D_s and n'_e(t)=K_e(n(t))/d_e, the chain rule gives (E_G(n(t)))'=-sum_e K_e(n(t))^2/d_e<=0. Division is the total real division used in the formula: at d_e=0 both the normalized velocity and the corresponding energy term are zero, while K_e=2*pi remains. The derivative is strictly negative whenever a positive-degree edge has nonzero curvature. This energy identity applies in particular to the supplied-box trajectories considered below, but it does not construct a box, equilibrium or trajectory. For the alternative supplied velocity n'_e=n_e*K_e, the corresponding identity is (E_G(n(t)))'=-sum_e n_e(t)*K_e(n(t))^2. Its nonpositivity requires nonnegative global coordinates, for example when every label occurs and n(t) is genuine. An unused negative coordinate is not constrained by D_s, so that alternative sign conclusion is not unconditional.

Application: the actual degree-normalized forward flow on a supplied genuine box. Use the compact genuine set C and uniform constants 0<mu<=M established in the preceding physical-energy application, with the original six local slots and arccos branches. These constants remain fixed before the finite incidence data and the global length box below.

For a nonempty box B=[a,b] in E->Real, require every p in B to have positive coordinates and every local read R_t(p)=(p(s.edge(t,q)))_q to belong to C. Put d_e=degree(s,e) and K_e(p)=curvature(s,cosh(p),e), the original occurrence-counted curvature. Assume K_e(p)>0 on every lower face p_e=a_e and K_e(p)<0 on every upper face p_e=b_e. Fix c in B with K(c)=0. These are the hypotheses of this application; the supplied zero is retained.

On this box the preceding physical-Hessian pullback and compact estimate give mu*sum_e d_e*v_e^2<=-v dot DK_p[v]<=M*sum_e d_e*v_e^2, with the same constants for every point of the box. This is the degree-weighted Hessian bound of the actual physical energy; repeated local occurrences are already included.

Integrate the lower derivative bound along the straight segment from x to y in the same box. It gives mu*sum_e d_e*(y_e-x_e)^2<=-sum_e(K_e(y)-K_e(x))*(y_e-x_e). The zero-curvature equation at c forces every d_e>0. Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz then gives mu*sqrt(sum_e d_e*(p_e-c_e)^2)<=sqrt(sum_e K_e(p)^2/d_e) for every p in B. In particular K(p)=0 implies p=c within this box.

Every initial x0 in B has a forward trajectory n with n(0)=x0, n(t) in B and n'_e(t)=K_e(n(t))/d_e for all t>=0, including the derivative at zero. To obtain a global solution, clamp coordinates to [a,b] and compose the actual normalized field with this clamp. Its derivative is bounded on the compact box, so the clamped field is globally Lipschitz and bounded. Picard-Lindelof supplies a uniform local time interval and the existing uniform local-to-global pasting theorem supplies a global clamped solution. The strict inward signs keep its nonnegative-time trajectory inside the same box, where the clamped and original fields agree.

For W(t)=sum_e d_e*(n_e(t)-c_e)^2 the actual differential equation and the preceding two-point inequality give W'(t)<=-2*mu*W(t). Pinned Mathlib Gronwall therefore yields sqrt(W(t))<=exp(-mu*t)*sqrt(W(0)) for every t>=0. This is the degree-weighted distance to the supplied equilibrium along the actual normalized flow, with the original time variable. It includes zero initial error and repeated local occurrences.

This application combines the actual physical-energy Hessian and original curvature derivative with box invariance, Picard-Lindelof, uniform local-to-global pasting and scalar Gronwall. It gives the stated conditional trajectory, residual estimate and uniqueness of a zero in the supplied box, together with the physical-energy dissipation identity above. *Commentary.*

The derivative-based local theorem is consumed directly. The proof checks the actual finite frame permutations, proves the trigonometric gaps from exact square-root comparisons, and constructs delta rather than assuming an unspecified sufficiently small number. It proves cos(t)<2(1-delta)/(2+delta), so on a high lower face each angle is below t. There are at most m occurrences at any edge; hence the angle sum is below pi and its curvature exceeds pi.

On low lower and upper faces the exact cardinality is eight. The corresponding arccos bounds are summed over the actual fibre. On a high upper face the cardinality is at least twelve and all arccos values are nonnegative. These give the three fixed gaps in eta. The same proof shows -1<C(s,x,o)<1 throughout the box, and the lower vector itself witnesses nonemptiness. No solution or face sign is assumed.

This is a real incidence-system theorem. The formal statement does not certify that the incidence system is a manifold, prove the hyperbolic realization of each angle formula, or invoke an unproved Brouwer or co-volume existence axiom. Those geometric identifications and the ordinary variational existence step remain separate.
