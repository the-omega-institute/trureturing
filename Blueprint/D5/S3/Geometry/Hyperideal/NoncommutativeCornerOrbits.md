# Noncommutative actual corner orbits

## Abstract

The actual corner relation of the noncommutative face pairing is the specified subgroup orbit relation.

**Definition 1.1 (Right action convention).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/NoncommutativeCornerOrbits.act`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/NoncommutativeCornerOrbits.act` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For any group G, type Omega and left action of the opposite group on Omega, act(x,g) is op(g) acting on x. Thus it represents the right action of G on Omega.

**Theorem 1.3 (Composition of right actions).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/NoncommutativeCornerOrbits.act_mul`

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/NoncommutativeCornerOrbits.act_mul` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Commentary.*

For every x in Omega and g,h in G, act(act(x,g),h)=act(x,g*h). The multiplication order is that of a right action.

**Definition 1.4 (The actual whole-face pairing).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/NoncommutativeCornerOrbits.facePair`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/NoncommutativeCornerOrbits.facePair` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For arbitrary u,v in G, the face pairing sends (x,0) to (act(x,u),3), (x,1) to (act(x,v),2), (x,2) to (act(x,v inverse),1), and (x,3) to (act(x,u inverse),0).

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/NoncommutativeCornerOrbits.slotMap` (`✓ std3`).

*Commentary.*

For omitted faces 0,1,2,3, respectively, the permutations of the three increasing corner slots are [0,1,2], [2,0,1], [1,2,0], and [0,1,2]. The displayed nonidentity maps are inverse to one another.

**Theorem 1.12 (The original face-slot corner step).**

$$\forall G \in Group,\; \forall Omega \in RightAction\left(G\right),\; \forall u \in G,\; \forall v \in G,\; \forall x \in Omega,\; \forall f \in Fin\left(4\right),\; \forall r \in Fin\left(3\right),\; EqvGen\left(cornerRel\left(pairing\left(u, v\right)\right), (x, faceVertex\left(f, r\right)), (first\left(facePair\left(u, v, (x, f)\right)\right), faceVertex\left(second\left(facePair\left(u, v, (x, f)\right)\right), slotMap\left(f, r\right)\right))\right)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/NoncommutativeCornerOrbits.actual_step` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Here G ranges over all groups and Omega over all types equipped with a left action of the opposite group, written as a right action of G. For every u,v in G, x in Omega, f in Fin(4) and r in Fin(3), the displayed corners are related in the equivalence closure of the original cornerRel for pairing(u,v). Fin(n) means the integers from zero through n-1, and first and second denote pair projections. No finiteness, faithfulness or freeness assumption is imposed. This existing helper is now public for direct reuse by ActualLinkCornerChains.move_carrier. Its statement and proof are unchanged: each actual face slot supplies a generator of the original corner EqvGen. The affine link construction uses this exact step to preserve the same corner-class tag; no copied private proof or alternative orbit relation is introduced.

**Theorem 1.13 (Actual corner equivalence is exactly the subgroup orbit relation).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/NoncommutativeCornerOrbits.actual_corner_orbit_iff`

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Geometry/Hyperideal/NoncommutativeCornerOrbits.actual_corner_orbit_iff` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For every group G, arbitrary type Omega with an action of the opposite group, u,v in G and actual corners c,d in Omega times Fin(4), the equivalence closure of ActualCornerTransport.cornerRel for pairing(u,v) relates c and d if and only if there exists g in boundaryGroup(u,v) with act(normalize(u,c),g)=normalize(u,d). The proof constructs actual face-slot paths from each corner to its normalized corner at vertex zero, constructs actual paths for the three subgroup generators, extends those paths over subgroup products and inverses, and proves that every original face-slot generator preserves the specified orbit relation. Empty Omega, infinite groups, nonfaithful actions and stabilizers are included. This statement supplies the corner-relation characterization only; it does not assert edge periods, Euler characteristic, surface links, manifold charts or a geometric metric.
