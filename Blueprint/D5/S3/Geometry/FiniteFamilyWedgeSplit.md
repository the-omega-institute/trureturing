# Splitting a family wedge at one factor

## Abstract

A distinguished factor separates from the family wedge with its original inclusions.

**Definition 1.1 (The based homeomorphism with a binary wedge).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Geometry/FiniteFamilyWedgeSplit.split`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Geometry/FiniteFamilyWedgeSplit.split` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Choose an index in a family of pointed spaces. The family wedge is homeomorphic to the binary wedge of that space and the wedge of the remaining family. Both directions descend continuous maps from the disjoint sums. The homeomorphism takes the shared basepoint to the binary wedge point and preserves the original inclusion of each factor on its designated side. The remaining family may be empty.
