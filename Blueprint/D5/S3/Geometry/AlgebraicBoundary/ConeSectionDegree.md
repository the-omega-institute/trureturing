# Whole section minimum degree

## Abstract

Exact restriction and polynomial homogenization on the original positive-unit section.

The original positive unit supplies an actual normalized ambient interior point. A finite basis of its kernel constructs linear injection and projection maps, with exact decomposition x=u(x)b+J(A(x)). Ambient dimension at least two forces this kernel to be nontrivial. The resulting coordinate body is compact, convex, has nonempty interior and nonempty entire frontier, and its frontier corresponds exactly to the original ambient frontier.

Apply chart_minimum_correspondence to every homogeneous entire-boundary minimum. Restriction is nonzero and preserves the exact total degree and minimum among all affine boundary equations. Conversely chartHomogenize is an actual polynomial on the whole coordinate space, including u=0: every nonzero equation of the entire section boundary lifts to a nonzero homogeneous polynomial of exactly the same total degree, agrees on the unit section, and vanishes on every original ambient boundary point including the vertex. These are applications of the existing polynomial owners, with no requested chart or representation assumed.
