# Exact defective strip colourings

## Abstract

Exact bond-defect transfer and integer recurrence.

A monochromatic bond is one unsatisfied bond of the three-state Potts antiferromagnet. Horizontal and antidiagonal bonds form a sheared square-lattice strip. Exactly one such bond is allowed. All equalities here concern finite combinatorial counts.

**Theorem 1.1 (Two output-class counts).**

$$\forall s: \operatorname{\mathit{Fin}}\left(32\right), (((22 \le \operatorname{\mathit{Fin}}.\operatorname{\mathit{val}}\left(s\right)) \land (\operatorname{\mathit{Fin}}.\operatorname{\mathit{val}}\left(s\right) < 24)) \implies (\forall p: \mathit{Boundary}, (\forall d: \operatorname{\mathit{Fin}}\left(2\right), ((\operatorname{\mathit{HardinA229584StripData}}.\operatorname{\mathit{Valid}}\left(p, \operatorname{\mathit{Fin}}.\operatorname{\mathit{val}}\left(d\right)\right)) \implies ((\operatorname{\mathit{HardinA233177Canonical}}.\operatorname{\mathit{Canonical}}\left(p\right)) \implies (\sum_{r: \mathit{Row}} ((\operatorname{\mathit{if}} ((\operatorname{\mathit{HardinA229584StripData}}.\operatorname{\mathit{cross}}\left(p, r\right) + \operatorname{\mathit{DefectTransfer}}.\operatorname{\mathit{bondDefects}}\left(\operatorname{\mathit{HardinA229584StripData}}.\operatorname{\mathit{horizontal}}, r, r\right) \le \operatorname{\mathit{Fin}}.\operatorname{\mathit{val}}\left(d\right)) \land (\operatorname{\mathit{HardinA229584StripData}}.\operatorname{\mathit{cl}}\left(\operatorname{\mathit{HardinA229584StripData}}.\operatorname{\mathit{boundary}}\left(r\right), \operatorname{\mathit{Fin}}.\operatorname{\mathit{val}}\left(d\right) - \left(\operatorname{\mathit{HardinA229584StripData}}.\operatorname{\mathit{cross}}\left(p, r\right) + \operatorname{\mathit{DefectTransfer}}.\operatorname{\mathit{bondDefects}}\left(\operatorname{\mathit{HardinA229584StripData}}.\operatorname{\mathit{horizontal}}, r, r\right)\right)\right) = s)) \operatorname{\mathit{then}} 1 \operatorname{\mathit{else}} 0)) = \operatorname{\mathit{HardinA229584StripData}}.\operatorname{T}\left(\operatorname{\mathit{HardinA229584StripData}}.\operatorname{\mathit{cl}}\left(p, \operatorname{\mathit{Fin}}.\operatorname{\mathit{val}}\left(d\right)\right), s\right)))))))$$
