# Normative Scale Reversal with Metanormative Conflict Data

## Abstract

Positive rescalings reverse aggregate action choice and require explicit metanormative data when doctrine permissions have empty intersection.

**Theorem 1.1 (Cross-doctrine choice is not fixed by probability and internal order).**

$$\forall alphaFirst \in \mathbb{R}, betaFirst \in \mathbb{R}, alphaSecond \in \mathbb{R}, betaSecond \in \mathbb{R}, probability \in Bool \to \mathbb{R}, utilityFirst \in Bool \to \left(Bool \to \mathbb{R}\right), utilitySecond \in Bool \to \left(Bool \to \mathbb{R}\right), metaData \in MetaNormativeData,\; \left(alphaFirst > 0 \land \left(betaFirst > 0 \land \left(alphaSecond > 0 \land \left(betaSecond > 0 \land \left(alphaFirst > betaFirst \land \left(betaSecond > alphaSecond \land \left(\left(\forall doctrine \in Bool,\; probability\left(doctrine\right) = \frac{1}{2}\right) \land \left(\left(utilityFirst\left(true, true\right) = alphaFirst \land \left(utilityFirst\left(true, false\right) = 0 \land \left(utilityFirst\left(false, true\right) = 0 \land utilityFirst\left(false, false\right) = betaFirst\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(utilitySecond\left(true, true\right) = alphaSecond \land \left(utilitySecond\left(true, false\right) = 0 \land \left(utilitySecond\left(false, true\right) = 0 \land utilitySecond\left(false, false\right) = betaSecond\right)\right)\right) \land \operatorname{intersection}\left(\operatorname{permissionSet}\left(metaData, true\right), \operatorname{permissionSet}\left(metaData, false\right)\right) = \emptyset\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\left(\forall doctrine \in Bool,\; probability\left(doctrine\right) = \frac{1}{2}\right) \land \left(\left(utilityFirst\left(true, true\right) = alphaFirst \land \left(utilityFirst\left(true, false\right) = 0 \land \left(utilityFirst\left(false, true\right) = 0 \land utilityFirst\left(false, false\right) = betaFirst\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(utilitySecond\left(true, true\right) = alphaSecond \land \left(utilitySecond\left(true, false\right) = 0 \land \left(utilitySecond\left(false, true\right) = 0 \land utilitySecond\left(false, false\right) = betaSecond\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(utilityFirst\left(true, true\right) > utilityFirst\left(true, false\right) \land utilityFirst\left(false, false\right) > utilityFirst\left(false, true\right)\right) \land \left(\left(utilitySecond\left(true, true\right) > utilitySecond\left(true, false\right) \land utilitySecond\left(false, false\right) > utilitySecond\left(false, true\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall doctrine \in Bool, leftAction \in Bool, rightAction \in Bool,\; utilityFirst\left(doctrine, leftAction\right) > utilityFirst\left(doctrine, rightAction\right) \Leftrightarrow utilitySecond\left(doctrine, leftAction\right) > utilitySecond\left(doctrine, rightAction\right)\right) \land \left(\left(probability\left(true\right) \cdot utilityFirst\left(true, true\right) + probability\left(false\right) \cdot utilityFirst\left(false, true\right) = \frac{alphaFirst}{2} \land probability\left(true\right) \cdot utilityFirst\left(true, false\right) + probability\left(false\right) \cdot utilityFirst\left(false, false\right) = \frac{betaFirst}{2}\right) \land \left(\left(probability\left(true\right) \cdot utilitySecond\left(true, true\right) + probability\left(false\right) \cdot utilitySecond\left(false, true\right) = \frac{alphaSecond}{2} \land probability\left(true\right) \cdot utilitySecond\left(true, false\right) + probability\left(false\right) \cdot utilitySecond\left(false, false\right) = \frac{betaSecond}{2}\right) \land \left(\left(probability\left(true\right) \cdot utilityFirst\left(true, true\right) + probability\left(false\right) \cdot utilityFirst\left(false, true\right) > probability\left(true\right) \cdot utilityFirst\left(true, false\right) + probability\left(false\right) \cdot utilityFirst\left(false, false\right) \land probability\left(true\right) \cdot utilitySecond\left(true, false\right) + probability\left(false\right) \cdot utilitySecond\left(false, false\right) > probability\left(true\right) \cdot utilitySecond\left(true, true\right) + probability\left(false\right) \cdot utilitySecond\left(false, true\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{intersection}\left(\operatorname{permissionSet}\left(metaData, true\right), \operatorname{permissionSet}\left(metaData, false\right)\right) = \emptyset \Rightarrow \left(\neg \left(\exists action \in Bool,\; *Commentary.*

The public carrier is exactly two Boolean doctrines and two Boolean actions, with real-valued utility and probability functions. Both doctrine probabilities are one half, and each doctrine's coordinates preserve its strict internal ranking under two positive utility scales.

The displayed weighted sums evaluate to alpha over two and beta over two. The first scale therefore selects action true while the second selects action false, exposing the cross-theory scale dependence rather than hiding it in a definition.

MetaNormativeData is an independent source primitive carrying cross-theory scale, rights priority, worst-case and regret scores, and the two doctrine permission predicates. The final implication states directly that an empty permission intersection licenses no universally permitted action.
