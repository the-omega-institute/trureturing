**Theorem 1.14 (Positive treatment mass is necessary).**

$$RandomAssignment\left(muBool, constFalse, constFalseProcess\right) \land \left(\left(\neg TreatmentPositivity\left(muBool, constFalse, true\right)\right) \land \left(\neg conditionalOutcomeLaw\left(muBool, constFalse, constFalseProcess, true\right) = potentialOutcomeLaw\left(muBool, constFalseProcess, true\right)\right)\right)$$

*Commentary.*

Take a fair Boolean sample, constant-false treatment, and a constant-false outcome mechanism, then query treatment true.

The treatment event has zero mass. Mathlib's conditional measure is therefore zero, while the potential law has total mass one.

$$measure\left(muInf, univ\right) = top \land \left(RandomAssignment\left(muInf, constUnit, constUnitProcess\right) \land \left(TreatmentPositivity\left(muInf, constUnit, unit\right) \land \left(\neg conditionalOutcomeLaw\left(muInf, constUnit, constUnitProcess, unit\right) = potentialOutcomeLaw\left(muInf, constUnitProcess, unit\right)\right)\right)\right)$$

**Theorem 1.16 (Random assignment is necessary).**

$$TreatmentPositivity\left(muBool, id, true\right) \land \left(\left(\neg RandomAssignment\left(muBool, id, copyProcess\right)\right) \land \left(\neg conditionalOutcomeLaw\left(muBool, id, copyProcess, true\right) = potentialOutcomeLaw\left(muBool, copyProcess, true\right)\right)\right)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/ConceptDynamics/InterventionsExchange/RandomizationBridge.random_assignment_is_necessary` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

On a fair Boolean sample, let treatment and every potential outcome equal the sampled bit.

The true-treatment fiber is positive, but its conditional outcome law assigns zero mass to false while the potential law assigns one half. Hence random assignment fails and is indispensable.
