# Actual boundaries for Fibonacci completion

## Abstract

Both actual Fibonacci starts retain the complete-boundary, closed and strict source laws and one finite actual reset map.

**Theorem 1.4 (Extremal literal return blocks encode the canonical endpoints).**

$$\forall s \in Guard, U \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Bool, \operatorname{List}\left(Label\right)\right), L \in Nat,\; \left(\operatorname{lt}\left(0, L\right) \land \left(\left(\forall i \in Bool,\; \operatorname{length}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(U, i\right)\right) = L\right) \land \left(\forall i \in Bool,\; \operatorname{LegalWord}\left(s, s, \operatorname{apply}\left(U, i\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\exists imin \in Bool, imax \in Bool,\; \operatorname{compose}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(U, imin\right), 0\right) = \operatorname{min}\left(\operatorname{compose}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(U, false\right), 0\right), \operatorname{compose}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(U, true\right), 0\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{compose}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(U, imax\right), 0\right) = \operatorname{max}\left(\operatorname{compose}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(U, false\right), 0\right), \operatorname{compose}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(U, true\right), 0\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{PeriodicTail}\left(s, \operatorname{ifThenElse}\left(\operatorname{lt}\left(0, \operatorname{power}\left(\operatorname{negate}\left(g\right), L\right)\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(U, imin\right), \operatorname{append}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(U, imin\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(U, imax\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{canonicalReturnLo}\left(U, L\right)\right) \land \operatorname{PeriodicTail}\left(s, \operatorname{ifThenElse}\left(\operatorname{lt}\left(0, \operatorname{power}\left(\operatorname{negate}\left(g\right), L\right)\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(U, imax\right), \operatorname{append}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(U, imax\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(U, imin\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{canonicalReturnHi}\left(U, L\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/ConceptDynamics/Coding/FibonacciFactorCompletion.canonical_periodic_endpoints` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

imin and imax attain the minimum and maximum of the two actual zero-tail translations. For positive signed slope the lower endpoint repeats U(imin), and the upper endpoint repeats U(imax). For negative slope the respective repeating words are U(imin)++U(imax) and U(imax)++U(imin). Their lengths are L or 2L, so the block periods are one or at most two, including equal translations.

PeriodicTail(s,w,z) means there are label, scalar and guard sequences a,x,path with path(0)=s and x(0)=z, every literal edge legal, every coordinate in that guard's support, and x(p)=branch(a(p),x(p+1)). At each p<length(w), a(p)=w[p]. For every p the label, scalar and guard at p+length(w) equal their values at p. The proof splices one legal block onto a supported tail and repeats the actual finite block itinerary, using the fixed endpoint coordinate and return guard to verify the joining edge. It does not infer this periodicity from an arbitrary lawful_tail witness or claim a finite-D representative for either endpoint.
