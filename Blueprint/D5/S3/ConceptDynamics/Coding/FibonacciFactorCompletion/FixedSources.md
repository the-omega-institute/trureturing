# Actual boundaries for Fibonacci completion

## Abstract

Both actual Fibonacci starts retain the complete-boundary, closed and strict source laws and one finite actual reset map.

**Theorem 1.1 (One finite tail supplies all high histories).**

$$\forall o \in Ownership, theta \in Real, s \in Guard, P \in \operatorname{List}\left(Label\right), h \in \operatorname{List}\left(Color\right), R \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Bool, \operatorname{List}\left(Label\right)\right), W \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Bool, \operatorname{List}\left(Color\right)\right), lo \in Real, hi \in Real,\; \left(\operatorname{le}\left(0, theta\right) \land \left(\operatorname{LegalWord}\left(G0, s, P\right) \land \left(\operatorname{length}\left(P\right) = \operatorname{length}\left(h\right) \land \left(\left(\forall i \in Bool,\; \operatorname{LegalWord}\left(s, s, \operatorname{apply}\left(R, i\right)\right) \land \operatorname{length}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(R, i\right)\right) = \operatorname{length}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(W, i\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{lt}\left(lo, hi\right) \land \left(\left(\forall z \in Real,\; \left(\operatorname{le}\left(lo, z\right) \land \operatorname{le}\left(z, hi\right)\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{InSupport}\left(s, z\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall i \in Bool, z \in Real,\; \left(\operatorname{le}\left(lo, z\right) \land \operatorname{le}\left(z, hi\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\operatorname{le}\left(lo, \operatorname{compose}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(R, i\right), z\right)\right) \land \operatorname{le}\left(\operatorname{compose}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(R, i\right), z\right), hi\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{EndpointCertificate}\left(theta, lo, hi, P, h\right) \land \left(\forall i \in Bool,\; \operatorname{EndpointCertificate}\left(theta, lo, hi, \operatorname{apply}\left(R, i\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(W, i\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\exists e \in Guard, w \in \operatorname{List}\left(Label\right),\; \operatorname{LegalWord}\left(s, e, w\right) \land \left(\operatorname{lt}\left(lo, \operatorname{coordinate}\left(w, 0\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{lt}\left(\operatorname{coordinate}\left(w, 0\right), hi\right) \land \left(\forall zs \in \operatorname{List}\left(Bool\right),\; \operatorname{OperationRecord}\left(o, theta, closed, \operatorname{address}\left(\operatorname{append}\left(\operatorname{append}\left(P, \operatorname{choiceBlocks}\left(R, zs\right)\right), w\right)\right), \operatorname{recordWithTail}\left(o, \operatorname{append}\left(h, \operatorname{choiceBlocks}\left(W, zs\right)\right), w\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{OperationFiniteSource}\left(\operatorname{address}\left(\operatorname{append}\left(\operatorname{append}\left(P, \operatorname{choiceBlocks}\left(R, zs\right)\right), w\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall p \in Nat,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(\operatorname{recordWithTail}\left(o, \operatorname{append}\left(h, \operatorname{choiceBlocks}\left(W, zs\right)\right), w\right), \operatorname{add}\left(\operatorname{length}\left(\operatorname{append}\left(h, \operatorname{choiceBlocks}\left(W, zs\right)\right)\right), p\right)\right) = \operatorname{observe}\left(o, \operatorname{coordinate}\left(w, p\right), 0\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)$$

*Commentary.*

The numerical hull is nondegenerate, supported and invariant under both legal returns; its stem and return endpoint costs satisfy EndpointCertificate. One finite word w is selected before all Bool choice lists. Each source is address((P++choiceBlocks(R,zs))++w), with the prescribed h++choiceBlocks(W,zs) past followed by the literal zero-error coordinate future of w. Errors are chosen on this same source and are zero after the past. Empty stems and histories remain included. A common positive margin over unbounded histories is not asserted.

**Theorem 1.2 (One lawful Omega tail supplies all rival histories).**

$$\forall o \in Ownership, theta \in Real, s \in Guard, Q \in \operatorname{List}\left(Label\right), V \in \operatorname{List}\left(Label\right), h \in \operatorname{List}\left(Color\right), W \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Bool, \operatorname{List}\left(Color\right)\right), a \in Real, y \in Real,\; \left(\operatorname{le}\left(0, theta\right) \land \left(\operatorname{LegalWord}\left(G0, s, Q\right) \land \left(\operatorname{LegalWord}\left(s, s, V\right) \land \left(\operatorname{length}\left(Q\right) = \operatorname{length}\left(h\right) \land \left(\left(\forall i \in Bool,\; \operatorname{length}\left(V\right) = \operatorname{length}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(W, i\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{lt}\left(\operatorname{negate}\left(1\right), a\right) \land \left(\operatorname{lt}\left(a, 1\right) \land \left(\operatorname{notEqual}\left(a, 0\right) \land \left(a = \operatorname{power}\left(\operatorname{negate}\left(g\right), \operatorname{length}\left(V\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{compose}\left(V, y\right) = y \land \operatorname{ifThenElse}\left(\operatorname{lt}\left(0, a\right), \operatorname{Nonempty}\left(\operatorname{CompetingT}\left(o, theta, s, Q, V, h, W\right)\right) \land \operatorname{member}\left(y, \operatorname{closure}\left(\operatorname{CompetingT}\left(o, theta, s, Q, V, h, W\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{member}\left(y, \operatorname{CompetingT}\left(o, theta, s, Q, V, h, W\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\exists tail \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Nat, Label\right), x \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Nat, Real\right), path \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Nat, Guard\right),\; \left(\operatorname{apply}\left(path, 0\right) = s \land \left(\left(\forall p \in Nat,\; \operatorname{nextGuard}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(path, p\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(tail, p\right)\right) = \operatorname{some}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(path, \operatorname{add}\left(p, 1\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall p \in Nat,\; \operatorname{InSupport}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(path, p\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(x, p\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall p \in Nat,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(x, p\right) = \operatorname{branch}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(tail, p\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(x, \operatorname{add}\left(p, 1\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall n \in Nat,\; \operatorname{member}\left(\operatorname{iterateApply}\left(\operatorname{composeMap}\left(V\right), n, \operatorname{apply}\left(x, 0\right)\right), \operatorname{CompetingT}\left(o, theta, s, Q, V, h, W\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall zs \in \operatorname{List}\left(Bool\right),\; \exists beta \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Nat, Label\right), X \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Nat, Real\right),\; \operatorname{OperationOmega}\left(beta, X\right) \land \left(\left(\forall p \in Nat,\; \operatorname{lt}\left(p, \operatorname{length}\left(\operatorname{append}\left(Q, \operatorname{choiceBlocks}\left(\operatorname{constantWordFamily}\left(V\right), zs\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{apply}\left(beta, p\right) = \operatorname{getElem}\left(\operatorname{append}\left(Q, \operatorname{choiceBlocks}\left(\operatorname{constantWordFamily}\left(V\right), zs\right)\right), p\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall p \in Nat,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(beta, \operatorname{add}\left(\operatorname{length}\left(\operatorname{append}\left(Q, \operatorname{choiceBlocks}\left(\operatorname{constantWordFamily}\left(V\right), zs\right)\right)\right), p\right)\right) = \operatorname{apply}\left(tail, p\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall p \in Nat,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(X, \operatorname{add}\left(\operatorname{length}\left(\operatorname{append}\left(Q, \operatorname{choiceBlocks}\left(\operatorname{constantWordFamily}\left(V\right), zs\right)\right)\right), p\right)\right) = \operatorname{apply}\left(x, p\right)\right) \land \operatorname{OperationRecord}\left(o, theta, closed, beta, \operatorname{recordWithOmegaTail}\left(o, \operatorname{append}\left(h, \operatorname{choiceBlocks}\left(W, zs\right)\right), x\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)$$

*Commentary.*

constantWordFamily(V)(i)=V and composeMap(V)(z)=compose(V,z). The actual fixed-point and slope equations identify the centered affine return. Positive-slope feasibility uses nonempty CompetingT and closure membership of its fixed point; negative-slope feasibility uses actual CompetingT membership. One supported scalar is lifted to one legal tail before all histories. Each finite choice sees only the remaining number of identical V returns, while both distinct W color choices remain in every orbit test. The full CompetingT orbit condition is equivalent to supported terminal coordinates and BlockSupply for every finite Q-prefixed history; the reverse direction extracts Q and both W blocks from those same histories. Every record uses its own actual finite errors and the unchanged zero-error tail future.

**Theorem 1.3 (Literal source construction at every first stem disagreement).**

$$\forall Configuration \in Type, action \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Configuration, \operatorname{Op}\left(Configuration, Color, Label\right)\right), initialConfiguration \in Configuration, o \in Ownership, theta \in Real, s1 \in Guard, s2 \in Guard, P \in \operatorname{List}\left(Label\right), Q \in \operatorname{List}\left(Label\right), h \in \operatorname{List}\left(Color\right), U \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Bool, \operatorname{List}\left(Label\right)\right), V \in \operatorname{List}\left(Label\right), W \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Bool, \operatorname{List}\left(Color\right)\right), L \in Nat, lo \in Real, hi \in Real, a \in Real, y \in Real, k \in Nat,\; \left(\operatorname{SynchronousSourceData}\left(o, theta, s1, s2, P, Q, h, U, V, W, L, lo, hi, a, y, k\right) \land \left(\operatorname{ClosedOperationSafety}\left(action, initialConfiguration, o, theta\right) \land \left(\operatorname{ClosedOperationLiveness}\left(action, initialConfiguration, o, theta\right) \land \operatorname{ExecutedPostprocessing}\left(action, initialConfiguration, o, theta\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\exists e \in Guard, w \in \operatorname{List}\left(Label\right), eta \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Nat, Label\right), x \in \operatorname{Function}\left(Nat, Real\right),\; \operatorname{LegalWord}\left(s1, e, w\right) \land \left(\operatorname{lt}\left(lo, \operatorname{coordinate}\left(w, 0\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{lt}\left(\operatorname{coordinate}\left(w, 0\right), hi\right) \land \left(\forall n \in Nat,\; \exists beta \in \operatorname{Function}\left(\operatorname{Function}\left(\operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right), Bool\right), \operatorname{Function}\left(Nat, Label\right)\right), cuts \in \operatorname{Function}\left(\operatorname{Function}\left(\operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right), Bool\right), \operatorname{Frame}\left(Configuration, Label\right)\right), states \in \operatorname{Finset}\left(Configuration\right),\; \left(\forall z \in \operatorname{Function}\left(\operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right), Bool\right),\; \operatorname{OperationRecord}\left(o, theta, closed, \operatorname{SynchronousHighSource}\left(P, U, z, w\right), \operatorname{recordWithTail}\left(o, \operatorname{synchronousPrefix}\left(h, W, z\right), w\right)\right) \land \operatorname{OperationFiniteSource}\left(\operatorname{SynchronousHighSource}\left(P, U, z, w\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall z \in \operatorname{Function}\left(\operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right), Bool\right),\; \operatorname{OperationRecord}\left(o, theta, closed, \operatorname{apply}\left(beta, z\right), \operatorname{recordWithOmegaTail}\left(o, \operatorname{synchronousPrefix}\left(h, W, z\right), x\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall z \in \operatorname{Function}\left(\operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right), Bool\right),\; \forall p \in Nat,\; \operatorname{lt}\left(p, \operatorname{length}\left(\operatorname{synchronousPrefix}\left(Q, \operatorname{constantWordFamily}\left(V\right), z\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{apply}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(beta, z\right), p\right) = \operatorname{getElem}\left(\operatorname{synchronousPrefix}\left(Q, \operatorname{constantWordFamily}\left(V\right), z\right), p\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall z \in \operatorname{Function}\left(\operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right), Bool\right), p \in Nat,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(beta, z\right), \operatorname{add}\left(\operatorname{add}\left(\operatorname{length}\left(P\right), \operatorname{multiply}\left(n, L\right)\right), p\right)\right) = \operatorname{apply}\left(eta, p\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall z \in \operatorname{Function}\left(\operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right), Bool\right), p \in Nat,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(\operatorname{recordWithTail}\left(o, \operatorname{synchronousPrefix}\left(h, W, z\right), w\right), \operatorname{add}\left(\operatorname{add}\left(\operatorname{length}\left(P\right), \operatorname{multiply}\left(n, L\right)\right), p\right)\right) = \operatorname{observe}\left(o, \operatorname{coordinate}\left(w, p\right), 0\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{Injective}\left(\operatorname{SynchronousHighFamily}\left(P, U, n, w\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall z \in \operatorname{Function}\left(\operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right), Bool\right),\; \operatorname{Cut}\left(action, initialConfiguration, \operatorname{front}\left(\operatorname{recordWithTail}\left(o, \operatorname{synchronousPrefix}\left(h, W, z\right), w\right), \operatorname{add}\left(\operatorname{length}\left(P\right), \operatorname{multiply}\left(n, L\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(cuts, z\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{le}\left(\operatorname{length}\left(\operatorname{output}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(cuts, z\right)\right)\right), k\right) \land \left(\operatorname{output}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(cuts, z\right)\right) = \operatorname{take}\left(\operatorname{take}\left(P, k\right), \operatorname{length}\left(\operatorname{output}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(cuts, z\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \operatorname{IsPrefix}\left(\operatorname{output}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(cuts, z\right)\right), \operatorname{take}\left(P, k\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{Injective}\left(\operatorname{cutStateOutputMap}\left(cuts\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall c \in Configuration,\; \operatorname{member}\left(c, states\right) \Leftrightarrow \left(\exists z \in \operatorname{Function}\left(\operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right), Bool\right),\; \operatorname{state}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(cuts, z\right)\right) = c\right)\right) \land \operatorname{le}\left(\operatorname{power}\left(2, n\right), \operatorname{multiply}\left(\operatorname{card}\left(states\right), \operatorname{add}\left(k, 1\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)$$

*Commentary.*

SynchronousSourceData expands as follows: theta>=0; P and Q are legal G0-to-s1/s2 stems with the same length as h; both U returns and the shared V return are legal and have common positive length L, equal to each W length; U(false) differs from U(true). The supported high hull [lo,hi] has positive width and is invariant under U, with the finite EndpointCertificate for P and each U/W pair. The actual V slope a=(-g)^length(V) is nonzero and lies between -1 and 1, compose(V,y)=y, and the sign-sensitive CompetingT feasibility holds. The stems agree before k<length(P) and differ at k. ClosedOperationSafety and ClosedOperationLiveness quantify all original closed OperationRecords and all primitive Runs; liveness is positionwise on eventual-L0 sources. ExecutedPostprocessing is a finite Drain only after actual executed acquisitions.

SynchronousHighSource(P,U,z,w)=address(synchronousPrefix(P,U,z)++w), and synchronousPrefix(P,U,z)=P++choiceBlocks(U,List.ofFn(z)); SynchronousHighFamily is this source map. The observation horizon is length(P)+nL. Literal block extraction proves its injection from U(false)!=U(true), without a history-injection premise. Independent lawful zero-error [L0] and [L3] records derive startup, preserving arbitrary k in the target family. cutStateOutputMap(cuts)(z) is the pair of readable state and output at that cut. The actual common-stem theorem gives exactly 2^n<=card(states)*(k+1), with n=0 included. Deriving the canonical hull and its nondegeneracy, the original singleton-return reduction and the necessity of the canonical theta maximum remains additional work; optional original return pieces retain their own selection and whole-prefix conditions.
