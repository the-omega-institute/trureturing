# A cyclic two-letter right-quotient construction

## Abstract

A nonidentity element generates a two-letter right-quotient permutation with exact support order.

**Theorem 1.1 (A cyclic subgroup supplies an exact two-letter realization).**

$$\forall G \in Typeu,\; \forall group \in \operatorname{Group}\left(G\right),\; \forall finite \in \operatorname{Fintype}\left(G\right),\; \forall eq \in \operatorname{DecidableEq}\left(G\right),\; \forall a \in G,\; \forall ha \in \left(\neg a = 1\right),\; \exists sigma \in \operatorname{EquivPerm}\left(G\right),\; \operatorname{SupportCard}\left(sigma\right) = \operatorname{orderOf}\left(a\right) \land \left(\forall x \in G,\; \operatorname{RightQuotient}\left(sigma, x\right) = a \lor \operatorname{RightQuotient}\left(sigma, x\right) = 1\right)$$

*Commentary.*

Let G be a finite group and let a be a nonidentity element. The subgroup generated by a is finite and has cardinality orderOf(a). Define a map on G by left multiplication by a on that subgroup and the identity outside it. The subgroup is closed under left multiplication, so this map is injective and hence a permutation of G.

Every point in the generated subgroup moves because a is nonidentity, while every point outside it is fixed. Thus the support has exactly orderOf(a) elements. Its literal right quotient sigma(x) times x inverse is a when x lies in the generated subgroup and is one otherwise. The result is a constructive two-letter realization; it does not assert the separate classification or counting formula for every possible support size.
