# Three Directions Cannot Fit into Two Cyclic Blocks

## Abstract

An actual rank-three elementary abelian action with projective cyclic restrictions has prime-field dimension at least 3p; the new step excludes two free cyclic blocks.

The lower bound uses the actual group algebra and actual cyclic subgroups throughout. The geometric_norm_polynomial and chain_independent declarations are reused from PrimeFieldCyclicProjectivityDimension in their original owner. The ordinary upper construction described below remains outside the formalized lower bound.

*Commentary.*

For every actual subgroup H, IsCyclic H must imply projectivity of (rho.comp H.subtype).asModule over the actual group algebra (ZMod p)[H]. The conclusion is 3*p <= Module.finrank (ZMod p) V. No faithfulness, constant residue, freeness, Jordan basis, local-ring, centralizer classification, or splitting premise appears in this endpoint.

First restrict along the injective map (a,b) to (a,b,0) and consume the existing public two_mul_prime_le_finrank_of_cyclic_projective endpoint. Its original norm and conjugation-trace arguments remain at their owner source; they are not copied here. A private reindexing bridge transports projectivity with the actual subgroup equivalence and its group-algebra ring equivalence. Consequently the rank-two theorem sees projectivity on every actual cyclic subgroup of its own domain, independently of the kernel of rho.

For a nonzero direction a in F_p^3, the map c to c*a from the actual cyclic group Multiplicative F_p is injective. Its range is an actual cyclic subgroup of the rank-three group. Projectivity transports along this equivalence, rather than along an assumed order of the image operator. Even when rho is nonfaithful, the subgroup used in the argument still has order p.

The local-free bridge is constructed on A=F_p[Multiplicative F_p]. Let z be the group-algebra element of the generator 1 and t=z-1. The augmentation epsilon sends each group element to 1. Frobenius gives f^p=epsilon(f) in the scalar copy of F_p: prove this on group elements, sums, and scalar multiples. If epsilon(f) is nonzero then f is a unit, since its pth power is a nonzero scalar; if epsilon(f)=0 then f is nilpotent and 1-f is a unit. This proves IsLocalRing A. Finite dimension over F_p supplies finite generation over A along the actual scalar tower. Projectivity supplies flatness, and Module.free_of_flat_of_isLocalRing supplies freeness. These are proved bridges, not assumptions about the input module.

The cyclic group algebra has F_p-dimension p. More precisely, the geometric-norm identity identifies nu=sum_c c with t^(p-1). The coefficient of the identity in nu is 1, so nu is nonzero. Multiplication by t has pth power zero and a length-p chain starting at 1 with nonzero top nu. The existing generic chain_independent proof, exposed by the visibility-only patch, constructs the actual basis 1,t,...,t^(p-1). Combining this with a free A-basis gives the promised free chains. The tower formula gives dim_F V=p*rank_A V. Under the remaining case dim_F V=2p, rank_A V=2 and an actual A-basis indexed by Fin 2 supplies V isomorphic to A^2. No nilpotent Jordan-basis theorem is imported or assumed.

Every group operator commutes with the chosen cyclic action. Convert that intertwining map to an A-linear endomorphism of the actual asModule and then use its A-basis to obtain B_g in Mat_2(A). The representation law, coordinate intertwining, and the distinguished equation B_s=z*I are proved. Thus the constant terms B_g(0) are retained throughout the argument. They are not set to the identity at the coordinate boundary.

Coefficient extraction uses a well-defined augmentation derivation delta on the actual group algebra: delta(c)=c.toAdd on group elements and F_p-linearity on their coefficients. Its product law is delta(fg)=epsilon(f)delta(g)+delta(f)epsilon(g). It has delta(1)=0, epsilon(t)=0, and delta(t)=1. The augmentation kernel is proved to be generated by t using c=z^val(c) and divisibility of z^n-1 by z-1. Therefore B-I=tQ when B(0)=I, and delta(B)=Q(0). In particular tQ=tQ' implies Q(0)=Q'(0) by applying delta. This proves the required uniqueness without cancelling the zero divisor t, also when p=2.

The exact rank identity is implemented as a factorization of F_p-linear maps. The constant-coordinate map A^2 to F_p^2 is onto, and the map x to nu*x from F_p^2 to A^2 is injective because nu is nonzero. For every matrix M over A, multiplication by nu*M is the composite of these two maps and multiplication by M(0). Hence rank_F(nu*M)=rank_F(M(0)). In each actual nonzero cyclic direction, its own projective-free coordinates give norm rank exactly two. The geometric identity identifies that norm with (B_h-I)^(p-1). If h has trivial constant reduction, this is t^(p-1)*Q_h^(p-1), so the exact factorization proves rank_F(L(h)^(p-1))=2 for L(h)=delta(B_h). Full rank makes this power injective, hence L(h) is injective. Since the target is two-dimensional over a field, this is equivalent to invertibility. The exponent p-1 is handled as p-2+1, valid at p=2.

The actual reduction kernel K is defined as an F_p-submodule of F_p^3. Closure under addition uses the representation product, and closure under field scalars uses actual natural powers with the representative in ZMod p. Its leading map L:K to Mat_2(F_p) is additive by the derivation product rule with both constant terms equal to I; prime-field linearity follows from the additive map and scalar-as-repeated-addition identity. The distinguished direction lies in K and L(s)=I. Every nonzero h in K has invertible L(h).

The bound dim K >= 2 is proved before any claim of trivial reduction. If reduction is nontrivial, choose a nonzero nilpotent D=B_g(0)-I. Nilpotency on a two-dimensional space gives D^2=0. Choose v with Dv nonzero; the pair Dv,v is a basis, so every commuting operator is aI+bD. Its pth power being I forces a=1 by evaluating on Dv. Uniqueness of b and D^2=0 make b a linear functional on F_p^3. The reduction kernel is precisely its kernel; rank-nullity gives dimension at least two. If reduction is already trivial the kernel has dimension three.

Choose h in K outside the line through s and put X=L(h). For every a in F_p, the element h-a*s is nonzero and L(h-a*s)=X-aI is injective. Thus X has no F_p eigenvalue. The same cyclic-pair centralizer calculation, now with Xv,v for any nonzero v, shows every operator commuting with X is aI+bX. Every nonzero such combination is injective: if b is nonzero, rescale to X-cI; otherwise it is a nonzero scalar. In ordinary algebra this identifies the centralizer with the quadratic field F_p[X]. The proof establishes and consumes the exact inverse-free consequence needed here: a nilpotent in this centralizer is zero. It does not install a field instance or assume a centralizer classification.

Commutation of B_g with B_h, followed by delta, gives [B_g(0),L(h)]=0; no leading-coefficient matrices are asserted to commute with each other. Since (B_g(0)-I)^p=0, the proved quadratic-centralizer consequence forces B_g(0)=I for every g. Thus K=F_p^3. Finally, for a fixed nonzero v in F_p^2, the linear evaluation h to L(h)v is injective: a nonzero h gives an injective L(h), which cannot kill v. Finite-dimensionality would then imply 3 <= 2, a contradiction.

The arithmetic endpoint uses the rank-two lower bound together with the genuine cyclic free-rank formula. If dim V were below 3p, these force the integer free rank to be exactly two, so the two-block obstruction applies. The endpoint therefore preserves the precise 3p conclusion. It proves no bound r*p for arbitrary elementary rank r.

The rank-three kernel bound is essential. For p=2, the regular module F_2[C_2^2] is cyclic-free of dimension four and can have nontrivial constant reduction relative to one generator. The argument therefore makes no all-rank reduction-rigidity claim.

Ordinary upper construction, not a formal theorem in this artifact: let K=F_(p^3), choose an F_p-basis beta_1,beta_2,beta_3, and let three generators act on K[t]/(t^p) by multiplication by 1+beta_i*t. For every nonzero actual direction a, its first coefficient sum_i a_i*beta_i is nonzero, hence a unit of K. Its cyclic restriction has three length-p chains and is free; the F_p-dimension is 3p. This explains expected attainment of the lower bound. Neither this construction, its projectivity, nor a kernel-attainment statement is claimed to have been formalized here.
