# Order-Three Conjugacy in SL₂(F₄₇)

## Abstract

Order-three elements of SL₂(F₄₇) are conjugate, and conjugacy transports to a faithful realization of the full lattice isometry group.

**Theorem 1.2 (Any two order-three elements are conjugate).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/Cyclic/SL47OrderThreeConjugacy.SL47_orderThree_isConj`

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/Cyclic/SL47OrderThreeConjugacy.SL47_orderThree_isConj` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For any x and y in SL47 with orderOf x = orderOf y = 3, there is g in SL47 with g x g⁻¹ = y. Mathlib's Matrix.card_SL_field gives group order 47(47² − 1) = 103776, divisible by 3 exactly once. Thus each order-three element generates a Sylow 3-subgroup.

The representative A has rows (0, −1) and (1, −1) and has order three. Sylow conjugacy carries the subgroup generated by x to the subgroup generated by A. The image of x under this conjugation is A or A². The determinant-one matrix N with rows (3, 20) and (23, −3) satisfies NA = A²N over ZMod 47. So every order-three element is conjugate to A, giving the result for x and y.

**Theorem 1.3 (Conjugacy in the full integral isometry group).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/Cyclic/SL47OrderThreeConjugacy.lattice_orderThree_conjugacy`

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/Cyclic/SL47OrderThreeConjugacy.lattice_orderThree_conjugacy` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Let D be lattice data, L its charge lattice, and B its integral bilinear form. Assume an injective homomorphism μ from the additive presentation of SL47 to AddAut(L), every μ(s) preserving B and every B-preserving additive automorphism lying in the image of μ. For any B-preserving x and y in AddAut(L) of additive order three, there exists r in AddAut(L) such that B(r a, r b) = B(a, b) for all a and b in L and r(x z) = y(r z) for all z in L.

Exhaustivity gives preimages of x and y, and injectivity ensures that both preimages have order three. The image of their SL47 conjugator preserves B and satisfies the required intertwining equation. This is the conjugacy step for applying a representative's spectral data in the P48q cyclic-type calculation, given the stated action.
