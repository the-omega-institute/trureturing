# Bounded lifts of a fixed quotient tuple

## Abstract

The alternating-section supplement of Lemma 2 supplies lifts of an arbitrary quotient tuple with the same bound on its generated subgroup.

*Commentary.*

For finite groups G and Q, a surjective homomorphism q from G to Q, arbitrary naturals d and k with k at least four, and a tuple y from Fin d to Q whose generated subgroup has alpha at most k, there is a tuple z from Fin d to G with q(z(i)) equal to y(i) for every i and alpha of the subgroup generated by z at most k. No rank bound on the full preimage is assumed. Apply Lemma 2 to the kernel of the map from the preimage of the subgroup generated by y, then correct each lift into the same supplement and include its generated subgroup.
