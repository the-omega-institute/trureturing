*Commentary.*

Assume positive q, M > q(2q+1), field size greater than 2(2q+1)^q, upper-unitriangular-preserving gamma, and prescribed beta acting on the central root by nonzero chi times a field automorphism. For positive divisors e of q, one beta correction tuple is chosen before all upper-unitriangular x and all natural powers r. Single-power gamma witnesses and q/e-powered central witnesses then reconstruct the original ordered powered product exactly.
