# Actual minimal-normal factor orbits

## Abstract

The action of a prescribed automorphism tuple partitions actual minimal normal factors into invariant dependent product blocks.

**Definition 1.1 (The image action).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.minimalFactorAction`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.minimalFactorAction` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For any group A, an automorphism acts on each actual minimal nontrivial normal subgroup M by its subgroup image. The instance proves the identity and composition action laws.

**Definition 1.2 (The factor permutation homomorphism).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.minimalFactorPermutation`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.minimalFactorPermutation` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The image action gives a homomorphism from MulAut(A) to permutations of MinimalNormalFactor(A). Its identity and multiplication laws are those of the actual group action.

**Definition 1.3 (The prescribed generated group).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.prescribedActionGroup`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.prescribedActionGroup` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For k indexed by Fin m, prescribedActionGroup(k) is the subgroup of MulAut(A) generated by the range of k.

**Definition 1.4 (The orbit quotient).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.FactorOrbit`

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

FactorOrbit(k) is the quotient of actual minimal normal factors by the action of prescribedActionGroup(k), rather than the full automorphism group.

*Commentary.*

OrbitBlock(k,o) is the dependent product of the underlying subgroup groups M over all M in OrbitFactor(k,o).

**Definition 1.7 (The restricted block automorphism).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.orbitBlockAut`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.orbitBlockAut` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Every element of prescribedActionGroup(k) defines an automorphism of OrbitBlock(k,o): the output at M applies the ambient automorphism to the input at its inverse image. Subgroup membership, both inverse laws and multiplication preservation are proved.

**Definition 1.8 (Restriction preserves multiplication).**

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.orbitBlockAutHom` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Restriction to each orbit block is a homomorphism from prescribedActionGroup(k) to its block automorphisms. It satisfies the identity and composition laws.

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Each k(i), with its membership in the generated group, restricts to an automorphism of every orbit block.

**Theorem 1.13 (Transitivity inside each orbit).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.prescribedOrbitPermutation_transitive`

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.prescribedOrbitPermutation_transitive` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For every k, orbit o and factors M,L in that orbit, some permutation in the subgroup generated by the prescribed orbit permutations sends M to L. Transitivity is obtained for this exact generated tuple; no transitivity of the tuple on the whole factor family is assumed.

**Definition 1.14 (Regrouping the actual factors).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.factorOrbitProductEquiv`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.factorOrbitProductEquiv` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For every k, the full dependent product over MinimalNormalFactor(A) is isomorphic to the dependent product of OrbitBlock(k,o) over FactorOrbit(k). Restriction and reconstruction are mutually inverse and preserve multiplication.

**Definition 1.17 (The actual group-to-block isomorphism).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.orbitProductEquiv`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.orbitProductEquiv` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For finite A with minimalNormalSocle(A) equal to top and center(A) equal to bottom, and every k, orbitProductEquiv is an isomorphism from A to the dependent product of its actual orbit blocks. It combines the internal-product isomorphism with orbit regrouping.

**Theorem 1.18 (Equivariance for the generated group).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.orbitProductEquiv_equivariant`

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.orbitProductEquiv_equivariant` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Under those same finite, full-socle and centerless hypotheses, for every k, generated automorphism alpha, x in A and orbit o, the o-coordinate of orbitProductEquiv(alpha(x)) equals orbitBlockAut(k,o,alpha) applied to the o-coordinate of orbitProductEquiv(x). Naturality is proved on the single-factor inclusions and hence on the full product.

**Theorem 1.19 (Exact equivariance for the tuple).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.orbitProductEquiv_prescribed`

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/FiniteGroups/NikolovSegal/FactorOrbits.orbitProductEquiv_prescribed` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Under the same hypotheses, for every k, i in Fin m, x in A and orbit o, the o-coordinate of orbitProductEquiv(k(i)(x)) equals prescribedOrbitAut(k,o,i) applied to the o-coordinate of orbitProductEquiv(x). This is the equivariance needed to combine the block value sets.

This is the invariant-orbit construction used on printed pages 227–228 of Nikolov and Segal, On finitely generated profinite groups, I, Annals of Mathematics 165 (2007), DOI 10.4007/annals.2007.165.171. The factor action, orbit splitting and equivariance are constructed. Coverage on these blocks remains a separate input.
