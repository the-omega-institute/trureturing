# Four-state obstruction to the coefficient-two reverse bound

## Abstract

A four-state CAR pair has equal pair collisions and directed deficiencies 1/9 and 5/18; an actual common public partition seed preserves the counterexample after halving.

Let A = Fin 4 = {0,1,2,3}. The observation alphabet B consists of all fifteen nonempty subsets: {0}, {1}, {2}, {3}, {0,1}, {0,2}, {0,3}, {1,2}, {1,3}, {2,3}, {0,1,2}, {0,1,3}, {0,2,3}, {1,2,3}, and A. Zero-weight letters remain present. `D5/S3/Estimation/DecisionRisk/CARApproximateRecovery.row` assigns u(B) when i belongs to B and zero otherwise. `D5/S3/Estimation/DecisionRisk/CARApproximateRecovery.pair` sums u(B) over the blocks containing both states.

`D5/S3/Estimation/SequentialDecisionRisk/FiniteDeficiencyRiskTransfer.finiteDeficiency` takes the target first and source second. Write d(u,z) for toReal(finiteDeficiency(row(z),row(u))). It is the infimum of the maximum statewise half-L1 error over every stochastic kernel on the full alphabet. All fifteen input rows are probability rows. One kernel acts at every state, with no support or block-containment restriction. Kernels can output zero-weight letters and blocks not containing the state. Bayes risk optimizes over all stochastic decision kernels from blocks to actions, using the same decision rule at every state.
