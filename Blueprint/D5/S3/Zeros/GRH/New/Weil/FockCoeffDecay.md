*Commentary.*

Let P be homogeneous of degree d in |ι| > 0 variables, v = v_P, σ in U(ε), A ≥ d + |ι| and n natural numbers, y ≠ 0. Then |⟨v, ϱ(y)ω(σ)v⟩| ≤ (4(A+n))^n |y|^{−2n} ‖σ‖^{|ι|+2n} ⟨v,v⟩, with Euclidean and ℓ² operator norms.
