Bessel Orthonormal

the coordinates of the full function of a Bessel system are orthonormal.

*Commentary.*

The coordinates W_i of the full function of a Bessel system are orthonormal in L²(𝖴∖G₁): the integral of W_i times the conjugate of W_j is δ_ij.
