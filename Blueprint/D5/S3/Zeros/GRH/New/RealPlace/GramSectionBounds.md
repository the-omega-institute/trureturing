*Commentary.*

Let r > 0, P ≥ 8, and let every y within r of Z lie in Ω with ‖y⁻¹‖, ‖y‖, δ(y)^{−1/2} ≤ P. Then the matrix of γ_Z has norm ≤ P¹², and its real derivative in Z has norm ≤ 2P¹²/r.
