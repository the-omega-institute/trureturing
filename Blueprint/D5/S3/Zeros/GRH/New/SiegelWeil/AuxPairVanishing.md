Aux Pair Vanishing

*Commentary.*

Suppose ψ ≠ 1 at each place of Q, the reflection sums of all nonzero rows at M kill y, and the conjugate u-coordinate of y has zero descent to W for all data P ⊗ Υ. Then its descents vanish for all data vacuum off M, Q and conjugates.
