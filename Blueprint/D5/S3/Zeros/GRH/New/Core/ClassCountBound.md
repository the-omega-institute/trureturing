*Commentary.*

If N is a nonzero natural number in the different of E and f is the number of infinite places, then the standard definite hermitian space of rank n has at most (1 + 2(6N(fn)²)³)^{4fn²} classes Γ\G_f/K, K = Kf the stabiliser of the standard lattice.
