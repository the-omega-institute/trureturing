*Commentary.*

Let A be a rectangular matrix over a valuation domain with a nonzero full column minor. There is a fixed injective column selection whose determinant is nonzero and divides the determinant of A times every compatible right multiplier. Total divisibility selects a minor dividing all full minors. Expand the determinant in the rows of the transposed product. Each selection with repeated columns has determinant zero, while each injective selection contributes a multiple of the fixed minor.
