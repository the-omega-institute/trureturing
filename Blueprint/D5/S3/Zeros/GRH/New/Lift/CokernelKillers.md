Cokernel Killers

Low and far weight windows, gap condition, sum of valuations over two intervals. Annihilators p^κ E^s of cokernels of Kisin modules on crystalline extensions. S ⊂ A_inf is the power series ring of the Kummer chart, z its variable, E = z^e·(unit) + p; a letter is a unit of the field with p^f elements (Lift.KummerChart.Letter).
