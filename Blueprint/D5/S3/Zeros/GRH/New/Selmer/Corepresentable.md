# Corepresentable

## Abstract

Corepresentable

New primes with anticyclotomic characters, auxiliary sets, families of interpolated L-values. Anticyclotomic character of order ℓ^N ramified at one new prime with prescribed Frobenius. Even classes, new places with tame generators, triangular system of primes. Balanced representations, pencils of tied primes, ties, twists at a point. Selmer groups are finitely presented: cocycles unramified off S read at finitely many points.
