# Hecke Stable Subspaces

## Abstract

Hecke Stable Subspaces

the auxiliary pairs have apt labels; representations attached to eigenforms are conjugate self-dual, and local conditions pass to the conjugate place; a Hecke-stable space of forms has a Hecke-stable complement and is spanned by its isotypic vectors.
