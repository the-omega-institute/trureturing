# Reciprocity Law

## Abstract

Reciprocity Law

A value of an even class vanishes if all its cyclic images vanish. Odd Kummer-type maps with reciprocity law against even classes, primitive, with prescribed zeros.

*Commentary.*

Let E be stably unramified with ≥ 4 infinite places, r ≥ 1. Given Frobenii x_i fixing μ_{ℓ^r}, at degree-one places off ℓ over distinct primes of E⁺, and at most as many y_i, some function z with IsLaw kills all y_i and takes a unit value on an element fixing μ_{ℓ^r}.
