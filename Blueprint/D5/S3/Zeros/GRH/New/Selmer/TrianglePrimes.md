*Commentary.*

Let E ⊂ ℚ(ζ_N) have ≥ 4 infinite places, N + t < y, cyc(γ)^a − 1 ∣ ℓ^h for 0 < a < t, ℓ ≤ y² or h = 0, and W contain, for all ν and g, most Frobenii near δ^(ℓ^ν)·g for some δ with cyc(δ) = 1. Then some finite ordered J with types ty carries, for each ν ≥ 1, Taylor–Wiles places of norm ≡ 1 mod ℓ^ν, with Frobenii in W, satisfying IsPrimeTriangle for γ⁻¹, yh, y²²h.
