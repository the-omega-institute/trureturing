# Tilting Lemma

## Abstract

Tilting Lemma

Selmer groups cut by relations lie in Selmer groups with local conditions. Ties cost only determinants along twists; a group no new prime shortens is short. tilting: generic members of a pencil of linear maps have short kernels.
