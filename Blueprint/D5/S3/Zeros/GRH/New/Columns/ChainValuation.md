# Chain Valuation

## Abstract

Chain Valuation

Determinant theta class sum: twist-free expansion and norm relation on adding a place. Short integral algebraic forms span; ramified columns have nearly full dimension. Record of a good finite datum: support, integrality, invariance, shape at N. The chain as a sum over classes; good data are standard at p. Valuation gains of single lifts add along the chain with modified data.

*Commentary.*

Let Ψ be good, twist primes prime to p, and t_j operators commuting with the levels on the data. If each modified datum multiplies gauges by at most p^{r_j} from d_j to d_{j+1}, the modified chain sends columns integral at d_0 to forms of gauge ≤ p^{∑r_j} at d_m.
