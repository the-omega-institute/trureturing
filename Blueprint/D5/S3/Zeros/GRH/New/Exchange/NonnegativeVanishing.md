*Commentary.*

Let X split over Z along b lines, Y have a positive sign and b more negative signs than Z, and weights of ν be below the number of positive signs of Z. For integrable terms, the theta integral with characters νυ^b has coefficient 0 at invertible T with ȷ⁻¹T not totally negative.
