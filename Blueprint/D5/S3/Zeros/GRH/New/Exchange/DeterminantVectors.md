Determinant Vectors

Determinant vectors in Fock space are cone integrals of determinant vectors one floor up.

**Theorem 1.1 (surjOn_cone_integral_detVectors).**

*Commentary.*

Let W′, V′ be W, V plus a hyperbolic plane, with adapted splittings, z ≥ 1 and rk W′ = 2k + 2·(negative signs of W′). Every determinant vector v (detVectors k m) is the cone integral at z, with weights k + m, of a determinant vector v′.
