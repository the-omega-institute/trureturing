*Commentary.*

In a triangle with G, let w₀⟪a x, a s y⟫ = ⟪x, s S_i y⟫ for the first arrow a of each row i < m. For i + n = m the weighted n-chain of row i has Gram form κ(w₀w₁ⁿ/w_n)⟪x, s T y⟫, det T = ∏_{i≤k<m} det S_k, with M(n², κ).
