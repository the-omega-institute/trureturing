# Standard Coefficients

## Abstract

Standard Coefficients

If coefficients at unimodular indices vanish, all standard ramified data are killed.

**Theorem 1.1 (apply_stdColDatum_eq_zero).**

*Commentary.*

Assume IsEquivariantLift for M, 𝒟, nested adapted splittings, the standard Ψ of the frame in M, and that ȷT-coefficients of 𝒟Ψ vanish on the parabolic for T = J_{V′} at unimodular lattices and for invertible T off the real class of V′. Then 𝒟 kills every standard datum of an integral z.
