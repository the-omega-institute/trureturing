# Row Dimension

## Abstract

Row Dimension

Dimension of rows at most group order times degree times mass.

*Commentary.*

The complex dimension of the space of rows is at most |𝖦|·deg·mass_r, with 𝖦 = Data.G, deg the product of the local degrees of the Bessel systems, and mass_r the mass of the genus of O_E^r.
