# Block Bound

## Abstract

Block Bound

Sum of inverse discriminant powers over classes and subspaces. Colouring count of kept near frames weighted by inverse index powers. Weighted count of near congruent pairs of terms, summed over classes, is bounded. Block amplitude bounded by a sum of sizes over kept near terms.
