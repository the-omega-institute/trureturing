Class Means

Integral conjugation-invariant means on generic Levi matrices splitting evaluation at 1 by weight.

*Commentary.*

Let O be a valuation domain of characteristic 0, the prime p a non-unit, W local, D < p^f, D + k < #residue field of W. For any property P of x-weights, some Levi-conjugation invariant linear form κ on polynomials in a generic matrix kills monomials with row weight ≠ column weight, sends monomials with row weight in P to p^{k²f}·(value at 1) (0 in degree > D), and is a combination of Levi conjugates of forms killing the monomials with row weight outside P.
