# Member Kernels

## Abstract

Member Kernels

Cauchy-type bounds for all conjugates of theta jets and Schur jets. Regular parameters come from weights; bounds for rows and boxes. Integrality and conjugate bounds for theta kernels of definite pairs; rational automorphy law. Point forms with prescribed residue datum: pairing, and theta descent as a kernel. Kernel as a vector, the free level, and one class sum at that level.
