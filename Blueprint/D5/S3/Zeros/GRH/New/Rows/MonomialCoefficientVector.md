Monomial Coefficient Vector

*Commentary.*

For a monomial α in rows e of degree dg_τ at each τ, some integral vector has Fock polynomial (∏_τ dg_τ!/α!) times that monomial and is fixed, over any characteristic 0 field, by all g whose columns e are those of 1.
