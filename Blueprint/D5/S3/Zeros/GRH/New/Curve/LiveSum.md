## Abstract

Live Sum

Names for asked divisibility, gain, loss, short and served primes. Coupling count and exponent of the jet divisibility theorem; weights of the data. The sum of p^{−e} over e ≥ 1, e odd or p = d, is (1/(p−1) − ρ_p)/ω_p for non-split p, ω_p = (1 − (p/d))/2, ρ_p = nonsplitWeight.
