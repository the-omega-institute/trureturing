# Formal Parameter

## Abstract

Formal Parameter

Jets of products of torsion translates of theta are integral after scaling. Valuations of coefficients of log theta: only two degrees keep a denominator. Property: series integral on substitutions killing the Frobenius couplings. Good formal parameter and three-term normal form of log theta at non-split primes.
