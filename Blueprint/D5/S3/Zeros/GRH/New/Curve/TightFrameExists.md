# Tight Frame Exists

## Abstract

Tight Frame Exists

For each prime p, tight frames of every large length with a vector v such that every s₁·conj τ(v) is a p-adic unit, s₁ = Parameters.periodFactor.

*Commentary.*

Tight frames: for m ≥ (dN₁)⁶(R + 1) and ι: ℂ → ℂ_p there is a finite tight frame (v_i) of length m with multiplicities c_i ≥ R (IsTightFrame) in which, for some i, ι(s₁·conj τ(v_i)) is a p-adic unit at every place τ; s₁ = Parameters.periodFactor.
