Period Matrix Basis

Kisin lattice of a period matrix is free and dense up to p-powers.

*Commentary.*

For a period matrix U there are Q over A_inf with det Q ≠ 0 and Z over S_B with UZ = E^h Q, such that Uy = E^h x, y over S_B, gives x in QS^d, and for any y over B^+, δ ∣ lQ and p ∤ δ give δ ∣ l·x.
