*Commentary.*

Let tr r (r continuous, with IsTraceNondeg) be a uniform limit of combinations of traces of continuous representations unramified or crystalline at A, finitely many in all or all with jumps J. So is r; jump multiplicities and, under coefficientwise uniform convergence, killing polynomials of bounded degree pass to r.
