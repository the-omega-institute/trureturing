Hodge Tate Projectors

Crystalline cocycles modulo p^m: continuity, kernel of reduction, stability under corestriction. Hodge–Tate projectors of crystalline lattices with bounded weights have rank-free bounded denominators.

*Commentary.*

Uniform reading of jumps, for j of open image. Some t in C_p, depending only on j, n, w, works for all crystalline lattices T with jumps in [0,n], bases ε and x in T: there are z_s, |z_s| ≤ 1, with t Σ z_s γ(tr u(ε_s)) the multiplicity of the jump w of r at γ, whenever r is potentially crystalline, u(gy) = r(g)u(y), u(x) = 1.
