# Bd R

## Abstract

Bd R

The ideal W(m♭) and continuity of the Galois action on W(C♭) modulo p^m. Galois acts on [ε]−1 by the cyclotomic character modulo its square. Existence of Fontaine's period t with φt = pt, gt = χ(g)t, without logarithm. Frobenius contraction: solutions of ξ^n v = uφ(w) in A_max lie in A_inf[1/p]. B_dR with Galois action, embedding of Q̄_p; surjectivity in the fundamental exact sequence.
