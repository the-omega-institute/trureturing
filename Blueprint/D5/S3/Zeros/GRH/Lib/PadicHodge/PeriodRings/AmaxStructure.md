## Abstract

Amax Structure

Adic extension of linear maps; existence of (p,z)-adic topological bases with coordinates. Subgroup of multiples of n in an abelian group; link with adic filtration. Separated module of bounded rank with regular parameters over complete base is finite free. Coordinates of an adic topological basis: approximation, submodule membership, separation. Colmez's A_max, B_max^+, B_max with Frobenius, Galois action and θ. A_max modulo p is a polynomial ring; how A_inf lies in A_max.
