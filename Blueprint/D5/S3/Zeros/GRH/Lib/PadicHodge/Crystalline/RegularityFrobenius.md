*Commentary.*

Let F be coarser than 'near 1', T finite free over R with IsAdmissible for F, and T'' ≅ Hom_R(T, R(c)) equivariantly. Then T'' has IsAdmissible for F, and on inertia what kills (s, 0) on T kills (s⁻¹, 0) on T''.
