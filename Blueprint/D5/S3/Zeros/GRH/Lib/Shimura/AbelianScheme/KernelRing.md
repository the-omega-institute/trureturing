## Abstract

Kernel Ring

Abelian schemes with ring action, translations, Picard pairing, parallelogram law: definitions. Spec of algebras as schemes over a ring; full faithfulness. Kernel of a finite homomorphism is the spectrum of a finite algebra.

*Commentary.*

Let B be a separated commutative group scheme over R and f : A → B finite. The fibre of f over the zero section is represented by a closed subscheme Spec S of A, S a finite R-algebra, flat if f is flat.
