*Commentary.*

Let N, an O-module of sections containing the coordinates, be stable under lifts of translations by Λ^∨; nΛ^∨ ⊂ Λ, n a unit, B positive. Finitely many theta functions f_j, not all zero, have multiples γf_j in N, γ ≠ 0, not all vanishing on any Λ^∨-orbit of a field-valued point.
