## Abstract

Theta Structure

Symmetric theta coordinates are unique; K(L) is killed by the order of the index set.

**Theorem 1.1 (card_nsmul_eq_zero_of_lift).**

*Commentary.*

Over a ring R, let global functions on A be constant and (N, c) a theta structure indexed by finite H, with N a projective embedding whose coordinates span H⁰(N). Then |H| kills every section y whose translation lifts to N (via Ψ); over a local ring R these are exactly the sections y in the kernel K([N]).
