# Flat Closure

## Abstract

Flat Closure

Schematic density of points passes to stalks and to fibre products. Coordinates locally regular combinations of a linear system form a system of same class. Closure of theta points over the base is flat with reduced fibres.

*Commentary.*

Let S be reduced, finitely many of the η n dense, r > 1; assume IsCutDegree, and IsCutOutAt at η n for the image of θ n and at a geometric specialisation of every point. Then some flat closed A ⊂ ℙ^J_S has reduced fibres over η n, in which the θ n z are dense, and at such specialisations.
