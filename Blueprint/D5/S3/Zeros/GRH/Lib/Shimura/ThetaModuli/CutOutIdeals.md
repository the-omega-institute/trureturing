*Commentary.*

Let φ : A → k be local, ψ n : A → K jointly injective, r > 1. Assume IsCutDegree, and IsCutOut for I₀ at φ(P) and I n at ψ n(P). Then T = ⋂ (ψ n)⁻¹(I n) extends along φ to I₀, and A[X]/T is A-flat.
