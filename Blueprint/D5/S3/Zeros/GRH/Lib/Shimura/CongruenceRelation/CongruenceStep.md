# Congruence Step

## Abstract

Congruence Step

Frobenius-twisted reduced Hecke neighbours of A depend only on A + W, some W ≠ 0.

**Theorem 1.1 (congruence_step).**

*Commentary.*

Let β = (1 + √−d₀)/2, let z and its Hecke neighbours z·g_A (A ⊆ k_wⁿ a subspace) have V-points s, α(A), and let V → k be onto an algebraically closed field, with s reducing above a safe, good split place w of norm q. Then for some subspace W ≠ 0, Frob_q^{n−dim A} of the reduction of α(A) depends only on A + W.
