# Universal Points

## Abstract

Universal Points

Deep principal levels for finitely many isogenies; finite torsion index sets. Hecke isogenies at a split place: multiplier, action on level points, kernel in w-torsion. Kuga–Sato fibre powers, their complex points up to isomorphism, and level covers as closures.
