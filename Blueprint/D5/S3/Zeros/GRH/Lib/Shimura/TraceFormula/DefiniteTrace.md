Definite Trace

Trace of a Hecke operator on definite algebraic modular forms as orbital sum.

*Commentary.*

Trace formula, definite side: for E⁺ ≠ ℚ, 𝔑 neat, W finite-dimensional, the forms are finite-dimensional and finitely many orbits of definite triples count; if a is regular and tr ξ(y⁻¹) = ∑_μ w(P, μ) for separable P, then tr a is their orbit sum.
