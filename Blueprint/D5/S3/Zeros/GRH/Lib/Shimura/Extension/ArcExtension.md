*Commentary.*

Let A = V/Λ over O and s': V → A'(O) additive killing Λ, with embeddings N, M obeying the parallelogram law and theta coordinates of positive type (B, χ), resp. (μB, χ^μ). If Λ^∨ maps into K(N) and nΛ^∨ ⊂ Λ, n a unit, then s' comes from a homomorphism ψ with ψ^*[M] = μ[N].
