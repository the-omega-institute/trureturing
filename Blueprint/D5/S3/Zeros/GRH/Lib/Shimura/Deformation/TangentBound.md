# Tangent Bound

## Abstract

Tangent Bound

Deformations of the PEL object have at most n-1 parameters. Only the upper bound for first-order deformations is proved here, at every good place. The proof works on the finite group scheme of w-torsion with Drinfeld's lifting lemma, without p-divisible groups or crystals. Source: N. Katz, Serre–Tate local moduli, LNM 868 (1981), §1 (Drinfeld's lemma); J. Lubin, J. Tate, Formal moduli for one-parameter formal Lie groups, Bull. SMF 94 (1966); M. Harris, R. Taylor, The Geometry and Cohomology of Some Simple Shimura Varieties (2001), III.4.1.
