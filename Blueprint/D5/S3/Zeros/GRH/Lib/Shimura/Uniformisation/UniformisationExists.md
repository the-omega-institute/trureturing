# Uniformisation Exists

## Abstract

Uniformisation Exists

Smooth is geometrically reduced; an abelian scheme over an algebraically closed field is integral. Torsion section of invertible order vanishing somewhere vanishes (unramified torsion). Rigidified line bundles descend along fpqc covers; pairing identities descend along isogenies. A complex abelian variety is a complex torus: charts, compactness, uniformisation.
