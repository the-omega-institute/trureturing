# Lifted Isogenies

## Abstract

Lifted Isogenies

Every full complex PEL abelian variety is uniformised by a frame and reflection. Polarisation kernel has no p-part; maps respecting level and pairing are identity. Lifts of multiplications are isogenies that keep the pairing; division of lifts.

*Commentary.*

Division of lifts. Let f: A_x → A_x' and F: A_x → A_x₁ be homomorphisms of deformations over k[ε] reducing to a ≠ 0 and ca. If F kills all points over finite local k[ε]-algebras killed by f and a², some homomorphism A_x' → A_x₁ reduces to c.
