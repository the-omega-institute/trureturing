# Crystalline Etale

## Abstract

Crystalline Etale

A_max as Frobenius-twisted A_inf-algebra; map from O_C to A_max modulo divided powers. Étale algebras lift along surjections; étale over perfect is perfect. Crystalline comparison is bijective on algebras etale over a perfectoid torus.

*Commentary.*

If G is a commutative monoid that is a module over ℚ≥0 and B is étale over the monoid algebra O_C[G], then the crystalline comparison is bijective (HasCrysComparison) for the functor Hom(B, −) on O_C-algebras.
