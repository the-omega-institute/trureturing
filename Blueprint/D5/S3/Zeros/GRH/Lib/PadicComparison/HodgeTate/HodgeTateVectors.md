*Commentary.*

Let Y (smooth, charted over Ω) and W be proper over R, A a place of Ω above p containing R, k ≅ Q̄_p compatibly over E. For x ∈ Hdg^q(Y_E), some v ∈ ℂ_p ⊗ V^{q+h}(Y) of Hodge–Tate weight h for the decomposition group of A over E and semilinear Θ satisfy λ_h(u^*x) = Θ(u^*v) for all u: W → Y.
