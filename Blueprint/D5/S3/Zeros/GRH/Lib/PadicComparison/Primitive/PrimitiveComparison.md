*Commentary.*

Primitive comparison. Let Ω be algebraically closed, Y proper with IsCharted over Ω, and B ↦ B ⊗ M satisfy IsAcyclicOnPerfect (acyclic for tilting at every perfect O^♭-algebra). Then ℤ[Y_Ω] satisfies FP, each Hⁿ(Y_Ω, 𝔽_p) is finite, and primitiveMap ⊗ M is bijective on cohomology along Y(S) → Y_Ω(S[1/p]).
