# Curve Theta

## Abstract

Curve Theta

Weierstrass functions cover the cubic; sigma doubling; zeros and derivative of ℘'. Taylor series, normalised CM lattice, theta function and coordinates at a 2-torsion origin. Generator of the imaginary quadratic integer ring; stability under conjugation. Lattice invariants via E4, E6, discriminant; uniformisation of elliptic curves. CM lattices correspond to scaled fractional ideals; existence and area formula. Theta function shifted by a two-torsion point: cubic, addition, doubling, multiplier.
