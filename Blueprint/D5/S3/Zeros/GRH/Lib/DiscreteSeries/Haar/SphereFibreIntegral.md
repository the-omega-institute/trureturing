*Commentary.*

For J = 1, integrating Cayley's density cayleyDensity over the coordinates θ of row and column k₀, the complementary block η being fixed, gives 2πⁿ/(n−1)! times the density of η in size n−1; both sides are divided by 2πⁿa/(n−1)!, a > 0.
