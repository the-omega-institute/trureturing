Splitting Char Exists

Existence of an unramified splitting Hecke character of a CM field.

*Commentary.*

Let the CM field E be stably unramified (StablyUnram), and let every root of unity of E be δ/δ̄ for some unit δ with ∏_w Φ_w(δ) = 1. Then E has a splitting character (SplittingChar).
