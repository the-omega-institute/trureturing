*Commentary.*

Relations at split primes. For U fixing M and μ_m, without complex conjugations, there are split primes, Frobenii φ_i and elements γ_i such that each family (n_i) with ∑ n_i(x(γ_i)) = 0 on Selmer cocycles x extends to an equivariant homomorphism on U giving such relations at other split primes. A bound on trivialised cocycles bounds their number below.
