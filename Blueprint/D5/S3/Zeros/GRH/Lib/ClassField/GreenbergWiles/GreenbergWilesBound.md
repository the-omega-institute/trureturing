*Commentary.*

Let K be totally complex, s auxiliary primes for l^r (r ≠ 0), N and U (SplitPlaces), indexed by ι, with Frobenii φ_i; V finite, l^r-torsion, U-fixed; z central modulo U, fixing l-power roots of unity, z − 1 onto V. If U fixes each l^r-th root, fixed by all φ_i, of a U-fixed N-unit, the trivialised cocycles unramified outside N and s are finite in number, at most (#V)^{#ι+1}.
