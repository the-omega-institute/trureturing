*Commentary.*

Equivariant Dirichlet theorem: with X the places above N∞ of the field of Q, its N-units E are finitely generated, rank(E × ℤ) ≤ #X, and there are ℝ-linearly independent additive maps Λ_z : E × ℤ → ℝ (z ∈ X) with Λ_z(g⁻¹y) = Λ_{gz}(y).
