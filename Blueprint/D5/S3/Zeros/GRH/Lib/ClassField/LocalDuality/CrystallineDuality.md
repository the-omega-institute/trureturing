*Commentary.*

Local duality: L, L' potentially crystalline, perfectly paired by b into ℤ_ℓ(1), Γ → G_ℓ an open embedding, A the image of Z_f(L) in V = L/ℓ^m. If A and S consist of continuous cocycles whose cup products with H bound in ℚ̄_ℓ^×, then [S : S∩A] divides [Z_f(L') : H∩Z_f(L')].
