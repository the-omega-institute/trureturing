## Abstract

Real Place Symbols

Invariant map Br(k) ≅ Q/Z of a henselian local field and its functoriality. Number of Kummer cocycles; finiteness of local 1-cocycles of finite modules. The local Artin map sends uniformisers to geometric Frobenius elements. Order bound for orthogonal cocycle groups, the inequality for local Tate duality. Complex conjugations; symbol of a totally positive element dies keeping real places real.
