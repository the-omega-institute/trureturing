*Commentary.*

L CM. A conjugation-invariant finite idele t is x z z̄, x ∈ L^×, with any signs of product 1 if t = 1. For real a ∈ L^×, a z z̄ = 1 forces ∏Φ_w(a) > 0. A splitting character is −1 on the uniformiser at every w = w̄.
