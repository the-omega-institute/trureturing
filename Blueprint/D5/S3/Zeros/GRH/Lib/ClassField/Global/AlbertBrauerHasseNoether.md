## Abstract

Albert Brauer Hasse Noether

Global class field theory, covering Albert–Brauer–Hasse–Noether, reciprocity, existence and norms. Only finite ideles are used, real places are handled by total positivity, and existence is proved only for totally complex fields. Reciprocity starts from cyclotomic characters of prime conductor, and the auxiliary character is chosen by B. H. Neumann's covering lemma. Source: J. S. Milne, Class Field Theory (v4.03, 2020), VI 4.9, VII 4–9, VIII 4; Tate, Global class field theory, in Cassels–Fröhlich 1967, ch. VII; H. Hasse, Die Struktur der R. Brauerschen Algebrenklassengruppe, Math. Ann. 107 (1933) (reciprocity from the sum formula).
