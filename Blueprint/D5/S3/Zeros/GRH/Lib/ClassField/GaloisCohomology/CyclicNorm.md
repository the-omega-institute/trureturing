# Cyclic Norm

## Abstract

Cyclic Norm

Index of n-th powers in the multiplicative group of a local field. Tate's index formula for norms on stable subgroups in prime-degree Galois extensions. Classes split by a cyclic extension are symbols; symbols vanish exactly on norms.

*Commentary.*

Let χ : Γ → ℚ/ℤ have open kernel U. A class in H²(Γ, Ω^×) restricts to zero on U exactly when it is a symbol (χ, w), w ∈ K^×; and (χ, w) = 0 exactly when w is a norm from the fixed field of U.
