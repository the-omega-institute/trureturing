# Cyclotomic Ramification

## Abstract

Cyclotomic Ramification

Cyclotomic p-power tower of Q_p is totally ramified; inertia hits each cyclotomic value.

*Commentary.*

Every natural number a prime to p is the value of the cyclotomic character at some element of the inertia group of G_p; that is, ℚ_p(μ_{p^∞}) is totally ramified.
