# Adele Norm

## Abstract

Adele Norm

Ideles to ideals and class group; large S; open subgroups that contain the principal ideles of totally positive elements have finite index. Dirichlet–Chevalley–Hasse S-unit theorem: finite generation and rank. Norm map on finite adeles of an extension and its basic properties.

*Commentary.*

For number fields L/K, a finite place w of L and y in L_w^×, the norm to K of the finite idele that is y at w and 1 elsewhere is 1 at every place except the one below w.
