Base Change Places

A totally complex number field with a conjugation for all embeddings is CM. E[X]/P is CM when roots have constant modulus; its finite adeles as polynomials. Cyclic prime-degree CM extension with test character; compatible CM types; matched places. Base change map and Galois action on finite adelic points of unitary groups. Places above a set, adelic matched pairs, basic compatibilities of base change maps.
