## Abstract

Orbit Class Sum

Polynomial roots inverting the norm, matched pairs of test functions, splitting of cocycles. Matching of unit functions at a principal tame level. Units with invertible skew part. Class sum of orbit counts equals the mass of the stabiliser's classes.
