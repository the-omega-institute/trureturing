# Unitary Roots

## Abstract

Unitary Roots

Hasse principle for norms in one CM field via reciprocity and Hasse's norm theorem. Adelically split unitary cocycles split rationally (Landherr); normal matrices are semisimple. Polynomial roots inverting the norm map; products of local matching pairs.
