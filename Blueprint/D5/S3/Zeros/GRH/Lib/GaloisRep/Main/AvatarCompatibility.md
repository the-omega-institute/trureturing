## Abstract

Avatar Compatibility

Galois representations for all eigenforms of odd rank, by l-adic limits of seeds. Descent of the Galois representation along quadratic steps, replacing the patching lemma. Local-global compatibility of l-adic avatars of algebraic Hecke characters at all finite places.
