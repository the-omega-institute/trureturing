Spherical All Ranks

*Commentary.*

For E with Weil data, every eigenform f on the definite U(n) has an attached ℓ-adic representation. If K_w fixes f, each attached one is unramified or crystalline at w, with the jumps of f's weight, and any Hecke polynomial there kills geometric Frobenius.
