*Commentary.*

Let y in x be pro-p-Iwahori fixed at w ∤ ℓ, the line stabiliser acting by χ of the top left entry, χ(u) ≠ 0, χ(u)^(n!) ≠ 1. Then tr((τ − 1)M)·G(U)y = tr(G(s)(τ − 1)M)·y via ι, in the Galois representation of x, for G in ℂ[X], any matrix M, U the Hecke operator of diag(ϖ,1,…,1).
