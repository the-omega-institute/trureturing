# Weight Congruence

## Abstract

Weight Congruence

Ultrametric Stone-Weierstrass for modules; density of matrix coefficients of lacunary weights. The l-adic model of an algebraic modular form: weight moved to l. Chebotarev uniqueness: a Hecke system determines the trace of attached representations. A form is an l-adic uniform limit of typed forms with free weights.

*Commentary.*
