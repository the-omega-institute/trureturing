# Minor Basis

## Abstract

Minor Basis

Products of minors form a basis over every ring, with character identity.

*Commentary.*

For finitely many coloured rows and finitely many columns of colours τ and heights h, there is one family of minor products that, over every commutative ring R, is linearly independent and whose span contains the semi-invariant module of the corresponding weight.
