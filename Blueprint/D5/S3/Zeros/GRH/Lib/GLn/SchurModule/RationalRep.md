Rational Rep

Rational representation of a product of GL_n on Weyl's lattice, twisted by determinants.

*Commentary.*

The monoid homomorphism from ∏_τ GL(n_τ,R) to square matrices sending g to ∏_τ det(g_τ)^{e_τ} times the Schur matrix of g, for integers e_τ.
