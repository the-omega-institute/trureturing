Adelic Modulus

Haar modulus of ideles and adelic matrices is the adelic absolute value.

*Commentary.*

For a in GL_n(𝔸) and b in GL_1(𝔸), the map x ↦ axb on n×1 adelic matrices pushes the standard measure forward to |det(b)^n det a|⁻¹ times itself, with |·| the module of an idele.
