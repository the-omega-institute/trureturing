Idele Hilbert90

Functoriality of places and finite adeles under field maps and complex conjugation. Base change of finite adeles as topological modules; Galois invariants of finite adeles. *Commentary.*

Hilbert's Theorem 90 for finite ideles: if σ ∈ Gal(L/K) has finite order n and a finite adele u of L has ∏_{i<n} σ^i(u) = 1, then u·σ(c) = c for some invertible finite adele c.
