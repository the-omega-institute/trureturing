Congruence Level

Reduction maps, principal levels, levels cut out by reductions at split places; index. Parahoric and pro-p Iwahori groups, dominant cone, exponents, multiplicativity of U-operators. Principal congruence subgroups: neighbourhood basis, index bound, quotients of profinite integers.

*Commentary.*

For a nonzero integer a of the CM field E, the index of the principal level K(a) in K₀ (Subgroup.relIndex, 0 if infinite) is at most N(a)^(n²), with N(a) the absolute norm of (a) and n the rank.
