*Commentary.*

Let φ: A → B, φ′: A′ → B be continuous homomorphisms sending A₀, A₁ into B₀, with φ(A) + φ′(A′) = B, quotients compact, A second countable. For continuous B₀-periodic F, the iterated mean of F(φa + φ′a′) over A′/A₁, then A/A₀, is the mean of F over B/B₀.
