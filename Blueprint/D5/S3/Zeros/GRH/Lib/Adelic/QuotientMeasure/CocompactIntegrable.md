# Cocompact Integrable

## Abstract

Cocompact Integrable

Unfolding quotient integrals in stages; pushing quotient measures along surjections. Unfolding a sum over an orbit or block to the stabiliser quotient. Quotient integral vanishes when averaging eigenvalue differs from modulus, or nontrivial phase. Continuous invariant functions for cocompact subgroups are integrable; cocompactness of products.

*Commentary.*

Let Γ be a discrete subgroup of G, μ left invariant, F continuous and left Γ-invariant. If F(uy) = cF(y) for all y, for some u in G and c with |c| = 1, c ≠ 1, then ∫_{Γ\G} F dμ = 0.
