# Unimodular

## Abstract

Unimodular

Proper actions have cutoff weights; discrete subgroups; unfolding a sum over a lattice. Covering radius of a subgroup at most n² over the first minimum of its dual. Quotient measure under equivariant measure-preserving maps and right translation. Groups with a lattice are unimodular; cocompact discrete subgroups have finite covolume.
