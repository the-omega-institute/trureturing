*Commentary.*

Let l be prime, U open normal in the compact group G, z central modulo U, and φ, with values in ℚ≥0, multiplicative in short exact sequences of finite l-torsion modules fixed by U on which z − 1 is injective. If φ = 1 on those with Higman's property, then φ = 1 on all.
