# Mass Basic

## Abstract

Mass Basic

Trace dual of a unimodular hermitian lattice; short bases by transference. Effective bounded fundamental domain for a CM field in its adeles. Mahler-Godement compactness criterion for unitary groups; finite class number for definite spaces. Mass is positive; weighted count of norm-one vectors equals mass of rank one less.
