# Landherr

## Abstract

Landherr

Landherr's Hasse principle for hermitian forms, and weak approximation for unitary groups. The form here covers only adelically unimodular spaces of equal signature over a CM field containing the roots of unity of order 5040. It is proved from Hasse's norm theorem instead of Hasse-Minkowski, and approximation uses a Cayley transform with a norm-one scalar. Source: W. Landherr, Äquivalenz Hermitescher Formen über einem beliebigen algebraischen Zahlkörper, Abh. Math. Sem. Hamburg 11 (1936) 245-248; M. Kneser, Lectures on Galois cohomology of classical groups, TIFR 47 (1969), §5.5.
