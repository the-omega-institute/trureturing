## Abstract

Spherical Hecke

Spherical Hecke operators at split places commute, are adjoint, and are simultaneously diagonalisable. Weights, Hecke polynomials, eigenforms, labels and expected Hodge-Tate jumps. Hecke polynomial at a split place, as operator; spherical eigenvectors. Lifts of finite reductions to K; action of normalising finite group on level vectors.
