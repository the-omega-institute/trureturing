# Entire Theta

## Abstract

Entire Theta

Theta function of a real vacuum datum is entire and determines the datum.

**Theorem 1.1 (differentiable_entireTheta).**

*Commentary.*

For V with all signs positive and f a real vacuum, that is, fixed by inserting the Gaussian as real datum, the theta function Θ_f(y) = f(y)·exp(|y|²/2) on real vectors is entire.
