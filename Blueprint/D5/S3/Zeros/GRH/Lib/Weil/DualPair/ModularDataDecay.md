Modular Data Decay

*Commentary.*

Let 𝔪 ≠ (1), Ψ be supported on left invertible matrices mod 𝔪, and θ the kernel of v ⊗ Ψ. For each M there are B, N, C with |θ(g,h)| ht(g)^M ≤ C mom_N(v) ht(h)^B for all g, h and all v with finite N-th moment.
