# Adapted Splitting

## Abstract

Adapted Splitting

Zero term along an isotropic subspace taken in stages; independent of the frame. Witt decomposition: every isotropic frame splits off hyperbolic planes plus a framed complement. Adapted splittings; the standard splitting of a space plus hyperbolic planes.
