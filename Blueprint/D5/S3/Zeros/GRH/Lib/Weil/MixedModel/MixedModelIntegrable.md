Mixed Model Integrable

The mixed Schrödinger model function of a vector is continuous and integrable.

*Commentary.*

For a splitting of V over V₀ with p hyperbolic planes and f in 𝒮(V), the 𝒮(V₀)-valued function x ↦ f[x] of the mixed model is integrable on 𝔸^p.
