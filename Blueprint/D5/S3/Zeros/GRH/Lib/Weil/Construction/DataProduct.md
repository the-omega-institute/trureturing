Data Product

*Commentary.*

If the matrix of g is block diagonal with blocks g₁, g₂ for a sign-compatible splitting of the coordinates, then ω(g)(f₁ ⊠ f₂) = ω(g₁)f₁ ⊠ ω(g₂)f₂.
