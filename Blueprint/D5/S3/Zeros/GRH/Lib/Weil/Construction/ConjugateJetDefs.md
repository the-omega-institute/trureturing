# Conjugate Jet Defs

## Abstract

Conjugate Jet Defs

Definition of tight frames in the unimodular lattice, with multiplicities. Definition: orthogonality relation between translates of the vacuum and of a finite vector. Relation: jets of one vector are Galois conjugates of those of another.
