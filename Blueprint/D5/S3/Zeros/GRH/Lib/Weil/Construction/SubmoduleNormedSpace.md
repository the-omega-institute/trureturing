# Submodule Normed Space

## Abstract

Submodule Normed Space

Elementary identities for the Heisenberg bicharacter and the vacuum function. Heisenberg representation twisted by a group element preserving the cocycle. Shortcut instance: a complex subspace of a normed space is normed.
