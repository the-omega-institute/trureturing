# Theta Functional

## Abstract

Theta Functional

The finite-adelic cocycle of the Weil operators written as a finite Gauss sum. Finite cocycle is trivial: metaplectic cover splits over the finite-adelic unitary group. Adelic multiplier, vacuum measure; the cocycle is trivial on the adelic unitary group. Existence and uniqueness of the invariant theta functional; Poisson-type sum over rational points.

*Commentary.*

If θ is a theta functional of V, every bounded functional ℓ on 𝒮(V) invariant under the Heisenberg operators ϱ(γ), γ a rational point of V, is a complex multiple of θ.
