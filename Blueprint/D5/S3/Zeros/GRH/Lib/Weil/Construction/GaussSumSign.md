*Commentary.*

Let X be a finite abelian group without 2-torsion, f(f(x)) = −x, F an f-invariant function with values roots of unity, F(0) = 1, ∑F real, r ≡ 1 mod 4 an integer. If |c| = 1 and c·r^a = (∑F)·r^b, then c = 1.
