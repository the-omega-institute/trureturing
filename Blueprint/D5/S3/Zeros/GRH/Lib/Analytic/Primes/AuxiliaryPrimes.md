Auxiliary Primes

A prime 1 modulo M^k with logarithm in a long window; zero-free disc.

*Commentary.*

Let M > 0 be a multiple of 4 and A natural. For large W, all k ≥ 2 with M^k ≤ W^A and all U in [W, W^A], there is a prime p ≡ 1 mod M^k with U ≤ log p ≤ U + W.
