# Adapted Hom

## Abstract

Adapted Hom

Adelic Iwasawa decomposition for unitary parabolics; centre of radical and Fourier coefficients. Fourier coefficients of automorphic functions along centre of a radical: basic laws, inversion. Radical of a line stabiliser: modulus on hermitian matrices, cocompact rational points. Adapted splittings pass to Hom spaces; slices there written in Kronecker frames.
