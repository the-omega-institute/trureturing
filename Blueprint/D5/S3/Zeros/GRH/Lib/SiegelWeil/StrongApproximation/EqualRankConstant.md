Equal Rank Constant

Automorphic function invariant at a split place, compact group and torus is constant.

*Commentary.*

Let D be continuous on [U(W)], W split along a line, right invariant under points supported on S ∪ S̄ (S nonempty) and integral points off S. If invariance under the radical makes D on the Levi independent of the torus, then D is constant.
