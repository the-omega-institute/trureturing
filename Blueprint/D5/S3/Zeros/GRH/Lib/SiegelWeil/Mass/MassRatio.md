*Commentary.*

For a CM field E with Weil data, f infinite places and root discriminant rd: if 8f ≤ 2^u and j ≥ 4u + 12, then log(mass_{j+1} / mass_j) differs from f·log(rd^{j+1/2} j! / (2π)^{j+1}) by at most 2/2^u.
