*Commentary.*

Change of signs. If each η_μ has product 1, some permutation of the GL_n(E)-orbits of triples has the form (q, y, μ) ↦ (Uq, y, μ), where U ∈ GL_n(𝔸_f) depends on y, μ only, commutes with the centraliser of y in GL_n(E), and (x, y) has type εη_μ iff (Ux, y) has type ε.
