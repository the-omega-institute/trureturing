# Label Characters

## Abstract

Label Characters

Shintani norm via IsRootChoice: descent keeps inner products, commutes with induction; extension. Frobenius reciprocity for parabolic induction from several diagonal blocks. Shintani lifts for GL_n: definition, stability under parabolic induction and pointwise operations. Torus-induced character lifts to the semidirect product; Brauer lift has a virtual lift. Green's characters with squarefree labels are orthonormal irreducible characters of GL_r(F_q).
