*Commentary.*

For smooth ρ, a uniformiser ϖ and any function χ, the pro-p Iwahori Hecke operator of diag(ϖ,1,…,1) preserves the pro-p Iwahori fixed vectors on which each integral k fixing the first coordinate line modulo ϖ acts by χ of its top left entry.
