Iwahori Conjugation

*Commentary.*

Let σ be a continuous isometric endomorphism of the local field F, surjective on GL_n(F), and t diagonal with strictly increasing absolute values. Every g in I₁tI₁ (I₁ the pro-p Iwahori subgroup) satisfies k⁻¹gσ(k) = td with k, d in I₁, d diagonal.
