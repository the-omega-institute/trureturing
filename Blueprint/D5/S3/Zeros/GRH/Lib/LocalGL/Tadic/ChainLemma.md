Chain Lemma

An unramified exponent with sorted roots is non-zero on spherical vectors. Chain lemma: spherical vector orthogonal to a polynomial in U; strings of Satake roots.

*Commentary.*

Let x ≠ 0 be a spherical Hecke eigenvector in a smooth unitary representation of GL_N(E_w), with Hecke polynomial P. If P(c) = 0 and |c|² q^k = q^N, then c, cq, …, cq^(k−1) are all roots of P.
