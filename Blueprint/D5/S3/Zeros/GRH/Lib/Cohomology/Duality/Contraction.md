# Contraction

## Abstract

Contraction

A degree-two Ext class between flat functors tensors naturally with every functor. Leray–Hirsch map on Ext groups: degree truncation, long exact ladders, local to global. Coefficient theories on test rings, closed supports, pushforward from zero loci, slices. Semilinear contraction on exterior algebras and its derivation rule.
