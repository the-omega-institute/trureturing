## Abstract

Point Class Product

Hodge class of a point of a product is the product of point classes.

*Commentary.*

The product of point classes is a point class: over a field k of characteristic 0, if X and Y have atlases of dimensions d and 1, then cl(z) × cl(w) = cl(z, w) in Hdg^{d+1}(X × Y) for k-points z, w.
