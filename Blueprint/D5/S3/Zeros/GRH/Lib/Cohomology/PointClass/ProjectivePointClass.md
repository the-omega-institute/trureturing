## Abstract

Projective Point Class

Hodge–Tate map does not kill the class of a point of projective space.

**Theorem 1.1 (hodgeTateMap_pointClass_ne_zero).**

*Commentary.*

For a field k with an embedding in Q̄_p and any k-point z of projective d-space projSpace, the Hodge–Tate map of weight d does not kill the class cl(z) ∈ Hdg^d.
