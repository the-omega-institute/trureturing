*Commentary.*

For a chart a over a field E, with ring R and q étale coordinates, some E-algebra section u of R ⊕ R^q → R is universal: composing with u is a bijection from maps of tests (R, R^q) → (A, M) to E-algebra maps R → A ⊕ M.
