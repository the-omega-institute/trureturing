*Commentary.*

Class of the diagonal: if X has an atlas of dimension d, some δ in Hdg^d(X × X, complement of the diagonal) is, near each diagonal field point, the symbol [df/f], where f_i = x_i ⊗ 1 − 1 ⊗ x_i for chart coordinates x cut out the diagonal.
