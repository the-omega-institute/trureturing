# Valuative Density

## Abstract

Valuative Density

Proper functors and the Riemann-Zariski type space of places covering them. Homotopy quotient by one endomorphism: Wang sequence and criterion via gamma minus one. Maps from perfect b to the tilt equal maps from O_C tensor W(b). Perfect algebras, perfection, and the two-chart exact sequence forcing f|g or g|f. A_inf-structures on tilted algebras; tensor with W(b) has no p-torsion. Coefficients tensored with the maximal ideal of the tilt; the ideal is idempotent. Affine maps surjective on valuation-ring points are covers: descent along v-covers.

*Commentary.*

Let V_i ⊂ Y be finitely many relatively representable subfunctors. A subfunctor U of Y on test rings is dense for Λ if every point of Y in a valuation ring of an algebraically closed field lies in some V_i contained in U on test rings.
