*Commentary.*

Let W be proper over R, ℓ invertible in R, T a test R-algebra, x ∈ H^n(W_T, ℤ/ℓ). The primes P of T with x zero on the fibre over κ(P) form an open set; if it is all of Spec T, then x = 0.
