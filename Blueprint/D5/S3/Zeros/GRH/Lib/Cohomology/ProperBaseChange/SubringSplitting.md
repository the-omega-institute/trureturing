Subring Splitting

A non-valuation subring splits into two larger ones with exact Mayer-Vietoris sequence.

*Commentary.*

Let K be algebraically closed, divisibility in D total, ℓ ∈ D^× prime, B in aicSubrings γ not a valuation ring of K. There are B₁, B₂ ⊋ B generating B₃, all in aicSubrings γ, such that every valuation ring of K containing B contains B₁ or B₂, and the Mayer–Vietoris sequence of locally constant ℤ/ℓ-valued functions on the spectra modulo γ(J) is short exact.
