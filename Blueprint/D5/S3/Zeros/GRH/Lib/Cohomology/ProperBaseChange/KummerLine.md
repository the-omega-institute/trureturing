*Commentary.*

Let D = B[1/t] = C[1/s], ts = 1, be a ring integral over A[t,s]. For finite G ⊂ D there are a subring R ⊂ A and a Noetherian R-submodule Y ⊃ G ∪ {1} such that each n has R-submodules P ⊂ im B, Q ⊂ im C and finitely generated M with Yⁿ, PQ ⊂ M ⊂ P+Q+Y.
