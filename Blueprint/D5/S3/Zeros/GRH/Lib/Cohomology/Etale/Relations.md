*Commentary.*

For finitely many f_i: W → Y and integers c_i, if the inclusion into W of the locus where ∑ c_i [f_i(w)] = 0 is an equivalence for F, then ∑ c_i f_i^* x = 0 for every x in H^n(Y,F).
