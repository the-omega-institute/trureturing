# Bijective Comparisons

## Abstract

Bijective Comparisons

Covers by sums of representables; Ext commutes with products of coefficient functors. A pointwise bijection of functors induces isomorphisms on cohomology. Bijectivity criteria for comparison maps: representables, Čech descent, fibrewise and universal points.
