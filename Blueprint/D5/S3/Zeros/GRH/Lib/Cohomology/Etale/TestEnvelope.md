Test Envelope

Functorial envelope: maps from given rings to tests factor through a test ring.

*Commentary.*

For any family of commutative rings R_o, there are an endofunctor E of commutative rings with every E(A) a test ring and a natural map θ: A → E(A) such that every homomorphism from an R_o to a test ring factors through θ at R_o.
