## Abstract

Connected Components

Locally constant functions on tests are additive for idempotent partitions. Tilts of products of valuation rings; kernel of theta is (xi); untilting isomorphism. Cohomological equivalence can be checked on connected components, by Zorn's lemma.

*Commentary.*

Let X, Y have continuous Λ-cohomology and U_a ⊂ Y (a ideals of R) be affine, behave like zero loci, and split densely along idempotents. If u: X → Y is an equivalence over U_a for each proper a containing e or 1 − e for every idempotent e, u is an equivalence.
