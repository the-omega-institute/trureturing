# Jet Rename

## Abstract

Jet Rename

The Fischer pairing is preserved by an injective renaming of the variables.

**Theorem 1.1 (jet_rename).**

*Commentary.*

The jet pairing P(∂)F(0) of a polynomial P with a power series F over a commutative ring is unchanged when both are renamed along an injective map of variables.
