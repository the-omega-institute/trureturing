*Commentary.*

Nakayama in tangent form: let x : S → V be a section of a finite type V-algebra, φ : V → k a field point, I ⊆ ker x. If every k[ε]-point over φ lifting φ∘x that kills I kills ker x, some b with b·ker x ⊆ I has φ(x(b)) ≠ 0.
