Generalised Index

Generalised index is invariant under the involution; lattice index is index of the determinant.

*Commentary.*

For M in GL_n(A) and L = MRⁿ the lattice spanned by its columns, [Rⁿ : Rⁿ ∩ L]/[L : Rⁿ ∩ L] equals the generalised index [R : (det M)R], when R ⊂ A satisfies IsAdelic.
