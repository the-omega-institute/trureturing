Separating Points

*Commentary.*

Let e_z, p_z be complex points of S-schemes X, G over the same η_z, with X reduced and the e_z dense. If a linear system W on X and a projective embedding M of G give e_z, p_z the same homogeneous coordinates, then some S-morphism d : X → G has d∘e_z = p_z and d^*M = W.
