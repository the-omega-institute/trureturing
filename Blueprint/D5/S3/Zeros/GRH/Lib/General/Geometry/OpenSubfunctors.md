*Commentary.*

Let Θ: C → k-algebras and T ⊂ X∘Θ be such that each field point of X lies in an open O with O∘Θ ⊂ T. Then for y in X(Θc) and m maximal in Θc, some f ∉ m has y in T wherever f becomes a unit.
