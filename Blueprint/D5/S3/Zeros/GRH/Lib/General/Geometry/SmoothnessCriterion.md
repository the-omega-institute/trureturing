*Commentary.*

Let X be locally of finite type over a normal domain R ⊂ ℂ, φ a d-parameter family of complex points over R, immersive at b, with φ(b) specialising to nearby members, and a: Spec V → X extend φ(b). If the relative tangent space at the closed point of a has dimension ≤ d, X is smooth of relative dimension d on an open set containing a.
