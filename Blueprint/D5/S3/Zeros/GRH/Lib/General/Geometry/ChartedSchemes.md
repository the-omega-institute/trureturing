## Abstract

Charted Schemes

Bijectivity of a comparison defines a closed class; charted schemes (IsCharted).

**Theorem 1.1 (isClosedClass).**

*Commentary.*

Given a comparison c from F to F' along φ, functors ψ, ψ' to D and η: ψ' → ψ∘φ, the property of N on D that c along N(η) is bijective in every degree holds for 0 and passes along short exact sequences (IsClosedClass).
