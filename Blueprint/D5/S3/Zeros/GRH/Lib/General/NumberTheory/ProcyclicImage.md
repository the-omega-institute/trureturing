# Procyclic Image

## Abstract

Procyclic Image

Compact subgroups of l-adic units (1 mod 4) are procyclic.

*Commentary.*

A continuous κ from a compact group to ℤ_ℓ^×, with values ≡ 1 mod 4 if ℓ = 2, has procyclic image: for some γ, every open subgroup containing γ and ker κ is the whole group.
