# Valuation

## Abstract

Valuation

Inertia at p fixes roots of unity of order prime to p. l-adic valuation on the algebraic closure; normalised lengths over integer rings.

*Commentary.*

Normalised length: the additive map sending n to n·v(ϖ), ϖ a uniformiser of O and v(ℓ) = 1, so that O mod ℓ has length 1.
