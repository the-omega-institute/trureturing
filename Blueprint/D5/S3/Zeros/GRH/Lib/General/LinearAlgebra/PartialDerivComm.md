## Abstract

Partial Deriv Comm

Characteristic polynomials over a subring pass from spanning elements to the whole algebra. Abstract Hecke operators, level eigenspaces, weighted class sums, indices of compact open subgroups. Jacobi's formula: derivative of the determinant at the identity is the trace.
