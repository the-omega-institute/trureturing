## Abstract

Extend Columns

Kronecker products, parabolic, unitriangular, Levi, parahoric subgroups; Bessel subgroups and frame of the space. Independent columns extend to an invertible matrix; standard inclusion is injective.

*Commentary.*

A matrix b over a field with linearly independent columns extends to an invertible matrix u: column ι(j) of u is column j of b.
