# Haar Unique

## Abstract

Haar Unique

Complex Gaussian integral for matrices of positive real part; linear change of variables. Determinant is monotone in the semidefinite order; matrix Bessel inequality for contractions. Reindexing, currying and flipping product measures preserve measure. Haar measures giving one function integral one are equal.
