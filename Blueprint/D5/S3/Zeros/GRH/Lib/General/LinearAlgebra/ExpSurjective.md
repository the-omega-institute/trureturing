# Exp Surjective

## Abstract

Exp Surjective

Products of principal minors of weight w: transpose, inverse, triangular laws, analyticity. Fischer's inequality for principal minors of positive semidefinite matrices. Maximal compact subgroup, its complexification, block diagonal part, Haar probability on K. Polar decomposition in U(p,q); basic properties of accretive matrices. Exponential is onto K; every element of U(p,q) is a product of two exponentials.
