## Abstract

Common Eigenvectors

Auerbach's lemma: basis dual to chosen forms with all forms bounded by one. Steinitz: embeddings of a countable ring extend to field isomorphism with C. Embeddings of countable rings into C extend to fields of continuum size. Semisimplicity by trace form, free integers, minors criterion for linear dependence. Common eigenvectors and eigencharacters of commuting operators; sorting by adjacent swaps.
