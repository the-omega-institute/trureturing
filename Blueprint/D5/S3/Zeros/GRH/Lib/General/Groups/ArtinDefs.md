Artin Defs

*Commentary.*

Pontryagin duality for profinite G: if s maps a group X homomorphically to the additive maps from continuous characters of G to ℚ/ℤ, some homomorphism f : X → G^ab has χ(f(x)) = s(x)(χ) for all χ, x.
