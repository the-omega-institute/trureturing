Residual Irreducibility

Tame pair with residually distinct conjugate eigenvalues spans all matrices of a lattice.

*Commentary.*

Let u be an O-lattice for R of rank n, R(γ^q) = R(φ⁻¹γφ), R(γ^{qⁿ}) = R(γ), and ζ ∈ O an eigenvalue of R(γ) with ζ^{q^i} (i < n) residually distinct. Then u(G) spans all matrices over O and u(φ^{−n}) is scalar.
