*Commentary.*

Let G be finite, i : G → H a homomorphism, χ a character of H, s ∈ H, ρ a finite-dimensional complex representation of G. If the average over G of χ(i(g))·tr ρ(g) vanishes, then ∑_g χ(i(g)s)·ρ(g) = 0.
