# Valuation Pair

## Abstract

Valuation Pair

Unitary group of a form over a star ring; integral, congruence subgroups; topology instances. Abstract Heisenberg bicharacter, representation, vacuum function and group action with cocycle. Twisted norm and its basic laws, tame cyclic actions, profinite rings. Ring pairs behaving as valuation rings after patching along idempotents.
