*Commentary.*

For every positive modulus the normalized two-index kernel becomes a signed exchange after a positive Fourier transform in the charge coordinate. The two transforms cancel by discrete Fourier inversion. In the cyclic Barnes–Wall calculation the exchange takes the order-four twist with central insertion to the reflection twist with inverse insertion.
