*Commentary.*

Suppose the affine realization of a finite simplicial graph map is nullhomotopic. At every sufficiently large ordinary source subdivision depth, composing the map with any choice of vertex in each original support gives a contiguity contraction in the fixed target. Open stars of the target supply a uniform Lebesgue number on the homotopy cylinder.
