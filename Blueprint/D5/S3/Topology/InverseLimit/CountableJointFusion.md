# Countable joint finite-block fusion

## Abstract

A countable finite-block selector jointly preserves fixed reservoirs and one-sided cofinite inclusions.

The active predicate records delayed proper-stage domains. The displayed finite set consists of exactly the labels k active in block j. Every block has a finite active fibre, and every label is active from some stage onward. I and J are inherited stage sets; N is the literal fixed reservoir family, with both Bool colours retained. EventuallySubset uses the cofinite filter, so the conclusion is one-sided finite difference rather than symmetric difference.

**Theorem 1.1 (Joint selector and countable limit sets).**

$$\forall active \in Nat \to Nat \to Prop,\; \forall I \in Nat \to \operatorname{Set}\left(Nat\right),\; \forall J \in Nat \to Nat \to \operatorname{Set}\left(Nat\right),\; \forall N \in Nat \to Bool \to \operatorname{Set}\left(Nat\right),\; \left(\left(\forall j \in Nat,\; \operatorname{Finite}\left(\{k: Nat | \operatorname{active}\left(k, j\right)\}\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall k \in Nat,\; \exists j0 \in Nat,\; \forall j \in Nat,\; \operatorname{Le}\left(j0, j\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{active}\left(k, j\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall k \in Nat,\; \forall j \in Nat,\; \operatorname{active}\left(k, j\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{Subset}\left(\operatorname{J}\left(k, j\right), \operatorname{I}\left(j\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall h \in Nat,\; \forall j \in Nat,\; \operatorname{Le}\left(h, j\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{EventuallySubset}\left(cofinite, \operatorname{I}\left(j\right), \operatorname{I}\left(h\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall k \in Nat,\; \forall h \in Nat,\; \forall j \in Nat,\; \left(\operatorname{active}\left(k, h\right) \land \left(\operatorname{active}\left(k, j\right) \land \operatorname{Le}\left(h, j\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{EventuallySubset}\left(cofinite, \operatorname{J}\left(k, j\right), \operatorname{J}\left(k, h\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall k \in Nat,\; \forall j \in Nat,\; \forall e \in Bool,\; \operatorname{active}\left(k, j\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{Infinite}\left(\operatorname{inter}\left(\operatorname{J}\left(k, j\right), \operatorname{N}\left(k, e\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\exists v \in Nat \to Nat \to Bool \to Nat,\; \exists Ilim \in \operatorname{Set}\left(Nat\right),\; \exists Jlim \in Nat \to \operatorname{Set}\left(Nat\right),\; \left(\forall j \in Nat,\; \forall k \in Nat,\; \forall e \in Bool,\; \operatorname{active}\left(k, j\right) \Rightarrow \left(\operatorname{Lt}\left(j, \operatorname{v}\left(j, k, e\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{Member}\left(\operatorname{v}\left(j, k, e\right), \operatorname{J}\left(k, j\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{Member}\left(\operatorname{v}\left(j, k, e\right), \operatorname{N}\left(k, e\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall h \in Nat,\; \operatorname{Le}\left(h, j\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{Member}\left(\operatorname{v}\left(j, k, e\right), \operatorname{I}\left(h\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall h \in Nat,\; \operatorname{Le}\left(h, j\right) \Rightarrow \left(\operatorname{active}\left(k, h\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{Member}\left(\operatorname{v}\left(j, k, e\right), \operatorname{J}\left(k, h\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall k \in Nat,\; \forall n \in Nat,\; \operatorname{Member}\left(n, \operatorname{Jlim}\left(k\right)\right) \Leftrightarrow \left(\exists j \in Nat,\; \exists e \in Bool,\; \operatorname{active}\left(k, j\right) \land n = \operatorname{v}\left(j, k, e\right)\right)\right) \land \left(Ilim = \operatorname{iUnion}\left(Jlim\right) \land \left(\left(\forall k \in Nat,\; \forall e \in Bool,\; \operatorname{Infinite}\left(\operatorname{inter}\left(\operatorname{Jlim}\left(k\right), \operatorname{N}\left(k, e\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall h \in Nat,\; \operatorname{EventuallySubset}\left(cofinite, Ilim, \operatorname{I}\left(h\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall k \in Nat,\; \forall h \in Nat,\; \operatorname{active}\left(k, h\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{EventuallySubset}\left(cofinite, \operatorname{Jlim}\left(k\right), \operatorname{J}\left(k, h\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)$$

*Commentary.*

For each active triple (j,k,e), the proof removes the finitely many earlier I and same-label J exceptions from the infinite pool J(k,j) intersect N(k,e), and also imposes j<n. Classical choice selects one witness. Inactive triples are not inserted into Jlim.

The lower bound j<v(j,k,e) makes every fixed Jlim(k) intersect N(k,e) infinitely often once k is eventually active. Values from blocks before a fixed h form a finite set because each block has finitely many active labels and two colours; all later values satisfy the earlier I and J constraints. This proves the stated one-sided cofinite inclusions.

The proper-limit manuscript application supplies active(k,j) from a cofinal sequence of proper stages, with N unchanged. The selector applies only at proper countable limits; no final-stage I, J or N bookkeeping is part of this target. This result does not supply the ordinal adapter, successor residual initialization, analytic F or G clauses, lift existence, or transfinite CH recursion. The final inverse-limit model and its analytic endpoints remain outside this supplier, as do the full Talagrand K_r/V_n construction and a solution of Problem 8.2.
