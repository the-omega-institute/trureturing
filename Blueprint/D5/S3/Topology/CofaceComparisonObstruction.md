*Commentary.*

Suppose the cofaces of a face s have at most d vertices and none is comparable by inclusion with every member of a finite family. At most d plus one minus the size of s of those requirements already rule out an extension. A requirement incomparable with the current face forces every extension to include their union. Each feasible enlargement raises the cardinality.
