# Finite marginals and ordered split geometry

## Abstract

Fixed finite signed marginals and exact clopen traces in ordered closed splits.

K is a compact Borel space. L is a Hausdorff Borel space and p is continuous. The measures are arbitrary real SignedMeasure values. A candidate and its reference have the same signed base projection and the same total variation mass as that base measure. The base variation vanishes outside the given clopen support. Each fixed Boolean coordinate eventually becomes constant on the whole base fibres in that support.

*Commentary.*

For the finite coordinate map, each clopen atom has the same signed mass for every admissible lift. The signed reference limit gives convergence on these finitely many atoms. *Commentary.*

The target is an arbitrary real signed measure nu on a compact Hausdorff zero-dimensional Borel space L. Its Jordan parts are regular, its variation vanishes off the clopen V, and its positive mass is at least one half. The continuous p maps K to L. For every lift preserving signed projection and total variation mass, the clopen exclusion R has variation mass at most one sixteenth.

Positive-clopen approximation gives a clopen B inside V with nu(B) greater than one third. Put H=p inverse(B) minus R. For every such signed lift t, its mass on p inverse(B) is nu(B), while t on the removed intersection is at most its variation on R. Thus t(H) is greater than one third minus one sixteenth, namely thirteen forty-eighths, which is greater than one sixth. The selected H is shared by all lifts. The output retains the clopen B, its containment in V, and the literal equation H=p inverse(B) minus R.
