*Commentary.*

The first projection has a section through a fixed face. The retraction survives every ordinary subdivision. A one-piece cover would contract the subdivided factor. Choosing a vertex in the image of each singleton face turns this order-map fence into a contiguity chain. In the subdivided triangular boundary, two distinct original vertices must stay fixed throughout that chain. The explicit two-piece cover at depth two therefore attains the minimum over all depths.
