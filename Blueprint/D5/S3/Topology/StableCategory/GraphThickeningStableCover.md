*Commentary.*

The poset consists of the faces of the cone on the edge subdivision of a finite simple graph, together with an extra triangular maximum over each two-edge path. The strict lower chains at an added maximum pass through one of the two side faces. The relative cover theorem extends the common cone-face region by folding towards one side on each piece. Both categorical lower sets lie in the same ordinary subdivision.
