*Commentary.*

Suppose a poset is covered by a family of a lower sets, each with an inclusion that factors through a poset covered by b categorical lower sets. Pull back each of those sets to its region and compose its contraction into the ambient poset. This gives a categorical lower cover by a times b sets.
