*Commentary.*

A finite alphabet of full chains contains every chain of the factor and supplies a section of the rank for each letter. A word then specifies a synchronized chain in the power. A monochromatic Hales–Jewett line forces one lower cover set to contain the image of the entire subdivided factor. Projection at any variable coordinate splits that embedding. A categorical contraction of the set would contract the subdivided factor.
