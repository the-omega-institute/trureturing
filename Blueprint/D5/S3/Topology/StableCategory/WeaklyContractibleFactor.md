*Commentary.*

Let an order map between nonempty finite posets have nullhomotopic order-complex realization. There is a finite poset with contractible order complex, an order embedding of the source into it, and an order map from it to the target. Their composite equals the given order map. The chain-maximum projection is a homotopy equivalence on realizations. A finite extension of the constant fence and its mapping cylinder agree on a common lower row, so the relative deformation retract glues to a contraction of the factor realization.
