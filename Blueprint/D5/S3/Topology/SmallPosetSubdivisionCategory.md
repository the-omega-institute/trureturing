*Commentary.*

A connected poset without beat points has at least two minimal points below each nonminimal point, with the dual property above. In a poset of at most eleven points with four or more maxima, this permits a partition into two categorical induced subposets. The exceptional four-intermediate-point case uses a pair sharing an upper bound and a complementary pair sharing a lower bound. The subdivision transfer turns the partition into two categorical opens. This supplies the connected weak-category-at-least-two part of Conjecture 6.1.

*Commentary.*

The five points give at least ten incidences with the upper extrema. One of at most four upper extrema lies above three of the points. The remaining pair shares a lower extremum among the at most three choices. The two common bounds give the categorical partition.

*Commentary.*

For a connected core with at least two maxima, the two-minimum case is categorical after two subdivisions. Otherwise at most seven nonmaximal points leave at most four intermediate points. If there are four, there are exactly three minima and at most seven maxima, so the common-bound partition applies.
