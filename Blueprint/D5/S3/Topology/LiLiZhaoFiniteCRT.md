**Definition 1.6 (Prime-tail subbasis and topology).**

Lean statements: `D5/S3/Topology/LiLiZhaoFiniteCRT.tailSubbasis`, `D5/S3/Topology/LiLiZhaoFiniteCRT.tailTopology`

*Formalization.* For a tail index `n`, the subbasis consists of residue classes modulo primes whose indices are at least `n`; `tailTopology n` is the topology generated by this subbasis.

**Theorem 1.7 (Tail topologies are Hausdorff).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Topology/LiLiZhaoFiniteCRT.tailTopology_t2`

*Proof.* For distinct natural numbers, choose a sufficiently large prime modulus. Their residue classes are disjoint open neighborhoods. ∎

**Theorem 1.8 (Common topology is T1 and irreducible).**

Lean statements: `D5/S3/Topology/LiLiZhaoFiniteCRT.commonTopology_t1`, `D5/S3/Topology/LiLiZhaoFiniteCRT.commonTopology_univ_irreducible`

*Proof.* The supremum topology is T1 because every tail topology is Hausdorff. Any two nonempty common-open sets contain finite cylinders whose prime supports can be separated into disjoint tails; the disjoint-support CRT theorem then gives a common point. ∎

**Theorem 1.9 (Failure of quasi-sobriety).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Topology/LiLiZhaoFiniteCRT.commonTopology_not_quasiSober`

*Proof.* The whole space is irreducible, while T1 makes every singleton closed and hence prevents a singleton generic point from being dense in the whole infinite space. ∎
