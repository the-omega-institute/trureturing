# Countdown Memory and an All-Time Noise Lower Bound

## Abstract

A finite countdown hides a long block of weak target memory from every noisy return observation.

*Commentary.*

The hidden state is supported on 0,...,J-1. One update shifts it by one coordinate and multiplies it by theta, discarding the last coordinate. The initial state is the first basis vector; output weight i is delta/theta^i. The target is defined by propagating this state and applying that readout, with no assumed eigenbasis.

*Commentary.*

For every natural J,n and every nonzero real theta, the actual readout is delta when n<J and zero otherwise. The proof first computes the whole hidden orbit by induction, then evaluates its finite readout. Repeated eigenvalues and zero coupling are included. The theorem does not claim a stochastic or operator-norm bound for arbitrary parameters.

**Theorem 1.6 (No estimator can resolve the two targets below J delta / 2).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Analytic/GoldenTomography/CountdownMemoryNoiseLowerBound.countdown_noise_lower_bound`

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Analytic/GoldenTomography/CountdownMemoryNoiseLowerBound.countdown_noise_lower_bound` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Assume delta>=0, theta nonzero, and J*delta<=1/2. There exists one observation sequence within delta of both true return responses at every natural time. For every estimated target sequence f, the larger of its two prefix losses is at least J*delta/2.

Strong induction on the actual renewal recurrence proves that all positive-time responses lie between zero and 2*delta. The common data are the midpoint of the responses. A coefficientwise triangle inequality, summed over the J actual taps, gives the minimax obstruction. The all-time quantifier makes additional observations at the same worst-case precision insufficient to remove it.

Here J ranges over arbitrary natural values subject to the stated hypothesis J*delta<=1/2. The theorem lower-bounds the first-J absolute (l1) loss, and also any full-memory loss that dominates that prefix. It makes no claim about losses outside those scopes.

A killed Markov realization, a similarity realization with strictly contractive full Hilbert dynamics, and matching asymptotic upper and lower rates are outside this module and are not established here.
