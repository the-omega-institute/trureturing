*Commentary.*

For any sequence of natural capacities and any total row of circle points, continuity of the associated character at one finite capacity state implies that the sum of each capacity times the absolute centered representative is finite. A finite coordinate neighborhood controls all admissible tail states. Single-coordinate multiples and separate positive and negative sums then bound the tail mass without assigning a group structure to the capacity states.
