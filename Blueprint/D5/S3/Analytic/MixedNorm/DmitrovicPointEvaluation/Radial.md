# Radial

## Abstract

Mixed norms and power-family point evaluations.

**Definition 1.1 (The radial norm integral).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Analytic/MixedNorm/DmitrovicPointEvaluation/Radial.radialIntegral`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Analytic/MixedNorm/DmitrovicPointEvaluation/Radial.radialIntegral` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For finite positive p and q, this real interval integral is the q-th power of the power-family mixed norm. Its parameter t is the square of the affine parameter a. The weight may have an integrable singularity at radius one.

**Definition 1.2 (The normalized evaluation power).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Analytic/MixedNorm/DmitrovicPointEvaluation/Radial.evaluationExcess`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Analytic/MixedNorm/DmitrovicPointEvaluation/Radial.evaluationExcess` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Put c=1/(1+alpha p). The expression (1+ct) raised to 2q/p times (1-c squared t) raised to alpha q+q/p is the q-th power of the normalized evaluation at ca, where t=a squared.
