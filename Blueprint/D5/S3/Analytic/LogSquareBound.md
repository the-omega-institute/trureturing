# Squared logarithm bound

## Abstract

A power majorant controls the logarithmic singularity and growth.

**Theorem 1.1 (A global positive-half-line majorant).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Analytic/LogSquareBound.log_square_bound`

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Analytic/LogSquareBound.log_square_bound` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Commentary.*

For every positive real x, log(x)^2 is at most 16*x^(-1/2)+x^2. Below one the reciprocal power controls the logarithmic singularity; above one the square controls its growth.
