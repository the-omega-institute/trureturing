*Commentary.*

For a nontrivial character of a cyclotomic Galois extension L/K with m modulo four unequal to two, there is a function analytic on the half-plane Re(s) > 1 - 1/[K:Q]. On Re(s) > 1 it agrees with the Dirichlet series weighted by the character on nonzero ideals of K.
