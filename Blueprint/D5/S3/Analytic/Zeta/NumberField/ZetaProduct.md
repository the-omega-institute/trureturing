*Commentary.*

For a finite abelian cyclotomic Galois extension L/K of modulus m with m ≥ 1 and m % 4 ≠ 2, let χ be a nontrivial Galois character. Every function F analytic on Re(s) > 1 - 1/[K:ℚ] that agrees on Re(s) > 1 with the χ-weighted nonzero-ideal Dirichlet series satisfies F(1) ≠ 0.
