*Commentary.*

The coprime-norm-unramified prime sum is asymptotic to log(1/(s-1)): it differs from the universal prime sum (primeIdealZetaSum_univ_tendsto_log) by the finitely many excluded primes — ramified or with norm not coprime to m — whose bounded contribution is negligible against log → ∞.
