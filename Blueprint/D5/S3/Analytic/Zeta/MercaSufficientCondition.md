*Commentary.*

For every admitted Merca prime greater than five, take the exact rational quotient of the genuine quartic and real quadratic ideal class numbers. If its natural integer representative is a square and no prime divisor is one modulo four, the absolute normalized Bernoulli coordinates agree. A prime-divisor descent proves the sum-of-two-squares step, including the case of relative class number one. A square alone is not asserted sufficient.
