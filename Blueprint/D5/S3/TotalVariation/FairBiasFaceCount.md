# Counting the faces of the fair-bias link

## Abstract

The signed fair-bias complex has explicit face counts and a Tutte evaluation of its reduced Euler characteristic.

**Theorem 1.1 (A support of size k gives two to the k signed faces).**

*Commentary.*

Take a finite labelled family of columns in an additive commutative group and a positive integer k. The number of faces with k signed labels is two to the power k times the number of k-element sets of labels whose complementary columns generate the subgroup generated by all columns. For each allowed support, a sign function recovers the face by attaching exactly one sign to every label.

**Theorem 1.2 (The reduced Euler characteristic is a Tutte evaluation).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/TotalVariation/FairBiasFaceCount.reducedEuler_eq_tutte`

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/TotalVariation/FairBiasFaceCount.reducedEuler_eq_tutte` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For a family of n labelled columns in a finite vector space over the binary field, let h be the rank of all columns. The reduced Euler characteristic of the signed complex is minus the product of negative two raised to the power n minus h and the rank-subset Tutte polynomial of the original columns evaluated at one and one half. The empty face contributes minus one. The evaluation at first coordinate one retains precisely the subsets whose columns span the full column space.
