# The local link of the fair-bias coordinates

## Abstract

Signed faces describe the local directions that retain a spanning set of fair inputs.

*Commentary.*

Fix a finite labelled family of columns in an additive commutative group. The link consists of real coordinate vectors whose absolute values sum to one and whose zero coordinates still index a generating family for the subgroup generated by all columns. A nonempty face of the signed complex uses at most one sign at each label and leaves a generating family at the unused labels. There is a homeomorphism from the link to the barycentric realization that sends a coordinate to its positive and negative parts. Taking the difference of the two weights recovers each coordinate, and the nonzero weights record its sign. The result includes the empty link.
