# Elementary collapses in the fair-bias shelling

## Abstract

A shelling step with a nonempty proper restriction face collapses to the preceding prefix.

*Commentary.*

Start with the subcomplex generated by a facet B and all earlier facets of the signed fair-bias complex. Suppose the restriction face at B is nonempty. Fix a vertex v of B outside it. List every new face avoiding v exactly once, in weakly decreasing cardinality. There is a sequence of elementary collapses onto the subcomplex of earlier facets whose lower faces occur in exactly that list and whose upper faces adjoin v. Every other strict coface of a scheduled lower face belongs to a pair with a larger lower face, so it has already been deleted.
