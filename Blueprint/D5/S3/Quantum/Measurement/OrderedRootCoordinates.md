# Ordered Root Coordinates

## Abstract

Ordered roots describe the exact angular incompatibility interval.

**Definition 1.1 (Qubit parameter body).**

$$V = \{p: (\mathbb{R} \times \operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(3\right)\right)) \mid \left|(p).1\right| + \left\lVert (p).2 \right\rVert \le 1\}$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.V` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Lin Zhang; Hua Xiang; Xiao Li-Jost; Shao-Ming Fei (2019). *Incompatibility probability of random quantum measurements*. DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevE.100.062139](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.100.062139). URL: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.12321>.

*Commentary.*

Section III, p. 3, uses V = {(x₀, x) ∈ R⁴ : |x₀| + |x| ≤ 1}. The vector carrier is EuclideanSpace R (Fin 3); the first component is the bias.

**Definition 1.2 (Yu function).**

$$\forall x : \mathbb{R}, \forall a : \operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(3\right)\right), \operatorname{hYu}\left(x, a\right) = \frac{\operatorname{Real.sqrt}\left((1 + x)^{2} - (\left\lVert a \right\rVert)^{2}\right) + \operatorname{Real.sqrt}\left((1 - x)^{2} - (\left\lVert a \right\rVert)^{2}\right)}{2}$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.hYu` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Sixia Yu; Nai-le Liu; Li Li; C. H. Oh (2010). *Joint measurement of two unsharp observables of a qubit*. DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevA.81.062116](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.81.062116). URL: <https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.1538>.

*Commentary.*

Yu, Liu, Li and Oh, Eq. (5), p. 2, and Zhang et al., Appendix C, use squares on 1 ± x under the square roots.

**Definition 1.3 (Incompatible measurement pairs).**

$$NJM = \{q: ((\mathbb{R} \times \operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(3\right)\right)) \times (\mathbb{R} \times \operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(3\right)\right))) \mid ((q).1 \in V) \land (((q).2 \in V) \land ((\operatorname{inner}\left(\mathbb{R}, ((q).1).2, ((q).2).2\right) - (((q).1).1) \cdot (((q).2).1))^{2} < (1 - (\operatorname{hYu}\left(((q).1).1, ((q).1).2\right))^{2} - (\operatorname{hYu}\left(((q).2).1, ((q).2).2\right))^{2}) \cdot (1 - \frac{(((q).1).1)^{2}}{(\operatorname{hYu}\left(((q).1).1, ((q).1).2\right))^{2}} - \frac{(((q).2).1)^{2}}{(\operatorname{hYu}\left(((q).2).1, ((q).2).2\right))^{2}})))\}$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.NJM` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Lin Zhang; Hua Xiang; Xiao Li-Jost; Shao-Ming Fei (2019). *Incompatibility probability of random quantum measurements*. DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevE.100.062139](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.100.062139). URL: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.12321>.

*Commentary.*

Section III.C, p. 5: “can be identified as the set that Eq. (4) is violated.” Both points belong to V, and the strict inequality is the violation of the joint-measurability criterion.

**Definition 1.4 (Incompatibility probability).**

$$incompProb = \frac{\operatorname{ENNReal.toReal}\left(\operatorname{MeasureTheory.volume}\left(NJM\right)\right)}{(\operatorname{ENNReal.toReal}\left(\operatorname{MeasureTheory.volume}\left(V\right)\right))^{2}}$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.incompProb` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Lin Zhang; Hua Xiang; Xiao Li-Jost; Shao-Ming Fei (2019). *Incompatibility probability of random quantum measurements*. DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevE.100.062139](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.100.062139). URL: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.12321>.

*Commentary.*

Section III.C, p. 5, defines the probability as the incompatible volume divided by the volume of the full parameter body V × V. All volumes are product Lebesgue volumes.

**Definition 1.5 (One-quarter conjecture).**

$$(claim) \Leftrightarrow (incompProb = \frac{1}{4})$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.claim` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Lin Zhang; Hua Xiang; Xiao Li-Jost; Shao-Ming Fei (2019). *Incompatibility probability of random quantum measurements*. DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevE.100.062139](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.100.062139). URL: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.12321>.

*Commentary.*

Section III.C, PDF p. 5: We *conjecture* that the incompatibility probability of a pair of random qubit measurements $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$, generated by (a₀, 𝒂) and (b₀, 𝒃) in Eq. (4), is given by 𝐏𝐫[Θᴺᴶᴹ₂,₂] = $\frac{1}{4}$. Here claim says that the real-valued probability equals one quarter.

**Definition 1.6 (Positive biases).**

$$positiveSector = \operatorname{Set.inter}\left(NJM, \{q: ((\mathbb{R} \times \operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(3\right)\right)) \times (\mathbb{R} \times \operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(3\right)\right))) \mid (0 < ((q).1).1) \land (0 < ((q).2).1)\}\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.positiveSector` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Both bias coordinates are strictly positive.

**Definition 1.11 (Bias coordinate).**

$$\forall u : \mathbb{R}, \forall v : \mathbb{R}, \operatorname{xCoord}\left(u, v\right) = (\operatorname{Real.sqrt}\left(u\right)) \cdot (\operatorname{Real.sqrt}\left(v\right))$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.xCoord` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The bias coordinate is sqrt(u) times sqrt(v).

**Definition 1.12 (Radius coordinate).**

$$\forall u : \mathbb{R}, \forall v : \mathbb{R}, \operatorname{rCoord}\left(u, v\right) = (\operatorname{Real.sqrt}\left(1 - u\right)) \cdot (\operatorname{Real.sqrt}\left(1 - v\right))$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.rCoord` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The radius is sqrt(1-u) times sqrt(1-v).

**Theorem 1.13 (Criterion coordinates).**

$$\forall u : \mathbb{R}, \forall v : \mathbb{R}, ((0 \le v) \land ((v \le u) \land (u \le 1))) \Rightarrow ((0 < u) \Rightarrow (((\operatorname{hYu}\left(\operatorname{xCoord}\left(u, v\right), \operatorname{PiLp.single}\left(2, 0, \operatorname{rCoord}\left(u, v\right)\right)\right))^{2} = u) \land (\frac{(\operatorname{xCoord}\left(u, v\right))^{2}}{(\operatorname{hYu}\left(\operatorname{xCoord}\left(u, v\right), \operatorname{PiLp.single}\left(2, 0, \operatorname{rCoord}\left(u, v\right)\right)\right))^{2}} = v)))$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.root_coordinates` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For ordered roots, the squared Yu function is u and the squared bias divided by u is v.

**Theorem 1.14 (Exact angular interval volume).**

$$\forall u : \mathbb{R}, \forall v : \mathbb{R}, \forall U : \mathbb{R}, \forall V : \mathbb{R}, ((0 \le v) \land ((v \le u) \land (u \le 1))) \Rightarrow (((0 \le V) \land ((V \le U) \land (U \le 1))) \Rightarrow (\operatorname{MeasureTheory.volume}\left(\{s: \mathbb{R} \mid (-1 \le s) \land ((s \le 1) \land ((((\operatorname{rCoord}\left(u, v\right)) \cdot (\operatorname{rCoord}\left(U, V\right))) \cdot (s) - (\operatorname{xCoord}\left(u, v\right)) \cdot (\operatorname{xCoord}\left(U, V\right)))^{2} < (1 - u - U) \cdot (1 - v - V)))\}\right) = \operatorname{ite}\left(0 < 1 - u - U, \operatorname{ENNReal.ofReal}\left(\frac{(2) \cdot (\operatorname{Real.sqrt}\left((1 - u - U) \cdot (1 - v - V)\right))}{(\operatorname{rCoord}\left(u, v\right)) \cdot (\operatorname{rCoord}\left(U, V\right))}\right), 0\right)))$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.angular_volume_ordered` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Only 1-u-U > 0 contributes. In that region the incompatible cosine interval lies entirely in [-1,1].

**Definition 1.15 (Ordered-root map).**

$$\forall p : (\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}), \operatorname{coordinateMap}\left(p\right) = (\operatorname{xCoord}\left((p).1, (p).2\right), \operatorname{rCoord}\left((p).1, (p).2\right))$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.coordinateMap` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The two coordinates are the bias and radius.

**Definition 1.16 (Strictly ordered roots).**

$$rootDomain = \{p: (\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}) \mid (0 < (p).2) \land (((p).2 < (p).1) \land ((p).1 < 1))\}$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.rootDomain` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The roots satisfy 0 < v < u < 1.

**Definition 1.17 (Strict radial body).**

$$radialBody = \{p: (\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}) \mid (0 < (p).1) \land ((0 < (p).2) \land ((p).1 + (p).2 < 1))\}$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.radialBody` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The bias and radius are positive and their sum is less than one.

**Theorem 1.19 (Ordered-root change of variables).**

$$\forall f : (\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}) \to ENNReal, \int^{-}_{p: (\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}) \in radialBody} (f\left(p\right)) d\operatorname{MeasureTheory.volume} = \int^{-}_{p: (\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}) \in rootDomain} ((\operatorname{ENNReal.ofReal}\left(\frac{(p).1 - (p).2}{((4) \cdot (\operatorname{xCoord}\left((p).1, (p).2\right))) \cdot (\operatorname{rCoord}\left((p).1, (p).2\right))}\right)) \cdot (f\left(\operatorname{coordinateMap}\left(p\right)\right))) d\operatorname{MeasureTheory.volume}$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Quantum/Measurement/OrderedRootCoordinates.coordinate_change_lintegral` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The absolute Jacobian is (u-v)/(4 xCoord(u,v) rCoord(u,v)). The identity holds for nonnegative extended-real integrands.
