*Commentary.*

For an input P/r expressed through a frame V, the pinched projection decomposes into its fiber compressions. These blocks are V_jV_j† or their complements. Rectangular spectral transport compares them with V_j†V_j. Preservation of trace cancels the scaling terms involving log r, leaving the direct or reflected spectral sum divided by the original rank r.
