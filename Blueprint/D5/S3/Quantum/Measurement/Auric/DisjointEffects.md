*Commentary.*

For distinct labels i, k, a, assume capacity m_a covers the working dimension and m_i + m_k is at most that dimension. The trace data lie between zero and their capacities and sum to the dimension. Both objectives attain m_i h₂(t_i/m_i) + m_k h₂(t_k/m_k). Flat factors and diagonal Jensen bound every tuple. Two disjoint coordinate subspaces give a common attaining tuple.
