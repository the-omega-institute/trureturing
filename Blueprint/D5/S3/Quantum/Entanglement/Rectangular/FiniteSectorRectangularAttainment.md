*Commentary.*

For a real or complex finite-dimensional linear map between possibly unequal dimensions, every natural k at most both dimensions admits orthonormal source and target k-families attaining the real paired trace. The original Gram eigenbasis provides the source family; normalization on the positive singular directions and orthonormal extension provide the target family. The empty family remains legal. This is the original attainment argument used by sector_pair, with no additional completeness or nontriviality assumptions.
