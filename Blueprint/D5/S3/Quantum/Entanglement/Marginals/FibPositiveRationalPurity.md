*Commentary.*

Assume the edge data are rational and admit a pure common extension at positive middle occupation. If either coupling to a singly occupied endpoint is nonzero, that edge data force the hidden coupling to be rational. The pure extension then has rational entries.

If both couplings vanish, the marginal equations constrain only the squared modulus of the hidden coupling. It equals the middle occupation times the occupied diagonal entry of the left endpoint block. A rational pure extension exists exactly when that number is a sum of two rational squares.
