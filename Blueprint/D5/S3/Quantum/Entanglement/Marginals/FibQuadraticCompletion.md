*Commentary.*

Suppose the middle occupation is positive, the two endpoint blocks and three observed couplings have rational real and imaginary parts, and a compatible trace-one positive semidefinite extension exists. There is a nonnegative rational d, an unobserved coupling, and a trace-one positive semidefinite extension matching all these data. Its rank is at most three, and all its entries belong to the field generated by the Gaussian rationals and the real square root of d.

Start at a rational point of the two residual disks and extend its horizontal slice to the right endpoint of the intersection. That endpoint is one of the two individual slice endpoints, each given by a rational offset and a single square root of a nonnegative rational. A boundary equality makes at least one positive semidefinite Schur residual singular.

For positive residual trace the normalized tensor product has rank at most two since one factor has rank at most one. At zero trace both residuals vanish. Restoring the scalar Schur pivot adds one to the rank and uses operations within the same field.
