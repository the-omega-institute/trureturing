# FiveRowP1

## Abstract

The full five-row scalar upper bound in dimensions five through eighteen.

**Theorem 1.1 (All dimensions five through eighteen, both branches, every real j).**

*Commentary.*

For n from 5 through 18 and every real j in [10,5*n-15], the stated scalar hypotheses imply energy <= ((5*n+8)/(5*n+2)^2) times squared mass. Both tail branches are covered for each N=5*n. Deriving these hypotheses from literal APPT, assigning the spectral variables, and transferring the scalar result to the physical theorem require separate arguments.
