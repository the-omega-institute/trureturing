# ReflectionEvaluation

## Abstract

Polynomial evaluation and nonnegativity for the four-qubit entropy certificate.

The generic square law ref_eval_square_nonneg proves nonnegativity for any polynomial with a nonnegative integer weight. The evaluations ref_eval_m0 through ref_eval_m47 use literalMinor at the corresponding indices. The resulting identities ref_eval_lhs and ref_eval_rhs_nonneg connect the certificate to the marginal third elementary symmetric sums.
