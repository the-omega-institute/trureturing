# Standard concrete coefficient series

## Abstract

Actual square-summable coefficient series equal the norm-closed complex coefficient span in the inherited continuous-dual norm.

H is any complete complex Hilbert space and M is an actual concrete von Neumann algebra on H. No separability, countable dimension or nonzero-space assumption is imposed. The functional norm is the norm inherited from StrongDual C M.toStarSubalgebra. E denotes ConcreteCoefficientPredual.coefficientSpace M, already defined as the norm closure of the complex span of restricted vector coefficients.
