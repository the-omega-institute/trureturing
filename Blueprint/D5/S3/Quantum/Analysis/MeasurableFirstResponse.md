## Abstract

The original vectorwise measurable evolution has an actual Bochner first response, sharp local exponential remainder and signed derivative uniform on the closed time triangle.

Let E be a complex Banach space in any universe, including the trivial space. S is the original NonautonomousDysonPhillips.Contraction E: a strongly continuous contraction semigroup on nonnegative real times with identity at zero. Fix a real T>=0. For each real s, B(s) and H(s) are bounded complex-linear endomorphisms of E. For each fixed vector z separately, s maps to B(s)z and to H(s)z are AE strongly measurable for real Lebesgue measure restricted to Icc(0,T). The scalar functions g,h are nonnegative and integrable on this interval and dominate the operator norms pointwise there.

Write U_Q(t,r)=MeasurableNonautonomousDysonPhillips.measurableEvolution(S,Q,t,r), the SAME native realization of the original ordered vector Bochner series. Write G_k(r,t)=the actual interval integral of k from r to t. Define V(B,H,t,r,x)=response(S,B,H,t,r,x) as the actual interval Bochner integral from r to t of U_B(t,s)H(s)U_B(s,r)x with respect to real Lebesgue measure. These definitions introduce neither a new evolution nor an assumed response formula.

*Commentary.*
