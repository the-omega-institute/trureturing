*Commentary.*

The coordinate space is EuclideanSpace Real (Fin 3), identified with triples (x,y,z). Its measure is raw Lebesgue volume, and State is the complex L2 space for this measure. The frame consists, in order, of U = partial_x - (y/2) partial_z, V = partial_y + (x/2) partial_z, and Z = partial_z. The derivatives are real directional derivatives of complex functions.

In the formula, Space, State, Components and Ambient denote exactly EuclideanSpace Real (Fin 3), Lp Complex 2 volume, Fin 3 to State, and State times Components. The indices 0, 1 and 2 select U, V and Z. fst and snd are the product projections; rep(v) is the function coercion (v : Space to Complex) of an L2 class. axis(i) is EuclideanSpace.single i 1. The expression fderiv(Real,f,x,b) means the real Frechet derivative fderiv Real f x applied to b, and conj is complex conjugation.

compactCore consists of precisely the compactly supported complex Schwartz functions. Every complex smooth compactly supported function gives such a Schwartz function through HasCompactSupport.toSchwartzMap, with the same values and derivatives. coreJet records its L2 state together with all three L2 field derivatives. jointGraph is the topological closure of the range of this one linear map in the product of the state space and the three component spaces. Thus one approximating function supplies all four coordinates simultaneously. No component is discarded and no replacement by isotropic Euclidean H1 is made.

More explicitly, field(i) acts on a Schwartz function by fderiv Real in the direction frame(i,x).
