# The two original masses and the same readout twice

## Abstract

Strictly positive factorization of the actual masses precedes original scale calibration.

All mathematical definitions below use precisely source (79.2): K(a)=pL(omega,a), M(a,b)=pL(K(a),b), Q(a,b,c)=L(M(a,b),c). The normalization functionals are the original uA,uD,uE. The actual M and L spans imply that the actual Q spans E, by linear extension in its D input. The completed mass theorem is the pre-classification corollary (79.8), independent of Jordan coordinates. Positive constants are obtained from cone positivity and nonzero original mass functionals; they are not extra hypotheses. The scale theorem is an explicitly conditional successor using the source full recovery law, whose derivation from complete PRE is not supplied here. Neither it nor the mass theorem asserts Cjoint, common orientation, H13, event CP, physical resets, physical tensor copies, a time group or the complete source 81.8 quantum candidate. These applications and their proof organization are repository-derived; no novelty or independent-review claim is made.

*Commentary.*

This is Corollary 79.4 for arbitrary nonzero finite-dimensional real A,D,E, closed generating cones and their strictly positive original masses. L is positive on the entire original D cone times the entire A cone, p is positive on the entire original E cone, and omega is positive. The actual M spans D and L spans E over the reals. Phi is positive and mass preserving and has no real fixed dual functional outside the line of uA. The two identities hold on every positive input, including zero and unequal masses, with weights (t,1-t) and (s*t,s*(1-t),1-s). Nothing assumes either mass factorization. The proof applies the new arbitrary-slot maximum principle to the actual two- and three-linear masses, extends to all real inputs, and obtains strict positivity from positive normalized states and real spanning. Real span is never identified with positive generation of an entangled cone.

*Commentary.*

This successor derives both mass factorizations from the same original cone data and positive asynchronous laws. Its additional assumptions are the original omega of mass one and the full real recovery identity p(Q(a,b,c))=uA(a)*M(b,c). No factorization or fixed-tensor conclusion is assumed. Span extension gives uD composed with p=(lambda/kappa)*uE and uE(L(d,c))=(kappa/lambda)*uD(d)*uA(c). Their composition calibrates pL. Applying that calibration twice to the original omega gives lambda=1. The theorem does not prove the PRE-to-recovery bridge, the pre-C13 classification, the Cjoint equivalence, the original scalar anchor, H13, or the whole quantum consumer. It is a conditional successor to the complete mass-factorization theorem, not a claim of completion of source Theorem 79.5.
