# Noncommuting fractional powers without a spectral gap

## Abstract

Fractional powers are uniformly Holder continuous on the positive cone.

**Theorem 1.1 (Dimension-free positive-cone estimate).**

$$\forall A \in OrderedUnitalComplexCStarAlgebra, a \in A, b \in A, p \in Real,\; \left(0 \le a \land \left(0 \le b \land \left(0 < p \land p \le 1\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{norm}\left(\operatorname{subtract}\left(\operatorname{rpow}\left(a, p\right), \operatorname{rpow}\left(b, p\right)\right)\right) \le \operatorname{rpow}\left(\operatorname{norm}\left(\operatorname{subtract}\left(a, b\right)\right), p\right)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Quantum/Analysis/FractionalPowerHolder.norm_rpow_sub_rpow_le` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Citation.* Fuad Kittaneh; Hideki Kosaki (1987). *Inequalities for the Schatten p-norm V*. DOI: [10.2977/PRIMS/1195176547](https://doi.org/10.2977/PRIMS/1195176547). URL: <https://ems.press/journals/prims/articles/3346>.

*Commentary.*

For every ordered unital complex C-star algebra, positive a and b, and real exponent 0<p<=1, the norm of a^p-b^p is at most the pth power of the norm of a-b. The order is the C-star positive-cone order. The inputs need not commute or be invertible. The algebra may be trivial and no dimension or positive lower spectral bound enters the estimate.

Set delta=norm(a-b). Self-adjoint norm bounds give a<=b+delta I and b<=a+delta I. Operator monotonicity compares their pth powers. For each positive x and t>=0, scalar subadditivity on the spectrum of x and continuous functional calculus give (x+t I)^p<=x^p+t^p I. This step takes place in the commutative algebra generated by x and the identity. The two comparisons bound a^p-b^p between plus and minus delta^p I, so its norm is at most delta^p. Continuity of the scalar power at zero retains zero spectral values.

Kittaneh and Kosaki, Theorem 2.3, prove the corresponding operator-norm inequality for operator-monotone functions continuous at zero and vanishing there, on arbitrary Hilbert spaces. Fractional powers are a classical special case. The present statement uses an abstract unital C-star algebra and proves the estimate from the pinned functional-calculus and operator-monotonicity results. No mathematical novelty is claimed.

Let rho be a positive trace-class operator of trace one on an arbitrary complex Hilbert space. Let P project onto finitely many vectors of a full Hilbert basis, and let q be the discarded diagonal mass. The existing TraceClassCompression.norm_sub_compress_le gives trace-norm error at most 2 sqrt(q), and opNorm_le_traceNorm bounds the operator norm by that trace norm. Applying the positive-cone estimate in the bounded-operator C-star algebra, followed by scalar monotonicity, gives norm(rho^p-(P rho P)^p)<=(2 sqrt(q))^p for 0<p<=1.

These powers are bounded operators on the original Hilbert space. The power rho^p need not be trace class when p<1. The compressed power is (P rho P)^p and no equality with P rho^p P is asserted. At p=1/2 the bound is sqrt(2) q^(1/4). Empty finite spans and zero discarded mass remain allowed. This is a consequence of the two cited trace-class estimates and the positive-cone theorem above.
