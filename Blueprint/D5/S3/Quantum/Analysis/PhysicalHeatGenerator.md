# Physical Oscillator Heat Generator

## Abstract

Positive complex heat contractions have full right generator minus the actual Schwartz oscillator closure.

**Theorem 1.1 (Heat on the actual Euclidean complex L2 space).**

$$\forall d \in Nat, hbar \in \mathbb{R}, m \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right) \to \mathbb{R}, omega \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right) \to \mathbb{R},\; \left(\left(0 < hbar \land \left(\forall j \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right),\; 0 < m\left(j\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall j \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right),\; 0 < omega\left(j\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\exists e \in \operatorname{HilbertBasis}\left(\operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right) \to Nat, \mathbb{C}, \operatorname{Lp}\left(\mathbb{C}, 2, \operatorname{volume}\left(\operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right)\right)\right)\right)\right), T \in \operatorname{NNReal}\left(\right) \to \operatorname{ContinuousLinearMap}\left(\mathbb{C}, \operatorname{Lp}\left(\mathbb{C}, 2, \operatorname{volume}\left(\operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right)\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{Lp}\left(\mathbb{C}, 2, \operatorname{volume}\left(\operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right)\right)\right)\right)\right), S \in \operatorname{StronglyContinuousContractionSemigroup}\left(\operatorname{Lp}\left(\mathbb{C}, 2, \operatorname{volume}\left(\operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right)\right)\right)\right)\right),\; \left(\left(\left(\left(\left(\left(\forall n \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right) \to Nat,\; \operatorname{EventuallyEq}\left(\operatorname{volume}\left(\operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{representative}\left(e\left(n\right)\right), \operatorname{physicalHermite}\left(d, hbar, m, omega, n\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall t \in \operatorname{NNReal}\left(\right),\; S\left(t\right) = \operatorname{restrictScalars}\left(\mathbb{R}, T\left(t\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall t \in \operatorname{NNReal}\left(\right),\; \forall f \in \operatorname{Lp}\left(\mathbb{C}, 2, \operatorname{volume}\left(\operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right)\right)\right)\right),\; \forall n \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right) \to Nat,\; \operatorname{coefficient}\left(e, T\left(t, f\right), n\right) = \operatorname{mul}\left(\operatorname{ofReal}\left(\operatorname{exp}\left(\operatorname{neg}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(\operatorname{toReal}\left(t\right), \operatorname{sum}\left((j: \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right) \mapsto \operatorname{mul}\left(hbar, omega\left(j\right), \operatorname{add}\left(\operatorname{toReal}\left(n\left(j\right)\right), \frac{1}{2}\right)\right))\right)\right)\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{coefficient}\left(e, f, n\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall t \in \operatorname{NNReal}\left(\right),\; \operatorname{IsPositive}\left(T\left(t\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall f \in \operatorname{Lp}\left(\mathbb{C}, 2, \operatorname{volume}\left(\operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right)\right)\right)\right),\; \forall v \in \operatorname{Lp}\left(\mathbb{C}, 2, \operatorname{volume}\left(\operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right)\right)\right)\right),\; \operatorname{Tendsto}\left((t: \operatorname{NNReal}\left(\right) \mapsto \operatorname{smul}\left(\operatorname{inv}\left(\operatorname{toReal}\left(t\right)\right), \operatorname{sub}\left(T\left(t, f\right), f\right)\right)), \operatorname{nhdsWithin}\left(0, \operatorname{Ioi}\left(0\right)\right), \operatorname{nhds}\left(v\right)\right) \Leftrightarrow \operatorname{Member}\left(\operatorname{Pair}\left(f, \operatorname{neg}\left(v\right)\right), \operatorname{graph}\left(\operatorname{closure}\left(\operatorname{schwartzOperator}\left(d, (j: \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right) \mapsto \frac{\operatorname{pow}\left(hbar, 2\right)}{\operatorname{mul}\left(2, m\left(j\right)\right)}), (j: \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right) \mapsto \frac{\operatorname{mul}\left(m\left(j\right), \operatorname{pow}\left(omega\left(j\right), 2\right)\right)}{2})\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall R \in \operatorname{StronglyContinuousContractionSemigroup}\left(\operatorname{Lp}\left(\mathbb{C}, 2, \operatorname{volume}\left(\operatorname{EuclideanSpace}\left(\mathbb{R}, \operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right)\right)\right)\right)\right),\; *Commentary.*

For every natural dimension, positive hbar and positive coordinate masses and frequencies, K is the closure of the Schwartz operator whose j term is minus hbar squared over twice the mass times the second coordinate derivative, plus one half mass times frequency squared times position squared. The theorem constructs a Hilbert basis represented almost everywhere by the normalized physical product Hermites, a family T of bounded complex linear operators, and a strongly continuous contraction semigroup S on the underlying real space. S is exactly the restriction of scalars of T.

At every nonnegative time the coefficient is exp of minus time times the energy, where the energy is the sum of hbar times frequency times occupation plus one half. Each bounded operator is positive in Mathlib’s sense, including symmetry and a nonnegative quadratic form. The full right difference quotient tends to v if and only if the pair consisting of f and minus v belongs to the graph of K. Any strongly continuous contraction semigroup with that same full generator graph equals S.

The multiplier bound and finite Hermite approximants establish strong continuity. Existing physical graph-core approximants enter the source-selected closed-generator theorem imported from ContractionSemigroup. Conversely, continuous coordinate maps identify every coefficient of any asserted derivative. Physical basis equality is discharged inside the proof from the frozen orthonormality, totality and Schwartz tests.
