# Measurable coefficients and the integrable Dyson–Phillips clock

## Abstract

Vectorwise almost everywhere strongly measurable bounded-operator coefficients, controlled by any nonnegative integrable scalar function, generate the original nonautonomous Dyson–Phillips evolution on arbitrary complex Banach spaces.

Let E be a complex normed space, in an arbitrary universe. The evolution and series assertions assume E is complete; the zero space is included. Let S be the original NonautonomousDysonPhillips.Contraction: a strongly continuous semigroup of bounded complex-linear contractions on nonnegative real times, with identity at zero. Write A(t)=S.action(t)=S.operator(Real.toNNReal(t)). All integrals use real Lebesgue measure volume and are vector Bochner integrals unless their integrand is real. No continuity of A in operator norm is assumed.

For real r<=H, write I(r,H)=Icc(r,H) and mu(r,H)=volume restricted to I(r,H). The condition Meas(B,r,H) means that s maps to B(s)x is AE strongly measurable for mu(r,H), separately for every x in E. Control(B,g,r,H) means that g is real and IntegrableOn I(r,H), and that 0<=g(s) and norm(B(s))<=g(s) at every s in that closed interval. Neither condition asserts operator-valued measurability of B. The clock G(g,r,t) is the scalar interval integral of g from r to t.
