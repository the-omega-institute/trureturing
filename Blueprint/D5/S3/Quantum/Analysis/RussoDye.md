# RussoDye

## Abstract

Triangle equality and norm scale in complex C-star algebras.

**Theorem 1.1 (closedConvexHull unitary).**

$$\forall A \in \operatorname{Type}\left(\right),\; [\operatorname{CStarAlgebra}\left(A\right)] \operatorname{closedConvexHull}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right), (\operatorname{unitary}\left(A\right)):\operatorname{Set}\left(A\right)\right) = \operatorname{Metric}.\operatorname{closedBall}\left(0:A, 1\right)$$

*Commentary.*

The Russo-Dye theorem identifies the closed real convex hull of the unitaries with the closed unit ball of a unital complex C-star algebra, including the zero algebra.
