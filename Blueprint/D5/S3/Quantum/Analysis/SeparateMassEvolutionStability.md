# Separate scalar masses and strong stability of the original evolution

## Abstract

Strong L1 convergence on each fixed vector, under separate integrable scalar controls of uniformly bounded mass, gives uniform strong convergence of the original measurable nonautonomous Banach evolution on the full closed ordered time triangle.

Let E be a complex normed space in any universe, with its normed additive commutative group and complex normed-space structures. The evolution assertions assume CompleteSpace E. The zero space in every universe is included. Let S be the original NonautonomousDysonPhillips.Contraction E: bounded complex-linear contractions on nonnegative real times, with identity at zero, the semigroup law and continuous vector orbits. Write A(t)=S.action(t)=S.operator(Real.toNNReal(t)). Evolution integrals are the original vector Bochner integrals for real Lebesgue measure volume. Scalar norm integrals are real integrals with their displayed measures.

For T>=0, set J=Icc(0,T), mu=volume restricted to J and Delta(T)={(t,r):0<=r<=t<=T}. B_n(s) and B(s) are bounded complex-linear endomorphisms of E for each real absolute time s. Every fixed-vector slice is AE strongly measurable for mu, separately for each n and x. Every n has its own arbitrary nonnegative real integrable control g_n on J, and B has another such control g. Require norm(B_n(s))<=g_n(s) and norm(B(s))<=g(s) at every s in J. A single real C>=0 bounds the integral of every g_n and the integral of g over J. These integral bounds do not require a common pointwise integrable envelope or uniform integrability of the controls. No hypothesis concerns their values outside J.
