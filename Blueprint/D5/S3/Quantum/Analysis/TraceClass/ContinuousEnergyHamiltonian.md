## Abstract

Continuous singular reference energy constructs one full-space all-temperature Hamiltonian.

**Definition 1.1 (Finite levels on the complete spectral basis).**

*Commentary.*

H is a complete complex Hilbert space, sigma is a positive trace-class contraction, and r is an actual full SpectralResolution of sigma.
