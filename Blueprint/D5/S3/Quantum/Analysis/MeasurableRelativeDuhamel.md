# Relative Duhamel identities for measurable coefficients

## Abstract

The original measurable nonautonomous Dyson–Phillips evolutions obey both ordered relative Duhamel identities, with actual vector Bochner integrability under separate vectorwise almost everywhere strong measurability and integrable scalar controls.

Let E be a complex Banach space in an arbitrary universe, including the zero space. Let S be the original NonautonomousDysonPhillips.Contraction E: a strongly continuous semigroup of bounded complex-linear contractions on nonnegative real times, with identity at zero. Write A(t)=S.action(t). Fix any real T>=0. For each real s, B(s) and H(s) are bounded complex-linear endomorphisms of E. For every fixed vector z separately, B(s)z and H(s)z are AE strongly measurable for real Lebesgue measure volume restricted to Icc(0,T). Let g and h be arbitrary nonnegative real functions integrable on this closed interval, with norm(B(s))<=g(s) and norm(H(s))<=h(s) at each point there.

For any coefficient Q write U_Q(t,r)=MeasurableNonautonomousDysonPhillips.measurableEvolution(S,Q,t,r). The existential family in measurable_evolution is identified with this native family using its HasSum clause and measurableEvolution_apply. No new evolution is chosen.
