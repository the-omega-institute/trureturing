*Commentary.*

For every natural degree p at least eleven and logarithmic coordinates u and v with 0 < u ≤ 1/2 and 1 ≤ v − u ≤ 3/2, the v derivative of the u derivative of the original logarithmic kernel is strictly negative. For each integration coordinate 0 < a < u, the gap v − a lies in [1, 2]. The exact Taylor certificates for the first three bands give scalar positivity throughout that interval, which transfers to the original kernel.
