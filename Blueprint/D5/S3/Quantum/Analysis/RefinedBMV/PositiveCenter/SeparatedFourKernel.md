*Commentary.*

For every natural number p at least eleven, the mixed derivative of the original normalized kernel is strictly negative when the lower logarithmic coordinate u is between one half and four and the gap v minus u is at least four. The leading square and the signed linear correction give a positive first derivative in u and a negative mixed derivative before the positive decreasing upper normalization. The radical difference is controlled by averaging its divided difference against a decreasing density. Both mixed derivatives remain strictly negative in the original coordinates.
