*Commentary.*

For every natural number p at least eleven and real logarithmic coordinates u and v with 0 < u ≤ 1/2 and 0 < v − u ≤ 1, the v derivative of the u derivative of the original logarithmic kernel is strictly negative. The interval between zero and u parametrizes the regularized integrand used in the derivative identity. For v at most one, the low-strip bound already makes the integrand positive. From v = 1 to v = 3/2, outward-rounded marginal and joint Taylor enclosures give the same strict sign uniformly in 3/p.
