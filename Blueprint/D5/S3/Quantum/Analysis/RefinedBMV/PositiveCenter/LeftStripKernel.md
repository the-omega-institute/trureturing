*Commentary.*

For every natural number p at least eleven and independent real logarithmic coordinates u and v with zero less than u, u at most one half, and v minus u at least seven halves, the v derivative of the u derivative of the normalized positive-center kernel is strictly negative. The geometric term, the correlated root difference, and the finite exponential tail bound the weighted third derivative of the numerator by strictly less than negative three five-hundredths. A logarithmic derivative bound for the negative marginal and the lower-collision cancellation transfer this margin to the mixed derivative of the normalized kernel.
