For every natural degree p at least eleven and logarithmic coordinates u and v with 0 < u ≤ 1/2 and v − u ≥ 1, the v derivative of the u derivative of the original logarithmic kernel is strictly negative. Geometric reduction bounds the weighted derivative using a scalar whose positivity is checked by outward-rounded Taylor models up to integration gap 7/2.
