*Commentary.*

For every natural degree p at least eleven and logarithmic coordinates 4 ≤ u < v, the v derivative of the u derivative of the original kernel is strictly negative. The proof uses the short-gap sign up to gap two and the analytic tail estimate from gap four. For intermediate gaps, dyadic Taylor enclosures bound the normalized mixed derivative on the boxes of a finite cover.
