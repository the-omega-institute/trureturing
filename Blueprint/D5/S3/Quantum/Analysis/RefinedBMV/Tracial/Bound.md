*Commentary.*

For positive elements A and B of any unital C*-algebra with a finite positive trace, the gap is the mean trace of the twenty words with three copies of each element minus the trace of the cube of AB. Forty times the trace of AB times the gap bounds three times the square of the trace of the commutator's adjoint times the commutator. The proof keeps one rank-one term of the centered cubic kernel, uses cyclicity and trace Cauchy–Schwarz on finite positive partitions, and passes to the norm limit. For a faithful trace, zero gap forces A and B to commute.
