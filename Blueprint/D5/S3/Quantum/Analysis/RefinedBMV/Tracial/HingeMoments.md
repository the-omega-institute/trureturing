*Commentary.*

For a uniform arc count from four independent marks and the count from a uniformly rotated four-point grid, the exponentially weighted difference of their fourth-product averages controls the square of the signed spectral gap times the sum of the squared spectral values. This is the scalar estimate for the two-insertion term of the four-copy trace comparison near a commuting pair. The proof integrates the three hinge corrections and checks the resulting quadratic form for each power weight.
