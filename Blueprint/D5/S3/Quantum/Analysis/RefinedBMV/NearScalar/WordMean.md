*Commentary.*

Let p be any natural number, H Hermitian and B positive semidefinite in any finite complex dimension. Set A = exp H. If p ‖H‖op ≤ 1/100000, the mean of the real parts of the unnormalized traces of all ordered words with p copies of A and four of B is at least the real part of Tr((A^(p/4) B)^4) plus p² ‖B(HB − BH)‖F² / 25. The power p/4 is real, and ‖·‖op and ‖·‖F are the operator and Frobenius norms. For p > 0 the sufficient radius for ‖H‖op is 1/(100000 p), independent of matrix size. This gives a proved neighborhood of the identity for Hägele’s product lower comparison (arXiv:2611.00129).
