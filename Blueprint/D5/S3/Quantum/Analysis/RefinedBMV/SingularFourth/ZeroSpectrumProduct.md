*Commentary.*

Let p be a positive integer and A and B positive semidefinite in any common finite complex dimension. Suppose the spectrum of A is contained in {0,r,t}, where 0 < r < t. The mean of the traces of all ordered words with p copies of A and four copies of B is at least Tr((A^(p/4) B)^4). The spectral multiplicities and B are unrestricted. The proof groups cyclic contractions into three positive Gram blocks and checks the five Schur polynomials by exact integer arithmetic and an exponential sum positivity argument.
