*Commentary.*

For a fixed positive integer first degree, suppose the mixed derivative of the normalized upper kernel is nonpositive at every ordered pair of positive logarithmic coordinates. The two endpoint limits give the missing edge inequalities for the interval Gram decomposition. This proves positive semidefiniteness on arbitrary finite samples at positive centers, including repeated points. For a fixed sample, continuity of the unnormalized kernel and closedness of the positive semidefinite cone give the result at a zero center. The criterion supplies the upper block in the existing product and exponential trace comparisons.
