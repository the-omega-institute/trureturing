# Probability Continuation of the Double-Wall Response

## Abstract

A lawful probability displacement has no fixed affine update on four current responses.

**Theorem 1.1 (One common command on every legal starting height).**

$$\forall Z,s,a,b\in \mathbb{R}, 0<Z\land Z<1\land s\neq 0\land a<b\land \operatorname{Legal}\left(Z, s, a, b\right)\Rightarrow \operatorname{ValidBase}\left(\operatorname{B}\left(Z\right)\right)\land \operatorname{Commands}\left(Z, s, a, b\right)\land \neg \operatorname{Affine}\left(Z, s, a, b\right)$$
