Gamma n is the set of actual subsets of Fin n with no consecutive occupied positions. Position i in Fin n denotes position i+1 in the one-based path. The empty subset is retained. Every subset of a legal pattern is legal.

The two integer matrix products are identities. Integer matrices act by finite integer multiples on every additive commutative group.
