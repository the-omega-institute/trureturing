Notation: Nat, Int and Real denote the natural numbers, integers and real numbers. Pattern(n) is exactly the finite subsets I of Fin n with no i,j in I whose natural values satisfy i.val+1=j.val. Low(n,q) and High(n,q) are the subtypes with |I|<=q and q<|I|. Matrix 1 is the identity. moments(p)(T)=sum over I containing T of p(I); probabilities(mu)(I)=sum over T containing I of (-1)^(|T|-|I|) mu(T); phases(theta)(T)=sum over I contained in T of (-1)^(|T|-|I|) theta(I); originalPhases(Phi)(I)=sum over T contained in I of Phi(T). Integer coefficients act by integer multiples in G.

PositiveProbability(n) consists of p in V(n) with every p(I)>0 and sum p(I)=1. ProbabilityFibre(q,p0) consists of all such p with moments(p)(T)=moments(p0)(T) for every T in Low(n,q). fillMoments(q,p0,x) fills the low slots from moments(p0) and the high slots from x. positiveDomain(q,p0)={x:High(n,q) to Real | every probabilities(fillMoments(q,p0,x))(I)>0}; using a set as a type means its subtype. Nonempty, IsOpen and Convex(Real,...) refer to this actual subset of the full high real function space.

occupationAction(n,q,G,t)(I) is the sum of t(T) over low T contained in I. actionHom(n,q,G,t)=originalPhases(lowEmbedding(t)), where lowEmbedding extends t by zero. PhaseOrbit(n,q,G,theta,eta) means there exists t with eta=theta+occupationAction(n,q,G,t). highPhases(q,theta)(T)=phases(theta)(T.val), with the source's implicit q shown explicitly. polarMap(n,x)(I)=sqrt(x.1(I)) exp(-i x.2(I)); polarTorusMap(n,p,theta)(I)=sqrt(p(I)) times the unit-circle image of -theta(I). polarDerivative(n,x)(d)(I)=exp(-i x.2(I)) times (d.1(I)/(2 sqrt(x.1(I)))-i sqrt(x.1(I)) d.2(I)). amplitudeOneForm(n,x,d) is minus the imaginary part of sum conjugate(polarMap(n,x)(I)) polarDerivative(n,x)(d)(I). sourceTwoForm(n,d,e)=sum over I of d.2(I)e.1(I)-e.2(I)d.1(I). Every sum here is over Pattern(n). HasFDerivAt asserts the actual real Frechet derivative, and IsLocalHomeomorph asserts local homeomorphism for the ordinary period projection.
