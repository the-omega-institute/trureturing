# Five-mode local density fibres

## Abstract

Full-rank five-mode density fibres and their affine coordinates.

**Theorem 1.1 (The full-rank mean fibre has 21 real local dimensions).**

$$(\forall Rho: \operatorname{ComplexMatrix}\left(5, 5\right), ((\operatorname{PosDef}\left(Rho\right) \land (\operatorname{Trace}\left(Rho\right) = 1)) \implies (((\operatorname{RealFinrank}\left(\operatorname{Kernel}\left(\operatorname{modeReadout}\left(\right)\right)\right) = 21) \land (\forall Sigma: \operatorname{ComplexMatrix}\left(5, 5\right), (\operatorname{IsHermitian}\left(Sigma\right) \implies (((\operatorname{Trace}\left(Sigma\right) = 1) \land (\operatorname{means}\left(Sigma\right) = \operatorname{means}\left(Rho\right))) \iff (\exists ! h: \operatorname{Kernel}\left(\operatorname{modeReadout}\left(\right)\right), (Sigma = \operatorname{fibrePoint}\left(Rho, h\right))))))) \land (\exists epsilon: \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), ((0 < epsilon) \land (\forall h: \operatorname{Kernel}\left(\operatorname{modeReadout}\left(\right)\right), ((\operatorname{CstarOperatorNorm}\left(h\right) < epsilon) \implies ((\operatorname{PosDef}\left(\operatorname{fibrePoint}\left(Rho, h\right)\right) \land (\operatorname{Trace}\left(\operatorname{fibrePoint}\left(Rho, h\right)\right) = 1)) \land (\operatorname{means}\left(\operatorname{fibrePoint}\left(Rho, h\right)\right) = \operatorname{means}\left(Rho\right))))))))))$$

*Commentary.*

The ordered modes are empty, one, two, three, and thirteen. The three means, ordered X,Z,Y, are rho(1,1)+rho(4,4), rho(2,2), and rho(3,3)+rho(4,4), with real parts understood. Trace-zero Hermitian five-by-five matrices have real dimension 24, by BipartiteSectorDecomposition.trace_zero_hermitian_finrank. Explicit diagonal trace-zero matrices independently realize the three means, so the kernel has dimension 21.

For any positive-definite trace-one rho, every Hermitian matrix with trace one and the same means is uniquely rho+h with h in that kernel. Positive semidefiniteness is still a restriction on these affine coordinates. The real spectrum of rho has a strictly positive lower bound epsilon. A self-adjoint perturbation h satisfies minus its C-star operator norm times the identity <= h. Consequently every kernel perturbation with operator norm below epsilon remains positive definite, with unchanged trace and means. This gives an open 21-dimensional neighbourhood within the affine fibre at each full-rank point. The scalar identity divided by five is one such point. The conclusion does not impose the same dimension on lower-rank boundary fibres.

Writing kappa=rho(4,4), the diagonal probabilities are 1-X-Z-Y+kappa, X-kappa, Z, Y-kappa, and kappa. The single diagonal direction kappa is independent of the ten complex off-diagonal Hermitian coordinates, which supply twenty real directions. Positivity constrains both sets of coordinates jointly.

The pure states generated by (mode-one+mode-three)/sqrt(2) and (mode-one-mode-three)/sqrt(2) have X=Y=1/2, Z=kappa=0. They give expectations +1 and -1 for the self-adjoint observable C13=|one><three|+|three><one|. Thus three means do not determine this coherent readout. C13 is an observable with signed expectation; it is not a positive quantum effect. The empty mode is a nonzero basis vector. The probability-coordinate equalities and both positive trace-one coherence matrices are exact finite-matrix applications, rather than separately retained theorem wrappers.
