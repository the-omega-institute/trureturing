*Commentary.*

For a nonzero periodic binary bond-two trace tensor at length at least four, vanishing of the raw 11,11 population forces zero trace, zero determinant and square zero for the occupied core. The corner is a sum of squared moduli, so every trace word starting in 11 vanishes. Comparing these vanishing traces with traces of powers, and using the two-by-two Cayley–Hamilton identity, gives the restrictions on the core.
