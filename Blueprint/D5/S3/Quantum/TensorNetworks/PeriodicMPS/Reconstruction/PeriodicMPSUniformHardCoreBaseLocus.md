*Commentary.*

For every positive period, the full periodic trace-word tensor vanishes exactly on the listed trace-invariant locus. When at least one trace is nonzero, the three quadratic invariants are proportional to the corresponding trace products with coefficients fixed by a root of the power-trace polynomial. When both traces vanish, vanishing holds at odd periods and requires all three quadratic invariants to vanish at even periods.
