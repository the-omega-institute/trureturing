*Commentary.*

For an odd n and a two-by-two polynomial matrix whose trace is zero at the parameter origin, the n-th power trace is zero there. Its parameter derivative is n times the derivative of the trace, multiplied by the negative determinant at the origin raised to (n − 1)/2.
