*Commentary.*

If a relatively open dense subset of the compact seven-site trace graph consists of singleton fibers of the Pauli observation, the compact state source has a relatively open dense subset of singleton fibers of the adjacent two-site marginal.

The state projection forgets the weighted trace projector and is continuous and surjective. Equality of marginals agrees with equality of the graph readings. The singleton-fiber locus contains every lift of each state in its image, so openness descends through the quotient map.
