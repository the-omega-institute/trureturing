*Commentary.*

In the affine chart with first trace fixed to one, the full seven-letter trace polynomial vanishes exactly when the vacuum polynomial is zero and the second determinant and mixed trace satisfy the proportionality equations. The trace-word recurrence evaluates literal products of two bond matrices.

The same trace polynomial gives two injective normal maps. One is taken along a nonzero traceless base pencil. The other is taken along the proportional locus at a root of the vacuum polynomial.
