# Original-unit normalization and entire original geometry

## Abstract

Native support compression produces actual original-unit normalization and geometric transport.

Retain the native real or complex field and the arbitrary nonzero finite-dimensional real original carrier. The generating pointed cone has an arbitrary strictly positive original unit, a compact entire unit-one base, and a faithful real-linear representation whose whole PSD section equals the original cone. OriginalUnitSupport supplies the native compression and unique determinant maximizer. The same original point and principal inverse square root normalize every original vector. No ambient full support or original trace unit is assumed.
