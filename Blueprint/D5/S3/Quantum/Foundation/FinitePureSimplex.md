# One-relation zero gluing on the entire original base

## Abstract

One-relation zero gluing on the entire original base.

Finiteness applies to the full set of extreme points of the original normalized base. The carrier, cone, unit, full proper interval and original infimum comparison are unchanged. The one-relation equivalence imposes no ball, antipodal, Jordan or matrix premise.

*Commentary.*

If the full pure coordinate kernel is zero, there exists an original effect e_p for every original pure p. For any two actual mixed-state exclusions, least domination forces both coordinate readings to vanish, so the same coordinate effect excludes their midpoint. No claim that an arbitrary mixed x different from p has zero p-coordinate is used.

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

If the real dimension of the entire kernel of Phi is at most one, then original mixed-state zero gluing is equivalent to the entire base being a simplex. The zero-nullity branch constructs least coordinate effects against all confirmers. In the nonzero one-dimensional branch, a globally minimal nonzero relation coefficient is oriented positively. Extremality excludes a relation with a single vertex on either sign side. Full-kernel-annihilating original effects exclude each negative vertex separately. Original midpoint gluing supplies a common effect at a midpoint; nonnegativity makes that effect vanish at both endpoints, hence at every convex combination. This gives convexity of the actual exclusion set without assuming general infimum attainment. The same relation forces a positive lower bound at the source barycenter, contradicting any excluding effect. The normalized weighted theorem supplies the sharper attained partition values on the same original objects.

*Commentary.*

For the same finite full pure carrier, original mixed-state midpoint gluing together with a failure of the quantified intrinsic pure-antipode separation implies both dim ker Phi >= 2 and |P|-dim A >= 2. Under nullity at most one the main theorem gives a simplex and coordinate effects distinguish every distinct pure pair. In the source ball domain this is the consequence (92.7); no ball premise is needed by this stronger finite-carrier obstruction.
