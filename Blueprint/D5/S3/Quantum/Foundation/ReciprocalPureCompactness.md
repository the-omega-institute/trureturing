*Commentary.*

On the same arbitrary finite dimensional original closed generating pointed cone and strictly positive original unit, assume full comparison reciprocity only on pairs of original pure endpoints. Then all of P=ext(K) is compact. No EB3, weakPair, label injectivity, closedness of P, least effect or finite pure decomposition is assumed. This proves the compactness conclusion of IX114.4 without using a finite-dimensional strengthening of Krein-Milman.

For a sequence of original pure points tending to x in the compact full K, intersect the value-one faces of every original effect confirming x. This common face H contains x, is compact, and is extreme in K. A pinned compact extreme-point existence theorem supplies r extreme in H; extreme-face transfer makes r an original pure point. Select actual full-fiber minimizers at r along the sequence and take an effect subsequence limit. Joint evaluation shows the limit confirms x, so membership of r in H makes its value at r equal one. Pure PR compares those same minimizer values with any original confirmer of r along the labels. The pre-existing reciprocal endpoint exposure theorem supplies a confirmer whose whole value-one face is {r}. Taking limits forces this confirmer to equal one at x and hence x=r. Thus every ambient sequence limit is pure, closing P inside compact K.

The common complete-fiber face and its moving-minimizer limit are the live cross-label bridge. Pinned Krein-Milman is used only for existence of an extreme point in a nonempty compact face, never as an unproved finite pure decomposition theorem. Compactness and exposedness alone carry no physical or literature-priority assertion.
