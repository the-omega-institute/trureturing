# Canonical Product Recovery

## Abstract

Actual marginal support cancels singular tensor logarithms in product recovery.

**Theorem 1.1 (Singular joint states satisfy the product-reference chain rule).**

$$\forall a: \operatorname{Type}, \forall b: \operatorname{Type}, [\operatorname{Fintype}(a)] [\operatorname{DecidableEq}(a)] [\operatorname{Fintype}(b)] [\operatorname{DecidableEq}(b)] \forall \rho: \operatorname{DensityState}(a \times b), \forall c: \operatorname{DensityState}(a), \forall d: \operatorname{DensityState}(b), \operatorname{PosDef}(\operatorname{underlyingMatrix}(c)) \land \operatorname{PosDef}(\operatorname{underlyingMatrix}(d)) \Rightarrow (\operatorname{SupportContained}(\rho, \operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{productState}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{marginalRight}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\rho)))), \operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(d)))) \land \operatorname{finiteTraceLogRelativeEntropy}(\rho, \operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{productState}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(c), \operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(d)))) - \operatorname{finiteTraceLogRelativeEntropy}(\operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{marginalRight}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\rho))), c) = \operatorname{finiteTraceLogRelativeEntropy}(\rho, \operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{productState}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{marginalRight}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\rho)))), \operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(d)))) \land \operatorname{extendedQuantumRelativeEntropy}(\rho, \operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{productState}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(c), \operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(d)))) = \operatorname{extendedQuantumRelativeEntropy}(\rho, \operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{productState}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{marginalRight}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\rho)))), \operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(d)))) + \operatorname{extendedQuantumRelativeEntropy}(\operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{marginalRight}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\rho))), c) \land \operatorname{extendedQuantumRelativeEntropy}(\rho, \operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{productState}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(c), \operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(d)))) \neq \infty \land \operatorname{extendedQuantumRelativeEntropy}(\rho, \operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{productState}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{marginalRight}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\rho)))), \operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(d)))) \neq \infty \land \operatorname{extendedQuantumRelativeEntropy}(\operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{marginalRight}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\rho))), c) \neq \infty \land \operatorname{extendedQuantumRelativeEntropy}(\operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{marginalLeft}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\rho))), d) \neq \infty \land \operatorname{finiteTraceLogRelativeEntropy}(\rho, \operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{productState}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{marginalRight}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\rho)))), \operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(d)))) = \operatorname{quantumMutualInformation}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\rho)) + \operatorname{finiteTraceLogRelativeEntropy}(\operatorname{fromLegacyDensityState}(\operatorname{marginalLeft}(\operatorname{toLegacyDensityState}(\rho))), d))$$

*Commentary.*

The whole chain is a repository-derived composition of known mechanisms, with no novelty claim. The adapted Alex Meiburg Physlib sources and the paper's full-rank boundary are attributed in `D5/L/QuantumStates/meiburg2026singulartensorrecovery`.

In the statement c and d denote gammaA and gammaB; underlyingMatrix is CStarMatrix.ofMatrix.symm applied to the state matrix, and infinity is WithTop top. The marginal and recovered state use the existing partial trace and product state. Only the reference factors are positive definite. The proof contracts actual marginal zero directions, retains support projections in the tensor logarithm, and cancels them under the same joint-state weighted trace. Every divergence in the displayed chain is in its supported finite branch. The finite statement does not establish oscillator operator domains or Gibbs traces.

The supported finite Pinsker application in `D5/S3/Quantum/Divergence/CanonicalChannelDPI` applies to this same joint state and its recovered state. Write mat(tau) = CStarMatrix.ofMatrix.symm tau.1 and D(tau||upsilon) = finiteTraceLogRelativeEntropy tau upsilon, using natural logarithms. For the finite decidable carriers a and b, put rhoA = fromLegacyDensityState(marginalRight(toLegacyDensityState rho)), rhoB = fromLegacyDensityState(marginalLeft(toLegacyDensityState rho)), sigma = fromLegacyDensityState(productState(toLegacyDensityState rhoA, toLegacyDensityState gammaB)), and omega = fromLegacyDensityState(productState(toLegacyDensityState gammaA, toLegacyDensityState gammaB)). Thus sigma is the actual recovered state, not an independently chosen state with the same marginal.

Use the Pinsker parameters n = a x b and the ordered pair (rho,sigma). DensityState means a positive semidefinite complex matrix of trace one. The chain theorem supplies SupportContained rho sigma, namely ker(mat(sigma)) contained in ker(mat(rho)); it is not an added recovery assumption. The trace-one reference states make a and b nonempty, as in the chain proof, so the finite nonempty carrier condition is satisfied. Only gammaA and gammaB have the positive-definiteness hypotheses of the displayed chain. Neither rho nor rhoA nor sigma is required to be invertible, and rho may be correlated and need not commute with sigma.

The measurement is formed from this very difference A = mat(rho)-mat(sigma). Choose the Hermitian spectral unitary U and eigenvalues lambda_i of A, and set s(i) = decide(0 < lambda_i). The Kraus rows K_i(y,j) = conjugate(U(j,i)) if y = s(i), and zero otherwise, give a QuantumChannel Phi from a x b to Bool since sum_i K_i* K_i = I. Here * is conjugate transpose. On every complex matrix X, its underlying matrix action is diag_y(sum_i if y = s(i) then (U* X U)(i,i) else 0). Both outputs are the actual Phi.mapState images, and their support inclusion is preserved. If p and q are their respective real true diagonal entries, then the unhalved norm N = traceNorm(A) equals 2(p-q). This uses the same spectral positive mass and zero trace of A; an attaining measurement is not an input hypothesis.

For these outputs, support transfer gives q = 0 implies p = 0 and 1-q = 0 implies 1-p = 0. Their trace-log entropy is the Bernoulli KL value k = p log(p/q) + (1-p) log((1-p)/(1-q)), with the supported zero terms interpreted by the totalized logarithm. The diagonal-log calculation and channel data processing described in `D5/S3/Quantum/Divergence/CanonicalChannelDPI` give k <= D(rho||sigma); `D5/S3/TotalVariation/Pinsker.binary_pinsker` gives 2(p-q)^2 <= k, including the endpoints. Substituting N = 2(p-q) therefore gives N^2 <= 2 D(rho||sigma) and D(rho||sigma) >= 0. The cap N <= 2 follows from `D5/S3/Quantum/Foundation/FiniteTraceDistance.traceDistance_le_one` because traceDistance(rho,sigma) = N/2.

The product-recovery correspondence identifies the entropy in that bound without changing either state: Delta = D(rho||omega)-D(rhoA||gammaA) = D(rho||sigma) = quantumMutualInformation(toLegacyDensityState rho) + D(rhoB||gammaB). Consequently N^2 <= 2 Delta, Delta >= 0, and N <= 2; equivalently 2 traceDistance(rho,sigma)^2 <= Delta. These are finite consequences of the displayed chain and the supported Pinsker application. The general Pinsker application itself works on every finite decidable nonempty carrier with only the support inclusion on the state pair; it requires no tensor decomposition or faithful reference state. Dimension one, singular states, and zero measurement probabilities remain included.

These statements concern finite density matrices and a mathematical quantum channel. They do not identify the reference factors with Gibbs states of a common Hamiltonian, realize the channel by Weyl or oscillator dynamics, or supply infinite-dimensional operator domains, thermal trace-class bounds, or a conservation-law implementation. Those physical identifications require their own hypotheses and arguments.
