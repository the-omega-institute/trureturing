# Uniform scalar kernel bounds

## Abstract

Uniform scalar kernel bounds

The spectral Gram polynomial gives the rho triangle inequality. The cyclic residual identity and its positive denominator yield the uniform defect-kernel bound. These scalar estimates are consumed by the uniform Frobenius and cubic trace bounds.
