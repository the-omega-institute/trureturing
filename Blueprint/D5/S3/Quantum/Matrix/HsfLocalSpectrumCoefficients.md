## Abstract

Spectral coefficients and finite local estimates

The original ordered cube-root jet recurrences determine the first Schur multiplier and the second spectral kernel. Frobenius estimates, cross-level gap bounds, trace normalization and radial identities are retained in the original supplier namespace. These are consumed by the actual finite-spectrum and uniform-radius suppliers.
