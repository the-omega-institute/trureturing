## Abstract

Ordered matrix moment transfer

The coefficient Gram laws transfer entrywise to arbitrary finite fibers with complex phases. The ordered row-square identity retains both triangle orientations. The actual factorial CFC shifted identity is supplied by HsfLocalSpectrumSupplier; no scalar-matrix restriction is introduced.
