A flat real skew coupling has an exact physical Clifford ground density.

*Commentary.*

A real orthogonal paired basis has frequency squareRoot(d) in every plane. Orthogonal combinations preserve the Clifford relations and the parity anticommutation. Their joint minus density attains the lower bound for the original double-sum operator.
