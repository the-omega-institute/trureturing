*Commentary.*

Let C be any subset of the original real normed space E detected exactly by all original real-linear evaluations p. J is the original bilinear Jordan product and D is the same arbitrary total function. Suppose every original directed two-table is positive semidefinite. Every C-positive real-linear functional f then reads the whole directed table positively, for every original pair and every complex test vector. Its determinant gives the stated quadratic budget. No nonzero, unit-mass or continuity premise is imposed on f or the original evaluations. The proof uses the positive quadratic original label and the exact cone, rather than an extension to an ambient associative algebra.

originalCorner(i,JX,k) is exactly the original inclusion inverse image of the Peirce two-space ker(JX(i(k))-id). Thus its elements are literally original labels in E intersect X two(k). ActualMatrixCorner supplies the source's closed-corner interface: an isometric original inclusion, inherited Jordan multiplication, the original k as corner unit, closure under J and the same D, and a real-linear equivalence onto all self-adjoint complex matrices. The equivalence preserves the inherited norm using the matrix L2 operator norm, the unit, the Jordan product, and the exact inherited cone. This is a conditional interface, not a construction from abstract JB axioms. Density and completeness of a real JB completion are not needed once this actual finite interface is supplied.

cornerJordan and cornerResponse are literal restrictions: their underlying original labels are J(a,b) and D(a,b). Every original evaluation restricts by composition with the original subtype inclusion, so exact-cone detection and all original directed tables restrict without changing preparations or their masses. The matrix equivalence is onto the whole finite self-adjoint matrix space; no proper surrogate matrix subsystem is substituted.

cornerOp is ordinary matrix action on EuclideanSpace over the complex scalars. cornerVectorRead is the real part of the vector expectation, is real linear and nonnegative on the inherited cone, and its complex expectation is real. It reads k as the squared Euclidean norm, J(a,a) as the squared Euclidean norm of a acting on the vector, and J(a,b) as the real inner product of the two resulting vectors. Whole-table transfer and the determinant give the budget on the literal original D(a,b). The index type may be empty and the vector may be zero. The zero corner and k=0 remain in scope whenever the supplied interface exists; no ambient extension, GNS representation or normalized vector is used.

*Commentary.*

OriginalData retains the original real normed label space E, its bilinear Jordan product J, unit u, and arbitrary total response D:E times E to E. Its supplied inclusion i into an arbitrary unital complex C-star algebra A is real linear, isometric and self-adjoint valued, maps u to one, and satisfies i(J(a,b))=(i(a)i(b)+i(b)i(a))/2. The original preparation family is arbitrary and its cone is exact: i(a) is positive exactly when every original preparation evaluates a nonnegatively. Every original directed Hermitian two-table is positive semidefinite. No mass is divided or normalized; zero, unequal and subnormalized original masses remain in the domain.

B and C are unital star subalgebras of the same A, C is norm closed, C is contained in B, and every element of C commutes with every element of B. The theorem accepts a specified unital star character chi:C to the complex scalars. Its existence forces this character branch to be proper. compressedOp sends every original a in E to the orthogonal compression of pi(i(a)) to that single cyclicSpace, regarded as a real linear operator.
