# Degeneracy Graph Determinant: Multiplicity

## Abstract

Two-root tail witnesses give the exact arbitrary-depth multiplicities in the determinant recurrence.

The multiplicity owner implements source equations (6.23)–(6.27) literally. StrictTailLevel, PairDescendantParent, and pairDescendantFiberMax retain actual descendant fibers; SourcePairTailWitness is the finite source witness whose cardinality becomes the exponent. Ambient witnesses and the restriction/lift/pruning equivalences establish the recurrence's multiplicity bijection without assuming a global coordinate grid.
