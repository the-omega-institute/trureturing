*Commentary.*

For finite complex matrices, Hermitian A₀ and A₁ and positive-definite B₀ and B₁ satisfy the Loewner-order inequality Aₜ Bₜ⁻¹ Aₜ ≤ t A₀ B₀⁻¹ A₀ + (1-t) A₁ B₁⁻¹ A₁ for every t in [0,1], where Aₜ and Bₜ are the corresponding affine mixtures. The proof averages two positive block matrices and applies the Schur-complement characterization. No commutativity assumption is used.
