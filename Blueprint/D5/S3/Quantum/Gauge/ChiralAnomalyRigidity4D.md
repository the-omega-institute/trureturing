# Chiral anomaly rigidity in four dimensions

## Abstract

Local anomaly cancellation for one generation and the parity of weak doublets.

The charges are all left handed. The coordinate order is the quark doublet, the conjugate up and down singlets, the lepton doublet, the conjugate charged lepton and, for six species, the conjugate neutrino. Write q, d and ν for the quark doublet, conjugate down and conjugate neutrino charges.
