# A rational coordinate obstruction for tetrahedral Bloch readings

## Abstract

The tetrahedral inner product on the rational Bloch sphere.

**Theorem 1.1 (Three rational Bloch unit vectors cannot have the tetrahedral inner product).**

*Commentary.*

Suppose three unit vectors have rational coordinates in the three-dimensional pure-imaginary quaternion subspace. Multiply each vector by a common positive denominator d. The integer vectors have squared lengths d squared. An inner product of minus one third would make three times each off-diagonal inner product equal to minus d squared. The theorem excludes this system.

In three coordinates the Gram determinant is the square of the determinant of the coordinate matrix. Equating it to the tetrahedral Gram determinant would give a rational square root of three.
