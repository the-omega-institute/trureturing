# L∞-contraction for a weight fails on two points

## Abstract

A damped rotation on two points is L∞-bounded but admits no contractive positive weight.

F. Madou, I. Remizov, M. Vafadar (eds.), Open problems in one-parameter operator semigroups theory, arXiv:2410.00416v2, §6 ‘L∞-bounds’, equation (1), verbatim: “The semigroup (T(t))t≥0 is called L∞-bounded, if there exists M > 0 such that ‖T(t)f‖∞ ≤ M‖f‖∞, for all f ∈ L²(Ω) ∩ L∞(Ω). (1)” Equation (3), verbatim: “(3) ‖gT(t)f‖∞ ≤ ‖gf‖∞ for all f ∈ L∞(Ω) ∩ L²(Ω)” Problem 22 and its comment, verbatim: “Problem 22 (L∞-contraction for a weight problem). In the setting of Problem 21 assume that the semigroup (T(t))t≥0 is L∞-bounded, i.e., satisfies (1) for some M ≥ 1. Does there exist a function g ∈ L∞(Ω) satisfying g > 0 µ-a.e., 1/g ∈ L∞(Ω), and (3)? Comments. In general, this might be more than one can hope for.”

**Definition 1.4 (The finite-dimensional weight assertion).**

$$claim \Leftrightarrow (\forall n: \mathbb{N}, \forall A: \operatorname{Matrix}\left(\operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right), \operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right), \mathbb{C}\right), (\operatorname{IsSectorialMatrix}\left(A\right)) \Rightarrow ((\exists M: \mathbb{R}, \forall t: \mathbb{R}, (0 \le t) \Rightarrow (\forall u: \operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right) \to \mathbb{C}, \operatorname{supNorm}\left(\operatorname{mulVec}\left(\operatorname{semigroup}\left(A, t\right), u\right)\right) \le M \cdot \operatorname{supNorm}\left(u\right))) \Rightarrow (\exists g: \operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right) \to \mathbb{R}, (\forall i: \operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right), 0 < \operatorname{coord}\left(g, i\right)) \land (\forall t: \mathbb{R}, (0 \le t) \Rightarrow (\forall u: \operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right) \to \mathbb{C}, \operatorname{supNorm}\left(\operatorname{weighted}\left(g, \operatorname{mulVec}\left(\operatorname{semigroup}\left(A, t\right), u\right)\right)\right) \le \operatorname{supNorm}\left(\operatorname{weighted}\left(g, u\right)\right))))))$$

*Commentary.*

The claim ranges over every natural n and complex n×n matrix A. Sectoriality and a uniform real-time sup-norm bound imply the existence of a positive real weight g satisfying supNorm(g T(t)u)≤supNorm(g u) for every t≥0 and every complex vector u. weighted(g,u) means the function i↦(g i : ℂ)u i; mulVec is matrix action. The bound quantifies a real M without a separate M≥1 condition; the witness uses M=2. On Fin n with counting measure, L²∩L∞ is the full vector space, and a strictly positive weight and its inverse are bounded. Thus this is the finite-dimensional instance of Problem 22.

The algebra map φ(a+bi)=aI+bJ, its continuity, and semigroup_formula give T(t)=e⁻ᵗ[[cos(2t),−sin(2t)],[sin(2t),cos(2t)]]. The lemmas entry_bound and bounded prove supNorm(T(t)u)≤2 supNorm(u) for t≥0. For any positive weight, no_weight tests (1/g₀,−1/g₁) in coordinate zero and (1/g₀,1/g₁) in coordinate one. Contractivity forces e⁻ᵗ(cos(2t)+c sin(2t))≤1 both for c=g₀/g₁ and for c=g₁/g₀. Hence result proves ¬ claim. In the source setting, use the two-point counting measure and V=ℂ². The form is closed and dense in finite dimension, with associated operator A.
