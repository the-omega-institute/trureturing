# Two Circulant X Seed

## Abstract

Unit-phase triples with opposite sums yield an order-six Hadamard seed.

*Commentary.*

For complex a,b,c,d, let A be the circulant matrix generated by (a,1,b) and B the one generated by (c,1,d). The order-six seed has block rows (A,B) and (B-adjoint,-A-adjoint).
