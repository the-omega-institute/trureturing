*Commentary.*

Let f be positive on Boolean strings, with every change from zero to one increasing f by a factor in [1,2]. The expectation of f under independent inclusion rates p, multiplied by the expectation of its reciprocal under independent rates w, is bounded by the product of max(1,(1+pᵢ)(1−wᵢ/2)). The proof conditions on one bit and optimizes the ratio of its two conditional averages.
