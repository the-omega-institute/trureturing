*Commentary.*

Hilbert–Schmidt Cauchy–Schwarz controls the endpoint, midpoint and imaginary mixed moments of two Hermitian observables under a positive weight.

Computational and Hadamard scores on the qubit reduce to centered Pauli Z and X. On the qutrit the scores are diagonal and circulant. The centered covariance estimate bounds the joint Simpson expression and yields the anchor quadratic inequality.
