# Real-rooted coefficient permutations

## Abstract

Coefficient orders of products of positive linear factors.

**Definition 1.1 (Coefficient sequence).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Combinatorics/RealRootedPermutations/Defs.coeffs`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Combinatorics/RealRootedPermutations/Defs.coeffs` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For a finite family of real parameters, coeffs r k is the coefficient of X^k in the product of the factors 1 + r i X.
