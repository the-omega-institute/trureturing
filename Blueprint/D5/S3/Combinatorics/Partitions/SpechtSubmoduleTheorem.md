# Specht SubmoduleTheorem

## Abstract

Specht SubmoduleTheorem

For the literal Young diagram, failure of a repeated row-column intersection gives a row-column factorization. A repeated intersection supplies an odd transposition fixing the tabloid, so column antisymmetrization annihilates it. In the other case the image is a signed polytabloid. By linearity its image on the whole permutation module lies on that polytabloid line. This gives James's submodule dichotomy. Positivity then makes the polytabloid span a minimal nonzero subrepresentation, and group transport preserves the original partition label. The conclusion concerns rational irreducibility; simplicity of the complex scalar extension requires the separate absolute irreducibility argument.
