# Specht TabloidForm

## Abstract

Specht TabloidForm

The tabloid pairing makes distinct cosets orthogonal. A permutation preserves this pairing, and its inverse moves the action between the two arguments. Positivity over the rational numbers makes the restriction to each subspace nondegenerate, so the orthogonal subspace is an invariant complement.
