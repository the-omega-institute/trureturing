# The Entry Following the Maximum

## Abstract

A successor greater than one is incompatible with the fixed indecomposable 312-avoiding configuration.

*Commentary.*

There is no sum-indecomposable 312-avoiding permutation of size greater than three fixed by the third inverse iterate whose entry immediately after the maximum, read as zero beyond the word, is greater than one.
