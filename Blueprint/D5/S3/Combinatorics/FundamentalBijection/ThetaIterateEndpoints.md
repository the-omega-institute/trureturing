# Location of the Parameter Maximum

## Abstract

The maximum of an eligible parameter lies at exactly one endpoint.

**Theorem 1.1 (Exactly one maximal endpoint).**

*Commentary.*

A parameter permutation of size at least two whose P image avoids 132 through depth two has its maximum at its first or last position, but not at both.
