# A Cycle Forced by Two Successors

## Abstract

Two specified successor edges force a single initial record block.

*Commentary.*

For a 132-avoiding permutation q of size h at least four, if the cyclic successors of one and two are h and h minus one and the successor of h exceeds one, then the successor of h minus one is one and q begins with h.
