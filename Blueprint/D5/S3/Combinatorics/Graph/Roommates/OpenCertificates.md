**Definition 1.12 (Sixteen ranking words).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/Roommates/OpenCertificates.forbiddenWord`

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/Roommates/OpenCertificates.forbiddenWord` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The index is one orientation bit and three independent Boolean choices. In the forward orientation, positions zero, one and two respectively prefer signed offsets (1,2), (1,-1) and (-2,-1), in that order, to both exterior offsets. Each Boolean chooses the order of the bottom two offsets. Reflection gives the other orientation. The sixteen ranking words are distinct.

**Theorem 1.13 (Every listed profile is impossible).**

$$\forall (s:\operatorname{TriangleIndex}\left(\right)), \neg \exists (d:\operatorname{Assignments}\left(\right)), \operatorname{Certificate}\left(\operatorname{forbiddenWord}\left(s\right), d\right)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/Roommates/OpenCertificates.forbidden_not_certified` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

TriangleIndex abbreviates Bool times (Fin 3 to Bool), and Assignments abbreviates Fin 3 to the integers. The forward case applies exclusive_triangle_not_certified. The reverse case transports a hypothetical certificate through reflection and applies the same theorem. No matching witness is enumerated; exterior partners and singles remain allowed.

**Theorem 1.15 (Exact removal cardinality).**

$$\operatorname{card}\left(\operatorname{forbiddenProfiles}\left(\right)\right) = 16$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/Roommates/OpenCertificates.forbidden_card` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The rank of offset 1 at the middle position recovers the orientation: it is zero forward and one after reflection. After undoing reflection, ranks at coordinates 2, 1 and 0 of positions 0, 1 and 2 respectively recover the three exterior-order bits. This word-level injection proves that precisely sixteen ranking profiles are removed, without enumerating all pairs of profiles.

**Theorem 1.16 (Three-site count bound).**

$$\operatorname{count}\left(3\right) \le 13808$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/Roommates/OpenCertificates.count_three_bound` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The map sends each existentially certified word to its underlying ranking word in the complement of the sixteen forbidden profiles. It is injective on words. The whole space has 24 cubed = 13824 elements; complement cardinality gives 13824 minus 16 = 13808. GrowthTransfer.result uses this bound to obtain strict exponential growth below 24.
