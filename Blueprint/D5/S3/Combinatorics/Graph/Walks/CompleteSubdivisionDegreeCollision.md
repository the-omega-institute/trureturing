Repeated augmented degrees force collisions

A resolving set would force distinct augmented selected degrees. Equal original bits give a collision between reverse incidences. With unequal original bits, cancel the shared midpoint by swapping the two indices in a row sum; a strict comparison at an outside index supplies two distinct incidences with equal four-bin counts. The cancellation excludes both distinguished indices.
