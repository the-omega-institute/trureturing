# Generating leap groups within parity classes

## Abstract

Cross-edge swaps and generation of a parity-class stabilizer.

**Definition 1.1 (Hops on a labelled graph).**

$$\forall V \in \mathrm{Type},\; \forall G \in \operatorname{SimpleGraph}\left(V\right),\; \forall h \in \operatorname{Perm}\left(V\right),\; \operatorname{IsHop}\left(G, h\right) \iff \forall x \in V,\; \operatorname{Adj}\left(G, x, \operatorname{h}\left(x\right)\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/LeapGroups/LeapGroup.IsHop` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Mark Schachner and Gabe Udell (2026). *The group generated by a graph’s derangements*. DOI: [10.48550/arXiv.2610.18463](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.18463). URL: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18463v1>.

*Commentary.*

A hop sends every vertex to a neighbor. The original vertex set is both the domain and codomain of the bijection. See Question 1.1 on PDF page 2.

**Definition 1.2 (Subgroup generated by the hops).**

$$\forall V \in \mathrm{Type},\; \forall G \in \operatorname{SimpleGraph}\left(V\right),\; \operatorname{leapGroup}\left(G\right) = \operatorname{closure}\left(\{h \mid \operatorname{IsHop}\left(G, h\right)\}\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/LeapGroups/LeapGroup.leapGroup` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Mark Schachner and Gabe Udell (2026). *The group generated by a graph’s derangements*. DOI: [10.48550/arXiv.2610.18463](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.18463). URL: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18463v1>.

*Commentary.*

The closure is the subgroup closure in the full permutation group of the given vertex type. See Question 1.3 on PDF page 2.

**Definition 1.3 (Stabilizer of the unordered parity fibers).**

$$\forall V \in \mathrm{Type},\; \forall p \in V \to \operatorname{ZMod}\left(2\right),\; \operatorname{parityStabilizer}\left(p\right) = \operatorname{stabilizer}\left(\operatorname{Perm}\left(V\right), \left\{\{x \mid \operatorname{p}\left(x\right) = 0\}, \{x \mid \operatorname{p}\left(x\right) = 1\}\right\}\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/LeapGroups/LeapGroup.parityStabilizer` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The action sends each subset to its image under the permutation and acts on the outer set by taking the image of every member. The fibers may be empty.

**Theorem 1.4 (Two cross-edges suffice for a transposition).**

$$\forall V \in \mathrm{Type},\; \forall G \in \operatorname{SimpleGraph}\left(V\right),\; [\operatorname{DecidableEq}\left(V\right)] \forall h \in \operatorname{Perm}\left(V\right),\; \operatorname{IsHop}\left(G, h\right) \Rightarrow \left(\forall a \in V,\; \forall c \in V,\; \operatorname{Adj}\left(G, \operatorname{inv}\left(h, a\right), c\right) \Rightarrow \left(\operatorname{Adj}\left(G, \operatorname{inv}\left(h, c\right), a\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{swap}\left(a, c\right) \in \operatorname{leapGroup}\left(G\right)\right)\right)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/LeapGroups/LeapGroup.swap_mem_of_cross_edges` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Postcompose the hop with the transposition. Only the two preimages of its endpoints can change their images, and the cross-edges supply adjacency there. The new hop times the inverse of the old hop is the transposition.

**Theorem 1.5 (Same-class swaps and a hop generate the stabilizer).**

$$\forall V \in \mathrm{Type},\; \forall G \in \operatorname{SimpleGraph}\left(V\right),\; [\operatorname{Finite}\left(V\right)] [\operatorname{DecidableEq}\left(V\right)] \forall p \in V \to \operatorname{ZMod}\left(2\right),\; \left(\forall x \in V,\; \forall y \in V,\; \operatorname{Adj}\left(G, x, y\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{p}\left(y\right) = \operatorname{p}\left(x\right)+1\right) \Rightarrow \left(\left(\forall x \in V,\; \forall y \in V,\; \operatorname{p}\left(x\right) = \operatorname{p}\left(y\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{swap}\left(x, y\right) \in \operatorname{leapGroup}\left(G\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\forall h \in \operatorname{Perm}\left(V\right),\; \operatorname{IsHop}\left(G, h\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{leapGroup}\left(G\right) = \operatorname{parityStabilizer}\left(p\right)\right)\right)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/LeapGroups/LeapGroup.eq_parityStabilizer` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Every hop exchanges parity. A permutation preserving parity sends each vertex within its orbit under the available swaps, so the transposition closure theorem supplies membership. Multiplication by the fixed hop reduces an exchanging permutation to a preserving one.
