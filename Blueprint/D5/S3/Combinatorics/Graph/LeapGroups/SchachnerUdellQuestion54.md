# Schachner–Udell Question 5.4

## Abstract

The exact leap subgroups of all prisms and positive-dimensional cubes.

**Definition 1.1 (Prism graph).**

$$\forall n \in \mathrm{Nat},\; \operatorname{prism}\left(n\right) = \operatorname{boxProd}\left(\operatorname{complete}\left(\operatorname{ZMod}\left(2\right)\right), \operatorname{circulant}\left(\operatorname{ZMod}\left(n\right), \left\{1\right\}\right)\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/LeapGroups/SchachnerUdellQuestion54.prism` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The first factor is the complete graph on the two residues. The circulant on the second factor has step one in either direction. For n at least three this gives the n-cycle, with all wraparound edges.

**Definition 1.2 (Prism parity).**

$$\forall n \in \mathrm{Nat},\; \forall h : (2 \mid n), \forall x \in \operatorname{ZMod}\left(2\right) \times \operatorname{ZMod}\left(n\right),\; \operatorname{prismParity}\left(h, x\right) = \operatorname{fst}\left(x\right)+\operatorname{castHom}\left(h, \operatorname{snd}\left(x\right)\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/LeapGroups/SchachnerUdellQuestion54.prismParity` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The ring homomorphism reduces the cycle index modulo two. Its existence uses the divisor proof h. On the stated cycle range the formula is the residue of the sum of the two least nonnegative coordinates.

**Definition 1.3 (Cube parity).**

$$\forall n \in \mathrm{Nat},\; \forall x \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right) \to \mathrm{Bool},\; \operatorname{cubeParity}\left(x\right) = \sum_{i \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right)} \operatorname{bit}\left(\operatorname{x}\left(i\right)\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Combinatorics/Graph/LeapGroups/SchachnerUdellQuestion54.cubeParity` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The bit function takes false to zero and true to one in ZMod(2). The resulting sum is the Hamming weight reduced modulo two.

**Theorem 1.4 (Exact subgroup equalities on the original vertices).**

$$(\forall n \in \mathrm{Nat},\; 3 \leq n \Rightarrow \left(\operatorname{Odd}\left(n\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{leapGroup}\left(\operatorname{prism}\left(n\right)\right) = \operatorname{Perm}\left(\operatorname{ZMod}\left(2\right) \times \operatorname{ZMod}\left(n\right)\right)\right)) \land \left((\forall n \in \mathrm{Nat},\; 4 \leq n \Rightarrow \forall h : (2 \mid n), \operatorname{leapGroup}\left(\operatorname{prism}\left(n\right)\right) = \operatorname{stabilizer}\left(\operatorname{Perm}\left(\operatorname{ZMod}\left(2\right) \times \operatorname{ZMod}\left(n\right)\right), \left\{\{x \mid \operatorname{prismParity}\left(h, x\right) = 0\}, \{x \mid \operatorname{prismParity}\left(h, x\right) = 1\}\right\}\right)) \land (\forall n \in \mathrm{Nat},\; 1 \leq n \Rightarrow \operatorname{leapGroup}\left(\operatorname{hypercube}\left(n\right)\right) = \operatorname{stabilizer}\left(\operatorname{Perm}\left(\operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right) \to \mathrm{Bool}\right), \left\{\{x \mid \operatorname{cubeParity}\left(x\right) = 0\}, \{x \mid \operatorname{cubeParity}\left(x\right) = 1\}\right\}\right))\right)$$
