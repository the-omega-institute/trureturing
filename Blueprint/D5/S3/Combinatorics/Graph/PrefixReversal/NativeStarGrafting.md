# Actual One-Contact Native E Grafting

## Abstract

An actual retained b-edge at one complete contact circle allows a whole deletion star to be grafted onto a Hamilton cycle.

Configurations are permutations of Fin(m+1), and a, b, c reverse the first m+1, m, m-1 positions. SupplierStar(z) is the independently defined deletion star of the last label of z, with the remaining tuple circle fixed up to whole reversal. GraftDomain(U,z) is U union SupplierStar(z), and domainGraph(D) is the actual three-generator Cayley graph induced on D. OldVertices(U) and UnionVertices(U,z) denote the corresponding subtypes. liftOld(z,u) includes an old vertex into that union. The contact and all gap circles are specified independently of the chosen paths. configurationEdges(w) maps the actual typed edge list of w through the subtype value map. OldUncut(w,x,y,e) means e occurs in that old list and differs from the actual contact {x.val,y.val}. NewUncut(z,e) means e is a NativeCircleEdge in a nonzero gap and differs from its literal b cut. ESeam(z,e) means e is {z_i*b,z_i*b*c} for an actual gap i.

*Commentary.*

Assume m is at least three, w is an actual Hamilton cycle on an independent old domain U, x and y are old vertices with y=x*b, and their actual undirected edge occurs in w. Assume also that U meets SupplierStar(x) exactly in the complete circle Circle(x). Then there is an actual Hamilton cycle q on their full union, based at the configuration x. Every old edge other than {x,y} occurs in q. In each nonzero supplier gap, every native a/b edge other than that gap's actual b cut occurs in q. For every supplier gap i, the actual c edge from z_i*b to z_i*b*c also occurs in q, including the final return to gap zero. For every arbitrary undirected configuration edge e, membership in configurationEdges(q) is equivalent to OldUncut(w,x,y,e), NewUncut(x,e), or ESeam(x,e). Thus the full edge set contains exactly these three classes. The retained edges and new seams belong to this same constructed cycle, including old c edges; the theorem does not choose unrelated witnesses.

Open the supplied old cycle at its actual contact edge, rotate and reverse as needed, and use the resulting complete Hamilton path from x to y as gap zero. At each other gap z_i=x*(b*c)^i, use the existing complete native b-complement from z_i to z_i*b. Lift these actual walks to the union's induced graph. The independent star coordinate partition exhausts all added vertices. Its gap circles are disjoint, and the one-contact assumption excludes every nonzero gap from the old domain. Thus the full piece supports are disjoint and contain every union vertex exactly once.

Each consecutive pair is joined by the actual generator c, since z_i*b*c=z_(i+1). The previously proved complete E trace earns (b*c)^m=1, so the final c edge returns to gap zero. PathRingSplicing constructs a genuine Hamilton cycle from these actual full paths and real connections. Opening the old cycle removes exactly its contact edge. The native complement inventory retains every other a/b edge in a new circle, and injective induced edge maps and the exact spliced edge list carry both inventories into q. Adjacent indexed pieces place every interior seam into that literal edge list. The final piece is identified from the list's actual last element, and (b*c)^m=1 identifies the initial closing edge as the final supplier seam. Exact opening and complete native complement equivalences characterize every piece edge. An exhaustive classification of the literal chainEdges list shows that any remaining edge is one of the consecutive c seams. The closing first edge is the last E seam. These are both directions of the complete edge inventory.
