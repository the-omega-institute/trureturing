## Abstract

Symmetric A A Exclusion

The actual A/A parameters force adjacent high leaves and a closed twelve-cycle in the actual high graph. At most three actual lows join two cycle points, while the twelve high points require twelve incidences from eight lows, giving the contradiction 12 <= 8 + 3. No cube embedding is needed for this branch.

This module contributes to the live proof path of SymmetricSliceExclusion.result. Private helper declarations are proof organization and carry no independent novelty claim.
