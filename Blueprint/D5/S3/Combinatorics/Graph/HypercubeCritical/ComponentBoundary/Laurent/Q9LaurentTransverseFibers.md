# Shared-triple deletion and transverse fibers

## Abstract

Neighbor partitions stay constant on transverse paths.

**Theorem 1.1 (Neighbor partitions stay constant on transverse paths).**

*Commentary.*

After deleting edges in the common triple, every two-step move retains a nonshared block. Two distinct realized partitions have only the deleted block in common, so the partition is constant along the resulting paths.
