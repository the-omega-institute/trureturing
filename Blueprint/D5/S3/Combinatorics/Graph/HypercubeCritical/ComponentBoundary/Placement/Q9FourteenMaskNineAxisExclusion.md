*Commentary.*

Hong, Wu and Xia ask whether f2(Q9) equals three in Problem 2 of Graph Sensitivity of Cartesian Products with Matched Bridges (https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09483v1#Thmproblem2). A distance-two contact pair of saturated components gives a graph on the active axes. Coordinate translations force the additional common-neighbor condition at every cubic edge. The graph counting obstruction then excludes the fourteen-mask case on nine axes. Other contact graphs remain possible under this theorem.
