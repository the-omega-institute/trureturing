*Commentary.*

Each occurring pair of axes in these three-axis displacements has at least three extensions by signed coordinate cancellation. The triple-system bound leaves only the complete family on five axes below fifteen. The signed boundary at a tight contact cell excludes that complete family when the initial support is balanced and cubic. <https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09483v1#Thmproblem2>
