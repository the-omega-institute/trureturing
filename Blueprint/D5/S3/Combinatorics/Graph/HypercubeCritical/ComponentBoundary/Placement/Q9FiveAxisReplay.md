# Kernel replay of the five-axis certificates

## Abstract

Every selected input is proved from the same signed normalized geometry. Resolution hints then derive the empty clause in the Lean kernel.

Each initial clause is tied to the signed geometry of its own orbit representative. The replay groups the resolution hints into batches and checks every resulting proof term in Lean.
