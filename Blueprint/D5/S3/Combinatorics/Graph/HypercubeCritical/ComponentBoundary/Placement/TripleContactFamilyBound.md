## Abstract

Triple systems with at least three extensions of each occurring pair.

*Commentary.*

Counting triples through each axis gives the bound for at least eight axes. On seven axes the only lower count would force every link to be the complete graph on four axes, hence each component of the pair graph would have five axes. On six axes, two absent pairs would force an unused axis. At least four links therefore have at least eight edges, and every link has at least six. On five axes the lower bound fills all ten triples.
