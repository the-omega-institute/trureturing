*Commentary.*

In a hypothetical 258-vertex subcubic set, two disjoint components support universal weighted boundary kernels. The smaller has at most 126 vertices by isotropic edge counts. Each eight-column positive block requires a full star and distinct axes have disjoint 32-vertex footprints. At least six axes therefore carry a fixed occupied-connected block of at least nine columns with a strictly positive switched kernel at positive weights. This is an actual necessary condition for Hong–Wu–Xia Problem 2. https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09483v1#Thmproblem2
