*Commentary.*

Suppose a cube edge outside two separated Huang-balanced cubic supports has three parallel covers from one support and two from the other.

At each endpoint the smaller side uses the same pair of directions. Its two-direction corner belongs to the smaller support at exactly one endpoint.

If both corners belonged, the four translated vertices would form a selected square. If both were absent, cubicity would force a shared fourth direction at the two ends of a selected edge, again making a square.
