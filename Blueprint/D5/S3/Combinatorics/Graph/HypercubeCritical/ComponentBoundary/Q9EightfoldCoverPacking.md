*Commentary.*

An exterior edge with eight parallel covers forces 16 selected first- shell vertices and 16 selected corners. Different eightfold edges force disjoint sets of both shells.

Each unordered edge therefore accounts for 32 distinct vertices of the balanced component. A component of order at most 186 can support at most five such edges.
