# Q9SixtyThinHalfProbe

## Abstract

Q9SixtyThinHalfProbe

Use the existing small slice obstructions to show that each coordinate half of a sixty-vertex balanced cubic support has at least twenty-two vertices. Summing coordinate cut sizes gives the lower bound on the ordered distance sum.
