# Degree classes in a twenty-vertex coordinate half

## Abstract

This case arises in Hong–Wu–Xia's Problem 2 on the nine-dimensional cube. A balanced cubic support of order 72 has 12 edges in each direction. When a coordinate half has 20 selected vertices, its internal edges exhaust the incidences between its 12 cut endpoints and its eight degree-three vertices.

**Theorem 1.1 (Every edge joins the two degree classes).**
