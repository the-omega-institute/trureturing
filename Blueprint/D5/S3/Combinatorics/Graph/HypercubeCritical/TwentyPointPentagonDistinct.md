# Distinct matching coordinates

## Abstract

Distinct matching coordinates

Five distinct actual matching coordinates determine the high vertices relative to an actual Boolean cube center. For label i, the first high vertex differs from the center on the two matching coordinates whose labels are nonadjacent to i in the first five-cycle. The matched high vertex differs on those two coordinates and its own matching coordinate.

The center takes, on each matching coordinate, the bit of the vertex carrying that matching edge. On every other coordinate it takes the bit of the zeroth high vertex. The forced matching-coordinate differences already exhaust the Hamming distance bound between each high vertex and the zeroth vertex, so all other coordinates agree. This proves the normalization for the actual vertices without a support search or a signed classification.
