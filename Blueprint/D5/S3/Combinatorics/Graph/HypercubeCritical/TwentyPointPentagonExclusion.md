Paired pentagon exclusion

Two compressed five-cycles cannot arise from the actual induced twenty-point support with independent degree-two vertices and original Huang balance. The proof follows the local twenty-point obstruction argument for Hong–Wu–Xia Section 7 Problem 2. It derives the matching and complementary cycle labeling from actual high and low incidence, rather than assuming a labeled signed graph.

The common-center negative ten-cycle excludes constant matching directions. With five distinct matching directions, the derived coordinate normalization gives two actual common-neighbor choices for each low vertex. A three-coordinate first-side low would force incompatible two-coordinate and four-coordinate choices on the opposite side by actual low independence. Hence all first-side lows are the singleton choices. These five distinct actual lows have the normalization center as a common cube neighbor; the intervening selected high vertices are the other square corners. The original Huang direction anticommutation again gives five sign reversals and a contradiction.

This excludes the paired-pentagon branch in every ambient dimension. The paired squares with leaves and the complete twenty-point obstruction are separate conclusions. The original external problem remains unresolved.
