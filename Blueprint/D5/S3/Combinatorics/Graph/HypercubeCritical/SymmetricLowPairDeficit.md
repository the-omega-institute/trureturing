## Abstract

Symmetric Low Pair Deficit

Distinct actual highs sharing a low have disjoint relevant low-neighbor capacities. Combining the capacity with weighted high-neighbor counts gives a local deficit bound; four lows and two degree-two high points force zero deficit.
