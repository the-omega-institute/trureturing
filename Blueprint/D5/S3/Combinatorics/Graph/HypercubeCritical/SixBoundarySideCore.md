# Actual auxiliary edges and partners

## Abstract

Private helpers consumed by the actual six-boundary side-gap theorem.

All helpers are private and consumed by SixBoundarySideGap. The auxiliary vertices are the actual degree-three right vertices. Ordinary left vertices with two high neighbors label core edges. Existing FifteenSliceStructure.core and its girth uniqueness helpers are reused. The code proves the exact neighbor bijection, triangle-freeness and degrees. Every ordinary left label has an actual degree-two right middle and a distinct left partner. Girth forbids shared high endpoints and cross edges between their high endpoint sets. The twelve case yields a cubic core with induced-disjoint partners; the eleven case yields one degree-two core vertex and cubic degrees elsewhere.
