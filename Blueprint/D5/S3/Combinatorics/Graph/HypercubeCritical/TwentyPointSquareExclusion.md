# Paired square nonembedding

## Abstract

Paired square nonembedding

The actual high edges a2-b4-a4-b2 form a three-step path. For i equal to one or three, expanding the compressed edges gives a five-step path from a2 through a degree-two vertex, ai, bi and another degree-two vertex to b2. The bipartition, degrees and injectivity of the high labels show that each pair of paths gives a simple eight-cycle.

Take coordinate differences relative to a2. Write a, b and c for the three directions on the shared path. For the five-step word dcbad, a0 must be at distance two from both the pair ab at a4 and the disjoint pair cd at ai. Its six possible differences either identify it with a2 or a selected low vertex, or give a forbidden high adjacency to b4 or bi.

Two words of type dbcda would share their final degree-two vertex. Two of type cdabd would share their first degree-two vertex. A shared low vertex would have three distinct high neighbors. Thus one word of each type occurs, with different fourth coordinates by the exact actual high-edge table.

Write e for the fourth coordinate of the path of type cdabd. The same distance-two constraints force a0 to have difference be. Its common degree-two neighbor with the high vertex of difference ce has difference e or bce. The first choice makes a2 the low vertex’s third neighbor. The second is adjacent to the selected low vertex of difference bc.
