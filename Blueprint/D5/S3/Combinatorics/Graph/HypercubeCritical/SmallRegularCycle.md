# Small Regular Cycle

## Abstract

Small Regular Cycle

A nonempty finite two-regular graph of girth at least eight and fewer than sixteen vertices consists of one cycle. A maximal simple path closes, its cycle exhausts the graph, and the actual induced complement is used by the slice proofs.

This module contributes to the live proof path of SymmetricSliceExclusion.result. Private helper declarations are proof organization and carry no independent novelty claim.
