# Orthogonal intertwining of regular adjacency matrices

## Abstract

Regular adjacency-cospectral graphs admit an orthogonal intertwiner fixing the constant vector.

**Theorem 1.1 (An intertwiner fixing the all-ones vector).**

*Commentary.*

For two separately regular graphs on the same finite vertex type, equality of the real adjacency characteristic polynomials gives a spectral conjugacy. On a nonempty carrier the regular degrees agree. A reflection in the common row-sum eigenspace corrects the conjugacy to fix the all-ones vector. On the empty carrier the identity already satisfies the equations.
