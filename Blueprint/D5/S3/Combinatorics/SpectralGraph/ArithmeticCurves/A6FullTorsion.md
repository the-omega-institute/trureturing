A6FullTorsion

Every finite-order point of the full actual E(Q), Ed(Q), E0(Q), E0(K) and E(K) groups is the identity. The proof handles infinity and every finite point, excludes nonidentity2-torsion and3-division exceptions, proves actual duplication and triplication coordinates, and forces any putative nonzero torsion point into the region of dyadic infinite order on E0(K).
