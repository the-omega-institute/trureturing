## Abstract

Full quadratic elliptic group descent

Every point of the full actual E0(K) group has a rational trace and a rational twist component. Their product is an injective homomorphism into E0(Q) times Ed(Q). The full short cubic has no root in K, and the full actual E(K) and E0(K) point groups have no nonidentity two-torsion. Every anti-invariant point maps to a rational point of the actual twist, including the point at infinity. No point enumeration or assumed torsion-free group replaces these full-domain statements.
