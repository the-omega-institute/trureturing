## Abstract

Full Galois-difference fiber and translated rational group

On the actual elliptic group E0(Q(sqrt43645)), fixed points of conjugation are exactly the embedded rational group. The entire fiber P-sigma(P)=T is Pstar+E0(Q). Every positive rational quartic point, with both W signs, satisfies the exact polynomial translation P=Pstar-R and has this Galois difference. Integrality is imposed on P, not on R. This module supplies the full Galois fiber. A6DyadicInfiniteOrder proves infinite order of T, full nonzero-multiple nonrationality and the received rank lower bounds. A6FullTorsion proves the full rational/quadratic torsion conclusions. A6RationalCurveIsogeny proves the full-coset obstruction in its field-universal rational coordinate-atlas representation. Exact ranks, full bases, saturation and a separate intrinsic scheme-isogeny API equivalence theorem are not claimed.
