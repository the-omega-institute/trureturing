# Complete positive-tail quartic finiteness and actual graph application

## Abstract

Complete positive-tail quartic finiteness and actual graph application

Every fixed integer a>=2 and every positive integer pair b,c are retained without ordering or coprimality. Direct sign bounds and full polynomial root fibers prove finiteness. The complete quartic is separable and squarefree and remains separable over every field extension. The original smooth projective normalization, geometric irreducibility, genus-one model and Siegel-method obligations remain open; the alternative arithmetic proof does not certify them. All A=0 fibers remain linear with B>0; both signs and exact integer recovery are retained. Actual fixed-a graph finiteness holds on arbitrary four distinct primes and does not assert emptiness or spectrum sufficiency.
