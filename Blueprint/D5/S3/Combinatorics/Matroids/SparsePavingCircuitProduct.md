*Commentary.*

The uniform core and the three cores obtained by omitting either or both bases have the same weighted insertion equations after choosing a pivot. For each omitted basis, its coefficient vanishes on the associated tuple of differences. An integral basis puts both tuples along separate intersections with the zero-weight indices. The unrestricted and doubly restricted modules then have the same product as the two singly restricted modules.
