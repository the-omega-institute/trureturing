*Commentary.*

On a nonempty ground, a rational top cycle with integral circuit readouts determines a saturated integral tuple. Clearing a common denominator gives an integral source cycle for a nonzero multiple of the tuple. Conversely, dividing an integral lift by that multiple gives a rational representative. Injectivity of joint restriction makes the representative unique. The comparison identifies the integral cycles with the original image and induces an equivalence on the two quotients.
