# Concentrating Circuit Weights within Each Block

## Abstract

Integral changes of coordinates on the fibers of a partition.

*Commentary.*

A partition of the indices gives a product of the corresponding integral coordinate modules. Choose an integral basis in each fiber so its weight vector is the fiber gcd times a single basis vector. The combined basis is supported within the original fibers and the weight is zero on every other basis coordinate.
