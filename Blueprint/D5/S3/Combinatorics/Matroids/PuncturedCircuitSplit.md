*Commentary.*

A coordinate containing both weight directions is killed by the total size and both weights. A coordinate containing neither is killed by the total size alone. Subsets containing exactly one direction give pairs indexed by their common deletion. The omitted coefficient is the first coordinate of one particular pair.
