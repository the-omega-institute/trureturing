*Commentary.*

Concentrate the weights within the blocks determined by the two faces and the pivot. Both face coefficients retain their vanishing conditions. The two faces intersect the zero-weight indices differently, so the product of the unrestricted and doubly restricted modules is equivalent to the product of the two single restrictions.
