# The Bivariate Pure-History Series

## Abstract

The pure-history series records both expenditure and length.

*Commentary.*

The coefficient of z to the power s and x to the power l in P(z,x) is the number of pure histories of expenditure s and length l. Coefficients are nonnegative integers.
