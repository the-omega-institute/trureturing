# The Height of a Weak Ascent Sequence

## Abstract

The weak ascent count bounds every entry of a weak ascent sequence.

**Theorem 1.1 (The maximum lies below the height).**

*Commentary.*

For every weak ascent sequence, its maximum entry is strictly less than one plus its weak ascent count, taking the maximum of the empty sequence as zero.
