# Parents of Vincular-Avoiding Permutations

## Abstract

Restricting a 2-41-3-avoiding permutation to its smallest letters preserves avoidance.

**Theorem 1.1 (Restriction to an initial set of letters).**

*Commentary.*

For every permutation of one through n avoiding 2-41-3 and every nonnegative m at most n, retaining only the entries at most m gives a permutation of one through m avoiding 2-41-3.
