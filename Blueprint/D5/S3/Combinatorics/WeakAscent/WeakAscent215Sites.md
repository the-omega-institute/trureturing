# Active Letters in the First Class

## Abstract

Appendable letters in the first class separate into new records, the current maximum and lower sites.

*Commentary.*

Let w be a nonempty weak ascent sequence avoiding 100, 101, 110 and 201, with maximum M and last entry L. The bound one plus the weak ascent count is strictly greater than M. Every integer strictly above M and at most that bound is appendable and does not occur in w. The letter M is appendable exactly when L = M. Every appendable letter at most M other than M is strictly less than L.
