*Commentary.*

For every n at least two, the number of pairs consisting of a Fishburn permutation of length n avoiding 2143, 1423 and 3124 and a positive insertion position for n plus one that preserves these conditions is one plus twice the binomial coefficient choosing two from n. Insertion positions range from one through n inclusive.
