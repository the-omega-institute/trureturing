*Commentary.*

For every positive real period p and real increment b with irrational b divided by p, and every positive real error epsilon, one common natural threshold M works for every N at least M and every real target T. There are integers x and z with N at most x at most three N and absolute value of x b plus z p minus T smaller than epsilon. Dense integer rotations supply an open cover of the compact circle; a finite subcover bounds all required integer indices. Translation moves the symmetric bounded indices into the prescribed positive window. Nearest-integer rounding lifts the metric back to real units.
