*Commentary.*

The ratio log(2/3) divided by log(4/3) is irrational. A rational equality would force equal positive integer powers of two and three, contradicted modulo two. Both logarithms are the actual real natural logarithms. This supplies the irrationality hypothesis for feasible multiplicity approximation.
