*Commentary.*

For every finite decidable simple graph G with no isolated vertices, the full number of actual inclusion-minimal nonempty forts of its cone equals the full minimal-fort count of G plus the full rooted choice count of G. Root-free and root-containing subsets are disjoint and exhaustive; both inverse maps recover actual subsets. No formula for either count is a hypothesis.
