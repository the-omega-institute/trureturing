*Commentary.*

For every finite nontrivial vertex type, a vertex subset is a minimal nonempty fort of the complete graph if and only if its cardinality is two. The cardinality of the entire minimal-fort family is therefore the vertex count choose two. The theorem retains arbitrary finite nontrivial carriers and proves the family equality with all two-element subsets before counting.
