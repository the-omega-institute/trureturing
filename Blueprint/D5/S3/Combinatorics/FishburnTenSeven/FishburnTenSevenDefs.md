*Commentary.*

The proposition states that for every positive n, the cardinality of each of the Fishburn permutation classes of length n avoiding respectively 1324, 2143 and 1423; 1324, 2143 and 3124; and 1324, 1423 and 3124 equals two to the power n minus n.
