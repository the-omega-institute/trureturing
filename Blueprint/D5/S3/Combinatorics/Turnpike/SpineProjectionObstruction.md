# Full and endpoint-spine triangle projections differ

## Abstract

A realizable seven-point distance multiset has a spine-feasible assignment with no full triangle coupling.

The numerical relation and linear constraints are Proposition 10 and System 11 of Elder, Marçais and Kingsford. Each interval and each numerical label is retained. The comparison fixes the entire assignment array P. The two existential coupling arrays are independent; there is no requirement to extend a chosen spine array, and no common joint distribution over all intervals is assumed.

Publication indices run from 1 to n and 1 to d. In Lean, vertex i and label q are represented by i−1 in Fin n and q−1 in Fin d. Thus I consists of ordered pairs i<j, Delta consists of ordered triples i<j<k, and S consists of triples (1,j,k) with 1<j<k. No containment pruning, endpoint fixing, or presolve restrictions are present.

Write R for the complete ordered numerical two-partition relation:

$$
R = \{\left(a,b,c: \operatorname{cube}\left(\operatorname{Fin}\left(d\right)\right)\right)\mid\left(y\left(a\right) + y\left(b\right) = y\left(c\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\neg a = b\right) \lor \left(2 \le \mu\left(a\right)\right)\right)\}
$$

For H equal to Delta or S, write E(H,P) for the following explicit existential system. All sums over R include its entire guarded relation; an empty sum is zero. The components of r are a,b,c, assigned respectively to ij,jk,ik.
