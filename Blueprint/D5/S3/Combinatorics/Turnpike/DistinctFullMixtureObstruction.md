# The all-distinct full triangle LP does not equal the actual-ruler convex hull

## Abstract

The all-distinct full containment-pruned triangle LP has an assignment outside every finite convex mixture of actual real rulers for its input.

Elder, Marçais and Kingsford, WABI 2026, DOI 10.4230/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.20, Sections 3.1–3.5 and 5, supply the guarded ordered numerical relation, triangle marginal relaxation and containment context. Proposition 10 and System 11 are the marginal-system locators. The source is licensed CC BY 4.0. The finite-mixture assertion below concerns every coordinate of the same assignment.

All indices below are zero-based, exactly as in Lean: vertices lie in Fin n, labels in Fin d, I(n) contains every pair (i,j) with i<j, and Delta(n) contains every triple i<j<k. The complete ordered relation R(y,mu) contains every label triple (a,b,c) satisfying y_a+y_b=y_c and (a≠b or mu_a≥2); both orders of distinct summands remain. In formulas triple(r) is the underlying triple of a relation member. coordinate(0,r), coordinate(1,r), coordinate(2,r) select its first, second and outer labels; edge(tau,0), edge(tau,1), edge(tau,2) select ij,jk,ik.

The closed multiplicity-rank-block intersection mask is A(e,q)=rankMask(mu,e,q). For e=(i,j), its value is one precisely when (i+1)(n−j)≤sum_{r≤q}mu_r and 1+sum_{r<q}mu_r≤choose(n,2)−choose(j−i+1,2)+1; otherwise it is zero. These are the original rank bounds, with no extra pruning or endpoint restriction.

Realizes(y,mu,x) means strict increase of the real-valued ruler, a numerical label for every interval difference, and exactly mu_q occurrences of y_q for every label. The formulas below expand these conditions as well as the full feasible system and the finite convex mixture. indicator(E) is one if E is true and zero otherwise. Cardinalities are expressed as finite sums of these indicators.

*Commentary.*

For every real ruler x realizing this input, x_3−x_2 differs from y_8=7. Subtract x_0 from every coordinate. Each noninitial coordinate is an input label because it is the difference on interval (0,i), so it is an integer between one and seventeen. The initial coordinate becomes zero. Strict increase and all fifteen interval-difference histograms transfer exactly to the resulting natural coordinates z.

If z_3−z_2=7, write z=(0,a,b,b+7,d,f). Strict increase and the bound seventeen give 1≤a≤7, a+1≤b≤8, b+8≤d≤16 and d+1≤f≤17. These bounds contain exactly 210 configurations. For each such configuration, at least one of the counts of distances 6, 5, 1 and 3 differs from one. The finite histogram calculation therefore excludes every such configuration, using necessary counts from the entire input. It assumes no classification or selected catalog of realizing rulers.

The feasible assignment has P[(2,3),8]=1/2. For every actual real ruler realizing this same input, the corresponding indicator of x_3−x_2=7 is zero. Thus every finite convex mixture of arbitrary such real rulers has coordinate zero there. The mixture equation for this entire assignment would give 1/2=0, a contradiction. The generic mixture_forbidden theorem supplies this final implication. This refutes the stated universal assertion while retaining its realizability premise and its restriction that every distance multiplicity is one.
