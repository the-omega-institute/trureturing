# Successor multiplicities and powers through 24

## Abstract

Initial powers of the height-seven column quotient

Each of the 50 classes has a multiset of successor classes. The multiplicity of a class is the entry of the quotient matrix. Its action sums the entries of a vector over that multiset. The table starts with one on each class and the kernel verifies all 24 successive multiplications through exponent 24.
