# Powers from 25 through 48

## Abstract

Later powers of the height-seven column quotient

The first step multiplies the checked vector at exponent 24 by the same quotient matrix. Every subsequent step through exponent 48 is checked componentwise. Appending these vectors to the initial table gives the powers used by the full-state height-seven certificate.
