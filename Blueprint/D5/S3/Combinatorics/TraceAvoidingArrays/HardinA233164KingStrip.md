# Horizontal, vertical and both diagonal exclusions

## Abstract

The full generating series of normalized four-column eight-colour king strips.

OEIS A233164, revision 8, defines literal n by four arrays with colours zero through seven. Every horizontal, vertical and both diagonal bonds exclude equal colours and colours summing to seven. There is no wraparound. The upper-left value is zero. Every occurrence of 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 has a strictly earlier 1; every 3, 4 or 5 has a strictly earlier 2; every 4 has a strictly earlier 3, in row-major order. Colours may be absent, and no precedence condition is imposed on 7. No group action or orbit divisor enters the count.

*Commentary.*

The complementary pairs are {0,7}, {1,6}, {2,5} and {3,4}. A pair boundary with adjacent entries distinct is productive precisely when its endpoints differ. Its alternating type has first equal to third and second equal to fourth. Once 1, 2 and 3 have appeared, every legal next row preserves the productive type, with 32 colour-row continuations in the alternating type and 16 otherwise. The finite certificates verify these counts, preservation, and saturation of every two-row seed. There are 16 alternating seeds and 48 other productive seeds. The continuation induction gives a(n+2)=16 times 32 to the n plus 48 times 16 to the n for every natural n. There are 11 first rows.
