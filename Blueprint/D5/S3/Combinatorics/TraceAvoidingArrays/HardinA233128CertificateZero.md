# Single-row transfer identities

## Abstract

Internal single-row certificate for the seven-column square strip.

Exact scanner fibres turn the individual-colour sum into a sum over proper label rows. A proper seven-cell label row is determined by its first label and six choices between the two remaining labels. Thus there are 3 times 2^6 = 192 rows to check. Their weighted vertical compatibility sums depend only on the completed boundary class, or on the sector before colour 2. These one-row identities yield the finite_certificate theorem of D5.S3.Combinatorics.TraceAvoidingArrays.HardinA233128SquareStrip.
