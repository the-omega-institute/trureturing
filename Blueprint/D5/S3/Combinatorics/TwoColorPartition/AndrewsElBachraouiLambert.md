*Commentary.*

For every natural number n, the coefficient of degree n in the finite shifted Lambert sum, increased by two, equals the number of positive divisors of 2n plus 5. The correspondence sends a summand indexed by j to the odd divisor 2j plus 3 and removes the two endpoint divisors.
