*Commentary.*

Let R be a commutative ring, let a, q and z belong to R, and let N be natural. If q raised to N and z raised to N are zero, then the product over i below N of (1 minus a z q raised to i) times the inverse of (1 minus z q raised to i) equals the sum over r below N of z raised to r times the corresponding finite product with factors (1 minus a q raised to i) and inverses of (1 minus q raised to i plus 1).
