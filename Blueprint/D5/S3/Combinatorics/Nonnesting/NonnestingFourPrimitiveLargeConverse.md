# Adding the Doubled Largest Prefix

## Abstract

An increasing-order tail yields an avoider after a doubled largest prefix.

*Commentary.*

For n at least two, prefixing an increasing-order avoider of size n minus one with two copies of n gives a four-pattern avoider of size n.
