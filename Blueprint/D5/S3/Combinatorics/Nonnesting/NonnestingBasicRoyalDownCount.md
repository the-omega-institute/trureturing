# Downstep-Weighted Dyck Enumeration

## Abstract

Adjacent downsteps determine a weight on Dyck paths.

*Commentary.*

For semilength n, the downstep weight sum is the sum over all Dyck paths of two to the power of their number of adjacent downstep pairs.
