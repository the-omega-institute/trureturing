# Catalan Enumeration of Level Sequences

## Abstract

The level sequences avoiding 101 and 102 are counted by the Catalan numbers.

*Commentary.*

For every positive n, the number of level sequences of length n avoiding 101 and 102 equals the Catalan number at index n.
