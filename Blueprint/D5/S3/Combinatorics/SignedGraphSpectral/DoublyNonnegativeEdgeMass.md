*Commentary.*

A positive semidefinite entrywise nonnegative matrix supported on a graph splits into two such matrices on the sides of a valid cut. Their Gram vectors come from orthogonal projection onto the span of the left-side vectors and the orthogonal remainder. Both total entry mass and the sum of square roots over edges add across the cut.
