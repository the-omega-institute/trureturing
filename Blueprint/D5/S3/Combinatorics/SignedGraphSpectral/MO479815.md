# MO479815 signed-graph spectral deletion

## Abstract

A pendant-edge counterexample to signed-graph spectral-deviation monotonicity.

**Definition 1.1 (The source monotonicity claim).**

$$\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall G \in \operatorname{SimpleGraph}\left(Fin\left(n\right)\right),\; \forall sigma \in \operatorname{Sym2}\left(Fin\left(n\right)\right) \to Bool,\; \forall u \in Fin\left(n\right),\; \forall v \in Fin\left(n\right),\; (\operatorname{SimpleGraph.Adj}\left(G, u, v\right)) \Rightarrow (beta\left(\operatorname{SimpleGraph.deleteEdges}\left(G, \operatorname{Set.singleton}\left(\operatorname{Sym2.mk}\left(u, v\right)\right)\right), sigma\right) \le beta\left(G, sigma\right))$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Combinatorics/SignedGraphSpectral/MO479815.claim` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Shahul Hameed K, Biju K and Zoran Stanić (2025). *Spectral measures of balance for signed graphs*. URL: <https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/v93.p198>.

*Commentary.*

For every finite simple graph, every Boolean signing of unordered vertex pairs, and every edge, delete the edge on the same vertex carrier and compare the full decreasing real spectra, with multiplicity, of the signed and underlying adjacency matrices. The source quantity is the sum of the squared coordinate differences, beta_A(Sigma). The claim asserts that this quantity cannot increase after deletion; no connectedness, non-bridge, non-isolation, sign, or dimension restriction is imposed.

**Theorem 1.2 (The monotonicity claim is refuted).**

$$\neg (\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall G \in \operatorname{SimpleGraph}\left(Fin\left(n\right)\right),\; \forall sigma \in \operatorname{Sym2}\left(Fin\left(n\right)\right) \to Bool,\; \forall u \in Fin\left(n\right),\; \forall v \in Fin\left(n\right),\; (\operatorname{SimpleGraph.Adj}\left(G, u, v\right)) \Rightarrow (beta\left(\operatorname{SimpleGraph.deleteEdges}\left(G, \operatorname{Set.singleton}\left(\operatorname{Sym2.mk}\left(u, v\right)\right)\right), sigma\right) \le beta\left(G, sigma\right)))$$

*Commentary.*

The literal universal claim is false. On Fin 4 take the paw graph with edges 01, 02, 12 and 03, give every edge the negative sign, and delete the pendant edge 03. The underlying matrices are the paw matrix and K3 disjoint union K1. If a, b, c are the three ordered roots of x^3 - x^2 - 3x + 1 in (2,3), (0,1), and (-2,-1), respectively, the full ordered spectra are (a,b,-1,c), (-c,1,-b,-a) before deletion and (2,0,-1,-1), (1,1,0,-2) after deletion. Thus beta before is 4(1-b)^2 and beta after is 4, with a strict increase.

The Lean proof establishes the graph and signing correspondence, both Hermitian matrices, characteristic polynomials, existence and ordering of all roots with multiplicity, and the strict beta inequality on the same Fin 4 carrier.
