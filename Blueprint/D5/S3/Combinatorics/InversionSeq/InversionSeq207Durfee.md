# Durfee-Square Decomposition

## Abstract

Partition diagrams split at their maximal square and produce the normalized pentagonal inverse series.

*Commentary.*

With the discrete topology on the rationals, the sum over natural sizes of q raised to the square of the size times the square of the inverse Euler denominator converges to the inverse of the pentagonal power series. The decomposition is obtained by removing the maximal square from each partition diagram and separating the right and bottom pieces.
