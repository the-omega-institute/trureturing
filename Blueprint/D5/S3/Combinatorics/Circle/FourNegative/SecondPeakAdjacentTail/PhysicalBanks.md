# Paired source arithmetic

## Abstract

Literal physical banks and inventory

Write the source chains in the physical order U,V,W,X, of lengths d,c,b,a. The constructed word orders their negative terminals as U,X,W,V. The first bank is every positive U label; the second contains every positive X label and the selected W and V bit labels. The third and fourth contain the respective complementary W and V labels.

The existing chamberLabels splitter preserves every occurrence. Its weighted sum theorem and the native source chains give all eight old and new bank masses from the same physical inventory. A mask and its complement partition a full chain even when either selected bank is empty.
