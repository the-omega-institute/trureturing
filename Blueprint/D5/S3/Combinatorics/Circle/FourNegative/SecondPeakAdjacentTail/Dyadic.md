# Paired source arithmetic

## Abstract

Dyadic residues and exact integer quotients

Euclidean division c+s=a ell+j gives 0<=j<a and 2^(c+s) congruent to (-1)^ell 2^j modulo 2^a+1. The representative is 2^j for even ell and 2^a+1-2^j for odd ell, with an exact integer quotient.

Exponents in the same class modulo 2a have powers differing by a nonnegative multiple of 2^a+1. The q=4 lower orthants follow from the exponent identity and parity of ell, so they hold for every unbounded natural c.
