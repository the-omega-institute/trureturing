# Paired source arithmetic

## Abstract

High-ratio integer ceiling selection

For q>=16, the ceiling of (K-B)/L supplies the W mask when q>=32 or x>=8. Its integer residual lies between zero and L-1, while the common stock inequality places the ceiling in the original mask stock.

The identity F=2E+(qz-m)(z+1) transfers the lower residual bound to the new row. A second-window polynomial bound supplies both upper bounds. The remaining x=4,q=16 family uses exact periodic integer formulas; this includes all natural c>=3.
