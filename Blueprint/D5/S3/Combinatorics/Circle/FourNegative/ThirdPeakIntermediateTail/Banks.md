# Original-exponent substitution without caps

## Abstract

Native dyadic masks and physical sums

Each positive stock is an explicit local bit list shifted to its original physical chain start. The generic shift mass proof sums the actual values f(off+e)=2^e*F. The range-weight identity gives exactly 2^hi-2^lo. Original power-addition identities prove t=r*x, y=s*r*x and z=q*s*r*x; half and quarter powers are exact rational equalities with their original exponent lower bounds.

Bank0 mass is (z/2-q) times the V head. Bank1 mass is (y/2-(p+1)) times the W head, where p=h in I with r=1, p=sr/2-1 in I with r>=2, p=r-1 in II and p=1 in III. These scalars are derived from actual masks rather than supplied numerically.

For I the bank2 mass is (p-h) times the W head plus (x/2-1) times the X head, and bank3 is full U plus (q-1) times the V head plus h times the W head. For II bank2 is (q/2) times the V head plus (r-2) times the W head plus full X; bank3 is full U plus (q/2-1) times V plus one W. III has the same V split and final bank, with no bank2 W stock.

The mass theorem treats both old and new rows by substituting the native base vector or enlarged base vector. Rational arithmetic only expresses exact coefficients; actual labels, centered values and all strip sums remain integers. Original exponent inequalities choose exactly one of I with r=1, I with r>=2, II and III and prove the corresponding universal window hypotheses.

At r=2 or q=2 the corresponding empty bit intervals contribute zero. The same mass formulas cover these boundary tuples and all larger original exponents in 2<=a<=d<b<c.
