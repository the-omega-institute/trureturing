# Original labels and exact unbounded domain

## Abstract

Small-b families including the literal exception

The unbounded small_A,small_B,small_C constructions cover (b,c-a)=(2,1),(2,2),(3,2), using the residue selectors above their thresholds and explicit boundary constructions below them.

Inside small_A at a=4, the sole exceptional point (4,2,5,4) uses pre=[13,11,6,10] and post=[12,7,14,9,5,4,8,2,1,0,3]. The local physical proof inserts the fresh label 15 at cut 4. It is a necessary branch of the complete unbounded endpoint and has no standalone fixed-positive theorem.
