Paired after-W windows

With x=sy, s>=2,y>=4,z>=2sy, the exact slacks combine the chosen r,v with delta=rA-vB and epsilon=2rA-vSU. Only the selected residue branch must satisfy the selector premises. All eight old/new windows are preserved.
