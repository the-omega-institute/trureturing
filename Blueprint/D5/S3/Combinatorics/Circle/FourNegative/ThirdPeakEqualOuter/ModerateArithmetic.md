# Original labels and exact unbounded domain

## Abstract

Moderate high and near selector windows

For a<c<=a+b-2, put m=2^(c-a), so m>=2 and 4m<=y. Exact polynomial inequalities provide the first two paired windows. The high case x>=4y and the near case x=2y use different selectors; separate m=2 and m>=4 cases determine the W-ear contribution.
