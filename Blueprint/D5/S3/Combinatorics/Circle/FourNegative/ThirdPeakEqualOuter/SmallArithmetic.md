# Original labels and exact unbounded domain

## Abstract

Unbounded small-b residue selectors

Three parameter families are represented by (b,c-a)=(2,1),(2,2),(3,2). Their fixed dyadic y,m values leave s=2^(a-b) unbounded. Exact residue bounds and selector certificates prove the after-W windows above the family thresholds; this is a proof for all qualifying s rather than finite enumeration of exponents.
