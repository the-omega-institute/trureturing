# Signed ceiling and parity remainder

## Abstract

Signed ceiling and parity remainder

The signed integer ceiling works for negative numerators as well as positive ones. Choosing M=D*ceil((K-B+L)/(D*L))-1 places the second remainder in [0,D*L), and a dyadic D makes all low bits available. Parity adjustment chooses j in the required congruence class and proves the exact first remainder before converting integers to natural masks.
