# Integral universal tail inequalities

## Abstract

Integral universal tail inequalities

The universal rational coefficient inequalities are transported to the exact integer endpoint polynomials. The casts preserve the strict direction of each inequality and all original cone hypotheses. These consumed bounds supply second-window stock, first-window slack and the six universal parity tails.
