# Physical construction

## Abstract

Exact strict physical inventories

For every 2<=a<c<b,d=a, take W masks full xor (s-1), (s-1) xor (t-1), and t-1, and complementary V masks v-1 and full xor (v-1). The selector proof establishes the literal bit-submask t-1 subset s-1, including the binary 101 boundary, rather than only t<=s. Banks in W,X,U,V order are the first W mask; all X; all U plus the selected V mask plus the middle W mask; remaining V plus the last W mask. Insert actual fresh k at the end of the X-positive bank immediately before nX. The cut is the actual prefix length; no popcount API or cyclic transport is needed.
