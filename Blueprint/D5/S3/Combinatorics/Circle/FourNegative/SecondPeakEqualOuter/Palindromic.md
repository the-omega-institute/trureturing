# Physical construction

## Abstract

The actual palindromic word and its single exchange

For b>=a+2,c=b use banks all V; W(M1); all X plus W(M2 xor M1); all U plus W(full xor M2), with terminal order V,W,X,U. In exactly the x=4,q=8 case exchange the actual first U and X occurrences: PX becomes U(1) plus the complementary X stock, and PU becomes X(1) plus the complementary U stock. Each physical occurrence remains once. The ear slot is selected from the sign of corrected ep0 and is realized by a literal prefix-list length. The old and new endpoint checks consume the corrected W and all three dyadic X regimes.
