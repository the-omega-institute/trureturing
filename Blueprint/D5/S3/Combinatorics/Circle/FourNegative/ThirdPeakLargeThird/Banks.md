# Original labels and exact unbounded domain

## Abstract

Physical banks partition original labels

Each stock is the increasing decoder of selected binary exponents of one actual positive chain. A mask and its XOR complement partition the complete stock. The banks retain the U,V,W,X physical labels and four actual terminal labels. Inventory and source-weight identities track both rows with the same labels and cuts; coincident numerical weights do not identify occurrences.
