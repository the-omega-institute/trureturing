# Original labels and exact unbounded domain

## Abstract

Adjacent diagonal ceiling payment

On b=a and d=a+1, set t=2x. The polynomial identity 8(u+4)^3-7(u+4)^2-23(u+4)-3=8u^3+89u^2+305u+305 supplies a positive margin for every x>=4. The constant is 305.

If z<=2x^3 the payment is zero. Otherwise z=4px^3 with p>=1, and the ceiling has the exact decomposition v=p(2x^2-1)+q, 0<=q<p. This gives the literal payment bit and the paired diagonal windows, including the upper boundary of the zero-payment branch.
