# Original labels and exact unbounded domain

## Abstract

Literal paid bit in the original V stock

For every a,c put p=0 if c<=3a+1, otherwise p=2^(c-3a-2). In the paid branch p=2^r, the exact decomposition v=2^r(2x^2-1)+q with q<2^r and odd coefficient proves testBit(v,r)=true. Thus p is a genuine submask of v. A numerical inequality p<=v is insufficient and is not used as a containment argument.
