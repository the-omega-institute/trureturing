The public theorem asserts a full physical strict-J competitor OR the conjunction of an actual LARGE occurrence in the ORIGINAL A and the original two-generation placement alternative. Even L gives f(gamma)=L/2>0 with abs(f(gamma))<Q; odd L gives f(gamma)=-M for M=(N-L)/2 and D<L<M<P<Y. Neither predecessor availability nor a LARGE occurrence in A is an assumption. The competitor retains Legal(V), V.Nodup, InStrip(f,C,0,V), every natural prefix bound, inserted and returned physical lists I,Ret, the exact (V++Ret).Perm(W++I), and J(V)<J(W).

Here is the actual normalization when A has no LARGE occurrence. Classical integer order gives abs(f(i))<Q for EVERY i in A. Let a0=mass(f,A).
