# Arithmetic separation of all physical weights

## Abstract

Arithmetic separation of all physical weights.

For every natural r including zero, d=18r+12 and B=2^d. The integer Q=(B^2+B+1)/19 exceeds 2^(3d/2)+1. It divides 2^(3d)-1, while the strict size bound excludes divisibility of 2^a-1 or 2^a+1 for every positive a at most 3d/2. Every proper divisor of 3d is at most half; consequently 2 has exact order 3d modulo Q. Squaring and the same bound exclude every power -1 with exponent below 3d.

The exact identity 4Q-a(2B+5)=1 makes a a unit modulo Q. The actual centered physical weight is 19(B-1) times centered_Q(a*2^i). Cancellation of the nonzero integer scale, reduction modulo Q, the unit a and exact order separate every pair of original physical labels. The exclusion of opposite powers also excludes every opposite pair of actual weights, including a weight paired with itself. These private lemmas are consumed by the full permutation rigidity proof.
