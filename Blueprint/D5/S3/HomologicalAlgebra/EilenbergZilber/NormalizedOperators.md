# Structural norms and degeneracy annihilation

## Abstract

Structural norms and degeneracy annihilation

An operator is a norm when it is generated by a diagonal noninjective simplex operator with an arbitrary remaining operator, and is closed under zero, addition and negation. This is a structural predicate on finite integer combinations of letters, in all natural degrees. Priming preserves norms.

For any abelian category C with independent object and morphism universes and any bisimplicial X, realizing a norm and then applying diagonal Moore retraction gives zero. Each first-direction or second-direction degenerate input to the shuffle map becomes diagonally degenerate. Therefore the diagonal projector PInfty makes the shuffle insensitive to the two-direction normalization projector. These are the degeneracy facts required for both normalized composites.

*Commentary.*

**EZ–degeneracy kill, inner (vertical) direction (the per-summand combinatorial core).** A `(p, m+1)`-shuffle component of a chain that is *degenerate in the inner simplicial direction* (in the image of the inner degeneracy `s_i = (X_⦋p⦌).map (σ i).op : X_{p,m} → X_{p,m+1}`) is sent by the Eilenberg–Zilber component to a *diagonally* degenerate chain, hence annihilated by `PInfty`. EM Lemma I.5.3 content: `∇ ∘ (1 ⊗ s_i) = s_? ∘ ∇'` lands in a diagonal degeneracy.

In this notation, lemma ezComponent_inner_degeneracy_comp_PInfty (X : BisimplicialObject C) (p m : ℕ) (i : Fin (m + 1)) : (X.obj (Opposite.op ⦋p⦌)).map (SimplexCategory.σ i).op ≫ ezComponent X p (m + 1) ≫ diagPInfty X (p + (m + 1)) = 0.

*Commentary.*

The degenerate part of `FOne` is sent by `shuffleMap` to a diagonal degeneracy, hence is killed by `PInfty`. Concretely, after decomposing the complementary projector `𝟙 - retractionNOne ≫ inclusionNOne` bidegreewise, the resulting inner- and outer-degenerate pieces are annihilated by `ezComponent_inner_degeneracy_comp_PInfty` and `ezComponent_outer_degeneracy_comp_PInfty`.

In this notation, lemma degenerate_shuffleMap_comp_PInfty (X : BisimplicialObject C) : (𝟙 (FOne.obj X) - retractionNOne X ≫ inclusionNOne X) ≫ shuffleMap X ≫ (PInfty : FTwo.obj X ⟶ FTwo.obj X) = 0.
