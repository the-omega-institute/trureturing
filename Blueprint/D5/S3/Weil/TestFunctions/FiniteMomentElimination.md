# Finite-Moment Elimination

## Abstract

An endpoint delta jet cancels finitely many centered moments without changing the Weil correlation pairing.

**Theorem 1.1 (Finite moment elimination by an endpoint delta jet).**

$$\begin{aligned}\forall L: \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), K: \operatorname{Natural}\left(\right), epsilon: \operatorname{SignedMeasure}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right),\\hpos: \operatorname{restrict}\left(\operatorname{posPart}\left(\operatorname{toJordanDecomposition}\left(epsilon\right)\right), \operatorname{Icc}\left(0, 2 \cdot L\right)\right) = \operatorname{posPart}\left(\operatorname{toJordanDecomposition}\left(epsilon\right)\right), hneg: \operatorname{restrict}\left(\operatorname{negPart}\left(\operatorname{toJordanDecomposition}\left(epsilon\right)\right), \operatorname{Icc}\left(0, 2 \cdot L\right)\right) = \operatorname{negPart}\left(\operatorname{toJordanDecomposition}\left(epsilon\right)\right),\\f, h: \operatorname{Real}\left(\right) \to \operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), hfSmooth: \operatorname{ContDiff}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right), \operatorname{infinity}\left(\right), f\right),\\hhSmooth: \operatorname{ContDiff}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right), \operatorname{infinity}\left(\right), h\right), hfCompact: \operatorname{HasCompactSupport}\left(f\right),\\hhCompact: \operatorname{HasCompactSupport}\left(h\right), hfSupport: \operatorname{tsupport}\left(f\right) \subseteq \operatorname{Ioo}\left(-L, L\right),\\hhSupport: \operatorname{tsupport}\left(h\right) \subseteq \operatorname{Ioo}\left(-L, L\right) \Rightarrow\\\operatorname{let} b := 2 \cdot L,\\\operatorname{let} moment := \operatorname{lambda}\left(j, \operatorname{signedIntegral}\left(u, \operatorname{pow}\left(u - b, j\right), epsilon\right)\right),\\\operatorname{let} measureDistribution: \operatorname{TemperedDistribution}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right), \operatorname{Complex}\left(\right)\right) := \operatorname{toTemperedDistribution}\left(\operatorname{posPart}\left(\operatorname{toJordanDecomposition}\left(epsilon\right)\right)\right) - \operatorname{toTemperedDistribution}\left(\operatorname{negPart}\left(\operatorname{toJordanDecomposition}\left(epsilon\right)\right)\right),\\\operatorname{let} correction: \operatorname{TemperedDistribution}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right), \operatorname{Complex}\left(\right)\right) := \operatorname{sum}\left(j, \operatorname{range}\left(K + 1\right), \operatorname{smul}\left(\operatorname{complex}\left(\frac{\operatorname{pow}\left(-1, j + 1\right) \cdot moment\left(j\right)}{\operatorname{factorial}\left(j\right)}\right), \operatorname{iterate}\left(\operatorname{temperedDerivative}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right)\right), j, \operatorname{delta}\left(b\right)\right)\right)\right),\\\operatorname{let} correctedDistribution := measureDistribution + correction,\\\operatorname{let} correlation := \operatorname{weilTest}\left(f, h\right),\\\operatorname{let} hcorrelationCompact: \operatorname{HasCompactSupport}\left(correlation\right) := \operatorname{weilTestHasCompactSupport}\left(hfCompact, hhCompact\right),\\\operatorname{let} hcorrelationSmooth: \operatorname{ContDiff}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right), \operatorname{infinity}\left(\right), correlation\right) := \operatorname{weilTestContDiff}\left(hfSmooth, hhSmooth, hhCompact\right),\\\operatorname{let} correlationTest := \operatorname{toSchwartzMap}\left(correlation, hcorrelationCompact, hcorrelationSmooth\right),\\\forall test \in \operatorname{SchwartzMap}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right), \operatorname{Complex}\left(\right)\right),\; correctedDistribution\left(test\right) = \operatorname{signedIntegral}\left(u, test\left(u\right), epsilon\right) + \operatorname{sum}\left(j, \operatorname{range}\left(K + 1\right), \operatorname{complex}\left(\frac{\operatorname{pow}\left(-1, j + 1\right) \cdot moment\left(j\right)}{\operatorname{factorial}\left(j\right)}\right) \cdot \operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{complex}\left(-1\right), j\right) \cdot \operatorname{iterate}\left(\operatorname{schwartzDerivative}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right), \operatorname{Complex}\left(\right)\right), j, test\right)\left(b\right)\right) \land \forall p \in \operatorname{Polynomial}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right),\; \operatorname{natDegree}\left(p\right) \le K \Rightarrow \operatorname{signedIntegral}\left(u, \operatorname{eval}\left(p, u\right), epsilon\right) + \operatorname{sum}\left(j, \operatorname{range}\left(K + 1\right), \frac{\operatorname{pow}\left(-1, j + 1\right) \cdot moment\left(j\right)}{\operatorname{factorial}\left(j\right)} \cdot \operatorname{pow}\left(-1, j\right) \cdot \operatorname{eval}\left(\operatorname{iterate}\left(\operatorname{polynomialDerivative}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), j, p\right), b\right)\right) = 0 \land correctedDistribution\left(correlationTest\right) = measureDistribution\left(correlationTest\right).\end{aligned}$$

*Commentary.*

The signed measure is embedded canonically through its Jordan parts. A finite Taylor expansion proves polynomial annihilation, while strict doubled-window support makes every endpoint jet vanish on the constructed Weil correlation.
