# Unique Finite-Atomic Residual

## Abstract

A singular positive truncated Toeplitz matrix has one representing residual measure, exactly rank many positive atoms, and one weighted Haar-floor completion.

**Theorem 1.1 (Unique finite-atomic residual).**

$$\forall N \in \mathbb{N}, m \in \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{C}, v \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(N + 1\right) \to \mathbb{C}, alpha \in \operatorname{NonnegativeReal}\left(\right), D \in \operatorname{ContinuousMap}\left(\operatorname{Circle}\left(\right), \mathbb{C}\right),\; \left(\left(\left(\left(\left(\forall ell \in \mathbb{Z},\; m\left(\operatorname{neg}\left(ell\right)\right) = \operatorname{star}\left(m\left(ell\right)\right)\right) \land \operatorname{PosSemidef}\left(\operatorname{Matrix}\left((j,k\in\operatorname{Fin}\left(N + 1\right) \mapsto m\left(\operatorname{toInt}\left(j\right) - \operatorname{toInt}\left(k\right)\right))\right)\right)\right) \land \operatorname{dotProduct}\left(\operatorname{star}\left(v\right), v\right) = 1\right) \land \operatorname{mulVec}\left(\operatorname{Matrix}\left((j,k\in\operatorname{Fin}\left(N + 1\right) \mapsto m\left(\operatorname{toInt}\left(j\right) - \operatorname{toInt}\left(k\right)\right))\right), v\right) = 0\right) \land \operatorname{let} Tstar = \operatorname{Matrix}\left((j,k\in\operatorname{Fin}\left(N + 1\right) \mapsto m\left(\operatorname{toInt}\left(j\right) - \operatorname{toInt}\left(k\right)\right))\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\exists tau \in \operatorname{FiniteMeasure}\left(\operatorname{Circle}\left(\right)\right),\; \left(\left(\forall ell \in \mathbb{Z},\; \operatorname{natAbs}\left(ell\right) \le N \Rightarrow \operatorname{integral}\left(z, \operatorname{Circle}\left(\right), \operatorname{zpow}\left(z, \operatorname{neg}\left(ell\right)\right), tau\right) = m\left(ell\right)\right) \land \left(\forall rho \in \operatorname{FiniteMeasure}\left(\operatorname{Circle}\left(\right)\right),\; \left(\forall ell \in \mathbb{Z},\; \operatorname{natAbs}\left(ell\right) \le N \Rightarrow \operatorname{integral}\left(z, \operatorname{Circle}\left(\right), \operatorname{zpow}\left(z, \operatorname{neg}\left(ell\right)\right), rho\right) = m\left(ell\right)\right) \Rightarrow rho = tau\right)\right) \land \left(\exists point \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(\operatorname{rank}\left(Tstar\right)\right) \to \operatorname{Circle}\left(\right), weight \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(\operatorname{rank}\left(Tstar\right)\right) \to \operatorname{NonnegativeReal}\left(\right),\; \left(\left(\operatorname{Injective}\left(point\right) \land \left(\forall a \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(\operatorname{rank}\left(Tstar\right)\right),\; 0 < weight\left(a\right)\right)\right) \land \operatorname{toMeasure}\left(tau\right) = \operatorname{sum}\left(a, \operatorname{Fin}\left(\operatorname{rank}\left(Tstar\right)\right), \operatorname{smul}\left(weight\left(a\right), \operatorname{dirac}\left(point\left(a\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\exists muStar \in \operatorname{FiniteMeasure}\left(\operatorname{Circle}\left(\right)\right),\; \left(\left(\exists tau \in \operatorname{FiniteMeasure}\left(\operatorname{Circle}\left(\right)\right),\; \left(\forall ell \in \mathbb{Z},\; \operatorname{natAbs}\left(ell\right) \le N \Rightarrow \operatorname{integral}\left(z, \operatorname{Circle}\left(\right), \operatorname{zpow}\left(z, \operatorname{neg}\left(ell\right)\right), tau\right) = m\left(ell\right)\right) \land \operatorname{toMeasure}\left(muStar\right) = \operatorname{toMeasure}\left(\operatorname{smul}\left(alpha, \operatorname{normalizedCircleHaar}\left(\right)\right)\right) + \operatorname{withDensity}\left(\operatorname{toMeasure}\left(tau\right), (z \mapsto \operatorname{ofReal}\left(\operatorname{normSq}\left(D\left(z\right)\right)\right))\right)\right) \land \left(\forall nu \in \operatorname{FiniteMeasure}\left(\operatorname{Circle}\left(\right)\right),\; \left(\exists tau \in \operatorname{FiniteMeasure}\left(\operatorname{Circle}\left(\right)\right),\; \left(\forall ell \in \mathbb{Z},\; \operatorname{natAbs}\left(ell\right) \le N \Rightarrow \operatorname{integral}\left(z, \operatorname{Circle}\left(\right), \operatorname{zpow}\left(z, \operatorname{neg}\left(ell\right)\right), tau\right) = m\left(ell\right)\right) \land \operatorname{toMeasure}\left(nu\right) = \operatorname{toMeasure}\left(\operatorname{smul}\left(alpha, \operatorname{normalizedCircleHaar}\left(\right)\right)\right) + \operatorname{withDensity}\left(\operatorname{toMeasure}\left(tau\right), (z \mapsto \operatorname{ofReal}\left(\operatorname{normSq}\left(D\left(z\right)\right)\right))\right)\right) \Rightarrow nu = muStar\right)\right) \land \operatorname{toMeasure}\left(muStar\right) = \operatorname{toMeasure}\left(\operatorname{smul}\left(alpha, \operatorname{normalizedCircleHaar}\left(\right)\right)\right) + \operatorname{sum}\left(a, \operatorname{Fin}\left(\operatorname{rank}\left(Tstar\right)\right), \operatorname{smul}\left(weight\left(a\right) \cdot \operatorname{ofReal}\left(\operatorname{normSq}\left(D\left(point\left(a\right)\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{dirac}\left(point\left(a\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)$$

*Commentary.*

The residual measure is constructed from the positive semidefinite truncated moment matrix. A unit kernel vector confines its support to the roots of the associated contact polynomial.

Lagrange interpolation makes every representing measure agree on each root mass, proving uniqueness. Removing zero masses leaves positive distinct atoms, while a weighted Vandermonde Gram factorization identifies their number with the Toeplitz rank.

Multiplication by the denominator norm-square density constructs the completion. Residual uniqueness gives completion uniqueness, and density of a Dirac mass yields the displayed weighted atomic sum.
