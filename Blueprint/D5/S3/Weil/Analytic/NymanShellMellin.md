*Commentary.*

A continuous complex test W vanishing outside (1, 2) gives the scaled sum of μ(n)W(nt). Multiplication of its Mellin transform by zeta recovers the test transform by absolute convergence for Re(s) > 1. The square-root bound with arbitrary positive slack continues the identity to Re(s) > 1/2 away from s = 1 and forces the test transform to vanish at each zeta zero with 1/2 < Re(s) < 1.
