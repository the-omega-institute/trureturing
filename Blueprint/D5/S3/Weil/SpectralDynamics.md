## Theorem: Labeled zeta vectors follow the coordinate evolutions

Statement: `D5/S3/Weil/SpectralDynamics.labeled_zeta_evolution_spec` `✓ std3`

The ordering hypothesis makes the source's forward dissipative direction explicit; no reverse-domain identity is asserted.
