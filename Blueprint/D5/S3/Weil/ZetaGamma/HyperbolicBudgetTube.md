# Hyperbolic Budget Tubes

## Abstract

Local correlation bounds confine completion budgets and their extremal profiles.

**Theorem 1.1 (The hyperbolic budget tube).**

$$\forall Completion \in Type, L \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), a \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), budget \in Completion \to \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), correlation \in Completion \to \left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right) \to \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), globalCompletion \in Completion, localCompletion \in Completion,\; \left(0 < L \land \left(0 < a \land \left(\left(\forall completion \in Completion, t \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; \left|\operatorname{apply}\left(correlation, completion, t\right)\right| \le \operatorname{apply}\left(budget, completion\right)\right) \land \left(\forall t \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; \left|t\right| < \operatorname{mul}\left(2, L\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(correlation, localCompletion, t\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(correlation, globalCompletion, t\right)\right) = \operatorname{mul}\left(\operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(budget, localCompletion\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(budget, globalCompletion\right)\right), \operatorname{cosh}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(a, t\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\operatorname{mul}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(budget, globalCompletion\right), \operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{tanh}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(a, L\right)\right), 2\right)\right) \le \operatorname{apply}\left(budget, localCompletion\right) \land \operatorname{apply}\left(budget, localCompletion\right) \le \operatorname{mul}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(budget, globalCompletion\right), \operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{div}\left(\operatorname{cosh}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(a, L\right)\right), \operatorname{sinh}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(a, L\right)\right)\right), 2\right)\right)\right)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Weil/ZetaGamma/HyperbolicBudgetTube.hyperbolic_budget_tube` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The endpoint estimate is obtained from the local correlation law by closedness at the edge of the observation window. The two signs of the budget difference give the lower and upper walls separately.

**Theorem 1.2 (Natural-budget profile and exponential width bounds).**

$$\forall Completion \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right) \to Type, lambdaOne \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), budget \in \operatorname{Pi}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(Completion, L\right) \to \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), correlation \in \operatorname{Pi}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(Completion, L\right) \to \left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right) \to \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right)\right), naturalCompletion \in \operatorname{Pi}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(Completion, L\right)\right),\; \left(0 < lambdaOne \land \left(\left(\forall L \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; \operatorname{apply}\left(budget, L, \operatorname{apply}\left(naturalCompletion, L\right)\right) = \operatorname{mul}\left(2, lambdaOne\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall L \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), completion \in \operatorname{apply}\left(Completion, L\right), t \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; \left|\operatorname{apply}\left(correlation, L, completion, t\right)\right| \le \operatorname{apply}\left(budget, L, completion\right)\right) \land \left(\forall L \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), completion \in \operatorname{apply}\left(Completion, L\right), t \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; \left|t\right| < \operatorname{mul}\left(2, L\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(correlation, L, completion, t\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(correlation, L, \operatorname{apply}\left(naturalCompletion, L\right), t\right)\right) = \operatorname{mul}\left(\operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(budget, L, completion\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(budget, L, \operatorname{apply}\left(naturalCompletion, L\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{cosh}\left(\operatorname{div}\left(t, 2\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{let} lowerBudget = \operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{sInf}\left(\operatorname{range}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(budget, L\right)\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{let} upperBudget = \operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{sSup}\left(\operatorname{range}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(budget, L\right)\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{let} budgetWidth = \operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(upperBudget, L\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(lowerBudget, L\right)\right)\right), \left(\forall L \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; 0 < L \Rightarrow \left(\forall completion \in \operatorname{apply}\left(Completion, L\right),\; \operatorname{mul}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(2, lambdaOne\right), \operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{tanh}\left(\operatorname{div}\left(L, 2\right)\right), 2\right)\right) \le \operatorname{apply}\left(budget, L, completion\right) \land \operatorname{apply}\left(budget, L, completion\right) \le \operatorname{mul}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(2, lambdaOne\right), \operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{div}\left(\operatorname{cosh}\left(\operatorname{div}\left(L, 2\right)\right), \operatorname{sinh}\left(\operatorname{div}\left(L, 2\right)\right)\right), 2\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall L \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; 0 < L \Rightarrow \left(0 \le \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(2, lambdaOne\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(lowerBudget, L\right)\right) \land \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(2, lambdaOne\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(lowerBudget, L\right)\right) \le \operatorname{div}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(2, lambdaOne\right), \operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{cosh}\left(\operatorname{div}\left(L, 2\right)\right), 2\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall L \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; 0 < L \Rightarrow \left(0 \le \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(upperBudget, L\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(2, lambdaOne\right)\right) \land \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(upperBudget, L\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(2, lambdaOne\right)\right) \le \operatorname{div}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(2, lambdaOne\right), \operatorname{pow}\left(\operatorname{sinh}\left(\operatorname{div}\left(L, 2\right)\right), 2\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{IsBigO}\left(\operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(2, lambdaOne\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(lowerBudget, L\right)\right)\right), atTop, \operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{exp}\left(\operatorname{neg}\left(L\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{IsBigO}\left(\operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(upperBudget, L\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(2, lambdaOne\right)\right)\right), atTop, \operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{exp}\left(\operatorname{neg}\left(L\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{IsBigO}\left(budgetWidth, atTop, \operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{exp}\left(\operatorname{neg}\left(L\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{IsBigO}\left(\operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{max}\left(0, \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(2, lambdaOne\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(lowerBudget, L\right)\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(8, lambdaOne, \operatorname{exp}\left(\operatorname{neg}\left(L\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right), atTop, \operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{exp}\left(\operatorname{neg}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(2, L\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\operatorname{IsBigO}\left(\operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{max}\left(0, \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(upperBudget, L\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(2, lambdaOne\right)\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(8, lambdaOne, \operatorname{exp}\left(\operatorname{neg}\left(L\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right), atTop, \operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{exp}\left(\operatorname{neg}\left(\operatorname{mul}\left(2, L\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \operatorname{IsBigO}\left(\operatorname{lambda}\left(\operatorname{typed}\left(L, \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right), \operatorname{max}\left(0, \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(budgetWidth, L\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(16, lambdaOne, *Commentary.*

The lower and upper profiles are the infimum and supremum of the actual completion-budget range at each scale. Their exact hyperbolic errors give the three leading exponential estimates and the three refined second-order positive-excess bounds.
