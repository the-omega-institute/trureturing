# Möbius cancellation in the degree sum

## Abstract

Signed sums of trivial-zero root responses

Abel summation of the signed degree weights uses the uniform Möbius prefix bounds. The uniform little-o prefix bound gives a sublinear trivial-root response.

At log cutoff u≥0 the jth trivial zero contributes n/[2j(n+2j)] times exp(−2ju/n). This response is nonnegative, increases with n, and is at most half of one plus log n. Abel summation uses the shorter-prefix bound and controls the signed weighted sum.

The exact centered root formula includes the constant −log(2π). Its contribution and the summed response are bounded by a constant times m exp(−u/2).
