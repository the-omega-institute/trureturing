# Cancellation in the logarithmically weighted sum

## Abstract

Uniform logarithmic bounds for Möbius prefixes

The convolution for μ(n) log n and the Chebyshev estimate under RH bound the Möbius partial sum times log N by a constant times N. Splitting at the square root of N gives the same order uniformly over shorter prefixes.

For the little-o estimate consider the weight floor(N/n) − ψ(N/n) + log(N/n). On n≤N/L the signed floor and Chebyshev terms differ by a sublinear power of N/n. On n>N/L all three weights have bounded variation at fixed L, so Abel summation gives an error of order N/log N with a constant depending on L. First choose L large to make the first coefficient small, then let N grow. Splitting the prefix again at the square root of N yields the uniform little-o bound.
