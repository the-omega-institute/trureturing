# Uniform estimates over unit bins

## Abstract

Inverse-root masses of actual zero occurrences

Each occurrence indexes a nontrivial zero with its analytic multiplicity. The count in the half-open unit bin indexed by k is bounded by a constant times log(3+|k|).

RH and the ordinate constraint give an inverse root norm of order (1+|k|)^(−1). Absorbing the logarithm into any positive power η gives bin mass of order (1+|k|)^(η−1).
