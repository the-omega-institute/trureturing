# Integrate over the original endpoints

## Abstract

Transfer a pointwise density bound to a prime window

Assume RH. For any m≥2 put s(m)=1/2−1/(m+1). Let C exp(−s(m)u) bound the absolute signed log-density residual for u≥log 2. On ordered endpoints 2≤a≤B the integrals of the retained densities give the signed arithmetic powers and the selected root spectra.

The eventual sign of the prime-only tail gives one lower threshold for all orders and coefficients C. Integrating on [log a,log B] multiplies the endpoint power gap by C/s(m). Since s(m)≥1/6, six times C gives the claimed bound. The equal-endpoint window is included.
