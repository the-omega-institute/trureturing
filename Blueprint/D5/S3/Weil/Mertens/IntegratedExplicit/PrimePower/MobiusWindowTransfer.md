# Integrate over the original endpoints

## Abstract

Transfer a pointwise density bound to a prime window

Assume RH, a nonnegative factor for each truncation order m, and a pointwise bound by K times that factor times exp(−s(m)u) for the signed log-density residual. The retained densities are integrable and their primitives supply the arithmetic powers and the selected root spectra.

Choose the lower threshold from the eventual positive negative-tail mass. Transport its integral to the original log window. Summing the retained primitives gives the endpoint differences. The integral of exp(−s(m)u) is the endpoint power gap divided by s(m). Since s(m) is at least one sixth, the residual is bounded by six times K and the same factor times the gap. The argument includes equal endpoints.
