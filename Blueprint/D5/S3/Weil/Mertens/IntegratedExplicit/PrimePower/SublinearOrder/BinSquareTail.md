# The tail for negative exponents

## Abstract

Power tails of symmetric frequency bins

For real s<−1 and integer H>0 the sum of (1+|k|)^s over |k|>H is at most 2 H^(s+1)/(−s−1). Split the integers by sign and compare each shifted tail with the integral of the decreasing real power.
