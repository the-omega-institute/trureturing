# Local square-mass estimates on the negative half-line

## Abstract

Damped spectral sums of signed prefixes

For each integer j≥0, integrate on the cell (−j−1,−j]. The cell lies in the interval of radius one about −j and exp(v/2)≤exp(−j/2) there. Sum the local spectral integral bounds with these geometric weights.

Write A(t) for the sum of a(n) over n in J with t≤v(n). For z=1/2+iγ, integrate A(t) exp(zt) on t≤0. Each v(n)≤0 contributes a(n) exp(zv(n))/z, including v(n)=0.

Summing the finitely many modes inside the integral gives one finite phase sum against the same bounded prefix. The weighted half-line estimate bounds its norm using the square masses of all frequency bins.
