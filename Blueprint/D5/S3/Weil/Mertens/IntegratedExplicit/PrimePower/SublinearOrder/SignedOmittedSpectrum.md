# Frequency cutoff in the actual zero-occurrence sum

## Abstract

Signed sums over omitted root degrees

Fix q=m+1 and the omitted degrees 2n≥q. After extracting exp(−(1/2−1/q)u), the normalized mode has envelope exp(−u/q+u/(2n)), root coefficient −n/(ρ(n−ρ)), and phase exp(i Im(ρ)u/n).

For each low occurrence, apply finite complex Abel summation in the degree. The mode differences split into increments of the decreasing envelope, the root coefficient and the phase. Telescoping the envelope and the coordinates u/n gives total variation of order (1+|Im(ρ)|u/q)/|ρ|.

On the complementary bins use the square-mass estimate with the mask outside the cutoff. Finite occurrence estimates pass to the summable series at each degree, keeping the analytic multiplicities. When q≤u≤q^(29/28), the cutoff floor(q^(1/7)) gives a q^(27/28) coefficient under a q^(3/4) ordinary Möbius prefix bound. For larger u, the uncut spacing estimate supplies the same power.
