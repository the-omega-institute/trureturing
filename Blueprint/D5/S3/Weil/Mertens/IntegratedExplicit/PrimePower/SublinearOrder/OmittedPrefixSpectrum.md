# Signed degree cancellation in the low bins

## Abstract

The damped degree prefix for the omitted zero spectrum

For p≤n≤m use v(n)=u/n−2u/q≤0. Since u≥0, the v(n) decrease with n. The indices with t≤v(n) form an initial interval of degrees whose signed coefficient sum is a difference of two ordinary prefixes.

The local logarithmic count bounds the square bin mass by a square-root power of the number of bins with arbitrary slack.

The second coefficient has modulus at most 3/q. Summing over at most q degrees costs a constant times the number of low occurrences. The high side uses the existing weighted sampling estimate.
