# Split the omitted degrees at twice the cutoff

## Abstract

The arithmetic Möbius tail at late starting points

For q=m+1 and u≥q^(3/2), the absolute normalized arithmetic tail beyond m is bounded by C(1+q²/u) exp(−(1/2−1/q)u), with one positive C for all m≥2 and real u with exp(u)≥2.

In the pointwise Chebyshev sum, isolate the degrees q through 2q−1. Their normalized powers are bounded by a geometric sum with ratio exp(−log(t)/(2q²)). The existing truncation at 2q−1 bounds higher degrees, whose contribution relative to the first omitted power has damping exp(−log(t)/(2q)).

For q>0 and u≥q^(3/2), the quadratic bound for the exponential gives q exp(−u/(2q))≤8. Integrating the pointwise estimate for t>exp(u) uses 1+q²/log(t)≤1+q²/u.
