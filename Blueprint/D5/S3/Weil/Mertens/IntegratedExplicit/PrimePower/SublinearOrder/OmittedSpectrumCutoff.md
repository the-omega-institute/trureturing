# Degree cancellation and weighted sampling

## Abstract

Signed omitted-root frequency cutoffs

Below the cutoff, complex Abel summation takes the Möbius sum over degrees first. The weighted adjacent-phase increment is paid for by the decrease of the exponential envelope, giving a degree variation bound uniform in u.

Above the cutoff, a square sum of inverse-root bin masses controls the separated samples u/n. The two bounds hold on the same finite occurrence set. Summability at each degree extends the bound to the series with analytic multiplicities.
