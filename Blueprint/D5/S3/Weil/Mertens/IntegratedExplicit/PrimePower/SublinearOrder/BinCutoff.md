# Finite head and infinite square tail

## Abstract

Quantitative bounds for the symmetric bin cutoff

Split the signed bins into the nonnegative and negative sides. The finite sum of (1+|k|) to the power −3/4 is bounded by eight times the fourth root of one plus the cutoff. Outside the cutoff H>0, the sum with exponent −3/2 is bounded by four times H to the power −1/2.

For log root cutoff at least L times the order scale, choose the integer bin cutoff to be the ceiling of L². The fourth-root growth in the finite sum is offset by the geometric degree weights. The square tail supplies the same inverse-square-root decay in L.

Taking L=(1+log log(m+1))/8 also controls the arithmetic terms at large order. Its exponential enters the near-range Abel bound.
