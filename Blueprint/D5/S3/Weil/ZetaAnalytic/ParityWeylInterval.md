# Parity Weyl Interval

## Abstract

The affine even and odd channels determine a coordinate-independent resolvent interval and its positive spectral completions.

**Theorem 1.1 (Parity Weyl interval).**

$$\forall Even \in Type, Odd \in Type, Source \in Type, evenBase \in Even \to \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), evenBoundary \in Even \to \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), oddBase \in Odd \to \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), oddBoundary \in Odd \to \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), referenceBudget \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), source \in Source, spectralReading \in \operatorname{Measure}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right) \to Source, resolventMoment \in \operatorname{Measure}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right) \to \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; \left(\left(\forall e \in Even,\; 0 \le \operatorname{apply}\left(evenBoundary, e\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall o \in Odd,\; 0 \le \operatorname{apply}\left(oddBoundary, o\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall e \in Even,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(evenBoundary, e\right) = 0 \Rightarrow 0 \le \operatorname{apply}\left(evenBase, e\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall o \in Odd,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(oddBoundary, o\right) = 0 \Rightarrow 0 \le \operatorname{apply}\left(oddBase, o\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\exists e \in Even,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(evenBoundary, e\right) \ne 0\right) \land \left(\left(\exists o \in Odd,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(oddBoundary, o\right) \ne 0\right) \land \left(\operatorname{BddAbove}\left(\left\{\exists e \in Even,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(evenBoundary, e\right) \ne 0 \land q = \operatorname{div}\left(\operatorname{neg}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(evenBase, e\right)\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(evenBoundary, e\right)\right) \mid q \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right\}\right) \land \operatorname{BddBelow}\left(\left\{\exists o \in Odd,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(oddBoundary, o\right) \ne 0 \land q = \operatorname{div}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(oddBase, o\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(oddBoundary, o\right)\right) \mid q \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right\}\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{let} evenRatios = \left\{\exists e \in Even,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(evenBoundary, e\right) \ne 0 \land q = \operatorname{div}\left(\operatorname{neg}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(evenBase, e\right)\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(evenBoundary, e\right)\right) \mid q \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right\}, \operatorname{let} oddRatios = \left\{\exists o \in Odd,\; \operatorname{apply}\left(oddBoundary, o\right) \ne 0 \land q = \operatorname{div}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(oddBase, o\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(oddBoundary, o\right)\right) \mid q \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right\}, \operatorname{let} lower = \operatorname{add}\left(referenceBudget, \operatorname{sSup}\left(evenRatios\right)\right), \operatorname{let} upper = \operatorname{add}\left(referenceBudget, \operatorname{sInf}\left(oddRatios\right)\right), \operatorname{let} admissible = R: \operatorname{Real}\left(\right) \mapsto 0 \le R \land \left(\left(\forall e \in Even,\; 0 \le \operatorname{add}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(evenBase, e\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(\operatorname{sub}\left(R, referenceBudget\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(evenBoundary, e\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall o \in Odd,\; 0 \le \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(oddBase, o\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(\operatorname{sub}\left(R, referenceBudget\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(oddBoundary, o\right)\right)\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{let} shiftedAdmissible = delta: \operatorname{Real}\left(\right) \mapsto R: \operatorname{Real}\left(\right) \mapsto 0 \le R \land \left(\left(\forall e \in Even,\; 0 \le \operatorname{add}\left(\operatorname{add}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(evenBase, e\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(delta, \operatorname{apply}\left(evenBoundary, e\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(\operatorname{sub}\left(R, \operatorname{add}\left(referenceBudget, delta\right)\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(evenBoundary, e\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall o \in Odd,\; 0 \le \operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{sub}\left(\operatorname{apply}\left(oddBase, o\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(delta, \operatorname{apply}\left(oddBoundary, o\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{mul}\left(\operatorname{sub}\left(R, \operatorname{add}\left(referenceBudget, delta\right)\right), \operatorname{apply}\left(oddBoundary, o\right)\right)\right)\right)\right), \operatorname{let} completion = R: \operatorname{Real}\left(\right) \mapsto \exists nu \in \operatorname{Measure}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right),\; \operatorname{map}\left((x: \operatorname{Real}\left(\right) \mapsto \operatorname{neg}\left(x\right)), nu\right) = nu \land \left(\operatorname{apply}\left(spectralReading, nu\right) = source \land \operatorname{apply}\left(resolventMoment, nu\right) = R\right), \left(\forall R \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; \operatorname{apply}\left(admissible, R\right) \Leftrightarrow R \in \operatorname{Icc}\left(\operatorname{max}\left(0, lower\right), upper\right)\right) \land \left(\left(lower > upper \Rightarrow \left(\neg \left(\exists R \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; \operatorname{apply}\left(admissible, R\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\left(\forall delta \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; \left\{\operatorname{applyTwo}\left(shiftedAdmissible, delta, R\right) \mid R \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right\} = \left\{\operatorname{apply}\left(admissible, R\right) \mid R \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right\}\right) \land \left(\left(\left(\forall R \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; \operatorname{apply}\left(admissible, R\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{apply}\left(completion, R\right)\right) \land \left(\forall nu \in \operatorname{Measure}\left(\operatorname{Real}\left(\right)\right),\; \operatorname{map}\left((x: \operatorname{Real}\left(\right) \mapsto \operatorname{neg}\left(x\right)), nu\right) = nu \Rightarrow \left(\operatorname{apply}\left(spectralReading, nu\right) = source \Rightarrow \operatorname{apply}\left(admissible, \operatorname{apply}\left(resolventMoment, nu\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\forall R \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; R \in \operatorname{Icc}\left(\operatorname{max}\left(0, lower\right), upper\right) \Leftrightarrow \operatorname{apply}\left(completion, R\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)$$

*Commentary.*

The proof treats zero boundary channels by the two kernel hypotheses. For nonzero channels, conditional-completeness bounds turn affine positivity into the lower and upper Rayleigh endpoint inequalities. Direct ring identities prove invariance under recentering.
