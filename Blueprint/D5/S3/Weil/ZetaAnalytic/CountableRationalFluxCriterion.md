# Countable Rational Flux Criterion

## Abstract

Rational rectangles detect every isolated zero in the open right half-plane.

**Theorem 1.1 (Countable rational flux criterion).**

$$\forall zeros \in \operatorname{Set}\left(\operatorname{Complex}\left(\right)\right), flux \in \operatorname{Rat}\left(\right) \to \left(\operatorname{Rat}\left(\right) \to \left(\operatorname{Rat}\left(\right) \to \left(\operatorname{Rat}\left(\right) \to \operatorname{Nat}\left(\right)\right)\right)\right),\; \left(\left(\forall z \in \operatorname{Complex}\left(\right),\; \left(\operatorname{mem}\left(z, zeros\right) \land \operatorname{re}\left(z\right) > 0\right) \Rightarrow \left(\exists x0 \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), x1 \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), y0 \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right), y1 \in \operatorname{Real}\left(\right),\; x0 > 0 \land \left(x0 < \operatorname{re}\left(z\right) \land \left(\operatorname{re}\left(z\right) < x1 \land \left(y0 < \operatorname{im}\left(z\right) \land \left(\operatorname{im}\left(z\right) < y1 \land \left(\forall w \in \operatorname{Complex}\left(\right),\; \left(\operatorname{mem}\left(w, zeros\right) \land \operatorname{mem}\left(w, \operatorname{Rectangle}\left(\operatorname{ComplexMk}\left(x0, y0\right), \operatorname{ComplexMk}\left(x1, y1\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow w = z\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall a \in \operatorname{Rat}\left(\right), b \in \operatorname{Rat}\left(\right), c \in \operatorname{Rat}\left(\right), d \in \operatorname{Rat}\left(\right),\; \left(\left(a > 0 \land \left(a < b \land c < d\right)\right) \land \left(\forall z \in \operatorname{Complex}\left(\right),\; \operatorname{mem}\left(z, \operatorname{RectangleBorder}\left(\operatorname{ComplexMk}\left(\operatorname{toReal}\left(a\right), \operatorname{toReal}\left(c\right)\right), \operatorname{ComplexMk}\left(\operatorname{toReal}\left(b\right), \operatorname{toReal}\left(d\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(\neg \operatorname{mem}\left(z, zeros\right)\right)\right)\right) \Rightarrow \left(flux\left(a, b, c, d\right) = 0 \Leftrightarrow \left(\forall z \in \operatorname{Complex}\left(\right),\; *Commentary.*

Axis isolation gives a real rectangle containing only the selected zero. Density of the rationals supplies four rational sides strictly between that zero and the isolating sides. The canonical rectangle boundary then contains no zero, while the selected zero lies in its interior. The public flux law converts this enclosure into a nonzero flux certificate.
