*Commentary.*

The seed space U is arbitrary and measurable, nu is its original probability law, a and b are strictly positive with a+b=1, and mu is any probability prior on the closed unit interval supported almost everywhere in its interior. The unit-interval parameter b is the original sourceLaw parameter alpha. The other root mass is a=1-b. The notation AE(mu,q maps to P(q)) means that P holds mu-almost everywhere. moment(mu,j) is the integral of q to the power j against mu, and momentSum(mu,N) is the sum of these moments for indices below N. coefficient is the existing integral of (1-q)(b-aq)q to the power m-1; the final conjunct gives its exact difference-of-moments form.
