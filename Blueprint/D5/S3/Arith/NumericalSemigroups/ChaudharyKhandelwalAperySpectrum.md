# The Apéry spectrum of {0, a, b, kb}

## Abstract

The Apéry spectrum at modulus kb for the family {0,a,b,kb}.

At modulus d = kb, the left semigroup is generated by a, b, d and the right semigroup by d − a, d − b, d. All arithmetic is in the natural numbers. Fin(m) ranges from 0 through m − 1, a residue in Fin(d) is identified with its natural representative in modular arithmetic, and range(m) lists 0 through m − 1 in order.

**Definition 1.1 (Parameters of the family).**

$$\mathit{Params} = \{(a,b,k) \mid (a,b,k) \in \mathbb{N}^{3} \land \left(\left(\left(0 < a \land a < b\right) \land \operatorname{\mathit{gcd}}\left(a, b\right) = 1\right) \land 3 \le k\right)\}$$

*Commentary.*

**Definition 1.2 (Modulus).**

**Theorem 1.8 (Unique simultaneous Apéry representatives).**

$$\forall P \in \mathit{Params}, s \in \operatorname{\mathit{Fin}}\left(\operatorname{d}\left(P\right)\right),\; \exists! q: \operatorname{\mathit{Rect}}\left(P\right), (\left(\operatorname{\mathit{rightRes}}\left(P, q\right) = s \land \operatorname{\mathit{IsLeast}}\left(\operatorname{L}\left(P, q_{1}, q_{2}\right), \{t \mid t \in \operatorname{\mathit{closure}}\left(\left\{P.a, P.b, \operatorname{d}\left(P\right)\right\}\right) \land t \bmod \operatorname{d}\left(P\right) = \operatorname{\mathit{negRes}}\left(P, s\right)\}\right)\right) \land \operatorname{\mathit{IsLeast}}\left(\operatorname{W}\left(P, q_{1}, q_{2}\right), \{t \mid t \in \operatorname{\mathit{closure}}\left(\left\{\operatorname{d}\left(P\right) - P.a, \operatorname{d}\left(P\right) - P.b, \operatorname{d}\left(P\right)\right\}\right) \land t \bmod \operatorname{d}\left(P\right) = s\}\right))$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Arith/NumericalSemigroups/ChaudharyKhandelwalAperySpectrum.residue_witness` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

IsLeast(tw, E) means that tw belongs to E and is at most every element of E. Here closure is additive-submonoid closure. Every right residue has exactly one rectangle point. Reducing a coefficient modulo b and then performing the second division by k yields two nonnegative carries. Substituting the carries shows that any other semigroup value in the corresponding residue is the rectangle value plus a nonnegative multiple of d.

**Definition 1.11 (Executable left block count).**

$$\forall P \in \mathit{Params},\; \forall i \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{\mathit{evalBeta}}\left(P, i\right) = \operatorname{\mathit{runTotal}}\left(\{\operatorname{\mathit{rightRes}}\left(P, q\right) \mid \left(q \in \operatorname{\mathit{Rect}}\left(P\right) \land 0 < \operatorname{\mathit{rightRes}}\left(P, q\right)\right) \land i < \left\lfloor\frac{\operatorname{L}\left(P, q_{1}, q_{2}\right) + \operatorname{d}\left(P\right) - 1}{\operatorname{d}\left(P\right)}\right\rfloor\}, \operatorname{d}\left(P\right) - 1\right)$$

*Commentary.*

**Definition 1.15 (Spectrum defined from the two monoids).**

$$\forall P \in \mathit{Params},\; \forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{\mathit{sourceSpectrum}}\left(P, n\right) = \operatorname{\mathit{getD}}\left(\operatorname{\mathit{sortDescending}}\left(\operatorname{\mathit{append}}\left(\operatorname{\mathit{sourceLeftEntries}}\left(P\right), \operatorname{\mathit{sourceRightEntries}}\left(P\right)\right)\right), n, 0\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/NumericalSemigroups/ChaudharyKhandelwalAperySpectrum.sourceSpectrum` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Prashant Chaudhary and Kapil Khandelwal (2026). *Undershoot of h-fold sumsets: Apéry spectra, similar ordering and the three-distance theorem*. DOI: [10.48550/arXiv.2611.33046](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.33046). URL: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33046v1>.

*Commentary.*

Let wL(r) and wR(r) be the least elements of the left and right closures in residue r. For each s in Fin(d), use u(s) = (s + wL(negRes(P,s))) / d and v(s) = (wR(s) − s) / d. For sourceLeftEntries, take, in increasing order, each i from 1 up to but excluding the maximum of u and count all nonempty intervals in {1 ≤ s < d : i < u(s)}. For sourceRightEntries, take each j from 0 up to but excluding the maximum of v and count the intervals in {1 ≤ s < d : j < v(s)}. Both maxima range over Fin(d). Each remaining block count is zero. Index n is the paper's λ at index n + 1.

**Theorem 1.16 (Spectrum for every admissible a, b, k).**

$$\forall a \in \mathbb{N}, b \in \mathbb{N}, k \in \mathbb{N},\; \left(\left(\left(0 < a \land a < b\right) \land \operatorname{\mathit{gcd}}\left(a, b\right) = 1\right) \land 3 \le k\right) \Rightarrow \left(\left(\left(\left(\left(\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{\mathit{spectrum}}\left(\operatorname{P}\left(a, b, k\right), n\right) = \operatorname{\mathit{sourceSpectrum}}\left(\operatorname{P}\left(a, b, k\right), n\right)\right) \land \operatorname{\mathit{SortedGE}}\left(\operatorname{\mathit{spectrumList}}\left(\operatorname{P}\left(a, b, k\right)\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; 0 < \operatorname{\mathit{spectrum}}\left(\operatorname{P}\left(a, b, k\right), n\right) \Leftrightarrow n < b + k - 3\right)\right) \land \left(\forall z \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{\mathit{count}}\left(z, \operatorname{\mathit{spectrumList}}\left(\operatorname{P}\left(a, b, k\right)\right)\right) = \operatorname{\mathit{card}}\left(\{i \mid i \in \operatorname{\mathit{range}}\left(\operatorname{U}\left(\operatorname{P}\left(a, b, k\right)\right) - 1\right) \land \operatorname{\mathit{evalBeta}}\left(\operatorname{P}\left(a, b, k\right), i + 1\right) = z\}\right) + \operatorname{\mathit{card}}\left(\{j \mid j \in \operatorname{\mathit{range}}\left(\operatorname{V}\left(\operatorname{P}\left(a, b, k\right)\right)\right) \land \operatorname{\mathit{evalGamma}}\left(\operatorname{P}\left(a, b, k\right), j\right) = z\}\right)\right)\right) \land \left(\forall z \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{\mathit{count}}\left(z, \operatorname{\mathit{spectrumList}}\left(\operatorname{P}\left(a, b, k\right)\right)\right) \le 2\right)\right)$$
