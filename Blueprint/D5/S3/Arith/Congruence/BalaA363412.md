*Commentary.*

The coordinate recursion modulo 3 has period 3. At 3n+1 its value is (1,1), so the imaginary coordinate is nonzero modulo 3.

**Theorem 1.3 (Two integer reflection congruences).**

$$\forall m \in \mathbb{N},\; (3 \cdot (m : \mathbb{Z}) \mid 2 \cdot \operatorname{BalaA363411}.\operatorname{b}\left(m\right) - (m : \mathbb{Z}) \cdot \left((m : \mathbb{Z}) + 1\right)) \land (3 \cdot \left((m : \mathbb{Z}) + 1\right) \mid 2 \cdot \operatorname{BalaA363411}.\operatorname{b}\left(m\right) - (m : \mathbb{Z}) \cdot \left((m : \mathbb{Z}) + 1\right))$$

*Commentary.*

For every finite integer coefficient family, a uniform shift by c changes the real coordinate by a multiple of 3c and the imaginary coordinate by c times the cardinality modulo 3c. Apply this to k mapped to m-k on 0 through m, and to m+1-k on 1 through m. Conjugation reverses the imaginary coordinate. The two displayed divisibilities follow and determine the paired three-adic valuations.
