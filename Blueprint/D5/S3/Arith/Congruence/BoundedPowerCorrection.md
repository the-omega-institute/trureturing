*Commentary.*

For every natural exponent s at least two and every bounded integer correction sequence, divisibility of the corrected-weight differences at successive powers of every prime forces a(p^r)=p-1 for all positive r. The base step uses a(1)=0 and the residue at p. Higher steps use s*(r-1)>=r and uniqueness of the bounded residue. The original square proof and the uniform integer-weight family use this same induction. The known square result is due to Su; no literature novelty is inferred.
