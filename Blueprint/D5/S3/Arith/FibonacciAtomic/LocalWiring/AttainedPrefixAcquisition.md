# Native mature-prefix acquisition

## Abstract

A source-blind native program actually earns its mature bits and ends with a correct installed generator and the three requested forms of readiness.

**Theorem 1.1 (Joint read and update coordinates of the attained program).**

$$Q=m,\quad U=N,\quad W\leq m(N-1),\quad M_{addr}\leq N-1$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Arith/FibonacciAtomic/LocalWiring/AttainedPrefixAcquisition.attained_prefix_acquisition` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The public target m is 2d for binary recurrence order at most d, 2K−1 for nonempty unary Moore carrier of size at most K, and r for the supplied entire row of length r with unknown seed. For m>0, N is the least n≥3 with F_n−1≥m; for m=0, N=2, the law is already zero and Q, W and the maximum are all zero. The zero law is known before that cut; the comparison here is for mature cuts.

At each eligible cut the program replays the old labelled tree from the word already earned, traverses its beta leaves in physical left-first order, and requests their literal parent-aa addresses on the actual updated root. The answer equals the older birth bit, not a newly hidden orientation. The final batch is limited to the remaining quota. Update acknowledgements, literal addresses and Read replies agree with the actual native observer run.

The weights are exact: W is the sum of the full parent-aa lengths and the maximum is the largest such length, or zero if no address was requested. These are measured on the same trace, with exactly the first m ordered births read, not on separately optimized runs.

Actual service effects alternate with source-free local phases for traversal, writing literals, retaining replies, finite first-match synthesis or fixed-row initialization, and reconstruction. The terminal register contains the installed generator, current labelled tree, its consumed-bit cursor and the exact next-cut and future answers. Predicted unread values are not paid Read replies. Local blocks record finite computation without a primitive work bound, and no physical installation or minimum-Read claim is made.
