*Commentary.*

For each valid positive target promise a single actual lawful source works across all earlier mature cuts. It is a fixed periodic pulse for the bounded binary class, a fixed cycle for the bounded Moore class, or a fixed seed for the entire public row. At every earlier mature cut there is a lawful alternative with the same mature prefix but different labels, a different literal next-cut presence answer, and different future behavior.

The alternative is chosen per cut, not per random tape. Under one common proper tape law, intersect the finitely many relevant alternative success events and the hard-source success event. At the realized early cut, the full accessible record coincides on the paired runs, including the stop and observed replies. The distinct correct tasks make early zero-error stopping impossible for each task separately; no bound on local or service step counts was imposed.

Together with the attained program, this proves the sharp class-uniform worst update deadline for mature readiness. It does not minimize paid reads, address costs or total work, and it does not say every source is difficult.
