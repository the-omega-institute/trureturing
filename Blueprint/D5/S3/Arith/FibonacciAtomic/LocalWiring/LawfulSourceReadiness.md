# Individual and uniform readiness for two lawful source classes

## Abstract

The current labels and all literal next-cut presence replies require only agreement through the bits already born. Complete finite future behavior requires agreement of the whole output stream.

**Theorem 1.1 (Attained-fiber criteria and exact uniform thresholds).**

$$n\ge 2, v_{n}=F_{n}-1\quad \mathcal{R}_{d}: v_{n}\ge 2d\quad \mathcal{M}_{K}: v_{n}\ge 2K-1$$

*Commentary.*

At a reached cut n≥2, the input is already acquired current incidence or terminal boundary. Its inverse yields the attained epoch and the mature ordered word of v_n bits. Among legal streams compatible with that word, current labelled-tree recovery and answering every literal next-cut presence address before the update are each equivalent to agreement through the B_n bits already born.

Complete future behavior means every finite word of actual Read and Update actions, including absent-address replies and the physical incidence and labelled successor after each update. Adaptive policies receive the same accessible reply history and their fixed independent local inputs, not the task snapshots. A differing source digit is distinguished by finitely many real updates and a literal Read reply; no private state names, timing or work coordinates are compared.

For each task separately the thresholds apply to unknown-order binary recurrences of order at most d≥0, or autonomous unary Boolean Moore sources with a nonempty carrier of size at most K≥1. At the threshold, the original finite synthesizer runs on the required initial part of the mature word, produces an extensionally correct generator and simulates the actual service. Order zero gives the known zero law.

Below threshold, the actual sharpness witnesses share the full mature word and therefore the same acquired geometry, but disagree at the first bit of a nonempty newest birth batch. This defeats one uniform recovery rule for each task; particular attained fibers may be ready sooner. There is no claim about paid queries, minimum total resources or implementing an observer's source acquisition.
