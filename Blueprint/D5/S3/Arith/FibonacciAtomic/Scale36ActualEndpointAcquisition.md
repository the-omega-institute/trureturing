# Scale36 Actual Endpoint Acquisition

## Abstract

The literal Scale36 family and its conditional routing addresses.

The family has k active slots and one compensation slot. P0 uses C in every active slot and KT at the tail. Uj replaces slot j by T and the tail by B; Vj replaces slot j by W1 and the tail by A. All brackets are retained.

The public definitions preserve the original preimages and the four literal request addresses a, b, q, r. The complete all-source controller and exact endpoint theorem are open.
