## Abstract

CommonCode is the product of the existing rowWord constructor list, the lexicographically sorted finite Q address list, the existing chronological mixed-epoch history codes and N. The four reply codes remain 0,1,2,3. Contradictory and unreachable finite histories remain computation inputs. The decoder and cell factory are uniformly primitive recursive before quantification over old rows, Q, H and N. Numeric old class names are preserved, including gaps and repeated occurrences; names are never replaced by dense class ranks. The exact depth is the current requiredDepth. This stage does not decide feasible cells, run the unifier, compute the ground-binding criterion or budget, or certify the whole controller.

The matching Reg mirror proves typed whole-family reconstruction, variation, role sensitivity and actual observational dependence on the complete infinite domain. Contract source-selection coordinates and raw source-occurrence binding remain unregistered. No validated four-slot audit status is claimed.
