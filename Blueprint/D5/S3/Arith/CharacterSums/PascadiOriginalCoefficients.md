# Actual original endpoint coefficients

## Abstract

Physical integer endpoints define the actual L,Q coefficients and pencil L₀Q−LQ₀. Actual original word solutions give αL(β)+Q(β)=0 at β=V₁/R₁ and therefore p³ divisibility at its literal integer representative. Literal endpoint coordinate bounds give coefficient constant C=3 and pencil height H=18, and the actual degree is at most three. The archived expansion is reused. Constructing the required bounded lattice basis, all exceptional polynomial strata and the final original count remains open.
