# QuadraticPrefixEnergy

## Abstract

Finite sign prefixes have their length parity. Reflected interval sums give a triangular reduction of the literal double sum. The two quotient vectors are proved integral, their reflection and midpoint values give square norms, and an odd natural h satisfies the exact sign-square identity modulo32.
