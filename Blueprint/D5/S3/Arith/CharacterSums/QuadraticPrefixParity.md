# QuadraticPrefixParity

## Abstract

A disjoint sigma union applies the sign-product rule with exact multiplicities. The central interval lengths sum to m(m+1). The resulting integral congruence modulo4 supplies quotient parity; reflection supplies the even square norm. These are consumed in both prime families.
