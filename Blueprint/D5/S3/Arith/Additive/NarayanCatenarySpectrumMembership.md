# Narayan's catenary spectrum membership

## Abstract

A factorization fiber on the original arithmetic generators realizes Narayan's catenary degree.

**Definition 1.1 (The original generating tuple).**

$$\forall a \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall h \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall d \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall k \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall i \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(k + 1\right),\; \operatorname{generators}\left(a, h, d, k, i\right) = \operatorname{ite}\left(i = 0, a, a \cdot h + \operatorname{val}\left(i\right) \cdot d\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/Additive/NarayanCatenarySpectrumMembership.generators` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Ashwin Narayan (2026). *The Delta Set of a Numerical Monoid Generated by a Generalized Arithmetic Sequence*. DOI: [10.48550/arXiv.2610.02495](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.02495). URL: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02495v1>.

*Commentary.*

The coordinates have indices zero through k. Coordinate zero represents a, and coordinate i greater than zero represents ah + id. The element ah is represented by h copies of a.

**Definition 1.2 (Atoms of an additive submonoid).**

$$\forall S \in \operatorname{AddSubmonoid}\left(\mathbb{N}\right),\; \forall m \in \mathbb{N},\; (\operatorname{IsAtom}\left(S, m\right)) \Leftrightarrow ((m \in S) \land ((m \ne 0) \land (\forall u \in \mathbb{N},\; (u \in S) \Rightarrow (\forall v \in \mathbb{N},\; (v \in S) \Rightarrow ((u + v = m) \Rightarrow ((u = 0) \lor (v = 0)))))))$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/Additive/NarayanCatenarySpectrumMembership.IsAtom` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Acknowledgement.* Ashwin Narayan (2026). *The Delta Set of a Numerical Monoid Generated by a Generalized Arithmetic Sequence*. DOI: [10.48550/arXiv.2610.02495](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.02495). URL: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02495v1>.

*Commentary.*

An atom is a nonzero monoid element that cannot be split as a sum of two nonzero monoid elements.

**Definition 1.3 (Residual factorization distance).**

$$\forall p \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall z \in (\operatorname{Fin}\left(p\right) \to \mathbb{N}),\; \forall w \in (\operatorname{Fin}\left(p\right) \to \mathbb{N}),\; \operatorname{distance}\left(z, w\right) = \operatorname{max}\left(\sum_{i : \operatorname{Fin}\left(p\right)} (z\left(i\right) - \operatorname{min}\left(z\left(i\right), w\left(i\right)\right)), \sum_{i : \operatorname{Fin}\left(p\right)} (w\left(i\right) - \operatorname{min}\left(z\left(i\right), w\left(i\right)\right))\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/Additive/NarayanCatenarySpectrumMembership.distance` (`✓ std3`).

*Commentary.*

Subtract the pointwise minimum from both vectors and take the larger residual coordinate sum.

**Definition 1.4 (Bounded-distance chains).**

$$\forall p \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall g \in (\operatorname{Fin}\left(p\right) \to \mathbb{N}),\; \forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall B \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall z \in \operatorname{Z}\left(g, n\right),\; \forall w \in \operatorname{Z}\left(g, n\right),\; (\operatorname{Chain}\left(g, n, B, z, w\right)) \Leftrightarrow (\operatorname{Relation}.\operatorname{ReflTransGen}\left((u : \operatorname{Z}\left(g, n\right)) (v : \operatorname{Z}\left(g, n\right)) \mapsto \operatorname{distance}\left(\operatorname{val}\left(u\right), \operatorname{val}\left(v\right)\right) \leq B, z, w\right))$$

The reflexive transitive closure uses edges of distance at most B. Every vertex has the same weighted value n.

**Definition 1.5 (The elementwise catenary spectrum).**

$$\forall p \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall g \in (\operatorname{Fin}\left(p\right) \to \mathbb{N}),\; \operatorname{catenarySpectrum}\left(g\right) = \{N : \mathbb{N} \mid \exists n \in \mathbb{N},\; (\operatorname{Nonempty}\left(\operatorname{Z}\left(g, n\right)\right)) \land (\operatorname{IsLeast}\left(\{B : \mathbb{N} \mid \forall z \in \operatorname{Z}\left(g, n\right),\; \forall w \in \operatorname{Z}\left(g, n\right),\; \operatorname{Chain}\left(g, n, B, z, w\right)\}, N\right))\}$$

*Commentary.*

Each represented value contributes the least threshold at which its entire factorization fiber is connected.

**Definition 1.6 (The ceiling parameter).**

$$\forall a \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall k \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{ceilParameter}\left(a, k\right) = \operatorname{ceilDiv}\left(a - k, k - 1\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/Additive/NarayanCatenarySpectrumMembership.ceilParameter` (`✓ std3`).

*Commentary.*

With k greater than one and less than a, the denominator and numerator are positive.

**Definition 1.7 (The element attaining the threshold).**

$$\forall a \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall h \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall d \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall k \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{let} t := \operatorname{ceilParameter}\left(a, k\right), \operatorname{witness}\left(a, h, d, k\right) = a \cdot h \cdot \left(t + 1\right) + \left(a + t\right) \cdot d$$

**Definition 1.8 (The low-index-weight class).**

$$\forall k \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall h \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall d \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall t \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall z \in (\operatorname{Fin}\left(k + 1\right) \to \mathbb{N}),\; (\operatorname{Low}\left(h, d, t, z\right)) \Leftrightarrow ((\sum_{i : \operatorname{Fin}\left(k\right)} (z\left(\operatorname{succ}\left(i\right)\right) \cdot \left(\operatorname{val}\left(i\right) + 1\right)) = t) \land (z\left(0\right) = h \cdot \left(t + 1 - \sum_{i : \operatorname{Fin}\left(k\right)} (z\left(\operatorname{succ}\left(i\right)\right))\right) + d))$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/Additive/NarayanCatenarySpectrumMembership.Low` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The weighted sum of the positive indices is t. The zero coordinate is h times the deficit from t + 1 in the positive-index count, plus d.

**Definition 1.9 (The high-index-weight class).**

$$\forall k \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall a \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall t \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall z \in (\operatorname{Fin}\left(k + 1\right) \to \mathbb{N}),\; (\operatorname{High}\left(a, t, z\right)) \Leftrightarrow ((\sum_{i : \operatorname{Fin}\left(k\right)} (z\left(\operatorname{succ}\left(i\right)\right) \cdot \left(\operatorname{val}\left(i\right) + 1\right)) = a + t) \land ((\sum_{i : \operatorname{Fin}\left(k\right)} (z\left(\operatorname{succ}\left(i\right)\right)) = t + 1) \land (z\left(0\right) = 0)))$$

*Commentary.*

The weighted sum of the positive indices is a + t, the positive-index count is t + 1, and the zero coordinate vanishes.

**Definition 1.10 (The atom tuple and the two fiber classes).**

$$\forall a \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall h \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall d \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall k \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{let} g := \operatorname{generators}\left(a, h, d, k\right), \operatorname{let} t := \operatorname{ceilParameter}\left(a, k\right), \operatorname{let} n := \operatorname{witness}\left(a, h, d, k\right), \operatorname{let} N := t + h + d, (\operatorname{WitnessCheckpoint}\left(a, h, d, k\right)) \Leftrightarrow (\operatorname{let} S := \operatorname{closure}\left(\operatorname{range}\left(g\right)\right), (\operatorname{Injective}\left(g\right)) \land ((\operatorname{Finite}\left(\operatorname{compl}\left(S\right)\right)) \land ((\forall m \in \mathbb{N},\; (\operatorname{IsAtom}\left(S, m\right)) \Leftrightarrow (m \in \operatorname{range}\left(g\right))) \land ((n \in S) \land ((\forall z \in (\operatorname{Fin}\left(k + 1\right) \to \mathbb{N}),\; (z \in \operatorname{Z}\left(g, n\right)) \Leftrightarrow ((\operatorname{Low}\left(h, d, t, z\right)) \lor (\operatorname{High}\left(a, t, z\right)))) \land ((\forall z \in (\operatorname{Fin}\left(k + 1\right) \to \mathbb{N}),\; \neg \left((\operatorname{Low}\left(h, d, t, z\right)) \land (\operatorname{High}\left(a, t, z\right))\right)) \land ((\exists z \in \operatorname{Z}\left(g, n\right),\; \operatorname{Low}\left(h, d, t, \operatorname{val}\left(z\right)\right)) \land ((\exists w \in \operatorname{Z}\left(g, n\right),\; \operatorname{High}\left(a, t, \operatorname{val}\left(w\right)\right)) \land ((\forall z \in (\operatorname{Fin}\left(k + 1\right) \to \mathbb{N}),\; \forall w \in (\operatorname{Fin}\left(k + 1\right) \to \mathbb{N}),\; (\operatorname{Low}\left(h, d, t, z\right)) \Rightarrow ((\operatorname{High}\left(a, t, w\right)) \Rightarrow (N \leq \operatorname{distance}\left(z, w\right)))) \land (\forall B \in \mathbb{N},\; (B < N) \Rightarrow (\forall z \in \operatorname{Z}\left(g, n\right),\; \forall w \in \operatorname{Z}\left(g, n\right),\; (\operatorname{Low}\left(h, d, t, \operatorname{val}\left(z\right)\right)) \Rightarrow ((\operatorname{High}\left(a, t, \operatorname{val}\left(w\right)\right)) \Rightarrow (\neg \operatorname{Chain}\left(g, n, B, z, w\right))))))))))))))$$

*Commentary.*

The tuple is injective and its range is exactly the atoms of a cofinite monoid. Coprimality reduces the index weight modulo a to t. The generator bounds then allow only weights t and a + t. The low representative is (h + d)e₀ + te₁. The high representative is teₖ + e₂₊ᵨ, where ρ is the remainder of a − 2 upon division by k − 1. Coincident indices add with multiplicity. The high class uses neither coordinate zero nor one. Any shared copies contribute at most t − Y to the common part, where Y is the low vector's positive-index count. Its length is N + (h − 1)(t − Y), so cancellation leaves distance at least N. Thus every chain below N stays in one class.

*Commentary.*

Narayan's Conjecture 18.2 on p. 22 asserts that the ceiling parameter plus h + d belongs to the catenary spectrum under the hypotheses in §3. The exchange of h copies of a and one copy of generator i for one copy each of generators 1 and i − 1 preserves value and reduces t − Y. Iteration joins the low class to its representative. At i = 2 the exchange adds two copies of generator 1. The high class is connected by edges of size at most t + 1. At N − 1 these two classes are exactly the components. At N the low representative is adjacent to every high vector, establishing the least connecting threshold. The proof includes k = 2, k = a − 1, a = 3 and h = 2.
