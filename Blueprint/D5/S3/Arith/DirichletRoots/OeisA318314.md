# The rational Dirichlet square root in OEIS A318314

## Abstract

The rational recurrence, local coefficients and denominator mechanism of OEIS A318314.

**Definition 1.1 (Odd unitary divisors).**

$$\operatorname{g}\left(n\right) = \operatorname{oddUnitaryCount}\left(n\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/DirichletRoots/OeisA318314.oddUnitaryCount` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Antti Karttunen; Andrew Howroyd; OEIS Foundation Inc. (2018). *OEIS A318314: denominators of the rational Dirichlet square root of A068068*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A318314/internal>.

*Commentary.*

For each natural n, g(n) counts d in the positive divisors of n for which d is odd and gcd(d,n/d)=1. This is the independent A068068 definition.

**Definition 1.2 (Interior divisors).**

$$\operatorname{interior}\left(n\right) = \operatorname{properInteriorDivisors}\left(n\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/DirichletRoots/OeisA318314.interior` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Acknowledgement.* Antti Karttunen; Andrew Howroyd; OEIS Foundation Inc. (2018). *OEIS A318314: denominators of the rational Dirichlet square root of A068068*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A318314/internal>.

*Commentary.*

The interior set consists exactly of the positive divisors d of n satisfying 1<d<n; both endpoint divisors are excluded.

**Definition 1.3 (The actual rational recurrence).**

$$\begin{aligned}\operatorname{f}\left(0\right) = 0\\\operatorname{f}\left(1\right) = 1\\\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; 1 < n \Rightarrow \operatorname{f}\left(n\right) = \frac{\operatorname{g}\left(n\right) - \operatorname{interiorSquareSum}\left(f, n\right)}{2}\end{aligned}$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/DirichletRoots/OeisA318314.f` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Acknowledgement.* Antti Karttunen; Andrew Howroyd; OEIS Foundation Inc. (2018). *OEIS A318314: denominators of the rational Dirichlet square root of A068068*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A318314/internal>.

*Commentary.*

The interior square sum is the sum of f(d)f(n/d) over interior(n). Both recursive arguments are strictly below n. The extension f(0)=0 leaves the entire positive source domain unchanged.

**Definition 1.4 (The source specification).**

$$\operatorname{SourceSpec}\left(u\right) = \left(\operatorname{u}\left(1\right) = 1 \land \left(\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; 1 < n \Rightarrow \operatorname{u}\left(n\right) = \frac{\operatorname{g}\left(n\right) - \operatorname{interiorSquareSum}\left(u, n\right)}{2}\right)\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/DirichletRoots/OeisA318314.SourceSpec` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Acknowledgement.* Antti Karttunen; Andrew Howroyd; OEIS Foundation Inc. (2018). *OEIS A318314: denominators of the rational Dirichlet square root of A068068*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A318314/internal>.

*Commentary.*

A source solution has unit head and the published recurrence at every n>1. No multiplicativity, nonzero value, finite support or exponent bound is assumed.

$$\operatorname{SourceSpec}\left(f\right) \land \left(\forall u \in \mathbb{Q}^{\mathbb{N}},\; \operatorname{SourceSpec}\left(u\right) \Rightarrow \left(\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; 0 < n \Rightarrow \operatorname{u}\left(n\right) = \operatorname{f}\left(n\right)\right)\right)$$

*Commentary.*

The explicitly recursive f inhabits the source specification. Strong induction identifies every other source solution at every positive index, since d and n/d in an interior term are positive and strictly smaller than n.

**Definition 1.6 (Central-binomial rational coefficients).**

$$\operatorname{C}\left(e\right) = \frac{\operatorname{centralBinom}\left(e\right)}{4^{e}}$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/DirichletRoots/OeisA318314.central` (`✓ std3`).

*Commentary.*

Here centralBinom(e) is binom(2e,e). In particular C(0)=1; no rational denominator is defined by the conjectured formula.

**Theorem 1.7 (The local square at two).**

$$\forall e \in \mathbb{N},\; 0 < \operatorname{C}\left(e\right) \land \operatorname{centralConvolution}\left(e\right) = 1$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Arith/DirichletRoots/OeisA318314.central_local_square` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Commentary.*

The convolution sums C(i)C(j) over all nonnegative i+j=e. The central-binomial recurrence identifies C(e) with (-1)^e choose(-1/2,e); Chu-Vandermonde then gives the self-convolution exactly.

**Definition 1.8 (Binary digit sum).**

**Theorem 1.9 (Exact central denominators).**

$$\forall e \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{den}\left(\operatorname{C}\left(e\right)\right) = 2^{2 \cdot e - \operatorname{s}\left(e\right)} \land \operatorname{integerCast}\left(\operatorname{naturalSub}\left(2 \cdot e, \operatorname{s}\left(e\right)\right)\right) = 2 \cdot \operatorname{integerCast}\left(e\right) - \operatorname{integerCast}\left(\operatorname{s}\left(e\right)\right)$$

*Commentary.*

Kummer's digit-sum theorem gives the two-adic valuation s(e) of binom(2e,e). Removing precisely this power leaves an odd numerator and proves the reduced denominator, including e=0. The second conjunct states the integer-subtraction correspondence explicitly.

**Definition 1.10 (The independent A005187 recurrence).**

$$\begin{aligned}\operatorname{T}\left(0\right) = 0\\\forall e \in \mathbb{N},\; 0 < e \Rightarrow \operatorname{T}\left(e\right) = e + \operatorname{T}\left(\operatorname{halfFloor}\left(e\right)\right)\end{aligned}$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/DirichletRoots/OeisA318314.halvingSum` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Antti Karttunen; Andrew Howroyd; OEIS Foundation Inc. (2018). *OEIS A318314: denominators of the rational Dirichlet square root of A068068*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A318314/internal>.

*Commentary.*

T is independently defined by halving recursion. The binary digit identity proves T(e)=2e-s(e) for every natural e.

**Definition 1.11 (Prime-local rational factors).**

$$\begin{aligned}\operatorname{L}\left(2, e\right) = \operatorname{C}\left(e\right)\\\forall p \in \mathbb{N},\; p \ne 2 \Rightarrow \operatorname{L}\left(p, e\right) = \operatorname{C}\left(\operatorname{halfFloor}\left(e\right)\right)\end{aligned}$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/DirichletRoots/OeisA318314.localCoefficient` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Acknowledgement.* Antti Karttunen; Andrew Howroyd; OEIS Foundation Inc. (2018). *OEIS A318314: denominators of the rational Dirichlet square root of A068068*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A318314/internal>.

*Commentary.*

The local coefficient at two is C(e); at every other prime it is C(floor(e/2)). Both choices give one when e=0.

**Theorem 1.12 (All local denominator formulas).**

$$\left(\forall e \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{den}\left(\operatorname{L}\left(2, e\right)\right) = 2^{\operatorname{T}\left(e\right)}\right) \land \left(\left(\forall p \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall e \in \mathbb{N},\; \left(\operatorname{Prime}\left(p\right) \land p \ne 2\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{den}\left(\operatorname{L}\left(p, e\right)\right) = 2^{e - \operatorname{s}\left(e\right)}\right) \land \left(\forall p \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall e \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{Prime}\left(p\right) \Rightarrow \left(\operatorname{den}\left(\operatorname{L}\left(p, e\right)\right) = 2^{\left(2 - \operatorname{mod}\left(p, 2\right)\right) \cdot e - \operatorname{s}\left(e\right)} \land \operatorname{integerCast}\left(\operatorname{naturalParityExponent}\left(p, e\right)\right) = \left(2 - \operatorname{mod}\left(\operatorname{integerCast}\left(p\right), 2\right)\right) \cdot \operatorname{integerCast}\left(e\right) - \operatorname{integerCast}\left(\operatorname{s}\left(e\right)\right)\right)\right)\right)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Arith/DirichletRoots/OeisA318314.local_denominators` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Acknowledgement.* Antti Karttunen; Andrew Howroyd; OEIS Foundation Inc. (2018). *OEIS A318314: denominators of the rational Dirichlet square root of A068068*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A318314/internal>.

*Commentary.*

For odd primes, 2 floor(e/2)-s(floor(e/2))=e-s(e). A prime is even precisely when it equals two. These facts give all three local formulas and the explicit ordinary-integer exponent identity.

*Commentary.*

The odd convolution sums C(floor(i/2))C(floor(j/2)) over i+j=e. Its series is (1+x)B(x^2), whose square is (1+x)/(1-x). The constant coefficient is one and every positive coefficient is two.

**Theorem 1.20 (The exact source divisor-square identity).**

The full divisor sum has endpoint contributions f(1)f(n) and f(n)f(1), each equal to f(n). Removing both endpoints recovers precisely the original recurrence. At n=1 both sides are one.

$$\forall p \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall e \in \mathbb{N},\; \operatorname{Prime}\left(p\right) \Rightarrow \operatorname{dirichletSquare}\left(R, p^{e}\right) = \operatorname{g}\left(p^{e}\right)$$
