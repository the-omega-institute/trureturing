# The Actual Merca Quartic Field

## Abstract

The actual fixed field of the normalized quartic character kernel is cyclic quartic and CM. For conductor r greater than five its roots of unity have order two and its Hasse unit index is one.

This is the actual-object field construction needed by the historical Ma–Zhang theorem. It does not define a field through its desired class number. Identification of the maximal real field with Q(sqrt(r)) and the analytic relative-class-number formula are supplied by MercaQuadraticSubfield and MercaRelativeClassNumber. *Commentary.*

For every prime r congruent to five modulo eight, the actual fixed field has degree four, cyclic Galois group and CM structure. Its maximal real subfield has degree two. The index-four kernel gives the degree by Galois correspondence. Ambient conjugation maps to minus one and lies outside the kernel; extending a hypothetical real embedding would force it to fix the field, contradicting that exclusion.

**Theorem 1.3 (Exactly two roots of unity).**

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Arith/CyclotomicFields/MercaQuarticField.torsion_order` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Citation.* Haobo Ma; Wenlin Zhang (2026). *A class-number criterion for the quartic equality after Theorem 5.5*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A392984>.

*Commentary.*

For r greater than five the genuine maximal-order torsion group has order two. Its order divides the ambient cyclotomic torsion order 2r. An r-th primitive root inside a degree-four field would force r minus one at most four. Thus r cannot divide the torsion order, whereas minus one supplies order two. The conductor-five exception is explicitly excluded.

*Commentary.*

For r greater than five the index of the subgroup generated by real units and roots of unity in the actual maximal-order unit group is one. A putative index two supplies a unit whose ratio to its conjugate is minus one. If sigma generates the cyclic group of order four, x equals u times sigma(u) and sigma(x) equals minus x. The four-conjugate norm is x squared and is an integer unit, hence x to the fourth is one. The proved torsion order then makes x equal to plus or minus one, contradicting sigma(x) equals minus x.
