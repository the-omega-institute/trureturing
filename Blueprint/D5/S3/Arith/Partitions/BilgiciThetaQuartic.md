# A quartic cofactor in Bilgici's theta quotient

## Abstract

The weight-eleven component of Bilgici's polynomial factors by the cubic in the theta quotient.

Work in the polynomial ring R over the field of 19 elements on nine independent variables, indexed from 1 through 9. In Lean, index j is variable j−1.

**Definition 1.1 (Signed integer indices).**

$$Z_{x}=\begin{cases}0&r=0\\(-1)^{q}Z_{\operatorname{min}(r,19-r)}&r\neq0\end{cases}$$

*Commentary.*

Write x = 19q + r with 0 ≤ r < 19. The value is zero when r = 0. Otherwise it is the variable at min(r, 19−r) multiplied by (−1) to the integer power q. This gives reflection about 19/2, oddness, and sign reversal under translation by 19.

*Commentary.*

The same cofactor Q is nonzero in the polynomial ring and in the theta quotient. Each monomial has total degree four and weight nine. The ideal membership is equivalent to the equality of the classes of the entire weight-eleven component and S_Z Q.

The cofactor is the sum of 5 to the j times the j-fold signed doubling image of 6 Z₁ Z₂ Z₃ Z₉ + 14 Z₁² Z₅ Z₇, for j from zero through eight. The difference from the weight component is a sum of thirteen weighted orbits of cubic multiples of theta relations. Doubling transports each relation to the relation at the doubled tuple. The polynomial identity comes from checking every coefficient.
