# EtaSignK10

## Abstract

The complete unshifted BHHK denominator-ten sign pattern at every natural index.

The original quotient is F10=E(q)^9/E(q^3)^10, with the denominator defined by its actual unit inverse and C10(n) its unshifted natural coefficient. Its first cubic trisection has constants -9 and 27 and zero component G10=E(q)^2/E(q^3)^3. The exact squared dissection identifies G10 with the sections UP,-VP,-WP. Positive ordinary partition coefficients smooth the W hole in every degree. Thus C10(9m+6) is strictly negative for every m, including m=0. The endpoint proves both residue-nine biconditionals and nonvanishing with no eventual or finite-range qualifier; C10_zero proves the empty-index value one.
