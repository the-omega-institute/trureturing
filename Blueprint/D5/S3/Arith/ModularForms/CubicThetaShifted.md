# Three affine cosets in a shifted Eisenstein lattice

## Abstract

Three affine cosets in a shifted Eisenstein lattice

The full lattice exponent Q(x,y)-x-y is nonnegative and has finite integer fibres. Three explicit affine cosets, selected by (x-y) modulo three, identify these fibres with three copies of the previously defined core fibres.
