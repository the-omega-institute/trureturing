# EulerNineSquareDissection

## Abstract

Cubic residue extraction and positive decompositions control every squared section.

Write A=Q4, B9=Q2, C=Q1, and use the actual coloured partition product D from EulerNineComparison. The residue-two section of R^3 vanishes by the existing cubic theta dissection. Expanding the proved R dissection and cancelling the constant-one unit A gives B9^2=AC+qCD. Hence W=2AC-B9^2=(1-q)AC+q(AC-CD). Both summands are nonnegative by the paid restricted-product comparisons and exact AD=B9C multiplicities. This proves the difficult lower bounds without division of an inequality. The sole auxiliary degree-one hole is removed by ordinary partition smoothing in EtaSignK10.
