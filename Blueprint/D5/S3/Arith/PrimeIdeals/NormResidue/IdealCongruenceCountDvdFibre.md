*Commentary.*

For each sign orthant and lattice coset, the product-ideal residue fibre count has a leading cell volume term divided by the norm of the additional ideal, with an error bounded by a constant times the dilation to degree d - 1. The leading term vanishes when the original cell does not carry the residue.
