# Zhi-Wei Sun's OEIS A228884 composite gcd-determinant bound

## Abstract

Prime divisors of composite gcd determinants are smaller than the index.

**Definition 1.1 (The gcd-entry matrix).**

$$\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; \forall i \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right),\; \forall j \in \operatorname{Fin}\left(n\right),\; \operatorname{C}\left(n\right)\left(i, j\right) = (\operatorname{Int}.\operatorname{gcd}\left(((\operatorname{val}\left(i\right): \mathbb{Z}) + 1) - ((\operatorname{val}\left(j\right): \mathbb{Z}) + 1), (n: \mathbb{Z})\right): \mathbb{Z})$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/Divisors/SunA228884.C` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Zhi-Wei Sun (2013). *Determinant of the n X n matrix with (i,j)-entry equal to the greatest common divisor of i-j and n*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A228884/internal>.

*Commentary.*

OEIS A228884 NAME, page not numbered: “Determinant of the n X n matrix with (i,j)-entry equal to the greatest common divisor of i-j and n.” The source program is “a[n_]:=Det[Table[GCD[i-j,n],{i,1,n},{j,1,n}]]”. The Lean expression uses Fin n labels, adds one to each label, performs integer subtraction, and applies Int.gcd before casting the result to the matrix entry type.

**Definition 1.3 (The composite-index prime-divisor conjecture).**

$$claim = \left(\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; (4 \le n) \Rightarrow ((\neg (\operatorname{Nat}.\operatorname{Prime}\left(n\right))) \Rightarrow (\forall q \in \mathbb{N},\; (\operatorname{Nat}.\operatorname{Prime}\left(q\right)) \Rightarrow (((q: \mathbb{Z}) \mid \operatorname{D}\left(n\right)) \Rightarrow (q < n))))\right)$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Arith/Divisors/SunA228884.claim` (`✓ std3`).

*Citation.* Zhi-Wei Sun (2013). *Determinant of the n X n matrix with (i,j)-entry equal to the greatest common divisor of i-j and n*. URL: <https://oeis.org/A228884/internal>.

*Commentary.*

OEIS A228884 COMMENTS clause (ii), page not numbered: “(ii) For any composite number n, all prime divisors of a(n) are smaller than n.” The source definition is encoded with n ≥ 4, ¬Nat.Prime n, integer divisibility of D n by the natural prime q, and q < n.

**Theorem 1.4 (The prime-divisor bound holds).**

$$\forall n \in \mathbb{N},\; (4 \le n) \Rightarrow ((\neg (\operatorname{Nat}.\operatorname{Prime}\left(n\right))) \Rightarrow (\forall q \in \mathbb{N},\; (\operatorname{Nat}.\operatorname{Prime}\left(q\right)) \Rightarrow (((q: \mathbb{Z}) \mid \operatorname{D}\left(n\right)) \Rightarrow (q < n))))$$

*Commentary.*

CRT reindexing gives D(uv)=D(u)^v D(v)^u for coprime positive u and v. The gcd Fourier transform factors over prime powers, and its local eigenvalue products at exponent at least two have prime divisors below the local order. Exponent-one factors are bounded by 2p-1 and the remaining cofactor in a composite index supplies 2p ≤ n.

The proof therefore establishes the complete statement for every n ≥ 4 with ¬Nat.Prime n. Positivity and the separate divisibility clause of the OEIS entry are not asserted here.
