*Commentary.*

Fix a strict divisor chain from one to an integer greater than one. A prime word together with marks realizing its prefix products is equivalent to a family of prime words, one for each successive quotient of the chain. Splitting at the marks gives the family; concatenation reconstructs the word. Each mark is forced to equal the total prime multiplicity of its observed divisor.
