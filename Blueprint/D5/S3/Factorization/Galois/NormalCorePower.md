# NormalCorePower

## Abstract

Noncommutative algebra for the power-subgroup route to strong completeness.

**Theorem 1.1 (Normality without commutativity).**

$$\forall (m: \mathbb{N}), \operatorname{Normal}(\operatorname{powerSubgroup}(G, m))$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Factorization/Galois/NormalCorePower.powerSubgroup_normal` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Conjugation carries each mth power to another mth power. The subgroup generated by all such powers is therefore normal in any group.

**Definition 1.2 (Power-generated subgroup).**

$$\operatorname{powerSubgroup}(G, m) = \operatorname{SubgroupClosure}(\operatorname{Range}(g \mapsto g^{m}))$$

*Formalization.* `D5/S3/Factorization/Galois/NormalCorePower.powerSubgroup` (`✓ std3`).

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

The subgroup is generated algebraically by all mth powers in an arbitrary group. No commutativity or topology is assumed.

**Theorem 1.3 (Each power belongs to the subgroup).**

$$\forall (m: \mathbb{N}), \forall (g: G), g^{m} \in \operatorname{powerSubgroup}(G, m)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Factorization/Galois/NormalCorePower.pow_mem_powerSubgroup` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For every natural m and group element g, the element g to the mth power belongs to the generated subgroup.

**Theorem 1.4 (Exponent quotient criterion).**

$$\forall (m: \mathbb{N}), \forall (N: \operatorname{NormalSubgroup}(G)), \operatorname{powerSubgroup}(G, m) \leq N \iff \forall (q: \operatorname{Quotient}(G, N)), q^{m} = 1$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Factorization/Galois/NormalCorePower.powerSubgroup_le_iff` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For any normal subgroup N, containment of the power subgroup in N is equivalent to every element of G modulo N having mth power one. The criterion also includes m equal to zero.

**Theorem 1.5 (Powers in a finite-index normal core).**

$$\forall (H: \operatorname{Subgroup}(G)), {\operatorname{FiniteIndex}(H)} \Rightarrow {\forall (g: G), g^{\operatorname{Index}(\operatorname{NormalCore}(H))} \in \operatorname{NormalCore}(H)}$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Factorization/Galois/NormalCorePower.pow_normalCore_index_mem` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

If H has finite index, its normal core also has finite index. Lagrange in that abstract quotient puts the normal-core-index power of every group element in the core. No normality of H or continuity of this quotient is needed.

**Theorem 1.6 (Containment in the normal core).**

$$\forall (H: \operatorname{Subgroup}(G)), {\operatorname{FiniteIndex}(H)} \Rightarrow {\operatorname{powerSubgroup}(G, \operatorname{Index}(\operatorname{NormalCore}(H))) \leq \operatorname{NormalCore}(H)}$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Factorization/Galois/NormalCorePower.powerSubgroup_le_normalCore` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Every finite-index subgroup H contains the power subgroup at the positive index of its normal core, through the containment of that power subgroup in the core.
