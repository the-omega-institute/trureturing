# Galois Structure and the Absolute Different

## Abstract

The rational automorphism structure and absolute different of actual common golden cubic fields.

**Theorem 1.1 (Semidirect product of actual automorphisms).**

*Commentary.*

For J actual golden blocks, the rational automorphism group of their common splitting field is the semidirect product of J independent cyclic cubic coordinates by a cyclic group of order two. Its generator acts by inversion on every cubic coordinate.

**Theorem 1.3 (Unramified primes outside the radical support).**

*Commentary.*

For a rational prime other than three whose valuation in every golden block through J is divisible by three, every prime of the actual common field above it has absolute ramification index one. Inertia fixes the cubic root of unity and every positive-root generator.

**Theorem 1.4 (Sixth power of the absolute different).**

Lean statement: `D5/S3/Factorization/Galois/GoldenCubicCommonDifferentSupport.actual_common_cubic_absolute_different_sixth_power`

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Factorization/Galois/GoldenCubicCommonDifferentSupport.actual_common_cubic_absolute_different_sixth_power` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Let R be the product, over the first J golden blocks, of the radicals of each block after removal of its largest cubic divisor. In the ring of integers of the actual common field, the sixth power of the different over the rational integers equals the product of the cube of the ideal generated by three and the fourth power of the ideal generated by R. The proof checks multiplicity at every prime ideal, including the distinct cases over three, over R, and outside both supports.
