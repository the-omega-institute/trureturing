*Commentary.*

Frobenii of coprime-norm primes generate the Galois group (abelian case). A subgroup of Gal(L/K) containing the Frobenius representative of every nonzero prime of K that is unramified in L and has norm coprime to m is all of Gal(L/K). The κ-uniformity realization in ZetaProduct.lean uses this theorem for residues arising from coprime- norm ideal Frobenius values.
