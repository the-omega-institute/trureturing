*Commentary.*

For an abelian Galois extension L/K, σ in Gal(L/K), m ≥ 1 with m % 4 ≠ 2 and (disc L).natAbs coprime to m, there is a family S indexed by H_n = {τ in (ℤ/mℤ)ˣ : |Gal(L/K)| divides ord τ}. These sets are pairwise disjoint. Each lies in the unramified σ-Frobenius fibre of prime ideals of K and has Dirichlet density 1/(|Gal(L/K)|·|(ℤ/mℤ)ˣ|).
