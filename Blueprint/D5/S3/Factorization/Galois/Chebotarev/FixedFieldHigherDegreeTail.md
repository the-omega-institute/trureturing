*Commentary.*

For T₂ defined as the fixed-field Frobenius set after removing degree-one primes over K unramified in L, the partial prime sum over T₂ divided by the universal prime sum over E tends to zero as s decreases to one from above. The higher-degree part is bounded above by a convergent square-power prime sum; primes lying above the finitely many ramified base primes contribute a finite remainder.
