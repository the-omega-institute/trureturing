*Commentary.*

Let E = L^⟨σ⟩. If 𝔓 is a prime of L above an unramified prime of K, its arithmetic K-Frobenius is σ, and ord(σ) = |Gal(L/E)|, then 𝔓 is the unique prime of L above 𝔓 ∩ 𝓞 E. The hypotheses make the stabilizer of 𝔓 in Gal(L/E) the whole group; transitivity on primes above 𝔓 ∩ 𝓞 E gives uniqueness.
