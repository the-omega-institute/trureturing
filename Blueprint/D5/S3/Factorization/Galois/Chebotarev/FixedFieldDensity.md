*Commentary.*

Density-lift through the fixed-field subextension (Sharifi 7.2.2 Step 1, p. 143). Let σ ∈ Gal(L/K), E = L^⟨σ⟩ the fixed field of the cyclic subgroup ⟨σ⟩, and σ_E ∈ Gal(L/E) the corresponding element. Given the abelian-case density over E for the Frobenius-fibre of σ_E (value 1/|Gal(L/E)|), the density over K of the Frobenius class of σ is |C|/|G|.
