# Affine words and typed local quotient coordinates

## Abstract

Finite positive words realize gcd-restricted affine actions; computed local quotient coordinates preserve every affine depth response.

**Theorem 1.1 (Every mixed word retains an affine form).**

$$\forall H \in \mathbb{N}, \forall A \in \operatorname{List}(\mathbb{N}_{>0}), \forall w \in \operatorname{List}(\operatorname{Operation}(A)), \exists a, t \in \mathbb{N}, 0 < a \land \operatorname{libraryGcd}(H, A) \mid t \land \forall x \in \mathbb{N}_{>0}, \operatorname{runWord}(\operatorname{update}(A), w, x) = a x + t$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Factorization/PrimePowers/AffineGcdBehavior.affine_word_translation` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Let A be a finite list of positive addends. The named libraryGcd(H,A) is the fold of natural gcd through A, starting at H; in particular an empty list has libraryGcd(H,[]) equal to H. Operation(A) is the sum of positive multipliers and indices in A. Its update multiplies or adds the indexed positive value, and runWord evaluates a list of these operations from left to right on a positive source.

The quantifier over words includes the empty list and imposes no common length bound. The slope is a positive natural number. Every addition contributes a multiple of the library gcd, and subsequent positive multiplications preserve that divisibility.

**Theorem 1.2 (Every permitted affine residue has an original-library word).**

$$\forall H \in \mathbb{N}, \forall A \in \operatorname{List}(\mathbb{N}_{>0}), \forall a \in \mathbb{N}_{>0}, \forall t \in \mathbb{N}, (2 \le H \land \operatorname{libraryGcd}(H, A) \mid t) \Rightarrow \exists u \in \operatorname{List}(\operatorname{Fin}(\operatorname{length}(A))), \forall x \in \mathbb{N}_{>0}, \operatorname{residue}(H, \operatorname{runWord}(\operatorname{update}(A), \operatorname{cons}(\operatorname{inl}(a), \operatorname{mapInr}(u)), x)) = \operatorname{residue}(H, a x + t)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Factorization/PrimePowers/AffineGcdBehavior.affine_action_realization` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For H at least two, every positive slope a and every natural translation t divisible by libraryGcd(H,A) is realized modulo H. The witnessing word first multiplies by a, then executes a finite list of indexed additions from A. A is allowed to be empty; the list of additions and its length are not constrained in advance.

Bézout's identity places the gcd residue in the additive subgroup generated by the library. In the finite group ZMod(H), the positive addition closure has the same carrier. Closure induction extracts one concrete list of original additions, valid for every positive source x.

**Theorem 1.3 (Exact signed quotient coordinates preserve affine responses).**

$$\forall p \in \mathbb{N}, \forall h \in \mathbb{N}, \forall e \in \mathbb{N}, (\operatorname{Prime}(p) \land 1 \le h \land e \le h) \Rightarrow ((\forall x \in \operatorname{ZMod}(p^{h}), \forall X \in \mathbb{Z}, (\operatorname{residue}(p^{h}, X) = x) \Rightarrow ((\forall r \in \operatorname{Fin}(e), \forall u \in \operatorname{Units}(\operatorname{ZMod}(p^{h-e})), (\operatorname{localEncoding}(p, h, e, x) = \operatorname{S}(r, u)) \Rightarrow (\operatorname{depth}(p, h, 0, x) = r \land p^{r} \mid X \land \operatorname{residue}(p^{h-e}, \operatorname{div}(X, p^{r})) = \operatorname{value}(u))) \land (\forall z \in \operatorname{ZMod}(p^{h-e}), (\operatorname{localEncoding}(p, h, e, x) = \operatorname{D}(z)) \Rightarrow (e \le \operatorname{depth}(p, h, 0, x) \land p^{e} \mid X \land \operatorname{residue}(p^{h-e}, \operatorname{div}(X, p^{e})) = z)))) \land (\forall X \in \mathbb{Z}, \forall Y \in \mathbb{Z}, (\operatorname{localEncoding}(p, h, e, \operatorname{residue}(p^{h}, X)) = \operatorname{localEncoding}(p, h, e, \operatorname{residue}(p^{h}, Y))) \Rightarrow \forall a \in \mathbb{N}_{>0}, \forall b \in \mathbb{Z}, \operatorname{depth}(p, h, 0, \operatorname{residue}(p^{h}, aX+p^{e}b)) = \operatorname{depth}(p, h, 0, \operatorname{residue}(p^{h}, aY+p^{e}b))))$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Factorization/PrimePowers/AffineGcdBehavior.local_encoding_signed_response` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

Let p be prime, 1 <= h, 0 <= e <= h, and M = p^(h-e). LocalCode(p,h,e) is the tagged sum of Fin(e) times Units(ZMod(M)) and ZMod(M). For an actual residue x, localEncoding computes its zero-aware depth r. If r < e, it returns S(r,u), where u is the unit obtained from the exact canonical quotient x.val/p^r. Otherwise it returns D(x.val/p^e modulo M). Nondivisibility at depth r+1 proves the low canonical quotient coprime to p. This includes e=0, e=h, M=1, and the zero residue.

For every signed integer representative X of x, a low tag has depth r, p^r divides X, and X/p^r modulo M is u. A deep tag has depth at least e, p^e divides X, and X/p^e modulo M is its recorded z. Changing the representative by a multiple of p^h changes the quotient by a multiple of M. Equal codes give equal depths after every positive multiplier a and every signed translation p^e b. For low tags, below e the depth is r+v_p(a); after crossing e the multiplied residues agree modulo p^h. Deep tags already retain the full source residue.

**Theorem 1.4 (The local code is exactly the affine response quotient).**

$$\forall p \in \mathbb{N}, \forall h \in \mathbb{N}, \forall e \in \mathbb{N}, (\operatorname{Prime}(p) \land 1 \le h \land e \le h) \Rightarrow (\forall X \in \mathbb{Z}, \forall Y \in \mathbb{Z}, (\operatorname{localEncoding}(p, h, e, \operatorname{residue}(p^{h}, X)) = \operatorname{localEncoding}(p, h, e, \operatorname{residue}(p^{h}, Y))) \Leftrightarrow (\forall a \in \mathbb{N}_{>0}, \forall b \in \mathbb{Z}, \operatorname{depth}(p, h, 0, \operatorname{residue}(p^{h}, a \times X + p^{e} \times b)) = \operatorname{depth}(p, h, 0, \operatorname{residue}(p^{h}, a \times Y + p^{e} \times b))) \land \forall c \in \operatorname{LocalCode}(p, h, e), \exists X \in \mathbb{Z}, \operatorname{localEncoding}(p, h, e, \operatorname{residue}(p^{h}, X)) = c)$$

*Proof.* Machine-checked in Lean as `D5/S3/Factorization/PrimePowers/AffineGcdBehavior.local_encoding_complete` (`✓ std3`). ∎

*Source.* Repository-derived.

*Commentary.*

For every signed pair of sources, equality of the computed tagged local codes is equivalent to equality of every response depth under every positive multiplier and every signed p^e translation. The reverse implication separates unequal depths, distinct low unit coordinates, distinct deep coordinates, and mixed tags by one explicitly constructed response.

Every LocalCode is represented by an integer source. The construction uses an exact p^e multiple for deep tags, and a p^r times a unit representative for low tags; when M is one the unique unit class is handled directly. Zero and saturation are included.
