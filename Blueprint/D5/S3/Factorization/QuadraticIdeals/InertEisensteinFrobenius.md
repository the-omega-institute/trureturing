# Inert Eisenstein Quotients and Frobenius

## Abstract

Reduction modulo an inert rational prime identifies the Eisenstein quotient with a quadratic finite field, where Frobenius acts by conjugation.

**Theorem 1.1 (An explicit quadratic quotient and its Frobenius action).**

$$\begin{aligned}\forall ell \in N, \operatorname{Prime}\left(ell\right) \land \operatorname{mod}\left(ell, 3\right) = 2 \Rightarrow \operatorname{Maximal}\left(I\right) \land \operatorname{card}\left(Q\right) = ell^{2} \land \operatorname{CharP}\left(Q, ell\right)\\\forall ell \in N, \operatorname{Prime}\left(ell\right) \land \operatorname{mod}\left(ell, 3\right) = 2 \Rightarrow \exists e \in \operatorname{RingEquiv}\left(Q, K\right), (\forall z \in E, \operatorname{re}\left(\operatorname{e}\left(\operatorname{class}\left(z\right)\right)\right) = \operatorname{mod}\left(\operatorname{re}\left(z\right), ell\right) \land \operatorname{im}\left(\operatorname{e}\left(\operatorname{class}\left(z\right)\right)\right) = \operatorname{mod}\left(\operatorname{im}\left(z\right), ell\right)) \land (\forall z \in E, \operatorname{class}\left(z\right)^{ell} = \operatorname{class}\left(\operatorname{conj}\left(z\right)\right))\end{aligned}$$

*Commentary.*

Let E be the Eisenstein order Z[omega], with omega squared plus omega plus one equal to zero. Let ell be a rational prime congruent to two modulo three, and let I be the ideal generated by ell. The ideal I is maximal. The quotient Q = E/I has ell squared elements and characteristic ell.

Write K for the quadratic algebra over Z/ellZ with a distinguished element satisfying the same relation as omega. There is a ring equivalence e from Q to K which sends the real and imaginary integer coordinates of every z in E to their residues modulo ell. This specifies the equivalence on all classes represented by Eisenstein integers.

For every z in E, raising its class in Q to the ell-th power gives the class of its quadratic conjugate. This Frobenius identity includes ell equal to two. The statement applies to the actual ideal generated by ell.
