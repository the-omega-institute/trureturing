# Alternating Polygon Real Boundary

## Abstract

Every positive or negative real eigenvalue is a signed canonical real root. Every complex eigenvalue with nonzero real part is real. The dipole and square have only spectral values on the imaginary axis; this statement does not assert bounded flow.

The source extension is the 4 November 2026 note by Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang, in that order. Barry and Kevrekidis retain prior equilibrium, instability and small-spectrum credit. The original Conjecture 4.4 real-root proof and its field, rotation, Fourier and determinant suppliers are reused; no priority certification is made.
